yogakuṇḍalyupaniṣat

saha nāvavatu- iti śāntiḥ

prathamo'dhyāyaḥ

vāyujayopāyatrayam

hetudvayaṃ hi cittasya vāsanā ca samīraṇaḥ |
tayorviniṣṭa ekasmiṃstaddvāvapi vinaśyataḥ || 1 ||

tayorādau samīrasya jayaṃ kuryānnaraḥ sadā |
mitāhāraścāsanaṃ ca śakticālastṛtīyakaḥ || 2 ||

vivaraṇam

yogakuṇḍalyupaniṣadyogasiddhahṛdāsanam |
nirviśeṣabrahmatattvaṃ svamātramiti cintaye ||

	iha khalu kṛṣṇayajurvedapravibhakteyaṃ yogakuṇḍalyupaniṣat 
haṭhalambikāyogaprakaṭanavyagrā nirviśeṣabrahmamātraparyavasannā 
vijayate | asyāḥ saṃkṣepato vivaraṇamārabhyate | 
ādāvavāntaravākyarūpeṇa haṭhayogamavatārayati- 
hetudvayamityādinā | vāsanāprāṇarodhābhyāṃ cittacalanācalane bhavataḥ | 
etadanyatararodhe'pi dvayamapi niruddhaṃ bhavatītyarthaḥ || 1 || tadupāyatayā 
mitāhāra ityādi || 2 ||

p. 308) 			mitāhāraḥ

eteṣāṃ lakṣaṇaṃ vakṣye śṛṇu gautama sādaram |
susnigdhamadhurāhāraścaturthāṃśāvaśeṣakaḥ || 3 ||

bhujyate śivasaṃprītyai mitāhāraḥ sa ucyate |

tatra susnigdheti || 3-4 ||

padmavajrāsane

āsanaṃ dvividhaṃ proktaṃ padmaṃ vajrāsanaṃ tathā || 4 ||

ūrdhvorupari ceddhatte ubhe pādatale yathā |
padmāsanaṃ bhevedetat sarvapāpapraṇāśanam || 5 ||

vāmāṅghrimūlaṃ kandādhaḥ anyaṃ tadupari kṣipet |
samagrīvaśiraḥkāyo vajrāsanamitīritam || 6 ||

tatra padmāsanalakṣaṇaṃ tu ūrvoriti || 5-6 ||

śakticālanaṃ tatsādhanadvayaṃ ca

kuṇḍalyeva bhavecchaktistāṃ tu saṃcālayed budhaḥ |
svasthānādābhruvormadhyaṃ śakticālanamucyate || 7 ||

tatsādhane dvayaṃ mukhyaṃ sarasvatyāstu cālanam |
prāṇarodhamathābhyāsād ṛjvī kuṇḍalinī bhavet || 8 ||

p. 309) kiṃ tacchakticālanamityatra kuṇḍalyeva || 7 || tatsādhanaṃ 
kimityatra tatsādhane iti || 8-14 ||

sarasvatīcālanam

tayorādau sarasvatyāścālanaṃ kathayāmi te |
arundhatyaiva kathitā purāvidbhiḥ sarasvatī || 9 ||

yasyāḥ saṃcālane naiva svayaṃ calati kuṇḍalī |
iḍāyāṃ vahati prāṇe baddhvā padmāsanaṃ dṛḍham || 10 ||

dvādaśāṅguladairghyaṃ ca ambaraṃ caturaṅgulam |
vistīrya tena tannāḍīṃ veṣṭayitvā tataḥ sudhīḥ || 11 ||

aṅguṣṭhatarjanībhyāṃ tu hastābhyāṃ dhārayed dṛḍham |
svaśaktyā cālayedvāme dakṣiṇena punaḥ punaḥ || 12 ||

muhūrtadvayaparyantaṃ nirbhayāccālayet sudhīḥ |
ūrdhvamākarṣayet kiṃcit suṣumnāṃ kuṇḍalīgatām || 13 ||

tena kuṇḍalinī tasyāḥ suṣumnāyā mukhaṃ vrajet |
jahāti tasmāt prāṇo'yaṃ suṣumnāṃ vrajati svataḥ || 14 ||

tunde tu tāṇaṃ kuryācca kaṇṭhasaṃkocane kṛte |
sarasvatyāścālanena vakṣaḥ syādūrdhvago marut || 15 ||

sūryeṇa recayedvāyuṃ sarasvatyāstu cālane |
kaṇṭhasaṃkocanaṃ kṛtvā vakṣaḥ syādūrdhvago marut || 16 ||

p. 310) tasmāt saṃcālayennityaṃ śabdagarbhāṃ sarasvatīm |
yasyāḥ saṃcālanenaiva yogī rogaiḥ pramucyate || 17 ||

gulmaṃ jalodaraplīho ye cānye tundamadhyagāḥ |
sarve te śakticālena rogā naśyanti niścayam || 18 ||

	mūlādibandhatrayapūrvakaṃ sarasvatīcālanataḥ vakṣaḥ vakṣasaḥ 
urdhvago marut syāt || 15 || uktārthametat || 16 || yasmādevaṃ tasmāt || 17##-

prāṇāyāmabhedāḥ

prāṇarodhamathedānīṃ pravakṣyāmi samāsataḥ |

prāṇaśca dehago vāyurāyāmaḥ kumbhakaḥ smṛtaḥ || 19 ||

sa eva dvividhaḥ proktaḥ sahitaḥ kevalastathā |
yāvat kevalasiddhiḥ syāt tāvat sahitamabhyaset || 20 ||

sūryojjāyī śītalī ca bhastrī caiva caturthikā |
bhedaireva samaṃ kumbho yaḥ syāt sahitakumbhakaḥ || 21 ||

sūryakumbhakaḥ

pavitre nirjane deśe śarkarādivivarjite |
dhanuḥpramāṇaparyante śītāgnijalavarjite || 22 ||

pavitre nātyuccanīce hyāsane sukhade sukhe |
baddhapadmāsanaṃ kṛtvā sarasvatyāstu cālanam || 23 ||

p. 311) dakṣanāḍyā samākṛṣya bahiṣṭhaṃ pavanaṃ śanaiḥ |
yatheṣṭaṃ pūrayedvāyuṃ recayediḍayā tataḥ || 24 ||

kapālaśodhane vāpi recayet pavanaṃ śanaiḥ |
catuṣkaṃ vātadoṣaṃ tu kṛmidoṣaṃ nihanti ca || 25 ||

punaḥ punaridaṃ kāryaṃ sūryabhedamudāhṛtam |

catuṣkaṃ caturvidham || 25-42 ||

ujjāyīkumbhakaḥ

mukhaṃ saṃyamya nāḍībhyāmākṛṣya pavanaṃ śanaiḥ || 26 ||

yathā lagati kaṇṭhāttu hṛdayāvadhi sasvanam |
pūrvavat kumbhayet prāṇaṃ recayediḍayā tataḥ || 27 ||

śīrṣoditānalaharaṃ galaśleṣmaharaṃ param |
sarvarogaharaṃ puṇyaṃ dehānalavivardhanam || 28 ||

nāḍījalodaraṃ dhātugatadoṣavināśanam |
gacchatastiṣṭhataḥ kāryamujjāyyākhyaṃ tu kumbhakam || 29 ||

śītalīkumbhakaḥ

jihvayā vāyumākṛṣya pūrvavat kumbhakādanu |
śanaistu ghrāṇarandhrābhyāṃ recayedanilaṃ sudhīḥ || 30 ||

gulmaplīhādikān doṣān kṣayaṃ pittaṃ jvaraṃ tṛṣām |
viṣāṇi śītalī nāma kumbhako'yaṃ nihanti ca || 31 ||

p. 312) 			bhastrakumbhakaḥ

tataḥ padmāsanaṃ baddhvā samagrīvodaraḥ sudhīḥ |
mukhaṃ saṃyamya yatnena prāṇaṃ ghrāṇena recayet || 32 ||

yathā lagati kaṇṭhāttu kapāle sasvanaṃ tataḥ |
vegena pūrayet kiṃciddhṛtpadmāvadhi mārutam || 33 ||

punarvirecayet tadvat pūrayecca punaḥ punaḥ |
yathaiva lohakārāṇāṃ bhastrāvegena cālyate || 34 ||

tathaiva svaśarīrasthaṃ cālayet pavanaṃ śanaiḥ |
yathā śramo bhaveddehe tathā sūryeṇa recayet || 35 ||

yathodaraṃ bhavet pūrṇaṃ pavanena tathā laghu |
dhārayannāsikāmadhyaṃ tarjanībhyāṃ vinā dṛḍham || 36 ||

kumbhakaṃ pūrvavat kṛtvā recayediḍayānilam |
kaṇṭhotthitānalaharaṃ śarīrāgnivivardhanam || 37 ||

kuṇḍalībodhakaṃ puṇyaṃ pāpaghnaṃ śubhada sukham |
brahmanāḍīmukhāntasthakaphādyargalanāśanam || 38 ||

guṇatrayasamudbhūtagranthitrayavibhedakam |
viśeṣeṇaiva kartavyaṃ bhastrākhyaṃ kumbhakaṃ tvidam || 39 ||

bandhatrayavidhiḥ

caturṇāmapi bhedānāṃ kumbhake samupasthite |
bandhatrayamidaṃ kāryaṃ yogibhirvītakalmaṣaiḥ || 40 ||

p. 313) prathamo mūlabandhastu dvitīyoḍḍīyaṇābhidhaḥ |
jālaṃdharastṛtīyastu teṣāṃ lakṣaṇamucyate || 41 ||

mūlabandhaḥ

adhogatimapānaṃ vai ūrdhvagaṃ kurute balāt |
ākuñcanena taṃ prāhurmūlabandho'yamucyate || 42 ||

apāne cordhvage yāte saṃprāpte vahnimaṇḍale |
tato'nalaśikhā dīrghā vardhate vāyunā hatā || 43 ||

tato yātau vahnyapānau prāṇamuṣṇasvarūpakam |
tenātyantapradīptena jvalano dehajastathā || 44 ||

tena kuṇḍalinī suptā saṃtaptā saṃprabudhyate |
daṇḍāhatabhujaṅgīva niśvasya ṛjutāṃ vrajet || 45 ||

bilapraveśato yatra brahmanāḍyantaraṃ brajet |
tasmānnityaṃ mūlabandhaḥ kartavyo yogibhiḥ sadā || 46 ||

hatā calitā || 43-62 ||

uḍḍiyāṇabandhaḥ

kumbhakānte recakādau kartavyastūḍḍiyāṇakaḥ |
bandho yena suṣumnāyāṃ prāṇastūḍḍīyate yataḥ || 47 ||

tasmāduḍḍīyaṇākhyo'yaṃ yogibhiḥ samudāhṛtaḥ |
sati vajrāsane pādau karābhyāṃ dhārayed dṛḍham || 48 ||

p. 314) gulphadeśasamīpe ca kandaṃ tatra prapīḍayet |
paścimaṃ tāṇamudare dhārayeddhṛdaye gale || 49 ||

śanaiḥ śanairyadā prāṇastundasaṃdhiṃ nigacchati |
tundadoṣaṃ vinirdhūya kartavyaṃ satataṃ śanaiḥ || 50 ||

jālaṃdharabandhaḥ

pūrakānte tu kartavyo bandho jālaṃdharābhidhaḥ |
kaṇṭhasaṃkocarūpo'sau vāyumārganirodhakaḥ || 51 ||

adhastāt kuñcanenāśu kaṇṭhasaṃkocane kṛte |
madhye paścimatāṇena syāt prāṇo brahmanāḍigaḥ || 52 ||

pūrvoktena krameṇaiva samyagāsanamāsthitaḥ |
cālanaṃ tu sarasvatyāḥ kṛtvā prāṇaṃ nirodhayet || 53 ||

abhyāsakumbhakasaṃkhyāniyamaḥ

prathame divase kāryaṃ kumbhakānāṃ catuṣṭayam |
pratyekaṃ daśasaṃkhyākaṃ dvitīye pañcabhistathā || 54 ||

viṃśatyalaṃ tṛtīye'hni pañcavṛddhyā dine dine |
kartavyaḥ kumbhako nityaṃ bandhatrayasamanvitaḥ || 55 ||

yogābhyāsavighnāḥ, tattyāgaśca

divā suptirniśāyāṃ tu jāgarādatimaithunāt |
bahusaṃkramaṇaṃ nityaṃ rodhānmūtrapurīṣayoḥ || 56 ||

p. 315) viṣamāsanadoṣāśca prayāsaprāṇacintanāt |
śīghramutpadyate rogaḥ stambhayedyadi saṃyamī || 57 ||

yogābhyāsena me roga utpanna iti kathyate |
tato'bhyāsaṃ tyajedevaṃ prathamaṃ vighnamucyate || 58 ||

dvitīyaṃ saṃśayākhyaṃ ca tṛtīyaṃ ca pramattatā |
ālasyākhyaṃ caturthaṃ ca nidrārūpaṃ tu pañcamam || 59 ||

ṣaṣṭhaṃ tu viratirbhrāntiḥ saptamaṃ parikīrtitam |
viṣayaṃ cāṣṭamaṃ caiva anākhyaṃ navamaṃ smṛtam || 60 ||

alabdhiryogatattvasya daśamaṃ procyate budhaiḥ |
ityetadvighnadaśakaṃ vicāreṇa tyajed budhaḥ || 61 ||

yogābhyāsena kuṇḍalinībodhaḥ

prāṇābhyāsastataḥ kāryo nityaṃ sattvāsthayā dhiyā |
suṣumnā līyate cittaṃ na ca vāyuḥ pradhāvati || 62 ||

śuṣke male tu yogī ca syād gatiścālitā tataḥ |
adhogatimapānaṃ vai ūrdhvagaṃ kurute balāt || 63 ||

ākuñcanena taṃ prāhurmūlabandho'yamucyate |
apānaścordhvago bhūtvā vahninā saha gacchati || 64 ||

prāṇasthānaṃ tato vahniḥ prāṇāpānau ca satvaram |
militvā kuṇḍalīṃ yāti prasuptā kuṇḍalākṛtiḥ || 65 ||

p. 316) tenāgninā ca saṃtaptā pavanenaiva cālitā |
prasārya svaśarīraṃ tu suṣumnāvadanāntare || 66 ||

	evaṃ sattvāsthayā prāṇāyāmaṃ kurvataḥ cittādikaṃ na calati | na ca 
kadāpi vāyuḥ pradhāvati | kevalakumbhakato nāḍīgatamale śuṣke satyatha 
suṣumnāyāṃ prāṇagatiḥ cālitā stambhitā yadi tadā tato'yaṃ yogī 
syādityarthaḥ || 63-64 || yāti yātaḥ || 65 || cālitā kuṇḍalinī || 66-67 ||

granthitrayabhedena kuṇḍalinyāḥ sahasrāragamanam

brahmagranthiṃ tato bhittvā rajoguṇasamudbhavam |
suṣumnāvadane śīghraṃ vidyullekheva saṃsphuret || 67 ||

viṣṇugranthiṃ prayātyuccaiḥ satvaraṃ hṛdi saṃsthitā |
ūrdhvaṃ gacchati yaccāste rudragranthiṃ tadudbhavam || 68 ||

bhruvormadhyaṃ tu saṃbhidya yāti śītāṃśumaṇḍalam |
anāhatākhyaṃ yaccakraṃ dalaiḥ ṣoḍaśabhiryutam || 69 ||

tatra śītāṃśusaṃjātaṃ drava śoṣayati svayam |
calite prāṇavegena raktaṃ pittaṃ ravirgrahāt || 70 ||

yātenducakraṃ yatrāste śuddhaśleṣmadravātmakam |
(tatra siktaṃ grasatyuṣṇaṃ kathaṃ śītasvabhāvakam) || 71 ||

tathaiva rabhasā śuklaṃ candrarūpaṃ hi tapyate |
ūrdhvaṃ pravahati kṣubdhā tadaivaṃ sravatetarām || 72 ||

p. 317) tasyāsvādavaśāccittaṃ bahiṣṭhaṃ viṣameṣu yat |
tadeva paramaṃ bhuktvā svasthasyātmarato yuvā || 73 ||

	tadudbhavam ājñācakramūlodbhavaṃ rudragranthim || 68 || dalaiḥ 
ṣoḍaśabhiryutam- pradhānadalānyaṣṭau, tathā upadalāni, 
āhatya ṣoḍaśa || 69 || yadā prāṇo hṛdaye śītāṃśujaṃ dravaṃ śoṣayati 
tadā tatratyaraktādau prāṇavegena calite sati raviḥ raktapittādikaṃ grahāt 
graset śoṣayedityarthaḥ || 70 || tataḥ prāṇe yātenducakramiti || 71 || 
sravatetarāṃ- amṛtadhāretyarthaḥ || 72 || asyā amṛtadhārāyāḥ 
āsvādavaśāt yaccittaṃ viṣameṣu viṣayeṣu udāsīnatayā bahiṣṭhaṃ 
bahirbhūtaṃ bhavati tadvānātmarato muniḥ tadeva paramam amṛtākhyaṃ 
havirbhuktvā svasthaḥ syāt svasthasyātmarata ityatra visargatakārākārāṇāṃ 
lopaḥ || 73 ||

prāṇādivilayaḥ, vilinānāṃ bahiḥprasaraśca

prakṛtyaṣṭakarūpaṃ ca sthānaṃ gacchati kuṇḍalī |
kroḍīkṛtya śivaṃ yāti kroḍīkṛtya vilīyate || 74 ||

ityadhordhvarajaḥśukle śive tadanu mārutaḥ |
prāṇāpānau samau yāti saha jātau tathaiva ca || 75 ||

bhūte'lpe ca manolpe vā vācake tvativardhate |
dhāvayatyakhilā vātā agnimūṣāhiraṇyavat || 76 ||

p. 318) tataḥ kimityata āha- prakṛtīti |

bhūmirāpo'nalo vāyuḥ khaṃ mano buddhireva ca |
ahaṃkāra itīyaṃ me bhinnā prakṛtiraṣṭadhā ||

iti smṛtyanurodhena hitvā svāttaprakṛtyaṣṭakarūpam | sahasrārasthānam | 
dṛṅmanaḥprāṇādikaṃ kroḍīkṛtya || 74 || yadā kuṇḍalinī sarvaṃ 
kroḍīkṛtya sahasrāre vilīyate ityathordhvarajaḥśukle śive ityatra 
kuṇḍalinīvilayādathordhvakṣaṇaṃ sauraṃ prākṛtaṃ jīvatattvaṃ rajaśca 
cāndram aiśvaraṃ śuklaṃ ca rajaḥśukle sahasrārālaṃkāre 
svātiriktāśivagrāse śive paramātmani vilīyate tadanu taduttarakṣaṇaṃ 
māruto'pi vilīyate | sahajaprāṇādeḥ kathaṃ vilaya ityatra yathā ahorātre 
sahaje saṃdhyayoḥ same bhavataḥ tathaiva sahajātau sahajau 
turyasvarūpāvasthānalakṣaṇanirvikalpakāvasthāyāṃ prāṇāpānau samau 
ūrdhvādhogativiralau nirvyāpāratāṃ yāti yātaḥ | ayameva prāṇādivilaya 
ityarthaḥ || 75 || evaṃ vilīnaprāṇamanovāgādiḥ kathamantarbāhye 
prasaratītyatra bāhye svātirekeṇa ghaṭādyatyalpaṃ bhavatīti bhūtamalpaṃ 
tasmin bhūte alpe ca smṛte prāṇaḥ prasarati | svātirekeṇa alpaṃ vā 
kiṃcidastīti yanmanute tanmanalpaṃ manolpaṃ tasmin manolpe saṃkalpite 
saṃkalpavikalpātmakaṃ manaḥ prasarati | svātirekeṇa vācake tu smṛte tadā 
vāgvṛttiḥ prasarati | evaṃ prāṇamanovāgādirutpannaḥ san vardhate | evaṃ 
prāṇādiniṣpattau satyāṃ 
mūṣāniṣiktadrutasuvarṇavadāpādamastakamakhilāḥ prāṇādivātā 
dhāvayati dhāvanti āpādamastakaṃ vyāpya vartante ityarthaḥ || 76 ||

samādhau sarvasya cinmātratvānubhavaḥ

ādhibhautikadehaṃ tu ādhidaivikavigrahe |
deho'tivimalaṃ yāti cātivāhikatāmiyāt || 77 ||

p. 319) jāḍyabhāvavinirmuktamamalaṃ cinmayātmakam |
tasyātivāhikaṃ mukhyaṃ sarveṣāṃ tu madātmakam || 78 ||

jāyābhavavinirmuktiḥ kālarūpasya vibhramaḥ |
iti taṃ svasvarūpā hi matī rajjubhujaṅgavat || 79 ||

mṛṣaivodeti sakalaṃ mṛṣaiva pravilīyate |
raupyabuddhiḥ śuktikāyāṃ strīpuṃsorbhramato yathā || 80 ||

piṇḍabrahmāṇḍayoraikyaṃ liṅgasūtrātmanorapi |
svāpāvyākṛtayoraikyaṃ svaprakāśacidātmanoḥ || 81 ||

	punaḥ samāhitāvasthāyāṃ etat sarvaṃ cinmātraparyavasannaṃ 
bhavatītyāha- ādhibhautiketi | svājñavikalpitabhautikadehamadhikṛtya 
bhavatītyādhibhautikadehaḥ taṃ tu ādhibhautikadeham ātmānaṃ pratyañcaṃ 
devamadhikṛtya bhavatītyādhidaivikavigrahaḥ tasmin ādhidaivikavigrahe 
pratyagabhinnaparamātmani tanmātratayā pravilāpayet | tataḥ 
pūrvādhiṣṭhitadehaḥ malavaddehādibhāvaṃ vihāya 
vimalabrahmabhāvameva yāti svājñavikalpitaprapañcādhāratayā 
samantādvahatītyātivāhikatāṃ brahmatāmiyādityarthaḥ || 77 || yat 
svātiriktajaḍaśarīrādikamanubhūtaṃ tat sarvaṃ 
jāḍyabhāvavinirmuktamamalaṃ cinmayātmakam | yat sarveṣāṃ dehādīnāṃ 
adhiṣṭheyānāṃ āropāpavādādhiṣṭhānaṃ madātmakam 
asmatpratyayālambanabhūtapratyagabhinnaṃ brahma tasyātivāhikatvameva 
mukhyaṃ vāstavamityarthaḥ || 78 || yadi te parāksāpekṣapratyagabhinne 
brahmaṇi saviśeṣadhīḥ jāyate tadā janitvā bhavatīti tajjāyābhāvaḥ 
saviśeṣatvasphūrtiḥ | tadapahnavasiddhiḥ tadvinirmuktiḥ | evaṃ 
saviśeṣāpahnavoktirapi kālarūpasya brahmaṇo jñānavibhramaḥ 
sarvāpahnavasiddhabrahmamātrasya niṣpratiyogikatvena svātiriktamasti nāstīti

p. 333) 		vāgvṛttyadhiṣṭhānaṃ brahma

	vyavahārikaprapañcadṛṣṭīnāṃ bhavasāgaratāraṇaṃ kathamityā##-

niṣpratiyogikaṃ brahma

tataḥ stimitagambhīraṃ na tejo na tamastatam || 24 ||

anākhyamanabhivyaktaṃ sat kiṃcidavaśiṣyate |

	paramārthadṛṣṭyā kathaṃ tadbrahma bhavatītyatrāha- tata iti | 
yataḥ svājñādidṛṣṭyā svātiriktaprapañcaḥ tadapavādo'pi bhavati tato 
vastuyāthārthyadarśanāt nirguṇaniravayavavyomavat stimitagambhīraṃ 
bhūtabhautikatejastamaḥparicchedāsaṃbhavāt na tejo na tamastatam || 24 || 
tathā nāmarūpāsaṃbhavād anākhyamanabhivyaktaṃ svājñādidṛṣṭimohe
