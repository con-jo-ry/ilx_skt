p. 1a) oṃ śrīgurave namaḥ || śrīgurusmaran pūrvvaka sūryāyārghaṃ datvā || 
karaṃ nyāsāṃgaṃ nyāsaṃ kṛtvā sāmānyārghapātraṃ ca sādhayitvā ||
pūrvvadvāraṃ gatvā || nirīkṣaṇādi ca tu suddhi kṛtvā || pūjayet || 

śānti toraṇake pūjya ghaṭe śāntikalāture | 
bhūlokotha bhuvaloka pradyumnotha prakāsakaḥ || 

svavāmaśāṣayo pūjyā śomāṃgārau ghaṭaṃ jayet |
taddakṣanandinaṃ caiva mahākāla ghaṭāntara || 

gaṃgā yamunā ca taddakṣe ṛguvedo kṛta jugastathā | 
praśānta śiśaraścaiva nadī kalasake yajet ||

pradakṣiṇa krame nete pūjyā bīja svanāmabhiḥ |
gaṇaṃ saraśvatī lakṣmī ūrdho dumbarake yajet ||

pūṣā dhātā vidhā ca triśūla pūjayet kramāt |

p. 1b) īśvarādhipatiścaiva tatpuruṣo aka vargakau |
dehalyāṃ pratipūjet | 

dvāre pūjyā kramenāyaṃ dhvajāyā kumudo nyaset ||

patākāḍhyāṇca vajrena hūṃ phaḍantena pūjayet |
hetukāya balin datvā ete śrotrādike mate ||

athāgneya koneṣu kadalyāṃ siddhikaṃ yajet |
dhvajāyāṃ kumudākṣaśca patākāyāṇca śaktikaṃ ||

prapūjyaṃ ca balideyaṃ tripuraghnāya svamaṃtrake ||

atha dakṣiṇadvāre tu saṃprokṣāstrena vāriṇā |
bhūtitoraṇake pūjyā ghaṭe vidyā kalā yajet |

p. 2a) svarlokoya mahaloka ajaghno jaghna eva ca ||

svavāmasākhayo pūjyo budhaścaiva bṛhaspati |
tasya pūrvva ghaṭe pūjyo bhṛṅgirīṭi vināyakaṃ ||

tasya pūrvvaghaṭe dvaṃde pūjyo yamaghaṭāntaraṃ |
mahimā narmadā pūjyo tatpaścima ghaṭe punaḥ || 

yajurvedo yugatretā tatpaścima ghaṭe yajet |
parjanyāśoka nāmāno nadī kalasake yajet ||

p. 2b) gaṇaṃ śaraśvatī lakṣmī ūrddhodumbarake yajet |
bhageṃdra parjanyā śūle rudrādhipatireva ca ||

aghoro ca ṭavargaśca dhvajāyāṃ puṃḍarīkakaṃ |
patākāyāṇca daṇḍāya hūṁ phaḍantena pū * yet ||

agni betālāya balindatvā prapūjayet ||

atha nai-ṛtyakoṇeṣu saṃprokṣāstena vāriṇa ||

kadalyāṃ kīrttikāṃ yajet dhvajāyāṃ vāmanākṣaśca |
patākāyāṇca khaḍgakaṃ saṃpūjya yama jihvāya balindatvā kṣamāpayet ||

atha paścimake gatvā dvāraṃ prokṣaśarāṇunā |
valantoraṇake pūjya nivṛrttiśca kalāghaṭe ||

p. 3a) svavāmatotha pūjyāsyātaṃjanaṃ lokastapastathā |
dhātā caiva vidhātā ca dvāraśākhe prapūjayet ||

śukraśaneścaraścaiva taduttara ghaṭaṃ yajet |
vṛṣaskando ghaṭe pūjyo śindhūścaiva śaraśvatī ||

yugaṃ dvāparake caiva śāmavedau ghaṭaṃ yajet |
bhūtasaṃjīvanākṣaśca amṛtogha ghaṭaṃ yajet ||

gaṇaṃ śaraśvatī lakṣmī ūrddhodumbarake yajet |
aryamāmitra tvaṣṭā ca triśūleṣu prapūjayet ||

nārāyanādhipatyaśca sadyojātaṃ prapūjayet | 
tapa vargastathāstraṃ ca dehalyāṃ prati pūjayet ||

p. 3b) dhvajāyāṃ śaṃku karṇaṃ ca patākāyāṃ ca pāśakaṃ |
kālākṣāya baliṃ dadyāt dhūpa dīpādi tarppanaiḥ ||

atha vāyavya koneṣu kadalyāṃ dhṛtikaṃ yajet |
sarvvanetra dhvajāyāṇca patākāyāṃ dhvajaṃ tathā ||

karāline banindadyāt dhūpa dīpādi tarppanaiḥ ||

athottarasya dvāre tu saṃprokṣāstrena mantravit |
ārogyaṃ toraṇe pūjya pratiṣṭhā ca kalāghaṭe ||

satyaloko dhruvaścaiva āvahāvivahāstathā |
śāṣayo dvāra deśasya vāmadakṣa kramāt svataḥ ||
rāhuke bhūghaṭe pūjyaude devī caṇḍau ghaṭadvaye |

p. 4a) tāpikā ca payoṣṇī ca ghaṭe pūjye svavāmataḥ ||

vedotharvvā kaliścaiva tatpūrvvaghaṭake yajet |
dhanada śrīpado pūjyo nadī kalaśake kvacit ||

gaṇaṃ śaraśvatī lakṣmī ūrddhodumbarake yajet |
viṣṇuśca savitā caiva aṃśumāṃśca triśūlake || 

brahmādhipatiścaiva vāmadevo tathorddhake |
yaśavargettadehalyāṃ dhvajāyāṃ sumukhastathā || 

gadā ye hūṃ phaṭ patākāyāṃ balimekā hi kṣetrayo |
datvā dhūpādinā devaṃ tarppanaṃ ca purasaraṃ ||

ayai?śānesumantrena saprokṣe puṣṭikaṃ yajet |

p. 4b) dhvajāyāṃ suprasiddhākhyaṃ patākāyā triśūlakaṃ ||

hūṃ phaḍantena sapūjya bhīma darppa baliṃ haret |
atha ūrddhapatākāyāṃ ca kramo? prapūjayet ||

āgneyaiśānta koneṣu kadalī stambhake yajet |
siddhi kīrtti dhṛti puṣṭi nitye tena prapūjayet ||

kambīṣū pṛthivītatvaṃ ārpyaṃ tṛṇa ca yādiṣu |
baṃdhyeṣu tejasaṃ proktaṃ daṃḍake vāyavaṃ mataṃ ||

sthunāsa? cambara? proktaṃ sadyojātādi paṇcakaṃ ||

atha paścimadvāramāgatya saprokṣāstena vāriṇāṃ |
divyadṛṣṭi nipātena divyānuparivarttinā ||

jvalaṃ nārā ca śastrena puṣpa kṣiptvāntarikṣagān |

p. 5a) dakṣapādaistribhirghātairbhūmisthāṃ trividhānapi ||

vighnānatsārayet maṃtrī yāga maṃdira madhyataḥ ||

apasarppantu ye bhūtā ye bhūtā bhuvi saṃsthitā |
ye bhūtā vighna karttāraḥ te nasyantu śivāgyayā ||

dehalī laṃghayan kiṇcit vāmaśākhā samāśrayan |
pravisya dakṣapādena vinyasyāstramudumbare ||

ghāṭayana seṣadvārāṇi madhye vāstupati patiṃ yajet |
pradakṣiṇa kramāgatya niviṣṭo deva dakṣiṇe ||

karaṃ nyāsādikaṃ kṛtvā bhūtasuddhi samācaret |
virecya piṃgalā vāyuṃ gṛhitvā tacca vāmayā ||

p. 5b) madhyayā ca thirī kṛtya tadvāyuṃ muṃnayet tataḥ ||

brahmādi kāraṇa tyāgāt dvādaśāntaṃ layet kramāt |
padmaṃ śivamayaṃ śubhraṃ śravantamamṛtaṃ bahu || 

dhyāyet sadā lalāṭasthaṃ caṃdrakalpita karṇikaṃ |
tatsudhā plāvitaṃ dehaṃ samutpannaṃ navaṃ dṛḍhaṃ ||

citsvarūpaṇca tanmadhye jīvaṃ saṃcintayennataṃ ||

śivātparājñāta mathon tasmāt piṇḍaṇca tasmāt punareva sūkṣmaṃ |
tasmāt paraṃ te ca parādbhavetāmithaḥ viciṃtya khalu bhūtasuddhi ||

anena gurupadeśa kramena bhūtasuddhiṃ vidhāya ||
dehaṃ nyāsādikaṃ kṛtvā vaktraṃ nyāsādikaṃ punaḥ ||

p. 6a) evaṃ ca śakalīkṛtya viyahaṃtaryāgaṃ kuryāt || yathā ||
hṛdayakamalakarṇikopari anantādi pretāsana gataḥ sihāsanasthaṃ bhāvopanītaṃ 
śraddhā kuśumanicaya phala prabhṛtibhiḥ svaciccamatkārānanda sandohā 
bhedabhāve śivatvābhivyakta bhāvana bhagavantaṃ santoṣyediti antaryāga || 

tato arghapātramādāyāsta kṣālitaṃ lalāṭakṛṣṇa pīyuṣa bhāvanayā nīre 
prapūritaṃ || 

āpaḥ kṣīra kuśagrāni taṇtrālāsu manasastilā |
aghoṣṭāṃga ida proktāḥ yavai siddhārthakaiḥ saha ||

etatsamagrāna

p. 6b) saṃpūryaḥ ṣaḍaṃgena mūlenābhyarccyaḥ candanākṣataṃ datvā 
dhenumudrayā prarocya | astrena rakṣayitvā kavacenāvagunṭhya | tenātmānaṃ 
saṃprokṣa | yāgasaṃbhāraṇca astrena saṃprokṣa | ātmāsanaṃ ātmamūrtti 
saṃpūjya | tilakaṃ kṛtvā mantraśodhana mārabhet ||

kaṃdakṣaṃ ulanī sthānaṃ ṛtucakra kramena tu | 
utthāpya madhyame cāgniṃ tatra cidvyakti saṃśrayāt ||

ānandamaramandhyātvā matrīmatra manusmaran ||
tadāso jāyate suddhirmmatra cakrasya matriṇa | 

ityanena saṃsodhya svadakṣahaste gandhacandanamaṇḍalakaṃ kṛtvā | 
dūrvvākṣatena śāgaṃ sādhya devaṃ saṃpūjyaḥ || 

sphurattārakā kāraṃ vibhāvya svaśirasyadhiropayet ||
tato hamādi sarvvajña mama yajña pradhānatā |

p. 7a) ityahaṃkārī bhūya mūlamuccārya svetoṣṇīṣena śiraśi veṣṭayet |
tadanu alakāraṃ kuryānnijaṃ dehaṃ gandhai vastrai vibhūṣaṇaiḥ |

kaṭakādi hārakeyūraiḥ nopurādi vibhūṣaṇaḥ ||

tata aghorāstrena vikirāṇabhimatryaḥ |
jñānakhaḍgaṇca pāśupatāstrenābhimatryaḥ ||

darbhānātālamānena kṛtvā ṣaṭtriṃśatādalaiḥ |
saptajapta śivāstrena veṇī bodhāsimutamaṃ ||

evaṃ jñānakhaḍgaṃ gṛhītvā paṃcagavyamupakramet ||

madhye pūrvvādudīcyante nyaset koṣṭaka pañcakaṃ |
śivaṃ śadāśivaṃ vidyā kālañca puruṣa kramāt ||

supratiṣṭhaṃ suśāntañca tejo vada mṛtātmakaṃ |
ratnodayaṃ ca paṃcātra tadvatpātrāni ropayet ||

gomaya gojalaṃ sarppi dadhikṣīraṃ ca pañcamaṃ |

p. 7b) pratīcyāmuttare yāmye pūrvvamadhye kṣipe kramāt ||

sadyojātena vāmena rupinā puruṣena ca |
īśeneti yathāsaṃkhyaṃ gomayādyabhimantrayet ||

pūrvvaṃ pūrvvaṃ kṣipyedagre mūlamantramudīrayan |
kuśodakaṃ tu gāyatryā mūlaṃ sāṣṭottaraṃ japet ||

pañcagavyamiti proktaṃ mahāpātakanāsanaṃ |
gomayasyārddhamaguṣṭhaṃ gomūtraṃ palasaṃmitaṃ ||

paladvaya ghṛtaṃ syoktaṃ mānaṃ dadhna palatrayaṃ ||
palāni pañcadugyasya kuśāmbapalamātrakaṃ ||

kṛṣṇāyā gomayaṃ mūtraṃ nīlāyāḥ kapilā ghṛtaṃ |
surabhe dadhiśuklāyāstāmrāyāḥ kṣīramāharet ||

abhāve sarvvavarṇānāṃ kapile kā prasasyate ||
svarṇarūpya palāśānāṃ pātratāmrasya vā śubhaṃ ||

pañcagavyavidhānena śiva kūrccamidaṃ smṛtaṃ |

p. 8a) mūlasyāṣṭaśatā labdhaṃ pivedekatra yojitaṃ ||
saṃhāramiva tūlasya sāmadva iva pāvakaṃ ||

śiva kūrccodahatyāsu pāpāni sumahāntyapi ||
pañcagavya arghāmbunāmaṇḍapaṃ saṃprokṣa ||
pradakṣina gatyā nai-ṛtyakone sthitvā ||
maṇḍapasaṃskāramārabhet ||

nirīkṣa prokṣaṇa tāḍana abhyukṣaṇa khanana uddharaṇa pūraṇa samīkaraṇa 
secana kuṭhana saṃmārjana lepana kalā parikalpana trisūtrī paridhāna prokṣaṇa 
pūjana rekhā vajrīkaraṇa catuṣpathāṃte sambhṛtya kalāmayamaṇḍapaṃ 
bhāvayet ||

p. 8b) tato aghorāstu japan vikirāna vikīrya ||
kavacena kuśa kūrccikayānūtanamārjinyā 
saṃhṛtyaiśānakone varddhanyāsanāyopakalpayet || 

nai-ṛtya varuṇayormadhye śāṃgaṃ vāstu nāthaṃ prapūjayet || paścime ca 
mahālakṣmī sampūjyaḥ || yatnena mukha maṇḍapaṃ dhyātvā | atha ratnoṣadhī 
toya pūrṇai candana carccite bhūta palava vaktraiśca praṇavena calāsanaṃ || 

īśānyādiśi saṃsthīto saṃpūjyāṣṭaka puṣpakaiḥ || 

kuṃbhasya dakṣiṇe pārsva paścimāsyaṃ salakṣaṇaṃ | 
yajedastrena mantrena ācārye mantrako vidaiḥ || 

śivāstra varddhinyo

p. 9a) svasvamaṃtrena ṣaḍagena saṃpūjya ||
digapālān pūjayet || 

indrāya vajrahastāya surādhipataye namaḥ || 
airāvata gajārūḍhaṃ svarṇavarṇa kirīṭinaṃ |
sahaśranayanaṃ śakraṃ vajrapāniṃ vibhāvayet ||

āsana mūrttimūlena bali tasmai pradāpayet ||
agnaye śaktihastāya tejādhipataye namaḥ ||

saptārcci pañcavibhrāṇaṃ muktamālā kamaṇḍaluṃ ||
jvālāmālā kularaktaṃ chāgasthaṃ śaktihastakaṃ |
baliṃ tasmai pradātavyaṃ sarvvavighnapranāsanaṃ ||

yamāya daṇḍahastāya pitṛṇāṃ pataye namaḥ | 
kṛtānta mahiṣārūḍhaṃ daṇḍa hastaṃ bhayānakaṃ |

p. 9b) kālapāsadharaṃ kālaṃ dhyāyet dakṣiṇa digpatiṃ ||

nai-ṛtyā pāśihastāya rakṣādhipataye namaḥ ||

raktanetraṃ śavārūḍhaṃ nīlotpaladalaprabhaṃ ||
kṛpāni pāniṃ maśrodyaṃ pivataṃ rākṣasesvaraḥ ||

varuṇāya pāśahastāya apāṃ hi pataye namaḥ |
nāgapāśadharaṃ hṛṣṭaṃ ratnodyadyutivigrahaṃ ||

śaśāṃkadhavalaṃ dhyāyet varuṇaṃ makarāsanaṃ ||

vāyave dhvajahastāya prāṇādhipataye namaḥ |
āpītaharitacchāyaṃ vilola dhvajadhāriṇaṃ ||

prāṇabhūtañca bhūtānāṃ hariṇasthaṃ samīraṇaṃ ||

p. 10a) kuberāya gadā hastāya dhanādhipataye namaḥ |
kubera meṣamāśīnaṃ sagarvvaḥ kharvvavigrahaṃ ||

svarṇacchāyaṃ gadāhastaṃ uttarādhipatiṃ smaret ||

īśānāya triśūlahastāya bhūtādhipataye namaḥ |
īśānaṃ vṛṣabhāruḍhaṃ triśūlavyāladhāriṇaṃ ||

śaracandrāvadātaṃñca candramūli trilocanaṃ ||

anaṃtāya cakrahastāya nāgadhipataye namaḥ |
anaṃtaṃ puṃḍarīkākṣaṃ phanādaśaparite yutaṃ ||

vidyudāmapratīkāsaṃ kūrmārūḍhaṃ prapūjayet ||

brahmane padmahastāya svargādhipataye namaḥ |
pīnaṃ pītaṃ caturvvāhuṃ brahmāṇaṃ caturānanaṃ ||

p. 10b) haṃśayānañca vibhrāṇaṃ daṇḍākṣaśrukkamaṇḍaluṃ ||
vāyukoṇe dakṣiṇaghaṭe kṣetrapālaṃ saṃpūjyaḥ ||

tato astra varddhinī gṛhitvā avicchinna payodhārāṃ śiva kuṃbhānugāminīṃ |

śivājñāṃ lokapālānāṃ śrāvayan mūlamucaran ||

pradakṣina kramena bhrāmayet || 

yajñasaṃrakṣaṇārthāya vighnānādhvasanāya ca | 
yūyaṃ svavāhanāstraiśca saṃnaddhāya śivājñayā ||
iti paṭhan pūrvva śivakalasaṃ saṃsthāpyaḥ ||

paścāt astravarddhinīṃ sthāpayet | śiva kuṃbhe nityavat parameśvaraṃ 
saṃpūjya |

p. 11a) varddhanyā astrajātaṃ sāgaṃ saṃpūjyaḥ ||
āvāhānādi candanākṣata puṣpa mālāṃ datvā ||
mūlamucāryāṃguṣṭhena śivakuṃbhaṃ muṣṭyāvarddhanīṃ spṛset ||

bhagasya hṛdayaṃ liṃgaṃ liṃgasya hṛdayaṃ bhagaḥ | 
tasmai tai bhagaliṃgaya umārudrāyate namaḥ ||

iti bhagaliṃgīkaraṇaṃ kṛtvā gyānakhaḍga samarppayet ||

oṃ yajñasyāsya patistvaṃ hi mūrttireṣātavācalāḥ |
eṣa te gyāninastriṃśaḥ tata gṛhāna svamāyudhaṃ ||

hūṃ phaḍantāstreṇa gyāna khaḍgaṃ samarpya |
śivamantramaṣṭottaraśataṃ japtvā samarppayet ||

p. 11b) aṃgānāñca daśāṃsena varddhanyāścaḥ ||

oṃ umāyai bhagarūpiṇyai liṃgarūpadharāya ca |
śaṃkarāya namastubhyaṃ sarvvānugrahakāriṇe ||

oṃ yathaidaṃ kṛta yatnena bhagavanmakha mandiraṃ |
rakṣaṇīyaṃ jagaṃnātha sarvvādhvara varatvayā ||

iti rakṣāvijñāpanaṃ kṛtvā ||

vāyukone gaṇapatiṃ saṃpūjya || īśāne gurukalasaṃ saṃpūjya ||
atha sthaṃḍile vā śivamūto vā bhagavantaṃ sāṃgasamārādhya ||

namaskṛtya ājñāprāthya agnikārya karomīti ||
tato agnikārya kuryāt ||
