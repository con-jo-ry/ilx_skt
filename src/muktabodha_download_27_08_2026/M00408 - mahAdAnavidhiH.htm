p. 1a) śrīgaṇeśāya namaḥ ||
candanāgarukarpūratta mālajala kuṃkumam ||
kuśītaṃ kuṣṭasaṃyuktaṃ śaivaṃ gandhāṣṭakaṃ smṛtam || 1 ||
candanāgarukarpūra tatmālajala kuṃkumam || kuṣṭaṃ rakta candatam || 2 || 
candanāgaru hnīvera kuṣṭaṃ kuṃkuma sevyaka || 
jaṭāmāṃsī surabhirtiviṣṇorgandhāṣṭakaṃ smṛtam || 3 || 
candanā guruvālā kuḍakuṃkumaśvetavirāya mūla jaṭāmāṃsīdevadāru iti 
viṣṇorgandhāṣṭakaṃ smṛtaṃ || 
svarūpa candane cora rocanāguru meva ca || 4 || 
madaṃ mṛgachayo bhūtaṃ kastracandra saṃyutam || 
gaṃdhāṣṭakaṃ vinirdiṣṭaṃ gaṇeśasya maheśituḥ || 5 ||
p. 1b) candanāguru karpūra corakuṃkuma rocanā ||
jaṭāmāṃsī kapiyutā śaktyai gandhāṣṭakaṃ viduḥ || 6 ||
corakākittavaṃ ścorāḍo'puka sevitoripuḥ ||
silūkaḥ kapijodhūmraḥ turuṣkaḥ piṇḍikaviḥ || 7 || silājit ||
dīpaṃ dakṣiṇatodadyā puratovāta pṛṣṭhataḥ ||
vāmatastu tathā dhūpamagrevāna tu dikṣiṇe || 8 ||
naivedyaṃ dakṣiṇe vāme puratovani pṛṣṭhataḥ || devatāyāḥ ||
nivaraṃ dakṣṇekarṇe vāmecaivaṃ tathādyacchat || 9 ||
dvijātī nāmeṣavidhi strī śrūdrāṇāṃ ca vāmake || 
p. 2) mantradani ||
tvat prasādādahaṃ devakṛttakṛtyosmi sarvataḥ ||
māyāmṛtyu mahāpāśādvitirmuktosmi śivosmiti || 1 ||
iti śiṣyo vadet || uttiṣṭovat samuktosisamyagācāravān bhavaḥ || hrīti 
śrīkāntiputrāyurvalā rogyaṃ sadā stute || 2 ||
iti gururvadet || iti mahākāla saṃhitāyāṃ maṃtradāna vidhiḥ ||

dharmarāja tṛpāṭhī dvijena || svārthaṃ parārthaṃ ca ||
