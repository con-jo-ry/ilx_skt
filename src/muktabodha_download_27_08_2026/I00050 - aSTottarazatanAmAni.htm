Description:
RE31378 Astottarasatanamani. This bundle, entitled Devy­aṣṭottaraśatanāmāvalī, is a compact yet extensive collection of Aṣṭottaraśatanāmāvalīs (lists of 108 sacred names) dedicated to a wide range of deities. The contents include Śivāṣṭottaraśatam, Vināyakāṣṭottaraśatam, Ṣoḍaśagaṇapatināmāni (the sixteen forms of Gaṇapati), Vallīdevasenāsameta Śrīsubrahmaṇyāṣṭottaraśatam, Devyaṣṭottaraśatam, Durgāparameśvaryaṣṭottaraśatam, Lakṣmyaṣṭottaraśatanāmāvalī, Sarasvatyaṣṭottaraśatam, Ramāṣṭottaraśatam, Rāmāṣṭottaraśatam, Kṛṣṇāṣṭottaraśatam, Narasimhāṣṭottaraśatam, Viṣṇvaṣṭottaraśatam, Hariharāṣṭottaraśatam, Saptarṣiṛṣy­aṣṭottaraśatam, and Tripurasundary­aṣṭottaraśatam. The manuscript thus reflects a broad devotional spectrum encompassing Śaiva, Śākta, Vaiṣṇava, and Saura traditions.

The bundle consists of 43 folios, each measuring 11 cm × 1.5 cm, written in 7 lines per folio, arranged in 3–5 columns. Despite its small physical format, the manuscript preserves a rich liturgical collection intended for arcana and daily worship, making it an important source for understanding multi-deity devotional practices and name-recitation traditions.
