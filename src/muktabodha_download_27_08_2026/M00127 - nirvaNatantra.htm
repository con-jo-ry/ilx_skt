prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ |


śrīgaṇeśāya namaḥ

p. 1) kailāśaparvate ramye nānāratnopaśobhite |
viparitaratāśaktā caṃḍī papracha śaṅkaram || 1-1 ||

śrīcaṃḍikovāca

nirākāraṃ nirguṇañca stutiniṃdāvivarjitam |
sunityaṃ sarvakartāraṃ varṇātītaṃ suniścalam || 1-2 ||

saṃjñādirahitaṃ śāṃtaṃ kimākāraṃ pratisthitam |
tasmādutpattirdeveśa kimākāreṇa jāyate || 1-3 ||

śrīśaṃkara uvāca

śṛṇu devī paraṃ tatvaṃ varṇātītāṃ ca vaiṣṇavīm |
guṇālayāṃ guṇātītāṃ stutinindāvivarjitām || 1-4 ||

ākārarahitāṃ nityāṃ yasmād rāgādivarjitām |
pūjanārthāya deveśi svayamutpattikāriṇīm || 1-5 ||

yena rūpeṇa brahmāṇḍo jāyate śṛṇu tat śive |
ākāśājjāyate vāyurvāyorutpadyate raviḥ || 1-6 ||

kherutpadyate toyaṃ toyāddutpadyate mahī |
paṃcabhūtaiśca brahmāṇḍā bhaveyuḥ parvatātmaje || 1-7 ||

brahmāṇḍotthāpanārthāya kūrmapṛṣṭhed hyanaṃtaka |
tanmūrddhni vālukākārā brahmāṇḍo bahavaḥ sthitāḥ || 1-8 ||

kāruṇyavārimadhye tu kūrmaścarati nityaśaḥ |
ahameva triśūlena pālayāmi punaḥ punaḥ || 1-9 ||

śrīcaṇḍikovāca

kimākāraṃ tu brahmāṇḍaṃ tanme brūhi maheśvara |
sṛṣṭiprakāraṃ tanmadhye kimākāraṃ hi tatvavit || 1-10 ||

śrīśaṃkara uvāca

jaṃtvākāraṃ tu brahmāṇḍaṃ nānāvigrahi pārvati |
brahmāṇḍavigrahaṃ proktaṃ sthūlakṣudrādikaṃ hi yat || 1-11 ||

merustanmadhyake devī tathā sasakulācalāḥ |
mūlādimastakāṃtaṃ vai sumerurnāma parvataḥ || 1-12 ||

sthitaṃ neroradhobhāge hyaṃgulyāścorddhadeśataḥ |
bhūrlokādi maheśāni saptasvarge krameṇa hi || 1-13 ||

aṃgulyāṃ sapta pātālāstiṣṭhanti parameśvari |
satyaloke nirākārāṃ mahājyotisvarūpiṇīm || 1-14 ||

māyayā chāditātmānaṃ caṃpakākārarūpiṇīm |
hastapādādirahitāṃ candrasūryāgnirūpiṇīm || 1-15 ||

māyābaṃdhanaṃ saṃtyajya dvidhā bhinnā yadonmukhī |
śivaśaktivibhāgena jāyate sṛṣṭikalpanā || 1-16 ||

prathame jāyate putro braṃhasaṃjño hi pārvati |

śrīkālikovāca

śṛṇu putra mahāghora vivāhaṃ kuru yatnataḥ || 1-17 ||

etat chrutvā tato brahmā uvāca sādaraṃ priye |
tvāṃ vinā jananī nāsti śaktiṃ me dehi suṃdari || 1-18 ||

tacchrutvā jagatāṃ mātā svadehānmohinīṃ dadau |
dvitīyā sā mahāvidyā sāvitrī paramā kalā || 1-19 ||

asyāḥ saṃgaṃ samāsādya vedavistaraṇaṃ kuru |
anāyāse sṛṣṭikartā bhava tvaṃ mahimaṇḍale || 1-20 ||

p. 2) dvitīye jāyate putro viṣṇuḥ satvaguṇāśrayaḥ |
śṛṇu putra mahāghora vivāhaṃ kuru yatnataḥ || 1-21 ||

tava darśanamātreṇa niṣkāmo jāyate pumān |
satyaṃ karomi he māta mohināṃ dehi me śive || 1-22 ||

dehāt śaktiṃ viniṣkṛtya dadau tasmai ca kālikā |
vaiṣṇavī tāṃ mahāvidyāṃ śrīvidyāṃ parameśvarī || 1-23 ||

tāmāśritya mahāviṣṇu pālayatyakhilaṃ jagat |
tṛtīye jāyate putro mhāyogī sadāśivaḥ || 1-24 ||

taṃ dṛṣṭvā sā mahākālī tuṣṭiyuktā bhavan mudā |
śṛṇu putra mahāyogin madvākyaṃ hṛdaye kuru || 1-25 ||

tvāṃ vinā puruṣaḥ ko vāmā vinā kāpi mohinī |
atastva paramānanda vivāhaṃ kuru me śiva || 1-26 ||

yaduktaṃmapi he mātaḥ svāṃ vinānāsti mohinī |
satyametajjaganmātarmāṃ vinā puruṣo na ca || 1-27 ||

asmin dehe saṃsthite ca na karomi vivāhakam |
kuru dehāṃtaraṃ mātaḥ karuṇā yadi vartate || 1-28 ||

tatkṣaṇe sā mahākālī dadau bhuvanasuṃdarīm |
ādibhūtā yathā kālī tathā bhuvanasundarī || 1-29 ||

tāmāśritya mahāyogī saṃharatyakhilaṃ jagat |
śaṃbhuraṣṭavibhāgaśca śaktiścāṣṭavidhā mavet || 1-30 ||

kālikādyā mahāvidyā hyanena parameśvari |
iti  te kathitaṃ kāṃte yathā brahmanirūpaṇam || 1-31 ||

gopanīyaṃ prayatnena vidyotpatiryathā priye |

iti caṇḍikāśaṃkarasaṃvāde nirvāṇataṃtre prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ || 1 ||



dvitīyaḥ paṭalaḥ |


śrīcaṃḍikovāca

tvatprasādān mahādeva śrutaṃ brahmanirūpaṇam |
idānīṃ śrotumicchāmi kṣitausṛṣṭiryathā bhavet || 2-1 ||

śrīśiva uvāca

śṛṇu devī pravakṣyāmi yathāsṛṣṭiḥ prajāyate |
satyaloke mahākāmnī mahārudreṇasaṃpuṭā || 2-2 ||

caṃpakākṛtivistārā caṃdrasūryāgnirūpakā |
anādipuruṣaṃ yuktā tadaṃśājīvasaṃjñakāḥ || 2-3 ||

jvaladagneryathā devi sphuraṃtivisphuliṃgakāḥ |
tasyāścutaṃ paraṃ vidruṃ yadābhūmaupatatyapi || 2-4 ||

tadaivasahasā devī saṅgāyuktobhavatyapi |
sthāvarādiṣu kīṭeṣu paśupaṃkṣiṣu śailaje || 2-5 ||

caturaśītilakṣaṃ vai janma cāpnoti socyayaḥ |
tatolanet pareśāni mānuṣyaṃ durlabhaṃ tanum || 2-6 ||

yato mānuṣadehastu dharmādharmādhipaśca saḥ |
tatopi labhate janma punarmṛtyumavāpnuyāt || 2-7 ||

jāyate mṛyataṃ caiva karmapāśāt suniścayaḥ |
caturaśītilakṣeṣu nānāyoniṣu śailaje || 2-8 ||

śrīcaṃḍikovāca

kathaṃ vā labhate janma kathaṃ mṛtyurbhavet prabho |
tat prakāraṃ mahādeva śrotumichāmi tattvataḥ || 2-9 ||

śrīśaṃkara uvāca

p. 3) ihayat kriyate karma tat pare copabhujyate |
jīvastṛṇajalauke va dehā dehāṃtaraṃ vrajet || 2-10 ||

saṃprāpya cottaraṃ dehaṃ dehaṃ tyajati pūrvajam |
iti śrutvā ca sā caṃḍī paprachaparameśvaram || 2-11 || 

prāse cottaradehe tu piṇḍaṃ dānādikaṃ katham |
śṛṇu devī pravakṣāmi māyādehaṃ tadeva hi || 2-12 ||

māyā dehaḥ pareśāni vāyurūpo na cānyathā |
vāyurūpo yato deha ākāśastho nirāśrayaḥ || 2-13 ||

ataśca piṃḍadānena vāyu sthirataro bhavet |
prathame mastakaṃ devī jāyate cakramāvadhi || 2-14 ||

tato yamapuraṃ gatvā dharmādikaṃ hi yat |
tad bhuktvā cāpare kiṃcit yadikarma na vidyate || 2-15 ||

yamājñayā tadā jīvaḥ prayayau brahmasādhanam |
tasmāt karmānusāreṇa yadi syāt durlabhaṃ tanum || 2-16 ||

mahāvidyāṃ bhāgyavaśāt prāpnoti paramaṃ gurum |
tatvajñānaṃ maheśāni yadi bhāgyavaśāllabhet || 2-17 ||

tadaiva paramaṃ mokṣaṃ yāvad brahmāṇḍasṛṣṭhati |
brāhmaṇasya mahāmokṣaṃ sā yujyaṃ kṣatriyasya ca || 2-18 ||

sā rupyaṃ corujātasya śrūdrasya sahalokakam |
mahāvidyā prasādena punarāgamanaṃ na hi || 2-19 ||

vṛhadbrahmāṃḍaśeṣe tu sarvamokṣaṃ yadā śive |
tadā sarvasya nirvāṇaṃ bhavatyeva na saṃśayaḥ || 2-20 ||

śrīcaṇḍikovāca

vṛhadbrahmāṇḍavāhye tu kiṃ punaḥ parameśvara |
tat sarvaṃ śrotumicchāmi yadi snehostimāṃ prati || 2-21 ||

śrīśaṃkara uvāca

brahmāṇḍasya vāhyadeśe brahmāṇḍā vahavaḥ sthitāḥ |
anaṃtasya pramāṇaṃ tu kiṃ vaktuṃ śakyate mayā || 2-22 ||

sā eva nirmitaṃ sarvaṃ saivasarvaṃ sureśvari |

iti śrīcaṃḍikāśaṃkarasaṃvāde nirvāṇatantre dvitīyaḥ paṭalaḥ || 2 
||



tṛtīyaḥ paṭalaḥ |


śrīśiva uvāca

prakṛtyā jāyate puṃsāṃ prakṛtyā sṛjyate jagat |
toyād yad vudvudaṃ devī yathā toye vilīyate || 3-1 ||

prakṛtyā jāyate sarvaṃ punastasyāṃ vilīyate |
tasmāt prakṛtiyogena jāyate nānyathā kvacit || 3-2 ||

brahmaviṣṇuśivaṃ devī prakṛtyā jāyate dhruvam |
tathā pralayakāle tu prakṛtyā lupyate punaḥ || 3-3 ||

śāktā eva dvijāḥ sarve na śaivā na ca vaiṣṇavāḥ |
upāsate yato devīṃ gāyatrīṃ paramākṣarīm || 3-4 ||

gāyatrīṃ śṛṇu cārvaṅgi caturvedaprapūjitām |
vedamāteti vikhyātāṃ trivargaphaladāyinīm || 3-5 ||

hālāhalaṃ samuddhṛtya nābhyakṣaraṃ samuddharet (samuddhet) |(?)
vāmakarṇasamāyuktaṃ punarvārbhisamuddharet || 3-6 ||

karṇayuktaṃ mūrddhnirephaṃ tataśca suravandite |
vāruṇaṃ ca samāyuktaṃ caṃdrabījaṃ tataḥ param || 3-7 ||

nādayuktaṃ khagayutaṃ caikaṃ vyāhṛtimuddharet |
tat padañca samuddhṛtya savitustadanaṃtaram || 3-8 ||

vareṇyamiti cocāryā bhargodevasya dhīmahī 

dhiyo yo naḥ pracodayāt praṇavaṃ tadanaṃtaram || 3-9 ||

tataścādhītagāyatrīṃ praṇavaṃ tadanaṃtaram |
iti japtvā maheśāni sākṣānnārāyaṇo bhavet || 3-10 ||

dhiyo yormadhyabhāge ca yakāradvayameva ca |
tatra tatra mahādevi anaṃtaśrutimeva ca || 3-11 ||

iti japtvā maheśāni yukto bhavati tatkṣaṇāt |
aṃtyayakārayo sthāne yokāratratiyaḥ paṭhet || 3-12 ||

sa cāṃḍāla iti khyāto brahmahatyā dine dine |
ata eva maheśāni tava snehāt prakāśitam || 3-13 ||

śāvitrī paramā vidyā trailokyeṣu ca durlabhā |
asyā grahaṇamātreṇa bhūpūjyo nātra saṃśayaḥ || 3-14 ||

aṃganyāsasya maṃtraṃ yat tacchṛṇuṣva priyaṃ vade |
praṇavadvayaṃ hṛtpadme bhukāraṃ śīrṣadeśake || 3-15 ||

bhūvaḥ śikhāyāṃ śvaḥkāraṃ kavace vinyaset sudhīḥ |
netratraye bhūrbhuvaḥ svaḥ svaḥkāraṃ karaṃ yugmake || 3-16 ||

namaḥ svāhā vaṣaṭ hūṃ vauṣaṭ phaṭ ca kramato nyaset |
dhyānaṃ śṛṇu varārohe yathā dhyātvā yajennaraḥ || 3-17 ||

śvetavarṇā samuddiṣṭā kauśeyavasanā tathā |
śvetairvilepanaiḥ puṣpairalaṃkāraiśca bhūṣitā || 3-18 ||

ādityamaṇḍalāṃtasthā brahmalokagatāthavā |
akṣasūtradharā devī padmāsanagatāthavā || 3-19 ||

oṃ tejo'si śukramasya mṛtamasi dhāmanāmāsi |
priyandevānāṃ gāyatrī ekapadī dvipadī tripadī catuṣpadyaḥ || 3-20 ||

namaste turīyāya darśatatvāya pradāya parorajase |
evaṃ dhyātvā yajed devīṃ gāyatrīṃ paramākṣarīm || 3-21 ||

punardhyātvāyaṃ trīpīṭhe puṣpaṃ dadyād varānane |
ādaubhikoṇaṃ vinyasya ṣaṭkoṇaṃ tad vahirnyaset || 3-22 ||

vṛttaṃ cāṣṭadalaṃ padmaṃ tad bahiścaturasrakam |
caturddharisamāyuktaṃ sāvitrīyaṃtramīritam || 3-23 ||

jīvanyāsādikaṃ kṛtvā pūjaye tāṃ trivargadām |
hātyahalādikaṃ maṃtraṃ samastaṃ parameśvari || 3-24 ||

samuccārya vaden pādyaṃ sāvitrīṃ te yutāṃ tataḥ |
tyāgātmakamanuḥ paścāt ta yathā vibhavavistaraiḥ || 3-25 ||

pūjayed bahuyānena cānyathā brahmaṇaḥ smṛtaḥ |
dravyābhāve vārārohe pādyādyairudakātmakaiḥ || 3-26 ||

pūjayitvā yajed devīṃ gāyatrīṃ paramākṣarīm |
daśabhirjanmajanitaṃ sa tena ca purākṛtam || 3-27 ||

bhiyutaṃ tu sahasreṇa gāyatrīṃ haṃti pātakam |
maheśavadanotpannā viṣṇohṛdayasaṃsthitā || 3-28 ||

braṃhmaṇā samanujñātā gacha devi yathechayā |
iti maṃtraṃ samuccārya devyā vāmakare budhaḥ || 3-29 ||

samarpayet (samarpayeyej) japaphalaṃ tata stotrādikaṃ paṭhet |
vidhivallakṣajāpena puraścaraṇamīritam || 3-30 ||

tadaśāṃśaṃ hunet paścāt puraścaraṇasiddhaye |
p. 5) homasya tu daśāṃśaikaṃ tarpaṇaṃ tadanaṃtaram || 3-31 ||

tarpaṇasya daśāṃśaikamabhisrekaṃ tataḥ param |
abhiṣekasya daśāṃśeka kuryād brāhmaṇabhojanam || 3-32 ||

tataḥ siddhā bhaved devī trivargaphalasādhinī |
māhātmyaṃ cāsya mantrasya caturvedenabhāṣitam || 3-33 ||

api mārjanamaṃtrasya prakāraṃ śṛṇu yatnataḥ | 
bhūmau śirasi cākāśe ākāśe bhuvi mastakam || 3-34 ||

mūrddhni bhūmau tathākāśe yajurvede sureśvari |
samātharvedinaṃ devi ṛgvede śṛṇu śailaje || 3-35 ||

śūme śirasi cāvaṇyāṃ bhūmau śūnye śire tathā |
bhūmau śūnye tathā mūrddhni cāpanārjanamācaret || 3-36 ||

āpohiśceni? ye maṃtrāḥ aṣṭākṣarapadena tu |
mārjanaṃ yat krameṇaiva sarvapāpavināśanam || 3-37 ||

vyatikrameṇa cārvaṃgī brahmahatyā pade pade |
ata evaṃ kramaṃ sarvaṃ tava snehāt prakāśitam || 3-38 ||

stutiṃ ca kavacaṃ devī pavitvā praṇame sudhīḥ |

śrīdevyuvāca

turiyaṃ dhāmayo deva paramātmāḥ sa eva hi || 3-39 ||

śivaḥ padme sthite vāhye namaskāraḥ kathaṃ bhavet |

śrīśiva uvāca

śirapadme mahādevastathaiva paramoguru || 3-40 ||

tat samo nāsti deveśi pūjyo hi bhuvanatraye |
tadrūpaṃ cintayenmantrī vāhye gurucatuṣṭayam || 3-41 ||

tadaṃśā bhāvasaṃbhūtā ye cānye guravo janāḥ |
tathaiva brāhmaṇāḥ sarve cāṃsāvatārasaṃsthitāḥ || 3-42 ||

yadaiva vāhye caitāṃśca pratyakṣe bhāvayet tadā |
sahasrāre mahāpadme tadā cintāṃ vivarjayet || 3-43 ||

pratyakṣe darśane devī vāhye tad brahmaciṃtayan |

namaskārādikaṃ devi kuryāt sādhakasattamaḥ || 3-44 ||

ebhyo darśanamātreṇa namaskārādikaṃ caret |

na kuryād yadi mohena sa bhaved āpadāśrayaḥ || 3-45 ||

brāhmaṇādīna samālokya brahmacāri yatitrayam |
dṛṣṭimātreṇa girije praṇamed daṃḍavad bhuvi || 3-46 ||

mahāpātakayuktopi mukto bhavati tatkṣaṇāt |
na kuryād yadi mohena mahāpātakavān bhavet || 3-47 || 

raktavastraṃ samālokya tathā tasmāṅgabhūṣaṇam | 
daṃḍahastaṃ triśūlaṃ ca dṛṣṭvā pradakṣiṇatrayam || 3-48 ||

prakuryāt sādhakaśreṣṭhaścānyathā pātakī bhavet |

iti nirvāṇatantre tṛtīyaḥ paṭalaḥ || 3 ||



caturthaḥ paṭalaḥ |

śrīdevyuvāca

sa nyāsaṃ kīdṛśaṃ nātha avadhūtaṃ ca kīdṛśam |
kīdṛśaṃ vā brahmacārī gṛhasyovātha kīdṛśaḥ || 4-1 ||

śrīśiva uvāca

divyaprakāśamūrtiṃ ca ciṃtayed daṃḍadhāriṇam |
vīrasya mūrti deveśi sadā bhasmādi bhūṣaṇam || 4-2 ||

kaulikasya gṛhasthasya mūrtiṃ tad brahmacāriṇam |
gṛhasthasya divyamūrtiṃ caṃdanādivibhūṣitam || 4-3 ||

sarveṣāṃ pitṛrūposau gṛhasthaḥ sādhurūpakaḥ |

p. 6) śrīśiva uvāca

śṛṇu devī pravakṣāmi vṛhadbrahmāṇḍalakṣaṇam || 4-4 ||

meruparvatamadhye tu sarvadevāśrayaṃ priye |
mahāvīrā nadī tatra madhyadeśe sadā sthitā || 4-5 ||

sumeroścāddhūdeśe tu śatyalokaṃ varānane |
adhobhāge maheśāni pratiṣṭhati rasātalam || 4-6 ||

evaṃ krame merumadhye bhuvanānicaturdaśa |
pātālasaptakasyoddhe brahmapadmaṃ sureśvari || 4-7 ||

adhovakraṃ hi tat padmaṃ dharāmadhye caturdalam |
padmamadhye bījakoṣe kṣiticakraṃ manoharam || 4-8 ||

valayākārarūpeṇa samudrāḥ saptasaṃsthitāḥ |
jambudvīpaṃ madhyadeśe catuṣkoṇaṃ manoharam || 4-9 ||

trikoṇaṃ madanāgāraṃ kaṃdarpaścādhi devatām |
aindrabījaṃ hi laṃ rūpaṃ gajeṃdravāhanaṃ śive || 4-10 ||

trikoṇe madanāgāre vindurūpo maheśvaram |
māyāśaktirmaheśāni bhujagākārarūpiṇī || 4-11 ||

tayaiva veṣṭhitaṃ liṃgaṃ sārddha trivalayākṛtiḥ |
liṃgaṃ chidraṃ khavaktreṇa samāchādya sthitā sadā || 4-12 ||

aindrabījaṃ varārohe liṃgasya vāmadeśake |
susiddhaṃ brahmasadanaṃ nādopari susundaram || 4-13 ||

tatraiva nivased brahmā sṛṣṭikartā prajāpatiḥ |
vāmabhāge ca sāvitrī vedamātā sureśvarī || 4-14 ||

tasyāḥ prasādamāsādya sṛṣṭiṃ vitanu te sadā |
sadrūpaṃ brahmasadanaṃ lakṣayojanavistṛtam || 4-15 ||

tat sarvaṃ parameśāni ṛgvedākhye mayodinam |
trikoṇaṃ parameśāni dviṣaṣṭhi tamakoṣṭhakā || 4-16 ||

tadeva parvataṃ proktaṃ sarvadevāśrayaṃ hi tat |
trikoṇamadhye tadvāhyapaścāt pūrvaṃ varānane || 4-17 ||

sthāvaraṃ parvataṃ paśya kīṭaṃ paśumanuttamam |
khagaṃ nārādikaṃ devī nāsti? kiṃ pṛthivītale || 4-18 ||

trikoṇavāhye girije parvataṃ vahurūpakam |
nīlācalaṃ maṃdaraśca parvataṃ caṃdraśekharam || 4-19 ||

himālayaṃ sumeruṃśca malayaṃ bhasmaparvatam |
