darśanopaniṣat

āpyāyantu- iti śāntiḥ

prathama khaṇḍaḥ

jīvanmuktisādhanam aṣṭāṅgayogaḥ

dattātreyo mahāyogī bhagavān bhūtabhāvanaḥ |
caturbhujo mahāviṣṇuryogasāmrājyadīkṣitaḥ || 1 ||

tasya śiṣyo munivaraḥ sāṃkṛtirnāma bhaktimān |
papraccha gurumekānte prāñjalirvinayānvitaḥ || 2 ||

bhagavan brūuhi me yogaṃ sāṣṭāṅgaṃ saprapañcakam |
yena vijñānamātreṇa jīvanmukto bhavāmyaham || 3 ||

sāṃkṛte śṛṇu vakṣyāmi yogaṃ sāṣṭāṅgadarśanam |

vivaraṇam

yamādyaṣṭāṅgayogeddhabrahmamātraprabodhataḥ |
yogino yatpadaṃ yānti tatkaivalyapadaṃ bhaje ||

iha khalu samāvedapravibhakteyaṃ darśanopaniṣat 
nirviśeṣabrahmajñānasahakṛtayamādyaṣṭāṅgayogaprakaṭanavyagrā 
niṣpratiyogikabrahmamātraparyavasannā vijṛmbhate | asyāḥ svalpagranthato 
vivaraṇamārabhyate | 
sāṃkṛtidattātreyapraśnaprativacanarūpeyamākhyāyikā vidyāstutyarthā | 
ākhyāyikāmavatārayati- dattātreya iti || 1-2 || kimiti ? bhagavan 
ityādi || 3 || sāṃkṛtinā evaṃ pṛṣṭo bhagavānāha- sāṃkṛta iti ||

p. 153) 				aṣṭāṅgoddeśaḥ

yamaśca niyamaścaiva tathaivāsanameva ca || 4 ||

prāṇāyāmastathā brahman pratyāhārastataḥ param |
dhāraṇā ca tathā dhyānaṃ samādhiścāṣṭamaṃ mune || 5 ||

	aṣṭāṅgāni kānīyata āha- yamaśceti || 4-5 ||

daśavidhayamaḥ

ahiṃsā satyamasteyaṃ brahmacaryaṃ dayārjavam |
kṣamā dhṛtirmitāhāraḥ śaucaṃ ceti yamā daśa || 6 ||

	daśadhā bhinnayamāvayavamāha- ahiṃseti || 6 ||

ahiṃsā

vedoktena prakāreṇa vinā satyaṃ tapodhana |
kāyena manasā vācā hiṃsā hiṃsā na cānyathā || 7 ||

ātmā sarvagato'cchedyo na grāhya iti yā matiḥ |
sā cāhiṃsā varā proktā mune vedāntavedibhiḥ || 8 ||

	upāyopeyabhedena ahiṃsādidaśayamalakṣaṇamāha- vedeti | 
vedoktayajñīyapaścādihiṃsā ahiṃsaivetyarthaḥ  || 7 || yadvā- 
ātmetyādi || 8 ||

satyam

cakṣurādīndriyairdṛṣṭaṃ śrutaṃ ghrātaṃ munīśvara |
tasyaivoktirbhavet satyaṃ vipra tannānyathā bhavet || 9 ||

p. 154) sarvaṃ satyaṃ varaṃ brahma na cānyāditi yā matiḥ |
tacca satyaṃ varaṃ proktaṃ vedāntajñānapāragaiḥ || 10 ||

	upāyopeyasatyalakṣaṇaṃ tu- cakṣurityādi || 9-25 ||

asteyam

anyadīye tṛṇe ratne kāñcane mauktike'pi ca |
manasā vinivṛttiryā tadasteyaṃ vidurbudhāḥ || 11 ||

ātmanyanātmabhāvena vyavahāravivarjitam |
yattadasteyamityuktamātmavidbhirmahāmune || 12 ||

brahmacaryam

kāyena vācā manasā strīṇāṃ parivivarjanam |
ṛtau bhāryāṃ tadā svasya brahmacaryaṃ taducyate || 13 ||

brahmabhāve manaścāraṃ brahmacaryaṃ paraṃtapa |

dayā

svātmavat sarvabhūteṣu kāyena manasā girā || 14 ||

anujñā yā dayā saiva proktā vedāntavedibhiḥ |

ārjavam

putre mitre kalatre ca ripau svātmani saṃtatam || 15 ||

ekarūpaṃ mune yattadārjavaṃ procyate mayā |

p. 155) 				kṣamā

kāyena manasā vācā śatrubhiḥ paripīḍite || 16 ||

buddhikṣobhanivṛttiryā kṣamā sā munipuṃgava |

dhṛtiḥ

vedāveda vinirmokṣaḥ saṃsārasya na cānyathā || 17 ||

iti vijñānaniṣpattirdhṛtiḥ proktā hi vaidikaiḥ |
ahamātmā na cānyo'smītyevamapracyutā matiḥ || 18 ||

mitāhāraḥ

alpamṛṣṭāśanābhyāṃ ca caturthāṃśāvaśeṣakam |
tasmādyogānuguṇyena bhojanaṃ mitabhojanam || 19 ||

śaucam

svadehamalanirmokṣo mṛjjalābhyāṃ mahāmune |
yattacchaucaṃ bhavedbāhyaṃ mānasaṃ mananaṃ viduḥ || 20 ||

ahaṃ śuddha iti jñānaṃ śaucamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ |
atyantamalino deho dehī cātyantanirmalaḥ || 21 ||

ubhayorantaraṃ jñātvā kasya śaucaṃ vidhīyate |
jñānaśaucaṃ parityajya bāhye yo ramate naraḥ || 22 ||

sa mūḍhaḥ kāñcanaṃ tyaktvā loṣṭaṃ gṛhṇāti suvrata |

p. 156) 				brahmātmavedanavidhiḥ

jñānamṛtena tṛptasya kṛtakṛtyasya yoginaḥ || 23 ||

na cāsti kiṃcit kartavyamasti cenna sa tattvavit |
lokatraye'pi kartavyaṃ kiṃcinnāstyātmavedinām || 24 ||

tasmāt sarvaprayatnena mune'hiṃsādisādhanaiḥ |
ātmānamakṣaraṃ brahma viddhi jñānāttu vedanāt || 25 ||

iti prathamaḥ khaṇḍaḥ

dvitīyaḥ khaṇḍaḥ

daśavidhaniyamaḥ

tapaḥ saṃtoṣamāstikyaṃ dānamīśvarapūjanam |
siddhāntaśravaṇaṃ caiva hrīrmatiśca japo vratam || 1 ||

ete ca niyamāḥ proktāstān vakṣyāmi kramāchṛṇu |

	daśadhā bhinnaniyamāvayavānāha- tapa iti || 1 ||

tapaḥ

vedoktena prakāreṇa kṛcchracāndrāyaṇādibhiḥ || 2 ||

śarīraśoṣaṇaṃ yattattapa ityucyate budhaiḥ |
ko vā mokṣaḥ kathaṃ kena saṃsāraṃ pratipannavān || 3 ||

ityālocanamarthajñāstapaḥ śaṃsanti paṇḍitāḥ |

	upāyopeyabhedena tapa ādiniyamalakṣaṇamāha- vedeti || 2-10 
||

p. 157) 				saṃtoṣaḥ

yadṛcchālābhato nityaṃ prītiryā jāyate nṛṇām || 4 ||

tat saṃtoṣaṃ viduḥ prājñāḥ parijñānaikatatparāḥ |
brahmāvalokaparyantādviraktyā yallabhet priyam || 5 ||

sarvatra vigatasnehaḥ saṃtoṣaṃ paramaṃ viduḥ |

āstikyam

śraute smārte ca viśvāso yattadāstikyamucyate || 6 ||

dānam

nyāyārjitadhanaṃ śrānte śraddhayā vaidike jane |
anyadvā yat pradīyante taddānaṃ procyate mayā || 7 ||

īśvarapūjanam

rāgādyapetaṃ hṛdayaṃ vāgaduṣṭānṛtādinā |
hiṃsādirahitaṃ karma yattadīśvarapūjanam || 8 ||

siddhāntaśravaṇam

satyaṃ jñānamanantaṃ ca parānandaṃ paraṃ dhruvam |
pratyagityavagantavyaṃ vedāntaśravaṇaṃ budhaḥ || 9 ||

p. 158) 				hrīḥ

vedalaukikamārgeṣu kutsitaṃ karma yadbhavet |
tasmin bhavati yā lajjā hrīḥ saiveti prakīrtitā || 10 ||

matiḥ

vaidikeṣu ca sarveṣu śraddhā yā sā matirbhavet |
guruṇā copadiṣṭo'pi tatra saṃbandhavarjitaḥ || 11 ||

	vedaviruddhamārgaḥ guruṇā upadiṣṭo'pi tatra sambandhavarjitaḥ san 
vedoktānuṣṭhānameva kuryādityarthaḥ || 11- 16 ||

japaḥ

vedoktenaiva mārgeṇa mantrābhyāso japaḥ smṛtaḥ |
kalpasūtre yathā vede dharmaśāstre purāṇake || 12 ||

itihāse ca vṛttiryā sa japaḥ procyate mayā |
japastu dvividhaḥ prokto vāciko mānasastathā || 13 ||

vācikopāṃśuruccaiśca dvividhaḥ parikīrtitaḥ |
mānaso mananadhyānabhedāt dvaividhyamāśritaḥ || 14 ||

uccairjapādupāṃśuśca sahasraguṇamucyate |
mānasaśca tathopāṃśoḥ sahasraguṇamucyate || 15 ||

p. 159) uccairjapaśca sarveṣāṃ yathoktaphalado bhavet |
nīcaiḥ śrotreṇa cenmantraḥ śrutaścenniṣphalaṃ bhavet || 16 ||

iti dvitīyaḥ khaṇḍaḥ

tṛtīyaḥ khaṇḍaḥ

āsanāni nava

svastikaṃ gomukhaṃ padmaṃ vīrasiṃhāsane tathā |
bhadraṃ muktāsanaṃ caiva mayūrāsanameva ca || 1 ||

sukhāsanasamākhyaṃ ca navamaṃ munipuṃgava |

yamaniyamalakṣaṇamuktvā āsanalakṣaṇamāha- svastikamiti || 
1-14 ||

svastikam

jānūrvorantare kṛtvā samyak pādatale ubhe || 2 ||

samagrīvaśiraḥkāyaḥ svastikaṃ nityamabhyaset |

gomukham

savye dakṣiṇagulphaṃ tu pṛṣṭhapārśve niyojayet || 3 ||

dakṣiṇe'pi tathā savyaṃ gomukhaṃ tat pracakṣate |

padmam

aṅguṣṭhāvadhi gṛhṇīyāddhastābhyāṃ vyutkrameṇa tu || 4 ||

ūrvorupari viprendra kṛtvā pādataladvayam |
padmāsanaṃ bhavet prāña sarvarogabhayāpaham || 5 ||

p. 160) 			vīrāsanam

gulphau ca vṛṣaṇasyādhaḥ sīvinyāḥ pārśvayoḥ kṣipet |
dakṣiṇaṃ savyagulphena dakṣiṇena tathetarat || 6-1 ||

hastau jānau samāsthāpya svāṅgulīṃśca prasārya ca |
vyaktavaktro nirīkṣeta nāsāgraṃ susamāhitaḥ || 6-2 ||

siṃhāsanaṃ bhavedetat pūjitaṃ yogibhiḥ sadā || 6-3 ||

bhadrāsanam

gulphau tu vṛṣaṇasyādhaḥ sīvinyāḥ pārśvayoḥ kṣipet |
pārśvapādau ca pāṇibhyāṃ dṛḍhaṃ baddhvā suniścalam || 7 ||

bhadrāsanaṃ bhavedetadviṣarogavināśanam |

muktāsanam

nipīḍya sīvinīṃ sūkṣmāṃ dakṣiṇetaragulphataḥ || 8 ||

vāmaṃ yāmyena gulphena muktāsanamidaṃ bhavet |
meḍhrādupari nikṣipya savyaṃ gulphaṃ tatopari || 9 ||

gulphāntaraṃ ca saṃkṣipya muktāsanamidaṃ mune |

p. 161) 			mayūrāsanam

kūrparāgre muniśreṣṭha nikṣipennābhipārśvayoḥ || 10 ||

bhūmyāṃ pāṇitaladvandvaṃ nikṣipyaikāgramānasaḥ |
samunnataśiraḥpādo daṇḍavadvyomni saṃsthitaḥ || 11 ||

mayūrāsanametat syāt sarvapāpapraṇāsanam |

sukhāsanam

yena kena prakāreṇa sukhaṃ dhairyaṃ ca jāyate || 12 ||

tat sukhāsanamityuktamaśaktastat samāśrayet |

āsanajayaphalam

āsanaṃ vijitaṃ yena jitaṃ tena jagattrayam || 13 ||

anena vidhinā yuktaḥ prāṇāyāmaṃ sadā kuru ||

iti tṛtīyaḥ khaṇḍaḥ

caturthaḥ khaṇḍaḥ

dehapramāṇam

śarīraṃ tāvadeva syāt ṣaṇṇavatyaṅgulātmakam |
dehamadhye śikhisthānaṃ taptajāmbūnadaprabham || 1 ||

trikoṇaṃ manujānāṃ tu satyamuktaṃ hi sāṃkṛte |
gudāttu dvyaṅgulādūrdhvaṃ meḍhrāttu dvyaṅgulādadhaḥ || 2 ||

p. 162) dehamadhyaṃ vijānīhi manujānāṃ tu sāṃkṛte |
kandasthānaṃ muniśreṣṭha mūlādhārānnavāṅgulam || 3 ||

caturaṅgulamāyāmavistāraṃ munipuṃgava |
kukkuṭāṇḍavadākāraṃ bhūṣitaṃ tu tvagādibhiḥ || 4 ||

tanmadhye nābhirityuktaṃ yogajñairmunipuṃgava |

	prāṇāyāmasya nāḍīpuñjadehaprāṇanirvartyatvena 
dehatatkāryeyattāparijñānaṃ vinā prāṇāyāmasiddhiḥ na syāditi 
dehanāḍīprāṇeyattāṃ prapañcayati- śarīramiti | śikhisthānam 
agnisthānam || 1-10 ||

nāḍīparigaṇanam

kandamadhyasthitā nāḍī suṣumneti prakīrtitā || 5 ||

tiṣṭhanti paritastasya nāḍayo munipuṃgava |
dvisaptatisahasrāṇi tāsāṃ mukhyāścaturdaśa || 6 ||

suṣumnā piṅgalā tadvadiḍā caiva sarasvatī |
pūṣā ca varuṇā caiva hastijihvā yaśasvinī || 7 ||

alambusā kuhūścaiva viśvodārā payasvinī |
śaṅkhinī caiva gāndhārā iti mukhyāścaturdaśa || 8 ||

āsāṃ mukhyatamāstisrastisṛṣvekottamottamā |
brahmanāḍīti sā proktā mune vedāntavedibhiḥ || 9 ||

pṛṣṭhamadhyasthitenāsthnā viṇādaṇḍena suvrata |

p. 163) 			kuṇḍalyāḥ sthānaṃ svarūpaṃ ca

nābhikandādadhaḥ sthānaṃ kuṇḍalyā dvyaṅgulaṃ mune |
aṣṭaprakṛtirūpā sa kuṇḍalī munisattamaḥ || 11 ||

yathāvadvāyuceṣṭāṃ ca jalānnādīni nityaśaḥ |

svamukhena sadāveṣṭya brahmarandhramukhaṃ mune |

	kuṇḍalinīsthānaṃ tatsvarūpaṃ cāha- nābhīti |

prakṛtayaḥ || 11- 12 ||

nāḍīsthānāni

suṣumnāyā iḍā savye dakṣiṇe piṅgalā sthitā || 13 ||

sarasvatī kuhūścaiva suṣumnāpārśvayoḥ sthite |
gāndhārā hastijihvā ca iḍāyāḥ pṛṣṭhapūrvayoḥ || 14 ||

pūṣā yaśasvinī caiva piṅgalāpṛṣṭhapūrvayoḥ |
kuhośca hastijihvāyā madhye viśvodarā sthitā || 15 ||

yaśasvinyāḥ kuhormadhye varuṇā supratiṣṭhitā |
pūṣāyāśca sarasvatyā madhye proktā yaśasvinī || 16 ||

gāndhārāyāḥ sarasvatyā madhye proktā ca śaṅkhinī |
alambusā sthitā pāyuparyantaṃ kandamadhyagā || 17 ||

pūrvabhāge suṣumnāyā rākāyāḥ saṃsthitā kuhūḥ |

p. 164) iḍā tu savyanāsāntaṃ saṃsthitā munipuṃgava |
yaśasvinī ca vāmasya pādāṅguṣṭhāntamiṣyate || 19 ||

pañcabhūteṣu dhāraṇā

athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi dhāraṇāḥ pañca suvrata |
dehamadhyagate vyomni bāhyākāśaṃ tu dhārayet || 1 ||

prāṇe bāhyānilaṃ tadvat jvalane cāgnimaudare |
toyaṃ toyāṃśake bhūmiṃ bhūmibhāge mahāmune || 2 ||

hayarāvalakārākhyaṃ mantramuccārayet kramāt |
dhāraṇaiṣā parā proktā sarvapāpaviśodhinī || 3 ||

jānvantaṃ pṛthivī hyaṃśo hyapāṃ pāyvantamucyate |
hṛdayāṃśastathāgnyaṃśo bhrūmadhyānto'nilāṃśakaḥ || 4 ||

ākāśāṃśastathā prājña mūrdhāṃśaḥ parikīrtitaḥ |
brahmāṇaṃ pṛthivībhāge viṣṇuṃ toyāṃśake tathā || 5 ||

agnyaṃśe ca maheśānamīśvaraṃ cānilāṃśake |
ākāśāṃśe māhāprajña dhārayettu sadāśivam || 6 ||

	pratyāhāralakṣaṇamuktvā dhāraṇālakṣaṇamāha- atheti || 1##-
