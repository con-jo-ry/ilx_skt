namaśśivāya nāthāya cidrūpānandarūpiṇe | 
śrīmate caṭulāpāṅga  pāṭitātaṅkaśaṅkave || 1 || 
vande tanmithunaṃ divyamādyamānandaciddhanam |    
anuttaraṃ paraṃ jyotiriti yadbhāvyate budhaiḥ || 2 || 
śrīmate naṭanānandayogine paramātmane |    
raktaśuklaprabhāmiśratejase  gurave  namaḥ || 3 || 
praṇamata nāthānandaṃ parayā bhaktyā cidaikyabodhānandam |    
upaniṣadarthanigūḍhaṃ  sakalajanānandabhadrapīṭhārūḍham || 4 || 
puṇyānandamunīndrāt kāmakalā nāma viśrutā jātā |    
ākhyāṃ kāñcidamuṣyā naṭanānandaḥ karoti savyākhyām || 5 || 
     iha hi śaktipātasamunmiṣatsakalavedavedāntatātparyanirṇayaviśāradāḥ satsampradāya-niṣṇātāḥ
sarvāgamārthāvadhāritātmayāthārthyavidaḥ saccidānandasindhuviharaṇapaṭīyāṃso'tra-bhavantaḥ 
puṇyānandāḥ svayaṃ paramātmānaṃ prakāśayantaḥ parameśvaramātmatvena samāviśantaḥ pareṣāmapi 
bhaktibhājāṃ puruṣāṇāmujjihīrṣutayā paramātmaikatvalakṣaṇaṃ rakṣaṇamāśāsate--

" cidvallī "
     atra puṇyānandayoginaḥ puṇyavaśāt svābhāvikabhaktiparitoṣitaparamaguruvarakaruṇā-kaṭākṣanirdhūtanikhilapāśastomāḥ
sakalavidyādhidevatābhūtamahātripurasundarīmantracakrapūjākrama-vyācikīrṣutayā tadadhiṣṭhānabhūtakāmakāmeśvarīrūpamādāvācakṣate
sakaletyādi | sakalānāṃ bhuvanānīm udayaḥ utpattiḥ, sthitiḥ rakṣā, layaḥ nāśaḥ, etattritayaṃ
tirodhānānugrahayorupa-lakṣaṇam | yathā trivṛtkaraṇaṃ pañcīkaraṇasya etatpañcavidhakṛtyapracurā
yā līlā tayā vinodanaṃ krīḍanam, tasminnudyuktaḥ jāgarūkaḥ | mayā'tra prācuryārthe vihitaḥ | 
annamayo yajña  itivat | na tādṛśe cinmaya itivat | kutaḥ pañcavidhakṛtyaṃ hi kāryaṃ jaganniṣṭham | 
līlā tu parameśvaraniṣṭhā | tasmādatra sakalaśabdaviśeṣitabhuvanapadena bhavatyasmāditi 
śivādidharaṇyantaṃ tattvajātamucyate | eteṣāṃ tattvānāṃ brahmaṇaḥ sakāśādutpannatvāttasmin  
sthitattvāttasminneva layāt || tathā ca taittirīyaśrutiḥ----"yato vā imāni bhūtāni jāyante, 
yena jātāni jīvanti yatprayantyabhisaṃviśanti | tadvijijñāsasva, tadbrahmeti |" 
udyukta ityanena śa deva somyedamagra āsīt | ekamevādvitīyaṃ brahmeti | 
svāntaḥsthitaṃ prapañcaṃ bahusyāṃ prajāyeyeti | tadātmānaṃ svayamakuruta" iti 
saṅkalpaviśiṣṭalakṣaṇasṛṣṭyunmukho'bhūdityuktaṃ bhavati || 
tathā rahasyaguravaḥ----"cidātmaiva hi devo'ntaḥ sthitamicchāvaśādbahiḥ | 
yogīva nirupādānamarthajātaṃ prakāśayet ṅgha" iti | ata eveśvarasya jagatsṛṣṭyādikaṃ līlāmātraṃ na prayojanamasti || 
tathā ca hṛdayasūtram----śvecchayā svabhittau viśvamunmīlayati |" tathā vacanamapi----śvecchayaiva jagatsarvaṃ nigiratyud 
giratyapi ṅgha" iti | śrutirapi----"yathorṇanābhiḥ sṛjate gṛhṇate ca" iti || vacanāntaramapi----
jagaccitraṃ samālikhya svecchā tūlikayātmani | 
svayameva  samālokya  prīṇāti  parameśvaraḥ || 
     evaṃ ca maheśvara eka eva jagadutpādananimittopādānatāmayatebahusyāmiti bahubhavanaśruteḥ
| ekamevādvitīyamityadhiṣṭhānāntaraniṣedhācca | pratyabhijñānasūtre'pi----"citireva cetanapadādhirūḍhā
ca |" ityasaṅkocanīcittamityanena paramātmaiva jīvasvarūpamagamadityuktam || śrutirapi----"anena
jīvenātmanānupraviśya nāmarūpe vyākaravāṇi" ityabravīt || tathā lalitopākhyāne'pi----
          hṛdayasthāpi lokānāmadṛśyā mohanātmikā | 
          nāmarūpavibhāgaṃ ca  yā karoti svalīlayā || iti |
     catuśśatyāṃ ca----
asyāṃ pariṇatāyāṃ tu na kiñcitparidṛśyate |
     ityādismṛtijāla mudāhartavyam | tathā chāndogye----śtabdho'syuta tamādeśamaprākṣyo yenāśrutaṃ
śrutaṃ bhavatyamataṃ matamavijñātaṃ vijñātam" iti ekavijñānena sarvavijñānaṃ pratijñāya dṛṣṭāntamāha----"vācārambhaṇaṃ
vikāro nāmadheyaṃ mṛttiketyeva satyaṃ" ityādivacanāt, brahmaiva tattadākāreṇa pariṇamya viharatīti
siddham || nanu etāvatā prakaraṇena jagato brahmasvarūpāvyati riktatvamuktam | evaṃ ca sati
jagadvyatiriktalakṣaṇaṃ brahmasvarūpaṃ kīdṛśamityākāṅkṣāyāmāha-antarlīnavimarśa ityādinā | 
vimṛśyate parāmṛśyate idamiti vimarśaḥ prapañcaḥ | idamityeva hi paramātmanā sṛṣṭasya jagataḥ 
prasiddhaparāmarśaḥ || tathā ca śrutiḥ----"ātmana ākāśaḥ sambhūta" ityārabhya----śa 
vā eṣa puruṣo'nnarasamaya" iti śarīrasṛṣṭimuktvā tasyedameva śiraḥ, ayaṃ dakṣiṇaḥ pakṣaḥ, 
ayamuttaraḥ pakṣaḥ, ayamātmā, idaṃ pucchaṃ pratiṣṭhā, ityādivākyena idamidamityeva 
hi prapañcaparāmarśo dṛśyate || parāpañcāśikāyāmapi----
ahami pralayaṃ kurvannidamaḥ pratiyoginaḥ | iti |
     brahmapratiyogibhūtasya prapañcasya idamānirdeśaḥ kṛtaḥ | evambhūto vimarśaḥ prapañcaḥ
antarlīno'ntargato vimarśaḥ yasyeti saḥ antarlīnavimarśaḥ | tadayamarthaḥ-svātmasātkṛtā-khilaprapañcaḥ
paripūrṇāhaṃ bhāvabhāvanāgabhītaḥ paramānandaparaṃ jyotissvarūpaḥ paramātmeti |
     etacca uttaratra ahamarthanirṇayaprakaraṇe prapañcayiṣyate | ihāpi prasaṅgāt kiñciducyate
| brahma cāhamiti paramānandī bhavati | tathā copaniṣat----"tasmādvā etasmādvijñānamayāt anyo'ntara
ātmānandamayaḥ" iti paramātmānaṃ nidīśya----"tasya pirayameva śiraḥ | 
modo dakṣiṇaḥ pakṣaḥ | pramoda uttaraḥ pakṣaḥ | ānanda ātmā | brahmapucchaṃ pratiṣṭhā |" iti 
pūrṇāhantāviśiṣṭaṃ parabrahma bravīti | nanu ānanda ātmeti ānandarūpatvamātmanaḥ pratipādyate | 
na tu pūrṇāhantāviśiṣṭatvaṃ, tatkathamucyate pūrṇāhantāviśiṣṭatvaṃ parabrahmeti | 
ānando nāma viśvātmabhāvanā || tathā subālopaniṣadi----"annamayo bhūtātmā, prāṇamaya indriyātmā, 
manomayaḥ saṅkalpātmā, vijñānamayaḥ kālātmā,  ānandamayo  layātmā |"  iti | atra layaḥ pūrṇāhambhāvanā || 
vacanamapi----"dhyānakoṭisamo layaḥ" iti cidānandaghanaṃ paramityatra vyākhyānam | 
amṛtānandayogibhiḥ ciccaitanyakalā, ānando viśvāhantāpariṇāma ityuktatvāt evaṃ vyākhyātam || 
athavā jagadutpattisthitilaya-hetubhūtā kṛtrimāhambhāvaparāmarśo vimarśaḥ  || 
tathā nāgānandāḥ----"vimarśo nāma viśvākāreṇa vā, viśvaprakāśena vā viśvopasaṃhāreṇa vā 
akṛtrimo'hamiti sphuraṇam | tasyāṃ tallīnatvaṃ nāma antarmukhatvam ṅgha" tathā 
cāyamarthaḥ----antarlīno'ntarmukhībhūto vimarśaḥ pūrṇāhambhāvanā yasyeti | tathā śrīsvacchandaśāstre----
               sa tu yo'ntarmukho bhāvaḥ sarvajñādiguṇāspadaḥ | 
               tasya lopaḥ kadācitsyādanyasyānupalambhanāt || iti |
     virūpākṣapañcāśikāyāmapi----
               svaparāvabhāsanakṣamaḥ ātmā viśvasya yaḥ prakāśo'sau | 
               ahamiti sa eka  uktaḥ  ahantāsthitirīdṛśī  tasya || iti |
     śivānandasvāmino'pi----
               ahamityaklamākrānta samasta bhuvanatrayam | iti |
     tattādṛśaparabrahmaṇaḥ parameśvarasya prakāśaikasvabhāvatvaṃ sarvasmātparatvaṃ cāha----prakāśa-mātratanurmaheśvara
iti----prakāśamātrā prakāśānatiriktā tanuḥ || tathā ca chāndogye
               yadataḥ paro divo jyotirdīpyate, 
               taṃ devā jyotiṣāṃ jyotirāyurhopāsate'mṛtam | iti |
     śa eṣa saprasādo'smāccharīrātsamutthāya paraṃ jyotirupasampadya svena rūpeṇābhi-niṣpadyata"
iti || ṅa tatra sūryo bhāti na candratārakaṃ nemā vidyuto bhānti kuto'yamagniḥ | tameva bhāntamanubhāti
sarvaṃ tasya bhāsā sarvamidaṃ vibhāti |" ityādi || tathā rahasyāgamaśca----
               svarūpajyotirevāntaḥ parāvāganapāyinī | 
               vande jyotiranuttaram | iti ca |
     parāpañcāśikāyāmapi----śa eko'ntaridaṃ jyotirasastejāṃsi tamāṃsi ca |" iti | saubhāgyahṛdaye'pi----"tanmahaḥ
paramaṃ naumi" ityādi | atra prakāśatvaṃ nāma icchāmi jānāmi karomi ityuttamapuruṣāntargatasphuraṇarūpāhaṃ
parāmarśa eva | tataśca sarvajñatvasarveśvaratva sarvakartṛtvapūrṇatvavyāpakatvarūpapañcaśaktisaṃvalitaṃ
paraṃ brahmetyuktaṃ bhavati || nanu sūryādīnāmapi prakāśakatvaṃ dṛṣṭam | tatkathamasyaiva paraprakāśarūpatvamityata
āha----maheśa iti----mahāṃścāsāvīśaśca iti maheśa iti | mahattvañca deśakālākārairanavacchinnasvarūpatvāt
| īśatvaṃ ca sarvaniyantṛtvam || tathā ca yajuśśrutiḥ----"yato vāco nivartante"----iti sarvaprakāreṇāparicchinnaṃ
brahmeti nidīśya----
               tasyaivaiṣā  parā   devī  svabhāvāmarśanotsukā | 
               pūrṇatvaṃ sarvabhāvānāṃ yasyā nālpaṃ na vādhikam || 
     bravīti | tathā'tharvaṇavedopaniṣadi----"atha kasmāducyate maheśvaraḥ | sarvān lokān udgṛhṇāti
sṛjati visṛjati vāsayati | tasmāducyate maheśvaraḥ" iti | upaniṣadantare'pi----
               yo vedādau svaraḥ prokto vedānte ca pratiṣṭhitaḥ | 
               tasya  prakṛtilīnasya  yaḥ  parassa maheśvaraḥ || 
     "bhīṣāsmādvātaḥ pavata" ityādi || "etasya vā akṣarasya praśāsane gāgī sūryācandramasau
vidhṛtau tiṣṭhata" ityādi || tathā pratyakṣopaniṣat----"yatparaṃ brahma sa eko rudraḥ sa īśānaḥ
sa bhagavān maheśvaraḥ sa mahādevaḥ" iti || parāpañcāśikāyāmapi----
               sa eva sarvabhūtānāṃ svabhāvaḥ parameśvaraḥ | 
               sa eva bhairavo devo  jagadbharaṇalakṣaṇaḥ || 
     iti parameśvaraṃ nidīśya----
               tasyaivaiṣā  parā   devī  svabhāvāmarśanotsukā | 
               pūrṇatvaṃ sarvabhāvānāṃ yasyā nālpaṃ na vādhikam || 
     iti sampūrṇarūpatvaparāśaktiyogaṃ paramātmano vidhāya----
               eṣa devo'nayā devyā nityaṃ krīḍārasotsukaḥ | 
               vicitrān   sṛṣṭisaṃhārānvidhatte   yugapatprabhuḥ || 
               atidurghaṭakāritvamasyānuttarameva          tat | 
               etadeva     svatantratvamaiśvaryaṃ     parabodhitam || 
     iti brahmasvarūpaṃ samyakprakalpya prapañcitam | "evaṃ parābhaṭṭārikāsahitaḥ śrī parameśvaraḥ
| eṣa sarveśvaraḥ, eṣa bhūtādhipatiḥ eṣa bhūtapāla eṣa setuvīdharaṇaḥ eṣāṃ lokānāmasambhedāya"
iti bṛhadāraṇyakoktarītyā sarvaprakāreṇa sarvarakṣakaḥ parameśvaraḥ paramātmā yuṣmān pātviti
niravadhikadayāśālinaḥ puṇyānandāḥ puṇyapuruṣānāśāsate | pālanaṃ cātra paramātmaikatvarūpameva
|| tathā cidvilāse----"bhedalakṣaṇavipakṣasaṅkaṭāt tāraṇaṃparamihātmarakṣaṇam |" 
ityabhedena cintanamabhipretam | idaṃ cātra puṇyānandānāmākūtaṃ sarvabhūtasuhṛtparamātmaivātra 
loke sadgururūpamāsthāya katicana bhaktibhājaḥ saṃrakṣatīti || 
yajuśśrutiḥ----śa cācāryavaṃśo jñeyo bhavatyācāryāṇāmasāvasāvityābhagavattaḥ |" 
"ācāryavān puruṣo veda ācāryastu te gatiṃ vaktā|" "tvaṃ hi naḥ pitā yo'smākamavidyāyāḥ paraṃ pāraṃ tārayatīti |" 
"parīkṣya lokān karmacitān brāhmaṇo nirvedamāyānnāstyakṛtaḥ kṛtena | 
tadvijñānārthaṃ sagurumevābhigacchet samitpāṇiḥ śrotriyaṃ brahmaniṣṭham |" 
ityādiśrutijālaṃ śrīgurūpasadanadvārā brahmaprāptimabhidhatte || yathā cāgamaśca----
               śivaśaktisamāyogājjanmāntarakṛtācchubhāt | 
               śivapūjānucintā syātkarma sāmyaṃ yadā bhavet || 
               śiva  eva   tadā   sākṣādāsthāya guruvigraham | 
               dīkṣāṃ karoti viśvātmā śambhuśaktyanu vedhataḥ || iti |
     gītā ca----
               tadviddhi  praṇipātena  paripraśnena sevayā | 
               upadekṣyanti te jñānaṃ jñāninastattvadaśīnaḥ || iti |

maheśaḥ prakāśamātratanuḥ |" ityanena gurudevatāmantrāṇāmaikyaṃ pratipāditam | katham ācāryasya
prakāśakatvam astyeva | tathā sūtram----"tathā gururupāyaḥ" iti | tathā tadvṛttikāro'pi----"gṛṇātīti

mantrasyāpyasti | mananāt trāyata iti mantraśabdārthaḥ | yathāgamaśca----"mananāttrāṇadharmāṇo

pratipādyate | ayamarthaḥ catuśśatyāpi athaṣaṭkanirūpaṇa-prakaraṇe nigarbhābhidheyayayoḥ pratipādyate----

               tannirīkṣaṇasāmarthyādātmanaśca    śivātmatā || iti |

               śrīmate     caṭulāpāṅgapāṭitātaṅkaśaṅkave || ityādi |
