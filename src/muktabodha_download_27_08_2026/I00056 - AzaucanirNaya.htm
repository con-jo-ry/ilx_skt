Description:
RE30577 Āśaucanirṇayaḥ. The bundle, entitled Āśaucanirṇayaḥ, contains two main sections that address ritual discipline and devotional practice. The first section, which constitutes the majority of the bundle, is entitled Āśaucanirṇaya and discusses the niyama (rules) and ācāra (codes of conduct) to be observed in states of ritual impurity (āśauca). It outlines the prescribed regulations for purity, observance, and daily conduct. Composed in the Maṇipravāḷa style, this section employs a mixed use of Grantha and Tamil scripts, reflecting a traditional South Indian exegetical method. The second section comprises the complete Śrīlalitāsahasranāmāvalī, a celebrated devotional text central to Śākta worship.

The codex consists of 61 folios, each measuring 2.4 cm × 30.2 cm, written in 7–8 lines per folio. The text is complete and well structured, and its combination of prescriptive ritual discourse with foundational devotional hymns makes it a valuable source for understanding both normative religious practice and Śrīvidyā-oriented devotion.
