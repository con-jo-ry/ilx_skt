p. 1a)

śrīgurugaṇapataye namaḥ || 

yadyapi nānāvidhāni sādhanopāyāni likhitāni tathāpi bhāvena vinā maṃtrāa

taṃtra cūḍāmaṇau || 

śrībhairava uvāca || 

mātarddevi mahāmāye vandhamokṣa pravarttini |

śrīdevyuvāca ||

guru kramastu vahudhā maṃtrāvistara gauravāt |
kālānāmapyanāditvāt kathaṃ tat kathayāmite ||

naiva jñātvā gurukulaṃ naṣṭamārgo bhaviṣyati |
naṣṭamārgāt mahāvidyā na tādṛk phalagocarā ||

gurūṇāṃ śiṣyabhūtānāṃ nāsti cetsaṃtati kramaḥ |
taṃtra maṃtrāśca vidyāśca niḥphalāḥ nātra saṃśayaḥ ||

viṃśati puruṣān vāpi nava saptatrayopi vā |
na jñātvā guruvaṃśānāṃ śiṣyaḥ syānnaṣṭa saṃtatiḥ ||

svavaṃśādadhikaṃ jñeyaṃ guruvaṃśaṃbhunāśubhaṃ |
janakādadhikaṃ jñeyaṃ maṃtrādasya maheśvara ||

tasmāt sarvatra deveśa saṃkṣepā chṛṇutān gurūn |
ādau sarvatra deveśa maṃtradaḥ paramo guruḥ ||

parāpara gurustvaṃ hi parameṣṭhirahaṃ tataḥ |

p. 1b)

sarvamaṃtreṣṭa prabhavaḥ sarvatra śubhadāyakaḥ |
śivaḥ śaivāśca ye viprāstepi sarvatra deśikāḥ ||

gaṇeśo gāṇapatyāśca gaṇadīkṣā prabhurmataḥ |
sūraḥ saurāstathā vatsa viṣṇurviṣṇuparāyaṇaḥ ||

divyaugha guravodeva siddhaugha guravastathā |
mānavaughā samāsena kathayāmi tavāgrataḥ ||

sarvatantreṣu maṃtreṣu proktā maṃtra catuṣṭayī |
tatrādau kālikā devī tasyāḥ śṛṇu gurukramaṃ ||

mahādevi mahākālastripuraścaiva bhairavaḥ |
divyaugha guravaḥ proktā siddhaughān kathayāmite ||

brahmānandaḥ pūrṇadevaścala vittaścalācalaḥ |
kumāraḥ krodhanaścaiva varadaḥ smaradīpanaḥ ||

māyā māyāvatī caiva mānavaughān śṛṇu priyaṃ |
vimanaḥ kṛpaṇaścaiva bhīmasenaḥ sudhākaraḥ ||

p. 2a)

ūrddhvakeśo vyomakeśo nīlakaṇṭho vṛṣadhvajaḥ |
divyaughāḥ siddhidā vatsa śṛṇuṣvāvahitātmanā ||

vaśiṣṭha kūrmanāthaśca nīlanātho maheśvara |
harinātho mānavaughānatha vakṣyāmi sadgurūn ||

tārāvatī bhānumatī jayā vidyā mahodarī |
mukhānandaḥ parānandaḥ pārijātaḥ kuleśvaraḥ ||

virūpākṣau bhairavī ca kathitaṃ tāriṇī kulaṃ |
siddhaughe mānavaughe ca divyaughe parameśvara ||

akṣaubhyaḥ sarvamūrddhanyastrimūrttirnnāga rūpadhṛk |
atha svapnāvatī devān kathayāmi tava priye ||

parānandaśca pūrṇeśo devadevo janārdanaḥ |
pūrṇeśī sundarī caiva mohinī ratikāmike ||

pulastyo bhāvasaṃpanno vīrākarṣaṇakarmiṇaḥ |
siddhaughe ca satī devī bhairavaścandraśekharaḥ ||

mānavaughe satī devī kṣamāpūrṇā kalāvatī |
maheśo viṣṇunāmā ca gaṃgeśaḥ smaranāyakaḥ ||

p. 2b)

kalānandaḥ kumārī ca sarvāśca saṃskṛtastriyaḥ |
vidagdhāḥ sarvajātīnāṃ sarvāvasthā gatāśca yā ||

vālā vā yauvanonmattā niḥpatirvā sa putrikā |
gurubhāvaṃ parityajya yoṣitsu yadi sādhakaḥ ||

svapnāvatī jayedvārcā manasā vapuṣāpi vā |
sarvadoṣasamāyuktaḥ tyājyosau kulapāṃśanaḥ ||

tasmāt sarvaprayatnena vipriyaṃ parayoṣiti |
nijāsvapi ca kāmārthaṃ madaṃśāyoṣito yataḥ ||

atha vakṣyāmi te vatsa ṣoḍaśīnāṃ gurukramaṃ |
ānandanātha devaśca paraprakāśakastathā ||

tataḥ paraśivo devaḥ paraśaktistataḥ paraṃ |
kauleśvaraḥ śaktidevo'kuleśānaśca kāmukaḥ ||

suddhaughān kathayiṣyāmi bhogakṣinnaśca bhairava |
samayo deva sahajau mānavaughān śṛṇu priya ||

gagano viṣṇu vimalau sahajo bhuvanastathā |
nīlasvādupriyaḥ paścāt kathitā gurusattamāḥ ||

śrībhairava uvāca ||

vidyā catuṣṭayī proktā durgayā sadṛśī huta |
tāṃ vihāya kathaṃ tārā proktāṃ parama durlabhā ||

p. 3a)

yathā tārā tathā durgā vismayo nāsti yadyapi ||

tathāpi tatprakāśāya bhayaṃ jātaṃ pade pade |

kṛṣṇaḥ kālo kalānātho divyaughā bhairavādikāḥ ||

nāradaḥ kaśyapaḥ śaṃbhurbhārgavaḥ kulakaulikaḥ |
ete paṃcamahādeva siddhaughā parikīrtitāḥ ||

rudrācāryaḥ kṣamācāryaḥ pavanāśana saṃjñakaḥ |
kumārīśaḥ śaktidharo ghanānaṃdaḥ prakāśakaḥ ||

hariśarmā viṣṇuśarmā dattātreyaḥ priyaṃvadaḥ |
vahulā śākinī devī caryānāthaḥ prakīrtitaḥ ||

lopāmudrā gurūn vakṣye prasaṃgā chṛṇu bhairava |
ādau pareśvaro devaḥ parameśī tataḥ paraṃ ||

mitrīśaścaiva sajjīśo uḍḍīśo divyasādhakāḥ |
caryānātho lopāmudrā agastyaḥ kāladīpanaḥ ||

mānavaughāstato vakṣye dharmācāryāstataḥ paraṃ |
muktakeśīśvaro deva dīpakonātha eva ca ||

viṣṇuḥ prabhākaraścaiva manojñastadanaṃtaraṃ |

p. 3b)

kalyāṇa devaratna devau śrīrāmānaṃda devakaḥ ||

ānandanāthaśabdāntā guravaḥ sarvasiddhidāḥ |
atha vā sarvaśāstreṣu guravaḥ sarvasūcitāḥ ||

kulacūḍāmaṇau deva śṛṇu deva maheśvarā |
yeṣu yeṣu ca maṃtreṣu ye ye ṛṣigaṇaḥ smṛtāḥ ||

te te pūjyāḥ sa pūjyādau saṃkṣepādgaditaṃ mayā |
ajñānādguru nāmnāṃ vai guru tritayamarccayet ||

catuṣṭayaṃ vā sapto vā na kāryaśca tataḥ paraṃ |
gurūnparamaguruṃścaiva parāpara gurūṃstataḥ ||

parameṣṭhi gurūṃścaiva kathitā guravastathā |
gurūpūjāṃ vinā vatsa yadi pūjāṃ samācaret ||

taddoṣa śamane vatsa kulapūjāṃ samācaret |
triguṇaṃ yatra yad vā syāt sa guṇaṃ kulapūjanāt ||

kulāvalokanaṃ vetsyāt kutaḥ prokṣaṇamārjanaṃ |
kvastaniṃ kva ca vā chuddhiḥ kvavanyāsa viśodhanaṃ ||

dīkṣā prabhukulīnaścet kulaṃ vā vaṭukeśvara |
tat dehe gurumānīya tvat pāde smaraṇaṃ caret ||

na jānāmi gurornāma na ca śuddhiṃ maheśvara |
tvat pādāmvujareṇūnāṃ kṛpayā dehi dāsatāṃ ||

p. 4a)

iti stavena saṃstutya nijaṃ kulaṃ gurūṃ yadi |

na deyaṃ yatra kutrāpi yo pi ceṣṭe ca cuṃvake ||

yojayitvā tato jīvaṃ nītvā nādāvasānakaṃ ||

gamāgamaṃ kārayitvā siddho bhavati mānavaḥ |
pīyate khādyate yattastatsarvaṃ kuṇḍalī mukhe ||

hutvā siddhi mavāpnoti na sa baṃdhena budhyate |
bhitvā kāryā na catvāryaṃ kuṇḍalinyā kṛte punaḥ ||

hemātarddehime bhikṣāṃ kuṇḍalī tarpayāmyahaṃ |
avadhūtāmra me sthitvā bhairavānaṃda tatparaḥ ||

bhairavohaṃ na cānyosmin na devomatparaḥ kvacit |
maṃtra tatrārddha na sarvamayijātaṃ na cānyathā ||

p. 8a)

śivohaṃ bhairavohaṃ nādo maṃtroha kulanāyakaḥ |
raktacandanadigdhāṃ pariktakaupīnabhūṣitaḥ ||

raktamālyāmvaradharohetu yuktaḥ sambhavet(?) |
evaṃ bhikṣāṃ vrajan bhikṣuḥ bhairavācāra tatparaḥ ||

lipyate na ca pāpena vadhyate na tu jaṃtunā |
yatra dveṣe tvayaṃ yāti saṃdeśaḥ kṣiti nāyakaḥ ||

taṃtra nindā parā ye tu te naśyaṃti kṣaṇāt prabho |
tasmādyatrāt pūjitavyo na tiraskāra eva ca ||

yatechā vidyate tasya tatkāryaṃ priyamapriyaṃ |
tadanye devadeveśa vilopācāravistaraṃ ||

pūjayanti mahādevī kulāṃga dṛṣṭimātrataḥ |
puṣpaṃgaṃdhaṃ ca tāṃvūlaṃ naivedyaṃ yatra tadbhavet ||

manasā tatsamutsṛjya vāhyato kulavāriṇā |
ātmanai tatsamāyojya devī rūpaṃ kuleśvara ||

na pūjāṃ nārpitaṃ nyāsa na niṣṭhā na vratādikaṃ |
pūrṇohaṃ bhairavaścāhaṃ nityānaṃdohamavyayaḥ ||

niraṃjana svarūpohaṃ niraṃkārastvahaṃ prabhuḥ |
sarvaśāstrādi yuktohaṃ sarvamaṃtrārtha pāragaḥ ||

nātmātparataro devo na jāto na janiṣyati |
ānandarūpavān bhūtvā sarveṣāṃ priyakārakaḥ ||

p. 8b)

muktadurgaṃdha matsyādi tyājyaṃ divyavaliṃ priya |
nānāsugaṃdhasaṃyuktaṃ māṃsādi bhojanaṃ caret ||

sugaṃdhāmalatailādyairnānāgaṃdhāṃgavistaraiḥ |
snānaṃ kāryaṃ cāti muddhiṃ sūkṣmāṃ vāsobhidhāpayet ||

sugaṃdhi puṣpamālābhirmuktālaṃkārabhūṣaṇaiḥ |
snigdhaṃ sumadhuraṃ dehaṃ kāmarūpaṃ manoharaṃ ||

kṛtvā tu maṃgalaṃ dehaṃ mṛdubhāṣaṇa tatpara |
kulālāpānu saṃdhāyīṃ kularūpaṃ vicintya ca ||

vihared divase caiva rātrā vā hṛṣṭi tatparaḥ |
ānīya yoṣitaḥ sarvājātyaṣṭaka samudbhavāḥ ||

gaṃdhānukramanāddeva nāma kuryānmaheśvara |
yā syāt prathama śaktistu śuddhāntaḥ karaṇā dvijā ||

śuddhāpi malamattāṃgī divyagaṃdhā manoramā |
sā hi svapnāvatī devī tanmaṃtraṃ tatra yojayet ||

śuddha puṣpaiśca naivedyairllavaṇairmadhusaṃyutaiḥ |
pūjayitvā yathā pūrvaṃ sādhayennija sādhanaṃ ||

yā kṛṣṇā nīla kṛṣṇābhā madhu piṃgalalocanā |
sādhakānāṃ *? hṛdayāyugalā nityagaṃdhikā ||

tasyāṃ kālī samāyonyā sā kālī parikīrtitā |
tatpuṣpe naiva tāṃ kālī tatrai vedyaiśca pūjayet ||

p. 9a)

yā syāt tṛtīyagaurāṃgīṣu sugaṃdhānu locanā |
tripurā nāma sā devī raktapuṣpeṇa pūjayet ||

madagaṃdhā tu yā nārī madasphālanamandhalā |
muktā āmiṣagaṃdhena mahiṣāsuramardinī ||

madya gaṃdhā tu yā nārī rakta netrā vinisthulā |
sogratāreti saṃproktā patyuḥ pāpavināśinī ||

aśvagaṃdhā tu yā nārī dīrghājaṃghāmanoharā |
sāsyāccakita lolākṣī aśvārūḍhāvarapradā ||

āmagaṃdhā ca vadane pūtigaṃdhā ca saṃdhiṣu |
nīlavarvarakeśīṃ yā vidyādurgāmahotsukhī ||

sarvalakṣaṇasaṃyuktā nānāgaṃdhasamāgatā |
sudatī cārūkeśī yā dīrghanetrāmanoharā ||

nānāvilāsa kuśalā kulaśāstravicāriṇī |
guruvakṣasthalākrāṃta madhyāviraladarśinī ||

sudatī sā bhavedgaurī sarvakāryapravarddhinī |
etad gaṇaiḥ samāyuktā nānā vā ekajātayā ||

rūpagaṃdhasamāyuktā nāmataḥ parikīrtitā |
rakṣitavyā prayatnena akṣatāstā yathā bhavet ||

svayaṃ kāmeśvaro nāmnā bhūtvā kāmaparāyaṇaḥ |

p. 9b)

bhāvavṛddhiṃ vidhāyaiva mūlayogaṃ samācaret |
mūlayoge kṛte tatra saptāhāt maraṇaṃ bhavet ||

na viṣṇurna hariḥ śaṃbhurnna devī na ca ṣoḍaśī |
na ca mṛtyuṃjayo vāpi na nyāso nāpi bhāvanā ||

tasya mṛtyukṣayo deva prabhavaṃti na devatāḥ |
sā nārī mūlayogena nāgadaṃṣṭu vidhena vā ||

vajreṇābhidyagarbhe ca mahāvyādhi vaśādapi |
mriyate tena pāpena mahādeva suniścitaṃ ||

devakṛtyā tu vahudhā dṛṣṭādṛṣṭavibhedataḥ |
viśeṣatostrādeveśa kimanyad vahujalpitaiḥ ||

māyayāchādya cātmānaṃ nijastrī rūpadhāriṇī |
āgatya ramate nityaṃ tenaiva naśyate dhruvaṃ ||

tasmāt svadeśe naiva tatkartavyaṃ siddhivāṃchayā |
kiṃ vā kāmavilopārthaṃ yatitavyaṃ sureśvare ||

karmadaṇḍaṃ samādāya śivasāmyaṃ nidhāya ca |
tatpātraṃ cakra sūtreṇa veṣṭanaṃ saptadhā kṛtaṃ ||

pāśatvaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ rakṣaṇāyasvayaṃbhuddā? |
nirjitosi mahābhāga ugravaṃdhavidhīyatāṃ ||

eva mā maṃtrya tatsūtraṃ śivāṅge saptaveṣṭhitaṃ |
jāgraddṛśāṃ samāsādya svāpamaṃtraṃ tato japet ||

p. 10a)

saṃhāra svapnatodhinyā puṭitaṃ praṇava dvayaṃ |
svapnamaṃtrasamākhyātaḥ svāpadaḥ sarvajaṃtuṣu ||

evaṃ kṛtvā svayaṃ devo maheśādikya eva ca |
kulaṃ yadi syād yogeśa yaśca saṃsmaralolupa ||

tadāpekṣā vidhātavyā kāryāśca karhicidbudhaiḥ |
varāṅga śṛṇu madvākyamāvaya śubhahetukaṃ ||

etatkāryasamāsādya kartavyaṃ tavaveṣṭhitaṃ |
evamuktā nijaṃ kāryaṃ vidhāya sādhakottamaiḥ ||

vaktavyaṃ na ca kartavyaṃ kulayogaṃ maheśvaraḥ |
vīrapatnīvīrakanyā vīrāṃgajavarāṃganā ||

nāhṛṣyāsādhakaiḥ pūrvaṃ yāvat syāt mama saṃsthitiḥ |
manaḥ kṣobhe tatra yāte mūlayogena yatphalaṃ ||

tatphalaṃ jāyate deva satyaṃ satyaṃ na cānyathā |
vīrapatnī tu paramāsvayameva maheśvarī ||

tatsutākula vidyānāmādyāvaiti purātanī |
tadvadhū bhuvaneśānī sarvamaṃtraprabodhanī ||

nyāyato'nyāyato vāpi vīrapatnī gurūryadi |
vīkṣaṇādeva sarvārthaṃ * * * sādhako bhavet ||

yadi bhāgyaṃ vaśāddeva vīrapatnī tu labhyate |

p. 10b)

phalapākaṃ tatra kṛtvā kinnasiddhyaṃti bhūtale ||

phalapākavidhānaṃ tu sarvadeva namaskṛtaṃ |
kathayāmi mahādeva na prakāśyaṃ kathaṃcana ||

dṛṣṭādṛṣṭa kulaṃ deva devagaṃdharvakiṃnarāḥ |
āyāṃti phalapākārthaṃ tasmādyatnaviciṃtayet ||

visarjanaṃ vidhātavyaṃ gopanaṃ bhāvaśāṃtikaṃ |
tattatkāryavidhānena kulaṃ kuryāt suśikṣitaṃ ||

mātaḥ pratidunā dharmā dharmo na hi jagat traye |
patiḥ prāṇaparonityaṃ patireva parāgatiḥ ||

patireva paraṃ brahmapatiḥ prāṇādhikaḥ prabhuḥ |
sarvatīrthamayaḥ svāmi sarvadevasamaḥpatiḥ ||

sarvadeva mayañcaiva patireva mahāguruḥ |
āgatyopi viśetpārśvaṃ mukhaṃ vīkṣyasthitā bhavet ||

patibhāveṃgite dakṣā kṛṣṇāsyātpatidarśane |
uttareṇottaraṃ dadyāt pāhareddūrakarmaṇi ||

sarvadevamayaṃ hitvā yā nārīparagocarā |
śūkarīgarddabhīkākīśṛgālī jāyate dhruvaṃ ||

ahaṃ tu putravad devi namebhrītirna cetrayā |
tava saṃge yo hi yāti pati rūpadharaḥ kvacit ||

p. 11a)

mūlamaṃtra japantoyaṃ tadgātre deyamīśvarī |
tayā yadi vahirna syāt tadā svapatireva ca ||

itopi śiṣya yā kāryā śikṣitā kulayoṣitā |
rakṣākāryā vidhānena pūrvokte naiva cātmanā ||

yoginīnāṃ mahāpūjāṃ vaṭukānāṃ ca bhairavaṃ |
kāryāvighnavināśārtha kulavārarkṣayogataḥ ||

yāsāṃ mūlatarormūle mūlavidyā tu kāmikā |
likhitvāneka kāleṣu tato vighnairnna lipyate ||

vīrapatnyāmūladeśena kālaṃ maṃtrayojanaṃ |
visarjanavidhau deva yadīchetphalapātanaṃ ||

yatra ye prīti retasya kulasya kulavittama |
tadrūpaṃ vigrahaṃ kṛtvā bhairavā vaṭukādayaḥ ||

tatra vīrārpaṇaṃ kṛtvā kurvaṃti phalalakṣaṇaṃ |
liṅgairvākyaiḥ padairdeva jñātvā phalasamudbhavaṃ ||

devairjātaṃ narairjātaṃ yathārthajñānaśāstrataḥ |
jñātvā phalavibhāgaṃ tu paṃcamāt parataḥ prabho ||

saptamāṣṭamamāsādvā vṛkṣamadhye sarovare |
hasto dattaṃ pādukābhyāṃ vidyāsaṃhārayogataḥ ||

japedaṣṭa sahasraṃ vai svayaṃ vikṣātpatiṣpati |
evaṃ kṛte yatpatati tatphalaṃ devanirmitaṃ ||

p. 11b)

aśvāmikaṃ ca patati vaiparītyagamāgamāt |
etalliṃgaṃ phalaṃ jñātvā ānīyakṣālayet tataḥ ||

doṣabhāgaṃ parityajya phalavṛkṣaṃ praṇamya ca |
tāmrapātre mahāraṇye phalapākaṃ samācaret ||

rakṣādikaṃ vidhāyaiva vadunnaya samanvitaṃ |
nānāsugaṃdhi saṃmiśraṃ siddhaṃ jñātvā vicakṣaṇaḥ ||

saṃstutānalamadhye tu aghoraṃ homayet tataḥ |
hetu mudrāṃ mṛtī kṛtya homaṃ kuryādalakṣitaḥ ||

rātrāvevaṃ vidhāyaiva yajñaśeṣaṃ bhajet tataḥ |
bhuktamātre tu tasmin vai ciraṃjīvīkṣitau bhavet ||

sadyo madhumatī siddhiḥ karasthāḥ bhogadāyinī |
divyabhāvaḥ sādhakeṃdramartya śakrasamo bhavet ||

devānāṃ priyakāritvāt mokṣaṃ datvā maheśvara |
sādhakānāṃ kulajñānāṃ na doṣo kalpapātane ||

etatphalanipātārtha phalavatpāpade pade |
vighnaṃ karoti sarvatra nijasaṃkoca kāraṇāt ||

phalavṛkṣopari tathā vighnaṃmācarati dhruvaṃ |
phalavṛkṣasya hāniścet kadāciddaiva yogataḥ ||

sādhakopi tathā deva bhavennātra vikalpanaṃ |

p. 12a)

vācāvya? manasā vāpi kāyādvā yadi vādhyate ||

phalavṛkṣastadā devo naśyati smarasāsanaṃ |
vādhyate kulavṛkṣāścet sādhakasya kṛte prabho ||

tāvanna kula pūjā syād yāvanta nisṛtirbhavet |
tāvannijakulālāpo varjanīyaḥ prayatnataḥ ||

etatkārya vidhāne tu yogyaṃ nāsti mahītale |
atidūrāṃtare deśe nāmavarṇavivarjitaḥ ||

karoti yadi bhadraṃ syād vighnaṃ vā phalasādhanaṃ |
na doṣastatra vādhyante sādhakāḥ kuladevatā ||

vighnapradhānametat tu kadā citphalasādhanaṃ |
saphalaṃ samanuprāpya kriyate yadi pāmaraḥ ||

phalaṃ bhavati deveśi pāpena vadhyate dhruvaṃ |
nindā madhumatī tatra jāyate nātra saṃśayaḥ ||

madhumatyāphalaṃ deva kathayāmi śṛṇuṣvame |
sudhādhāradharāvānī vaktrāntasya prajāyate ||

sarvajña sarvaśāstrāṇāṃ madhipaḥ kulanāyakaḥ |
candrasūryāvadhipṛṣṭyāṃ vibhūti vahulaṃ sukhaṃ ||

devakanyāpsarobhistu divyagaṃdharvacāraṇaiḥ |
amṛtārthe prītikarairdevabhogyaiḥ sudullabhaiḥ ||

devakanyā hastagataiḥ rathamārūhya sotsukā |

p. 12b)

āyāti sādhakākāṃkṣa hṛdayā parameśvara ||

yatra kāmeśvaro devaściṃtāvaśamupāgataḥ |
tatraiva yāṃti satataṃ devakanyāḥ sahasraśaḥ ||

indrayogyapurī kṛtvā ramate sādhakaiḥ samaṃ |
tato pāgatamuktāt sa muktā tu vahudhā sukhaṃ ||

cirakālaṃ bhuvi sthitvā cāṃte mukti mavāpnuyāt |
eṣato kathito deva divyaṃ bhāvaḥ sukhāvahaḥ ||

na devo yasya kasyāpi paśau gopyaḥ prayatnataḥ |
vīrabhāvo mahādeva kathyate gurubhairava ||

nirdvandva mānaso bhūtvā hṛdi kāmakalātanuḥ |
niśāpūjāsu pravarohe tu yuktaḥ sadaiva hi ||

nijakulaṃ samādāya svayaṃ bhairava rūpadhṛk |
kulaṃ ca bhairavī rūpaṃ tadgātre nyāsa vistaraṃ ||

vinyasya sakalaṃ nyāsaṃ navayonyātmakaṃ tathā |
prasūna tūlikā madhye puṣpe prakarasaṃkule ||

nānāgaṃdha samākīrṇe kuladravye na yaṃtrakaṃ |
likhitvā pūjayechaṃkhaṃ ghaṭasthāpanapūrvahaṃ ||

svavāmabhāge ṣaṭkoṇaṃ tanmadhye brahmaraṃdhrakaṃ |
likhitvā tatra kuṃbhaṃ vai sauvarṇe rājataṃ ca * *? ||

p. 13a)

tāmraṃ bhūmi mayaṃ cāpi yadvā loha vivarjitaṃ |
sthāpayet kalayā deva kuṃbhaṃ gaṃdhasuvāsitaṃ ||

hetu dravyaṃ brāhmaṇādi * * ? bhedena pūjayet |
tatra maṃtraṃ vilikhyādau yadyatkula samudbhavaṃ ||

dhyātjeṣṭa (?) devatāṃ tatra japedaṣṭottaraṃ śataṃ |
dhenumudrāṃ pradarśyāya amṛtaṃ tadviciṃtayan ||

dṛṣṭvārghasya ca pātraṃ vai lolupāḥ sarvadevatāḥ |
nṛtyaṃti bhairavāḥ sarve nṛtyaṃti mātaro gaṇāḥ ||

indrādayaḥ surāḥ sarve nṛtyaṃti madhulolupāḥ |
brahmāviṣṇumaheśādyā nṛtyaṃti harṣatatparā ||

arghabhāṇḍaṃ dvidhā kṛtvā gurave caikabhāgakaṃ |
evaṃ kulāpadatvā vai ekena devatarpaṇaṃ ||

pītvā kula rasaṃ deva nānālaṃkāra bhūṣitaḥ |
sākṣāyadi gurūrna syāt tadātoye visarjayet ||

ānandarūpavān bhūtvā pūjayet parameśvarīṃ |
tattatkalpokta vidhinā tattadyaṃtre prapūjya ca ||

visarjanaṃ vidhāyātha svakule yojayet tataḥ |
tadviśvara sa pānena amṛtaṃ bhujyate mayā ||

taccakoṇarasāsvādaiḥ sapyatu sasajāyate |
tatphalagrahaṇādeva sumerū śṛṃgarohaṇaṃ ||

p. 13b)

latāliṃga na mātreṇa sudhādhauta kalevarā |
mūlayoge kṛte tatra japedaṣṭa sahasukaṃ ||

japapūtaṃ havi dravyaṃ gṛhītvā tarpayet tataḥ |
vidhāya tarpaṇaṃ deva pradakṣiṇa manuvrajan ||

praṇamya stutvā kalpoktastavena toyaṃ yettataḥ |
idaṃ vīra kulaṃ deva suṃdaraṃ sumanoharaṃ ||

yaddeśe vidyate vīrastatkulaṃ vāpi bhairava |
vanamālī bhayaṃ tatra na ca rājabhayādikaṃ ||

sumaṃgalaṃ sadā tatra dhana putra vivarddhanaṃ |
lakṣmīstatra mahādeva susthirā sarvadā bhavet ||

maṃtraṃ svapna pravodhinyā avaśyaṃ vṛkṣamūlake |
yojanīyaṃ prayatnena na ca vighnaṃ prajāyate ||

nānyavīreṇa tadyogādgrahaṇe daivikairapi |
yoginībhistu yuktaṃ tu na ca pāpairnna yujyate ||

yatra tatra kujevāre śmaśāne gamane kṛte |
pūjāphalaṃ bhavet tatra saptavāsarasaṃmitaṃ ||

caturdaśyā gate tatra pakṣapūjāphalaṃ labhet |
nāgatenārcite sthāne paśureva na saṃśayaḥ ||

nānyasyādadhiko deva iti ciṃtāparāyaṇa |
sādhako kṣobhamāpannemapi kṣobha? prajāyate ||

p. 14a)

tasyādyatnādbhograyuto bhavedvīravaraḥ sukhī |
bhogena mokṣamāpnoti bhogena kulasādhanaṃ ||

vinā hetu samāsādya kṣobhayukta maheśvara |
na pūjā mama saṃkuryānna ca dhyana na ciṃtanaṃ ||

tasmād bhuktvā ca pītvā ca pūjayet parameśvaraṃ |
na kramadyuti doṣosti nācārādavidūṣaṇaṃ ||

yadyadgadati nidrāti yatkaroti yadarghati |
tatsarvaṃ kularūpaṃ tu dhyātvā ca viharet sukhī ||

ekākī nirjane deśe śmaśāne parvate vane |
śūnyāgāre nadītīre niḥsaṃge viharet sadā ||

vīrāṇāṃ japakārastu sarvakāla praśasyate |
sarvadeśe sarvapīṭhe kartavyaṃ na ca saṃśayaḥ ||

yadi vipro bhaved bhraṣṭaḥ kuladravyaṃ parāyaṇaḥ |
tadānena vidhānena kartavyaṃ kulatoṣaṇaṃ ||

aparāpuṣpagarbhe tu kulasthānaṃ manoharaṃ |
sarvadeva mukhaṃ tatra mahākāmakalātmakaṃ ||

hayā'ri kusume deva svayamasti sadāśiva |
tanmadhye na ghumādāya puṣpamadhye tu caṃdanaṃ ||

raktaṃ yuktamarāgaṃ vā datvā tatra śivātmakaṃ |
yojayet śiva śaktyā tu aikyaṃ saṃbhāvayandhiyā ||

p. 14b)

kṣaṇaṃ tatraiva saṃpūjya niścitya parameśvarī |
japtvā tadeva kuṇḍosthaṃ dravyaṃ paramadurlabhaṃ ||

amṛtādadhikaṃ jñeyametadravyaṃ sudurlabhaṃ |
yatrā parājitā puṣpaṃ javā puṣpaṃ ca bhairava ||

karavīre rakta muktai droṇaṃ vā tatra tiṣṭhati |
tatra devī vasennityaṃ tadante pūjanaṃ mama ||

dattaikaikaja vā puṣpaṃ paṭṭavastraphalaṃ labhet |
brahmahatyādikaṃ pāpaṃ kṣaṇānnaśyati niścitaṃ ||

aparā yā tu māhātmyaṃ ca vaktuṃ na hi maheśvara |
vīra sādhana kārye tu kartavyaṃ vīra puruṣaiḥ ||

divyairapi ca kartavyaṃ paśubhirnna ca pānaraiḥ |
varaṃ pāmara kāryaṃ tu na paśau ceti niścayaḥ ||

yadyadyadā purāproktaṃ śaktena maṃtrasādhanaṃ |
aṃjanaṃ guṭikādīṃśca kuryād vīro mahābalaḥ ||

divye vīreṇa bhedo striyo bhedaḥ sa ca kathyate |
santo vinprato(?) madhuraḥ kalālāvanya saṃsthitaḥ ||

divyastu devavatprāyo vīraścorddhata mānasaḥ |
vibhūti bhūṣaṇaṃ vāpi candanairvā vilepitaṃ ||

ākāragopanaṃ vāpi vyakto vā kulabhairava |
raktacaṃdanadigdho vā vaiṣṇavo vāpya vaiṣṇavaḥ ||

p. 15a)

asaṃskṛte saṃskṛto vā nityayukta kuleśvara |
apamāne ca pūjāyāṃ hṛṣṭaḥ puṣṭaḥ sadā bhavet ||

devanindā paro vāpi tatpūjādi paropi vā |
pūjāratastadrahitaḥ kulīnamatate sthitāḥ ||

nijabhāvasamāyukto devavadviharatkṣito |
svakulāntaṃ puraścaryā kārya rātrau cānyathā ||

vedahīne dvije deva yathā na śruti saṃskriyā |
viṣṇu bhaktinivādeva bhaktirna prabhaved yathā ||

śāktajñānaṃ vinā mukti yathā hāsyāya kalpyate |
guruṃ vināyathā taṃtre nādhikāraḥ kathaṃcana ||

patihīnā ca yā nārī sarvakarmavivarjitā |
kulaṃ vinā tathā devo vīro vā mama sādhakaḥ ||

nādhikārīti kauleya tasmād bhāva parobhavā |
avinītaṃ kulaṃ yasya sa kathaṃ mama pūjaka ||

tasmād yatnāt tathā kāryaṃ yathā syād vinayānvitaṃ |
bhāvābhāva kule śāstre nādhikāraḥ kathaṃcana ||

tena bhāva viśuddhastu sādhakaḥ kauliko bhavet |

śrīdevyuvāca ||

paśubhāvaṃ pravakṣyāmi śṛṇu vatsa samāhitaḥ |

yathāvidhi paśorddāṃ
