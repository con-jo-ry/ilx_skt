śivābhyāṃ nama

kulacūḍāmaṇitantram

prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ

śrībhairava uvāca-

asaṃkhyā tripurādevī asaṃkhyātā [asaṃkhyā caiva] ca kālikā |
vāgīśvarī tathāsaṃkhyā tathā ca sukulākulā || 1 ||

mātāṅginī tathā pūrṇā vimalā caṇḍanāyikā |
tripuraikajaṭā durgā yā cānyā kulasundarī || 2 ||

vaiṣṇavaṃ gāṇapatyaṃ ca mahāsauramataṃ tathā |
śaivaṃ śāṃkarajātaṃ ca yatkiṃcid bhūtasaṃbhavam || 3 ||

catuḥṣaṣṭiśca [catuḥṣaṣṭiśca tantrāṇi mātṛṇāmuttamāni ca (1-13) ityataḥ 
paraṃ vāmakeśvaratantre mahāmāyāśambaraṃ ca ityupakramya 
catuḥṣaṣṭitantranāmānyuktāni | tatratyapāṭho'ntarāntara kiṃcidanyathā 
dṛśyate so'yaṃ pāṭho mahājanaparigṛhītatayā samīcīnatayā ca dhriyate 
vyākhyāyate ca |
mahāmāyāśambaraṃ ca yogino jālaśambaram | tattvaśambarakaṃ caiva 
bhairavāṣṭakameva ca || 
atra pūrvārdhe catvāri tantrāṇi - mahāmāyā (1) śambaram (2) yoginījālam (3) 
śambararūpāṇi | śambarasya dvidhollekhastu śambara-bṛhacchambaratvena 
vyākhyeyaḥ | kaścit yoginītantraṃ jālaśambaramityevaṃ vyācaṣṭe | 
tattvaśambarakamekaṃ bhairavāṣṭakamaparamityaparārdhokte dve iti ṣaṭ tantrāṇi 
|] tantrāṇi [tantrāṇām] mātṝṇāmuttamāni ca |
mahāsārasvataṃ [mahāmāyāśambaraṃ ca | asitāṅgo ruruścaṇḍaḥ krodha 
unmatta eva ca | kapālo bhīṣaṇaśceti saṃhāraścāṣṭa bhairavāḥ || 
ityuktāṣṭabhairavaviṣayakaṃ tantraṃ bhairavāṣṭakanāmnā prasiddham |

bahurūpāṣṭakaṃ caiva yāmalāṣṭakameva ca | candrajñānaṃ vāsukiṃ ca 
mahāsaṃmohanaṃ tathā ||

bahurūpāṣṭaka śivadūtīsaṃhitānāṃ saptānāṃ mātṝṇāmaṣṭau tantrāṇi 
bahurūpatantranāmnābhihitāni | yāmalāṣṭakam - brahmayāmalaṃ (1) 
viṣṇuyāmalaṃ (2) rudrayāmalaṃ (3) lakṣmīyāmalaṃ (4) umāyāmalaṃ (5) 
skandayāmalaṃ (6) gaṇeśayāmalaṃ (7) grahayāmalaṃ (8) cetyaṣṭasaṃkhyakam 
| bhāskarastu - grahayāmalasthāne jayadrathayāmalaṃ papāṭha | candrajñāna-
vāsuki-mahāsaṃmohanāni trīṇi ityasmin śloke ūnaviṃśatitantrāṇyuktāni |

mahocchuṣyaṃ mahādeva ! vātulaṃ vātulottaram | hṛdbhedaṃ tantrabhedaṃ ca 
guhyatantraṃ ca kāmikam ||
kalāvādaṃ kalāsāraṃ tathānyat kubjikāmatam | tantrottaraṃ ca vīṇākhyaṃ 
trotalaṃ trotalottaram ||
pañcāmṛtaṃ rūpabhedaṃ bhūtoḍḍāmarameva ca | kulasāraṃ kuloḍḍīśaṃ 
kulacūḍāmaṇiṃ tathā ||
sarvajñānottaraṃ deva ! mahākālīmataṃ tathā |
mahālakṣmīmataṃ caiva siddhayogeśvarīmatam ||

kurūpikāmataṃ devarūpikāmatameva ca |
sarvavīramataṃ caiva vimalāmatamuttamam ||

atra pañcaślokyā aṣṭāviṃśati tantrāṇi saṃkhyātāni yathā - mahocchuṣyam 
(1) vātulam (2) vātulottaram (3) hṛdbhedam (4) tantrabhedam (5) guhyatantram (6) 
kāmikam (7) kalāvādam (8) kalāsāram (9) kubjikāmatam (10) tantrottaram (11) 
vīṇākhyam (vīṇātantram) (12) trotalam (13) trotalottaram (14) pañcāmṛtam (15) 
rūpabhedam (16) bhūtoḍḍāmaram (17) kulasāram (18) kuloḍḍīśam (19) 
kulacūḍāmaṇiḥ (20) sarvajñānottaram (11) mahākālīmatam (22) 
mahālakṣmīmatam (23) siddhayogeśvarīmatam (24) kurūpikāmatam (25) 
devarūpikāmatam (26) sarvavīramatam (27) vimalāmatam (28) iti |

pūrvapaścimadakṣaṃ ca uttaraṃ ca niruttaram |
tantraṃ vaiśeṣikaṃ jñānaṃ vīrābali rathāparam ||
aruṇeśaṃ mohinīśaṃ viśuddheśvarameva ca ||

anena sārdhaślokena ekādaśa tantrāṇyuktāni-

yathā - pūrvāmnāyaḥ (1) paścimāmnāyaḥ (2) dakṣiṇāmnāyaḥ (3) 
uttarāmnāyaḥ (4) niruttaratantram (5) vaiśeṣikatantram (6) jñānārṇavatantram (7) 
vīrābalitantram (8) aruṇeśatantram (9) mohinīśatantram (10) 
viśuddheśvaratantram (11) iti śaktipradhānatantrāṇāṃ madhye etāni 
catuḥṣaṣṭitantrāṇi uttamāni śreṣṭhāni jñeyānītyākūtam |] caiva yogino 
jālaśambaram || 4 ||

p. 2)

tattvaśambarakaṃ nāma bhairavāṣṭakameva ca |
bahurūpāṣṭakaṃ jñānaṃ yāmalāṣṭakameva ca || 5 ||

tantrajñānaṃ vāsukiṃ ca mahāsaṃmohanaṃ tathā |
mahāsūkṣmaṃ mahādevi ! vāhanaṃ vāhanottaram [mahāsāṃkhyaṃ 
mahādevaṃ vātulaṃ vātulottaram] || 6 ||

p. 3)

hṛdbhedaṃ mātṛbhedaṃ ca guhyatattvaṃ ca kāmikam |
kalāvādaṃ kalāsāraṃ tathānyat kubjikāmatam || 7 ||

māyottaraṃ ca vīṇākhyaṃ trotalaṃ trotalottaram |
pañcāmṛtaṃ rūpabhedaṃ bhūtaḍāmarameva ca || 8 ||

kulasāraṃ kuloḍḍīśaṃ tantraṃ viśvātmakaṃ yathā |
sarvajñānātmakaṃ devi ! mahāpitṛmataṃ tathā || 9 ||

mahālakṣmīmataṃ devi ! siddhayogeśvarīmatam |
kurūpikāmataṃ devi ! rūpikāmatameva ca || 10 ||

sarvavīramataṃ devi ! vimalāmatamuttamam |
pūrvapaścimadakṣaṃ ca uttaraṃ ca niruttaram || 11 ||

tantraṃ vaiśeṣikaṃ jñānaṃ śivābalimathāparam |
aruṇeśaṃ mohaneśaṃ viśuddheśvarameva ca || 12 ||

evametāni tantrāṇi tathānyānyapi koṭiśaḥ |
śrutvā devi ! na kutrāpi jātānandāsi bhairavi || 13 ||

tatkathaṃ vada suśroṇi ! mayi tvatpādavartini || 14 ||

devyuvāca -

śṛṇu deva ! parānanda parāprakulātmaka |
manmāyāmudritāśeṣatantrajñānakulārṇava || 15 ||

ahaṃ prakṛtirūpā ceccidānandaparāyaṇā |
kva brahmā kva hariḥ śaṃbhuḥ kva devaḥ kva manorjapaḥ || 16 ||

kva sṛṣṭi kva sthitirvāpi kva vā saṃhṛtibhūmikā |
kva rāgaḥ kva sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ kva muktiḥ kva ca sādhutā || 17 ||

āstikyaṃ kva nu nāstikyaṃ kva gurutvaṃ kva śiṣyatā |
māyayācchādya cātmānaṃ tridhā bhūtvā madonmukhī || 18 ||

parākularasonmādamodinī ca vikāriṇī |
pañcabhūtāni liṅgāni caikottaraśatāni ca || 19 ||

p. 4)

brahmādibhūrbhuvaḥsvaḥsvabhāvavyaktirajāyata |
sarve'pyāvirbhavantyatra tato bhāvādikalpanā || 20 ||

śivaśaktiprabhedena guṇotpattistu jāyate |
sarve brahmādayo yūyaṃ madaṃśā bhāvasaṃbhavāḥ || 21 ||

parasparāvibhinnāśca tantramantrasamākulāḥ |
pañcatvaṃ prāpayiṣyāmi nirvāṇapadalālitāḥ || 22 ||

punarāvirbhavantyete prakṛteḥ parataḥ pumān |
ahaṃkāraḥ pañcamātrāḥ sattvaṃ cātha rajastamaḥ || 23 ||

etanmātrātmakaṃ viśvaṃ naṣṭaṃ cāvirbhavatyapi | [etattattvātmakaṃ jñātvā 
brahmacārī bhavatyapi]
yadi māṃ viddhi sarvajña! kva cāmnāyaḥ kva yājanam || 24 ||

na viddhi māṃ cet sarvajña! kva cāmnāyaḥ kva yājanam |
nārīrūpaṃ samāsthāya sṛṣṭisāraṃ madātmakam || 25 ||

bhavantaṃ bhavayogasthaṃ guruṃ jñātuṃ vijṛmbhitā |
etāvatāpi deveśa ! mama tattvaṃ na jāyate || 26 ||

devyuvāca -

śṛṇu putra parānanda paricaryāviśārada |
yoganirmuktakānuṣṭha upāyaḥ kathyate mayā || 27 ||

sarvatantraikasāraṃ ca sarvadevanamaskṛtam |
sarvasārvajñyadaṃ guhyaṃ tattvabodhaprabodhakam || 28 ||

pāpapuṇyaikanāśārhaṃ bhogamuktipradāyakam |
sarvāścaryamayaṃ vatsa ! viduṣāmapi mohanam || 29 ||

nānārthaprasavaprājña nānārthasaṃkulākulam |
sadācāryaparijñātaṃ [jñānam] sacchiṣyaparamāspadam || 30 ||

sarvavādisadācāraṃ sarvavādivigarhitam |
na mayā viṣṇave proktaṃ na dhātre gaṇapāya na || 31 ||

p. 5)

gopanīyaṃ tu hṛdaye yatnataḥ paripālaya |
etattantraṃ vinā vatsa ! nādhikāro'sti [kāraḥ surairapi] yadyapi || 32 ||

kulācāre tathā vatsa ! jñānaśuddhiṃ vadāmyaham |
sādhakaḥ prātarutthāya kulavṛkṣaṃ praṇamya ca || 33 ||

mūlādibrahmarandhrāntaṃ kulaṃ dhyātvā guruṃ smaret |
prahlādānandanāthākhyaṃ sanakānandameva ca || 34 ||

kumārānandanāthaṃ ca vasiṣṭhānandanāthakam |
krodhānandasukhānandau jñānānandamataḥparam || 35 ||

bodhānandamathābhyarcya dhyāyet kulamathopari |
mahārasarasollāsahṛdayānandalocanāḥ || 36 ||

kulāliṅganasaṃbhinnacūrṇitāśeṣatāmasāḥ |
kulaśiṣyoparikṛpāpūrṇāntaḥkaraṇodyatāḥ || 37 ||

varābhayayutāśeṣakulatantrārthavedinaḥ |
evaṃ kulaguruṃ natvā visṛjya kulamātṛkām || 38 ||

kulasthānaṃ samāliṅgya snānārthaṃ tīrthamāśrayet |
śāktaṃ kulaguruṃ vatsa ! kathitaṃ ca sukhāvaham || 39 ||

rahasyamadbhutaṃ vatsa ! goptavyaṃ paśusaṃkaṭe |
kulanāthaṃ parityajya ye śāktāḥ kulasevinaḥ [parasevinaḥ] || 40 ||

teṣāṃ dīkṣā ca yāgaśca abhicārāya kalpate |
tasmāt sarvaprayatnena kulīnaṃ gurumāśrayet || 41 ||

kulīnaḥ sarvavidyānāmadhikārīti gīyate |
dīkṣāprabhuḥ sa evātra sarvamantrasya nāparaḥ || 42 ||

iti kulacūḍāmaṇitantre prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ ||


dvitīyaḥ paṭalaḥ

devyuvāca -

atha vakṣyāmi te vatsa ! snānaṃ kulasukhāvaham |
kṛṣṇaraktaharinnīlā vividhā mama mūrtayaḥ || 1 ||

tatra yaḥ kulagaḥ śiṣyaḥ sa tadrūpaṃ parāmṛśan |
divaṃ sarvamathorvīṃ ca pātālaṃ bhūtasaṃbhavam || 2 ||

ācāntaḥ kuladarbheṇa sadarbhakulapuṣpakam |
kulapātre sadūrvaṃ ca satilaṃ sajalaṃ tathā || 3 ||

gṛhītvā kuladevasya prītaye snānamācaret |
kṛtasaṃkalpa evādau kulacakraṃ jale nyaset || 4 ||

kulamūlaṃ samānīya kulamudrāṅkuśena ca |
kulatīrthāni tatraiva samāvāhya kulātmakam || 5 ||

tattoyaṃ ca tridhā pītvā tridhā ca prokṣaṇaṃ tanoḥ |
kulamūlāya devāya tridhārghyaṃ parikalpya ca || 6 ||

devān pitṝn ṛṣiṃścaiva tarpayet kulavāriṇā |
kulātmakān punardhyātvā kuladevāṃstu toṣayet || 7 ||

bhairavītantra evātra teṣāṃ saṃjñā ca vidyate |
bhairavāya ca devāya bhairaveṇa ca kartṛṇā || 8 ||

bhairavākhyaṃ pradātavyaṃ mantramuccārya pūrvataḥ |
dātṛdānagṛhītaṃ ca tato liṅgānurūpataḥ || 9 ||

bhairavī bhairavātmānaṃ bhāvayettadaśeṣataḥ [bhāvayedyadaśeṣataḥ] |
śrāddhe vivāhe dāne ca snāne cāṅgaprapūjane || 10 ||

p. 7)

evaṃ cintāpare deva ! prasīdāmi na saṃśayaḥ |
tatastṛptā tadāśeṣalokān prīṇāmi tattvataḥ || 11 ||

utthāya kulavastre dve paridhāya kulena ca |
tilakaṃ kularūpaṃ ca kṛtvācamya samāpya ca || 12 ||

kulapīṭhaṃ samāruhya kuladevasya [sarvaṃ] pūjanam |
dvāradeśe tato gītanṛttavādyāditoṣitaḥ || 13 ||

kulabhūtaṃ samutsārya [samuccārya] kulasthānaṃ prapūjayet |
kulāsanaṃ tataḥ kṣiptvā tadabhyarcya yathāsukham || 14 ||

kulāsanaṃ tato baddhvā gurupūjākrameṇa ca |
ātmaśuddhibhūmiśuddhidehaśuddhivibhedataḥ [ātmaśuddhiṃ pīṭhaśuddhiṃ 
dehaśuddhyādibhedataḥ] || 15 ||

kṛtvā cārghyaṃ tato vidvān kuryāt kulaviceṣṭitam |
dīkṣitābhiḥ suśīlābhiryuvatībhiḥ kulātmabhiḥ || 16 ||

devatāgurubhaktābhiḥ saṃyutaṃ [saṃcitaṃ] yāgabhūmiṣu |
nānāvidhāni puṣpāṇi gandhāni vividhāni ca || 17 ||

karpūrajātidhūpādivāsitaṃ paṭavāsitam |
tāmbūlaṃ deyadravyaṃ ca dhūpadīpādikaṃ ca yat || 18 ||

sarvālaṃkārabhūṣābhirbhūṣitaḥ kaulikastadā |
mūlavidyājaptatoyaiḥ prokṣitaṃ sthāpayettataḥ || 19 ||

sarvaṃ svadakṣiṇe sthāpyaṃ vāme cārghyaṃ niveśayet |
paścime devatāyāṃ ca kuladravyāṇi dhārayet || 20 ||

kuṇḍagolodbhavairdravyaiḥ svayaṃbhūkusumena [kusumādibhiḥ] ca |
rocanālākṣayāraktaiḥ [lākṣayā laktaiḥ] kuṅkumāruṇacandanaiḥ || 21 ||

yantraṃ kṛtvā tatra pūjāṃ kṛtvā ca japamācaret [japamārabhet] |
yathāśakti manuṃ japtvā stutvā devīṃ visarjayet || 22 ||

p. 8)

tāstāḥ pradakṣiṇīkṛtya nijā vāpyanyayoṣitaḥ |
kulāmṛtarasaṃ pūrvaṃ [pūrṇaṃ] gurave vinivedayet || 23 ||

yoṣitastvavaśeṣaṃ tu svātmanyeva niyojayet |
brahmarandhre guptasthāne yantralepaṃ tu dhārayet || 24 ||

nāstikebhyo na mūrkhebhyo na paśubhyo na vā dviṣe |
kulīnāya ca dātavyamathavā jalamadhyataḥ || 25 ||

tataḥ so'hamiti dhyātvā vaiṣṇavācāratatparaḥ |
harināmnā jātabhāvo bhaṅgyanvitaviceṣṭitaḥ || 26 ||

coravat praviśedekaḥ sadā saṅgavilambitaḥ |
yāmamātre gate rātrau kulagehaṃ tataḥ pumān || 27 ||

prasūnatūlikāmadhye sthitvā kulaparāyaṇaḥ |
kulayantraṃ sādhyanāmnā saṃkulaṃ kulacakrake || 28 ||

svanāmādyaṅkitaṃ kṛtvā kulācāraṃ samācaret |
svaśaktyā paraśaktiṃ ca samānayati sādhakaḥ || 29 ||

paraśaktyākarṣaṇaṃ ca śṛṇu vatsa ! samāhitaḥ |
nijakāntāṃ samānīya suśīlāṃ suyaśasvinīm || 30 ||

kulabhaktaṃ guruṃ prāpya dīkṣayet kuladīkṣayā |
parānandarasāghūrṇalocanāṃ kulajāṃ satīm || 31 ||

tāmbūlagrāsapūrāsyo gururakṣo'bhitaḥ sudhīḥ |
nijaputrīvadācāryastadbhālapaṭṭake likhet || 32 ||

śakticakraṃ trirāvṛttyā likhet kāmakalāṃ tataḥ |
tanmadhye deyamantreṇa darbhitaṃ nāmalāñchitam || 33 ||

tatra devīṃ samāvāhya dhyātvā devīṃ prapūjya ca |
tatastatputrikākarṇe ṛṣicchandasamanvitam || 34 ||

p. 9)

mūlamantraṃ trirāvṛtya kathayedvāmakarṇake |
adya prabhṛti putri ! tvaṃ kulapūjārcane ratā || 35 ||

a(su)kulājñāṃ samādāya lajjālasyavivarjitā |
yathopadiṣṭavidhinā [athopa] sa me vasyaṃ (śyaṃ) samānaya || 36 ||

ityanujñāṃ gurorlabdhvā praṇameddaṇḍavadbhuvi |
trāhi nātha ! kulācāra padminīkulanāyaka || 37 ||

tvatpādāmbhoruhacchāyāṃ dehi mūrdhni yaśodhana ! |
gurave dakṣiṇāṃ dattvā tāmbūlāruṇalocanā || 38 ||

svakulaṃ paramīkṛtya yathābhīṣṭaṃ samācaret |
sāṅgāvaraṇapūjādau [aṅgāvaraṇa] yadi na kṣamate kulam || 39 ||

tadā mūrdhni guruṃ dhyātvā kulāmṛtarasena ca [tu] |
tarpayitvā tato [kulaṃ dhyātvā] devaṃ japenmantraṃ nirākulam || 40 ||

iti kulacūḍāmaṇitantre dvitīyaḥ paṭalaḥ |


tṛtīyaḥ paṭalaḥ

devyuvāca 

atha tadrātrisamaye svakulaṃ tūlikopari [bhūmikopari] |
vāmabhāge samāsannaṃ raktavastravibhūṣitam || 1 ||

svarṇālaṃkārabhūṣāḍhyaṃ raktagandhavibhūṣitam |
gandhapuṣpadhūpadīpaveṣṭitaṃ sumanoharam || 2 ||

sarvaśṛṅgāraveṣāḍhyaṃ sphuraccakitalocanam |
jitāmṛtakucadvandaṃ [jitāmita] viśālakarikumbhakam || 3 ||

lalāṭayantramālikhya sādhyanāmavigarbhitam |
tatskandhe bāhumādāya bhaṅgyā dhṛtakucāvalam || 4 ||

tāmbūlapūritamukhaṃ kulaṃ tadabhisaṃhitam |
kūlākulajapaṃ kṛtvā [kuryāt] samānayati tatkṣaṇāt || 5 ||

yannāmnā likhitaṃ yantraṃ tamānayati tatkṣaṇāt |
śatayojanadūrasthāṃ nadīparvatamadhyagām || 6 ||

dvīpāntarasahasreṣu rakṣitāṃ nigaḍādibhiḥ |
payodharabharakṣubdhamadhyamāṃ lolalocanām || 7 ||

nitambabimbavidhvastasphurajjaghanamaṇḍalām |
sādhakākāṅkṣihṛdayāṃ vivarāntaḥprasarpiṇīm || 8 ||

kapāṭalohasaṃnaddhaprākāravivarāntare |
sādhakāntaḥsamāsīnāṃ devatāmiva cāriṇīm || 9 ||

vaśayanti mahādeva sādhakastatkṣaṇādimām |
evamākṛṣṭi siddhiścet sādhakaḥ kauliko bhavet || 10 ||

p. 11)

dīkṣitā na ca yoṣā cet kathaṃ syātkulapūjanam |
kulapūjā na cedvatsa ! kulamantrāḥ [kulapūjā parāṅmukhā] parāṅmukhāḥ || 
11 ||

anyayoṣā yadā vatsa ! svayaṃ tasyā gurubhavet |

p. 35)

tenāyaṃ nirupadruto nirupamaśrīpādapadmāṭavī-
prāptānantarasārṇave mama manohaṃsaściraṃ nandatu || 22 ||

hitvā caṇḍi ! hiraṇyadāraṇapaṭuproddāmahastāṅguli-
sphāyat kamrasumerusodarasaṭāṭopaṃ nṛsiṃhaṃ surāḥ |
mātastvatpaśupāśapeṣaṇapaṭuśrīpādasaṃsevinaṃ
sevante karivairiṇaṃ kimaribhirbhītirbhavetsevinaḥ || 23 ||

caṇḍi ! tvadviṣayāntarākṣarapadaṃ śrotrāntaraṃ cedgataṃ
tattattvaṃ puruṣaprakṛtyanugataṃ brahmādibhirgīyate |
tasmāddevi ! samastadaivatasudhāsāraikadhāmasphura-
cchrīmatpādapayojacumbanaparaṃ māmadya saṃbhāvaya || 24 ||

mannindā yadi vāstu te kulapathācārādvaraṃ [dvayaṃ] māstu vā
kīrtiḥ keśavakauśikārcanacarī naivāstu matsaṃnidhiḥ |
mātarbrahmaharismarārihutabhugdaityārisevāspada-
śrīmatpādapayojacintanavidhau cittaṃ sadaivāstu naḥ || 25 ||

nirdiṣṭo'smi yadi tvadīyapadayukpūrvāparībhāvane 
nirdiṣṭasya tadā mamāpi viralaṃ kiṃvāstu siddhāspadam |
tasmāddevi ! kṛpābharāñcitataraṃ śrīpādapadmadvayaṃ
maccitte'kṣatasaṃpadi prasaratu kṣemaṃkari ! kṣamyatām || 26 ||

ātmānaṃ [svātmānaṃ] parirabhya bhūtapatirapyunmādamāsāditaḥ
sphāraṃ jīvanarakṣaṇe sa ca kṛtī naivābhaviṣyatprabhuḥ |
daivādvicyutacandracandanarasaprāgalbhyagarbhasrava-
nmādhvīpūrṇabhavatpadaikakamalāmodena nāsvāditaḥ || 27 ||

hā hā mātaranādimohajaladhivyāhāraviddhākhila-
brahmānandarasābhiṣekavirasasvāntodare mādṛśi |
yuṣmākaṃ suravṛndanirbharamanastāpābhibhūtikṣamaḥ
śrīmadbhaktirasātidurdinaparīvāhaḥ sadā sarpatu || 28 ||

sargaṃ saṃsṛjate sthitiṃ vitanute sṛṣṭiṃ punarlampate 
prodbhinnāñjananīlanīradamahaccitte sadaivāstu naḥ || iti tantrasārādṛtaḥ 
pāṭhaḥ] || 29 ||

yā śaśvanmahiṣacchalasphuṭamiladgarjadvidhāvatskhala-
dvaktāntaḥprasarattamastamaśiro daityaṃ samālambate |
sā durgā bhayadurgadurgatiharā lakṣāntaratrāsinī
dṛpyaddaivatavairdāraṇapaṭurjīyājjayāhlādinī || 30 ||

nṛtyatkheṭakacāmarāñcalabalaccakrādyakharvāvara-
sphāyatsainyaśilīmukhocchaladanalpājihmatāmrāmbudhau |
jhañjhāvātavisarpinartitaśiraḥsāṭopaduṣṭāsura-
truṭyatkhaṇḍavikhaṇḍitākhilaśakuntakṣutpipāsojjvale || 31 ||

cañcatkamravirāmakālakalatīvrāsphālasaṃpādako-
