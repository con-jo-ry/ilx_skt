haṃsopaniṣat

pūrṇamadaḥ- iti śāntiḥ

brahmavidyāsādhanahaṃsavidyāyā atiguhyatvam

gautama uvāca-

bhagavan sarvadharmajña sarvaśāstraviśārada |
brahmavidyāprabodho hi yenopāyena jāyate || 1 ||

sanatkumāra uvāca-

vicārya sarvadharmeṣu mataṃ jñātvā pinākinaḥ |
pārvatyā kathitaṃ tattvaṃ śṛṇu gautama tanmama || 2 ||

anākhyeyamidaṃ guhyaṃ yogine kośasaṃnibham |
haṃsasyākṛtivistāraṃ bhuktimuktiphalapradam || 3 ||

vivaraṇam

haṃsākhyopaniṣatproktanādāliryatra viśramet |
tadādhāraṃ nirādhāraṃ brahmamātramahaṃ mahaḥ ||

	iha khalu haṃsopaniṣadaḥ śuklayajurvedapravibhaktatvena 
īśāvāsyabṛhadāraṇyakādau ya upodghātāḥ sa eva atrāpi draṣṭavyaḥ | 
sanatkumāragautamapraśnaprativacanarūpeyamākhyāyikā

p. 560) vidyāstutyarthā | tatrādau gautamaḥ sanatkumāraṃ brahmajñānaṃ 
tadupāyaṃ ca pṛcchatītyāha- bhagavanniti | he bhagavan 
ṣaṇguṇaiśvaryasaṃpanna, sarvadharmajña, sarvaśāstraviśārada, iti 
saṃbodhanatrayeṇa tvaṃ sarvajño'si sarvajñeśvarabhāvārūḍhatvāt 
yenopāyena me jhaṭiti anāyāsena brahmavidyāprabodho hi 
nirviśeṣabrahmamātrajñānaṃ jāyate tadupāyaṃ kṛpayā upadiśa || 1 || iti 
gautamena pṛṣṭaḥ sanatkumāro muniḥ śrotubuddhiprarocanāya 
vakṣyamāṇavidyāṃ stuvannidamuvācetyāha- sanatkumāra uvāceti | he 
gautama sarveśituḥ pinākino yanmataṃ svātiriktasarvakalanāviralaṃ 
brahma svamātramiti tadeva tanmukhato jñātvā tadanurodhena 
svātiriktāsadapahnavasiddhaṃ brahma sanmātramiti dhāraṇārho dharmo 
yeṣu vedānteṣu dhāryate teṣu sarvadharmeṣu 
īśādyaṣṭottaraśatavedānteṣu samyak vicārya niścitya lokamātrā 
brahmavidyārūpayā pārvatyā yat brahmatattvaṃ me kevalakṛpayā 
kathitaṃ yat pinākino'bhimataṃ tanmataṃ sāvadhānamanā bhūtvā 
śṛṇu, śrutvā samyagavadhārayetyarthaḥ || 2 || tat kīdṛśamityata āha- 
anākhyeyamiti | yat mayā vakṣyamāṇaṃ brahmavijñānaṃ tadidaṃ 
guhyam ayogiṣu gopanīyamityāha- anākhyeyamiti | kadāpi na 
vaktavyamityarthaḥ | anadhikāriṣu khyāpane mahānanartho bhavedityatra-

ajñasyārdhaprabuddhasya sarvaṃ brahmeti yo vadet | 
mahānarakasāmrājye sa tena viniyojayet || iti,
na buddhibhedaṃ janayedajñānāṃ jñānasaṅginām ||

iti ca śrutismṛtī mānam | tarhi idaṃ kasmai ākhyeyamityata āha- yogina iti | 
svāśramācārapurassaraṃ brahmavidyopayogasādhanayuk yogī tasmai 
yogine deśikena idaṃ vaktavyamityarthaḥ | tat kīdṛśaṃ 
kiṃphaladamityatrāha- haṃsasyeti | svamātraprabodhena svātiriktakalanāṃ 
hanti apahnavaṃ karotīti haṃsaḥ paramātmā tasyākṛtivistāro 
viśvavirāḍotrādiḥ taṃ tadeva brahma jñānaratnakośasaṃnibhaṃ 
svātiriktamukteḥ svājñatāyā muktiḥ apahnavaḥ tato yat phalaṃ 
svamātrāvasthānalakṣaṇakaivalyamavaśiṣyate tatphalapradaṃ, mayā 
ucyamānaṃ śṛṇviti pūrveṇa anvayaḥ || 3 ||

p. 561)		haṃsavidyādhikārisvarūpam 

atha haṃsaparamahaṃsanirṇayaṃ vyākhyāsyāmo brahmacāriṇe 
dāntāya gurubhaktāya haṃsahaṃseti sadā dhyāyan || 4 ||

	haṃsasya ākṛtivistāraḥ tadadhikārī vā kīdṛśaḥ kiṃ 
tadvidyāphalaṃ kiṃ tatsādhanamityākāṅkṣāyāṃ 
yogyādhikāriṇamupalabhya muniḥ etat sarvamācaṣṭe- atheti | atha 
yathoktādhikārasaṃpattyanantaraṃ krameṇa jāgradādyavasthātrayaṃ 
hatvā turyapadaṃ gacchatīti haṃsaḥ vyaṣṭituryaḥ pratyagātmā, sa eva 
paramaḥ sarvotkṛṣṭaḥ samaṣṭyavasthātrayaṃ hatvā tatturyapadaṃ 
gacchatīti paramahaṃsaḥ tayoḥ haṃsaparamahaṃsayoḥ pratyakparayoḥ 
nirṇayaṃ yathātmyaṃ pratyakparavibhāgaikyāsahabrahmamātrajñānaṃ 
vispaṣṭamākhyāsyāmaḥ vakṣyāmaḥ | kasmā adhikāriṇe vakṣyasītyatra 
haṃsaḥ ho'hamityarthe haṃsahaṃsetyāvṛttiḥ | yaḥ sadaivaṃ haṃsaḥ 
so'hamityanusaṃdhānapūrvakaṃ tatsvarūpaṃ dhyāyan brahmaṇi carati 
brahmaniṣṭhāmāroḍhumicchati sa brahmacārī tasmai brahmacāriṇe 
dāntāya vaśīkṛtakaraṇagrāmāya paramārthopadeṣṭṭagurubhaktāya 
gurūpadiṣṭārthaśraddhālave tubhyaṃ vyākhyāsyāma iti 
pūrveṇānvayaḥ || 4 ||

haṃsasvarūpaṃ tajjñānaphalaṃ ca

	sarveṣu deheṣu vyāpya vartate yathā hyagniḥ kāṣṭheṣu tileṣu 
tailamiva | taṃ viditvā na mṛtyumeti || 5 ||

	sa haṃsaḥ kiṃparicchinnaḥ kutrāsanamarhatītyatra 
sadṛṣṭāntamāha- sarveṣviti | yathā hyagniḥ kāṣṭheṣu svāvārakeṣu 
svadāhyeṣu ca vyāpya vartate tileṣu tailamiva ca tathā 
svājñadṛṣṭiprasaktasarveṣu brahmādistambāntadeheṣu satsu 
tadāropāpavādādhikaraṇajīveśapratyakpararūpeṇa vyāpya vartate, 
svajñānena svājñānahānau satyāṃ

haṃsajñānopāyo yogaḥ

gudamavaṣṭabhyādhārādvāyumutthāpya svādhiṣṭhānaṃ triḥ 
pradakṣiṇīkṛtya maṇipūrakaṃ gatvā anāhatamatikramya śiśnābhe 
pārśve bhavataḥ | viśuddhau prāṇānnirudhyājñāmanuyāyan 
brahmarandhraṃ dhyāyan trimātro'hamityevaṃ sarvadā 
paśyatyanākāraśca bhavati || 6 || eṣo'sau paramahaṃso 
bhānukoṭipratīkāśo yenedaṃ sarvaṃ vyāptam || 7 ||

	evaṃ samyagjñānāvāptau upāyaḥ kaḥ ityākāṅkṣāyāṃ 
tadupāyatvena yogamupadiśati- gudamavaṣṭabhyeti | taṃ viditvā na 
mṛtyumetītyuktito vedanādeva mṛtyugrāsabrahmāptiravagamyate | 
keṣāṃcinmalinacittānāṃ mumukṣāyāṃ satyāmapi kevalavedānta-
śravaṇato mṛtyugrāsabrahmajñānaṃ nodeti | tatrāntarāyo malinacittatā | 
yogākhyaśuddhavāriṇā yadi sā śodhitā tadā pūrvaśrutajñānameva 
svagatābhāsatāṃ vihāya samyagjñānapadavīṃ bhajati | tataḥ 
samyagjñānasamakālaṃ mṛtyugrāsabrahmāptiriṣyate | tasmāt 
brahmāptihetujñānasādhanatvena aṣṭāṅgayogo'bhyasanīyaḥ | 
ko'yamaṣṭāṅgayogaḥ ? yamaniyamāsanaprāṇāyāmapratyāhāra-
dhāraṇādhyānasamādhayaḥ ityaṣṭāṅgāni gurusaṃnidhau 
yathāvadabhyasya atha samādhyarthaṃ janasaṃbādharahitaguhāṃ 
praviśya yogī siddhāsanārūḍhaḥ san vāmagulphena gudamavaṣṭabhya 
nāsikābhyāṃ vaktreṇa vā vāyumāpūrya kumbhayitvā 
samyagākuñcanena mūlādhārādvāyumutthāpya prāṇāpānayoraikyaṃ 
kṛtvā ayaṃ mūlādhāratrikoṇagatavahniprāṇāpānān militvā 
kuṇḍalinīṃ bodhayitvā tayā 
mūlādhārasthasuṣumnākavāṭabrahmagranthiṃ vibhidya caturdalayuktaṃ

p. 563) mūlādhāracakraṃ praviśya virājaṃ tatturyaṃ vā dhyātvā atha 
svādhiṣṭhānaṃ ṣaḍdalaśobhitaṃ triḥ pradakṣiṇīkṛtya atha 
daśāraśobhitaṃ maṇipūrakaṃ gatvā anāhatacakraṃ dvādaśadalaṃ 
tanmūlasthaviṣṇugranthiṃ vibhidya anāhatacakramadhye sūtraṃ tatturyaṃ 
vā dhyātvā tatra nirvikalpasamādhimetya, atha anāhatamatikramya, atha 
viśuddhicakraṃ ṣoḍaśadalaṃ, tadadhobhāge śiśnābhe pārśve bhavataḥ 
viśuddhacakrādhobhāge lambamānaśiśnatulyamāṃsakhaṇḍe bhavataḥ, 
ya eṣaḥ stana ivāvalambate | sendrayoniḥ iti śrutyantarāt | tatpārśvastha-
mārgadvayaṃ vihāya tanmāṃsakhaṇḍamadhyamārgeṇa viśuddhicakraṃ 
praviśya tatra viśuddhau viśuddhicakre prāṇānnirudhya atha bhrūmadhye 
dvidalāḍhyam ājñācakraṃ tanmūlastharudragranthiṃ vibhidya 
ājñācakraṃ praviśya tanmadhye bījaṃ tatturyaṃ vā anuyāyan dhyāyan 
nirvikalpakasamādhimetya, atha candrasūryāgryaikyasaṃjātāmṛta-
māsvādya ajarāmarapiṇḍo yogī sahasrāraśobhi brahmarandhraṃ praviśya, 
tatratyatrimātrāropādhikaraṇaturyastrimātro'haṃ, yadvā tripañcamātrā 
yatrāpahnavaṃ bhajati tatturyaturyaṃ vāhamasmīti yadi sarvadā paśyati 
tadā yogī trimātrādhikaraṇakiṃcitsākāravat turyo vā, anākāravat 
turyaturyo vā bhavati | turyaturyasya niṣpratiyogikatvakhyāpakaḥ caśabdaḥ || 
6 || yaḥ turyaturīyatvena uktaḥ eṣo'sau paramahaṃsaḥ paramātmā 
svāṃśajaturyābhedena bhānukoṭipratīkāśaḥ 
yugapaduditānantakoṭibhānusadṛśaprakāśaḥ yena turyaprakāśena idaṃ 
sarvaṃ jagat vyāptaṃ kabalitam || 7 ||

nidrālasyādayo bhavanti | yāmye kraurye matiḥ | nair-ṛte pāpe manīṣā |

p. 564) yadā haṃse nādo vilīno bhavati tat turīyātītam || 8 || atho nāda 
ādhārād brahmarandhraparyantaṃ śuddhasphaṭikasaṃkāśaḥ sa vai 
brahma paramātmetyucyate || 9 ||

	tasyaiva punarvyaṣṭiprapañcāropādhikaraṇajīvabhāvamāpannasya 
aṣṭadhā aṣṭaprakārā vṛttirbhavati | kathamityatra- tadadhiṣṭheyaṃ 
hṛtpadmaṃ dvādaśadalayuktam ityuktam | tatra dalacatuṣṭayaṃ 
haṃsāspṛśyaṃ, śiṣṭāṣṭadalaṃ vyavahārayogyaṃ bhavatīti kṛtvā 
tatprakāra ucyate | tatra haṃsaḥ prāṇopādhikajīvaḥ yadā pūrvadale 
praviśati tadā puṇye karmaṇi matiḥ bhavati | yadā āgneyadale praviśati 
tadā nidrālasyādayo bhavanti | yadā yāmyadale praviśati tadā kraurye 
karmaṇi matirjāyate | yadā nair-ṛte dale praviśati tadā pāpe karmaṇi 
kartuṃ manīṣā udeti | yadā vāruṇyāṃ diśi vidyamānadale praviśati 
tadā krīḍāvinodamatirbhavati | vāyavyāṃ diśi vidyamānadale yadā 
praviśati tadā calanagamanādau buddhiḥ udeti | yadā saumye dale praviśati 
tadā vidvadaviduṣoḥ ātmānātmaratiprītiḥ udeti | yadā īśānye dale 
praviśati tadā ihāmutropayogidravyādānamatiḥ jāyate | yadā dalānāṃ 
madhye praviśati tadā svātiriktaviṣaye vairāgyamudeti | yadā tatkesare 
saṃcarati tadā jāgradavasthā bhavati | tadānīmahaṃkṛtiḥ 
pūrṇavikāsatāmeti | yadā tatkarṇikāyāṃ saṃcarati tadā svapnaṃ 
paśyati | tadānīmahaṃkṛteḥ ardhavikāso bhavati | tadantaḥ suṣiraṃ tatra 
yadā praviśati tadā suṣuptiḥ bhavati | yathoktalakṣaṇapadmatyāge turīyaṃ 
tatsākṣātkāro bhavati | tadānīṃ haṃsaḥ pratyagabhinnaparamātmatayā 
avaśiṣyate | yadā haṃse pratyagabhinnaparamātmani nādo'rdhamātrāt-
mako vilīno bhavati tadā svādhiṣṭheyaguṇasāmyādiprapañcavairalyāt 
tannirūpitādhiṣṭhānatāviralaṃ niradhiṣṭhānaṃ turīyātītaṃ 
niṣpratiyogikabrahmamātraṃ avaśiṣyate || 8 || evaṃ 
bhāvanāviśiṣṭayoginaḥ svadehe turyātmā prakāśata ityāha- atho iti | atho

prathamaḥ ciṇiciṇīti dvitīyaḥ | ghaṇṭānādastṛtīyaḥ | 
śaṅkhanādaścaturthaḥ | pañcamastantrīnādaḥ | ṣaṣṭhastālanādaḥ | 
saptamo veṇunādaḥ | aṣṭamo bherīnādaḥ | navamo mṛdaṅganādaḥ | 
daśamo meghanādaḥ || 16 ||

kevalājapātmahaṃsamantrajapaṃ kurvataḥ svānubhavajanyanādeyattaṃ

p. 569) ciṇiti prāthamiko nādo ya uktaḥ tasmin prathame nāde 
yathāvadanubhūte tadā svasya ciñciṇīgātraṃ pratibhāti | tathā dvitīye 
tadgātrabhañjanaṃ bhavati | tṛtīye tu hṛdayaṃ bhedanaṃ yāti hṛtpadmaṃ 
vikāsameti | caturthe tu svaśiraḥ kampate- ṣaṇmukhīmudrārūḍhasya 
kumbhakāvasthāyāṃ vāyāvājñācakraṃ praviṣṭe śiraḥ 
kampayatītyarthaḥ || 18 || pañcame tantrināde'bhivyakte svatālū sravatī | 
ṣaṣṭhākhyatālanādābhivyaktau candrasūryamaṇḍaladvayaikyasaṃjātā-
mṛtaniṣevaṇamamṛtāsvādanaṃ bhavati | saptamaveṇunādābhivyaktau 
svātiriktajanānāṃ yat gūḍhaṃ brahma tadviṣayakavijñānaṃ jāyate | 
aṣṭamabherīnādābhivyaktau sarvavedaśāstrarūpiṇyā parāvācā ayaṃ 
yogī vijṛmbhate,  sakalaviṣayakaśāstravijñānī sarvajño bhavati || 19 || 
nāvamikamṛdaṅganādābhivyaktau dehaṃ paśyatāmadṛśyaṃ bhavati 
antardhānādiśaktimān bhavati | nirmaladivyacakṣuḥ trikālajñaḥ īśvaro 
bhavati | prathamādinavamāntanādatadbhavanānāvidhasiddhyupekṣā-
pūrvakaṃ daśamameghanādābhivyaktau brahmātmasaṃnidhau 
pratyagabhinne brahmaṇi svājñādidṛṣṭiprasaktatadgatabhedaikyakalanā-
pahnavasiddhabrahma bhavet, tanmātratayā avaśiṣyata ityarthaḥ || 20 ||

manolayāt brahmātmatvaprakāśaḥ

	tasmānmano vilīne manasi gate saṃkalpavikalpe dagdhe puṇyapāpe 
sadāśivaḥ śaktyātmā sarvatrāvasthitaḥ svayaṃjyotiḥ śuddho buddho 
nityo nirañjanaḥ śāntatamaḥ prakāśayatīti vedānuvacanaṃ 
bhavatītyupaniṣat || 21 ||

	kathaṃ punareva brahmamātramavaśiṣyate 
svātiriktamanastatkāryasaṃkalpapuṇyapāpādisattvādityata āha- tasmāditi 
| yasmāt svājñānavikalpito'yaṃ mana-ādivikārapūgaḥ tasmāt 
svajñānena mano manasi vilīne nirmūlabhāvaṃ

p. 570) gate tatkāryasaṃkalpavikalpe tatkāryapuṇyapāpādāvapi dagdhe 
samyagjñānāgninā bhasmīkṛte'tha svātiriktamanastatkāryāropāpa-
vādādhikaraṇatayā (brahmapraṇavārthaturyaturyatayā) sadāśivaḥ 
parameśvaraḥ tattatpadārthagatasarvaśaktyātmā sarvatra vyaktāvyaktapra-
pañce saccidānandātmanā avasthitaḥ | jaḍaprapañcānuyogato jaḍatvaṃ 
syādityatra āha- svayaṃjyotiriti | svājñadṛṣṭiprasaktajaḍaprapañcasya 
svajñadṛṣṭyā mithyātvena tadanuyogasyāpi tathātvāt svayaṃjyotirātmā 
hyajaḍātmakaḥ | ata evāyaṃ śuddhaḥ aśuddhamāyāvairalyāt | māyā-
pāye tadanugatasyāpi tadātvaṃ syādityata āha- nitya iti | sattāmātratvāt | 
mātraśabdasyetaravyavacchedakatvena tatrāñjanatā syādityata āha- 
nirañjana iti | sattāmātrasya niṣpratiyogikatvenetararañjanatā kathaṃ 
seddhuṃ pārayati ? svātiriktāstitāhetusvājñānahetutamaḥsattve 
niṣpratiyogikasattāmātratā kuta ityatra āha- śāntatamaḥ prakāśayatīti | 
yadavagatisamakālaṃ svājñānatatkāryaṃ tamo'pahnavapadaṃ bhajati 
so'yaṃ śāntatamaḥ ata evāyaṃ niṣpratiyogikatayā prakāśayati |
