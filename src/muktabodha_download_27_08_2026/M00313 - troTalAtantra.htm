Description:
Description by Mark S.G. Dyczkowski:
A Garuḍā tantra
Troṭalā is another name for the goddess Tvaritā whose visualized form is described
in 1/28-59. She is called Troṭalā because she smashes apart
(troṭana) bondage. She is Travitā because her Vidyā works quickly
(travritam). This is not a well known goddess nowadays but there is
evidence that she was more popular in the past. Thus she is well known to the
Kubjikā Tantras prior to the 12th century. Her association with Kubjikā is also
implied in this Tantra by identifying her with KubjikA as Trikaṇṭhikā
(1/94). This Tantra contains details of her worship both externally and
inwardly as the rise the vital breath in the course of which she ascends through
the barriers and attains union with Bhairava at the summit of her ascent. She is
associated with ghosts and demons )gūhyaka, piśācas, Yakṣas etc. of
which she is said to govern (IzvarI) and so her Vidyā and worship
also serve as means to exorcise and control them.

troṭalamahātantram


p. 1a)

oṃ namaḥ śivāya |

kailāśaśikharāsīnamaśeṣabhuvana prabhum |
anantādi śivāntasya śraṣṭāraṃ parameśvaram || 1 ||

arūpaṃ paramaṃ śāntaṃ pārvatyā hṛdinandanam |
sarvaśāstra pravaktāraṃ sarvasiddhaistu saṃstutam || 2 ||

brahmaviṣṇumahendrādyairdevadānava pūjitam |
skandanandigaṇādyaiśca koṭyānekaistu sevitam || 3 ||

ṛṣibhiśca tathā cānyairgandharvādyaiḥ supūjitam |
siddhavidyādharairyakṣai rākṣasaiśca susaṃstutam || 4 ||

anantādyaistathā nāgaiḥ kulikāntaiḥ sadāvṛtam |
apsarogaṇasaṃkīrṇaṃ siddhakanyā susaṃstutam || 5 ||

kecit stuvanti deveśaṃ kecinnṛtyanti cāgrataḥ |
kecit sāmānigāyanti kecit praṇatamūrddhani || 6 ||

stotraiḥ stuvanti tatrānye kecit puṣpaṃ kṣipanti ca |
hasanti kilikilāyanti garjanti cotpatanti ca || 7 ||

na tatra mṛtyurna jarā na vyādhirna ca duḥkhitāḥ |
evaṃ pramuditaṃ dṛṣṭvā śivaṃ suravarārcitam || 8 ||

praṇamya daṇḍavadbhūtau jānubhyāṃ dharaṇīgataḥ |
kṛtāñjalipuṭo bhūtvā tārkṣyo vacanamavravīt || 9 ||

p. 1b)

bhagavandeva deveśa saṃsārārṇavatāraka |
praśnametanmahāguhyaṃ vadabhaktasya me prabho || 10 ||

tantrāṇāntu paraṃ tantraṃ mantrāṇāṃ mantramuttamam |
vividhāḥ siddhayaścitrāḥ saṃkṣepā yatra vartate || 11 ||

mokṣañca labhate yena svacchandatvañca jāyate |
rājyaprasādhanaṃ cānyadartha sādhanameva ca || 12 ||

nāgakrīḍā ca vividhā viṣāpaharaṇantathā |
yakṣāgrahājvarāghorā lāmā śākinī pūtanā || 13 ||

kuṣmāṇḍā yakṣavetālā daityāścaiva mahāvalāḥ |
bhūtapretapiśācānāṃ nigrahākarṣaṇañcayat || 14 ||

khecaratvaṃ jigīṣanti sādhakāstu mahītale |
mahīpātāla siddhiñca khaḍgamañjanapāduke || 15 ||

guṭikā paṭa mahāstraṃ pāśānāṃ stobhanaṃ param |
chedanaṃ dahanañcaiva dīkṣā ca vividhā katham || 16 ||

adhikāre ca mokṣe ca sadyotkrāntiḥ kathaṃ bhavet |
ātmānaśca parasyaiva paradeha praveśanam || 17 ||

p. 2a)

kathamanagnijvalanaṃ nirvījīkaraṇaṃ katham |
sthāvarāṇāñcarāṇāñca dahanāpyāyanantathā || 18 ||

ghaṭamagni jalaṃ koṣaṃ viṣastambhaṃ kathaṃ bhavet |
kāṣṭhapāṣāṇa bhūtānāṃ sadyāveśaḥ kathaṃ bhavet || 19 ||

kuṣṭhādīnāṃ ca vyādhīnāṃ nāśanaṃ kena karmaṇā |
chedanaṃ paramantrāṇāṃ mātmavidyā prarakṣaṇam || 20 ||

kṣetrasyā lambhanaṃ caiva kṣetrarakṣā kathaṃ bhavet |
kīlanaṃ pararājyasya bhedanaṃ mohanaṃ tathā || 21 ||

māraṇaṃ caiva māravyāhi cakravarttitva sādhanam |
kāmarūpadharatvaṃ ca svacchanda khecare pade || 22 ||

divyakhecarabhaumānāṃ yoginīnāñcamelakam |
etatsarvaṃ samākhyā hi mamabhaktasya śaṅkara || 23 ||

divyaṃ varṣa sahasrantu tapasā'rādhito mayā |
tato brahmā samāyāto varaṃ vrūhi mahātapa || 24 ||

brahmovāca ||

aśaktovara kāryeṣu varaṃ jñānaṃ vadāmyaham |
kailāśaṃ gaccha pakṣīśa īśasyaiva samīpataḥ || 25 ||

p. 2b)

mahātapaḥ prasannastu īśvarodāsyatevaram |
tato vaikṛṣṭa manasāṃ padmayoneḥ samāgataḥ || 26 ||

varaṃ mamepsitaṃ vrūhi idaṃ mama sureśvara |
sādhakānāṃ hitārthāya anugraharato bhava || 27 ||

īśvara uvāca ||

garutmīśa śṛṇuṣvedaṃ tatra sadbhāvamuttamam |
āsīt pūrvaṃ mahāghoraṃ bhairavaṃ rūpamuttamam || 28 ||

mama dehād viniṣkrāntamugrarūpāstu yogajāḥ |
asaṃkhyātāstvanekāstu raktamāṃsāśavapriyāḥ || 29 ||

atṛptā lolupā bhīmā ugrarūpadharā sadā |
jijñāsārthaṃ mayā tāsāṃ vijñānaṃ yogasambhavam || 30 ||

preritaṃ ca paśutvena ahaṃ prārthitamādarāt |
adhiṣṭhastasureśāna carūkāryeṣu maṇḍale  || 31 ||

adhiṣṭhāmi mayā coktaṃ gatohaṃ merumūrddhani |
puraṃ vajrārgalantatra rajaiḥ pañcabhirnirmitam || 32 ||

madhye cāṣṭadalaṃ padmaṃ pītakarṇikamujjvalam |
vajrāṇi tu caturdikṣu vidikṣu vajra saṃpuṭam || 33 ||

dvārāṇi vīthayastatra dakṣiṇadvāra nirgamam |
tatrāhaṃ karṇikāmadhye sthitaṃ śaktivalotkaṭā || 34 ||

p. 3a)

dhyātvā tu paramāṃ śaktimakṣobhyāṃ paramāvyayām |
tāvat kailāśa pṛṣṭhe tu gauryānandi pracoditaḥ || 35 ||

kutrāsau bhagavānnudraḥ paśutve devi saṃsthitaḥ |
yoginībhiścarukārye prārthito maṇḍale sthitaḥ || 36 ||

tataḥ kruddhā tu sā gaurī taḍitkoṭi samaprabhā |
tvaritaṃ tvaritā vidyā trivījā triguṇīkṛtā || 37 ||

taḍitpuñjanibhā dīptā vyāpayantī carācaram |
sphuradraśmisahasraistu koṭhyānekairdiśo daśa || 38 ||

tayā tu dīptapiṇḍena brahmādyāḥ staṃbhitāḥ surāḥ |
hāhāravaṃ prasūcanti trāhi devi śivapriye || 39 ||

āgatāṃ pādamūle tu varaistotraiḥ stuvanti ca |
prītā sā vividhaiḥ stotraiḥ śiraṣpādādilakṣaṇaiḥ || 40 ||

ekaikamakṣaraṃ devyā dvau bhujau parikīrtitau |
aṣṭādaśabhujā devī dharmasiṃhāsanasthitā || 41 ||

gokṣīradhavalaśrīmān mahālāṅgūlavarcasaḥ |
lalajjihvamahogrāsya tīkṣṇadaṃṣṭrasphuranmahat || 42 ||

p. 3b)

jvalannetro mahāghora stabdha karṇasubhīṣaṇaḥ |
garjanto balavān bhīmo jvalatkuñcitamūrddhajaḥ || 43 ||

tīkṣṇogranakhamuktistu bhuvanāni vidārayan |
pūrvānanasthito dīpto grasanniva carācaram || 44 ||

tvaritā tatra cārūḍhā vāmajaṃghāpralamvitām |
dakṣiṇā dviguṇā tasyāḥ pādapṛṣṭhe samarpitā || 45 ||

sphurattārakavad devyā bhrājante pādayornakhāḥ |
ambhojāruṇavarṇābhā padmalāñchanalakṣaṇā || 46 ||

rājate caraṇādhastātsusamāṅgulimāyatā |
suvṛttaṃ jaṃghapīnoruvistīrṇo jaghanorūmā || 47 ||

gambhīranābhistrivalī romarājī suśobhitā |
vistīrṇā kaṭideśe tu hemakumbha payodharā || 48 ||

kamvugrīvā tu vimvoṣṭhī nīlotpaladalekṣaṇā [ṣaḍvaktrāṃ 
vimvoṣṭhī'ṣṭādaśekṣaṇām] |
sunāsācāpa bhūbhaṅgā sukarṇā mukuṭojvalā || 49 ||

visphurenmaṇibhirdīptaḥ sahasraphaṇidīptimān |
nāgarāja kṛto hāra karṇakuṇḍalabhāsinī || 50 ||

nāgavandhakṛtāśobhā bhujau devyā virājate |
mekhalā kaṭideśe tu mahāhi kṛtabhūṣaṇā || 51 ||

p. 4a)

pādayornūpurau ghorau devyāḥ kṛtabhujaṅgamau |
sahasradale cāmbhoje vāmapāda kṛtodarām || 52 ||

viyattaḍillatābhāsāṃ vāsaḥ kusumaśobhitām |
indrāyudhanibhaṃ cānyaṃ vāsaṃ kṛta payodharau || 53 ||

aneka puṣpa racitaṃ dṛḍhavandha mahoragam |
kṛtāyudhakarā devī tīkṣṇāgra nakhaśūktayaḥ || 54 ||

vajradaṇḍāsi cakraṃ ca gadāśūlaṃ mahojvalam |
śaraṃ śaktiśca varadaṃ dakṣiṇena kṛtāyudhā || 55 ||

dhanuṣpāśadharaṃ ghaṇṭā tarjanī śaṃkhamaṅkuśam |
abhayaṃ ca tathā padmaṃ vāmapārśva kṛtā yudhā || 56 ||

taḍitsahasrasaṃkāsā visphurat kiraṇākulā |
chādayanti svatejobhiḥ śivāghavanigocarā || 57 ||

tvaranti tvaritā proktā hūṅkārakṛta bhīṣaṇi |
tarjayanti diśaḥ sarvāḥ duṣprekṣā durgadevatāḥ || 58 ||

mohitaṃ yoginī cakraṃ mūrchitaṃ merupṛṣṭhataḥ |
patitaṃ śuṣyamānaṃ tu trāhi devisurādhipe || 59 ||

p. 4b)

stutvā tu vividhaiḥ stotraiḥ oṃkārārdhakṛto mayā |
arcayitvā tu tāṃ devīṃ praṇamitvā tu yoginīm || 60 ||

māmevaṃ yoginīcakraṃ nigrahasva surādhipe |
tiṣṭha tiṣṭha mahādīpte atra maṇḍalamadhyataḥ || 61 ||

praviśya maṇḍale devī āsīnā karṇikopari |
pūjitā vividhairmālyairgandhadhūpopahārakaiḥ || 62 ||

mantramudrā samopetaiḥ stutvā stotrairanekaśaḥ |
sthāpitā samaye devī yastu smarati sādhakaḥ || 63 ||

tasyaiva kāmikāṃ siddhiṃ dāsyate acireṇa tu |
yoginyo bhūcarāstasya sarvakāmārtha siddhidāḥ || 64 ||

devā daityāḥ sagandharvāṃ yakṣarakṣoragāpsarāḥ |
dadāti kāmikāṃ siddhiṃ tvarite tava pūjanāt || 65 ||

yastu pūjayate bhaktyā gṛhe nityaṃ svaśaktitaḥ |
śatravo vilayaṃ yānti iṣṭa satvaparāṅmukhāḥ || 66 ||

jayate pararāṣṭrāṇi līlayā pūjinā tu saḥ |
vibhūtayaśca vipulāmārogyaṃ dīrghamāyuṣam || 67 ||

siddhyanti sarvakāryāṇi divyādivyānyanekadhā |
etasminnantare brahmā merupṛṣṭhaṃ samāgataḥ || 68 ||

p. 5a)

pūjitānena vidhināstutvāstotrairanekaśaḥ |
viṣṇunā vāsave nāpi agninā ca yamena ca || 69 ||

nair-ṛta varuṇe naiva vāyunā dhanadena ca |
īśānena umādevī siddhairdaityāpsarogaṇaiḥ || 70 ||

pūjitā ca stutā devī tuṣṭā prītimanā vadet |
evaṃ karomi deveśa yastu māṃ smarate naraḥ || 71 ||

tasyāhaṃ kāmikāṃ siddhiṃ dāsyāmi acireṇa tu |
brahmaṇā ca tadā pṛṣṭā vadavidyā prasādhanam || 72 ||

visarjjitāstataḥ sarve kathitaṃ tantramuttamam |
troṭalaṃ tu paraṃ guhyaṃ lakṣaṃ pādādhikaṃ khaga || 73 ||

tadevamupa saṃhṛtya sārātsārataraṃ param |
vadāmi paramaṃ guhyaṃ śṛṇuṣva kamale'dhunā || 74 ||

tatra vidyāḥ sthitāstārkṣya navavījātmikāḥ parāḥ |
sā vidyā paramāguhyāguhyānāmapiguhyakā || 75 ||

guhyakānāṃ parāvidyā sādhanī sarvakāmikā |
marddanī sarvanāgānāṃ ripupakṣa kṣayaṅkarī || 76 ||

sarveṣāścaiva duṣṭānāṃ nāśanī sā prakīrttitā |
divyādivyāstriyo yāstu tāsāṃ kṣobhakarī tu sā || 77 ||

utpattiḥ sarvabījānāṃ yonireṣā prakīrttitā |
hṛdayaṃ sarvadevīnāṃ yoginīnāṃ hṛdisthitā || 78 ||

p. 5b)

sarvakarmakarī vidyācāśeṣa bhuvanāntikā |
siddhidā sarvakāryeṣu ihaloke paratra ca || 79 ||

durlabhā sarvadevīnā mṛṣīṇāṃ yogināmapi |
kalpe kalpe tu ye siddhā muni vidyādharādayaḥ || 80 ||

yakṣa rakṣaḥ piśācāśca pretabhūtāni caiva hi |
ekāvidyeśvarī hyeṣā sarvāṃstrāsayate kṣaṇāt || 81 ||

taleti saptapātālā jvālāgni bhuvanāntikān |
oṃkārādi svarārabhya yāvadbrahmāṇḍa vācakam || 82 ||

oṃkārāccāyate sarvāṃstrāsate caiva sarvataḥ |
troṭalā tena ākhyātā tantrārthoyaṃ pratiṣṭhitaḥ || 83 ||

iyaṃ vidyā purā siddhā vratayoga vivarjitāḥ |
siddhidā jñānahīnepi gurūyāga vināpi ca || 84 ||

kāryākāryamavijñāyavādhyāvādhyamajānatā |
kurvantyugrāṇi karmāṇi devānāmapi hiṃsakāḥ || 85 ||

punaryāgādikaṃ siddhā gurūrājñā pracoditāḥ |
kliśyanti mānavā martye pūrvakarma śubhāśubhaiḥ || 86 ||

yena siddhyantite kṣipraṃ mokṣaṃ yānti parāparam |
vidyādharatvamicchanti cakravarttitvameva ca || 87 ||

p. 6a)

nāgakrīḍāṃ prakurvanti martyā kautūhalārthinaḥ |
khecaratvaṃ jigīṣanti siddhayogeśvaraiḥ saha || 88 ||

svacchandamṛttyumicchanti yāvaddevaḥ sadāśivaḥ |
rājyasiddhiṃ prayacchanti dravyasiddhistu kākathā || 89 ||

ākarṣaṇādi kāryāṇi manasā kurute dhruvam |
māraṇoccāṭanaṃ vaśyaṃ chedanaṃ dahanaṃ tathā || 90 ||

parasainya vibhedaṃ ca śatrūṇāñca nikṛntanam |
mohanaṃ sarvaduṣṭānāṃ bhayārtānāñca rakṣaṇam || 91 ||

yastvimāṃ bhvāsate vidyāṃ sakṛduccārayethavā |
devīdevaśca garuḍa stritayaṃ tiṣṭhate tanau || 92 ||

trikaṇṭaketi vikhyātā sānnidhyaṃ tritayaṃ bhavet |
vāmadakṣiṇa siddhānte tritayeṣu vyavasthitāḥ || 93 ||

trikaṇṭaketi vikhyātā vidyeyaṃ sarvakāmadā |
siddhyate sā tu tvaritā tvaritā tena cocyate || 94 ||

ityādye troṭale tvaritāmūlasūtre prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ || 1 ||

athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi prastāraṃ bhuvi durlabham |
siddhagandharva devānāṃ mṛṣiyakṣa mahoragām || 1 ||

p. 6b)

dānavānāṃ garutmīśa brahmādyairgopitaṃ purā |
susame bhūpradeśe tu puṣpaprakara saṃkule || 2 ||

snigdhe manorame deśe usakeśāsthi varjite |
ālikhenmātṛkāṃ tatra varga saṃjñā samanvitām || 3 ||

yena tārkṣya vijānanti mantroddhāraṃ ca sādhakāḥ |
atastvevaṃ prayatnena saṃjñādau kīrtitāmayā || 4 ||

yena mantra mahājālaṃ visphurantaṃ diśodaśa |
prathamaḥ svaravargastu ṣoḍaśākṣaramānataḥ || 5 ||

pañcapañcākṣarā cānye dvau cānye caturakṣarau |
prathamaḥ svaravargastu vargādau kīrttito mayā || 6 ||

tāluvarge dvītīyastu tṛtīyo jihvatālukaḥ |
caturthastālu jihvāgro jihvadantastu pañcamaḥ || 7 ||

ṣaṣṭhoṣṭhapuṭasampanno miśravargastu saptamaḥ |
ūṣmāṇaścāṣṭamaḥ proktaḥ kathitaste khageśvara || 8 ||

avinaṣṭā bhavedvidyā prastāreṇoddhṛtā khaga |
sarvasiddhikarī devī navabījātmikā parā || 9 ||

imāṃ vidyāṃ mayā tārkṣya uddharāmi salakṣaṇam |
mātṛkānta vinirgūḍhairguhya bījaiḥ svaraistathā || 10 ||

ṣaṣṭha svarasamārūḍhāṃ mūṣmāṇānta savindukam |
mūlavidyādikaṃ bījaṃ kathitantu khageśvara || 11 ||

p. 7a)

tāluvarga dvitīyastu svaraikādaśa yojitam |
jihvātālu samāyogo prathamaṃ kavacaṃ bhavet || 12 ||

tadevata dvitīyantu adhastād viniyojayet |
ekādaśa svarairyuktaṃ prathamaṃ tāluvargataḥ || 13 ||

ūṣmāṇasya dvitīyaṃ tu adhastāttasya yojayet |
ṣoḍaśasvara saṃyuktamūṣmāṇasya tṛtīyakam || 14 ||

jihvādanta samāyoge prathamaṃ yojayedadhaḥ |
miśravarga dvitīyaṃ tu adhastāt punareva ca || 15 ||

caturthasvarasaṃbhinnaṃ tāluvargādi saṃyutaḥ |
ūṣmāṇasya dvitīyaṃ tu adhastādviniyojayet || 16 ||

svaraikādaśa saṃbhinna mūṣmāṇāntaṃ sa vindukam |
pañcamasvaramārūḍhamoṣṭha saṃpuṭa yogataḥ || 17 ||

dvitīyamakṣaraṃ cānyaṃ jihvāgre tāluyogataḥ |
prathamaṃ pañcameyojyaṃ svarārddhenoddhṛtā imā || 18 ||

tvariteyaṃ mahāvidyā sarvasiddhipradāyikā |
oṃkārādi samāyuktā mante namo japetsadā || 19 ||

svāhāntamagni kāryeṣu aṅgāni ca tataḥ śṛṇu |
ūṣmāṇāntaṃ caturthena svareṇākrānta bindukam || 20 ||

p. 7b)

miśravarga dvitīye tu sthita mūṣmāṇamantagam |
ṣaṣṭha svarāsanaṃ kuryādvindūnāṃ mūrdhni bhūṣitam || 21 ||

ādyaṃ binduvihīnaṃ tu ṣoḍaśasvara bhūṣitam |
akṣaraṃ hṛdayaṃ proktamūṣmāṇaṃ tu dvidhā bhavet || 22 ||

ādyaṃ dvitīya saṃyuktaṃ kārayecca vicakṣaṇaḥ |
ṣoḍaśasvara saṃyuktaṃ dvirabhyā sa siro bhavet || 23 ||

oṣṭhadvayasya saṃyogedvitīyaṃ bindusaṃyutam |
caturthasvarasaṃyuktaṃ miśravargadvaye sthitam || 24 ||

jihvātālu samāyoge tṛtīyaṃ miśravargataḥ |
caturthena samārūḍhaṃ punarmiśra tṛtīyakam || 22 ||

dvirabhyā sa samāyuktāṃ śikhāpañcākṣarāḥ smṛtāḥ |
pañcamasvarasaṃyukta mūṣmāṇāntaṃ sabindukam || 23 ||

punastatkevalaṃ yojyaṃ miśravarga tṛtīyakam |
atrārūḍhaṃ punastattu punaśvādyaṃ niyojayet || 24 ||

pañcākṣaraṃ tu kavaca mūṣmāṇānta sabindukam |
miśrādyena samārūḍha mūṣmāṇasyādimaṃ punaḥ || 25 ||

vinduyuktaṃ tu miśrasya dvitīyāsana saṃsthitam |
ūṣmāṇasya dvitīyaṃ tu tāluvargādi saṃyutam || 26 ||

p. 8a)

binduyuktaṃ tu miśrasya dvitīyaṃ dāpayedbudhaḥ |
dvitīyaścaturthaḥ ṣaṣṭhaḥsvarāyojyāḥ krameṇa tu || 27 ||

sūryasomāgnirutpannā vījā netratrayaṃ viduḥ |
dakṣiṇaṃ vāmataścorddhve yojyā ca kramataḥ khaga || 28 ||

tāluvargādi saṃyojya uṣmāṇasya dvitīyakam |
caturdaśe pañcadaśe yojyaṃ miśravarga dvitīyakam || 29 ||

adhastāt tasya saṃyojya mūṣmāṇāntaṃ sa ṣoḍaśam |
tāluvargādvitīyaṃ tu visargeṇa samāyutam || 30 ||

pañcamasvarasaṃrathaṃ ca ūṣmāṇānta sa bindukam |
caturakṣaramidamastraṃ phaṭkāreṇa vidarbhitam || 31 ||

triśūlakhaḍgacakraṃ ca śaraṃ caiva prakīrtitam |
pūrvadakṣiṇa vāruṇyāmuttarāyāṃ niyojayet || 32 ||

ekaikaṃ cātha saṃghātamastrametatsudurjayam |
guhyāṅgāstu ime tārkṣya brahmaṇā gopitāḥ purā || 33 ||

ebhirvinā na siddhiḥ syāttantresmintroṭalottare |
gopitavyā prayatnena cā parīkṣyena dāpayet || 34 ||

vidyāṅgāḥ kīrttyamānāstu śṛṇuṣyāvahitodhunā |
ādidvihṛdayaṃ proktaṃ tricatuḥ śiramiṣyate || 35 ||

p. 8b)

pañcaṣaṣṭha śikhā proktā kavacaṃ saptamāṣṭakam |
tārakāṃ tu bhavennetraṃ navārddhākṣara rakṣaṇam || 36 ||

dūtī mantrāstathā cānye śṛṇu tārkṣya yathā sthitāḥ |
hūṁlaleti samālikhya vajra tuṇḍe tato bhavet || 37 ||

khakhehūṁ ca samāyojya daśabījāḥ prakīrttitāḥ |
vajratuṇḍā samākhyātā śakradūtī mahāvalā || 38 ||

khecara jvālinīrlikhya jvaleti viniyojayet |
khakheti ca samāyuktā jvālinīnāmabhīṣaṇā || 39 ||

agnidūtī mahādīptā daśabījaiḥ samuddhṛtāḥ |
carceśavarīmālikhya bhīṣaṇī ca tato bhavet || 40 ||

he caiva yojya vidyeyaṃ śavarīnāma bhīṣaṇī |
daśākṣarā samākhyātā yamadūtī mahābalā || 41 ||

chechedanī padaṃ yojyaṃ karālī tadanantaram |
khagaccheti punaryojyaṃ karālī cograrūpiṇī || 42 ||

nair-ṛtyasya tviyaṃ dūtī daśabījā mahābalā |
kṣaḥ śravadravaṃ saṃlikhya plavaṅgī śroṁkṣaḥ uddhṛtāḥ || 43 ||

daśākṣarī plavaṅgī tu dūtyeṣā varuṇasya tu |
strīcaleti samālikhya kaleti dhūnanī tathā || 44 ||

p. 9a)

rāstrī caiva samāyojyā daśavījāḥ samuddhṛtāḥ |
manovegā imāvidyā vāyudūti mahābalā || 45 ||

kṣepakṣe kapile likhya hasayakṣe tato bhavet |
kapilānāma vidyaiṣā daśākṣara samuddhṛtā || 46 ||

yakṣadūtī mahābhīmā saumyogrā divyarūpiṇī |
hūṁ tejovatī raudrī māte hūṁ caiva prakīrttitā || 47 ||

rudradūtī mahāraudrī raudrīnāma mahābalā |
daśākṣarā iyaṃ vidyārudradūtī prakīrttitā || 48 ||

pharephaghne samālikhya phe cakreti niyojayet |
pharepha saṃyutāhyeṣā viṣṇudūtī mahābalā || 49 ||

daśavījā vinirdiṣṭā cakravegā tu kāmadā |
duṣṭeghuṭṭe samālikhya khaṭṭe ghore ca dustathā || 50 ||

daśavījā samākhyātā dūtyeṣā brahmaṇo varā |
brahmavetālinī nāmaghorātīva valotkaṭā || 51 ||

daśavījā sāmākhyātā troṭalesmin khageśvara |
atra vidyāḥ sahasrāṇi nirgatāni anekadhā || 52 ||

teṣāṃ sāraṃ viniṣkāntaṃ sārāt sāramihoddhṛtam |
ayaṃ mantragaṇaḥ siddhastyaja cānyaṃ palālavat || 53 ||

ityādye troṭale mahātantre tvaritāmūlasūtre dvitīyaḥ paṭalaḥ || 2 ||
p. 9b)

athānyaṃ saṃpravakṣyāmi śṛṇuṣvaikamanā khaga |
astreṇa ca karau sodhyau paraspara nimajjitau || 1 ||

uttarābhimukho mantrī tarjanyastraṃ niyojayet |
tālatrayaṃ saśabdantu tarjanyā tarjayeddiśam || 2 ||

vāmayā vāma pārśve tu dakṣiṇāyāntu dakṣiṇe |
ūrddhvādho dakṣiṇaṃ vāmā mantrī cāstraṃ samuccaret || 3 ||

guhyāstreṇānvitaṃ kṛtvā vidyāstreṇa punastathā |
vinyasya karayormantrī diśāsu vidiśāsu ca || 4 ||

nadevairdānavairvāpi na vighnairabhibhūyate |
guhyāṅgaṃ prathamaṃ nyasya hṛdayaṃ kanyase nyaset || 5 ||

śiramanāmike yojyaṃ śikhā madhyāṅgulīṣu ca |
jyeṣṭhe tu kavacaṃ yojyaṃ netraṃ hastatale nyaset || 6 ||

tarjanyastraṃ punaryojyaṃ vidyāṅgāni punastathā |
dūtayaśca tathā yojyā jyeṣṭhādārabhya saṃmukhāḥ || 7 ||

ṣaṇmukha pañcavidyāni pṛṣṭhataḥ pañcavinyaset |
panastu mūlavidyāṃ tu nakhaśuktiṣu vinyaset || 8 ||

tatonyaseccharīreṣu vilomāccānulomataḥ |
pādādārabhya mūrddhāntaṃ śirādārabhya pādayoḥ || 9 ||

p. 10a)

guhyāṅgāni nyasitvā tu yathāsthāne niyogataḥ |
vidyāṅgāni tato nyasya digvastraṃ viniyojayet || 10 ||

aṅghrijānūruguhyeṣu nābhihṛtkaṇṭhatālu ca |
vatkamaṇḍalamūrddhāntamadhorddhvaṃ cādi bījataḥ || 11 ||

yathokteṣu nyasenmantrī vījāni durjayanti ca |
savāhyābhyantaraṃ vighnāndhvaṃ sa māyānti tatkṣaṇāt || 12 ||

dehaśuddhiśca bhavate sa bāhyābhyantareṇa tu |
nyāsaṃ kuryādyathoktaṃ tu tatontaḥ karaṇa kuru || 13 ||

somarūpaṃ tadoṅkāraṃ dhārāmṛta suvarṣaṇam |
viśantaṃ brahmarandhreṇa sādhakastu vicintayet || 14 ||

muṣṭiṃ vaddhvā tu vāmena aṅguṣṭhantu adhomukham |
mūrddhāsya kaṇṭhahṛnnābhau guhyorujānu jaṃghayoḥ || 15 ||

ādibījaṃ nyasenmantrī aṅguṣṭhādi punaḥ punaḥ |
aṅgulīṣu yathokteṣu vījānyaṅgāni yojayet || 16 ||

pañcabhistu namaskāraṃ padmarūpamadho nyaset |
ūrddhva somādadhaḥ padmaṃ śarīraṃ vījavigraham || 17 ||

yo vijāti tatvena sa nityaṃ cāmṛtī bhavet |
na mṛtyurnajarātasya na vyādhirna ca hiṃsakāḥ || 18 ||

p. 10b)

kṣipraṃ ca labhate siddhi mihaloke paratra ca |
pakṣākāro karau kṛtvā choṭhayet pañcabhirnakhaiḥ || 19 ||

pañcavidyāṃ tu vāmena dakṣiṇe pañca eva tu |
aśvārūḍhā sudīptāśca kumāryobhinna cūcukāḥ || 20 ||

bhujaṅgābharaṇā kanyākaraveṇī pralambhitāḥ |
khaṅgakheṭakadhāriṇyā svāstramambhojapāṇayaḥ || 21 ||

indrāyudhanibhaṃ vāsaḥ kusumāneka śobhitam |
nibaddhavarmakakṣāstu bhujagendrottarīyakāḥ || 22 ||

tvaritātvaramāṇā tu vīrā pracaṇḍa vigrahā |
nyasitvā cādi pādeṣu dvitīyā jaṃghayornyaset || 23 ||

tṛtīyā jānu deśe tu caturthī ūrudeśataḥ |
pañcamī guhyadeśe tu ṣaṣṭhīnābhau ca vinyaset || 24 ||

saptamī hṛdaye deśetvaṣṭamī kaṇṭhadeśataḥ |
navamī vaktravinyastā daśamī mūrddhni vinyaset || 25 ||

evaṃ sannaddha dehastu rājā vijayakāṅkṣiṇaḥ |
jitvā tu pararāṣṭrāṇi akṣatāḥ punarāgamet || 26 ||

nirvighnaḥ sādhakaḥ kṣipraṃ sarvasiddhiṃ pradāpayet |
anena vidhinā nyāsaṃ kṛtvā cāṣṭa śataṃ japet || 27 ||

p. 11a)

dhyātvā tu tvaritāṃ devīṃ masādhyamapi sādhayet || 

ityādye troṭale mahātantre tvaritāmūlasūtre tṛtīyaḥ paṭalaḥ || 3 ||

atha mudrāṃ pravakṣyāmi sarvasiddhi pradāyakām |
mudrābhiḥ sahitāṃ devīṃ tvaritā siddhidā bhavet || 1 ||

sānnidhyā bhavati kṣipraṃ pūjāñcāpi pragṛhṇati |
tasmānmudrāṃ pravakṣyāmi śṛṇuṣvaika manākhaga || 2 ||

praṇītā pañcadhākhyātāvandhaṃ tāsāṃ vadāmyaham |
granthitau tu karau kṛtvā madhyeṅguṣṭhau nipīḍayet || 3 ||

tarjanyau mūrddhni saṃlagnau vinyasettaṃ śiropari |
praṇīteyaṃ samākhyātā hṛddeśetāṃ samānayet || 4 ||

ūrdhvā tu kanyasā madhye sa vījānāṃ viduḥkhaga |
niyojya tarjanī madhye mukhalagnā parasparam || 5 ||

jyeṣṭhāgraṃ nikṣipen madhye bhedinī sā prakīrttitā |
nābhideśe tu tāṃ vaddhvā aṅguṣṭhāvut kṣipettataḥ || 6 ||

karālī tu mahāmudrā hṛdayeyojya mantriṇaḥ |
punastu pūrvavadvadhvā jyeṣṭhalagnau samutkṣipet || 7 ||

vajratuṇḍā samākhyātā vaktradeśe tu vandhayet |

p. 11b)

ubhābhyāṃ caiva hastābhyāṃ maṇivandhe tu vandhayet || 8 ||

trīṇitrīṇi prasāryeta vajramudrā vidhīyate |
ekā tu madhyamā ūrddhvaṃ śaktireva vidhīyate || 9 ||

dvābhyāṃ caiva tu hastābhyāṃ maṅgulyastu parasparam |
saṃmukhāgraṃ sthitā kṛtvā hṛddeśe daṇḍamucyate || 10 ||

dakṣiṇaṃ kiñcidūnāmya muṣṭiṃ vaddhādvayāṅgulī |
madhyamā tarjanī mūrddhva khaṅgasyaivaṃ vidhīyate || 11 ||

vāmena madhyamāṅgulyā tarjanyāgraṃ nipīḍayet |
madhyamātsuṣiraṃ tāsāṃ pāśamudrā vidhīyate || 12 ||

vāmahastorddhvagaṃ kṛtvā saṃhṛtā caturāṅgulī |
tāryāṅguṣṭhatale kṛtvā tarjanī cālanādhvajaḥ || 13 ||

ubhābhyāṃ caiva hastābhyāṃ muṣṭiṃ vaddhvā tu hastayoḥ |
adho vāmorddhvadakṣaṃ ca dakṣiṇāṅge niyojayet || 14 ||

gadā tu bhavate hyevaṃ kuṃcitākanyasā tataḥ |
aṅguṣṭhenā kramettrīṇi triśūlamūrddhvato bhavet || 15 ||

ekā tu madhyamā corddhva śaktitvevaṃ vidhīyate |
vaddhā tu dakṣiṇe muṣṭiṃ kaniṣṭhāṃ tu prasārayet || 16 ||

vāhu śīrṣeṣu saṃlagnā sarasyevaṃ vidhīyate | 
uttānaṃ dakṣiṇaṃ hastaṃ dakṣiṇejānu saṃsthitam || 17 ||

p. 12a)

varadasya imā mudrāḥ kathitāste khageśvaraḥ |
vāmaṃ bhujaṃ prasaṃsyārye cakramuṣṭiṃ tu bandhayet || 18 ||

jyeṣṭhaṃ tu kārayedūrddhvaṃ dhanurevaṃ vidhīyate |
vāmena madhyamāṅgulyā tarjanyagraṃ nipīḍayet || 19 ||

madhye tu tayoḥ śuṣiraṃ pāśamudrā vidhīyate |
muṣṭiṃ vaddhvā tu vāmena hṛddeśetaṃ niveśayet || 20 ||

harasyeyaṃ bhavenmudrā vāmaṃ kṛtvā tvadhomukham ||
vṛttāstukaraśākhāstu ghaṇṭā bhavati cālanāt || 21 ||

muṣṭiṃ vaddhātu vāmasya kṛtvā corddhvāttu tarjanī |
vāmasya tarjanīrkuñcya ānayet ṣaṣṭhamūlataḥ || 22 ||

jyeṣṭhāgraṃ madhyamāparve tṛtīye tu niyojayet |
śaṅkhasyaivaṃ bhavenmudrā vāme muṣṭiṃ tu vandhayet || 23 ||

vakrā tu tarjanī kṛtvā aṅkuśasya vidhīyate |
vāme tu kiñcidunnāmyakaraśākhāṃ prasārayet || 24 ||

abhayasya imāṃ mudrāṃ karau dvau saṃmukhau punaḥ |
adholagnau corddhvaśākhau vṛttākārau tu kārayet || 25 ||

madhyeṅguṣṭhau viniḥkṣipya padmākārau sugranthitau |
padmasyaivaṃ bhavenmudrā hṛdayetāṃ viniḥkṣipet || 26 ||

grahaṇīmokṣaṇī caiva jvālinī sūkarīmṛgī |
praṇītāḥ pañcamudrāstu pūjāhome niyojayet || 27 ||

p. 12b)

dīkṣākāleṣu śiṣyāṇāṃ yojitavyāstu deśikaiḥ |
aṣṭāviṃśati mudrāstu kathitāstroṭalottare || 28 ||

yojitavyāḥ sadākālaṃ siddhikāṅkṣaiḥ khageśvara ||

ityādye troṭale mahātantre tvaritāmūlasūtre caturthaḥ paṭalaḥ || 

atha dīkṣāṃ pravakṣyāmi siddhidāṃ mokṣadāyikām |
vajrārgalaṃ samālikhya pūrvalakṣaṇalakṣitām || 1 ||

ekahastaṃ samārabhya navahastātparaṃ na hi |
sitāmbujehyaṣṭadale vinyaset karṇikopari || 2 ||

pañcānanaṃ mahābhīmaṃ vajradehaṃ mahābalam |
mantreṇānena pakṣīśayojayeccavarāsanam || 3 ||

hahevajraṃ samālikhya deheti ca ghuruṃ tathā |
punarghuruguluñcaivagulugarjaṃ punastathā || 4 ||

hehaṃ punastu saṃyojya viṃśākṣaramudīritam |
pañcānanaṃ bhaveddevyā āsanamambujopari || 5 ||

vyāptaṃ tu sakalaṃ viśvamanantādi śivāntagam |
atrāsane samārūḍhāṃ tvaritāṃ pūjayet khaga || 6 ||

tāṃ sānnidhyāṃ bhaveddevīṃ kṣipraṃ siddhipradāyikām |

garuḍa uvāca

pañcānanaṃ tu deveśaṃ lakṣaṇena vada prabho || 7 ||

vajrārgalapuraṃ śreṣṭhaṃ devyāḥ sānnidhyakārakam |

p. 13a)

īśvara uvāca ||

tiryagūrddhvagatārekhāścatvāraścaturo bhavet || 8 ||

navabhāga vibhāgena koṣṭhakānkārayedbudhaḥ |
grāhyā diśā gatāḥ koṣṭā vidiśāstu vināśayet || 9 ||

madhyakoṣṭhasya koṇeṣu vāhārekhāṣṭakaṃ sthitam |
bāhyakoṣṭhasyakoṇeṣu madhye yāvat samānayet || 10 ||

vajrasya madhyamaṃ śṛṅgaṃ vāhyarekhādvidhorddhataḥ |
vāhyarekhābhavedvajrādvibhaṅgāḥ kārayedbudhaḥ || 11 ||

madhyakoṣṭhe bhavet padmaṃ pītavarṇikamujvalam |

vāhyasaścaturastraṃ tu vajrasaṃpuṭa lāñchitam |

p. 13b)

sthitaṃ na vādhate mṛtyurnāgarakṣagrahādayeḥ |
naśyanti bhaya saṃtrastā dṛṣṭvā maṇḍalapuṅgavam || 18 ||

vajrāgra sandhi madhye tu yenyastāripavovalim |
stambhitā mohitāḥ sarve niruddhā vādhyate sukham || 19 ||

atraiva pūjitā devī kṣipraṃ siddhyanti sādhake |
pūjitā karṇikā madhye pūrvokta kṛta lakṣaṇā || 20 ||

mūrtti praṇava saṃdīptā hūṁkāreṇa niveśayet |
mūlavidyāṃ samuccāryamaruddhyomagataṃ khaga || 21 ||

prathamena punaścaiva karṇikāyāṃ prapūjayet |
evaṃ pradakṣiṇaṃ pūjya ekaikaṃ bījamāditaḥ || 22 ||

dalamadhye tu vidyāṅgā āgneyyāṃ yāvadāsanī |
netraṃ madhye dikṣu cāstraṃ guhyamārgeṣu lakṣaṇam || 23 ||

pūrvādi dakṣiṇāvartta netramadhyādrimāyudham |
dūtayaḥ keśarasthāturvāmadakṣiṇapārśvayoḥ || 24 ||

pañcapañca prapūjyāstusvaiḥsvairnāmairniyojayet |
lokapālānnyaseccāṣṭau vāhye ca garbhamaṇḍale || 25 ||

varṇānta agnimārūḍhaṃ ṣaṣṭhamasvarabheditam |
pañcadaśenanākrāntaṃ svaiḥsvairnāmaistu yojayet || 26 ||

vāmato dakṣiṇe caiva pañcapañca prapūjayet |
vidyāṅgāni ca guhyāṅgāḥ punaḥ piṇḍena yojayet || 27 ||

p. 14a)

punastu mūlavidyāntu karṇiko paripūjayet |
trīnvārān pūjayitvā tu puṣpairgandhaiḥ sugandhibhiḥ || 28 ||

dhūpairnānāvidhairbhakṣyaiḥ pānaiścaiva susaṃskṛtaiḥ |
māsaiśca vividhākāraiḥrphalairnānāvidhaistathā || 29 ||

madyaiḥ surāsasraiścaiva tathā ikṣurasādibhiḥ |
vastrairvitāna racitaiḥ puṣpaśragdāmaśobhitaiḥ || 30 ||

svacchāmbhaparitaiḥ kumbhaiḥ sitavastrāvaguṇṭhitaiḥ |
mukhapallava saṃchannaiḥ ratnagarbha suśobhitaiḥ || 31 ||

aṣṭabhirveṣṭayet kumbhairmaṇḍalaṃ sārvakāmikam |
navamaṃ yadbhavet kumbhamīśānyā varddhanīyutam || 32 ||

vikaropari saṃsthāpya tatra devīṃ prapūjayet |
bherīdundubhinirghoṣaiḥ śaṅkhakāhalanāditaiḥ || 33 ||

dakṣiṇāṃ mūrttimāśritya japenmantraṃ sahasradhāḥ |
daśabhāgena cāṅgānāṃ tato homaṃ ca kārayet || 34 ||

agnikuṇḍaṃ tataḥ kṛtvā vṛttaṃ vā caturasraṃkām |
dvātriṃśadaṅgulaṃ tasya khātanemi vivarjitam || 35 ||

khātaṃ pṛthutvaṃ ca samaṃ mekhalā tribhiḥ saṃyutam |
dvitri caturbhistāḥ kāryā aṅgulaiḥ svapramāṇataḥ || 36 ||

p. 14b)

muktvātvaṅgulaṃ hyekaṃ tato nemiṃ tu kārayet |
nemīnāṃ tu pramāṇena yoniḥ kāryā gajoṣṭhavat || 37 ||

jyeṣṭhaṃ dviraṃṅgulaṃ kāryaṃ pṛthutvena na cāṅgulam |
ucchrāyeṇāṅgulaṃ hyekaṃmagni kuṇḍasya madhyataḥ || 38 ||

caturaṅgulaṃ bhavedgarbhaṃ yonirevaṃ vidhīyate |
saptāṅgulā bhavedvīthī śrucā vāhu pramāṇataḥ || 39 ||

ṣaḍaṅgulaṃ bhavedagraṃ kaṇṭhamadhya tribhāgakam |
agredviraṅgulaṃ sthaulyaṃ khātamaṅgula nirgamam || 40 ||

vīthi ca madhyadeśe tu khātaṃ tasya triraṅgulam |
adha ūrdhvaṃ tu kartavyamājñādhāraṃ khageśvara || 41 ||

ṣaḍaṅgulaṃ tu kartavyamājñādhāraṃ khageśvara |
ṣaḍaṅgulaṃ tu kartavyaṃ veṣṭadaṇḍa pramāṇataḥ || 42 ||

ghaṇṭikā tu yathā śobhā tathā cāmarasārakam |

krodhalobhānvitā duṣṭāḥ kāmāśaktāśca mānavāḥ |
prāṇāpahāraṃ kurvanti prastāraṃ gopayet sadā || 7 ||

abhakto vyasanī lubdhāḥ samayaghnastu lolupaḥ |
vivādī piśunastabdhonāstiko nindakaḥ śaṭhaḥ || 8 ||

gurudrohī tathā mānī sadā prāṇivadherataḥ |
śuṣkatarkāvalambī ca prastāraṃ gopayet sadā || 9 ||

p. 22b)

yuktāyukta vivekī ca prastāraṃ dāpayet khaga ||

garuḍa uvāca ||

śrutā mayākhilā devavidyā mudrāstathārcanam || 10 ||

prastāreṇa tu guptena sarvaṃ bhavati gopitam |
prastāreṇa tu ye hīnāste hīnāḥ sarvakarmasu || 11 ||

prastāra gopitānāṃ tu karmasiddhirna jāyate |
prasādaṃ kurudevaśaprastāraṃ darśasvame || 12 ||

vṛhatkarmārtha siddhintu brūhi bhaktasya me prabho |

īśvara uvāca ||

utpattiṃ śṛṇu pakṣīśa pūrvasṛṣṭaṃ yathā yathā || 13 ||

āsīt pūrvaṃ namo ghoraṃ māyayā vyāpitaṃ mahat |
parā sūkṣmākarā jātā śivatatvaṃ su tejasam || 14 ||

tasmāt sadāśivaṃ dīptaṃ tasmāddīpta kalā bhavet |
māyātatva samāyogāt sṛjecchakti manonmanī || 15 ||

manasa unmatvena śaktyā īśakalāgatā |
tatra cāṇḍaṃ samutpannaṃ koṭi yojana māyatam || 16 ||

p. 23a)

suraktaṃ durbhedya dīptaṃ valaṃ garjanmahāsvanam |
ahaṃ vismayamātaḥ kare dhyātvā manonmanī || 17 ||

praṇītā tu tadā vaddhvā tarjanī śikhi tejasā |
niḥsṛtāḥ śatarudrāstu vajrakāyā mahābalāḥ || 18 ||

śatamekaṃ mahādīptaṃ trinetra mukuṭojvalā |
brahmabrahmeti vadato yugmayugmaṃ vyavasthitāḥ || 19 ||

sthitāḥ pañcāśa bhedaistu śaktayaścojvalīkṛtāḥ |
pañcāśadakṣarāstatra mātṛkāḥ śaktinirgatāḥ || 20 ||

rakṣitā jñānaśaktyā tu vedadhvani samudgirān |
brahmāṇḍaṃ dhāritaṃ taistu tadā tatsthiratāṃ gatām || 21 ||

praṇītāṃ bhedinī kṛtvā vā mayā tu kaniṣṭhayā |
ākṛṣya paramāṃ śaktiṃ munmanatvena yojitā || 22 ||

vajratuṇḍā tato vaddhvā brahmarandhrasya kāraṇāt |
tatratvaṃ tārkṣya saṃjāto vajratuṇḍā bhavet sukham || 23 ||

tatastu vajrakuṇḍena āhataṃ tu tadaṇḍakam |
tatra randhra samutpannaṃ mūṣmā jvālaṃ vikāsitam || 24 ||

vedadhvani samāyuktaṃ jātavedaḥ kṛtānanam |
sa rudrodbhavaṃ śabdaṃ brahmāṇḍaṃ mujvanalaṃ bhavet || 25 ||

p. 23b)

tatsomasya sṛtaṃ khyātaṃ sa śabdaṃ yojitā dhvaniḥ |
evaṃ vedāḥ samākhyātāstatsomaṃ veda sayutam || 26 ||

saṃyogāt padmamukulīkande viṣṇu samudbhavam |
brahmagarbhastu mukulīṃ vedadhvani kṛtodare || 27 ||

tasmiñ jvālāmukhe randhre praviṣṭastvaṃ khageśvara |
tasya cāṇḍasya madhye tu kṣīravarṇa dravā sṛte || 28 ||

tiṣṭhate tu mahādīpta sphaṭikābhaḥ sutejasaḥ |
gadāśaṅkhadhara śrīmānnābhe'svujadharodvijaḥ || 29 ||

vajrakāyo mahātejoḥ puṃsastatvamahābalaḥ |
sarvajñaṃ sarvakarttāraṃ prabhubhaktaṃ yamavyayam || 30 ||

uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha kiṃ tiṣṭhedrandhreṇānena nirgamaḥ |
kruddhastu tena śabdena ahaṃ viṣṇustvaṃ ko bhavān || 31 ||

tayoryuddhaṃ samāyātaṃ bhīṣaṇaṃ lomaharṣaṇam |
pakṣapātena tenaiva nirghāte tu parasparam || 32 ||

vikasitā padmamukulī brahmagarbhāsu tejasā |
tayośca yuddhamāpanne vudvuda utthito mahān || 33 ||

tato haṃsaḥ samutpannaṃ kṣīravarṇamivadyutiḥ |
brahmā tatra samāsīnaḥ kamalagarbhādviniḥsṛtaḥ || 34 ||

p. 24a)

garuḍena samākrāntā tatra haṃsa samudbhavam |
brahmā tatra smāsīnaṃstadāviṣṇuḥ sthalīkṛtaḥ || 35 ||

dhyāyamāna paraṃ tatvaṃ śivamadhyayamakṣayam |
garuḍenoddhṛto viṣṇurvahīrandhrādvinirgatam || 36 ||

mayāte mopahārārthaṃ svatejo netramadhyataḥ |
gṛhīta padmamudrāyāstejāṃ piṇḍaṃ mahadbhutam || 37 ||

jyeṣṭhāstu pihito rudrovajreakāyestadā bhavet |
ādityasya samutpattistejapiṇḍa samanvitam || 38 ||

śaktīśaragurunmīśo viṣṇurbrahmāmanonmanī |
saṃsthitāḥ śatarudrāstu svaśaktyā daṇḍadhārakāḥ || 39 ||

phaṭkārakṛta ghoṣāstu bhedinī mudrayā saha |
saṃntaptaṃ raśmibhiḥ sarvaṃ bhuvanāśeṣa gocaram || 40 ||

anivārya yathā tāstu śaktayastejasambhavāḥ |
īśvareṇa tadā vaddhā karālī trāsinī mahān || 41 ||

trāsitāḥ śaktayaḥ sarvāstā pitā ca surottamāḥ |
na ca khaṇḍa vibhāgena na ca bījāni copitāḥ || 42 ||

brahmaṇoṇḍasya madhye tu śivatatvaṃ nidhāpayet |
sadāśivaṃ tu pūrveṇa īśvaraṃ tu manonmanīm || 43 ||

p. 24b)

garūtmīśastathā viṣṇurbrahmā tejo vidāraṇaḥ |
īśānāntāḥ śivādityāstejaḥ piṇḍavyavasthitāḥ || 44 ||

tejasteṣāṃ samudbhūtaṃ mindrādyādūtībhiḥ saha |
tataḥ prāptaṃ jagatsarvaṃ diśāsu vidiśāṣu ca || 45 ||

troṭale sthāpitā vidyā trāyakātrāsakā sadā |
troṭaleti samākhyātāṃ tantraṃ paramasiddhidam || 46 || 

evaṃ vidyā samudbhūtā sarvatejomayī parā |
tasyāḥ karma pravakṣyāmi prastāreṇa yathā sthitam || 47 ||

tiryagūrddhvagatārekhāścatvāraścaturo bhavet |
navakoṣṭhā bhavedevaṃ madhyadeśe tu ādimam || 48 ||

pradakṣiṇena tu saṃsthāpya prastāro bhedayettataḥ |
prastāra kramayogena prayogā nirgatāstume || 49 ||

avinaṣṭhā sarvakarme anyathā tu nirarthakāḥ |
prastāra kramayogena prayogān yastu vindati || 50 ||

karamuṣṭhi sthitāḥ sarvāḥ siddhayaḥ sādhakasya tu |
trailokyaṃ sakalaṃ tasya tiṣṭhate pādamūlataḥ || 51 ||

bhuktiścākaṇṭakāṃ sarvāṃ na ca khaṇḍāṃ vasudharām |
cakravarttitvamāpnoti siddhiṃ labhati kāmikām || 52 ||

p. 25a)

ityādye troṭale mahātantre tvaritāmūlasūtre saptamaḥ paṭalaḥ || 7 || 
garuḍa uvāca |

divyaṃ varṣaiḥ sahasrantu tapasārādhito mayā |
prasanno yadi deveśa mama bhaktiñca vindasi || 1 ||

vidyā bhedena karmāṇi vada bhaktasya me prabho ||

īśvara uvāca ||

sakaleyaṃ purā vidyā sakṛduccāritādvija |
manasā sarvakarmāṇi kārayennātra saṃśayaḥ || 2 ||

praṇītā vaddhamātrā tu sakṛduccārito yadi |
trailokyaṃ sakalaṃ kruddhaṃ kṣaṇāñcaivopa saṃharet || 3 ||

upadrutāḥ surāḥ sarvesiddhavidyairanekadhā |
tataḥ sāmudritā vidyā samayestāpitā punaḥ || 4 ||

prastāra bhedayogena gurupūrvakrameṇa tu |
yastu karma vijānāti tasya siddhistu nānyathā || 5 ||

tadā prabhṛti sā vidyā prastāra krama siddhidā |
praṇītā pañcabhedena jñātvā siddhyanti tatkṣaṇāt || 6 ||

viṣāpahāriṇī vidyā bhūtidārājyadāyikā |
khecaratvañca āyuśca ṣaṣṭhaṃ padamanāmayam || 7 ||

p. 25b)

ṣaḍviretaistviyaṃ vidyā mudritā tu khageścara |
anyāśca siddhayaścitrā śṛṇutārkṣya yathā sthitāḥ || 7 ||

kṛtvāhyadala padmaṃ bhūrje rocanayā vudhaḥ |
kapāle tu samālikhya śivatatvaṃ samantataḥ || 8 ||

śmaśāna karpaṭe vātha vāhye niṣkamya mantriṇaḥ |
tasya garbhe likhennāma karṇiko parisaṃsthitam || 9 ||

tāpayet khadirāṅgārairbhūrjamākramya pādayoḥ |
saptāhādānayet sarvaṃ trailokyaṃ sa carācaram || 10 ||

vajra saṃpuṭa madhye tu dvādaśāre tu lekhayet |
madhye tu garbhagannāma sadāśiva vidarbhitam || 12 ||

kuḍye vā phalake vātha śilāpaṭṭe haridrayā |
mukhastambha gatistambha sainyastambhaśca jāyate || 13 ||

trikoṇe maṇḍale likhya jvālā lāñchana lāñchite |
śaktyugre vinyased rudraṃ triṣu koṇeṣu caiva hi || 14 ||

śaktyā mūle tu madhyāgre ripusā mantrugarbhagam |
śmaśāna kharpare likhya kapāle vā suśobhane || 15 ||

tāpite ca jvaraṃ ghoraṃ kurute nātra saṃśayaḥ |
viṣaraktena saṃlikhya śmaśāna karpaṭe budhaḥ || 16 ||

p. 26a)

ṣaṭkoṇe daṇḍamākrāntaṃ samantācchaktiyojitam |
mārayede cirādeṣaḥ śmaśāna nihitaṃ ripum || 17 ||

chedaṃ karoti rāṣṭrasya cakramadhye nyasedripum |
cakradhārāgatāṃ śaktiṃ ripunāma samanvitam || 18 ||

acirāt pararāṣṭrantu kṣayaṃ yāti na saṃśayaḥ |
tārkṣyeṇaiva tu bījena khaḍgamadhye vilekhayet || 19 ||

dvidarbhya ripunāmantu śmaśānāṅgāralekhitam |
ekānte tu samālikhya tāḍayecciti bhasmanā || 20 ||

saptāhāt pararāṣṭrañca ripuṃ vātha mahābalam |
sādhayennātra sandeho mūḍhaṃ vihvala cetasam || 21 ||

viṣṇunā cārddha candrantu yoṣinnāma kapālake |
rocanā likhitaṃ taptaṃ mānaye dūrvasīṃ kṣaṇāt || 22 ||

dhvajāgre likhitannāma brahmaṇena samanvitam |
dhvajāmūle tathāgre ca madhyeṣu ca vidarbhayet || 23 ||

śmaśānāṅgāralikhitaṃ śmaśānakarpaṭe dhvaje |
bhūtavṛkṣe samārūḍhaṃ saptāhoccāṭanaṃ bhavet || 24 ||

p. 26b)

triṣu koṇeṣu gadāyāṃ bhāskaranmasa saṃyutam |
likhedrocanayā mantrī tāḍayejjāti kudmalaiḥ || 25 ||

aśoka phalake likhya ānayedguhyakādayaḥ |
bhedanamīśadigbhāge śūlāgre tu vidarbhayet || 26 ||

śmaśāna karpaṭe mantrī śmaśānāṅgāralekhitam |
dhattūrakarase naiva tāḍayet karavīrakaiḥ || 27 ||

grahā bhūtāḥ piśācāśca jvarānasyanti tatkṣaṇāt |
svaiḥ svaiḥ karmavibhāgena dūtī vidyāṃ niyojayet || 28 ||

vidyāṅgāni pravakṣyāmi yeṣu karmasu yujyate |
aṣṭāmbujeṣu hṛdayaṃ śāntipuṣṭyādikīṃ kriyām || 29 ||

vasyākarṣaṇa karme tu kapāleṣu niyojayet |
bhedanaṃ chedanaṃñcaiva māraṇeṣu śiro bhavet || 30 ||

ṣaṭkoṇe maṇḍale nyastaṃ ripunāma samanvitam |
śikhā strīṇāṃ kapāleṣu arddhacandreṣu vinyaset || 31 ||

rocanānāma saṃyukte tāḍitāt karṣayet kṣaṇāt |
kavacenāṣṭa kīlāntu kṣīravṛkṣa samudbhavam || 32 ||

mantritā nitvarenmantrī gṛhe vādhvā na jāyate |
astreṇa vā vidhānajñaḥ sarvarakṣāṃ tu kārayet || 33 ||

p. 27a)

tārakaṃ netramuddiṣṭaṃ śāntipuṣṭe niyojayet |
kapāle bhūrjapatre ca likhedaṣṭa dalāmbujam || 34 ||

suraktā rocanā caiva likhayecca diśāgatām |
guhyāṅgānāma saṃyuktaṃ tāpayet khadirānale || 35 

bhūrjamākramya pādena japedaṣṭa śataṃ budhaḥ |
ānayeddevadaityāstu siddhavidyā dharāstathā || 36 ||

yakṣarakṣapiśācāśca vaśyā jāyanti kiṅkarāḥ |
trikoṇe tu jvaraṃ dāhaṃ ṣaṭkoṇe chedana māraṇam || 37 ||

khaḍgeṣu punarevai tadarddha candre tu pauṣṭikam |
śāntiñca bhavate kṣipraṃ dhvaje voccāṭanaṃ bhavet || 38 ||

gadāyāḥ stambhanaṃ kuryād guhyakānāṃ tu sādhanam |
śūleṣu devadaivatyānāṃ trāsaṃ kurvanti yoṣitāḥ || 39 ||

dūtī mantreṇa saṃyojyaṃ dūtīnāṃ karmameva ca |
guhyāṅgānāṃ tu yatkarma dūtīnāñcaiva yadbhavet || 40 ||

tritrikeṣu ca sā vidyā nibaddhā piṇḍa saṃsthitāḥ |
tribhāgabhāgabhāgañca praṇītā mudramudritā || 41 ||

tribhirādau tribhirmadhye tribhiścānte pratiṣṭhitā |
tribhiranyaistu sā vidyā mudrairummudritā khaga || 42 ||

p. 27b)

bhedinī vajratuṇḍā ca karālī ca tritīyāsā |
prastāreṇa tu sā siddhā anekākāra bhedataḥ || 43 ||

āgneyyāṃ vajratuṇḍā ca tribhirvīje vyavasthitā |
dahanaṃ śoṣaṇañcaiva jvarañca kurute kṣaṇāt || 44 ||

karālī nair-ṛte caiva tribhireva vyavasthitā |
chedanaṃ bhedanañcaiva unmanīkaraṇantathā || 45 ||

praṇītā tribhiḥ sāmīre vidveṣoccāṭanaṃ prati |
īśāne tribhire vai tadbhedinī tatra saṃsthitā || 46 ||

kīlanaṃ pararāṣṭrasya pātanaṃ māraṇaṃ tathā |
apasavyena trīṇyevaṃ mastrasaṃghānnipātayet || 47 ||

karṣaṇīścāyudhānāñcaraṇe mohakarī bhavet |
arcanī kramayogena madhye dvitripradakṣiṇān || 48 ||

pūrvādi kramayogena mudrā pūrvakrameṇa tu |
nṛtyanaṃ vapanañcaiva bandhanañca pralāpanam || 49 ||

apasavyena trīṇyevamaśmavātajalaṃ taḍit |
vidyāvyaktamatodbhītā bhedinī vandhanī kṣaṇāt || 50 ||

p. 28a)

paśyanti vigrahāvasthaṃ mantriṇaṃ ripusainyakāḥ |
karālī kramasodbhītā tejaḥ piṇḍaṃ mahadbhutam || 51 ||

paśyanti ripavaḥ sarve trastāyānti diśodaśa |
evañcaturbhiḥ sā devī tribhiranyaiḥ khageśvara || 52 ||

sannaddhā vividhairastrairmarudganiḥ khagocare |
bhedaṃ caiva tu bījānāṃ mudrā bhedaṃ caturvidham || 53 ||

yopātāti naraḥ prājñaḥ saśakraṃ vijayiṣpati |
ekākī devadaityāśca yakṣarakṣogaṇānapi || 54 ||

nāgavidyādharāścaiva gandharvannṛpasainyakān |
kṣaṇāddhijjayati saṅgrāme bhuṅkte rājyamakaṇṭakam || 55 ||

idaṃ sannacakraṃ tu ṣoḍaśāre likhedbudhaḥ |
tatrastho dhārayettaṃ turaṇe dyūte jayo bhavet || 56 ||

mohanaṃ sarvaśatrūṇāṃ stambhanañca parājayam |
dhāraṇādasya cakrasya jayate ripusainikam || 57 ||

kṣīravṛkṣodbhavekīle kumārī sūtraveṣṭite |
alaktena tu saṃdvannaiḥ kramādvīja stribhistribhiḥ || 58 ||

japtvācāṣṭaśatañcaikaṃ maṣṭa dikṣu viniḥkṣipet |
nyaset pūrvādi yogena svarāṣṭrasya vidhānataḥ || 59 ||

p. 28b)

vyutkramāt pararāṣṭrasya bījairmantraistribhistribhiḥ |
āyasāsthimayā vāpi nikhaneddigvidikṣu ca || 60 ||

svarāṣṭrasya bhavedrakṣāviṣāgnistvodakeṣupi |
mantravidyāparairyuktāparacakropaghātanam || 61 ||

kīlitaṃ pararāṣṭraṃ tu mohitaṃ satya varjitam |
buddhivīryabalabhraṣṭaṃ manekopadravairyutam || 62 ||

vighnairvināśakaiḥ kṛtyairmārī vyādhini pīḍitam |
sādhayeda cirādeva mahādurga samāśritam || 63 ||

svarāṣṭrasyaiva rakṣante pararāṣṭrasya kīlanam |
yo vijānāti tatvena sabhuṅkte pṛthivītalam || 64 ||

calādi trikramāyogaṃ śirenābhyaṅghri yogataḥ |
kajratuṇḍā samāyuktaṃ manasā mantramuccaret || 65 ||

vaddhyate kāladaṣṭopi koṣṭhāgne tu nirodhanāt |
dravādi triprayogoyaṃ karālā mudrayā saha || 66 ||

jalpaṃ krīḍāñca kurute icchayā sādhaka tu |
nair-ṛtyādi prayogoyaṃ praṇītā sahayojitaḥ || 67 ||

p. 29a)

nirviṣaṃ kurute pātraṃ harate trividhaṃ viṣam |
vyomāgni yogayuktastu vilomena tadeva tu || 68 ||

śiro nā bhautā cāṃhrau yojito nirviṣī bhavet |
pūrvādi bhedanī vaddhvā daṣṭrikā yā tu kīlanaḥ || 69 ||

vajrā sanījvaladvahniḥ kṣaṇādeva niyojayet |
nāgarākṣasa yakṣāṃścapiśācān bhūtasākinīm || 70 ||

grahānsārdūla turagāntrājān saṃsvodyatānnarān |
śakaṭādyātmahāyantrān sambhayet potakānapi || 71 ||

trināḍimārga saṃrodhī dehasthaḥ kurute kṣaṇāt |
īśānādi prayogoyaṃ bhavedinī mudrayā saha || 72 ||

pararāṣṭrāṇi sarvāṇi mudrayennātra saṃśayaḥ |
sphoṭayet pararāṣṭrāṇi bhagnaṃ kurvanti tatkṣaṇāt || 73 ||

agnyākāśagato mantrī sarvadurgāṇi sādhayet |
īśānādyagni yogena karālyā śastrakhātanam || 74 ||

tatkāle dhārayet krodhaṃ mārayettridaśānapi |
uttarādiṣu yogoyaṃ sarvagranthiṃ nivārayet || 75 ||

chedayet sarvapāṣāṇi pāśasthopi bhaveddhruvam |
vāyavyādi prayogoyaṃ vajratuṇḍā samanvitaḥ || 76 ||

p. 29b)

stambhanāveśanañcaiva kāladaṣṭasya mantriṇaḥ |
yaṣṭyupānahacchatre vā yojitoṣṭa śataṃ khaga || 77 ||

tīrthaṃ yāti sahatena naiva prāṇān vimuñcati |
nair-ṛtyādi calāntaṃ tu nāgākarṣaṇa kārakam || 78 ||

yoginī tu paṭe likhya jāti puṣpaistu tāḍayet |
āgacchati kṣaṇādeva arthadā kāmadā bhavet || 79 ||

yāmyādi triprayogoyaṃ yakṣakanyāṃ samānayet |
dahanādi prayogoyaṃ śākinīkarṣayet kṣaṇāt || 80 ||

yatra sthāne japedvīraḥ karālyā mudrayā saha |
āgnyādi tridalāntaṃ tu karālyā mudrayā japet || 81 ||

prasannādarśayecchīghraṃ khaḍgāṅguṣṭe ca darpaṇe |
dīpe vā sarvakāryāṇi pṛṣṭāste kathayanti ca || 82 ||

īśānādi nair-ṛtyāṃ tu triṣu sthāneṣu yojitaḥ |
praṇītā saṃjñitā hyeṣā kṣaṇānnāśayate grahān || 83 ||

dahyanti viklavamānāstu pātra muktvā palāyate |
calādidanāntaṃtu triṣu sthāneṣu yojitam || 84 ||

p. 30a)

praṇītā saṃjñitā hyeṣā paṭe likhya piśācikāḥ |
śmaśānāṅgārakairlikhya kuḍye vā tāḍayettataḥ || 85 ||

citibhasmanā saptāhāt siddhyatenmāmadā bhavet |
nair-ṛtyādyantu īśānāṃ vajra tuṇḍāṃ tu bandhayet || 86 ||

ānayed garuḍa nāgāñjapamānasya mantriṇaḥ |
kāmadāśca bhavantyete sādhakasya na saṃśayaḥ || 87 ||

madhyādi savya caturo bhedinī mudrayojitaḥ |
kāṣṭhapāṣāṇakāveśaḥ svasthāveśaśca jāyate || 88 ||

madhyādi vāruṇīṃ yāvadvajra tuṇḍā samanvitaḥ |
kuṣṭādyā vyādhayo ye tu nāśayennātra saṃśayaḥ || 89 ||

madhyādi uttarāntaṃ tu karālī vandhanyājjayet |
rakṣayedātmano vidyāṃ prativādī yadā śivaḥ || 90 ||

rakṣayedātmano he haṃ svavalañca nirodhayet |
madhyādi aindraparyantaṃ bhedinī mudrayā saha || 91 ||

chedayet paramantrāṇi viṣaṃ nirviṣatāṃ nayet |
āgneyīkhagatāṃ kṛtvā sarvaduṣṭānnipātayet || 92 ||

yāmyādi vyutkramā madhyaṃ vajra saṃpuṭa yojitam |
madhyenāma tu vinyasya jalakoṣa vināśanam || 93 ||

p. 30b)

vāruṇyādi tato madhyaṃ yuktaṃ koṣadaleṣu ca |
śuddhavā'śuddhatāṃ neti kṣaṇānmantrī na saṃśayaḥ || 94 ||

saumyādi madhyamāntaṃ tu gurutvañjāyate ghaṭe |
pūrvādi madhyamāntaṃ tu laghutvaṃ kurute kṣaṇāt || 95 ||

koṣṭhakenādi karmaṃ tu vyomagena dvitīyakam |
praṇītāṃ vajratuṇḍāñca mudrān tatra niyojayet || 96 ||

bhedastejaḥ śivaḥ śaktirīśvaraḥ pañca āyase |
pātre ca viṣaraktena niyuktā vajramaṇḍale || 97 ||

pūrvādi kramayogeṇa madhyenāma vidarbhayet |
śuddhopi dahyate tena svayaṃ yadi jalādhipaḥ || 98 ||

īśaḥ sadāśivo viṣṇustārkṣyaścaiva tu pañcamaḥ |
vāruṇaṃ maṇḍalaṃ bhūrje gorocana yā samālikhet || 99 ||

kulālacakra mṛlliṅgaṃ madhye nikṣipya mantravit |
nikṣipet kumbhamadhye tu divyastambho bhaveddhruvam || 100 ||

divyastambhakarāyetu sarvedyustejasā vahāḥ |
bhūrje rocanayā likhya vajramaṇḍalamaṃ dhyataḥ || 101 ||

p. 31a)

saṃjñā dūtībhirguhyāṅgaiḥ pratipakṣasya darbhayet |
ākrāmyadhārayettaṃ tu dyūtejayati mānavaḥ || 102 ||

tārkṣyaḥ śaktiḥ śivastejo brahmā pañca ime khaga |
vivaradvāramāśritya bhedinī mudrayā japet || 103 ||

agnistha khecaro bhūtvā sarvavāndvāri niḥkṣipet |
santrastā nirgame kṣipraṃ kanyā pātālavāsinī || 104 ||

kare gṛhya praviśanti kriḍate kalpamuttamam |
divyāśraye japenmantrī siddhyate nātra saṃśayaḥ || 105 ||

brahmāviṣṇuḥ śivaścai sadā śiva sabhedanam |
sādhakaḥ sthiracittastu akṣatāṅgaśavopari || 106 ||

japet sahakarālyā tu vajramaṇḍala madhyagaḥ |
kṣaṇānniṣkamate jihvā khaḍgaścā guṭikātha vā || 107 ||

śavasyaiva yadvā sastatpaṭaṃ gṛhṇate sadā |
antardhānī bhavet sahijavairbheda sa dṛśyate || 108 ||

tatpādacarmamārūḍho jambudvīpaṃ kṣaṇāt kramet |
taccakṣurañjaneryuktaṃ grahaṇaṃ siddhamañjanam || 109 ||

īśaḥ śaktiḥ śivastejodāraṇaṃ piṅgalā khaga |
bhedinīṃ vandhayen mantrī japedaṣṭaśataṃ tadā || 110 ||

p. 31b)

jvalategniṃ na sandeha ārdrakāṣṭhepi yojitaḥ |
agnināḍīniruddhā tu koṣṭhagā vījasaṃyutā || 111 ||

bhedanī garbha nikṣiptā japedaṣṭaśataṃ kudhaḥ |
nirvījīkaraṇaṃ hyetad vṛkṣaṃ śoṣayate kṣaṇāt || 112 ||

paśūnāṃ grahaṇaṃ kṣipraṃ pāśānāṃ dahanaṃ tathā |
vyomasthaṃ tu japeddhyetajjapāṣṭa śatasaṃkhyayā || 113 ||

bhedaḥ sadāśivaḥ śivoviṣṇurbrahmāpunastathā |
amṛtaṃ vyomagaṃ kṛtvā tatkṣaṇā'pyāyanaṃ bhavet || 114 ||

brahmātejaḥ śivaḥ śaktirgaruḍaśca karālinī |
niruddho marutaḥ saṃcāraḥ pararāṣṭrasya sanmukham || 115 ||

japtvā cāṣṭaśataṃ yāvat sainyamindravalopamam |
kīlayet stambhayet sarvaṃ niśceṣṭaṃ balavarjitam || 116 ||

khagoviṣṇuḥ śivaścaiva sadāśiva umāpatiḥ |
ekayā nirgamo mantrī vajratuṇḍā tu bandhayet || 117 ||

nigrahennāga yakṣāṇāṃ grahāṇāṃ śākinīṣu ca |
bhūtapreta piśācānāṃ daṃṣṭriṇāṃ ca nigrahe || 118 ||

p. 32a)

narāye duṣṭamanasaḥ pātayed vihvalāṃ kṣaṇāt |
īśaḥ śaktiḥ khago viṣṇuḥ śivaścaiva sadāśivaḥ || 119 ||

praṇītā mṛtarodhena kaṇṭhamātra jalesthitaḥ |
nāgāgrahāṃstathā meghānānayeṣṭa śatena tu || 120 ||

mahāvṛṣṭiñca kurute yāvajjalamavasthitaḥ |
sadāśivaḥ śivo viṣṇurbrahmatejovidāraṇaḥ || 121 ||

paṭhe likhya balārūpaṃ surūpāstrī tathāṅganā |
svaliṅgasūtra jālena purādyena tu dhūpitam || 122 ||

bhedanī agni saṃcārayogādaṣṭaśataṃñjapet |
tadābharaṇa rūpeṇa paṭe kanyāvatāraṇam || 123 ||

tayā saharamedvīraḥ purājyena vivarjanam |
bhedanaṃ tejapadmorthaṃ viṣṇuḥ śivaḥ sadāśivaḥ || 124 ||

karālī bhūtavṛkṣeṣu piśācīṃ darśayeddhruvam |
siddhyate caiva saptāhājjaptvāṣṭaśatasaṃkhyayā || 125 ||

karṇe tu vārttāṃ kathyante trailokye yā vyavasthitā |
bhūtaṃ bhavyaṃñca yatkiñcidvarttamānañca yadbhavet || 126 ||

bhāryā tu bhavate sā tu ānayenmarttya yoṣitaḥ |
sadāśivaḥ śivoviṣṇuḥ suparṇaḥ śaktirīśvaraḥ || 127 ||

p. 32b)

ṣaḍete japamānastu praṇītāṃ vaśvayetkṛdiṃ |
marutkoṣṭha samāruddhaṃ yatpuṣpaṃ hṛdi cintayet || 128 ||

pārijātakamevātra ākāśātpātayeddhruvam |
patraṃ puṣpaṃ halaṃ bhakṣyaṃ mānayennātrasaṃśayaḥ || 129 ||

gandhān manoramā divyādivyā ratnasemasuvāsasām |
darśayen mantrasiddhiṃ tu varabhogyāṃ tu mantriṇe || 130 ||

devāgni gurukāryeṣu sthityarthamātmanamā tat |
dhārayet preṣayedanyaṃ bhedanyāṃ khagarudgataḥ || 131 ||

suparṇaḥ śaktirīśānaḥ sadāśivaḥ śivo hariḥ |
preṣayitvā nilaṃvyome praṇītāṃ guhyadeśataḥ || 132 ||

kanyasorddhvāṅgulānkṛtvā japedaṣṭaśataṃ budhaḥ |
nāmasthā na diśaṃ jātivarṇarūpaṃ vayastathā || 133 ||

jñātvā caivānayetchīghraṃ yojanānāṃ śatādapi |
preṣaṇe vyomagāṃ kṛtvā ātmasthañcaivakarṣaṇe || 134 ||

nayane vyomagānāḍī karme tadvā mayā bhavet |
vāruṇemaṇe madhyage aindra īśasaumya ca vāyave || 135 ||

vaddhvā tu bhedinī mudrāṃ makṣate strī śavesthitā |
vyomagastu japedvīraḥ ruttiṣṭhennātra saṃśayaḥ || 136 ||

p. 33a)

tatrārūḍhaḥ kramenmantrī trailokyaṃ sa carācaram |
kāmarūpadharaḥ śrīmān svacchandaḥ śaktidīpitaḥ || 137 ||

samīrottara sīmāne aindramadhye tu vāruṇe |
visuvena tu ruddhena karālyā mayutaṃ smaret || 138 ||

mudrayet tatkṣaṇādeva paśuṃ pātayate kṣaṇāt |
āpyākāśaṃ sthitaṃ dīptaṃ yāmyāṃ rakṣo diśādhipam || 139 ||

śayanastho japenmantrī yāvannidrāmupāgatā |
sañcitya hṛdaye kārya vajratuṇḍā niyogataḥ || 140 ||

svapramāṇavakī vidyāprayogo bhuvidurlabhaḥ |
svapneṣu dṛśyate sarvaṃ yatkāryaṃ hṛdi saṃsthitam || 41 ||

anenaiva prayogena sarvajño jāyate naraḥ |
rākṣasaṃ dravasāmīraṃ saumyaṃ śivamanonmanī || 142 ||

tejasaṃvārayogena karālī vandhanā japet |
śatrūṇāṃ duṣṭa satvānāṃ te lakṣaṃ kurute sadā || 143 ||

gacchenniḥ śaṅkaḥ cittastu mantraṃ mantrī hṛdisthitam |
śatrūnvijayate sarvānte lakṣāścaiva mohitān || 144 ||

p. 33b)

dakṣamadhyottarasteha pūrvācāgni daleṣu ca |
ṣaḍetābhirjapenmantrī bhedinī nābhiyojitā || 145 ||

recanāttasya devasya jalaṃ pāvaka varcasam |
kuṣṭhādīnāṃ tu vyādhīnāṃ pāpānāñcaiva nāśanam || 146 ||

āgneyamaindramaiśāne uttare madhyadakṣiṇe |
ekayā vajratuṇḍā tu astu sanmukhamāgatam || 147 ||

stambhayet khecaro bhūtvā kṛtyānena pracoditāḥ |
yāmyavyomyottaradale vāyavye cāpare khaga || 148 ||

koṣṭhagādyagni nāḍī tu vaddhvā mudrāṃ karālinīm |
śākinī grahavetālā bhūtāḥ pretāśca rākṣasāḥ || 149 ||

nāgādahyanti muhyanti bhītāstrastārudanti ca |
ānayet pārśvato mantrīkṛtakāryaṃ vimokṣayet || 150 ||

caladravaḥ khagaḥ śaktiḥ śivatejaḥ śatena tu |
ṣaḍguṇāhomite mantrī samahometha gocaraḥ || 151 ||

karṣayennāma saṃyukto divyādivyāstu yoṣitaḥ |
uttaraṃ vyomagaṃ yāmyamagnigaṃ pūrvamīśagam || 152 ||

p. 34a)

homayet sanmukho mantrī rājikāṣṭa śatena tu |
ānayedurvaśīñcāpi dahyamānāṃ na saṃśayaḥ || 153 ||

īśagaṃ pūrvamagnistvaṃ dakṣiṇaṃ madhyamottaram |
yogayukto yadā mantrī siddhārthe śatahomataḥ || 154 ||

sa kṣipramānayeddivyāṃ yoṣitāṃ kāmavihvalām |
uttaraṃ vyomagaṃ śaktirgaruḍo viṣṇupadmajam || 155 ||

agnicāra samāyogānnimbatailākta homataḥ |
vidveṣayetkṣaṇādevaṃ kāmadeva samaḥ priyaḥ || 156 ||

dahanāntakarakṣāya calatejo vidārakam |
itistu saptabhirbījaiḥ rakṣatāṅgaṃ salakṣaṇam || 157 ||

āruhyorasibhedinyā viṣuve koṣṭhagā yadā |
vajrārgalapurasthaṃ tu śatamaṣṭottarañjapet || 158 ||

mahāśabdo bhavedbhāmo jvālā jihvā subhīṣaṇā |
āruhya śavavaktre tu kṣīraṃ bhuktvā vrajettataḥ || 159 ||

śyāmā katarāḍulāstatra kṣiptvā ca mārabhet |
devāgni guruve datvā sahāyyebhyaśca sādhakaḥ || 160 ||

ekaikaṃ taṇḍulaṃ bhakṣet utpattet khecare pade |
tacchavamakṣayaṃ divyaṃñjātarūpamayaṃ bhavet || 161 ||

p. 34b)

rājyārthaṃ khecarāṃ siddhiṃ sādhakasya dhruvaṃ bhavet |
vyutkramāt khecaro mantrī vajratuṇḍāllabhedasim || 162 ||

ūrddhvaṅgacchatya saṃdehyevaṅga vidyādharo bhavet |
rakṣadravamaruttejo bhedanaṃ pṛthivī śiraṣī || 163 ||

vyomasañcārayogenayadastraṃñcālabhet khaga |
uttiṣṭhanti na sandehaḥ kare gṛhyatadotpatet || 164 ||

apasavyena tānyeṣayaṃ yaṃ rocati vāhanam |
yasya devasya yatproktaṃ tadārūhya jayo bhavet || 165 ||

tadastramāsane mantrī utpatennātra saṃśayaḥ |
siddhyate tatpuraṃ tasya yasya devasya vāhanam || 166 ||

maruttejo bhedamaindraṃ śikhi śaktiḥ khageśvara |
parāstra mālabhenmantrītamāruhya samutpatet || 167 ||

vilomena tu te vījāḥ praṇītā bandhanā japet |
marutkoṣṭha samopetānmānayen mṛgapakṣiṇaḥ || 168 ||

bhedamaindraḥ śikhiśaktiḥ suparṇovaruṇo mahān |
ekāṃ vai vā likhet kanyāṃgairikārocanātha vā || 169 ||

p. 35a)

surūpāṃ tu bhagoddeśe vāma pādena cākramet |
praṇītāṃ kanyasāṃ vāmā ūrddhvāṃ kṛtvā supīḍitām || 170 ||

tāḍitā sarṣapairmantraiḥ śatenaikena na nāmataḥ |
vihvalā tatkṣaṇādeva niḥśaṅkā mūḍhacetasā || 171 ||

āgacchati na sandeho mahārakṣaiḥ surakṣitā |
dakṣiṇāṃ kanyasāṃ pīḍya dakṣiṇena kramedvṛti || 172 ||

ānayet tatkṣaṇādevapurandara samaṃ nṛpam |
brahmāviṣṇuḥ suparṇaśca śaktirīśaḥ sadāśivaḥ || 173 ||

dāraṇaṃ sapta etāni siddhavījā ime khaga |
svaliṅgasūtranāryāyāḥ prathamaṃ taddine budhaḥ || 174 ||

kṣīraṃ kanyākapāle tu pañcānana puresthitaḥ |
niśācaro yadā mantrī niśāyāṃ sādhanaṃ bhavet || 175 ||

karālī vandhanādvīro japenmantrī śatāṣṭakam |
śyāmākataṇḍulāndatvā pāpaṃ saṃsādhayet khaga || 176 ||

devāgni gurave bhāgaṃ sahāyyebhyaśca dāpayet |
utpatet tatkṣaṇādeva svacchandaṃ krīḍate khaga || 177 ||

kapālañca dhruvaṃ siddhiṃ cintāmaṇi samaṃ khaga |
yadyaccintayate dravyaṃ tattadānayate kṣaṇāt || 178 ||

p. 35b)

dāraṇaṃ tejayo brahmāviṣṇuḥ khaga manonmanī |
rudraḥ sadāśivaḥścāṣṭau karālīhyagni yogataḥ || 179 ||

marudvyoma gato mantrī citi bhasmantu homayet |
trisandhyaṃ tridinañcaiva cakravartti samaṃ nṛpam || 180 ||

mārayen nātrasandehonāmamantre niyojayet |
bhedanamaindrañca chedanaṃ rākṣasādhipam || 181 ||

drāvaṇaṃ cañcalaṃ dīptaṃ maṣṭāvete vidhānataḥ |
nīlārdra paṭamāvṛtya karālyā sahitañjapet || 182 ||

nirudhya koṣṭhagovīraḥ śmaśānāṅgāralekhitam |
śmaśānakarpaṭemantrī śaktibhinnamadhorddhvataḥ || 183 ||

daṇḍena cākramennābhau pādenākramya corasi |
dakṣiṇenāgni mārgasthaṃ paṭoyāṣadviśuṣyati || 184 ||

raktārdramārayettattumantrauṣadhi surakṣitaḥ |
jalārdraṃ triguṇe naiva brahmāviṣṇurumādayaḥ || 185 ||

mārayennātra sandehaḥ prayogohyanivarttakaḥ |
sadāśiveśaśaktiścaśca suparṇo viṣṇupadmajaḥ || 186 ||

p. 36a)

tejo bhedāṣṭa vījāstu bhedinī mudrayā saha |
vyāpayet sakalaṃ viśvaṃ vāyunāvyomagena tu || 187 ||

tuṣāra raktasaṃbhīte ābrahmabhuvanāntikām |
nāsāgne tu parānāḍī indragopakarānnibhā || 188 ||

padmasūtranibhā sūkṣmābhinnā sā sūcyamaṇḍalam |
gatvā somapuraṃ yāvajjapen mantraśataṃ budhaḥ || 189 ||

marutkoṣṭhagataṃ kṛtvā karṣayettāṃ śanaiḥ śanaiḥ |
lakṣaṇādvaktra madhye tu amṛtaṃ karṣayet mukham || 190 ||

tenāmṛtena dīvyena dehaṃ saṃtarpayedbudhaḥ |
kramāt kramādidaṃ yogaṃmabhyasennityameva ca || 191 ||

ajarāmaro bhavenmantrī svacchandaḥ sarvagaḥ prabhuḥ |
cakravarttitvamāpnoti mahākalpāyuṣo bhavet || 192 ||

svadikthairaṣṭabhirbījairmantrayitvāṣṭakoṣṭakam |
sarkarāṣṭā tathā cāṣṭau ekaikaṃ tu śanaiḥ śatam || 193 ||

vadhvā karālinīṃ mudrāṃmagnināḍī nirodhanāt |
japedvyaṣṭaśataṃ mantrī aṣṭabījaistadeva tu || 194 ||

p. 36b)

niravane kṣetrarakṣāṃ tu bhasmaṃ mantrī prakīrayet |
āsutṛṇajalaukāśca śalabhāyāḥ sarīsṛpāḥ || 195 ||

drādyabandho bhavedeṣāṃ pakṣiṇāṃ mṛgasūkarām |
pañcānanaṃ śilāpaṭṭe vajrasaṃpuṭalāñchitam || 196 ||

likhet puravaraṃ mantrī tvaritā hṛdinandanam |
śmaśānāṅgāralikhitaṃ tālakena samanvitam || 197 ||

unmatta rasa saṃyuktaṃ likhedaṣṭa dalāmbujam |
volomā tvaritāṃ likhyabhedanaṃ karṇikopari || 198 ||

ripornāmāni sarvāṇi karṇikopari ālikhet |
veṣṭayeccakravegā yā brahmavetālinī punaḥ || 199 ||

vajrasaṃpuṭa madhye tu diśāsu vidiśāsu ca |
dūtikāṣṭau samālikhya garbhe tu catusrake || 200 ||

guhyāṅgāni yathā sthāne hṛdayaṃ karṇikopari |
bhedanañcakravegāyā madhye yojyaṃ khageśvara || 201 ||

śivaścottara garbhe tu pūrvagarbhe śikhā bhavet |
dakṣiṇe kavacaṃ lekhyaṃ netraṃ paścimadiggatam || 202 ||

sarvāsu vajrakoṭīṣu astreṇaiva tu veṣṭayet |
pūrvapaścimadiktaṃsthaṃ dakṣiṇottaradiggatam || 203 ||

p. 37a)

vajrāgreṇa viniṣkāntaṃ paridhena tu bhedayet |
pūrvāgre vajratuṇḍā tu śabarī caiva dakṣiṇe || 204 ||

plavaṅgī paścime bhāge piṅgalā cottaresthitā |
īśāgnistha vajrasya yā rekhā tu vinirgatā || 205 ||

jvālinīṃ tu samālikhya agnikoṇe tu nair-ṛte |
karālīṃ tu samālikhya nair-ṛtyāṃ vāyavottagā || 206 ||

manovegāṃ likhet tatra vāyavādīśa vajragām |
raudrīntatra samālikhya viparītākṣarairbudhaḥ || 207 ||

lekhyābhi sarvamantrāṇi asminvajrārgaleṣu ca |
dvitīye tu śilāpṛṣṭhe punareva samālikhet || 208 ||

puṭitvānyonya sandhānivālarajvā nibandhayet |
lepayitvā tu śiktena pūjayedvidhinā tataḥ || 209 ||

pītakṛṣṇaistathā puṣpairgandhapuṣpaupahārakaiḥ |
niḥkṣipejjalamadhye tu bhūmau vāpi nikhātayet || 210 ||

catuṣkālaṃ bhavet pūjāṃ mantrasyāsya khageśvaraḥ |
stambhayet parasainyāni mohayedbandhayet punaḥ || 211||

p. 37b)

bhedayen mārayedvāpi śakrasainyānyanekaśaḥ |
vajrārgalena saṃruddhvā bhayatrastāḥ suvihvalāḥ || 212 ||

nirastāgatavīryāśca vepamānābhayārditāḥ |
śatravaḥ sukhasādhyāśca indratulyā mahābalāḥ || 213 ||

vajrārgalena saṃruddhya padamekaṃ nayāntite |
manastambhaṃ vaktrastambhaṃ gatistambhaṃ bhaveddhruvam || 214 ||

pañcānanasya vinyāsaṃ vajrārgalasya mudraṇam |
yo jānāti khageśedaṃ sa ripuṃ vijayiṣyati || 215 ||

bhedanaṃ paridhairbāhyaṃ divyānāṃ stambhanaṃ sphuṭam |
ghaṭāgnyāyaviṣaṃ koṣaṃ stambhayennātra saṃśayaḥ || 216 ||

aśuddhaṃ śuddhatāṃ neti viparīta padesthitam |
kramākṣare niyuktā tu vidyā pañcānane pure || 217 ||

śuddhaṃ tu dahate kṣipraṃ māyase pātra nirmale |
śikhipittena vā likhya śuddhañcā śuddhatāṃ nayet || 218 ||

bhūrje rocanayālikhya etadvajrārgalapuram |
tatra smairmantrabījaistu rakṣāṃ dehe tu kārayet || 219 ||

veṣṭitā tārahome narakṣeyaṃ tu mahābalā |
akālamṛttyu dahanīrājopadravanāśanī || 220 ||

p. 38a)

divyādivyāni vighnāni viṣāgnyāpabhayānyapi |
daṃṣṭriṇāñcaripūnāñca dhvaṃsanī tu surakṣiṇī || 221 ||

prārabdhānāṃ tu kāryāṇāṃ siddhidā puṣṭidāyikā |
saubhāgyakaraṇī nityaṃ dhāryā khageśvara || 222 ||

dyūtena mohayeddhyeṣā pratipakṣaṃ raṇeṣu ca |
vandhyānāṃ putradāhyeṣāsau bhāgyakaraṇī bhavet || 223 ||

cintāmaṇirivātreyaṃ yatrayatropa yujyate |
sādhayet sarvakramāṇi vidhiṃ vijñāya tattvataḥ || 224 ||

uktāye lekhya yantrāstu guhyāṅgāsra raṇaṃ likhet |
punastu dūtyāvaraṇaṃ tato mantrāstu siddhidāḥ || 225 ||

pañcānanasya yā vidyā viṃśākṣaramudīritā |
pañcānanapure vidyāṃ pañcabhāgena cālikhet || 226 ||

ādimadhye tu pūrveṇa sādhyena tu vilekhayet |
yatra mākramate tena tataḥ kṣipraṃ prasiddhyati || 227 ||

dūtayonāḍi kāryeṣu ādau yojya vicakṣaṇaḥ |
mudrāstu caiva saṃyojya tataḥ karmāṇi kārayet || 228 ||

p. 38b)

vajratuṇḍā ca jvālinī viṣuve tu niyojayet |
vāmayā caiva saṃyojya śavarī ca karālinī || 229 ||

plavaṅgī ca manovegā dūtayaśca tathāgrataḥ |
kapilā caiva raudrī ca cakravegā tṛtīyakā || 230 ||

brahmavetālinī caiva catasroṣu hyanāḍikāḥ |
vajratuṇḍā jvālinī ca vajratuṇḍe niyojayet || 231 ||

śavarī karālinī caiva karālyā tu niyojayet |
plavaṅgī manovegā ca praṇīt mayāṃ niyojayet || 232 ||

kapilā caiva raudrī ca bhedanyātu niyojayet |
cakravegā sa bījānāṃ brahmavetālinīṃ nyaset || 233 ||

nāḍībhiścāra saṃyuktaṃ daśadūtī samanvitam |
sannaddhaṃ pañcamudrābhiḥ stvaritā bhedaniścayaḥ || 234 ||

guhyāṅgaṃ mantrasaṃyukto astreṇa ca surakṣitaḥ |
sādhayet sarvatsiddhīniyogahīnopi sādhakaḥ || 235 ||

ityādye troṭale tvaritāmūlasūtre aṣṭamaḥ paṭalaḥ || 
p. 39a)

athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi kriyājñānañca mantrajam |
prathamaṃ brahmarandhre tu śatañvāraṃ niyojayet || 1 ||

sa tu madhye dvibhiścānyairdvibhiranyairvibhāvasuḥ |
somaṃ dvibhiḥ susaṃruddhaṃ sūryamekena rodhayet || 2 ||

bhedakena hṛdisthānaṃ rodhayitvā tu mantriṇaḥ |
dehasthaṃ tatkṣaṇādeva ātmasthena pareṇa tu || 3 ||

preritastva balā bījaistejarūpairmaharddhikaiḥ |
gacchate pahnanālena vāyunā caiva niṣkramet || 4 ||

etatkarma praṇītāyāḥ kārayen mantriṇastataḥ |
ādau pretasya kartavyaṃ karmacaitatkhageśvara || 5 ||

nivodhayeddhṛdisthāne vāyudeśe ca tadbhavet |
bhedanyā bhedayedvāyuṃviśet parapuraṃ sukham || 6 ||

yadyapi kṛmisaṃkīrṇaṃ kucchitaṃ śopamāgatam |
chinnaṃ bhinnaṃ mahāstraistu nayate yatra rocate || 7 ||

athavā śobhanaṃ pātraṃ puruṣo vā valāpi vā |
bhogāśakta praviṣṭastu krīḍate yāvadrocate || 8 ||

punastena prayogeṇa niṣkāmyet praviśetha vā |
anenaiva prayogeṇa śrotraṃ śiṣṇe nirodhayet || 9 ||

p. 39b)

prathamaṃ brahmarandhre tu naiva yojyaṃ tadeva tu |
nirodhaṃ vā gude deśe bhedanaṃ hṛdi vinyaset || 10 ||

bhedinīmudrayā mantrī jvalatpiṇḍaṃ sutejasam |
kṣaṇenotkramaṇaṃ kṛtvā ātmanaśca parasya vā || 11 ||

niṣkamed brahmarandhreṇa mokṣaṃ yāti parāparam |
yaṃ gatvā na nivarteta sarvavyāpī śivo bhavet || 12 ||

tārakaṃ brahmarandhre tu prathamaṃ se tu madhyataḥ |
punastrikamagnimārge somamārge punastrikam || 13 ||

bhedanañcaiva saṃyojya sūryamaṇḍalamadhyagam |
tārakaṃ hṛdaye sthāne yojayecchiṣyadehataḥ || 14 ||

praṇītā vandhanān mantrī vyomasthaṃ naiva kārayet |
kṣaṇenotthāpanaṃ kuryād dṛṣṭvā sarvamidaṃ jagat || 15 ||

svapurā sūkṣmamūrtisthaḥ śivādava nigocaram |
vaddhvā tu bhedinī mudrāṃ recayitvā samīraṇam || 16 ||

bhedanañcaiva saṃyojya gulphadeśe tu mantriṇaḥ |
viśate granthimārge tu bhidyate nātra saṃśayaḥ || 17 ||

jānudeśe tu mantrajñasteja piṇḍaṃ niyojayet |
kaṭinābhi hṛdikaṇṭhe vaktre bhūmadhyamūrddhani || 18 ||

p. 40a)

vilomānyasta vījāstu praṇītāṃ bandhayed hṛdi |
granthibhedaṃ tu hṛddeśe yāvat kurvanti mantriṇaḥ || 19 ||

atītānāgataṃ sarvaṃ varttamānañca vindati |
tālagranthi vibhedena mantriṇaśca khageśvara || 20 ||

śravaṇaṃ mananañcaiva rakṣadūrāt pravartate |
udgiret sarvaśāstrāṇi divyā vāṇī pravartate || 21 ||

bhrūmadhye bhedayitvā tu paśyate vindumujvalam |
paśyate bhuvanān sarvānātmanā dehamadhyataḥ || 22 ||

mūrddhni granthi vibhedena bhavate vyāpakaḥ śivaḥ |
niṣkalaḥ paramaḥ sūkṣmaḥ pañcabhūta vyavasthitaḥ || 23 ||

yāvanna bhedayedraktaṃ prāṇāyāmayutena tu |
dvipañcaguṇite naiva svacchandaḥ sarvago bhavet || 24 ||

praṇītāṃ bandhanān mantrī vyomastho japamārabhet |
mūrddhvā mūrddhni gato vīra ekānte vijanesthitaḥ || 25 ||

dhyāyecca sakalāṃ vidyāṃ navavīja samanvitām |
svarūpeṇa svadehena japet sādhaka puṅgavaḥ || 26 ||

caturthe tu tato lakṣe darśanaṃ śrūyate niśi |
mudrā mūrddhni gato mantrī praṇītā japamācaret || 27 ||

p. 40b)


nirghoṣañca mahāraudramaṭṭahāsaṃ subhīṣaṇam |
āgacchanti mahāguhyāḥ siddhayogeśvareśvarāḥ || 28 ||

dūtībhiḥ sahitā bhīmāstevā marghaṃ pradāpayet |
hṛdaye guhyāṅgāṇāṃ nāmastṛkṣyusya jalena tu || 29 ||

praṇītā mudrayā caiva tena tṛpyanti guhyakāḥ |
siddhiṃ manepsitāt kāmāṁllabhate nātra saṃśayaḥ || 30 ||

tatohya dṛśyatāṃ yānti punareva japet sudhīḥ |
labdhānujño japenmantraṃ sādhakaśca na saṃśayaḥ || 31 ||

pañcame tu punarlakṣe tvaritāṃ paśyate svayam |
vajraṃ putraṃ mahāvīra khecaratvaṃ sudurlabham || 32 ||

pūrvoktārtha pradānena vidyāyā tu navākṣarā |
praṇītāṃ bandhayen mūrddhni daṇḍavadva sudhātale || 33 ||

praṇamya tṛptā sā devī tatraivāntaradhīyata |
yāgaṃ kṛtvā tu pūrvoktaṃ darśane darśane sudhīḥ || 34 ||

homayellakṣamekaṃ tu tilājyaṃ puraphalluṣam |
meṣājaṃ mahiṣaṃ nāraṃ lakṣe pūrṇe'gni madhyataḥ || 35 ||

dṛśyate tvaritā devī pūrṇāhutyāṃ vaśānataḥ |
tataśca hṛṣṛ manasā arghyaṃ dadyāt subhāṣitaḥ || 36 ||

p. 41a)

vīro hṛt kṣubhita cittastu tacca sāhasamācaret |
uktavatī tadā devī sādhuputra mahātapaḥ || 37 ||

ārādhitā subhaktena uttiṣṭhottiṣṭhagamyatām |
khecaratvaṃ mahāsiddhiṃ sahāyasahito vraja || 38 ||

tataḥ ścottiṣṭhate vīraḥ sahāya sahito nabham |
tejo rāśiṃ mahādīpto vrajate yatra rocate || 39 ||

svacchandaḥ sarvago nityaṃ śivatvamakṣayaṃ labhet |
asthi carmāvaśeṣaṃ tu śuṣkapiṇḍāśca dehinaḥ || 40 ||

sādhakānucarāye tu sahāyāmṛttyumāgatāḥ |
tepyardha binduṃ saṃspṛṣṭāstapaḥ śaktyopabṛṃhitāḥ || 41 ||

devyāḥ śaktisamopetā mantra tejaḥ samanvitā |
divyārūpadharo bhūtvā trinetra mukuṭojvalā || 42 ||

utpatanti mahātmānaḥ khecaratvaṃ sudurlabham |
krīḍante sarvalokeṣu śivaśaktyopabṛhitāḥ || 43 ||

eṣā śaivī mahāśaktiravāryā sarvakarmasu |
kalauyugepi pakṣīśa siddhidā nātrasaṃśayaḥ || 44 ||

p. 41b)

tvaritā hṛdgamāśīghrā kṣiprā dīptāsu siddhidā |
tīkṣṇā bhayāvahā caṇḍā ugrā saṃkṣipta siddhidā || 45 ||

tena sātvaritā devī vīrāṇāṃ tvarita siddhidā |
troṭaletantrasāre tu navavījāḥ parāḥ smṛtāḥ || 46 ||

sarvakarmakarī vidyā tārkṣyate kathitā mayā |
navavīja vidhiṃ jñātvā bhedaṃ bhedena bheditam || 47 ||

ekabījā navaproktā dvibījā pañca eva tu |
tribhirbījaiśca dvātriṃśaccaturbhiraṣṭa eva tu || 48 ||

pañcabhiśca bhavedaṣṭa ṣaḍbhiścaiva tu ṣoḍaśaḥ |
saptabījairbhavedaṣṭa aṣṭabhiśca turo bhavet || 49 ||

miśrakarma dvayaṃ proktaṃ guhyāṅgairaṣṭa eva tu |
dūtībhiścāṣṭa karmāṇi eva maṣṭottaraṃ śatam || 50 ||

guruvaktrādimāṃ prāpya sarvasiddhiphalaṃ labhet |
aṣṭottara karmaśataṃ vastuvindati deśikaḥ || 51 ||

sarudraḥ saśivaḥ sākṣādbhuktimuktiṃ labheddhruvam |
sādhayet pararāṣṭrāṇi rājyaśca pṛthivī tale || 52 ||

pāpahāsarvamartyānāṃ sarvānugrahakārakaḥ |
yaḥ smarettvaritā vidyāṃ jñānato'jñānatopi vā || 53 ||

p. 42)

sakṛduccārayeccāpinyāsa mātreṇa vartate |
sasnātaḥ sarvatīrtheṣu sarvayajñaphalaṃ labhet || 54 ||

dharmādharmairnalipyeta mokṣaṃ yāti sanātanam |
yatkiñcidvāṅmayaṃ sarvamaśeṣa bhuvanāntikam || 55 ||

tatsarvaṃ tvaritā madhye praviṣṭaṃ tu khageśvara |
yena jñātā parā vidyā sarvagā kāmagā śubhā || 56 ||

tasya siddhamidaṃ sarvaṃ trailokyaṃ sa carācaram |
anyatantre mayā tārkṣya bhrāmitā mantramudrayā || 57 ||

gopitaṃ siddhikāraṃ tu troṭale tu prakāśitam |
troṭalaṃ ye vijānanti bhedabhinnamanekadhā || 58 ||

macchakti preritā rudrā jambudvīpeṣu vartate |
mānuṣāṇāṃ hitārthāya anugraharatā sadā || 59 ||

niyuktā adhikāreṣu ācāryāste khageśvara |
vandhāḥ pūjyāścānumodyāmānyāste nityameva tu || 60 ||

yenedaṃ kāraṇaṃ divyaṃ rahasyaṃ mantra nirgatam |
niḥsaṃdigdhaṃ tantrārthaṃ khyāpitaṃ vidhinā sphuṭam || 61 ||

p. 43)

bhuktimuktipradātāraṃ tadguruṃ pūjayet sadā |
rahasyaṃ paramaṃ hyetat sarvajñānottamaṃ khaga || 62 ||

aparīkṣya na dātavyaṃ kāryaṃ guptataraṃ tvayā |
dātavyaṃ tu suśiṣyāya bhaktāya ca tapasvine || 63 ||

niveditātmano caiva troṭalaṃ tantramuttamam |
mūlavidyā tu vidyāṅgāḥ sapta eva prakīrtitāḥ || 64 ||

ṣaṭsaṃkhyātāstu guhyāṅgā dūtayastu daśa smṛtāḥ |
ekaṃ pañcānanaṃ viṃśacaturbhiradhikāni ca || 65 ||

akṣarāṇāṃ tu caikatra trisaptatyadhikaṃ śatam ||
śataiḥ saptabhirākhyātā paṭalairnavabhistathā || 66 ||

lakṣādhikā samuddhṛtya sārātsāraṃ susiddhidam |

ityādye troṭale mahātantre tvaritāmūlasūtre navamaḥ paṭalaḥ samāptaḥ ||
