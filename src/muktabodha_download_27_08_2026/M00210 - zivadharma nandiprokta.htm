brāhmaṃ sitaṃ kāpilavarṇayuktaṃ
kṣātraṃ tu vaiśyaṃ hyubhayātmakaṃ tat |
kṛṣṇaṃ tu śūdraṃ kathayanti bījaṃ
rudrākṣabījaprabhavantu jātyā || 3-2 ||

ekānanaṃ śaṅkara eva sākṣāt 
rudrākṣabījaṃ vinihanti hatyām |
dvivaktrabījaṃ bahugovadhaghnaṃ
deveśadaivatyamalabhyamakṣṇām || 3-3 ||

āgneyamuktaṃ vadanatrayantu
yoṣidvadhaghnaṃ dadhatāṃ kṣaṇena |
caturmukhaṃ yaccaturānanīyaṃ 
narasya hatyāṃ kṣaṇato nihanti || 3-4 ||

p. 16)

kālāgni * * ṇa tu pañcavaktraṃ
bhakṣyadussaṅgagapātakaghnam |
skāndaṃ hi yat ṣaḍvadanaṃ kareṇa
nareṇa dhārya dvijahantṛ madhye || 3-5 ||

saptānanaṃ bhogipatihyanantat
steyādviśuddhiṃ vidadhāti sadyaḥ |
vaināyakaṃ hyaṣṭamukhaṃ suvarṇaṃ
kūṭaṃ gurustrīgamanādyaghaghnam || 3-6 ||

tavānanaṃ bhairava eva devo
bhuktiṃ ca muktiṃ śivatulyakīrtim |
karoti vāmena kareṇa dhāryaṃ
hatyāyutānāṃ vinihantikoṭim || 3-7 ||

harissvayaṃ yaddaśavaktrabījaṃ
piśāca bhūtagraharākṣasāśca |
vetāla sarpapratiyodhisihma 
vyādikāṃstāṃśca nihanti śīghram || 3-8 ||

ekādaśāsyaṃ daśacaikaraudraṃ
dhāryaṃ śikhāyāṃ vidhinānavadye |
narāśvamedhāyutavājapeya-
sahasragodānasahasratulyam || 3-9 ||

p. 17)

yadvaktrāṇi daśadvayena kathitaṃ tadbhāskarīyaṃ smṛtaṃ kaṇṭhasthaṃ 
ravituṣṭi kṛddhayamakhaissamyaktapuṇyaissamam hastyaśva śva 
sṛgālamūṣikakhagaklībāndhanārīmṛgavyālaśśukabhekasarpamahiṣa-
vyādhātadoṣāpaham || 3-10 ||

yadvaktrāṇi daśadvayañca na labhet tatkārtikeyaṃ smṛtaṃ 
sarvaṃ siddhyati bhogabhogyamakhilaṃ siddhyet tat pādukā |
iṣṭārthāścarasārasādhanamatha bhrūṇāryamātā 
pitṛbhrātṛstrī vasato viśuddhyati dadhannāsmāt paraṃ vidyate || 3-11 ||

rudrākṣamekaṃ śikhayāpi dhāryaṃ 
ṣaṭtriṃśamālāvidadhītamūrdhni |
dvātriṃśakaiḥ karṇayuge ca sādhyā
śatārdhakḷptā hṛdayekṣamālā || 3-12 ||

kaṇṭhe dhṛtā yena sadākṣamālā
japtvāpnuyādyajñasahasrapuṇyam |
śvetaṃ raktamathāpi pītamasitaṃ viprādikānāṃ smṛtaṃ
rudrākṣannalabhedyathā gatamidandhāryaṃ śikhāyāṃ kare || 3-13 ||

kaṇṭhe karṇayugopavītahṛdayeṣvābadhyaraupyādibhiḥ
pañcārṇapraṇavātmakākhyamanubhiḥ kovānavadye bhavet |
snāne vā japahomayorvratavidhau devārcanādānayoḥ 
dīkṣāśrāddhakayoḥ kṛtaubhasabhasito rudrākṣamālādharaḥ ||

kuryātkarma ca vai kañca sakalaṃ syādakṣayaṃ yatphalaṃ

p. 18) 

no cedvyarthamidaṃ bhavatyayamaho pāpo navadye dhruvam |
kecit sūtakaśāva * * * malotsarge ca saṅge striyaḥ 
śuddhāvakṣakamālikānna dadhate tyāyuḥ kṣayasyāditi || 3-15 ||

anye pāpaharaṃ sadā dhṛtamiti procyaṃ bhuje karṇayoḥ
akṣaṃ mantrapurassaraṃ tu mahatī mālā na dhāryā śucau |
pāpiṣṭhopi narokṣamekamapi vā dhatte sa śuddho bhavet 
kurvanvāpi na lipyate hyanucite tattanmukhākṣoditaiḥ || 3-16 ||

puśchaṃ yojya mukhaṃ mukhena sadṛśaṃ muktāpravālāntaraiḥ
sauvarṇairmaṇibhirdadhanmakhaphalaṃ martyo navadyo bhavet |
rudrākṣaṃ vapuṣā dadhanyadi mṛtaśvāno'pi mukto bhavet |
khādannantyamuhūrtake śivapuraṃ gacchet sa no jāyate || 3-17 ||

kinnūktairbahubhissamantrakamidaṃ svāṅge sadādhārakaḥ
saṃsārannapunaḥ prayāti manujo vakṣye navadye tava |
badhvāsvarṇena rudrākṣaṃ rajate nātha vā mune |
śikhāyāṃ dhārayennityaṃ karṇayorvā samāhitaḥ || 3-18 ||

yajñopavīte haste vā kaṇṭhadeśethavā naraḥ |
śrīmatpañcākṣareṇaiva praṇavenaiva vāthavā || 3-19 ||

ātmamantreṇa medhāvī rudrākṣandhārayenmudā |
rudrākṣadhāraṇaṃ sākṣāt śivajñānasya sādhanam || 3-20 ||

sarvāśramāṇāṃ varṇānāṃ rudrākṣāṇāñca dhāraṇam |

p. 19) kartavyaṃ mantrataḥ proktaṃ dvijānānnānyavarṇinām || 3-21 ||

rudrākṣadhāraṇe lajjā yeṣāmasti mahāmune |
teṣānnāsti vinirmokṣaḥ saṃsārājjanmakoṭibhiḥ || 3-22 ||

rudrākṣadhāriṇaṃ dṛṣṭvā parivādaṃ karoti yaḥ |
utpattau tasya sāṃkaryamastyeveti viniścayaḥ || 3-23 ||

amantrakaṃ vā mantreṇa sahitaṃ bhāvavarjitam |
yo vā kovā naro nityaṃ dhārayellajjayā vinā || 3-24 ||

sarvapāpavinirmuktaḥ samyak jñānamavāpnuyāt |
aho rudrākṣamāhātmyaṃ mayā vaktuṃ na śakyate || 3-25 ||

tasmāttvaṃ sarvayatnena kuru rudrākṣadhāraṇam |
tasmātsarvaissadā kāryaṃ rudrākṣāṇāñca dhāraṇam || 3-26 ||

bhogamokṣapradaṃ puṃsāṃ mayā caitaddhṛtaṃ sadā |
rudrākṣadhāraṇācchreṣṭhaṃ na kiñcidiha vidyate || 3-27 ||

iti śrīśivadharme caturtho dhyāyaḥ ||




p. 20)

śrīḥ

atha pañcamodhyāyaḥ

īśvaraḥ | tripurahara śivādidevaśaṃbho
triṇayana śaṅkara rudraśūlapāṇe |
bhavaśaraṇamitīrayanti ye'smin
bhavajaladhau na patanti te manuṣyāḥ || 4-1 ||

śaive | oṃ namo nīlakaṇṭhāya iti puṇyākṣarāṣṭakam 
mantramāhasakṛdvā yamapātakaistu pramucyate || 4-2 ||

navāṃbunidhisanta * * na pātrandṛdīkṛtam |
svargārohaṇaniśreṇī mahādeveti kīrtanam || 4-3 ||

vimocanaṃ hi bandhānāmandhānānnadhanauṣadham |
dīnānāmāśrayasthānaṃ mahādeveti kīrtanam || 4-4 ||

mahādeveti vāgvallī caturakṣarapallavā |
bhaktisiktāphalatyekā caturvargaphalānyaho || 4-5 ||

mahādeva mahādeva mahādevetyayaṃ dhvaniḥ |
sudhiyo muktikāntāyāḥ pāṇigrahaṇaḍiṇḍimaḥ || 4-6 ||

koṭayo brahmahatyānāmagamyāgamakoṭayaḥ |
sadyaḥ pralayamāyānti mahādevetikārtanāt || 4-7 ||

p. 21)

śiveti maṅgalannāma yasya vāci pravartate |
bhasmībhavanti tasyāśu mahāpātakakoṭayaḥ || 4-8 ||

prāṇaprayāṇapātheyaṃ saṃsāravyādhibheṣajam |
anantaduḥkhaśamanaṃ śiva ityakṣaradvayam || 4-9 ||

śiva ityeṣa śabdo'sti vāgasti vaśavartinī |
tathāpi narake martyaḥ patantītyatisāhasam || 4-10 ||

śive trivācaṃ yo nityaṃ caṇḍālo'pi vadenmudā |
sahateva vadedvācaṃ saha tena vaset sadā || 4-11 ||

prasaṃṅgāt kautukāllobhāt bhayādajñānato'pi vā |
hara ityuccaranmartyaḥ sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate || 4-2 ||

āhara praharasaṃhareti ca vyāharan sakṛdaghaiḥ pramucyate |
mocayet prakṛtikāmakarmato nāmakīrtanamumāpatessadā || 4-13 ||

ādau namaḥ prayoktavyaṃ śivāyetyakṣaratrayam |
jihvāyāṃ vartate yasya saphalantasya jīvitam || 4-14 ||

kintasya bahubhirmantraiḥ śastrairvā bahuvistaraiḥ |
yasyonnamaśśivāyeti mantroyaṃ hṛdi vartate || 4-15 ||

tenādhītaṃ śrutaṃ sarvaṃ tena sarvamanuṣṭhitam |
yasyonnamaśśivāyeti mantrābhyāsasthirīkṛtaḥ || 4-16 ||

yaḥ prātaḥ devadeveśaṃ śivaliṅgasvarūpiṇam |
paśyet sayāti sarvasmādadhikāṃ gatimuttamām || 4-17 ||

p. 22) madhyāhne ca mahādevaṃ dṛṣṭvā yajñaphalaṃ labhet |
sāyāhne sarvayajñānāṃ phalamāpya vimucyate || 4-18 ||

na pradoṣe hariṃ paśyet trayodaśyāṃ viśeṣataḥ |
yadi pramādāt paśyedvā dvādaśābdavṛṣakṣayaḥ || 4-19 ||

trayodaśyāntathā rātrau sopahāraṃ trilocanam |
dṛṣṭvāśvamedhamāpnoti mucyate sarvapātakaiḥ || 4-20 ||

gaṅgāpuṣkarakedāranaimiśādīnyanekaśaḥ |
tīrthasnānena yatpuṇyaṃ śivandṛṣṭvaiva tallabhet || 4-21 ||

apavitraḥ pavitro vā sarvāvasthāṃ gato'pi vā |
yassmaret sadā rudraṃ śuddha eva na saṃśayaḥ || 4-22 ||

ye vāñchanti mahābhogān rājyañca tridaśālayam |
arcayantu sadā bhaktyā harasya caraṇāṃbujam || 4-23 ||

brahmaṇassṛṣṭikartṛtvaṃ viṣṇordānavamardanam |
svargādhipatyamindrasya śivapūjāvidheḥ phalam || 4-24 ||

saubhāgyaṃ kāntimadrūpaṃ satyantyāgaḥ pravaktṛtā |
śoryañca jagati khyātiḥ śivapūjāvidheḥ phalam || 4-25 ||

yaddvāre mattamātaṅgāḥ vāyuvegāsturaṃgamāḥ |
pūrṇenduvadanānāryaḥ śiva * * * * phalam || 4-26 ||

bhavanāni manojñāni vidyā jñānaṃ śive ratiḥ |
dhanañcādṛṣṭaparyantaṃ śiva * * * * phalam || 4-27 ||

p. 23) 

āyurārogyamaiśvaryaṃ satputrāḥ sukhasaṃpadaḥ |
svargāpavargau dharmaśca śiva * * * * phalam || 4-28 ||

liṅgārcanarato nityaṃ mahāpātakasaṃbhavaiḥ |
doṣaiḥ kṛtairnalipyeta padmapatramivāṃbhasā || 4-29 ||

merumandaratulyāni pāpāni subahūnyapi |
īśvaraṃ vaidyamāsādya naśyante duṣṭarogavat || 4-30 ||

kāyāssanti hi sāpāyāḥ saṃpadaḥ padamāpadām |
samāgamāssāpagamāḥ sarvamutpātabhaṅguram || 4-31 ||

punarjanma punarmṛtyuḥ punaḥ kleśaparaṃparā |
agharaṭṭaghaṭanyāyo na kadācidanīdṛśam || 4-32 ||

merupṛṣṭhe prayāge ca gaṅgāsāgaranaimiśe |
himavante tapastaptaṃ tatphalaṃ liṅgapūjane || 4-33 ||

kimatra grandhamālābhiḥ saṃkṣepādupadiśyate |
vimuktasarvavyāpārāḥ janāḥ pūjayateśvaram || 4-34 ||

kiṃ vidyairakhilaiśśāstraiḥ dāna yajñavratādibhiḥ |
śivassaṃpūjyatānnityamupadeśoyamuttamam || 4-35 ||

ayameva paro dharmaḥ pūjyate ca sadāśivaḥ |
etāvaddhi śivajñānaṃ etāvaddhiparaṃ padam || 4-36 ||

īśvaraḥ | bhasmaviddhi paraṃbrahmasatyaṃ bodhasukhādvayam |
bhatsanātsarvapāpānāṃ pāśānāñca mahāmune || 4-37 ||

p. 24) 

bhayebhyo rakṣaṇādrakṣā bhāsanādbhasitaṃ tathā |
kṣārayatyakhilaṃ doṣaṃ tataḥ kṣāramudāhṛtam || 4-38 ||

gauṇannānāvidhaṃ viddhi brahman brahmavidāṃvara |
agnihotrāgnijantadvat virajāhomajaṃ mune || 4-39 ||

aupāsanasamutpannaṃ grahasthānāṃ vidhīyate |
samidagnisamutpannaṃ dhāryaṃ vai brahmacāriṇām || 4-40 ||

śūdrāṇāṃ śrotriyāgāre pacanāgnisamudbhavam |
anyeṣāmapi sarveṣāṃ dhāryandāvānalodbhavam || 4-41 ||

pañcākṣareṇa mantreṇa praṇavena yutena ca |
lalāṭe hṛdaye caiva do * ye ca mahāmune || 4-42 ||

tripuṇḍraṃ dhārayennityaṃ grahasthaśca vanāśramī |
anyeṣāmapi sarveṣāṃ vināmantreṇa suvrata || 4-43 ||

uddhūlanaṃ tripuṇḍraṃ ca kartavyam bhaktito mune |

iti śivadharme pañcamodhyāyaḥ ||
