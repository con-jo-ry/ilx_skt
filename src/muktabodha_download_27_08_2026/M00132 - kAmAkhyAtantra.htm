|| oṃ namaḥ paradevatāyai ||

kāmākhyātantre

prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ

śrīdevī uvāca


bhagavan sarvadharmajña  sarvavidyāpriya prabho |

sarvadānandahṛdaya sarvāgamaprakāśaka || 1 ||

śrutāni sarvatantrāṇi yāmalāni ca bhūriśaḥ |

vidyāstāḥ sakalā deva phalāni tvatprasādataḥ || 2 ||

sārāt sārataraṃ tantraṃ jānāsi kiṃ vada prabho |

atīvedaṃ rahaḥsthānaṃ tenāhaṃ śravaṇodyatā || 3 ||


śrīśiva uvāca


śṛṇu devi mudā bhadre madīye prāṇavallabhe |

yonirūpā mahāvidyā kāmākhyā varadāyinī || 4 ||

varadā''nandadā nityā mahāvibhavavarddhinī |

sarveṣāṃ jananī sāpi sarveṣāṃ tāriṇī matā || 5 ||

ramaṇī caiva sarveṣāṃ sthūlā sūkṣmā sadā śubhā |

tasyāstantraṃ pravakṣyāmi sāvadhānā'vadhāraya || 6 ||

nikhilāsu ca vidyāsu ye ye sidhyanti sādhakāḥ |

yatra kutrāpi kenāpi kāmākhyā phaladāyinī || 7 ||

kāmākhyāvimukhā lokā ninditā bhuvanatraye |

vinā kāmātmikāṃ kāpi na dātrī siddhisampadām || 8 ||

kāmākhyā ca sadā dharmaḥ kāmākhyā cārtha eva ca |

kāmākhyā kāmasampattiḥ kāmākhyā mokṣa eva ca || 9 ||

nirvāṇaṃ saiva deveśi saiva sāyujyamīritā |

sālokyaṃ saharūpaṃ ca kāmākhyā paramā gatiḥ || 10 ||

śivatā brahmatā devi viṣṇutā candratāpi ca |

devatvaṃ sarvadevānāṃ niścitaṃ kāmarūpiṇī || 11 ||

sarvāsāmapi vidyānāṃ laukikaṃ vākyameva ca |

kāmākhyāyā mahādevyāḥ svarūpaṃ sarvameva hi || 12 ||

paśya paśya priye sarvaṃ cintayitvā hṛdi svayam |

kāmākhyāṃ na vinā kiñcid vidyate bhuvanatraye || 13 ||

lakṣakoṭimahāvidyāstantrādau parikīrtitāḥ |

sārāt sāratamā devi sarveṣāṃ ṣoḍaśī matā || 14 ||

tasyāśca kāraṇaṃ devi kāmākhyā jagadambikā |

candrakāntiryathā devi jāyate līyate punaḥ || 15 ||

sthāvarāṇi carāṇīha nityā'nityāni yāni ca |

satyaṃ satyaṃ punaḥ satyaṃ vinā tāṃ naiva jāyate || 16 ||

|| iti śrīkāmākhyātantre devīśvarasaṃvāde prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ || 1 ||



atha dvitīyaḥ paṭalaḥ


śrīśiva uvāca


mantroddhāraṃ pravakṣyāmi śṛṇu devi parāt param |

yajjñātvā sādhayet siddhiṃ devānāmapi durlabhām || 1 ||

mantrasyāsya pratāpena mohayedakhilaṃ jagat |

brahmādīn mohayed devi bālakaṃ jananīṃ yathā || 2 ||

devadānavagandharvakinnarādīn sureśvari |

mohayet kṣaṇmātreṇa prajāsu nṛpatiryathā || 3 ||

mantrasya purato devi rājānaṃ sacivādayaḥ |

anye ca mānavāḥ sarve meṣādijantavo yathā || 4 ||

mohayennagaraṃ rājñaḥ sahastyaśvarathādikam |

ūrvaśyādyāstusvarveśyā rājapatnyādikāḥ kṣaṇāt || 5 ||

stambhanaṃ māraṇaṃ devi kṣobhaṇaṃ jṛmbhaṇaṃ tathā |

drāvaṇaṃ bhīṣaṇaṃ caiva vidveṣoccāṭane tathā || 6 ||

ākarṣaṇaṃ ca nārīṇāṃ viśeṣeṇa maheśvari |

vaśīkaraṇamanyāni sādhayet sādhakottamaḥ || 7 ||

agniḥ stambhati vāyuśca sūryo vārisamūhakaḥ |

kaṭākṣeṇaiva sarvāṇi sādhakasya na cānyathā || 8 ||

jagajjayati mantrajñaḥ kāmadevo yathā jayī |

tasyā'sādhyaṃ tribhuvane na kiñcit prāṇavallabhe || 9 ||

jṛmbhaṇāntaṃ tyaktapāśaṃ yātrāvāraṇarohakam |

vyaṅgavarṇayutaṃ devi nādabinduyutaṃ punaḥ || 10 ||

etat tu triguṇīkṛtya kalpavṛkṣamanuṃ japet |

ekaṃ vāpi dvayaṃ vāpi caturthaṃ vā japet sudhīḥ || 11 ||

kāmākhyāsādhanaṃ kāryaṃ sarvavidyāsu sādhakaiḥ |

anyathā siddhihāniḥ syād vighnasteṣāṃ pade pade || 12 ||

kiṃ śāktā vaiṣṇavāḥ kiṃ vā kiṃ śaivā gāṇapatyakāḥ |

mahāmāyāvratāḥ sarve tailayantre vṛṣā iva || 13 ||

asyāśca sādhanaṃ devi śāktānāmeva sundari |

nātra cakraviśuddhistu kālādiśodhanaṃ na ca || 14 ||

kṛte ca narakaṃ yāti sarvaṃ tasya vinaśyati |

kleśaśūnyaṃ paraṃ devi sādhanaṃ drutapoṣakam || 15 ||


śrīdevī uvāca


kālītārāmantradāne cakracintāṃ karoti yaḥ |

kā gatistasya deveśaḥ nistāro vidyate na vā || 16 ||


śrīśiva uvāca


vayamagniśca kālo'pi ye ye yātā maheśvari |

kiṃ brūmo devadeveśi kālikātāriṇīmanum || 17 ||

ajñānād yadi vā mohāt kālītārāmanau priye |

kṛte cakrādigaṇane śūnīviṣṭhākṛmirbhavet || 18 ||

kalpānte ca mahādevi nistāro vidyate na ca |

asyāḥ pūjāṃ pravakṣyāmi trailokyasādhanapradām || 19 ||

haṭhāddhaṭhācca deveśi yā yā siddhiśca jāyate |

prāpyate tatkṣaṇenaiva nātra kāryā vicāraṇā || 20 ||

sindūramaṇḍalaṃ kṛtvā trikoṇaṃ ca samālikhet |

nijabījāni tanmadhye yojayet sādhakottamaḥ || 21 ||

caturasraṃ likhed devi tato vajrāṣṭakaṃ priye |

aṣṭadikṣu yajet tāṃ tu nyāsajālaṃ vidhāya ca || 22 ||

akṣobhyaśca ṛṣiḥ proktaśchando'nuṣṭubudāhṛtam |

kāmākhyā devatā sarvasiddhaye viniyojitā || 23 ||

karāṅganyāsakaṃ caiva nijabījena kārayet |

ṣaḍdīrghabhājāṃ dhyāyet tu kāmākhyāmiṣṭadāyinīm || 24 ||

raktavastrāṃ varodyuktāṃ sindūratilakānvitām |

niṣkalaṅkāṃ sudhādhāmavadanāmalakojjvalām || 25 ||

svarṇādimaṇimaṇikyabhūṣaṇairbhūṣitāṃ parām |

nānāratnādinirmāṇasiṃhāsanoparisthitām || 26 ||

hāsyavaktrāṃ padmarāgamaṇikāntimanuttamām |

pīnottuṅgakucāṃ kṛṣṇāṃ śrutimūlagatekṣaṇām || 27 ||

kaṭākṣaiśca mahāsampaddāyinīṃ parameśvarīm |

sarvāṅgāninditāṃ nānāvidyābhiḥ pariveṣṭitām || 28 ||

ḍākinīyoginīvidyādharībhiḥ pariśobhitām |

kāminībhiryutāṃ nānāgandhādyaiḥ parigandhitām || 29 ||

tāmbūlādikarābhiśca nāyikābhirvirājitām |

samastasiddhavargāṇāṃ praṇatānāṃ pratīkṣaṇām || 30 ||

trinetrāṃ mohanakarīṃ puṣpacāpeṣu bibhratīm |

bhagaliṅgasamākhyānāṃ kinnarībhyo'pi śṛṇvatīm || 31 ||

vāṇī - lakṣmī - sudhā - vākya - prativākya - samutsukām |

aśeṣaguṇasampannāṃ karuṇāsāgarāṃ śivām || 32 ||

āvāhayet tato devīmevaṃ dhyātvā ca sādhakaḥ |

pūjayitvā yathoktena vidhānena harapriye || 33 ||

kuṅkumādyai raktapuṣpaiḥ sugandhikusumaistathā |

javā - yāvaka - sindūraiḥ karavīrairviśeṣataḥ || 34 ||

karavīreṣu deveśi kāmākhyā tiṣṭhati svayam |

javāyāṃ ca tathā viddhi mālatyādisamīpake || 35 ||

karavīrasya māhātmyaṃ kathituṃ naiva śakyate |

pradānāt tu javāyāśca gāṇapatyamavāpnuyāt || 36 ||

pūjayedambikāṃ devīṃ pañcatatvena sarvadā |

pañcatattvaṃ vinā pūjāmabhicārāya kalpayet || 37 ||

pañcatattvena devyāstu prasādo jāyate kṣaṇāt |

pañcamena mahādevi śivo bhavati sādhakaḥ || 38 ||

pañcatattvasamaṃ nāsti nāsti (kiñcit) kalau yuge |

pañcatattvaṃ paraṃ brahma pañcatattvaṃ parā gatiḥ || 39 ||

pañcatattvaṃ mahādevi pañcatattvaṃ sadāśivaḥ |

pañcatattvaṃ bhuktimuktī mahāyogaḥ prakīrtitaḥ || 40 ||

pañcatattvena deveśi mahāpātakakoṭayaḥ |

naśyanti tatkṣaṇenaiva tūlarāśimivānalaḥ || 41 ||

yatraiva pañca tattvāni tatra devī vased dhruvam |

pañcatattvavihīne tu vimukhī jagadambikā || 42 ||

madyena modate svarge māṃsena mānavādhipaḥ |

matsyena bhairavīputro mudrayā sādhutāṃ vrajet || 43 ||

pareṇa ca mahādevi sāyujyaṃ labhate naraḥ |

bhaktiyukto yajed devīṃ tadā sarvaṃ prajāyate || 44 ||

svayambhūkusumaiḥ śuklaiḥ kuṇḍagolodbhavaiḥ śubhaiḥ |

kuṅkumādyairāsavena cārghyaṃ devyai nivedayet || 45 ||

nānopahāranaivedyairannādipāyasairapi |

vastrabhūṣādibhirdevīṃ prapūjyāvaraṇaṃ yajet || 46 ||

indrādīn pūjayet tatra teṣāṃ yantrāṇi śāṅkari |

pārśvayoḥ kamalāṃ vāṇīṃ pūjayet sādhakottamaḥ || 47 ||

annadā dhanadā caiva sukhadā jayadā tathā |

rasadā mohadā devi ṛddhidā siddhidāpi ca || 48 ||

vṛddhidā śuddhidā caiva bhuktidā muktidā tathā |

mokṣadā sukhadā caiva jñānadā kāntidā tathā || 49 ||

etāḥ pūjyā mahādevyaḥ sarvadā yantramadhyataḥ |

ḍākinyādyāstahā pūjyāḥ siddhayo yatnataḥ śive || 50 ||

ṣaḍaṅgāni tato devyā yajet puṣpāñjalīn kṣipet |

yathāśakti japenmantraṃ śuddhabhāvena sādhakaḥ || 51 ||

japaṃ samarpya vādyādyaistoṣayet paradevatām |

paṭhet stotraṃ ca kavacaṃ praṇamed vidhinā'munā || 52 ||

tataśca hṛdaye devīṃ saṃyujya śeṣikāṃ prati |

nirmālyaṃ nikṣipedaiśe trikoṇaṃ parikalpayet || 53 ||

nirmālyaṃ sādhakaiḥ sārdhaṃ gṛhṇīyād bhaktibhāvataḥ |

viharet pañcatattvena yathāvidhi svaśaktitaḥ || 54 ||

śaktimūlaṃ sādhanaṃ ca śaktimūlaṃ japādikam |

śaktimūlā japāścaiva śaktimūlaṃ ca jīvanam || 55 ||

aihikaṃ śaktimūlaṃ ca paratra śaktimūlakam |

śaktimūlaṃ tapaḥ sarvaṃ caturvargastathā priye || 56 ||

śaktimūlāni sarvāṇi sthāvarāṇi carāṇi ca |

ajñātvā pāpino devi rauravaṃ yānti niścitam || 57 ||

tasmāt sarvaprayatnena sādhakaiśca śivājñayā |

śaktyucchiṣṭaṃ pīyate hi nānyathā yānti rauravam || 58 ||

śaktyai yad dīyate devi tat tu devārpitam bhavet |

saphalaṃ karma tat sarvaṃ satyaṃ satyaṃ kuleśvari || 59 ||

śaktiṃ vinā kaulacakraṃ karoti kārayedapi |

sa yāti narakaṃ ghoraṃ nistāro na hi vidyate || 60 ||

evaṃ tu pūjayed devīṃ yaṃ yaṃ coddiśya sādhakaḥ |

taṃ taṃ kāmaṃ kare kṛtvā priye jayati niścitam || 61 ||

puraścaraṇakāle tu lakṣamekaṃ japet sudhīḥ |

taddaśāṃśaṃ hunedājyaiḥ śarkarāmadhupāyasaiḥ || 62 ||

tarpayeddhudigbhāgairjalaiścandanamiśritaiḥ |

gandhādyaiḥ sādhakādhīśastatkuśairabhiṣecayet || 63 ||

abhiṣekadaśāṃśena bhojayecca dvijottamān |

miṣṭānnaiḥ sādhakān devi devībhaktāṃśca pañcamaiḥ || 64 ||

evaṃ puraskriyāṃ kṛtvā siddho bhavati sādhakaḥ |

prayogeṣu tato devi yogyo bhavati niścitam || 65 ||

etat pūjādikaṃ sarvaṃ kāmākhyāprītikārakam |

mahāprītikarī pūjā yonicakre kuleśvari || 66 ||

yonipūjāṃ mahāpujā tatsamā na hi siddhidā |

tatra devīṃ yajed dhimān saiva devī na cā'nyathā || 67 ||

āvāhanādikarmāṇi na tatra sarvathā priye |

lepayet tāṃ tu gandhādyaiḥ pūjayed vividhena ca || 68 ||

mahāprītirbhaved devyāḥ siddho bhavati tatkṣaṇāt |

yonipūjāsamā pūjā nāsti jñāne tu māmake || 69 ||

cimbanāllehanād yonau kalpavṛkṣamatikramet |

darśanāt sādhakādhīśaḥ sparśanāt sarvamohanaḥ || 70 ||

liṅgayonisamākhyānaṃ kāmākhyāprītivarddhanam |

tadeva jīvanaṃ tasyā girije bahu kiṃ vacaḥ || 71 ||

tasmāt sarvaprayatnena yonipūjāṃ samācaret |

parastrīyonimāsādya viśeṣeṇa yajet sudhīḥ || 72 ||

veśyāyoniḥ parā devi sādhanaṃ tatra kārayet |

trirātrādeva sidhyanti nātra kāryā vicāraṇā || 73 ||

ṛtuyuktaṃ latāmadhye sādhayed vidhivanmudā |

acirāt siddhimāpnoti devānāmapi durlabhām || 74 ||

sārāt sāratamaṃ yonisādhanamīritam |

vinā tat sādhanaṃ devi mahāśāpaḥ prajāyate || 75 ||

yoninindāṃ ghṛṇāṃ devi yaḥ karoti narādhamaḥ |

acirād rauravaṃ yāti devi satyaṃ śivājñayā || 76 ||

yonimadhye vased devi yoginyāścikure sthitāḥ |

tasmānna nindayed yoniṃ yadīcchedātmano hitam || 77 ||

yoninindāṃ prakurvāṇaḥ sa vaṃśena vinaśyati |

yonistutiparo devi śivaḥ syāt tatkṣaṇena ca || 78 ||

kiṃ brūmo yonimāhātmyaṃ yonipūjāphalāni ca |

cāñcalyād gaditaṃ kiñcit kṣantavyaṃ kāmarūpiṇi || 79 ||

taṃ dṛṣṭvā sādhakendraṃ ca vādī skhalati darśanāt |

ato hi dakṣiṇākālī kathyate varavarṇini || 9 ||

sarvadevāśayo meruryathā siddhaṃ tathā punaḥ || 14 ||

sarvavidyāyutaṃ tantraṃ śapathena vadāmyaham |

yasya gehe sthitaṃ devi tantrametad bhayāpaham || 15 ||

rogaśokapātakānāṃ leśo nāsti kadācana |
