p. 57)

śrīmatpadmarasārādhyapadābhyāṃ namaḥ |

siddhāntārthasamuccayaḥ |

yenānādi malādi pāśa nivarddhvaṃsāyabaddhātmanāṃ
sarvajñena sadoditena kṛpayā jñānaṃ samāviṣkṛtam |
natvā taṃ paramaṃ śivaṃ paragurun sarvān guruṃścāparān 
siddhāntārthasamuccayo gurumukhātprāptaḥ sphuṭaṃ likhyate || 

pramāṇaścanā tatra kriyate bahudhā yataḥ |
sarveṣu śivaśāstreṣu vyākhyāneṣu ca dṛśyate || 

tasmādāgamayuktibhyāṃ yathā yo'rtho guruttamaiḥ |
nirṇītaḥ sa tathāsmābhiḥ saṃkṣepeṇa pradaśyate || 

tatra tāvat pārameśvaraṃ jñānaṃ dvirūpamavabodharūpaṃ śabdarūpaṃ ca | 
tatrāvabodharūpamamalamanādinidhanaṃ sarvārthaprakāśakaṃ sarvānugrāhakaṃ 
śivajñānātmakaṃ ca | śabdarūpaṃ tu śāstrātmakameva | avabodharūpaṃ punaḥ 
śabdarūpārūḍhaṃ paramakāruṇikaḥ parameśvaraḥ pratisargaṃ sṛṣṭikāle 
sarvānujighṛkṣayā guruparamparayā yogyeṣu pravartayati | yaduktaṃ 
śrimatsvāyambhuve -

athātmamalamāyākhya karmabandhavimuktaye |
vyaktaye ca śivatvasya śivājjñānaṃ pravartate ||

mokṣakārikāsu ca -

anādikālasaṃsiddhaṃ tadguṇatve'pi śaṃkare |

p. 58)

tacca samyak paricchedi śāṃkaraṃ jñānamīritam iti ||

guruparamparākramaśca dvividhaḥ mahaughakramalakṣaṇaḥ 
pratisaṃhitagurukramalakṣaṇaśca | tatra mahaughakramalakṣaṇaḥ pradarśyate | tatra 
parameśvaraḥ prathamaṃ vidyeśāṃnanugṛhya tebhyo'ṣṭāviṃśati saṃhitāḥ 
prakāśayati | te ca punaḥ śrīkaṇṭhādi krameṇa munimanuṣyāntaṃ sarva saṃhitā 
avatārayanti | soyaṃ mahaughalakṣaṇaḥ śāstrāvatāraḥ | taduktaṃ 
śrīmanmālinīvijaye - 

sisṛkṣurjagataḥ sṛṣṭerādāveva nijecchayā |
vijñānakevalānaṣṭau bodhayā māsa pudgalāniti ||

eṣa ca gurukramaḥ śrīmadrauravādau śrūyate -

jagataḥ kāraṇaṃ devamanaleśaṃ paraṃ gurum |
tenoktaṃ parameśena śrīkaṇṭhāya mahātmane || iti |

śivodgīṇamidaṃ jñānaṃ mantra mantreśvareśvaraiḥ |
kāmadatvātkāmiketi pragītaṃ bahuvistaram ||

tebhyo'vagatya dṛgjyotirjvālālīrḍhasmaradrumaḥ |

dadāvumāpatirmahyaṃ sahasrairbhava saṃmitairiti | pratisaṃhita gurukramaḥ 
pradarśyate | yatuktaṃ śrīmatkiraṇe -

sṛṣṭyanantarameveśaḥ śivān sṛṣṭvā daśātmajān |
jñānamekaṃ vibhajyāśu teṣāṃ tatsaṃkhyayāvadat ||

kāmikaṃ praṇavākhyasya sudhākhyasya ca yogajam |

p. 59) 

dīptākhyasyāpyacintyaṃ tu kāraṇākhyasya kāraṇam |
ajitaṃ suśivākhyasya īśasyāpi sudīptakam ||

sūkṣmaṃ tu sūkṣmasaṃjñasya kālasyāpi sahasrakam |
suprabhadaṃ gaṇeśasya aṃśusaṃjñasya cāṃśumat ||

evaṃ prāthamikaṃ proktaṃ punaranyo gurukramaḥ |
praṇavātrikalaḥ prāptaḥ kāmikaṃ trikalāddharaḥ ||

sudhākhyādyogajaṃ tantraṃ bhasmasaṃjñastataḥ prabhuḥ |
dīptasaṃjñādacintyaṃ tu gopatiśca tato'mbikā ||

kāraṇaṃ kāraṇāccharvastataḥ prāptaḥ prajāpatiḥ |
ajitaṃ suśivātprāpto hyumeśo hyacyutastataḥ || 

īśād dīptaṃ trimūrtistu tataḥ prāpto hutāśanaḥ |
sūkṣmaṃ sūkṣmādbhavaḥ prāptastasmātprāptaḥ prabhañjanaḥ ||

kāla saṃjñāt sahasrākhyaṃ bhīmo dharmastataḥ khaga |
gaṇeśāt suprabhedaṃ tu vigraheśastataḥ śaśī ||

aṃśumaccāṃśu saṃjñāt tu prāptohyugrastato raviḥ |
evamete samākhyātāḥ śivabhedādaśādyate ||

daśāṣṭasaṃkhyayāpyanyo rudrabhedo'paro mataḥ |
daśāṣṭasaṃkhyayā rudrān sṛṣṭvā pūrvamukhādyataḥ ||

śivastat saṃkhyayaive ca punastān bodhayet khaga |

rudrasyānādi saṃjñasya vijayaṃ tena bhāṣitamityādinā

phalabhedo na kalpyo'tra jñānabhedaḥ prakalpyate ityantena | 

atrāyamarthaḥ - tantrāṇāṃ śivabhedarudrabhedatva bhedena tāvad

śivajñānasyāvatārabhedāt kriyābhedācca bhedo na tvarthavyavasthā bhedādvā 
phalabhedādvā | taduktaṃ śrīmatsvāyambhuve - 

ekameva śivajñānaṃ vibhinnaṃ daśadhā punaḥ |
tathāṣṭādaśadhā bhūyo bhedāntaravisarpitam iti ||

ekameva śivajñānaṃ vibhinnaṃ bahudhā punaḥ |
upabhedopabhedānāmasya saṃkhyā na vidyate || 

bhedāntarāṇi sarvāṇi mahānti na mahāntyapi |
dīkṣā saṃpratyayārthāni siddhimukti phalāni ceti ||

p. 61)

anyepyāhu - sarvaśākhāpratyayamekaṃ karmeti | eṣāṃ ca tantra bhedānāṃ 
gurupūrvaka saṃbandhaḥ kvacit pañcaprakāraḥ kvacit ṣaṭprakāraḥ | 
śrīmataṅgoktavatpañcaprakāraḥ | tathā hi - śivānantayoḥ paraḥ 
sambandhaḥ | ananta śrīkaṇṭhayoḥ mahān | śrīkaṇṭhamataṅgayordivyo 
mataṅgamuninā manuṣyāṇāṃ divyādivyo manuṣyaiśca 
manuṣyāṇāmadivyātmakaḥ | śrīmanmṛgendre tu ṣaṭprakāraḥ yathā - 
śivānantayoḥ paraḥ sambandhaḥ anantaśrīkaṇṭhayoḥ mahān 
śrīkaṇṭhaśakrayorantarādhaḥ śakrabharadvājayoddhivyo bharadvāja 
hārītayordivyādivyo hārītena tacchiṣyāṇāmadivya ityeva manyeṣvapi yathāyogaṃ 
draṣṭavyam | evamāparameśvarādavicchinna guru saṃpradāyasmaraṇāt 
tatkṛtatvenasya śāstrasya prāmāṇyaṃ siddham | taduktaṃ śrīmanmataṅge - 

pramāṇamekaṃ tadvākyaṃ tattvamīśvara bhāṣitam iti |

nanu nityatvenāgamasya prāmāṇyamiti kecit | tadayuktam asya kṛtaka āgamaḥ 
saṃbhavati racanāvatvādabhinava viracitavākyavat | racanā ca setvādivat | tasyā 
kartāraṃ na vyabhicarati | tasmādāpta praṇītatvenaivāgamasya prāmāṇyam | 
parameśvaraśca paramāptaḥ | nityanirmalaniratiśaya sarvārtha jñānakriyā 
yuktatvādrāgadveṣā bhāvena vipralambhāsaṃbhavāt sarvānugrāhakatvācca | 
taduktaṃ śrīmanmohaśūrottare - yadyathāvasthitaṃ vastu sarvajñastat tathā 
vadediti | īśvaraśca yogi pratyakṣādanumānācca siddhaḥ |

p. 62)

tathā hi śrīmanmataṅge - śaktisiddhipūrvamīśvarasiddhimuktvā yogi 
pratyakṣagamyatvamuktam |

śaktaśca śaktimān siddhopyathavā munipuṅgava |
akṣāṇi pratipratyakṣaṃ manaścākṣamanuttamam || 

saṃkalpaviṣayaṃ siddhaṃ saṃkalpādeva kevalāt |
prapattavyaṃ munivyāghra kimanyat paripṛcchasi ||

iti na ca yogino'siddhā iti vācyam | yacchruyate -

ātmānamaraṇiṃ kṛtvā praṇavaṃ cottarāraṇim |
jñānanirmathanābhyāsād devaṃ paśyen nigūḍhavaditi ||

anumānasiddhatvamapyuktaṃ śrīmanmṛgendrādau -

athopalabhya dehādi vastu kāryatvadharmakam |
kartāramasya jānīmo viśiṣṭamanumānataḥ || 

vaiśiṣṭyaṃ kāryavaiśiṣṭyād dṛṣṭaṃ lokasthitāvapīti | 
āgamasīddhatvamapyuktaṃ śrīmanmataṅge -

pañcamantratanuḥ śrīmān sakalaḥ paripaṭhyate |
suprasannendriyagrāmaḥ sarvajñaḥ sarvagocaraḥ ||

yogayogeśvaraḥ svāmī svatantraḥ paśupāśahā |
tasyoktiḥ pauruṣaiḥ sthūlaiḥ pramāṇairna ca bādhyate || iti |

eṣāceśvarasiddhiḥ sarvāsu saṃhitāsu nyāsādiṣu ca vistareṇa sādhitatvānnātra 
likhyate | na ceśvara kṛtādāgamādīśvarasiddhā - 

p. 63)

vitaretarāśrayadoṣaḥ āśaṅkanīyaḥ dvayorapi visadṛśakriyatvāt | 
īśvarohyāgamasya kartā āgamastu taṃ jñāpayatīti | kiṃ ca tadukteṣu ca 
dṛṣṭārtheṣu phalavattvamupalabhyādṛṣṭārthasyāpi tacchāstrasyāvitathatvaṃ 
manumīyate | taduktaṃ śrīmanmataṅgepi - 

viśeṣasyānumānasya trividhasyāpi niścayāt |
āgamādāptavaktrotthānnānyathā munipuṅgava ||

āptaścaivāgamāt siddhaḥ sacedānīṃ vicāryate |
āyurvedāt sagaṇitān mantra vādācca sasphuṭāt ||

rasopaniṣadāṭopādāptaḥ saṃparigṛhyate iti |

nanu devataiva tāvanna siddhā | tatsaṃbhave  hi tadviśeṣasyeśvarasya siddhiḥ | sā tu 
śabdamātraiva | tathāhi - karmānuṣṭhānādeva phalaṃ na devatāyāḥ 
tadbādhakatvenābhimatarudrendrādiśabdabhedena pramāṇāntarairasiddheḥ | ata eva 
tena saha saṃbandhagrahaṇā saṃbhavādāgamenāpya siddhiḥ | tata eva ca 
saṃbandhagrahaṇeti taretarāśrayato'siddireva | tataśca raudraṃ caruṃ 
nivarpetsvargakāma ityādābuddeśyakārakatayā viśiṣṭakarmāṅgatvena sa eva 
devatāśabdaḥ pratīyata iti kriyāta eva phalaṃ yuktaṃ na devatāta iti jaiminīyāḥ |

atrocyate | evaṃ hi devatāyāḥ śabdamātratve phalamapi 

p. 64)

śabdamātrameva syādyato jyotiṣṭomena svargakāmo yajetetyādau 
svargādyātmakaṃ phalamapi tadvadeva pramāṇāntarasiddham | atha yadi śabdādi 
yogaṃ vināpi nārtharahita paraḥ śabdaḥ saṃbhavatīti purāṇetihāsāṃdiprasiddhaṃ 
viśiṣṭamevātra svargaśabdaḥ phalatvenābhidadhātityucyate tadihāpi samānamiti 
na śabda eva devatāpi tu tadvācyo'rthaviśeṣa eva svavācakādbhinnā devadatrādi 
śabdavācyātmavaditi saiva phaladāna tu karmamātraṃ tasya kṛṣyādi karmavat 
phalajananamātra eva sāmarthyādacetanatvenedaṃ phalamasmai dāsyāmīti 
vivekābhāvāt | śabdo hi viditapadapadārtha pratyāyakatvena bhavatopi siddha iti 
indrādi śabdānāmapi śabdatvenaivārtha pratyāyakatva siddhestatpratyeyo devatā 
lakṣaṇo'rthaḥ svargalakṣaṇa phalavat siddha evamanumānādi 
siddhatvamapīśvaralakṣaṇa devatāyāḥ prāgevoktamiti na pramāṇāntarāsiddhaṃ 
nāpītestarāśraya doṣaḥ | yadāhuḥ - śrutisiddhyarthaṃ śrutopalabdho 
yatnavatā bhavitavyam na śrutisiddhau yatna viśeṣaśaithilyamācaraṇīyamiti | 
tataśca somapāḥśatakraturityādi śrutisiddhasya devatā viśeṣasya 
karmaṇonirūpasya ca vajrahastaṃ sahasrākṣamindramīḍe caturbhujamityādi 
śrutisiddhasya śabdamātrasyāsaṃbhavāt tat tad viśeṣeṇa viśiṣṭā devatā 
siddhaiva | śrutirapi tāviśeṣayā devamaitraḥ
