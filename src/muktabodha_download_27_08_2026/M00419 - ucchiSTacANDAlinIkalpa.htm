ucchiṣṭacāṇḍālinīkalpa


oṃ ganesāya namaḥ ||
ugrarudrayāmūle rudrapārvati saṃvāde śrīsāradātilake 
ucciddhiṣṭacāṇḍālinī kalpaviṃsāṃ talīṣyane ||
athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi devi guhyatamaṃ priye |
ucchiṣṭapurvadevi mātaṃgi sarvakāmadā || 1 ||
sarva pāpaprasamani sarvakaṃ pavitraviṃtaḥ  yayā vijñānaye davyo ||
kṛyaṃ tacchaṃti cā padaṃ || 2 ||
sthāvalanaṃ gaganaṃvajratriyaṃ divyadaṃviṣaṃ ||
vipretivajaye devi aparāgasamanvitaḥ || 3 ||
savidyā paramādevi sarvapāpa praṇāsani |
svaryadāmoṅkayā devi rājyasaukhya phalapradā || 4 ||
ayaṃ devi mayā cātvā sādhitā viṣavarlitā ||
sakṛ uccaritāvidyā vrīhmahatyā visācanī ||
dhyāstavaṃ pāpaharā devī lokasaubhāgya dāyīnī ||
yadyuriṣṭastu kāṁ vāskare devī kadācana || 5 ||

1a) dyuriṣṭasukho tvāsmare devī kadācana || 6 ||
tadā cātemadevī vinevārumane kṛte |
dunnagāsagābhutvā duḥkhaītāstraṣamāghnini || 7 ||
vaṃdhyācchu labhatepunāṃ satādāyusajīvati |
nirdhanodhanamāprītimukṣevidyāmavāpnoyāt || 8 ||
vidyāsasyapravakṣyāmi prātudevi manoramāṃ |
yayā prārthaya devī vinaivādhamanotkṛte || 9 ||
valiṃ dadyānprathamato muttamaṃtreṇasākaḥ |
tato maṃtrajapaṃ dhyātvā vaṃhyavaciṣṭha sādhayet || 10 ||

1b) maṃtra śrīmāṃsamahādevi japāvanu kathayāmi te |
ādau uciṣṭasavdaṃ tu tathā cāṃḍālinīlica || 11 ||
sumukhī ca tato devi kirtayaṃ tadanaṃtaraṃ |
mahāpisācinīta smaret layāvitaṃ tataḥ paraṃ || 12 ||
nādavindusamājuktaṃ ṭhakāraṃ tritayaṃti ca |
savirsargamahādevi sarvasiddhipradāyinī || 13 ||
navavarṣanavamāsaṃ ca napāhkṛnavāsaṃ caṃ |
na tithī na dunnaṃ navarāvidhireva ca || 14 ||
na kalanaṃ navayāgaṃ ca nāgaṃnyāsamānnikaṃ |
hetu kāmasyasatrunāṃ krodhaṃrādyākṣatrakāranaṃ || 15 ||
haridraṃ naiva duḥkāni nāstisākaṃ bhavodaha |
yasya tiṣṭanimaṃtreya na ca vighnāravāpaye || 16 ||
dhyānaṃ tasya pravaṣyāmi jathāvratajagadisvari |
bhojanasyātade devi smare dhyāna manurtamaṃ || 17 ||

2a) saṃkhopari samāsināṃ raktāṃvaraparicchadaṃ |
raktāṃ laṃkāra saṃjundhyā guṃjahāravirājitāṃ || 18 ||
ṣoṣasasavdāṃ  catuvati | pīnonanapayodharāṃ |
kapālakṛtakāhstā mudrāsanimārjanikarāṃ || 19 ||
siṃhāsana mābhināṃ ca trikuṭagiri mamvarāṃ |
dvrabhujaṃ ca caturvāhuṃ trinetrāratkalocanāṃ || 20 ||
pāsabhāṃ kusasaraṃ cāpaṃ palamaṃ jyoti rupaṇi |
raktālaṃkāla saṃjuktāṃ raktacandanacarcitāṃ || 21 ||
vāmadakṣiṇavogena dhyāyenmantramaṇḍatamaṃ |
ucchiṣṭanavaliṃ dadyātjaparatra tamānasaḥ priye || 22 ||

2b) bhojanānāṃ tade devi vinaivā ca manekṛte |
ḍāparaṃ ca pravakṣyāmi śṛṇudevi phalapradā || 23 ||
japāṃsaṇarpaṇaṃ kārja paścāt karmasamālabhet |
caṃḍilamaṃḍalaṃ kṛtvātura saṃsamāhitaḥ || 24 ||
tanamadhye jatramālikhya trikāṃ caivāṣṭaṣodasa |
pujenamaṇḍaladevi mulamantreṇa sādhakaḥ || 25 ||
tato devī samādhyātvā vahnirūpā vyavasthita ||
vidyākāmaṃ ca rasomaṃ dadhisiddhārthataṃdulai || 26 ||
siddhārthatilamiśraistraivvimenmadhunāsaha ||
kanavarasamaṃ homaṃ vilvaḍalasamaṃvita || 27 ||
sahasraika vidhānena rājāveti vasyaśī ||

3a) sadyamāṃ jālamāṃsena svadevi homakārayet || 28 ||
prāpnoti saphalaṃ vidyāṃ sannusastra ca svīkṛtaḥ |
kurjyā ca chāvamāṃsenaddhṛte ca madhunāsaha || 29 ||
sahasrai vidhānena siddhiriṣṭā bhaveddhruvaṃ |
vidyākāmaṃ carehomaṃ satkṛtāvvṛpayesaiḥ || 30 ||
namanavadhedevī sadyavidyāṃ caturdasa |
rajasvarāsa vastreṇa madhu pābhayesaina ca || 31 ||
homakuryānmahādevī trailokyaṃ vasamānayet |
nāgavalyāṃ dalahome madhunāsarpiṣi pāyasaiḥ || 32 ||
ayutaikavidhānena bhavaṃtidhanasiddhaya || 33 ||
sasakasyasānumāsyana madhunārālitena ca |
krītvā homamavāpnoti vasyāṃ vāpisurastriyaḥ || 34 ||
dhaturakāṣṭayogenacitāṃ vahitīsamaṃtravita ||
kaukilākākapaṃ chibhyāṃ homaṃ sastu vināsanaṃ || 34 ||

3b) urcāṭanāyasatrunāṃ homaṃ kujyāniraṃtalaṃ |
puvotkina vidhānena citākāṣṭahunāsanaḥ || 36 ||
ulukakākapaṃchināṃ kṛtrāhomaṃ samaṃtravit |
vidveṣejathārātru anyoanyakalahākulaḥ || 37 ||
ulukapaṃchi homena garbhapātaṃ tu ristriya |
vilvapatraṃ samānāhajyaṃ māsamekaṃ niraṃtaraṃ |
bhupivandhyā labhyeputraṃ cirakājasamuttamaṃ || 39 ||
vaṃdhukaṃ kusumaṃ kuryādrācchāmadhu samanvitaṃ |
durbhagāsagābhutaṃ saubhāgyaṃ sukhakāmyayā || 40 ||
khatumāsena yogena bhaverdakarṣanaṃ dhruvaṃ |
devakanyānāgakanyā jakṣekanyādhakiṃnarī || 41 ||
pravesavatīkamātā valādaliṃgaviṃdataṃ ||
rekhāṃdeva samācyāye najācālasamanvitaḥ || 42 ||
devidevi mahādevi vāmācāraphalaprade |

4a) dakṣinacārajogena bhavetphalapradāyinī || 43 ||
bhojanānaṃ taredevi valiṃdatvā bhavedine |
uciṣṭe na maṃtrena satamaṣṭā taraṃnṛṇaṃ || 44 ||
alanyevā smasānevā sunyāgāre catuṣpathe |
jarāśraye śiromadhye tu rāmekānenapivā || 45 ||
valiṃ pradānato devī vīpratyakṣaṃ bhavetadhuvaṃ |
yadaṃ vīmayājapuratbhairavākāramichata  || 46 ||
putānikathitādevī sarva pāpapranāśinī |
mantrasyo cāraṇādevī sarvasiddhapradāyakā || 47 ||
oṃ puṁ hrīṁ krīṁ uciṣṭacāḍāli nī sanmukhīdevi mahāpicāsinī hrīṁ ṭhaḥ ṭhaḥ 
ṭhaḥ svāhā iti mantra ||
athajaṃtradvāra || 0 ||

4b) jaṃtramaṃtrapravakṣyāmi sanmukhāphaladaṃ mahat ||
trikuṭaṃ cāṣṭakāṃ caiva ṣoḍasāṣṭa catuścatu || 1 ||
staṃbhaṇemālarecaiva vidveṣa ca vasikṛte |
mohonerājā saṃgrāme dutejucāṭasaṃkaṭe || 2 ||
yaṃtrācālamimaṃ devi trailokyasyāpī durlabhaṃ |
bhumauparilikhyate jaṃtraṃ pūjayesarvakarmaṇī || 3 ||
mākhyārthabhujaṃ patre ca kanyārthikāṃ ca nerapi |
mārane cāyasepatre vasyārthakāṃsya bhajane || 4 ||
ranedutevicāde ca saṃgrāmettāṃ vrabhājane |
saṃgrāme siṣitapārasve māyāsyianāvidhirdiset || 5 ||
iyaṃ vidyāparaṃ gopyā nadeyājasya kasyacit |
kulināyasethāya jñānī ca jita īndriyaḥ || 6 ||

5a) udyānesidhyamahāvidyāṃ sarvasiddhikaraṃ nṛnāṃ |
bhojanānataraṃ devi viliṃjāpyaṃ dinedine || 7 ||
uciṣṭe na jape jñāpyaṃ uciṣṭe ca liṃ dāpayet ||
sadadaṃ kathitaṃ devi sarvasiddhipradāyinī nisī nisī || 8 ||
kāmārāṃ tapayocayo viṣacārakapurihāraṃti ca |
śrīṣaṃḍeti vicānalā janagajāsā liṃgasevaṃti ca || 9 ||
dāsyaṃ tadbhutaṃ satraṃ vākimaparaṃ vajraṃtipuṣpāvitaṃ ||
iti ucciṣṭakalpaḥ sthaṃḍilemaṃḍalaṃ kṛtvā caturahasrāṃ samāhitaḥ ||
pūjayet maṇḍalaṃ devi mulamaṃtreṇamaṃtravit ||
āgachatrayaṃ hijvālāye āsanaṃ namaḥ |
iti āvāhanaṃ || athahoma || dadhisiddhārthatadulai sahasrai 1000 ka vidhānena ||

4b) rājābhavativasyagā cheṃ chāṃ chīṃ chaṃ chaiṃ chauṃ chaḥ 
vegādunsamāṃjārasya māṃsyana devī homasamācaret ||
saṃprāpnoti paraṃ divyaṃ sastrasāstravasīkutaḥ sastrasāsādhanaṃ ||
maṃ māṃ mīṃ muṃ maiṃ mauṃ māṃjāra netrāda namaḥ || 1000 ||
homaṃ ca chāgamāsena dhṛtenamadhunāsahaḥ sahasraikavidhānena bhavaṃti 
dhanasiddhayaḥ ||
dhanaṃ prāpti 1000 || 
chaṃ chāṃ chīṃ chraṃ chaiṃ chauṃ chaḥ ||
bhujāmarddhaṇī namaḥ ||
vidyākāmacare homaṃ sarkarāmadhupāyayā ||
kāmaṃ tasyabhavedevi sadyovidyā caturdasaṃ ||
puṃ vāṃ vīṃ kakuṃ caiṃ vauṃ caḥ ||
vāyuvagāye namaḥ ||

6a) rajasvalāvastre namadhunā pāyesena tu ||
homaṃ kṛtvāmāṃ devī trailaukyaṃ vasya mānayet ||
aiṃ rāṃ rīṃ ruṃ raṃ rauṃ raḥ ||
rāsavatīmāṃtaṃgi namaḥ ||
nāgavalyāṃ calehomaṃ madhunānāsahasakrāyaḥ ||
ayutaika vidhānena siddhiriṣṭā bhavedhruvaṃ ||
aṣṭamahāsiddhiṃ aiṃ nāṃ nīṃ nuṃ naiṃ nauṃ naḥ sadyāmāṃ jālasyaṃ 
senāddhṛtena madhunāsahaḥ ||
cāṃḍālake sajutkena bhaverdākarṣaṇaṃ dhruvaṃ || bhavati ||
aiṃ cāṃ cīṃ cuṃ caiṃ cauṃ caḥ māṃjārībhakṣaṇi cāṃḍālīya ca 
svāhāḥ ||
sasaṃkasyena māṃsyana madhunālālatena sahataḥ  śrīvasya aiṁ sāṃ sīṃ suṃ 
saiṃ sauṃ saḥ ||
sivasatkibhyāṃ namaḥ bhujākāṣṭajogena cītāvahanaumaṃ ca 
vitakokilākākapachai svahomaṃ satruvināsanaṃ satrunāsanaṃ aiṃ kāṃ kīṃ 
kuṃ kauṃ kaḥ kapālabharchani namaḥ ||
urcāṭaṇa ||
purvoktena vidhānena citākāṣṭa hutāsane ||
ullukaḥ kākaparchi homaṃkṛtvā vānate ||
urcāṭanā aiṃ mālinīyaṃ namaḥ ||
ulukaparchihomena garbhapātaṃ tu striyaḥ ||
sahasraika vidhānena homaṃ kṛtvā samāhitaḥ ||
aiṃ paṃ pāṃ puṃ paiṃ pauṃ paḥ ||
padmakamalāsanāya namaḥ ||
vilvapatramamāṃ vījāṃ māsamekaṃ nilaṃtaraṃ ||
trayīṃ vidhyalabhet putraṃ ciraṃjīvī manunmani ||

7a) calaṃ cīcaḥ cīcu cṛcīi kalaradaputradāya namaḥ ||
vaṃdhukakusumaṃchitvāratkamadhusamanvitaḥ ||
durbhagāṃya bhavedevi saubhāgyasukhadāyakaḥ ||
aiṃ drāṃ drīṃ drūṃ draiṃ drauṃ draḥ durbhagāyestu bhagāyenamaḥ ||
ṣanamāsenayogena bhaverdākarṣaṇaṃ dhruvaṃ ||
devakanyā ca gaṃdhamīnāgakanyā ca kasyacit tatva dāturāgatya kāmārtā 
jihvalāṃ vālalocanā ||
uciṣṭe na valiṃ dadyāt rātrorātreṇa sādhakaṃ ||
iti śrīvisvasāro ucāramahādevatraṇītaṃ śrīpārvatiyodhasanmukhī kalpaṃ 
samāptaḥ ||

7b) oṃ uciṣṭacāḍālinī āṃguṣṭābhyāṃ namaḥ ||
sanmukhī devi tarjabhyāṃ namaḥ ||
svāhā saktīḥ karatalakapṛṣṭābhyāṃ namaḥ ||
ūcchiṣṭacāṇḍālinī hṛdayāya namaḥ ||
sumukhī sirase svāhā ||
mahāpisācinī sidhaṣāyai vauṣaṭ ||
hriṃ kavacāye huṃ ṭhaḥ ṭhaḥ ṭhaḥ netrātraye phaṭ svāhā astraye phaṭ ||
ayadhyānaṃ savāṃ parī itidhyanāṃ ||
