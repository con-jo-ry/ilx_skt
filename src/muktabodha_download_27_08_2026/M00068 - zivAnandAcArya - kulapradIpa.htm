śrīgaṇeśāya namaḥ


guruṃ praṇamya prathamaṃ tato'haṃ gaṇādhipaṃ śrīparadevatāṃ ca |
mude mahopāsakakaulikānāṃ nibaṃdhamekaṃ racayāmi ramyam || 1-1 ||

grantheṣu yeṣveṣu sadāśivena savistaraḥ śrīkulamārga uktaḥ |
prāyaḥ kalau te sulabhā na saṃti jñānaṃ ca teṣāṃ paramaṃ durāpam || 1-2 ||

guroḥ kṛpā yatra bhavet prabhūtā śrīdevatāyāśca mahān prasādaḥ |
tasyaiva puṃsaḥ kulaśāstrabodhastasyātra bhaktirna khalāpanoghā || 1-3 ||

yeṣāṃ dṛḍhā bhaktirihāsti śāstre vidaṃti samyak sakalaṃ rahasyam |
prāgjanmapuṇyād vimalā manīṣā tai niṃdakānāṃsya bhavanti lokāḥ || 1-4 ||

bhītāḥ paśubhye nijagopanārthaṃ nidaṃti kecit kulaśāstrametat |
kecit tu niṃdāniratā abodhā dauṣṭyaina ye te niraye navastum || 1-5 ||

sādhasya bhaktiḥ sudṛḍhātra śāstre pravarttatāṃ svasvaśubhāpanūnam |
yasyāsti sā naivasabhinnaśāstre pravartatāṃ nindakatā tu nārhāḥ || 1-6 ||

mārgā dakṣiṇavāmasaṃjñau pramāṇabhūtau vacanairanekaiḥ |
śāstraiśca śaivairapi vaiṣṇavaiśca sarvairniṣavyau samayādibhedāt || 1-7 ||

brahmaitā pādakahetuyuktaṃ śrāvyaṃ samastāgamasārabhūtam |
kūlapradīpākhyamidaṃ nibaṃdhaṃ vilokya kaulāḥ sukhino bhavantu || 1-8 ||

tatrādau kuladharmaprasaṃsā || śrīkulārṇave ||


śrīdevyuvāca


kuleśa śrotumichāmi sarvabhūtadayānidhe |
kuladharmastvayā deva sūcitā naprakāśitā || 1-9 ||

tasya dharmasya māhātmyaṃ sarvadharmottamasya ca |
vada me parameśāna yaditestiṣṭaṃ yā mayi || 1-10 ||


īśvara uvāca


śṛṇu devi pravakṣyāmi yanmāṃ tvaṃ pariprachasi |
tasyā śravaṇamātreṇa yoginīnāṃ priyo bhavet || 1-11 ||

brahmāviṣṇuguhādīnāṃ na mayā kathitaṃ purā |
kathayāmi tava snehā śṛṇuṣvekāgracetasā || 1-12 ||

pāraṃparyakramāyātaṃ vaktrād vaktreṣu saṃsthitam |
akathyaṃ pāramārthena tathāpi kathayāmi te || 1-13 ||

tvayāpi gopitavyaṃ hi na deyaṃ yasya kasyacit |
deṣaṃbhaktāya śiṣyāya anyathā patanaṃ bhavet || 1-14 ||

sarvebhyaścottamā vedāvedebhyo vaiṣṇavaṃ param |
vaiṣṇavāduttamaṃ śaivaṃ śaivād dakṣiṇamuttamam || 1-15 ||

dakṣiṇāduttamaṃ vāmaṃ vāmāt sidhāṃatamuttamam |
siddhāṃtāduttamaṃ kaulaṃ kaulāt parataraṃ na hi || 1-16 ||

guhyā guhyataraṃ devi sārāt sārataraṃ param |
sākṣāt paraśivaṃ devi karṇāt karṇagataṃ kulam || 1-17 ||

mathitvā jñānadaṇḍena vedāgamamahārṇave |
sārajñena mayā devi kuladharmaḥ samuddhṛtaḥ || 1-18 ||

ekataḥ sakalā dharmāḥ yajñatīrthavratādayaḥ |
ekataḥ kuladharmastu tatra kaulodhika priye || 1-19 ||

praviśanti yathā nadyaḥ samudraṃ tuchacakragāḥ |
tathaiva samayāḥ sarve praviṣṭāḥ kulameva hi || 1-20 ||

yathā hastipade līnaṃ sarvaprāṇipadaṃ bhavet |
darśanāni ca sarvāṇi samayānyaḥ samo bhavet || 1-21 ||

yathā maraturaṃginyā na samāḥ sakalāyagāḥ |
tathaiva samayāḥ sarve kuladharmeṇa nosamāḥ || 1-22 ||

merusarvapayoryadvat sūryakhadyotayoriva |
tathā ca samayāsyāṃ ca kulasya mahadaṃtaram || 1-23 ||

asticetvat samānārī matsamaḥ puruṣosti cet |
kulena samadharmaṃ tu kathayet tadā priye || 1-24 ||

kuladharmaṃ hi mohena yonya dharmeṇadurmatiḥ |
sakṛt sādhāratāṃ brūyāt sotyajonāṃ tyajotyajaḥ || 1-25 ||

yo vai kulādhikaṃ dharmamajñānādvadati priye |
brahmahatyādhikaṃ pāpaṃ samāpnoti na saṃśayaḥ || 1-26 ||

kuladharmapravahaṇaṃ samāruhyanarottamaḥ |
svargadvīpāntaraṃ gatvā mokṣaratnaṃ samukta te || 1-27 ||

darśaneṣu ca sarveṣu cirābhyāsena mānavāḥ |
mokṣaṃ labhante kaule tu sadya eva na saṃśayaḥ || 1-28 ||

bahune hi kimukte na śṛṇumatprāṇavallabhe |
na kaulasamadhrmosti tvāṃ śape kulanāyike || 1-29 ||

yogī cainnaiva bhogī syād bhogī cainnaiva yogavit |
bhogayogātmakaṃ kaulaṃ tasmāt sarvādhikaṃ priye || 1-30 ||

bhogo yogāyate sākṣāt pātakaṃ sukṛtāyate |
mokṣāyate ca saṃsāraḥ kuladharme kuleśvari || 1-31 ||

brahmendrācyutarudrādi devatāmunirākṣasāḥ |
kuladharmaparā devi mānaveṣu ca kākathā || 1-32 ||

vihāya sarvadharmāṃśca nānāgurumatāni ca |
kulameva hi jānīyād yadichet siddhimātmanaḥ || 1-33 ||

pūrvajanmakṛtābhyāsāt kulajñānaprakāśite |
suptotthitapraduddhirvā upadeśādikaṃ vinā || 1-34 ||

janmāntarasahastreṣu yā vuddhirvihitānṛṇām |
tāmeva labhate jaṃtu rūpadeśonirarthakaḥ || 1-35 ||

śaivavaiṣṇavadaurgārka gaṇapatyendusaṃbhavaiḥ |
etairviśuddhacittasya kaulajñānaprakāśate || 1-36 ||

sarvadharmāśca deveśi pusarāvartakā smṛtāḥ |
kuladharme sthitā ye ca te sarvepyanivartakāḥ || 1-37 ||

purā kṛta tapodānaṃ yajñatīrthajapavrataiḥ |
kṣīṇādyānāṃ nṛṇāṃ devi kulajñānaṃ prakāśate || 1-38 ||

śuddhacittasya śāntasya dharmiṇo gurusevina |
api guhyasya bhaktasya kaulajñānaṃ prakāśate || 1-39 ||

sasadbhāvini harṣāḍhye sadācāre dṛḍhavratai |
gurvājñā pālake dhanye kaulajñānaṃ prakāśite || 1-40 ||

anarhekulavijñānaṃ na tiṣṭhati kadācana |
tasmāt parīkṣavaktavyaṃ kulajñānaṃ mayoditam || 1-41 ||

na brūyāchivadharmaṃ tu ayogye śivaśāsanam |
ājñābhaṃgahiyaḥ kuryā devatāśāpamāpnuyāt || 1-42 ||

abodhya samayācāraṃ kuladharmaṃ vadedyadi |
saguruścāpiśiṣyaśca yoginīnāṃ bhavet paśuḥ || 1-43 ||

bodhayitvā guruḥ śiṣyaṃ kulajñānaṃ prakāśayet |
labhate tā bubhausākṣāt yoginīvīramelanam || 1-44 ||

anāyasena saṃsārasāgaraṃ yastitīryati |
kuladharmamimaṃ jñātvā mucyate nātrasaṃśayaḥ || 1-45 ||

kuladharmamahāmārgagantā muktipurīṃ brajet |
acirānnātrasaṃdeha tasmātkaulaṃ samācaret || 1-46 ||

kulaśāstramanādṛtya paśuśāstrāṇiyobhyaset |
svagṛhe pāya saṃktā bhikṣāaṭatidurmatiḥ || 1-47 ||

vihāyakuladharmaṃ yaḥ paśudharmaṃ paro bhavet |
karasthaṃ ratnamutsṛjya kācakhaṇḍaṃ samṛgdhate || 1-48 ||

saṃtyajya kulamantrāṇi paśumantrāṇi yobhyaset |
p. 4) sadhānyarāśimutsṛjya pāṃśuśāśeṃjighṛkṣati || 1-49 ||

kulānyayantiraskṛtya yovānānvayamīkṣate |
taḍāgaṃ tyajya nṛṣṇārtā mṛgatṛṣṇāṃ pradhāvati || 1-50 ||

pathendrajālajalabhāḥ kṣaṇameva sukhāvahā |
śrīkaulādanya samayāstādṛśāḥ kulanāyike || 1-51 ||

kuladharmamajananyo mokṣamichati durmatiḥ |
pārāvāramapāraṃ sapāṇibhyāṃ tat tu michati || 1-52 ||

yovānya darśanebhyaśca bhuktimuktiṃ ca kāṃkṣati |
svapnalabdhe dhanenaiva dhanavyāna sabhad yadi || 1-53 ||

suktau rajatavibhrāntiryathā jāyet pārvati |
tathānya samayebhyaśca bhuktirmuktiḥ prasasyate || 1-54 ||

sarvakarmavahīnopi varṇāśramavivarjitaḥ |
kulaniṣṭaḥ kuleśāni bhuktimuktyoḥ sabhājana || 1-55 ||

kulajñānavihīnopi kulabhaktyāḥ śrayobhavet |
yopitadgatimāpnoti kimutāsya parāyaṇaḥ || 1-56 ||

kuladharmohitohantī rakṣito rakṣati priye |
pūjitaḥ pūjayātyāśu tasmāt ta na parityajet || 1-57 ||

nindantu bāndhavā sarve tyajantu strīsutādayaḥ |
janāsantu māṃ dṛṣṭvā rājino daṇḍayaṃ tu vāḥ || 1-58 ||

seve seve punaḥ sevet vāmeva paradevate |
tva dharmaṃ nevamucāmi manovākkāya karmabhiḥ || 1-59 ||

evamāpadgatasyāpi yasya buddhiḥ suniścitāḥ |
sa tu saṃpūjyate deviramutrāṃba śivo bhavet || 1-60 ||

rogādāridryaduḥkhādyaiḥ pāḍitopyaniśaṃ śive |
yastvāmupāsataṃ bhaktyā śaśvatpadamāpnuyāt || 1-61 ||

janāstuvantunindantu lakṣmīstiṣṭhatu gachatu |
mṛtyuradyayugānte syāt kulaṃ naparibarjayet || 1-62 ||

yo jāto nārcayet tvā tu kuladharmaṃ samāśritaḥ |
kuṃsito jātamātreṇa bhūbhāreṇātmaśatruṇā || 1-63 ||

pulākā iva dhānyeṣu pūjyaṃ dhā-ī ca jantuṣu |
vudbudā iva toyeṣu ye kaulavimukhāhi te || 1-64 ||

taravopi hi jīvanti jīvantī mṛgapakṣiṇaḥ |
sajīvatimano yasya kuladharme vyavasthitam || 1-65 ||

kuladharmāvihīnasya dinānyāyānti yānti ca |
p. 5) salohakārabhasneva śvasannapi najīvati || 1-66 ||

gachatatiṣṭhatocyāpi jāgrato svapatopi vā |
kuleśvari kulājñasya tat paśoriva jīvitam || 1-67 ||

vidvānapi ca mūkhosaudhārmikovāpyadhārmikaḥ |
vratasthopyavratasthaḥ syād yaḥ kaulavimukhojanaḥ || 1-68 ||

jātāsta eva jagatijaṃtavaḥ sādhujīvinaḥ |
kuladharmaparā devi śeṣā jaṭharagarddabhāḥ || 1-69 ||

suṣumānucyate sadbhiḥ kuladharmaparāyaṇāḥ |
aparastu paraṃ pṛṣṭyā matsyakūṭatvacāvṛtaḥ || 1-70 ||

caturvedikalājñānīścapacādadhamaḥ priye |
ścapacopikulajñānī brāhmaṇāditiricyate || 1-71 ||

gurukāruṇyapūtastu dīkṣānirdhūtakalmaṣaḥ |
kulapūjārato devi kāyakāyo nacetaraḥ || 1-72 ||

kaulikaḥ kulajñānaṃ napaśyanti kadā navandite |
napūjayati śāktasya tat kākasyeva jīvitam || 1-73 ||

te vandyāḥ puṇyakarmāṇaste saṃtaste ca yoginaḥ |
yeṣāṃ bhāgyavaśād devi kulajñānaṃ prakāśate || 1-74 ||

te dhanyāste mahātmānaḥ kṛtārthāstenarottamāḥ |
yeṣāmutpadyate citte kulajñānaṃ mayoditam || 1-75 ||

sarvaprākāragamanaṃ sarvatīrthāvagāhanam |
yat sarvayajñācaraṇaṃ kuladharmapraveśanam || 1-76 ||

praviśanti kuladharmaṃ ye cāsukṛtinonarāḥ |
te punaḥ jananīgarbhaṃ naviśanti kadācana || 1-77 ||

prasaṅgenāpi yatkiṃcit kulaṃkulamitārayet |
kulaṃ tasyā kulaṃ devi bhavati tvadanugrahāt || 1-78 ||

kulajñasya kuleśāni nānyadharmaiḥ prayojanam |
kulājñasyāpi ca tathā nānyadharmaiḥ prayojanam || 1-79 ||

kuleśikulaniṣṭānāṃ kaulikānāṃ mahātmanām |
dadāmi paramaṃ jñānamaṃtyaḥ kāle na saṃśayaḥ || 1-80 ||

cirāyāsonakaladaṃ kāṃkṣāṃtasamayaṃ janāḥ |
sukhyena sarvaphaladaṃ kaulaṃ kopina vetyaho || 1-81 ||

kulajño hi sasarvajñau vedaśāstrodgitopi vā |
vedaśāstrāgamajñopi kulājñopyajña eva hi || 1-82 ||

jānanti kulamāhātmyaṃ tvadbhaktā eva nāpare |
cakārā eva jānanti nānye candrarucīruciḥ || 1-83 ||

kulajñāna eva tuṣyanti śrutvā kula kathaṃ priye |
p. 6) alpākūpāyi vivarddhante jyotsnā yā kiṃ samudravat || 1-84 ||

nānya dharmamapekṣante kaulikāḥ sākhedinaḥ |
bhṛṅgāḥ puṣpāntaraṃ yadvan mandārāmodasevina || 1-85 ||

mānayanti hi sarvajñā kuladharmānecetare |
śivaḥ śirasi dhattendu saihiveyogilatyaho || 1-86 ||

abhijñā eva jānanti nābhijñāḥ kuladarśanām |
jalamiśrapayaḥ pānaṃ pakṣī kiṃ vettāhaṃ savati || 1-87 ||

śivaśaktimayolokaḥ kaulapratiṣṭhitaḥ |
tasmāt sarvādhikaṃ kaulaṃ sarvasādhādhāraṇaṃ katham || 1-88 ||

ṣadrar*?nānimeṃgāni pādau kukṣiḥ karauśiraḥ |
teṣu bhedaṃ tu yaḥ kuryān madaṅgachedako hi saḥ || 1-89 ||

etānyeva kulasyāpi ṣaḍaṅgāni bhavanti hi |
tasmānmadātmakaṃ kaulaṃ kaulātmakaḥ priye || 1-90 ||

darśaneṣvakhiledyeka phaladacaika daivatam |
bhuktiṃ muktipradaṃ cāmbakulesmindaivata priye || 1-91 ||

lokadharmaviruddhopi siddhayogīśvarīmate |
kulaṃ pramāṇatāṃ yāti pratyakṣaphaladaṃ yataḥ || 1-92 ||

pratyakṣaṃ ca pramāṇānāṃ sarveṣāṃ pravaraṃ priye |
upalabdhe valāt tasya hatāḥ sarve kutārkikaḥ || 1-93 ||

parokṣe konujānīte kakiṃnubhaviṣyati |
yadvā pratyakṣaphaladaṃ tadevottamadarśanam || 1-94 ||

kuladharmamimaṃ jñātvā mucyante sarvamānavāḥ |
iti matvā maheśāni mayā loke vigarhitam || 1-95 ||

yasya janmāntare pāpakarmabādhodhikaṃ bhavet |
na tasya gurukāruṇyaṃ kaulajñānaṃ na jāyate || 1-96 ||

tvat kāruṇyavihīnānāṃ kulajñānavirodhinām |
paśūnāmanabhijñānāṃ kuladharmovigarhitaḥ || 1-97 ||

yathāṃdhātai?vapaśyanti sūryaṃ sarvaprakāśakam |
tathā kulaṃ na jānanti tava māyā vimohitāḥ || 1-98 ||

śaiva vaiṣṇava bauddhādi darśanānyati bhaktitaḥ |
bhajante mānavā nityaṃ pṛthāyāsaphalānivau || 1-99 ||

vedaśāstragamaprokta bhogamokṣaikasādhanam |
mūḍhā nidanti hāhaṃtamat priyaṃ tava darśanam || 1-100 ||

vāmitāśca mayā devi paśavaḥ śāstrakoṭiṣu |
kuladharmaṃ najānanti vṛthā jñānābhimāninaḥ || 1-101 ||

paśuśāstrāṇi sarvāṇi mayaiva kathitā na hi |
p. 7) mūrtyantaraṃ tu gatvaiva mohanāya durātmanaḥ || 1-102 ||

mahāpāpavaśānnṛṇāṃ teṣu vāṃchā vijāyate |
teṣāṃ hi sadgatirnāsti kalpakoṭiśatairapi || 1-103 ||

praryamāṇopi pāpātmā kule naiva pravartate |
vāryamāṇopi puṇyātmā kulamevābalamvato || 1-104 ||

kuladharmeṇa dūtatvaṃ devāḥ saṃpratipedire |
munīyogīśvarādyāstu saṃsiddhiṃ paramāṃ gatāḥ || 1-105 ||

paśuvratādiniratāḥ sulabhāmāṃ dāṃbhikā bhuvi |
ye kaulameva sevante te mahāṃto hi dullabhāḥ || 1-106 ||

mānavābahavaḥ santi mithyā tattvārthavādina |
durllabho'yaṃ kuleśāni kulatattvaviśāradaḥ || 1-107 ||

durllabhaṃ sarvalokeṣu kulācārasya darśanam |
vipāke naiva puṇyānāṃ labhate nānyathā priye || 1-108 ||

kulaśāstrāṇi sarvāṇi mayaivoktāni pārvati |
pramāṇāni nasaṃdeho nahantavyāni hetubhiḥ || 1-109 ||


iti kuladharmaprasaṃsā |

dvitīyaḥ paṭalaḥ

atha kaulikaprasaṃsā 


kulapūjā samaṃ nāsti puṇyamanyajjagattraye |
tasmād yaḥ pūjayed bhaktyā bhuktimuktyoḥ sabhājanam || 2-1 ||

anadhītopyaśāstrajño gurubhakto dṛḍhavrataḥ |
kulapūjāratoyastu same priyatamo bhavet || 2-2 ||

kīrtyamāne kule yeṣāṃ romāṃcogadgadasvaraḥ |
ānandaḥ śrūyate devi kathitāḥ kaulikeśvarāḥ || 2-3 ||

sarvadharmādhiko loke kuladharmaḥ śivoditaḥ |
iti ye niścatadhīyaḥ proktāste kaulikottamāḥ || 2-4 ||

yo bhavet kulatattvajñaḥ kuladharmaviśāradaḥ |
kulārcanarataḥ sasyāt kaulikodāmbhikāḥ priye || 2-5 ||

kulabhaktān kulācārān kulajñānān kulavratān |
prīto bhavati yo dṛṣṭvā kaulikaḥ satu me priyaḥ || 2-6 ||

saṃsmṛtya kīrtito dṛṣṭvā vandito bhāṣitopi vā |
punāti kuladharmīṣṭaścāṃḍālopi yadachayā || 2-7 ||

sarvajño vāpi mūrkho vāpyuttamo vādhamo'pi vā |
devi yatra kulajñānī tatrāhaṃ ca tvayā saha || 2-8 ||

nāhaṃ vasāmi kailāśe na merau na ca mandire |
p. 8) kulajñā yatra tiṣṭhanti tatra tiṣṭhāmi bhāminī || 2-9 ||

tatvatraya śrīcaraṇamūlamantrārthatatvavit |
devatā gurubhaktaśca kaulikaḥ syānnadīkṣayā || 2-10 ||

kulayogī cared yatra sasanmārgaḥ kuleśvari |
sadūramapigantavyaṃ yatra māheśvarojanaḥ || 2-11 ||

iṣṭavyaśca prayatnena tatra sannihitastvayam |
atidūrasthito vāpi dṛṣṭavyaḥ kuladeśikaḥ || 2-12 ||

samīpe vartamāno'pi na dṛṣṭavya paśuḥ priye |
kulajñānī bhaved yatra sadeśaḥ puṇyabhāgjanaḥ || 2-13 ||

darśanādarcanāt tasya trisaptakulamuddharet |
kulajñāni namālokya svasantānagṛhasthitam || 2-14 ||

nṛtyanti pitaraḥ sarve yāsyāma paramāṃ gatim |
samyak saṃśanti pitaraḥ suvṛṣṭirmivavarṣikāḥ || 2-15 ||

kuleṣu putro vā pautro vā kauliko bhavet |
sadhanyaḥ khalulokesmin puruṣaḥ kṣīṇakalmaṣaḥ || 2-16 ||

devatā gurubhaktaśca niyatātmārcanaḥ priyaḥ || 7-1 ||

kulāgamarahasyajño devatārādhanotsukaḥ |
gurūpadeśasaṃyuktaḥ pūjayet kulanāyike || 7-2 ||

māprasādena bhūyācce rudraśaktisamāgataḥ |
tadūrdhaṃ tarpaṇaṃ kuryād dravyaiḥ śrībharavoditai || 7-3 ||

gurupadeśavidhinā cānyathā patanaṃ bhavet |
mantrayogana deveśi kuryā chrīcakrapūjanam || 7-4 ||

tadahaṃ ca tvayā sārddhaṃ gṛhṇāmisvayamādarāt |
bhairavohamiti jñātvā sarvojñohaṃguṇānvitaḥ || 7-5 ||

iti sañcintya yogīndra kulapūjāṃ samācaret |
ityādi lakṣaṇopetaḥ kaulikoniyatavrataḥ || 7-6 ||

yastāṃ samarcayed devi bhuktimuktyoḥ sabhājanam |
naṣṭaiḥ paryuṣitaiḥ chiṣṭairdurgandhairgandhavarjitaiḥ || 7-7 ||

hetubhiḥ parapātrasthaistarpaṇaṃ niḥphalaṃ bhavet |
svādiṣṭaiścamadiṣṭaiśca dravyairamṛtasaṃbhavaiḥ || 7-8 ||

manoharairmaheśāni tarpaṇaṃ saphalaṃ bhavet |
svaśaktiṃ paraśaktiṃ vā dīkṣitāṃ gururagraṇīm || 7-9 ||

pāyayitvāpi veddravyamiti śaṅkarabhāṣitam |
adīkṣitastriyaḥ kuryāt sadyaḥ saṃskāramambike || 7-10 ||

mantradīkṣāvidhānajñaḥ śuddhā bhavati nānyathā |
tasmāt sulakṣaṇāṃ śaktiṃ gandhapuṣpākṣatādibhiḥ || 7-11 ||

abhyarcya devatā budhyā bhogapātraṃ nivedayet |
tadante kanyakāścāpi pramadāśca manoharāḥ || 7-12 ||

saṃpūjya devatā budhyā pātraṃ dadyāt pṛthak pṛthak |
anivedya tu yaḥ śaktaiḥ kuladravyaṃ niṣevate || 7-13 ||

sā pūjā niḥphalājñeyā devatā na prasīdati |
satsaṃpradāyasaṃyuktaiḥ vīraiśca sahapūjayet || 7-14 ||

anyonya vandanaṃ kṛtvā piveta tadanujñayā |
savyenoddhṛtyapātraṃ tu mudrāṃ prakṛtvāpasavyataḥ || 7-15 ||

yathā vidhi dvitīyena gṛhṇīyānmantramuccaran |
śrīgurujyeṣṭa pūjyānāṃ purataḥ kulanāyike || 7-16 ||

nopaviśya pive dravyamiti śāstrasya niścayaḥ |
nirmantraṃ napive dravyaṃ prāyaścitaṃ vidhīyate || 7-17 ||

tasmānmantravidhānena kartavyaṃ kulanāyike |
idaṃ pavitramamṛtaṃ pivāmi bhava bheṣajam || 7-18 ||

paśupāśasamuchedakāraṇaṃ bhairavoditam |
cittasvāt yatra sāratvāt tasyānandaṃ mayatvataḥ || 7-19 ||

tanmayatvātvabhāvānāṃ bhāvāṃścātarhitārase |
svasvātantrya vikāśāya svarasaḥ svenapīyate || 7-20 ||

tasmād imāṃ surāṃ devīṃ pūrṇādūtāṃ pivāmyaham |
mantreṇānena deveśi mūlamantreṇamantravit || 7-21 ||

anākulamitiḥ kuryād alipānaḥ śanaiḥ śanaiḥ |
bhojanānte viṣaṃ dravyaṃ dravyānte bhojanaṃ viṣam || 7-22 ||

amṛtaṃ tad vijānī yādyadannaṃ surayā saha |
carvaṇena yutaṃ pānamamṛtaṃ kathitaṃ priye || 7-23 ||

carvaṇena vināpānaṃ kevalaṃ viṣavarddhanam |
pānaṃ tu trividhaṃ proktaṃ * *? vīrapaśukramaiḥ || 7-24 ||

divyaṃ devyagrataḥ pānaṃ vīramudvāsane smṛtam |
svechayā paśuvat pītaṃ paśupānamitīritam || 7-25 ||

bhuktimuktipradaṃ divyaṃ vīraṃ muktipradaṃ bhavet |
narakaṃ paśupānaṃ syād iti pānaphalaṃ bhavet || 7-26 ||

dṛṣṭimānasavākvāye yāvanna calanabhramaḥ |
tāvat pānaṃ prakurvīta paśupānamataḥ param || 7-27 ||

yāvannendra ya vai kalpaṃ yāvannomukha vai kṛtiḥ |
tāva deva pived dravyamanyathā na narakaṃ vrajet || 7-28 ||

pūrṇābhiṣekayuktānā pānaṃ devi nigadyate |
karābhyāṃ pātramuddhṛtya smarenmūlaṃ ca pādukām || 7-29 ||

āgalāntaṃ pivenmadyaṃ samuktonātrasaṃśayaḥ |
pītvā pītvā punaḥ pītvā yāvat patati (pa * ti) bhūtale || 7-30 ||

utthāya ca punaḥ pītvā punarjanma navidyate (* vidyate) |
ānandāt tapyate devī mūrchāyā bhairava svayam || 7-31 ||

vamanāt sarvadevāśca tasmāt tritayamācaret |
divyapānaratānāṃ vai yatsukhaṃ kulayoginām || 7-32 ||

tat sukhaṃ sārvabhaumasya nṛpasyā'pi na vidyate |
yat saukhyaṃ kulaniṣṭānāṃ kuladravya niṣevaṇām || 7-33 ||

tat saukhyameva mokṣaḥ syāt satyametad varānane |
āraṃbhastaruṇaścaiva yauvanaḥ prauḍha eva ca || 7-34 ||


tadantaścaiva taruṇollāsasaptamaḥ |


tattvatrayaṃ syād ārabhyaḥ kathitaṃ kulanāyike |
kathitastaruṇollāsastaruṇaṃ mukhamaṃvike || 7-35 ||

yauvanaṃ manasa samyagullāsaḥ kathitaḥ priye |
skhalanaṃ dṛṅnano vācāṃ prauḍha ityabhidhīyate || 7-36 ||

samullāsa pare cakre ya ichet pātramelanam |
arvāk prauḍhasamullāsān naiva kuryāt kadācana || 7-37 ||

yathādhikāraṃ tatrāpi kartavyāṃ pātramelanam |
adīkṣitairanācārairatatajñairadaivataiḥ || 7-38 ||

dūṣakaiḥ samayabhṛṣṭaurnakuryā dravyasaṅgatiḥ |
padavākyapramāṇajñāḥ śrutismṛtyarthavodinaḥ || 7-39 ||

kuladharmā nabhijñāścet tat saṅgaṃ varjayet priye |
satkule ca prasūtā vā vṛddhāḥ svācāravartinaḥ || 7-40 ||

tvat pūjā vimukhāḥ syuścet tat saṅgaṃ varjayet priye |
strī putramitrabandhūnāṃ snigdhāṃnāmapi pārvati || 7-41 ||

kulācārā na bhijñānāṃ saṅgati varjayet priye |
adṛṣṭapuruṣāṇāṃ ca deśāntaranivāsinām || 7-42 ||

vinā saṃketayogena na kuryād dravyasaṅgati |
ekapātraṃ na kurvīta yadi sākṣāt kuleśvaraḥ || 7-43 ||

mantrāḥ parāṅmukhāyānti vighnāścaiva pade pade |
svapātrasthitahetuṃ ca na dadyād bhairavāya ca || 7-44 ||

yadi dadyānmaheśāni devatā śāpamāpnuyāt |
āsanaṃ bhojanaṃ pātramaṃbaraṃ śayanādikam || 7-45 ||

anabhijñeranarhaiśca śaṅkaraṃ naiva kārayet |
yogibhiryoginībhiśca pradattaṃ pūrṇapātrakam || 7-46 ||

samātṛpādukā mūlamaṃtrajaptaṃ pivet priye |
kvacid yadi chayā prāptamalipātraṃ ca bhaktitaḥ || 7-47 ||

ādāya pūrvavajjaptvā pived devi guruṃ smaran |
guruśaktisutānāṃ ca gurujyeṣṭakaniṣṭayoḥ || 7-48 ||

svajyeṣṭasyāpi cochiṣṭaṃ khādennānyasya pārvati |
śaktyuchiṣṭaṃ pived dravyaṃ vīrochiṣṭaṃ tu carvaṇam || 7-49 ||

ātmochiṣṭaṃ na dātavyaṃ parochiṣṭaṃ na bhakṣayet |
uchiṣṭaṃ bhakṣayet strīṇāṃ tābhyo nochiṣṭamarpayet || 7-50 ||

cakramadhye tu deveśi cānyathā patanaṃ bhavet |
kaniṣṭānāṃ svaśiṣyāṇāṃ dadyāt svochiṣṭamaṃvake || 7-51 ||

dadyāt snehena yonyebhyaḥ sabhavedāpadāṃ padam |
asamochiṣṭapātraṃ tu yo vā gṛhṇātimūḍhadhīḥ || 7-52 ||

snehāllobhādbhayādvāpi devatā śāpamāpnuyāt |
svābhiṣṭaceṣṭā caraṇaṃ prauḍhāntaḥ parikīrtitaḥ || 7-53 ||

prauḍhāntollāsake devi mudite yogamaṇḍale |
yoginīmaṇḍale caiva cakramānandamucyate || 7-54 ||

tarūḍheṣu vīreṣu kāryāṃ kāryaṃ na vidyate |
ichaivaśāstrasaṃpattirivyājñā pārameśvari || 7-55 ||

tatra yadyat kṛtaṃ karma śubhaṃ yadi vā śubham |
tat sarvaṃ devatā prītyai jāyate surasundarī || 7-56 ||

jalpojapaphalaṃ tandrī samādhirabhidhīyate |
vikriyā pūjanaṃ devi chardinaṃ bhairavo baliḥ || 7-57 ||

muktiḥ syāḥ chaktisaṃyogastotraṃ tatkālabhāṣaṇam |
nyāso vayavasaṃsparśaḥ kuṇḍubhirhavanakriyā || 7-58 ||

vīkṣaṇaṃ dhyānamīśāni śayanaṃ vandanaṃ bhavet |
tadullāsaikatānāṃ yā ceṣṭā sā ca satkriyā || 7-59 ||

kāryākāryavicāraṃ tu ṣaḥkaroti sapātakī |
etaccakragatā vīrā yoginyo madamaṃtharāḥ || 7-60 ||

samācaranti deveśi yathollāsaṃ manogatam |
śanaiprachanti pārśvasthān vismṛtyātmavivakṣitam || 7-61 ||

nidhāya vadane pātraṃ nirghṛṇā vahasanti ca |
mattāsvapuruṣaṃ mattāṃ kāntānyamabalaṃbite || 7-62 ||

tathaiva puruṣaṃścāpi prauḍhāntollāsasaṃyuteḥ |
puruṣaḥ puruṣamohādāliṃgatyaṃganāṃ naṃganām || 7-63 ||

pṛchanti svapatirmugdhā kastvakohamimaṃbake |
kiṃ kāryaṃ vayamāyātāḥ kimarthamihasaṃsthitā || 7-64 ||

udyānaṃ kimudaṃ hatadgṛhaṃ kiṃ vāṃgaṇaṃ kimum |
mukhesaṃpūrṇamadirāṃ pāyayanti strityaḥ priyān || 7-65 ||

upadaṃśaṃ mukhe kṣiptvā nikṣipati priyānane |
gṛhṇāṃtyanyonyapātrāṇi vyañjanāni ca śāṃbhavī || 7-66 ||

dhṛtvā śirasi nṛtyanti madyabhāṇḍāni yoginī |
ajñātakaratālaṃ tamaspṛṣṭākṣaragītakam || 7-67 ||

praskhalat padavinyāsaṃ nṛtyanti kulaśāktayaḥ |
yoginomadamattāśca patanti pramadorasi || 7-68 ||

madākulāśca yoginyaḥ patanti puruṣorasi |
manorathaṃ ca saṃpūrṇaṃ kurvantī ca parasparam || 7-69 ||

ityādi vividhāśceṣṭāḥ kurvanti kulanāyike |
vikṛtiṃ manaso hitvā yathollāsaḥ pravartate || 7-70 ||

tadā tu devatā bhāvaṃ bhajante yogipuṃgavāḥ |
kaulikān bhairavāveśān yo vā nindanti mūḍhadhīḥ || 7-71 ||

tenāśayanti na sandeho yoginyaḥ kulanāyikāḥ |
manindennahasetkaulāścakre madākulān || 7-72 ||

etaccakragatāṃ vārtāṃ vahirnaiva prakāśayet |
tebhyo drohanakurvīta nāhitaṃ ca samācaret || 7-73 ||

bhaktyā saṃrakṣayed vārtā gopayecca prayatnataḥ |
cakre madākulāṃ dṛṣṭvā cintayed devatādhiyā || 7-74 ||

modate vandate bhaktyā sagachedyoginī pade |
paśye devaṃ vidhaṃ cakrayobhaktyā kaulikaḥ priye || 7-75 ||

vratatīrthatapodāna yajñakoṭiphalaṃ bhavet |
unmanāḥ patanotthānaṃ mūrchānācca muhurmhuḥ || 7-76 ||

unmanākhye tadullāse ṣaṣṭe vīrasamanvite |
ciraṃ sannidhidāvetau yaucidarṇaparākṣarau || 7-77 ||

parabrahmānusandhāna kākṣiṇau kulanāyike |
dehendriyāṇāmavaśaśvānavasthānigadyate || 7-78 ||

anyavasthābhidhānesmin nullāse saptame priye |
parāmantrasvarūposau jāyate mūrchanāparaḥ || 7-79 ||

pravarte bhairavīcakre sarve varṇādvijātayaḥ (vaṇā-) |
nivarte bhairavīcakre sarve varṇāḥ pṛthak pṛthak || 7-80 ||

strī vātha puruṣāḥ ṣaṇḍhaścāṇḍālo vā dvijottamaḥ |
cakramadhye na bhedosti sarve deva samāḥ smṛtāḥ || 7-81 ||

nagarī nirjharādyāṃvu gaṅgāṃ prāpya tathaikatām |
yāti śrīcakramadhye'pi caikatvaṃ mānavāstathā || 7-82 ||

kṣīreṇasahitaṃ toyaṃ kṣīrameva yathā bhavet |
tathā śrīcakramadhye'pi jātibhedo na vidyate || 7-83 ||

svargādipuṇyalokeṣu devādanyad yathā na hi |
tathaiva cakramadhye'pi devatāḥ sarvamānuṣāḥ || 7-84 ||

jātibhedo na cakresti sarve śivasamāsmṛtāḥ |
vede'pi sthitamevaṃ hi sarvaṃ brahmeti cābravīt || 7-85 ||

bahunātra kimuktena cakramadhye kuleśvari |
drūpāḥ puruṣāḥ sarvetvadrūpāḥ pamadāḥ priye || 7-86 ||

cakramadhye ca mūḍhātmā jātibhedaṃ karotiyaḥ |
taṃ bhakṣayanti yoginyastvāṃśaye kulanāyike || 7-87 ||

strīṇāmanyatamaṃ sthānaṃ pusāmanyatamaṃ pṛthak |
athavā mithunaṃ kṛtvā kramāt samupaveśayet || 7-88 ||

paṅktyākāreṇa vā samyak cakrākāreṇa vā priye |
śivaśaktidhiyā sarvaṃ cakramadhye samarcayet || 7-89 ||

avibhaktau yathā cāvāṃ lakṣmīnārāyaṇau priye |
yathā vīṇīśatānando tathā vīraḥ saśaktikaḥ || 7-90 ||

asnātvā nāsanastho vā bhuttkā vā pralayannapi |
gandhapuṣpākṣatā kalpavastrādyairavilaṅkṛtaḥ || 7-91 ||

avinyasta śarīro vā kulapūjāṃ na kārayet |
vināmantreṇa yā pūjā vināmantreṇatarpaṇam || 7-92 ||

vināśaktyā tu yat pānaṃ niḥphalaṃ kathitaṃ priye |
śrīcakrameko no kuryādeka pātre tu nārcayet || 7-93 ||

nārcayed ekahastena na pived ekapāṇinā |
matsyamāṃsāsavairdevi nārcayet paśusannidhau || 7-94 ||

praṇamya praviśeccakraṃ vinirgachet praṇamya ca |
śrīcakramāsane tiṣṭhen na ca vīrāsane tathā || 7-95 ||

śrīcakradarśanaṃ devi netrayoḥ pāpanāśanam |
tannāsti cedbraṇadvaṃdvaṃ kaulikasyākṣiyugmakam || 7-96 ||

anācarān sadācārān cakrasthān śaktikaulikān |
śivagaurīdhīyā devi bhāvayennāvamānayet || 7-97 ||

kulācārya gṛhaṃ gatvā bhaktyā pāpaviśuddhaye |
yācayedamṛtaṃ cānnaṃ tadabhāvelaṃ pivet || 7-98 ||

namaskṛtya ca gṛhṇīyādanyathā narakaṃ vrajet |
asnātvā vāpi bhuttkāvāpya bhaktā vā kuleśvari || 7-99 ||

yaḥ seve te kuladravyaṃ sadāridryamavāpnuyāt |
uṣṇīṣīkaṃcukīnagno muktakeśogalāvrataḥ || 7-100 ||

parāṅmukhī vivādī ca na sevet kulāmṛtam |
yogāmṛtasya niṣṭhīvān madyabhāṇḍaparibhramāt || 7-101 ||

rudane nānnapānācca devatāśāpamāpnuyāt |
ekāsane niviṣṭā ye bhuñjānāścaikabhājane || 7-102 ||

ekapāṇi pivaṃto ye te yānti narakapriye |
yaḥ seveta kuladravyamekagrāme sthite gurau || 7-103 ||

tat kulajñe ca tat putre svajeṣṭakuladeśiko |
vinānujñāṃ kuleśāni sokṣayaṃ narakaṃ vrajet || 7-104 ||

uchiṣṭo no spṛśeccakre kuladravyāṇi pārvati |
vahiḥ prakṣālya ca karau kuladravyāṇi dāpayet || 7-105 ||

madyabhāṇḍaṃ samuddhṛtya na pātraṃ pūrayet priye |
bhogapātraṃ surākuṃbhe na kṣipennakadācana || 7-106 ||

cakramadhye śucidhiyā karaspṛkṣālanādikam |
yaḥ karoti hi mūḍhātmā sabhavedāpadāṃ padam || 7-107 ||

naṣṭīvanaṃ malaṃ mūtramadhye vāyurvisarjanam |
śrīcakramadhye yaḥ kuryāt sabhaved yoginī paśuḥ || 7-108 ||

cakramadhye ghaṭe bhinne pātre ca skhalite priye |
dīpanāśe ca va chāntyai śrīcakraṃ kārayet priye || 7-109 ||

matājapanti dhyāyanti stuvanti praṇamanti ca |
rudanti striyamichanti rudantyājñānataḥ priye || 7-110 ||

parihāsaṃ pralāpaṃ ca vitaṇḍaṃ bahubhāṣaṇam |
audāsīnaṃ bhayaṃ krodhaṃ cakramadhye vivarjayet || 7-111 ||

pātrahasto mahādevi nabhrameccakramaṇḍale |
pātraṃ karābhyāṃ dhṛtvā thanatiṣṭeccaciraṃ priye || 7-112 ||

cālayet pātrahastaṃ na nabhiṃdyāt pātramaṃvike |
pādābhyāṃ na spṛśet pātraṃ nabinduṃ pātayedadhaḥ || 7-113 ||

naikahastena dātavyaṃ namudrāvarjitaṃ pivet |
pātraṃ na cālayet sthānānnakuryāt pātraśaṅkaram || 7-114 ||

saśabdaṃ na pivenmadyaṃ tathaiva ca na pūrayet |
nānyonyaṃ tāḍayet pātraṃ na pātraṃ pātayedadhaḥ || 7-115 ||

sādhāraṃ noddharet pātramanādhāreṇa nakṣipet |
riktapātraṃ na kurvīta na pātraṃ bhrāmayet priye || 7-116 ||

na pātraṃ laṃghayeddhīmān pātraṃ notpātayet priye |
prakṣālyagopayet pātramityājñā pārameśvari || 7-117 ||

yadā sandīpitollāsaḥ kaulikaḥ paśumīkṣate |
paṭhed vā paśuśāstrāṇi saṃgached vā paśustriyaḥ || 7-118 ||

kuryāt paśuprasaṅgaṃ vā paśukāryāṇi vā caret |
dharmārthā puryaśolābho jñānasaukhyādi nasyati || 7-119 ||

śrīcakrasya kuladravyaṃ yaḥ paśubhyaḥ prayachati |
snehāllobhād bhayādvāpi sabhaved yoginī paśuḥ || 7-120 ||

ripuṇāpinakartavyo vāgvādaścakramadhyame |
pitṛmātṛsamaṃ paśye te noktaṃ sparśanaṃ sahet || 7-121 ||

yathā strī putramitrādīn dṛṣṭvā cittaṃ pradṛrśyati |
tathā cet kaulikaṃ paśyat sabhaved yoginī priyaḥ || 7-122 ||

brahmādistaṃbaparyantaṃ yasya me gurusantatiḥ |
tasya me sarvaśiṣyasya ko napūyomahītale || 7-123 ||

iti niścitavuddhi yaḥ sabhaved āvayoḥ priyaḥ |
ahaṃ gururahaṃ jyeṣṭatvaṃhaṃ vettetigarvita || 7-124 ||

ahamevamatiryeṣāṃ kaulikā na bhavanti te |
śrīguruṃ kulaśāstrāṇi pūjyasthānāni yāni ca || 7-125 ||

bhaktyā śrīpūrvakaṃ devi praṇamya parikīrtayet |
guruṃ nāmnā nabhāṣeta japakālādṛte priye || 7-126 ||

śrīnātha deva svāmīti vivāde bodhane vadet |
śrīguruṃ pādukāṃ mudrāṃ mūlamantraṃ svapādukām || 7-127 ||

śiṣyādanyatra deviśi na vadedasya kasyacit |
pāraṃparyakramāmnāya mantrā cādikā priye || 7-128 ||

sarvaṃ gurumukhāladhbaṃ saphalaṃ syānnacānyathā |
śrīśāstrāśraya saṃbhūtaṃ pustakaṃ daivataṃ priye || 7-129 ||

nityaṃ samarcayed bhaktyā paśugehena nikṣipet |
svadāravannivaṣeveta kulaśāstrāṇi pārvati || 7-130 ||

paśuśāstrāṇi sarvāṇi varjayet paradāravat |
