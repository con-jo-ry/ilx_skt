prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ

kailāsaśikharāvāsaḥ kālakālaḥ kṛpānidhiḥ |
apāramahimādhāro mahādevo maheśvaraḥ || 1 ||

aśeṣajagadādhāraṃ sarvakāraṇakāraṇam |
ādimadhyāntarahitamaprameyamanākulam || 2 ||

asaṃkhyātādbhutācintyasvaśaktipariśobhitam |
paraṃ śivaṃ samāgamya praṇamyovāca bhaktimān || 3 ||

rudra uvāca

ādideva kṛpāsindho pañcakṛtyaparāyaṇa |
yatastvaṃ sarvakartā'si sarvajñaḥ sarvamapyasi || 4 ||

ataḥ sarvopakārāya nigamāgamasaṃhitāḥ |
śabdārthamukhyā bhavatā niśvāsavadudīritāḥ || 5 ||

śrīmanmukhādadhigataṃ makuṭaṃ makuṭāyitam |
bhagavan śrotukāmo'smi tadīyaṃ bhāgamuttaram || 6 ||

p. 2) uttamā yatra kathyante sākṣānmokṣapradāyinaḥ |
viśiṣṭadharmā iti hi śāmbhavavratadhāriṇām |
uktaṃ purastād bhavatā tadidānīṃ nibodha me || 7 ||

paraśiva uvāca

śṛṇuṣvāvedayiṣyāmi rahasyaṃ makuṭottare |
śāmbhavavratamāhātmyamāhnikaṃ vratināmapi || 8 ||

arcāviśeṣāḥ pūjopakaraṇānāṃ ca sādhanam |
tadantyeṣṭiprakāraśca kathyante hyatra saṃgrahāt || 9 ||

śāmbhavavratācaraṇam

titīrṣurjanmavārāśiṃ nānāduḥkhagrahākulam |
vedāgamāntaviditaṃ śāmbhavavratamācaret || 10 ||

p. 3) tanutrayagatānādimalatrayaviśodhanāḥ |
liṅgatrayānusandhānaviśoṣitabhavābdhayaḥ || 11 ||

kalyāṇadeśikakṛpākartitāśeṣabandhanāḥ |
bhaktidūtīsamānītamuktikāntāsamāgamāḥ || 12 ||

jñānasūryodayakṛtatamaḥkūṭavipāṭanāḥ |
vedhāmanukriyādīkṣā yatra hyantargatāḥ parāḥ || 13 ||

śirovrataṃ mahāguhyamidaṃ tenaiva labhyate |
yaḥ karmasāmyasaṃgatyā na punarjanmabhāgbhavet || 14 ||

mokṣaśāmbhavavratayoḥ sādhyasādhanātmakatvam

amṛtatvaṃ yadā rudra viṣeṇa labhate janaḥ |
vratametad vihāyātha duḥkhasyāntaṃ sameṣyati || 15 ||

p. 4) na māmadhanyo yajate nādhanyo mokṣamicchati |
nāpyadhanyaḥ samāpnoti śāmbhavavratamuttamam || 16 ||

śāmbhavavratahīnaḥ kevalaḥ paśuḥ

yaḥ sarvaśāstravidapi karmayogarato'pi ca |
śāmbhavavratahīno'yaṃ procyate kevalaḥ paśuḥ || 17 ||

śāmbhavavratamāhātmye yo'rthavādaṃ hi manyate |
sa sarvayātanābhogī bhavatyeva na saṃśayaḥ || 18 ||

iti śrīmakuṭāgame kriyāpāde śāmbhavavratamāhātmyanirūpaṇaṃ nāma 
prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ || 1 ||




dvitīyaḥ paṭalaḥ

rudra uvāca

parameśvara sarvātman paripūrṇaguṇāmbudhe |
śāmbhavānāmanuṣṭheyamāhnikaṃ saṅgirasva me || 1 ||

paraśiva uvāca

āhnikaṃ tu pravakṣyāmi śāmbhavānāmanuttamam |
śṛṇuṣvāvahito bhūtvā bhavapāśanikṛntanam || 2 ||

prātaḥkṛtyāni

buddhyamāno hi madbhakto muhūrte brahmasammite |
saṃsmaran māmiṣṭaliṅgaṃ karābhyāṃ sparśayed dṛśoḥ ||
dharmārthāvanucintyātha māheśān praṇamedapi || 3 ||

vratinaṃ satriṇaṃ dāntaṃ bhaktaṃ liṅgāṅgasaṅginam |
prātaḥkāle tu yaḥ paśyet sa īpsitamavāpnuyāt || 4 ||

pāṣaṇḍaṃ patitaṃ krūramabhaktaṃ devanindakam |
prātarutthāya yaḥ paśyet so'niṣṭaṃ samavāpnuyāt || 5 ||

p. 6) śaucavidhiḥ

bahirgacchedapi tato dūrādāvasathād budhaḥ |
upavītaṃ liṅgasūtraṃ pṛṣṭhataḥ kaṇṭhalambitam |
kṛtvā prāvṛtya ca śiro viṇmūtrotsargamācaret || 6 ||

tato yāvanmanaḥśuddhirmṛtsnayā ca jalena ca |
śaucaṃ kuryāt prayatnena śaucahīno patatyadhaḥ || 7 ||

prakṣālitāṅghripāṇiśca gaṇḍūṣān visṛjedapi |
ātmavidyāśivākhyāni tattvāni svāhayā saha |
ācāmeduccarannevaṃ trivāraṃ śāmbhavavratī || 8 ||

p. 7) dantadhāvanasnānādividhānam

tata āmrādibhiḥ kāṣṭhairdantadhāvanamācaret |
prakṣālya ca mukhaṃ dhīmānācamya ca tataḥ param |
śivatīrthaṃ vidhāyātha prātaḥsnānaṃ samācaret || 9 ||

saṃkalpaḥ sūktapaṭhanaṃ mārjanaṃ cāghamarṣaṇam |
devatātarpaṇaṃ caiva snānapañcāṅgamucyate || 10 ||

apravāhodakasnānaṃ viprapādāvanejanam |
gāyatrījapamarghyaṃ ca ādityābhimukhaścaret |
devarṣitarpaṇaṃ kuryād yakṣmāṇamapi tarpayet || 11 ||

tato dhaute parīdadhyād vāsasī bhānudarśite |
ācamya caiva saṃkalpya bhasmasnānaṃ samācaret || 12 ||

bhasmasnānatripuṇḍradhāraṇādikam

śivāgnijanitenātha yathākalpārjitena vā |
p. 8) uddhūlya caiva sarvāṅgaṃ bhasmanā cāvaguṇṭhya ca |
dvātriṃśatsu pradeśeṣu tripuṇḍraṃ dhārayet kramāt || 13 ||

uttamāṅge lalāṭe ca karṇayornetrayordvayoḥ |
nāsāvaktragaleṣvevamaṃsadvayamanantaram || 14 ||

kūrpare maṇibandhe ca hṛdaye pārśvayordvayoḥ |
nābhau guhyadvaye caiva ūrvoḥ sphigbimbajānuṣu || 15 ||

jaṅghādvaye pādayośca dvātriṃśat sthānamuttamam |
tataḥ punaśca saṃkalpya rudrākṣān bibhṛyānnaraḥ || 16 ||

rudrākṣadhāraṇam

śikhāyāmekarudrākṣaṃ triṃśattu śirasā vahet |
ṣaṭtriṃśattu gale dadyād bāhvoḥ ṣoḍaśa ṣoḍaśa || 17 ||

dvādaśa maṇibandhe'pi skandhe pañcaśataṃ vahet |
aṣṭottaraśatairmālāṃ japayajñe prakalpayet |
supte pīte sadā kālaṃ rudrākṣān bibhṛyānnaraḥ || 18 ||

p. 9) triśataṃ tvadhamaṃ pañcaśataṃ madhyamamucyate |
sahasramuttamaṃ proktamevaṃ bhedena dhārayet || 19 ||

śirasīśānamantreṇa mukhe tatpuruṣeṇa tu |
aghoreṇa gale dhāryaṃ tenaiva hṛdaye'pi ca || 20 ||

aghorākhyena mantreṇa karayordhārayet sudhīḥ |
pañcāśadakṣasahitāṃ vyomavyāpīti codare || 21 ||

pañcabrahmabhiraṅgaiśca trimālāḥ pañca sapta ca |
athavā mūlamantreṇa sarvāṇyakṣāṇi dhārayet || 22 ||

sūryārghyadānaṃ gāyatrījapaḥ

tato madvapuṣe sūryāyārghyatritayamarpayet |
atha maddevatāṃ devīṃ sāvitrīṃ prayato japet || 23 ||

p. 10) śivāgnisaptajihvopāsanam

ghorāṃ mama tanuṃ vahniṃ samupāsīta saṃyataḥ |
suvarṇā kanakā raktā kṛṣṇā caiva tu suprabhā |
bahurūpā'tiraktā ca sapta jihvāḥ prakīrtitāḥ || 24 ||

suvarṇā vāruṇī jihvā kanakā madhyamā smṛtā |
raktā caivottarā jihvā kṛṣṇā yāmyadiśi sthitā || 25 ||

suprabhā pūrvadigjihvā atiraktā'gnigocarā |
aiśānī bahurūpā ca jihvāsthānānyanukramāt || 26 ||

vivāhe vāruṇī jihvā madhyamā yajñakarmasu |
uttarā copanayane dakṣiṇā pitṛkarmasu || 27 ||

pūrvadik sarvakāmyeṣu āgneyī śāntikarmasu |
aiśānī cograkāryeṣu sadā homasya śasyate || 28 ||

pañcavaktrayutaṃ raktaṃ saptajihvāvirājitam |
daśahastaṃ trinetraṃ ca sarvābharaṇabhūṣitam || 29 ||

p. 11) raktavastraparīdhānaṃ paṅkajopari saṃsthitam |
baddhapadmāsanāsīnaṃ daśāyudhasamanvitam || 30 ||

kanakā bahurūpā cātiraktā tu tataḥ param |
suprabhā caiva kṛṣṇā ca raktā cānyā hiraṇmayī || 31 ||

ūrdhvavaktre sthitāstisraḥ śeṣāḥ prāgādidiksthitāḥ |
śivāgnimevaṃ dhyātvaiva sāyaṃprātarhuned budhaḥ || 32 ||

guruśaraṇāgatiḥ

agnikāryaṃ vidhāyātha kartavyamabhivādanam |
śaraṇāgatistu kartavyā gurvādibhyo yathā śṛṇu || 33 ||

dakṣahastena saṃgṛhya niścalo liṅgapeṭikām |
pādāṅguṣṭhau guroḥ savyahastena parigṛhya ca |
saṃsparśayannetrayostrirgurvādīn śaraṇaṃ vrajet || 34 ||

p. 12) divasadvitīyatṛtīyacaturthabhāgakṛtyāni

ebhiścāṣṭāṃśakamaha ādyamevaṃ samāpayet |
dvitīye ca tathā bhāge hyabhyasennigamāgamān |
japedadhyāpayeccātha śāstrāṇyapi vicārayet || 35 ||

samitpuṣpakuśādīni yathā lābhamupāharet |
bhāge yateta tārtīye poṣyavargārthasiddhaye || 36 ||

bhāge tvatha caturthe tu snānārthaṃ mṛdamāharet |
śivatīrthaṃ vidhāyātha madhyāhnasnānamācaret || 37 ||

madhyāhnasnānasandhyopāsanam

bhasmasnānaṃ vidhāyātha tripuṇḍramapi dhārayet |
mādhyāhnikyau tathā sandhye nirvartya ca yathāvidhi |
atha pañca mahāyajñāḥ kartavyā gṛhiṇā'nvaham || 38 ||

p. 13) devān ṛṣīṃstadgaṇāṃśca tarpayitvā yathākramam |
pitṝn divyānadivyāṃśca tarpayet pitṛtīrthataḥ |
vaiśvadevaṃ tu nirvartya pūjāṃ mādhyāhnikīṃ caret || 39 ||

madhyāhnapūjābhedāḥ

mahatīṃ vā guruṃ vāpi laghvīṃ vā yatacetanaḥ |
kāleṣu ṣaṭsu pūjyo'haṃ trisandhyamathavā punaḥ || 40 ||

abhojane vinirdiṣṭā'vasarā tvanyadā parā |
ṣaṭsthaloktavidhānena mahāpūjāṃ samācaret || 41 ||

bhasmaśayyāñcite vāmakarapīṭhe'bhimantrite |
iṣṭaliṅgaṃ suvinyasya uttarāsyābhisammukhaḥ |
pūjayed avadhānena bhāvanāpūrṇasādhanaḥ || 42 ||

p. 14) paṇḍitaṃ vā'tha mūrkhaṃ vā'tithiṃ pūjānta āgatam |
māmeva matvā sampūjya toṣayenmatimān naraḥ || 43 ||

bhuñjāno'pi hi māṃ dhyāyed vācā saṃkīrtayedapi |
na vidyate tadāśaucaṃ pavitraḥ sarvadā'smyaham || 44 ||

upabhujya prasādaṃ me tato māheśvaro'nvaham |
mukhaṃ karaṃ ca prakṣālya dvirācamyekṣaṇe spṛśet || 45 ||

stotrāṇyapi paṭhannevaṃ tadannaṃ pariṇāmayet |
tataścāsāyamapi ca sādhayedarthamātmanaḥ || 46 ||

sāyaṃsandhyopāsanam

nirvartya snānamāgneyaṃ sāyaṃ tu prayataḥ śuciḥ |
upāsya paścime sandhye homakāryaṃ vidhāya ca || 47 ||

sāyantanīmavasarāṃ vaiśvadevaṃ samācaret |
athārdharātrikīṃ pūjāṃ nirvartyātithimarcayet || 48 ||

p. 15) rātriśayanakramaḥ

tataḥ śayyāṃ sukhakarīmadhiśayya śucistu mām |
dhyāyan śayīta ca sukhamutsṛṣṭākhilacintanaḥ || 49 ||

uktaṃ mayā kṛtyajātamāhnikaṃ matpadapradam |
ye narā nānutiṣṭhanti te yānti narakān bahūn || 50 ||

iti śrīmakuṭāgame kriyāpāde āhnikavidhinirūpaṇaṃ nāma dvitīyaḥ paṭalaḥ || 
2 ||



tṛtīyaḥ paṭalaḥ

rudra uvāca

aprameyaguṇādhāra jagadādhāra śāśvata |
arcāviśeṣānadhunā nibodhaya mahāguro || 1 ||

paraśiva uvāca

śṛṇu rudra pravakṣyāmi pūjābhedānanuttamān |
pūjā hi trividhā proktā gurvī ca mahatī laghuḥ || 2 ||

trividhā pūjā

mahatīmuttamaḥ kuryād gurvīṃ madhyama eva ca |
laghvīmaśaktaḥ kuryācca sāyaṃmadhyāhnakālayoḥ || 3 ||

abhojane hyavasarā niyatā prātarādiṣu |
ṣaṭkālamarcanāṃ kuryāt trikālamathavā budhaḥ || 4 ||

maṇḍalavidhānam

padmaṃ tvavasarākhyāyāṃ navapadmaṃ laghau smṛtam |
gurvyāṃ mahatyāmapi ca bhadraṃ tattvaṃ hi maṇḍalam || 5 ||

p. 17) vartikāsaṃkhyā

tisrastvavasarāyāṃ syurvartikā vihitāḥ kila |
laghvyāṃ nava dvādaśa vā tāḥ punaḥ samprakīrtitāḥ |
ṣaṭtriṃśadaṣṭādaśa vā gurvarcāyāṃ samīritāḥ || 6 ||

aṣṭottaraśataṃ vāpi triśataṃ vā sahasrakam |
gurvyāṃ mahatyāmapi ca vartikā jvālayedapi || 7 ||

dīpārādhanam

laghvyāmavasarāyāṃ vā dīpa ekaḥ samīritaḥ |
catvāro vā tathā dvau vā mahatyāṃ ca gurāvapi || 8 ||

japasaṃkhyāvidhānam

laghvyāmavasarāyāṃ cāpyaṣṭottaraśataṃ japet |
gurvyāṃ mahatyāmapi ca mūlaṃ daśaśataṃ japet || 9 ||

nīrājanam

darśanaṃ tvavasarābhikhyamuttarābhikhyamityapi |
nīrājanatrayaṃ proktamavasarāyāṃ mahāmate || 10 ||

darśanākhyāvasarākhyaṃ majjanābhikhyamityapi |
māṅgalyākhyaṃ ca karpūraṃ śṛṅgārākhyaṃ mahābhidham || 11 ||

p. 18) ānandākhyamasaṃkhyātaṃ navanīrājanāni tu |
laghvarcanāyāmetāni kīrtitāni bhavanti hi || 12 ||

sānurāgaṃ ca tāmbūlābhikhyaṃ pūrvoditaiḥ saha |
ekādaśa mahatyāṃ ca gurvyāṃ nīrājanāni hi || 13 ||

apradīpyaiva dīpaṃ tu na ca saṃkalpamācaret |
raṅgavallīṃ pradīpaṃ ca gandhādyairabhipūjayet || 14 ||

naivedyam

avasarāyāmavasaraṃ nivedyaṃ samprakīrtitam |
mahānivedanaṃ kāryaṃ laghvyādiṣu mahāmate || 15 ||

mahānivedyaṃ tatproktaṃ kṣucchāntikaraṇakṣamam |
nivedyamavasaraṃ taddhi kṣucchāntyanupayogi yat || 16 ||

iti śrīmakuṭāgame kriyāpāde'rcāviśeṣavidhinirūpaṇaṃ nāma tṛtīyaḥ 
paṭalaḥ || 3 ||



caturthaḥ paṭalaḥ

rudra uvāca

acintyamahimādhāra kṛpākūpāra śaṅkara |
pūjopayuktadravyāṇāṃ sādhanaṃ brūhi me'dhunā || 1 ||

paraśiva uvāca

pañcāmṛtādinā'bhiṣekaḥ kartavyaḥ

madhu gavyaṃ dadhi kṣīraṃ ghṛtaṃ śarkarayā samam |
abhiṣekāya śastaṃ syācchuddhodakamanuttamam || 2 ||

elośīralavaṅgāni kastūrī candrakaṃ tathā |
pañcadravyāṇi vā candraṃ kastūrī kuṅkumaṃ tu vā |
abhiṣekajale yojyamelośīrayugaṃ tu vā || 3 ||

candanam

rocanaṃ kuṅkumaṃ cailā karpūraṃ koṣṭhameva ca |
kṛṣṇāgaruśca kastūrī samacandanasaṃyutam || 4 ||

p. 20) kṣodo vā vilvakhaṇḍasya kṛtamālasya vā tathā |
gandho vā devadārośca mama priyakarāḥ smṛtāḥ || 5 ||

puṣpāṇi

droṇaṃ vakaṃ ca punnāgaṃ tathā mandārapuṣpakam |
nandyāvartaṃ śriyāvartaṃ karavīrārkake tathā || 6 ||

śatapatraṃ kuvalayaṃ lodhraṃ dhattūrameva ca |
pāṭalaṃ campakaṃ vilvaṃ tamālaṃ karṇikārakam || 7 ||

mātuluṅgamunī caiva priyaṅgurdevadārukam |
kṛtamālāgnimanthau ca mālatī mallikā tathā || 8 ||

nirguṇḍī ca vikarṇī ca bahuparṇī tathā'jitā |
kahlāramatasīpuṣpaṃ kusumbhaṃ kamalaṃ tathā |
śastāni mama pūjāyāṃ tasmāt tairmāṃ supūjayet || 9 ||

sāttvika - rājasa - tāmasapuṣpāṇi

śubhravarṇāni puṣpāṇi sāttvikāni bhavanti hi |
tāni muktipradāni syurbhaktānāṃ mahyamarpaṇāt || 10 ||

rājasānyaruṇānyevaṃ pradadyurbhogamīpsitam |
miśrāṇi pītavarṇāni bhuktimuktipradāni hi |
putrapautrasuvarṇādisarvābhīṣṭapradāni ca || 11 ||

p. 21) kṛṣṇāni tāmasāni syurvinā nīlotpalaṃ bhuvi |
varjanīyāni yatnena na tairmāmarcayejjanaḥ || 12 ||

kālabhedena praśastāni puṣpāṇi

nandyāvartaṃ śriyāvartaṃ śvetārkaṃ śvetapaṅkajam |
lakṣmīpāṭalapunnāgā mālatī śaṅkhinī tathā || 13 ||

palāśāśokabakularaktāgastyasumāni ca |
prātaḥkālikapūjāyāṃ praśastāni bhavanti hi || 14 ||

kṛtamālaṃ ca dhattūraṃ karavīraṃ ca droṇakam |
campakaṃ pāṭalaṃ caiva kamalaṃ cotpalaṃ tathā |
madhyāhnakālapūjāyāṃ praśastāni smṛtāni hi || 15 ||

jātirnīlotpalaṃ caiva kadambaṃ ketakī tathā |
sthalapadmaṃ ca pūgaṃ ca nāgadantisumaṃ tathā |
ardharātrikapūjāyāṃ praśastāni bhavanti hi || 16 ||

kanakaṃ ca kadambaṃ ca ketakī jātireva ca |
ardharātre'rpaṇīyāni nānyathā bhaktitatparaiḥ || 17 ||

pārijātaṃ prātareva sāyaṃ syāccandrakāntakam |
madhyāhna eva yuktā syānnityaṃ madhyāhnamallikā || 18 ||

p. 22) trikālamallikā caiva kanakāmbarameva ca |
droṇaṃ ca vilvapatraṃ ca praśastāni hi sarvadā || 19 ||

varjyāni grāhyāṇi ca puṣpāṇi

yūthikā madayantī ca mādhavī ca śirīṣakam |
bandhūkaṃ sarjakaṃ caiva vibhītaṃ kundameva ca || 20 ||

lāṅgalī dāḍimaṃ dīptaṃ nimbaṃ kārpāsameva ca |
kūṣmāṇḍaṃ śālmalī caiva matsyākṣī śigrupuṣpakam || 21 ||

śrīkarṇaṃ ca kapitthaṃ ca tintriṇīkusumaṃ tathā |
sarvadā varjanīyāni madanugrahakāṅkṣibhiḥ || 22 ||

vilvāragvadhadūrvāpāmārgaṃ campakapatrakam |
jambūkadambadamanadroṇamaruvakapatrakam || 23 ||

śaṅkhinīpadmahrīberasindhuvārādipatrakam |
śastaṃ syānmama pūjāyāmetairmāṃ samyagarcayet || 24 ||
