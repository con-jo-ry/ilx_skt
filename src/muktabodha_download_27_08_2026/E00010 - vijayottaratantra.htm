vijayottaratantram

śivārcana paṭalaḥ

iṣṭirvidhīyate śambhossāmānye tadarūpiṇe |
rājādravyaparityāge jīva * * diśyabhāvaḥ ||

sā ca liṅgāgrayāgreṣu śivena paribhāvitaḥ |
liṅgasya gurumūrtiñca vahnirvā mantrasaṃskṛtaḥ ||

śivavidyādhipāsārdhaṃ śivedyarthavivekalam |
liṅgāgniśivavidyāsu tulyaprītiṃ nivasthitiḥ ||

sthaṇḍile(maṇḍala)maṇḍalaṃ yadvā samūrtiraparaṃ ca yat |
yukte sthāna tayā sarvaṃ śambhomūrtiraninditā ||

mūrdhniktistu dvividyā śambho sākṣikā ca nirākṣikā |
kāmyāmanyāparāmūrti nityāvijñānarūpiṇī ||

tayā nityaṃ śarīrīśaḥ parairvijñātanuśśivaḥ |
liṅgādyanyatamāmūrtimevaṃ niścityasādhakaḥ ||

pratyahaṃ pūjayeddevaṃ pañcaśuddhi purassaram |
ātmasthāna guṇādhāra dravyamantraviśuddhayaḥ ||

śuddhayaḥ pañcamantroktavidhyucyate krameṇa tu |
tantramantrakṛtasnānaṃ kṣālikāṃghrikaraḥ śuciḥ ||

kṛtahastāṅgavinyāsaḥ sāmānyārghyeṇa pūjayet |
gaṇeśaṃ bhāratīmūrdhve lakṣmīṃ madhye prapūjayet ||

p. 2)

dvārasya dakṣiṇe bhāge nandi gaṅge prapūjayet |
anyatrakālayane gandhaiḥ puṣpākṣataiḥ śubhaiḥ ||

praviśya savyapādena vidyāṅgāstramathā yajet |
vāstu madhye tadīśasyaśca tataḥ saṃśrutyaviṣṭaram ||

harahastañca sampādya tenaiva bhavarūpiṇā |
saṃyojayecchive śuddhe mārgaṃ dhyātvā siraśśamam ||

hṛtpuṭena sāntena śivātīte śivātmani |
pradīptakaraṇe vibhunā yadvā sūryamaṇḍale ||

hṛdguhā * hino bimbācca śaktitejāt sṛje saḥ |
udyatpataṅgasaṅkāśamātmānaṃ prāṇarūpiṇam ||

ātmānamevasaṃyojya bhūtaśuddhiṃ samācaret |
bhūtādyaṃ trinivṛttādya kalāstatvādi dhārajaḥ ||

* ktaṃ mantritaṃ pītaṃ aśrudhyāyet kamaṇḍalum |
pañcotpattau itāntaṃ saṃhitenaiva śodhayet ||

dharāṃ śuddhyāṃ svataḥ prāptamaptatvaṃ kṣīrasannibham |
nābhi bī * tamabjābhaṃ smaret khaṇḍendusannibham ||

lāntasthaṃ śikhayāśodhyaṃ prāṇāyāmacatuṣṭayam |
evaṃ pratiṣṭhāṃ saṃśodhya karṇāntapādatonalam ||

trayaśraṃ svastike vidyāt rūpaṃ vidyāntarociram |
samṛ * tvoddhāte stribhiḥ paścādbhrūyurtamanilaṃ kṣipet ||

p. 3)

ṣaṭkoṇaṃ bindu ṣaṭkāsyāṃ kṛṣṇaṃ hye * śodhayet |
huṃ phaḍantāḥ samāyojyā nivṛttādyā viśuddhaye ||

vyomatantraṃ tatogātraṃ vyāpakaṃ bindu saṃjñakam |
śuddhasphaṭikasaṅkāśaṃ vṛttamastreṇa śodhayet ||

astraṃ śivāstramevoktaṃ nānyatpāśupatāstrakam |
evaṃ kalādi śuddhyādi śodhitāśeṣabandhanam ||

mūrtyāsañcintayenmūrtiṃ praṇavātmikayātmakāḥ |
ānīya tasya sātmānamamṛtaṃ cintayet tataḥ ||

mūlena vauṣaḍantena tava madhye śivāsanam |
tatra mūrtiśca sambhāvya brahmāṇyaṅguṣṭhato nyaset ||

ru(ī)śādīni vibhāpresya śakyataṃ mantravigraham |
datvānetraṃ samāvāhya śivamaṅgāni vinyaset ||

kaniṣṭhāntaḥ śivaṃ bhūyaḥ paraṃ karaṇāya ca |
vinyasya * vaṃmaṇāveṣṭya yojayet sarvakarmasu ||

deha nyāsaṃ tataḥ kuryāt samudrābhi yathocyate |
ru(ī)śaṃ tatpuruṣāghoraṃ guhyakājātaśambarāt ||

āsya hṛdguhyajānyugmatakrūruddhavāditi mukheṣu ca |
brahmavaktrākhya yāgaṃsyādālālobhaṃśca vinyaset ||

ru(ī)śānaṃ parimūrdhnāmnā puruṣeṇa mukhāni tu |
dutkarṇāṃsadvayaṃ nābhirjaṭharaṃ cāvaro rasi ||

p. 4)

kuryādaghoramantreṇa vasurūpeṇa mantravit |
guhyaliṅgo gajassānu jaṅghāsīt dvitayaḥ kaṭiḥ ||

pārśve ca kalpayenmantrī vāmadevamanu smaran |
pādapāṇītalaṃ ghrāṇaṃ śirobāhūru jānuṣu ||

evaṃ vidyātanuṃ smṛtvā śivastada * paṃ nyaset |
śivāṅgāni nyaset paścāt hṛdādīni hṛdādiṣu ||

hṛdaye hṛdayaṃ mantrī śiraḥ śirasi vinyaset |
śikhāyāṃ tu śikhāṃ nyasya kavacaṃ sarvagātrakam ||

svahastatalayorastraṃ avasthānaṃ prakṛtyajet |
antaryāgaṃ tataḥ kuryāt hṛdaye bhāva bhaktibhiḥ ||

āsanaṃ manasā kṛtvā śivamūrti dvayaṃ tataḥ |
āvāhya ca śivaṃ sāṅgaṃ arghyapādyādi kalpayet ||

pañcāmṛtābhiṣekādyairdivyagandhānulepanaiḥ |
dukūlapaṭṭamevāṅgavāsobhirvividhairapi ||

hemaraktamayaiḥ puṣpaiḥ dhūpadīpapavitrakaiḥ |
naivedyairvividhairdevaṃ sāvāraṃ bāhyavadyajet ||

nā'stinegaṃ tathā kṛtvā siddhaṃ sandarpayeddivi |
bindornirargalasyanti pariśuddhamṛtaiśśivaiḥ ||

nirdhūpaṃ dhūpadīpāhaṃ bindau śaiva smare saha |
evaṃ kṛtvāntariṣṭhasya sthānaśuddhiṃ samācaret ||

p. 5)

talaṃ trayāsta prakāraṃ kavacāveṣṭitaiḥ kramāt |
tato'rghyapātramānīya prakṣālyāstreṇa vāriṇā ||

puṣpataṇḍulasiddhārtha yavadarbhayavastilaiḥ |
arghyaṃ saṃhitayā kṛtvā surabhyācāmṛtīkṛtam ||

amṛtaṃ toyamādhāra śaktistasya ca kuṇḍalī |
tadvikārasthilādyarthāmṛtādhārodharādayaḥ ||

arghyaṃ viśiṣyate tena yena śambhuḥ prasīdati |
tena dravyāṇi saṃprokṣya śareṇa(ma)mantrayeddhṛdā ||

tanāsnānaṃ samamarcya mantraśuddhiṃ samācaret |
oṃkārādi namaḥ proktān mantrānekādaśoccaran ||

vārāvanniṣṭhurapadaṃ prāptānu tanu liṅgakaḥ |
gāyatriṣvāpurāpūjāṃ sāmānyārghyeṇa vācayet ||

sapīṭhaliṅgamastreṇa kṣālayennirmalaṃ yathā |
āsanaṃ kalpayet tasya vidhivattadvidhīyate ||

śaktimādau hṛdā kuryātsamastādhārarūpiṇīm |
tathā nandaṃ hṛdā * yamya jñānaṃ vairāgyasampadaḥ ||

agnitastadviparyāseḥ prāpte gātrāṇi kalpayet |
māyā vidyemadibhrūte padmakarṇikayā saha ||

vāmādi śaktisūryendu vahnibimbārisādhipam |
hṛdāsaṃkalpayeddevaṃ pañcāsanamayaṃ vibho ||

p. 6)

śvetaraktañca pītañca kṛṣṇaṃ pādānvicintayet |
nānāvarṇāni gātrāṇi * dagnena raktapāṇḍare ||

padmamaṣṭadalaṃ śvetaṃ karṇikāṃ pītavarṇakam |
śaktayaḥ sūryasaṃkāśamā navamī sphaṭikodayam ||

yogapīṭhe vibhoryevaṃ mūrtisminniveśayet |
hṛnmantraṃ puṭiaṃ sadya mūrtimabindvatagocaram ||

caturmūrtimayaṃ raktaṃ śāntyatītakalānvitam |
brahmabhiḥ sakalī kuryāt ṣaṭsaṃkhyaistaṇḍulocanaiḥ ||

eṣā vidyātanuḥ śambhoḥ svaśakti karaṇātmikā |
tatra vidyā tanuṃ kṛtvā brahmarandhrāntagocaram ||

śuddhasphaṭikasaṃkāśaṃ pañcavaktraṃ trilocanam |
daśahastaṃ jaṭājūḍairāraktaiḥ pañcabhiryutam ||

khaḍgaśca śūlaśśakti prasādābhaya satkarām |
sarpākṣamālā samarutpalasāḍimavāhiram ||

suśveteśānavadanaṃ kuṅkumābhapurānanam |
karālaṃ yāmyapadamāmuktottara diṅmukham ||

kṣīrābdhipaścimośāsyāt ṣoḍaśābdopalakṣitaḥ |
dhyātvaivaṃ paramāṃ śambhostanāṃ sakalarūpiṇīm ||

tasyamāvāhaye * * * * sarpādhvamūrdhvagam |
pūrṇā puṣpāñjaliḥ kumbhinivṛttānya manogiti ||

p. 7) 

hṛtkaṇṭhatālubhrūrandhra binduparyantagocaram |
inena sahodgīrya * * * * * susannibham ||

sadyamantraṃ samāviśya viśantīṃ brahmavartmanām |
sphuradrakta * * nādyaiḥ lalaṭe kūṭasannidhim ||

apāne samare dvi dvārā vāhanamidaṃ |
sadīpta kiraṇa pūrṇā mārgeṇa nirgataṃ ||

puṣpaṃ jalagataṃ mūrtau hṛdāsaṃgassthāpayecchivam |
sannidhiṃ sannirodhañca hṛdādyaṅgāni vinyaset ||

mahāmudrā prayogañca mūlena vidhivannayet |
evamāvāhanādyaistu mahāmudrā vipaścimaiḥ ||

yāgādhikaraṇaṃ kuryāt saṃskāraiṣṣaḍbhirīśvaram |
tatra brahmādisopāna tyāgenātha śivātmanā ||

ahyatiradhvāmisya bindāvasuda * * sūtiḥ |
āhatya pure * * * * * śasthāpanaṃ vibhoḥ ||

āhatasyopaviṣṭasya svadāsya vinayodabhiḥ |
tattatpratigṛhītatvaṃ yattadīśasyasannidhiḥ ||

asya sannidhi tasya nirodha iti vyākhyāto prabhāvataḥ |
hṛdayādibhirastrāntaimaktitthara pradhṛṣyati ||

avakuṇḍyata manmoktaṃ vyāpī * * * * * * |
paśutvasya śivatvasya mocanātvādrāvaṇādaṇoḥ ||

p. 8)

dvyakṣarārthopapanneṣu mudrārucivardhanī |
asmādatvikriyāyāṃte viśvānugrahakāriṇī ||

tvadhīṃ * * * sapta prāptavyamayamayākhilam |
ebhūṣādapi vicchinnaṃ madhi daiva tathāgataḥ ||

ata prahṛṣṭavadanaṃ sarvāvayavasundaram |
smṛtvāsapātramutkṣipya pādyamācamanaṃ tathā ||

sammukhena kareṇākhyaṃ saṃvidadyuddhudā vibho |
dhūpayitvā tataśśambhuṃ dūrvāpuṣpākṣatairapi ||

gavyapañcāmṛtādyaistu snāpayitvā savistaram |
to * prasūnadhūpārghyapradānāntaritaṃ yathā ||

piṣṭāmalaka rajanyādyairvirukṣyairāṃ sapiṇḍakam |
prakṣālya nirmalaistoyairante gandhodakena ca ||

candracandanakāśmīrapiṣṭenālipya dhūrjaṭim |
somaṃ dukūlapaṭṭādyairvasanaiḥ paridhāya ca ||

mukuṭādyābharaṇā proktā visṛjaināvidhairapi |
datvā brahmāṅgasaṃghātaṃ yathāsthānaṃ vibhostanau ||

iṣṭasudhūpamārātryā datvā camanamarghyayoḥ |
udbhūtau tu havirdadyātsamudgamabrahmabhirvibhoḥ ||

tatastāmbuladānānte kanyādibhyo baliṃ kṣipet |
paścādāvṛtisaṃpūjya brahmāṇyaṅgānyanujñayā ||

tattatsthānāt samāhṛtya gandhapuṣpādibhiryajet |
madhyaṃ prāgādyasāmyāntaṃmagnīśāsu racāyuṣu ||

yajedvidhivadastraṃ tu cāturdikṣudalāgrataḥ |

p. 9)

tato dhūpaṃ samutkṣipya prokṣitaṃ hṛdayārcitam |
ārātrikaṃ ca parito dīpāścāvṛtakalpitāḥ ||

rakṣoghnaṃ tilakaṃ datvā lalāṭeṣu ca bhasmanā |
ādarśaṃ cāmaraṃ chatraṃ yadanyamapi maṅgalam ||

hṛdā nivedayet pūrvaṃ śivaṃ vādyādi maṅgalaiḥ |
ārātrikapradānānte rātrau nīrājana kriyā ||

kalpyā tadvidhiśceha prasaṅgāducyate'dhunā |
mahānase'thavā'nyatra kṛte gomayamaṇḍale ||

pātrāṇi hemaraupyādi kṛtāni kṣālitāni ca |
nyasyā tatra nyaset vahni kuṇḍānterārateśvarā ||

nocedāvatitodbrahme karāguruvartiṣu |
ghṛtākteṣu tatastāni gavādvyādi maṅgalaiḥ ||

vāhayitvā'ṅganābhirvā yadi vā paricārakaiḥ |
śivāgre maṇḍalaṃ kṛtvā sadayoreṣu madhyataḥ ||

nava * bāpañca yadvaikamujjvaddīpasaṃcayāt |
saṃprokṣyahetinārghyeṇa hṛdādhūpāntamarcayet ||

sāmānyavṛttisaṃbhūtaya * dīpāssamāhṛtāḥ |
tadā nābhyudinādagnessaṃyojyāhṛtyavinyaset ||

purakoda * * ktibhyāṃ nītvā sadyādi pañcakam |
tatā pūrvoktasaṃskārān kṛtopamabhramayedvibhoḥ ||

p. 10)

tridhātrisyekadhā vāpi lavaṇādyaiśca hṛṣṭavat |
tato bhasmapradānānte dīpāntāt prakṣipedbahiḥ ||

tatraiva nityahomaṃ vā kurvīta samidādibhiḥ |
hemaraupyādi pātreṣu yantrikāsārapatriṣu ||

naivedyābhyāya saṃsiddhi naivedyaṃ vividhaṃ tataḥ |
sopaskāraviśeṣeṇa sāvārāya triśūline ||

pānīyaṃ ca pṛthak datvā bhastodbāhāya candanam |
tāmbūlaṃ candrasaṃyuktaṃ datvā caṇḍāyadāpayet ||

nandyādi parivārāṇāṃ baliṃ ca parito'tha vā |
parivāratayā yatra bahirbāhyāṅgasaṃsthitiḥ ||

tadā bali pradānānte naivedyāpanayādikam |
dūrvā puṣpākṣataṃ yajña sūtrañca parameṣṭhinaḥ ||

sauvarṇaṃ vāsakārpāsaṃ datvā mūlāṇunādataḥ |
pūrvavacchivamāvāhya dhūpamārātrikaṃ nayet ||

japtvā śaktyā hṛdā dadyāt guhyepyādikayā girā |
gandhāmbu vidhinā guptaṃ astreṇa kavacena ca ||

geya nṛtta stuti stotrairjayaśabdādi maṅgalaiḥ |
praṇāmastavanādyaiśca toṣayet parameśvaram ||

tato homāya labdhajño yāyādanalasaṃśrayam |
hutvā ca tatra vidhivat bālyā camanapaścimam ||

p. 11)

kṛtvāṅgakara vinyāso homakarma samarpayet |
kārayitvotsavaṃ nityaṃ tāṇḍavaṃ ca vidhānataḥ ||

parāṅmukhena hastena sadātādiva mūrdhniṣu |
dattvārghyamaṅgānutthāpya hetimudrā prayogataḥ ||

yathā sthānaṃ tato śambhoḥ aṅgajālamupāharet |
visarjanasyāpekṣaśca śiṣṭaliṅgeṣu santatam ||

bhūyopyarghya pradānena śivasyāpyupasaṃhṛtiḥ |
gamidaṃ nirapekṣaṃ syāt sthaṇḍilādiṣu sarvadā ||

nirapekṣaṃ śivaṃ tyakte liṅgaśuddhirvidhīyate |
nirmālyamanyathā liṅge puṣpādikamavasthitam ||

pratimāyā yajeddevaṃ catuḥsandhyāmathā'pi vā |
pūrvamadhyāhna tatprātaḥ sāyaṃ sāyāntakeṣu vā ||

pūrvamadhyāhna sāyāhneṣu pūrva madhyāhnayostu vā |
niyatātmā yajeddevaṃ gatihaṃsa tato tadā ||

aṣṭapuṣpikāyā sovyādi snapanāntaram |
aśakto hīnavṛtto vā sā ca leśānnigadyate ||

śivāsanaṃ tato mūrti mūlamaṅgāni pañca ca |
dānamebhi prasūtānāṃ syācchivāyāṣṭapuṣpikā ||

aṣṭapuṣpikayā pūrva sasvanaṃ na samāpyate |
sāyānte devadeveśa devyā saha samarcayet ||

puṣkalairgandhapuṣpādyairapūpādyupadaṃśakaiḥ |
samantātpuṣpamālābhiralaṃ kṛtyasuvistaram ||

p. 12)

pūjāvasāne svaguruṃ gandhapuṣpapavitrakaiḥ |
sampūjya śraddhayā nityaṃ sveṣṭaliṅgavidhe varam ||

svasya dhāraṇaṃ liṅga sveṣṭamanyatpareṣvapi |
sveṣṭaṃ svasyaiva phaladaṃ anyadanyatphalāvaham ||

brāhmaṇa kṣatriyovaiśyaśśudrā vāpi tadaṅganā |
sauvarṇādyanu lomāśca sveṣṭayāgādhikāriṇaḥ ||

anyeṣvapi ca pūjyeṣṭā pradakṣiṇa javātmikā |
sāmānyaliṅge pūjāṃ vā nānye jātyantaro'pi vā ||

sādhāraṇeṣu liṅgeṣu parā'nugraha rūpiṇī |
pūjayettatastatra samayanāthakāravit ||

abhiṣiktastato nyeva sādhako dīkṣito'thavā |
sa sāraṇārcaye yogyā śivatatvādi tatparāḥ ||

sveṣṭaliṅgā vidhau naṣṭau vidhinityotsavātmikā |
coditā śivapūjaiṣā sarvamantrasanātanī ||

sarvatantra praveśo'sya śeṣyate śaṃkarāya tat |
gopanīyā prayatnena prāga niryāganirmabhiḥ ||

ata ūrdhvaṃ samāsena vahnikāryaṃ vidhīyate |

iti vijayottare tri sahastrisaṃhitāyāṃ śivārcanapaṭalaḥ ||


sarpārcitoyāgavidhiḥ 

atha sarpārcito yāgaṃ kāmāyāto vidhīyate |
āgneyyāṃ kārayet kuṇḍaṃ vṛttaṃ vā caturaśrakam ||

hāstikaṃ mekhalā sūtraṃ yonyā copara śobhitam |
hastamātrāyatā kuṇḍaṃ dvitrivedāṅgulodayāḥ ||

mekhalāstaddhanaścāntaḥ karṇamānādbahiḥ sthitaḥ |
kuṇḍikā tatpramāṇena vistāra sadṛśaṃ matam ||

dvihastādi mānāntā tryeṣṭa vibhājite |
teṣu bhātoṅguko jñeyaṃ stena tammekhalāṃ nayet ||

vasu mātra tatiḥ kāryā dairghyato dvādaśāṃgulā |
gajeṣu sadṛśe yoni kārya vā kuṇḍasannidhiḥ ||

karṇapādavavṛttārdha puraḥ pañcāśayogatā |
yoni kuṇḍaṃ bhavedeva dyonya hanyadyogikam ||

vasu bhāgaṃ drumādarghaṃ śabhu krodha śaśāṅgakaiḥ |
tribhāga vṛddhitomatsyai stribhirnaiśācaraṃ bhavet ||

daśāṃśa vṛddhito vṛttaṃ magramādakhilakāmadam |
ṣaḍbhāsavṛddhito matsyeṣasa śṛccāganainilaiḥ ||

padmābhamekhalaṃ padmaṃ mardanaṃ daṇḍana sthitam |
gāyatriyāṃgayikadāra karṇāt dyumānataḥ ||

ā karṇāṃ locane yaṣṭā paṭṭani * di kāṃkṣiṇām |
sthaṇḍilaṃ tadabhāve tu yadvā gomayamaṇḍalam ||

sampādyā lokyadūlena hetinā prokṣyavarmaṇā |
