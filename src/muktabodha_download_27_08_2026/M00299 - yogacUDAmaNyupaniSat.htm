āpyāyantu- iti śāntiḥ

yogāṅgaṣaṭkam

yogacūḍāmaṇiṃ vakṣye yogināṃ hitakāmyayā |
kaivalyasiddhidaṃ gūḍhaṃ sevitaṃ yogavittamaiḥ || 1 ||

āsanaṃ prāṇasaṃrodhaḥ pratyāhāraśca dhāraṇā |
dhyānaṃ samādhiretāni yogāṅgāni bhavanti ṣaṭ || 2 ||

vivaraṇam

mūlādhārādiṣaṭcakrasahasrāroparisthitam |
yogajñānamukhyaphalaṃ rāmacandrapadaṃ bhaje ||

	iha khalu sāmavedapravibhakteyaṃ yogacūḍāmaṇyupaniṣat 
ṣaḍaṅgayogopāyapradarśanapūrvaka 
niṣpratiyogikabrahmamātraparyavasannā vijayate | asyāḥ svalpagranthato

mūlādhārādicakrāṇi

caturdalaṃ syādādhāraṃ svādhiṣṭhānaṃ ca ṣaḍdalam || 4 ||

nābhau daśadalaṃ padmaṃ hṛdayaṃ dvādaśārakam |
ṣoḍaśāraṃ viśuddhākhyaṃ bhrūmadhye dvidalaṃ tathā || 5 ||

sahasradalasaṃkhyātaṃ brahmarandhre mahāpathi |

	yat tvayā pṛṣṭaṃ tat 
mūlādhārādisahasrārānteṣvantarbhavatītyāha- caturdalamiti || 4-5 ||

yonisthāne paramajyotirdarśanam

yonisthānaṃ dvayormadhye kāmarūpaṃ nigadyate |

p. 239) tanmadhye procyate yoniḥ kāmākhyā siddhavanditā |
tasya madhye mahāliṅgaṃ paścimābhimukhaṃ sthitam || 8 ||

nābhau tu maṇivadbimbaṃ yo jānāti sa yogavit |
taptacāmīkarābhāsaṃ taḍillekheva visphurat || 9 ||

trikoṇaṃ tatpuraṃ vahneradhomeḍhrāt pratiṣṭhitam |
samādhau paramaṃ jyotiranantaṃ viśvatomukham || 10 ||

tasmin dṛṣṭe mahāyoge yātāyāto na vidyate |

	prathamadvitīyacakratayā nāmoddiśya krameṇa vivṛṇoti- 
ādhāramiti || 6 || yonisthānaṃ kimityatra- yonisthānamiti | dvayoḥ 
mūlādhārasvādhiṣṭhānayoḥ | yogasiddheḥ tatpīḍanapūrvakatvāt tadeva 
viśadayati- kāmākhyamiti | yogasiddhikāmākhyam || 7 || yoniḥ 
kuṇḍalinīśaktiḥ | asyā jagatprasūtihetutvāt yonitvamupapadyate | ata eva 
kāmākhyā | tasya suṣumnāmadhyavilasitakuṇḍalinībhogasya madhye 
vāyavyaṃ paścimābhimukhaṃ mahāliṅgaṃ kevalakumbhake 
yogipratyakṣatayā sthitaṃ bhavati || 8 || evaṃjñānavadabhyāsaphalamāha##-
tapteti || 9 || yasya evaṃ yogakāle pāvakī jvālā dṛśyate tasya 
punariḍādimārgeṇa yātāyāto na vidyate ityāha- samādhāviti || 10 ||

	svāadhiṣṭhānalakṣaṇamāha- sveti || 11 || meḍhrasya

nāḍimahācakram

ūrdhvaṃ meḍhrādadhonābheḥ kandayoniḥ khagāṇḍavat || 14 ||

teṣu nāḍīsahasreṣu dvisaptatirudāhṛtā || 15 ||

pradhānāḥ prāṇavāhinyo bhūyastāsu daśa smṛtāḥ |
iḍā ca piṅgalā caiva suṣumnā ca tṛtīyagā || 16 ||

gāndhārī hastijihvā ca pūṣā caiva yaśasvinī |
alambusā kuhūścaiva śaṅkhinī daśamī smṛtā || 17 ||

etannāḍīmahācakraṃ jñātavyaṃ yogibhiḥ sadā |

	nāḍīkandaṃ tadgatanāḍībhedaṃ cāha- ūrdhvamiti || 14-15 ||

p. 341) 			nāḍīsthānāni

iḍā vāme sthitā bhāge dakṣiṇe piṅgalā sthitā || 18 ||

suṣumnā madhyadeśe tu gāndhārī vāmacakṣuṣi |
dakṣiṇe hastijihvā ca pūṣā karṇe tu dakṣiṇe || 19 ||

yaśasvinī vāmakarṇe cānane cāpyalambusā |
kuhūśca liṅgadeśe tu mūlasthāne tu śaṅkhinī || 20 ||

evaṃ dvāraṃ samāśritya tiṣṭhante nāḍayaḥ kramāt |

	madhyasthasuṣumnāyāḥ iḍā vāme sthitā || 18-20 ||

nāḍīsaṃcārivāyavaḥ tadvyāpāraśca

iḍāpiṅgalasauṣumnāḥ prāṇamārge ca saṃsthitāḥ || 21 ||

satataṃ prāṇavāhinyaḥ somasūryāgnidevatāḥ |
prāṇāpānasamānākhyā vyānodānau ca vāyavaḥ || 22 ||

nāgaḥ kūrmo'tha kṛkaro devadatto dhanaṃjayaḥ |
hṛdi prāṇaḥ sthito nityamapāno gudamaṇḍale || 23 ||

samāno nābhideśe tu udānaḥ kaṇṭhamadhyagaḥ |
vyānaḥ sarvaśarīre tu pradhānāḥ pañca vāyavaḥ || 24 ||

udgāre nāga ākhyātaḥ kūrma unmīlane tathā |
kṛkaraḥ kṣutkaro jñeyo devadatto vijṛmbhaṇe || 25 ||

p. 342) na jahāti mṛtaṃ vāpi sarvavyāpī dhanaṃjayaḥ |
ete nāḍīṣu sarvāsu bhramante jīvajantavaḥ || 26 ||

	tatra ayogiyogibhedena iḍeti || 21 || nāḍīgataprāṇādisvarūpaṃ 
tadvyāpāraṃ cāha- prāṇeti || 22-25 || jīvajantavaḥ- jīvarūpiṇa 
ityarthaḥ, teṣāṃ jīvādhāratvāt || 26 ||

prāṇacalanācalanābhyāṃ jīvacalanācalane

ākṣipto bhujadaṇḍena yathoccalati kandukaḥ |
prāṇāpānasamākṣiptastathā jīvo na tiṣṭhati || 27 ||

prāṇāpānavaśo jīvo hyadhaścordhvaṃ ca dhāvati |
vāmadakṣiṇamārgābhyāṃ cañcalatvānna dṛśyate || 28 ||

rajjubaddho yathā śyeno gato'pyākṛṣyate punaḥ |
guṇabaddhastathā jīvaḥ prāṇāpānena karṣati || 29 ||

prāṇāpānavaśo jīvo hyadhaścordhvaṃ ca gacchati |
apānaḥ karṣati prāṇaṃ prāṇo'pānena karṣati || 30 ||

ūrdhvādhaḥsaṃsthitāvetau yo jānāti sa yogavit |

	prāṇacalanācalanasāpekṣaṃ jīvacalanācalanamityāha- ākṣipta iti 
|| 27 || tadeva viśadayati- prāṇeti || 28-30 ||

p. 343) 			ajapāgāyatryā anusaṃdhānam

hakāreṇa bahiryāti sakāreṇa viśet punaḥ || 31 ||

haṃsahaṃsetyamuṃ mantraṃ jīvo japati sarvadā |
ṣaṭśatāni divā rātrau sahasrāṇyekaviṃśatiḥ || 32 ||

etatsaṃkhyānvitaṃ mantraṃ jīvo japati sarvadā |
ajapā nāma gāyatrī yogināṃ mokṣadā sadā || 33 ||

asyāḥ saṃkalpamātreṇa sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate |
anayā sadṛśī vidyā anayā sadṛśo japaḥ || 34 ||

anayā sadṛśaṃ jñānaṃ na bhūtaṃ na bhaviṣyati |
kuṇḍalinyāṃ samudbhūtā gāyatrī prāṇadhāriṇī || 35 ||

prāṇavidyā mahāvidyā yastāṃ vetti sa vedavit |

	prāṇāpānocchvāsādivyāpṛtireva haṃsamanuḥ ajapāgāyatrī 
jīvanirvartyā bhavatīti ajapeyattāṃ tajjñānaphalaṃ cāha- hakāreṇeti || 
31-33 || ucchvāsaniḥśvāsayoḥ haṃsaḥ so'hamityajapānusaṃdhānena 
jīvaḥ sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate | ajapāṃ stauti- anayeti || 34 || ajapā 
kuṇḍalinījanyeti jñānaphalamāha kuṇḍalinyāmiti || 35 ||

kuṇḍalinyā mokṣadvārabhedanam

kandordhve kuṇḍalīśaktiraṣṭadhā kuṇḍalākṛtiḥ || 36 ||

brahmadvāramukhaṃ nityaṃ mukhenācchādya tiṣṭhati |
yena dvāreṇa gantavyaṃ brahmadvāramanāmayam || 37 ||

p. 344) mukhenācchādya taddvāraṃ prasuptā parameśvarī |
prabuddhā vahniyogena manasā marutā saha || 38 ||

sucīvadgātramādāya vrajatyūrdhvaṃ suṣumnayā |
udghāṭayet kavāṭaṃ tu yathā kuñcikayā gṛham |
kuṇḍalinyā tathā yogī mokṣadvāraṃ prabhedayet || 39 ||

kṛtvā saṃpuṭitau karau dṛḍhataraṃ baddhvātha padmāsanaṃ
gāḍhaṃ vakṣasi saṃnidhāya cubukaṃ dhyānaṃ ca tacceṣṭitam |
vāraṃvāramapānamūrdhvamanilaṃ proccārayet pūritaṃ
muñcan prāṇamupaiti bodhamatulaṃ śaktiprabhāvānnaraḥ || 40 ||

aṅgānāṃ mardanaṃ kṛtvā śramasaṃjātavāriṇā |
kaṭvamlalavaṇatyāgī kṣīrabhojanamācaret || 41 ||

brahmacārī mitāhārī yogī yogaparāyaṇaḥ |
abdādūrdhvaṃ bhavet siddho nātra kāryā vicāraṇā || 42 ||

susnigdhamadhurā hāraścaturthāṃśāvaśeṣitaḥ |
bhuñjate śivasaṃprītyā mitāhārī sa ucyate || 43 ||

kandordhve kuṇḍalīśaktiraṣṭadhā kuṇlākṛtiḥ |
bandhanāya ca mūḍhānāṃ yogināṃ mokṣadā sadā || 44 ||

p. 345) sarvaprāṇyādhārakuṇḍalinīsvarūpaṃ tadvyāpṛtiṃ cāha- 
kandeti || 36 || suṣumnākhyaṃ brahmadvāramukham || 37 || ayogināṃ 
mūlādhārapradeśe prasuptā | yogināṃ yogakāle prabuddhā || 38 || yataḥ 
iyaṃ mokṣadvārabhedanapaṭuḥ ataḥ kuñcikayā devālayakavāṭamiva yogī 
tayā mokṣadvāraṃ vibhedayedityāha- udghāṭayediti || 39 || 
tadbhedanopāyaṃ tatphalaṃ cāha- kṛtveti | naro yogī 
svahṛdayasamaṃ svakarau saṃpuṭitau kṛtvā padmāsanaṃ baddhvā 
gāḍhaṃ vakṣasi saṃnidhāya cubukaṃ 
kaṇṭhasaṃkocalakṣaṇajālaṃdharabandhamāropya mūlabandhena 
vāraṃvāram apānam ūrdhvam anilapūritaṃ proccārayet ūrdhvaṃ 
prāṇenaikyaṃ nayet | aikyānantaraṃ kevalakumbhakamāsthāya 
kuṇḍalinīśaktiprabhāvāt tatkuṇḍalinīceṣṭitaṃ prāṇādivyāpāraṃ muñcan 
śarīratrayakalanāviralaṃ brahmāsmīti mananaṃ dhyānaṃ ca kṛtvā samyak 
prabodhametya tatsamakālaṃ prāṇaṃ paramātmānam upaiti || 40 || evaṃ 
sādhanānuṣṭhānavān yogī acirāt siddho bhavatītyāha- aṅgānāmiti | 
yadyabhyāsakāle svedo jāyate tadā aṅgānāṃ           vāriṇā dṛḍhakāyo 
bhavedityarthaḥ | yogānukūlāhāraniyamamāha- kaṭviti || 41-43 || 
ayogiyogināṃ iyaṃ śaktiḥ bandhamokṣahetuḥ syādityāha- kandeti | 
kandādhobhāge yadi sthitā tadā bandhanāya | yadi suṣumnāmārgeṇa 
kandordhvamārgaṃ gatā tadā yogināṃ mokṣadā || 44 ||
