namāmi devīṃ kamalāyatākṣīṃ surendrabṛndādi natāṃghriyugmām | samunnatāśeṣasuraprapālinīṃ namāmi durgāṃ duritāpahantrīm || 1 ||
kirīṭakoṭyullikhitābjapādāṃ manojavāṃ tāṃ parameśabhāminīm | śaśāṃkarekhānvitamastakāgrāṃ sureśvarīṃ tāṃ praṇamāmi durgām || 2 ||
mūlaśrībhairavātmatrividhapadamahādevatābhiḥ parīte divye siṃhāsanendre sthitamakhilakalābodhakaṃ deśikeśam | tasyāṅke sanniṣaṇṇāmaruṇarucicayā rañjitāśāvakāśāṃ devīmānandapūrṇāmamṛtakaratalāmāśraye'bhīṣṭasiddhyai || 3 ||
kalpāntānalakoṭikoṭisadṛśaṃ ghoraṃ triśūlāyudhaṃ pāṇibhyāṃ parigṛhya pañcavadanaskandhe'dhiruhya sthitām | viśvāsāvṛtavīraśaktivividhakrīḍāviśeṣāravaiḥ tattatkṣudrasamastaśatrunivahāṃ vande mahāśūlinīm || 4 ||
bhāsākundaśaśāṅkaśaṃkhasadṛśāṃ kaṇṭhīravādhyāsinīṃ kodāṇḍeṣukapālapustakajapasrak śūladānābhayām | sarveśīṃ nikhilāghasaṃghaśamanīṃ bhaktānukampāparāṃ devīṃ viśvamanorathaikavaradāṃ dhyāyetparāmambikām || 5 ||
meghaśyāmalakomalāṃ smitamukhīṃ siṃhāsanāṃ śāṅkarīṃ cāpajyodgatavāruṇāstragalitairāpūritāśāṣṭakaiḥ | toyairbhaktaviruddhasainikagaṇaṃ saṃstambhayantyanvahaṃ viśvākāraniyāpanaṃ vidadhatīṃ dhyāyenmahāśūlinīm || 6 ||
āruhya siṃhamasikheṭarathāṅgaśaṃkhacāpeṣvabhītivaradāṅkuśapāśahastā | pathyagratassakalavairimadāpahaya gacchāniśaṃ vijayaśūlini viśvabhūtyai || 7 ||
viśvajyotiprakāśe vividhatanumaye viśvahṛtpadmavāse viśvavyāpārabhedānuguṇavividhairviśvaśikṣaikadakṣe | viśvadrohaṃ kṣamasvākhilavidhaduritaṃ nāśayatvāśu śaśvat trāhi śrīśūlinīśe sahajakaruṇayā santataṃ sannidhehi || 8 ||
