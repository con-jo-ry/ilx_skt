|| ānandasāgarastavaḥ ||
vijñāpanārhaviralāvasarānavāptyā mandodyame mayi davīyasi viśvamātuḥ |
avyājabhūtakaruṇāpavanāpaviddhānyantaḥ smarāmyahamapāṅgataraṅgitāni || 1 ||
āvedyatāmaviditaṃ kimathāpyanuktaṃ
vaktavyamāntararujopaśamāya nālam |
ityarthyase kimapi tacchravaṇe nidhātuṃ
mātaḥ prasīda malayadhvajapāṇḍyakanye ||2||
ākranditaṃ ruditamāhatamānane vā
kasyārdramastu hṛdayaṃ kimataḥ phalaṃ vā |
yasyā mano dravati yā jagatāṃ svatantrā
tasyāstavāmba purataḥ kathayāmi khedam || 3 ||
paryākule manasi vāci pariskhalantyāṃ
āvartagarta iva cakṣiṣi ghūrṇamāne |
kaste'bhidāsyati śive mamatāmavasthāṃ
kāle dayasva kathayāmi tavādhunaiva || 4 ||
bhaktiṃ karotu nitarāṃ surajātimātre grāmīṇajanturiva paurajaneṣu lokaḥ |
anyatra devi bhavadīyapadāravindādākṛṣyamāṇamapi me hṛdayaṃ na yāti || 5 ||
aṅgīkuru tvamavadhīraya vā vayaṃ tu
dāsāstaveti vacasaiva jayema lokān |
etāvataiva sukaro nanu viśvamātaḥ
uddaṇḍadaṇḍadharakiṅkaramaulibhaṅgaḥ || 6 ||
vedāntavākyajanitaṃ vimalaṃ vicāraiḥ āsādya bodhamanucintanato'parokṣam |
muktiṃ vrajanti manujā iti sūktimādyāmālambya kastaritumarhati śailakanye || 7 ||
ekaikavedaviṣayāḥ kati nāmaśākhāstāsāṃ śirāṃsi kati nāma pṛthagvidhāni |
arthāvabodhavidhuro'kṣaralābha eva keṣāṃ nṛṇāṃ katibhirastu śarīrabandhaiḥ || 8 ||
nyāyāḥ parasparavibhinnadiśaḥ sahasramucchāvacāni ca bhavantyupabṛṃhaṇāni |
evaṃ sthite girisute nigamopalānāṃ tātparyasāramavadhārayituṃ kṣamaḥ kaḥ || 9 ||
astvakṣaragrahavidhirjanuṣāṃ sahasrairāpātato bhavatu nāma tato'rthabodhaḥ |
durvādikalpitavikalpataraṅgasāndrān duṣpūrvapakṣajaladhīn kathamuttareyaḥ || 10 ||
brahmeti śaktiriti bandhavimocanīti
māyāmayīti madanāntakavallabheti |
saptāṣṭaśabdaparivartanamātra eva
sāmarthyamāvahati śāstrapariśramo'yam || 11 ||
tasyai prasīdasi girīndrasute ya itthaṃ sampādayeta śanakairaparokṣabodham |
yasmai prasīdasi sa ca kṣamate'vaboddhumitthaṃ parasparasamāśrayametadāste || 12 ||
ākarṇaya tvamimamabhyupagamya vādaṃ
jānātu ko'pi yadi vā hṛdayaṃ śrutīnām |
tasyāpyasaṅkhyabhavabandhaśatārjito'yaṃ
dvaitabhramo galatu janmaśataiḥ kiyadbhiḥ || 13 ||
kāle mahatyanavadhāvapatankvadāpi
kvāpyantime januṣi ko'pi gatiṃ labheta |
itthaṃ samarthanavidhiḥ paramāgamānāṃ
paryāyasūktividhayā nayanaṃ nañarthe ||14 ||
ekāpavargasamaye jagato'pavargaḥ
sarvāpavargasamaye punarastaśaṅkaḥ |
īdṛgvidhaṃ kamapi pakṣamihāvalambya
sthātuṃ sukhaṃ kṣamamaneva pathā pravṛttaiḥ || 15 ||
abhyasya vedamavadhārya ca pūrvatantramālakṣya śiṣṭacaritāni pṛthagvidhāni |
adhyāpanādibhiravāpya dhanaṃ ca bhūri śarmāṇi mātaralasāḥ kathamācareyuḥ || 16 ||
āyasya tāvadapi karma karotu kaścittenāpi mātaradhikaṃ kimivānubhāvyam |
aste sukhaṃ ya iha bhāratavarṣasīmanyāste sa kiñcidita uttarato'pasṛtya || 17 ||
karma tyajema yadi nūnamadhaḥ patema
yadyācarema na kadāpi bhavaṃ tarema |
karma tyajediti carediti ca pravṛttāḥ
bhāvena kena nigamā iti na pratīmaḥ || 18 ||
karmaṇyakarmavidhireṣa yadācaranti
karmāṇi tattadanubandhajihāsayeti |
satyaṃ tathāpyabhinavo bhavitā na bandhaḥ
prācīnabandhaharaṇe ka ivābhyupāyaḥ || 19 ||
prārabdhakarma kiyadārabhate kiyadvā
prārapsyate kiyadidaṃ ka ivāvadhattām |
kālaḥ kiyāniva mayā pratipālanīyo
yasya kṣaṇārdhamapi kalpaśatatvameti || 20 ||
puṃsaḥ kṣaṇārdhamapi saṃsaraṇākṣamasya
sāṅkhyādayaḥ saraṇayo na viśanti karṇam |
saṅkhyāya gāṅgasikatāḥ sakalāśca sūkṣmā
bhuṅkṣveti vāgiva mahākṣudhayārditasya || 21 ||
bhaktistu kā yadi bhavedratibhāvabhedastatkevalānvayitayā viphalaiva bhaktiḥ |
prītistvayi trijagadātmani kasya nāsti svātmadruho na khalu santi janāstrilokyām || 22 ||
ātmā samastajagatāṃ bhavatīti samyagvijñāya yadvitanute tvayi bhāvabandham |
sā bhaktirityabhimataṃ yadi siddhamiṣṭaṃ vyarthaṃ viśeṣyamalamastu viśeṣaṇaṃ naḥ || 23 ||
svātmetaratvamavadhārya paratvabuddhyā
yatprīyate gurujaneṣviva saiva bhaktiḥ |
syādetadevamiyameva tu me jihāsyā
dvaitabhramātkimadhikaṃ bhavabandhamūlam || 24 ||
sevaiva bhaktiriti karmapathapraveśaḥ
sevyaprasādaphalakā kila karmasevā |
dhyānapravāha iti cecchravaṇāt tṛtīyaḥ
prāgeva mātarayamākalito'bhyupāyaḥ || 25 ||
atraiva dāsyasi vimuktimathāpi yāce
mātaḥ śarīrapatanaṃ maṇikarṇikāyām |
astu svakṛtyamanukampanamīśvarāṇāṃ
dāsasya karmakarataiva tathā svakṛtyam || 26 ||
sadyo bhavetsukṛtināmupadeśalābhaḥ
pāpātmanāṃ bahutithe samaye vyatīte |
ityādibhiḥ kila purāṇavacobhiramba
vārāṇasīmapi na yācitumutsuko'smi || 27 ||
ākrāntamantararibhiḥ madamatsarādyaiḥ
gātraṃ valīpalitarogaśatānuviddham |
dāraiḥ sutaiśca gṛhamāvṛtamuttamarṇaiḥ
mātaḥ kathaṃ bhavatu me manasaḥ prasādaḥ || 28 ||
dhanyāḥ kati tribhuvane paramopabhāgyaṃ
saṃsārameva parameśvari bhāvayantaḥ |
ābhāsarūpamavabodhamimaṃ sametya
kliśye kiyatkiyadahaṃ tvamunā bhavena || 29 ||
kā saṃskṛtiḥ kimapacāranibandhaneyaṃ
kīdṛgvidhasya tava kiṃ kṣatametayeti |
praśne tu nāsmi kuśalaḥ prativaktumeva
khedastu me janani ko'pyayamevamāste || 30 ||
evaṃ gatasya mama sāmpratametadarhamatredamaupayikamitthamidaṃ ca sādhyam |
asminpramāṇamidamityapi boddhumamba śaktirna me bhuvanasākṣiṇi kiṃ karomi || 31 ||
na jñāyate mama hitaṃ nitarāmupāyo
dīno'smi devi samayācaraṇākṣamo'smi |
tattvāmananyaśaraṇaḥ śaraṇaṃ prapadye
mīnākṣi viśvajananīṃ jananīṃ mamaiva || 32 ||
kiñcinmayā śrutiṣu kiñcidivāgameṣu
śāstreṣu kiñcidupadeśapatheṣu kiñcit |
āghrātamasti yadato bhavatīṃ varītuṃ
goptrīti kācidudapadyata buddhireṣā || 33 ||
brahmaivamevamahameṣa tadāptyupāya
ityāgamārthavidhurāḥ prathame dayārhāḥ |
tvadrakṣakatvaguṇamātravido dvitīyā
ityarthaye sadadhikāra nirūpaṇāya || 34 ||
mātā karoṣi mamatāṃ mayi yāvadīśattāvadyate mama tataḥ kimivāsti sādhyam |
māmitthamitthamupayuṅkṣva na vismareti kiṃ svāminaṃ tvarayate kvacana svabhṛtyaḥ || 35 ||
tyājyaṃ tyajāni vihitaṃ ca samācarāṇi nityeṣu śaktimanurudhya hu vartitavyam |
tadbuddhiśaktimanuruddhya na kāryaśaktimityetadeva tu śive vinivedayāmi || 36 ||
ātmaiva bhāra iti taṃ tvayi yo nidhatte
so'ṅgāni kāni kalayatvalasaḥ prapatteḥ |
viśvasya sākṣiṇi vilakṣaṇalakṣaṇā yā
visrambhasampadiyameva samastamaṅgam || 37 ||
tvatpreraṇena miṣataḥ śvasato'pi mātaḥ
prāmādike'pi sati karmaṇi me na doṣaḥ |
mātraiva dattamaśanaṃ grasataḥ sutasya
ko nāma vakṣyati śiśoratibhuktidoṣam || 38 ||
muktiṃ niṣādhayiṣatāṃ nijayaiva buddhyā
prārabdhakarma bhavatu pratibandhahetuḥ |
tvāmeva sādhanatayāpi samāśritānāṃ
tulyaṃ tadamba yadi kastava vīravādaḥ || 39 ||
prārabdhakarma girije bhavadāśritānāmanyatra saṃkramaya nāśaya vā samūlam |
martyāśca khalvapi viṣaṃ vapuṣi prasaktaṃ saṃkrāmayanti parato'pi ca nāśayanti || 40 ||
tvaddarśanaśravaṇacintanavandanādiṣvakṣāṇi devi viniyujya yathādhikāram |
rakṣetyasaṅkhyabhavasambhṛtayaiva maitryā rundhyāṃ yadi sthiramamūnyadhunaiva na syuḥ || 41 ||
trātavya eṣa iti cetkaruṇā mayi syāttrāyasva kiṃ sukṛtaduṣkṛtacintayā me |
kartuṃ jagattirayituṃ ca viśṛṃkhalāyāḥ karmānurodha iti kaṃ prati vañcaneyam || 42 ||
tvayyarpitaṃ prathamamappayayajvanaiva
svātmārpaṇaṃ vidadhatā svakulaṃ samastam |
kā tvaṃ maheśi kuladāsamupekṣituṃ māṃ
ko vānupāsitumahaṃ kuladevatāṃ tvām || 43 ||
mauḍhyādahaṃ śaraṇayāmi surāntaraṃ cetkiṃ tāvatā svamapi tasya bhavāmi mātaḥ |
ajñānataḥ paragṛhaṃ praviśanparasya svatvaṃ prayāsyati paśuḥ kimu rājakīyaḥ || 44 ||
ādhāya mūrdhani vṛthaiva bharaṃ mahāntaṃ
mūrkhā nimajjatha kathaṃ bhavasāgare'smin |
vinyasya bhāramakhilaṃ padayorjananyā
visrabdhamuttarata palvalatulyamenam || 45 ||
kvedaṃ patiṣyati vapuḥ kva tato nu gamyaṃ
ko daṇḍayiṣyati kiyaṃtamanehasaṃ vā |
kiṃ tasya santaraṇasādhanamityanantā
cintā sthitā tvayi śanairavatāritā sā || 46 ||
jñānaṃ viśeyamuta tena vinoddhareyaṃ
prārabdhamapyapalapeyamutānuruṃdhyām |
itthaṃ sakṛtprapadanaikavaśaṃvadāyā
māturmayi pravavṛte mahatīha cintā || 47 ||
etajjaḍājaḍavivecanametadeva
kṣityāditatvapariśodhanakauśalaṃ ca |
jñānaṃ ca śaivamidamāgamakoṭilabhyaṃ
māturyadaṅghriyugale nihito mayātmā || 48 ||
ṣaṭtriṃśadāvaraṇamadhyajuṣi tvadaṅghrau
hālāsyanāthadayite nihito mayātmā |
bhūbhutalatridivavartiṣu kaḥ kṣameta
taccakṣuśādi nibhṛtena nirīkṣituṃ mām || 49 ||
bandhaṃ hariṣyasi sukhaṃ vitariṣyasīti
niścapracaṃ nikhilamamba tadāsta eva |
sampratyahaṃ tvayi nidhāya bharaṃ samastaṃ
yānirvṛṇomikimito'pi mamāpavarge || 50 ||
kāśyāṃ nipātaya vapuḥ śvapacālaye vā
svargaṃ naya tvamapavargamadhogatiṃ vā |
adyaiva vā kuru dayāṃ punarāyatau vā
kaḥ sambhramo mama dhane dhaninaḥ pramāṇam || 51 ||
nāhaṃ sahe tava kathāśravaṇāntarāyaṃ
nāhaṃ sahe tava padārcanavicyutiṃ vā |
mokṣaṃ diśaitadaviruddhamidaṃ na cetyānnaivāstu mātarapavargamahopasargaḥ || 52 ||
ācūḍamācaraṇamamba tavānuvāramantaḥsmaranbhuvanamaṅgalamaṅgamaṅgam |
ānandasāgarataraṅgaparamparābhirāndolito na gaṇayāmi gatānyahāni || 53 ||
pāṣāṇato'pi kaṭhine śirasi śrutīnāṃ prāyaḥ parikramavaśādiva pāṭalābham |
amba smareyamamṛtārṇavamāthalabdhahaiyyaṅgavīnasu kumāramidaṃ padaṃ te || 54 ||
ye nāma santi katicidguravistrilokyāṃ teṣāmapi svayamupetavatā gurutvam |
pādena mūrdhni vidhṛtena vayaṃ tavāmba
saṃsārasāgaramimaṃ sukhamuttarāmaḥ || 55 |
sādhāraṇe smarajaye nitilākṣisādhye
bhāgī śivo bhajatu nāma yaśaḥ samagram |
vāmāṅghrimātrakalite janani tvadīye
kā vā prasaktirapi kālajaye purāreḥ || 56 ||
syātkomalaṃ yadi mano mama viśvamātaḥ
tatpādayormṛdulayostava pādukā'stu |
syātkarkaśaṃ yadi karagrahaṇe purāreḥ
aśmādhiropaṇavidhau bhavatūpayogaḥ || 57 ||
prasnigdhamugdharucipādatale bhavatyā
lagnaṃ dṛḍhaṃ yadiha me hṛdayāravindam |
eṣaiva sāgrabhuvanadviśatīpatitvasāmrājyasūcanakarī tava padmarekhā || 58 ||
aprākṛtaṃ mṛdulatāmavicintya kiñcidālambitāsi padayoḥ sudṛḍhaṃ mayā yat |
tanme bhavārṇavanimajjanakātarasya
mātaḥ kṣamasva madhureśvari bālakṛtyam || 59 ||
yatrānamanpaśupatiḥ praṇayāparādhe mandaṃ kila spṛśati candrakalāñcalena |
puṣpārcane'pi mṛditaṃ padayoryugaṃ tanmātastudanti na kathaṃ paruṣā giro me || 60 ||
avyājasundaramanuttaramaprameyamaprākṛtaṃ paramamaṅgalamaṅghripadmam |
saṃdarśayedapi sakṛdbhavatī dayārdrā draṣṭāsmi kena tadahaṃ tu vilocanena || 61 ||
divyā dṛśo'pi diviṣadgrahaṇocitāni
vastūni kāmamavadhārayituṃ kṣamante |
tvanmātravedyavibhave tava rūpadheye
tvadbhāva eva śaraṇaṃ pariśeṣito naḥ || 62 ||
asminmahatyanavadhau kila kālacakre
dhanyāstu ye katipaye śukayogimukhyāḥ |
līnāstvadaṅghriyugale pariśuddhasatvān
tānātmanastava nakhānavadhārayāmaḥ || 63 ||
ā śaiśavānmamatayā kalitastvayāsāvānṛṇyamamba tava labdhumanā mṛgāṅgakaḥ |
svātmānameva niyataṃ bahudhā vibhajya tvatpādayorvinidadhe nakharāpadeśāt || 64 ||
nāntaḥ praveśamayate kimapi śrutaṃ me nāstikyavādaśilayā pratirudhyamānam |
tatpātayāmyahamimāṃ mahatīmadhastātpādodakena kiyatā paradevatāyāḥ || 65 ||
sannāhibhiḥ yamabhaṭaiḥ parivāryamāṇe
mayyarbhake karuṇayā svayamāpatantyāḥ |
ākarṇayeyamapi nāma virāmakāle
mātastavāṅghrimaṇinūpuraśiñjitāni || 66 ||
brahmeśakeśavamukhairbahubhiḥ kumāraiḥ
paryāyataḥ parigṛhītavimuktadeśam |
utsaṅgamamba tava dāsyasi me kadā tvaṃ
mātṛpriyaṃ kila jaḍaṃ sutamāmananti || 67 ||
ūrau śirastava niveśya dayāvitīrṇasaṃvyānapallavasamīravinītakhedam |
atraiva janmani vibhoḥ paramopadeśamākarṇayeyamapi kiṃ maṇikarṇikāyām || 68 ||
kāñcīguṇagrathitakāñcanaceladṛśyacaṇḍātakāṃśukavibhāparabhāgaśobhi |
paryaṅkamaṇḍalapariṣkaraṇaṃ purāreḥ dhyāyāmi te vipulamamba nitambabimbam || 69 ||
garbhe niveśya bhuvanāni caturdaśāpi saṃrakṣituṃ kalitaniścitayā bhavatyā |
prākārameva racitaṃ parito'pi nūnamūhe suvarṇamayamedurapaṭṭabandham || 70 ||
muktāśca khalvapi yadi tripure bhavatyāḥ stanyāśayā stanataṭaṃ na parityajanti |
asmākamudbhaṭabhavajvaratāpitānāmārdrībhavan tu vadanāni kuto na hetoḥ || 71 ||
naṣṭopalabdhamadhigatya śiśuṃ cirānmāṃ
vātsalyavidrutahṛdaḥ paradevatāyāḥ |
klidyatpayodharaviniḥsṛtadugdhabinduniṣyandapaṅktiriva dīvyati hārayaṣṭiḥ || 72 ||
yattaddhanurjanamanomayamaikṣavaṃ te tasyāstu devi hṛdayaṃ mama mūladeśaḥ |
cāpādhiropaṇavidhau caraṇāñcalena sambhāvyate kila samākramaṇaṃ kadācit || 73 ||
āsthāya dāruṇataraṃ kamapi svabhāvamatyantaduṣkṛtakṛtāmapi śikṣaṇāya |
gṛhṇāsi sāyakapade kusumānyamūni mātaḥ suteṣu mahatī kila rūkṣateyam || 74 ||
pāśaṃ sṛṇiṃ ca karayostava bhāvayantaḥ
saṃstambhayanti vaśayanti ca sarvalokān |
cāpaṃ śaraṃ ca sakṛdamba tava smaranto
bhūpālatāṃ dadhati bhogapathāvatīrṇaḥ || 75 ||
pāśāṅkuśau tava kare paricintya rāgadveṣau jayanti paramārthavidastu dhanyāḥ |
ekatra cāpamitaratra śaraṃ ca matvā vyāvartayanti hṛdayaṃ viṣayāndhakūpāt || 76 ||
utkrāntamāntaramidaṃ śaraṇaṃ janānāmapyeti candramiti he śrutayo vadanti |
āstāmidaṃ mama tu devi mano'dhunaiva līnaṃ dṛḍhaṃ vadanacandramasi tvadīye || 77 ||
vidyātmano janani tāvakadantapaṅkteḥ
vaimalyamīdṛgiti varṇayituṃ kṣamaḥ kaḥ |
tatsambhavā yadamalā vacasāṃ savitrī
tanmūlakaṃ kaviyaśo'pi tatastarāṃ yat || 78 ||
svacchāpi te vahati yatkila dantapaṅktiḥ
svacchandanirdalitadāḍimabījaśobhām |
tanme rajovyatikarādhikapāṭalimni
citte paraṃ paricayāditi cintayāmi || 79 ||
ardhaṃ jitatripuramamba tava smitaṃ cedardhāntareṇa ca tathā bhavitavyameva |
taccintaye janani kāraṇasūkṣmarūpasthūlātmakatripuraśāntikṛte smitaṃ te ||80 ||
matkleśadarśanaparidravadantaraṅgahaiyyaṅgavī naparivāhanibhaṃ jananyāḥ |
antastamopahamanusmaratāṃ janānāṃ mandasmitaṃ bhuvanamaṅgalamastu bhūtyai || 81 ||
sāṃsiddhikānanasaroruhadivyagandhasāndrīkṛtenduśakalākalitādhivāsam |
tāmbūlasāramakhilāgamabodhasāraṃ mātarvidhehi mama vaktrakalācikāyām || 82 ||
nāsāmaṇistava śive cirasaṃstavena
pratyāhṛte manasi bhāti tapodhanānām |
ajñānasantatiniśātyayasūcanārthaṃ
āvirbhavantyasuradeśikatārakeva || 83 ||
tāmbūlagarbhapariphullakapolalakṣyatāṭaṅkamauktikamaṇipratibimbadambhāt |
astadvayavyatikarāmalasatvamādyaṃ varṇaṃ bibharti jaṭhare tava vaktrabimbam || 84 ||
datte śriyaṃ bahuvidhāṃ kuśalāni datte
datte padaṃ surapaterapi līlayaiva |
īdṛgvidhāmba tava dṛṣṭirito'dhikā vā
nādyāpi karṇamativartitumīśvarīyam || 85 ||
pāśāṇakūṭakaṭhiṇe janadurvigāhe
vyarthaṃ mahatyupaniṣadvipine pravṛttā |
sevyeta kena tava locanacandrikeyamenāṃ nipātaya sakṛnmayi tapyamāne || 86 ||
kāmaṃ śivena śamitaṃ punarujjagāra dṛṣṭistaveti kimiyaṃ janani stutiste |
līlāprasūtapuruṣārthacatuṣṭayāyāstasyāḥ paraṃ tu sa bhavatyavayutyavādaḥ || 87 ||
somo jagajjanayiteti yadāha vedo nedaṃ latāparamiti bhramitavyamāryaiḥ |
yaḥ śaivavāmatanuvartibhavaddṛgātmā
candro jagatsṛjati tatpara eṣa vādaḥ || 88 ||
sūcyagravadvasumatīmaṇuvacca meruṃ
dṛṣṭiryadamba tava paśyati dānaśauṇḍā |
dṛṣṭāstvayā vayamapīha tataḥ smarāmo
veśantameva bhavasāgaramuttaraṅgam || 89 ||
vāṇīniketanatayā ghanasāragaurāḥ
kalhārakesararucaḥ kamalānuṣaṅgāt |
mātarjayanti śaraṇāgatalokacetomālinyamārjanavaśādasitāḥ kaṭākṣāḥ || 90 ||
ākarṇamullasati mātarapāṅgadeśe kālāñjanena ghaṭitā tava bhāti rekhā |
śaivālapaṅktirivasantatanirjihānakāruṇyapūrapadavīkalitānubandhaḥ || 91 ||
viśvaṃ sṛjati hanti ca yaḥ kaṭākṣo viśvasyatāṃ kathamasau capalasvabhāvaḥ |
eṣo'pi yāmanusarallabhate yaśāṃsi
tāmeva viśvasimi devi tavānukampām || 92 ||
ardhaṃ kalaṅkarahitā karuṇaiva śambhorardhaṃ guṇāstaditare sakalāḥ sametāḥ |
ityamba samprati kila sphuritaṃ rahasyaṃ sampaśyato mama bhavanmayamaiśamardham || 93 ||
amba bhruvostava viceṣṭitamapramattaṃ
sampaśyatāṃ nijanijārthanideśahetoḥ |
tanmūladeśanihitā nibhṛtā surāṇāṃ
dṛṣṭiḥ prayāti mṛganābhiviśeṣakatvam || 94 ||
sāraṃ kaṇaṃ kaṇamagharmarucāṃ sahasrātsaṅgṛhya nirmitamidaṃ tava vaktrabimbam |
tāvatsudhākarakalaṅkakulāni paścādekatra devi nihitāni kacāpadeśāt || 95 ||
vinyastamindramaṇikandalasundareṣu keśeṣu te sphaṭikanirmalamindukhaṇḍam |
ādhārasaṅgativaśādasitāyamānamindīvaracchadavataṃsadaśāṃ bibharti || 96 ||
cintāmaṇistribhuvaneśvari kaustubhaśca
khyātau maṇī tava gṛhāṅgaṇakuṭṭimasthau |
kiṃ ratnamanyadupalabhya kirīṭakoṭiṃ
vācaspatiprabhṛtayastava varṇayantu || 97 ||
prādurbhavattaraṇibimbaśatāruṇāni
paryāptaśītakiraṇāyutaśītalāni |
śṛṅgārasāraparivāhamayāni mātaraṅgāni ke'pi carame januṣi smaranti || 98||
pratyugrakuṅkumarasākalitāṅgarāgaṃ pratyaṅgadattamaṇibhūṣaṇajālaramyam |
tāmbūlapūritamukhaṃ taruṇenducūḍaṃ
sarvāruṇaṃ kimapi vastu mamāvirastu || 99 ||
ardhaṃ striyastribhuvane sacarācare'sminnardhaṃ pumāṃsa iti darśayituṃ bhavatyā |
strīpuṃsalakṣaṇamidaṃ vapurādṛtaṃ yattenāsi devi viditā trijagaccharīrā || 100 ||
nirmāsi saṃharasi nirvahasi trilokīṃ vṛttāntametamapi vetti na vā maheśaḥ |
tasyeśvarasya girije tava sāhacaryājjātaḥ śrutiṣvapi jagajjanakatvavādaḥ || 101 ||
sattāsyakhaṇḍasukhasaṃvidasi trilokīsargasthitipratihatiṣvapinirvyapekṣā |
tvāmantareṇa śiva ityavaśiṣyate kimardhaṃ śivasya bhavatītyanabhijñavādaḥ || 102 ||
nāsminravistapati nātra vivāti vāto
nāsya pravṛttimapi veda jagatsamastam |
antaḥpuraṃ tadidamīdṛśamantakārerasmādṛśāstu sukhamatra caranti bālāḥ || 103 ||
tvatsannidhānarahito mama māstu deśastvattattvabodharahitā mama māstu vidyā |
tvatpādabhaktirahito mama māstu vaṃśastvaccintayā virahitaṃ mama māstu cāyuḥ || 104 ||
tvaṃ devi yādṛgasi tādṛgasi tvamīdṛgeveti vaktumapi boddhumapi kṣamaḥ kaḥ |
māmeva tāvadavidannatipāmaro'haṃ
mātaḥ stutiṃ tvayi samarpayituṃ vilajje || 105 ||
kācitkṛtā kṛtiriti tvayi sā'rpiteti
kāpi pramodakaṇikā na mamāṃtaraṅge |
mauḍhyaṃ madīyamiha yadviditaṃ mamaiva
kiṃ tvamba viśvasimi dīnaśaraṇyatāṃ te || 106 ||
kālānapāsya viṣuvāyanasaṅkramādīnastaṅgate himakare ca divākare ca |
amba smareyamapi te caraṇāravindamānandalakṣaṇamapāstasamastabhedam || 107 ||
caturadhyāyīrūpaṃ kalahaṃsavyañjanaṃ jaganmātuḥ |
aparabrahmamayaṃ vapurantaḥ śaśikhaṇḍamaṇḍanamupāse || 108 ||
|| iti śrī nīlakaṇṭhadīkṣitaviracitaḥ
śrīānandasāgarastavaḥ sampūrṇaḥ ||
