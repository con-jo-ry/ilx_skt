mūṣikavāhana modakahasta cāmarakarṇa vilambitasūtra | vāmanarūpa maheśvaraputra vighnavināyaka pāda namaste ||
devadevasutaṃ devaṃ jagadvighnavināyakam | hastirūpaṃ mahākāyaṃ sūryakoṭisamaprabham || 1||
vāmanaṃ jaṭilaṃ kāntaṃ hrasvagrīvaṃ mahodaram | dhūmrasindūrayudgaṇḍaṃ vikaṭaṃ prakaṭotkaṭam || 2||
ekadantaṃ pralamboṣṭhaṃ nāgayajñopavītinam | tryakṣaṃ gajamukhaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ sukṛtaṃ raktavāsasam || 3||
dantapāṇiṃ ca varadaṃ brahmaṇyaṃ brahmacāriṇam | puṇyaṃ gaṇapatiṃ divyaṃ vighnarājaṃ namāmyaham || 4||
devaṃ gaṇapatiṃ nāthaṃ viśvasyāgre tu gāminam | devānāmadhikaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ nāyakaṃ suvināyakam || 5||
namāmi bhagavaṃ devaṃ adbhutaṃ gaṇanāyakam | vakratuṇḍa pracaṇḍāya ugratuṇḍāya te namaḥ || 6||
caṇḍāya gurucaṇḍāya caṇḍacaṇḍāya te namaḥ | mattonmattapramattāya nityamattāya te namaḥ || 7||
umāsutaṃ namasyāmi gaṅgāputrāya te namaḥ | oṅkārāya vaṣaṭkāra svāhākārāya te namaḥ || 8||
mantramūrte mahāyogin jātavede namo namaḥ | paraśupāśakahastāya gajahastāya te namaḥ || 9||
meghāya meghavarṇāya megheśvara namo namaḥ | ghorāya ghorarūpāya ghoraghorāya te namaḥ || 10||
purāṇapūrvapūjyāya puruṣāya namo namaḥ | madotkaṭa namaste'stu namaste caṇḍavikrama || 11||
vināyaka namaste'stu namaste bhaktavatsala | bhaktapriyāya śāntāya mahātejasvine namaḥ || 12||
yajñāya yajñahotre ca yajñeśāya namo namaḥ | namaste śuklabhasmāṅga śuklamālādharāya ca || 13||
madaklinnakapolāya gaṇādhipataye namaḥ | raktapuṣpa priyāya ca raktacandana bhūṣita || 14||
agnihotrāya śāntāya aparājayya te namaḥ | ākhuvāhana deveśa ekadantāya te namaḥ || 15||
śūrpakarṇāya śūrāya dīrghadantāya te namaḥ | vighnaṃ haratu deveśa śivaputro vināyakaḥ || 16||

japādasyaiva homācca sandhyopāsanasastathā | vipro bhavati vedāḍhyaḥ kṣatriyo vijayī bhavet ||
vaiśyo dhanasamṛddhaḥ syāt śūdraḥ pāpaiḥ pramucyate | garbhiṇī janayetputraṃ kanyā bhartāramāpnuyāt ||
pravāsī labhate sthānaṃ baddho bandhāt pramucyate | iṣṭasiddhimavāpnoti punātyāsattamaṃ kulaṃ ||
sarvamaṅgalamāṅgalyaṃ sarvapāpapraṇāśanam | sarvakāmapradaṃ puṃsāṃ paṭhatāṃ śruṇutāmapi ||
