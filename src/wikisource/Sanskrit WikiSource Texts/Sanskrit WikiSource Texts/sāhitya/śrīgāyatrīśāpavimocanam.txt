|| śrī gāyatrī śāpa vimocanam || śāpa muktā hi gāyatrī caturvarga phala pradā | aśāpa muktā gāyatrī caturvarga phalāntakā ||
oṃ asya śrī gāytrī | brahmaśāpa vimocana mantrasya | brahmā ṛṣiḥ | gāyatrī chandaḥ | bhukti muktipradā brahmaśāpa vimocanī gāyatrī śaktiḥ devatā | brahma śāpa vimocanārthe jape viniyogaḥ ||
oṃ gāyatrī brahmetyupāsīta yadrūpaṃ brahmavido viduḥ | tāṃ paśyanti dhīrāḥ sumanasāṃ vācagrataḥ | oṃ vedānta nāthāya vidmahe hiraṇyagarbhāya dhīmahī | tanno brahma pracodayāt | oṃ gāyatrī tvaṃ brahma śāpat vimuktā bhava ||
oṃ asya śrī vasiṣṭa śāpa vimocana mantrasya nigraha anugraha kartā vasiṣṭa ṛṣi | viśvodbhava gāyatrī chandaḥ | vasiṣṭa anugrahitā gāyatrī śaktiḥ devatā | vasiṣṭa śāpa vimocanārthe jape viniyogaḥ ||
oṃ sohaṃ arkamayaṃ jyotirahaṃ śiva ātma jyotirahaṃ śukraḥ sarva jyotirasaḥ asmyahaṃ | (iti yuktva yoni mudrāṃ pradarśya gāyatrī trayaṃ paditva )| oṃ devī gāyatrī tvaṃ vasiṣṭa śāpat vimukto bhava ||
oṃ asya śrī viśvāmitra śāpa vimocana mantrasya nūtana sṛṣṭi kartā viśvāmitra ṛṣi | vāgdehā gāyatrī chandaḥ | viśvāmitra anugrahitā gāyatrī śaktiḥ devatā | viśvāmitra śāpa vimocanārthe jape viniyogaḥ ||
oṃ gāyatrī bhajāṃyagni mukhīṃ viśvagarbhāṃ yadudbhavāḥ devāścakrire viśvasṛṣṭiṃ tāṃ kalyāṇīṃ iṣṭakarīṃ prapadye | yanmukhānnisṛto akhilaveda garbhaḥ | śāpa yuktā tu gāyatrī saphalā na kadācana | śāpat uttarīta sā tu mukti bhukti phala pradā ||
prārthanā ||
brahmarūpiṇī gāyatrī divye sandhye sarasvatī | ajare amare caiva brahmayone namo'stute | brahma śāpat vimuktā bhava | vasiṣṭa śāpat vimuktā bhava | viśvāmitra śāpat vimuktā bhava ||
