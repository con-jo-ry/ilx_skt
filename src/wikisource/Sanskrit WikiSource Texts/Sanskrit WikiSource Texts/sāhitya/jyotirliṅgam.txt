ujjayinyām mahākālam moṃkālamamaleśvaram |
paralyāṃ vaijanāthaṃ ca ḍākiṃnyāṃ bhīmaśaṃkaram

etāni dvādaśa jyotirliṃgānī sāyaṃprātaḥ paṭhennaraḥ
saptajanmakṛtapāpaṃ smaraṇena vinaśyati |
