Text:

prahlādahlādahetuṃ sakalaguṇagaṇaṃ saccidānandamātraṃ
sauhyāsahyogramūrtiṃ sadabhayamariśaṅkhau ramāṃ bibhrataṃ ca।
aṃhassaṃhāradakṣaṃ vidhibhavavihagendrendrādivandyaṃ
rakṣovakṣovidārollasadamaladṛśaṃ naumi lakṣmīnṛsiṃhama॥1॥

vāmāṅkasthadharākarāñjalipuṭapremātihṛṣṭāntaraṃ
sīmātītaguṇaṃ phaṇīndraphaṇagaṃ śrīmānyapādāṃbujama।
kāmādyākaracakraśaṅkhasuvaroddhāmābhayodyatkaraṃ
sāmādīḍyavarāharūpamamalaṃ he mānasemaṃ smara॥2॥

kolāya lasadākalpajālāya vanamāline।
nīlāya nijabhaktaughapālāya haraye namaḥ॥3॥

dhātrīṃ śubhaguṇapātrīmādāya aśeṣavibudhamodaya।
śeṣetamimadoṣe dhātuṃ hātuṃ ca śaṃkinaṃ śaṃke॥4॥

namo'stu haraye yukti giraye nirjitāraye।
samastagurave kalpatarave paravedināma॥5॥

॥iti śrīvādirājayatikṛtaṃ varāhapañcakaṃ saṃpūrṇama॥
