cāṇakyanītidarpaṇaḥ
<1>
prathamo'dhyāyaḥ
praṇamya śirasā viṣṇuṃ trailokyādhipatiṃ prabhum |
nānāśāstroddhṛtaṃ vakṣye rājanītisamuccayam ||1||
adhītyedaṃ yathāśāstraṃ naro jānāti sattamaḥ |
dharmopadeśavikhyātaṃ kāryākāryaṃ śubhāśubham ||2||
tadahaṃ sampravakṣyāmi lokānāṃ hitakāmyayā |
yena vijñātamātreṇa sarvajñatvaṃ prapadyate ||3||
mūrkhaśiṣyopadeśena duṣṭastrībharaṇena ca |
duḥkhitaiḥ saṃprayogeṇa paṇḍito'pyavasīdati ||4||
duṣṭā bhāryā śaṭhaṃ mitraṃ bhṛtyaścottaradāyakaḥ |
sasarpe ca gṛhe vāso mṛtyureva na saṃśayaḥ ||5||
āpadarthe dhanaṃ rakṣeddārānrakṣeddhanairapi |
ātmānaṃ satataṃ rakṣeddārairapi dhanairapi ||6||
āpadarthe dhanaṃ rakṣecśrīmatāṃ kuta āpadaḥ |
kadāciccalate lakṣmīḥ saṃcito'pi vinaśyati ||7||
yasmindeśe na saṃmāno na vṛttirna ca bāndhavāḥ |
na ca vidyāgamo'pyasti vāsaṃ tatra na kārayeta||8||
dhanikaḥ śrotriyo rājā nadī vaidyastu pañcamaḥ |
pañca yatra na vidyante na tatra divasaṃ vaseta||9||
lokayātrā bhayaṃ lajjā dākṣiṇyaṃ tyāgaśīlatā |
pañca yatra na vidyante na kuryāttatra saṃsthitim ||1.10||
jānīyātpreṣaṇe bhṛtyānbāndhavānvyasanāgame |
mitraṃ cāpattikāleṣu bhāryāṃ ca vibhavakṣaye ||11||
āture vyasane prāpte durbhikṣe śatrusaṅkaṭe |
rājadvāre śmaśāne ca yastiṣṭhati sa bāndhavaḥ ||12||
yo dhruvāṇi parityajya adhruvaṃ pariṣevate |
dhruvāṇi tasya naśyanti adhruvaṃ naṣṭameva hi ||13||
varayetkulajāṃ prājño virūpāmapi kanyakām |
rūpaśīlāṃ na nīcasya vivāhaḥ sadṛśe kule ||14||
nadīnāṃ śastrapāṇīnāṃ nakhīnāṃ śṛṅgiṇāṃ tathā |
viśvāso naiva kartavyaḥ strīṣu rājakuleṣu ca ||15||
viṣādapyamṛtaṃ grāhyamamedhyādapi kāñcanam |
amitrādapi sadvṛttaṃ bālādapi subhāṣitam ||16||
strīṇāṃ dviguṇa āhāro lajjā cāpi caturguṇā |
sāhasaṃ ṣaḍguṇaṃ caiva kāmaścāṣṭaguṇaḥ smṛtaḥ ||17||
iti cāṇakyanītidarpaṇe prathamo'dhyāyaḥ ||1||
<2>
dvitīyo'dhyāyaḥ .
anṛtaṃ sāhasaṃ māyā mūrkhatvamatilobhitā |
aśaucatvaṃ nirdayatvaṃ strīṇāṃ doṣāḥ svabhāvajāḥ ||1||
bhojyaṃ bhojanaśaktiśca ratiśaktirvarāṅganā |
vibhavo dānaśaktiśca nālpasya tapasaḥ phalam ||2||
yasya putro vaśībhūto bhāryā chandānugāminī |
vibhave yaśca santuṣṭastasya svarga ihaiva hi ||3||
te putrā ye piturbhaktāḥ sa pitā yastu poṣakaḥ |
tanmitraṃ yatra viśvāsaḥ sā bhāryā yatra nirvṛtiḥ ||4||
parokṣe kāryahantāraṃ pratyakṣe priyavādinam |
varjayettādṛśaṃ mitraṃ viṣakumbhaṃ payomukham ||5||
na viśvasetkumitre ca mitre cāpi na viśvaseta|
kadācitkupitaṃ mitraṃ sarvaṃ guhyaṃ prakāśayeta||6||
manasā cintitaṃ kāryaṃ vācā naiva prakāśayeta|
mantreṇa rakṣayedgūḍhaṃ kārye cāpi niyojayeta||7||
kaṣṭaṃ ca khalu mūrkhatvaṃ kaṣṭaṃ ca khalu yauvanam |
kaṣṭātkaṣṭataraṃ caiva paragehanivāsanam ||8||
śaile śaile ca māṇikyaṃ mauktikaṃ na gaje gaje |
sādhavo na hi sarvatra candanaṃ na vane vane ||9||
putrāśca vividhaiḥ śīlairniyojyāḥ satataṃ budhaiḥ |
nītijñāḥ śīlasampannā bhavanti kulapūjitāḥ ||2.10||
mātā śatruḥ pitā vairī yābhyāṃ bālā na pāṭhitāḥ |
sabhāmadhye na śobhante haṃsamadhye bako yathā ||11||
lālanādbahavo doṣāstāḍane bahavo guṇāḥ |
ataśchātraśca putraśca tāḍayenna tu lālayeta||12||
ślokena vā tadardhena tadardhārdhākṣareṇa vā |
abandhyaṃ divasaṃ kuryāddānādhyayanakarmabhiḥ ||13||
kāntāviyogaḥ svajanāpamānaṃ ṛṇasya śeṣaṃ kunṛpasya sevā |
dāridryabhāvādvimukhaṃ ca mitraṃ vināgninā pañca dahanti kāyam ||14||
nadītīre ca ye vṛkṣāḥ parageheṣu kāminī |
mantrihīnāśca rājānaḥ śīghraṃ naśyantyasaṃśayaḥ ||15||
balaṃ vidyā ca viprāṇāṃ rājñāṃ sainyaṃ balaṃ tathā |
balaṃ vittaṃ ca vaiśyānāṃ śūdrāṇāṃ pāricaryakam ||16||
nirdhanaṃ puruṣaṃ veśyā prajā bhagnaṃ nṛpaṃ tyajeta|
khagā vītaphalaṃ vṛkṣaṃ bhuktvā cābhyāgato gṛham ||17||
gṛhītvā dakṣiṇāṃ viprāstyajanti yajamānakam |
prāptavidyā guruṃ śiṣyā dagdhāraṇyaṃ mṛgāstathā ||18||
durācārī durādṛṣṭirdurāvāsī ca durjanaḥ |
yanmaitrī kriyate pumbhirnaraḥ śīghraṃ vinaśyati ||19||
samāne śobhate prītiḥ rājñi sevā ca śobhate |
vāṇijyaṃ vyavahāreṣu divyā strī śobhate gṛhe ||2.20||
iti cāṇakyanītidarpaṇe dvitīyo'dhyāyaḥ ||2||
<3>
tṛtīyo'dhyāyaḥ
kasya doṣaḥ kule nāsti vyādhinā ko na pīḍitaḥ |
vyasanaṃ kena na prāptaṃ kasya saukhyaṃ nirantaram ||1||
ācāraḥ kulamākhyāti deśamākhyāti bhāṣaṇam |
saṃbhramaḥ snehamākhyāti vapurākhyāti bhojanam ||2||
sukule yojayetkanyāṃ putraṃ vidyāsu yojayeta|
vyasane yojayecchatruṃ mitraṃ dharme niyojayeta||3||
durjanasya ca sarpasya varaṃ sarpo na durjanaḥ |
sarpo daṃśati kāle tu durjanastu pade pade ||4||
etadarthe kulīnānāṃ nṛpāḥ kurvanti saṃgraham |
ādimadhyāvasāneṣu na te gacchanti vikriyām ||5||
pralaye bhinnamaryādā bhavanti kila sāgarāḥ |
sāgarā bhedamicchanti pralaye'pi na sādhavaḥ ||6||
mūrkhastu prahartavyaḥ pratyakṣo dvipadaḥ paśuḥ |
bhidyate vākyaśalyena adṛśaṃ kaṇṭakaṃ yathā ||7||
rūpayauvanasampannā viśālakulasambhavāḥ |
vidyāhīnā na śobhante nirgandhāḥ kiṃśukā yathā ||8||
kokilānāṃ svaro rūpaṃ strīṇāṃ rūpaṃ pativratam |
vidyā rūpaṃ kurūpāṇāṃ kṣamā rūpaṃ tapasvinām ||9||
tyajedekaṃ kulasyārthe grāmasyārthe kulaṃ tyajeta|
grāmaṃ janapadasyārthe ātmārthe pṛthivīṃ tyajeta||3.10||
udyoge nāsti dāridryaṃ japato nāsti pātakam |
maunena kalaho nāsti nāsti jāgarite bhayam ||11||
atirūpeṇa va sītā atigarveṇa rāvaṇaḥ |
atidānādbalirbaddho hatysarvatra varjayeta||12||
ko hi bhāraḥ samarthānāṃ kiṃ dūraṃ vyavasāyinām |
ko videśaḥ suvidyānāṃ kaḥ paraḥ priyavādinām ||13||
ekenāpi suvṛkṣeṇa puṣpitena sugandhinā |
vāsitaṃ tadvanaṃ sarvaṃ suputreṇa kulaṃ yathā ||14||
ekena śuṣkavṛkṣeṇa dahyamānena vahninā |
dahyate tadvanaṃ sarvaṃ kuputreṇa kulaṃ yathā ||15||
ekenāpi suputreṇa vidyāyuktena sādhunā |
āhlāditaṃ kulaṃ sarvaṃ yathā candreṇa śarvarī ||16||
kiṃ jātairbahubhiḥ putraiḥ śokasantāpakārakaiḥ |
varamekaḥ kulālambī yatra viśrāmyate kulam ||17||
lālayetpañcavarṣāṇi daśavarṣāṇi tāḍayeta|
prāpte tu ṣoḍaśe varṣe putre mitravadācareta||18||
upasarge'nyacakre ca durbhikṣe ca bhayāvahe |
asādhujanasaṃparke yaḥ palāyetsa jīvati ||19||
dharmārthakāmamokṣāṇāṃ yasyaiko'pi na vidyate |
janmajanmani martyeṣu maraṇaṃ tasya kevalam ||3.20||
mūrkhā yatra na pūjyante dhānyaṃ yatra susañcitam |
dāmpatye kalaho nāsti tatra śrīḥ svayamāgatā ||21||
iti cāṇakyanītidarpaṇe tṛtīyo'dhyāyaḥ ||3||
<4>
caturtho'dhyāyaḥ
āyuḥ karma ca vittaṃ ca vidyā nidhanameva ca |
pañcaitāni hi sṛjyante garbhasthasyaiva dehinaḥ ||1||
sādhubhyaste nivartante putramitrāṇi bāndhavāḥ |
ye ca taiḥ saha gantārastaddharmātsukṛtaṃ kulam ||2||
darśanadhyānasaṃsparśairmatsī kūrmī ca pakṣiṇī |
śiśuṃ pālayate nityaṃ tathā sajjanasaṅgatiḥ ||3||
yāvatsvastho hyayaṃ deho yāvanmṛtyuśca dūrataḥ |
tāvadātmahitaṃ kuryātprāṇānte kiṃ kariṣyati ||4||
kāmadhenuguṇā vidyā hyakāle phaladāyinī |
pravāse mātṛsadṛśī vidyā guptaṃ dhanaṃ smṛtam ||5||
eko'pi guṇavānputro nirguṇena śatena kim |
ekaścandrastamo hanti na ca tārāḥ sahasraśaḥ ||6||
mūrkhaścirāyurjāto'pi tasmājjātamṛto varaḥ |
mṛtaḥ sa cālpaduḥkhāya yāvajjīvaṃ jaḍo daheta||7||
kugrāmavāsaḥ kulahīnasevā
kubhojanaṃ krodhamukhī ca bhāryā |
putraśca mūrkho vidhavā ca kanyā
vināgninā ṣaṭpradahanti kāyam ||8||
kiṃ tayā kriyate dhenvā yā na dogdhrī na garbhiṇī |
ko'rthaḥ putreṇa jātena yo na vidvānna bhaktimāna||9||
saṃsāratāpadagdhānāṃ trayo viśrāntihetavaḥ |
apatyaṃ ca kalatraṃ ca satāṃ saṅgatireva ca ||4.10||
sakṛjjalpanti rājānaḥ sakṛjjalpanti paṇḍitāḥ |
sakṛtkanyāḥ pradīyante trīṇyetāni sakṛtsakṛta||11||
ekākinā tapo dvābhyāṃ paṭhanaṃ gāyanaṃ tribhiḥ |
caturbhirgamanaṃ kṣetraṃ pañcabhirbahubhī raṇaḥ ||12||
sā bhāryā yā śucirdakṣā sā bhāryā yā pativratā |
sā bhāryā yā patiprītā sā bhāryā satyavādinī ||13||
aputrasya gṛhaṃ śūnyaṃ diśaḥ śūnyāstvabāndhavāḥ |
mūrkhasya hṛdayaṃ śūnyaṃ sarvaśūnyā daridratā ||14||
anabhyāse viṣaṃ śāstramajīrṇe bhojanaṃ viṣam |
daridrasya viṣaṃ goṣṭhī vṛddhasya taruṇī viṣam ||15||
tyajeddharmaṃ dayāhīnaṃ vidyāhīnaṃ guruṃ tyajeta|
tyajetkrodhamukhīṃ bhāryāṃ niḥsnehānbāndhavāṃstyajeta||16||
adhvā jarā dehavatāṃ parvatānāṃ jalaṃ jarā |
asaṃbhogo jarā strīṇāṃ vākśalyaṃ manaso jarā ||17||
kaḥ kālaḥ kāni mitrāṇi ko deśaḥ kau vyayāgamau |
kaścāhaṃ kā ca me śaktiriti cintyaṃ muhurmuhuḥ ||18||
agnirdevo dvijātīnāṃ munīnāṃ hṛdi daivatam |
pratimāsvalpabuddhīnāṃ sarvatra samadarśinaḥ ||19||
iti cāṇakyanītidarpaṇe caturtho'dhyāyaḥ ||4||
<5>
pañcamo'dhyāyaḥ
gururagnirdvijātīnāṃ varṇānāṃ brāhmaṇo guruḥ |
patireva guruḥ strīṇāṃ sarvasyābhyāgato guruḥ ||1||
yathā caturbhiḥ kanakaṃ parīkṣyate
nigharṣaṇacchedanatāpatāḍanaiḥ |
tathā caturbhiḥ puruṣaḥ parīkṣyate
tyāgena śīlena guṇena karmaṇā ||2||
tāvadbhayeṣu bhetavyaṃ yāvadbhayamanāgatam |
āgataṃ tu bhayaṃ vīkṣya prahartavyamaśaṅkayā ||3||
ekodarasamudbhūtā ekanakṣatrajātakāḥ |
na bhavanti samāḥ śīle yathā badarakaṇṭakāḥ ||4||
niḥspṛho nādhikārī syānnākāmo maṇḍanapriyaḥ |
nāvidagdhaḥ priyaṃ brūyātspaṣṭavaktā na vañcakaḥ ||5||
mūrkhāṇāṃ paṇḍitā dveṣyā adhanānāṃ mahādhanāḥ |
parāṅganā kulastrīṇāṃ subhagānāṃ ca durbhagāḥ ||6||
ālasyopagatā vidyā parahastagataṃ dhanam |
alpabījaṃ hataṃ kṣetraṃ hataṃ sainyamanāyakam ||7||
abhyāsāddhāryate vidyā kulaṃ śīlena dhāryate |
guṇena jñāyate tvāryaḥ kopo netreṇa gamyate ||8||
vittena rakṣyate dharmo vidyā yogena rakṣyate |
mṛdunā rakṣyate bhūpaḥ satstriyā rakṣyate gṛham ||9||
anyathā vedaśāstrāṇi jñānapāṇḍityamanyathā |
anyathā tatpadaṃ śāntaṃ lokāḥ kliśyanti cānyathā ||5.10||
dāridryanāśanaṃ dānaṃ śīlaṃ durgatināśanam |
ajñānanāśinī prajñā bhāvanā bhayanāśinī ||11||
nāsti kāmasamo vyādhirnāsti mohasamo ripuḥ |
nāsti kopasamo vahnirnāsti jñānātparaṃ sukham ||12||
janmamṛtyū hi yātyeko bhunaktyekaḥ śubhāśubham |
narakeṣu patatyeka eko yāti parāṃ gatim ||13||
tṛṇaṃ brahmavidaḥ svargastṛṇaṃ śūrasya jīvitam |
jitāśasya tṛṇaṃ nārī niḥspṛhasya tṛṇaṃ jagata||14||
vidyā mitraṃ pravāseṣu bhāryā mitraṃ gṛheṣu ca |
vyādhitasyauṣadhaṃ mitraṃ dharmo mitraṃ mṛtasya ca ||15||
vṛthā vṛṣṭiḥ samudreṣu vṛthā tṛptasya bhojanam |
vṛthā dānaṃ samarthasya vṛthā dīpo divāpi ca ||16||
nāsti meghasamaṃ toyaṃ nāsti cātmasamaṃ balam |
nāsti cakṣuḥsamaṃ tejo nāsti dhānyasamaṃ priyam ||17||
adhanā dhanamicchanti vācaṃ caiva catuṣpadāḥ |
mānavāḥ svargamicchanti mokṣamicchanti devatāḥ ||18||
satyena dhāryate pṛthvī satyena tapate raviḥ |
satyena vāti vāyuśca sarvaṃ satye pratiṣṭhitam ||19||
calā lakṣmīścalāḥ prāṇāścale jīvitamandire |
calācale ca saṃsāre dharma eko hi niścalaḥ ||5.20||
narāṇāṃ nāpito dhūrtaḥ pakṣiṇāṃ caiva vāyasaḥ |
catuṣpadāṃ śṛgālastu strīṇāṃ dhūrtā ca mālinī ||21||
janitā copanetā ca yastu vidyāṃ prayacchati |
annadātā bhayatrātā pañcaite pitaraḥ smṛtāḥ ||22||
rājapatnī guroḥ patnī mitrapatnī tathaiva ca |
patnīmātā svamātā ca pañcaitā mātaraḥ smṛtāḥ ||23||

ṣaṣṭho'dhyāyaḥ
śrutvā dharmaṃ vijānāti śrutvā tyajati durmatim |
śrutvā jñānamavāpnoti śrutvā mokṣamavāpnuyāta||1||
pakṣiṇaḥ kākaścaṇḍālaḥ paśūnāṃ caiva kukkuraḥ |
munīnāṃ pāpaścaṇḍālaḥ sarvacāṇḍālanindakaḥ ||2||
bhasmanā śuddhyate kāsyaṃ tāmramamlena śuddhyati |
rajasā śuddhyate nārī nadī vegena śuddhyati ||3||
bhramansampūjyate rājā bhramansampūjyate dvijaḥ |
bhramansampūjyate yogī strī bhramantī vinaśyati ||4||
yasyārthāstasya mitrāṇi yasyārthāstasya bāndhavāḥ |
yasyārthāḥ sa pumāṃlloke yasyārthāḥ sa ca paṇḍitaḥ ||5||
tādṛśī jāyate buddhirvyavasāyo'pi tādṛśaḥ |
sahāyāstādṛśā eva yādṛśī bhavitavyatā ||6||
kālaḥ pacati bhūtāni kālaḥ saṃharate prajāḥ |
kālaḥ supteṣu jāgarti kālo hi duratikramaḥ ||7||
na paśyati ca janmāndhaḥ kāmāndho naiva paśyati |
madonmattā na paśyanti arthī doṣaṃ na paśyati ||8||
svayaṃ karma karotyātmā svayaṃ tatphalamaśnute |
svayaṃ bhramati saṃsāre svayaṃ tasmādvimucyate ||9||
rājā rāṣṭrakṛtaṃ pāpaṃ rājñaḥ pāpaṃ purohitaḥ |
bhartā ca strīkṛtaṃ pāpaṃ śiṣyapāpaṃ gurustathā ||6.10||
ṛṇakartā pitā śatrurmātā ca vyabhicāriṇī |
bhāryā rūpavatī śatruḥ putraḥ śatrurapaṇḍitaḥ ||11||
lubdhamarthena gṛhṇīyātstabdhamañjalikarmaṇā |
mūrkhaṃ chando'nuvṛttyā ca yathārthatvena paṇḍitam ||12||
varaṃ na rājyaṃ na kurājarājyaṃ
varaṃ na mitraṃ na kumitramitram |
varaṃ na śiṣyo na kuśiṣyaśiṣyo
varaṃ na dārā na kudāradāraḥ ||13||
kurājarājyena kutaḥ prajāsukhaṃ
kumitramitreṇa kuto'bhinirvṛtiḥ |
kudāradāraiśca kuto gṛhe ratiḥ
kuśiṣyaśiṣyamadhyāpayataḥ kuto yaśaḥ ||14||
siṃhādekaṃ bakādekaṃ śikṣeccatvāri kukkuṭāta|
vāyasātpañca śikṣecca ṣaṭśunastrīṇi gardabhāta||15||
prabhūtaṃ kāryamalpaṃ vā yannaraḥ kartumicchati |
sarvārambheṇa tatkāryaṃ siṃhādekaṃ pracakṣate ||16||
indriyāṇi ca saṃyamya rāgadveṣavivarjitaḥ |
samaduḥkhasukhaḥ śāntaḥ tattvajñaḥ sādhurucyate ||17||
pratyutthānaṃ ca yuddhaṃ ca saṃvibhāgaṃ ca bandhuṣu |
svayamākramya bhuktaṃ ca śikṣeccatvāri kukkuṭāta||18||
gūḍhamaithunacāritvaṃ kāle kāle ca saṃgraham |
apramattamaviśvāsaṃ pañca śikṣecca vāyasāta||19||
bahvāśī svalpasantuṣṭaḥ sanidro laghucetanaḥ |
svāmibhaktaśca śūraśca ṣaḍete śvānato guṇāḥ ||6.20||
suśrānto'pi vahedbhāraṃ śītoṣṇaṃ na ca paśyati |
santuṣṭaścarate nityaṃ trīṇi śikṣecca gardabhāta||21||
ya etānviṃśatiguṇānācariṣyati mānavaḥ |
kāryāvasthāsu sarvāsu ajeyaḥ sa bhaviṣyati ||22||

saptamo'dhyāyaḥ
arthanāśaṃ manastāpaṃ gṛhe duścaritāni ca |
vañcanaṃ cāpamānaṃ ca matimānna prakāśayeta||1||
dhanadhānyaprayogeṣu vidyāsaṅgrahaṇeṣu ca |
āhāre vyavahāre ca tyaktalajjaḥ sukhī bhaveta||2||
santoṣāmṛtatṛptānāṃ yatsukhaṃ śāntireva ca |

pañcadaśo'dhyāyaḥ
