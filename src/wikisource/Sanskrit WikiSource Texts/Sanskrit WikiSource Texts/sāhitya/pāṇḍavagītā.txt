Text:

pāṇḍava
prahlādanāradaparāśarapuṇḍarīkavyāsāmbarīśaśukaśaunakabhīṣmadālbhyān|
rukmāṅgadārjunavasiṣṭhavibhīṣaṇādīn puṇyānimānparamabhāgavatānsmarāmi||1||
lomaharṣaṇa
dharmo vivirdhati yudhiṣṭhirakīrtanena pāpaṃ praṇaśyati vṛkodarakīrtanena|
śatrurvinaśyati dhanañjayakīrtanena mādrīsutau kathayatāṃ na bhavanti rogāḥ||2||
brahmā
ye mānavā vigatarāgaparāvarajñā nārāyaṇaṃ suraguruṃ satataṃ smaranti|
dhyānena tena hatakilbiṣa cetanāste mātuḥ payodhararasaṃ na punaḥ pibanti||3||
yudhiṣṭhira
meghaśyāmaṃ pītakauśeyavāsaṃ śrīvatsāṅkaṃ kaustubhodbhāsitāṅgam|
puṇyopetaṃ puṇḍarīkāyatākṣaṃ viṣṇuṃ vande sarvalokaikanātham||4||
kuntī
svakarmaphalanirdiṣṭāṃ yāṃ yāṃ yoniṃ vrajāmyaham|
tasyāṃ tasyāṃ hṛṣīkeśa tvayi bhaktidṛrḍhā'stu me||5||
drupada
kīṭeṣu pakṣiṣu mṛgeṣu sarīsṛpeṣu rakṣaḥpiśācamanujeṣvapi yatra yatra|
jātasya me bhavatu keśava tvatprasādāt tvayyeva bhaktiracalā'vyabhicāriṇī me||6||
abhimanyu
govinda govinda hare murāre govinda govinda rathāṅgapāṇe|
govinda govinda mukunda kṛṣṇa govinda govinda namo namaste||7||
uddhava
vāsudevaṃ parityajya yo'nyaṃ devamupāsate|
tṛṣito jāhnavītīre kūpaṃ vāñchati durbhagaḥ||8||
vidura
vāsudevasya ye bhaktāḥ śāntāstadgatamānasāḥ|
teṣāṃ dāsasya dāso'haṃ bhave janmani janmani||9||
bhīṣma
viparīteṣu kāleṣu parikṣīṇeṣu bandhuṣu|
trāhi māṃ kṛpayā kṛṣṇa śaraṇāgatavatsala||10||
droṇa
ye ye hatāścakradhareṇa rājaṃ strailokyanāthana janārdanena|
te te gatā viṣṇupuriṃ prayātāḥ krodho'pi devasya vareṇa tulyaḥ||11||
gāndhārī
tvameva mātā ca pitā tvameva tvameva bandhuśca sakhā tvameva|
tvameva vidyā draviṇaṃ tvameva tvameva sarvaṃ mama deva deva||12||
prahlāda
nātha yonisahasreṣu yeṣu yeṣu vrajāmyaham|
teṣu teṣvacalā bhaktiracyuta'su sadā tvayi||13||
yā prītiravivekanāṃ viṣayeṣvanapāyini|
tvāmanusmarataḥ sā me hṛdayānmā'pasarpatu||14||
bhāradvāja
lābhasteṣāṃ jayasteṣāṃ kutasteṣāṃ parājayaḥ|
yeṣāmindīśvaraśyāmo hṛdayastho janārdanaḥ||15||
atri
govindeti sadā snānaṃ govindeti sadā japaḥ|
govindeti sadā dhyānaṃ sadā govindakīrtanam||16||
āvirhotra
kṛṣṇa tvadīyapadapaṅkajapañjarānte adyaiva me viśatu mānasarājahaṃsaḥ|
prāṇaprayāṇasamaye kaphavātapitāiḥ kaṇṭhāvarodhanavidhau smaraṇaṃ kutaste||17||
vidura
hararnāmaiva nāmaiva nāmāmaiva mama jīvanam|
kalau nāsyeva nāsteva nāstyeva gatiranyathā||18||
vaśiṣṭha
kṛṣṇeti maṅgalaṃ nāma yasya vāci pravartate|
bhasmībhavantu tasyāśu mahāpātakakoṭayaḥ||19||
duryodhana
jānāmi dharmaṃ na ca me pravṛttirjānāmyadharmaṃ na ca me nivṛttiḥ|
kenāpi devena hṛdisthitena yathā niyukto'smi tathā karomi||20||
subhadra
eko'pi kṛṣṇasya kṛtaḥ praṇāmo daśāśvamedhāvabhṛthena tulyaḥ|
daśāśvamedhī punareti janma kṛṣṇapraṇāmī na punarbhavāya||21||
garga
nārāyaṇeti mantro'sti vāgasti vaśavartini|
tathāpi narake ghore patantītyetadadbhutam||22||
parāśara
sakṛduccaritaṃ yena harirityakṣaradvayam|
baddhaḥ parikarastena mokṣāya gamanaṃ prati||23||
vyāsa
satyaṃ satyaṃ punaḥ satyaṃ bhujamutthāpya cocyate|
na vedācca paraṃ śāstraṃ na devaḥ keśavātparaḥ||24||
dhanvantari
acyutānanta govinda nāmoccāraṇabheṣajāt|
naśyanti sakalā rogāḥ satyaṃ satyaṃ vadāmyaham||25||
mārkaṇḍeya
sā hānistanmahacchidraṃ sā cāndhajaḍamūḍhatā|
yanmuhūrtaṃ kṣaṇaṃ vāpi vāsudevaṃ na cintayet||26||
śrīśuka
āloḍya sarvaśāstrāṇi vicāryaivaṃ punaḥ punaḥ|
idamekaṃ suniṣpannaṃ dhyeyo nārāyaṇaḥ sadā||27||
śrīmahādeva
śarīra ca navacchidraṃ vyādhigrastaṃ kalevaram
auṣadhaṃ jāhnavītoyaṃ vaidyo nārāyaṇo hariḥ||28||
śaunaka
bhojanācchādane cintāṃ vṛthā kurvanti vaiṣṇavāḥ|
yo'sau viśvambharo devaḥ sa bhaktānkimupekṣate||29||
ākāśātpatitaṃ toyaṃ yathā gacchati sāgaram|
sarvadevanamaskāraḥ keśavaṃ prati gacchati||30||
