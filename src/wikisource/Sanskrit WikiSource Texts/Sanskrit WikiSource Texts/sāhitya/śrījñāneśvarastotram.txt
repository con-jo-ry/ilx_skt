Text:

sadguruṃ saccidānandaṃ kevalaṃ karuṇākaram |
jñānayogeśvaraṃ vande jñānadevaṃ punaḥ punaḥ || 1 ||
vāṅmanobuddhibhāvānāmatītaṃ parameśvaram |
niṣkalaṃ saguṇaṃ vande jñānadevaṃ punaḥ punaḥ || 2 ||
nirmalaṃ sadayaṃ śāṃtaṃ praṇatākhilamaṅlam |
jñānaikarūpiṇaṃ vande jñānadevaṃ punaḥ punaḥ || 3 ||
bhaktikalpatarormūlaṃ bījaṃ sādhanasampadaḥ |
sarvalokahitaṃ vande jñānadevaṃ punaḥ punaḥ || 4 ||
āvirbhūto hariḥ sākṣāt pāvane gautamītaṭe |
rupeṇa yasya taṃ vande jñānadevaṃ punaḥ punaḥ || 5 ||
nivṛttimārgiṇo muktiḥ sopānasulabhā kṛtā |
kṛpayā yasya taṃ vande jñānadevaṃ punaḥ punaḥ || 6 ||
jñānaṃ bhaktirasaṃ yasya yadbhaktirjñānabhāsvatī |
tamadvaitaparaṃ vande jñānadevaṃ punaḥ punaḥ || 7 ||
dṛṣṭiḥ kṛpāvatī yasya nityaṃ pīyūṣavarṣiṇī
natāśrayāṃghriṃ taṃ vande jñānadevaṃ punaḥ punaḥ || 8 ||
sudhāvijayinī tāpaśamanī lokapāvanī
bhāratī yasya taṃ vande jñānadevaṃ punaḥ punaḥ || 9 ||
bhāvārthadīpikā yasya anubhavāmṛtajīvanī
taṃ buddhibhāskaraṃ vande jñānadevaṃ punaḥ punaḥ || 10 ||
śaradindumanojñāṅgaṃ kamalāyatalocanam |
padmāsanasthitaṃ vande jñānadevaṃ punaḥ punaḥ || 11 ||
prasannaṃ paramodāraṃ varadaṃ susmitānanam |
ālandivallabhaṃ vande jñānadevaṃ punaḥ punaḥ || 12 ||
suhṛdaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ mātaraṃ pitaraṃ prabhūm |
premalaṃ vatsalaṃ vande jñānadevaṃ punaḥ punaḥ || 13 ||
buddhinadyāḥ śaratkālaṃ pragrahaṃ cittavājinaḥ
pāpatāpaharaṃ vande jñānadevaṃ punaḥ punaḥ || 14 ||
he tāta ! bhavataivoktaṃ śiśormātā svayaṃ hitā |
cittavṛtteḥ samādhānaṃ buddheḥ sthairyaṃ sadāsstu me || 15 ||
