Text:

mukhyena sampradāyitvaṃ sampradāyavidāṃ naye ।
sampradāyigurordīkṣāmantragrahaṇato bhaveta॥1॥

śiṣṭaparamparācāryopadiṣṭamantrā eva hi ।
sampradāya iti khyātaḥ sudhībhiḥ sampradāyibhiḥ ॥2॥

śiṣṭatvaṃ nāma cāmnāyaprāmāṇyābhyupagantṛtā ।
vedānāṃ viṣṇupāramyātśiṣṭo vaiṣṇava ucyate ॥3॥

atatparasparatvena vaiṣṇavatvaṃ na siddhyati ।
avaiṣṇavopadiṣṭenetyādi śāstraprakopaṇāta॥4॥

tasmātśiṣṭānuśiṣṭānāṃ paramparāṃ rirakṣiṣuḥ ।
svaniḥśvasitavedo'pi gauro mādhvamataṃ gataḥ ॥5॥

sarvajagadguruḥ śrīmadgaurāṅgo lokaśikṣayā ।
purīśvaraṃ guruṃ kṛtvā svīcakre sampradāyakam ॥6॥

kañcinmataviśeṣo'pi nirastastattvavādinām ।
śrīmadgaurāṅgadevena sampradāyasya tena kim ॥7॥

sampradāyaikadīkṣāṇāṃ mithaḥ kiñcinmatāntarāta।
śākhābhedo bhavenmātraṃ sampradāyo na bhidyate ॥8॥

rāmānandī yathā rāmānujīyāntargato bhaveta।
nimbārkasampradāye ca harivyāsādayo yathā ॥9॥

gauḍīyastattvavādī ca tathā mādhvamataṃ gatau ।
na hyatra bādhakaḥ kaścitdṛśyate tattvavittamaiḥ ॥10॥

tuṣyatviti matenāpi sampradāyaviniścaye ।
svīkṛtaṃ sādhakatvena cetsādhyādivivecanam ।
tathāpyatyantabhedo na śrīgauramādhvayormate ॥11॥

madhvamate ca yā muktiḥ sādhyatvena prakīrtitā ।
viṣṇvaṅghriprāptirūpā sā bhāṣyakṛdbhiḥ pradarśitā ॥12॥

sādhanaṃ cārpitaṃ karmajīvādhikārabhedataḥ ।
svīkṛtamapi madhvena bhakteḥ śraiṣṭhyaṃ bahustutam ॥13॥

pramāṇaṃ bhārataṃ mātraṃ madhvamate'nṛtaṃ vacaḥ ।
yattena trividhaṃ proktaṃ mukhyaṃ śabdapramāṇakam ॥14॥

śrīmannartakagopālasevā yena pratiṣṭhitā ।
iṣṭatvena kathaṃ tasya nirṇīto dvārakāpatiḥ ॥15॥

niścito dvārkādhīśo yadyapi vā kṣatiḥ kutaḥ ।
yo nandanandanaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ sa eva dvārakāpatiḥ ।
svarūpayordvayo aikyaṃ kṛṣṇatvamaviśeṣaḥ ॥16॥

līlābhimānabhedena pūrṇatamaśca pūrṇakaḥ ।
na tu svarūpato bhedastayorasti kathañcana ॥17॥

bhedābhedamataṃ yaccācintyākhyaṃ kīrtyate budhaiḥ ।
śrīcaitanyamatābhijñaiḥ tacca madhvamateṅgitam ॥18॥

jīvānāṃ brahmavaijātye guṇāṃśatvādabhinnatā ।
pratiyogitvabhedatve cinmātratvāttadekatā ॥19॥

tadvyāpyatvatadāyattavṛttikatvādihetutaḥ ।
sāmānādhikaraṇyaṃ ca gosvāmimadhvayoḥ samam ॥20॥

vicāramātranaipuṇyaṃ śaktiśaktimatoriha ।
gaurakṛpodbhavo'cintyavādo gosvāmibhiḥ smṛtaḥ ।
tattvanirdhāraṇe mukhyaḥ kāraṇavāda ucyate ॥21॥

parākhyaśaktimadbrahma nimittakāraṇaṃ bhaveta।
upādānaṃ tu tadbrahma jīvapradhānaśaktiyuka।
iti kāraṇavāde'pi hyubhayormatayoḥ samam ॥22॥

śrīgovindābhidhaṃ bhāṣyaṃ pramāṇaṃ yadi manyate ।
prameyaratnasiddhāntaniṣkṛṣṭā tatsamāhṛtiḥ ॥23॥

vakti śrīgaurasammatiṃ madhvaḥ prāhetyupakrame ।
yadi vopekṣate kaiścittarhyardhakukkuṭinayaḥ ॥24॥

iti śrīśrīgauḍīyavaiṣṇavasāhitye śrīharidāsadāsamahodayena dhṛtam ॥
