Text:

śrī yādagiri lakṣmīnṛsiṃha suprabhātam
śrī vaṅgīpuram narasiṃhācāryaracita ।
kausalyā suprajā rāma pūrvā saṃdhyā pravartate ।
uttiṣṭha naraśārdūla! kartavyaṃ daivamāhnikam ॥ 1॥

uttiṣṭhottiṣṭha goviṃda uttiṣṭha garuḍadhvaja ।
uttiṣṭha kamalākānta trailokyaṃ maṅgalaṃ kuru ॥ 2॥

yādādrināthaśubhamandirakalpavalli
padmālaye janani padmabhavādivandye ।
bhaktārtibhañjani dayāmayadivyarūpe
lakṣmīnṛsiṃhadayite tava suprabhātam ॥ 3॥

jvālānṛsiṃha karuṇāmaya divyamūrte
yogābhinandana nṛsiṃha dayāsamudra! ।
lakṣmīnṛsiṃha śaraṇāgatapārijāta
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 4॥

śrīraṅgaveṅkaṭamahīdharahastiśaila
śrī yādavādrimukhasattvaniketanāni ।
sthānāni tekila vadanti parāvarajñāḥ
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 5॥

brahmādayassuravara munipuṅgavāśca
tvāṃ sevituṃ vividhamaṅgalavastuhastāḥ ।
dvāre vasanti narasiṃha bhavābdhipota
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 6॥

prahlāda nārada parāśara puṇḍarīka
vyāsādibhaktarasikā bhavadīyasevām ।
vāñchantyananyahṛdayā karuṇāsamudra
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 7॥

tvaddāsyabhogarasikāśśaṭhajinmukhāryāḥ
rāmānujādimahanīyagurupradhānāḥ ।
sevārtha matra bhavadīyagṛhāṅgaṇasthāḥ
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 8॥

bhaktā stvadīyapadapaṅkajasaktacittāḥ
kālyaṃ vidhāya tavakandara mandirāgre ।
tvaddarśanotsukatayā nibiḍaṃ śrayante
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 9॥

divyāvatāradaśake narasiṃha te tu
divyāvatāramahimā nahi devagamyaḥ ।
prahlādadānavaśiśoḥkila bhaktigamyaḥ
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 10॥

śrīyādavādriśikhare tvamahobile'pi
siṃhācale ca śubhamaṅgalaśailarāje ।
vedācalādi giri mūrdhasu susthitosi
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 11॥

kāmyārthino varadakalpaka kalpakaṃ tvāṃ
sevārthinaḥ ssujanasevyapadadvayaṃ tvām ।
bhaktyā vinamra śirasā praṇamanti sarve
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 12॥

tvannāmamantrapaṭhanena luṭhanti pāpāḥ
tvannāmamantrapaṭhanena luṭhanti daityāḥ ।
tvannāmamantrapaṭhanena luṭhantirogāḥ
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 13॥

lakṣmīnṛsiṃha! jagadīśa! sureśa! viṣṇo
jiṣṇo! janārdana! parātpara viśvarūpa ।
viśvaprabhātakaraṇāya kṛtāvatāra
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 14॥

tvannāmamantra paṭhana।mkilasuprabhāta।m
asmākamastu tavacāstu ca suprabhātam ।
asmatsamuddharaṇameva vicitragādha
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 15॥

tvat pūjakā parivruḍhā paricārakāśca
nityārcanāya vidhivadvihita svakrutyāḥ ।
yattastvadīya śubhagahvara mandirāgre
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 16॥

prābhātakīmupacitim parikalpayantaḥ
kuṇḍāśca pūrṇajalakumbhamupāharantaḥ ।
śrīvaiṣṇavāḥ samupayāntihare! nṛsiṃha
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 17॥

sūryo'bhyudeti vikasanti saroruhāṇi
nīlotpalāni hi bhavanti nimīlitāni ।
prāgdiṅmukheruṇagabhastigaṇo'bhyudeti
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 18॥

mandānilassura nadīkamalodareśu
mandaṃvigāhya śubhasaurabha mādadhānaḥ ।
harṣaprakarṣamupayāti ca sevituṃ tvāṃ
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 19॥

tārāgaṇoviyati majjati suprabhāte
sūryeṇasākamavalokayituṃ tvadīyam ।
śrīsuprabhātamavabhāsita sarvalokaṃ
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 20॥

pakṣisvanāścaparitaḥparisampatanti
kūjantikokilagaṇāḥkalakaṃṭharāvaiḥ ।
vācā viśuddhakalayānuvadantikīrāḥ
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 21॥

pallīṣu vallavajanāḥsvagṛhāṅgaṇeṣu
dhenūrduhanti vinibhānti viśeṣadṛṣṭyā ।
gopālabāla iva bhaktahṛdambujeṣu
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 22॥

gāḍhāṃdhakārapaṭalam gaganaṃjahāti
mohāndhakāra iva sanmanujam samastam ।
rāgovirāga iva saṃviśati prakāmaṃ
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 23॥

nidrājahāti hi janān sumuniṃ yadhāvat
prajñāpyudeti hi janeṣu munauyadhāvat ।
saktirjaneṣu hi yathā ca munauviraktiḥ
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 24॥

phullānipaṅkajavanāni viśuddhasattva
phullāni sajjanamanaḥkamalāni yadvat ।
bhāśśuddhasatvamiva bhāti vidikṣu dikṣu
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 25॥

brahmāsvayaṃsuragaṇaissahalokapālaiḥ
dhāmapraviśya tava maṇḍapagopurāḍhyam ।
pañcāṅgaśuddhimabhivarṇayati tvadīyāṃ
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 26॥

vikhyātavaidyajana vañcakarogajāla
vikhyātavaidya iti roganipīḍitāstvām ।
niścityadhāma tava dūrata āpatanti
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 27॥

bhūtagrahādi balavattara tāpayuktāḥ
bāṇāvatīmukha mahograpiśāca viddhāḥ ।
snātāḥpradakṣiṇavidhā vupayāntinātha
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 28॥

santānahīna vanitāssarasi tvadīye
snātvājalārdravasanāstava darśanāya ।
āyāntisantativaraprada devadeva
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 29॥

yadduṣṭa saṃharaṇamuttamalokarakṣā
dīkṣāṃ vyanakti tavarūpa mahonṛsiṃha ।
taccātra yādagirimūrthanisaṃvibhāti
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 30॥

prahlāda puṇyajani puṇyabalātpratītaṃ
rūpaṃ janastavahare nigamāntavedyam ।
prātaḥsmaraṃstarati saṃsmāraṇāmburāśiṃ
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 31॥

śrīsuprabhātamidamacyutakaitavokta
mapyacyutaṃ bhavatubhaktajanaikavedyam ।
lakṣmīnṛsiṃha tava nāmaśubhaprabhāvāt
yādādrinātha nṛhare! tava suprabhātam ॥ 32॥

itthaṃ yādādrināthasya suprabhāta matandritāḥ ।
ye paṭhanti sadā bhaktyā te narāssukhabhāginaḥ ॥ 33॥

iti śrī lakṣmī narasiṃha suprabhātam ।
