āryāpañcadaśīstotram ||
karakalitacāpabāṇāṃ kalhārāṅghriṃ namāmi kalyāṇīm |
kaṃdarpadarpajananīṃ kaluṣaharāṃ kāmitārthaphaladātrīm || 1 ||
eṣā stotum vāṇī naiva samarthā taveśi mahimānam |
śeṣo'pyabdasahasraiḥ śeṣaṃ kṛtavān maheśi tava caritam || 2 ||
īśitvādisupūjyāmindindirakeśābhārasūllasitām |
indīvaradalanayanāmīpsitadātrīṃ namāmi śarvāṇīm || 3 ||
lasadaruṇabhānukoṭidyutinidhimambāṃ surendralakṣyapadām |
lalitāṃ namāmi bāle lalitaśivahṛdayakamalakalahaṃsīm || 4 ||
hrīṃkārabījarūpe himagirikanye harīndrabhavavandye |
himakaradhavalacchatre hitāya bhava naḥ sadā mahārājñi || 5 ||
harṣotkarajanayitrī hasitajyotsnā taveyamanavadye |
haragalahālāhalamapi harati trailokyamohatimiraṃ te || 6 ||
sakalamanorathadāne satyapi caraṇe natasya tava nipuṇe |
saṃsevyate surataruḥ sadājñalokairnu kṛcchraphaladātā || 7 ||
kanakaruce caṭulagate kaṭhinastanabhāranamrakṛśamadhye |
kānte kaṅkaṇahaste kambugrīve namo'stu te karuṇe || 8 ||
haranayanānandakare harāṅkasaṃsthe haripramukhavandye |
haranaṭanasākṣibhūte harārdhadehe namo'stu te sukṛpe || 9 ||
lakṣmīpradakaruṇā yā lakṣmīpatimalpamamba kartumalam |
lakṣyam kuru māṃ tasyā lāvaṇyāmṛtataraṅgamāle tvam || 10 ||
hrīṃkāraratnagarbhe hemācalamandarastanollasite |
herambapriyajanani he vasudhe dehi me kṣamāṃ nityam || 11 ||
satsaṃpradāyavidite sakalāgamanigamasāratattvamayi |
sāvitryarpaya vadane sakalarasāśrayasuvāksudhādhārām || 12 ||
karakaṅkaṇamaṇidinamaṇikaravikasitacaraṇakamalamakarandam |
karuṇāpayonidhe me kāmākṣi svāntaṣaṭpadaḥ pibatu || 13 ||
lasadikṣucāpasumaśaralakṣitadorvallivīryamabhayena |
lakṣādhikadaityakulaṃ lavupaṭavāsaṃ kṛtaṃ kathaṃ citram || 14 ||
hrīṃkārakelibhavane himakaramaulyaṅkamañjuparyaṅke |
hṛdayasaroje me vasa hṛdayānandaprabodhaparahaṃsi || 15 ||
āryāpañcadaśīṃ tāmāryāṃ yo bhajati śuddhadhīrnityam |
bhārye lakṣmīvāṇyau paryātāt tasya sādaraṃ bhavataḥ || 16 ||
ityānandanāthapādapadmopajīvinā kāśyapagotrotpannenāndhreṇa
tyāgarājanāmnā viracitamāryāpañcadaśīstotraṃ saṃpūrṇam
