Text:

śrī gaṇeśāya namaḥ ।
caturmukhādisaṃstutaṃ samastasātvatānutam ।
halāyudhādisaṃyutaṃ namāmi rādhikādhipam ॥ 1॥
bakādidaityakālakaṃ sagopagopipālakam ।
manoharāsitālakaṃ namāmi rādhikādhipam ॥ 2॥
surendragarvagañjanaṃ virañcimohabhañjanam ।
vrajāṅganānurañjanaṃ namāmi rādhikādhipam ॥ 3॥
mayūrapicchamaṇḍanaṃ gajendradantakhaṇḍanam ।
nṛśaṃsakaṃsadaṇḍanaṃ namāmi rādhikādhipam ॥ 4॥
pradattavipradārakaṃ sudāmadhāmakārakam ।
suradrumāpahārakaṃ namāmi rādhikādhipam ॥ 5॥
dhanañjayājayāvahaṃ mahācamūkṣayāvaham ।
pitāmahavyathāpahaṃ namāmi rādhikādhipam ॥ 6॥
munīndraśāpakāraṇaṃ yaduprajāpahāraṇam ।
dharābharāvatāraṇaṃ namāmi rādhikādhipam ॥ 7॥
suvṛkṣamūlaśāyinaṃ mṛgārimokṣadāyinam ।
svakīyadhāmamāyinaṃ namāmi rādhikādhipam ॥ 8॥
idaṃ samāhito hitaṃ varāṣṭakaṃ sadā mudā ।
japañjano janurjarādito drutaṃ pramucyate ॥ 9॥
॥ iti śrīparamahaṃsabrahmānandaviracitaṃ śrīkṛṣṇāṣṭakaṃ sampūrṇam ॥
