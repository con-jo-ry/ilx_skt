tāṃ vidyāmiha labhemahi|
yā darśayed jñānasūryam,ajñānatimirāpahā| yā dadyāt śāśvataṃ saukhyaṃ, tāṃ vidyāmiha labhemahi||1||
dainyaṃ duḥkhaṃ lopayed yā, pāpaṃ tāpaṃ ca vārayet| cittaṃ śāntaṃ ca yā kuryāt ,tāṃ vidyāmiha labhemahi||2||
mokṣakāraṇabhūtā yā, nānātve caikyadarśinī| antaḥ prasannatāṃ dadyāt, tāṃ vidyāmiha labhemahi||3||
ahaṃbhāvaṃ lopayed yā,hitaṃ viśvasya sādhayet| anāthāya karaṃ dadyāt, tāṃ vidyāmiha labhemahi||4||
dharmādipuruṣārthānāṃ yatra sārthakatā bhavet| sārthā sākṣaratā yatra, tāṃ vidyāmiha labhemahi||5||
ānandajā, tathā viśvaṃ yānandena prapūrayet| ānande yā bhavellīnā tāṃ vidyāmiha labhemahi||6||
