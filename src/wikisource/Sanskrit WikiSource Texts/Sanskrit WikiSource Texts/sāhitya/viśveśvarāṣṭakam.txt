Text:

॥ viśveśvarāṣṭakam ॥ 
gaṅgātaraṅgakamanīyajaṭākalāpaṃ gauranirantaravibhūṣitavāmabhāgam ।
nārāyaṇapriya manaṅgamadāpahāraṃ vārāṇasīpurapatiṃ bhaja viśvanātham ॥ 1
vācāmagocaramameyaguṇakharūpaṃ vāgīśaviṣṇusurasektipādapīṭham ।
vāmena vigrahabhareṇa kalatravantaṃ vārāṇasī ...॥ 2
rāgādidoṣarahitaṃ suguṇānurāgaṃ vairāgyaśāntinilayaṃ girijāsahāyam ।
mādhuryadhairyanilayaṃ garalābhirāmaṃ vārāṇasī ...॥ 3
tejomayaṃ sakalaniṣkalamadvitīyamānandakandamaparājitamaprameyam ।
nānātmakaṃ saguṇanirguṇamādidevaṃ vārāṇasī...॥ 4
bhūtādhipaṃ bhujagapuṅgavabhūṣitāṅgaṃ vyāghrājināmbaradharaṃ jaṭilaṃ trinetram ।
pāśāṅkuśābhayavarapradaśūlapāṇiṃ vārāṇasī ...॥ 5
āśāṃ vihāya parihṛtya parasvanindāṃ pāpe ratiñca vinivārya manassamādhau ।
ādhārahṛtkamalamadhyagataṃ pareśaṃ vārāṇasī ... ॥ 6
śītāṃśuśobhitakirīṭavirājamānaṃ phālekṣaṇānalavināśitapañcabāṇam ।
nāgādhipāracitabhāsurakarṇapūraṃ vārāṇasī ... ॥ 7
paścānanaṃ duritamattamataṅgajānāṃ nāgāntakaṃ danujapuṅgavapannagānām ।
dāvānalaṃ maraṇaśokabhayāṭavīnāṃ vārāṇasī ...॥ 8
vārāṇasīpurapateḥ parameśvarasya vyāghroktamaṣṭakamidaṃ paṭhate manuṣyaḥ ।
vidyāśśriyaṃ vipulasaukhyamanantakīrtiṃ samprāpya dehavilaye labhate ca mokṣam ॥ 9
॥ śrīviśveśvarāṣṭakaṃ sampūrṇam ॥
