Text:

śrī dattātreya stutī
dattātreyaṃ sanātanaṃ brahma nirañjanam |
ādidevaṃ nirākāraṃ vyaktaṃ guṇavivarjitam || 1 ||
cinmayaṃ vyāpitaṃ sarvaṃ cidākāśaṃ digambaram |
nirvikalpaṃ nirābhāsaṃ dṛśyadarśanavarjitam || 2 ||
agocaraṃ nirālambaṃ brahmacārī yatīśvaraḥ |
bargalokanāyakaṃ smpūrṇaṃ paramātmanarakṣakaḥ || 3 ||
āśāpāśavibandhanamuktaḥ śaucāśaucavivarjitayuktaḥ |
śūnyāgāre samarasamajñaḥ śuddhaviśuddhaṃ satatasamajñaḥ || 4 ||
dattātreya nāthottamaṃ sukhadaṃ paramānandasāgaram |
citkīrtibhūṣaṇaṃ vande smartṛgāmī sa mā'vatu || 5 ||
harṣavardhanaṃ vande kauvalyasukhadāyakaṃ |
sakalāgamapūjitaṃ vande smartṛgāmī sa mā'vatu || 6 ||
saṃsāratamanāśanaṃ saṃkalpaduḥkhadalanam |
tāpatrayanivārakaṃ vande smartṛgāmī sa mā'vatu || 7 ||
saṃśayārṇavakhaṇḍanaṃ doṣatrayavibhedinam |
brahmaprakāśātmānaṃ vande smartṛgāmī sa mā'vatu || 8 ||
bhārgavapriyakṛttamaṃ dūratvaparināśanam |
jagadārjavapālanaṃ vande smartṛgāmī sa mā'vatu || 9 ||
namaste kālāgniśamanāya yogijanavallabhāya namo'stu te |
namaste atriputrāya dattātreyāya namo'stu te || 10 ||
namaste līlāviśvambharāya avadhūtāya namo'stu te |
namaste anasūyānandanāya digambarāya namo'stu te || 11 ||
namaste satvasādhyāya satvasākṣiṇe namo'stu te |
namaste guhyatamāya cid vilāsāya namo'stu te || 12 ||
namaste kṣetrādhārāya kṣetraśūnyāya namo'stu te |
namaste rupakāraṇāya gaganākṛtaye namo'stu te || 13 ||
śrī dattātreya pāhi māṃ prasīda digambara |
kṣamasva avadhūta rakṣa rakṣa śrīguro || 14 ||
