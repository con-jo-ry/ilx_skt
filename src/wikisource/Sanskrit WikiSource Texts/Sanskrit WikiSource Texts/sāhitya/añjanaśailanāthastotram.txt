|| añjanaśailanāthastotram ||
pulakini bhujamadhye pūjayantaṃ purandhrīṃ
bhuvananayanapuṇyaṃ pūritāśeṣakāmam |
punarapi vṛṣaśaile phullanīlotpalābhaṃ
puruṣamanubhaveyaṃ puṇḍarīkāyatākṣam || 1||
ājānasauhṛdamapārakṛpāmṛtābdhiṃ
avyājavatsalamavelasuśīlamādyam |
ānandarāśimanurāgamayāvarodhaṃ
ārādhayāmi harimañjanaśailanātham || 2||
ātāmrapādamavadātasuvarṇacelaṃ
āpīnabāhuśikharojjvalaśaṅkhacakram |
āvissmitānanamamandadayākaṭākṣaṃ
ārādhayāmi harimañjanaśailanātham || 3||
aprākṛtāvayavasaṃhitasaṃniveśaṃ
ārūḍhayauvanamahīnakumārabhāvam |
amlānakāntimativāṅmanasānubhāvaṃ
ārādhayāmi harimañjanaśailanātham || 4||
āśāvakāśasamuditvarasarvagandhaṃ
āsvādayatsugamasarvarasasvabhāvam |
āśleṣagamyasukhasaṃspṛśanātirekaṃ
ārādhayāmi harimañjanaśailanātham || 5||
ādarśayantamatisaṃkucitākṣiśaktiṃ
āśrāvayantamakhilān badhirān prakṛtyā |
ābhāṣayantamabhito natamūkavargaṃ
ārādhayāmi harimañjanaśailanātham || 6||
ādhāvayantamatimārutameva paṅgūn
ājānulambibhujayantamaho kubāhūn |
anyāṃśca kṛtaśirasaḥ pratijīvayantaṃ
ārādhayāmi harimañjanaśailanātham || 7||
ājanmanirdhanajanānalakeśayantaṃ
ajñānapi tridaśadeśikadeśyayantam |
ahnāya vandhyamapi martyamavandhyayantaṃ
ārādhayāmi harimañjanaśailanātham || 8||
ābaddhakaṅkaṇamaśeṣaśaraṇyatāyāṃ
āpatsahāyamaparādhasahaṃ natānām |
āsannasāmagasukhālasasūrivargaṃ
ārādhayāmi harimañjanaśailanātham || 9||
adhyāsitāsanasarojasanūpurāṅ ghriṃ
āmuktavīrakaṭakāyatavṛttajaṅgham |
āścaryajānuyugamapratimorukāṇḍaṃ
ārādhayāmi harimañjanaśailanātham || 10||
āvartinimnanikhilāṇḍanidānanābhiṃ
āyāmidorvivarakeligṛhāvarodham |
ābaddharatnamayabhūṣabhujācatuṣkaṃ
ārādhayāmi harimañjanaśailanātham || 11||
aṃsāvalambimaṇikuṇḍalakāntagaṇḍaṃ
āvissmitāṃśumadhurādharabandhujīvam |
āsyābjasaurabhasamutsukadīrghanāsaṃ
ārādhayāmi harimañjanaśailanātham || 12||
ardhendubhāsvadalikollasadūrdhvapuṇḍraṃ
ālolanīlakuṭilālakacāruvaktram |
āvirmayūkhamaṇicūḍamahākirīṭaṃ
ārādhayāmi harimañjanaśailanātham || 13||
vādibhītikarāryeṇa racitā bhāvabandha taḥ
śobhate veṅkaṭādrīśaviṣayā stutiradbhutā ||
|| iti añjanaśailanāthastotraṃ saṃpūrṇam ||
