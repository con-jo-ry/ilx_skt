śrīviṣṇoḥ ṣoḍaśanāmastotram |
śrīgaṇeśāya namaḥ||
auṣadhe cintayedviṣṇuṃ bhojane ca janārdanam |
śayane padmanābhaṃ ca vivāhe ca prajāpatim ||1||
yuddhe cakradharaṃ devaṃ pravāse ca trivikramam |
nārāyaṇaṃ tanutyāge śrīdharaṃ priyasaṃgame ||2||
duḥsvapne smara govindaṃ saṃkaṭe madhusudanam |
kānane nārasiṃhaṃ ca pāvake jalaśāyinam ||3||
jalamadhye varāhaṃ ca parvate raghunandanam |
gamane vāmanaṃ caiva sarvakāryeṣu mādhavam ||4||
ṣoḍaśaitāni nāmāni prātarutthāya yaḥ paṭhet |
sarvapāpavinirmukto viṣṇuloke mahīyate ||5||
iti śrīviṣṇoḥ ṣoḍaśanāmastotraṃ saṃpūrṇam||
