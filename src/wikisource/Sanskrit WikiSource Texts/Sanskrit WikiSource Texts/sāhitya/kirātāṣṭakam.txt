Text:

pratyarthi vrātavakṣaḥsthalarudhirasurāpānamattampṛṣatkaṃ
cāpe sandhāya tiṣṭhan hṛdayasarasije
māmake tāpahantā |
piñchottaṃsaḥ śaraṇyaḥ paśupatitanayo
sāvadhānaḥ sadā naḥ || 1 ||
ākheṭāya vanecarasya girijāsaktasya śambhoḥsutaḥ trātuṃ yo bhuvanaṃ purā samajani khyātaḥ kirātākṛtiḥ |
kodaṇḍaḥ churikādharo ghanaruciḥ piñchāvataṃsojvalaḥ sa tvaṃ māmava sarvadā ripugaṇa trastaṃ dayāvāridhe || 2 ||
yo māṃ pīḍayati prasahya satataṃ
dehī tvadekāśrayaṃ
bhitvā tasya riporuraḥ churikayā
śātāgrayā durmateḥ |
deva tvatkarapaṅkajolasitayā
śrīman kirātākṛte
tatprāṇān vitarāntakāya bhagavan
kālāriputrāñjasā || 3 ||
viddho marmamasu durvacobhirasatāṃ
santaptaśalyopamaiḥ
dṛptānāṃ dviṣatāmaśāntamanasāṃ
khinno'smi yāvad bhṛśaṃ
tāvat tvaṃ churikāśarāsanadharaḥ
citte mamāvirbhavan
svāmin deva kirātarūpa śamaya
pratyarthivargaṃ kṣaṇāt || 4 ||
hartuṃ vittadharmato mama ratāḥchorāśca ye durjanāḥ
teṣāṃ marmasu tāḍayāśu viśikhaistvatkārmukānniḥsṛtaiḥ |
śāstāraṃ dviṣatāṃ kirātavapuṣaṃ sarvārthadaṃ tvāmṛte
paśyāmyatra purāriputra śaraṇaṃ nānyaṃ prapanno'smyaham || 5 ||
yakṣapretapiśācabhūtanivahāḥ duḥkhapradā bhīṣaṇāḥ bādhānte naraśoṇitotsukadhiyo ye tāṃ ripupreritāḥ |
cāpajyāninadaistvamīśa sakalān saṃhṛtya duṣṭagrahān gaurīśātmaja daivateśvara kirātākāra saṃrakṣa mām || 6 ||
drogdhuṃ ye niratāstvadīyapadapadmaikāntabhaktāya me māyāḥchannakalebarāśca viṣadā nādyaiḥsudā karmabhiḥ |
vaśyastambhanamāraṇādi kuśalaprārambha dakṣānarīn duṣṭānsaṃhara devadeva śabarākāra trilokeśvara || 7 ||
tanvā vā manasā girā'pi satataṃ doṣāṃ cikīrṣantyalaṃ tvatpādapraṇatasya me niraparādhasyāpi ye mānavāḥ |
sarvān sahera tān girīśasuta me tāpatrayaughānapi tvāmekaṃ śabarākṛte bhayaharaṃ nāthaṃ prapanno'smyaham || 8 ||
kṛṣṭo rājabhaṭaiḥ sadā'pi paribhūtoaṃ khalairvairibhiścānyarghoratarairvi pajjalanidhau magno'smi duḥkhāturaḥ hā hā kiṃ karavai vibho śabaraveṣaṃ tvāmabhīṣṭārthadaṃ vande'haṃ paradaivataṃ kuru kṛpāṃ nāthārtha bandho mayi || 9 ||
stotraṃ yaḥ prajapet praśāntakaraṇairnityaṃ kirātāṣṭakaṃ sa kṣipraṃ vaśagān karoti nṛpatīnābaddhavairānapi |
saṃhṛtyātma virodhinaḥ khalajanān duṣṭagrahānapyasau yātyante yamadūtabhītirahitodivyāṃ gatiṃ śāśvatīm || 10 ||
iti kirātāṣṭakaṃ sampūrṇam ||
