॥ bṛhatstotraratnākaraḥ॥
॥ śrīśivāṣṭottaraśatanāmastotram ॥
śivo maheśvaraśśaṃbhuḥ pinākī śaśiśekharaḥ ।
vāmadevo virūpākṣaḥ kapardī nīlalohitaḥ ॥ 1
śaṅkaraśśūlapāṇiśca khaṭvāṅgī viṣṇuvallabhaḥ ।
śipiviṣṭoṃ'bikānāthaḥ śrīkaṇṭho bhaktavatsalaḥ ॥2
bhavaśśarvastrilokeśaśśitikaṇṭhaśśivāpriyaḥ ।
ugraḥ kapālī kāmārirandhakāsurasūdanaḥ ॥3
gaṅgādharo lalāṭākṣaḥ kālakālaḥ kṛpānidhiḥ।
bhīmaḥ paraśuhastaśca mṛgapāṇirjaṭādharaḥ ॥4
kailāsavāsī kavacī kaṭhorastripurāntakaḥ।
vṛṣāṅko vṛṣabhārūḍho bhasmoddhūlitavigrahaḥ ॥
sāmapriyaskharamayastrayīmūrtiranīśvaraḥ ।
sarvajñaḥ paramātmā ca somasūryāgnilocanaḥ ॥
haviryajñamayassomaḥ pañcavaktrasadāśivaḥ ।
viśveśvaro vīrabhadro gaṇanāthaḥ prajāpatiḥ ॥
hiraṇyaretā durdharṣo girīśo giriśo'naghaḥ ।
bhujaṅgabhūṣaṇo bhargo giridhanvā giripriyaḥ॥
kṛttivāsāḥ purārātirbhagavān pramathādhipaḥ ।
mṛtyuñjayassūkṣmatanurjagadvyāpī jagadguruḥ ॥
vyomakeśo mahāsenajanakaśvāruvikramaḥ ।
rudro bhūtapatiḥ sthāṇurahirbudhnyo digambaraḥ ॥
aṣṭamūrtiranekātmā sāttvikaśśuddhavigrahaḥ ।
śāśvataḥ khaṇḍaparaśurajaḥ pāśavimocakaḥ ।।
mṛḍaḥ paśupatirdevo mahādevo'vyayo hariḥ ।
pūṣadantabhidavyagro dakṣādhvaraharo haraḥ ॥
bhaganetrabhidavyaktassahasrākṣassahasrapāt ।
apavargaprado'nantastārakaḥ parameśvaraḥ ॥
evaṃ śrīśaṃbhudevasya nāmnāmaṣṭottaraṃ śatam ॥
॥śrīśivāṣṭottaraśatanāmastotraṃ samāptam ॥
