Text:

॥ śivaśaṅkarāṣṭakam ॥
atirbhāṣaṇa kaṭubhāṣaṇa yamakiṅkarapaṭalīkṛtatāḍanaparipīḍanamaraṇāgamasamaye। 
umayā saha mama cetasi yamaśāsananivasaṃ śivaśaṅkara śiva śaṅkara hara me hara duritam ॥ 1
asadindriyaviṣayodayasukhasāskṛtasukṛteḥ para dūṣaṇa paritoṣaṇa kṛtapātakavikṛteḥ । 
paramālaya paripālaya paritāpana miti māṃ śivaśaṅkara śivaśaṅkara hara me hara duritam ॥ 2
viṣayābhidha baḍiśāyudha piśitāyutasubhagaṃ masikāyutamatisantatimarubhūmiṣu niratam । 
mṛḍa māmava sugaterava śivayā saha kṛpayā śivaśaṅkara śivaśaṅkara hara me hara duritam ॥ 3
dayitāmava duhitāmava jananīmava janakaṃ mama kalpitamatisantati marubhūmiṣu niratam । 
janitāsukhavanitāsakha vasatiṃ kuru sumatiṃ śivaśaṅkara śivaśaṅkara hara me hara duritam ॥ 4
jananāśana mṛtimocana śivapūjana niraterabhitokṛśamitayedṛśamahamāharamivaham । 
gajakacchapajanitaśramamuralīkurasumatiṃ śivaśaṅkara śivaśaṅkara hara mehara duritam ॥ 5
tvayi tiṣṭhati sakalasthitiparamātmani hṛdaye vasumārgaṇakṛpaṇekṣaṇa manasā śiva vimukham । 
akṛtāhnikamanupoṣaka bhavatādgirisutayā śivaśaṅkara śivaśaṅkara hara me hara duritam ॥ 6
pitarāviti nu kadāviti yutayokṛta hṛdaye śivayoḥ padamabhiyāhyuru paṇasattava sukṛteḥ । 
iti me śiva hṛdayaṃ bhavadāttatarayayā śivaśaṅkara śivaśaṅkara hara me hara duritam ॥ 7
śaraṇāgata bharaṇādṛta karuṇāmṛtajaladhe śaraṇaṃ tava caraṇaṃ śivabhava saṃsṛtivasate । 
vara cinmaya jagadāmayabhiṣagānatamavatāt śivaśaṅkara śivaśaṅkara hara me hara duritam ॥ 8
tripurāntaka tridaśeśvaragaṇanāyaka śiva te śaraṇaṃ bhava kṛtasāgaraphaṇikaṅkaṇacaraṇam । 
vṛṣavāhana viṣadūṣaṇa patitoddhara śaraṇaṃ śivaśaṅkara śivaśaṅkara hara me hara duritam ॥ 9
śaśiśekhara śivadāyaka hariśāyaka girijāpriyanāyaka gajakandhara gajadānavaharaṇa । 
kanakāsanakanakāmbaravināśana śaraṇaṃ śivaśaṅkara śivaśaṅkara hara me hara duritam ॥ 10
vijitendriya vibudhārcita vimalāmbujacaraṇa bhavanāśana bhayanāśana bhajitāṅkitahṛdaya । 
phaṇibhūṣaṇamunipoṣaṇa madanāntaka śaraṇaṃ śivaśaṅkara śivaśaṅkarahara me hara duritam ॥ 11
॥ iti śrī śivaśaṅkarāṣṭakam ॥
