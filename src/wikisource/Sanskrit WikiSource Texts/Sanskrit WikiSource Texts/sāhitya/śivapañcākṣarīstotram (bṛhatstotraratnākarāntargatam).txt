॥ śivapañcākṣarīstotram ॥
oṅkārasañjātasamastavedapurāṇapuṇyāgamapūjitāya ।
coṅkārarūpapriyadarśanāya coṅkārarūpāya namaśśivāya ॥ 1
nānājarāvyādhivināśanāya nāthāyalokāya jagaddhitāya । 
nānākalajñānanidarśanāya tasmai nakārāya ... ॥ 2
mātsaryadoṣāntakasambhavāya mātuḥ piturduḥkhanivāraṇāya । 
maheśvarīsūkṣmavarāya nityaṃ tasmai makārāya ... ॥ 3
śīlavratajñānadṛḍhavratāya śīlāsuvarṇāya samutsukāya ।
śīghrāya nityaṃ surasevitāya tasmai śikārāya ... ॥ 4
vāmārdhavidyutpratimaprabhāya vācā manaḥkarmavimocanāya।
vāgīśvarīsūkṣmavarāya nityaṃ tasmai vakārāya ... ॥ 5
yakṣoragendrādisuravratāya yakṣāṅganājanmavilocanāya ।
yakṣeṣu lokeṣu jagaddhitāya tasmai yakārāya ... ॥ 6
utphullanīlotpalalocanāyai kṛśānucandrārkavilocanāya।
nirīśvarāyai nikhileśvarāya namaviśavāyai ca namaśśivāya ... ॥ 7
॥ iti śivapañcākṣarīstotram ॥
