saṅakṣipyeteti | prārthanāyāṃ liṅ | sarvāvasthāsu grīṣmamadhyandināvasthāsu | mandamandātapaṃ ‘prakāre guṇavacanasya' iti dvirvacanam | aharapi mandātapameva syādityarthaḥ | durlabhaprārthanaṃ durlabhatriyāmāsaṃkṣepādiprārthanam | gāḍhoṣṇābhiḥ, anena divasasya mandātapasya vighāto vivakṣitaḥ | aśaraṇam arakṣakaṃ tvadviyogavyathābhiḥ | anena triyāmāsaṃkṣepapratibandho vivakṣitaḥ ||41||
idānīṃ svaśokāvasthākarṇanena sā duḥkhitā bhaviṣyatītyāśaṅkya tāmāśvāsayati --
na tvātmānaṃ bahu vigaṇayannātmanā nāvalambe
tat kalyāṇi ! tvamapi nitarāṃ mā gamaḥ kātaratvam |
kasyātyantaṃ sukhamupanataṃ duḥkhamekāntato vā
nīcairgacchatyupari ca daśā cakranemikrameṇa|| 42 ||
neti | tuśabdo'vadhāraṇe | bahu vigaṇayan bahu bhadrakaṃ bhaviṣyatīti | ātmanā svayameva | abalambe dhārayāmi | bahu vigaṇayannātmānaṃ svadehaṃ svayameva dhārayāmītyarthaḥ | asya mūlaṃ --
"duṣkaraṃ kurute rāmo hīno yadanayā prabhuḥ |
dhārayatyātmano dehaṃ na śokenāvasīdati"||
iti śrīrāmāyaṇavacanam | tat tasmāt | kalyāṇi ! kalyāṇārthini ! tvamapi kalyāṇāśaṃsinī bhūtvā kātaratvaṃ mā gaccherityarthaḥ | madviśiṣṭasyāpi sarvasya janasya sukhaduḥkhe paryāyeṇa bhavata ityāha -- kasyeti | atyantam atikrāntāvasānaṃ, nityamiti yāvat | ekāntata ekāntam | prathamārthe tasil ||42||
kadā kalyāṇamāvayorbhaviṣyatītyapekṣāyāmāha -- 
śāpānto me bhujagaśayanāda utthite śārṅgapāṇau
śeṣāna māsāna gamaya caturo locane mīlayitvā
paścāda āvāṃ virahaguṇitaṃ taṃ tama ātmābhilāṣaṃ
nirvekṣyāvaḥ pariṇataśaraccandrikāsu kṣapāsu|| 43 ||
śāpānta iti | bhujagaśayanādutyite śārṅgaṣāṇāviti, anena yasmin śaratkāle paramapuruṣaḥ prabudhyate tasmin kāle mama śāpānta ityuktam | tathā kaurme purāṇe -- 
"kṣīrābdhau śeṣaparyaṅka āṣāḍhayāṃ saṃviśedhdariḥ |
nidrāṃ tyajati kārtikyāṃ tayoḥ saṃpūjayedhdarim" ||
iti | māsānanyān gamaya catura iti, āṣāḍhamāsādikānāśvayujamā sāntānityarthaḥ | nanu āṣāḍhasya prathamadivasa ityuktam | āṣāḍhaśrāvaṇau prāvṛd | tataḥ paraṃ śaraditi prasidhdam | tasmāt kathamāṣāḍhāt prabhṛti māsacatuṣṭayāt paraḥ śaratkāla ityuktam | ucyate | ṛtūnāṃ kalpanā dvidhā | kecit ṣaḍṛtava iti vadanti | apare traya iti | atra tāvad ṛtutrayapakṣāśrayeṇoktam | tathā śrīrāmāyaṇe -- 
"catvāro vārṣikāḥ proktā gatā varṣaśatopamāḥ"
iti | locane mīlayitveti vacanaṃ lokokticchāyayoktam | virahagaṇitamiti pāṭhaḥ | virahakāle paścādevamanubhaviṣyāva iti gaṇitaṃ saṅkalpitam | ātmābhilādhaṃ gaṇyitvā hṛdaye nihitamityarthaḥ | nirvekṣyāvo'nubhaviṣyāvaḥ ||43||
sarvamidaṃ vañcakavacanamiva pratibhāti | mayā jñātapūrva kimapilakṣaṇaṃ vadetyāśaṅkayāha -- 
bhūyaścāha tvama api śayane kaṇṭhalagnā purā me
nidrāṃ gatvā kima api rudatī sasvaraṃ viprabuddhā
sāntarhāsaṃ kathitamasakṛt pṛcchtaśca tvayā me
dṛṣṭaḥ svapne kitava ! ramayan kāmapi tvaṃ mayeti||
bhūya iti | bhūyaścāha iti pāṭhaḥ | bhūyaścāha punarapi manmukhenāhetyarthaḥ | manmukhenedamāha ityuktatvāt ! tvamasīti pāṭhaḥ | sasvaraṃ saśabdam | kitava ! dhūrta ! ayamarthaḥ -- nidrāsamaye ruditvā saśabdaṃ prabuddhāsi | kimiti bahuśaḥ pṛcchato me kitava ! svapne tvaṃ kāmapi ramayan mayā dṛṣṭa iti sāntarhāsaṃ purā tvayā kathitamiti te priyatamo bhūyaśca manmukhenedamabhijñānamāheti ||44||
etasmānmāṃ kuśalinamabhijñānadānād viditvā mā kaulīnādasitanayane ! mayyaviśvāsinī bhūḥ |
snehānāhuḥ kimapi virahe hrāsināste hyabhogādiṣṭe vastunyupacitarasāḥ premarāśībhavanti||45||
etasmāditi | abhijñānadānād abhijñānasya vācā pradānāt | kaulīnāllokavādād, etāvatā kālena tava bhartā parāsuḥ syānnocedāgamiṣyatīti janavādādityarthaḥ | asitanayane !, idamapyabhijñānam | kaulīnānmayyaviśvāsinī mā bhūḥ ityarthaḥ | etāvantaṃ kālaṃ virahitayorāvayoḥ snehā vigalantītyāha -- snehānāhuḥ kimapi virahe hrāsina iti | snehān virahe hāsina ityāhuḥ | tat kimapi kiñcidatitucchamityarthaḥ | pratyuta snehā virahe vardhante iti svamatenāha -- te hmabhogāditi | abhogād bhogālābhāt | vastuni prayojanādiviṣaye | upacitarasā upacitaśrṛṅgārādirasāḥ | te snehāḥ iṣṭe vastuni bhogālābhādeva upacitarasāḥ premarāśībhavantītyarthaḥ | nanu ‘premā nā priyatā hārdam' ityamarasiṃhavacanāt premasnehayoraikyaṃ pratīyate | kathaṃ tayorbhedavacanamiti | ucyate | snehādanyat prema | yathāhuralaṅkāravidaḥ -- 
"avalokanābhilāṣau rāgasnehau tataḥ prema |
ratiśrṛṅgārau yoge viyogagato vipralambhaśca" ||
iti ||45||
[āśvāsyaivaṃ prathamavirahodagraśokāṃ sakhīṃ te
śailādāśu trinayanavṛṣotkhātakūṭānnivṛttaḥ |
sābhijñānaprahitakuśalaistadvacobhirmamāpi
prātaḥ kundaprasavaśithilaṃ jīvitaṃ dhārayethāḥ||]
idānīṃ meghaṃ stauti -- 
kaccit saumya ! vyavasitamidaṃ bandhukṛtyaṃ tvayā me
pratyādeśānna khalu bhavato dhīratāṃ kalpayāmi |
niḥśabdo'pi pradiśasi jalaṃ yācitaścātakebhyaḥ
pratyuktaṃ hi praṇayiṣu satāmīpsitārthakriyaiva||46||
kacciditi | idaṃ bandhukṛtyaṃ tvayā vyavasitaṃ kaccidityanvayaḥ | ahaṃ kariṣyāmīti pratyuttaraṃ na brūṣe | tathāpyanyeṣāmiva pratyuttarapradānāt tava dhairyaṃ na kalpayāmītyāha -- pratyādeśāditi | ādeśo vacanaṃ, pratyādeśaḥ prativacanam ityuttaraṃ vināpi bhavato dhīratāṃ kalpayāmītyabhiprāyaḥ | yadyapi pratyādeśaśabdaḥ pratyākhyānavacanaḥ, tathāpyatra prativacanavācako bhavati pratyuktamiti vakṣyamāṇatvāt | uktamarthamupapādayati -- niśśabdo'pīti | anena niruttaratvaṃ vivakṣitam | pratyuktaṃ prativacanam | praṇayiṣu prārthayamāneṣu | satāmuttamānām | īpsitārthakriyā īpsitārthasampādanam | praṇayiṣu īpsitārthādanatirvṛttireva prativacanaṃ bhavatītyarthaḥ ||46||
etat kṛtvā priyamanucitaprārthanāvartmano me sauhārdād vā vidhura iti vā mayyanukrośabuddhyā |
iṣṭān deśān vicara jalada ! prāvṛṣā saṃbhṛtaśrīrmā bhūdevaṃ kṣaṇamapi ca te vidyutā viprayogaḥ|| 47 ||
etaditi | anucitaprārthanāvartmanaḥ bhavataḥ prādhānyamanādṛtya matpriyāyai sandeśaṃ haretyevamanucitaprārthanāprakarasya | sauhārdād vā etāvantaṃ kālamābhāṣaṇādijanitāt suhṛdbhāvādityarthaḥ | uktaṃ ca -- ‘sambandhamābhāṣaṇapūrvamāhuḥ' iti | vidhura iti vā | vidhuraḥ priyāviśliṣṭaḥ | mayi vidhura ityanugrahabuddhyā vetyarthaḥ | vicara, vipūrvaścaratiḥ sakarmako bhavati | ‘adhijyadhanvā vicacāra dāvam' ityuktatvāt | prāvṛṣā saṃbhṛtaśrīḥ varṣākālena saṃbhṛtaśobhaḥ | ayamatra samādhiḥ -- kaścit pathiko deśāntarāṇi gacchan kayāpi dāsyā saṃbhṛtārtho bhavatīti | evaṃ mama viyogaprakāreṇa | tava vidyutā viprayogo mā bhūditi deśāntaragamanāt pathikāyamānasya bhavataḥ kṣaṇamapi mama kāntayeva vidyutā viprayogo mā bhūdityabhiprāyaḥ ||47||
iti śrīdakṣiṇāvartanāthaviracito meghasandeśapradīpaḥ samāptaḥ |
śubhaṃ bhūyāt ||
