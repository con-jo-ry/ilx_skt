|| atha rāmāṣṭakam ||
sugrīvamitraṃ paramaṃ pavitraṃ sītākalatraṃ navameghagātram |
kāruṇya pātraṃ śatapatranetraṃ śrīrāmacandraṃ satataṃ namāmi || 1||
saṃsārasāraṃ nigamaṃ pracāraṃ dharmāvatāraṃ hata bhūmi bhāram |
sadā nirvikāraṃ sukha sindhusāraṃ śrīrāmacandraṃ satataṃ namāmi || 2||
lakṣmī vilāsaṃ jagatāṃ nivāsaṃ bhūdeva vāsaṃ sukha sindhu hāsam |
laṅkā vināśaṃ bhavanaṃ prakāśaṃ śrīrāmacandraṃ satataṃ namāmi || 3||
maṃdāramālaṃ vacane rasālaṃ guṇayaṃ viśalaṃ hata saptatālam |
kravyādakālaṃ suralokapālaṃ śrīrāmacandraṃ satataṃ namāmi || 4||
vedāṃta jñānaṃ sakala satānaṃ haṃtāramānaṃ tridaśaṃ pradhānam |
gajendrapālaṃ vigatā viśālaṃ śrīrāmacandraṃ satataṃ namāmi || 5||
khelā'tibhītaṃ svajane punītaṃ syāmo pragītaṃ vacane ahītam |
rāgenagītaṃ vacane ahītaṃ śrīrāmacandraṃ satataṃ namāmi || 6||
līlāśarīraṃ raṇaraṅga dhīraṃ viśvai kavīraṃ raghuvaṃśadhāram |
gambhīranādaṃ jitasarvavādaṃ śrīrāmacandraṃ satataṃ namāmi || 7||
śyāmābhirāmaṃ nayanābhirāmaṃ guṇābhirāmaṃ vacanābhirāmaṃ |
viśvapraṇāmaṃ katabhaktikāmaṃ śrīrāmacandraṃ satataṃ namāmi || 8||
|| iti rāmāṣṭakaṃ sampūrṇam ||
