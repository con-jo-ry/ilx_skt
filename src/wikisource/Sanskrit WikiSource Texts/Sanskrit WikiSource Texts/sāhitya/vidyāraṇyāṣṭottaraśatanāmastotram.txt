Text:

॥ śrīvidyāraṇyāṣṭottaraśatanāmastotram ॥
vidyāraṇyamahāyogī mahāvidyāprakāśakaḥ ।
śrīvidyānagaroddhartā vidyāratnamahodadhiḥ ॥ 1
rāmāyaṇamahāsapta koṭimantraprakāśakaḥ ।
śrīdevīkaruṇāpūrṇaḥ paripūrṇamanorathaḥ ॥ 2
virūpākṣamahākṣetra svarṇavṛṣṭiprakalpakaḥ ।
vedatrayollasadbhāṣyakartā tatvārthakovidaḥ ॥ 3
bhagavatpādanirṇīta siddhāntasthāpanaprabhuḥ ।
varṇāśramavyavasthātā nigamāgamasāravit ॥ 4
śrīmatkarṇāṭarājyaśrī sampatsiṃhāsanapradaḥ ।
śrīmadbukkamahīpālarājyapaṭṭābhiṣekakṛt ॥ 5
ācāryakṛtabhāṣyādi granthavṛttiprakalpakaḥ ।
sakalopaniṣadbhāṣyadīpikādiprakāśakṛt ॥ 6
sarvaśāstrārthatatvajño mantraśāstrābdhimandaraḥ ।
vidvanmaṇi śiraśślādhya bahugrantha vidhāyakaḥ ॥ 7
sārasvatasamuddhartā sārāsāravicakṣaṇaḥ ।
śrautasmārtasadācāra saṃsthāpanadhurandharaḥ ॥ 8
vedaśāstra bahirbhūta durmatāmbhodhiśoṣakaḥ ।
durvādigarvadāvāgniḥ pratipakṣebhakesarī ॥ 9
yaśo jaivātṛkajyotsnā prakāśitadigantaraḥ ।
aṣṭāṅgayoganiṣṇātassāṅkhyogaviśāradaḥ ॥ 10
rājādhirājasandoha pūjyamānapadāmbujaḥ ।
mahāvaibhavasampanna audāryaśrīnivāsabhūḥ ॥ 11
tiryagāndolikāmukhya samastabirudāñcitaḥ ।
mahābhogī mahāyogī vairāgyaprathamāśrayaḥ ॥ 12
śrīmānparamahaṃsādisadguruḥ karuṇānidhiḥ ।
tapaḥprabhāvanirdhūtadurvārakalivaibhavaḥ ॥ 13
nirantaraśivadhyāna śoṣitākhilakalmaṣaḥ ।
nirjitārātiṣaḍvargo dāridryonmūlanakṣamaḥ ॥ 14
jitendriyassatyavādī satyasandho dṛḍhavrataḥ ।
śāntāsmā sucaritrāḍhya sarvabhūtahitotsukaḥ ॥ 15
kṛtakṛtyo dharmaśīlo dānto lobhavivarjitaḥ ।
mahābuddhirmahāvīryo mahātejā mahāmanāḥ ॥ 16
taporāśirjñānarāśiḥ kalyāṇaguṇavāridhiḥ ।
nītiśāstrasamuddhartā prājñamauliśiromaṇiḥ ॥ 17
śuddhasatvamayo dhīro deśakālavibhāgavit ।
atīndriyajñānanidhirbhūtabhāvyarthakovidaḥ ॥ 18
guṇatrayavibhāgajñassanyāsāśramadīkṣitaḥ ।
jñānātmakaikadaṇḍāḍhyaḥ kausumbhavasanojjavalaḥ ॥ 19
rudrākṣamālikādhārī bhasmoddhūlitadehavān ।
hastamālālasaddhasta stripuṇḍrāṅkitamastakaḥ ॥ 20
dharāsuratapassampatphalaṃ śubhamahodayaḥ ।
candramaulīśvara śrīmatpādapadmārcanotsukaḥ ॥ 21
śrīmacchaṅkarayogīndracaraṇāsaktamānasaḥ ।
ratnagarbhagaṇeśāna prapūjanaparāyaṇaḥ ॥ 22
śāradāmbādivyapīṭha saparyātatparāśayaḥ ।
avyājakaruṇāmūrtiḥ prajñānirjitagīṣpatiḥ ॥ 23
sujñāna satkṛtajagallokānandavidhāyakaḥ ।
pāṇīvilāsabhavanaṃ brahmānandaikalolupaḥ ॥ 24
nirmamo nirahaṅkāro nirālasyo nirākulaḥ ।
niścinto nityasantuṣṭo niyatātmā nirāmayaḥ ॥ 25
gurubhūmaṇḍalācāryo gurupīṭhapratiṣṭhitaḥ ।
sarvatantrasvatantraśca yantramantravicakṣaṇaḥ ॥ 26
śiṣṭeṣṭaphaladātā ca duṣṭanigrahadīkṣitaḥ ।
pratijñātārthanirvoḍhā niprahānugrahaprabhuḥ ॥ 27
jagatpūjyassadānandassākṣācchaṅkararūpabhṛta ।
mahālakṣmī mahāmantra puraścaryāparāyaṇaḥ ॥ 28
vidyāraṇyamahāyogi nāmnāmaṣṭottaraṃ śatam ।
yaḥpaṭhetsatataṃ sampatsārasvatanidhirbhavet ॥ 29
॥ iti śrīvidyāraṇyāṣṭottaraśatanāmastotram ॥
