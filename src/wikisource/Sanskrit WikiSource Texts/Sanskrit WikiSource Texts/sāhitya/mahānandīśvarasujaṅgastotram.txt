॥ śrīmahānandīśvarabhujaṅgastotram ॥
kirīṭendugaṅgāmanojñottamāṅgaṃ lalāṭekṣaṇātpuṣpadehānuṣaṅgaṃ । 
tuṣārādrikanyā sasaṅgaṃ sada'haṃ namāmyantaraṅgaṃ mahānandiliṅgam ॥ 1
digīśādidigbhittisannāṭyaliṅgaṃ lasatkumbhicarmāmbaracchāditāṅgam । 
jaganmātṛgaurī mukhāmbhojabhṛṅgaṃ namā॥ 2
caturvedavedyaṃ mahāvedavādyaṃ namadbhaktahṛdyaṃ murāriprasādhyam । 
gurubrahmabhūtaṃ cidānandarūpaṃ namā ॥ 3
purākāśikāpaṭṭaṇaprāṅgaṇānte navasvarṇadībhūtabhṛtkṛṣṭarānte । 
haribrahmamūrdhābhiṣiktaṃ videhaṃ namā ...॥ 4
suhṛtputrasampanmukhādyutsukhādiṃ vihāyarivargaṃ mahāsevyakāyam । 
jaṭājūṭamadhyapradeśapragaṅgaṃ mahībhṛddhavāṅgaṃ mahānandiliṅgam ॥ 5
caturdvāradauvārikodāryamāṇaṃ namanhṛdyagandharvagītānuraktaṃ । 
sunāṭyaṃ prakurvāṇamīśaṃ sureśaṃ namā ...॥ 6
॥ iti śrīmahānandīśvara bhujaṅgastotram ॥
