na vā nindako matsarī bhaktihīnaḥ |
na yatsaṃgabādhā na vittāpahārī
jagatyāṃ tu yo brahmabodhaḥ sa sādhuḥ || 181||
hṛdā kāmuko niḥspṛhatvaṃ vadecched
vinā tatkriyāṃ vākpaṭutvaṃ vṛthaiva |
yathā vakti tādṛk pravṛttirhi yasya
manaḥ sadgururniścitavyaḥ sa eva || 182||
(?)ānī vivekī viraktaśca bhaktaḥ
kṛpālurmanasvī kṣamī yogayuktaḥ |
sudakṣaḥ prabhuścāturo yaśca vidvān
bhavet tasya saṅge samādhānalābhaḥ || 183||
ajaṃ yat tathā'nāgataṃ caiva yaddhi
vacobhistu taj(?)āyate sajjanānām |
anirvācyamevāsti yat tacca vācyaṃ
manaḥ saccidānandamanveṣaṇīyam || 184||
nilīno bhavedādarād rāmarūpe
bhayātītatā niścitā sve svarūpe |
kadāpyeha nāsādyate mārgyamāṇaḥ
sadaikaṃ bhavedyanna tasmin vibhedaḥ || 185||
manaḥ saṃnidhau te sadā rāghavo'sti
manaḥ sajjana ! mārgaya tvaṃ hi satyam |
tavā'khaṇḍito rāghaveṇāsti yogo
viniṣkāsaya svāntarāt tāmahaṃtām || 186||
manaḥ piṇḍabrahmāṇḍayorbhūtasāmyaṃ
samastaṃ tvidaṃ brahmarūpe na kiṃcid |
mano bhāti yadyat na tattat sadasti
sukhe saṃsthitiḥ saṃgamutsṛjya kāryā || 187||
ahaṃpāśamucchidya vi(?)ānaśastraiḥ
videhasthitirbhaktitaḥ sevanīyā |
virakterbalāt sarvanindyaṃ vivarjya
sukhe saṃsthitiḥ saṅgamutsṛjya kāryā || 188||
pṛthivyādikṛd deva ālokanīyo
yadālokane muktilābho'sti sadyaḥ |
guṇaiḥ saṃyuto nirguṇa sevanīyaḥ
sukhe saṃsthitiḥ saṅgamutsṛjya kāryā || 189||
sa no kāryakartā na vā sṛṣṭibhartā
parāyāḥ paro no vivartena liptaḥ |
sa vai nirvikalpo vikalpyaiva sevyaḥ
parityajya saṅgaṃ sukhaṃ stheyamatra || 190||
na dehātmadhīniścayo yasya naṣṭo
na kalpāntakāle'pi sa (?)ānavān syāt |
paraṃ brahma no'haṃtayā vetti kaścid
mano'(?)ānajāṃ śūnyatāṃ no jahāti || 191||
manaḥsparśahīnaṃ sthiraṃ yatsvarūpaṃ
abhedena cintyaṃ hi sarvottamasya |
na dṛṣṭāntayogyaṃ bhavet tasya kiṃcid
na niḥsaṃgatā saṃgatā tatra satyā || 192||
pareśe na vi(?)ātatā'(?)ātatā vā
sa no varṇyate vedaśāstraiḥ purāṇaiḥ |
adṛśyo na dṛśyo na sākṣī tayorvā
śrutiryasya nāntaṃ vijānāti naiva || 193||
hṛdistho'sti kaḥ kīdṛśo devavaryo
guruḥ sādaraṃ sādhakenā'tha pṛṣṭaḥ |
parityajya dehaṃ vasatyeṣa kutra
pnarvāsayogyaṃ kva saṃsthānamasya || 194||
iti prāha jānīhi devo hi hṛtstho
nabhovatsadā vyāpako'pyapraliptaḥ |
na gacchatyatho nopagacchatyajasraṃ
ṛte tena riktaṃ sthalaṃ naiva kiṃcit || 195||
nabhovibhramadreṇumātraṃ sthalaṃ no
raghūṇāmadhīśena riktaṃ vibhāti |
pravṛtto hi taddarśane yaḥ sa tasmin
layaṃ yātyalakṣyaṃ ca lakṣyaṃ ca līnam || 196||
nabhovaddhi tad vyāpakaṃ rāmarūpaṃ
hṛdā cintitaṃ syād bhavabhraṃśahetuḥ |
vilīnā bhaveddehadhīstasya bodhād
yathecchaṃ sudṛṣṭe'pi tṛptistu nāsti || 197||
nabhaḥ saṃsthitaṃ vyāpya viśvaṃ yathedaṃ
tathā rāma itthaṃ na sāmyaṃ kathaṃcit |
sa cāstyadvitīyaḥ svabhāvād hi rāmaḥ
tadarthaṃ hi vyarthaṃ padaṃ vyāpaketi || 198||
purāṇaṃ tataṃ sarvato yatsvarūpaṃ
na tarkasya leśo'pi yasminvidheyaḥ |
nigūḍhaṃ paraṃtvāśu tatsyātsugamyaṃ
pratāpād guroradvitīyetisaṃ(?)m || 199||
svarūpaṃ tu yaj(?)āyate (?)ānayogāt
sphuṭa sākṣitā'ntarhitā tatra sarvā |
bhavedunmanī kuṇṭhitā vākca sadyaḥ
sa eva svayaṃ rāmadṛk sarvato yaḥ || 200||
kadāpyātmabodhe dvitīyaṃ na bhāti
na vai mānase dvaitaleśo'sti kiṃcit |
gatairjanmasaṃghaiḥ svarūpopalabdhiḥ
videhasthitau sarvakāyānirāsaḥ || 201||
mano yatsuguhyaṃ tvayā taddhi labdhaṃ
prakāryastvayā rakṣaṇe tasya yatnaḥ |
dṛḍhapratyayārthaṃ sadā śrāvyametan
manaḥ sādhusaṃgāddhi dhanyatvameṣi || 202||
manaḥ sarvasaṃgaṃ parityajya dūrād
vidheyaḥ sadā sādaraṃ sādhusaṅgaḥ |
mahāduḥkhabhaṅgaḥ satāṃ saṃgamena
vinā sādhanaṃ tena sanmārgalābhaḥ || 203||
manā sarva saṅgāpahaḥ sādhusaṅgo
manastatkṣaṇaṃ mokṣadaḥ saṃgamaiṣaḥ |
manaḥ sādhakān mocayatyeṣa śīghraṃ
manaḥ sarvathā dvaitanāśakṣamo'yam || 204||
manobodhamākarṇya doṣāḥ prayānti
jaḍāḥ sādhanāyogyatāṃ copayānti |
tato (?)ānavairāgyasāmarthyalābho
vimuktistato dāsavākyapratītyā || 205||
