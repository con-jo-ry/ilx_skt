Text:

॥ bilvāṣṭakam ॥
tridalaṃ triguṇākāraṃ trinetraṃ ca triyāyudhaṃ ।
trijanmapāpasaṃhāraṃ ekabilvaṃ śivārpitam ॥ 1
triśākhairbilvapatraiśca acchidraiḥ komalaiśśubhaiḥ ।
tava pūjāṃ kariṣyāmi eka ...॥ 2
darśanaṃ bilvavṛkṣaiśca sparśanaṃ pāpanāśanam ।
aghorapāpasaṃhāraṃ eka ...॥ 3
sālagrāmeṣu vipreṣu taṭāke vanakūpayoḥ ।
yajñakoṭisahasrāṇāṃ eka ...॥ 4
dantikoṭisahasreṣu aśvamedhaśatāni ca ।
koṭikanyāpradānena eka ...॥ 5
ekaṃ ca bilvapatraiśca koṭiyajñaphalaṃ labhet ।
mahādevaiśca pūjārtha eka ...॥ 6
kāśīkṣetre nivāsaṃ ca kālabhairavadarśanam ।
gayāprayāgame dṛṣṭvā eka ...॥ 7
umayā saha deveśaṃ vāhanaṃ nandiśaṅkaram ।
mucyate sarvapāpebhyo ekabilvaṃ śivārpitam ॥ 8
॥ iti śrībilvāṣṭakam ॥
tulasī bilva nirguṇḍī apāmārgakapitthako ।
śamī cāmalakaṃ dūrvā aṣṭabilvāḥ prakīrtitāḥ ॥ 9
॥ityaṣṭabilvāni॥
