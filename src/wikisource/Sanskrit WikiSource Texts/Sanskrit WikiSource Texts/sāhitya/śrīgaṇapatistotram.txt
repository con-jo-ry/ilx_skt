||śrīgaṇapatistotram||
dviradānana vighnakānanajvalana tvaṃ prathameśanaṃdana|
madanapatimākhuvāhana jvalanābhāsitapiṃgalocana||1||
ahibaṃdhana raktacaṃdana priyadūrvāṅkurabhārapūjana||
śaśibhūṣaṇa bhaktapālana jvalanākṣāsva nijānnijāvana||2||
vividhāmaramartyanāyakaḥ prathitastvaṃ bhuvane vināyakaḥ||
tava kope'pi hi naiva nāyakastata eva tvamajo vināyaka||3||
balinigraha īśa keśavastripurākhyāsuranigrahe śivaḥ||
jagadudbhavavanesbjasaṃbhavaḥ sakalānjetumaho manobhavaḥ||4||
mahiṣāsuranigrahe śivā bhavamuktyai munayo dhutāśivāḥ||
yamapūjayadiṣṭasiddhaye varado me bhava ceṣṭasiddhaye||5||
gajakarṇaka mūṣakasthite varade tvayyabhaye hṛdi sthite||
jayalābharameṣṭasaṃpadāḥ khalu sarvatra kuto vadāpadāḥ||6||
saṃkalpitaṃ kāryamavighnamīśa drāksiddhimāyātu mamākhileśa||
pāpatrayaṃ me hara sanmatīśa tāpatrayaṃ me hara śāṃtyadhīśa||7||
gaṇādhīśo dhīśo hariharavidhīśosbhayakaro
guṇādhīśo dhīśo vijayata umāhṛtsukhakaraḥ|
budhādhīśo nīśo nijabhajakavighnaughaharakaḥ
mudādhīśo pīśo yaśasa ubhayardheśca śaraṇam||8||
iti śrīvāsudevānandasarasvatīviracitaṃ gaṇapatistotraṃ saṃpūrṇam||
