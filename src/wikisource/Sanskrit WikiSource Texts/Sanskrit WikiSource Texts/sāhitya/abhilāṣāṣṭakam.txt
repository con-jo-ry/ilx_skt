kadā pakṣīndrāṃsopari gatamajaṃ kañcanayanam
ramāsaṃśliṣṭāṅgaṃ gaganarucamāpītavasanam |
gadāśaṅkhāmbhojārivaramālokya suciraṃ
gamiṣyatyetanme nanu saphalatāṃ netrayugalam || 1||
kadā kṣīrābdhyantaḥ surataruvanāntarmaṇimaye
samāsīnaṃ pīṭhe jaladhitanayāliṅgitatanum |
stutaṃ devairnityaṃ munivarakadambairabhinutam
stavaiḥ santoṣyāmi śrutivacanagarbhaiḥ suragurum || 2||
kadā māmābhītaṃ bhayajaladhitastāpasatanuṃ
gatā rāgaṃ gaṅgātaṭagiriguhāvāsasahanam |
lapantaṃ he viṣṇo suravara rameśeti satataṃ
samabhyetyodāraṃ kamalanayano vakṣyati vacaḥ || 3||
kadā me hṛdpadme bhramara iva padme prativasan
sadā dhyānābhyāsādaniśamupahūto vibhurasau |
sphurajjyotīrūpo raviriva rasāsevyacaraṇo
hariṣyatyajñānājjanitatimiraṃ tūrṇamakhilam || 4||
kadā me bhogāśā nibiḍabhavapāśāduparataṃ
tapaḥśuddhaṃ buddhaṃ guruvacanatodairacapalam |
mano maunaṃ kṛtvā haricaraṇayoścāru suciraṃ
sthitiṃ sthāṇuprāyāṃ bhavabhayaharāṃ yāsyati parām || 5||
kadā me saṃruddhākhilakaraṇajālasya parito
jitāśeṣaprāṇānilaparikarasya prajapataḥ |
sadoṅkāraṃ cittaṃ haripadasaroje dhṛtavataḥ
sameṣyatyullāsaṃ muhurakhilaromāvaliriyam || 6||
kadā prārabdhānte pariśithilatāṃ gacchati śanaiḥ
śarīre cākṣaughe'pyuparatavati prāṇapavane |
vadatyūrdhvaṃ śaśvanmama vadanakañje muhuraho
kariṣyatyāvāsaṃ haririti padaṃ pāvanatamam || 7||
kadā hitvā jīrṇāṃ tvacamiva bhujaṅgastanumimāṃ caturbāhuścakrāmbujadarakaraḥ pītavasanaḥ |
ghanaśyāmo dūtairgaganagatinīto natiparairgamiṣyāmīśasyāntikamakhiladuḥkhāntakamiti || 8||
|| iti śrīmatparamahaṃsasvāmibrahmānandaviracitaṃ
abhilāṣāṣṭakaṃ sampūrṇam ||
