Text:

॥ candramaulīśvara bhujaṅgastotram ॥
sadāmudāya divyamānase saroruhāntare vihāraṇeṣu sañcayaṃ vidāriṇe cidātmane । 
nirastatoya toyamañci kāyakāyaśāline namaśśivāya sāmba śaṅkarāya candramaulaye ॥ 1
namo namoṣṭamūrtaye namo samānakīrtaye namo namo mahātmane namaśśubhapradāyine। 
namo dayārdrasetave namostu kṛttivāsase nama ... ॥ 2
pitāmahādyavedyakasvabhāvake mamāyate samastadevatā samaprapūjitāṅghricarcite । 
bhavāya śakraratnasadguṇaprabhāvaśāline nama ... ॥ 3
śivohamasmi bhāvayan śivaṃ śivena rakṣasa śśiva svapūrṇavarcasa svamarcase padadvayam । 
śivatparaṃ na vidyate śive jagatpravartane nama ... ॥ 4
kapardine'rkacandramaḥ kṛpīṭayonisandṛśe tapodhanāgraṇīmanassarojasatyavāsine । 
phaṇāphaṇicchabijvaladbhujaṅgabhūṣaṇāñcite nama ...॥ 5
śivānanāravindasanmilindabhāvibhāsvine vinodine dineśakoṭidikprabhākṛtāyine । 
svasīdasāmba sādarāvalokanaikavartine nama ...॥ 6
jaṭātaṭīluṭhadvayaddhunīgalaṃ galadhvaniddhagarjitograbuddhi sambhramabhramacchikhaṇḍine। 
vikhaṇḍitārimaṇḍalapracaṇḍago triśūline nama ... ॥ 7
akharvagarvadurvijṛmbhakumbhidambhadānavaṃ jitenataddhanatrinākacāriṇe vihāriṇe । 
sudṛksudṛkṣu hṛcchayāpahariṇe vidāriṇe nama ...॥ 8
akhaṇḍitāṇḍabāhudaṇḍadantitopradindhimapradinddhimiddhimi dhvani pramoditogratāṇḍavam । 
vidhāpine dhanādhinātha sādhusakhyadāyite nama ... ॥ 9
prahṛṣṭaruṣṭaduṣṭapuṣṭa diṣṭaviṣṭapāyasaṃ namadviśiṣṭa bhaktaciṣṭakīrtayeṣṭamūrtaye । 
yatheṣṭadaṣṭaśailajādharoṣṭapallavāya te sadānama ... || 10
harāya vā harāya vājanandinandine vinodine sanātanāya bhūtanāyarāyate । 
yatīśvareśvarāya śāśvatāya viśvarūpiṇe nama ... ॥ 11
bhavāya divyavaibhavāya nāyanāgnihṛṣṭahṛdbhavāya bhūri bhūmabhṛddhanurdharāya vardhine । 
samṛdbhiruddhimṛddhanupramṛddhavanditāya te nama ...॥ 12
marandatundilāravindasundarasmitānanonmilindabṛnda bālanīlajālaśailakuntalā । 
kalākalāpaśāline śivāmapekṣatoṣaṇe nama ...॥ 13
॥iti śrīmacchaṅkarānandācāryaviracitaṃ śrīcandramaulīśvarabhujaṅgastotram ॥
