<maṅgalācaraṇam>
amarīkabarībhārabhramarīmukharīkṛtam |
dūrīkarotu duritaṃ gaurīcaraṇapaṅkajam || 1||
amarīṇāṃ devastrīṇāṃ kabarībhāreṣu keśapāśeṣu saugandhyalobhātsaṃsṛṣṭā yā bhramaryaḥ tābhiḥ mukharīkṛtaṃ gauryāḥ caraṇameva paṅkajamiti mayūravyasakāditvātsamāsaḥ, athavā caraṇaṃ paṅkajamivetyupamitasamāsaḥ, paṅkajasadṛśaṃ caraṇaṃ duritaṃ pāpaṃ dūrīkarotu vināśayatviti prārthanāyāṃ loṭ |
parasparatapaḥ sampatphalāyitaparasparau |
prapañcamātāpitarau prāñcau jāyāpatī stumaḥ ||2||
parasparasyānyonyasya tapaḥ sampadaḥ phalāyita phalavadācaritaṃ parasparamanyonyasvarūpaṃ yayostau, pārvatītapaḥsampattiphalāyitaḥ parameśvaraḥ, parameśvaratapaḥsampattiphalāyitā ca pārvatītyarthaḥ, prapañcasya sarvasyāpi mātāpitarau janikartārau hitakāriṇau ca prāñcau purātanau jāyāpatī daṃpatī pārvatīparameśvarau stumaḥ, stotreṇa ca namratā pratīyata iti namaskriyātmakaṃ maṅgalam |

<granthasya viṣayaprayojane>
alaṃkāreṣu bālānāmavagāhanasiddhaye |
lalitaḥ kriyate teṣāṃ lakṣyalakṣaṇasaṃgrahaḥ || 3 ||
alaṅkāreṣu prakṛte arthālaṅkāreṣu upamādiṣu bālānāṃ grahaṇadhāraṇaśaktānāmapyalaṅkāraviṣaye avyutpannānāṃ avagāhanasiddhaye vyutpattisiddhayarthaṃ teṣāmalaṅkārāṇāṃ lakṣyalakṣaṇayoḥ saṃgrahaḥ, saṃkṣepeṇa lakṣaṇamudāharaṇaṃ ca, lalitaḥ sugrāhyaḥ kriyate | atra pūrvārdhe prayojanamuktam, uttarārdhe ca pratipādyo viṣayaḥ pratijñātaḥ | asādhāraṇo dharmaḥ lakṣaṇam, lakṣyamudāharaṇam | tathā ca bālavyutpatyarthamupamādayaḥ arthālaṅkārāḥ lakṣaṇodāharaṇābhyāṃ saṅgraheṇa nirūpyante iti granthakāraḥ pratijānīte|

<upamālaṅkāraḥ>
upamā yatra sādṛśyalakṣmīrullasati dvayoḥ |
haṃsīva kṛṣṇa te kīrtiḥ svargaṅgāmavagāhate || 4||
yatra yasmin kāvye dvayorvastunorupamānopameyatvena prasiddhayoḥ sādṛśyasya lakṣmīḥ saṃpat, sahṛdayahṛdayāhlādi sādṛśyamityarthaḥ,ullasati udbhūtatayā bhāti (vyaṅgyamaryādāṃ vinā) spaṣṭaṃ prakāśate sā tathābhāsamānā sādṛśyalakṣmīrupamā iti lakṣaṇam | he kṛṣṇa te tava kīrtiḥ haṃsīva svargaṅgāmākāśagaṅgāmavagāhate ityudāharaṇam | atra kīrtihaṃsyoḥ sādṛśyalakṣmīḥ spaṣṭaṃ prakāśata ityupamālakāraḥ |
upamāyāḥ santi catvāro’vayavāḥ upamānam, upameyam, sādhāraṇadharmaḥ, upamāvācakaśceti| tatrādhikaguṇavatvena prasiddhamupamānaṃ candrādi | prakṛte varṇyamānaṃ mukhādyupameyaṃ nyūnaguṇam | āhlādakatvādikaṃ sādhāraṇo dharmaḥ | ivādi śabdāḥ sādṛśyavācakāḥ | sādṛśyaṃ ca tadbhinnatve sati tadgatabhūyodharmavattvam |
saṃkṣepataḥ upamā dvividhā pūrṇā, luptāceti | yatra caturṇāmapyupamāvayavānāmupādānaṃ tatra pūrṇopamā, yathā haṃsīvetyādipūrvodāhṛte haṃsī upamānam, kīrtirupameyam, svargaṅgāvagāhanaṃ sādhāraṇo dharmaḥ, ivaśabdaḥ upamāvācaka iti caturṇāmapyupādānātpūrṇopamā |
pūrṇopamāyā aparamudāharaṇam
guṇadoṣau budho gṛhṇan indukṣveḍāviveśvaraḥ|
śirasā ślāghate pūrvaṃ paraṃ kaṇṭhe niyacchati ||
atra budhaḥ upameyam, īśvaraḥ upamānam, ivaśabdo vācakaḥ, sādhāraṇadharmastūpamānopameyayornekaḥ, kintu upamānagatayoścandragaralayoḥ upameyagatayorguṇadoṣayośca bimbapratibimvabhāvenābhedādhyavasāyāt, tathā upādānajñānayoḥ gṛhṇannityekapadopādānenābhedādhyavasāyācca sādhāraṇadharmatā parikalpyate | tasmādatra pūrṇopamaiva |
vastuto bhinnayorapyupamānopameyadharmayoḥ parasparasādṛśyādabhinnatayādhyavasitayoḥ pṛthagupādānaṃ bimbaprativimbabhāvaḥ |
luptopamā, tadbhedāśca |
varṇyopamānadharmāṇāmupamāvācakasya ca |
ekadvitryanupādānādbhinnā luptopamāṣṭadhā || 5||
upamānopameyasādhāraṇadharmavācakānāṃ caturṇāṃ madhye ekasya, dvayostrayāṇāṃ vā anupādānādaprayogāt luptopamā bhavati | avayavalopād luptetyanvarthasaṃjñā | varṇyaṃ upameyam, avarṇyamupamānam |
luptopamāṣṭadhā bhinnā tadyathā vācakamātralopādvācakaluptā, dharmamātralopāddharmaluptā, dharmavācakayorlopārddharmavācakaluptā, vācakopameyayorlopādvācakopameyaluptā, upamānamātralogadupamānaluptā, vācakopamānayorlopādvācakopamānaluptā, dharmopamānayo lopāddharmopamānaluptā, dharmopamānavācakānāṃ lopāddharmopamānavācakaluptā iti |
luptopamodāharaṇāni
taḍidgaurīndutulyāsyā karpūrantī dṛśormama |
kāntyā smaravadhūyantī dṛṣṭā tanvī raho mayā ||6||
yattayā melanaṃ tatra lābho me yacca tadrateḥ|
tadetatkākatālīyamavitarkitasambhavam ||7||
taḍigaurī tanvī (mayā rahasi dṛṣṭā) iti vācakaluptāyā udāharaṇam | taḍidiva gaurītyarthe vivakṣite "upamānāni sāmānyavacanaiḥ" iti samāse ivaśabdasya vācakasya lopādvācakaluptā| tanvī upameyam taḍidupamānam, gauravaṃ sādhāraṇo dharmaḥ |
indutulyāsyā (tanvī mayā dṛṣṭā) iti dharmaluptāyā udāharaṇam | indunā tulyaṃ āsyaṃ yasyāḥ sā indutulyāsyā | atra indurupamānam, āsyamupameyam, tulyaśabdo vācakaḥ, sādhāraṇadharmastu kāntyādirnopātta iti dharmaluptā |
 (mama dṛśoḥ) karpūrantī tanvīti dharmavācakaluptāyā. udāharaṇam | karpūramivācarantītyarthe vivakṣite ānandādhāyakatvarūpācārārthakasya "sarvaprātipadikebhyaḥ kin vā vaktavyaḥ" iti vihitasya kir ivaśabdena saha lopāddharmavācakaluptā| karpūraḥ upamānam, tanvī upameyamiti dvayamupāttam, netrānandādhāyakatvarūpo dharmaḥ vācakaśabdaśca nopātau |
kāntyā smaravadhūyantī iti vācakopameyaluptāyā udāharaṇam | atra kāntyā ātmānaṃ smaravadhūmivācarantītyarthe vivakṣite "upamānādācāre" iti kyac pratyayo vihitaḥ | atrātmā upameyam, tacca nopāttam | vācakaśabdo’pi nopātta iti vācakopameyaluptā| smaravadhūrupamānam kāntimattvaṃ sādhāraṇo dharmaḥ |
tatra (vijanasthale) me tadreterlābha iti yat avitarkita sambhavaṃ (yādṛcchikaṃ) tadetat kākatālīyamityupamānaluptāyā udāharaṇam |
kākagamanamiva tālapatanamiva kākatālaṃ, tataḥ kākatālamiva kākakṛtatālopabhoga ivetyarthe vivakṣite “ive pratikṛtau” ityadhikārasthena “samāsācca tadviṣayāt” iti sūtreṇa chapratyayo vihitaḥ, tasya ca āyaneyītyā’’disūtreṇa īyādeśaḥ | tathā ca mama tanvīsuratalāmarūpaṃ yadvastu avitarkitasambhava tat kākakṛtatālopabhogasadṛśamiti niṣkṛṭo’rthaḥ| atra tanvīsuratalābhaḥ upameyam, avitarkitasambhavatvaṃ sādhāraṇo dharmaḥ, chapratyayaśca sādṛśyavācaka iti trayāṇāmupādānamasti| kākakṛta tālopabhogarūpamupamānaṃ nopāttamityupamānaluptā| atra samāsaviṣaye kākatālaśabdau lakṣaṇayā tatsamavetakriyābodhakau |
tatra mama tayā melanaṃ mama tatra gamanaṃ tasyāstatrāgamanaṃ cāvitarkitasambhavaṃ kākāgamanamiva tālapatanamiva kākatālamiti vācako’mānaluptāyā udāharaṇam | mama tanmelanaṃ kākāgamanatālapatanasadṛśamavitarkitasambhavamiti phalito’rthaḥ | atra kākatālamiti “samāsācca tadviṣayāt” iti jñāpakātsamāse vācakasya lopaḥ| upamānamapi kākāgamanatālapatanātmakaṃ samāsāntargatamapi na śabdopāttamiti vācakopamānaluptā | mama tatsamāgamaḥ, upameyam, yādṛcchikatvaṃ sādhāraṇo dharmaḥ|
udāharaṇaśloke "avitarkitasambhavam" ityasya sthāne “iti jānīhi he sakhe” iti pāṭhe sādhāraṇadharmasyāpi lope pañcamaṣaṣṭhe eva saptamāṣṭamayorudāharaṇe bhavataḥ | me tadraterlābha iti yat tadetatkākatālīyam iti dharmopamānaluptāyāḥ, mama tayā tatra melanamiti yat tadetatkākatālamiti dharmopamānavācakaluptāyāścodāharaṇam |

<ananvayālaṅkāraḥ>
upamānopameyatvaṃ yadekasyaiva vastunaḥ |
indurinduriva śrīmānityādau tadananvayaḥ ||8||
ekasyaiva vastunaḥ yadupamānatvamupameyatvaṃ ca vivakṣyate tadananvayālaṅkāraḥ| svasya svenaiva sādṛśyaṃ nānvetītyananvayasaṃjñā | tathā varṇanasya prayojanaṃ sadṛśavastvantaravyavacchedenānupamatvadyotanam | upamāyāṃ dvayoḥ sādṛśyavarṇanamatra tvekasya tenaiveti bhedaḥ | indurinduriva śrīmānityatra indorekasyaivopamānatvamupameyatvaṃ ca varṇitamityananvayālaṅkāraḥ |

<ananvayasyāparamudāharaṇam>
gaganaṃ gaganākāraṃ sāgaraḥ sāgaropamaḥ|
rāmarāvaṇayoryuddhaṃ rāmarāvaṇayoriva ||
atra triṣvapyananvayeṣu krameṇa vaipulyagāmbhīryadāruṇatvānāṃ sādhāraṇadharmāṇāmupādānaṃ nāsti, indurinduriva śrīmānityatra tu caturṇāmapyavayavānāmupādānamastīti viśeṣaḥ|

<upameyopamālaṅkāraḥ>
paryāyeṇa dvayostaccedupameyopamā matā |
dharmortha iva pūrṇaśrīrartho dharma iva tvayi || 9 ||
dvayorupamānopameyayoḥ paryāyeṇa vākyabhedena tadupamānopameyatvaṃ vivakṣyate cedupameyopamālaṅkāraḥ| prathamavākye upamānatvena vivakṣitasya dvitīyavākye upameyatvaṃ, tathā prathamavākye upameyasya dvitīye upamānatvaṃ ca yatra varṇyate tatropameyopameti niṣkarṣaḥ| tṛtīyasadṛśavastu vyavacchedaḥ prayojanam| tvayi dharmaḥ arthaḥ iva pūrṇaśrīrityudāharaṇam| atra prathamavākye upameyopamānayordharmārthayordvitīyavākye upamānopameyatvaṃ yathākramaṃ varṇitamityupameyopamālaṅkāraḥ |
upameyopamāyā aparamudāharaṇam
khamiva jalaṃ jalamiva khaṃ haṃsa iva candraścandra iva haṃsaḥ|
kumudākārāstārāstārākārāṇi kumudāni ||
atra nirmalatvadhavalatvādayo dharmā nopāttāḥ,dharmo’rtha iveti pūrvodāharaṇe pūrṇaśrīriti dharma upātta iti viśeṣaḥ |

<pratīpālaṅkāraḥ>
prathamaṃ pratīpam
pratīpamupamānasyopameyatvaprakalpanam
tvallocanasamaṃ pahmaṃ tvadvaktrasadṛśo vidhuḥ||10||
upamānasya upamānatvena prasiddhasya candrāderupameyatvaprakalpanamiti yattatpratīpam| prasiddhopamānopameyayorvaiparītyakalpanātpratīpamityanvarthasaṃjñā |(he tanvī) tava locanasadṛśaṃ pahmam ityekamudāharaṇam tava vaktrasadṛśaḥ mukhatulyo vidhuścandra ityaparamudāharaṇam | tatrādye prasiddhopamānasya padmasya, dvitīye vidhoścopameyatvaṃ kalpitamiti lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ | upamānasyopameyatvakalpane upameyasyopamānatvaṃ siddhayati | tena copamānāpekṣayā upameyasyotkarṣaḥ dyotyate | tadeva pratīpaprayojanam | pratīpaṃ pañcavidham | tatredamādyaṃ pratīpam |
prathamapratīpasyāparamudāharaṇam
yattvannetrasamānakānti salile magnaṃ tadindīvaraṃ
meghairantaritaḥ priye tavamukhacchāyānukārī śaśī|
ye'pi tvadgamanānusāri gatayaste rājahaṃsā gatāḥ
tvatsādṛśyavinodamātramapi me daivena na kṣamyate ||
atra prasiddhopamānānāṃ indīvaraśaśirājahaṃsānāmupameyatvakalpanātpratīpam | kāntyādisādhāraṇadharmāṇāmatropādānamastīti pūrvasmādvailakṣaṇyam|
dvitīyaṃ pratīpam
anyopameyalābhena varṇyasyānādaraśca tat |
alaṃ garveṇa te vaktra kāntyā candro bhavādṛśaḥ ||11||
anyadavarṇyaṃ upamānaṃ, tadrūpaṃ yadupameyaṃ tasya lābhena prāptyā varṇyasya mukhādeḥ prakṛtasyānādarastiraskāra iti yat tat dvitīyaṃ pratīpam | upamānasyopameyatvakalpane samāne’pi varṇyasyānādaro’tra viśeṣaḥ | he vaktra ! te tava garveṇālaṃ, garvo māstu, yataḥ kāntyā guṇena candro’pi bhavādṛśastvattulyo’sti, ityudāharaṇam | atrāvarṇyacandrarūpopameyalābhena varṇyasya mukhasya “garvo māstvi”tyanādaravarṇanāt lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ|
dvitīyapratīpasyānyadudāharaṇam |
garvamasaṃvāhyamimaṃ locanayugalena kiṃ vahasi bhadre |
santīdṛśāni diśi diśi sarassu nanu nīlanalināni||
atra nīlanalinānāmupameyatvaṃ prakalpya locanayugalasya varṇyasya garvo māstvityanādaro darśitaḥ| sādhāraṇadharmastu nopātta iti pūrvodāharaṇādbhedaḥ |
tṛtīyaṃ pratīpam
varṇyopameyalābhena tathānyasyāpyanādaraḥ |
kaḥ krauryadarpaste mṛtyo tvattulyāḥ santi hi striyaḥ ||12||
varṇyaṃ yadupameyaṃ tasya lābhena anyasyāvarṇyasya prasiddhopamānasya anādaro’pi tathā, tṛtīyam pratīpam | utkṛṣṭaguṇatayānyadupameyamasahamānasyāvarṇyasya varṇyamupameyaṃ pradarśyānādaro’pi pratīpabheda ityarthaḥ| he mṛtyo ! te krūratāgarveṇālaṃ, tava garvo māstu, (kraurye) tvattulyāḥ striyaḥ santi ityudāharaṇam | atra varṇyamupameyaṃ krūrastriyaḥ, tallābhena mṛtyoravarṇyasya krauryaviṣaye prasiddhopamānasya anādaravarṇanāt lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ| dvitīyapratīpavaiparītyādevedaṃ pratīpam | idamatra cintyam | prasiddhopamānopameyabhāvaprātilomyātpratīpamityanvarthaṃ nāma dattam | tathā sati yatra tādṛśaṃ prātilobhyaṃ nāsti tatra kathaṃ pratīpālaṅkārabhedatvakalpanamiti | vastutastvasya pratīpabhedatvena parigaṇanaṃ na yuktam | upamānaprabhedatvaṃ parikalpya anādaropameti nāmakaraṇaṃ samucitaṃ syāditi pratibhāti | pūrvapratīpavaiparītyātpratīpamiti tu na samīcīnam |
tṛtīyapratīpasyānyadudāharaṇam
ahameva guruḥ sudāruṇānāmiti hālāhala ! māsma tāta! dṛpyaḥ |
nanu santi bhavādṛśāni bhūyo bhuvane’sminvacanāni durjanānām ||
atra dāruṇatāviṣaye hālāhalatulya durjanavacanarūpopameyalābhena avarṇyasya hālāhalasyānādara iti tṛtīyaṃ pratīpam |
caturthaṃ pratīpam
varṇyenānyasyopamāyā aniṣpattivacaśca tat |
mudhāpavādo mugdhākṣi tvanmukhābhaṃ kilāmbujam ||13||
varṇyena mukhādinā anyasyāvarṇyasya candrādeḥ (upameyatvena kalpitasya) upamāyā sādṛśyasyāniṣpativacanaṃ yattatpūrvebhyo’pyutkarṣaśāli caturthaṃ pratīpam | “he mugdhākṣi ambujaṃ tvanmukhābhaṃ tvanmukhatulyaśobhaṃ, (kileti vārtāyām ) iti vārtā mudhāpavādaḥ asatyo janapravādaḥ” ityudāharaṇam | atra ambujasya prasiddhopamānasya tanvīmukhenopameyena sādṛśyasyāniṣpattiḥ “mudhāpavāda” ityanenokta iti lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ|
caturthapratīpasyāparamudāharaṇam
ākarṇaya sarojākṣi vacanīyamidaṃ bhuvi |
śaśāṅkastava vaktreṇa pāmarairupamīyate ||
atra vaktreṇa śaśāṅkasya sādṛśyaṃ pāmarairevocyate, tacca vacanīyamityanena sādṛśyasyāniṣpattireva prakāśyate|
pañcamaṃ pratīpam
pratīpamupamānasya kaimarthyamapi manvate |
dṛṣṭaṃ cedvadanaṃ tanvyāḥ kiṃ padmena kimindunā || 14 ||
upamānasya candrādeḥ kaimarthyaṃ nirarthakatvakathanaṃ (tulyaguṇopameyalābhāddhetoḥ) pañcamaṃ pratīpam | tanvyāḥ vadanaṃ dṛṣṭaṃ cet padmenendunā vā kim ? kimapi prayojanaṃ nāstītyarthaḥ | atra tulyaguṇopameyasya mukhasya lābhāt prasiddhopamānayoḥ padmacandrayoniṣprayojanatvaṃ darśitamiti lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ | ko’thaḥ prayojanaṃ asyeti kimarthaḥ, tasya bhāvaḥ kaimarthyam |

viṣayī upamānabhūtaṃ padmādi | viṣayaḥ upameyabhūtaṃ mukhādi | viṣayiṇo rūpeṇa viṣayasya abhedatādrūpyābhyāṃ rañjanaṃ svoparaktabuddhiviṣayīkaraṇaṃ rūpakam | svapadopāttasya viṣayasya viṣayirūpeṇa rañjanaṃ kvacitprasiddhaviṣayyabhede paryavasitaṃ, kvacidbhede pratīyamāna eva tadīyadharmāropamātre paryavasitam | tataśca rūpakaṃ dvividham- abhedarūpakaṃ, tādrapyarūpakaṃ ceti | tadvividhamapi viṣayyapekṣayā viṣayasyādhikyoktyā, nyūnatvoktyā, anubhayoktyā ca pratyekaṃ trividham | tathā ca rūpakaṃ ṣaḍvidham, anubhayābhedarūpakam, nyūnābhedarūpakam, adhikābhedarūpakam, anubhayatādrūpyarūpakam, nyūnatādrūpyarūpakam, adhikatādrūpyarūpakam ceti| eteṣāṃ ṣaṇṇāṃ udāharaṇakrameṇātra nirdeśaḥ kṛtaḥ, na tu mūlaślokoddiṣṭakrameṇa| anenaiva krameṇodāharaṇāni dīyante| anubhayābhedarūpakasyodāharaṇam |

asyaivāparamudāharaṇam
kiṃ padmasya ruci na hanti nayanānandaṃ vidhatte na kiṃ
vṛddhi vā aṣaketanasya kurute nālokamātreṇa kim

atra viṣayaviṣayiṇormukhacandrayorbhede pratīyamāne’pi vaktrendā viti mukhe candratvāropaḥ candrakāryakāritvamātre paryavasitaḥ, utkarṣā pakarṣayorapratipādanāccānubhayatādrūpyarūpakam |
nyūnatādrūpyarūpakasyodāharaṇam
sādhvīyamaparā lakṣmīrasudhāsāgaroditā |
iyaṃ sādhvī pativratā asudhāsāgaroditā kṣīrasamudrādanutpannā aparā anyā lakṣmīḥ | atrāparā lakṣmīrityanena prasiddhalakṣmībhedaḥ asyā lakṣmyāḥ pratīyate | tathā ca sādhvyāṃ lakṣmītvāropo dharmamātrāropapayevasāyī| na sudhāsāgaroditeti nyūnatvoktyā ca nyūnatādrapyarūpakam |
asyaivāparamudāharaṇam
acaturvadano brahmā dvibāhuraparo hariḥ |
aphālalocanaḥ śambhurbhagavān bādarāyaṇaḥ ||

asyaivāparamudāharaṇam
kimasubhirglapitairjaḍa ! manyase mayi nimajjatu bhīmasutāmanaḥ|

atra damayantīkṛtacandropālambhe prasiddhacandraḥ na prāṇaniryāṇakālamanaḥ praveśārthakaśrutestātparyaviṣayaḥ, kiṃtu nalamukhacandra eveti prasiddhacandrāpekṣayā nalamukhacandrasyādhikya(utkarṣa)pratipādanādadhikatādrūpyarūpakam |
pariṇāmālaṅkāraḥ
pariṇāmaḥ kriyārthaścedviṣayī viṣayātmanā |
prasannena dṛgabjena vīkṣate madirekṣaṇā || 19 ||
viṣayī upamānaṃ viṣayātmanā upameyātmanā kriyārthaścet prakṛtakāryopayogī cetpariṇāmālaṅkāraḥ| atrāpi rūpakavadāropo’sti | paraṃtu āropyamāṇaḥ viṣayī viṣayarūpeṇa kāryopayogī bhavatīti viśeṣaḥ | rūpake tu na tathetyanayorbhedaḥ| viṣayaviṣayiṇoḥ svapadanirdiṣṭayorabhedapratipattirāropaḥ| madirekṣaṇā sundaralocanā tanvī prasannena nirmalena dṛgevābjaṃ dṛgaja tena vīkṣate paśyati ityudāharaṇam | atra dṛśi locane abjatvamāropitaṃ, tasya ca prakṛtavīkṣaṇakriyānvayaḥ svarūpato na sambhavatītyupameyadṛgātmanā pariṇāmādeva kriyopayogitvātpariṇāma ityanvartha nāma |

ullekho dvividhaḥ | nānādharmayoginaḥ ekasyānekairanekadhā lekhanarūpaḥ, ekasyaikenaivānekadhollakhanarūpaśceti | tatrādyasya lakṣaṇodāharaṇe śloke’smin pratipādyate | ekasyānekadharmaviśiṣṭasya bahubhiranekairgrahītṛbhirbahudhā anekaprakāreṇa ullekhāt viṣayīkaraṇādullekha ityanvarthanāmā alaṅkāraḥ| saḥ saundayaudāryaparākramaśālī rājā strībhiḥ kāmaḥ aikṣi, (strībhiḥ madana iti gṛhītaḥ), arthibhiḥ svadruḥ kalpavṛkṣaḥ aikṣi, (kalpavṛkṣa eveti tarkitaḥ), śatrubhiḥ kālaḥ antakaḥ aikṣi, (antakatvena dṛṣṭaḥ), ityudāharaṇam | atra eka eva rājā saundaryādyanekadharmaviśiṣṭaḥ bahubhiḥ syarthiśatrubhiḥ bahudhā kāmasvardvamāntakatvenollikhita iti lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ | ullekhe’pi bahutra rūpaka ivāropo’sti | kintvatra ekasmin viṣaye grahītṛbhedādviṣayabhedādvā bahūnāṃ viṣayiṇāmāropaḥ, rūpake tu na tatheti bhedaḥ |

ekena bahudhollekhepyasau viṣayabhedataḥ |

<utprekṣālaṅkāraḥ>
saṃbhāvanā syādutprekṣā vastuhetuphalātmanā|
uktānuktāspadāyātra siddhā’siddhāspade pare ||30||
saṃbhāvanā utprekṣā syāt | utprekṣā iti lakṣyanirdeśaḥ | saṃbhāvanā iti lakṣṇam | niścayabhinnaṃ sandehabhinnaṃ ca jñānaṃ saṃbhāvanā, utkaṭaikatarakoṭikaḥ saṃśayaḥ sambhāvaneti kecit | anyadharmasaṃbandhena nimittena anyasya anyatādātmyasaṃbhāvanaṃ utprekṣā iti niṣkarṣaḥ | utprekṣā trividhā vastu athavā svarūpotprekṣā hetūtprekṣā phalotprekṣā iti | vastunaḥ kasyacidvastvantaratādātmyasaṃbhāvanā vastūtprekṣā | vastutvaṃ ca hetutvena phalatvena vā vivakṣitādbhinnatvam | ahetoḥ hetubhāvena saṃbhāvanā hetūtprekṣā| aphalasya phalatvena saṃbhāvanā phalotprekṣā) atra trividhāyāmutprekṣāyāṃ ādyā vastūtprekṣā uktāspadā uktaviṣayā, anuktāspadā anuktaviṣayā, iti dvividhā | pare hetuphalotprekṣe siddhaviṣayā, asiddhaviṣayā ceti pratyekaṃ dvividhe | tathā ca ṣaḍvidhā utprekṣā| uktaviṣayā vastūtprekṣā, anuktaviṣayā vastūtprekṣā, siddhaviṣayā hetūprekṣā, asiddhaviṣayā hetūtprekṣā, siddhaviṣayā phalotprekṣā, asiddhaviṣayā phalotprekṣā | eteṣāṃ krameṇodāharaṇāni dīyante|
uktaviṣayā vastūtprekṣā |
dhūmastomaṃ tamaḥ śaṅke kokīvirahaśuṣmaṇām |
'ahaṃ tamaḥ andhakāraṃ kokīnāṃ cakravākāṅganānāṃ virahaśuṣmaṇāṃ virahāgnīnāṃ dhūmastomaṃ dhūmapaṭalaṃ śaṅke tarkayāmi, ityatra rātrau pravartamānamandhakārātmakaṃ vastu kokīvirahāgnīnāṃ dhūmapaṭalatvena kokīvirahānīnāṃ dhūmapaṭalatvena vastvātmanā utprekṣyate | atra ca nimittaṃ nīlatvadṛṣṭipratirodhakatvādi saṃbandhaḥ| tamaḥ dhūmastomaṃ śaṅke iti tamaso viṣayasyoktatvāduktaviṣayā vastūtprekṣā | śaṅke ityutprekṣādyotakaḥ śabdaḥ | evaṃ manye, dhruvam, prāyaḥ, nūnam, iva ityādayo’pi utprekṣādyotakāḥ jñātavyāḥ|

yasyāmītyudite tanvyāḥ valayo’bhavadurmikā ||

asyā aparamudāharaṇam
ādātuṃ sakṛdīkṣite’pi kusume hastāgramālohitaṃ
lākṣārañjanavārtayāpi sahasā raktaṃ talaṃ pādayoḥ |
aṅkānāmanulepanasmaraṇamapyantakhedāvahaṃ
hantādhīradṛśaḥ kimanyadalakāmodo’pi bhārāyate ||
atra sahajasaukumāryāyāḥ nāyikāyāḥ virahāvasthāyāṃ ādānecchayā kusume kṣaṇamātreṇa hastagraṃ lohitamabhūt, lākṣārasavārtayāpi pādatalaṃ raktamabhūdityādau kāraṇaprasaktimātreṇa kāryotpattivarṇanāt capalātiśayoktiḥ |
asyā evodāharaṇāntaram
yāmi na yāmīti dhave vadati purastātkṣaṇena tanvaṅgyāḥ |
galitāni puro valayāni, aparāṇi tathaiva dalitāni ||
atra yāmi na yamīti virahatadābhāvayoḥ prasaktimātreṇa yugapatsaṃjāta -kṛśatvapīnatvarupakāryadvārā purovalayagalanaṃ apareṣāṃ dalanaṃ ca yugapatsañjātamiti hetuprasaktijanyakāryayorapi samakālatvaṃ varṇitamiti viśeṣaḥ |
atyantātiśayoktiḥ
atyantātiśayoktiḥsyātpaurvāparyavyatikrame |
agre māno gataḥ paścādanunītā priyeṇa sā ||
kāryakāraṇayoḥ paurvāparyasya pūrvottarakramasya vyatikrame ullaṅghena sati atyantātiśayoktiḥ | pūrvaṃ kāraṇaṃ paścāt kāryaṃ iti siddhaṃ kramamullaṅghya kāryotpatteranantaraṃ kāraṇasaṃbhavavarṇanaṃ atyantātiśayoktirityarthaḥ | mānaḥ praṇayakopaḥ agre prathamaṃ gataḥ, paścāt sā nāyikā priyeṇa anunītā ityatra prasiddhakāraṇāt anunayāt pūrvameva kāryasya mānatyāgasyotpattivarṇanāt (kāryakāraṇayoḥ paurvāparyaviparyayarūpāyāḥ) atyantātiśayokteḥ udāharaṇamidam |
asyā evāmaparamudāharam
kavīndrāṇāmāsan prathamataramevāṅgaṇabhuvaḥ
calad bhruṅgāsaṅgākulakarimidāmodamadhurāḥ |
amī paścātteṣāmupari patitā rudranṛpateḥ
kaṭākṣāḥ kṣīrodaprasaraduruvīcīsahacarāḥ |||

<tulyayogitālaṅkāraḥ>
tulyānāṃ sādṛśyavatāṃ ygitā ekadharmānvayitvaṃ tulyayogitā | sā caturvidhā prastutaikadharmānvayarūpā, aprastutaikadharmānvayarūpā, hitāhitavṛttitaulyarupā, utkṛṣṭasamīkaraṇoktirupā ceti āsāṃ krameṇa lakṣaṇodāharaṇāni dīyante |
prathamadvitīye tulyayogite
varṇyānāmitareṣāṃ vā dharmaikyaṃ tulyayogitā |
saṃkucanti sarojāni svairiṇīvadanāni ca ||
varṇyānāṃ prastutānāmeva, athavā itareṣāṃ aprastutānāmeva dharmaikyaṃ guṇakriyārupaikadharmānvayaḥ tulyayogitālaṅkāraḥ | yatra dvayoḥ prastutayoreva, bahūnāṃ prastutānāmeva vā ekadharmānvayaḥ varṇyate tatra prathamā tulyayogitā | yatra dvayoḥ bahūnāṃ vā aprastutānameva ekadharmānvayaḥ tatra dvitīyā tulyayogitā iti niṣkarṣaḥ | mūle “varṇyānāṃ itareṣāṃ” ityubhayatra bahuvacanamanekatvābhiprāyeṇa prayuktam | tathā ca anekaprastutamātrasambandhaika dharmatāṃ prathamāyāḥ, anekāprastutamātrasambandhaikadharmatvaṃ dvitīyāyāśca tulyayogitāyā lakṣaṇaṃ |
sarojāni padmāni svairiṇīnām abhisārikāṇāṃ vadanāni mukhāni ca saṃkucanti saṅkocam prāpnuvanti iti (prathamāyāḥ) prastutatulyayogitāyāḥ udāharaṇam | candrodaye sarojāni saṃkucanti nimīlanti, svairiṇīvadanāni ca prakāśabhayāt saṃkucanti | tathā ca varṇyānāmeva sarojānāṃ svairiṇivadanānāṃ ca saṃkocārupaikakriyānvayavarṇyānāt prastutatulyayogitā |
tvadaṅkamārdave dṛṣṭe kasya citte na bhāsate |
mālatīśaśabhṛllekhākadalīnāṃ kaḍhoratā ||
he priye ! tava aṅgamārdave dṛṣṭe sati kasya sacetasaḥ citte mālatīkusumacandralekhākadalīnāṃ kaṭhoratā na bhāsate? iti (dvitīyāyāḥ) aprastutatulyayogitāyāḥ udāharaṇam | atra nāyikāsaukumāryavarṇane prastute, aprastutānāṃ mālatīśaśabhṛllekhākadalīnāṃ kaḍhoratārūpaikaguṇānvaya varṇānāt aprastutatulyayogitā |
prathamadvitīyayoḥ tulyayogitayoḥ apare udāharaṇe
saṃjātapatraprakarānvitāni samudvahanti sphuṭapāṭalatvam |
vikasvarāṇyarkakarābhimarśād dināni padmāni ca vṛddhimīyuḥ||
atra grīṣmavarṇane dināni padmāni ca prastutāni sūryakiraṇasaṃsparśād vṛddhiṃ āpuḥ iti prastutānāmeva vṛddhiprāptirūpaikakriyānvayavarṇanāt prastutatulyayogitā|
nāgendrahastāstvaci karkaśatvādekāntaśaityātkadalīviśeṣāḥ|
labdhvāpi loke pariṇāhi rūpaṃ jātāstadūrvorupamānabāhyāḥ ||
atra ūruvarṇane aprastutāni nāgendrahastāḥ kadalīviśeṣāśca | te ca ūrvorupamānabāhyāḥ jātāḥ iti aprastutānāṃ nāgendrahastakadalīviśeṣāṇāṃ upamānabāhyatvarūpaikadharmānvayavarṇanād aprastutatulyayogitā |
tṛtīyā tulyayogitā
hitāhite vṛttitaulyamaparā tulyayogitā|
pradīyate parābhūtirmitraśātravayostvayā ||44||
hitāhite mitrāmitraviṣaye vṛtteḥ vyāpārasya taulyaṃ sāmyaṃ ekavidhavyavaharaṇaṃ aparā anyā tṛtīyā tulyayogitā| he rājan ! tvayā mitraśātravayoḥ viṣaye (mitrebhyaḥ śatrubhyaśca) parābhūtiḥ parā utkṛṣṭā bhūtiraiśvarya, parābhavaśva pradīyate ityudāharaṇam | atra hite mitre, ahite śatrau ca parābhūtidānarūpasamānavyāpāravarṇanāt tulyayogitā| utkṛṣṭabhūtidānasya parābhavadānasya ca śleṣeṇābhedādhyavasānāt tulyatvam |
asyā evāparamudāharaṇam
yaśca nimbaṃ paraśunā, yaścainaṃ madhusarpiṣā |
yaścainaṃ gandhamālyādyaiḥ, sarvasya kaṭureva saḥ ||
atra vṛścati, siñcati, arcati iti kriyāpadānyadhyāhāryāṇi | paraśunā chedādahite madhusarpiṣā gandhamālyādibhiśca pūjanāt hiteca ubhayatra nimbaḥ kaṭureveti samānavyāpāravarṇanāt tulyayogitālakṣaṇasaṅgatiḥ| pūrvodāharaṇaṃ rājñaḥ stutiparyavasāyi, idaṃ tu nimbasya nindā-. paryavasāyīti bhedaḥ|
caturthī tulyayogitā
guṇotkṛṣṭaḥ samīkṛtya vaco’nyā tulyayogitā|
lokapālo yamaḥ pāśī śrīdaḥ śakro bhavānapi || 45 ||
gaṇotkṛṣṭaḥ śauryādiguṇaiḥ śreṣṭhaiḥ samīkṛtya samānaṃ kṛtvā (sāmyaṃ vāmitvā) vacaḥ vacanaṃ varṇanaṃ anyā caturthī tulyayogitā | utkṛṣṭaguṇaiḥ dharmataḥ samānikṛtya varṇanaṃ anyā tulyayogitetyarthaḥ | yamaḥ, pāśīvarūṇaḥ, śrīdaḥ, kuceraḥ, śakraḥ, indraḥ, bhavānapi lokapālaḥ ityatra varṇanīyo rājā utkṛṣṭaguṇaiḥ yamādibhiḥ lokapālatvarūpadharmataḥ samānīkṛtya varṇita iti lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ |
asyāḥ anyadudāharaṇam
saṅgatāni mṛgākṣīṇāṃ taḍidvilasitāni ca |
kṣaṇadvayaṃ na tiṣṭhanti dhanārabdhānyapi svayam ||
atra strīṇāṃ samāgamāḥ vidyutāṃ vilasitāni ca kṣaṇadvayaṃ na tiṣṭantīti kṣaṇadvayānavasthānarūpadharmataḥ mṛgākṣīsaṃgatānāṃ taḍitdilasitaḥ samīkṛtya pratipādanāt tulyayogitālakṣaṇasaṅgatiḥ | pūrvodāharaṇa stutiparyavasāyi, idaṃ tu nindāparyavasāyīti bhedaḥ |

<dīpakālaṅkāraḥ>
vadanti varṇyāvarṇyāṃnāṃ dharmaikyaṃ dīpakaṃ budhāḥ |
madena bhāti kalabhaḥ pratāpena mahīpatiḥ || 16 ||
varṇyāvarṇyānāṃ prastutāprastutānāṃ dharmaikyaṃ ekadharmānvayaḥ dīpakaṃ iti budhāḥ vadanti | atrāpi varṇyāvarṇyānāṃ iti bahuvacanaṃ anekābhiprāyeṇeti bodhyam | varṇyāvarṇyānvitaikadharme dīpakam iti lakṣaṇam | tulyayogitāyāṃ varṇyānāmeva, athavā avarṇyānāmeva ekatharmānvayaḥ, dīpake tu varṇyāvarṇyānāṃ iti bhedaḥ| kalabhaḥ karikalabhaḥ madena bhāti śobhate, mahīpatiḥ pratāpena śauryaṇa (bhāti śobhate) iti udāharaṇam | atra varṇyasya rājñaḥ avarṇyasya kalabhasya ca bhānarūpaikakriyānvayoṃ varṇita iti lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ| sādṛśyamatra gamyam | prastutopayogitayā prayukto dharmaḥ (atra bhānarūpaḥ) aprastutopayogyāpi bhavati, yathā gṛhapra kāśanārthaṃ pradīpito dīpaḥ rathyāmapi prakāśayati iti dīpasādṛśyāt dīpaka ityanvarthasaṃjñā | dīpa iva dīpakaṃ iti ivārthe kan pratyayaḥ |
dīpakasyāparamudāharaṇam
maṇiḥ śāṇollīḍhaḥ samaravijayī hetidalitaḥ
madakṣīṇo nāgaḥ śaradi saridāśyānapulinā|
kalāśeṣaścandraḥ suratamṛditā bālavanitā
tanimnā śobhante galitavibhavāścārthiṣu nṛpāḥ ||
atra arthiṣu galitavibhavāḥ nṛpāḥ, śāṇollīḍhamaṇyādayaśca tanimnā kāryena śobhante iti prakṛtānāṃ nṛpāṇāṃ aprakṛtānāṃ maṇyādīnāṃ ca śobhārūpaikadharmānvayavarṇanāt dīpakalakṣaṇasaṅgatiḥ |
āvṛttidīpakālaṅkāraḥ
trividhaṃ dīpakāvṛttau bhavedāvṛttidīpakam |
dīpakasya anekopakārārthatayā dīpasthānīyasya padasya, arthasya, ubhayo; āvṛttau satyāṃ āvṛttidīpakaṃ trividhaṃ bhavati padāvṛttidīpakam, arthāvṛttidīpakam, padārthobhayāvṛttidīpakam iti | eteṣāṃ krameṇodāharaṇāni dīyante|
padāvṛttidīpakasya udāharaṇam
varṣatyambudamāleyaṃ varṣatyeṣā ca śarvarī ||47||
iyaṃ ambudamālā medhapaṅktiḥ varṣati jalaṃ muñjati; eṣā śarvarī ca rātriśca varṣati varṣaḥ saṃvatsara ivācarati ityatra śleṣavaśāt prakṛtāprakṛtopakārayogyasya “varṣati” iti padasya vākyadvaye āvṛtteḥ satvāt, arthasya ca bhinnatvāt padāvṛttidīpakam | virahiṇīvacanatvāt rātreḥ saṃvatsarāyamānatvaṃ yuktam |vastutastvatra dīpakalakṣaṇaṃ nāsti meṣamālāyāḥ rātreśca prakṛtatvāt | tasmādāvṛttirevālaṅkāraḥ dīpakachāyāyāḥ satvāt āvṛttidīpakamiti vyapadeśaḥ|
asyevāparamudāharaṇam
utkaṇṭhayati meghānāṃ mālā varga kalāpinām |
yūnāṃ cotkaṇṭhayatyadya mānasaṃ makaradhvajaḥ ||
atra meghamālā mayūrasamūhaṃ utkaṇṭhayati ūrdhvakaṇṭaṃ karoti. makaradhvajaḥ yūnāṃ manaḥ utkaṇṭhayati utsukaṃ karoti iti arthabhede sati padasyāvṛtteḥ padāvṛttidīpakam |
arthāvṛttidīpakasya udāharaṇam
unmīlanti kadambāni sphuṭanti kuṭajadrumāḥ |
kadambāni kadambakusumāni unmīlanti, kuṭajadrumāḥ girimallikāḥ sphuṭanti vikasanti ityatra śabdayoḥ bhede’pi vikāsasyārthamasya āvṛteḥ arthāvṛttidīpakam |
asyaivāparamudāharaṇam
śamayati jaladharadhārā cātakayūnāṃ tṛpaṃ ciropanatān |
kṣapayati ca vadhūlocanajaladhārā kāmināṃ pravāsajatrim ||
atra śapayati, kṣapayati iti śabdayoḥ bhede’pi ekasyaivārthasya āvṛtiriti arthāvṛttidīpakam |
śabdārthobhayāvṛttidīpakasya udāharaṇam
mādyanti cātakāstṛptāḥ mādyanti ca śikhāvalāḥ || 48 ||
cātakāḥ (meghodakapānena) tṛptāḥ santuṣṭāḥ mādyanti mattā bhavanti, śikhāvalā mayūrāśca (meghadarśanena tadgarjanaśravaṇena ca) mādyanti mattā bhavanti ityatra mādyanti iti padasya nadaprātirūpārthamya ca āvṛtatvāt śabdārthobhayāvṛttidīpakam |
asyaivāparamudāharaṇam
vadanena nirjitaṃ tava nilīyate candrabimbamambudhare|
aravindamapi ca sundari ! nilīyate pāthasāṃ pūre||

vākyayoḥ upamānopameyavākyayoḥ ekasāmānye ekasmin samānadharme sati, pṛthaṅ nirdiṣṭaikasamānadharmānvaye sati ityarthaḥ, prativastūpamā matā dṛṣṭā | yatropamānopameyaghaṭitavākyayoḥ ekaḥ samānadharmaḥ śabdabhedena pṛthaṅ nidiśyate tatra prativastūpameti niṣkarṣaḥ | prativastuprativākyārthaṃ upamā samānadharmaḥ asyāmiti vyutpatteḥ anvarthanāmā alaṃkāraḥ | sūraḥ sūryaḥ tāpena ātapena bhrājate prakāśate, śūraḥ vīrapuruṣaḥ cāpena dhanuṣā rājate prakāśate ityudāharaṇam | atra bhrājate, rājate iti bhinnapadābhyāṃ eka eva dharmaḥ prakāśanarūpaḥ upamānopameyaghaṭitavākyayoḥ nirdiṣṭaḥ | sūraḥ upamānaṃ, śūraḥ upameyam |
prativastūpamāyā aparamudāharaṇam
sthirā śailī guṇavatāṃ khalabuddhayā na bādhyate |
ratnadīpasya hi śikhā vātyayāpi na nāśyate ||
śailī - sadbhutam | rānameva dīpaḥ ratnadīpaḥ | pūrvārdha upameyavākyam, uttarārdhaṃ upamānavākyam | nāśābhāvarūpaḥ samānadharmaḥ ubhayatra śabdabhedenopātta iti prativastūpamālakṣaṇasaṅgatiḥ |

<dṛṣṭāntālaṅkāraḥ>
cedvimbapratibimbatvaṃ dṛṣṭāntastadalaṅkatiḥ|
tvameva kīrtimāna rājan vidhureva hi kāntimān ||50||
vākyayorityanuvartate | vākyayoḥ upamānopameyavākyayoḥ bimbapratibimbatvaṃ cet, bhinnāveva dharmoṃ sādṛśyādabhinnatvena adhyavasitau pṛthak nirdiṣṭau cet tarhi dṛṣṭāntaḥ alaṅkatiḥ alaṅkāraḥ| he rājan tvameva kīrtimān, vidhuḥ candra eva kāntimān ityudāharaṇam | atra prathamamupameyavākyaṃ, dvitīyamupamānavākyam | tayoḥ kīrtimantrakāntimatvarūpau dharmoṃ bimbapratibimbabhāvena nirdiṣṭau iti lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ |
vastuto bhinnayoḥ sādṛśyādabhinnatayā adhyavasitayoḥ dharmayoḥ pṛthaka nirdeśaḥ bimbapratibimbabhāvaḥ | vastutaḥ abhinnasya (ekasyaiva dharmasya śabdabhedena pṛthagupādānaṃ vastuprativastubhāvaḥ | prativastūpamāyāṃ vastuprativastubhāvaḥ, dṛṣṭānte bimbapratibimvabhāva ityanayorbhedaḥ |
dṛṣṭāntasyāparamudāharaṇam
kāmaṃ nṛpāḥ santi sahasraśo’nye rājanvatīmāhuranena bhūmim |
nakṣatratārāgrahasaṃkulāpi jyotiṣmatī candramamaiva rātriḥ||
atra bhūmiḥ anena rājñā śobhanarājavatī, rātriḥ candreṇa jyotiṣmatī praśastajyotiryuktā iti vākyadvaye rājanvatī-jyotiṣmatīti bhinnayoḥ sadṛśayodharmayoḥ pṛthaṅ nirdeśāt bimbapratibimbabhāvena dṛṣṭāntālaṅkāraḥ|

<nidarśanālaṅkāraḥ>
nidarśanā trividhā vākyāthavṛttiḥ, padārthavṛttiḥ, sadasadarthabodhanarūpā ceti | āsāṃ krameṇa lakṣaṇodāharaṇāni dīyante |
vākyāthavṛttiḥ nidarśanā
vākyāthayoḥ sadṛśayorekyāgepo nidarśanā |
yaddātuḥ saumyatā seyaṃ pūrṇandorakalaṅkatā || 51 ||
sadṛśayoḥ tulyayoḥ vākyārthayoḥ aikyāropaḥ abhedāropaḥ (vākyārthavṛttiḥ) nidarśanā | dātuḥ vitaraṇaśīlasya saumyatā apārūṣyamiti yat sā iyaṃ pūrṇendoṃ akalaṅkatā kalakarahitatvam ityudāharaṇam | yathā pūrṇendorakalaṅkatā asaṃbhāvyā tathā dātuḥ saumyatā durlabheti bhāvaḥ | yaditi sāmānye napuṃsakam | seyamiti vidheyābhiprāyeṇa strīliṅgam | atra dātur aparuṣatvarūpopameyavākyārthasya, pūrṇacandrasya kalaṅkarahitatvarūpopamānavākyārthasya ca yattacchabdābhyāmabhedāroṃpāt vākyārthavṛttinidarśanā |
rūpake kevalopamānopameyayoḥ abhedāropaḥ, nidarśanāyāṃ | upamānopameyaghaṭitavākyārthayoḥ abhedāropa iti bhedaḥ | dṛṣṭānte sahaśayorvākyārthayoḥ bimbapratibimbabhāvena nirdeśaḥ, nidarśanāyāṃ tu aikyāropa iti bhedaḥ|
vākyāvṛttinidarśanāyāḥ aparamudāharaṇam
araṇyaruditaṃ kṛtaṃ śavaśarīramudvartitaṃ
sthale’jamavaropitaṃ suciramūṣare varṣitam |
śvapucchamavanāmitaṃ badhirakarṇajāpaḥ kṛto
dhṛto’ndhamukhadarpaṇo yadabudho janaḥ sevitaḥ ||
atra yadabudhoṃ janaḥ sevitaḥ tadaraṇyarudita kṛtamityādau abudhajanasevārūpopameyavākyārthasya araṇyaruditādirūpopamānavākyāthaiḥ abhedāropaḥ | araṇyaruditādivad abudhajanasevā niṣphaleti bhāvaḥ |
padārthavṛttiḥ nidarśanā
padārthavṛttimapyeke vadantyanyāṃ nidarśanām |
tvannetrayugalaṃ dhatte līlāṃ nīlāmbujanmanoḥ || 52 ||
eke kecit padārthavṛtti ekasmin padārthe anyapadārthadharmaśya āroparūpāṃ anyāmapi nidarśanāṃ vadanti | (he kānte) tvannatrayugalaṃ tava locanadvandva nīlāmbujanmanoḥ nīlāravindayoḥ līlāṃ vilāsaṃ dhatte gharati ityudāharaṇam | atra kāntānetrayugale anyapadārthasya nīlāravindayugalasya yo dharmo vilāsarūpaḥ tasya āropo’stīti padārthavṛttinidarśanā | atrāropyamāṇo dharmaḥ padapratipādya iti padārthavṛttitvam, nīlāravindalīlāyāḥ netre asaṃbhavāt līleva līlā iti sadṛśadharmākṣepāt āropaḥ aupamye paryavasyati |
viśiṣṭayodharmayoḥ aikyāropo vākyārthavṛttinidarśanā, upamānopameśyoranyatarasmin anyataradharmāropaḥ padārthavṛttinidarśanā ityanayorbhedaḥ |
padārthavṛttinidarśanāyā aparamudāharaṇam
viyoge gauḍanārīṇāṃ yo gaṇḍatalapāṇḍimā |
adṛśyata sa kharīmañjarīgabhareṇuṣu ||
atra anyadharmasya gauḍanārīgaṇḍapāṇḍinnaḥ anyasmin padārtha ravarjūrīmajjarīgarbhareṇuṣu āropātpadāthavṛttinidarśanā | pūrvodāharaṇe upameye upamānadharmāropaḥ, atratapamāne upameyadharmāropa iti bhedaḥ | atrāpyanyadharmasya anyatrāsaṃbhavāt tatsadṛśadharmākṣepeṇa aupamye paryavasānam |
sadasadathabodhanarūpā nidarśanā
aparāṃ bodhanaṃ prāhuḥ kriyayā asatsadarthayoḥ |
kriyayā, (kiñcikriyāviśiṣṭasya kasyacit ) svakriyayā (parān prati) asatsadarthayoḥ asataḥ doṣaphalasya, sataḥ guṇaphalasya vā bodhane jñāpane (varṇyamāne) aparāṃ pūrvavilakṣaṇāṃ anyāṃ nidarśanāṃ āhuḥ | tathā ceyamapi dvividhā kriyayā asadarthabodhanarūpā, kriyayā sadārthabodhanarūpā ceti | nidarśayati (kriyayā) pradarśayatīti nidarśanā |
kriyayā asadathabodhanarūpāyāḥ nidarśanāyā udāharaṇam
naśyedrājavirodhīti kṣīṇaṃ candrodaye tamaḥ || 53 ||
rājñaḥ virodhī naśyet nāśamāmuyād iti (parān bodhayan ) candrodaye tamaḥ andhakāraḥ kṣīṇaṃ naṣṭam ityatra candrodaye tamasaḥ kṣayarūpakriyayā rājavirodhī naśyediti doṣaphalasya asadarthasya bodhanāt lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ | rājaśabde śleṣeṇa nṛpaticandrayoḥ pratipatiḥ|
asyā evāparamudāharaṇama
unnataṃ padamavāpya yo laghuḥ helayaiva sa patediti bruvan |
śailaśekharagataḥ pṛṣadgaṇaḥ cārumārutadhutaḥ patatyadhaḥ ||
atra parvataśikharagataḥ vṛṣṭibinduḥ mandamārutamātreṇa adhaḥ lāghavayukto yadi unnataṃ padamārohati tarhi anāyāsena adha. kriyayā asadarthabodhanam |
kriyayā sadarthabodhanarūpāyā nidarśanāyā udāharaṇam
udayanneva savitā padmaṣvarpayati śriyam |
vibhāvayan samṛddhīnāṃ phalaṃ suhṛdanugrahaḥ || 54||
savitā sūryaḥ udayanneva abhyudayaṃ prāpnuvanneva samṛddhīnāṃ sampadāṃ phalaṃ suhṛdanugrahaḥ iti vibhāvayan parān prati bodhayan pajheṣu svasuhṛtyu śriyaṃ śobhāmarpayati ityatra pajheṣu śobhārpaṇarūpa kriyayā sūryaḥ parān prati ahamivānyo’pi svodayakāle suhṛdbhayaḥ samṛddhiṃ dadyāt iti śreyaskaramarthaṃ bodhayatīti varṇanāt sadarthabodhanarūpā nidarśanā|
asyā evāparamudāharaṇam
cūḍāmaṇipade dhatte yo devaṃ ravimāgatam
satāṃ kāryātitheyīti bodhayan gṛhamedhinaḥ ||
atra udayagiriḥ prāta kāle samāgataṃ sūryaṃ śirasi vahan gṛhasthaiḥ atithīnāṃ saparyā kartavyeti svakriyayā bodhayatīti varṇanāt sadarthabodhanarūpā nidarśanā|

<vyatirekālaṅkāraḥ>
vyatireko viśeṣaścedupamānopameyayoḥ |
śailā ivonnatāḥ santaḥ kintu prakṛtikomalāḥ || 55 ||
upamānopameyayoḥ viśeṣo vailakṣaṇyaṃ (varṇitaṃ) cet vyatirekaḥ alaṃkāraḥ | santaḥ sajjanāḥ śailāḥ parvatā iva unnatāḥ (śailā ākāre, santastu cite aunnatyayuktāḥ), kintu (santaḥ) prakṛtyā svabhāvena komalā mṛdulāḥ, (śailāstu kaṭhinā iti bhāvaḥ)| atra prakṛtikomalā iti upamānabhūtaśailebhyaḥ upameyabhūtasajjanānāṃ vailakṣaṇyaṃ varṇitamiti vyatirekalakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ |
(vyatirako nāma vailakṣaṇyam | tacca kadācidupamānāpekṣayā upameyasya ādhikye nyūnatāyāṃ anubhaye vā paryavasyati | atra upameyotkarṣaparyavasāyī |)
sahoktaraparamudāharaṇam
chāyā saṃśrayate talaṃ viṭapināṃ śrānteva pānthaiḥ samaṃ
mūlaṃ yāti sarojalasya jaḍatā glāneva mīnaiḥ saha |
ācāmatyahimāṃśudīdhitirapastapteva lokaiḥ samaṃ
nidrā garbhagṛhaṃ saha praviśati klānteva kāntājanaiḥ ||
atra madhyāhnavarṇane chāyā pānthaiḥ saha tarutalaṃ saṃśrayate, jaḍatā śītalatā mīnaiḥ saha jalādhobhāgaṃ gacchati, sūryadīdhitir lokaiḥ saha jalaṃ pibati, nidrā kāntājanaiḥ sahābhyantaragṛhaṃ gacchatīti janarañjanasahabhāvavarṇanāt sahoktiḥ| anena sārdhaṃ viharāmburāśestīreṣu tālīdalamarmareṣu ityādau sahabhāvasya acamatkāritvāt na sahotyalaṅkāraḥ|

<vinoktyalaṅkāraḥ>
ādyā vinoktiḥ
vinoktiścedvinā kiñcitprastuta hīnamucyate |
vidyā hṛdyāpi sāvadyā vinā vinayasaṃpadam ||57||
kiñcidvinā prastutaṃ varṇyaṃ vastu hīnaṃ nikṛṣṭaṃ ucyate cet vinoktiralaṅkāraḥ| vidyā hṛdyāpi manoharāpi vinayasampadaṃ vinā avadyā duṣṭā ityudāharaṇam | atra kiñcidvinā vinayasamṛddhiṃ vinā prastutasya vidyāyāḥ hīnatvokteḥ lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ | vinoktiḥ dvividhā| tatreyamādyā vinoktiḥ|
asyā aparamudāharaṇam
yaśca rāmaṃ na paśyettu yaṃ ca rāmo na paśyati |
ninditaḥ sa bhavelloke svātmāpyenaṃ vigarhate ||
atra rāmadarśanamaprāptavataḥ hīnatvaṃ vināśabdaprayogaṃ vinaiva darśitamiti pūrvodāharaṇādbhedaḥ| rāmadarśanena vinā puruṣaḥ nindito bhavatītyarthaḥ |
dvitīyā vinoktiḥ
tacvetkiñcidvinā ramyaṃ vinoktiḥ sāpi kathyate|
vinā khalairvibhātyeṣā rājendra ! bhavataḥ sabhā ||58||
tacchabdenātra prastutaṃ parāmṛśyate| tat prastutaṃ kiñcidvinā ramya sundaraṃ cen sāpi vinoktiriti kathyate| he rājendra ! bhavataḥ sabhā khalaivinā durjanairvinā vibhāti ityatra
sabhāyāḥ khalasaṃparkaṃ vinā ramyatvavarṇanād dvitīyā vinoktiḥ|
asyā aparamudāharaṇam
āvirbhūbhate śaśini tamasā mucyamāneva rātriḥ
naiśasyārcihutabhuja iva chinnabhūyiṣṭhadhūmā|
mohenāntarvaratanuriyaṃ lakṣyate muktakalpā
gaṅgā rodhaḥpatanakaluṣā gṛhṇatīva prasādam ||
atra tamasā vinā rātre, dhūmena vinā hutabhujaḥ, mohena vinā tanvyāḥ, kāluṣyeṇa
vinā gaṅgāyāśca ramyatā vināśabdaprayogaṃ vinaiva ni bodhiteti pūrvodāharaṇādbhedaḥ|

<samāsoktyalaṅkāraḥ>
samāsoktiḥ parisphūrtiḥ prastute’prastutasya cet |
ayamaindrīmukha paśya raktaścumbati candramāḥ || 59||
prastute prastutavṛttānta varṇyamāne viśeṣaṇasāmyāt aprastutasya aprastutavṛttāntasyāpi parisphūrtiḥ pratītiḥ cet samāsoktiralaṅkāraḥ| samāsena saṃkṣepeṇa prastutāprastutayoḥ uktirityanvarthasaṃjñā| ayaṃ raktaḥcandramāḥ aindrayāḥ prācīdiśaḥ
mukhaṃ prārambhabhāgaṃ cumbati spṛśati paśya, ityudāharaṇam | atra candrodayavaṇene prastute raktādiviśeṣaṇa sāmyāta nāyikānāyakavṛttāntasyāprastutasya pratīter lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ | tathāhi raktaśabdasya śleṣeṇa lohitānuraktasādhāraṇyāt, mukhaśabdasya prārambhavadanasādhāraṇyāt, cumbatītyasya saṃsparśavadanasaṃyogasādhāraṇyāt “candramaḥ” śabdagatapuṃliṅgena, “aindrī”śabdagatastrīliḍena tatpratipādyendrasambandhitvena ca paravanitāsaktakāmukavṛttāntaḥ pratīyate, sa ca pratīto’rthaḥ prastutameva candrodayavṛttāntaṃ upaskaroti ityalaṅkāratvam | samāsoktau aprastutavyavahārasamāropaḥ, rūpake tu aprastutarūpasamāropa iti bhedaḥ|
samāsokteḥ aparamudāharaṇam
vyāvalgatkucabhāramākulakacaṃ vyālolahārāvali
preṅkhatkuṇḍalaśobhigaṇḍayugalaṃ prasvedivakrāāmbujam| śaśvaddattakaraprahāramadhikaśvāsaṃ rasādetayā
yasmātkanduka! sādaraṃ subhagayā saṃsevyase tatkṛtī||
atra kandukakrīḍāvṛttānte varṇyamāne’vyāvalgatkucabhāraṃ ityādikriyāviśeṣaṇa sāmyāt viparītaratāsaktanāyikāvṛttāntaḥ pratīyate| pūrvodāharaṇe viśeṣaṇāni śliṣṭāni, atra tu śleṣaṃ vinaiva prakṛtāprakṛtayoranugatāni iti bhedaḥ|

<parikarālaṅkāraḥ>
alaṅkāraḥ parikaraḥ sābhiprāye viśepaṇe |
sudhāṃśukalitottaṃsaḥ tāpaṃ haratu vaḥ śivaḥ || 60||
viśeṣaṇe sābhiprāye sati, prakṛtārthopapādakasya arthaviśeṣasya vyañjakatvābhiprāyeṇa viśeṣaṇaprayoge sati, parikaro nāma alaṅkāraḥ| atra viśeṣaṇe ityekavacanamavivakṣitam | vyaṅgayasyārthasya vācya the prati parikaratvāt aṅgatvāt parikarālaṅkāra iti vyapadeśaḥ | sudhāṃśunā kalitaḥ kṛtaḥ uttaṃsaḥ śirobhūṣaṇaṃ yena saḥ śivaḥ vaḥ yuṣma,kaṃ tāpaṃ duḥkha haratu ityudāharaṇam | atra sudhāṃśukalitottaṃsa iti viśeṣaṇa prakṛtatāpaharaṇarūpārthopapādakatvena sābhiprāyaṃ prayuktam | sudhāṃśohi tāpaharaṇasāmaya upapanna, tadviśiṣṭaśca śivaḥ manastāpaṃ haratu iti prakṛtārthopaskārakatvaṃ vyajyamānasyārthasyeti lakṣaṇasaṅgatiḥ |
parikarasyāparamudāharaṇam
tava prasādānusumāyudho’pi sahāyamekaṃ madhumeva labdhvā |
kuryāṃ harasyāpi pinākapāṇer dhairyacyutiṃ ke mama dhanvino’nye|
atra pinākapāṇeriti viśeṣaṇaṃ śivasya prabalāyuṣatvaṃ, kusumāyudha iti viśeṣaṇaṃ manmathasya nissārāyudhatvaṃ ca dyotayataḥ | tena ca kaimuti kanyāyena pinākapāṇerapi dhairyacyutiṃ kuryāṃ, kimuta vaktavyamanyadhanvināmiti sarvadhanvidhairyabhañjakatvarūpo vācyārtha evopaskriyata iti parikarālaṅkāraḥ|

<parikarāṅkurālaṅkāraḥ>

caturṇā puruṣārthānāṃ dātā devaścaturbhujaḥ || 61||
viśeṣye sābhiprāye sati prakṛtārthasaṃpādanasamarthārthavyañjake sati parikarāṅkuro nāmālaṃkāraḥ| caturṇā puruṣārthānāṃ dharmārthakāmamokṣāṇāṃ dātā devaḥ caturbhujo viṣṇuḥ ityatra caturbhuja iti viśeṣyapadaṃ puruṣārtha catuṣṭayadānasāmarthyābhiprāyeṇa prayuktamiti parikarāṅkurasya udāharaṇamidam |
parikarārasyāparamudāharaṇam
phaṇīndraste guṇānvaktuṃ likhituṃ haihayādhipaḥ|

<śleṣālaṅkāraḥ>

sarvaṃ dadātīti sarvadaḥ sakalābhīṣṭapradātā, māyāḥ lakṣmyāḥ dhavaḥ patiḥ viṣṇur yo’gaṃ parvataṃ govardhana, gāṃ pṛthivīṃ ca adhīgharat dhṛtavān saḥ pāyāt rakṣet ityekaḥ prakṛtārthaḥ | sarvadā umāyāḥ dhavaḥ śivaḥ, yaḥ ḍāmadhīdharat saḥ pāyāditi dvitīyo’pyarthaḥ prakṛta eveti prakṛtaśleṣaḥ |
asyaivāparamudāharaṇam
trātaḥ kākodaro yena dogdhāpi karuṇātmanā |
pūtanāmāraṇakhyātaḥ sa me’stu śaraṇaṃ prabhuḥ ||
yena karuṇātmanā rāmeṇa adaraḥ bhayarahitaḥ drogdhāpi kākoṃ rakṣitaḥ, pūtanāmā pavitranāmadheyaḥ raṇe yuddhe khyātaḥ saḥ me śaraṇam, yena kākodaraḥ sarpaḥ (kāliyaḥ) rakṣitaḥ, pūtanāyāḥ māraṇena khyātaḥ saḥ kṛṣṇo me śaraṇam ityatra ubhayorapi rāmakṛṣṇayoḥ stotavyatvena prakṛtatvāt prakṛtaśleṣaḥ |
aprakṛtaślepasyodāharaṇam
abjena tvanmugvaṃ tulyaṃ hariṇāhitasaktinā |
tava mukha hariṇā sūryeṇa āhitasaktinā kṛtāsaktinā ajena pamena tulyam ityeko'rthaḥ | tvanmukha hariṇena mṛgeṇa āhitasaktinā kṛtasaṅgena abjena candreṇa tulyamiti dvitīyo’rthaḥ | ubhayorapi padmacandrayo. upamānatvena aprakṛtatvād aprakṛtaśleṣaḥ |
asyaivāparamudāharaṇam
nītānāmākulībhāvaṃ lubdhairbhūriśilīmukhaiḥ|
sadṛśe vanavṛddhānāṃ kamalānāṃ tvadīkṣiṇe ||
tava īkṣaṇe lubdhai madhulolupaiḥ bhūriśilīmuravaiḥ bahubhiḥ bhramaraiḥ ākulībhāvaṃ nītānāṃ vane jale vṛddhānāṃ vṛddhi prāptānāṃ kamalānāṃ padmānāṃ sadṛśe, lubdhaiḥ vyādhaiḥ śilīmuravaiḥ bāṇaiḥ ākulībhāvaṃ nītānāṃ bane araṇye vṛddhānāṃ hariṇānāṃ sadṛśe iti padāhariṇayoḥ upamānatvenāprakṛta vād aprakṛtaśleṣaḥ | "mṛgapabhede kamalaḥ" iti viśvaḥ |
prakṛtāprakṛtaśleṣasya udāharaṇam
uccaradbharikīlālaḥ śuśubhe vāhinīpatiḥ || 63 ||
uccarad udgacchad bhūri kīlāla śoṇitaṃ yasya saḥ vāhinīpatiḥ lenāpatiḥ śuśubhe iti prākaraṇikārthaḥ | uccarada bhūri kīlālaṃ udakaṃ yasya saḥ vāhinīnāṃ nadīnāṃ patiḥ samudraḥ śuśubhe ityaprākaraṇikārthaḥ | jā ca śliṣpadānāṃ prakṛtāprakṛtārthagocaratvāt prakṛtāprakṛtaśleṣaḥ | (atra prakṛtāprakṛtayoḥ sādṛśyaṃ pratīyate)
asyaivāparamudāharaṇam
asāvudayamārūḍhaḥ kāntimān raktamaṇḍalaḥ |
rājā harati lokasya hṛdayaṃ mṛdulaiḥ karaiḥ||
atra udayamabhivṛddhi prāpto raktamaṇḍalo’anuraktaprajaḥ rājā nṛpatiḥ mṛdulaiḥ karaiḥ grāhyadhanaiḥ lokahṛdayaṃ harati āvarjayatīti prākaraṇikārthaḥ | udayagirimārūḍhaḥ aruṇamaṇḍalo rājā candraḥ mṛdu kiraṇair janahṛdayamākapatīti aprākaraṇikārthaḥ | śliṣṭapadānāṃ prakṛtā prakṛtārthagocaratvātprakṛtāprakṛtaśleṣaḥ | idamatrāvadheyam “sarvado mādhavaḥ”, “yo’gaṃgām”, “iśi hitasaktinā” ityādau paraspara vilakṣaṇa padabhagamapekṣya nānārthabodhanāta sabhaśleṣaḥ| “abjakīlālavāhinīpati” prabhṛtiṣu padeṣu tāmā padabhaḍamanapekṣyaiva nānārthabodhanād abhaṅgaśleṣaḥ iti prakārāntareṇāti śleṣasya dvaividhyam | prakṛtāprakṛtaśleṣe aprakṛtārthasya vyaṅgayatvamiti keṣāṃcinmatam | anye tu tasyāpi vācyatvamaṅgīkṛtya prakṛtāprakṛtayorabhedaḥ sādṛśyaṃ vā vyaṅgayamiti vadanti | atra prathamamate prakṛtāprakṛtaśleṣo nodāhartavyaḥ | dvitīyamate śabdaśaktimūlavastudhvanir nodāhartavyaḥ |

<aprastutapraśaṃsālaṅkāraḥ>
aprastutapraśaṃsā syāt sā yatra prastutāśrayā|
ekaḥ kṛtī śakuntepu yo’nyaṃ śakrānna yācate || 64 ||
aprastutamya praśaṃsā varṇanaṃ yatra prastutāśrayā prastutavṛttāntāvagati paryavasāyinī sā aprastutapraśaṃsā| yatra prastutavṛttāntāvagamanoddeśena aprastuvṛttānto varṇyate tatra aprastutapraśaṃsā iti niṣkarṣaḥ| śakunteṣu pakṣiṣu madhye yaḥ śakrād anya indrādanyaṃ prabhuṃ na yācate saḥ ekaḥ cātaka eva kṛtī kuśala ityudāharaṇam | atra sārvabhauma vinā anya śaṃdra prabhu na prārthaye ityabhimānaśālino vṛttāntasya prastutasya pratyāyanārtha tatsadṛśacātakavṛttānto varṇita iti lakṣaṇasaṅgatiḥ|
aprastutena prastutāvagatiḥ prastutāprastutayoḥ saṃbandhe sati bhavati | saṃvandhaśca sārūpyaṃ, sāmānyaviśeṣabhāvaḥ, kāryakāraṇabhāvā vā saṃbhavati | “ekaḥ kṛtī” ityādi sārūpyanibandhanāyāḥ aprastutapraśaṃsāyā udāharaṇam| sāmānyaviśeṣabhāve sāmānyādviśeṣasya, viśeṣātsāmānyasya vā avagatiriti dvaividhyam | evaṃ kāryakāraṇabhāve’pi kāryātkāraṇasya, kāraṇāt kāryasya vā avagatiriti dvaividhyam | eteṣāṃ caturṇā udāharaṇāni anubandhe draṣṭavyāni |
samāsoktau śliṣṭaviśeṣaṇabalāt prastutādaprastutavṛttāntāvagatiḥ, aprastutapraśaṃsāyāṃ sārūpyādisaṃbandhena aprastutāt prastutapratītir ityanayorbhedaḥ|
aprastutapraśaṃsāyā aparamudāharaṇam
ābaddhakṛtrimasaṭājaṭilāṃsabhittiḥ
āropito mṛgapateḥ padavīṃ yadi śvā|
maṃttebhakumbhataṭapāṭanalampaṭasya
nādaṃ kariṣyati kathaṃ hariṇādhipasya |
atra mṛgapateḥ padamāropito’pi śvā siṃhasya nādaṃ na kariṣyatīti aprastutaśunakavṛttāntavarṇanaṃ tatsārūpyāt kṛtrimaveṣavyavahāramātreṇa vidvajjanavadabhinayato mūrkhasya vṛttānte paryavasyatītyaprastutapraśaṃsā |

<prastutāikurālaṅkāraḥ>
prastutena prastutasya dyotane prastutāṅkuraḥ |
kiṃ bhṛṅga satyāṃ mālatyāṃ ketakyā kaṇṭakeddhayā ||65||
prastutena varṇyamānena anyasyābhimatasya prastutasyaiva dyotane prastutāṅkuro nāmālaṅkāraḥ | he bhṛṅga mālatyāṃ satyāṃ vidyamānāyāṃ kaṇṭakeddhayā kaṇṭakairvyāptayā ketakyā kiṃ ityudāharaṇam | atra priyatamena sākaṃ udyāne viharantyāḥ bahudoṣayuktaparavanitāsaṅgāt prastutāṅkurālakāraḥ| kāntaṃ nirvartayitumicchantyāḥ kulavanitāyāḥ vacane vācyārthasya bhṛṅgopāttasya paravanitāsaktamiyopālambharūpārthasya ca prastutatvameveti lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ |
prastutāṅkurasyāparamudāharaṇam |
anyāsu tāvadupamardasahāsu bhṛṅga
lolaṃ vinodaya manaḥ sumanolatāsu|
bālāmajātarajasaṃ kalikāmakāle
vyartha kadarthayasi kiṃ navamallikāyāḥ|
atrāpi bhṛṅgopālambhasya vācyārthasya tathā prauḍhāṅganāsu satīṣu bālikāṃ rataye kleśayataḥ kāminaḥ upālambharūpadyotyārthasya ca prastutatvāt prastutāṅkurālaṅkāraḥ | anayorudāharaṇayoḥ vācyārthasya prastutatvena tatrābhidhāyāḥ viśrānteḥ arthasaundaryabalādvivakṣitārthapratītirdhvanireva, na alaṅkāraḥ; yatra pratīyamānasya vācyārthopaskārakatvaṃ tatraivālaṅkāratvam iti kecit| tanmate idaṃ vakṣyamāṇaṃ prastutāṅkurālaṅkārasya udāharaṇam
kośadvandvamiyaṃ dadhāti nalinī kādambacañcukṣataṃ |
dhatte cūtalatā navaṃ kisalayaṃ puṃskokilāsvāditam |
ityākarṇya mithaḥ sakhījanavacaḥ sā dīrghikāyāstaṭe
celāntena tirodadhe stanataṭaṃ bimbādharaṃ pāṇinā ||
iyaṃ nalinī haṃsacañcukṣataṃ mukuladvandvaṃ dhatte, tathā iyaṃ cūtalatā kokilāsvāditaṃ navaṃ kisalayaṃ dhatte iti vāpītaṭe sakhījanasya parasparālāpaṃ śrutvā prastutā nāyikā kamalamukulacūtapallavavyājena ātmanaḥ stanādharakṣatavṛttāntaṃ kathayatīti jñātvā stanataṭaṃ vastrāntena, adharaṃ ca pāṇinā tirodadhe ityatra vyaṅgayārthasya prastutāntarasya svayameva kavinā uttarārdhe āviṣkṛtatvāt alaṅkāratvam| tathācoktaṃ dhvanyāloke -
śabdārthaśaktyā vākṣipto vyaṅgayo’rthaḥ kavinā punaḥ|
yatrāviṣkriyate svoktyā sānyaivālakṛtirdhvaneḥ ||

<paryāyoktālaṅkāraḥ>
prathamaṃ paryāyottam
paryāyoktaṃ tu gamyasya vaco bhaṅgayantarāśrayam |
namastasmai kṛtau yena mudhā rāhuvadhūkucau ||66||
gamyasya vivakṣitasya arthasya bhaṅgayantarāśrayaṃ vacaḥ vivakṣitādapi cārutareṇa prakāreṇa abhidhānaṃ paryāyoktam, paryāyeṇa bhaṅgayantareṇa uktam,abhihita vivakṣitaṃ yatra iti vyutpatteḥ | yena rāhuvadhūkucau mudhā vyarthau kṛtau, rāhoḥ śiromātrāvaśeṣatvakaraṇāt, tasmai vāsudevāya namaḥ ityudāharaṇam | atra svāsādhāraṇarūpeṇa vāsudevatvena gamyasya bhagavataḥ bhaṅgayantareṇa rāhuvadhūkucavaiyarthyakāritvena abhidhānāt paryāyoktalakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ|
asyaivāparamudāharaṇam-
lokaṃ paśyati yasyāḍghriḥ sa yasyāṅghriṃ na paśyati |
tābhyāmapyaparicchedyā vidyā viśvagurostava ||
yasyākṣapādasya aṅghriḥ lokaṃ paśyati sa ca lokaḥ yasya patañjaleḥ (ādiśeṣāvatārasya) aṅghriṃ na paśyati tābhyāṃ gautamapatañjalibhyāṃ viśvagurostava vidyā aparicchedyā ityatra svāsādhāraṇarūpābhyāṃ akṣapādatvapatañjalitvābhyāṃ gamyau rūpāntarābhyāmabhihitāviti paryāyoktam |
dvitīyaṃ paryāyoktam
paryāyoktaṃ tadapyāhuḥ yad vyājeneṣṭasādhanam ||
yāmi cūtalatāṃ draṣṭuṃ yuvābhyāmāsyatāmiha || 67||
vyājena kāryāntaracchalena iṣṭasyārthasya svasya parasya vā iṣṭasya kāryasya sādhanamiti yat tadapi paryāyoktaṃ āhuḥ| kāryāntaravyājena iṣṭakāryasādhanaṃ dvitīyaṃ paryāyoktamiti niṣkarṣaḥ| ahaṃ cūtalatāṃ draṣṭuṃ yāmi gacchāmi, yuvābhyāṃ iha atraiva āsyatāṃ sthīyatāṃ ityudāharaṇam | atra svasannidhau lajjamānayoḥ nāyikānāyakayoḥ vihārāya viviktatāsaṃpādanamicchantī sakhī cūtalatādarśanavyājena svābhīṣṭaṃ nāyikānāyakayoriṣṭaṃ vā sādhayatīti dvitīyaparyāyoktalakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ |
asyāmaparamudāharaṇam
dehi matkandukaṃ rādhe paridhānanigūhitam |
iti visraṃsayannīvīṃ tasyāḥ kṛṣṇo mude’stu naḥ||
atra kandukasadbhāvaśodhanārthaṃ nīvīvisraṃsanavyājena kṛṣṇaḥ svābhīṣṭaṃ sādhayatīti dvitīyaṃ paryāyoktam |

<vyājastutyalaṅkāraḥ>
uktirvyājastutinindāstutibhyāṃ stutinindayoḥ |
nindāstutibhyāṃ stutinindayoḥ uktiḥ abhivyaktiḥ vyājastutyalaṅkāraḥ| nindāvyājena stuteḥ, stutivyājena nindāyāścāvagamane vyājastutiriti niṣkarṣaḥ| tathā ca vyājastutiḥ dvividhā nindayā stutiḥ, stutyā nindā ceti | krameṇa, vyājena nindāvyājena stutiḥ, vyājarūpeṇa stutiśca iti ca nindāvyājastutiśabdasya vyutpattiḥ iti bodhyā |
nindayā stuteravagamanarūpāyāḥ vyājastuteḥ udāharaṇam
kā svardhuni vivekaste nayase pāpino divam || 68||
he svardhani gaṅge te vivekaḥ kaḥ ? nāsti te vivekaḥ (yasmāt ) pāpinaḥ divaṃ svarga nayase ityatra tava viveko nāstīti nindāvyājena mahāpātakināmapi pāpapraśamanena svargapradānaśakteti gaṅgāyāḥ prabhāvātiśayastutireva kriyata iti vyājastutiḥ |
asyāḥ aparamadāharaṇam-
kaste śauryamado yoddhuṃ tvayyekaṃ saptimāsthite|
saptasaptisamārūḍhā bhavanti paripanthinaḥ ||
atra yoddhuṃ rājñi ekāśvasamārūḍhe sati tacchatravaḥ saptāśvasamārūḍhāḥ bhavanti, tasmāt māstu rājñaḥ śauryamada iti nindāvyājena rājñā yuddhe hatāḥ sūryamaṇḍalaṃ bhitvā divaṃ gacchantīti śauryastutireva kriyata iti nindayā stutirūpā vyājastutiḥ | sapta saptayaḥ aśvāḥ yasya saḥ sūryaḥ, sapta ca te saptayaśca ityubhayathā yojanā |
stutyā nindāvagamanarūpāyāḥ vyājastuteḥ udāharaṇam
sādhu dūti punaḥ sādhu kartavyaṃ kimataḥ param |
yanmadarthaṃ vilūnāsi dantairapi nakhairapi || 69 ||
he dūti sādhu, punaḥ sādhu (tvayā praśaṃsārhaṃ kimapi kṛtaṃ, tasmāt ) ślāghanīyāsi | ataḥ paraṃ kartavyaṃ kimasti ? yadyasmāt tvaṃ madartha nakhaiḥ dantaiśca vilūnā vidāritā asi ityatra nāyakaṃ samānetuṃ preṣitā dūtī svayaṃ tatsambhogamanubhūya samāgatā śarīre paridṛśyamānadantanakhakṣataiḥ jñātatattvayā nāyikayā evaṃ stutivyājena nindyate | tasmāt stutyā nindāparyavasāyinī vyājastutiriyam |
asyā aparamudāharaṇam
ardha dānavavairiṇā girijayāpyadhaṃ śivasyāhṛtaṃ
devetthaṃ jagatītale smaraharābhāve samunmīlati |
gaṅgāsāgaramambaraṃ śaśikalā nāgādhipaḥ mātalaṃ
sarvajñatvamadhīśvaratvamagamattvāṃ, māṃ ca bhikṣāṭanam ||
atra haripārvatībhyāmapahṛtavigrahasya īśvarasya sarvajñatvamadhīśvaratvaṃ ca tvāmagamat, bhikṣāṭanaṃ tu mām iti rājānaṃ stuvanniva arthī, sarvajñaḥ sarveśvaro’pi ca san madīyaṃ vaiduṣyaṃ dāriyaṃ ca jñātvā na kimapi mahyaṃ dadāsīti rājñaḥ nindāmeva karoti iti vyājastutiḥ|

<vyājanindālaṅkāraḥ>
nindāyā nindayā vyaktiḥ vyājanindeti gīyate |
vidhe! sa nindyo yaste prāgekamevāharacchiraḥ || 70 ||
nindayā anyanindayā nindāyāḥ anyanindāyā vyaktiḥ abhivyaktiḥ vyājanindākhyālaṅkāraḥ| he vidhe brahman yaḥ haraḥ prāk | ekameva śiraḥ aharat saḥ nindyaḥ ityudāharaṇam | atra haranindayā dāruṇavipākaṃ saṃsāraṃ pravartayataḥ brahmaṇa eva nindā vivakṣitā gamyata iti vyājanindālaṅkāraḥ ||
vyājanindāyā aparamudāharaṇam
vidhireva viśeṣagarhaṇīyaḥ karaṭa tvaṃ raṭa kastavāparādhaḥ |
sahakāratarau cakāra yaste sahavāsaṃ saralena kokilena ||
atra vidhinindayā karṇakaṭhoraṃ raṭan kāko nindyate iti vyājanindā |

<ākṣepālaṅkāraḥ>
prathama ākṣepaḥ
ākṣepaḥ svayamuktasya pratiṣedho vicāraṇāt |
candra saṃdarśayātmānam athavāsti priyāmukham ||71 ||
svayamuktasya arthasya vicāraṇāt kiñcinnimittamabhisandhāya pratiṣedhaḥ ākṣeponāma alaṅkāraḥ | he candra ātmānaṃ darśaya, athavā priyāyāḥ mukham asti ityudāharaṇam | atra candradarśanamādau prārthitam | tadanantaraṃ priyāmukhasya āhlādakāriṇaḥ satvāt candradarśanasyānarthakyaṃ vicārya athaveti pakṣāntareṇa niṣedhasūcanādākṣepālaṅkāralakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ |
prathamākṣepasya anyadudāharaṇam
sāhityapāthonidhimanthanotthaṃ karṇāmṛtaṃ rakṣata he kavīndrāḥ |
yattasya daityā iva luṇṭhanāya kāvyārthacorā praguṇī bhavanti ||
gṛhṇantu sarve yadi vā yathecchaṃ nāsti kṣatiḥkāpi kavīśvarāṇām |
ratneṣu lupteṣu bahuṣvamartyai adyāpi ratnākara eva sindhuḥ ||
atra prathamaślokena kāvyārthacorebhyaḥ kāvyasyasaṃrakṣaṇaṃ prarthitam | anantaraṃ kavisūkti vaicitryāṇāṃ akṣayatvaṃ vicintya gṛhṇantu sarve ityādinā saṃrakṣaṇapratiṣedhaḥ kṛta ityākṣepaḥ |
dvitīya ākṣepaḥ
niṣedhābhāsamākṣepaṃ budhāḥ kecana manvate |
nāhaṃ dūtī tanostāpastasyāḥ kālānalopamaḥ ||72||
kecana budhāḥ niṣedhābhāsaṃ ābhāsarūpaṃ niṣedhaṃ ākṣepaṃ manvate | tathā ca niṣedhābhāsaḥ ākṣepa iti dvitīyākṣepalakṣaṇam | nāhaṃ dūtī, tasyāḥ (nāyikāyāḥ) tanoḥ tāpaḥ śarīratāpaḥ kālānalopamaḥ pralayakālāgnitulyaḥ (nitaramasahya ityarthaḥ) ityudāharaṇam | asahya virahavedanātaptayā nāyikayā preṣitāyāḥ dūtyāḥ nāyakaṃ prati vacanamdam | nāhaṃ dūtī iti niṣedho’tra bādhitaḥ san tadābhāsarūpo bhūtyā nāhamanunayakālocitakaitavavādinī, kintu satyameva vadāmītyarthe paryavasyan idānīmeva āgatya nāyikā ujjīvanīyeti viśeṣamākṣipan ākṣepālaṅkāraḥ |
dvitīyākṣepasya aparamudāharaṇam
narendramaule na vayaṃ rājasandeśahāriṇaḥ |
jagatkuṭumbinaste’dya na śatruḥ kaścidīkṣyate ||
atra sandeśavāhakānām uktau na vayaṃ sandeśahāriṇaḥ iti niṣedho bādhitaḥ san tadābhāsarūpo bhūtvā sandhisambhāṣaṇakālocitakaitavavacanaparihāreṇa yathārthavāditve paryavasyan sarvepi rājānaḥ (śatravopi) svakuṭumbavat saṃrakṣaṇīyā iti viśeṣamākṣipati |
tṛtīya ākṣepaḥ
ākṣepo’nyo vidhau vyakte niṣedhe ca tirohite |
gaccha gacchasi cetkānta ! tatraiva syājjanirmama ||73||
vidhau vyakte sphuṭe sati niṣedhe tirohite asphuṭe ca sati anyaḥ tṛtīya ākṣepaḥ bhavati | yatra anabhimataṃ vidhiṃ sphuṭaṃ prakaṭīkṛtya abhimato niṣedhaḥ bhaṅgyantareṇāsphuṭatayā prakāśyate tatrākṣepa iti niṣkarṣaḥ | he kānta gacchasi cet gaccha, avaśyaṃ gantavyaṃ cet gamyatām | tvaṃ yatra gacchasi tatraiva mama janiḥ janma syāt ityudāharaṇam | atra kāntasya dūradeśagamanasya anabhimatasyāpi gaccheti gamānumatiḥ sphuṭaṃ prakaṭitaḥ | tataśca me tatraiva punarjanma bhavatu iti bhaṅgyantareṇa virahe svasya maraṇameva bhaviṣyatīti sūcanāt abhimato gamananiṣedhaḥ gūḍhatayā prakāśyata iti lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ |
tṛtīyākṣepasya aparamudāharaṇam
na ciraṃmama tāpāya tava yātrā bhaviṣyati |
yadi yāsyasi yātavyamalamāśaṅkayāpi te ||
atra gamane svamaraṇasūcanena gamananiṣedhaḥ garbhīkṛtaḥ |

<virodhābhāsālaṅkāraḥ>
abhāsatve virodhasya virodhābhāsa iṣyate |
vināpi tanvi hāreṇa vakṣojau tava hāriṇau ||74||
virodhasya ābhāsatve āpātataḥ sphuraṇe, paryavasāne tatparihāre ca sati virodhābhāso nāma alaṅkāra iṣyate | he tanvi tava vakṣojau stanau hāreṇa vināpi hāriṇāviti virodhaḥ sphurati, tataśca hāriṇāvityasya manohāriṇāvityarthayojanayā virodhaparihārād virodhābhāsaḥ |
virodhābhāsasya aparamudāharaṇam
pratīpabhūpairiva kiṃ tato bhiyā viruddhadharmairapi bhettṛtojjhitā |
amitrajinmitrajidojasā sa yadvicāradṛk cāradṛgapyavartata ||
atra nalaḥ amitrajidapi ojasā mitrajit, vicāradṛgapi cāradṛk iti āpātataḥ virodhe pratīyamāne’pi amitrāṇāṃ śatrūṇāṃ jetāpi, ojasā mitrasya sūryasya jetā, tathā vicāre dattadṛṣṭiḥ cāradṛṣṭiśceti virodhaparihārāt virodhābhāsaḥ |

<vibhāvanālaṅkāraḥ>
prathamā vibhāvanā
vibhāvanā vināpi syāt kāraṇaṃ kāryajanma cet |
apyalākṣārasāsiktaṃ raktaṃ tvaccaraṇadvayam ||75
kāraṇaṃ vināpi prasiddhakāraṇābhāve’pi kāryotpattivarṇane vibhāvālaṅkāraḥ | vibhāvyate kāraṇāntaraṃ yasyāmiti vibhāvanā | he kānte tava caraṇadvayaṃ alākṣārasāsiktamapi lākṣārasalepanaṃ vināpi raktaṃ lohitaṃ jātaṃ ityatra caraṇayoḥ lauhityasya prasiddhalākṣārasalepanarūpa kāraṇābhāve’pi utpattivarṇanāt vibhāvanālaṅkāraḥ | lauhityasya svābhāvikatvād virodhaparihāraḥ |
vibhāvanā ṣaḍvidhā kāraṇaṃ vinā kāryotpattiḥ | hetuvaikalye'pi kāryotpattiḥ | pratibandhakatasatvepi kāryotpattiḥ | akāraṇāt kāryotpattiḥ | viruddhakāryotpattiḥ | kāryāt kāraṇotpattiḥ |
prathamavibhāvanāyā aparamudāharaṇam |
apītakṣībakādambaṃ asaṃmṛṣṭāmalāmbaram |
aprasāditasūkṣmāmbu jagadāsīnmanoharam ||
atra pānamārjanaprasādanarūpaprasiddhakāraṇābhāve’pi kṣīvatvāmalatvasūkṣmatva- rūpakāryotpatti varṇanād vibhāvanā | śaratsamayahetukatvena virodhaparihāraḥ |
dvitīyā vibhāvanā
hetūnāmasamagratve kāryotpattiśca sā matā |
astrairatīkṣṇakaṭhinairjagajjayati manmathaḥ ||76||
hetūnāṃ asamagratve apūrṇatva’pi kāryotpattivarṇane dvitīyā vibhāvanā | atīkṣṇaiḥ akaṭhinaiḥ cāstraiḥ manmathaḥ jagajjayati ityatra hetūnāmastrāṇāṃ tīkṣṇatvādiguṇavaikalye’pi jagajjayarūpa kāryotpattivarṇanād vibhāvanā |
asyā evāparamudāharaṇam
udyānamārutoddhatāḥ cūtacampakareṇavaḥ |
udasrayanti pānthānāmaspṛśanto vilocane ||
atra sparśanakriyāvaikalye’pi reṇubhiḥ bāṣpodgamanarūpakāryajananād vibhāvanā |
tṛtīyā vibhāvanā
kāryotpattistṛtīyā syāt satyapi pratibandhake |
narendrāneva te rājan ! daśatyasibhujaṅgamaḥ ||77||
pratibandhe satyapi yadi kāryotpattirvarṇyate sā tṛtīyā vibhāvanā | he rājan tava asireva khaḍga eva bhujaṅgamaḥ sarpaḥ narendrāneva viṣavaidyāneva (nṛpatīneva) daśati ityatra, sarpadaṃśapratibandhakamantrauṣadhādisatve’pi daśatīti kāryotpattivarṇanāt tṛtīyā vibhāvanā | narendra śabdasya śleśeṇa nṛpatyarthakatve virodhaparihāraḥ
asyāḥ aparamudāharaṇam
tilapuṣpātsamāyāti vāyuścandanaśītalaḥ|
indīvarayugācitraṃ niḥsaranti śilīmukhāḥ ||
atra candanaśītalasya vāyoḥ tilapuṣpāt kāraṇabhinnādutpattivibhāvanā|tilakusumanāyikānāsikayoḥ, vātaniśvāsayoścābhedādhyava sānād virodhaparihāraḥ|
pañcamīvibhāvanā
viruddhātkāryasampattidṛṣṭā kācidvibhāvanā |
śītāṃśukiraṇāstanvī hanta saṃtāpayanti tām ||
viruddhāt viruddhakāraṇāt kāryasampatiḥ kāryotpattiḥ pañcamī vibhāvanā| śītāṃśoḥ kiraṇāḥ tanvī santāpayantītyatra śītalatvāt tāpanivartakāḥ candrakiraṇāḥ tanvyāḥ tāpaṃ janayantīti viruddhakāraṇākāryotpattirūpā pañcamī vibhāvanā )| tanvyāḥ virahiṇītvād virodhaparihāraḥ|
asyāḥ aparamudāharaṇam
udite kumārasūrye kuvalayamullasati bhāti na kṣatram |
mukulībhavanti citra pararājakumārapāṇipadmāni ||
atra sūrya udite sati kuvalayamullasati, nakṣatraṃ prakāśate, padmāni mukulībhavanti iti viruddhāt kāraṇāt kāryotpattivaiṇitā| kumāra evaṃ sūryaḥ, tasmin udite bhūmaṇḍalaṃ ullasati, kṣatraṃ na bhāti, śatrarājakumāraṇyā pāṇaya eva padmāni mukulībhavantīti yojanayā virodhaparihāraḥ|
ṣaṣṭhī vibhāvanā
kāryātkāraṇajanmāpi dṛṣṭā kācidvibhāvanā|
yaśaḥ payorāśirabhṛt karakalpatarostava ||
kāraṇātkāryotpattiriti niyamamullaṅdhya kāryātkāraṇotpattivarṇane ṣaṣṭhī vibhāvanā | tava kara eva kalpataruḥ tasmād yaśa eva payorāśir adityatra kṣīrasamudrāt kalpatarorutpattiriti prasiddha kāryakāraṇabhāvamullaṅdhya kalpataroḥ . payorāśerutpattivarṇanād vibhāvanā)| rājño vitaraṇaśīlasya karād yaśasaḥ utpattiriti virodhaparihāraḥ |
asyāḥ aparamudāharaṇam
jātā latā hi śaile jātu latāyāṃ na jāyate śailaḥ |
saṃprati tadviparītaṃ kanakalatāyāṃ giridvayaṃ jātam ||
atra kanakalatāyāṃ giridvayaṃ jātamiti kāryāt kāraṇotpattiḥ varṇitā| kanakalatāśabdena kāminīdehasya, giridvayaśabdena stanaddhayasya ca vivakṣayā virodhaparihāraḥ |

<viśeṣoktyalaṅkāraḥ>
kāryājanirviśeṣoktiḥ sati puṣkalakāraṇe |
pati hṛdi snehakṣayo nābhūt smaradīpe jvalatyapi ||
puṣkalakāraṇe saṃpūrṇakāraṇe sati kāryājaniḥ kāryānutpattiḥ viśeṣoktināma alaṅkāraḥ| smaradīpe jvalati satyapi hṛdi snehakṣayaḥ tailakṣayaḥ premakṣayaśca nābhūt ityatra dīpajvalane talakṣayakāraṇe satya tadakṣayavarṇanāda viśepoktiḥ| smare dīpyamāne premakṣayo nābhaḍita vivakṣayā virodhaparihāraḥ| kāraṇābhāve kāryātpattivibhāvanāḥ kā satve kāryānutpattiḥ viśeṣoktiriti anayorbhadaḥ |
viśeṣokteraparamudāharaṇam
anurāgavatī sandhyā divasastatpuraḥsaraḥ |
aho devagatiścitrā tathāpi na samāgamaḥ ||
atra samāgamakāraṇasatve’pi tadanutpattivarṇanād viśeṣoktiḥ | tatra ca nimittamuktaṃ “aho daivagatiścitrā” iti |

<asaṃbhavālaṅkāraḥ>
asaṃbhavo’rthanippatteḥ asaṃbhāvyatvavarṇanam |
ko veda gopaśiśukaḥ śailamutpāṭayediti ||
arthanippatteḥ kāryasiddheḥ asaṃbhāvyatvavarṇanaṃ asaṃmadāladhāraḥ | apaśiśukaḥ bālakaḥ kṛṣṇaḥ śaila govardhana parvataṃ utpāṭayediti unmūlayemana ko veda, ko vā jānāti sma ? ityatra bālakena śailotpāṭanasya asaṃbhāvyatvavaṇanāt asaṃbhavālaṅkāraḥ|
asaṃbhavasya aparamudāharaṇam
ayaṃ vārāmeko nilaya iti ratnākara iti |
śrito’smābhistṛṣṇātaralitamanobhijalanidhiḥ |
ka evaṃ jānīte nijakarapuṭīkoṭaragataṃ
rakṣaṇādenaṃ tāmyattimimakaramāpāsyati muniḥ||
atra vārāṃnidhiṃ samudra muniḥ agastyaḥ evaṃ pāsyatīti ko jānīte ! iti muninā samudrapānasya asaṃbhāvyatvavarṇanād asaṃbhavā asaṃbhavālaṅkāraḥ|

<asaṃgatyalaṅkāraḥ>
prathamā asaṅgatiḥ
viruddhaṃ bhinnadeśatvaṃ kāryahetvorasaṅgatiḥ|
viṣaṃ jaladharaiḥ pītaṃ mūchitāḥ pathikāṅganāḥ ||
yayoḥ kāryahetvoḥ kāryakāraṇayoḥ bhinnadeśatvaṃ minnadeśāvasthā viruddha anupapannaṃ tayoḥ bhinnadeśatvavarṇane asaṃgatiralaṅkāraḥ| yayoḥ kāryahetvoḥ bhinnadeśatvaṃ na viruddhaṃ tatra nāsaṃgatiriti bhāvaḥ| jalagharaiḥ medhaiḥ viṣaṃ garalaṃ (jalaṃ) pītaṃ, pathikāṅganāḥ viyoṃgivadhvaḥ mūcchitāḥ ityatra viṣapāna meghe, tatkāya ca mūrchā pathikāṅganāsu iti kāryakāraṇayoḥ viruddhabhinnadeśatvavarṇanādasaṅgatilakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ | pītajalānāṃ bhedhānāṃ kāmoddīpakatvāt pathikāṅganā mūrchitā iti virodhaparihāraḥ| asaṃgatiḥ trividhā| kāryahetvoḥ viruddhabhinnadeśatvarūpā, ekatra kartavyasya anyatra kṛtirūpā, anyat cikīrṣoḥ tadviruddhakṛtirūpā ceti | tatredaṃ prathamāyāḥ asaṃgaterudāharaṇam |
asyāḥ aparamudāharaṇam
aho khalabhujaṅgasya vicitro’yaṃ vadhanamaḥ |
anyasya daśati śrotramanyaḥ prāṇairviyujyate ||
atra khalabhujaṃgaḥ ekasya śrotraṃ daśati, anyaḥ prāṇaḥ viyujyate sarpadaṃśamaraṇayoḥ. kāryakāraṇayoḥ bhinnadeśatvavarṇanādasaṅgatiḥ | balaḥ kasyacit karṇa kimapi kathayati, tena cānyasya kasyacit maraṇaṃ bhavatīti virodhaparihāraḥ|
dvitīyā asaṅgatiḥ
anyatra karaṇīyasya tato’nyatra kṛtiśca sā |
apārijātāṃ vasudhāṃ cikīpana dyāṃ tathākaroḥ ||
kā anyatra karaṇīyasya vastunaḥ tato’nyatra anyasminnadhikaraṇe kṛtiḥ dvitīyā asaṅgatiḥ| (he kṛṣṇa) vaṃ vasudha bhūmi apārijātāṃ apragataśatrusamūhāṃ kartuṃ icchan dyāṃ svarga tathā apārijātāṃ pārijātarahitāṃ akaroḥ ityatra anyatra bhūmau karaṇīyasya apārijātatvasya svarge karaṇavarṇanāt asaṅgatilakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ | apārijātāmityatra ślepamūlakābhedādhyavasānena yadasāṅgatyaṃ bhāsate tasya bhūmipakṣe svargapakṣe ca pṛthagarthayojanayā parihāraḥ |
asyāḥ aparamudāharaṇam
tvatsaṅgakhaṇḍitasapatnavilāsinīnāṃ
bhūṣā bhavantyabhinavā bhuvanekavīra |
netreṣu kaṅkaṇamathoruṣu patravallī |
colendrasiṃha tilakaṃ karapallaveṣu ||
atra colendreṇa hatāḥ ye śatravaḥ teṣāṃ badhvaḥ netreṣu kaṅkaṇaṃ, ūruṣu patravallī, karapallaveṣu tilakaṃ ca kurvantīti anyatra bhujastanalalāṭeṣu kartavyānāṃ bhūṣaṇānāṃ anyatra netrādiṣu karaṇavarṇanād asaṃgatiḥ| śirasi hataṃ nighāya rudatīnāṃ īdṛśī samavasthā dṛśyate iti virodha parihāraḥ|
tṛtīyā asaṅgatiḥ
anyan kartuṃ pravṛttasya tadviruddhakṛtiśca sā|
gotroddhārapravṛttopi gotrodbhedaṃ purākaroḥ || 85 ||
anyatkartuṃ pravṛttanya tadviruddhakṛtiḥ prakṛtakāryaviruddhakāryakaraṇaṃ tṛtīyā asaṅgatiḥ| (he kṛṣṇa) tvaṃ purā varāhāvatāre gotroddhāre bhūmyāḥ uddharaṇe pravṛtto’pi gotrodbhedaṃ gotrāṇāṃ parvatānāṃ udbhedaṃ dalanaṃ (khura vighaṭṭanādibhiḥ) akaroḥ ityatra gotroddhārapravṛttasya tadviruddhagotrodbhedakaraṇavarṇanād asaṅgatiḥ | ślaṣeṇa virodhaparihāraḥ |
asyā aparamudāharaṇam
mohaṃ jagatrayabhuvāmapanetumetad
ādāya rūpamakhileśvara dehabhājām |
niḥsīmakāntirasanīradhināmunaiva
mohaṃ pravardhayasi mugdhavilāsinīnām ||
atra mohamapanetuṃ svīkṛtena rūpeṇa īśvaraḥ mugdhavilocanānāṃ mohaṃ pravardhayati iti anyatkartuṃ pravṛttena tadviruddhakṛtirūpā asaṃgatiḥ | janānāṃ ajñānaṃ nivartayitumādattena rūpeṇa mugdhavilocanānāṃ anurāgaṃ vardhayatīti virodhaparihāraḥ ||

<viṣamālaṅkāraḥ>
prathamaṃ viṣamam
vipamaṃ varṇyate yatra ghaṭanānanurūpayoḥ |
kveyaṃ śirīpamṛdvaṅgī, kva tāvanmakaradhvajaḥ ||86||
ananurūpayoḥ visadṛśayoḥ arthayoryatra ghaṭanā saṃbandho varṇyate tatra viṣamālaṅkāraḥ | iyaṃ śirīṣamṛdvagī kva, tādṛk tathāvidhaḥ makaradhvajaḥ ka ityatra atimṛdulatvena atidussahatvena ca asadṛśayoḥ aṅganāmakaradhvajayor ghaṭanāda viṣamālaṅkāraḥ | ayaṃ ca viṣamālaṅkāraḥ trividhaḥ virūpaghaṭanārūpaḥ, virūpakāryotpatirūpaḥ, iṣṭārthodyamādaniṣṭāvāptirūpaśceti | tatredamādyasyodāharaṇam |
prathamaviṣamasya aparabhudāharaṇam
abhilapasi yadīndo vaktralakṣmī mṛgākṣyāḥ
punarapi sakṛdabdhau majja saṃkṣālayāṅkam | hā
suvimalamatha bimbaṃ pārijātaprasūnaiḥ
surabhaya vada nocet tvaṃ kva tasyā mukhaṃ kva ||
atrāsadṛśayoḥ candramukhalakṣyorghaṭanādvipamam |
dvitīyaṃ viṣamam
virūpakāryasyotpattiraparaṃ vipamaṃ matam |
kīrtiṃ prasūte dhavalāṃ śyāmā tava kṛpāṇikā || 87 ||
kāraṇaguṇāt viruddhakāryaguṇotpattivarṇane dvitīyaṃ viṣamam | śyāmavarṇāyāḥ kṛpāṇikāyāḥ tadviruddhadhavalakīrteḥ utpattivarṇanāt lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ | kāryasya vilakṣaṇaguṇaśālitve viṣamālaṅkāraḥ| kāraṇasya viruddhakāryanivartakatve pañcamī vibhāvanā ityanayorbhadaḥ | |
tṛtīyaṃ viṣamam
aniṣṭasyāpyavāptiśca tadiṣṭārthasamudyamāt |
na bhakṣyāśayā’himañjūṣāṃ daṣṭvākhustena bhakṣitaḥ || 88 ||
iṣṭārthasamudyamāt abhilaṣitārthodyogāt na kevalamiṣṭasya aprāptiḥ api tu aniṣṭasya avāptirapi, tat tṛtīyaṃ viṣamam | ākhurmūṣikaḥ bhakṣyāśayā bhakṣyadravyalābhatṛṣṇayā ahimañjūṣāṃ sarpapeṭikāṃ daṣṭvā darśanaiḥ chitvā tena sarpeṇa bhakṣita ityatra āhārārthaṃ pravṛttasya mūṣikasya na kevalaṃ bhakṣyānavāptiḥ api tu maraṇarūpāniṣṭāvāptirapīti tṛtīyaṃ viṣamam |
tṛtīyaviṣamasya aparamudāharaṇam
gopāla iti kṛṣṇa tvaṃ pracurakṣīravāñchayā |
śrito mātṛstanakṣīramapyalabhyaṃ tvayā kṛtam ||
atra iṣṭasya pracurakṣīrasya alābhaḥ mātṛstanakṣīrānavāptirūpā aniṣṭāvāptiśceti tṛtīya viṣamam |

<samālaṅkāraḥ>
prathamaṃ samam
samaṃ syādvarṇanaṃ yatra dvayorapyanurūpayoḥ |
svānurūpaṃ kṛtaṃ sama hāreṇa kucamaṇḍalam || 89 ||
viṣamālaṅkārapratidvandvitayā samālaṅkāro’pi trividhaḥ, arūpavarṇanarūpaḥ, sarūpakāryotpattirūpaḥ, vināniṣṭaṃ iṣṭāvāptirīpaśceti | yatra anurūpayoḥ dvayoḥ (saṃbandhaḥ) varṇyate tatra prathama samam | hāreṇa kucamaṇḍalaṃ svasyānurūrapaṃ sadma kṛtamityatra anurūpayoḥ hārakucamaṇḍalayoḥ saṃbandhavarṇanātsamālaṅkāraḥ |
prathamasamasya aparamudāharaṇam
kaumudīva tuhināṃśumaṇḍalaṃ jāhnavīva śaśikhaṇḍamaṇḍanam |
paśya kīrtiranurūpamāśritā tvāṃ vibhāti narasiṃhabhūpate ||
atra kaumudīcandramaṇḍala, jāhvavīvacandraśekhara, kīrtinarapatīnāṃ anurūpāṇāṃ ghaṭanāt samālaṅkāraḥ |
dvitīyaṃ samam
gata sārūpyamapi kāryasya kāraṇena samaṃ viduḥ |
nīcapravaṇatā lakṣmi jalajāyāstavocitā || 90 ||
kāryasya kāraṇena sārūpyaṃ samānaguṇavatvaṃ dvitīya samam viduḥ | he lakṣmi jalāt jātāyāḥ tava nīcapravaṇatā nikṛṣṭānusaraṇaśīla ucitā ityatra kāraṇabhūtajalaguṇasya nīcapravaṇatāyāḥ kārya lakṣmyāṃ varṇanātsamālaṅkāraḥ | nīco nikṛṣṭadeśaḥ, nikṛṣṭajanaśca |
dvitīyasamasya aparamudāharaṇam
davadahanādutpanno dhūmo ghanatāmavāpya varṣaistam |
yacchamayati tadyuktaṃ so’pi hi davameva nirdahati ||
atra davadahanādutpanno dhūmaḥ svajanakaṃ davadahanaṃ śamayatīti yat tadyuktaṃ yataḥ davadahano’pi svajanakaṃ davaṃ śamayatīti kāryakāraṇayoḥ davāmitaddhamayoḥ guṇasārūpyavarṇanāt samālaṅkāraḥ |
tṛtīyaṃ samam
vināniṣṭaṃ ca tat siddhiryadarthaṃ karturudyamaḥ|
yukto vāraṇalābho’yaṃ syāla te vāraṇārthinaḥ || 91 ||
yadarthaṃ yatkāryasiddhayarthaṃ kartuḥ udyamaḥ tasya kāryasya yadi aniṣṭa vinā siddhiḥ, tat tṛtīyaṃ samam | he syāla vāraṇārthinaḥ gajārthinaḥ te ayaṃ vāraṇalābhaḥ (rājapuruṣaiḥ nivāraṇasya lābhaḥ) yuktaḥ ityatra vāraṇasya, śleṣamūlakābhedādhyavasānena iṣṭasya, anāyāsena lābhavarṇanāt tṛtīyaṃ samam | gajaṃ yācituṃ rājadvāraṃ gataḥ dvārapālakaiḥ dūrādeva nivāritaḥ evamanyena sopahāsamucyate |
tṛtīyasamasya aparamudāharaṇam
uccairgajairaṭanamarthayamāna eva
tvāmāśrayanniha cirādupito’smi rājan |
uccāṭanaṃ tvamapi lambhayase tadeva
māmadya naiva viphalā mahatāṃ hi sevā ||
atra he rājan uccaṃ gajamāruhya gamanamicchantaṃ māṃ tvaṃ uccāṭanaṃ uccairgajairaṭanaṃ (dūranirasanaṃ) lambhayase prāpayasi iti anāyāsena iṣṭasiddheḥ varṇanāt samālaṅkāraḥ | uccāṭanaśabde ślepeṇa dūranirasanarūpārthāntarasphuraṇena nindā gamyate |

<vicitrālaṅkāraḥ>
vicitraṃ tatprayatnaścedviparītaḥ phalecchayā |
namanti santastrailokyādapi labdhaṃ samunnatim || 92 ||
phalecchayā kasyacitphalasya prāptyabhilāṣeṇa viparītaḥ prayatnaḥ yadi kriyate tad vicitram | samunnatiṃ labdhaṃ namanti ityatra samunnatirūpaphalecchayā tadviparītaṃ namanaṃ kriyata iti vicitram |
vicitrasya aparamudāharaṇam
malinayituṃ khalavadanaṃ vimalayanti jaganti deva kīrtiste |
mitrāhlādaṃ katuṃ mitrāya duhyati pratāpo’pi ||
atra malinayituṃ vimalayati, mitrahlādaṃ kartuṃ mitrāya (sūryāya) duhyati iti viparītaprayatnavarṇanādvicitram |

<aghikālaṅkāraḥ>
prathamaṃ adhikam
adhikaṃ pṛthulādhārādādheyādhikyavarṇanam |
brahmāṇḍāni jale yatra tatra mānti na te guṇāḥ ||93||
atipṛthutvena prasiddhādādhārād ādheyasya ādhikyavarṇanaṃ adhikam | brahmāṇḍānāmapyādhāratvādativistīrṇe jale rājñoḥ guṇā na mānti iti ādheyabhūtaguṇānāmādhikyavarṇanād adhikam |
asyāparamudāharaṇam
yugāntakālapratisaṃhṛtātmano
jaganti yasyāṃ savikāsamāsata |
tanau mamustasya na kaiṭabhadvipaḥ
tapodhanāmyāgamasaṃbhavā mudaḥ||
atra yasya bhagavataḥ śarīre caturdaśabhuvanāni pralayakāle sarikāsaṃ āsata tasya śarīre nāradāgamanajanitamudaḥ na mamurityaghikādheyavarṇanād adhikam |
dvitīyaṃ adhikam
pṛthvādheyādyadādhārādhikyaṃ tadapi tanmatam |
kiyadvārabrahma yatraite viśrāmyanti guṇāstava || 94 ||
pṛthutvena prasiddhādādheyād ādhārasya ādhikyavarṇanaṃ dvitīyaṃ adhikam| (he rāma) tavānantakalyāṇaguṇāḥ yatra viśrāmyanti asaṃbādhaṃ tiṣṭhanti tadvāgbrahma kiyat? kiṃ parimāṇaṃ syāt, tatparimāṇametāvaditi nirṇetuṃ na śakyata ityarthaḥ| atra ādheyaguṇāpekṣayā ādhārasya śabdabrahmaṇaḥ ādhikyamuktam|
asyāparamudāharaṇam
aho viśālaṃ bhūpāla bhuvanatritayodaram|
māti mātumaśakyo’pi yaśorāśiryadatra te||

<pratyanīkālaṅkāraḥ>
pratyanīkaṃ balavataḥ śatroḥ pakṣe parākramaḥ|
jaitranetrānugau karṇau utpalābhyāmadhaḥkṛtau||95||
balavataḥ prabalasya (ataḥ eva sākṣātpratikartumaśaktas) śatroḥ pakṣe pakṣāntaḥpātini mitrādisahāye parākramaḥ pratyanīkālaṅkāraḥ| utpalābhyāṃ indīvarābhyāṃ jaitranetrānugau svajetṛnetrānusāiṇau karṇau adhaḥ kṛtau tiraskṛtau (adhaḥ pradeśe kṛtau) ityatra utpalābhyāṃ sākṣāt netrajaye sāmarthyābhāvāt netrānusaraṇaśīle śrotre adhaḥ kṛte iti śatroḥ pakṣatiraskaraṇavarṇanāt pratyanīkam| adhaḥ kṛtaśabde śleṣeṇa tiraskāradhaḥpradeśayorabhedādhyavasāyaḥ||
pratyanīkasyāparamudāharaṇam
mama rūpakīrtimaradbhuvi yastadhanupraviṣṭahṛdayemiti |
tvayi matsarādiva nirastadayaḥ sutarāṃ kṣiṇoti khalu tāṃ madanaḥ ||
atra madanaḥ ātmano rūpakhyātiṃ hṛtavataḥ sundarapuruṣasya pratīkāraśabdaḥ tadanuraktāṃ taruṇīṃ nirdayaṃ kṣiṇotīti śatrupakṣatiraskāravarṇanāt pratyanīkam |

<kāvyārthāpatyālaṅkāraḥ>
kaumutyenārthasaṃsiddhiḥ kāvyārthāpattiriṣyate |
sa jitastvanmukhenduḥ kā vārtā sarasīruhām ||
kaimutyena kaimutikanyāyena arthasaṃsiddhiḥ vivakṣitārthasiddhiḥ kāvyārthāpattiḥ| yatra daṇḍāpūdikanyāyenārthasiddhirvaṇyate tatra kāvyārthāpattiriti niṣkarṣaḥ| śāstre’pyapatteḥ satvāt tadvāraṇāya kāvyapadamiti kecit | vastutastu arthāpattipramāṇāt vilakṣaṇaiva kāvyārthāpattiḥ|| saḥ sundaratvena āhvādakāritvena ca prasiddhaḥ, athavā padmajetā induḥ tava mukhena jitaḥ, (evaṃ sati) sarasīruhāṃ padmānāṃ kā vārtā kā kathā ityudāharaṇam | atra yena padmajetā induḥ jitaḥ tena padmāni jitānīti kiṃ vaktavyaṃ iti daṇḍāpūpikanyāyena padmajayarūpārthasya saṃsiddheḥ arthāpattiḥ|
kāvyārthāpatteraparamudāharaṇam
adharo’yamadhīrākṣyā bandhujīvaprabhāharaḥ |
anyajīvaprabhā hanta haratīti kimadbhutam ||
bandhujīvo bandhūkapuṣpaṃ tatprabhāharaḥ, bandhūnāṃ jīvānāṃ prabhāharaśca iti śleṣamūlābhedādhyavasānena bandhujīvaprabhāharasya anyajīvaprabhāharatvaṃ anukta siddhamityarthāpattiḥ|

<kāvyaliṅgālaṅkāraḥ>
samarthanīyasyārthasya kāvyaliṅgaṃ samarthanam |
jito’si manda kandarpa macitte’sti trilocanaḥ||97||
samarthanīyasya samarthanāpekṣasya arthasya samarthana kāvyaliṅga nāma alaṅkāraḥ| he manda mūḍha kandarpa tvaṃ jito’si, mama citte trilocanaḥ asti ityudāharaṇam | atra madanajayasya duṣkaratvāt tajjayopanyāsaḥ samarthanamapekṣate | svāntaḥkaraṇe madanadāhakatrilocanasannidhānapradarśanena tatsamarthana kṛtamiti kāvyaliṅgam | samarthako hetuḥ vākyārthaḥ pradāthoṃ vā bhavati | “macitte’sti trilocanaḥ” ityatra vākyārthī hetuḥ |
kāvyaliṅgasya aparamudāharaṇam
bhasmoddhalana bhadramastu bhavate rudrākṣamāle śubhe
hā sopānaparampare girisutākāntālayālaṅkṛte|
adyārādhanatoṣitena vibhunā yuṣmatsapāsukhā -
lokocchedini mokṣanāmani mahāmohe nilīyāmahe ||
atra mokṣasya mahāmohatvamaprasiddhaṃ samarthanamapekṣate| tacca yuṣmatsaparyāsukhālokoṃcchedini iti padārthena samarthitamiti padārthahetukaṃ kāvyaliṅgam ||

<arthāntaranyāsālaṅkāraḥ>
uktirarthāntaranyāsaḥ syātsāmānyaviśeṣayoḥ |
sāmānyaviśeṣayoḥ uktiḥ arthāntaranyāsaḥ| tayośca eka prastutaṃ, aparamaprastutam | tataśca viśeṣa prastute tena saha aprastutasāmānyasya nyāsaḥ ekaḥ arthāntaranyāsaḥ | sāmānye prastute tena saha viśeṣasya nyāso dvitīyaḥ | evamarthāntaranyāso dvividhaḥ | atredamavadheyam | sāmānyaviśeṣayoḥ nyāse samarthyasamarthakabhāvaḥ avaśyaṃ bhavati | kāvyaliṅge’pi samarthyasamarthakabhāvaḥ asti | evaṃ sati kāvyaliṅgārthāntaranyāsayorayaṃ bhedaḥ| samarthyasamarthakayoḥ sāmānyaviśeṣasaṃbandhe arthāntaranyāsaḥ, tadanyaniṣpādyaniṣpā- dakabhāvādisaṃbandhe kāvyaliṅgam iti |
sāmānyena viśeṣasamarthanarūpasya arthāntaranyāsasya udāharaṇam
hanūmānabdhimatarad duṣkaraṃ kiṃ mahātmanām ||98||
atra hanūmān abdhi samudraṃ atarat iti viśeṣo mahātmanāṃ duṣkaraṃ kiṃ ? iti sāmānyena samarthyate|
asyāparamudāharaṇam
divākarādrakṣati yo guhāsu līnaṃ divā bhītamivāndhakāram |
kṣudre’pi nūnaṃ śaraṇaṃ prapane mamatvamuccaiḥśirasāṃ satīva ||
viśeṣeṇa sāmānyasamarthanarūpasya arthāntaranyāsasya udāharaṇam
guṇavadvastusaṃsargādyāti svalpo’pi gauravam |
puṣpamālānupaṅgeṇa sūtraṃ śirasi dhāryate || 99 ||
svalpo’pi nikṛṣṭo’pi guṇavadvastusaṃbandhāt gauravaṃ yāti prāmoti | puṣpamālāyāḥ anuṣaṅgeṇa saṃparkeṇa sūtraṃ tantuḥ śirasi dhāryate ityatra nirguṇasya guṇavatsaṃparkād gauravaprāptirūpaṃ sāmānya prastutaṃ, tantoḥ puṣpamālāsaṃbandhena śirasā vahanarūpagauravaprāptirūpeṇa aprastutaviśeṣeṇa samarthitamiti arthāntaranyāsa lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ |
asyāparamudāharaṇam
nijadoṣāvṛtamanasāmatisundaramivābhāti viparītam |
paśyati pittopahataḥ śaśiśubhraṃ śaṃkhamapi pītam ||

<vikasvarālaṅkāraḥ>
yasminviśeṣasāmānyaviśeṣāḥ sa vikasbaraḥ |
sa na jigye mahānto hi durdharṣāḥ sāgarā iva ||10||
yasmin kāvye viśeṣasāmānyaviśeṣāḥ vinyasyante tatra vikasvarālaṅkāraḥ| yatra kasyacidviśeṣasya samarthanārthaṃ sāmānya vinyasya tatprasiddhāvapi aparituṣyatā kavinā punarviśeṣāntaraṃ upamānarītyā arthāntaranyāsavidhayā vā vinyasyate tatra vikasvarālakāra iti niṣkarṣaḥ | saḥ prasiddho rājā na jigye kenāpi na jitaḥ iti viśeṣavinyāsaḥ | yāti tatsamarthanaṃ mahānto durdharṣā hi iti sāmānyena kriyate| tataśca upamānarītyā sāgarā iva iti viśeṣanyāsaḥ sāmānyamartha mahatāṃ durjayatvaṃ draḍhayatīti vikasvarālaṅkāraḥ| vikasvarasyāparamudāharaṇam anantaratnaprabhavasya yasya himaṃ na saubhāgyavilopi jātam | eko hi doṣo guṇasatripāte nimajatīndoḥ kiraṇeṣvivāṅkaḥ|| atrāpi upamānarītyā viśeṣāntarasya nyāsaḥ | arthāntaranyāsavidhayā viśeṣāntaranyāsodāharaṇaṃ tu anubandhe draṣṭavyam ||

<vakroktyalaṅkāraḥ>
vakroktiḥ śleṣakākubhyāṃ aparārthaprakalpanam |
muñca mānaṃ dinaṃ prāptaṃ neha nandī harāntike ||101||
śleṣeṇa kākvā vā aparārthakalpanaṃ vivakṣitādanyārthe vākyatātparyakalpanaṃ vakroktiḥ| he priye mānaṃ kopaṃ muñca, dinaṃ ahaḥ prāptamiti nāyakenoktā nāyikā śleṣeṇa nāyakavākyasyārthāntaraṃ kalpayitvā samādhatte-iha mannikaṭe nandī nāsti, kintu harāntike śivasannidhau vartate iti | atra nāyakavākyasya śleṣeṇa nandinaṃ prāptaṃ mā muñca iti arthāntaraṃ kalpitamiti vakroktiḥ |
vakrokteraparamudāharaṇam
asamālocya kopaste nociteyamitīritā |
naivociteyamiti taṃ tāḍayāmāsa mālayā ||
atra naivociteyamiti kākusvarabhedena ucitamevetyarthāntaraṃ prakalpya nāyikā nāyakaṃ mālayā tāḍayāmāseti vakroktiḥ |

<svabhāvoktyalaṅkāraḥ>
svabhāvoktiḥ svabhāvasya jātyādisthasya varṇanam |
kuraṅgairuttaraṅgākṣaḥ stabdhakarṇarudīkṣyate ||102||
jātyādisthasya jātikriyāguṇādisaṃbandhinaḥ svabhāvasya varṇanaṃ svabhāvoktiralaṅkāraḥ| uttaraṅgākṣaiḥ cañcalalocanaiḥ stabdhakarṇaiḥ niścalakarṇaiḥ kuraṅgaiḥ hariṇaiḥ udīkṣyate ityatra kuraṅgasvabhāvavarṇanātsvabhāvoktiḥ|

<bhāvikālaṅkāraḥ>
bhāvikaṃ bhūtabhāvyarthasākṣātkārasya varṇanam |
ahaṃ vilokaye’dyāpi yudhyantetra surāsurāḥ || 103||
bhūtasya bhāvino vā arthasya yaḥ sākṣātkāraḥ tasya varṇana bhāvikam | yatra atītānāgate pratyakṣatvena varmyate tatra bhāvikamiti niṣkarṣaḥ | devāsurāḥ atra pradeśe yudhyanti iti ahaṃ adyāpi vilokaye ityatra atītasya devadānavayuddhasya pratyakṣatvena varṇanāt bhāvikālaṅkāraḥ |

<udāttālaṅkāraḥ>
udāttamṛddhiścaritaṃ ślādhyaṃ cānyopalakṣaṇam | sānau yasyābhavayuddhaṃ taddharjaṭikirīṭinoḥ ||104||
ṛddhiḥ saṃpatsamṛddhiḥ, anyopalakṣaṇaṃ ślādhyaṃ caritaṃ ca udāttam | yatra sampatsamṛddhiḥ varṇyate tatra udāttālaṅkāraḥ| yatra anyāṅgaṃ ślādhyaṃ caritaṃ varṇyate tatrāpi udāttālaṅkāra ityarthaḥ | upalakṣaṇaśabdenātra aṅgatvameva vivakṣyate | yasya himālayasya sānau dhūrjaṭikirīṭinoḥ śivārjunayoḥ tat prasiddhaṃ yuddhamabhavat so’yaṃ himavāniti śeṣaḥ| atra ślādhyaṃ caritaṃ śivārjunayoyuddhaṃ anyāṅgatvena himavanmāhāsyāṅgatvena varṇitamityudāttam || ṛddhivarṇanarūpodāttodāharaṇaṃ tu-
ratnabhittiṣu saṃkrāntaḥ pratibimbaśatairvṛtaḥ | jñāto laṅkeśvaraḥ kṛcchādāñjaneyena tattvataḥ ||
atra rāvaṇasya saṃpatsamṛddhiḥ varṇyata ityudāttālaṅkāraḥ |
