॥ medhādakṣiṇāmūrti mantravarṇapadastutiḥ ।।
omityekākṣaraṃ brahma vyāharanti trayaśśikhāḥ ।
tasmai tārātmane medhādakṣiṇāmūrtaye namaḥ ॥ 1
natvāyaṃ munayassarve paraṃ yānti durāsadam ।
nakārarūpiṇe medhādakṣiṇāmūrtaye namaḥ ॥ 2
mohajālavinirmukto brahmavidyāti yatpadam ।
mokārarūpiṇe medhādakṣiṇāmūrtaye namaḥ ॥ 3
bhavamāśritya yaṃ vidvān nabhavohyabhavatparaḥ ।
bhakārarūpiṇe medhādakṣiṇāmūrtaye namaḥ ॥ 4
gaganākāravaddhāntamanubhātyakhilaṃ jagat ।
gakārarūpiṇe medhādakṣiṇāmūrtaye namaḥ ॥ 5
vaṭamūlanivāso yo lokānāṃ prabhuravyayaḥ ।
vakārarūpiṇe ... ॥ 6
tejobhiryasya sūryo'sau kālaklṛptikaro bhavet ।
tekāra rūpiṇe ...॥ 7
dakṣatripurasaṃhāre yaḥ kālaviṣabhañjane ।
dakārarūpiṇe ... ॥ 8
kṣipraṃ bhavati vāksiddhiryannāmasmaraṇānnṛṇām ।
kṣikārarūpiṇe ... ॥ 9
ṇākāravācyo yassuptaṃ sandīpayati meṃ manaḥ ।
ṇākārarūpiṇe ...॥ 10
mūrtayohyaṣṭadhā yasya jagajjanmādikāraṇam ।
mūkārarūpiṇe ... ॥ 11
tattvaṃ brahmāsi paramamiti yadgurubodhitaḥ ।
sarephatātmaneme ....॥ 12
yeyaṃ viditvā brahmādyā ṛṣayo yānti nivṛtim ।
yekārarūpiṇe ... ॥ 13
mahatāṃ devamityāhurnigamāgamayośiśavaḥ ।
nakārarūpiṇe ... ॥ 14
sarvasya jagatohyantarbahiryo vyāpya saṃsthitā।
makārarūpiṇe ...॥ 15
tvameva jagatassākṣī sṛṣṭisthityantakāraṇam ।
mekārarūpiṇe ...॥ 16
dāmeti dhātṛsṛṣṭeryatkāraṇaṃ kāryamucyate ।
dhāṅkārarūpiṇe 17
prakṛteryatparaṃ dhyātvā tādātmyaṃ yāti vai muniḥ।
prakārarūpiṇe ... ॥ 18
jñāninoyamupāsyanti tatvātītaṃ cidātmakam ।
jñākārarūpiṇe ...॥ 19
prajñā sajāyate yasya dhyānanāmārcanādibhiḥ
prakārapiṇe ... ॥ 20
yasyasmaraṇamātreṇa naro muktassabandhanāt ।
yakārarūpiṇe ... ॥ 21
chaveryagnendriyāṇyāpurviṣayeṣviha jāḍyatām ।
ukārarūpiṇe ...॥ 22
svāntevidāṃ jaḍānāṃ yo dūre tiṣṭhati cinmayaḥ।
svākārarūpiṇe ...॥ 23
hāraprāyaphaṇīndrāya sarvavidyāpradāyine ।
hākārarūpiṇe ... ॥ 24
॥ iti śrīmedhādakṣiṇāmūrti mantravarṇapadastutiḥ॥
