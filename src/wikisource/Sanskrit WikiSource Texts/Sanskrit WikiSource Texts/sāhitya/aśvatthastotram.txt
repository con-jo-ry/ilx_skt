Text:

|| aśvatthastotram ||
śrī nārada uvāca
anāyāsena loko'yam sarvān kāmānavāpnuyāt |
sarvadevātmakaṃ caikaṃ tanme brūhi pitāmaha ||
brahmovāca
śruṇu deva mune'śvatthaṃ śuddhaṃ sarvātmakaṃ tarum |
yatpradakṣiṇato lokaḥ sarvān kāmān samaśnute ||
aśvatthāddakṣiṇe rudraḥ paścime viṣṇurāsthitaḥ |
brahmā cottaradeśasthaḥ pūrvetvindrādidevatāḥ ||
skandhopaskandhapatreṣu govipramunayastathā |
mūlaṃ vedāḥ payo yajñāḥ saṃsthitā munipuṅgava ||
pūrvādidikṣu saṃyātā nadīnadasaro'bdhayaḥ |
tasmāt sarvaprayatnena hyaśvatthaṃ saṃśrayedbudhaḥ ||
tvaṃ kṣīryaphalakaścaiva śītalasya vanaspate |
tvāmārādhya naro vindyādaihikāmuṣmikaṃ phalam ||
caladdalāya vṛkṣāya sarvadāśritaviṣṇave |
bodhisatvāya devāya hyaśvatthāya namo namaḥ ||
aśvattha yasmāt tvayi vṛkṣarāja nārāyaṇastiṣṭhati sarvakāle |
athaḥ śrutastvaṃ satataṃ tarūṇāṃ dhanyo'si cāriṣṭavināśako'si ||
kṣīradastvaṃ ca yeneha yena śrīstvāṃ niṣevate |
satyena tena vṛkṣendra māmapi śrīrniṣevatām ||
ekādaśātmarudro'si vasunāthaśiromaṇiḥ |
nārāyaṇo'si devānāṃ vṛkṣarājo'si pippala ||
agnigarbhaḥ śamīgarbho devagarbhaḥ prajāpatiḥ |
hiraṇyagarbho bhūgarbho yajñagarbho namo'stu te ||
āyurbalaṃ yaśo varcaḥ prajāḥ paśuvasūni ca |
brahma prajñāṃ ca medhāṃ ca tvaṃ no dehi vanaspate ||
satataṃ varuṇo rakṣet tvāmārāddṛṣṭirāśrayet |
paritastvāṃ niṣevantāṃ tṛṇāni sukhamastu te ||
akṣispandaṃ bhujaspandaṃ duḥsvapnaṃ durvicintanam |
śatrūṇāṃ ca samutthānaṃ hyaśvattha śamaya prabho ||
aśvatthāya vareṇyāya sarvaiśvarya pradāyine |
namo duḥsvapnanāśāya susvapnaphaladāyine ||
mūlato brahmarūpāya madhyato viṣṇu rūpiṇe |
agrataḥ śivarūpāya vṛkṣarājāya te namaḥ ||
yaṃ dṛṣṭvā mucyate rogaiḥ spṛṣṭvā pāpaiḥ pramucyate |
yadāśrayāccirañjīvī tamaśvatthaṃ namāmyaham ||
aśvattha sumahābhāga subhaga priyadarśana |
iṣṭakāmāṃśca me dehi śatrubhyastu parābhavam ||
āyuḥ prajāṃ dhanaṃ dhānyaṃ saubhāgyaṃ sarvasampadam |
dehi deva mahāvṛkṣa tvāmahaṃ śaraṇaṃ gataḥ ||
ṛgyajuḥsāmamantrātmā sarvarūpī parātparaḥ |
aśvattho vedamūlo'sāvṛṣibhiḥ procyate sadā ||
brahmahā guruhā caiva daridro vyādhipīḍitaḥ |
āvṛtya lakṣasaṃkhyaṃ tat stotrametat sukhī bhavet ||
brahmacārī haviṣyāśī tvadaḥśāyī jitendriyaḥ |
papopahatacitto'pi vratametat samācaret ||
ekāhastaṃ dvihastaṃ vā kuryādgomayalepanam |
arcet puruṣasūktena praṇavena viśeṣataḥ ||
maunī pradakṣiṇaṃ kuryāt prāguktaphalabhāgbhavet |
viṣṇornāmasahasreṇa hyacyutasyāpi kīrtanāt ||
pade padāntaraṃ gatvā karaceṣṭāvivarjitaḥ |
vācā stotraṃ mano dhyāne caturaṅgaṃ pradakṣiṇam ||
aśvatthaḥ sthāpito yena tatkulaṃ sthāpitaṃ tataḥ |
dhanāyuṣāṃ samṛddhistu narakāt tārayet pitṛn ||
aśvatthamūlamāśritya śākānnodakadānataḥ |
ekasmin bhojite vipre koṭibrāhmaṇabhojanam ||
aśvatthamūlamāśritya japahomasurārcanāt |
akṣayaṃ phalamāpnoti brahmaṇo vacanaṃ yathā ||
evamāśvāsito'śvatthaḥ sadāśvāsāya kalpate |
yajñārthaṃ chedite'śvatthe hyakṣayaṃ svargamāpnuyāt ||
chinno yena vṛthā'śvatthaścheditā pitṛdevatāḥ |
aśvatthaḥ pūjito yatra pūjitāḥ sarvadevatāḥ ||
|| iti aśvattha stotraṃ sampūrṇam ||
