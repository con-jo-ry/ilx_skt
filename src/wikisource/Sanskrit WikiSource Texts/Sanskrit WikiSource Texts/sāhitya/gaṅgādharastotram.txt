Text:

॥ gaṅgādharastotram ॥
kṣīrāmbhonidhimanthanodbhavaviṣātsandahyamānān surān brahmādīnavalokya yaḥ karuṇayā hālāhalākhyaṃ
viṣam । niśśaṅkaṃ nijalīlayā kabalayanlokānnarakṣādarādārtatrāṇaparāyaṇassabhagavān gaṅgādharo megatiḥ।। 1
kṣīraṃ svādu nipīya mātulagṛhebhuktvā svakīyaṃ gṛhaṃ kṣīrālābhavaśena khinnamanaseghoraṃ tapaḥ kurvate ।
kāruṇyādupamanyaveniravadhiṃ kṣīrāṃbudhiṃ dattavānārtatrāṇa parāyaṇassabhagavān gaṅgādharo me gatiḥ ।। 2
mṛtyuṃ vakṣasi tāḍayannijapadadhyānaikabhaktaṃ muniṃ mārkaḍeyamapālayatkaruṇayā liṃgādvinirgatya yaḥ ।
netrāmbhojasamarpaṇenaharayebhīṣṭaṃ rathāṅgaṃ dadā vārtātrāṇa parāyaṇassabhagavān gaṅgādharo me gatiḥ ।। 3
oḍhuṃ droṇajayadrathādirathikaissainyaṃ mahatkairavaṃ dṛṣṭvā kṛṣṇasahāyavantamapi taṃ bhītaṃ prapannārtihā । 
pārthaṃ rakṣitavā namoghaviṣaya divyāstra mudbodhayannārta...।। 4
bālaṃ śaivakulodbhavaṃ parihasatsvajñātipakṣākulaṃ khidyantaṃ tava mūrdhni puṣpanicayaṃ dātuṃ samudyatkaram ।
dṛṣṭvā namyaviriñciramyanagare pūjāṃ tvadīyāṃ bhajannārta ...॥ 5
saṃtrasteṣu purā surāsurabhayādindrādibṛndārakeṣvārūḍho dharaṇīrathaṃ śrutihayaṃ kṛtvā murāriṃ śaram ।
rakṣanyaḥ kṛpayā samastavibudhāt jitvā purārīn kṣaṇā dārtatrāṇa...॥ 6
śrautasmārtapatho parāṅbhuvamapi prodyanmahāpātakaṃ viśvādhīśamapatyameva gatirityālāpavantaṃ sakṛt ।
rakṣanyaḥ karuṇāpayonidhiriti prāptaprasiddhiḥ purā hyārtratāṇa...॥ 7
gāṅgaṃ vegamavāpyamānyavibudhaissoḍhuṃ purā yācito dṛṣṭvābhaktabhagīrathena vinato rudrojaṭāmaṇḍale। 
kāruṇyā davanītale suranadīmāpūrayanpāvanī mārtatrāṇa parāyaṇa...॥ 8
॥ iti śrīmadapparayyadīkṣitaviracitaṃ śrīgaṅgādharāṣṭakam ॥
