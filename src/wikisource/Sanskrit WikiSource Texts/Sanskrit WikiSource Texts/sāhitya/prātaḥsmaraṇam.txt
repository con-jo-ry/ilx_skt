Text:

vakratuṇḍa mahākāya sūryakoṭisamaprabha |
nirvighnaṃ kurū me deva sarvakāryeṣu sarvadā || 1 ||
śāṃtākāraṃ bhujagaśayanaṃ padmanābhaṃ sureśaṃ |
viśvādhāraṃ gaganasadṛśaṃ meghavarṇaṃ śubhāṅgam |
lakṣmīkāntaṃ kamalanayanaṃ yogibhirdhyānagamyam |
vande viṣṇuṃ bhavabhayaharaṃ sarvalokaikanātham || 2 ||
kṛṣṇāya vāsudevāya haraye paramātmane |
praṇatkleśanāśāya goviṃdāya namo namaḥ || 3 ||
yā kundeṃdutuṣārahāradhavalā yā śubhravastrāvṛtā |
yā vīṇāvaradaṃḍamaṃḍitakarā yā śvetapadmāsanā |
yā brahmācyutśaṃkaraprabhṛtibhiḥ devai sadā vanditā |
sā māṃ pātu sarasvatī bhagavatī niḥśeṣajāḍayāpahā || 4 ||
brahmānaṃdaṃ paramasukhadaṃ kevalaṃ jñānamūrtim |
dvaṃdvātītaṃ gaganasadṛśaṃ, tattvamasyādilakṣam |
ekaṃ nityaṃ vimalamacalaṃ sarvadhīḥ sākṣībhūtam |
bhāvātītaṃ triguṇarahitaṃ sadgurūṃ taṃ namāmi || 5 ||
gururbrahmā gururviṣṇuḥ gururdevo maheśvaraḥ |
guruḥ sākṣāt parabrahma tasmai śrīguravai namaḥ || 6 ||
puṇyaśloko nalo rājā puṇyaśloko yudhiṣṭhiraḥ |
puṇyaśloko videhaśca puṇyaśoko janārdanaḥ || 7 ||
ahilyā draupadī sītā tārā maṃdodarī tathā |
paṃcakanyā nā smarennityaṃ mahāpātakanāśanam || 8 ||
aśvatthāmā balirvyāso hanumānaśca vibhīṣaṇaḥ |
kṛpaḥ paraśurāmaśca saptaite cirajīvinaḥ || 9 ||
ayodhyā mathurā māyā kāśī kāṃcī avaṃtikā |
purī dvārāvatī caiva saptaitā mokṣadāyikāḥ || 10 ||
sarvamaṃgalamāṃgalye śive sarvārthasādhike
śaraṇye tryaṃbake gauri nārāyaṇi namosstute || 11 ||
