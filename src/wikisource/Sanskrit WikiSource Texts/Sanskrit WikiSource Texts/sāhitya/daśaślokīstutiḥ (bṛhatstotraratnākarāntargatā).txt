Text:

॥ daśaślokīstutiḥ ॥

sāmbo naḥ kuladaivataṃ paśupate sāmba tvadīyā vayaṃ sāmbaṃ staumi surāsuroragagaṇāssāmbena santāritāḥ।
sāmbāyāstu namo mayā viracitaṃ sāmbātparaṃ no bhaje sāmbasyānucaro'smyahaṃ mama ratissāmbe parabrahmaṇi ॥
viṣṇvādyāśca puratrayaṃ suragaṇā jetuṃ na śaktāssvayaṃ yaṃ śambhu bhagavanvayaṃ tu paśavo'smākaṃ tvameveśvaraḥ ।
sālalāniyojitāssumanassvasthā babhūvustatastasminme hṛdayaṃ sukhena ramatāṃ sāmbe parabrahmaṇi ॥2
kṣoṇī yasya ratho rathāṅgayugalaṃ candrārkabimbadvayaṃ kodaṇḍaḥ kanakācalo harirabhūdbāṇo vidhissārathiḥ।
tūṇīro jaladhirhayāśśruticayo maurvī bhujaṅgādhipastasminme...॥ 3
yenāpāditamaṅgajāṅgabhasitaṃ divyāṅgarāgaissramaṃ yena svīkṛtamabjasambhavaśirassauvarṇapātraissamaṃ ।
yenāṅgīkṛtamacyutasya nayanaṃ pūjāraviṃdassamaṃ tasminme ...॥
govindādadhikaṃ na devatamiti proccārya hastāvubhāvuddhṛtyātha śivasya sannidhigato vyāso munīnāṃ puraḥ।
yasya stambhitapāṇirānavikṛtā nandīśvareṇābhavattasminme ... ॥ 5
ākāśaśca kuṭhāyate daśa diśābhogo dukūlāyate śītāṃśuḥ prasavāyate sthiratarānandaḥ svarūpāyate ।
vedānto nilayāyate suvinayo yasya svabhāvāyate tasminme ... ॥ 6
viṣṇuryasya sahasranāmaniyamādambhoruhāṇyarcayannekonopaciteṣu netrakamalaṃ naijaṃ padābjadvayam ।
sampūjyāsurasaṃhatiṃ vidalayaṃstrailokyapālo'bhavattasminme ...॥ 7
śauriṃ satyagiraṃ varāhavapuṣaṃ pādāmbujādarśane cakre yo dayayā samastajagatāṃ nāthaṃ śirodarśane ।
midhyāvācamapūjyameva satataṃ haṃsasvarūpaṃ vidhiṃ tasminme ...॥ 8
yasyāsandharaṇījalāgnipavanavyomārkacandrādayo vikhyātāstanavo'ṣṭadhā pariṇatā nānyattato vartate।
oṃkārārthavivekinī śrutiriyaṃ cācaṣṭa turyaṃ śivaṃ tasminme ...॥ 9
viṣṇubrahmasurādhipaprabhṛtayassarve'pi devāsurāsambhūtājjaladherviṣātparibhavaṃ prāptā yametyeśvaram ।
yānārtaṃścharaṇāgatāniti surānyo'rakṣadardhakṣaṇāttasminme ...॥ 10
॥ iti śrīmacchaṅkarācāryaviracitā daśaślokīstutiḥ sampūrṇā ॥
