Text:

vāsudevaṃ hraṣīkeśaṃ vāmanaṃ jalaśāyinam।
janārdanaṃ hariṃ kṛṣṇaṃ śrīvakṣaṃ garuḍadhvajam॥

vārāhaṃ puṇḍarīkākṣaṃ nṛsiṃhaṃ narakāntakam।
avyaktaṃ śāśvataṃ viṣṇumanantamajamavyayam॥

nārāyaṇaṃ gadādhyakṣaṃ govindaṃ kīrtibhājanam।
govardhanoddharaṃ devaṃ bhūdharaṃ bhuvaneśvaram॥

vettāraṃ yajñapuruṣaṃ yajñeśaṃ yajñavāhakam।
cakrapāṇiṃ gadāpāṇiṃ śaṃkhapāṇiṃ narottamam॥

vaikuṇṭhaṃ duṣṭadamanaṃ bhūgarbhaṃ pītavāsasam।
trivikramaṃ trikālajñaṃ tramūrtiṃ nandikeśvaram॥

rāmaṃ rāmaṃ hayagrīvaṃ bhīmaṃ raudraṃ bhavodbhavam।
śrīpatiṃ śrīdharaṃ śrīśaṃ maṃgalaṃ maṃgalāyudham॥

dāmodaraṃ damopetaṃ keśavaṃ keśisūdanam।
vareṇyaṃ varadaṃ viṣṇumānandaṃ vasudevajam॥

hiraṇyaretasaṃ dīptaṃ purāṇaṃ puruṣottamam।
sakalaṃ niṣkalaṃ śuddhaṃ nirguṇaṃ guṇaśāśvatam॥

hiraṇyatanusaṃkāśaṃ sūryāyutasamaprabham।
meghaśyāmaṃ caturbāhuṃ kuśalaṃ kamalekṣaṇam॥

jyotirūpamarūpaṃ ca svarūpaṃ rūpasaṃsthitam।
sarvajñaṃ sarvarūpasthaṃ sarveśaṃ sarvatomukham॥

jñānaṃ kūṭasthamacalaṃ jñānadaṃ paramaṃ prabhum।
yogīśaṃ yoganiṣṇātaṃ yoginaṃ yogarūpiṇam॥

īśvaraṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ vande bhūtamayaṃ prabhuma।
iti nāmaśataṃ divyaṃ vaiṣṇavaṃ khalu pāpaham॥

vyāsena kathitaṃ pūrvaṃ sarvapāpapraṇāśanama।
yaḥ paṭheta prātarutthāya sa bhavedvaiṣṇavo naraḥ॥

sarvapāpaviśuddhātmā viṣṇusāyujyamāpnuyāt।
cāndrāyaṇasahasrāṇi kanyādānaśatāni ca॥

gavāṃ lakṣasahasrāṇi muktibhāgī bhavennaraḥ।
aśvamedhāyutaṃ puṇyaṃ phalaṃ prāpnoti mānavaḥ॥

॥oṃ namaḥ bhagavate vāsudevāye॥
