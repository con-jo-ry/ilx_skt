arthāvabodhakamanarthakañca sarvamapi śabdajātaṃ dhvanirityaparanāmnā vyavahriyate । uktañca pātañjalamahābhāṣyasya paspaśāhnike "athavā pratītapadārthako loke dhvaniḥ śabdaḥ" iti ।
anena sārthakasya śabdasya dhvanirūpatvaṃ pramāṇatāmāyāti । nirarthako'pi śabdo dhvaniriti tu loke prasiddhameva । kiñca, nūtanadhvanivijñānaloke sarvavidho'pi śabdaḥ dhvanirucyate । saṃvitsvarūpā mantravīryātmā parā vāga, atha ca paśyantī, madhyameti vāktrayamapi dhvanipadena vyapadiśyate । tadyathā
evaṃ trividhavisargāveśasamāpattidhāmni ya udeti ।
saṃvit parimarśātmā dhvanistadeva mantravīrya syāt ।।
atra "saṃvit parimarśātma" iti padena parāvāg gṛhyate । paśyantyāśca parāvāṅmūlatvāt sā'pi dhvanisvarūpā। madhyamāyā vāco dhvaniśabdena vyavahāro yogacūḍāmaṇyupaniṣadi proktaḥ । tadyathā
tailadhārāmivācchinnaṃ dīrghaghaṇṭāninādavat ।
praṇavasya dhvanistadvat tadanaṃ brahma cocyate ॥8॥
evaṃ sarvavidhasyāpi śabdasya śāstrakāravanītyaparanāmnā vyavahārāta sarvo'pi śabdo dhvanināmāhaḥ । tena "akārādivarṇā eva dhvanipadavācyāḥ, na tu parādivāca" iti na svīkaraṇīyam ।
dhvaniśabdayorabhedavyavahārādeva "dhvanireva śabdaḥ" iti vaiyākaraṇānāṃ samayaḥ । uktañca śrībhartṛhariṇā vākyapadīyasya brahmakāṇḍe
ātmabhedastayoḥ kecidastītyāhuḥ purāṇagāḥ ।
buddhibhedādabhedasya bhedamekepracakṣate ॥ iti ।
pātañjalamahābhāṣyasya paspaśāhnike ca
athavā "pratītapadārthako loke dhvaniḥ śabda ityucyate, tadyathā śabdaṃ kuru","mā śabdaṃ kārṣīḥ", "śabdakāryayaṃ māṇavakaḥ" iti dhvani kurvannevamucyate । tasmād dhvaniḥ śabdaḥ । atra dhvanireva śabdaḥ । dhvaniśabdayorabhedasambandhaḥ । dhvani vinā śabdasya tattvena na pratītirato dhvanireva śabdaḥ।
