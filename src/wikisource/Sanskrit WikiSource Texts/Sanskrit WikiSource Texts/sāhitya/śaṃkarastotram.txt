|| śaṃkarastotram ||
namastubhyaṃ bhagavate śaṃkarāya mahātmane| jagadutpattivināśānāṃ hetave mokṣahetave||1||
sarvadevādhidevāya pārvatīpataye namaḥ| ṛṣiyogimunīndrāṇāṃ tvameva paramā gatiḥ||2||
brahmāṃḍagolake deva dayālūnāṃ tvamagraṇīḥ| ata evolbaṇaṃ pītaṃ tvayā hālāhalaṃ viṣam||3||
gaṃgādhara mahādeva candrālaṃkṛtamastaka| parameśvara māṃ pāhi bhayaṃ vāraya vāraya||4||
sarvapāpaṃ praśamaya sarvatāpaṃ nivāraya| duḥkhaṃ hara harāśeṣaṃ mṛtyuṃ vidrāvaya drutam||5||
stutiṃ kartuṃ na me śaktistava vāggatyagocara| dehi satsaṃgatiṃ bhaktiṃ niścalāṃ tvayi śaṃkara||6||
sarvāriṣṭaṃ parihara sarvaśatrūnvināśaya| dāridryaṃ hara sarveśa sarvānkāmān prapūraya||7||
mukhe nāma dṛśo rūpaṃ hṛdaye tvatpadāmbujam| mamāstu te namaḥ sāṃba prasanno bhava sarvadā||8||
tvadarcanavidhiṃ jāne na bhaktistvayi me hṛdi| athāpyanugrahāṇeśa kevalaṃ dayayoddhara||9||
iti śrī pa. pa. śrīvāsudevānandasarasvatīviracitaṃ śaṃkarastotraṃ saṃpūrṇam||
