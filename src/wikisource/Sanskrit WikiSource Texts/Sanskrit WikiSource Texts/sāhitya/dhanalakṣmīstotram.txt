|| dhanalakṣmīstotram ||
dhanadā uvāca
devī devamupāgamya nīlakaṇṭhaṃ mama priyam |
kṛpayā pārvatī prāha śaṃkaraṃ karuṇākaram ||
devyuvāca
brūhi vallabha sādhūnāṃ daridrāṇāṃ kuṭumbinām |
daridra dalanopāyamaṃjasaiva dhanapradam ||
śiva uvāca
pūjayan pārvatīvākyamidamāha maheśvaraḥ |
ucitaṃ jagadambāsi tava bhūtānukampayā ||
sa sītaṃ sānujaṃ rāmaṃ sāṃjaneyaṃ sahānugam |
praṇamya paramānandaṃ vakṣye'haṃ stotramuttamam ||
dhanadaṃ śraddhānānāṃ sadyaḥ sulabhakārakam |
yogakṣemakaraṃ satyaṃ satyameva vaco mama ||
paṭhaṃtaḥ pāṭhayaṃto'pi brahmaṇairāstikottamaiḥ |
dhanalābho bhavedāśu nāśameti daridratā ||
bhūbhavāṃśabhavāṃ bhūtyai bhaktikalpalatāṃ śubhām |
prārthayattāṃ yathākāmaṃ kāmadhenusvarūpiṇīm ||
dhanade dhanade devi dānaśīle dayākare |
tvaṃ prasīda maheśāni! yadarthaṃ prārthayāmyaham ||
dharā'marapriye puṇye dhanye dhanadapūjite |
sudhanaṃ rdhāmike dehi yajamānāya satvaram ||
ramye rudrapriye rūpe rāmarūpe ratipriye |
śikhīsakhamanomūrtte prasīda praṇate mayi ||
āraktacaraṇāmbhoje siddhisarvārthadāyike |
divyāmbaradhare divye divyamālyānuśobhite ||
samastaguṇasampanne sarvalakṣaṇalakṣite |
śaraccandramukhe nīle nīla nīraja locane ||
caṃcarīka camū cāru śrīhāra kuṭilālake |
matte bhagavatī mātaḥ kalakaṇṭharavāmṛte ||
hāsā'valokanairdivyairbhaktacintāpahārike |
rūpa lāvaṇya tārūṇya kārūṇya guṇabhājane ||
kvaṇatkaṃkaṇamaṃjīre lasallīlākarāmbuje |
rudraprakāśite tattve dharmādhare dharālaye ||
prayaccha yajamānāya dhanaṃ dharmekasādhanam |
mātastvaṃ me'vilambena diśasva jagadambike ||
kṛpayā karurāgāre prārthitaṃ kuru me śubhe |
vasudhe vasudhārūpe vasu vāsava vandite ||
dhanade yajamānāya varade varadā bhava |
brahmaṇyairbrāhmaṇaiḥ pūjye pārvatīśivaśaṃkare ||
stotraṃ daridratāvyādhiśamanaṃ sudhanapradam |
śrīkare śaṃkare śrīde prasīda mayikiṃkare ||
pārvatīśaprasādena sureśa kiṃkareritam |
śraddhayā ye paṭhiṣyanti pāṭhayiṣyanti bhaktitaḥ ||
sahasramayutaṃ lakṣaṃ dhanalābho bhaved dhruvam |
dhanadāya namastubhyaṃ nidhipadmādhipāya ca |
bhavantu tvatprasādānme dhanadhānyādisampadaḥ ||
|| iti śrī dhanalakṣmī stotraṃ sampūrṇam
