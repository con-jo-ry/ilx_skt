athāntaratarāṇi savāṇyāgneyāni sarvāṇyaindrāṇi sarvāṇi svārāṇi prājāpatyāni sāmānītyantaratarāṇi ॥ 20 ॥

atha yānyṛkta ākhyāyante mātṛtastānyākhyāyante । atha yāni sāmata ākhyāyante pitṛtastānyākhyāyante । atha yāni svarata ākhyāyante prajāpatitastānyākhyāyante ॥ 24 ॥
sa vā eṣa udgītho bandhumān bandhumatyaḥ ॥ 25 ॥
iti vedārthaprakāśe devatādhyāye pañcame brāhmaṇe prathamaḥ khaṇḍaḥ ॥ 1 ॥
dvitīyaḥ khaṇḍaḥ
athātaśchandasāṃ varṇāḥ ॥ 1 ॥
śuklā gāyatryo rūpeṇa sāraṅgaṃ rūpamuṣṇihām । piśaṅgaṃ kakubhāṃ rūpaṃ kṛṣṇāmānuṣṭubhaṃ tataḥ ॥ rohitaṃ bṛhatīnāṃ tu nīlaṃ pāṅktaṃ tataḥ punaḥ । suvarṇaṃ triṣṭubhāṃ rūpaṃ gauraṃ jāgatamucyate ॥ 2 ॥
ato yānyanyāni cchandāṃsi śyāvaṃ teṣāṃ tataḥ punaḥ । nakulaṃ tvekapadānāṃ dvipadā babhrurucyate । sāraṅgaśuklakṛṣṇarūpāṇi ṛgyjuḥsāmabrāhmaṇānvitāni ॥ 3 ॥
ye yajñeṣu prayoktavyāsteṣāṃ daivatamucyate ॥ 4 ॥
virājaḥ pṛśnayo vidyāt ॥ 5 ॥
daivataṃ tata uttaram ॥ 6 ॥
agnergāyatryabhavatsayugvoṣṇihayā savitā saṃbabhūvānuṣṭubhā soma ukthairmahasvān bṛhaspaterbṛhatī vācamāvat ॥ 7 ॥
virāṇmitrāvaruṇayorabhiśrīrindrisya triṣṭubiha bhāgo ahnaḥ । viśvān devān jagatyāviveśa tena cakḷpta (pra?) ṛṣayo manuṣyāḥ ॥ 8 ॥
prājāpatyā aticchandaso vicchandaso vāyudevatāḥ । puruṣo dvipadānāṃ daivataṃ brāhmya ekapadāḥ smṛtāḥ ॥ 9 ॥
vāsavī paṅktiḥ ॥ 10 ॥

sāvitrīgeyaṃ yatrāgītam ॥ 23 ॥
