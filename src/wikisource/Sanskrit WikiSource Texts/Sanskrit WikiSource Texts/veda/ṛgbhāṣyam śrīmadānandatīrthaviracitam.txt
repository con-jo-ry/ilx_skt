dhārakāṃścaiva vāyvādīn dhārayan vartate tataḥ||37
jānītha viṣṇukarmāṇi prāṇikarmāṇyaśeṣataḥ|
yato jātānyavijñato bhagavān sarvadaiva ca|
spaśājñātadṛśau yasmādindrasya sahitaḥ sakhā||38
prāṇasyaivendranāmnaśca kasmin nvahamiti śruteḥ|
tvayedindretivacanāt prāṇa indrābhidho mataḥ||39
ramābrahmaśivādibhyo yadviṣṇoḥ paramaṃ padam|
rūpaṃ paśyanti nirmuktāḥ saṃsārāt sūrayaḥ sadā|
cakṣurdivīva vyāptaṃ yadadṛśyaṃ phaladarśitam||2.40
sandīpayanti vijñānaiḥ saṃsārāt prāptajāgarāḥ|
vipanyavo jñānitamā viṣṇoryatparamaṃ padam|
tātparyādhikyavijñaptyā arthametaṃ punarjagau||41
vīryavantaśca ye somā yajamāneṣṭasādhakāḥ|
piba tān sarvadevānāṃ jñānādhikyau divispṛśau||42
manasaḥ prerakau caiva bahujñānau dhiyaspatī|
jajñānau vyajyamānau tu yajamānasya śodhakau||43
pareṇa brahmaṇā tasmin vṛddhau satyapatī sadā|
satyaṃ jyotiḥ prakāśatvāt tau mitrāvaruṇau huve||44
saṃsiddhān kurutāṃ nastau sagaṇastṛpyatāṃ hariḥ|
pūṣā pūrṇatvato viṣṇustanmitrāḥ pūṣarātayaḥ||45
sahasā balarūpeṇa śakreṇa sahitāḥ surāḥ|
madajñānaṃ hatātīva dānaśīlā na śaknuyāt||46
durvāk tadvān pumān vā naḥ bhāratī pṛśnirucyate|
gorūpā ca kvacit sā'bhūt tatsutāścākhilāḥ surāḥ||47
anyatrāpi sutāstasyāḥ jayatāmiva taddhvaniḥ|
dhairyādyāti yadā viṣṇuṃ vāyuṃ vā yāthanottamam||48
doṣatyāgādguṇaprāpterhaskāro vāyurucyate|
vidyudviśiṣṭajñānatvādbhāratī tatsutā hi ye||49
sarve devāśca maruto rakṣantvetādṛśo hariḥ|
tattadantargatatvena pūṣā pūrṇatvataśca saḥ||2.50
aghṛṇiḥ pūṣnaraśmitvādaja naścitrabarhiṣam|
brahmāṇaṃ ca divo devyā bhartāraṃ vāyumāja gām||51
yathā gataṃ jagadrājamavindad bhagavān sutam|
svakukṣigū.dhaṃ brahmāṇaṃ mahyaṃ somaiśca tarpitaḥ||52
sādhayāmāsa ṣaḍ yuktānindriyāṇi cakāra ca|
sādhanaṃ śaktidānaṃ tu gobhirvārbhiryathā yavam||53
ambayo mātaro hyāpaḥ kāraṇatvāt sahodarāḥ|
jagato jāmayo yasmāt saha tābhirvivardhate||54
adhvaraṃ kurvatāṃ kṣīraṃ somaṃ cāpi prapṛñcatīḥ|
sampṛktāḥ yāḥ samīpasthāḥ sūryasya sahitā api||55
hvayāmi tāḥ yāḥ pibanti gāvo naḥ sarvathā haviḥ|
kāryaṃ nadībhyaḥ tāsvantaradṛśyamamṛtaṃ sthitam||56
apāmeva praśaṃsārthaṃ sahāyāḥ santu naḥ surāḥ|
tātparyārthaṃ punarvākyaṃ sukhādhāro hutāśanaḥ||57
varūthaṃ guptiruddiṣṭā jyotirviṣṇudṛśe tathā|
sūriprāpyo yato viṣṇuḥ sūrya ityabhidhīyate||58
pṛṇa dāne'khilaṃ pāpamāpaḥ pravahatāpi naḥ|
hiṃsājaṃ śepajaṃ caiva tathaivānṛtasambhavam||59
rasena saṅgatāśca smo yasmādanucarā hi vaḥ|
kuyurmāmapi vidvāṃsaṃ madarthaṃ sarvadā surāḥ|
etaṃ māmiti vā'rthaḥ syādanujānantu voditam||2.60
śunaḥśepo dadarśāta uttaraṃ paramṛkśatam|
yūpādātmavimokṣāya stuvan devān pṛthakpṛthak||61
tryūnaṃ śatamihaivānyaścaturthe pañcame'pi ca|
maṇḍale brāhmaṇoktastu kramo'trāpi hyṛcāṃ bhavet||62
kartuḥ parānandatanoridānīṃ manāmahe cāru viṣṇoḥ surāṇām|
nāmeśaścedindirāyai punardād dṛśeyaṃ tāṃ mātaraṃ taṃ ca devam||63
sa muktānāmamṛtānāmajeśamukhyānāṃ cābhyadhiko yat sukhādau|
tenokto'sau katamo'khaṇḍitatvāt sa cāditirmahatī tanmahī ca||64
deśānantyāt tanmahitvaṃ guṇaistu kālāccoktaṃ tasya cākhaṇḍitatvam|
sa eva mātā ca pitā ca devastasmin sadā saṃsthitāstat punardāt||65
atti viśvaṃ tena caivāditiḥ sa śrīrvā brahmā prokta evaṃ ka eva|
guṇāśca tasyābhyadhyadhikāḥ sadaiva jīvebhyastat so'pi tadvākyayogyaḥ||66
dadātvasau viṣṇave cendirāyai prāptānasmānapi tau sarvagatvāt|
viśeṣadeśaprāptitastasya cāptirvaikuṇṭhādirloka ukto hareśca||67
tasyaivānyadrūpamagnyākhyamuktaṃ yo yajñeśo hūyate jāmadagnyaḥ|
sa devānāṃ prathamo nāma tasya smṛtaṃ sārthaṃ sarvakāmapradaṃ syāt||68
savitetyaparaṃ rūpaṃ jagatkāraṇamīśituḥ|
vittānāmīśvaraṃ tattu vāryaṃ hi varaṇīyataḥ||69
vittaṃ rakṣannavannityamātmānaṃ bhāgamemyaham|
tvāmaiśvaryādisamitiṃ yo'pi te paramo bhagaḥ||2.70
itthambhūto'vikāritvācchaśamānaḥ sukhādhikaḥ|
nedīyaso brahmaṇo'pi pūrvo dveṣādivarjitaḥ|
hastayostaṃ dadhe deva tava rūpamanāmayam||71
nido davīya iti tu viruddhārthadvayābhidhau|
dūrasāmīpyavacanāvubhāvapi mahodhadhau||72
yadā'nyaḥ savitā devastava svāmī bhago hariḥ|
adveṣo viṣṇurityuktestamahaṃ hastayordadhe||73
viṣṇubhaktasya te rāyo mūrdhānaṃ rāya uttamam|
tavāvasā''rabhe vidyāmuccairaśnāmi tatphalam||74
aiśvaryādiguṇā yasya bhago bhaktaṃ parātmanaḥ|
tasya te vittamaśnāmi tava rakṣābalāddhare||75
kṣatāt trāṇaṃ te balaṃ saṃvidaṃ ca pūrṇaṃ nāpurmuktigā ye viśiṣṭāḥ|
api svayaṃ saṃsṛterutpatantaḥ patayanto'nyāñchiṣyabhaktānudārāḥ||76
sadā śubhānyācaranto'pi devā abdevatā āpa iti śrutiryat|
gaṅgādyā vā vāyudevasya viṣṇorutpattiṃ ye jānate devasaṅghāḥ|
vayaḥ suparṇā iti vā'pyabudhne prakāśataḥ sambhajataḥ svamuccaiḥ||77
stūpaṃ sthānaṃ dadate pūtadakṣo nīcasthā apyūrdhvagāstena tasthuḥ|
nīcāśca ye muktigā mānuṣādyāsteṣāṃ budhno nīcalokaḥ surebhyaḥ|
nīcā guṇā aprakāśo'thavaiṣāṃ nīcoccatvaṃ kramaśaścāviriñcāt||78
asmākamantarnihitāni nityaṃ jñānāni bhūyāsurutādhikāni|
bhavantyarhā bhavituṃ vā bhaviṣyāḥ syurdvigrahe prārthanaivādhikārthe||79
uruṃ rājā rañjanādrājanādvā sūryāya panthānamasau cakāra|
svājñānusāreṇa sadaiva gatyai pādāvapādāya ca gantave'karot|
ajñāya vijñānamakaḥ sa gantumātmānamityartha ihāñjasoktaḥ||2.80
buddherdhātuḥ śaṅkarasyāpi doṣān vaktā jñānācchikṣakatvācca viṣṇuḥ|
jagadrakṣāsādhanānyapyanantānyanantadhā santi punastaveśa|
agādharūpā mahatī śubhā ca matirmama tvadviṣaye sadā'stu||81
ṛtaṃ jñānaṃ pratirūpatvahetornirṛtyākhyāṃ durmatiṃ dūra eva|
bādhasva durjñānijanaṃ nirasya svargāt parākṛtya ca śaśvadeva||82
kṛtaṃ ca pāpaṃ pramumugdhi mattaḥ ṛkṣāṇyamī nihitāni tvayoccaiḥ|
dṛśyāni rātrau na divā tvayaivetyādīni karmāṇyanirodhitāni||83
paśyan parānandatanostavaiva candro nityaṃ dṛśyamānastvayaiva|
samprerito yāti pare ca devā evaṃ tvadājñākaraṇāḥ sadaiva||84
evaṅguṇo yat tvamato vrajāmi sadā śaraṇyaṃ śaraṇaṃ vedavācā|
namannanyo yajamāno havirbhistadāśāste tvadgatiṃ devadeva||85
anindannasmān naramātrabuddhyā sambodhayoccastuta mā'smadāyuḥ|
cchetsīḥ naktaṃ divase caitadeva stotraṃ tvadīyaṃ prāhurāryāḥ sukāryam||86
mahyaṃ keto jñānarūpo hṛdistho nārāyaṇo vaktyayaṃ te'nyarūpam|
paśyatyapīśo yaṃ samahvad gṛhītaḥ sa no devo mocayatvagryaduḥkhāt||87
śunaśśepo hyahvadgṛhītaḥ sthānatraye vikrayādyaiḥ subaddhaḥ|
drutaṃ prāptāni drupadāni vikrayo yūpe bandhaḥ sañjñapanodyamaśca||88
ādityo'sāvādisaṃsthaḥ pareśo vārīśāntaḥ saṃsthitaḥ stūyate'tra|
śunaśśepo yamityādikena vārīśo'pi hyṛṣiṇā stūyamānaḥ||89
guṇāstrayo drupadānīti coktā dravatyayaṃ jīvasaṅgho hi yatra|
karotvenamavasṛṣṭaṃ sṛteḥ sa prasanno'smān karmapāśānmumoktu||2.90
nindāṃ ca te'vāggaterhetubhūtāmavagacchāmaḥ seyamityapyayogyām|
namaskāraistvadgataistvanmakhaiśca haviryuktaistvatprasādāt sa ca tvam|
kṣitirasmabhyaṃ prāṇarantā'khilajño viyojayāsmatkṛtānyāśvaghāni||91
puṇyaṃ pāpaṃ miśramityeva pāśānyathāyogyaṃ śrathayānyeṣu datvā|
tvanniṣṭhayā tena vayaṃ tvadīyalakṣmyāḥ putrāḥ syāma muktāḥ sṛteśca||92
eṣā viṣṇoḥ kevalā'pyevamanyā ayogyā yā anyadeveṣu samyak|
vākyairviśiṣṭairapavādahīnairjñeyā guṇā anyadeveṣu caiva||93
patiḥ somo varuṇasyāpyathāgneḥ patiḥ sūryastāvubhau tulyavīryau|
sūryācandramasoḥ patirindro'sya rudraḥ patirbrahmā patirasyāpi viṣṇuḥ||94
patirmuktervāsudevo'tha dṛṣṭerbrahmā vidyāyāḥ śarva indra kriyāyāḥ|
kriyāṃśānāmadhipā anyadevāstathā phalānāmapi muktamārge||95
yathāyoyaṃ svendriyaprerakāśca tataḥ pūjyā devatā muktikāmaiḥ|
iti śrutestāratamyaṃ guṇānāṃ jñātvā yojyāḥ śrutayastatratatra||96
divase divase seva kurmo yadapi te vratam|
yathaiva sujanā mā no hantuṃ sādhaya nighnate|
jihīlānastyajan dharmaṃ harati mā ca manyave||97
manastvayi nibadhnīmo gīrbhiraśvaṃ viyojitam|
badhnāti sārathiryadvanmṛlīko rakṣako'dhikam|
tvanmano'smāsu vā gīrbhī rakṣaṇāya vi sīmahi||98
parāk patanti prajñā me vividhāḥ śubhalabdhaye|
pakṣiṇo vasatīryadvaddhā na tvāṃ prāpnuvanti ca||99
ato'khilakṣatrapatimāhvayāmaḥ kadā vayam|
avināśinaṃ rakṣaṇāya mahādarśanamañjasā||2.100
tatprasādād brahmavāyū samamevāvagacchataḥ|
venantau hi vijānantau tau hi jñānivarau matau||101
yathājñānaṃ prayogaṃ ca dāśuṣe kuruto na tau|
aśvinau varuṇaścet syāt ākāśe pakṣiṇāṃ padam|
vettyasau sūkṣmavijñānāt tathā nāvo jale'bdhigaḥ||102
muktānāṃ ca sthitiṃ veda jñānasya ca gatiṃ parām|
kṣīrābdhiśayano viṣṇuḥ kālaṃ kālodbhavaiḥ saha||103
ya ātmanaḥ samīpe ca jāyate taṃ ca padmajam|
muktaṃ vā vartanāṃ vāyormahato'tiprakāśinaḥ||104
bṛhato jñāninaścaiva bṛtahato mahato'pi vā|
adhikāni padānyeva devā adhyāsate'pi tān||105
sāmrājyāya sa pastyāsu niṣasāda prajāsu ca|
sujñānaḥ tatra san viśvānyadbhutāni ca paśyati|
kartā cikitvān sa kṛtakartavyāni ca paśyati||106
viśvānyahāni no devaḥ supathāni karotvasau|
dadātvāyūṃṣi no nityaṃ muktau vā'tidrutabhramam||107
hitaṃ ca ramaṇīyaṃ ca cakraṃ svarṇaṃ ca varṇataḥ|
abibhracca sunirṇiktaṃ śuddhaṃ vastramavasta ca||108
avijñātacarā devāḥ spaśastasya niṣedire|
parito varuṇaḥ pāśamabibhraddrutabandhanam||109
atambhayanti na yaṃ vīrāḥ stambhakā api rākṣasāḥ|
janānāṃ drohiṇo daityāḥ śatravaścābhimātayaḥ||2.110
asamaṃ ca yaśaścakre yo'smākaṃ hṛdayeṣu ca|
āmānuṣeṣu cānyeṣu samantādātmanaḥ sadā|
ajñeṣvapi ca martyeṣu vyatanodātmano yaśaḥ||111
mahājñānaṃ pareśānamicchantyo mama dhītayaḥ|
sarvato yānti yadvacca gāvaḥ śruṅgasvarānanu||112
āpūrṇaṃ madhu mokṣasthaṃ sukhaṃ pacasi me'gnivat|
tat saṃvocāvahai bhūyaḥ priyastvaṃ mama yat sadā||113
adarśaṃ taṃ ca sarvajñaṃ tadrathaṃ ca bhuvi sthitam|
sa giro me juṣata ca tvā'vanepsurahaṃ cake||114
paśyeyaṃ tvaṃ ca viśvasya rājā bhavasi medhiraḥ|
medhāvān gmaḥ pṛthivyāśca sa kāle ca prati śrudhi|
satvaṃ triguṇapāśānno mumugdhi cchindi cāñjasā||115
atha stautyagnigaṃ viṣṇumagnimapyagnigocaraiḥ|
vacanaiḥ sarvadevānāṃ pūjāyai prodyato bhavan||116
vasiṣvottamavastrāṇi bhūṣaṇairapyalaṅkuru|
kurvannutsavamatyantamasmadīyaṃ makhaṃ yaja||117
nityamedhya hare deva bhogyānnānāṃ sadā pate|
ūrjitānāṃ janānāṃ vā svasmin no adhvaraṃ yaja|
ramayatyadhvani śubhe yena tenādhvaro makhaḥ||118
vaco no ni nayātyantaṃ divi vyāptatayā sadā|
aśeṣadevagatvena manmabhirmatibhiḥ saha|
avamo daśadeveṣu vahnirviṣṇustu tatsthitaḥ||119
sakhye ca sūnave mahyamāyajatyeṣa devatāḥ|
āpiḥ prāpyo mama svāmī bhṛtyatvānmahyamāpaye||2.120
asya me'rthe kuru madaṃ svātantryānmādayasva vā|
śaśvadvistarataḥ sarvān yadā devān yajāmahe|
tadā tvayyeva hi havirhūyate sa priyo bhava||121
mandro madakaraḥ strīṇāṃ puṃsāṃ cobhayavargiṇām|
canaḥ sukhaṃ dhā asmāsu vāyoḥ putro'gnireva cet||122
bhagavān vāyunā vyaṅgyo vāravān baḍabāmukhaḥ|
svadṛṣṭānto hareryasmād viṣṇuḥ sa vaḍavāmukhaḥ||123
sūnuḥ sa śavaso vāyoḥ pṛthujñānaḥ suśevakṛt|
sukhakṛnno bhavenmī.dhvān svāmī naḥ sūnureva vā|
vyaṅgyatvādvāyunā sārdhaṃ suśevaḥ susukhātmakaḥ||124
āsāt samīpataścāsmānaghārthāyornipāhi ca|
pratiprati sado nityo viśvāyuḥ tvamimāṃ stutim||125
saniṃ dānasvarūpāṃ ca gāyatracchandaātmikām|
navyāṃsaṃ stutiṣu śreṣṭāṃ vada deveṣu sādaram||126
uttamādhamamadhyeṣu bhogeṣvasmānavāpnuhi|
rakṣakatvena śikṣāsmān jñānavasvarthameva ca|
ante mokṣe mitatvena jñānamantamamucyate||127
kṣīrābdhyūrmisamīpastho muktabhogavibhāgadaḥ|
yajamānāya sadyaśca kṣarasyakhilamīpsitam|
nityamuttamatejastvāccitrabhānurhariḥ smṛtaḥ||128
yaṃ martyaṃ rakṣasi vibho yaṃ yutsu prerayasyapi|
jñānājñānātmakeṣveva sa gantā nityasatsukham|
bhogān vā muktigān nityāniha cedupacārataḥ||129
paripūrṇo'sya naivāsti tvadīyasya tu kasyacit|
bhogo'pi bahulo yasmād ditsasyeva punaḥpunaḥ||2.130
sahantyaḥ santatabalaḥ sahasaḥ putra eva vā|
śravāyyaḥ śravaṇīyaśca suprasiddhatamo'pi vā||131
vājaṃ tārayitā''netā so'rvadbhirgosamāptitaḥ|
tvadīyastu bhavedviprairdātā vipuladānataḥ||132
yuddhasya tārako'nnasya dātā bhogasya vā bhavet|
indriyaistamasā yuddhaṃ tarutā jñānado'thavā||133
stutiboddhā yatastāṃ tvamavadhāraya sarvadā|
nityajñāno'pi datvā tatphalaṃ vetteti kathyate|
rujāṃ vidrāvakeśāya pratyakṣaṃ tava vā stutim||134
nitarāmamitatvena mahān purusukhaśca saḥ|
dhūtkaroti garutmān yaddhareśceddhūmaketutā|
tācchīlye ma iti hyasmāt sūtrāddhūma itīritaḥ||135
jñānāyānnāya ca sa naḥ prahiṇotu sukarmaṇām|
devānāṃ ketubhūto'sau vittavān viśpatiryathā|
asmadīyāni cokthāni bṛhattejāḥ śruṇotvatha||136
nārāyaṇabrahmapurassarāstu devā mahāntaḥ śiśavaḥ skandapūrvāḥ|
vaṭusvarūpāśca ta eva kutracid yuvāna indrādaya eta eva ca||137
mahattvameṣāṃ guṇato'pi yūnāmindrādayo madhyaguṇā yuvānaḥ|
āpekṣikālpaiśca guṇaistadīyaiḥ samīritā āśinā arbhakāśca|
kṛtaṃ namaḥ śaktimanto yajāma na jyāyasāṃ stutimātraṃ lupāmaḥ||138
ekasya viṣṇorapi rūpabhedā anīyāṃsaḥ santyathavā mahāntaḥ|
yuvattvasāmye'pi mahattvabhedād yuvā mahānityapi bheda uktaḥ||139
vistīrṇādhastanā yatra grāvāṇo'bhiṣavoddhṛtāḥ|
dhiṣaṇābhiṣutān somānava naḥ svīkṛtastute|
gura svīkāra ityasmād dhātoḥ stutyo hi jalgulaḥ||2.140
phalake'bhiṣavasyaiva striyo jaghanasāmyataḥ|
prokte upacyavaścāpacyacaḥ saṅgatibhedataḥ||141
jihvā grāvā'tha vāgyuktirucyate'bhiṣavastathā|
khalaścorustathā dehaḥ somo mana itīritaḥ|
jñānotpattau ca phalakasthāne oṣṭhe udīrite||142
mantaraśmīnaśvaraśmīn yathā yatra vibadhnate|
raśmayo'nyatra nāḍyaśca yasmād yukto gṛhe gṛhe|
dehedehe'thavā tena dyumaddundubhimadvada||143
pṛthivī devatā tasya tadgato vā svayaṃ hariḥ|
somāmātyo'paro devo vanaspatiritīritaḥ||144
somo vā tadgaviṣṇurvā śrutaḥ phalakadevatā|
vṛkṣāṇāmapi sarveṣāmataḥ vṛkṣāgra eva tu||145
vāyurvāti vanānāṃ vā bhajanīyadivaukasām|
patirviṣṇustu tasyāgre vāyurdevo hi gacchati|
pātave svasya somaṃ vā mano vā sunu viṣṇave||146
āyājyāvannalabdh.ṣṇāmuttamāvatibhakṣakau|
jaha bhakṣaṇa ityasmād yathā'śvāvannabhakṣakau||147

na pūryate ca kopāya svalpatvānmānuṣaḥ kvacit||93
bahuparvayuto'tyuccagirirmārge hi vo'tyajat|
mūlaṃ yeṣāṃ tathā'jmeṣu mārgeṣvatibhiyā dharā|
kampate jīrṇarājeva sthiraṃ vo jananaṃ yataḥ||94
jātā vāyordivi vayo mātuḥ prāptāni retave|
nirgantuṃ tatkṣaṇenaiva sarvalokāṭanāya hi|
yauvanaṃ te yuvāno hi nisargānmāturā janeḥ||95
mātureva prasādena samyak taddvividhaṃ balam|
prajñābāhubalaṃ yasmāt tasmāt diśa udāpire||96
te giraḥ sūnavo devyā vāśrā vacanaśīlinaḥ|
abhijanmānusāreṇa guṇān yātumudāpire||97
padbhyāṃ bhūmiriti śrutyā sutā viṣṇorvasundharā|
tajjatvāt parvatā viṣṇornaptāraḥ secanāddhi miṭ|
haristamapi mervādiṃ marutaścyāvayanti hi||98
amṛdhramapradhṛṣyatvāt balaṃ yadapi vo'khilam|
janāṃścyāvayituṃ sthānācchaktāḥ samyag girīnapi||99
kurvanti śāstrasaṃvādaṃ mārge śruṇvanti kecana|
teṣāṃ madhye hi marutāṃ śībhaṃ kṣipraṃ prayāta naḥ||3.100
madaṃ kuruta tatraiva dohanānyatra santi hi|
sarvamāyurjīvase'sti prasādānmarutāṃ hi naḥ||101
kadanayorhastayordevā dadhidhve priyatātavat|
kadā'pi yat priyastātaḥ kadhapriya itīritaḥ|
stṛtaṃ barhiryadviṣaye te proktā vṛktabarhiṣaḥ||102
divaḥ pṛthivyāścedānīṃ kva gacchatha ca kiṃ phalam|
na gacchatha kva vā'pyetajjñātavyaṃ tatprasādataḥ||103
āhvayanti kva vo gāvo yathaiva yavasārthinaḥ|
phalārthinaḥ stuvanto vaḥ kvāhvayanti tathaiva ca||104
stutyāni vaḥ sukhāni ca kathaṃ jñeyāni naḥ sadā|
ityabhiprāyataḥ praśnaḥ kasmin no jñāpakastviti|
suvitāni subhadrāṇi saubhagaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ param||105
pṛśnirvedaḥ praśnayogyaḥ pṛśnimātā haristataḥ|
jñeyastasyāpi vijñātā marutaścet sutā giraḥ|
mānuṣā yadi yūyaṃ vaḥ stotā devo bhavedapi||106
na syādasevyaḥ stotā'tra yathā yavasagocare|
goprāpye tu mṛgo'sevyo yamamārgaṃ na ca vrajet|
yāñcārītiviśeṣo'yaṃ nindā stutyekadeśayuk||107
mā vadhīnnaḥ kvacinmṛtyuḥ parataḥ parato'pi ca|
durhaṇā duḥkhadā hantrī yātu sā tṛṣṇayā saha||108
aprameyā yathā satyaṃ dhanvannapi hi secanam|
rucāṃ vidrāvakasyaiva vāyoḥ putrā hi rudriyāḥ|
vinā'pyecetanaṃ vātaṃ vṛṣṭiṃ kṛṇvanti hīcchayā||109
eṣāṃ sakāśād vṛṣṭiśca vidyud bhūmiṃ prakāśayet|
gaurvatsamiva bhūmyāṃ ca lokaṃ siñcati mātṛvat||3.110
mānuṣāditvamanyaccābibhedeṣāṃ svanādapi|
gṛhamāṇino gṛhādyaṃ vā cacālaiṣāṃ svanādapi||111
akhinnairgamanaiścaiva dṛ.dhasvīyaiśca pāṇibhiḥ|
anuyāta nadīścitrāḥ sthirāḥ santi rathādayaḥ||112
brahmaṇaśca patiṃ viṣṇuṃ yathāvad vyāptayā girā|
jarāyai saṃstutitvena tṛtīyā bhāvarūpiṇī||113
sarvā vibhaktayaścetthambhāvavācinya eva ca|
mahāvyākaraṇe sūtramiti saṃstutirūpataḥ|
vada viṣṇuṃ svaprakāśamagnivat sūryavat tathā||114
āha cātmānamevātra dār.dhye sva pratiyogitā|
iti sūtrāt kīrtimasya viṣṇoḥ kuru mukhe sadā||115
vistārayati parjanyo yadvad vṛṣṭiṃ tathaiva ca|
vistṛṇūhi tatheśasya tathā kīrtiṃ jagatyapi||116
panasyuṃ jñānaśīlaṃ ca vijñeyaṃ gaṇamarkiṇam|
abhavan jñāpakā vṛddhā asmākaṃ tadanugrahāt||117
parāvato hareḥ sthānānmānaṃ śocirivāsyatha|
sūryādayo yathā śocirdūrādāsyanti jantuṣu|
evaṃ jñānaṃ sampradāyānmaruto viṣṇureva vā||118
itthaṃ yathā sarvadṛśyaṃ tat tathā bhavatāṃ kṛtam|
evaṃ yatra kva vā jñānādbalādvā gacchata kvacit|
svatantratvāt parāpekṣā nahi vaḥ kvacidiṣyate||119
kiñcidyātha balāt kiñcid dhūnutha svabalāddhi tat|
śatrūṇāmapanodāya santi vastviha hetayaḥ|
stambhanāya ca śatrūṇāṃ sudṛ.dhānyeva tāḥ sadā||3.120
sthiratvaṃ dīrghakālatvaṃ vīLutvaṃ dṛ.dhatā tathā|
yuṣmākaṃ taviṣī tejo balamastyuttamaṃ sadā|
māyino nahi martyasya balaṃ tejo'thavottamam||121
sthiraṃ cājñānāpāpādi tadbhaktāya parāhatha|
gurūnapi narān nityaṃ guṇairvartayathāñjasā||122
parvatānāṃ pṛthivyāśca diśo nityaṃ viyāthana|
vaninaśca tarūn samyag bhajanīyān surān hariḥ||123
bhūmau divi ca vaḥ śatruḥ kvacinnaiva hi labhyate|
riśādasaḥ susukhinaḥ sukhena śamadanti yat||124
balaṃ tejo'pi vo hyasti vyāptā nityatayā yujā|
adyāpyādharṣaṇāyaiva nikhilāśubhasantateḥ||125
pravepayanti parvatān viviñcayanti vanaspatīn|
janiparvavato jīvān samprerayati keśavaḥ||126
bhajanīyapatīn brahmapūrvāṃśca phaladānataḥ|
tāratamyena samyak ca vivecayati sarvadā||127
sarvaprajāsvatiṣṭhanta prāptāstābhiḥ sahaiva ca|
mattā iva prerayanti śubhāśubhakṛtau narān||128
ayugdhvaṃ pṛṣatīścaiva rohitaṃ pṛṣṭhato rathe|
vo yāmaṃ prati dār.dhyāya śeṣamaśret punardharā||129
bhītimāpurmanuṣyāśca kṣipraṃ vaḥ prāptaye sukham|
rakṣāṃ vṛṇīmahe yuṣmad gantā'dyāpi ca no'vasā||130
bhītiyuktāya kaṇvāya mahyaṃ yuṣmābhireva ca|
prerito'tha niyantṛtvād yuṣmākaṃ mānuṣeṇa ca||3.131
bāhyadṛṣṭyā tvabhūtīrno ya icchati tamojasā|
balena ca drāvayata yuṣmadīyotibhistathā||132
ojo'samaṃ bibhṛtha ca makhejyāḥ samamuttamam|
sudānaśīlāḥ kaṇvāya dattāsamamabhīpsitam|
asamairno'pyabhiprāyairganta vṛṣṭiṃ na vidyutaḥ||133
devayantaḥ pūjayantaḥ stuvanto'tikṛtāśanaḥ|
prāśūḥ vāyoḥ sutetyeva sahasasputra ityatha|
tadvyaṅgyatvāddhariḥ proktaḥ śasrastatsuta eva ca||134
yuddhaṃ hitadhanaṃ proktaṃ nihitaṃ dhanameva vā|
svakhyaṃ tu svindriyaphalamācake'paśyadityapi||135
patistu brahmaṇo viṣṇurvāgīśatvāt samīraṇaḥ|
agrato yātvasāvasmatkāryeṣvevaṃ sarasvatī||136
sūnṛtā vaiṣṇavī vā vāg yajñaṃ vīraṃ naraṃ prati|
nayantu devāḥ sarve'pi yat pañcajanavardhanaḥ||137
paṅktirādhā haristena vīro naryaḥ sa eva ca|
devagandharvanṛpitṛdaityāḥ pañcajanābhidhāḥ||138
vāghate stuvate viṣṇurdadāti sūnaraṃ vasu|
suṣṭhu yena naro bhūyāt sunaraṃ jñānamucyate||139
sa dhatte cākṣayāṃ kīrtimiLāmannaṃ yajāmahe|
suvīryatvāt suvīraṃ tat supratūrtiḥ supeśakṛt|
prāṇaceṣṭānimittatvāt tadaneha itīritam||140
sa eva bhagavān viṣṇurmantraṃ tadviṣayaṃ vadet|
ukthamutthāpakatvena sa eva bhagavān hariḥ||3.141
tanmantravācyā devāśca yadvācyā devatāstu tāḥ|
tameva mantraṃ vocema jñānārthaṃ sukhakāraṇam||142
imāṃ vācaṃ svīkuruta sakāśādvo'khilaṃ śubham|
aśnavānatidaurlabhyād devabhaktaṃ ka āpnuyāt|
yajamānaṃ vā'nugacchet kaḥ samyak stṛtabarhiṣam||143
tatratatra prasthito'sau yajamānaḥ sukhaiḥ svayam|
sveṣṭasthāneṣu cāntarvā viṣṇurantargato yataḥ|
tadvattvena kṣayaṃ sthānaṃ prāpnoti jñānato yajan||144
upapṛktaḥ kṣatriyaiḥ sa āveśena janārdanaḥ|
hanti śatrūṃśca tairdevo bhaye ca svāśrayaṃ dadau||145
vartā'bhigantā tarutā jetā cāsya nahi kvacit|
yuddhaṃ mahādhanaṃ tvarbhaṃ prasiddhaṃ dhanameva hi||146
