Text:

jaṭāmaṇi
śrī vighneśamahaṃ natvā guruṃ sarvāvadhāninam
jaṭāmaṇiṃ pravakṣyāmi śrījaṭā valśabhaiḥ samam 1
śikṣādiśāstramakhilaṃ prasamīkṣya samyagvedārthalakṣaṇavidāṃ sukhabodhanāya
nemānabhairavabudhena maṇirjaṭāyā saṃkāśyate paraśivasya kṛpā kaṭākṣāt 2
yasya jihvāmukhe vāṇī vedaśāstramadhī sadā
nṛtaṃ karoti mātā taṃ veṅkaṭeśaṃ nato'smyaham 3
yasyeśvarasyādivarasya putryāḥ sahaikyamadvaitanatīva satyam
ānandayatyatra bhavajjanānāṃ taṃ śaṅkaraṃ lokaguruṃ nato'smi 4
prātiśākhyādiśāstrajñassarvaśikṣāviśāradaḥ
sūkṣmabuddhisamartho yassajaṭāṃ vaktumarhati 1
jaṭāṃ hitvā vicitrārthāmanulomādikaṃ vadan
sa vai narakamāpnoti svaravarṇavilopivat 2
asyāḥ pramāṇaṃ prathame prātiśākhye prapāṭhake
rephādeśe'pi śabdācca na dhāmetyārṣaśabdataḥ 3
jaṭāyāṃ pañcadhā sandhirārṣapauruṣabhedataḥ
ādyantamārṣasaṃjñeyaṃ śliṣṭamanyattu pauruṣam 4
trikrameṣvaṣṭadhā sandhirdvau dvāvādyantayorapi
ārṣasaṃjñāsta evātra sandhayonyetvanārṣakāḥ 5
padadvayamanukramya vyutkramyotkramya sandhimat
yathāvatsvarasaṃyuktaṃ prayuñjyātsā jaṭā matā 6
nyāyādanugrahādyacca dṛṣṭāntādyattaducyate
vilome nyāyataḥ prāptassandhirityabhidhīyate 7
yatrārṣe'nudbhūtasandhirjaṭāyāmudbhavedyadi
tadbrāhmaṇāraṇyakoktasandhirdṛṣṭānta ucyate 8
sakṛdādirdvitīyaṃ dviḥ paṭheccādi padaṃ sakṛt
sandhitaḥ kramavacceti procyate sā jaṭā budhaiḥ 9
trikramasya jaṭā jñeyā hyuktvā pūrvaṃ kramaṃ tathā
punaruktaṃ madhyaṃ cādipunarādyantaraṃ param 10
padatrayaṃ samāruhya trikrame tu sasandhikam
avaruhya yathānyāyaṃ punarārohaṇaṃ smṛtam 11
trikrame tu padānāṃ tu trayāṇāmavarohaṇam
tathaivārohaṇaṃ teṣāṃ paścādājpūrvamiṣyate 12
padadvayaṃ ṣaṭpadaṃ ca tathā navapadaṃ vadet
svaravarṇā viśeṣe ca viśeṣe trikrame kramāt 13
svaravarṇādabhedasya yatpadadvitayasya ca
jaṭā vilakṣaṇairuktāḥ kramoccāraṇameva hi 14
iti paribhāṣā
lopālopaṣatvaṇatvayatvādeśāgamādiṣu
saṃhitāṃ ca yathā prāptirjaṭāyāṃ tadvadeva hi 15
yānagnayontvatapyante'nyatrapūrvakṛte yaṇa
lopābhāvenagna iti nimittagrahaṇaṃ kṛtam 16
grahaṇaṃ tvekamuddiśya padaṃ vai kriyate yataḥ
tadanyatra tu na grāhyaṃ yajjaṭābhimatairiti 17
tathaivāśvājanītyatrāpyekādeśe kṛte tataḥ
ākāraśrutisaṃprāptaḥ tasmāllopo vilomake 18
tatheyātāṃ brāhmaṇāyetyopetyākṣarasaṃhitā
tatrāpi pūrvaprābalyādaikāro'tra na saṃbhavet 19
chatve prāpte śakārasya varṇaśliṣṭe tataḥ param
nakāraḥ prākṛtastasmācchnathadvṛtramiti sthitiḥ 20
anuśabde hyukārasya vakāre vaikṛte sthite
jaṭāyāṃ naivalopaḥ syādakārasyeti nirṇayaḥ 21
anuśabde hyukāraḥ syādvakāre vaikṛte sthite
jaṭāyāṃ lopa eva syādyathāsāṃhitamantarā 22
hrasvā bhājāntu sarveṣāṃ hrasvatā pūrvapauruṣe
ādāvante ca dīrghaḥ syānmaddhye uttarapauruṣe 23
yasyārṣe pragrahatvañca tasyaivānārṣe bhavet
uktalakṣaṇa bhāve'pi pauruṣe na bhavetkvacit 24
pradhānaṃ vā nimittaṃ vā dvipadagrahaṇaṃ yadi
vyutkrame sarvato grāhyaṃ krame tatraikadeśataḥ 25
padadvayannimittaṃ yadekābhāve'pi tatra tu
saṃhitāvat kramo jñeyāstadvat jñeyā jaṭā budhaiḥ 26
cādīnāmeva lopomolopokyapi parasya tu
udaḥ parassalopastu tasminsthāstanbhuke sati 27
utpūrvasya sakārasya vyañjane lopa iṣyate
pūrvatra tvanvoḥ sthitayorvalopastvavyaye sthite 28
lopābhāve makārasya kvibaṃ te rājatau pare
teṣāmeva prasiddhatvādaviśeṣeṇa sūcitam 29
teṣāmeva vilome'pi nānyeṣāṃ savidhismṛtaḥ
avagraho makāro yassaṃsāmiti na lupyate 30
savijñeya iti prājñastato'nyatrāpi saṃbhavet
yo lopassottarastiṣṭhantyekayā grahaṇe tathā 31
ityekamiti molopo na nimittamapekṣate
pṛktādapṛkte dīrghaḥ syādguṇo varṇādbhavedyadi 32
tasmāttaduttaro dīrghau bhavedanyatra vāgamaḥ
athavā deśa ūbhāvastattasmātsāṃhite'pi ca 33
āyoddhvaryokrato pūrvo lopa syādapyasāṃhite
lupto yadi bhavedārṣe kriyate nānyathā budhaiḥ 34
ṣatvaṇatvasvabhāveṣu yaduktamupadeśataḥ
grahaṇantatkarotyetatkāryaṃ sarvatra nānyathā 35
saṃhitāvatkramo jñeyo jaṭā ca vikṛtādiṣu
vikārāgamalopeṣu yathā sāṃhitakāryabhāk 36
nimittipratiṣedhe tu na nimittabalādvidhiḥ
iṅgyabhede tva saṃkhyāne vidhirna pratiṣedhabhāk 37
yathā punaḥkṛdhibalānna satvavidhiriṣyate
prādhānyātpunarityatra nimittatvātkṛdheriti 38
kṛddhyuttare punaśśabdādvilome satvamiṣyate
nāddhvarañceti sūtreṇāvagrahastho niṣiddhyate 39
satvekārasya pūrvatvāddīrghasyāpyupalakṣaṇam
patnī vegrahaṇātkiṃ ca gnāspatistatra darśanāt 40
udathetyatradīrghastu hrasvasyāpyupalakṣaṇam
tathā prapara ityatra hrasvo dīrghopalakṣaṇam 41
dhaṣavati niṣedhasyādviśvataḥ kṣatramatra ca
pade ceṅgyapade nityannāvissūtre kṛdhīti ca 42
kakhapeṣantathā varṇapūrvaṃ saṃyātyavagrahaḥ
atra devariṣaścāvirnniriddhaśśaśvatopasaḥ 43
yatra syānmahisṛjyaddhvaṃ tatra ṣatvaṃ na pauruṣe
ṛtavaḥ pratiṣedhācca mahi sapta ca darśanāt 44
ṛkārarephayuktaśca niśśabde vāyureva ca
ṣatvameva prasajyeta māhiṣe yena bhāṣitaḥ 45
kaṇṭhoktāddhikayoryaśca virodhaḥ smaryate kvacit
kaṇṭhoktastu balīyān syādāviśśabdastu sādhakaḥ 46
annārabhyānnyamannañcedyatra ṇatvaṃ prasajyate
tasmātpāryādhipūrve'pi tathāpi na vadanti hi 47
saṃhitāvadvidhiprāptaṃ bahavo na vadanti yat
addhyetāro jaṭākāle tadbāhvādṛtamucyate 48
śikṣā ca prātiśākhyaṃ ca virudhyetādvṛdho yadi
śikṣā durbalamityāhussiṃhītyevamṛgī yathā 49
prakṛtau prātiśākhyaṃ ca balavattviti kīrtitam
śikṣā balavatī caiva hyanārṣe sarvasandhiṣu 50
jaṭāyāṃ vakṣyamāṇāyāṃ kampasandhisvarādayaḥ
avadhāya prayoktavyā nipuṇena dvijena tu 51
na nūnaṃ nṛtyanti na hyatyapyannyaśabdennya uccakaḥ
nakāro dvitvagolakṣyāttakārorddhve'pi vāntakaḥ 52
ṭapūrvasya nakārasya ṇatvaṃ kuryādvicakṣaṇaḥ
pūrvaṃ paścāṭṭakārasya yathāvaṇṇamadarśanāt 53
sūtreṣvadṛṣṭaṃ yatkiñcitsūtrāntarabalādbhavet
anuktasyāviruddhasya mataṃ grahaṇamanyathā 54
ṭavargātparabhūtasya lasya duśliṣṭatā bhavet
anyathā cedvirāmācca dvitvā bhāvādviruddhyate 55
autvannodautpareramṛtyāraṃ propāvapūrvake
aminantasvapāṭhe ca padaṃ syānno yathā ṣuṣu 56
yasyaivārṣe yatvarephau syātāṃ tasya hi pauruṣe
sati svarapare tadvanniṣedho nānyathā bhavet 57
yatvarephau vinānyatra grahaṇaṃ dṛśyate yadā
ṣatvaṇatvādi sarvatra kartavyamanulomavat 58
yatra yatra ca yatvaṃ syāttatra tatvantathā bhavet
niṣedhaśca jaṭāyāntu tatra tatvaṃ tathā bhavet 59
ārṣe tu grahaṇādyatra prakṛtissaṃhitā bhavet
ādyantayorjaṭāyāmapyārṣatvātprakṛtirbhavet 60
tathaiva mithunītyatra vilome yatvamiṣyate
saṃhitāyāmabhāvācca mithunyaṣṭau ca darśanāt 61
stomāya jyotirityatra ṣatvaṃ sasya na vidyate
anavagrahapūrvatvādviddhyabhāvastathā sati 62
aprasaktirniṣedhasya na pratiprasavaḥsmṛtaḥ
pratiprasavatā yasya ṣatvaṃ tasyaiva nānyathā 63
suvorohāveti yaśca tatra lupto visargakaḥ
punargrahaṇasāmarthyāt otvamāpnoti rottare 64
kṛdhi suvasvānāsaśca divyadhityanna doṣabhāk
asminyajñe jaṭāyāñca hyagnayaḥ paprayophsukaḥ 65
etadvacanamaniṣṭam
tripadaprabhṛtinyāyo lopālopeṣu vidyate
tatra sthamanyayuktaṃ cetpūrvadharmo na vidyate 66
tripadaprabhṛtirllopālopayoreva saṃbhavet
na pragrahena yatvādāviti vedavido viduḥ 67
edodbhyāṃ vakṣyate tacca yadanekapade punaḥ
jaṭocyate'nyaśabdena tatrānaikyanna vidyate 68
prajñā tu kramato hyādau jaṭāyāndṛśyate yadā
alopalopakāryāṇi tripadādau tathottaram 69
prādhānyasya tṛtīyatvaṃ tripadaprabhṛti smṛtam
ṛci brāhmaṇavākyeṣu hyalaṃ dvitripadādikam 70
punaruktaṃ yataḥ pañca padamityuttarañca vā
pūrvavadbhavati jñeyaṃ sarvatrāpi vicakṣaṇaiḥ 71
paritvā punarūrjāyannosya rātīyato'pi ca
saptatepo anutvāgne tvaṃ gomāṃścādhirocane 72
citrañca bhavatandraphsassahasvapunaraikyataḥ
pavasva pratapāssūrye paro'gne ca punastathā 73
āyuryajñenavājaśca prāṇamevāyureva ca
yenāgne'smin sadhasthe ca vājyaddhvanastvanaikyataḥ 74
kramyāddhvano bhavatyagre pāvako'rpayatīti ca
viṣayeṃgira ityevāpyagra ityādi lupyate 75
dadhikrārpaṇṇarakṣā ca maryaśrīrjanevaca
tā asya hiraṇyagarbhassañcāriṇyamanatyapi 76
aśvājannyagnayaścaiva addhvaryo'tho atho bhavet
punaruktaṃ yathoktaṃ syātpadapañcakameva hi 77
tathā brāhmaṇavākyeṣu alaṃ dvistripadādikam
syāttāmavidvānityatrānusvārāgame yadi
anārṣenaiva vijñeyo'pyapyakārādisaṃgrahaḥ 78
dyauraha iti tu yannimittaṃ dṛśyate'tra vai
ṇatvaṃ syādbhinnadeśīya sthitvā caiva na vidyate 79
aśvājannyagnayaścaiva jāgarūkapradarśanam
śrutigrahaṇasāmarthyādātreyeṇāpi sūcitam 80
padākṣarāṅgavarṇānāṃ dvividhā padasaṃhitā
iyaṃ nānāpadokte ca ucyante pañca saṃhitā 81
saṃhitāñca padaṃ vāpi kramañcaiva jaṭāṃ paṭhan
lakṣaṇajñastathāpnoti brahmasthānaṃ hi śāśvatam 82
saṃhitāpāṭhamātreṇa yatphalaṃ procyate budhaiḥ
pade tu dviguṇaṃ vidyātkrame tu ca caturguṇam 83
varṇakrame śataguṇaṃ jaṭāyāṃ tu sahasrakam 84
tathā ca gāruḍapurāṇe
vidyānāmuttamā vidyā vedavidyā samīritā
atastadadāturasyaiva lābhasvargāpavargayoḥ 85
vidyānāñca parāvidyā brahmavidyā samīritā
atastaddānato rājan sarvadānaphalaṃ labhet 86
devīpurāṇe
veda eva dvijātīnāṃ sādhanaṃ śreyasaḥ param
tato syāddhyāpanābhyāsātparaṃ brahmādhigacchati 87
tameva śīlayetprājñaśśiṣyebhyastaṃ pradāpayet
tadabhyāsapradānābhyāṃ paraṃ brahmādhigacchati 88
yonadhītya dvijo vedānanyatra kurute śramam
sa janmanyeva śūdratvamāśu gacchati sānvayaḥ 89
vedavidyā vihīnasya vidyājālaṃ nirarthakam
kaṇṭharajjuvihīnāyāḥ kāminyā bhūṣaṇaṃ yathā 90
aśikṣitānāṃ vedeṣu śāstrābhyāso nirarthakaḥ
kimastyanupanītasya vājapeyādibhirmakhaiḥ 91
bhaviṣyatpurāṇe
aputro labhate putramadhano labhate dhanam
vidyāddhyayanasaṃyuktaḥ pare brahmaṇilīyate 92
yajurvedasvarūpamucyate
yajurvedaḥ piṃgalākṣaḥ kuśamaddhyo bṛhadgalaḥ
bṛhatkapālaḥ kṛṣṇāṅghristāmraḥ kāśyapagotrajaḥ 93
saṃbhavaparvaṇi
kṛtvārtthāṃśca prakāśadbhiryajurbhirnirmalasvaraiḥ
jaṭāvarṇavibhāgajñairucyamānānanekaśaḥ 94
anuśāsanike parvaṇi nāradastotre
saṃhitāyāṃ ca sarvasyāṃ sthitasyopasthitasya ca
padadvayakramasyāpi jaṭāyāṃ ca sa pāragaḥ 95
yo jaṭāmātravit brahmasandhijño viṣṇurucyate
īśvarassarvasandhijña ityevarṣibhirīritaḥ 96
jaṭāvarṇakramābhyāṃ ye vinā vedavino dinaḥ
te yānti narakaṃ ghoraṃ yāvadindrāścaturdaśa 97
jaṭāmaṇimūlaṃ saṃpūrṇam
