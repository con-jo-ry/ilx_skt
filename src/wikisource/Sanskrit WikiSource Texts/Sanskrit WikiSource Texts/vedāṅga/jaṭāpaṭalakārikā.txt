jaṭāpaṭalakārikā
śaiśirīye samāmnāye vyāḍinaiva maharṣiṇā
jaṭādyā vikṛtīraṣṭau vakṣyante nātivistaram 1
jaṭā mālā śikhā rekhā dhvajo daṇḍo ratho ghanaḥ
aṣṭau vikṛtayaḥ proktāḥ kramapūrvā manīṣibhiḥ 2
brūyātkramaviparyāsakramamīdṛgvidhāṃ diśet
jaṭākhyāṃ vikṛtiṃ dhīmān vijñāya kramalakṣaṇam 3
anulomavilomābhyāṃ trivāraṃ hi paṭhetkramam
vilome padavatsaṃdhiranulome yathākramam 4
ṣatvaṃ ṇatvaṃ ca dīrghatvaṃ kramasaṃdhau yaducyate
vyutkrame tanna kartavyaṃ svarakṣaiprādikaṃ bhavet 5
vilome padavatsaṃdhiranānupūrvyasaṃhite
ānupūrvyaṃ krame tyājyaṃ vyutkramaṃ padavatpaṭhet 6
catuḥkrame trikrame ca tathā pañcakrame'pi ca
ādyantābhyāṃ trivṛnmadhyaṃ brūyātkramaviparyayaiḥ 7
samayādau krame yadvatsamayātparatastathā
jaṭāyāṃ samayādisthaṃ śuddhena parivartayet 8
samayakramaṇaṃ nāsti samayādikramaṃ vinā
trikramaṃ samayādisthamādinā saha taṃ paṭhet 9
vyutkrame kramavatsaṃdhiḥ sa ca kṣaiprādiriṣyate
ḍakārādividhānaṃ tatsvarāntaḥparivartanam 10
tryakṣarāntapragṛhyāṇāmukārāmantritasya ca
vilome padasaṃdhau tu pragṛhyatvaṃ na vidyate 11
ekāraukāraparastvakāro'bhinihanyate
vyutkrame kramavaccaiva navadhā kampalopanam 12
vilomasaṃdhau praśliṣṭe kṣaiprābhinihiteṣu ca
varjayitvā svaraṃ jātyaṃ triṣu kampaniṣedhanam 13
parigrahāvagraheṣu dvyudāttādipadeṣu ca
udāttādividhānaṃ tacchaunakoktaṃ bhavediha 14
ādityairyātamityasminpāde cāṣṭākṣare bhavet
padādhyāye'nudāttaḥ syātsaṃhitāyāmudāttavat 15
padadvayamanukramya vyutkramyotmyakra saṃdhivat
svaralakṣaṇasaṃyuktā sā jaṭetyabhidhīyate te 16
iti jaṭāpaṭalakārikā
