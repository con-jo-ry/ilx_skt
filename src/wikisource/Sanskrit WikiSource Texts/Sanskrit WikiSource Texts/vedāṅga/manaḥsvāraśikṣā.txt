athāntyāvasāne hyeko jātyaḥ | kukkuṭo'si janayattyai tvā'gne vratapata' eṣa te gāyatrī dhruvo'si pṛthivīndevāndivam̐vvasavastvā'ditiṣṭvā'yandakṣiṇā sahasrasya pramā nama'āśave' imā me vājaśca vittañca sucaśca devo'agniryate svāhā kāya dvitīyāgnaye svāhā''yuryajñenaikasmai rohitau dhūmrarohitaḥ śuddhabālastṛtīyoktāḥ sañcarāḥ suparṇaḥ pārjanyaḥ khaḍgo vaiśvadevo'gneḥ pakṣatirbrahmaṇe brāhmaṇaṃ tapase kaulālamarmebhyo hastipambībhatsāyai paulkasamekatriṃśat 49
athāntyāvasāne kampaḥ | pṛthivi devayajanyadittyai vyundanamagne vaḥ samudraṅgaccha prathamaḥ svāṅkṛto'si svarṇacatuḥ kampāiḍaspade tanūnapāttsarasvatīnnarāśarṭha0 sanna dvikampo duro diśo vvājāya svāhā somāya kuluṅga indrāgnyoḥ pakṣatiragnaye'nīkavate'agnaye gāyatrāya narmāya pūm̐ścalūṃ sumitriyā no'ṣṭādaśa 50
athāntyāvasāne kampo jātyaḥ | bhūtāya tvā divivviṣṇurdvitīyaḥ siṃhyasi tejo'si tejo mayi dhehyābrahmannaśvastūparaḥ śilppā vaiśvadevya'etā' aindrāgnā dvitīyaḥ kṛṣṇagrīvā'āgneyaḥ kṛṣṇagrīvā daśa 51
athāntyāvasāne dvirjātyaḥ | askannamadyāggrayaṇo'sīdamuttarādāśustrivṛttsamudro'si nabhasvānpṛśnistiraścaunapṛśnistṛtīyaḥ kṛṣṇagrīvā'āgneyā'agnyavāpo'rddhamāsānāmindrasya kkroḍo nava 52
athāntyāvasāne trirjātyaḥ | siṃhyasi mitrāvaruṇābhyāntvā ṣaḍjātyo madhave tvā'gnaye tvā mahyaṃ caturo jātyaḥ pṛthivyāḥ sadhasthā vivṛdasi prathamādvitīyaiścatvāro jāttyā madhave svāhā śādandadbhirnava 53
athāntyāvasāne nakārāntāḥ | mano me prathamāgnaye'nīkavate vasantāya kapiñjalānāntsamudrāya śiśumārāntsomāya harṭha0sānagnaye kuṭarūntsomāya lavānanhe pārāvatānvasubhya' ṛśyānīśānāya parasvatedaśa 54
atha niravasāne hyādyudāttāḥ | namo va' indraśca marutaśca viśvedevāścamase'veṣṭā dandaśūkādvayamudīcīmāroha mā cchanda'ekayā stuvataivaśchando babhluśāya rohitāya kṛtsnāyoṣṇīṣiṇe visṛjadbhyaḥ sabhābhyo gaṇebhyastakṣebhyo'aśvebbhyo hrasvāya vannyāya viṃśatiḥ 55
atha niravasāne hyanudāttāḥ | prāṇāya me prohyamāṇaḥ somaḥ pūṣā pañcākṣareṇārtthetastthaḥ pratīcīmārohamūrdhvā vvayo navabhirastuvata raśminā sattyāya pratipadasyagnimadya sūpastthā'adya dvādaśa 56
atha niravasāne kampaḥ kṛṣṇo'si madhumatīrnnaḥ parameṣṭṭhayabhidhīta iti triḥ 57
atha niravasāne kampo jātyaḥ | samudro'syagnirekākṣareṇa yavānāmbhāgastriḥ 58
atha niravasāne hyeko jāttyaḥ viśvedevā navabhirastuvata namo hiraṇyabāhave namaḥ kapardine srutyāya vvātyāya pāryāya sapta 59
atha niravasāne dvirjātyaḥ | uśigasi bṛṣṇaūrmirjjyeṣṭhāya vilmine brajyāya pañca 60
atha niravasāne trijātyaḥ | sobbhyāya kūpyāya caturagnerbhāgo'si vasūnāmbhāgo'si sikattyāya śuṣkkyāya ṣaṭ 61
atha niravasāne madhyāvasāne dvitīyaśarmāsyagne vvājajidbhūrasyādade rāvā'si dvirjātyamindrāya ttvā bṛhadvate pañca 62
atha caturavasāne hyādyudāttāḥ | pratyuṣṭṭarṭha0 rakṣa' indhānāstvā tryambakandhāmānyuśmmāsi ma'hirbbhūrmmā pṛdākuragne pavasvādṛśśramasya vvājī kanikkradadedho'sīndrasyaujasttha svāhā rudrāyaikādaśa 63
atha caturavasāne hyādyanudāttāḥ | agnīṣomayorujjitiṃ dvikampāḥ sajūrddevenāṃśurarṭha0śuṣṭṭhe'gnīṣomābhyāmmarutvantam̐ vṛṣabhamuttiṣṭhannojasā yuvantadevasyāhamprathamo devasya ttvā pṛthivyāḥ suparṇo'si virāḍjyotirimarṭha0 sāhasrantisrovāyoḥ pūtaḥ punantu mā pitaraḥ sam̐vvatsaro'syagnimadyobhayo rṛjave tvā hiraṇmayena pātreṇeti dvāviṃśatiḥ 64
iti maharṣiyogiyājñavalkyakṛtā manaḥsvāraśikṣā samāptā
