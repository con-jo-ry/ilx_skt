ātreyokteṣu sūtreṣu sthito'niṅgyapadānyaham
ślokarūpeṇa vakṣyāmi saukaryāya supāṭhiṇām 1
niṣkaivalyaṃ ca niṣṭarkyaṃ niṣādebhyo niṣaṅgadhiḥ
mahimānaṃ ca niṣkāvamāhuvadhyai madintamaḥ 2
aśvataro vatsataraiḥ kikidiviḥ vidīgayaḥ
upahatnuṃ ca navatiṃ phaligaṃ ca hiraṇmayam 3
ṛdūdareṇaiḍabṛdāḥ śumbhamānā kanikradat
varimāṇaṃ ca saudāsān gaupatyaṃ gautamādapi 4
uccāvacānanantakaḥ prāśṛṅgo'psarasaḥ punaḥ
aṅgirasvatkṛkalāsaḥ krumukaṃ cāśuśukṣaṇiḥ 5
dhūmrimāṇaṃ svadayitā dandaśūkā anāgasaḥ
harimāṇaṃ ca devebhirapi prathamaniṣkṛtau 6
sautrāmaṇyā uttaredyuranena samanehasam
kṛkavākuratīkāśadatīkāśānanulbaṇam 7
prathamānaṃ tanūbhiśca pūrvedyuḥ puṇḍarisrajām
duṣṭarītuśca kakubhiraṇudaṃ codakaṃ tathā 8
nābhānediṣṭhamāgnīdhrātsarṇīkāya sanīti ca
gopau gopāya gobhiśca mathitvā giriśāya ca 9
avāśṛṅgajaṣau yaśca juhurāṇamayācitam
dūṣikābhiratīkāśān janamānaṃ kariṣyataḥ 10
anāśīrkeṇa vasukassahamāno ghanāghanaḥ
nīcakāro vājayantaḥ āyannucāditiṃti ca 11
paracchepaścakravākaḥ kṛṣamāṇaḥ pariṣkṛtaḥ
acchāvākaśśroṣamāṇāḥ śobhamānā kapardinaḥ 12
mānthīlavaḥ dacāvarta gabheyaśca canohitaḥ
pākalāya ca śṛṅgāṇi prāsacāya pratāyate 13
vaiśantāya avāryāya nīhārāyāyavastathā
ekādaśāsaskandhebhyo vahamānā vipaścitaḥ 14
aśītimne matasnābhyāṃ varimāṇaṃ ca dampatī
parikramya pravāyeta trasadasyuśca duṣṭaram 15
vāstavyaḥ śunaḥśepaṃ sāsahyāma priyaṅgavaḥ
antarikṣāccāpayitā naktayā ca kapardine 16
pare pakṣe'nuvāke yā dakārādi ca tatpade
ā...ya retaskāya tadvināniṅgyamiṣyate 17
ājīgartiṃ yunajate pataṅgaṃ ca sahasvatī
uditaṃ hi matasnābhyāṃ snāvanyābhyāṃ pravatvati 18
upāṃśuśabdassarvatra vāvṛdhānaḥ śayaṇḍakaḥ
draviṇasyuratharvāṇaḥ anamitrāya dvatsutaḥ 19
gāyatrībhiścikituṣe jagatībhiśca datvate
sokṣamāṇo dātasaśca rakṣamāṇaśca vājinam 20
jāyamānassakṛtvā yathāvamānena paśyate
jityai ca janiṣṭhyāya patayanta śacīpatiḥ 21
svaraṇaṃ jīvanasvāyai vṛṣadaikṣasalāvṛkī
surabhīṇi samābhiśca suvitāya viniśrutiḥ 22
uto tataśca marutvanta mārjayante dṛśīkavaḥ
sādhāraṇaṃ vivasvāṃśca suvite ca jihiṃsima 23
saṃskṛtyeti sarvatra bharamāṇā ayākayag
pratnathādīni catvāri sadṛśaṃ svadhitiriti ca 24
īdṛśāyādi catvāri satrasyardhyā vipaścitaḥ
uṣitvā prāvṛtā sā ca bījakā pippakā tathā 25
amīvā ambarīṣādvā ambarīṣe ca dundubhiḥ
taidhātavīyenāmartyamaśramiṣṭhāśca viśvate 26
prāṇāyano viśvatīnāṃ cayamānā ayasmayam
vaikaṅkatī sardigṛdimāśubhāca vihantavai 27
ātreya śikṣā saṃpūrṇam
ātreya śikṣā
atha ātreya śikṣā tṛtīyo bhāgaḥ
prathamau ślokau
atha kramavyutkramaṇotkramādyato jaṭā bhavedārṣajasaṃdhijasvarāt
vilokya śikṣādikamādarāttataḥ prakāśyate sā viduṣāṃ mude mayā 1
padadvayanimittaṃ syādekābhāveti tatra tu
saṃhitāvatkrame jñeyastadvajjñeyā jaṭā budhaiḥ 2
antimaḥ ślokaḥ
prathamasya dvitīyatvaṃ pūrvaṃ kurvīta paṇḍitaḥ
dhūdvakṣṇau dvandvakṣṇaṣamityatra anusvāro vidharmataḥ
ityātreyaśikṣā
