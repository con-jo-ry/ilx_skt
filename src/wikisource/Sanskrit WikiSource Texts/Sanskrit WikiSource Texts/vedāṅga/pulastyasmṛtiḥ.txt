atha pulastyasmṛtiḥ
varṇāśramadharmavarṇanam
kurukṣetre mahātmānaṃ pulastyamṛṣayo'bruvan
tāṃśca dharmānprakārāṃśca no vada smārtamāgamam 1
evaṃ pṛṣṭaḥ pratyuvāca sarvāṃstān pṛcchata ṛṣīn
paṃcadhā vāsthitaṃ dharmaṃ śṛṇudhvaṃ dvijasattamāḥ 2
varṇadharmaḥ smṛtastveka āśramāṇāmataḥ param
varṇāśramastṛtīyastu guṇanaimittikastathā 3
varṇamekaṃ samāśritya yo'dhikāraḥ pravartate
varṇadharmaḥ sa vijñayo yathopanayanaṃ triṣu 4
yastvāśrayaṃ samāśritya padārthaḥ saṃvidhīyate
ukta āśramadharmastu bhikṣāpiṇḍādikaṃ tathā 5
ubhayasya nimittena yo vidhiḥ sampravartate
naimittikaḥ savijñeyaḥ prāyaścittavidhiryathā 6
brahmacārī gṛhasthaśca vānaprasthaśca bhikṣukaḥ
ukta āśramadharmastu rājadharmastu pañcamaḥ 7
varṇadharmaścaturṇāṃ yaḥ āśramāṇāṃ tathaiva ca
dvayoḥ sādhāraṇo dharmo yaścāsaudvayaśeṣabhāk 8
ijyādhyayanadānāni yathāśāstraṃ sanātanam
brahma kṣatriya vaiśyānāṃ sāmānyo dharma ucyate 9
yājanādhyayane rājño bhūtānāñcābhirakṣaṇam
pāśupālyaṃ kṛṣiścaiva vaiśyasyājīvanaṃ smṛtam 10
śūdra sya dvijaśuśrūṣā dvijānāmanupūrvaśaḥ
śūdrā ca vṛttistatsevā kāruṇyaṃ karma tathaiva ca 11
gurauvāso'gniśuśrūṣā svādhyāyo vrata dhāraṇiṃ
trikālasnāpitā bhṛtyai gurau prāṇāṃtikī sthitiḥ 12
tadabhāve gurusute tathā sabrahmacāriṇi
kāmato vā samānatvaṃ svadharmo brahmacāriṇaḥ 13
samekhalo jaṭī daṇḍī muṃḍī gurugṛhāśrayaḥ
anyathā grahamedhitvaṃ gacchetkāmato vāśramāṃtaram 14
agnihotropacaraṇaṃ jīvanañca svakarmabhiḥ
dharmadāneṣu kāmyeṣu sarvatra janavikrayāḥ 15
devapitr! yatithibhyaśca bhūtānāmānukampitā
śrutismṛtyuktasaṃskāro dharmo'yaṃ gṛhamedhinaḥ 16
jaṭitvamagnihotritvaṃ bhūśayyājinadhāraṇam
vanevāsaḥ payomūlanīvārakaṇavṛttitā 17
pratigrahanivṛttiśca triḥsnānaṃ maunadhāraṇam
devatātithipūjā ca dharmo'yaṃ vanavāsinām 18
sarvāraṃbhaparityāgo bhaikṣānnaṃ vṛttamūlatā
niṣparigrahatā dro haḥ samatā sarvajantuṣu 19
priyāpriyapariṣvaṅgaḥ sukhaduḥkhāvikāritā
sa bāhyābhyantarāśaucaṃ niyamo vratakāritā 20
sarvendri yasamāhāro dhāraṇādhyānanityatā
bhāvaśuddhistathetyevaṃ parivrāḍdharma ucyate 21
ahiṃsā satyavādaśca satyaṃ śaucaṃ dayā kṣamā
varṇināṃ liṅgināñcaiva sāmānyo dharma ucyate 22
svajñānaṃ hṛdi sarveṣāṃ dharmo'yaṃ varṇiliṅginām
udṛṣṭārtho dvividhaḥ prokto dṛṣṭārthaśca dvitīyakaḥ 23
ubhayārthastṛtīyaśca nyāyamūlaścaturthakaḥ
ubhayāvyavahāraśca daṃḍadhāraṇameva ca 24
tulyārthānāṃ vikalpaḥsyānnyāyamūlaḥ prakīrttitaḥ
vede tu vidito dharmaḥ smṛtau tādṛśa eva tu 25
anuvākaḥ śrutisūktaṃ kāryārthamiti mānavāḥ
tadarthaśca prayāso'yaṃ sa ca sevyaḥ phalārthinā 26
uktaḥ pañca vidho dharmaḥ śreyo'bhyudayahetukaḥ
puruṣāṇāṃ yathāyogaṃ sa ca sevyaḥ phalārthinā 27
sadyastu prauḍhabālāyāmanyathā vatsarācchaciḥ
pradātā yāṃ trirātreṇa dattāyāṃ pakṣiṇī bhavet 28
iti pulastyaproktaṃ dharmaśāstraṃ samāptam
