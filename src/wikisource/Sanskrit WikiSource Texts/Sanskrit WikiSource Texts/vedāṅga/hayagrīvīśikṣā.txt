Text:

hayagrīvī śikṣā
krame yathokte padajātameva dvirabhyaseduttarameva pūrvam
abhyasya pūrvaṃ ca tathottare pade'vasānamevaṃ hi jaṭā vidhīyate 1
tribhiḥ padaiḥ syātkramako yatra tatra
madhyaṃ tvanabhyasya ca pūrvamabhyaset
arthācca madhyasya bhaved dviruktitā
saṃdehadaṃ tviṅganataḥ pradarśayet 2
abhikrame kramaśāstraṃ pramāṇaṃ
syād vyutkrame vyākaraṇaṃ pradhānam
uktakrame vyutkramaṇe viśeṣaḥ
svarāḥ prakāradvitayepyabhinnāḥ 3
catuḥkrame trikramavat parītya
saṃdarśayenmadhyapadasya rūpam
tatheṅgayetpadajātaṃ vikāri
vyāvartane saṃhitāvatpragṛhya 4
ṛte kramaṃ dvaipadasaṃhitāsvarajñānaṃ na ceti kramakaprayojanam
tathānyataḥ padasaṃdherviśeṣitaḥ
svarābhidhāne hi jaṭā prayojikā 5
kramasvareṇaiva jaṭāsu bhūyate
samajyate'totra na tiṅṅatiṅvidhiḥ
svarābhidheyā hi yathāśrutaiḥ svaraiḥ
padeṣvathāmantritajātikeṣvapi 6
iti jaṭāpaṭale prathamo vargaḥ

atha dvitīyo vargaḥ
madhyasvarastrikrame yatra lupyate
vyāvartane tadgrahaṇaṃ ca sarvataḥ
syādanyathā vigrahaṇe prayojanaṃ
kiṃ vābhijāte padaniścaye tadā 7
saṃdhirnipātasya kadāpi noktas
tathāpi pūrveṇa jaṭāsu dṛṣṭaḥ
svarā hi sarve kramavadvidheyāḥ
kampā api syuḥ svarasaṃdhijāśca 8
ardharcāntyaṃ padamekaṃ pradṛśyate
tadā tadabhyasya viramya ceṅgayet
ṛcyekameva prakaṭaṃ padaṃ bhavet
pareṇa sākaṃ parivartanaṃ tadā 9
okāra āmantritajopi dṛṣṭaḥ
saṃdhīyamāno na tu saṃhitāyām
svare pare'kāravarjaṃ jaṭāyām
upaiti saṃdhiṃ na paro viśeṣaḥ 10
anānupūrvyeṇa ca yatra saṃhitā
padakramād vyutkramaṇaṃ punaḥ kramaḥ
bhavetprakāro'yamanādiruttamaḥ
sadā jaṭāyā iti sarvasaṃmatat 11
iti dvitīyo vargaḥ
mūlaśikṣā samāptā
