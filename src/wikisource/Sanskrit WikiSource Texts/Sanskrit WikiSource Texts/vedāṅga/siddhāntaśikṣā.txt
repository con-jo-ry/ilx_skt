siddhāntaśikṣā
saṃpraṇamya sakalaika kāraṇaṃ
brahmarudramukhamauli bhūṣaṇam 1
lakṣaṇaṃ śrutigirāṃ vilakṣaṇaṃ
śrīnivāsamakhinā praṇīyate 2
bhūminindāpraśaṃsāsu nityayogo tiśāyante
saṃsargavyakti bhāve ca bhavanti matubādayaḥ iti 3
pūrvaśikṣāḥ parāmṛśya prātiśākhyaṃ ca sarvaśaḥ
siddhāntaśikṣāṃ vakṣyāmi vedabhāṣyānusāriṇīm 4
akārādi padānāṃ syādahodāharaṇaṃ kramaḥ
ādimadhyāntatastatra yāvadvedaṃ nirupyate 5
avātamānītapūrvaṃ syādidaṃ prāgattu no haviḥ
nahendraṃ vṛtramahata parasmai ca dadāśataḥ 6
vṛtraṃ nedaṃ ti nudate ceṣyase pūrvavānnati
asyāḥ paro pratiṣṭhāyā abhidavaiḥsadhetyapi 7
labhate'raṇyamaṃśūnāṃ māprajābhirhetipūrvavān
mahimānaṃ ca pūrvaścedakārādipetyayam 8
saṃ caskarasya bhūyāttu śaṃ novāto tra veḥparaḥ
asasarjādasaṃsparśya camasaṃcodakaṃ paraḥ 9
narmāyacedakārāntaścātsandhuścayutādyavam
vārayātpūrvavaraṇo'pyapiprayamadhibruvan 10
antodānte'pyaniṅgye ca paraśaunaṅpare'pi ca
arcandūmāca juhavāṃ veḥ paraṃ dravaṇe param 11
varāhapūrvamihataṃ deve'pyāyāmayānti ca
antodāttamatiṅgyaṃ cedakārāntaṃ stutaṃ bhavet 12
sadanaṃ ca ṛtasthāsthavitastaṃ ca parārdhataḥ
mānte mṛtyaḥvapaitaṃ syāttadantīsyādihāhitaḥ 13
athapravakṣyāmyādādīnāharāvadyapūrvakān
prayāsāyemato na sminnāṅpūrvastapate bhavet 14
āraṇye prāṇa āyāti bhavāmyetya vadānake
skhalayācāpade pādāyāsaṃśravasi pāuke 15
anavarṇā catasrohaḥ parato nityamāhitā
evaśabdastu dīrghāntaḥ niṣebhiḥ paripūrvakaḥ 16
iṣṭyai yajñasyasaṃpūrvo nādhamānāḥ parāstavate
idhai na barhiryāte'gne chandāṃsyupapare bhavet 17
rephādvyañjanapūrvāccedikāraḥ kvaciduttaraḥ
bhavedakapade hrasvo yakāre parataḥ sthite 18
lakṣmīyādipadānāṃ tu yaṇādeśo na vidyate
tatra bhinnapadatve'pi triyambakamiti sthitiḥ 19
bhartryo ya tryorātryapitryamāmantryāpi ca kāṭhake 20
patnyasvardhiṣṇya tanvāṃ ca syuryaṇādeśasaṃyutāḥ
saṃjñānamapi loko'si tubhyaṃ bhadraṃ saheti ca
pañcaite pūrvikaiḥ proktāḥ kāṭhakā ityudīritāḥ 21
aṣṭau kāṭhakasaṃjñāḥ syuḥ
hotrī naptrī śatādrudrīvaryasīti dvirūpakāḥ 22
kriṣṭidhrīstrī ca dīrghāntāḥ padaika hrasvagāḥ pare
chinnaṃ pratisatāṃ śikyaḥ śamyo goṣvabhilipya ca 23
pṛśchidaṃ tristha su mati tam̐hajīrṇaṃ parājiha
manīṣāṇa maniṣye ca svapityanitivāriti 24
yācitaḥ kṣipramajiramajirāsastaritrataḥ
prathayitvājamajayad dhvanayiccajinojinā 25
stanihiśraddhaya paya śayitā ca śayiṣyate
virājamevataiḥ pūrvaṃ diśitasmin prapāṭhake 26
saṃvatsaro ṣṭa -- pūrve na vāśiriṣyati mokṣaṇīḥ
bharantyetāvatīhagau rujanti pari ye priyāḥ 27
sumṛḍīkā ṛtena tvaṃ naktaṃ pūrvasarasvatī
māno mitra udasthaṃ sīt joṣonācaḥ prajavakṣara 28
agnirvāva ca devāvai keśavāpāvadhāyakaḥ
rakṣām̐sītyanu vākeṣu syādapyetirikāravān 29
sarasvati priyebhyo hi śucike to hiraṇyayoḥ
jyotiṣkṛṇoti yanma ta sṛmṛddheti ṣoḍaśī 30
ītvaṃ syādiyurīṣāyāṃ dvitīyaṃ nihitāvapi
prerate vīkṣamāṇāyāpyapratīkṣaṃ pratīkṣate 31
eva pūrvo'gnihotrīsyādaplīdvīpisamāvati
mayapūrvaśca dadhatī prarohanti mahiṣyapi 32
dhāvateḥ pragukārādiḥ iṣṭakāyāḥ paro'pyupa
pṛthivīmannameṣāyāṃ vrataṃ dhatte śvamutaraḥ 33
unattyunattutreruddhimupuṣṭairunnavāśinaḥ
hrasvo'ta ekamacchidre syādṛcchāṃ tu sadaṃ paraḥ 34
yudhmo yuvāṃ satyugrohanudyamāno'pyanūrudham
kurīrā rurucuḥ śuddhāḥ prasutena suśukvaniḥ 35
vatsaśabdātparo no ce dakārotau pare pyupa
drugāsthāṇvāsippasiṣu śritayāmaṃ śayeṣu ca 36
nṛmuṇantu parassupta yātujūnāṃ supadyapi
kuluñcānāṃ kuluṅgo'snuk sujihva ca vibandhuṣu 37
didyuturbabhlukaruṇaṃ bāhūru suṣuvurmadam
śutudrigauruvitaṃ ca gugupset tṛpṇutarbhuvaḥ 38
pupūpau triṣu pūrveṣu śaṣaheṣu pareṣu ca
rephādukāra evasyātsvarabhaktirna vidyate 39
bhāgyaṃ parastanū mo ṣū carmo dho ndratamūdimaḥ
agnā viṣṇū kratūsyātāmūrjo bhāgaṃ śatakratū 40
ṛkārādi vṛṣāsyaṃśau yasyāyamṛṣabhaḥ param
kvalairūrdhvamṛtāni syātsṛpyadṛśyāyati tṛcam 41
bibhṛhisyātpipṛhi ca trapattripalacakruṣuḥ
cakārākārayoḥ pūrve ṛkāraṃ caturhotṛṇām 42
ekārādistu tatraitaṃ etat śreṇaitya neṣi ca
mṛtyuṃ yaje prayatyahve te yajñe śreṣṭa vatsare 43
citra kirmīrakalmāṣaśabalaitāśca karbure
somaindrāstraivido daiti prairayannapyupaiṣyatha 44
asugāgamagambhīra some somyā i tīyate
pūtudraudhāṃ pare praukṣatān kena puruṣaṃ paraḥ 45
auhanta paurṇamāsassyādantodāttaśca kevalaḥ
iti prathamo'dhyāyaḥ
kakārādiḥ kamiṣyante syādamuṃ lokamuttaraḥ
kaṭadhātoḥ kaḍityāhuraśvibhyāṃ paritaḥ kṛtam 46
āśātikārbhakā kāra namo vṛttisuvṛktayaḥ
siktvā nārā jakasyā sṛk pūtika viviktatayaḥ 47
niṣaktaparivṛttyuktā sūtvā kṛṇa tṛṇatti ca
pradiśyṛtasya sudhurā nemiṣā maḍṛgityapi 48
khādirnireryānyakhanan ṣakhaṃ khādā khurā khare
ākho vaikhānasanakhāḥ khātākhātasukhādi tān 49
mitragartaṃ śrutaṃ gārtaganīṃ gantī ṛṣegada
niguto gupyate gardānavagadgāmayeti ca 50
jagārapalitaḥ prāk cedājagandhavidīga yaḥ
maināge rājagān rugṇaphaliṅgaṃ vaṃsa godhvagat 51
kuluṅgassānago jīgastalpago'nāgasogathāḥ
adha rāgabhiguryugbhirgamantāsailagodagāḥ 52
maghavāghaviṣā taṃgha yadvaccāghāṃ ghārijaṃghanat
ghaṇena ghātukā vā ghād ghāsaiścāghamaghukṣatām 53
tvaṅmāhyarvāṅ tato viṣvaṅtiryaṅnānāṅnatau sadā
śiṅte vṛṅte sma maṅtāṃ ca yuṅdhvaṃ vṛṅ dhṛṅdhiṅāstathā 54
itaḥ pratīcyāpi ca havicityā prati cāharat
ucathāya ca vāco gāyadacidhvaṃ samañcanam 55
daivyāḥ paśorvaikastvā ca cchayatirvargadvitīyabhāk
jayāti jīyate jitya jaratā pajitiṃ jaṣaḥ 56
juhvo jaritre jehāja tu jaye rujadajyate
ṣajau rajiṣṭhau copāñjyāt prāñjastityā javā jayā 57
vājañjhāvaryayā cantā jñi maṇṭatuṇṭau kaṭhodalāḥ
uparyadbhirvodave parṇarāṇmā baṇmā namṛnmaya 58
saha dvyānaṭsatigṛhe manute priyamuttaraḥ
takārassamadhattātrak yatīcchāyati pāyati 59
tamayitvā juṣetānte yativeti tatoha tat
viśvasṛjjīvariṣite ditsantaṃ hṛtasūvati 60
ṛjuthā saptathā sāthāmatho kopayathāsyatha
vyathanītha ca bādhethā mīthetho cconiṣaṅgathiḥ 61
ado yadbrahmaraditaṃ nidahannādadedado
trāde danda vidatyaṃ ca rademā śodanavibhīdakaḥ 62
pradhamādhodhirādhantāṃ paridhehi dhavaḥpradha
suvan kavyan pranakṣatra ṛjīṣe nyatpunarṇavaḥ 63
pipādīrghaḥ pakāraḥ syāt pipate ca nasaṃparaḥ
sauparṇaṃ pipato cchidra apaplā pratyapekṣate 64
praṇītāmakṣaraṃ nāpo yakṣmaprapata saṃsṛpau
vira phśin vila baṃ ṣaṃ ba sā bṛṇīhitu lambatī 65
vaiśambhalyā pramā bhāhi bharmā bharna nnabhipriyam
abhipāśo bhidhā śībhaṃ rebhaṃ ca śarabhaṃ sibhā 66
ādyudātto mimānaḥ syājjīvāṃ mo manmabhistamaḥ
samanā samane dve dve puṭādyāsa śatāntabhūḥ
brahmetyeteṣu parato mayaḍantā hiraṇmayaḥ 67
yajyu ijyu bhaye jyāyāssakadhyārthau svaritāntimau
tyajyamityā nyajuṃbhye ca rūṇmāmrayasomayā 68
sārṣṭisarjativargāddhahrādorukaṃ prayaṃ prati
yajñe karikradasaradanivatsyāvayantritam 69
jlaplakṣayadaśloṇadbabhluśahlādamalmalā
anavastavate śvaṃ ca prapede vada vañjanā 70
tvadṛṣpadhāvatve devā avṛṇvata tava tvamā
vakāravanto vakrāma avacuścota cāvavai 71
śuca veśyasamāśvaśvo maṣmaṣāraṇyakerṣati
meṣi saṃ ca samaṃ saṃhavidhestvaṃ tasya saṃprajāḥ 72
hīḍite mahisāhīti chinnāḥ kecana saṃjñayāḥ
anantasaṃśayāñchettumanantaḥ parameśvaraḥ 73
śrīnivāsādhvarīndreṇa catuṣkulasudhāṃśunā
ślokāḥ siddhāntaśikṣāyāṃ catuḥsaptatirīritāḥ 74
iti siddhāntaśikṣā samāptā
