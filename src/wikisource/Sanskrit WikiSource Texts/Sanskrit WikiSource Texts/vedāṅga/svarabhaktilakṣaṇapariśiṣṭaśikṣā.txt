svarabhaktilakṣaṇapariśiṣṭā śikṣā
athātaḥ sampravakṣyāmi sarvalakṣaṇalakṣitām
śikṣāṃ samāsatastatra svarāṃstvaṣṭau prakīrttitāḥ 1
tairovirāmaḥ kṣaipraśca tairovyañjakastathā
bhāvyo'bhinihito jātyaḥ pādavṛttaśca saptamaḥ 2
praśliṣṭa iti vijñeyāḥ procyante lakṣaṇānyatha
avagraha udāttaścetsvaritaḥ syāttataḥ param 3
avagrahātpadaṃ yastu taṃ vidyātprathamaṃ svaram
lakṣyamasya tu vijñeyaṃ gopatāviti go | patau 4
ikārokārayoḥ sthāne yavau syātāmudāttayoḥ
anudātte pade nityaṃ sa kṣipra iti kīrttitaḥ 5
tryambakaṃ dvranna ityādi lakṣyaṃ jñeyaṃ vicakṣaṇaiḥ
svaritaṃ chandasi padaṃ yatkiñcidapi dṛśyate 6
udāttapūrvaṃ tatsarvvantairovyañjana ucyate
iḍerante tathā havye kāmye ityādi darśanam 7
akāra ekodāttena repheṇāpahṛto bhavet
tañcā bhinihitaṃ prāhuḥ kukkuṭo'si nidarśanam 8
vivṛttyā dṛśyate yatra svaraṃ ca svaritaṃ pade
sa svaraḥ pādavṛttaḥ syātputra īdhe nidarśanam 9
yatrodātta ikāro hi nipātaikārasam̐yyutaḥ
sa praśliṣṭa iti jñeyo'bhīmamityādi darśanam 10
sādau pade pare pūrvaṃ kakārānte pade sthite
kasavarṇaṃ tu vijñeyaṃ bhiṣaksīsena darśanam 11
cakārānte pade tānte pūrvaṃ ca samavasthite
pratyārambho na tatra syādyaccāhamiti darśanam 12
sādau pade pare pūrvaṃ ṅakārānte pade sati
kasavarṇadvayaṃ tatra prāṅksoma iti darśanam 13
sādau pade pare nānte pūrve ca samavisthite
tasavarṇaṃ vijānīyāttrīntsamuddrānnidarśanam 14
mādau pade pare mānte pūrvamuccārite sati
masavarṇantu tatra syādimamma iti darśanam 15
padamadhye padādyanta ṛharephaiśca saṃyute
yakāre jaṃ vijānīyādyuñjānetyādikaṃ yathā 16
ṭavargamantarā ṣasya khoccāramchandasīritaḥ
pratyudāharaṇaṃ pṛṣṭo divīṣe ttvā nidarśanam 17
ūṣmordhvarephasyoccāraḥ saikāro halyuto na cet
pitṝṇāmiha pitreṇāmityādi ca nidarśanam 18
aṃ kāraḥ syādanusvārasthāne śali ca re pare
hrasvāddīrgho bhaveddīrghāddhrasvo gururatho mataḥ 19
sam̐yyoge parato haṃsa ityādi nidarśanam
tām̐ saviturityādi dam̐ṣṭretyādikaṃ tathā 20
hrasvadīrghagurūccāraḥ karttavyo'tra yathākramam
śal rephayorabhāvena siṃhī pratyudāhṛtiḥ 21
śareḥ pare yā nu ditī dvirbhavedrephabhāginām
visargācca pare yāṃ ṛkārahīnāṃ nidarśanam 22
askannamadya kasyāścitkṛṣṇo'syā iti ca kramāt
sūryastvādyauṣpitā cātha dadhātvasmāniti kramāt 23
bṛhantaḥ prastareṣṭṭheti punaḥ kkratve nidarśanam
pāṇinīyasya sūtrābhyāṃ dvittvaṃ sarvatra kīrttitam 24
yavayoḥ parayoḥ sasya dvittvaṃ na syāttu chandasi
vvasavastvā dhūpayantu tasya yoniṃ yathāyatham 25
halyukta ṛkārastu rekāraśchandasi smṛtaḥ
pitaṇāmiti pitreṇāmityādi ca nidarśanam 26
hrasvaṃ dīrghaṃ bhavetkiñcitsaṃhitāviṣaye svake
halvisargayute varṇā halyutāyutake ca na 27
sapaktnānyaddhvamagghnyeti kiñciddīrgho bhavediha
bhāgamprajāvatītyādau hrasvatā na tu dīrghatā 28
halyutākārasam̐yyogāditi jñeyaṃ vicakṣaṇaiḥ
iti kātyāyanapariśiṣṭe varṇoccāraprakāraḥ
sandhyaścaturdhā vijñeyā lopāgamavikārataḥ 29
prakṛttyā ca tathā tatra prathamaṃ hi nidarśanam
vikārato yathā cedaṃ preṣitetyādikaṃ bhavet 30
devo vaḥ savitetyādau prakṛttyādipradarśanam
ubhau svarau tayorantaḥ sandhiryatra na dṛśyate 31
sā vivṛttirbudhairjñeyā sa eveti ca paśyati
eṣā caturdhā vijñeyā prathamā tu pipīlikā 32
parā pākavatī caiva tathā vatsānusāriṇī
vatsānutsṛjitā caiva catasrastā vivṛttayaḥ 33
pipīlikādyantadīrghā nābbhyāsīnnidarśanam
dvitīyā tūbhayā hrasvā vvina indreti darśanam 34
tṛtīyā coditā dīrghā tāsyeti nidarśanam
ante caturthī dīrghā syāttānāvoḍhamaśvinā 35
svarabhaktiḥ pañcadhā syāttatrādyā kariṇī smṛtā
lakāreṇa hakāreṇa sam̐yyogo yatra dṛśyate 36
kariṇī sā tu vijñeyā varhirasīti darśanam
lakāreṇa hakāreṇa sam̐yyogo yatra dṛśyate 37
kurviṇī sā hi vijñeyā upavalheti paśyati
rakārasya śakāreṇa sam̐yyogo yatra dṛśyate 38
hariṇī sā tu vijñeyārśasa ityādi darśanam
lakāreṇa śakāreṇa sam̐yyogo yatra jāyate 39
tāṃ hariṇīṃ vijānīyācchatavalśeti darśanam
rephasyātha ṣakāreṇa sam̐yyogo yatra dṛśyate 40
haṃsapādeti vijñeyā varṣo varṣīyasīti ca
etallakṣaṇamākhyātaṃ svarabhaktervicakṣaṇaiḥ 41
jñātvaitanmanujo yāti brahmalokaṃ sanātanam
iti kāttyāyanenaiva pariśiṣṭaṃ kṛtaṃ mudā 42
iti kātyāyanoktā svarabhaktilakṣaṇapariśiṣṭaśikṣā samaptā
