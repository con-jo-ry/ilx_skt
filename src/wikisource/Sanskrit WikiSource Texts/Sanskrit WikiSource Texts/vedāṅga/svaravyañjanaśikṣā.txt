svaravyañjana śikṣā
rephānulome niyate praśritākāmayoḥ krame
raloṣmopahitorhrasvo ṅnavarjaṃ paripannage 1
abhyāse trīti saṃkhyāyāstṛtīyatisṛvarjitaḥ
riṇacchrito ripuḥ krimiḥ kriviḥ vriśo riśādasaḥ 2
triviṣṭittriṣṭujātasya tripadyastristridhāttritaḥ
śrīstrīprayoktuyastriṃśatkrivirādiṣu sopadā 3
riṣotha riṣato riśyaśśritaḥ saptakavarjitaḥ
yakāre ca riśoktau ca ādau cānupadā ripuḥ 4
riṇagniṣṭaṃ ririkṣāṃso rihate'tha riśādasaḥ
ityuktaṃ vyaṃjanaṃ sarvamidānīmucyate svaraḥ 5
iti prathamo vargaḥ

vivṛttikṣaiprabhugnāni purā reko'pivā yadi
vikramo nāpyanusvāra ṛkāraḥ sa sphuṭasvaraḥ 1
saṃyogācca paraḥ pūrvaḥ saṃyoge vyaṃjanopadhaḥ
vyaṃjanaṃ varjayitvaikahasanāmapamaṃ triṇaḥ 2
vyaṃjanāntaṃ padaṃ pūrve somadhe purataḥsthite
yakārodayasaṃkhyānāt klivivarjaṃ svaro bhavet 3
āsvasadmeti paṭitā triṣviti dvādaśasvarāḥ
ye cānye vyaṃjanaparāḥ saṃdigdhāste svarāḥ 4
iti dvitīyo vargaḥ 2

rivarṇo'nulomādau vivṛttyādiprasaṃgataḥ
nābhyāse kṣaipratā prāpteti śabdatraya ityuta 1
riṇantireka ityādau vṛṇakte rephaniścayāt
arimṛdhaścararipurdaurṇāṃ mātkrimiraśvinā 2
kriviṃ daśavriśodaivandevo mardariśādasaḥ
martyaṃ riṣo rīṣato nariṣyedgaurī adhiśritaḥ 3
pravobhriyaṃta ityādau riṅśayagliṅkṣviti smṛteḥ
buddhyo riṣe reṣaṇaṃ netrechādericyate kvipa 4
sāvekācastṛtīyādirityekāra udāttabhāk
riphate kakubho repho ralayorekatāsmṛteḥ 5
iti tṛtīyo vargaḥ

vivṛttyādau svare repho'nulomādiprasaṃgataḥ saṃyogāśca paro vyāptervaṃgṛdasya stribhiryathā 1
garmeti gardate siddhaḥ prakṛtyanupapattitaḥ
gṛdetītarayāmeccoraṇaṃ gardadavaṃśṛdaḥ 2
stṛbhistṛṇātestṛṇoterṇāstṛtetyakṛtetivat
saṃyoge vṛddhamityādivardhanyādiṣu niścayāt 3
tṛṣvitikṣipranāmedatṛṣyetarupamāvalāt
vṛkādayaśca vivṛtā jyotiṣkādinirecanāt 4
iti caturtho vargaḥ

sarvopadhaśca svaraghoṣavatparo rephaṃ rephī te purā rephasaṃdhayaḥ 1 rephodayo lupyate
drāghitopadhā hrasvasya
akāmaniyatāvubhāvimau
visarjanīya ākāramarephī ghoṣavatparaḥ
okāraṃ hrasva pūrvaḥ tau sandho niyatapraśritau 2
ninṛtiḥ na kramataḥ tadṛtaṃ
nāpyanusvāraḥ samṛtubhiḥ 3
saṃyogaśca paraḥ vṛṃgadasya saṃskṛtaḥ
pūrvasaṃyyoge vyañjanopadha iti kasmānnariṣyamakadā cana 4

iti pañcamo vargaḥ

āsusadma nṛṣu yadanne prācā
jihvaṃ dhvaṃsayantaṃ nṛṣu yadanneti
trīṇitviṣī samanto'dhvasyaiva
viyasyate pṛthivyāṃ yo'smānnaṃ 1
vātopadhūtoradhvaṃ vṛtraṃ vājintama
ayeti dvādaśanṛṣvasvareṇaya
dūmadhyamardhaṃ vīrasyātaptatanūr
yante rājan mainamagne śṛtaṃ yadā 2
dadarśan rateti sapnaśṛta
svareṇa yathā kavarge vṛkā mṛgaḥ 3
ṛghāyamaṇo'vitreke'gniguriti
prasādasaṃdhiyaṃ vyaṃjanam 4
cavarge ṛcā icchanti ṛjuriṃjaseriti cātha 5
ityāṃbhyāsasandhigdhaṃ vyaṃjanaṃ
ṭavarge mṛra ṛṇāni ririgiti saṃcito rephaḥ
tavarge ṛtaṃ vṛthādadūdareṇa
ṛṣak trita iti saṃcito rephaḥ 6
antasthāsvajāgṛjāgṛviṃ triviṣṭidhādviti rephaḥ 7
saṃkhyārthatvāt
yuṣmāsu ṛṣyaḥ
riśādasa iti saṃcito rephaḥ
ṛṣṭayo riṣamiti rephaḥ 8
bṛsayasya bṛhat bṛhaspatiḥ kakārādiḥ pakārādiścakārādiśca yo
bhavati antasthā yaralavāḥ kakārādi vyaṃjanaṃ vahiḥ 9
iti ṣaṣṭho vargaḥ
