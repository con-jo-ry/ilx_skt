anuyogino bhāvosnuyogitā yathā nīlo ghaṭa ityādau nīlaniṣṭhā pratiyogitā ghaṭaniṣṭhā anuyogitā | jñānaviṣayo ghaṭa ityādau jñānaniṣṭhā pratiyogitā, ghaṭaniṣṭhā anuyogitā | ghaṭo nāstītyādau ghaṭaniṣṭhā pratiyogitā, abhāvaniṣṭhā anuyogitā | evam viṣayitāsambandho'pi jñānā'nuyogikaviṣayasambandharūpo bhinna eva | jñānasya viṣaye sambandho viṣayatā | viṣayasya jñāne sambandho viṣayiteti phalitārthaḥ |viṣayatā ca viṣayabhedādbhidyate jñānabhedena viṣayatābhedo na bhavati | puruṣādibhedena jñānānāmbhinnatve'pi eka eva ghaṭo jñānamātraṃ bhāsate |
udāharaṇañca caitraputro maitraḥ maitraputraścaitro'nayorjñānayoḥ ko bhedaḥ pūrvavākye caitrattvacaitrādi viṣayaḥ,uttaravākye'pi viṣayagaṇanāyāṃ sāmyameva, viṣayabhedādeva jñānabhedaḥ | yathā "devadattasya putro yajñadattasya pitā" anayorjñānayossutarāṃ bhedaḥ | pūrvavākye devadattattvadevadattaputrattvādi viṣayaḥ, uttaravākye yajñadattattvayajñadattapitṛttvādi viṣaya iti bhedaḥ | pūrvavākyayorbhedavicāraprakārastvittham | caitra putromaitra iti jñānasya maitrapadārthena sākaṃ yassambandho viśeṣyatā dharmmitā vā, maitratvena sākaṃ viśeṣyatāvacchedakatā dharmmitācchedakatā ca, caitreṇa sākaṃ viśeṣaṇatā prakāratā vā, caitratvena sākaṃ viśeṣaṇatāvacchedakatā prakāratāvacchedakatā vā, prakāratāvacchedakatāvacchedakatā samavāye sāṃsargikaprakāratāvacchedakatā caitratvacaitrayossamavāyaḥ iti vā, maitraputraścaitra iti jñāne caitre viśeṣyatā caitratve viśeṣyatāvacchedakatā | pūrvajñāne prakāratāvacchedakatā caitratve, dvitīyajñāne prakāratā maitre prakāratāvacchedakatā maitrettve prakāratāvacchedakatāvacchedakatā samavāye sāṃsargikaprakāratāvacchedakatā vā samavāye | evaṃ yogyatānantasambandhānāṃ sattvād vākyārthayorbhedaḥ |

yasminvākye parasparamviśeṣasambandho'sti na tu sāmānyasambandhastatra viśiṣṭajñānaṃ savikalpakam | savikalpakajñānaṃ vā bhavati | yatra kālikādisāmānyasambandha eva na viśeṣastatra nirvikalpakannirvikalpakajñānaṃ vā bhavati |
nirvikalpakajñānaṃ savikalpakajñāne kāraṇamiti niyamaḥ ghaṭaghaṭattve iti sambandharahitaṃ pṛthak pṛthak jñānameva nirvikalpakam | paścātsamavāyajñāne sati savikalpakajñānamanekapadārthānāṃ sambandhajñānameva savikalpakajñānamiti niyamaḥ |

daṇḍī puruṣaḥ saṃyogasambandhāvacchinna daṇḍattvāvacchinnaprakāratānirūpitapuruṣattvāvacchinnaviśeṣyateti |
ghaṭavadbhūtalam saṃyogasambandhāvacchinnaghaṭattvāvacchinna prakāratānirūpitabhūtalatvāvacchinnaviśeṣyatā | vṛkṣe phalannāsti atra phalābhāvasya vṛkṣe svarūpasambandhaḥ | yadyapi vṛkṣasya phalābhāve ādheyatvaṃ phalābhāvasya vṛkṣe ādhāratvaṃ tathāpi tayorniyāmakaḥ niścāyakaḥ avacchedakaḥ saṅkocakaḥ paricāyakaḥ vyavasthāpakā vā kaścana vilakṣaṇassambandhaḥ sarvatra | yathā ghaṭavad bhūtalamityatra ghaṭaniṣṭhādheyatānirūpitādhāratāvad bhūtalamityeva śuddho bodhastathāpi tadavacchedakassaṃyogaḥ | bhūtale ghaṭābhāvaḥ atrādhārādheyabhāvaniyāmakassvarūpasambandhaḥ |
nanu yathā ghaṭavad bhūtalamiti vākyantathā bhūtalavān ghaṭaḥ, rūpavān ghaṭaḥ, ghaṭavapadrūm, ghaṭābhāvavad bhūtalam, bhūtalavān ghaṭābhāvaḥ, evamvidhāḥ, prayogāḥ, kathanna bhavanti saṃyogādīnāmubhayanirūpyattvāt iti cenna ? saṃyogasamavāyasvarūpāṇāmapi sambandhānānniścāyakaḥ kaścana sambandho bhavati | padārthadvaya eṣāṃ sambandhānānniścayākassambandho'nuyogitākhyaḥ pratiyogitākhyaśca |yadi saṃyogādīnāmanuyogitākhyassttadā tatra viśiṣṭabodhaḥ |yatra kevalapratiyogittvasambandha eva tatra na viśiṣṭabodhaḥ | ghaṭavad bhūtalamityatra ghaṭabhūtalayorthassaṃyogastasya niyāmakamanuyogittvambhūtale'to ghaṭavad bhūtalamiti bhavati bhūtalavān ghaṭa iti na bhavati |saṃyogasya ghaṭe pratiyogittvaṃ sambandho'nuyogittvābhāvāt | dugdhavān ghaṭa iti bhavati ghaṭavad dugdhamiti na bhavati |yadyapi saṃyogasyobhayatra tulyattvameva tathāpyanuyogitvampratītibalād ghaṭa eva , pratiyogittvam dugdha evāto dugdhavān ghaṭa ityeva bhavati | evaṃ rūparasādiguṇānāṃ suvarṇāmrādidravyāṇāmparasparaṃ samavāyaḥ | parantu guṇadravyayossamavāyasya niścāyakānuyogittvandravya eva na guṇādāvata eva rūpavatsuvarṇaṃ rasavadāmraphalam ityādi bhavati na tu suvarṇavad rūpam , āmraphalavān rasa ityādi bhavati | kriyā caitramaitradravyośca parasparaṃ samavāyasambandhaḥ | atra samavāyaniścayākānuyogittvamevātaścalanavān maitro bhāṣaṇavān caitra iti bhavati | maitravaccalanaṃ caitravadbhāṣaṇamati na bhavati ? anuyogitvasya niścāyakasya kriyāyāmabhāvāt iti |

viśeṣyatā dvividhā sāvacchinnā niravacchinnā ca| ayaṃ ghaṭa ityādau ghaṭattvaniṣṭhaprakāratānirūpitaghaṭaniṣṭhaviśeṣyatā idantvāvacchinnavatvena sāvacchinnā | kevalaghaṭa ityākārakajñāne ghaṭatvaniṣṭhaprakāratānirūpitaghaṭaniṣṭhā viśeṣyatā niravacchinnā | pūrvajñāna idantvādikameva dharmmitāvacchedakam padārthaḥ | viśeṣyatāvacchedakatāyā eva dharmmitāvacchedakatāpadārthatvāt |
prakāratāpi dvividhā sāvacchinnā niravacchinnā ca | ghaṭa ityākārakajñāne svarūpato ghaṭatvāderbhāvāt ghaṭaniṣṭhaviśeṣyatānirūpitā ghaṭatvaniṣṭhā prakāratā niravacchinnā |
ghaṭavad gṛhamityādau saṃyogasambandhāvacchinnagṛhaniṣṭhaviśeṣyatānirūpitaghaṭaniṣṭhāprakāratā ghaṭatvāvacchinnatvena sāvacchinnā |

avacchinnatvañca 
pratiyogittattvaviṣayatāvat viṣayatādāvapi ghaṭatvādessambandhaviśeṣa eva | ghaṭattvāvacchinnā viṣayatetyasya kesrthaḥ ? ghaṭattvaniṣṭhāvacchedakatānirūpitāvacchedyatāvatī viṣayatetyarthaḥ |
avacchedakattvañca 
śakyattve sati śakyavyāvarttakattvabhavacchedakattvam | yathā ghaṭa ityādau ghaṭatvam ghaṭapadaśakyam paṭādipadārthavyāvarttakañca |
anyūnānatipraśaktatvamavacchedakattvamvā | ghaṭa ityādau ghaṭattvanna nyūne varttate nādhike varttate kintu sakala eveti ghaṭattvamavacchedakam |
atrāyanniṣkarṣaḥ avacchedakatvaṃ svarūpasambandhaviśeṣaḥ sa ca kvacit pratiyogyaṃśaprakārībhūtadharmmatvam |
yathā prameyadhūmābhāvapratiyogitāyā avacchedakattvaṃ dhūmatve | kvacittu anatiriktavṛttitvantat | tacca dvividham | tatrādyam tacchūnyāvṛttitve sati tadadhikaraṇavṛtyabhāvāpratiyogitvam | yathā ghaṭābhāvapratiyogitāyā avacchedakatvaṃ ghaṭattve |
dvitīyantu vyāvarttakatvasāmānādhikaraṇyasvaniṣṭhāvacchedyatākattvaitattritayasambandhena yat kiñcid dharmaviśiṣṭatvam yathā ghaṭakāraṇatāyā avacchekatvaṃ daṇḍe | kvacittu tadadhikaraṇasya tanniṣṭhadharmmāvacchedatakatvam, yathā mūle vṛkṣe na kapisaṃyogaśśākhāyām ityādau vṛkṣādhikaraṇasya śākhādervṛkṣaniṣṭhakapisaṃyogāvacchedakatvañca | yathā vā iha parvate nitambe hutāśanaḥ na śikhare ityatra nitambarūpadeśasya hutāśanāvacchedakatvam śikharasya tu hutāśanābhāvāvacchedakattvañca | idānīṃṃ cattvare gaurnāstītyādau etatkālespi gavābhāvasyāvacchedakattvam |

nanvacchedakatvaṃ kathamaṅgīkriyate tadasvīkṛtidaśāyāmapi ghaṭosstītyādau bodhasambhava iti cenna ? yadyavacchekadatvanna syāttarhyabhāvaviśiṣṭabuddhipratibadhyapratibandhakabhāvādīnāṅkeṣāmapi vyavasthā na syāt | tadavyavasthāne ca sarva eva vyavahārā vilopamarheyuḥ tathā hi ghaṭo nāstīttyādiviśiṣṭabuddhau ghaṭattvāderavacchedakattvābhāve ghaṭapratiyogikābhāvakarttṛkāsattvettyarthasvīkāro "ghaṭo nāsti, nīla ghaṭo nāsti, ghaṭapaṭobhayannāsti" iti viśiṣṭabuddhīnāmparasparamvailakṣaṇyanna syāt | sarvatraiva ghaṭapratiyogikābhāvapratīteḥ | avacchedakatvasvīkāre tu ghaṭo nāstīti viśiṣṭabuddhau ghaṭattvamātraniṣṭhāvacchedakatādvayanirūpitapratiyogitākatvasya nīlaghaṭo nāstīti buddhau nīlattvaghaṭattvarūpadharmadvayaniṣṭhāvacchedakatādvayanirūpitapratiyogitākattvasya, ghaṭapaṭobhayannāstīti buddhau ca ghaṭattvapaṭattva ubhayatvarūpadharmatrayaniṣṭhāvacchedakatāpratiyogitākattvasyāvagāhanāt | viṣayavailakṣaṇyena pratītivailakṣaṇyamiti cenna ? ghaṭo nāstīti buddhau vaṭapratiyogitākābhāvaḥ | nīlaghaṭo nāstīti buddhau nīlattvaghaṭattvobhayattvādivailakṣaṇyena tayorvailakṣaṇyam avacchedakatāvailakṣaṇyameva tad vailakṣaṇyaṃ sādhayedattyasyāyuktattvāt |
avacchedakattvāssvīkāre saṃyogena ghaṭonāstīttyādau viśiṣṭattvāsambhavenaikavākyatvāsambhavaḥ | avacchedakatvasvīkāre tu saṃyokenetyasya saṃyogasambandhāvacchinnattvamarthaḥ tasya pratiyoginassambandhe pratiyogitāyāmanvayaḥ, pratiyogitāvacchedakasyasambandhesvacchinnapratiyogitāyāmanvayastathā ca saṃyogasambandhāvacchinnapratiyogitāsambandhena ghaṭavān abhāvaḥ, avacchinnasaṃyogasambandhāvacchinnapratiyogitāsambandhena vā ghaṭattvavānabhāva ityakāmenāpi bhavadbhiravacchedakattvamaṅgīkāryam ittyāstāṃ svarūpasambandhaviśeṣarūpāvacchedakattvaprabandhaḥ iti khaṇḍanapariśiṣṭagranthamatam |
atha jñānasya sattvaṃ kathamaṅgīkriyate viṣayasya kathanna sattvamaṅgīkriyate, vākyāt tathāṅgīkāre ubhayoreva tatsambhavāditi cenna ? vidhiniṣedhayordvayoreva jñānabhāsyattvāt | anyathobhāvapi na niṣpadyeyātām | aniṣpattirapi tathettyakāmenāpi jñānasatvamaṅgīkāryam |
