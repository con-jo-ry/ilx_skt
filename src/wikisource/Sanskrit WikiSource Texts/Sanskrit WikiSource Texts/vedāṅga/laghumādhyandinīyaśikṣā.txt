laghumādhyandinīya śikṣā
atha śikṣāṃ pravakṣyāmi mādhyandinamatam̐yyathā
ṣakārasya khakāraḥ syāṭṭukayoge tu no bhavet 1
iṣe lakṣyaṃ kṛṣṇa'ukṣā samudraḥ pratyudāhṛtiḥ
padādau vidyamānasya hyasam̐yuktasya yasya ca 2
ādeśo hi jakāraḥ syādyuktaḥ sanharaṇena tu
yajñena yajñaṃ vailakṣyaṃ mayūre pratyudāhṛtiḥ 3
tasmādyajjñāttsarvvahutaḥ samasmmādyattathaiva ca
repheṇātha hakāreṇa yuktasya sarvathā bhavet 4
sūryo bāhyantu vailakṣyaṃ śaṣpyyāya pratyudāhṛtiḥ
yakārarkārayuktasya jakāraḥ sarvathā bhavet 5
saharayyā tathā vyṛddhyā copasargaparasya na
upayajñammānuṣāṇāmapi yantītyudāhṛtiḥ 6
gururvakāro vijñeyaḥ padādau paṭhito bhavet
vibhrāḍudāhṛtirjñeyā savitāntarlaghuḥ smṛtaḥ 7
padānte vai laghutarastava vvāyavṛtaspate
upasargaparo yastu savakāro laghurmataḥ 8
vo vāṃ vā vai mantrapāṭhe laghavo guravaḥ pade
pravāyumacchā bṛhatī vāto veti nidarśanam 9
repho rephatvamāpnoti śaṣaheṣu pareṣu ca
dadarśa varṣo arhācca saṃyyoge naiva kārayet 10
vvarṣmmanvarṣyāyāntarhṛdā tatra tāvadudāhṛtiḥ
lakāro'pi ca sāvarṇyādekārasadṛśo bhavet 11
śatabalśā ca balhā ca tatra tāvadudāhṛtiḥ
anusvāro yatra kutra rṭha0kāro bhavati dhruvam 12
hrasvo dīrgho guruśceti trividhaḥ parikīrtitaḥ
hrasvātparo bhaveddīrgho harṭha0sa'iti darśanam 13
dīrghātparo bhaveddhrasvo māṃsebhya'iti darśanam
gurau pare hyanusvāro gurureva hi sa smṛtaḥ 14
siṃhyasīti tatra tāvadṛkāre dīrgha'eva saḥ
devānāṃ hṛdaye tadvattaskarāṇāṃ tathā sṛke 15
athāyogavāhānāha–
avarṇācca ṛkārācca visargaḥ kaṇṭhya eva saḥ
ivarṇācca tathovarṇāttathā caikārapūrvakaḥ 16
aukārapūrvakaścaiva tālavyo bhavati dhruvam
ekārācca kaṇṭhatālurvisargo bhavati dhruvam 17
kaṇṭhyoṣṭhyastu tathaukārād visargo bhavati dhruvam
devo vaḥ savitā cātra hakārasadṛśo bhavet 18
devīstisro visargastu hikārasadṛśo bhavet
ākhuste paśurityādau hakārasadṛśo bhavet 19
visargaścāgnerityādau hekārasadṛśo bhavet
visargo bāhvorityādau hokārasadṛśo bhavet 20
atha svairdṛkṣairityādau hikārasadṛśo bhavet
visargo dyauṣpitetyādau hakārasadṛśo bhavet 21
hakāro naiva mantavya iti śāstravyavasthitiḥ
phaṇiniśvāsasadṛśo visargo bhavati dhruvam 22
kaniṣṭhikāmocanaṃ tu nīce ca pracaye sati
namaḥ kūpyāya prathamo dvitīyo jāgratastathā 23
tarjanīmocanaṃ kuryādudātte tu visargake
devo dharmastathā hrasve svarite tūbhayaṃ kṣipet 24
aśvo martyo bhavellakṣyaṃ svāre dīrghe kaniṣṭhikām
ubhayorapi hrasve ca vakāre svarite sati 25
dīrghe'pi cobhayoḥ kṣepa'iti śāstravyavasthitiḥ
yathā sphaṭikadaṇḍādirupādhivaśato bhavet 26
tadvadūṣmā prayoktavyo hihuheho nidarśanam
vvasoḥ pavitraṃ vai tatra hyudāharaṇamucyate 27
ṛkāraḥ khalu sarvatra hyekārasadṛśo bhavet
hṛde mṛgastṛtīyā ca ṛcaṃ vvācamathāparam 28
iti laghumādhyandinīyā śikṣā samāptā
