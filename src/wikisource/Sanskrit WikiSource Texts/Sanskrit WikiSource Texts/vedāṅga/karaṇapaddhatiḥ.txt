vibhāvastṛṇaudho gajābho dhaneśo valeśo dhṛtāśaḥ kalātmā vipakṣaḥ | kṣamokṣo nilānto nirāsaḥ syurete kramādarddhitā vastrapūrvāḥ sphuṭāṃśāḥ || 4 || 
karaṇapaddhatau namyaḥ priyo munirmānī nityaṃ pūjyastanurnnanu | sthāne jñānī priyo loke sūnuḥ prājñaḥ sthale punaḥ || 5 || 
lagne bhānurjanasthāne sūnurnāgastilaṃ nanu | vakrastatra nanu kṣepabhāgā dasrāditaḥ kramāt || 6 || 
dasrāntakāryamabhagāditi vahni vāyuvasvekapādvihariśūrpagatāśca saumyāḥ | yāmyāḥ pare varuṇanaiṛtaśūrpabhānāṃ kṣepāstu divyanaganāgakalānvitāste || 7|| 
nakṣatrāṇāṃ sphuṭāḥ kāryāḥ sakalāḥ saṃskṛtāyanāḥ | teṣāṃ krāntiguṇāḥ spaṣṭāḥ svasvavikṣepasaṃskṛtāḥ || 8 || 
paramāpakramakoṭyā vikṣepajyāṃ nihatya tatkoṭyā | iṣṭakrāntiṃ cobhe trijyāpte yogavirahayogye staḥ || 9 || 
sadiśoḥ saṃyutiranayorviyutirvidiśorapakramaḥ spaṣṭaḥ | spaṣṭāpakramakoṭidyujyāvikṣepamaṇḍale vasatām || 10 || 
sphuṭakrāntiguṇo bhūyaḥ kevalakṣepasaṃskṛtaḥ | parakrāntiśarābhyastaḥ parakrāntihṛto guṇaḥ || 11 || 
guṇahatāt tribhayuk sphuṭadorguṇād dinaguṇāpta dhanustribhayuksphuṭe | ṛṇadhanaṃ samabhinnadiśo bhavedivasamadhyagakālavilagnakam || 12 || 
yahā sphuṭe nijakalāsu bhidāṃ ca kṛtvā tasmin punastribhayute svabhujāpamajyām | navamo'dhyāyaḥ | kṣepāhatāmapamakoṭihṛtāṃ purovat kuryāt svamadhyadinakālavilagnasiddhyai || 13 || 
yadvā krāntitadīyakoṭyasukalābhedāṃstribhonasphuṭādānīyāsukalāntaraṃ tribhayute kuryāt sphuṭe tatra tu | tatkrāntyorvadhato'rdhavistṛtihatād yujyāvadhāptād dhanuḥ krāntyorbhinnasamāśayordhanamṛṇaṃ madhyāhnakālāptaye || 14 || 
vikṣepakoṭīparamajīvāghātāt parakrāntito haraḥ syāt | kṣepāhate svasphuṭakoṭidojya hārāhūte koṭibhujāphale staḥ || 15 || 
bhujāphalaṃ vyāsadale dhanarṇa vikṣepadorjyāharidaikyabhedāt | tadvargakoṭīphalavargayogānmūlaṃ bhavedāyanasaṃjñakarṇaḥ || 16 || 
koṭīphalaṃ vyāsadalena hatvā karṇāmṛtaṃkūphalamāyanaṃ syāt | svarṇa sphuṭe tanmṛgakarkaṭāyoḥ kṣepe tu mepādigate'nyathā syāt || 17 || 
tasmin punaḥ prāṇakalāntaraṃ ca kuryāt tadā syānnatakāla papaḥ | natākhyakālastribhasaṃyuto'yaṃ mādhyāhikaṃ kālavilanakaṃ syāt || 18 || 
karaṇapaddhatī nakṣatramadhyāhajakālalagnāt svāsannarāśyantajakālalagnam | tyaktvāvaśeṣasya lavāḥ ghaḍāptāstandrāśiyātā ghaṭikā bhavanti || 19 || 
iti karaṇapaddhatī nayamo'dhyāyaḥ || 
atha daśamo'dhyāyaḥ | bhānāṃ sphuṭāśca vikṣepā bahudhokkā budhaistataḥ | svacchāyāthaiḥ parīkṣyaite nirṇayā yantrasādhitaiḥ || 1 || 
saumye śaṅkakṣaghāte tvamṛṇamiha diśābhābhujāṃ lambakanīṃ kṛtvāsmāt trijyayāptaṃ vidurapamaguṇastasya koṭirbujīvā | trijyābhākoṭighātād dinaguṇavihṛtaṃ cāpitaṃ kālalagne svarṇa pūrvāparāhe tribhavanarahitaṃ tadbhavedvāyukālaḥ || 2 || 
yadvā yāmyottarāpakramapalaguṇayoghatatastrijyayāptaṃ śaṅkī saṃskṛtya tasmāt triguṇakṛtihatāchambakakāntikoṭyoḥ | daśamo'dhyāyaḥ | ghātāptaṃ kālalagne kṣipatu kṛtadhanustyajyatāṃ prākkapāle kālo mādhyāhniko'yaṃ bhavati punarasau vāyukālastribhonaḥ || 3 || 
ghaṭikāmaṇḍale yatra spṛṣṭaṃ tannatamaṇḍalam | tatpradeśo vāyukālo natakālaḥ sa cocyate || 4 || 
sarveṣāmapi sadhyā mahācchāyaiva doḥprabhā | kālalagnaṃ nirāścūnaṃ natakālastadā bhavet || 5 || 
natakālabhujākrānti vyastadikkaṃ prakalpya tām | sphuṭāpakramakoṭina sphuṭakrāntiguṇe punaḥ || 6 || 
paramakrāntikoṭine kṛtvā tasmāt trijīvayā | labdho bhavati vikṣepastarakoṭiṃ ca samānayet || 7 || 
bhūyaḥ kṣepaguṇaṃ sphuṭāpamaguṇe kṛtvāmunā tāḍitā dorjyāsatribhavāyukālajanitā vikṣepakoṭyāhṛtā | antyakrāntiśarāhatā paramayā kāntyā hṛtā cāpitā svarṇa tulyabhidā śaghātavaśataḥ syādvāyukālasphuṭaḥ || 8 || 
yadvā svamadhyāhnagakālalabhe kṛtāsuliptāvivare svadorjyām | kṣepāntima krāntivadhena hatvā tarakoṭighātena vibhajya labdham || 9 || 
cāpīkṛtaṃ ca svamṛṇaṃ prakuryāda vikṣepadorjyā haridaikya bhedāt |karaṇapaddhatau daśamo'dhyāyaḥ | tribhonite'smin punarāyanāṃśaṃ vyastaṃ ca kuryāt sa nijasphuṭaḥ syāt || 10 || 
gaṇitamidamaśeṣaṃ yuktiyuktaṃ paṭhanto bhuvi gaṇakajanānāmagragaṇyā bhaveyuḥ | api ca gativiśeṣāt kālatulyasya viṣṇoḥ subhṛśamanubhavanto yānti taḍāma śuddham || 11|| 
ca iti śivapuranāmagrāmajaḥ ko'pi yajvā kimapi karaṇapaddhatyāhvayaṃ tantrarūpam | vyathita gaṇitametat samyagālokya santaḥ kathitamiha vidantaḥ santu santoṣavantaḥ || 12 || 
iti karaṇapaddhatī daśamo'dhyāyaḥ || śubhaṃ bhūyāt ||
