kalkipurāṇam |

<1>

prathamāṃśaḥ | 

<1.1>
prathamo’dhyāyaḥ |

sendrā devagaṇā munīśvarajanā lokāḥ sapālāḥ sadā
svaṃ svaṃ karma susiddhaye pratidanaṃ bhaktyā bhajanty uttamāḥ
taṃ vighneśam anantam acyutam ajam sarvajñasarvāśrayam
vande vaidikatāntrikādivividhaiḥ śāstraiḥ puro vanditam ||1
nārāyaṇaṃ namaskṛtya narañ caiva narottamam
devīṃ sarasvatīñ caiva tato jayam udīrayet ||2
yaddordaṇḍakarālasarpakavalajvālājvāladvigrahāḥ
tenuḥ satkaravāladaṇḍadalitā bhūpāḥ kṣitikṣobhakāḥ
śaśvat saindhavavāhano dvijajaniḥ kalkiḥ parātmā hariḥ
pāyāt satyayugādikvat sa bhagavān dharmapravṛttipriyaḥ ||3
iti sūtavacaḥ śrutvā naimiṣāraṇyavāsinaḥ
śaunakādyā mahābhāgāḥ papracchus taṃ kathām imām ||4
he sūta sarvadharmajña lomaharṣaṇaputraka
trikālajña purāṇajña vada bhāgavatīṃ kathām ||5
kaḥ kaliḥ kutra vā jāto jagatām īśvaraḥ prabhuḥ
kathaṃ vā nityadharmasya vināśaḥ kalinā kṛtaḥ ||6
iti teṣāṃ vacaḥ śrutvā sūto dhyātvā hariṃ prabhum
saharṣapulakodbhinnasarvāṅgaḥ prāha tān munīn ||7
sūta uvāca |
śṛṇudhvam idam ākhyānaṃ bhaviṣyaṃ paramādbhutam
kāthitaṃ brahmaṇā pūrvaṃ nāradāya vipṛcchate ||8
nāradaḥ prāha munaye vyāsāyāmitatejase
sa vyāso nijaputrāya brahmarātāya dhīmate ||9
sa cābhimanyuputrāya viṣṇurātāya saṃsadi
prāha bhāgavatān dharmān aṣṭādaśasahasrakān ||10
tadā nṛpe layaṃ prāpte saptāhe praśnaśoṣitam
mārkaṇdeyādibhiḥ pṛṣtaḥ prāha puṇyāśrame śukaḥ ||11
tatrāhaṃ tad anujñātaḥ śrutavān asmi yāḥ kathāḥ
bhaviṣyāḥ kathayāmīha puṇyā bhāgavatīḥ śubhāḥ ||12
tāḥ śṛṇudhvam mahābhāgāḥ samāhitadhiyo’niśam
gate kṛṣne svanilayam prādurbhūto yathā kaliḥ ||13 
pralayānte jagatsraṣṭā brahmā lokapitāmahaḥ
sasarja ghoraṃ malinaṃ pṛṣṭhadeśāt svapātakam ||14
sa cādharma iti khyātas tasya vaṃśānukīrtitām
śravaṇāt smaraṇāl lokaḥ sarvapāpaiḥ pramucyate ||15
adharmasya priyā ramyā mithyā mārjāralocanā
tasya putro’titejasvī dambhaḥ paramakopanaḥ ||16
sa māyāyāṃ bhaginyāntu lobhaṃ putrañ ca kanyakām
nikṛtiṃ janayām āsa tayoḥ krodhaḥ suto’bhavat ||17
sa hiṃsāyāṃ baginyāntu janayām āsa taṃ kalim
vāmahastadhṛtopasthaṃ tailābhyaktāñjanaprabham ||18
kākodaraṃ karālāsyaṃ lolajihvaṃ bhayānakam
pūtigandhaṃ dyūtamadyastrīsuvarṇakṛtāśrayam ||19
bhaginyāntu durukyāṃ sa bhayaṃ putrañ ca kanyakām
mṛtyuṃ sa janayāmāsa tayoś ca nirayo’bhavat ||20
yātanāyāṃ bhaginyāntu lebhe putrāyutāyutam
itthaṃ kalikule jātā bahavo dharmanindakāḥ ||21
yajnādhyayanadānādi vedatantravināśakāḥ
ādhivyādhijarāglāni duḥkhaśokabhayāśrayāḥ ||22
kalirājānugāśceruryūthaśo lokanāśakāḥ
babhūvuḥ kālavibhraṣtāḥ kṣaṇikāḥ kāmukā narāḥ ||23
dambhācāradurācārā stātamātṛvihiṃsakāḥ
vedahīnā dvijā dīnāḥ śūdrasevāparāḥ sadā ||24
kutakarvādabahulā dharmavikrayiṇo’dhamāḥ
vedavikrayiṇo vrātyā rasavikrayiṇas tathā ||25 
māṃsavikrayiṇaḥ krūrāḥ śiśnodaraparāyaṇāḥ
paradāraratā mattā varṇasaṅkarakārakāḥ ||26
hrasvākārāḥ pāpasārāḥ śaṭhā maṭhanivāsinaḥ
ṣoḍaśābdāyuṣaḥ śyālabāndhavā nīcasaṅgamāḥ ||27
vivādakalahakṣubdhāḥ keśaveśavibhūṣaṇāḥ
kalau kulinā dhaninaḥ pūjyā vārdhuṣikā dvijāḥ ||28
sanyāsino gṛhāsaktā grahasthāstvavivekinaḥ
gurunindāparā dharmadhvajinaḥ sādhuvañcakāḥ ||29
pratigraharatāḥ śūdrāḥ parasvaharaṇādarāḥ
dvayoḥ svīkāramudvāhaḥ śaṭhe maitrī vadānyatā ||30
pratidāne kṣamāśactau viraktikaraṇākṣame
vācālatvañ ca pāṇditye yaśo’rthe dharmasevanam ||31
dhanāḍhyatvañ ca sādhutve dūre nīre ca tīrthatā
sūtramātreṇa vipratvaṃ daṇḍamātreṇa maskarī ||32
alpaśasyā vasumatī nadītīre’varopitā
striyo veśyālāpasukhāḥ svapuṃsā tyakta mānasāḥ ||33
parānnalolupā viprāś caṇdālagṛhayājakāḥ
striyo vaidhavyahīnāś ca svacchandācaraṇapriyāḥ ||34
citravṛṣṭikarā meghā mandaśasyā ca medinī
prajābhakṣā nṛpā lokāḥ karapīḍāprapīḍitāḥ ||35
skandhe bhāraṃ kare putraṃ kṛtvā kṣubdhāḥ prajājanāḥ
giridurgaṃ vanaṃ ghoram āśrayiṣyanti durbhagāḥ ||36
madhumāṃsair mūlaphalair āhāraiḥ prāṇadhāriṇaḥ
evaṃ tu prathame pāde kaleḥ kṛṣṇavinindakāḥ ||37
dvitīye tannāmahīnās tṛtīye varṇasaṅkarāḥ
ekavarṇāś ca turthe ca vismṛtācyutasatcriyāḥ ||38
niḥsvādhyāyasvadhāsvāhāvauṣaḍoṃkāravarjitāḥ
devāḥ sarve nirāhārāḥ brahmāṇaṃ śaraṇaṃ yayuḥ ||39
dharitrīmagrataḥ kṛtvā kṣīṇāṃ dīnāṃ manasvinīm
dadṛśur brahmaṇo lokaṃ vedadhvaninināditam ||40
yajñadhūmaiḥ samākīrṇaṃ munivaryaniṣevitam
suvarṇavedikāmadhye dakṣiṇāvartamujjvalam ||41
vahni yūpāṅkvitodyānavanapuṣpaphalānvitam
sarobhiḥ sārasair haṃsair āhvayantam ivātithim ||42
vāyulolalatājāla kusumālikulākulaiḥ
praṇām āhvānasatkāramadhurālāpavīkṣaṇaiḥ ||43
tad brahmasadanaṃ devāḥ seśvarāḥ klinnamānasāḥ
viviśus tad anujñātā nijakāryaṃ niveditum ||44
tribhuvanajakaṃ sadāsanasthaṃ sanakasanandanasanātanaiś ca siddhaiḥ
parisevitapādakamalaṃ brahmāṇa devatā nemuḥ ||45
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye kalivivaraṇaṃ nāma prathamo’dhyāyaḥ ||1

<1.2>

dvitīyo’dhyāyaḥ |

sūta uvāca |
upaviṣṭās tato devā brahmaṇo vacanāt puraḥ
kaler doṣād dharmahāniṃ kathayām āsur ādarāt ||1
dūsarā adhyāya
devānāṃ tad vacaḥ śrutvā brahmā tān āha duḥkhitān
prasādayitvā taṃ viṣṇuṃ sādhayiṣyāmy abhīpsitam ||2
iti devaiḥ parivṛto gatvā golokavāsinam
stutvā prāha puro brahmā devānāṃ hṛdaye’psitam ||3
tac chrutvā puṇḍarīkākṣo brahmāṇam idam abravīt ||
śambhale viṣṇuyaśaso gṛhe prādurbhavāmy aham
sumatyāṃ mātari vibho kanyāyāṃ tvannideśataḥ ||4
caturbhir bhrātṛbhir deva kariṣyāmi kalikṣayam
bhavanto bāndhavā devāḥ svāṃśenāvatariṣyatha ||5
iyaṃ mama priyā lakṣmīḥ siṃhale saṃbhaviṣyati
bṛhadrathasya bhūpasya kaumudyāṃ kamalekṣaṇā
bhāryāyāṃ mama bhāryaiṣā padmānāmnī janiṣyati ||6
yāta yūyaṃ bhuvaṃ devāḥ svāṃśāvataraṇe ratāḥ
rājānau marudevāpī sthāpayiṣyāmy ahaṃ bhuvi ||7
punaḥ kṛtayugaṃ kṛtvā dharmān saṃsthāpya pūrvavat
kalivyālaṃ saṃnirasya prayāsye svālayaṃ vibho ||8
ity udīritamākarṇya brahmā devagaṇair vṛtaḥ
jagāma brahmasadanaṃ devāś ca tridivaṃ yayuḥ ||9
mahimāṃ svasya bhagavān nijajanmakṛtodyamaḥ
viprarṣa śambhalagrāmam āviveśa parātmakaḥ ||10
sumatyāṃ viṣṇuyaśasā garbham ādhatta vaiṣṇavam
grahanakṣatrarāśyādisevitaśrīpadāmbujam ||11
sarit samudrā giraye lokāḥ sasthāṇu jaṅgamāḥ
saharṣā ṛṣayo devā jāte viṣṇau jagatpatau ||12
babhūvuḥ sarvasattvānām ānandā vividhāśrayāḥ
nṛtyanti pitaro hṛṣṭās tuṣṭā devā jagur yaśaḥ ||13
cakrurvādyāni gandharvā nanṛtuś cāpsarogaṇāḥ ||14
dvādaśyāṃ śuklapakṣasya mādhave māsi mādhavam
jātaṃ dadṛśatuḥ putraṃ pitarau hṛṣṭamānasau ||15
dhātṛmātā mahāṣaṣṭhī nābhicchetrī tad ambikā
gaṅgodakakledamokṣā sāvitrī mārjanodyatā ||16
tasya viṣṇor anantasya vasudhādhāt payaḥsudhām
mātṛkā māṅgalyavacaḥ kṛṣṇajanmadine tathā ||17
brahmā tad upadhāryāśu svāśugaṃ prāha sevakam
yāhīti sūtikāgāram gatvā viṣṇuṃ prabodhaya ||18
caturbhujam idaṃ rūpaṃ devānām api durlabham
tyaktvā mānuṣavad rūpaṃ kuru nātha vicāritam ||19
iti brahmavacaḥ śrutā pavanaḥ surabhiḥ sukham
saśītaḥ prāha narasā brahmaṇo vacanādṛtaḥ ||20
tac chrutvā puṇḍarīkākṣas tat kṣaṇād dvibhujo’bhavat
tadā tat pitarau dṛṣṭvā vismayāpannamānasau ||21
bhramasaṃskāravat tatra menāte tasya māyayā
tatas tu śambhalagrāme sotsavā jīvajātayaḥ
maṅgalācārabahulāḥ pāpatāpavivarjitāḥ ||22
sumatis taṃ sutaṃ labdhvā viṣṇuṃ jiṣṇuṃ jagatpatim
pūrṇakāmā vipramukhyān āhūyādād gavāṃ śatam ||23
hareḥ kalyāṇakṛd viṣṇuyaśāḥ śuddhena cetasā
sāmargyajurvidbhir agryais tan nāmakaraṇe rataḥ ||24
tadā rāmaḥ kṛpo vyāso drauṇir bhikṣuśarīriṇaḥ
samāyātā hariṃ draṣtuṃ bālakatvam upāgatam ||25
tān āgatān samālokya caturaḥ sūryasannibhān
hṛṣṭaromā dvijavaraḥ pūjayāñ cakra īśvarān ||26
pūjitās te svāsaneṣu saṃviṣtāḥ svasukhāśrayāḥ
hariṃ kroḍagataṃ tasya dadṛśuḥ sarvamūrtayaḥ ||27
taṃ bālakaṃ narākāraṃ viṣṇuṃ natvā munīśvarāḥ
kalkiṃ kalkavināśārtham āvirbhūtaṃ vidurbudhāḥ ||28
nāmākurvaṃs tatas tasya kalkir ity abhiviśrutam
kṛtvā saṃskārakarmāṇi yayus te hṛṣṭamānasāḥ ||29
tataḥ sa vavṛdhe tatra sumatyā paripālitaḥ
kālenālpena kaṃsāriḥ śuklapakṣe yathā śaśī ||30
kalker jyeṣthāstrayaḥ śūrāḥ kaviprājñasumantrakāḥ
pitṛmātṛpriyakarā guruviprapratiṣṭhitāḥ ||31
kalker aṃśāḥ puro jātāḥ sādhavo dharmatatparāḥ
gārgyabhargyaviśālādyā jñātayas tad anubratāḥ ||32
viśākhayūpabhūpālapālitās tāpavarjitāḥ
brāhmaṇāḥ kalkim ālokya parāṃ prītim upāgatāḥ ||33
tato viṣṇuyaśāḥ putraṃ dhīraṃ sarvaguṇākaram
kalkiṃ kamalapatrākṣaṃ provāca paṭhanādṛtam ||34
tāta te brahmasaṃskāram yajñasūtramanūttamam
sāvitrīṃ vācayiṣyāmi tato vedān paṭhiṣyasi ||35
kalkir uvāca |
ko vedaḥ kā ca sāvitrī kena sūtreṇa saṃskṛtāḥ
brahmaṇā viditā loke tat tattvaṃ vada tāta mām ||36
pitovāca |
vedo harer vāk sāvitrī vedamātā pratiṣṭhitā
triguṇañ ca trivṛt sūtraṃ tena viprāḥ pratiṣṭhitāḥ ||37
daśayajñaiḥ saṃskṛtā ye brāhmaṇā brahmavādinaḥ
tatra vedāś ca lokānāṃ trayāṇām iha poṣakāḥ ||38
yajñādhyayanadānāditapaḥ svādhyāyasaṃyamaiḥ
prīṇayanti hariṃ bhaktyā vedatantravidhānataḥ ||39
tasmādyathopanayanakarmaṇo’haṃ dvijaiḥ saha
saṃskartuṃ bāndhavajanais tvām icchāmi śubhe dine ||40
putra uvāca |
ke ca te daśa saṃskārā brāhmaṇeṣu pratiṣṭhitāḥ
brāhmaṇāḥ kena vā viṣṇum arcayanti vidhānataḥ ||41
pitovāca |
brāhmaṇyāṃ brāhmaṇāj jāto garbhādhānādisaṃskṛtaḥ
sandhyātrayeṇa sāvitrīpūjajapaparāyaṇaḥ ||42
tapasvī satyavāg dhīro dharmātmā trāti saṃsṛtim
viṣnvarcanam idaṃ jñātvā sadānandamayo dvijaḥ ||43
putra uvāca |
kutrāste sa dvijo yena tārayanty akhilaṃ jagat
sanmārgeṇa hariṃ prīṇan kāmadogdhā jagattraye ||44
pitovāca |
kalinā balinā dharmaghātinā dvijapātinā
nirākṛtā dharmaratā gatā varṣāntarāntaram ||45
ye svalpatapaso viprāḥ sthitāḥ kaliyugāntare
śiśnodarabhṛto’dharmaniratā viratakriyāḥ ||46
pāpasārā durācārās tejohīnāḥ kalāviha
ātmānaṃ rakṣituṃ naiva śaktāḥ śūdrasya sevakāḥ ||47
iti janakavaco niśamya kalkiḥ kalikulanāśamano’bhilāṣajanmā
dvijanijavacanais tadopanīto gurukulavāsam uvāsa sādhunāthaḥ ||48
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye kalkijanmopanayanaṃ nāma dvitīyo’dhyāyaḥ ||2

<1.3>

tṛtīyo’dhyāyaḥ

sūta uvāca |
tato vastuṃ gurukule yāntaṃ kalkiṃ nirīkṣya saḥ
mahendrādristhito rāmaḥ samānīyāśramaṃ prabhuḥ ||1
prāha tvāṃ pāṭhayiṣyāmi guruṃ māṃ vidvi dharmataḥ
bhṛguvaṃśasamutpannaṃ jāmadagnyaṃ mahāprabhum ||2
vedavedāṅgatattvajñaṃ dhanurvedaviśāradam
kṛtvā niḥkṣattriyāṃ pṛthvīṃ dattvā viprāya dakṣiṇāṃ ||3
mahendrādrau tapas taptum āgato’haṃ dvijātmaja
tvaṃ paṭhātra nijaṃ vedaṃ yac cānyac chāstram uttamam ||4
iti tad vaca āśrutya samprahṛṣṭatanūruhaḥ
kalkiḥ puro namaskṛtya vedādhītī tato’bhavat ||5
sāṅgaṃ catuḥṣaṣṭikalaṃ dhanurvedādikañ ca yat
samadhītya jāmadagnyāt kalkiḥ prāha kṛtāñjaliḥ ||6
dakṣiṇāṃ prārthaya vibho yā deyā tava sannidhau
yayā me sarvasiddhiḥ syād yā syāt tvat toṣakāriṇī ||7
rāma uvāca |
brahmaṇā prārthato bhūman kalinigrahakāraṇāt
viṣnuḥ sarvāśrayaḥ pūrṇaḥ sa jātaḥ śambhale bhavān ||8
matto vidyāṃ śivād astraṃ labdhvā vedamayaṃ śukam
siṃhale ca priyāṃ padmāṃ dharmān saṃsthāpayiṣyasi ||9
tato digvijaye bhūpān dharmahīnān kalipriyān
nigṛhya bauddhān devāpiṃ maruñ ca sthāpayiṣyasi ||10
vayam etais tu saṃtuṣtāḥ sādhukṛtyaiḥ sadakṣiṇāḥ
yajñaṃ dānaṃ tapaḥ karma kariṣyāmo yathocitam ||11
ity etad vacanaṃ śrutvā namaskṛtya muniṃ gurum
bilvodakeśvaraṃ devaṃ gatvā tuṣṭāva śaṅkaram ||12
pūjayitvā yathānyāyaṃ śivaṃ śāntaṃ maheśvaram
praṇipatyāśutoṣaṃ taṃ dhyātvā prāha hṛdisthitam ||13
kalkir uvāca |
gaurīnāthaṃ viśvanāthaṃ śaraṇyaṃ
bhūtāvāsaṃ vāsukīkaṇthabhūṣam
tṛyakṣaṃ pañcāsyādidevaṃ purāṇaṃ
vande sāndrānandasandohadakṣam ||14
yogādīśaṃ kāmanāśaṃ karālaṃ
gaṅgāsaṅgāklinnamūrdhānam īśam
jaṭājūṭāṭoparikṣiptabhāvaṃ
mahākālaṃ candrabhālaṃ namāmi ||15
śmaśānasthaṃ bhūtavetālasaṃgaṃ
nānāśastraiḥ khaḍgaśūlādibhiś ca
vyagrātyugrā bāhavo lokanāśe
yasya krodhoddhūtaloko’stameti ||16
yo bhūtādiḥ pañcabhūtaiḥ sisṛkṣus
tanmātrātmā kālakarmasvabhāvaiḥ
prahṛtyedaṃ prāpya jīvatvam īśo
brahmānando ramate taṃ namāmi ||17
sthitau viṣnuḥ sarvajiṣnuḥ surātmā
lokān sādhūn dharmasetūn bibharti
brahmādyāṃśe yo’bhimānī guṇātmā
śabdādyaṅgais taṃ pareśaṃ namāmi ||18
yasyājñayā vāyavo vānti loke
jvalaty agniḥ savitā yāti tapyan
śītāṃśuḥ khe nārakaiḥ sagrahaiś ca
pravartate taṃ pareśaṃ prapadye ||19
yasyāśvāsāt sarvadhātrī dharitrī
devo varṣaty ambu kālaḥ pramātā
merur madhye bhuvanānāñ ca bhartā
tam īśānaṃ viśvarūpaṃ namāmi ||20
iti kalkistavaṃ śrutvā śivaḥ sarvātmadarśanaḥ
sākṣāt prāha hasanniśaḥ pārvatīsahitogrataḥ ||21
kalkeḥ saṃspṛśya hastena samastāvayavaṃ mudā
tam āha varaya preṣṭha varaṃ yat te’bhikāṃkṣitam ||22
tvayā kṛtam idaṃ stotraṃ ye paṭhanti janā bhuvi
teṣāṃ sarvārthasiddhiḥ syād iha loke paratra ca ||23
vidyārthī cāpnuyād vidyāṃ dharmārthī dharmam apnuyat
kāmān avāpnuyat kāmī paṭhanāc chravaṇād api ||24
tvaṃ gāruḍam idaṃ cāśvaṃ kāmagaṃ bahurūpiṇam
śukamenañca sarvajñaṃ mayā dattaṃ gṛhāṇa bhoḥ ||25
sarvaśāstrāstravidvaṃsaṃ sarvavedārthapāragam
jayinaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ tvām vadiṣyanti mānavāḥ ||26
ratnatsaruṃ karālañ ca karavālaṃ mahāprabham
gṛhāṇa gurubhārāyāḥ pṛthivyā bhārasādhanam ||27
iti tad vaca āśrutya namaskṛtya maheśvaram
śambhalagrāmam agamat turageṇa tvarānvitaḥ ||28
pitaraṃ mātaraṃ bhrātṝn namaskṛtya yathāvidhi
sarvaṃ tad varṇayām āsa jāmadagnyasya bhāṣitam ||29
śivasya varadānañ ca kathayitvā śubhāḥ kathāḥ
kalkiḥ paramatejasvī jñātibhyo’pyavadan mudā ||30
gārgyabhargyaviśālādyās tac chrutvā nanditāḥ sthitāḥ
kathopakathanaṃ jātaṃ śambhalagrāmavāsinām ||31
viśākhayūpabhūpālaḥ śrutvā teṣāñ ca bhāṣitam
prādurbhāvaṃ harer mene kalinigrahakārakam ||32
māhiṣmatyāṃ nijapure yāgadānatapovratān
bhrāhmanān kṣatriyān vaiśyāñ śūdrān api hareḥ priyān ||33
svadharmaniratān dṛṣṭvā dharmiṣṭho’bhūn nṛpaḥ svayam
prajāpālaḥ śuddhamanāḥ prādurbhāvāc chriyaḥ pateḥ ||34
adharmavaṃśyāṃs tān dṛṣṭvā janān dharmakriyāparān
lobhānṛtādayo jagmus tad deśād duḥkhitā bhayam ||35
jaitraṃ turagamāruhya khaḍgañ ca vimalaprabham
daṃśitaḥ saśaraṃ cāpaṃ gṛhītvāgāt purād bahiḥ ||36
viśākhayūpabhūpālaḥ prayāt sādhujanapriyaḥ
kalkiṃ draṣtuṃ harer aṃśam āvirbhūtañ ca śambhale ||37
kaviṃ prājñaṃ sumantuñ ca puraskṛtya mahāprabham
gārgyabhargyaviśālaiś ca jñātibhiḥ parivāritam ||38
viśākhayūpo dadṛśe candraṃ tārāgaṇair iva
purādbahiḥ surair yad vad indram uccaiḥ śravaḥ sthitam ||39
viśākhayūpo’vanataḥ saṃprahṛṣṭatanūruhaḥ
kalker ālokanāt sadyaḥ pūrṇātmā vaiṣṇavo’bhavat ||40
saha rajñā vasan kalkiḥ dharmān āha puroditān
brāhmaṇakṣatriyaviśām āśramāṇāṃ samāsataḥ ||41
mamāṃśān kalivibhraṣṭān iti majjanmasaṃgatān
rājasūyāśvamedhābhyāṃ māṃ yajasva samāhitaḥ ||42
aham eva paro loke dharmaś cāhaṃ sanātanaḥ
kālasvabhāvasaṃskārāḥ karmānugatayo mama ||43
somasūryakule jātau devāpimarusaṃjñakau
sthāpayitvā kṛtayugaṃ kṛtvā yāsyāmi sadgatim ||44
iti tad vacanaṃ śrutvā rājā kalkiṃ hariṃ prabhum
praṇamya prāha saddharmān vaiṣṇavān manasepsitān ||45
iti nṛpavacanaṃ niśamya kalkiḥ kalikulanāśanavāsanāvatāraḥ
nijajanapariṣadvinodakārī madhuravacobhir āha sādhudharmān ||46 
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāvagate bhaviṣye kalkivaralābhanāmakas tṛtīyo’dhyāyaḥ ||3

<1.4>

caturtho’dhyāyaḥ

sūta uvāca |
tataḥ kalkiḥ sabhāmadhye rājamāno ravir yathā
babhāṣe taṃ nṛpaṃ dharmamayo dharmān dvijapriyān ||1
kalkir uvāca
kālena brahmano nāśe pralaye mayi saṅgatāḥ
aham evāsam evāgre nānyat kāryam idaṃ mama ||2
prasuptalokatantrasya dvaitahīnasya cātmanaḥ
mahāniśānte rantuṃ me samudbhūto virāṭ prabhuḥ ||3
sahasraśīrṣā puruṣaḥ sahasrākṣaḥ sahasrapāt
tad aṅgajo’bhavad brahmā vedavaktro mahāprabhuḥ ||4
jīvopādher mamāṃśāc ca prakṛtyā māyayā svayā
brahmopādiḥ sa sarvajño mama vāgvedaśāsitaḥ ||5
sasarja jīvajātāni kālamāyāṃśayogataḥ
devā manvādayo lokāḥ saprajāpatayaḥ prabhuḥ ||6
guṇinyā māyayāṃśā me nānopādhāv sasarjāre
sopādhaya ime lokā devāḥ sasthāṇujaṅgamāḥ ||7
mamāṃśā māyayā sṛṣṭā yato mayyāviśaṃllaye 
evaṃ vidhā brāhmaṇā ye mac charīrā mad ātmikāḥ ||8
mām uddharanti bhuvane yajñādhyayanasatkriyāḥ
māṃ prasevanti śaṃsanti tapodānakriyāsv iha ||9
smaranty āmodayanty eva nānye devādayas tathā
brāhmaṇā vedavactāro vedā me mūrtayaḥ parāḥ ||10
tasmādime brāhmaṇajās taiḥ puṣṭās trijagajjanāḥ
jaganti me śarīrāṇi tat poṣe brahmaṇo varaḥ ||11
tenāhaṃ tān namasyāmi śuddhasattvaguṇāśrayaḥ
tato jaganmayaṃ pūrvaṃ māṃ sevante’khilāśrayāḥ ||12
viśākhayūpa uvāca |
viprasya lakṣaṇaṃ brūhi tvad bhaktiḥ kā ca tat kṛtā
yatas tavānugraheṇa vāgbāṇā brāhmaṇāḥ kṛtāḥ ||13
kalkir uvāca |
vedā mām īśvaraṃ prāhur avyaktaṃ vyaktimat param
te vedā brāhmaṇamukhe nānādharme prakāśitāḥ ||14
yo dharmo brahmaṇānāṃ hi sā bhaktir mama puṣkalā
tayāhaṃ toṣitaḥ śrīśaḥ saṃbhavāmi yuge yuge ||15
urdhvantu trivṛtaṃ sūtraṃ sadhavānirmitaṃ śanaiḥ
tantutrayamadhovṛttaṃ yajñasūtraṃ vidurbudhāḥ ||16
triguṇaṃ tadgranthiyuktaṃ vedapravarasaṃmitam
śirodharānnābhimadhyāt pṛṣṭhārdhaparimāṇakam ||17
yajurvidāṃ nābhimitaṃ sāmagānāmayaṃ vidhiḥ
vāmaskandhena vidhṛtaṃ yajñasūtraṃ balapradam ||18
mṛdbhasmacandanādyais tu dhārayet tilakaṃ dvijaḥ
bhāle tripuṇdraṃ karmāṅgaṃ keśaparyantam ujjvalam ||19
puṇdram aṅgulim ānantu tripuṇdraṃ tat tridhā kṛtam
brahmaviṣṇuśivāvāsaṃ darśanāt pāpanāśanam ||20
brāhmaṇānāṃ kare svargā vāco vedāḥ kare hariḥ
gātre tīrthāni rāgāś ca nāḍīṣu prakṛtis trivṛt ||21
sāvitrī kaṇthakuharā hṛdayaṃ brahmasaṃjñitam
teṣāṃ stanāntare dharmaḥ pṛṣṭho’dharmaḥ prakīrtitaḥ ||22
bhūdevā brāhmaṇā rājan pūjyā vandyāḥ saduktibhiḥ
caturāśramyakuśalā mama dharmapravartakāḥ ||23
bālāś cāpi jñānavṛddhās tapovṛddhā mama priyāḥ
teṣāṃ vacaḥ pālayitum avatārāḥ kṛtā mayā ||24
mahābhāgyam brāhmāṇānāṃ sarvapāpapraṇāśanam
kalidoṣaharaṃ śrutvā mucyate sarvato bhayāt ||25
iti kalkir uvāca vacaḥ śrutvā kalidoṣavināśakam
praṇamya taṃ śuddhamanāḥ prayayau vaiṣṇavāgraṇīḥ ||26
gate rājani sandhyāyāṃ śivadattaśuko budhaḥ
caritvā kalkipurataḥ stutvā taṃ purataḥ sthitaḥ ||27
taṃ śukaṃ prāha kalkis tu sasmitaṃ stutipāṭhakam
svāgataṃ bhavatā kasmād deśāt kiṃ khāditaṃ tataḥ ||28
śuka uvāca |
śṛṇu nātha vaco mahyaṃ kautūhalasamanvitam
ahaṃ gataś ca jaladher madhye siṃhalasaṃjñake ||29
yathāvṛttaṃ dvīpagataṃ tac citraṃ śravaṇapriyaṃ
bṛhadrathasya nṛpateeḥ kanyāyāś caritāmṛtam ||30
kaumudyām iha jātāyā jagatāṃ pāpanāśanam
caritaṃ siṃhale dvipe cāturvarṇyajanāvṛte ||31
prāsādaharmyasadanapurarājivirājite 
ratnasphāṭikakuḍyādisvarṇalatābhir vibhūṣite ||32 
strībhir uttamaveśābhiḥ padminībhiḥ samāvṛte
sarobhiḥ sārasair haṃsair upakūlajalākule ||33
bhṛṅgaraṃgaprasaṃgāḍhye padmaiḥ kahlārakundakaiḥ
nānāmbujalatājālavanopavanamaṇdite ||34
deśe bṛhadratho rājā mahābalaparākramaḥ
tasya padmāvatī kanyā dhanyā reje yaśasvinī ||35
bhuvane durlabhā loke’pratimā varavarṇinī 
kāmamohakarī cārucaritrā citranirmitā ||36 
śivasevāparā gaurī yathā pūjyā susammatā
sakhībhiḥ kanyakābhiś ca japadhyānaparāyaṇā ||37
jñātvā tāñ ca harer lakṣmīṃ samudbhūtāṃ varāṃganām
haraḥ prādur abhūt sākṣāt pārvatyā saha harṣitaḥ ||38
sā tamālokya varadaṃ śivaṃ gaurīsamanvitam
lajjitādhomukhī kiñcin novāca purataḥ sthitā ||39
haras tām āha subhage tava nārāyaṇaḥ patiḥ
pāṇiṃ grahīṣyati mudā nānyo yogyo nṛpātmajaḥ ||40
kāmabhāvena bhuvane ye tvāṃ paśyanti mānavāḥ
tenaiva vayasā nāryo bhaviṣyanty api tat kṣaṇāt ||41
devāsurās tathā nāgā gandharvāś cāraṇādayaḥ
tvayā rantuṃ yathā kāle bhaviṣyanti kila striyaḥ ||42
vinā nārāyaṇaṃ devaṃ tvat pāṇigrahaṇārthinam
gṛhaṃ yāhi tapastyaktvā bhogāyatanam uttamam ||43
mā kṣobhaya hareḥ patni kamale vimalaṃ kuru 
iti dattvā varaṃ somas tatraivāntardadhe haraḥ ||44
haravaram iti sā niśamya padmā samucitam ātmamanorathaprakāśam
vikasitavadanā praṇamya somaṃ nijajanakālayam āviveśa rāmā ||45
iti śrī kalkipurāne’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye haravarapradānaṃnāma caturtho’dhyāyaḥ ||4

<1.5>

pañcamo’dhyāyaḥ |

śūka uvāca |
gate bahutithe kāle padmāṃ vīkṣya bṛhadrathaḥ
nirūḍhayauvanāṃ putrīṃ vismitaḥ pāpaśaṅkayā ||1
kaumudīṃ prāha mahiṣīṃ padmodvāhe’tra kaṃ nṛpam
varayiṣyāmi subhage kulaśīlasamanvitam ||2
sā tam āha patiṃ devī śivena prati bhāṣitam
viṣṇurasyāḥ patir iti bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ ||3
iti tasyāvacaḥ śrutvā rājā prāha kadeti tām
viṣnuḥ sarvaguhāvāsaḥ pāṇimasyā grahīṣyati ||4
na me bhāgyodayaḥ kaścid yena jāmātaraṃ harim
varayiṣyāmi kanyārthe vedavatyā muner yathā ||5
imāṃ svayaṃvarāṃ padmāṃ padmām iva mahodadheḥ
mathane’suradevānāṃ tathā viṣṇur grahīṣyati ||6
iti bhūpagaṇān bhūpaḥ samāhvaya puraskṛtān
guṇaśīlavayorūpavidyādraviṇasaṃvṛtān ||7
svayaṃvarārthaṃ padmāyāḥ siṃhale bahumaṅgale
vicārya kārayāmāsa sthānaṃ bhūpaniveśanām ||8
tatrāyātā nṛpāḥ sarve vivāhakṛtaniścayāḥ
nijasainyaiḥ parivṛtāḥ svarṇaratnavibhūṣitāḥ ||9
rathān gajān aśvavarān samārūḍhā mahābalāḥ
śvetacchatrakṛtacchāyāḥ śvetacāmaravījitāḥ ||10
śastrāstratejasā dīptā devākh sendrā ivābhavan
rucirāśvaḥ sukarmā ca madirākṣo dṛḍhāśugaḥ ||11
kṛṣṇasāraḥ pāradaś ca jīmūtaḥ krūramardanaḥ
kāśaḥ kuśāmbur vasumān kaṅkaḥ kathanasañjayāv ||12
gurumitraḥ pramāthī ca vijṛmbhaḥ sṛñjayo’kṣamaḥ
ete cānye ca bahavaḥ samāyātā mahābalāḥ ||13
viviśuste raṅgagatāḥ svasvasthāneṣu pūjitāḥ
vāndyatāṇḍavasaṃhṛṣṭāś citramālyāmbarādharāḥ ||14
nānābhogasukhodriktāḥ kāmarāmā ratipradāḥ
tānālokya siṃhaleśaḥ suāṃ kanyāṃ varavarninīm ||15
gaurīṃ candrānanāṃ śyāmāṃ tārahāravibhūṣitām
maṇimuktāpravālaiś ca sarvāṅgālaṃkṛtāṃ śubhām ||16
kiṃ māyāṃ mohajananīṃ kiṃ vā kāmapriyāṃ bhuvi
rūpalāvaṇyasampattyā na cānyām iha dṛṣṭavān ||17
svarge kṣitau vā pātāle’py ahaṃ sarvatrago yadi
paścāddāsīgaṇākīrṇāṃ sakhībhiḥ parivāritām ||18
daivārikair vetrahastaiḥ śāsitāntaḥ purādbahiḥ
purobandigaṇākīrṇāṃ prāpayāmāsa tāṃ śanaiḥ ||19
nūpuraiḥ kiṅkiṇībhiś ca kvaṇantīṃ janamohinīm
svāgatānāṃ nṛpāṇāñ ca kulaśīla guṇān bhahūn ||20
śṛṇvantī haṃsagamanā ratnamālākaragrahā
rucirāpāṅgabhaṅgena prekṣantī lolakuṇḍalā ||21
nṛtyatkuntalasopānagaṇḍamaṇḍalamaṇditā
kiñcit smerollasadvaktradaśanadyotadīpitā ||22
vedīmadhyāruṇakṣaumavasanā kokilasvanā
rūpalāvaṇyapaṇyena kretukāmā jagattrayam ||23
samāgatāṃ tāṃ prasamīkṣya būpāḥ saṃmohinīṃ kāmavimūḍhacittāḥ
petuḥ kṣitau vismṛtavastraśastrāḥ rathāśvamattadvipavāhanāste ||24
tasyāḥ smarakṣobhanirīkṣaṇena striyo babhūvuḥ kamanīyarūpākh |
bṛhannitambastanabhāranamrāḥ sumadhyamāstatsmṛtijānarūpākh ||25
vilāsahāsavyasanāticitrāḥ kāntānanāḥ śoṇasarojanetrāḥ
strīrūpamātmānamavekṣya bhūpās tām anvagacchanviśadānuvṛttyā ||26
ahaṃ vaṭasthaḥ paridharṣitātmā padmāvivāhotsavadarśanākulaḥ
tasyā vaco’ntarhṛdi duḥkhitāyāḥ śrotuṃ sthitaḥ strītvāmiteṣu teṣu ||27
jahīhi kalke camalāvilāpaṃ śrutaṃ vicitraṃ jagatām adīśa
gate vivāhotsavamaṅgale sā śivaṃ śaraṇyaṃ hṛdaye nidhāya ||28
tān dṛṣṭvā nṛpatīn gajāśvarathibhis tyaktān sakhitvaṃ gatān
strībhāvena samanvitānanugatān padmāṃ vilokyāntike
dīnā tyaktavibhūṣaṇā vilikhatī padāṅgulaiḥ kāminī
īśaṃ kartuṃ nijanātham īśvaravacas tathyaṃ hariṃ sāsmarat ||29
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye padmāsvayaṃvare bhūpatīnāṃ strītvakathanaṃnāmapañcamo’dhyāyaḥ ||5

<1.6>

ṣaṣṭho’dhyāyaḥ |

śuka uvāca |
tataḥ sā vismitamukhī padmā nijajanair vṛtā
hariṃ patiṃ cintayantī provāca vimalāṃ sthitām ||1
vimale kiṃ kṛtaṃ dhātrā lalāṭe likhanaṃ mama
darśanād api lokānāṃ puṃsāṃ strībhāvakārakam ||2
mamāpi mandabhāgyāyāḥ pāpinyāḥ śivasevanam
viphalatvam anuprāptaṃ bījam uptaṃ yathoṣare ||3
harir lakṣmīpatiḥ sarvajagatām adhipaḥ prabhuḥ
mat kṛte’py abhilāṣaṃ kiṃ kariṣyati jagatpatiḥ ||4
yadi śambhor vaco mithyā yadi viṣṇur na māṃ smaret
tadāham anale dehaṃ tyakṣyāmi haribhāvitā ||5
kva cāhaṃ mānuṣī dīnā kvāste devo janārdanaḥ
nigṛhītā vidhātrāhaṃ śivena parivañcitā ||6
viṣṇunā ca parityaktā madanyā kātra jīvati ||7
iti nānā vilāpinyā vacanaṃ śocanāśrayam
padmāyāścāruceṣṭāyāḥ śrutvāyātastavāntike ||8
śukasya vacanaṃ śrutvā kalkiḥ paramavismitaḥ
taṃ jagāda punaryāhi padmāṃ bodhayituṃ priyām ||9
mat sandeśaharo bhūtvā mad rūpaguṇakīrtanam
śrāvayitvā punaḥ kīra samāyāsyasi bāndhava ||10
sā me priyā patir aham tasyā daivavinirmitaḥ
madhyasthena tvayā yogamāvayoś ca bhaviṣyati ||11
sarvajño’si vidhijño’si kālajño’si kathāmṛtaiḥ
tāmāśvāsya mamāśvāsakathās tasyāḥ samāhara ||12
iti kalker vacaḥ śrutvā śukaḥ paramaharṣitaḥ
praṇamya taṃ prītamanāḥ prayayau siṃhalaṃ tuaran ||13
khagaḥ samudrapāreṇa snātvā pītvāmṛtaṃ payaḥ
bījapūraphalāhāro yayau rājaniveśanam ||14
tatra kanyāpuraṃ gatvā vṛkṣe nāgeśvare vasan
padmām ālokya tāṃ prāha śuko mānuṣabhāṣayā ||15
kuśalaṃ te varārohe rūpayauvanaśālini
tvāṃ lolanayanāṃ manye lakṣmīrūpām ivāparām ||16
padmānanāṃ padmagandhāṃ padmanetrāṃ karāmbuje
kamalaṃ kālayantīṃ tvāṃ lakṣayāmi parāṃ śriyam ||17
kiṃ dhātrā sarvajagatāṃ rūpālāvaṇyasampadām
nirmitāsi varārohe jīvānāṃ mohakāriṇi ||18
iti bhāṣitam ākārṇya kīrasyāmitam adbhutam
hasantī prāha sā devī taṃ padmā padmamālinī ||19
kas tvaṃ kasmād āgato’si kathaṃ māṃ śukarūpadhṛk
devo vā dānavo vā tvam āgato’si dayāparaḥ ||20
śuka uvāca |
sarvajño’ham kāmagāmī sarvaśāstrārthatattvavit
devagandharvabhūpānāṃ sabhāsu paripūjitaḥ ||21
carāmi svecchayā khe tvām īkṣaṇārtham ihāgataḥ
tvām ahaṃ hṛdi saṃtaptāṃ tyaktabhogāṃ manasvinīm ||22
hāsyālāpasakhīsaṅgadehābharaṇavarjitām
vilokyāhaṃ dīnacetāḥ pṛcchāmi śrotum īritam
kokilālapasantāpajanakaṃ madhuraṃ mṛdu ||23
tava dantauṣṭhajihvāgralulitākṣarapaṅktayaḥ
yat karṇakuhare magnās teṣāṃ kiṃ varṇyate tapaḥ ||24
saukumāryaṃ śirīṣasya kva kāntir vā niśākare
pīyūṣaṃ kva vadanty evānandaṃ brahmaṇi te budhāḥ ||25 
tava bāhulatābaddhā ye pāśyanti sudhānanam 
taṣā tapodānajapair vyarthaiḥ kiṃ janayiṣyati ||26
tilakālakasaṃmiśraṃ lolakuṇḍalamaṇditam
lolekṣaṇollasadvaktraṃ paśyantāṃ na punarbhavaḥ ||27
bṛhadrathasute svādhiṃ vada bhāmini yat kṛte 
tapaḥ kṣīṇām iva tanuṃ lakṣayāmi rujaṃ vinā
kanakapratimā yadvat pāṃsubhir malinīkṛtā ||28
padmovāca |
kiṃ rūpeṇa kulenāpi dhanenābhijanena vā
sarva niṣphalatāmeti yasya daivamadakṣiṇam ||29
śruṇu kīra samākhyānaṃ yadi vā viditaṃ tava 
bālyapaugaṇḍakaiśore harasevāṃ karomy aham ||30 
tena pūjāvidhānena tuṣto bhūtvā maheśvaraḥ
varaṃ varaya padme tvam ity āha priyayā saha ||31
lajjayādhomukhīmagre sthitāṃ māṃ vīkṣya śaṅkaraḥ
prāha te bhavitā svāmī harir nārāyaṇaḥ prabhuḥ ||32
devo vā dānavo vānyo gandharvo vā tavekṣaṇāt
kāmena manasā nārī bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ ||33
iti dattvā varaṃ somaḥ prāha viṣnvarcanaṃ yathā
tathāhaṃ te pravakṣyāmi samāhitamanāḥ śṛṇu ||34
etāḥ sakhyo nṛpāḥ pūrvamāhṛtā ye svayaṃvare
pitrā dharmārthinā dṛṣṭvā ramyāṃ māṃ yauvanānvitām ||35 
svāgatāste sukhāsīnā vivāhakṛtaniścayāḥ
yuvāno guṇavantaśca rūpadraviṇasammatāḥ ||36
svayaṃvaragatāṃ māṃ te vilokya ruciraprabhām

sūta uvāca |

padmo’vāca |

pūrṇātmā deśiko mūlaṃ mantraṃ japati mantravit ||3

viṣvaksenādikānāntu dattvā viṣṇuniveditam ||4

śrotrānandakaraṃ devagandharvanarahṛtpriyam ||7

sānandaṃ paramānandadāyakaṃ prāha taṃ tadā ||32

pūjayitvā kare spṛṣṭvā payaḥ pānena tarpayan ||33

purīṃ kārumatīṃ tatra dadarśa purataḥ sthitām ||42
marālajālasañcālavilolakamalāntarām
unmīlitābjamālālikalitākulitaṃ saraḥ ||43

dadarśa svacchapathasāṃ laharīlolavījitam ||44
vanaṃ kadambakuddālaśālatālāmrakesaraiḥ
kapitthāśvatthakharjūrabījapūrakaraṃjakaiḥ ||45

tac chrutvā vinayānvitaḥ prabhumataṃ yāmīti padmāśramaṃ |
tatsandeśam iha prayāṇamadhunā gatvā sa kīro’vadat ||47

śrutvaitad vacanāmṛtaṃ kalikuladhvaṃsasya kalker alaṃ
dṛṣṭvā satpuruṣatvamasya muditā padmā sakhībhir vṛtā |
kāntaṃ klāntamanā kṛtāñjalipuṭā provāca tatsādaraṃ
dhīraṃ dhīrapuraskṛtaṃ nijapatiṃ natvā namatkandharā ||33
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye dvitīyāṃse padmākalkisākṣātsaṃvādo nāma dvitīyo’dhyāyaḥ ||2

<2.3>

tṛtīyo ’dhyāyaḥ |

sūta uvāca |
sā padmā taṃ hariṃ matvā premagadgadabhāṣiṇī
tuṣṭāva vrīḍitā devī karuṇāvaruṇālayam ||1
prasīda jagatāṃ nātha dharmavarman ramāpate
vidito’si viśuddhātman vaśagāṃ trāhi māṃ prabho ||2
dhanyāhaṃ kṛtapuṇyāhaṃ tapodānajapavrataiḥ
tvāṃ pratoṣya durārādhyaṃ labdhaṃ tava padāmbujam ||3
ājñā kuru padāmbhojaṃ tava saṃspṛśya śobhanam
bhavanaṃ yāmi rājānam ākhyātuṃ svāgataṃ tava ||4
iti padmā rūpasadmā gatvā svapitaraṃ nṛpam
provācāgamanaṃ kalker viṣṇor aṃśasya dautyakaiḥ ||5
sakhīmukhena padmāyāḥ pāṇigrahaṇakāmyayā
harer āgamanaṃ śrutvā saharṣo’bhūd bṛhadrathaḥ ||6
purodhasā brahmaṇaiś ca pātrair mittraiḥ sumaṅgalaiḥ
vādyatāṇḍavagītaiś ca pūjāyojanapāṇibhiḥ ||7
jagāmānayituṃ kalkiṃ sārdhaṃ nijajanaiḥ prabhuḥ
maṇḍayitvā kārumatīṃ patākāsvarṇatoraṇaiḥ ||8
tato jalāśayābhyāsaṃ gatvā viṣṇuyaśaḥsutam
maṇivedikayāsīnaṃ bhuvanaikagatiṃ patim ||9
ghanā ghanopari yathā śobhante rucirāṇyaho
vidyud indrāyudhādīni tathaiva bhūṣaṇānyuta ||10
śarīre pītavāsāgraghorabhāsā vibhūṣitam
rūpalāvaṇyasadane madanodyamanāśane ||11
dadarśa purato rājā rūpaśīlaguṇākaram
sāśruḥ sapulakaḥ śrīśaṃ dṛṣṭvā sādhu tam arcayat ||12
jñānāgocaram etan me tavāgamanam īśvara
yathā māndhātṛputrasya yadunāthena kānane ||13
ity uktvā taṃ pūjayitvā samānīya nijāśrame
harmyaprāsādasaṃbādhe sthāpayitvā dadau sutām ||14
padmāṃ padmapalāśākṣīṃ padmanetrāya padminīm
padmajādeśataḥ padmanābhāyādādyathākramam ||15
kalkir labdhvā priyāṃ bhāryāṃ siṃhale sādhusatkṛtaḥ
samuvāsa viśeṣajñaḥ samīkṣya dvīpam uttamam ||16
rājānaḥ strītvam āpannāḥ padmāyāḥ sakhitāṃ gatāḥ
draṣtuṃ samīyus tvaritāḥ kalkiṃ viṣṇuṃ jagatpatim ||17
tāḥ striyo’pi tam ālokya saṃspṛśya caraṇāmbujam
punaḥ puṃstvaṃ samāpannā revāsnānāt tad ājñayā ||18
padmākalkī gaurakṛṣṇau viparītāntarāv ubhau
bahiḥ sphuṭau nīlapītavāsovyājena paśyatu ||19
dṛṣṭvā prabhāvaṃ kalkes tu rājānaḥ paramādbhutam
praṇamya parayā bhaktyā tuṣṭuvuḥ śaraṇārthinaḥ ||20
jaya jaya nijamāyayā kalpitāśeṣaviśeṣakalpanāpariṇāma
jalāplutalokatrayopakaraṇamākalayya manum aniśamya pūritamavijanāvijanāvirbhūtamahāmīnaśarīra tvaṃ nijakṛtadharmasetusaṃrakṣaṇakṛtāvatāraḥ ||21
punar iha ditijabalaparilaṅghitavāsavasūdanādṛtajitatribhuvanaparākramahiraṇyākṣanidhanapṛthivyuddharaṇasaṃkalpābhinivaśena dhṛtakolāvatāraḥ pāhi naḥ ||22
punar iha jaladhimathanādṛtadevadānavagaṇamandarācalānayanavyākulitānāṃ sāhāyyenādṛtacittaḥ parvatoddharaṇāmṛtaprāśanaracanāvatāraḥ kūrmākāraḥ prasīda pareśa tvaṃ dīnanṛpāṇām ||23 
punar iha tribhuvanajayino mahābalaparākramasya hiraṇyakaśipor arditānāṃ devavarāṇāṃ bhayabhītānāṃ kalyāṇāya ditisutavadhaprepsurbrahmaṇo varadānādavadhyasya na śastrāstrarātridivāsvargamartyapātālatale devagandharvakinnaranaranāgair iti vicintya naraharirūpeṇa nakhāgrabhinnoruṃ daṃṣṭradantacchadaṃ tyaktāsuṃ kṛtavān asi ||24
punariha trijagajjayno baleḥ satre śakrānujo baṭuvāmano daityasaṃmohanāya tripadabhūmiyācñācchalena viśvakāyastadutsṛṣṭajalasaṃsparśavivṛddhamano’bhilāṣas tvaṃ bhūtale baler dauvārikatvam aṅgīkṛtam ucitaṃ dānaphalam ||25 
punariha haihayādinṛpāṇām amitabalaparākramāṇāṃ nānāmadollaṅghitamaryādāvartmanāṃ nidhanāya bhṛguvaṃśajo jāmadagnyaḥ pitṛhomadhenuharaṇapravṛddhamanyuvaśāttrisaptakṛtvo niḥkṣatriyāṃ pṛthivīṃ kṛtavān asi paraśurāmāvatāraḥ ||26 
punariha pulastyavaṃśāvataṃsasya viśravasaḥ putrasya niśācarasya rāvaṇasya lokatrayatāpanasya nidhanamurarīkṛtya ravikulajātadaśarathātmajo viśvāmitrād astrāṇy upalabhya vane sītāharaṇavaśātpravṛddhamanyunā ambudhiṃ vānarair nibadhya sagaṇaṃ daśakandharaṃ hatavān asi rāmāvatāraḥ ||27
punariha yadukulajaladhikalānidhiḥ sakalasuragaṇasevitapadāravindadvandvaḥ vividhadānavadaityadalanalokatrayaduritatāpano vasudevātmajo rāmāvatāro balabhadras tvam asi ||28
punariha vidhikṛtavedadharmānuṣthānavihitanānādarśanasaṃghṛṇaḥ saṃsārakarmatyāgavidhinā brahmābhāsavilāsacāturī prakṛtivimānanāmasampādayan buddhāvatāras tvam asi ||29
adhunā kalikulanāśāvatāro bauddhapākhaṇḍamlecchādīnāñ ca vedadharmasetuparipālanāya kṛtāvatāraḥ kalkirūpeṇāsmān strītvanirayād uddhṛtavān asi tavānukampāṃ kim iha kathayāmaḥ ||30
kva te brahmādīnām aviditavilāsāvataraṇaṃ
kva naḥ kāmā vāmākulitamṛgatṛṣnārtamanasām |
suduṣprāpyaṃ yuṣmac caraṇajalajālokanam idaṃ
kṛpāpārāvāraḥ pramuditadṛśāśvāsaya nijān ||31
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye dvitīyāṃśe nṛpāṇāṃ stavo nāma tṛtīyo’dhyāyaḥ ||3 

<2.4>

caturtho’dhyāyaḥ |

sūta uvāca |
śrutvā nṛpāṇāṃ bhaktānāṃ vacanaṃ puruṣottamaḥ
brahmaṇakṣatraviṭśūdravarṇānāṃ dharmam āha yat ||1
pravṛttānāṃ nivṛttānāṃ karma yat parikīrtitam
sarvaṃ saṃśrāvayām āsa vedānām anuśāsanam ||2
iti kalker vacaḥ śrutvā rājāno viśadāśayāḥ
praṇipatya punaḥ prāhuḥ pūrvāntu gatimātmanaḥ ||3
strītvaṃ vāpyathavā puṃstvaṃ kasya vā kena vā kṛtam
jarāyauvanabālyādi sukhaduḥkhādikaṃ ca yat ||4
kasmāt kuto vā kasmin vā kim etad iti vā vibho
anirṇītāny aviditāny api karmāṇi varṇaya ||5
tadā tad ākarṇya kalkir anantaṃ munim asmarat |
so’py ananto munivaras tīrthapādo bṛhadvrataḥ ||6
kalker darśanato muktim ākalayyāgatas tvaran
samāgatya punaḥ prāha kiṃ kariṣyāmi kutra vā
yāsyāmīti vacaḥ śrutvā kalkiḥ prāha hasan munim ||7
kṛtaṃ dṛṣtaṃ tvayā sarvaṃ jñātaṃ yāhyanivartakam
adṛṣṭam akṛtañ ceti śrutvā hṛṣṭamanā muniḥ ||8
gamanāyodyataṃ taṃ tu dṛṣṭvā nṛpagaṇās tataḥ
kalkiṃ kamalapatrākṣaṃ procur vismitacetasaḥ ||9
rājāna ūcuḥ |
kim anenāpi kathitaṃ tvayā vā kim u tān prati
sarvaṃ tac chrotum icchāmaḥ kathopakathanaṃ dvayoḥ ||10
nṛpāṇāṃ tad vacaḥ śrutvā tān āha madhusūdanaḥ
pucchatāmuṃ muniṃ śāntaṃ kathopakathanādṛtāḥ ||11
iti kalker vaco bhūyaḥ śrutvā te nṛpasattamāḥ
anantam āhuḥ praṇatāḥ praśnapāratitīrṣavaḥ ||12
rājāna ūcuḥ |
mune kim atra kathanaṃ kalkinā dharmavarmaṇā
durbodhaṃḥ kena vā jātaṃ tattvaṃ varṇaya naḥ prabho ||13
munir uvāca |
purikāyāṃ puri purā pitā me vedapāragaḥ
vidrumo nāma dharmajñaḥ khyātaḥ parahite rataḥ ||14
somā mama vibho mātā patidharmaparāyaṇā
tayorvayaḥ pariṇatau kāle ṣaṇdhākṛtis tv aham ||15
saṃjātaḥ śokadaḥ pitrorlokānāṃ ninditākṛtiḥ
māmālokya pitā klībaṃ duḥkhaśokabhayākulaḥ ||16
tyaktvā gṛhaṃ śivavanaṃ gatvā tuṣṭāva śaṅkaram
saṃpūjyeśaṃ vidhānena dhūpadīpānulepanaiḥ ||17
vidruma uvāca |
śivaṃ śāntaṃ sarvalokaikanāthaṃ bhūtāvāsaṃ vāsukīkaṇthabhūṣam |
jaṭājūṭābaddhagaṅgātaraṅgaṃ vande sāndrānandasandohadakṣam ||18
ity ādi bahubhiḥ stotraiḥ stutaḥ sa śivadaḥ śivaḥ
vṛṣārūḍhaḥ prasannātmā pitaraṃ prāha me vṛṇu ||19
vidrumo me pitā prāha mat puṃstvaṃ tāpatāpitaḥ
hasañ chivo dadau puṃstvaṃ pārvatyā pratimoditaḥ ||20
mama puṃstvaṃ varaṃ labdhvā pitāyātaḥ punar gṛham
puruṣaṃ māṃ samālokya saharṣaḥ priyayā saha ||21
tataḥ pravayasau tau tu pitarau dvādaśābdake
vivāhaṃ me kārayitvā bandhubhir mudamāpatuḥ ||22
yajñarātasutāṃ patnīṃ māninīṃ rūpaśālinīm
prāpyāhaṃ parituṣṭātmā gṛhasthaḥ strīvaśo’bhavam ||23
tataḥ katipaye kāle pitarau me mṛtau nṛpāḥ
pāralaukikakāryāṇi suhṛdbhir brāhmaṇair vṛtaḥ ||24
tayoḥ kṛtvā vidhānena bhojayitvā dvijān bahūn
pitror viyogatapto’haṃ viṣṇusevāparo’bhavam ||25
tuṣto harir me bhagavāñ japapūjādikarmabhiḥ
svapne mām āha māyeyaṃ snehamohavinirmitā ||26
ayaṃ piteyaṃ mateti mamatākulactasām
śokaduḥkhabhayodvegajarāmṛtyuvidhāyikā ||27
śrutveti vacanaṃ viṣnoḥ prativādārtham udyatam
māmālakṣyāntarhitaḥ sa vinidro’haṃ tato’bhavam ||28
tatraiva dakṣiṇe pārśve nirmāyāśramam uttamam
sabhāryaḥ sānugāmātyaḥ karomi harisevanam ||30
māyāsaṃdarśanākāṅkṣī harisadmani saṃsthitaḥ
gāyan nṛtyañ japan nāma cintayac chamanāpaham ||31
evaṃ vṛtte dvādaśābde dvādaśyāṃ pāraṇādine
snātukāmaḥ samudre’haṃ bandhubhiḥ sahito gataḥ ||32
tatra magnaṃ jalanidhau laharīlolasaṃkule
samutthātum aśaktaṃ māṃ pratudanti jalecarāḥ ||33
nimajjanonmajjanena vyākulīkṛtacetasam
jalahillolamilanadalitāṅgam acetanam ||34
jaladher dakṣiṇe kūle patitaṃ pavane’ritam
māṃ tatra patitaṃ dṛṣṭvā vṛddhaśarmā dvijottamaḥ ||35
sandhyāmupāsya saghṛṇaḥ svapuraṃ māṃ samānayat
sa vṛddhaśarmā dharmātmā putradāradhanānvitaḥ
kṛtvārugṇan tu māṃ tatra putravat paryapālayat ||36
ahan tu tatra dīnātmā digdeśābhijña eva na
dampatī tau svapitarau matvā tatrāvasaṃ nṛpāḥ ||37
sa māṃ vijñāya bahudhā vedadharmeṣv anuṣṭhitam
pradadau svāṃ duhitaraṃ vivāhe vinayānvitaḥ ||38
labdhvā cāmīkarākārāṃ rūpaśīlaguṇānvitām
nāmnā cārumatīṃ tatra māninīṃ vismito’bhavam ||39
tayāhaṃ parituṣṭātmā nānābhogasuḥānvitaḥ
janayitvā pañca putrān saṃmadenāvṛto’bhavam ||40
jayaś ca vijayaś caiva kamalo vimalas tathā
budha ity ādayaḥ pañca viditās tanayā mama ||41
svajanair bandhubhiḥ putrair dhanair nānāvidhair aham
viditaḥ pūjito loke devair indro yathā divi ||42
budhasya jyeṣṭhaputrasya vivāhārthaṃ samudyatam
dṛṣṭvā dvijavaras tuṣto dharmasāro nijāṃ sutām ||43
ditsuḥ karmāṇi vedajñaś cakārābhyudayāny api
vādyair gītaiś ca nṛtyaiś ca strīgaṇaiḥ svarṇabhūṣitaiḥ ||44
ahañ ca putrābhyudaye pitṛdevarṣitarpaṇam
kartuṃ samudravelāyāṃ praviṣtaḥ paramādarāt ||45
velālolāyitatanur jalād utthāya sattvaraḥ
tīre sakhīn snānasandhyāparānvīkṣyāham unmanāḥ ||46
sadyaḥ samabhavaṃ bhūpāḥ dvādaśyāṃ pāraṇādṛtān
puruṣottamasaṃvāsān viṣṇusevārthamudyatān ||47
te’pi māmagrataḥ kṛtvā tad rūpavayasāṃ nidhim
vismayāviṣṭamanasaṃ dṛṣṭvā mām abhruvañ janāḥ ||48
ananta viṣṇubhakto’si jale kiṃ dṛṣṭavān iha
sthale vā vyagramanasaṃ lakṣayāmaḥ kathaṃ tava ||49
pāraṇaṃ kuru tad brūhi tyaktvā vismayamātmanaḥ
tān abruvam ahaṃ naiva kiñcid dṛṣtaṃ śrutaṃ janāḥ ||50
kāmātmā tat kṛpaṇadhīr māyāsandarśanādṛtaḥ
tayā harer māyayāhaṃ mūḍho vyākulitendriyaḥ ||51
na śarma vedmi kutrāpi snehamohavaśaṃ gataḥ
ātmano vismṛtir iyaṃ ko veda viditāṃ tu tām ||52
iti bhāryādhanāgāraputrodvāhānuraktadhīḥ
ananto’haṃ dīnamanā na jāne svāpasammitam ||53
māṃ vīkṣya māninī bhāryā vivaśaṃ mūḍhavatsthitam
krandantī kim aho’kasmād ālapantī mamāntike ||54
iha tāṃ vīkṣya tāṃs tatra smṛtvā kātaramānasam
haṃso’py eko bodhayitum āgato māṃ saduktibhiḥ ||55
dhīro viditasarvārthaḥ pūrṇaḥ paramadharmavit ||56
sūryākāraṃ sattvasāraṃ praśāntaṃ dāntaṃ śuddhaṃ lokaśokakṣayiṣṇum
mamāgre taṃ pūjayitvā madaṅgāḥ papracchuste mac chubhadhyānakāmāḥ ||57
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye dvitīyāṃśe anantamāyādarśanaṃ nāma caturtho’dhyāyaḥ ||

pañcamo’dhyāyaḥ | 

sūta uvāca |
upaviṣte tadā haṃse bhikṣāṃ kṛtvā yathocitām
tataḥ prāhur anantasya śarīrārogyakāmyayā ||1
haṃsas teṣāṃ mataṃ jñātvā prāha māṃ purataḥ sthitam
tava cārumatī bhāryā putrāḥ pañca budhādayaḥ ||2
dhanaratnānvitaṃ sadma sambādhaṃ saudhasaṃkulam
tyaktvā kadāgato’sīha putrodvāhadine na tu ||3
samudratīrasañcāraḥ purād dharmajanādṛtaḥ
nimantṛya māmihāyātaḥ śokasaṃvignamānasaḥ ||4
tvañ ca saptati varṣīyas tatra dṛṣto mayā prabho
triṃśad varṣīyavat kasmād iti me saṃbhramo mahān ||5
iyaṃ bhāryā sahāyā te na tatrālokitā kvacit
ahaṃ vā kva kutas tasmāt kathaṃ vā kena kāśitaḥ ||6
sa eva vā na vāpi tvaṃ nāhaṃ vā bhikṣur eva saḥ
āvayor iha saṃyogaś cendrajāla yvābhavat ||7
tvaṃ grahasthaḥ svadharmajño bhikṣuko’haṃ parātmakaḥ
āvayor iha saṃvādo bālakonmattayor iva ||8
tasmād īśasya māyeyaṃ trijaganmohakāriṇī
jñānāprāpyādvātalabhyā manye’ham iti bo dvija ||9
iti bhikṣuḥ samāśrāvya yad anyat prāha vismitaḥ
mārkaṇdeya mahābhāga bhaviṣyaṃ kathayāmi te ||10
pralaye yā tvayā dṛṣṭā puruṣasyodarāmbhasi
sā māyā mohajanikā panthānaṃ gaṇikā yathā ||11
tamo hy anantasantāpā nodanodyatam akṣarī
yayedam akhilaṃ lokam āvṛtyāvasthayāsthitam ||12
laye līne trijagati brahmatanmātratāṃ gataḥ
nirupādhau nirāloke sisṛkṣur abhavat paraḥ ||13
brahmaṇyapi dvidhābhūto puruṣaprakṛtī svayā
bhāsā saṃjanayām āsa mahāntaṃ kālayogataḥ ||14
kālasvabhāvakarmātmā so’haṅkāras tato’bhavat
trivṛdviṣṇuśivabrahmamayaḥ saṃsārakāraṇam ||15
tanmātrāṇi tataḥ pañca jajñire guṇavanti ca
mahābhūtāny api tataḥ prakṛtau brahmasaṃśrayāt ||16
jātā devāsuranarā ye cānye jīvajātayaḥ
brahmāṇḍabhāṇḍasaṃbhārajanmanāśakriyātmikāḥ ||17
māyayā māyayā jīvapuruṣaḥ paramātmanaḥ
saṃsāraśaraṇavyagro na vedātmagatiṃ kvacit ||18
aho balavatī māyā brahmādyā yad vaśe sthitāḥ
gāvo yathā nasi protā guṇabaddhāḥ khagā iva ||19
tāṃ māyāṃ guṇamayyāṃ ye titīrṣanti munīśvarāḥ
sravantīṃ vāsanānakrāṃ ta evārthavido bhuvi ||20
śaunaka uvāca |
mārkaṇdeyo vaśiṣṭhaś ca vāmadevādayo’pare
śrutvā guruvaco bhūyaḥ kim āhuḥ śravaṇādṛtāḥ ||21
rājāno’nantavacanam iti śrutvā sudhopamam
kiṃ vā prāhur aho sūta bhaviṣyam iha varṇaya ||22
iti tad vaca āśrutya sūtaḥ satkṛtya taṃ punaḥ
kathayām āsa kārtsnyena kārtsnyena śokamohavighātakam ||23
sūta uvāca |
tatrānanto bhūpagaṇaiḥ pṛṣtaḥ prāha kṛtādaraḥ |
tapasā mohanidhanam indriyāṇāñ ca nigraham ||24
ananta uvāca |
ato’haṃ vanamāsādya tapaḥ kṛtvā vidhānataḥ
nendriyāṇāṃ na manaso nigraho’bhūt kadācana ||25
vane brahma dhyāyato me bhāryāputradhanādikam
viṣayañ cāntarā śaśvat saṃsmārayati me manaḥ ||26
teṣāṃ smaraṇamātreṇa duḥkhaśokabhayādayaḥ
pratudanti mama prāṇān dhāraṇādhyānanāśakāḥ ||27
tato’haṃ niścitamatir indriyāṇāñ ca ghātane
manaso nigrahas tena bhaviṣyati na saṃśayaḥ ||28
ato mām indriyāṇān ca nigrahavyagracetasam
tad adhiṣthātṛdevāś ca dṛṣṭvā māmīyurañjasā ||29
rūpiṇo mām athocus te bho’nanta iti te daśa

vibhrāntatārabhramaraṃ navakokanadaprabham
dīrghāpāṅge’kṣaṇaṃ yatra tatra kaḥ prahariṣyati ||22
vakṣojaśambhū sattārahāravyālavibhūṣitau
kandarpadarpadalanau tatra kaḥ prahariṣyati ||23
lolalīlālakavrātacakorākrāntacandrikam
mukhacandraṃ cihnahīnaṃ kas taṃ hantum ihārhati ||24
stanubhārabharākrāntanitāntakṣīṇamadhyamam
tanulomalatābandhaṃ kaḥ pumān prahariṣyati ||25
netrānandena netreṇa samāvṛtamaninditam
jaghanaṃ sughanaṃ ramyaṃ bāṇaiḥ kaḥ prahariṣyati ||26
iti kalker vacaḥ śrutvā prāhasya prāhur ādṛtāḥ
asmākaṃ tvaṃ patīn haṃsi tena naṣṭā vayaṃ vibho
hantuṃ gatānām astrāṇi karāṇy evāgatān yuta ||27
khaḍgaśaktidhanurbāṇaśūlatomarayaṣṭayaḥ
tāḥ prāhuḥ purato mūrtāḥ kārtasvaravibhūṣaṇāḥ ||28
śastrāṇy ūcuḥ |
yamāsādya vayaṃ nāryo hiṃsayāmaḥ svatejasā
tam ātmāntaṃ sarvamayaṃ jānīta kṛtaniścayāḥ ||29
tam īśam ātmanā nāryaḥ carāmo yad anujñayā
yat kṛtā nāmarūpādibhedena viditā vayam ||30
rūpagandharasasparśaśabdādyā bhūtapañcakāḥ
caranti yad adhiṣthānāt so’yaṃ kalkiḥ parātmakaḥ ||31
kālasvabhāvasaṃskāranāmādyā prakṛtiḥ parā
yasyecchayā sṛjaty aṇdaṃ mahāhaṅkārakādikān ||32
yan māyayā jagadyātrā sargasthityantasaṃjñitā
ya evādyaḥ sa evānte tasyāyaḥ so’yam īśvaraḥ ||33
asau patir me bhāryāham asya putrāptabāndhavāḥ
svapnopamāstu tanniṣthā vividhāś caindrajālavat ||34
snehamohanibandhānāṃ yātāyātadṛśāṃ matam
na kalkisevināṃ rāgadveṣavidveṣakāriṇām ||35
kutaḥ kālaḥ kuto mṛtyuḥ kva yamaḥ kvāsti devatāḥ
sa eva kalkir bhagavān māyayā bahulīkṛtaḥ ||36
na śastrāṇi vayaṃ nāryaḥ saṃprahāryā na ca kvacit
śastraprahartṛbhedo’yam avivekaḥ parātmanaḥ ||37
kalkidāsasyāpi vayaṃ hantuṃ nārhāḥ kathādbhutam
haniṣyāmo daityapateḥ prahlādasya yathā harim ||38
ity astrāṇāṃ vacaḥ śrutvā striyo vismitamānasāḥ
snehamohavinirmuktās taṃ kalkiṃ śaraṇaṃ yayuḥ ||39
tāḥ samālokya padmeśaḥ praṇatā jñānaniṣṭhayā
provāca prahasan bhaktiyogaṃ kalmaṣanāśanam ||40
karmayogañ cātmaniṣthaṃ jñānayogaṃ bhidhāśrayam
naiṣkarmyalakṣaṇaṃ tāsāṃ kathayāmāsa mādhavaḥ ||41
tāḥ striyaḥ kalkigaditajñānena vijitendriyāḥ
bhaktyā paramavāpus tad yogināṃ durlabhaṃ padam ||42
dattvā mokṣaṃ mlecchabauddhapriyāṇāṃ kṛtvā yuddhaṃ bhairavaṃ bhīmakarmā |
hatvā bauddhān mlecchasaṃghāś ca kalkis teṣāṃ jyotiḥ sthānam āpūrya reje ||43
ye śṛṇvanti vadanti bauddhanidhanaṃ mlecchakṣayaṃ sādarāl
lokāḥ śokaharaṃ sadā śubhakaraṃ bhaktipradaṃ mādhave |
teṣam eva punar na janmamaraṇaṃ sarvārthasampatkaraṃ
māyāmohavināśanaṃ pratidinaṃ saṃsāratāpacchidam ||44
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe mlecchanidhanaṃ nāma prathamo’dhyāyaḥ ||1

<3.2>

dvitīyo’dhyāyaḥ |

tato bauddhan mlecchagaṇān vijitya saha sainikaiḥ
dhanānyādāya ratnāni kīkaṭāt punar āvrajat ||1
kalkiḥ paramatejasvī dharmāṇāṃ parirakṣakaḥ
cakratīrthaṃ samāgatya snānaṃ vidhivadācarat ||2
bhrātṛbhir lokapālābhair bahubhiḥ svajanair vṛtaḥ
samāyātān munīṃs tatra dadṛśe dīnamānasān ||3
samudbhiyāgatāṃs tatra paripāhi jagatpate
ity uktavanto bahudhā ye tān āha hariḥ paraḥ ||4
vālakhilyādikānalpakāyāñcīrajaṭādharān
vinayāvanataḥ kalkis tān āha kṛpaṇān bhayayāt ||5
kasmād yūyaṃ samāyātāḥ kena vā bhīṣitā bata 
tam ahaṃ nihaniṣyāmi yadi vā syat purandaraḥ ||6
ity āśrutya kalkivākyaṃ tenollāsitamānasāḥ
jagaduḥ puṇḍarīkākṣaṃ nikumbhaduhituḥ kathāḥ ||7
munaya ūcuḥ |
śṛṇu viṣṇuyaśaḥputra kumbhakarṇātmajātmajā
kuthodarīti vikhyātā gaganārdhasamutthitā ||8
kālakañjasya mahiṣī vikañjajananī ca sā
himālaye śiraḥ kṛtvā pādau ca niṣadhācale
śete stanaṃ pāyayantī vikañjaṃ prasnutastanī ||9
tasyā niśvāsavātena vivaśā vayamāgatāḥ
daivenaiva samānītāḥ saṃprāptāstvatpadāspadam
munayo rakṣaṇīyās te rakṣaḥ suca vipatsu ca ||10
iti teṣāṃ vacaḥ śrutvā kalkiḥ parapurañjayaḥ
senāganaiḥ parivṛto jagāma himavadgirim ||11
upatyakāṃ samāsādya niśāmekāṃ nināya saḥ
prātarjigamiṣuḥ sainyair dadṛśe kṣīranimnagām ||12
śaṃkhendudhavalākārāṃ phenilāṃ bṛhatīṃ drutam
calantīṃ vikṣyate sarve stambhitā vismayānvitāḥ ||13
senāgaṇagajāśvādirathayodhaiḥ samāvṛtaḥ
kalkis tu bhagavāṃs tatra jñātārtho’pi munīśvarān ||14
papraccha kā nadī ceyaṃ kathaṃ dugdhavahābhavat
te kalkes tu vacaḥ śrutvā munayaḥ prāhur ādarāt ||15
śruṇu kalke payasvatyāḥ prabhavaṃ himavadgirau
samāyātā kuthodaryāḥ stanaprasravaṇād iha ||16
ghaṭikāsaptakaiścānyā payo yāsyati vegitam
hīnasārā taṭākārā bhaviṣyati mahāmate ||17
iti śrutvā munīnān tu vacanaṃ sainikaiḥ saha
aho kim asyā rākṣasyāḥ stanādekā tviyaṃ nadī ||18
ekaṃ stanaṃ pāyayati vikañjaṃ putram ādarāt
na jāne’syāḥ śarīrasya pramāṇaṃ katidhā bhavet ||19
balaṃ vāsyā niśācaryā ity ūcur vismayānvitāḥ
kalkiḥ parātmā sannahya senābhiḥ sahasā yayau ||20
munidarśitamārgeṇa yatrāste sā niśācarī
putraṃ stanaṃ pāyayantī girimūrdhni ghanopamā ||21
śvāsavātātivātena dūrakṣiptavanadvipāḥ
yasyāḥ karṇabilāvāse prasuptāḥ siṃhasaṃkulāḥ ||22
putrapauttraiḥ parivṛtā girigahvaravibhramāḥ
keśamūlam upālambya hariṇāḥ śerate ciram ||23
yūkā iva na ca vyagrā lubdhajātaṅkayā bhṛśam
tām ālokya girer mūrdhni girivatparamādbhutām ||24 
kalkiḥ kamalapatrākṣaḥ sarvāṃs tān āha sainikān
bhayodvignān buddhihīnāṃs tyaktodyamaparicchadān ||25
giridurge vahnidurgaṃ kṛtvā tiṣṭhantu māmakāḥ
gajāśvarathayodhā ye samāyāntu mayā saha ||26
ahaṃ svalpena sānyena yāmy asyāḥ saṃmukhaṃ śanaiḥ
prahartuṃ bāṇasandohaiḥ khaḍgaśaktiparaśvadhaiḥ ||27
ity uktvāsthāpya paścāt tān bāṇais tām ahanad balī
sā krudhotthāya sahasā nanarda paramādbhutam ||28
tena nādena mahātā vitrastās cābhavañ janāḥ
nipetuḥ sainikāḥ sarve mūrcchitā dharaṇītale ||29
sā rathāṃś ca gajāṃś cāpi vivṛtāsyā bhayānakā
jaghāsa praśvāsavātāyḥ samānīya kuthodarī ||30
senāgaṇās tad udaraṃ praviṣtāḥ kalkinā saha
yatharkṣamukhavātena praviśanti pipīlikāḥ ||31
tad dṛṣṭvā devagandharvā hāhākāraṃ pracakrire
tatrasthā munayaḥ śepurjepuś cānye maharṣayaḥ ||32
nipetur anye duḥkhartā brāhmaṇā brahmavādinaḥ 
ruruduḥ śiṣṭayodhā ye jahṛṣus tan niśācarāḥ ||33 
jagatāṃ kadanaṃ dṛṣṭvā sasmārātmānamātmanā
kalkiḥ kamalapatrākṣaḥ surārātiniṣūdanaḥ ||34
bāṇāgniṃ cailacarmabhyāṃ karmaṇe yānadārubhiḥ
prajvālyodaramadhye tu karavālaṃ samādade ||35
tena khaḍgena mahatā kukṣiṃ nirbhidya bandhubhiḥ
balibhir bhrātṛbhir vāhair vṛtaḥ śastrāstrapāṇibhiḥ ||36
bahir babhūva sarveśaḥ kalkiḥ kalkavināśanaḥ
sahasrākṣo yathā vṛtrakukṣiṃ dambholineminā ||37
yoniraṃdhrād gajarathās turagāś cābhavan bahiḥ
nāsikākarṇavivarāt ke’pi tasyā vinirgatāḥ ||38
te nirgatās tatas tasyāḥ sainikā rudhirokṣitāḥ
tāṃ vivyadur nikṣipantīṃ tarasā caraṇau karāv ||39 
mamāra sā bhinnadehā bhinnakukṣiśirodharā 
nādayantī diśo dyokhaṃ cūrṇayantī ca parvatān ||40
karañjo’pi tathā vīkṣya mātaraṃ kātaro’bhavat 
sa vikañjaḥ krudhādhāvat senāmadhye nirāyudhaḥ ||41
gajamālākulo vakṣovājirājivibhūṣaṇaḥ 
mahāsarpakṛtoṣnīṣaḥ kesarīmudritāṅguliḥ ||42
mamarda kalkisenāṃ tāṃ mātur vyasanakarṣitaḥ 
sa kalkis taṃ brāhmam astraṃ rāmadattaṃ jighāṃsayā ||43
dhanuṣā pañcavarsīyaṃ rākṣasaṃ śastram ādade
tenāstreṇa śiras tasya cchitvā bhūmāvapātayat ||44
rudhirāktaṃ dhātucitraṃ giriśṛṅgam ivādbhutam
saputrāṃ rākṣasīṃ hatvā munīnāṃ vacanād vibhuḥ ||45
gaṅgātīre haridvāre nivāsaṃ samakalpayat 
devānāṃ kusumāsārair munistotraiḥ supūjitaḥ ||46
nināya tāṃ niśāṃ tatra kalkiḥ parijanāvṛtaḥ
prātar dadarśa gaṅgāyās tīre munigaṇān bahūn |
tasyāḥ snānavyājaviṣṇor ātmano darśanākulān ||47
haridvāre gaṅgātaṭanikaṭapiṇdārakavane
vasantaṃ śrīmantaṃ nijagaṇavṛtaṃ taṃ munigaṇāḥ
stavaiḥ stutvā stutvā vidhivaduditair jahnutanayāṃ
prapaśyantaṃ kalkiṃ munijanagaṇā draṣṭum agaman ||48
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe kuthodarīvadhānantaraṃ munidarśanaṃ nāma dvitīyo’dhyāyaḥ ||2

<3.3>

tṛtīyo’dhyāyaḥ | 

sūta uvāca |
susvāgatān munīn dṛṣṭvā kalkiḥ paramadharmavit
pūjayitvā ca vidhivat sukhāsīnānuvāca tān ||1 
kalkir uvāca |
ke yūyaṃ sūryasaṅkāśā mama bhāgyādupasthitāḥ
tīrthāṭanotsukā lokatrayāṇām upakārakāḥ ||2
vayaṃ loke puṇyavanto bhagyavanto yaśasvinaḥ
yataḥ kṛpākaṭākṣeṇa yuṣmābhir avalokitāḥ ||3
tatas te vāmadevo’trir vasiṣṭho gālavo bhṛguḥ
parāśaro nārado’śvatthāmā rāmaḥ kṛpas tritaḥ ||4
durvāsā devalaḥ kaṇvo vedapramitir aṅgirāḥ |
ete cānye ca bahavo munayaḥ śaṃsitavratāḥ ||5
kṛtvāgre marudevāpī candrasūryakulodbhavau
rājānau tau mahāvīryau tapasyābhiratau ciram ||6
ūcuḥ prahṛṣṭamanasaḥ kalkiṃ kalkavināśanam
mahodadhes tīragataṃ viṣṇuṃ suragaṇā yathā ||7 
munaya ūcuḥ |
jayāśeṣajagannātha viditākhilamānasa
sṛṣṭisthitilayādhyakṣa paramātman prasīda naḥ ||8
kālakarmaguṇāvāsa prasāritanijakriya
brahmādinutapādābja padmānātha prasīda naḥ ||9
iti teṣāṃ vacaḥ śrutvā kalkiḥ prāha jagatpatiḥ
kāvetau bhavatāmagre mahāsattvau tapasvināu ||10
kathamatrāgatau stutvā gaṅgāṃ muditamānasau
kā vā stutis tu jāhnavyā yuvayornāmanīcake ||11
tayor maruḥ pramuditaḥ kṛtāñjalipuṭaḥ kṛtī
ādāv uvāca vinayī nijavaṃśānukīrtanam ||12
marur uvāca |
sarvaṃ vetsi parātmāpi antaryāmin hṛdi sthitaḥ
tavājñayā sarvam etat kathayāmi śṛṇu prabho ||13
tava nābher abhūd brahmā marīcis tat suto’bhavat
tato manus tat suto’bhūd īkṣvākuḥ satyavikramaḥ ||14
yuvanāśva iti khyāto māndhātā tat suto’bhavat
purukutsas tat suto’bhūd anaraṇyo mahāmatiḥ ||15
trasaddasyuḥ pitā tasmād dharyaśvas tṛy aruṇas tataḥ
triśaṅkus tat suto dhīmān hariś candraḥ pratāpavān ||16
haritas tat sutas tasmād bharukas tat suto vṛkaḥ
tat sutaḥ sagaras tasmād asamañjās tato’ṃśumān ||17
tato dilīpas tat putro bhagīratha iti smṛtaḥ
yenānītā jāhnavīyaṃ khyātā bhagīrathī bhuvi |
stutā nutā pūjite’yaṃ tava pādam usadbhavā ||18
bhagīrathāt sutas tasmān nābhas tasmād abhūd balī
sindhudvīpasutas tasmād āyutāyus tato’bhavat ||19
rtuparṇas tat suto’bhūt sudāsas tat suto’bhavat
saudāsas tat suto dhīmān aśmakas tat suto mataḥ ||20
mūlakāt sa dasarathas tasmād eḍaviḍas tataḥ
rājā viśvasahas tasmāt khaṭvāṅgo dīrghabāhukaḥ ||21
tato raghur ajas tasmāt suto daśarathaḥ kṛtī
tasmād rāmo hariḥ sākṣād āvirbhūto jagatpatiḥ ||22
rāmāvatāram ākarṇya kalkiḥ paramaharṣitaḥ
maruṃ prāha vistareṇa śrīrāmacaritaṃ vada ||23
sītāpateḥ karma vaktuṃ kaḥ samartho’sti bhūtale
śeṣaḥ sahasravadanair api lālāyito bhavet ||24
tathāpi śemuṣī me’sti varṇayāmi tavājñayā
rāmasya caritaṃ puṇyaṃ pāpatāpapramocanam ||25
ajādivibudhārthito’jani caturbhir aṃśauḥ kule
raver ajasutād ajo jagati yātudhānakṣayaḥ |
śiśuḥ kuśikajādhvarakṣayakarakṣayo yo balād 
balī lalitakandharo jayati jānakīvallabhaḥ ||26
muner anusahānujo nikhilaśastravidyātigo 
gayāvativanaprabho janakarājarājatsabhām |
vidhāya janamohanadyutimatīva kāmadruhaḥ
pracaṇakaracaṇdimā bhavanabhañjane janmanaḥ ||27
tamaḥ pratimatejasaṃ daśarathātmajaṃ sānujaṃ
muner anu yathā vidheḥ śaśivadādidevaṃ param |
nirīkṣya janako mudā kṣitisutāpatiṃ saṃmataṃ
nijocitapaṇakṣamaṃ manasi bhartsayann āyayau ||28
sa bhūpaparipūjito janakaje’kṣitair arcitaḥ
karālakaṭhinaṃ dhanuḥ karasaroruhe saṃhitaṃ |
vibhajya balavad dṛḍhaṃ jaya raghūdvahe’ty uccakair
dhvaniṃ trijagatīgataṃ parividhāya rāmo babhau ||29
tato janakabhūpatir daśarathātmajebhyo dadau
catasra uṣatīr mudā varacaturbhya udvāhane
svalaṃkṛtanijātmajāḥ pathi tato balaṃ bhārgavaś |
cakāra urarīnijaṃ raghupatau maho’graṃ tyajan ||30
tataḥ svapuram āgato daśarathas tu sītāpatiṃ
nṛpaṃ sacivasaṃyuto nijavicitrasiṃhāsane |
vidhātum amalaprabhaṃ parijanaiḥ kṛyākāribhiḥ
samudyatamatiṃ tadā drutam avārayat kekayī ||31
tato gurunideśato janakarājakanyāyutaḥ
prayāṇamakarotsudhīryadanugaḥ sumitrāsutaḥ |
vanaṃ nijagaṇaṃ tyajan guhagṛhe vasannādarāt
vsṛjya nṛpalāñ chanaṃ raghupatir jaṭācīrabhṛt ||32
priyānujayutas tato munimato vane pūjitaḥ
sa pañcavaṭikāśrame bharatam āturaṃ saṃgatam |
nivārya maraṇaṃ pituḥ samvadhārya duḥkhāturas
tapovanagato’vasad raghupatis tatas tāḥ samāḥ ||33
daśānanasahodarāṃ viṣamabāṇavedhāturāṃ
samīkṣya vararūpiṇīṃ prahasatīṃ satīṃ sundarīm |
nijāśrayam abhīpsatīṃ janakajāpatir lakṣmaṇāt
karālakaravālataḥ samakarodvirūpāṃ tataḥ ||34
samāpya pathi dānavaṃ kharaśaraiḥ śanair nāśayan
caturdaśasahasrakaṃ samahanat kharaṃ sānugam |
daśānanavaśānugaṃ kanakacārucañcan mṛgaṃ
priyāpriyakaro vane samavadhīd balād rākṣasam ||35
tato daśamukhas tvaraṃs tam abhivīkṣya rāmaṃ ruṣā 
vrajantam anulakṣmaṇaṃ janakajā jahārāśrame |
tato raghupatiḥ priyāṃ dalakuṭīrasaṃsthāpitāṃ 
na vīkṣya tu vimūrchito bahu vilapya sīteti tām ||36
vane nijagaṇāśrame nagatale jale palvale 
vicitya patitaṃ khagaṃ pathi dadarśa saumitriṇā  |
jaṭāyuvacanāt tato daśamukhāhṛtāṃ jānakīṃ
vivicya kṛtavān mṛte pitari vahnikṛtyaṃ prabhuḥ ||37
privāvirahakātaro’nujapuraḥ saro rāghavo dhanurdharadhurandharo haribalaṃ navālāpinam |
dadarśa ṛṣabhācalādravijavālirājānujapriyaṃ pavananandanaṃ pariṇataṃ hitaṃ preṣitam ||38
tatas tad uditam mataṃ pavanaputrasugrīvayos
tṛṇādhipatibhedanaṃ nijanṛpāsanasthāpitam |
vivicya vyavasāyakair nijasakhāpriyaṃ vālinaṃ
nihatya haribhūpatiṃ nijasakhaṃ sa rāmo’karot ||39
athottaram imāṃ harir janakajāṃ samanveṣayan
jaṭāyusahajoditairjalanidhiṃ taranvāyujaḥ |
dasananapuraṃ viśañ janakajāṃ samānandayann
aśokavanikāśrame raghupatiṃ punaḥ prāyayāv ||40
tato hanumatā balād amitarakṣasāṃ nāśanaṃ
jvalajjvalanasaṃkulajvalitadagdhalaṅkāpuram |
vivicya raghunāyako jalanidhiṃ ruṣā śoṣayan 
babandha hariyūthapaiḥ parivṛto nagair īśvaraḥ ||
babhañja purapattanaṃ vividhasargadurgakṣamaṃ
niśācarapateḥ krudhā raghupatiḥ kṛtī sadgatiḥ ||41
tato’nujayuto yudhi prabalacaṇḍakodaṇḍabhṛt
śaraiḥ kharataraiḥ kṛdhā gajarathāśvahaṃsākule |
karālakaravālataḥ prabalakālajihvāgrato
nihatya vararākṣasān narapatir babhau sānugaḥ ||42
tato’tibalavān arair girimahīruhodyatkaraiḥ 
karālataratāḍanair janakajāruṣā nāśitān |
nijaghnuramarārdanānatibalandaśāsyānugān
nalāṅgadaharīśvarāśugasutarkṣarājādayaḥ ||43
tato’tibalalakṣmaṇas tridasanāthasatruṃraṇe 
jaghāna ghanaghoṣaṇānugagaṇair asṛk prāśanaiḥ |
prahasta vikaṭādikānapi niśācarān saṅgatān
nikumbhamakarākṣakānniśitakhaḍgapātaiḥ krudhā ||44
tato daśamukho raṇe gajarathāśvapattīśvarair
alaṅghyagaṇakoṭibhiḥ parivṛto yuyodhāyudhaiḥ |
kapīśvaracamūpateḥ patimanantadivyāyudhaṃ 
raghūdvahamaninditaṃ sapadi saṅgato durjayaḥ ||45
daśānanam ariṃtato vidhivarasmayāvardhitaṃ
mahābalaparākramaṃ girim ivācalaṃ saṃyuge |
jaghāna raghunāyako niśitasāyakair uddhataṃ
niśācaracamūpatiṃ prabalakumbhakarṇaṃ tataḥ ||46
tayoḥ kharataraiḥ śarair gaganamacchamācchāditaṃ
babhau ghanaghaṭāsamaṃ mukharamattaḍidvahnibhiḥ |
dhanurguṇamahāśanidhvanibhir āvṛtaṃ bhūtalaṃ
bhayaṅkaranirantaraṃ raghupateś ca rakṣaḥpateḥ ||47
tato dharaṇijāruṣā vividharāmabāṇaujasā
papāta bhuvi rāvaṇas tridaśanāthavidrāvaṇaḥ |
tato’tikutukī harir jvalanarakṣitāṃ jānakīṃ
samarpya raghupuṅgave nijapurīṃ yayau harṣitaḥ ||48
purandarakathādaraḥ sapadi tatra rakṣaḥpatim
bibhīṣaṇam abhīṣaṇaṃ samakarot tato rāghavaḥ ||49
harīśvaragaṇāvṛto’vanisutāyutaḥ sānujo
rathe śivasakherite suvimale lasatpuṣpake |
munīśvaragaṇārcito raghupatis tv ayodhyāṃ yayau
vivicya munilāñchanaṃ guhagṛhe’tisakhyaṃ smaran ||50
tato nijagaṇāvṛto bharatam āturaṃ sāntvayan
svamātṛgaṇavākyataḥ pitṛnijāsane bhūpatiḥ |
vasiṣṭhamunipuṅgavāyḥ kṛtanijābhiṣeko vibhuḥ
samastajanapālakaḥ surapatir yathā saṃbabhau ||51
narā bahudhanākarā dvijavarās tapas tatparāḥ
svadharmakṛtaniścayāḥ svajanasaṅgatā nirbhayāḥ |
ghanāḥ subahuvarṣiṇo vasumatī sadā harṣitā
bhavaty atibale nṛpe raghupatāv abhūt sajjagat ||52
gatāyutasamāḥ priyair nijaguṇaiḥ prajā rañjayan
nijāṃ raghupatiḥ priyāṃ nijamanobhavair mohayan |
munīndragaṇasaṃyuto’py ayajad ādidevān makhair
dhanair vipuladakṣiṇair atulavājimeghais tribhiḥ ||53
tataḥ kimapi kāraṇaṃ manasi bhāvayan bhūpatir
jahau janakajāṃ vane raghuvaras tadā nirghṛṇaḥ |
tato nijamataṃ smaran samanayat pracetaḥ suto
nijāśramam udāradhī raghupateḥ priyāṃ duḥkhitām ||54
tataḥ kuśalavau sutau prasuṣuve dharitrīsutā
mahābalaparākramau raghupater yaśogāyanau |
sa tām api sutānvitāṃ munivaras tu rāmāntike
samarpayad aninditāṃ suravaraiḥ sadā vanditām ||55
tato raghupatis tu tāṃ sutayutāṃ rudantīṃ puro
jagāda dahane punaḥ praviśa śodhanāyātmanaḥ |
itīritam avekṣya sā raghupateḥ padābje natā
viveśa jananīyutā maṇigaṇojjvalaṃ bhūtalam ||56
nirīkṣya raghunāyako janakajāprayāṇaṃ smaran
vasiṣṭhaguruyogato’nujayuto’gamat svaṃ padam |
puraḥ sthitajanaiḥ suakaiḥ paśubhir īśvaraḥ saṃspṛśan
mudā sarayujīvanaṃ rathavaraiḥ parīto vibhuḥ ||57 
ye śṛṇvanti raghūdvahasya caritaṃ karnāmṛtaṃ sādarāt
saṃsārārṇavaśoṣaṇañ ca paṭhatām āmodadaṃ mokṣadam |
rogāṇām iha śāntaye dhanajanasvargādisampattaye
vaṃśānām api vṛddhaye prabhavati śrīśaḥ pareśaḥ prabhuḥ ||58
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe sūryavaṃśānuvarṇane śrīrāmacandracaritaṃ nāma tṛtīyo’dhyāyaḥ ||3

<3.4>

caturtho’dhyāyaḥ |

rāmāt kuso’bhūd atithis tato’bhūn niṣadhān nabhaḥ
tasmād abhūt puṇḍarīkaḥ kṣemadhānv’bhavat tataḥ ||1
devānīkas tato hīnaḥ pāripātro’tha hīnataḥ
balāhakas tato’rkaś ca rajanābhas tato’bhavat ||2
khāṇād vidhṛtas tasmād dhiraṇyanābhasaṅitaḥ 
tataḥ puṣpād dhruvas tasmāt syandano’thāgnivarṇakaḥ ||3
tasmāc chīghro’bhavat putraḥ pitā me’tulavikramaḥ
tasmān maruṃ māṃ ke’pīha budhañ cāpi sumitrakam ||4
kalāpagrāmam āsādya viddhi sattapasi sthitam
tavāvatāraṃ vijñāya vyāsāt satyavatīsutāt ||5
pratīkṣya kālaṃ lakṣābdaṃ kaleḥ prāptastavāntikam |
janmakoṭyaṃhasāṃ rāśer nāśanaṃ dharmaśāsanam |
yaśaḥkīrtikaraṃ sarvakāmapūraṃ parātmanaḥ ||6
kalkir uvāca |
jñātas tavānvayas tvañ ca sūryavaṃśasamudbhavaḥ
dvitīyaḥ ko’paraḥ śrīmānmahāpuruṣalakṣaṇaḥ ||7
iti kalkivacaḥ śrutvā devāpir madhurākṣarām
vāṇīṃ vinayasampannaḥ pravaktum upacakrame ||8
devāpir uvāca |
pralayānte nābhipadmāt tavābhūc caturānanaḥ
tad īyatanayād atreś candras tasmāt tato budhaḥ ||9
tasmāt purūrauā jajñe yayātir nahuṣas tataḥ
devayānyāṃ yayātis tu yaduṃ turvasum eva ca ||10
śarmiṣthāyāṃ tathā druhyuñ cānuṃ pūruñ ca satpate
janayām āsa bhūtādir bhūtānīva sisṛkṣayā ||11
pūror janmejayas tasmāt pracinvān abhavat tataḥ
pravīras tan manasyur vaiṃ tasmāc cābhayado’bhavat ||12
urukṣayāc ca tṛyaruṇis tato’bhūt puṣkarāruṇiḥ
bṛhatkṣetrād abhūd dhastī yan nāmnā hastināpuram ||13
ajamīḍho dvimīḍhaś ca puramīḍhas tu tat sutāḥ
ajamīḍhād abhūd ṛkṣas tasmāt saṃvaraṇāt kuruḥ ||14
kuroḥ parikṣit sudhanur jahnur niṣadha eva ca
suhotro’bhūt sudhanuṣaś cyavanāc ca tataḥ kṛtī ||15 
tato bṛhadrathas tasmāt kuśāgrād ṛṣabo’bhavat
tataḥ satyajitaḥ putraḥ puṣpavān nahuṣas tataḥ ||16
bṛhadrathānyabharyāyāṃ jarāsandhaḥ parantapaḥ
sahadevas tatas tasmāt somāpir yac chrutaśravāḥ  ||17
surathād vidūrathas tasmāt sārvabhaumo’bhavat tataḥ 
jayasenād rathānīko’bhūd yutāyuś ca kopanaḥ ||18 
tasmād devātithis tasmād dṛkṣas tasmād dilīpakaḥ
tasmāt pratīpakas tasya devāpir aham īśvara ||19
rājyaṃ śāntanave dattvā tapasy ekadhiyā ciram
kalāpagrāmam āsādya tvāṃ didṛkṣur ihāgataḥ ||20
maruṇānena munibir ebiḥ prāpya padāmbujam
tava kālakarālāsyād yāsyāmy ātmavatāṃ padam ||21 
tayor evaṃ vacaḥ śrutvā kalkiḥ kamalalocanaḥ
prahasya marudevāpi samāśvāsya samabravīt ||22
kalkir uvāca |
yuvāṃ paramadharmajñau rājānau viditāv ubhau
mad ādeśakarau bhūtvā nijarājyaṃ bhariṣyathaḥ ||23 
maro tvām abhiṣekṣyāmi nijāyodhyāpure’dhunā
hatvā mlecchān adharmiṣthān prajābhūtavihiṃsakān ||24
devāpe tava rājye tvāṃ hastināpurapattane
abhiṣekṣyāmi rājarṣe hatvā pukkasakān raṇe ||25
mathurāyām ahaṃ sthitvā hariṣyāmi tu vo bhayam
śayyākarṇān uṣṭramukhān ekajaṅghān vinodarān ||26
hatvā kṛtaṃ yugaṃ kṛtvā pālayiṣyāmy ahaṃ prajāḥ
tapoveśaṃ vrataṃ tyaktvā samāruhya rathottamam ||27
yuvāṃ śastrāstrakuśalau senāgaṇaparicchadau 
bhūtvā mahārathau loke mayā saha cariṣyathaḥ ||28
viśākhayūpabhūpālas tanayāṃ vinayānvitām
vivāhe rucirāpāṅgīṃ sundarīṃ tvāṃ pradāsyati ||29
sādho bhūpāla lokānāṃ svastaye kuru me vacaḥ 
rucirāśvasutāṃ śāntāṃ devāpe tvaṃ samudvaha ||30 
ity āśvāsakathāḥ kalkeḥ śrutvā tau munibhiḥ saha
vismayāviṣṭahṛdayau menāte harim īśvaram ||31
iti bruvaty abhayade ākāśāt sūryasannibhau |
rathau nānāmaṇivrātaghaṭitau kāmagau puraḥ |
samāyātau jvaladdivyaśastrāstraiḥ parivāritau ||32
dadṛśus te sadomadhye viśvakarmavinirmitau
bhūpā munigaṇāḥ sabhyāḥ saharṣāḥ kim itīritāḥ ||33
kalkir uvāca |
yuvām ādityasomendrayamaivaśravaṇāṅgajau
rājānau lokarakṣārtham āvirbhūtau vidantyamī ||34
kālenācchāditākārau mama saṅgādihoditau
yuvāṃ rathāv āruhatāṃ śakradattaṃ mamājñayā ||35 
evaṃ vadati viśveśe padmanāthe sanātane
devā vavarṣuḥ kusumais tuṣṭuvur munayo’grataḥ ||36 
gaṅgāvāripariklinnaśirobhūtiparāgavān
śanaiḥ parvatajāsaṅgaśivavat pavano vavau ||37 
tatrāyātaḥ pramuditatanustaptacāmīkarābho
dharmāvāsaḥ surucirajaṭācīrabhṛddaṇḍahastaḥ |
lokātīto nijatanumarun nāśitādharmasaṃghas
tejorāśiḥ sanakasadṛśo maskarī puṣkarākṣaḥ ||38
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe candravaṃśānukīrtanaṃ nāma caturtho’dhyāyaḥ ||4

<3.5>

pañcamo’dhyāyaḥ | 

śuka uvāca |
atha kalkiḥ samālokya sadasām patibhiḥ saha
samutthāya vavande taṃ pādyārghyācamanādibhiḥ ||1
vṛddhaṃ saṃveśya taṃ bhikṣuṃ sarvāśramanamaskṛtam
papraccha ko bhavānatra mama bhāgyād ihāgataḥ ||2
prāyaśo mānavā loke lokānāṃ pāraṇecchayā
caranti sarvasuhṛdaḥ pūrṇā vigatakalmaṣāḥ ||3
maskary uvāca |
ahaṃ kṛtayugaṃ śriśa tavādeśakaraṃ param
tavāvirbhāvavibhavam īkṣaṇārtham ihāgatam ||4 
nirupādhir bhavān kālaḥ so’pādhitvam upāgataḥ
kṣaṇadaṇḍalavādyaṅgair māyayā racitaṃ svayā ||5
pakṣāhorātramāsartusaṃvatsarayugādayaḥ
tavekṣayā caranty ete manavaś ca caturdaśa ||6
svāyambhuvas tu prathamas tataḥ svārociṣo manuḥ
tṛtīya uttamas tasmāc caturthas tāmasaḥ smṛtaḥ ||7
pañcamo raivataḥ ṣaṣṭhaś cakṣuṣaḥ parikīrtitaḥ
vaivasvataḥ saptamo vai tataḥ sāvarṇir aṣṭamaḥ ||8
navamo dakṣasāvarṇir brahmasāvarṇikas tataḥ
daśamo dharmasāvarṇir ekādaśaḥ sa ucyate ||9
rudrasāvarṇikas tatra manur vaiṃ dvādaśaḥ smṛtaḥ
trayodaśamanur vedasāvarṇir lokaviśrutaḥ ||10
caturdaśendrasāvarṇir ete tava vibhūtayaḥ
yāntyāyānti prakāśante nāmarūpādibhedataḥ ||11
dvādaśābdasahasraṇa devānāñ ca caturyugam
catvāri trīṇi dve caikaṃ sahasragaṇitaṃ matam ||12
tāvac chatāni catvāri trīṇi dve caicam eva hi
sandhyākrameṇa teṣān tu sandhyāṃśo’pi tathāvidhaḥ ||13
ekasaptatikaṃ tatra yugaṃ bhuṅkte manur bhuvi
manūnām api sarveṣām evaṃ pariṇātir bhavet |
divā prajāpates tat tu niśā sā parikīrtitā ||14
ahorātrañ ca pakṣas te māsasaṃvatsarar tavaḥ
sadupādhikṛtaḥ kālo brahmaṇo janmamṛtyukṛt ||15
śatasaṃvatsare brahmā layaṃ prāpnoti hi tvayi
layānte tvan nābhimadhyād utthitaḥ sṛjati prabhuḥ ||16
tatra kṛtayugānte’haṃ kālaṃ saddharmapālakam
kṛtakṛtyāḥ prajā yatra tan nāmnā māṃ kṛtaṃ viduḥ ||17
iti tad vaca āśrutya kalkir nijajanāvṛtaḥ
praharṣam atulaṃ labdhā śrutvā tad vacanāmṛtam ||18
avahitthām upālakṣya yugasyāha janān hitān
yoddhukāmaḥ kaleḥ pūryāṃ hṛṣto viśasane prabhuḥ ||19
gajarathaturagān narāṃś ca yodhān
kanakavicitravibhūṣaṇācitāṅgān |
dhṛtavividhavarāstraśastrapūgān
yudhinipuṇān gaṇayadhvam ānayadhvam ||20
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe kṛtayugāgamanaṃ nāma pañcamo’dhyāyaḥ ||5

<3.6>

ṣaṣṭho’dhyāyaḥ | 

sūta uvāca |
iti tau marudeuāpī śrutvā kalker vacaḥ puraḥ
kṛtodvāhau rathārūḍhau samāyātau mahābhujau ||1
nānāyudhadharaiḥ sainyair āvṛtau śūramāninau
baddhagodhāṅgulitraṇau daṃśitau baddhahastakau ||2
kārṣnāyasaśirastrāṇau dhanurdharadhurandharau |
akṣauhiṇībhiḥ ṣaḍgabhis tu kampayantau bhuvaṃ bharaiḥ ||3
viśākhayūpabhūpas tu gajalakṣaiḥ samāvṛtaḥ
aśvaiḥ sahasraniyutaiḥ rathaiḥ saptasahasrakaiḥ ||4
padātibhir dvilakṣaiś ca sannaddhair dhṛtakārmukaiḥ
vātoddhūtottaroṣnīṣaiḥ sarvataḥ parivāritaḥ ||5
rudhirāśvasahasrāṇāṃ pañcāśadbhir magārathaiḥ
gajair daśaśatair mattair navalakṣair vṛto babhau ||6
akṣauhiṇībhir daśabhiḥ kalkiḥ parapurañjayaḥ
samāvṛtas tathā devair evam indro divi svarāṭ ||7
bhrātṛputrasuhṛdbhiś ca muditaḥ sainikair vṛtaḥ
yayau digvijayākāṅkṣī jagatām īśvaraḥ prabhuḥ ||8
kāle tasmin dvijo bhūtvā dharmaḥ parijanaiḥ saha
samājagāma kalinā balināpi nirākṛtaḥ ||9
rtaṃ prasādam abhayaṃ sukhaṃ mudam atha svayam
yogam arthaṃ tato’darpaṃ smṛtiṃ kṣemaṃ pratiśrayam ||10
naranārāyaṇau cobhau harer aṃśau tapovratau
dharmas tv etān samādāya putrān strīś cāgatas tvaran ||11
śraddhā maitrī dayā śāntis tuṣtiḥ puṣtiḥ kriyonnatiḥ
buddhir medhā titikṣā ca hrīr mūrtir dharmapālakāḥ ||12
etās tena sahāyātā nijabandhugaṇaiḥ saha
kalkim ālokituṃ tatra nijakāryaṃ niveditum ||13
kalkir dvijaṃ samāsādya pūjayitvā yathāvidhi
provāca vinayāpannaḥ kas tvaṃ kasmād ihāgataḥ ||14
strībhiḥ putraiś ca sahitaḥ kṣīṇapuṇya iva grahaḥ
kasya vā viṣayād rājnas tat tat tvaṃ vada tāvataḥ ||15
putrāḥ striyaś ca te dīnā hīnasvabalapauruṣāḥ
vaiṣṇavāḥ sādhavo yadvat pākhaṇdaiś ca tiraskṛtāḥ ||16
kalker iti vacaḥ śrutvā dharmaḥ śarma nijaṃ smaran
provaca kamalānātham anāthas tv atikātaraḥ ||17
putraiḥ strībhir nijajanaiḥ kṛtāñjalipuṭair harim
stutvā natvā pūjayitvā muditaṃ taṃ dayāparam ||18
dharma uvāca |
śṛṇu kalke mamākhyānaṃ dharmo’haṃ brahmarūpiṇaḥ
tava vakṣaḥsthalāj jātaḥ kāmadaḥ sarvadehinām ||19
devānām agraṇīr havyakavyānāṃ kāmadhug vibhuḥ
tavājñayā carāmy eva sādhukīrtikṛd anv aham ||20
so’haṃ kālena balinā kalināpi nirākṛtaḥ
śakakāmbojaśabaraiḥ sarvair āvāsavāsinā ||21
adhunā te’khilādhāra pādamūlam upāgatāḥ
yathā saṃsārakālāgnisaṃtaptāḥ sādhavo’rditāḥ ||22
iti vāgbhir apūrvābhir dharmeṇa paritoṣitaḥ
kalkiḥ kalkaharaḥ śrīmān āha saṃharṣayañ chanaiḥ ||23
dharma kṛtayugaṃ paśya maruṃ caṇḍāṃśuvaṃśajam
māṃ jānāsi yathā jātaṃ dhātṛprārthitavigraham ||24
kiṭakair bauddhadalanam iti matvā sukhī bhava 
avaiṣṇavānām anyeṣāṃ tavopadravakāriṇām |
jighāṃsuryāmi senābhiś cara gāṃ tvaṃ vinirbhayaḥ ||25
kā bhītis te kva moho’sti yajñadānatapovrataiḥ
sahitaiḥ saṃcara vibho mayi satye vyupasthite ||26
ahaṃ yāmi tvayā gaccha svaputrair bāndhavaiḥ saha
diśāṃ jayārthaṃ tvaṃ śatrunigrahārthaṃ jagatpriya ||27
iti kalker vacaḥ śrutvā dharmaḥ paramaharṣitaḥ
gantuṃ kṛtamatistena ādhipatyamamaṃ smaran ||28
siddhāśrame nijajanān avasthāpya striyaś ca tāḥ ||29
sannaddhaḥ sādhusatkārair vedabrahmamahārathaḥ
nānāśāstrānveṣaṇeṣu saṃkalpavarakārmukaḥ ||30
saptasvarāśvo bhūdevasārathir vahnir āśrayaḥ
kriyābhedabalopetaḥ prayayau dharmanāyakaḥ ||31
yajñadānatapaḥpātrair yamaiśca niyamair vṛtaḥ
khaśakāmbojakān sarvāñ chabarān barbarān api ||32
jetuṃ kalkir yayau yatra kaler āvāsam īpsitam
bhūtāvāsabalopetaṃ sārameyavarākulam ||33
gomāṃsapūtigandhādhyaṃ kākolūcaśivāvṛtam
strīṇāṃ durdyūtakalahavivādavyasanāśrayam ||34
ghoraṃ jagadbhayakaraṃ kāminīsvāminaṃ gṛham
kaliḥ śrutvodyamaṃ kalkeḥ putrapautravṛtaḥ krudhā ||35
purād viśasanāt prāyāt precakākṣarathopari
bharmaḥ kaliṃ samālokya rṣibhiḥ parivāritaḥ ||36
yuyudhe tena sahasā kalkivākyapracoditaḥ
rtena dambhaḥ saṃgrāme prasādo lobham āhvayat ||37
samayād abhayaṃ krodho bhayaṃ sukham upāyayau
nirayo mudam āsādya yuyudhe vividhāyudhaiḥ ||38
ādhiryogena ca vyādhiḥ kṣemeṇa ca balīyasā
praśrayeṇa tathā glānir jarā smṛtim upāhvayat ||39
evaṃ vṛtto mahāghoro yuddhaḥ paramadāruṇaḥ
taṃ draṣṭum āgatā devā brahmādyāḥ khe vibhūtibhiḥ ||40
maruḥ khaśaiś ca kāmbojair yuyudhe bhīmavikramaiḥ
devāpiḥ samare caunair barbarais tad gaṇair api ||41 
viśākhayūpabhūpālaḥ pulindaiḥ śvapacaiḥ saha
yuyudhe vividhaiḥ śastrāyṛ astrair divyair mahāprabhaiḥ ||42
kalkiḥ kokavikokābhyāṃ vāhinībhir varāyudhaiḥ
tau tu kokavikokau ca brahmaṇo varadarpitau ||43
bhrātarau dānavaśreṣthau mattau yuddhaviśāradau
ekarūpau mahāsattvau devānāṃ bhayavardhanau ||44
padātikau gadāhastau vajrāṅgau jayinau diśām
śumbhaiḥ parivṛtau mṛtyujitāv ekatra yodhanāt ||45
tābhyāṃ sa yuyudhe kalkiḥ senāgaṇasamanvitaḥ
śubhānāṃ kalkisainyānāṃ samaras tumulo’bhavat ||46
hreṣitair bṛṃhitair dantaśabdaiṣ ṭaṅkāranāditaiḥ
śūrotkruṣtair bāhuvegaiḥ saṃśabdas talatāḍanaiḥ ||47
saṃpūritā diśaḥ sarvā lokā no śarma lebhire
devāś ca bhayasaṃtrastā divi vyastapathā yayuḥ ||48
pāśair daṇdaiḥ khaḍgaśaktyrṣtiśūlair 
gadāghātair bāṇapātaiś ca ghoraiḥ |
yuddhe śūrās chinnabāhvaṅghrimadhyāḥ 
petuḥ saṃkhye śataśaḥ koṭiśaś ca ||49
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe kalkisenāsaṃgrāmo nāma ṣaṣṭho’dhyāyaḥ || 

<3.7>

saptamo’dhyāyaḥ | 

sūta uvāca |
evaṃ pravṛtte saṃgrāme dharmaḥ paramakopanaḥ
kṛtena sahito ghoraṃ yuyudhe kalinā saha ||1
kalir damitrabāṇaughair dharmasyāpi kṛtasya ca
parābhūtaḥ purīṃ prāyāt tyaktvā gardabhavāhanam ||2 
vicchinnapecakarathaḥ sravadraktāṅgasañcayaḥ
chachurgandhaḥ karālāsyaḥ strīsvāmikam agād gṛham ||3
dambhaḥ sambhogarahitoddhṛtabāṇagaṇāhataḥ 
vyākulaḥ svakulāṃgāro niḥsāraḥ prāviśad gṛham ||4
lobhaḥ prasādābhihato gadayā bhinnamastakaḥ
sārameyarathaṃ chinnaṃ tyaktvāgād rudhiraṃ vaman ||5 
abhayena jitaḥ krodhaḥ kaṣāyikṛtalocanaḥ
gandhākhuvāhaṃ vicchinnaṃ tyaktvā viśasanaṃ gataḥ ||6
bhayaṃ sukhatalāghātād gatāsur nyapatad bhuvi 
nirayo mudamuṣṭibhyāṃ pīḍitā yamam āyayau ||7 
ādhivyādhyādayaḥ sarve tyaktvā vāham upādravan
nānādeśān bhayodvignāḥ kṛtabāṇaprapīḍitāḥ ||8
dharmaḥ kṛtena sahito gatvā viśasanaṃ kaleḥ
nagaraṃ bāṇadahanair dadāha kalinā saha ||9
kalir vipluṣṭasarvāṅgo mṛtadāro mṛtaprajaḥ
jagāmaiko rudandīno varṣāntaram alakṣitaḥ ||10 
marus tu śakakāmbojāñ jaghne divyāstratejasā
devāpiḥ śabarāṃś colān barbarāns tad gaṇān api ||11 
divyāstraśastrasampātair ardayām āsa vīryavān
viśākhayūpabhūpālaḥ pulindān pukkasān api ||12
jaghāna vimalaprajñaḥ khaḍgapātena bhūriṇā
nānāstraśastravarṣais te yodhā neśur anekadhā ||13
kalkiḥ kokavikokābhyāṃ gadāpāṇir yudhāṃ patiḥ
yuyudhe vinyāsavijño lokānāṃ janayan bhayam ||14
vṛkāsurasya putrau tau naptārau śakuner hariḥ
tayoḥ kalkiḥ sa yuyude madhukaiṭabhayor yathā ||15
tayor gadā prahāreṇa cūrnitāṃgasya tatpateḥ
karāc cyutāpatad bhūmau dṛṣtvocurityaho janāḥ ||16
tataḥ punaḥ krudhā viṣṇur jagajjisnur mahābhujaḥ
bhallakena śiras tasya vikokasyācchinat prabhuḥ ||17 
mṛto vikokaḥ kokasya darśanād utthito balī
tad dṛṣṭā vismitā devāḥ kalkiś ca paravīrahā ||18
pratikartur gadāpāṇeḥ kokasyāpy acchinac chiraḥ
mṛtaḥ koko vikokasya dṛṣṭipātāt samutthitaḥ ||19
punas tau militau tena yuyudhāte mahābalau
kāmarūpadharau vīrau kālamṛtyū ivāparau ||20
khaḍgacarmadharau kalkiṃ praharantau punaḥ punaḥ
kalkiḥ krudhā tayos tadvad bāṇena śirasī hate ||21
punar lagne samālokya hariś cintāparo’bhavat
visattvatvam athālokya turagas tāvatāḍayat ||22
kālakalpau durādharṣau turageṇārditau bhṛśam
kalkes taṃ jaghnatur bhāṇair amarṣāt tāmralocanau ||23 
tayor bhujāntaran so’śvaḥ krudhā samadaśad bhṛśam
tau tu prabhinnāsthibhujāv viśastāṅgadakārmukau
pucchaṃ jagṛhatuḥ sapter gopucchaṃ bālakāv iva ||24 
dhṛtapucchau murchitau tau tatkṣaṇāt punar utthitau
paścāt padbhyāṃ dṛḍhaṃ jaghne tayor vakṣasi vajravat ||25
tyakta pucchau murchitau tau tatkṣaṇāt punar utthitau
purataḥ kalkim ālokya babhāṣāte sphuṭākṣarau ||26 
tato brahmā tamābhyetya kṛtāñjalipuṭaḥ śanaiḥ
provāca kalkiṃ naivām ū śastrāstrair vadham arhataḥ ||27
karāghātād ekakāle ubhayor nirmito vadhaḥ
ubhayor darśanād eva nobhayor maraṇaṃ kvacit
viditveti kuruṣvātman yugapac cānayor vadham ||28
iti brahmāvacaḥ śrutvā tyaktaśastrāstravāhanaḥ
tayoḥ praharatoḥ svairaṃ kalkir dānavayoḥ krudhā
muṣṭibhyāṃ vajrakalpābhyāṃ babhañja śirasī tayoḥ ||29
tau tatra bhagnamastiṣkau bhagnaśṛṅgāv agāv iva
petatur divi devānāṃ bhayadau bhuvi bādhakau ||30
tad dṛṣṭvā mahadāścaryaṃ gandharvāpsarasāṃ gaṇāḥ
nanṛtur jagus tuṣṭuvuś ca munayaḥ siddhacāraṇāḥ
devāś ca kusumāsārair vavarṣur harṣamānasāḥ ||31
divi dundubhayo neduḥ prasannāś cābhavan diśaḥ |
tayor vadhapramuditaḥ kavirdaśasahasrakān |
sāśvān mahārathān sākṣād ahanad divyasāyakaiḥ ||32
prājñaḥ śatasahasrāṇāṃ yodhānāṃ raṇamūrdhani
kṣayaṃ nitye sumantras tu rathināṃ pañcaviśatiḥ ||33
evam anye gargyabhargaviśālādyā mahārathān
nijaghnuḥ samare kruddhā niṣādān mlecchabarbarān ||34
evaṃ vijitya tān sarvān kalkir bhūpagaṇaiḥ saha
śayyākarṇaiś ca bhallāṭanagarañ jetum āyayau ||35 
nānāvādyair lokasaṃghair varāstrair
nānāvastrair bhūṣaṇair bhūṣitāṅgaiḥ |
nānāvāhaiś cāmarair vījyamānair
yāto yoddhuṃ kalkir atyugrasenaḥ ||36
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe kokavikokādīnāṃ vadho nāma saptamo’dhyāyaḥ ||7

<3.8>

aṣṭamo’dhyāyaḥ | 

sūta uvāca |
senāgaṇaiḥ parivṛtaḥ kalkir nārāyaṇaḥ prabhuḥ |
bhallāṭanagaraṃ prāyāt khaḍgadhṛk saptivāhanaḥ ||1
sa bhallāṭeśvaro yogī jñātvā viṣṇuṃ jagatpatim
nijasenāgaṇaiḥ pūrṇo yoddhukāmo hariṃ yayau ||2
sa harṣotpulakaḥ śrīmān dīrghāṅgaḥ kṛṣṇabhāvanaḥ
śaśidhvajo mahātejā gajāyutabalaḥ sudhīḥ ||3
tasya patnī mahādevī viṣṇuvrataparāyaṇā
suśāntā svāminaṃ prāha kalkinā yoddhum udyatam ||4
nātha kāntaṃ jagannāthaṃ sarvāntaryāminaṃ prabhum
kalkiṃ nārāyaṇaṃ sākṣāt kathaṃ tvaṃ prahariṣyasi ||5
śaśidhvaja uvāca |
suśānte paramo dharmaḥ prajāpativinirmitaḥ
yuddhe prahāraḥ sarvatra gurau śiṣye harer iva ||6
jīvato rājabhogaḥ syān mṛtaḥ svarge pramodate
yuddhe jayo vā mṛtyur vā kṣatriyāṇāṃ sukhāvahaḥ ||7
suśānto’vāca |
devatvaṃ bhūpatitvaṃ vā viṣayāviṣṭakā minām
unmadānāṃ bhaved eva na hareḥ pādasevinām ||8
tvaṃ sevakaḥ sa cāpīsas tvaṃ niṣkāmaḥ sa cāpradḥ
yuvayor yuddhamilanaṃ kathaṃ mohād bhaviṣyati ||9
śaśidhvaja uvāca |
dvandvātīte yadi dvandvam īśvare sevake tathā
dehāveśāl līlayaiva sā sevā syāt tathā mama ||10
dehāveśād īśvarasya kāmādyā daihikā guṇāḥ
māyāṅgā yadi jāyante viṣayāś ca na kiṃ tathā ||11
brahmato brahmateśasya śarīritve śarīritā
sevakasyābhedadṛśas tv evaṃ janmalayodayāḥ ||12
sevyasevakatā viṣṇor māyā seveti kīrtitā
dvaitādvaitasya ceṣṭaiṣā trivargajanikā satām ||13
ato’haṃ kalkinā yoddhuṃ yāmi kānte svasenayā
tvaṃ taṃ pūjaya kānte’dya kamalāpatim īśvaram ||14
suśāntovāca |
kṛtārthāhaṃ tvayā viṣṇusevāsaṃmilitātmanā
svāminn iha paratrāpi vaiṣṇavī prathitā gatiḥ ||15
iti tasyā valguvāgbhiḥ praṇatāyāḥ śaśidhvajaḥ
ātmānaṃ vaiṣṇavaṃ mene sāśrunetro hariṃ smaran ||16
tām āliṅgya pramuditaḥ śūrair bahubhir āvṛtaḥ
vadan nāma smaran rūpaṃ vaiṣṇavair yoddhum āyayau ||17
gatvā tu kalkisenāyāṃ vidrāvya mahatīṃ camūm
śayyākarṇagaṇair vīraiḥ sannaddhair udyatāyudhaiḥ ||18
śaśidhvajasutaḥ śrīmān sūryaketur mahābalaḥ
marubhūpena yuyudhe vaisṇavo dhanvināṃ varaḥ ||19
tasyānujo bṛhatketuḥ kāntaḥ kokilanisvanaḥ
devāpinā sa yuyudhe gadāyuddhaviśāradaḥ ||20
viśākhayupabhūpas tu śaśidhvajanṛpeṇa ca
yuyudhe vividhaiḥ śastraiḥ karibhiḥ parivāritaḥ ||21
rudhirāśvo dhanurdhārī laghuhastaḥ pratāpavān
rajasyanena yuyudhe bhargaḥ śāntane dhanvinā ||22
śūlaiḥ prāsair gadāghātair bāṇaśaktyaṣṭitomaraiḥ
bhallaiḥ khaḍgair bhuśuṇdībhiḥ kuntaiḥ samabhavad raṇaḥ ||23
patākābhir dhvajaiś cihnais tomaraiś chatracāmaraiḥ
proddhatadhūlipaṭalair andhakāro mahān abhūt ||24
gagane’nughanā devāḥ ke vā vāsaṃ na cakrire
gandharvaiḥ sādhusandarbhair gāyanair amṛtāyanaiḥ ||25
draṣtuṃ samāgatāḥ sarve lokāḥ samaram adbhutam
śaṃkhadundubhisannādair āsphoṭair bṛṃhitair api ||26
hreṣitair yodhanotkruṣtair lokā mūkā ivābhavan
rathino rathibhiḥ sākaṃ padātiś ca padātibhiḥ ||27
hayā hayair ibhāś cebhaiḥ samaro’maradānavaiḥ
yathābhavat sa tu ghano yamarāṣṭravivardhanaḥ ||28
śaśidhvajacamūnāthaiḥ kalkisenādhipaiḥ saha 
nipetuḥ sainikā bhūmau chinnabāhvaṅghrikandharāḥ ||29
dhāvanto’tibruvantaś ca vikurvanto’sṛgukṣitāḥ
uparyupari saṃchannā gajāśvarathamarditāḥ ||30
nipetuḥ pradhane vīrāḥ koṭikoṭisaharaśaḥ
bhūte sānandasandohāḥ sravanto rudhirodakam ||31
uṣnīṣahaṃsāḥ saṃchinnagajarodhorathaplavāḥ
karorumīnābharaṇam asikāñcanavālukāḥ ||32 
evaṃ pravṛttā saṃgrāme nadyaḥ sadyo’tidāruṇāḥ
sūryaketus tu maruṇā sahito yuyudhe balī ||33
kālakalpo durādharṣo maruṃ bāṇair atāḍayat
marus tu tatra daśabhir mārgaṇair ārmūyad bhṛśam ||34 
marubāṇāhato vīraḥ sūryaketur amarṣitaḥ
jaghāna turagān kopāt pādoddhātena tad ratham ||35
cūrṇayitvātha tenāpi tasya vakṣasy atāḍayat
gadāghātena tenāpi marur mūrchām avāpaha ||36
sārathis tam apovāha rathe nānyena dharmavit
bṛhatketuś ca devāpiṃ bāṇaiḥ prācchādayad balī ||37
dhanur vikṛṣya tarasā nīhāreṇa yathā ravim
sa tu bāṇamayaṃ varṣaṃ parivārya nijāyudhaiḥ ||38
bṛhatketuṃ dṛḍhaṃ jaghne kaṅkapatrāḥ śilāśitaiḥ
bhinnaṃ śūlam athālokya dhanur gṛhya patat tribhiḥ ||39
śitadhāraiḥ svarṇapuṃkhair gāddhrapatrair ayomukhaiḥ
devāpim āśugair jaghne bṛhatketuḥ sasainikam ||40
devāpis tad dhanur divyaṃ ciccheda niśitaiḥ śaraiḥ
chinnadhanvā bṛhatketuḥ khaḍgapāṇir jighāṃsayā ||41
devāpeḥ sārathiṃ sāśvaṃ jaghne śūro mahāmṛdhe
sa devāpir dhanus tiaktvā talenāhatya taṃ ripum ||42
bhujayor antarānīya niṣpipeṣa sa nirdayaḥ
taṃ dvyaṣṭavarṣaṃ niṣkrāntaṃ mūrchitaṃ śatruṇārditam ||43
anujaṃ vīkṣya devāpimūrdhni sūryadhvajo’vadhīt
muṣṭinā vajrapātena so’patan mūrchito bhuvi
mūrchitasya ripuḥ krodhāt senāgaṇam atāḍayat ||44
śaśidhvajaḥ sarvajagannivāsaṃ kalkiṃ purastādabhisūryavarcasam |
śyāmaṃ piśaṅgāmbaram ambujekṣaṇaṃ bṛhadbhujaṃ cārukirīṭabhūṣaṇam ||45
nānāmaṇivrātacitāṅgaśobhayā nirāstalokekṣaṇahṛttamomayam |
viśākhayūpādibhir āvṛtaṃ prabhuṃ dadarśa dharmeṇa kṛtena pūjitam ||46
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe śaśidhvajakalkisenayor yuddhaṃ nāmāṣṭamo’dhyāyaḥ ||8

<3.9>

navamo’dhyāyaḥ | 

sūta uvāca |
hṛdidhyānāspadaṃ rūpaṃ kalker dṛṣṭvā śaśidhvajaḥ
pūrṇaṃ khaḍgadharaṃ cāruturagārūdham abravīt ||1
danurbāṇadharaṃ cāruvibhūṣaṇavarāṅgakam
pāpatāpavināśārtham udyataṃ jagatāṃ param ||2
prāha taṃ paramātmānaṃ hṛṣṭaromā śaśidhvajaḥ
ehy ehi puṇḍarīkākṣa prahāraṃ kuru me hṛdi ||3
athavātman bāṇabhiyā tamo’ndhe hṛdi me viśa
nirguṇasya guṇajñatvam advaitasyāstratāḍanam ||4
niṣkāmasya jayodyogasahāyaṃ yasya sainikam
lokāḥ paśyantu yuddhe me dvairathe paramātmanaḥ ||5
parabuddhir yadi dṛḍhaṃ prahartā vibhave tvayi
śivaviṣṇor bhedakṛte lokaṃ yāsyāmi saṃyuge ||6
iti rājño vacaḥ śrutvā akrodhaḥ kruddhavad vibhuḥ
bāṇair atāḍayat saṃkhye dhṛtāyudham arīn damam ||7
śaśidhvajas tat prahāramagaṇayya varāyudhaiḥ
taṃ jaghne bāṇavarṣeṇa dhārābhir iva parvatam ||8
tad bāṇavarṣabhinnāntaḥ kalkiḥ paramakopanaḥ
divyaiḥ śastrāstrasaṃghātais tayor yuddham avartata ||9
brahmāstrasya ca brahmāstrair vāyavyasya ca pārvataiḥ
āgneyasya ca pārjanyaiḥ pannagasya ca gāruḍaiḥ ||10 
evaṃ nānāvidhair astrair anyonyam abhijaghnatuḥ
lokāḥ sapālāḥ santrastā yugāntam iva menire ||11
devā bāṇāgnisantrastā agaman khagamāḥ kila
tato’tivitathodyogau vāsudevaśaśidhvajau ||12
nirastrau bāhuyuddhena yuyudhāte parasparam
padāghātais talāghātair muṣṭipraharaṇais tathā ||13
niyuddhakuśalau vīrau mumudāte parasparam
varāhoddhṛtaśabdena taṃ talenāhanad dhariḥ ||14
sa mūrchito nṛpaḥ kopāt samutthāya ca tatkṣaṇāt
muṣṭibhyāṃ vajrakalpābhyām avadhīt kalkim ojasā
sa kalkis tatprahāreṇa papāta bhuvi mūrchitaḥ ||15
dharmaḥ kṛtañ ca taṃ dṛṣṭvā mūrchitaṃ jagadīśvaram
samāgatau tam ānetuṃ kakṣe tau jagṛhe nṛpaḥ ||16
kalkiṃ vakṣasy upādāya labdhārthaḥ prayayau gṛham
yuddhe nṛpāṇām anyeṣāṃ putrau dṛṣṭvā sudurjayau ||17
kalkiṃ surādhipapatiṃ pradhane vijitya
dharmaṃ kṛtañ ca nijakakṣayuge nidhāya |
harṣollasad dhṛdaya utpulakaḥ pramāthī
gatvā gṛhaṃ harigṛhe dadṛśe suśāntām ||18
dṛṣṭvā tasyāḥ sulalitamukhaṃ vaiṣṇavīnāñ ca madhye 
gāyantīnāṃ hariguṇakathās tām atha prāha rājā |
devādīnāṃ vinayavacasā śambhale janmanā vā
vidyālābhaṃ pariṇayavidhimlecchapāṣaṇḍanāśam ||19
kalkiḥ svayaṃ hṛdi samāyam ihāgato’ddhā
mūrchicchalena tava sevanam īkṣaṇārtham |
dharmaṃ kṛtañ ca mama kakṣayuge suśānte
kānte vilokaya samarcaya saṃvidhehi ||20 
iti nṛpavacasā vinodapūrṇā harikṛtadharmayutaṃ praṇamya nātham |
saha nijasakhibhir nanarta rāmā hariguṇakīrtanavartanā vilajjā ||21
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe dharmakalkikṛtānām ānayanaṃ nāma navamo’dhyāyaḥ ||9

<3.10>

daśamo’dhyāyaḥ |

suśāntovāca |
jaya hare’marādhīśasevitaṃ tava padāmbujaṃ bhūribhūṣaṇam
kuru mam āgrataḥ sādhusatkṛtaṃ tyaja mahāmate moham ātmanaḥ ||1
tava vapur jagadrūpasampadā viracitaṃ satāṃ mānase sthitam
ratipater manomohadāyakaṃ kuru viceṣṭitaṃ kāmalampaṭam ||2
tava yaśo jagacchokanāśanaṃ mṛdukathāmṛtaprītidāyakam
smitasudhokṣitaṃ candravanmukhaṃ tava karotv alaṃ lokamaṅgalam ||3 
mama patis tv ayaṃ sarvadurjayo yadi tavāpriyaṃ karmaṇācaret
jahi tad ātmanaḥ śatrum udyataṃ kuru kṛpāṃ na ced īdṛgīśvaraḥ ||4 
mahadahaṃyutaṃ pañcamātrayā prakṛtijāyayā nirmitaṃ vapuḥ
tava nirīkṣaṇāl līlayā jagat sthitilayodayaṃ brahmakalpitam ||5
bhūviyanmarudvāritejasāṃ rāśibhiḥ śarīrendriyāśritaiḥ
triguṇayā svayā māyayā vibho kuru kṛpāṃ bhavatsevanārthinām ||6
tava guṇālayaṃ nāma pāvanaṃ kalimalāpahaṃ kīrtayanti ye
bhavabhayakṣayaṃ tāpatāpitā muhuraho janāḥ saṃsaranti no ||7
tava januḥ satāṃ mānavardhanaṃ jinakulakṣayaṃ devapālakam
kṛtayugārpakaṃ dharmapūrakaṃ kalikulāntakaṃ śaṃ tanotu me ||8 
mama gṛhaṃ patiṃ putranaptṛkaṃ gajarathair dhvajāyś cāmarair dhanaiḥ || 
maṇivarāsanaṃ satkṛtiṃ vinā tava padābjayoḥ śobhayanti kim ||9
tava jagadvapuḥ sundarasmitaṃ mukham aninditaṃ sundaratviṣam
yadi na me priyaṃ valguceṣṭite parikaroty aho mṛtyur astv iha ||10
hayacara bhayahara karaharaśaraṇa kharatara varaśara daśabaladamana
jaya hataparabhara bhavavaranaśana śaśadharaśatasama rasabharavadana ||11 
iti tasyāḥ suśāntāyā gītena paritoṣitaḥ
uttasthau raṇaśayyāyāḥ kalkir yuddhasthavīravat ||12
suśāntāṃ purato dṛṣṭvā kṛtaṃ vāme tu dakṣiṇe
dharmaṃ śaśidhvajaṃ paścāt prāheti vrīḍi tānanaḥ ||13
kā tvaṃ padmapalāśākṣi mama sevārthamudyatā
kānte śaśidhvajaḥ śūro mama paścād upasthitaḥ ||14
he dharma he kṛtayuga katham atrāgatā vayam
raṇāṅgaṇaṃ vihāyāsyāḥ śatror antaḥ pure vada ||15
śatrupatnyaḥ kathaṃ sādhu sevante mām ariṃ mudā
śaśidhvajaḥ śūramānī mūrchitaṃ hanti no katham ||16
śuśāntovāca |
pātāle divi bhūmau vā naranāgasurāsurāḥ
nārāyaṇasya te kalke ke vā sevāṃ na kurvate ||17
yat sevakānāṃ jagatāṃ mitrāṇāṃ darśanād api
nivartante śatrubhāvas tasya sākṣāt kuto ripuḥ ||18
tvayā sārdhaṃ mama patiḥ śatrubhāvena saṃyuge
yadi yogyas tad ānetuṃ kiṃ samartho nijālayam ||19
tava daso mama svāmī ahaṃ dāsī nijā tava |
āvayoḥ saṃprasādāya āgato’si mahābhuja ||20
dharma uvāca |
ahaṃ tavaitayor bhaktyā namarūpānukīrtanāt |
kṛtārtho’smi kṛtārtho’smi kṛtārtho’smi kalikṣaya ||21
kṛtayuga uvāca |
adhunāhaṃ kṛtayugaṃ tava dāsasya darśanāt
tuam īśvaro jagatpūjyasevakasyāsya tejasā ||22
śaśidhvaja uvāca |
daṇdyaṃ māṃ daṇḍaya vibho yoddhṛtvād udyatāyudham
yena kāmādirāgeṇa tuayy ātmany api vairitā ||23
iti kalkir vacas teṣāṃ niśamya hasitānanaḥ
tvayājito’smīti nṛpaṃ punaḥ punar uvāca ha ||24
tataḥ śaśidhvajo rājā yuddhād āhūya putrakān
suśāntāyā matiṃ buddhvā ramāṃ prādāt sakalkaye ||25
tadaitya marudevāpī śaśidhvajasamāhṛtau
viśākhayūpabhūpaś ca rudhirāśvaś ca saṃyugāt ||26
śayyākarṇanṛpeṇāpi bhallāṭaṃ puram āyayuḥ
senāgaṇair asaṃkhyātaiḥ sā purī marditābhavat ||27
gajāśvarathasaṃbādhaiḥ patticchatrarathadhvajaiḥ
kalkināpi ramāyāś ca vivāhotsavasampadām ||28
draṣtuṃ samīyus tvaritā harṣāt sabalavāhanāḥ
śaṃkhabherīmṛdaṅgānāṃ vāditrāṇāñ ca nisvanaiḥ ||29
nṛtyagītavidhānaiś ca purastrīkṛtamaṅgalaiḥ
vivāho ramayā kalker abhūd atisukhāvahaḥ ||30
nṛpā nānāvidhairbhojyaiḥ pūjitā viviśuḥ sabhām
brahmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā vaiśyāḥ śūdrāś cāvarajātayaḥ ||31 
vicitrabhogāvaraṇāḥ kalkiṃ draṣṭum upāviśan
tasyāṃ sabhāyāṃ śuśubhe kalkiḥ kamalalocanaḥ ||32
nakṣatragaṇamadhyasthaḥ pūrṇaḥ śaśadaro yathā
reje rājagaṇādhīśo lokān sarvān vimohayan ||33
ramāpatiṃ kalkim avekṣya bhūpaḥ sabhāgataṃ padmadalāyatekṣaṇam |
jāmātaraṃ bhaktiyutena karmaṇā vibudhya madhye niṣasāda tatra ha ||34
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe kalkinā ramāvivāho nāma daśamo’dhyāyaḥ ||

<3.11>

ekādaśo’dhyāyaḥ |

sūta uvāca |
tatrāhus te sabhāmadhye vaiṣṇavaṃ taṃ śaśidhvajam
munibhiḥ kathitāśeṣabhaktivyāsaktavigraham ||1
suśāntāñ ca kṛtenāpi dharmeṇa vidhivadyutām ||2
rājāna ūcuḥ |
yuvāṃ nārāyaṇasyāsya kalkeḥ śvaśuratāṃ gatau |
vayaṃ nṛpā ime lokā ṛṣayo brāhmaṇaś ca ye ||3
prekṣya bhaktivitānaṃ vāṃ harau vismitamānasāḥ
pṛcchāmas tvām iyaṃ bhaktiḥ kva labdhā paramātmanaḥ ||4
kasya vā śikṣitā rājan kiṃ vā naisargikī tava |
śrotum icchāmahe rājan trijagajjanapāvanīm |
kathāṃ bhāgavatīṃ tvattaḥ saṃsārāśramanāśinīm ||5
śaśidhvaja uvāca |
strīpuṃsorāvayos tat tac chṛṇutāmoghavikramāḥ |
vṛttaṃ yaj janmakarmādi smṛtiṃ tad bhaktilakṣaṇām ||6
purā yugasahasrānte gṛdhro’haṃ pūtimāṃsabhuk
gṛdhrīyaṃ me priyāraṇye kṛtanīḍo vanaspatau ||7
cacāra kāmaṃ sarvatra vanopavanasaṃkule
mṛtānāṃ pūtimāṃsaughaiḥ prāṇināṃ vṛttikalpakau ||8
ekadā lubdhakaḥ krūro lulobha piśitāśinau 
āvāṃ vīkṣya gṛhe puṣtaṃ gṛdhraṃ tatrāpy ayojayat ||9 
taṃ vīkṣya jātaviśrambhau kṣudhayā paripīḍitau
strīpuṃsau patitau tatra māṃsalobhitacetasau ||10
baddhavāvāṃ vīkṣya tadā harṣād āgatya lubdhakaḥ
jagrāha kaṇthe tarasā cañcvāgrāghāt apīḍitaḥ ||11
āvāṃ gṛhītvā gaṇḍakyāḥ śilāyāṃ salilāntike |
mastiṣkaṃ cūrṇayām āsa lubdhakaḥ piśitāśanaḥ ||12
cakrāṅkitaśilāgaṅgāmaraṇād api tat kṣaṇāt
jyotirmayavimānena sadyo bhūtvā caturbhujāu ||13
prāptau vaikuṇthanilayaṃ sarvalokanamaskṛtam
tatra sthitvā yugaśataṃ brahmaṇo lokam āgatau ||14
brahmaloke pañcaśataṃ yugānām upabhujya vai
devaloke kālavaśād gataṃ yugacatuḥśatam ||15
tato bhuvi nṛpās tāvad baddhasūnur ahaṃ smaran
harer anugrahaṃ loke śālagrāmaśilāśramam ||16
jātismaratvaṃ gaṇḍakyāḥ kiṃ tasyāḥ kathayāmy aham
yaj jalasparśamātreṇa mahātmyaṃ māhad adbhutam ||17
cakrāṃkitaśilāsparśamaraṇasyedṛśaṃ phalam
na jāne vāsudevasya sevayā kiṃ bhaviṣyati ||18
ity āvāṃ haripūjāsu harṣavihvalacetasau
nṛtyantāv anugāyantau viluṭhantau sthitāv iha ||19 
kalker nārāyaṇāṃśasya avatāraḥ kalikṣayaḥ
purā viditavīryasya pṛṣto brahmamukhāc chrutakh ||20
iti rājasabhāyāṃ sa śrāvāyitvā nijāḥ kathāḥ
dadau gajānām ayutam aśvānāṃ lakṣam ādarāt ||21
rathānāṃ ṣaṭsahasran tu dadau pūrṇasya bhaktitaḥ
dāsīnāṃ yuvatīnāñ ca ramānāthāya ṣaṭśatam ||22
ratnāni ca mahārghāṇi dattvā rājā śaśidhvajaḥ
mene kṛtārtham ātmānaṃ svajanair bāndhavaiḥ saha ||23
sabhāsada iti śrutvā pūrvajanmoditāḥ kathāḥ
vismaiāviṣṭamanasaḥ pūrṇaṃ taṃ menire nṛpam ||24
kalkiṃ stuvanto dhyāyantaḥ praśaṃsanto jagajjanāḥ 
punas tam āhū rājānaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ bhaktibhaktayoḥ ||25
nṛpā ūcuḥ |
bhaktikāmyād bhagavataḥ ko vā bhakto vidhānavit
kiṃ karoti kim aśnāti kva vā vasati vakti kim ||26
etān varṇaya rājendra sarvaṃ tvaṃ vetsi sādarāt
jātismaratvāt kṛṣṇasya jagatāṃ pāvanecchayā ||27
iti teṣāṃ vacaḥ śrutvā praphullavadano nṛpaḥ
sādhuvādaiḥ samāmantṛya tān āha brahmaṇoditam ||28
śaśidhvaja uvāca |
purā brahmasabhāmadhye maharṣigaṇasaṃkule |
sanako nāradaṃ prāha bhavadbhir yās tv ihoditāḥ ||29
teṣām anugraheṇāhaṃ tatroṣitvā śrutāḥ kathāḥ
yās tāḥ saṃkathayāmīha śṛṇudhvaṃ pāpanāśanāḥ ||30
sanaka uvāca |
kā bhaktiḥ saṃsṛtiharā harau lokanamaskṛtā
tāmādau varṇaya mune nāradāvahitā vayam ||31 
nārada uvāca |
manaḥ ṣaṣthānīndriyāṇi saṃyamya parayā dhiyā
gurāv api nyased dehaṃ lokatantravicakṣaṇaḥ ||32
gurau prasanne bhagavān prasīdati hariḥ svayam
praṇavāgnipriyāmadhye mavarṇaṃ tannideśataḥ ||33 
smared ananyayā buddhyā deśikaḥ susamāhitaḥ
pādyārghyācamanīyādyaiḥ snānavāsovibhūṣaṇaiḥ ||34
pūjayitvā vāsudevapādapadmaṃ samāhitaḥ
sarvāṅgasundaraṃ ramyaṃ smared dhṛtpadmamadhyagam ||35
evaṃ dhyātvā vākyamanobuddhīndriyagaṇaiḥ saha
ātmānam arpayodvidvān harāv ekāntabhāvavit ||36
aṅgāni devās teṣān tu nāmāni viditānyuta
viṣnoḥ kalker anantasya tāny evān yan na vidyate ||37
sevyaḥ kṛṇaḥ sevako’ham anye tasyātmamūrtaiaḥ
avidyopādhayo jñānād vadanti prabhavādayaḥ ||38
bhaktasyāpi harau dvaitaṃ sevyasevakavat tadā
nānyad vinā tam ity eva kvaca kiñcana vidyate ||39
bhaktaḥ smarati taṃ viṣṇuṃ tan nāmāni ca gāyati
tat karmāṇi karoty eva tad ānandasukhodaiaḥ ||40
nṛtyaty uddhatavad rauti hasati praiti tan manāḥ
viluṃṭhaty ātmavismṛtyā na vetti kiyad antaram ||41
evaṃvidhā bhagavato bhaktir avyabhicāriṇī
punāti sahasā lokān sadevāsuramānuṣān ||42
bhaktiḥ sā prakṛtir nityā brahmasampatprakāśitā
śivaviṣṇubrahmarūpā vedādy ānāṃ varāpi vā ||43
bhaktāḥ sattvaguṇādhyāsād rajasendriyalālasāḥ
tamasā ghorasaṃklpā bhajanti dvaitadṛgjanāḥ ||44
sattvān nirguṇatām eti rajasā viṣayaspṛhām
tamasā narakaṃ yānti saṃsārādvaitadharmiṇi ||45
ucchiṣṭam avaśiṣtaṃ vā pathyaṃ pūtam abhīpsitam
bhaktānāṃ bhojanaṃ viṣṇor naivedyaṃ sāttvikaṃ matam ||46
indriyaprītijananaṃ śukraśoṇitavardhanam
bhojanaṃ rājasaṃ śuddham āyurārogyavardhanam ||47
ataḥ paraṃ tāmasānāṃ kaṭvamloṣṇavidāhikam
pūtiparyuṣitaṃ jñeyaṃ bhojanaṃ tāmasapriyam ||48 
sāttvikānāṃ vane vāso grāme vāsas tu rājasaḥ
tāmasaṃ dyūtamadyādisadanaṃ parikīrtitam ||49
na dātā sa hariḥ kiñcit sevakas tu na yācakaḥ 
tathāpi paramā prītis tayoḥ kim iti śāśvatī ||50
ity etad bhagavata īśvarasya viṣṇor guṇakathanaṃ sanako vibudhya bhaktyā |
savinayavacanaiḥ surarṣivaryaṃ pariṇutvendrapuraṃ jagāma śuddhaḥ ||51 
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe nṛpagaṇaśaśidhvajasaṃvāde jātismaratvakathanaṃ nāma ekādaśo’dhyāyaḥ ||

<3.12>

dvādaśo’dhyāyaḥ |

śaśidhvaja uvāca |
etad vaḥ kathitaṃ bhūpāḥ kathanīyo’rukarmaṇaḥ
kathā bhaktasya bhacteś ca kim anyat kathayāmy aham ||1
bhūpā ūcuḥ |
tvaṃ rājan vaiṣṇavaśreṣthaḥ sarvasattvahite rataḥ
tavāveśaḥ kathaṃ yuddharaṅge hiṃsādikarmaṇi ||2
prāyaśaḥ sādhavo loke jīvānāṃ hitakāriṇaḥ
prāṇabuddhidhanair vāgbhiḥ sarveṣāṃ viṣayātmanām ||3
śaśidhvaja uvāca |
dvaitaprakāśinī yā tu prakṛtiḥ kāmarūpiṇī
sā sūte trijagatkṛtsnaṃ vedāṃś ca triguṇātmikā ||4
te vedās trijagaddharmaśāsanādharmanāśanāḥ
bhaktipravartakā loke kāmināṃ viṣayaiṣiṇām ||5
vātsyāyanādimunayo manavo vedapāragāḥ
vahanti balim īśasya vedavākyānuśāsitāḥ ||6
vayaṃ tad anugāḥ karmadharmaniṣthā raṇapriyāḥ
jighāṃsantaṃ jighāṃsāmo vedārthakṛtaniścayāḥ ||7
avadhyasya vadhe yāvāṃs tāvān vadhyasya rakṣaṇe
ity āha bhagavān vyāsaḥ sarvavedārthatatparaḥ ||8
prāyaścittaṃ na tatrāsti tatrādharmaḥ pravartate
ato’tra vāhinīṃ hatvā bhavatāṃ yudhi durjayām ||9
dharmaṃ kṛtañ ca kalkiṃ tu samānīyāgatā vayam
eṣā bhaktir mama matā tavābhipretam īraya ||10 
ahaṃ tad anuvakṣyāmi vedavākyānusārataḥ
yadi viṣnuḥ sa sarvatra tadā kaṃ hanti ko hataḥ ||11
hantā viṣṇur hato viṣṇur vadhaḥ kasyāsti tatra cet |
yuddhayajñādiṣu vadhe na vadho vedaśāsanāt ||12 
iti gāyanti munayo manavaś ca caturdaśa
itthaṃ yuddhaiś ca yajñaiś ca bhajāmo viṣṇum īśvaram ||13
ato bhāgavatīṃ māyām āśritya vidhinā yajan
sevyasevakabhāvena sukhī bhavati nānyathā ||14 
bhūpā ūcuḥ |
nimer bhūpasya bhūpāla guroḥ śāpān mṛtasya ca
tādṛśe bhogāyatane virāgaḥ katham ucyatām ||15 
śiṣyaśāpād vaśiṣṭhasya dehāvāptir mṛtasya ca
śruyate kila muktānāṃ janma bhaktavimuktatā ||16
ato bhāgavatī mayā durbodhyāvijitātmanām
vimohayati saṃsāre nānātvād indrajālavata ||17
iti teṣāṃ vaco bhūyaḥ śrutvā rājā śaśidhvajaḥ
provāca vadatāṃ śreṣṭho bhaktipravaṇayā dhivā ||18
śaśidhvaja uvāca |
bahūnāṃ janmanāmante tīrthakṣetrādiyogataḥ
daivād bhavet sādhusaṃgas tasmād īśvaradarśanam ||19
tataḥ sālokyatāmprāpya bhajanty ādṛtacetasaḥ
bhuktvā bhogān anupamān bhakto bhavati saṃsṛtau ||20
rajojuṣaḥ karmaparāḥ haripūjāparāḥ sadā
tan nāmāni pragāyanti tad rūpasmaranotsukāḥ ||21 
avatārānukāraṇaparvavratamahotsavāḥ
bhagavadbhaktipūjāḍhyāḥ paramānandasaṃplutāḥ ||22
ato mokṣaṃ na vāñchanti dṛṣṭamuktiphalodayāḥ 
muktvā labhante janmāni haribhāvaprakāśakāḥ ||23 
harirūpāḥ kṣetratīrthapāvanā dharmatatparāḥ
sārāsāravidaḥ sevyasevakā dvaitavigrahāḥ ||24
yathāvatāraḥ kṛṣṇasya tathā tat sevinām iha
evaṃ nimer nimiṣatā līlā bhaktasya locane ||25
muktasyāpi vasiṣṭhasya śarīrabhajanādaraḥ
etad vaḥ kathitaṃ bhūpā māhātmyaṃ bhaktibhaktayoḥ ||26
sadyaḥ pāpaharaṃ puṃsāṃ haribhaktivivardhanam |
sarvendriyasthaṃ devānām ānandasukhasañcayam |
kāmarāgādidoṣaghnaṃ māyāmohanivāraṇam ||27
nānāśāstrapurāṇavedavimalavyākhyāmṛtāmbhonidhir
saṃmathyāticiraṃ trilokamunayo vyāsādayo bhāvukāḥ |
kṛṣne bhāvam ananyam evam amalaṃ haiyaṅgavīnaṃ navaṃ
labdhvā saṃmṛtināśanaṃ tribhuvane śrīkṛṣṇatulyāyate ||28
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe bhaktibhaktamāhātmyaṃ nāma dvādaśo’dhyāyaḥ ||12

<3.13>

trayodaśo’dhyāyaḥ | 

sūta uvāca |
iti bhūpaḥ sabhāyāṃ sa kathayitvā nijāḥ kathāḥ
śaśidhvajaḥ prītamanāḥ prāha kalkiṃ kṛtāñjaliḥ ||1
śaśidhvaja uvāca |
tvaṃ hi nātha trilokeśa ete bhūpās tvad āśrayāḥ
māṃ tathā viddhi rājānaṃ tvan nideśakaraṃ hare ||2
tapas taptuṃ yāmi kāmaṃ haridhvāraṃ munipriyam
ete mat putrapautrāś ca pālanīyās tvad āśrayāḥ ||3
mamāpi kāmaṃ jānāsi purā jāmbavato yathā
nidhanaṃ dvividasyāpi tadā sarvaṃ sureśvara ||4
ity uktvā gantum udyuktaṃ bhāryayā saitaṃ nṛpam
lajjayādhomukhaṃ kalkiṃ prāhur bhūpāḥ kim ity uta ||5 
he nātha kim anenoktaṃ yac chrutvā tvam adhomukhaḥ
kathaṃ tad brūhi kāmaṃ naḥ kiṃ vā naḥ śādhi saṃśayāt ||6
kalkir uvāca |
amuṃ pṛcchata vo bhūpā yuṣmākaṃ saṃśayacchidam
śaśidhvajaṃ mahāprājñaṃ mad bhaktikṛtaniścayam ||7
iti kalker vacaḥ śrutvā te bhūpāḥ proktakāriṇaḥ
rājānaṃ taṃ punaḥ prāhuḥ saṃśayāpannamānasāḥ ||8 
nṛpā ūcuḥ |
kiṃ tvayā kathitaṃ rājañ chaśidhvaja mahāmate |
kathaṃ kalkis tadvad idaṃ śrutvaivābhūd adhomukhaḥ ||9 
śaśidhvaja uvāca |
purā rāmāvatāreṇa lakṣmaṇād indrajidvadham
mokṣañ cālakṣya dvivido rākṣasatvāt sa dāruṇāt ||10 
agnyāgāre brahmavīravadhenāikāhiko jvaraḥ 
lakṣmaṇasya śarīreṇa praviṣto mohakārakaḥ ||11
taṃ vyākulam abhiprekṣya dvivido bhiṣajāṃ varaḥ
aśvivaṃśena saṃjātaḥ khyāpayām āsa lakṣmaṇam ||12
likhitvā rāmabhadrasya saṃjñāpatrīm atandritaḥ |
lakṣmaṇaṃ darśayām āsa ūrdhvatiṣṭhan mahābhujaḥ ||13
lakṣmaṇo vīkṣya tāṃ patrīṃ vijvaro balavān abhūt
sa tato dvividaṃ prāha varaṃ varaya vānara ||14 
dvividas tad vacaḥ śrutvā lakṣmaṇaṃ prāha hṛṣṭavat
tvatto me maraṇaṃ prārthyaṃ vānaratvāc ca mocanam ||15 
punas taṃ lakṣmaṇaḥ prāha mama janmāntare tava
mocanaṃ bhavitā kīśa balarāmaśarīriṇaḥ ||16 
samudrasyottare tīre dvivido nāma vānaraḥ
aikāhikaṃ jvaraṃ hanti likhitaṃ yas tu paśyati ||17
iti mantrākṣaraṃ dvāri likhitvā tālapatrake
yas tu paśyati tasyāpi naśyaty aikāhikajvaraḥ ||18
iti tasya varaṃ labdhvā cirāyuḥ susthavānaraḥ
balarāmāstrabhinnātmā mokṣam āpākutobhayam ||19
tathā kṣetre sūtaputro nihato lomaharṣaṇaḥ
balarāmāstrayuktāmā naimiṣe’bhūt svavāñchayā ||20
jāmbavāṃś ca purā bhūpā vāmanatvaṃ gate harau 
tasyāpy ūrdhvagataṃ pādaṃ tatra cakre pradakṣiṇam ||21
manojavaṃ taṃ nirīkṣya vāmanaḥ prāha vismitaḥ
matto vṛṇu varaṃ kāmam ṛkṣādhīśa mahābala ||22
iti taṃ hṛṣṭavadano brahmāṃśo jāmbavān mudā
prāha bho cakradahanān mama mṛtyur bhaviṣyati ||23
ity ukte vāmanaḥ prāha kṛṣṇajanmani me tava
mokṣaś cakreṇa saṃbhinnaśirasaḥ saṃbhaviṣyati ||24
mama kṛṣanavatāre tu sūryabhaktasya bhūpateḥ
satrājitas tu maṇyarthe durvādaḥ samajāyata ||25
prasenasya mama bhrātur vadhas tu maṇihetukaḥ
siṃhāt tasyāpi maṇyarthe vadho jāmbavatā kṛtaḥ ||26
durvādabhayabhītasya kṛṣṇasyāmitatejasaḥ
maṇyanveṣaṇacittasya rkṣeṇābhūd raṇo bile ||27
sa nijeśaṃ parijñāya tac cakragrastabandhanam
mukto babhūva sahasā kṛṣanṃ paśyan salakṣmaṇam ||28
navadūrvādalaśyāmaṃ dṛṣṭvā prādān nijātmajām
tadā jāmbavatīṃ kanyāṃ pragṛhya maṇinā saha ||29
dvārakāṃ puram āgatya sabhāyāṃ mām upāhvayat
āhūya mahyaṃ pradadau maṇiṃ munigaṇārcitam ||30
so’haṃ tāṃ lajjayā tena maṇinā kanyakāṃ svakām
vivāhena dadāv asmai lāvaṇyāj jagṛhe maṇim ||31
tāṃ satyabhāmām ādāya maṇiṃ mayy arpya sa prabhuḥ
dvārakām āgatya punar gajāhvayam agād vibhuḥ ||32
gate kṛṣne māṃ nihatya śatadhanvāgrahīn maṇim
ato’ham iha jānāmi pūrvajanmani yat kṛtam ||33
mithyābhiśāpāt kṛṣṇasya naivābhūn mocanaṃ mama
ato’haṃ kalkirūpāya kṛṣnāya paramātmane |
dattvā ramāṃ satyabhāmārūpiṇīṃ yāmi sadgatim ||34
sudarśanāstraghātena maraṇaṃ mama kāṅkṣitam
maraṇe’bhūd iti jñātvā raṇe vāñchāmi mocanam ||35
ity asau jagatām īśaḥ kalkiḥ śvaśuraghātanam
śrutvaivādhomukhas tasthau hriyā dharmabhiyā prabhuḥ ||36
atyāścaryam apūrvam uttamam idaṃ śrutvā nṛpā vismitā 
lokāḥ saṃsadi harṣitā munigaṇāḥ kalker guṇākarṣitāḥ |
ākhyānaṃ paramādareṇa sukhadaṃ dhanyaṃ yaśasyaṃ paraṃ
śrīmadbhūpaśaśidhvajeritavaco mokṣapradaṃ cābhavan ||37
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe śaśidhvajeritacakramaraṇākhyānaṃ nāma trayodaśo’dhyāyaḥ ||13

<3.14>

caturdaśo’dhyāyaḥ | 

sūta uvāca |
tataḥ kalkir mahātejāḥ śvaśuraṃ taṃ śaśidhuajam
samāmantṛya vacaścitraiḥ saha bhūpair yayau hariḥ ||1
śaśidhvajo varaṃ labdhā yathākāmaṃ maheśvarīm
stutvā māyāṃ tyaktamāyaḥ sapriyaḥ prayayau vanam ||2
kalkiḥ senāgaṇaiḥ sārdhaṃ prayayau kāñcanīṃ purīm
girdurgāvṛtāṃ guptāṃ bhogibhir viṣavarsibiḥ ||3
vidārya durgaṃ sagaṇaḥ kalkiḥ parapurañjayaḥ
chittvā viṣāyudhān bāṇais tāṃ purīṃ dadṛśe’cyutaḥ ||4
maṇikāñcanacitrāḍhyāṃ nāgakanyāgaṇāvṛtām
haricandanavṛkṣāḍhyāṃ manujaiḥ parivarjitām ||5
vilokya kalkiḥ prahasan prāha bhūpān kim ity aho
sarpasye’yaṃ purī ramyā narāṇāṃ bhayadāyinī |
nāganārīgaṇākīrṇā kiṃ yāsyāmo vadantv iha ||6
iti kartavyatāvyagraṃ ramānāthaṃ hariṃ prabhum
bhūpāns tad anurūpāṃś ca khe vāgāhāśarīriṇī ||7
vilokya nemāṃ senābhiḥ pravaṣtuṃ bhos tvam arhasi
tvāṃ vinānye mariṣyanti viṣakanyādṛśād api ||8
ākāśavāṇīm ākarṇya kalkiḥ śukasahāyakṛt
yayāv ekaḥ khaḍgadharas turageṇa tvarānvitaḥ ||9
gatvā tāṃ dadṛśe vīro dhīrāṇāṃ dhairyanāśinīm
rūpeṇālakṣyalakṣmīśaṃ prāha prahasitānanā ||10
viṣakanyovāca |
saṃsāre’smin mama nayanayor vīkṣaṇakṣīṇadehā |
lokā bhūpāḥ kati kati hatā mṛtyum atyugravīryāḥ ||
sāhaṃ dīnāsurasuranaraprekṣaṇapremahīnā |
te netrābjadvayarasasudhāplāvitā tvāṃ namāmi ||11
kvāhaṃ viṣekṣaṇā dīnā kvāmṛtekṣaṇasaṅgamaḥ |
bhave’smin bhāgyahīnāyāḥ kenāho tasā kṛtaḥ ||12
kalkir uvāca |
kāsi kanyāsi suśroṇi kasmād eṣā gatis tava |
brūhi māṃ karmaṇā kena viṣanetraṃ tavābhavat ||13
viṣakanyovāca |
citragrīvasya bhāryāhaṃ gandharvasya mahāmate
sulocaneti vikhyātā patyur atyantakāmadā ||14
ekadāhaṃ vimānena patyā pīṭhena saṅgatā
gandhamādanakuñjeṣu reme kāmakalākulā ||15
tatra yakṣamuniṃ dṛṣṭvā vikṛtākāram āturam
rūpayauvanagarveṇa kaṭākṣeṇāha saṃmadāt ||16
sopālambhaṃ muniḥ śrutvā vacanaṃ ca mamāpriyam
śaśāpa māṃ krudhā tatra tenāhaṃ viṣadarśanā ||17
nikṣiptāhaṃ sarpapure kāñcanyāṃ nāginīgaṇe |
patihīnā daivahīnā carāmi viṣavarṣiṇī ||18
na jāne kena tapasā bhavad dṛṣṭipathaṃ gatā
tyaktaśāpāmṛtākṣāhaṃ patilokaṃ vrajāmy ataḥ ||19
aho teṣām astu śāpaḥ prasādo mā satām iha
patyuḥ śāpād rṣer mokṣāt tava pādābjadarśanam ||20
ity uktvā sā yayau svargaṃ vimānenārkavarcasā
kalkis tu tat purādhīśaṃ nṛpaṃ cakre mahāmatim ||21
amarṣas tat suto dhīmān sahasro nāma tat sutaḥ |
sahasrataḥ sutaś cāsīd rājā viśrutavān asiḥ ||22
bṛhannalanāṃ bhūpānāṃ saṃbhūtā yasya vaṃśajāḥ
taṃ manuṃ bhūpaśārdūlaṃ nānāmunigaṇair vṛtaḥ ||23
ayodhyāyāṃ cābhiṣicya mathurām agamad dhariḥ
tasyāṃ bhūpaṃ sūryaketum abhiṣicya mahāprabham ||24
bhūpaṃ cakre tato gatvā devāpiṃ vāraṇāvate 
aristhalaṃ vṛkasthalaṃ mākandañ ca gajāhvayam ||25
pañcadeśeśvaraṃ kṛtvā hariḥ śambhalam āyayau
śaumbhaṃ pauṇdraṃ pulindañ ca surāṣtraṃ magadhaṃ tathā
kaviprājñasumantebhyaḥ pradadau bhrātṛvatsalaḥ ||26
kīkaṭaṃ madhyakarṇāṭam andhram oḍraṃ kaliṅgakam
aṅgaṃ vaṅgaṃ svagotrebhyaḥ pradadau jagadīśvaraḥ ||27
svayaṃ śambhalamadhyasthaḥ kaṅkakena kalāpakān
deśaṃ viśākhayūpāya prādāt kalkiḥ pratāpavān ||28
colabarbarakarvākhyān dvārakādeśamadhyagān
putrebhyaḥ pradadau kalkiḥ kṛtavarmapuraskṛtān ||29
pitre dhanāni ratnāni dadau paramabhaktitaḥ
prajāḥ samāśvāsya hariḥ śambhalagrāmavāsinaḥ ||30
padmayā ramayā kalkir gṛhastho mumude bhṛśam
dharmaś catuṣpād abhavat kṛtapūrṇaṃ jagattrayam ||31
devā yathoktaphaladāś caranti bhuvi sarvataḥ |
sarvaśasyā vasumatī hṛṣṭapuṣṭajanāvṛtā |
śāṭhyacauryānṛtair hīnā ādhivyādhivivarjitā ||32
viprā vedavidaḥ sumaṅgalayutā nāryas tu cāryāvratāḥ |
pūjāhomaparāḥ pativratadharā yāgodyatāḥ kṣatriyāḥ |
vaiśyā vastuṣu dharmato vinimayaiḥ śrīviṣṇupūjāparāḥ |
śūdrās tu dvijasevanād dharikathālāpāḥ saparyāparāḥ ||33
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe
viṣakanyāmokṣakṛtadharmapravṛttikathanaṃ nāma caturdaśo’dhyāyaḥ ||14

<3.15>

pañcadaśo’dhyāyaḥ |

śaunaka uvāca |
śaśidhvajo mahārājaḥ stutvā mayāṃ gataḥ kṛtaḥ |
kā vā māyāstutiḥ sūta vada tattvavidāṃ vara |
yā tvat kathā viṣṇukathā vaktavyā sā viśuddhaye ||1
sūta uvāca |
śṛṇudhvaṃ munayaḥ sarve mārkaṇdeyāya pṛcchate |
śukaḥ prāha viśuddhātmā māyāstavam anuttamam ||2
tac chṛṇuṣva pravakṣyāmi yathādhītaṃ yathāśrutam
sarvakāmapradaṃ nṝṇāṃ pāpatāpavināśanam ||3
śuka uvāca |
bhallāṭanagaraṃ tyaktvā viṣṇubhaktaḥ śaśidhvajaḥ |
ātmasaṃsāramokṣāya māyāstavam alaṃ jagau ||4
śaśidhvaja uvāca |
oṃ hrīṃkārāṃ sattvasārāṃ viśuddhāṃ
brahmādīnāṃ mātaraṃ vedabodhyām |
tanvīṃ svāhāṃ bhūtatanmātrakakṣāṃ
vande vandyāṃ devagandharvasiddhaiḥ ||5
lokātītāṃ dvaitabhūtāṃ samīḍe
bhūtair bhavyāṃ vyāsasāmāsikādyaiḥ |
vidvadgītāṃ kālakallolalolāṃ
līlāpāṅgakṣiptasaṃsāradurgām ||6
pūrṇāṃ prāpyāṃ dvaitalabhyāṃ śaraṇyām
ādye śeṣe madhyato yā vibhāti |
nānārūpair devatiryaṅ manuṣyais
tām ādhārāṃ brahmarūpāṃ namāmy ||7
yasyā bhāsā trijagadbhāti bhūtair
na bhāty etat tadabhāve vidhātuḥ |
kālo daivaṃ karma copādhayo ye
tasyā bhāsā tāṃ viśiṣtāṃ namāmi ||8
bhūmau gandho rāsatāpsu pratiṣthā
rūpaṃ tejasv eva vāyau spṛśatvam |
khe śabdo vā yac cidābhāti nānā
tām abhyetāṃ viśvarūpāṃ namāmi ||9
sāvitrī tvaṃ brahmarūpā bhavānī
bhūteśasya śrīpateḥ śrīsvarūpā |
śacī śakrasyāpi nākeśvarasya
patnī śreṣthā bhāsi māye jagatsu ||10
balye bālā yuvatī yauvane tvaṃ
vārdhakye yā sthāvirā kālakalpā |
nānākārair yāgayogair upāsyā
jñānātītā kāmarūpā vibhāsi ||11
varenyā tvaṃ varadā lokasiddhyā
sādhvī dhanyā lokamānyā sukanyā |
caṇdī durgā kālikā kālikākhyā
nānādeśe rūpaveṣair vibhāsi ||12
tava caraṇasarojaṃ devi devādivandyaṃ
yadi hṛdayasaroje bhāvayantīha bhaktyā |
śrutiyugakuhare vā saṃśrutaṃ dharmasampaj
janayati jagadādye sarvasiddhiñ ca teṣām ||13
māyāstavam idaṃ puṇyaṃ śukadevena bhāṣitam
mārkaṇdeyād avāpyāpi siddhiṃ lebhe śaśidhvajaḥ ||14 
kokāmukhe tapas taptvā hariṃ dhyātvā vanāntare
sudarśanena nihato vaikuṇthaṃ śaraṇaṃ yayau ||15
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe māyāstavo nāma pañcadaśo’dhyāyaḥ ||15

<3.16>

ṣoḍaśo’dhyāyaḥ | 

sūta uvāca |
etad vaḥ kathitaṃ viprāḥ śaśidhvajavimokṣaṇam |
kalkeḥ kathām apratimāṃ śṛṇvantu vibudharṣbhāḥ ||1
vedo dharmaḥ kṛtayugaṃ devā lokāś carācarāḥ |
hṛṣtāḥ puṣtāḥ susaṃtuṣtāḥ kalkau rājani cābhavan ||2
nānādevādiliṅgeṣu bhūṣaṇair bhūṣiteṣu ca |
indrajālikavadvattikalpakāḥ pūjakā janāḥ ||3 
na santi māyāmohāḍhyāḥ pākhaṇdāḥ sādhuvañcakāḥ |
tilakācitasarvāṅgāḥ kalkau rājani kutracit ||4 
śambhale vasatas tasya padmayā ramayā saha |
prāha viṣṇuyaśāḥ putraṃ devān yaṣtuṃ jagaddhitān ||5
tac chrutvā prāha pitaraṃ kalkiḥ paramaharṣitaḥ |
vinayāvanato bhūtvā dharmakāmārthasiddhaye ||6
rājasūyair vājapeyair aśvamedhair mahāmakhaiḥ |
nānāyāgaiḥ karmatantrair īje kratupatiṃ harim ||7
kṛparāmavasiṣṭhādyair vyāsadhaumyākṛtavraṇaiḥ |
aśvatthāmamadhucchando mandapālair mahātmanaḥ ||8
gaṃgāyamunayor madhye snātvāvabhṛtham ādarāt 
dakṣiṇābhiḥ samabhyarcya brāhmaṇān vedapāragān ||9
carvyaiś coṣyaiś ca peyaiś ca pūgaśaṣkuliyāvakaiḥ |
madhumāṃsair mūlaphalair anyaiś ca vividhair dvijān ||10
bhojayām āsa vidhivat sarvakarmasamṛddhibhiḥ
yatra vahnir vṛtaḥ pāke varuṇo jadado marut ||11
pariveṣṭā dvijān kāmaiḥ sadannādyair atoṣayat
vādyair nṛtyaiś ca gītaiś ca pitṛyajñamahotsavaiḥ ||12
kalkiḥ kamalapatrākṣaḥ praharṣa pradado vasu |
strībālasthavirādibhyaḥ sarvebhyaś ca yathocitam ||13
rambhā tāladharā nandī hūhūr gāyati nṛtyati
dattvā dānāni pātrebhyo brāhmaṇebhyaḥ sa īśvaraḥ ||14
uvāsa tīre gaṃgāyāḥ pitṛvākyānumoditaḥ |
sabhāyāṃ viṣṇuyaśasaḥ pūrvarājakathāḥ priyāḥ ||15
kathayanto hasantaś ca harṣayanto dvijā budhāḥ |
tatrāgatas tumburuṇā nāradaḥ surapūjitaḥ ||16
taṃ pūjayām āsa mudā pitrā saha yathāvidhi |
tāv saṃpūjya viṣṇuyaśāḥ provāca vinayānvitaḥ |
nāradaṃ vaiṣṇavaṃ prītyā vīṇāpāṇiṃ mahāmunim ||17
viṣṇuyaśā uvāca |
aho bhāgyam aho bhāgyaṃ mama janmaśatārjitam |
bhavadvidhānāṃ pūrṇānāṃ yan me mokṣāya darśanam ||18
adyāgnayaś ca suhutās tṛptāś ca pitaraḥ param
devāś ca parisantuṣṭās tavāvekṣaṇapūjanāt ||19 
yat pūjāyāṃ bhavet pūjyo viṣṇur yan mama darśanam |
pāpasaṃghaṃ sparśanāc ca kim aho sādhusaṃgamaḥ ||20
sādhūnāṃ hṛdayaṃ dharmo vāco devāḥ sanātanāḥ |
karmakṣayāṇi karmāṇi yataḥ sādhur hariḥ svayam ||21
manye na bhautiko deho vaiṣṇavasya jagattraye |
yathāvatāre kṛṣṇasya sato duṣṭavinigrahe ||22
pṛcchāmi tvām ato brahman māyāsaṃsāravāridhau |
naukāyāṃ viṣṇubhaktyā ca karṇadhāro’si pārakṛt ||23
kenāhaṃ yātanāgārān nirvāṇapadam uttamam |
lapsyāmīha jagadbandho karmaṇā śarma tad vada ||24
nārada uvāca |
aho balavatī māyā sarvāścaryamayī śubhā |
pitaraṃ mātaraṃ viṣṇur naiva muñcati karhicit ||25
pūrṇo nārāyaṇo yasya sutaḥ kalkir jagatpatiḥ |
taṃ vihāya viṣṇuyaśā matto muktim abhipsati ||26
vivicyetthaṃ brahmasutaḥ prāha brahmayaśaḥsutam
vivikte viṣṇuyaśasaṃ brahmasaṃpadvivardhanam ||27
nārada uvāca |
dehāvasāne jīvaṃ sā dṛṣṭvā dehāvalambanam |
māyāha kartum icchantaṃ yan me tac chṛṇu mokṣadam ||28
vindhyādrau ramaṇī bhūtvā māyovāca yathecchayā ||29 
māyovāca |
ahaṃ māyā mayā tyaktaḥ kathaṃ jīvitum icchasi ||30
jīva uvāca |
nāhaṃ jīvāmy ahaṃ māye kāye’smiñ jīvanāśraye |
aham ity anyathā buddhir vinā dehaṃ kathaṃ bhavet ||31
māyovāca |
dehabandhe yathāśleṣās tathā buddhiḥ kathaṃ tava  |
māyādhīnāṃ vinā ceṣtāṃ viśiṣtāṃ te kuto vada ||32
jīva uvāca |
māṃ vinā prājñatā māye prakāśaviṣayaspṛhā ||33
māyovāca |
māyayā jīvati naraś ceṣṭate hatacetanaḥ |
niḥsāraḥ sāravad bhāti gajabhuktakapitthavat ||34
jīva uvāca |
mama saṃsargajātā tvaṃ nānānāmasvarūpiṇī |
māṃ vinindasi kiṃ mūḍhe svairiṇī svāminaṃ yathā ||35
mamābhāve tavābhāvaḥ prodyat sūrye tamo yathā |
māmāvarya vibhāsi tvaṃ raviṃ navaghano yathā ||36
līlābījakuśūlāsi mama māye jaganmaye |
nādyante madhyato bhāsi nānātvād indrajālavat ||37
evaṃ nirviṣayaṃ nityaṃ manovyāpāravarjitam |
abhautikam ajīvañ ca śarīraṃ vīkṣya sātyajat ||38
tyaktvā māṃ sā dadau śāpam iti loke tavāpriya |
na sthitir bhavitā kāṣṭhakuḍyopama kathañcana ||39
sā māyā tava putrasya kalker viśvātmanaḥ prabhoḥ |
tāṃ vijñāya yathā kāmaṃ cara gāṃ haribhāvanaḥ ||40
nirāśo nirmamaḥ śāntaḥ sarvabhogeṣu nispṛhaḥ |
viṇau jagad idaṃ jñātvā viṇur jagati vāsakṛt |
ātmanātmānam āveśya sarvato virato bhava ||41
evaṃ taṃ viṣṇuyaśasam āmantṛya ca munīśvarau |
kalkiṃ pradakṣiṇākṛtya jagmatuḥ kapilāśramam ||42
nāraderitam ākarṇya kalkiṃ sutam anuttamam |
nārāyaṇaṃ jagannāthaṃ vanaṃ viṣṇuyaśā yayau ||43
gatvā badarikāraṇyaṃ tapas taptvā sudāruṇam |
jīvaṃ bṛhati saṃyojya pūrṇas tatyāja bhautikam ||44
mṛtaṃ svāminam āliṅgya sumatiḥ snehaviklavā |
viveśa dahanaṃ sādhvī suveśair divi saṃstutā ||45
kalkiḥ śrutvā munimukhāt pitror niryāṇam īśvaraḥ |
sabāṣpanayanaṃ snehāt tayoḥ samakarotkriyām ||46
padmayā ramayā kalkiḥ śambhale suravāñchite |
cakāra rājyaṃ dharmātmā lokavedapuraskṛtaḥ ||47
mahendraśikharād rāmas tīrthaparyaṭanādṛtaḥ |
prāyāt kalker darśanārthaṃ śambhalaṃ tīrthatīrthakṛt ||48
taṃ dṛṣṭvā sahasotthāya padmayā ramayā saha |
kalkiḥ praharṣo vidhivat pūjāñ cakre vidhānavit ||49
nānārasair guṇamayair bhojayitvā vicitrite |
paryaṅke’narghavastrāḍhye śāyayitvā mudaṃ yayau ||50
taṃ bhuktavantaṃ viśrāntaṃ pādasaṃvāhanair gurum |
saṃtoṣya vinayāpannaḥ kalkir madhuram abravīt ||51
tava prasādāt siddhaṃ me guro traivargikañ ca yat |
śaśidhvajasutāyās tu śṛṇu rāma niveditam ||52
iti pativacanaṃ niśamya rāmā nijahṛdayepsitaputralābham iṣṭam |
vratajapaniyamair yamaiś ca kair vā mama bhavatīha mudāha jāmadagnyam ||53
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe
viṣṇuyaśaso mokṣo rāmadarśanaṃ ca nāma ṣoḍaśo’dhyāyaḥ ||16

<3.17>

saptadaśo’dhyāyaḥ |

jāmadagnyaḥ samākarṇya ramāṃ tāṃ putragardhinīm |
kalker abhimataṃ buddhvākārayad rukmiṇīvratam ||1
vratena tena ca ramā putrāḍhyā subhagā satī |
sarvabhogena saṃyuktā babhūva sthirayauvanā ||2
śaunaka uvāca |
vidhānaṃ brūhi me sūta vratasyāsya ca yat phalam |
purā kena kṛtaṃ dharmyaṃ rukmiṇīvratam uttamam ||3
sūta uvāca |
śṛṇu brahman rājaputrī śarmiṣthā vārṣaparvaṇī |
avagāhya saronīraṃ somaṃ haram apaśyata ||4
sā sakhībhiḥ parivṛtā devayānyā ca saṃgatā |
śambhubhītyā samutthāya paryadhād vasanaṃ drutam ||5
tatra śukrasya kanyāyā vastravyatyayamātmanaḥ |
saṃlakṣya kupitā prāha vasanaṃ tyaja bhikṣuki ||6
iti dānavakanyā sā dāsībhiḥ parivāritā |
tāṃ tasyā vāsasā baddhvā kūpe kṣiptvā gatā gṛham ||7
tāṃ magnāṃ rudatīṃ kūpe jalarthī nahuṣātmajaḥ |
kare spṛśya samuddhṛtya prāha kā tvaṃ varānane ||8
sā śukraputrī vasanaṃ paridhāya hriyā bhiyā |
śarmiṣṭhayā kṛtaṃ sarvaṃ prāha rājānam īkṣatī ||9
yayātis tad abhiprāyaṃ jñātvānuvrajya śobhanam |
āśvāsya tāṃ yayau gṛhaṃ tasyāḥ pariṇayādṛtaḥ ||10
sā gatvā bhavanaṃ śukraṃ prāha śarmiṣṭhayā kṛtam |
tac chrutvā kupitaṃ vipraṃ vṛṣaparvāha sāntvayan ||11
daṇdyaṃ māṃ daṇḍaya vibho kopo yady asti te mayi |
śarmiṣthāṃ vāpy apakṛtāṃ kuru yan manasepsitam ||12
rājānaṃ praṇataṃ pāde pitur dṛṣṭvā ruṣābravīt |
devayānī tv iyaṃ kanyā mama dāsī bhavatv iti ||13
samānīya tadā rājā dāsye tāṃ viniyujya saḥ |
yayau nijagṛhaṃ jñānī daivaṃ paramakaṃ smaran ||14
tataḥ śukras tam ānīya yayātiṃ pratilomakam |
tasmai dadau tāṃ vidhivad devayānīṃ tayā saha ||15
dattvā prāha nṛpaṃ vipro’py enāṃ rājasutāṃ yadā |
śayane hvayase sadyo jarā tvām upabhokṣyati ||16
śvkrasyaitad vacaḥ śrutvā rājā tāṃ varavarṇinīm |
adṛśyāṃ sthāpayām āsa devayānyanugāṃ bhiyā ||17
sā śarmiṣthā rājaputrī duḥkhaśokabhayākulā |
nityaṃ dāsīśatākīrṇā devayānīṃ tu sevate ||18
ekadā sā vanagatā rudatī jāhnavītaṭe |
viśvāmitraṃ muniṃ sā taṃ dadṛśe strībhir āvṛtam ||19
vratinaṃ puṇyagandhābhiḥ surūpābhiḥ suvāsitam |
kārayantaṃ vrataṃ mālyadhūpadīpopahārakaiḥ ||20
nirmāyāṣṭadalaṃ padmaṃ vedikāyāṃ sucihnitam |
rambhāpotaiś caturbhis tu catuṣkoṇaṃ virājitam ||21
vāsasā nirmitagṛhe svarṇapaṭtair vicitrite |
nirmitaṃ śrīvāsudevaṃ nānāratnavighaṭṭitam ||22
pauruṣeṇa ca sūktena nānāgandhodakaiḥ śubhaiḥ |
pañcāmṛtaiḥ pañcagavyair yathā mantrair dvijeritaiḥ ||23 
snāpayitvā bhadrapīṭhe karṇikāyāṃ prapūjayet |
pañcabhir daśabhir vāpi ṣoḍaśair upacārakaiḥ ||24
pādyam adhvaśramaharaṃ śītalaṃ sumanoharam |
paramānandajanakaṃ gṛhāṇa parameśvara ||25
dūrvācandanagandhāḍhyam arghyaṃ yuktaṃ prayatnataḥ |
gṛhāṇa rukmiṇīnātha prasannasya mama prabho ||26
nānātīrthodbhavaṃ vāri sugandhi sumanoharam |
gṛhāṇācamanīyaṃ tvaṃ śrīnivāsa śriyā saha ||27
nānākusumagandhāḍhyāṃ sūtragrathitam uttamam |
vakṣaḥśobhākaraṃ cāru mālyaṃ naya sureśvara ||28
tantusantānasandhānaracitaṃ bandhanaṃ hare |
gṛhāṇāvaraṇaṃ śuddhaṃ nirāvaraṇa sapriya ||29
yajñasūtram idaṃ deva prajāpativinirmitam |
gṛhāṇa vāsudeva tvaṃ rukmiṇyā ramayā saha ||30
nānāratnasamāyuktaṃ svarṇamuktāvighaṭṭitam |
priyayā saha deveśa gṛhāṇābharaṇaṃ mama ||31
dadhikṣīraguḍānnādipūpalaḍdukakhaṇḍakān |
gṛhāṇa rukmiṇīnātha sanāthaṃ kuru māṃ prabho ||32
karpūrāgurugandhāḍhyaṃ paramānandadāyakam |
dhūpaṃ gṛhāṇa varada vaidarbhyā priyayā saha ||33
bhaktānāṃ gehaśaktānāṃ saṃsāradhvāntanāśanam |
dīpam ālokaya vibho jagad ālokan ādara ||34
śyāmasundara padmākṣa pītāmbara caturbhuja |
prapannaṃ pāhi deveśa rukmiṇyā sahitācyuta ||35
iti tāsāṃ vrataṃ dṛṣṭvā muniṃ natvā suduḥkhitā |
śarmiṣthā miṣṭavacanā kṛtāñjalir uvāca tāḥ ||36
śarmiṣthovāca |
rājaputrīṃ durbhagāṃ māṃ svāminā parivarjitām |
trātum arhatha he devyo vratenānena karmaṇā ||37
śrutvā tu tā vacas tasyāḥ kāruṇyāc ca kiyat kivat |
pūjopakaraṇaṃ dattvā kārayām āsur ādarāt ||38
vrataṃ kṛtvā tu śarmiṣthā labdhvā svāminam īśvaram |
sūtvā putrān susantuṣṭā samabhūt sthirayauvanā ||39
sītā cāśokavanikāmadhye saramayā saha |
vrataṃ kṛtvā patiṃ lebhe rāmaṃ rākṣasanāśanam ||40
bṛhadaśvaprasādena kṛtvemaṃ draupadī vratam |
patiyuktā duḥkhamuktā babhūva sthirayauvanā ||41
tathā ramā site pakṣe vaiśākhe dvādaśīdine |
jāmadagnyād vrataṃ cakre pūrṇaṃ varṣacatuṣṭayam ||42
paṭṭasūtraṃ kare baddhvā bhojayitvā dvijān bahūn |
bhuktvā haviṣyaṃ kṣīrāktaṃ sumṛṣtaṃ svāminā saha ||43
bubhuje pṛthivīṃ sarvām apūrvāṃ svajanair vṛtā |
sā putrau suṣuve sādhvī meghamālabalāhakau ||44
devānām upakartārau yajñadānatapovrataiḥ |
mahotsāhau mahāvīryau subhagau kalkisampatau ||45
vratavaram iti kṛtvā sarvasampatsamṛddhyā
bhavati viditatattvā pūjitā pūrṇakāmā |
haricaraṇasarojadvandvabhaktyaikatānā
vrajati gatim apūrvāṃ brahmavijñair agamyām ||46
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe rukmiṇīvrataṃ nāma saptadaśo’dhyāyaḥ ||17

<3.18>

aṣṭādaśo’dhyāyaḥ | 

sūta uvāca |
etad vaḥ kathitaṃ viprā vrataṃ trailokyaviśrutam |
ataḥ paraṃ kalkikṛtaṃ karma yac chṛṇuta dvijāḥ ||1
śambhale vasatas tasya sahasraparivatsarāḥ |
vyatītā bhrātṛputrasvajñātisambandhibhiḥ saha ||2
śambhale śuśubhe śreṇī sabhāpaṇakacatvaraiḥ |
patākādhvajacitrāḍhyair yathendrasyāmarāvatī ||3
yatrāṣṭaṣaṣṭi tīrthānāṃ sambhavaḥ śambhale’bhavat |
mṛtyor mokṣaḥ kṣitau kalker akalkasya padāśrayāt ||4
vanopavanasantānanānākusumasaṃkulaiḥ |
śobhitaṃ śambhalaṃ grāmaṃ manye mokṣapadaṃ bhuvi ||5
tatra kalkiḥ purastrīṇāṃ nayanānandavardhanaḥ |
padmayā ramayā kāmaṃ rarāma jagatīpatiḥ ||6
surādhipapradattena kāmagena rathena vai |
nadīparvatakuñjeṣu dvīpeṣu parayā mudā ||7
ramamāṇo viśan padmāramādyābhī ramāpatiḥ |
divāniśaṃ na bubudhe straiṇaś ca kāmalampaṭaḥ ||8
padmāmukhāmodasarojaśīdhu vāsopabhogī suvilāsavāsaḥ |
prabhūtanīlendramaṇiprakāśe guhāviśeṣa praviveśa kalkiḥ ||9
padmā tu padmāśatarūparūpā ramā ca pīyūṣakalāvilāsā |
patiṃ praviṣtaṃ girigahvare te nārīsahasrākulite tv agātām ||10
padmā patiṃ prekṣya guhāniviṣtaṃ rantuṃ manojñā praviveśa paścāt |
ramābalāyūthasamanvitā tat paścād gatā kalkimaho’grakāmā ||11
tatrendranīlotpalagahvarānte kāntābhir ātmapratimābhir īśam |
kalkiñ ca dṛṣṭvā navanīradābhaṃ tataḥ sthitaṃ prastaravan mumoha ||12
ramā sakhībiḥ pramadābhir ārtā vilokayantī diśamākulākṣī |
padmāpi padmāśataśobhamānā viṣaṇnacittā na babhau sma cārtā ||13
bhūmau likhantī nijakajjalena kalkiṃ śukaṃ taṃ kucakuṃkumena |
kastūrikābhistu tad agram agre nirmāya cāliṅgya nanāma bhāvāt ||14
ramā kalālāpaparā stuvantī kāmārditā taṃ hṛdaye nidhāya |
dhyātvā nijālaṅkaraṇaiḥ prapūjya tasthau viṣaṇnā karuṇāvasannā ||15
kṣaṇāt samutthāya ruroda rāmā kalāpinaḥ kaṇthanibhaṃ svanātham |
hṛdopagūḍhaṃ na punaḥ pralabhya kāmārditety āha hare prasīda ||16
padmāpi nirmucya nijāṅgabhūṣāś cakāra dhūlīpaṭale vilāsam |
kaṇthañ ca kastūrikayāpi nīlaṃ kāmaṃ nihantuṃ śivatām upetya ||17
kalāvatīnāṃ kalayākalayya kṣīṇekṣaṇānāṃ harir ārtabandhuḥ |
kāmaprapūrāya sasāra madhye kalkiḥ priyāṇāṃ suratotsavāya ||18
tāḥ sādareṇātmapatiṃ manojñāḥ kareṇavo yūthapatiṃ yatheyuḥ |
sānandabhāvā viṣadānuvṛttā vaneṣu rāmāḥ paripūrṇakāmāḥ ||19
vaibhrājake caitrarathe supuṣpe sunandane mandarakandarānte |
reme sa rāmābhir udāratejā rathena bhāsvatkhagamena kalkiḥ ||20
padmāmukhābjāmṛtapānamatto ramāsamāliṅganavāsaraṅgī |
varāṃganānāṃ kucakuṃkumākto ratiprasaṃge viparītayuktaḥ |
mukhe vidaṣṭā rasanāvaśiṣṭāmodaḥ sa kalkir na hi veda deham ||21
ramāsamānāḥ puruṣottamaṃ taṃ vakṣojamadhye vinidāya dhīrāḥ |
parasparāśleṣaṇajātahāsā remur mukundaṃ vilasac charīrāḥ ||22
tataḥ sarovaraṃ tvarā striyo yayuḥ klamajvarāḥ
priyeṇa tena kalkinā vanāntare vihāriṇā |
saraḥ praviśya padmayā vimoharūpayā tayā
jalaṃ dadur varāṅganāḥ kareṇavo yathā gajam ||23
iti ha yuvatilīlā lokanāthakh sa kalkiḥ
priyayuvatiparītaḥ padmayā rāmayādyaḥ |
nijaramaṇavinodaiḥ śikṣayaṃllokavargāñ
jayati vibudhabhartā śambhale vāsudevaḥ ||24
ye śṛṇvanti vadanti bhāvacaturā dhyāyanti santaḥ sadā
kalkeḥ śrīpuruṣottamasya caritaṃ karṇāmṛtaṃ sādarāḥ |
teṣāṃ no sukhayaty ayam muraripor dāsyābhilāṣaṃ vinā
saṃsāraḥ parimocanañ ca paramānandāmṛtāmbhonidheḥ ||25
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe kalkivarṇanaṃ nāmāṣṭādaśo’dhyāyaḥ ||18

samājagmuḥ pramuditāḥ śambhalaṃ surapūjitam ||2

tejonidhiṃ prapannānāṃ janānām abhayapradam ||3
nīlajīmūtasaṃkāśaṃ dīrghapīvarabāhukam |
kirīṭenārkavarṇena sthiravidyunnibhena tam ||4
śobhamānaṃ dyumaṇinā kuṇḍalenābhiśobhinā |
saharṣālapavikasadvadanaṃ smitaśobhitam ||5
kṛpākaṭākṣavikṣepaparikṣiptavipakṣakam |
tārahārollasadvakṣaścandrakāntamaṇiśriyā ||6
kumudvatīmodavahaṃ sphuracchakāyudhāmbaram |
sarvadānandasandoharasollasitavigraham ||7
nānāmaṇigaṇodyotadīpitaṃ rūpam adbhutam |
dadṛśur devagandharvā ye cānye samupāgatāḥ ||8

kalkiṃ kamalapatrākṣaṃ tuṣṭuvuḥ paramādarāt ||9
devā ūcuḥ |
jayāśeṣasaṃkleśakakṣaprakīrṇānaloddāmasaṃkīrṇahīśa deveśa viśveśa bhūteśa bhāvaḥ |
tavānanta cāntaḥ sthito’ṅgāptaratnaprabhābhātapādājitānantaśakte ||10

tyaktvā śāsitabhūkhaṇdaṃ satyadharmāvirodhataḥ ||12
kalkis teṣām iti vacaḥ śrutvā paramaharṣitaḥ |
pātramitraiḥ parivṛtaś cakāra gamane matim ||13

rajye nikṣipya sahasā dharmiṣthān prakṛtipriyān ||14
tataḥ prajāḥ samāhūya kathayitvā nijāḥ kathāḥ |
prāha tān nijaniryāṇaṃ devānām uparodhataḥ ||15

prajā ūcuḥ |

prayayau klinnahṛdayaḥ patnībhyāṃ sahito vanam ||19
himālayaṃ munigaṇair ākīrṇaṃ jāhnavījalaiḥ |
paripūrṇaṃ devagaṇaiḥ sevitaṃ manasaḥ priyam ||20

uṣitvā jāhnavītīre sasmārātmānam ātmanā ||21

babhau sūryasahasrāṇāṃ tejorāśisamadyutiḥ ||22
śaṃkhacakragadāpadmaśārṅgādyaiḥ samabhiṣṭutaḥ |
nānālaṅkaraṇānāñ ca samalaṅkaraṇākṛtiḥ ||23
vavṛṣus taṃ surāḥ puṣpaiḥ kaustubhāmuktakandharam |
sugandhikusumāsārair devadundubhiniḥsvanaiḥ ||24
tuṣṭuvur mumuhuḥ sarve lokāḥ sasthāṇujaṃgamāḥ |

dharmaḥ kṛtayugaṃ kalker ājñayā pṛthivītale |
niḥsapatnau susukhinau bhūlokaṃ ceratuś ciram ||27
devāpiś ca maruḥ kāmaṃ kalker ādeśakāriṇau |
prajāḥ saṃpālayantau tu bhuvaṃ jugupatuḥ prabhū ||28
viśākhayūpabhūpālaḥ kalker niryāṇam īdṛśam |
śrutvā svaputraṃ viṣaye nṛpaṃ kṛtvā gato vanam ||29
anye nṛpatayo ye ca kalker virahakarṣitāḥ |
taṃ dhyāyanto japantaś ca viraktāḥ syur nṛpāsane ||30
iti kalker anantasya kathāṃ bhuvanapāvanīm |
kathayitvā śukaḥ prāyān naranārāyaṇāśramam ||31
mārkaṇdeyādayo ye ca munayaḥ praśamāyanāḥ |
śrutvānubhāvaṃ kalkes te taṃ dhyāyanto jagur yaśaḥ ||32
yasyānuśāsanād bhūmau nādharmiṣthāḥ prajājanāḥ |

nirmatsarāḥ sadānandā babhūvur jīvajātayaḥ ||34
ity etat kathitaṃ kalker avatāraṃ mahodayam |
dhanyaṃ yaśasyam āyuṣyaṃ svargyaṃ svastyayanaṃ param ||35
śokasantāpapāpaghnaṃ kalivyākulanāśanam |
sukhadaṃ mokṣadaṃ loke vāṃchitārthaphalapradam ||36

śrīkalker avatāravākyam amalaṃ bhaktipradaṃ śrīhareḥ
śuśrūṣuḥ punar āha sādhuvacasā gaṃgāstavaṃ satkṛtaḥ ||38
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate tṛtīyāṃśe kalkiniryāṇo nāmonaviṃśatitamo’dhyāyaḥ ||19

ṛṣaya ūcuḥ |
iyaṃ surataraṃgiṇī bhavanavāridhestāriṇī
stutā haripadāmbujād upagatā jagatsaṃsadaḥ |
sumeruśikharāmarapriyajalā malakṣālanī
prasannavadanā śubhā bhavabhayasya vidrāviṇī ||4
bhagīrathamathānugā surakarīṃdradarpāpahā
maheśamukuṭaprabhā giriśiraḥpatākā sitā |
surāsuranaroragair ajabhavācyutaiḥ saṃstutā
vimuktiphalaśālinī kaluṣanāśinī rājate ||5
pitāmahakamaṇḍaluprabhavamuktibījālatā
śrutismṛtigaṇastutādvijakulālavālāvṛtā |
sumeruśikharābhidā nipatitā trilokāvṛtā
sudharmaphalaśālinī sukhapalāśinī rājate ||6
caradvihagamālinī sagaravaṃśamuktipradā munīṃdravaranandinī divi matā ca mandākinī |
sadā duritanāśinī vimalavārisaṃdarśanapraṇāmaguṇakīrtanādiṣu jagatsu saṃrājate ||7
mahābhidhasutāṅganā himagirīśakūṭastanī
saphenajalahāsinīsitamarālasaṃcāriṇī |
calallaharisatkarā varasarojamālādharā
rasollasitagāminī jaladhikāminī rājate ||8
kvacit kalakalasvanā kvacid adhīrayādogaṇā
kvacin munigaṇaiḥ stutā kvacid anantasaṃpūjitā |
kvacid ravikarojjvalā kvacid udagrapātākulā
kvacij janavigāhitā jayati bhīṣmamātā satī ||9
sa eva kuśalo janaḥ praṇamatīha bhagīrathīṃ
sa eva tapasāṃ nidhir japati jāhnavīmādarāt |
sa eva puruṣottamaḥ smarati sādhu mandākinīṃ
sa eva vijayī prabhuḥ surataraṃgiṇīṃ sevate ||10
tavāmalajalācitaṃ khagaśṛgālamīnakṣataṃ
calal laharilolitaṃ ruciratīrajambālitam |
kadā nijavapur mudā suranaroragaiḥ saṃstuto
‘py ahaṃ tripathagāmini priyamatīva paśyāmy aho ||11
tvat tīre vasatiṃ tavāmalajalasnānaṃ tava prekṣaṇaṃ
tvan nāmasmaraṇaṃ tavodayakathāsaṃlāpanaṃ pāvanam |
gaṃge me tava sevanaikanipuṇo’pyānanditaś cādṛtaḥ
stutvā tvad gatapātaco bhuvi kadā śāntaś cariṣyāmy aham ||12
ity etad rṣibhiḥ proktaṃ gaṃgāstavam anuttamam |
svargyaṃ yaśasyam āyuṣyaṃ paṭhanāc chravaṇād api ||13
sarvapāpaharaṃ pusāṃ balam āyur vivardhanam |
prātarmadhyāhnasāyāhne gaṃgāsānnidhyatā bhavet ||14 
ity etad bhārgavākhyānaṃ śukadevān mayā śrutam |
paṭhitaṃ śrāvitaṃ cātṛ puṇyaṃ dhanyaṃ yaśaskaram ||15
avatāraṃ mahāviṣnoḥ kalkeḥ paramam adbhutam |
paṭhatāṃ śṛṇvatāṃ bhaktyā sarvāśubhavināśanam ||16
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe gaṃgāstavo nāma viṃśatitamo’dhyāyaḥ ||20

ekaviṃśo’dhyāyaḥ |

atrāpi śukasaṃvādo mārkaṇdeyena dhīmatā |
adharmavaṃśakathanaṃ kaler vivaraṇaṃ tataḥ ||1
devānāṃ brahmasadanaprayāṇaṃ gobhuvā saha |
brahmaṇo vacanād viṣṇor janma viṣṇuyaśogṛhe ||2
sumatyāṃ svāṃśakair bhrātṛcaturbhiḥ śambhale pure |
pituḥ putreṇa saṃvādas tathopanayanaṃ hareḥ ||3
putreṇa saha saṃvāso vedādhyayanam uttamam |
śastrāstrāṇāṃ parijñānaṃ śivasaṃdarśanaṃ tataḥ ||4
kalkeḥ stavaṃ śivapuro varalābhaḥ śukāpanam |
śambhalāgamanaṃ cakre jñātibhyo varakīrtanam ||5
viśākhayūpabhūpena nijasarvātmavarṇanam |
mahābhāgyād brāhmaṇānāṃ śukasyāgamanaṃ tataḥ ||6
kalkinā śukasaṃvādaḥ siṃhalākhyānam uttamam | 
śivadattavarā padmā tasyā bhūpasvayaṃvare ||7
darśanād bhūpasaṃghānāṃ strībhāvaparikīrtanam |
tasyā viṣādaḥ kalkes tu vivāhārthaṃ samudyamaḥ ||8
śukaprasthāpanaṃ dautye tayā tasyāpi darśanam |
śukapadmāparicayaḥ śrīviṣṇoḥ pūjanādikam ||9
pādādidehadhyānañ ca keśāntaṃ parivarṇitam |
śukabhūṣaṇadānañ ca punaḥ śukasamāgamaḥ ||10
kalkeḥ padmāvivāhārthaṃ gamanaṃ darśanaṃ tayoḥ |
jalakrīḍāprasaṅgena vivāhas tadanantaram ||11
puṃstvaprāptiś ca bhūpānāṃ kalker darśanam ātrataḥ |
anantāgamanaṃ rājñā saṃvādas tena saṃsadi ||12
ṣaṇdhatvād ātmano janma karma cātra śivastavaḥ |
mṛte pitari tad viṣnoḥ kṣetre māyāpradarśanam ||13
atrākhyānam anantasya jñānavairāgyavaibhavam |
rājñāṃ prayāṇaṃ kalkeś ca padmayā saha śambhale ||14
viśvakarmavidhānañ ca vasatiḥ padmayā saha |
jñātibhrātṛsuhṛtputraiḥ senābhir buddhanigrahaḥ ||15
kathitaś cātra teṣāñ ca strīṇāṃ saṃyodhanāśrayaḥ |
tato’tra vālakhilyānāṃ munīnāṃ svanivedanam ||16
saputrāyāḥ kuthodaryā vadhaś cātra prakīrtitaḥ |
haridvāragatasyāpi kalker munisamāgamaḥ ||17
sūryavaṃśasya kathanaṃ somasya ca vidhānataḥ |
śrīrāmacaritaṃ cāru sūryavaṃśānuvarṇane ||18
devāpeś ca maroḥ saṃgo yuddhāyātra prakīrtitaḥ |
mahāghoravane kokavikokavinipātanam ||19
bhallāṭagamanaṃ tatra śayyākarṇādibhiḥ saha |
yuddhaṃ śaśidhvajenātra suśāntābhaktikīrtanam ||20
yuddhe kalker ānayanaṃ dharmasya ca kṛtasya ca |
suśāntāyāḥ stavas tatra ramodvāhas tu kalkinā ||21
sabhāyāṃ pūrvakathanaṃ nijagṛdhratvakāraṇam |
mokṣaḥ śaśidhvajasyātra bhaktiprārthayitur vibhoḥ ||22
viṣakanyāmocanañ ca nṛpāṇāmabhiṣecanam |
māyāstavaḥ śambhaleṣu nānāyajñādisādhanam ||23
nāradād viṣṇuyaśaso mokṣaś cātra prakīrtitaḥ |
kṛtadharmapravṛttiś ca rukmiṇī vratakīrtanam ||24
tato vihāraḥ kalkeś ca putrapautrādisambhavaḥ |
kathito devagandharvagaṇāgamanam atra hi ||25
tato vaikuṇthagamanaṃ viṣnoḥ kalker ihoditam |
śukaprasthānam ucitaṃ kathayitvā kathāḥ śubhāḥ ||26
gaṃgāstotram iha proktaṃ purāṇe munisaṃmatam |
jagatām ānandakaraṃ purāṇaṃ paṃcalakṣaṇam ||27
sakalkasiddhidaṃ lokaiḥ ṣaṭ sahasraṃ śatādhikam |
sarvaśāstrārthatattvānāṃ sāraṃ śrutimanoharam ||28
caturvargapradaṃ kalkipurāṇaṃ parikīrtitam |
pralayānte harimukhān niḥsṛtaṃ lokavistṛtam ||29
aho vyāsena kathitaṃ dvijarūpeṇa bhūtale |
viṣṇor kalker bhagavataḥ prabhāvaṃ paramādbhutam ||30
ye bhaktyātra purāṇasāram amalaṃ śrīviṣṇubhāvāplutaṃ
śṛṇvantīha vadanti sādhusadasi kṣetre sutīrthāśrame |
dattvā gāṃ turagaṃ gajaṃ gajavaraṃ svarṇaṃ dvijāyādarāt
vastrālaghgkaraṇaiḥ prapūjya vidhivanmuktās tu evottamāḥ ||31
śrutvā vidhānaṃ vidhivadbrāhmaṇo vedapāragaḥ |
kṣatriyo bhūpatir vaiśyo dhanī śūdro mahān bhavet ||32
putrārthī labhate putraṃ dhanārthī labhate dhanam |
vidyārthī labhate vidyāṃ paṭhanāc chravaṇād api ||33
ity etat puṇyam ākhyānaṃ lomaharṣaṇajo muniḥ |
śrāvayitvā munīn bhaktyā yayau tīrthāṭanādṛtaḥ ||34
śaunako munibhiḥ sārdhaṃ sūtam āmantṛya dharmavit |
puṇyāraṇye hariṃ dhyātvā brahma prāpa saharṣibhiḥ ||35
lomaharṣaṇajaṃ sarvapurāṇajñaṃ yatavratam |
vyāsaśiṣyaṃ munivaraṃ taṃ sūtaṃ praṇamāmy aham ||36
ālokya sarvaśāstrāṇi vicārya ca punaḥ punaḥ |
idam eva suniṣpannaṃ dhyeyo nārāyaṇaḥ sadā ||37
vede rāmāyaṇe caiva purāṇe bhārate tathā |
ādāv ante ca madhye ca hariḥ sarvatra gīyatee ||38
sajalajaladadeho vātavegaikavāhaḥ
karadhṛtakaravālaḥ sarvalokaikapālaḥ |
kalikulavanahantā satyadharmapraṇetā
kalayatu kuśalaṃ vaḥ kalkirūpaḥ sa bhūpaḥ ||39
iti śrīkalkipurāṇe’nubhāgavate bhaviṣye tṛtīyāṃśe ekaviṃśo’dhyāyaḥ ||21
