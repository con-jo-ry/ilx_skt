|| śrīḥ ||
śrīmadānandatīrthabhāgavatatpādapraṇītasarvamūlagranthāḥ 
tithinirṇayaḥ|| śrīmaddhanumadbhīmamadhvāntargatarāmakṛṣṇavedavyāsātmakaśrīlakṣmīhayagrīvāya namaḥ ||
viṣṇuṃ viśveśvaraṃ natvā tadupoṣaṇaśuddhaye |
mūlagranthānusāreṇa kriyate tithinirṇayaḥ || 1 ||
bhūśrībhinnākicintyo nāt kalyabdāt kālavarddhitāt |
garuḍadhyeyavākyāptaṃ tyaktvā sauraṃ vṛthāphalam || 2 ||
rāśayādyaṃ maddhyamaṃ kuryād goghnād dhīsūnunāgajāḥ |
kalāstyaktvā dhruvaṃ kuryād deśādhāraharārcakam || 3 ||
anantavṛddhād baudhāṅgatulye nenduḥ śukāhatāt |
prājñāñjalibhṛdāptonastāraśobhātinākinā || 4 ||
dinebhyo rāgarāgāptaścāndrāccaḥ syānnibhāharāt |
jagatsenāṅgalabdhonaḥ śreṣṭhacintyombunārcane || 5 ||
laṅkāsāmyādirekhāyāḥ pūrvapaścimadeśayoḥ |
grahaṇāṃ maddhyasaṃskāraliptā ṛṇadhanaṃ kramāt || 6 ||
pāpaghnādadhvasaṅkhyānādarkkalabdhaviliptikāḥ |
arkkadorjyophalācchuddhā yathārke tatphalā tathā || 7 ||
arkadorjyāphalācchuddhāt yatha'rke tatphalāt tathā
goghnadivyaprajābhyāṃ ca viliptāścandanābjayoḥ || 8 ||
deśadoḥśuddhaye dānakṣviṣṇudyāṃ syurviliptikāḥ |
uccaṃ sūryasya niyataṃ dṛṣṭā strībhāgarāśayaḥ || 9 ||
śarīranut dhībhavanaḥ kathañcano nalījano mānapaṭuḥ śukālayaḥ |
nirāmayo dhīḥpathiko ...budhonaraḥ suptaparaḥ kalāvirāṭ || 10 ||
mahāśaro dūrasaro dhamīhariḥ hasandhuro vedanagaḥ susaṅkulaḥ |
tamaḥkhagāḥ pārabalaṃ rasābalaṃ dhanāvaliḥ kārabhṛgurjagadbhagaḥ || 11 ||
imāścaturviṃśatijyāḥ sphuṭatvāyārkasomayoḥ |
caturviṃśativākyāni trirāśīnāmimān viduḥ || 12 ||
śubhāṅgaparimāṇena yadi jyārddhaṃ na pūryate |
varttamānajyayā hitvā murāruphalasaṅgrahaḥ || 13 ||
rāśicakraṃ catuṣpādayojanodvipādayoḥ |
atītānāgatau bhāgau bhuja ityucyate budhaiḥ || 14 ||
socconārkābjayorddorjyo gosadbhyāṃ varddhitāḥ kramāt |
ajalabdhakarāḥ svasvagolayoḥ saṛṇaṃ dhanam || 15 ||
tadekadinagā liptā grahāṇāṃ svasvabhuktayaḥ |
kalyabdaugho dhenubhavo yuktaḥ saurairvṛthāphalaiḥ |
etasmānmāpatelabdhaṃ rāśyādyāyanamucyate || 16 ||
prabhāratnaṃ dhīsavanaṃ gānasthānaṃ janendhanam |
dehinityaṃ sugaprāyaṃ sāvalokyaṃ taṭidvapuḥ || 17 ||
navabhāryeti vākyāni jño'nantoṛtuhārakaḥ |
taddorjyāliptikā bhānau yuktvā tyaktvā'tha golayoḥ || 18 ||
arkapūjyādivākyoktadorviliptāścaroditāḥ |
arkaḥ pūjyaḥ sudhākāro ratikrīḍo nutaḥ prabhuḥ || 19 ||
alaṅkṛṣṇo hitoddeśo gatibhūtaḥ smarājitaḥ |
śaśidhāteti vākyāni jñonanto'tra tu hārakaḥ || 20 ||
tadekadinagā liptā grahāṇāṃ svasvabhuktayaḥ |
carārddhāt svasvabhuktighnādanantāṅgahṛtāḥ kalāḥ |
ṛṇaṃ prātarddhanaṃ sāyamuttare dakṣiṇe'nyathā || 21 ||
deśāntaradorvivarajasaṃskāravidhirvidhīyate madhye |
caradalasaṃskāravidhiḥ sphuṭakriyānantaraṃ sadbhiḥ || 22 ||
saddhiḥ candrāt pūrvoktasaṃskārakṛtād dinapatiṃ tyajet |
śeṣaṃ ṣaṣṭyā ghaṭībhistu vibhajyāptā gatā tithiḥ || 23 ||
varttamānadvādaśabhirliprikābhiharettithim |
gātāstu nāḍikā jñeyāḥ śiṣṭā liptāḥ prakīrtitāḥ || 24 ||
ekā'tidvādaśīvṛddhau no ced vṛddhau tu ṣoḍaśa |
dvyekaliptī same hnāse catuṣkāduttaraṃ tvidam || 25 ||
ativṛddhāvaṣṭayugaṃ vṛddhāvaṣṭa same catuḥ |
dvyekaliptī same hnāse catuṣkāduttaraṃ tvidam || 26 ||
udavyavyāpinī darśā paurṇṇamāsī tu vārāhe iti vidhya |
maddhyāhṛnavyāpinī śroṇā upoṣyā viṣṇutatparaiḥ || 27 ||
upavāsaphalaprepsurjjahyād bhuktacatuṣṭayam |
pūrvāpare tu sāyāhne sāmayampratastu maddhyame || 28 ||
kalārddhaṃ dvādaśīṃ dṛṣṭvā niśīthādūrdhvameva tu |
āmaddhyāhnāḥ kriyāḥ sarvāḥ karttavyāḥ śambhuśāsanāt || 29 ||
|| iti śrīmadānandatīrthabhagavatpādaviracitaḥ tithinirṇayaḥ samāptaḥ ||
