kecittu `bhūtale ghaṭo nāsti' `bhūtale na ghaṭaḥ' ityādipratyakṣaśābdabodhādikaṃ pūrvamatoktarītyaiva| parantu kāraṇībhūtajñānantu `bhūtale ghaṭo'sti' `bhūtale ghaṭaḥ' ityākārakamāroparūpaṃ nāpekṣyate| mūlakṛṭṭīkākṛccaraṇaistatkāraṇatāyāḥ kutrāpyanuktatvānniryuktikatvācca| kvaciṭṭippaṇyādau taduktirmatāntarānusāreṇānirbhireṇaiva| viśṛṅkhalaghaṭabhūtalādyupasthityaiva `bhūtalavṛttighaṭapratiyogikābhāvaḥ' iti `bhūtalādhikaraṇakaghaṭakartṛkasattābhāvaḥ' ityādyākārakajñānaṃ jāyate| tāvatā'pyaprāmāṇikasya viśiṣṭasyābhāvāṃśe bhānādabhāvasyāprāmāṇikapratiyogikatvopapattiḥ|
nanvevaṃ tarhyabhāvajñāna evāprāmāṇikabāvanena kāraṇībhūtajñānena kathamatroktaṃ pratiyogitvalakṣaṇaṃ tatra sambhavatīti cenna| niṣedhatvāvagāhijñāne tadatiriktadharmāntarabhānasyānubhavaviruddhatvenābhāvāṃśe nañullekhapratītau sattvāderabhānena tatra sattvādhikaraṇaviśeṣavṛttitvādisandehānutpatteranubhavasiddhāyā nirvāhāya taddvitīyakṣaṇe sattvādyavagāhipratītyantaraṃ niyamenāṅgīkartavyam| tathā ca `bhūtale ghaṭo nāsti' iti jñānottaraṃ niyamena jñānasya `bhūtalavṛttighaṭābhāvavadbhūtalam' ityākārakasya dvitīyasyābhāvajñānapadena dhartavyatayā tadupayuktaṃ yat `bhūtale ghaṭo nāsti' iti jñānaṃ tadviṣayatāyā viśiṣṭāprāmāṇike sattvāttatra lakṣaṇopapattiḥ| `abhāvajñānopayogijñānaviṣayatvam' ityatrābhāvajñānopayogītyetat dvitīyajñānopayogiprathamajñānasya paricāyakam| tathā ca nañullekhaprayojakaviṣayatvameva pratiyogitvam| tatra pūrvajñānīyāṃ nañullekhaprayojakībhūtaniṣedhatvaviṣayatānirūpitāmabhāvavaviṣayatāmādāyātivyāptivāraṇāya tadbhinnatvārthakaṃ abhāvaniṣṭhaviśeṣyatānirūpitaprakāratārūpatvārthakaṃ vā viśeṣapadamapi sārthakam| tādṛśaviṣayatārūpapratiyogitāyāḥ saṃsargatvaṃ vā pratiyogipadapravṛttinimittatvaṃ veti sarvā'pi pūrvoktarītiranusandheyā| etena prācāṃ prāmāṇikeṣvapi ghaṭādiṣu śāstre bahuśaḥ pratiyogitvavyavahāro'pi nirvyalīkaḥ| `bhūtale ghaṭo nāsti' iti prāthamikajñānajanakasya `ghaṭaḥ' iti viśṛṅkhalajñānasya viṣayatārūpāmukhyapratiyogitāmādāya bhrātṛsute sutatvavyavahārasyevāmukhyasya tādṛśavyavahārasyasambhavāt bhūtalādau tādṛśavyavahārasya vāraṇāya viśeṣapadaṃ tadanyatvādyarthakam| na ca bhūtalādau gauṇavyavahārakaraṇe bījābhāva iti vācyam| tadicchāyā eva niyāmakatvāt| ārope satīti nyāyenoktaśaṅkāyā evāyogācca|
evañca `śuktirajate sattvaṃ nāsti' `ghaṭe asattvaṃ nāsti' iti jñānotpattaye `śuktirajate sattvam' `ghaṭe asattvam' iti jñānasya pūrvamanapekṣaṇāt na tajjñānavato bhrāntatvamasmākamāpadyate| niṣedhāṃśe'prakāratayā sato'sattvāvagāhitvasyāsataḥ sattvāvagāhitvasyaiva bhrāntitvarūpatvāt|
na ca bhūtalaghaṭādīnāṃ viśṛṅkhalajñānena bhūtalavṛttitvaviśiṣṭaghaṭatvāvacchinnapratiyogitākābhāvabuddhiḥ kathamupapadyatām| pratiyogitāvacchedakaviśiṣṭapratiyogijñānarūpakāraṇābhāvāt| anyathā `vahnirmahānasīyaśca' iti viśṛṅkhalajñānottaraṃ `mahānasīyavahnirnāsti' iti ghaṭanirvikalpakottaraṃ `ghaṭo nāsti' iti dhiyaścāpatteriti vācyam| vakṣyamāṇarītyā pratiyogitāvacchedakatvādiprakriyāyā aprāmāṇikatayoktaniyamāsiddheḥ| `vahnirna mahānasīyaḥ' iti niścayavatāmapi `mahānasīyavahnirnāstyeva' ityādipratīterapalapitumaśakyatayā'bhāvabuddhau pratiyogyaṃśe sākṣātparamparayā bhāsamānadharmapuraskāreṇa pratiyogijñānasyānāvaśyakatvam| `vahnirmahānasīyaśca' iti viśṛṅkhalajñānottaramasaṃsargāgrahavatāṃ `vahnirmahānasīyaḥ' iti jñānodayadvārā `mahānasīyavahnirnāsti' iti buddhirjāyate tṛtīyakṣaṇa iti tvayā svīkriyate| tatra dvitīyakṣaṇa eva `mahānasīyavahnirnāsti' iti buddhirjāyata iti mayā svīkriyate| kṣaṇavilambasya śapathanirṇeyatvāttādṛśajñānāntarākalpanāprayuktalāghavācca| yatra tu `mahānasīyavahniḥ' iti viśṛṅkhalajñānottaraṃ `mahānasīyavahniḥ' iti jñānasambhave'pi anubhavasiddhāyā `mahānasīyavahnirnāsti' iti buddhyanutpatterupapattiryadbalena bhavatopapādyate mayā'piri tenaivopapādyate| anayaiva rītyā ghaṭanirvikalpakottaraṃ ghaṭo nāstīti buddhyāpattirapi nirastā| siddhānte nirvikalpakābhāvāccetyāhuḥ|
nanu `śaśaviṣāṇaṃ nāsti' iti pratītyā'satpratiyogikābhāvavat `atra bhūtale ghaṭo nāsti' iti pratītyā prāmāṇikaghaṭapratiyogiko'bhāvaḥ bhūtalavṛttitvena pratīyata iti kuto na svīkriyate| tatra tatra vidyamānaṭīkāvākyāni aprāmāṇikapratiyogikatvaniyamavatpratīyamānāni kathañcit yojayituṃ śakyānīti na tadvirodho'pi| na cātyantābhāvapadenābhāve ātyantikatvaṃ pratīyate| ātyantikatvañca deśakālaparicchedarāhityameva| tataścātyantābhāvasya satpratiyogikatve'bhāvasya pratiyoginā saha virodhātpratiyogyadhikaraṇadeśāvṛttitvaprāptyā deśaparicchedasya prāptyā ātyantikatvaṃ bhajyata iti asatpratiyogikatvaniyamaḥ svīkriyata iti vācyam| doṣavatsvapi jīveṣu bhagavatīvāprāmāṇikadoṣātyantābhāvasattvena nirdoṣatvalakṣaṇasyātivyāptimāśaṅkya `pratiyogyārope yatpradhānaṃ tenāpi sākamabhāvasya virodhāt' ityādigranthenāprāmāṇikapratiyogikasyāpyabhāvasya deśapraricchedamaṅgīkṛtyaiva vaṃśapallyācāryairativyāpteḥ parihṛtatvena ācāryairapi `prākpradhvaṃsasadātvena' iti `pūrvāparasadātvena' iti cātyantābhāvasya kālāvadhirāhityamātrasyoktatvena deśaparicchedarāhityasyā'tyantikapadārthatvābhāvena tannirvāhāyāsatpratiyogikatvaniyamāṅgīkārāyogāt| yuktyantarasyābhāvāditi cenna| yato abhāvānāṃ svapratiyogivirodhastāvatsammataḥ| sa ca naikādhikaraṇāvṛttitvarūpaḥ| prāgabhāvadhvaṃsayorabhāvāt| kintvekakālāvṛttitvam| itthañcātyantābhāvasyāpi tadeva svīkartavyam| vaiṣamye bījābhāvāt|
tathā ca prayogaḥ| atyantābhāvaḥ svaniṣṭhanañullekhaprayojakaviṣayatānirūpitaprakāratāśrayaviśiṣṭādhikaraṇakālāvṛttirabhāvatvāt| prāgabhāvādivaditi| atyantābhāvatvañca sārvakālikābhāvatvam| pakṣatāvacchedakāvacchedena sādhyasiddhiruddeśyā| evañca tādṛśarūpāvacchinnapakṣe tādṛśaprakāratārūpapratiyogitāśrayādhikaraṇakālāvṛttitvasiddhau tādṛśaviśiṣṭarūpapratiyoginaḥ kālāsambaddhatvameva siddhyatīti sarvātyantābhāvānāmaprāmāṇikapratiyogikatvaniyamaḥ siddhyati| ghaṭātyantābhāvaḥ paṭātyantābhāvo neti nañullekhaprayojakabhedatvarūpadharmāvacchinnaviśeṣyatānirūpitaprakāratāśrayībhūtapaṭātyantābhāvarūpaviśiṣṭarūpapratiyogyadhikaraṇakālavṛttitvamādāya bādhasya tadrītyaiva dṛṣṭānte sādhyavaikalyasya cāpattivāraṇāya nāstītyullekhaprayojakaviṣayatetyeva niveśaḥ| sā ca bhedatvātiriktena prāgabhāvatvādivilakṣaṇena paramate saṃsargābhāvatvapadavyavahnayamāṇena dharmeṇaivāvacchinnā bhavatīti na tādṛśabhedatvāvacchinnaviṣayatāmādāyoktadoṣaḥ| na ca hetoraprayojakatā| abhāvatvasāmye pramāṇaṃ vinātyantābhāvaviśeṣasyaniruktapratiyoginā daiśikavirodhasyānyayoḥ śaśaviṣāṇādyatyantābhāvānāñca kālikavirodhasya kalpane gauravarūpatarkasyaiva bādhakatvāt|
api cātyantābhāvasya daiśikavirodhasyāvaśyakatve pratiyogyadhikaraṇakāle pratiyogyadhikaraṇadeśāvṛttitvasyaikasyaiva sāmayikātyantābhāvasyasvīkaraṇenaiva ghaṭotpatteḥ pūrvaṃ parataśca ghaṭo nāstītyādipratītiprāmāṇyādyupapattau dhvaṃsaprāgabhāvāṅgīkārasyaiva nirbījatvāpātaḥ| na ca `atra ghaṭo bhaviṣyati' `atra ghaṭo dhvastaḥ' iti pratītibalāttadubhayāṅgīkāraḥ| `etaddeśavṛttitvaviśiṣṭaghaṭa etaduttarakālasambandhī' iti bhaviṣyatīti pratyayenāvagāhanāt na cottaratvādikamapi prāgabhāvadhvaṃsānaṅgīkāre nirvaktumaśakyam| pūrvatvottaratvayorakhaṇḍayoḥ kāle svato vidyamānayoḥ kiñcinnirūpitayoḥ saiddhāntikaśiromaṇibhiryādavācāryaistatvasaṃkhyānaṭippaṇyāmuktatvena tayoḥ prāgabhāvadhvaṃsāghaṭitatvāt| na cotpannasya punarutpattyādiparihārāya prāgabhāvāṅgīkāra iti vācyam| utpattirhi tatkṣaṇapūrvakṣaṇāsambaddhasya kṣaṇasambandhaḥ| tatra sāmagrī kṣaṇasambandhamātrasya nirvāhikā| na tu tatpūrvakṣaṇāsambandhasyāpi sāmagrī| pūrvamapi svato vidyamānasya sāmagrīniyamyatvābhāvāt| evañca daṇḍādirūpā ghaṭasāmagrī ghaṭasya kṣaṇasambandhamāpādayet| na tu viśiṣṭakṣaṇasambandharūpotpattimapīti kutaḥ prāgabhāvāṅgīkāraḥ|

api ca svarasato yādṛśaśabdāt yādṛśabodho jāyate tadabhilāpyapratyayasyāpyutsargatastadākāratvaṃ niyatam| evañca bhūtale ghaṭo nāstītyatra bhūtalādhikaraṇakasattāvaddhaṭābhāvo bodhyata iti tadabhilāpyapratītestādṛśaviśiṣṭaghaṭapratiyogikābhāvo viṣayo vācyaḥ| yatra bhūtale ghaṭaḥ prasiddhastaddhaṭitaviśiṣṭapratiyogikaḥ prāgabhāvo dhvaṃso vā bhavati| na caivaṃ sati śuddhapratiyogikaprāgabhāvadhvaṃsau na siddhyetāmiti vācyam| yatra ghaṭastatsaṃsargaścobhāvapyutpananau naṣṭau vā tatra `ghaṭastatsaṃsargaśca utpatsyate, bhaviṣyati, ghaṭastatsaṃsargaśca naṣṭaḥ' ityādipratyayāntarānurodhena siddhaviśeṣaṇaviśeṣyapratiyogikaprāgabhāvadhvaṃsānāmeva viśiṣṭapratiyogikatvena bhānamaṅgīkriyata iti tasya śuddhapratiyogikatvasiddhiḥ| vastutastu caitro rūpīti caitratvaviśiṣṭe rūpāvagāhipratītyaiva śuddhe'pi rūpitvaṃ siddhyati| anyathā śuddhasyārūpitvaṃ syāt| evaṃ viśiṣṭe pratiyogitvāvagāhipratītyā śuddhe'pi pratiyogitvaṃ viṣayīkriyata eva|
yadvā mā'stu taddharmaviśiṣṭe pratiyogitvāvagāhitvam| taddharmopalakṣita eva tadavagāhitvamastu| yatra viśeṣyāṃśe tadā bhāsamānābhāvapratiyogitvaṃ bādhitaṃ tatra viśiṣṭe tatra viśeṣaṇībhūtaghaṭabhūtalasaṃsarge vā pratiyogitvaṃ bhāsate| yatra bhūtale ghaṭaḥ sarvathā nāsti tatra ghaṭo nāstīti pratītirjāyate| tatra tadbhūtalavṛttighaṭasyāsataḥ pratiyogitvena bhānamiti atyantābhāvasyasarvasyāpyasatpratiyogikatvaṃ siddham| na ca yatra `ghaṭābāvavadbhūtalam' iti pratītirjāyate tatraitadbhūtalāsaṃsṛṣṭasyaiva ghaṭasya pratiyogitayā bhānātsatpratiyogikābhāvo'pi svīkriyatāmiti vācyam| yatroktapratītiḥ prātyakṣikī tadā tasyā `atra bhūtale ghaṭo nāsti' iti prāthamikapratīterāvaśyakatayā tatroktarītyā'sata eva pratiyogitayā bhānāttadviṣayībhūtārthāvagāhinyā dvitīyapratīterapyasatpratiyogikābhāvaviṣayakatvāvaśaykatve ghaṭādipadasyābhilāpakavākyasthasya bhūtalavṛttighaṭādau lakṣaṇā''śrayaṇīyā| yatra `ghaṭābhāvavadidam' ityānumānikaḥ pratyayaḥ śādbo vā tatrādhikaraṇe `atra ghaṭo nāsti' iti pratyayasya kadācitkasyacidapi durapahnavatayoktānumānikādyabhāvapratyayasyāpi tadviṣayatvamiti rītyopapatteḥ|
na ca `bhūtale ghaṭo nāsti' iti vākyāt `ghaṭābhāvo bhūtalādhikaraṇakasattāśrayaḥ' `bhūtalavṛttighaṭābhāvaḥ sattāśrayaḥ' iti vā bodhaḥ svīkriyata iti tadabhilāpyapratīterapi tatsamānaviṣayakatayā ghaṭapratiyogikatvāvagāhitayā nāsatpratiyogikatvasiddhiriti vācyam| naño niyatatvena tadupasthāpye ākhyātādyarthasyānvayāyogāduktarītyā bodhasya vaktumaśakyatvāt| anyathā `bhūtalavṛttighaṭābhāvo dṛśyo bhavati' ityādyarthe uktavākyaprayogavat `bhūtale ghaṭo na dṛśyo bhavati' iti `bhūtale ghaṭo na prameyaḥ' ityādiprayogasya prasaṅgaḥ| `bhratale ghaṭau na staḥ' `bhūtale ghaṭā na santi' ityādau ākhyātasya dvicanabahuvacanānupapatteśca| abhāvasyaikatvena dvitvādyanupapatteḥ| na hi tatra abhāvadvayaṃ trayaṃ vā bodhyate| tathātvāṅgīkāre ekadvighaṭādhikaraṇe tādṛśapratyayānudayāpatteḥ| na ca tarhi `ghaṭakartṛkasattābhāvo bhūtalavṛttiḥ' iti vā `bhūtalavṛttyabhāvīya pratiyogī ghaṭa sattāśrayaḥ' iti vābodhaḥ svīkriyatām| tāvatā'pi prāmāṇikapratiyogikatvākṣateriti vācyam| ādyakalpe bhūtalapadottaraṃ saptamyanupapatteḥ| tasyāḥ dhātvarthanirūpitādhikaraṇārthakatva evānuśiṣṭatvāt| ataḥ sattāyāmevānvayasya vaktavyatayā bhūtalādhikaraṇakaghaṭakartṛkasattābhāvabodha evāṅgīkārya iti siddhamaprāmāṇikapratiyogikābhāvaviṣayatvam| dvitīyakalpastvayukta eva| tathātve `sarvatra śaśaviṣaṇaṃ nāsti' iti vākyādasyāprāmāṇyaprasaṅgāt| śaśaviṣāṇe sattāyā bādhitatvāt| naiyāyikamate'pi `bhūtale gaganaṃ nāsti' iti vākyasyāprāmāṇyaprasaṅgāt| `hnadavṛttyabhāvapratiyogī vahniḥ parvatavṛttiḥ' iti bodhatātparyeṇa `hade parvate vahnirnāsti' iti prayogaprasaṅgācca| tasmāduktarītyaiva bodho yuktaḥ|
kiñca `bhūtale ghaṭo'sti kim' iti pṛṣṭe `na' ityuttarayati. tatra kiṃpadātiriktapraśnabhāgārthānvitārthabodhakatvasyottaravākye klṛptatayā tadvākyabodhyaviśiṣṭapratiyogikābhāvabodhasya tadvākye klṛptatvādanyatrāpi tathaiva bodha āvaśyakaḥ| `nedaṃ caitrasya' ityādau caitrasambandhaviśiṣṭedaṃpadārthamya asattvena tadabhāvo bodhyate| na ca ṣaṣṭhyarthacaitrasambandhābhāva evedamarthe pratyāyyatām| ghaṭo nāstītyādāvivātra bādhakābhāvāt. tathā ca prāmāṇikapratiyogikābhāvasiddhiriti vācyam| caitrasambandhārthakasarvanāmatatpadaghaṭitena `tadatra nāsti' iti vākyena bodhyā bhāvātiriktaprāmāṇikapratiyogikābhāvāntarasya tadvākyena bodhane gauraveṇoktarītyā'satpratiyogikābhāvaviṣayakabodhajanakatvasyaivāṅgīkārāt| evaṃ `caitro na pacati' ityasyātyantābhāvabodhakatve'pyeṣaiva gatirūhyā|
nanvevaṃ pūrvoktayuktibhiḥ `ghaṭaḥ paṭo na bhavati' ityanenāpi paṭakartṛkabhavanāśrayaghaṭābhāvabodhotpattyāpattyā tasya ghaṭasyāprāmāṇikatayoktavākyasyātyantābhāvabodhakatvameva syāt| na bhedabodhakatvam| tathā ca `nānyonyābhāvatā pṛthak' iti tatvavivekamūlavyākhyāvasare bhāvaśabdasya kartṛghañantatvamabhyupetyānyonyaṃ na bhavatīti vyutpattyā `anyonyābhāvaśabdena bhedavān bodhyate. taduttarabhāvapratyayena punaranyonyābhāvo bodhyate' iti ṭīkākṛdvyākhyānaṃ viruddhyeteti cenna| yato `ghaṭaḥ paṭo na bhavati' ityatra nañarthe bhede paṭasya pratiyogitayānvayaḥ| tasya ca bhavanarūpāsādhāraṇadharme abhedenānvayaḥ| sarvatra nāmārthayorabhedānvayanaye `ghaṭo na paṭaḥ' ityatra bhedavallākṣaṇikanañarthasya itarapadārthe abhedānvayasyāṅgīkṛtatvena prakṛte nañarthasya bhedasya bhavane abhedānvaye kṣativirahāt| tathā ca paṭabhedātmakāsādhāraṇadharmāśrayo ghaṭa iti bodhaḥ| ghaṭasvarūpabhedasya ghaṭāsādhāraṇatvamanapavādam|
yadvā bhavanaṃ svarūpasattā ghaṭabhedasvarūpā ghaṭaniṣṭhā bhavatīti na doṣaḥ| athavā `ghaṭaḥ paṭo na bhavati' ityatra paṭasyaiva svarūpasattārūpabhavane āśrayatayā'nvayaḥ| tathā ca `paṭakartṛkabhavanāśrayabhinno ghaṭaḥ' iti bodhaḥ ākhyātasyāśrayarūpakartrarthakatvapakṣe ākhyātārthapratiyogikabhedāvagāhī jāyate|
atha vā'sminneva pakṣe paṭasya dhātvarthabhavanānvitākhyātārthāśraye abhedenānvayaḥ. tathā ca `paṭarūpabhavanakartṛbhedavān ghaṭaḥ' iti bodho jāyate|
atha vā paṭānvitadhātvarthabhavanarūpasattāyā nañarthābhāvarūpabhede tasyākhyātārthe kartari āśrayarūpe tasya ghaṭe anvayaḥ| `na kalañjaṃ bhakṣayet' ityādau `kalañjabhakṣaṇābhāvaviṣayakaṃ kāryam' iti bodhābhyupagantṝṇāṃ gurūṇāṃ mata iva dhātvarthākhyātārthayorantarā nañarthabhānābhyupagamasambhavāt| `nāmūḍhasyetarotpatteḥ' `nānupamṛdya prādurbhāvāt' ityādau pañcamīprakṛtipratiyogikābhāvasya pañcamyarthānvayasyābhiyuktairaṅgīkṛtatvācca| etatkalpe paṭakartṛkasattābhāvastadbhedarūpa eva bhavatīti sarvapakṣeṣvapi bhedabodhakatānirvāhaḥ|
nanu kuta evamāśrīyate| paṭakartṛkasattāvaddhaṭaniṣedhaḥ kiṃ na syāt `bhūtale ghaṭo nāsti' ityādāviveti cenna| tatra ghaṭakartṛkasattābhāvasya bhūtale bodhane bhūtala iti saptamyanupapatternañarthe ākhyātārthānvaye pūrvoktānupapatteriva uktarītyā bodhāṅgīkāre prathamādyanupapatterabhāvāt| na ca `ghaṭaḥ paṭo bhavati kim' iti pṛṣṭe `na' iti brūte| tatra paṭakartṛkabhavanaviśiṣṭaghaṭaniṣedhabodhakatvaṃ tadvākyasya pūrvaṃ bhavaduktarītyaiva tavāvaśyakam| evaṃ `ghaṭe paṭatādātmyaṃ nāsti' ityabhilāpānurodhāt tādṛśapratīterghaṭaniṣṭhapaṭatādātmyābhāvaviṣayakatvasya klṛptatvāttadanusāreṇa `ghaṭaḥ paṭo na bhavati' iti pratīterapi `nedaṃ caitrasya' ityādāvuktarītyā ghaṭaniṣṭhapaṭatādātmyābhāvaviṣayakatvameva vācyamiti kuto'syāḥ bhedaviṣayakatvapratyāśeti vācyam| `ghaṭaḥ paṭātpṛthak' iti śabdābhilāpyapratīterniṣedhaviṣayakatvenānubhavasiddhāyā atyantābhāvavilakṣaṇabhedaviṣayakatvamāvaśyakam| evaṃ `ghaṭa evāyam' ityetāvanmātrākārapratyayasyāpi svarūpabhedarūpavilakṣaṇaviṣayakatvaṃ durapahnavam| `ghaṭaḥ paṭo na' ityatrāpi `nāsti' ityādipratyaye bhāsamānānuyogitāvilakṣaṇabhedatvādirūpānuyogitāviśiṣṭaviṣayakatvamanubhavasiddham| na cābhilāpakavākye `bhavati' ityasyādhyāhriyamāṇatvātpaṭabhavanaviśiṣṭaghaṭarūpāsata evātyantābhāvarūpaniṣedhapratiyogitvena bhānaṃ tatreti vācyam| sārvajanīnānubhavavirodhāt|
prasajyapratiṣedho'yaṃ kriyayā saha yatra nañ|
paryudāsaḥ sa vijñeyo yatrottarapadena nañ||
iti kārikayā'bhiyuktaiḥ `ghaṭaḥ paṭo na' ityādāvuttarapadarūpapaṭapadārthānvitasvārtharūpabhedabodhakatvena nañaḥ paryudāsasaṃjñakatvoktyā atra kriyādhyāhāraṃ vinaiva bodhasyoktatvāt. prasajyapratiṣedhātmakasaṃsargābhāvabodhasthale kriyāyā āvaśyakatvoktyā tatrāśrutakriyādhyāhārasyāvaśyakatvāt| `aghaṭaṃ bhūtalam' `nirmakṣikam' ityādau kriyāṃ vinā'tyantābhāvabodhakatvaṃ tādṛśasamāsavidhāyakaśāstrabalādeva siddhyati| tatrāsatpratiyogikābhāvabhānanirvāhaḥ pūrvoktarītyā lakṣaṇayā bodhyaḥ| evañcoktapratītibhirabhedasyāpi klṛptatvena `ghaṭaḥ paṭo bhavati' iti vākyasya tadabhilāpyapratyayasya ca tadviṣayakatvakalpane gauravābhāvāt| `ghaṭaḥ paṭo na' iti vākyajanyabodhasya yatkāryaṃ madhye jñānāntaraṃ vinā `ghaṭaḥ paṭātpṛthak' iti vyavahārādi tajjanakatvādinā `ghaṭaḥ paṭo na bhavati' iti vākyajanyabodhasya tatsamānaviṣayakatvasya niṣpratyūhatayoktarītyā'nyonyābhāvababodhakatvaṃ nirābādhamiti yuktaiva tatvavivekaṭīkā| `ghaṭaḥ paṭo bhavati kim' ityuttaravākyasthanañ‌padasamabhivyāhṛtena paṭabhavaniviśiṣṭaghaṭaniṣedho bodhyatāṃ nāma kvacit| na hyetāvatā'nantaramuktayuktyā siddhamuktavākyasyānyonyābhāvaviṣayakatvaṃ hīyate| `ghaṭe paṭatādātmyaṃ nāsti' ityasmāddhaṭavṛttipaṭatādātmyarūpāsatpratiyogikātyantābhāvasya bodhaḥ| talliṅgakabhedānumitermānasajñānasya votpattyaiva `ghaṭaḥ paṭātpṛthak' iti vyavahārādikāryamaṅgīkriyate| tādātmyābhāvasya bhedavyāpyatāgraharahitānāṃ tadanudayāditi sarvamanavadyam|
kecittu prāmāṇikapratiyogikasyātyantābhāvasyāṅgīkāre tadvadbhedasyaiva tadatyantābhāvarūpatāyā naiyāyikairaṅgīkṛtatvena prakṛtayorbhede mānābhāvena ca tatraiva paryavasānādavyāpyavṛttyatyantābhāvasya siddhānte'naṅgīkārāt so'tyantābhāvo bheda eva| śuddhātyantābhāvastu kevalānvayī aprāmāṇikapratiyogika eveti śuddhātyantābhāvasyāprāmāṇikapratiyogikatvaniyamasya na kācatkṣatiḥ| atyantābhāvasya śuddhatvantu yādṛśayādṛśapratiyogikātyantābhāvatvaviśiṣṭādhikaraṇaṃ yadyat tattatsvarūpabhūtabhedaniṣṭhātyantābhāvatvanirūpikā yā yā tādṛśatādṛśapratiyogitā tattadanyapratiyogitānirūpakātyantābhāvatvavattvam| yādṛśī ghaṭātyantābhāvīyaghaṭaniṣṭhapratiyogitā tannirūpakātyantābhāvatvaviśiṣṭādhikaraṇāni ghaṭavadbhinnāni nānādhikaraṇāni tattadātmakabhedāstu tattatsvarūpabhūtaghaṭavadbhedāḥ tanniṣṭhātyantābhāvatvaṃ ghaṭātyantābhāvatvaṃ, prāmāṇikapratiyogikātyantābhāvasya bhedarūpatāyā upapāditatvāt| tannirūpikāḥ yā yāḥ pratiyogitāḥ prāmāṇikaghaṭādiniṣṭhā eva| tattadanyā śaśaviṣāṇādirūpā'sanniṣṭhā bhavati| tasyā yādṛśapadenopādānāsambhavāt| tathā hi, śaśaviṣāṇādiniṣṭhapratiyogitā yādṛśī cet tannirūpakātyantābhāvatvaviśiṣṭādhikaraṇaṃ yadyat bhūtalādikaṃ tattadātmakabhedāstu padārthāntarapratiyogikabhedāḥ| tanniṣṭhaṃ yadatyantābhāvatvaṃ śaśaviṣāṇābhāvatvaṃ tasya dharmisvarūpatvābhāvena tadātmakabhedarūpatvābhāvāt| kintūktarītyā ghaṭātyantābhāvatvameva| tannirūpakatvaṃ na śaśaviṣāṇādiniṣṭhapratiyogitāyā iti| atra yadyadadhikaraṇamitivīpsānādare śaśaviṣāṇaniṣṭhapratiyogitā'pi tādṛśī bhavatyeva| śaśaviṣāṇābhāvasya svasminnapi sattvāt śaśaviṣāṇaniṣṭhapratiyogitākātyantābhāvatvaviśiṣṭādhikaraṇaṃ śaśaviṣāṇābhāvo'pi| tadātmako bhedastanniṣṭhaghaṭabhedaḥ| tanniṣṭhamatyantābhāvatvaṃ śaśaviṣāṇātyantābhāvatvam| tannirūpakatvāt| atastadādaraḥ| yādṛśapratiyogitākābhāvādhikaraṇamityetāvatā pūrtāvatyantābhāvatvāśrayetyuktiranyātyantābhāvapratiyogitā yādṛśapadenopādeyeti sūcanāya| na tvatiprasaṅgaparihārāyeti jñātavyam|
yadvā vyatirekibhinnatvameva śuddhatvam| tattaddharmisvarūpabhedātmakānāṃ prāmāṇikapratiyogikātyantābhāvānāṃ vyatirekitvāttena tadvāraṇam|

nanvabhāvasyaivaikyapratītirabhāvatvaikyamavalambate| rūpāṇāṃ rūpābhāvābhāvātmakatve'pi ghaṭapaṭavṛttirūpābhāvaikyapratītivaditi cenna| tathātve'pi sadasadvilakṣaṇatvābhāvavyāpakībhūtetyādirītyā jñānaṅgīkāre pratiṣedhākāratvamāgatameva| tādātmyena `sadvyāpakībhūtābhāvapratiyogibādhyatvavān' evam `asadvyāpakībhūtābhāvapratiyogibādhyatvavān' iti samūhālambanādanumityaṅgīkāre'numitirapi samūhālambanarūpaiva syāt| na sadvilakṣaṇatvaviśiṣṭāsadvilakṣaṇatvarūpasādhyavidheyikā| tatra tu asadvailakṣaṇyasya śuktirūpye siddhatayāṃśe siddhasādhanatāyā durvāratvāt| etadarthameva `mithyaiva rajatam' iti pratyakṣamubhayasampratipannasadvilakṣaṇatvāvagāhitākamupanyastaṃ prāk|
na caikatra dvayamiti rītyā bādhyatve tādṛśapratiyogitvadvayāvagāhiparāmarśādekatra dvayamityādirītyaiva sadvilakṣaṇatvāsadvilakṣaṇatvāvagāhinī anumitiḥ syāt| tatsamānākārakajñānasya pūrvamasiddhatayā na siddhasādhanamiti vācyam| tādṛśajñānānāmaprāmāṇikatvāt| prāmāṇikatve vā'numitiparāmarśayostādṛśarūpeṇa kāryakāraṇabhāve mānābhāvādvidheyatāvacchedakakoṭāvadhikāvagāhitvābhāvenāṃśe siddhasādhanataiva| etadapyuktam| pratiṣedhākārabuddheḥ śukterūpye sadvilakṣaṇatvabuddheḥ anivāraṇāt| aśe siddhasādhanatvasyetyarthaḥ|
yadi sadasadubhayavyāpakībhūtābhāvapratiyogītyādiparāmarśāccobhayatvāvacchinnabhedāvagāhīnī anumitiḥ syāttadā śuktirajatasyobhayātmakatvānaṅgīkārātsphuṭaṃ siddhasādhanataiva| sadasadanyataravyāpakībhūtetyādiparāyarśādare'numiterapyanyataratvāvacchinnabhedāvagāhitveneṣṭasiddhirityuktāvapi sadbhedaviśiṣṭāsadbhedāvacchinnapratiyogitākabhedavattva rūpānyataratvasyoktasadbhedaviśiṣṭāsadbhedarūpapratiyogitāvacchedakādyaprasiddhyā taddhaṭitavyāptigrahasya pratiṣedhaviṣayiṇyāḥ vyāptigraho na ghaṭata ityākārāyā buddheḥ tvayā vārayitumaśakyatvāt|
atha tadbhedaitadbhedaitadubhayatvāvacchinnapratiyogitākābhāva evānyataratvam| tadgahaśac pratyekaṃ tādṛśabhedagrahādapi sambhavatīti ucyate| tadāpi tādṛśobhayaṃ nāsti iti pratiṣedhākārabuddhestayorvirodhagrahamūlāyāḥ śaśaviṣāṇaghaṭādiṣviva śuktirūpyādiṣu pakṣeṣvapi uktānumānapravṛtteḥ prāgeva lokānāmavāraṇāt bādhyatvarūpahetorabhāvasya tadvyāpakatvapratiṣedhabuddheravāraṇameva| etena sadasadubhayatvavṛttisvarūpasambandhāvacchinnāvacchedakatākapratiyogitākabhedo'numitiviṣayaḥ| ato na siddhasādhanatā| kāraṇībhūtaparāmarśaśca tatsambandhenobhayatvāśrayasya tādātmyena vyāpyatāvagāhī hetvabhāvasya vyāpakatvāvagāhī iti nirastam| sattvāsattvayorlokānāṃ parasparaviruddhatvarūpapratiṣedhākārabuddheranumānapravṛtteḥ prāganivāraṇāt| sattvābhāvaprayuktāsattvavattvena gṛhyamāṇatvāt| tādātmyena tadubhayatvāśrayatayā guhyamāne śuktirūpyādau bādhyatvarūpahetugraheṇa hetvabhāvasya pakṣādipravibhāgātpūrvameva vyāpakatvapratiṣedhabuddheravāraṇāt idamayuktamiti|
ayamūhaprakāraḥ| atraniviṣṭasatpadartho yadyabādhyatvaviśiṣṭastadātattādātmyamabādhyatvatādātmyameva| tathā ca bādhyatve satyabādhyatvamevoktaṃ syāt| itthañca kathaṃ na svavyāhatiḥ| yadi ca saditi pratīmayānastadā tattādātmyaṃ sattvena pratīyamānatvaṃ tadā artāntaratā| asatkhyātivādinā'pi śuktirajatādeḥ saditi pratītigocaratāṅgīkārāt| atha tādātmyaṃ sambandhaviśeṣaḥ| tathā cābādhyatādātmyaṃ nābādhyatvarūpamiti kathaṃ vyāhatiriti cenna| tādṛśasambandhaḥ kaścana nīlaghaṭayoḥ yādṛśatādātmyaṃ tajjātīyādanyastacchabdabodhyaścettasya cābādhyādhiṣṭhānakabhramaviṣayatvādetajjñā(tva abādhyajñā)nanivartyajñānaviṣayatvādeḥ kasyacitsambandhasya satyapi sambhavena tatsādhanamātreṇāsadvyāvṛttyayogaḥ| prasiddhatādātmyasājātye tasya bhedābhedasāpekṣatvenābādhyena sahābhedasyāpi prāptyā'bādhyatvāvacchinnātyantabhedātmakabādhyatvaṃ tatra kathaṃ syāt| anyathā `nīlo ghaṭaḥ' iti pratītivat `bādhyo'bādhyaḥ' iti pratītiprasaṅgaḥ| taddharmāvacchinnena sahātyantabhedagrahasyaiva tādātmyena taddharmāvacchinnagrahaṃ prati virodhitvenābādhyatādātmyena vidyamānasya bādhyatvasyābādhyātyantabhedarūpatvābhāvāt| na cābādhyabhedasya tādātmyāpekṣayā'dhikasattākatvānna virodha iti vācyam| svārthānumityā'nirvacanīyatvasiddheḥ prāguktasattākabhedānupasthityā virodhaśāntyayogāt| jagati uktabhedatattādātmyayoḥ vyāvahārikatvasyaiva vaktavyatayā virodhaśāntyayogācca|
na ca śuktitādātmyaṃ rajate nābādhyatvāvacchinnapratiyogitākaṃ kintvidantvādyavacchinnapratiyogitākameva| abādhyatvāvacchinnapratiyogitākātyantabhedarūpabādhyatvasya sattvānna `abādhyo bādhyaḥ' iti vyavahārāpattiḥ| anirvacanīyatvaśarīre'pi abādhyatvāśrayanirūpitatādātmyaniveśānna ko'pi doṣa iti vācyam| yena kenāpi rūpeṇa tena sahātyantabhedavati kenāpi rūpeṇa tena saha bhedābhedayostadadhīnatādātmyasya ca sarvānubhavaviruddhatvāt| anyathā karakāditātparyakajalapadaghaṭitajalaṃ peyamiti vākyajānamānajajñānaprāmāṇyaprasaṅgaḥ| karakāditvena peyatvenātyantabhedasadbhāve'pi tvadrītyā jalatvāvacchinnena saha tādātmyasyābādhāt| uktañca tvayā'pi vācaspatikārikāvyākhyāvasare `yugapadbhinnadeśakālavṛttinoḥ kaṭakakuṇḍalayorhematvādinā'pi nābhedaḥ' iti| na caikahemavyaktyupādānakayoḥ kaṭakakuṇḍalayorhematvādinā'bhedaḥ tatkaṭakatvena tatkuṇḍalatvenātyantabhedo'pi tatraivokta iti yena kenāpi rūpeṇātyantabhinnasya yena kenāpi rūpeṇābhedo nāstītyetadayuktameveti vācyam| tatrāpi `etatkuṇḍalaṃ hematvena tatkaṭakam' ityapratyayena hematvenāpi rūpeṇa tatkuṇḍalapratiyogikābhedasya tatkaṭake'naṅgīkārāt| tadupādānavṛttihematvāśrayaṇena sahaivābhedasyāṅgīkṛtatvāt| ata eva `atyantabhinnaikaghaṭaniṣṭharūparasādivyavasthaivameva' ityabhihitaṃ tatraiva|
na caivameva sadupādānakasya rajatasya satā saha tādātmyamastviti vācyam| tena sahātyantabhinnatvena pratīyamāne tadupādānakatvasya mṛdātyantabhinnatvanopalabhyamāne paṭādāvadṛṣṭatvenābādhyatvānāśraye rajate tadupādānakatvāyogāt| kiñca bādhyatvasyaiva hetūkṛtatvena tena bādhyatvaviśiṣṭasattādātmyasādhane viśeṣaṇāṃśasya sādhyaśarīre nātiprayojanakatvam| viśiṣṭasādhyatve viśeṣaṇaparityāgenābādhyatādātmyarūpasattādātmyasādhane vā bādhyatvahetorvirodhaśca| na cārthakriyākāritvameva sattvam| tadavacchinnatādātmyaṃ sādhyata ityapi yuktam| bādhyasyārthakriyākāribhinnatvenaivāvagatasya tattādātmyasādhane bādhādvirodhācca| asatkhyātivādinā saigatena bādhyasyāpyarthakriyākāritvābhyupagamena tasyaiva jagatyanirvacanīyatvarūpatvāṅgīkāre saugatavailakṣaṇyāsiddhyā tatsiddhyarthaṃ anyasyaivānirvacanīyatvasya jagati nirvaktavyatayā tatsādhakānumāne dṛṣṭāntabhūte śuktirajatādau tādṛśasyaiva vaktavyatvācca|
napi `sarvaṃ satprameyam' ityākārā kācana buddhistattadviṣayatādātmyam| tatsādhane tasyā buddhitvenaiva paricaye `sarvaṃ sat' ityākārasya śuktirajatādisarvaviṣayasya bhramasyāpi paricitatvena tadviṣayībhūtasya tādātmyasya śuktirajatāderasattvapakṣe'pi sattvena siddhasādhanāparihārāt| tādṛśākārajñāneṣu `iyaṃ pramaiva, ayaṃ bhrama eva' iti nirdhāraṇāyogena viśiṣya vyaktiviśeṣasya niveśayitumaśakyatvāt| tādṛśākārabhramatvena paricaye tadviṣayatāyāḥ śuktirūpye'ṅgīkārātsiddhasādhanatā nāpi tādṛśī kācana pramā vyaktiḥ| tattvena paricayaḥ| ghaṭātyantabhinnatvena gṛhyamāṇe ghaṭatvaprakārakapramāviṣayātyantabhedagrahasyaucityāvarjitsya sārvalaukikasya sārvakālikasya durapahnavatāyā iva atrāpi `asadeva rajatam' iti rūpeṇātyantasadbhinnatvena gṛhyamāṇarajate sattvaprakārakapramāviṣayātyantabhedasya tādṛśapramāvyaktiviśeṣaviṣayātyantabhedasya ca yo grahastasya durapahnavatayā tādṛśarajate tādṛśapramāviṣayatādātmyasādhane bādhasya bādhyatvahetoravirodhasya ca durvāratvāt| anyathā `nīlaḥ' iti pramāviṣayabhinne vā `sarvaṃ nīlam' iti pramāviśeṣaviṣayātyantabhedavattvena vā niścite'pi ghaṭe nīlatādātmyasiddhiprasaṅgaḥ| na cānirvacanīyavādināmayaṃ virodho na sammata iti vācyam| anirvacanīyapratīteḥ prāguktavirodhasya sarvalokasiddhasya svecchāmātreṇa parihartumaśakyatayā svārthānumiterevāyogasyoktatvāt|
parārthānumāne tūktaprakāraḥ sarvathā na saṅgacchate| yadi sā vyaktiḥ `idaṃ rajatam' iti jñānaprakārarajataviṣayakatvena svaparasammatā tadā tādṛśavyakteḥ pramātvameva māṃ pratyasammatamiti maṃ prati tatparicayāyogaḥ| yadi tadaviṣayakatvena matadvayasiddhaṃ jñānamupādīyate tadā bādha eva| yadi madhyasthasya prativādinaśca śuktirajatādyaviṣayatvena sammataṃ svamatamātreṇa tadviṣayakatvena sammataṃ jñānamupādīyate tadā tadviṣayātyantabhinnatvena gṛhyamāṇe tattādātmyānumitiḥ madhyasthasya vādino vā na ghaṭata eva| yadi cobhayamate'pi pramātvena śuktirajataviṣayakatvena ca sammataṃ `sarvaṃ satprameyam' iti jñānamupādīyate tadā tadvyakteḥ pramātvena sammatiḥ śuktirajate sattvasammatiṃ vinā na ghaṭata iti tatra sattādātmyasādhanasya vaiyarthyameveti draṣṭavyam|
kiñca yadi sadityākārakaprametyasya satpadaghaṭitavākyābhilāpyaprametyarthaḥ tadā śuktibhrameṇa lakṣaṇayā ādhunikasaṅketena ca satpadenāsato'pi bodhasambhavāttadviṣayatādātmyasādhane siddhasādhanatvādikameva| yadi śuktibhramādyanadhīnatādṛśavākyasādhyapramocyate tadā `sarvaṃ satprameyam' iti vākyena śaktibhramādikaṃ vinā'pi jāyamānāyāḥ samīcīnatvādiprakārakapramāyā api grahaṇāpattyā tadviṣayatādātmyasādhane arthāntarādyanistārāt| na hi samīcīnatvādau kośasiddhe śaktirnāsti| abādhyatvāvacchinne vartata iti rājñāmājñāsti| abādhyatvaprakārakapramātātparyakatādṛśavākyasādhyapramātvena tadupādāne abādhyatvaprakārakapramātvena tadupādānasya sambhave śiroveṣṭananyāyāpātaḥ| tatparityāge tathaivopādāne abādhyabhinnatvena niścīyamāne tatpramāvyaktiviṣayatādātmyasādhane pūrvoktarītyā bādhādireva|
nanu sattājātimattādatmyaṃ sādhya iti cenna| dravyatvarajatatvādimattādātmyenāpi gṛhyamāṇe rajate asattvasadadhiṣṭhānakatvādi saṃśayanivṛtteriva tanmate uktasattādātmyajñānenāpi tadanivṛtteśtulyatvenārthāntarānistārāt| dravyatādātmyajñānāderapi tannivartakatve `rajataṃ dravyam' iti vyavahārādibalasiddhatādṛśajñānabalenoktasaṃśayasya nivṛttatayā anutpannatayā vā sattādātmyasādhanasyākiñcitkaratvāpātaḥ| dravyatādātmyajñānasyoktasaṃśayāvirodhitvaṃ sattādātmyajñānasyaiva virodhitvamityatra niyāmakābhāvaḥ| dravyatvādijāteriva sattājāterapi pratyakṣagṛhītatayā tattādātmyasādhanāyogaḥ|
etena sadityākārapratītau svarūpataḥ prakāro yaḥ tadavacchinnatādātmyaṃ sādhyate| samīcīnatvādikantu na tatheti parāstam| tādṛśadharmo yadi sattā tadā'rthāntaram| yadyabādhyatvaṃ tadā bādha evetyādipūrvoktadoṣānistārāt| kālasambaddhatādātmyamapyarthāntarabādhādinā nirastam|
astu vā sattādātmyaṃ yādṛśatādṛśaṃ `idaṃ sat' iti pratītisiddham| tatsādhanasyopayogo vaktavyaḥ| bauddhābhihitāsatkhyātinirāsārthamiti cenna| `śaśaviṣāṇaṃ sat' iti pratītibalena śaśaviṣāṇādirūpāsatyapi sattādātmyasyāvarjanīyatayā tasyāsattvavyāvartakatvāyogāt| `anirvacanīyasattādātmyamasati na' iti viśeṣopapādanaṃ, `śaśaviṣāṇādivyavahāre heturvikalpa eva| śuktirajatādivyavahārahetuśca jñānam| ataḥ pratītisiddhasattādātmyameva niveśyate| ato'sadvyāvṛttiḥ iti bhāṣaṇam, `śaśaviṣāṇaṃ sadityādipratītau kālasambandha eva pratīyate| na tu satādātmyam' iti viśeṣakathanaṃ, `śaśaviṣāṇaṃ sadityādipratītāvanirvacanīyameva pratīyate| asacca sarvathā nirupākhyameva| ato na sattādātmyavat' iti jalpanaṃ, `śaśaviṣāṇamiti vākyādvyadhikaraṇaprakārakameva jñānaṃ jāyate' iti bhāṣaṇañcetyevamādīni durbhāṣaṇānyarthāpattyādinirāsaprastāve nirasiṣyante|
