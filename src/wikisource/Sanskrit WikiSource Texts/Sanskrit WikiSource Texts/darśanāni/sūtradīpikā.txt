sūtradīpikā
kṛṣṇaṃ rāmaṃ nārasihmaṃ vyāsaṃ natvā gurūnapi |
jagannāthākhyāyatinā kriyate sūtradīpikā ||
|| prathamādhyāyasya prathamaḥ pādaḥ ||
|| jijñānādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ athāto brahmajijñāsā oṃ ||
atha adhyayanaśamadamādirūpādhikārasampattyanantaram | ataḥ mokṣākhyaprayojanasya satvādviṣṇoḥ prasādādvā | oṃ guṇapūṇarsya brahmaṇoḥ jijñāsā śravaṇādirūpā kartavyetyarthaḥ || 1 ||
|| janmādhikaraṇam || 2 ||
oṃ janmādyasya yataḥ oṃ ||
asya pramitajīvajaḍātmakaprapaṃcasya yataḥ yasmāt | janmādi sṛṣyayādyaṣyakam | bhavatīti śrutaṃ tadeva brahma nānyadityarthaḥ || 1 ||
|| śāstrayonitvādhikaṇam || 3 ||
oṃ śāstrayonitvāt oṃ ||
nānumānataḥ rudrāderjagatkāraṇatvaṃ kalpanīyam | kutaḥ śāstrayonitvāt, śāstraṃ ṛgādirūpaṃ yoniḥ jñaptikāraṇaṃ yasya tacchāstrayoni | tasya bhāvaḥ śāstrayonitvaṃ, tasmāt jagatkāraṇaṃ brahma | śāstraikasamadhigamyatvādityarthaḥ ||
|| samanvayādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ tattu samanvāyāt oṃ ||

|| ikṣatyadhikaraṇam || 5 ||
oṃ ikṣaternāśabdaṃ oṃ ||
tadbrahma | aśabdaṃ na, śabdāvācyaṃ na, kintu vācyameva | kutaḥ īkṣateḥ īkṣatirjñānam || tadviṣayatvādityarthaḥ ||
oṃ gauṇaścennātmaśabdāt oṃ ||
gauṇaḥ sattvādiguṇabaddho jīva eva | "sa etasmāditi" īkṣatiśrutyukta iti cenna | kutaḥ ātmaśabdāt | īkṣaṇīyavastuni ātmānaṃ paśyedityātmaśabdaśravaṇādityarthaḥ || 2 ||
oṃ tanniṣṭhasya mokṣopadeśāt oṃ ||
tanniṣṭhasya paramātmajñāninaḥ | mokṣopadeśāt "yasyānuvittaḥ" ityādau mokṣasya śrutatvāt | ātmaśabdamukhyavācyo na jīva ityarthaḥ || 3 ||
oṃ heyatvāvacanācca oṃ ||
heyatvādacanāt brahmaṇo mokṣārthaṃ prādhānyenājñeyatvarūpaheyatvasyāpratipādanāt pratyuta "jānatheti" aheyatvavacanācca na gauṇa; ātmaśabdavācyaḥ | kintu nirguṇo viṣṇurevetyarthaḥ || 4 ||
oṃ svāpyayāt oṃ ||
svasya svasmin apyayāt apyayapadoktalayavacanāt | ātmānaṃ vilāpayatīti śrutyukto nirguṇa eva na gauṇaḥ | ata eva cāsau śabdavācya ityarthaḥ || 5 ||
oṃ gatisāmānyāt oṃ ||
gaternānāśāsvājanyajñānasya | sāmānyāt ekarūpatvaśravaṇāt | na viṣṇoranyaḥ kāraṇatvena śākhāntarapratipādya ityarthaḥ || 6 ||
oṃ śrutatvācca oṃ ||
"eko devaḥ" iti śrutyaktatvācca na śabdāvācyaṃ brahmetyarthaḥ || 7 ||
|| ānandamayādhikaraṇam || 6 ||
oṃ ānandamayo'bhyāsāt oṃ ||
ānandamayādiśabdavācyo viṣṇureva na prakṛtyādi | kutaḥ? abhyāsāt pratiprakaraṇamānandamayādau brahmaśabdaśravaṇādityarthaḥ || 1 ||
oṃ vikāraśabdānneticenna prācuryāt oṃ ||
vikāraśabdāt vikārārthakamayaṭśabdāt ānandamayādirviṣṇunareticenna | kutaḥ? prācuryāt mayaṭśabdasya prācuryapadoktapūrṇatvābhidhāyakatvādityarthaḥ || 2 ||
oṃ taddhetuvyapadeśācca oṃ ||
caḥ samuccaye | tasya ānandamayasya | viṣṇutve lokaceṣyakatvākhyahetorvyapadeśāt | "kohyevānyāt" iti śrutau uktatvācca ānandamayo viṣṇurevetyarthaḥ || 3 ||
oṃ māntravarṇikameva ca gīyate oṃ ||
yato māntravarṇikaṃ "satyaṃ jñānamanantaṃ brahma" iti mantravarṇoktaṃ brahma gīyate ānandamayādiśabdaiḥ pratipādyate | ata ānandamayādiśabdavācyaṃ brahmaivetyarthaḥ || 4 ||
oṃ netaro'nupapatteḥ oṃ ||
itaraḥ catumarukhādiḥ | na kutaḥ? anupapatteḥ "brahmavidāpnoti param" iti mokṣajanakajñānaviṣayatvāyogādityarthaḥ || 5 ||
oṃ bhedavyapadeśācca oṃ ||
cartumukhāderānandamayasya bhedakadhamarokteśca nānandamaya itara ityarthaḥ || 6 ||
oṃ kāmācca nānumānāpekṣā oṃ ||
advaitaśrutyanukūlānumānasyābhāvāt | kevalānumānasya ca kāmāt icchāmanusṛtya pravṛttatvānnānumānāpekṣā atīndriyārthaviṣaye śuṣkānumānāpekṣā na kāryātyarthaḥ | 7 ||
oṃ asminnasya ca tadyogaṃ śāsti oṃ ||
yuktisamuccaye caśabdaḥ | yataḥ asminnānandamayaprakaraṇe mokṣe'pyasya jīvasya | tadyogaṃ ānandamayādinā yogaṃ sambandham | śāsti śrutiḥ pratipādayati | ato'pi ānandamayādiḥ brahmaiva, na dehādirityarthaḥ || 8 ||
|| antasthatvādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ antastaddharmopadeśāt oṃ ||
antaḥ "antaḥ praviṣyam" iti hṛdayāntaḥ śrutaṃ brahmaiva na indrādiḥ | kutaḥ? taddharmopadeśāt tasminnantaḥ śrute kṣīrābdhimadhyavartitva, pralayodadhisthitatva, brahmāṇḍavīryatvādirūpaviṣṇudharmāṇāmantaḥ samudra ityādau śrutatvādityarthaḥ || 2 || || ākāśādhikaraṇam || 8 ||
oṃ ākāśastalliṅgāt oṃ ||
ākāśaḥ ākāśśabdavācyaḥ viṣṇureva, na prasiddhākāśaḥ | kutaḥ? talliṅgāt chandoga śrutyuktākāśe parovarīyastvādirūpaviṣṇuliṅgasya śravaṇaśadityarthaḥ ||
|| prāṇādhikaraṇam || 9 ||
oṃ ata eva prāṇaḥ oṃ ||
"tadvai tvaṃ prāṇo abhavaḥ" ityuktaḥ prāṇo viṣṇureva nānyaḥ | kutaḥ? ataeva "śrīśca te" iti śrīpatitvaliṅgādeva ityarthaḥ || || jyotiradhikaraṇam || 10 ||
oṃ jyotiścaraṇābhidhānāt oṃ ||
jyotiḥ jyotiśśabdavācyaṃ brahmaiva natvanyam | kutaḥ ? caraṇābhidhānāt karṇādīnāṃ vicaraṇābhidhānāt | "vi me karṇā" iti jyotiṣaḥ sākalyena karṇādividūratvaśravaṇādityarthaḥ ||
|| chandebhidhānādhikaraṇam || 11 ||
oṃ chando'bhidhānānneticenna tathā cetorpaṇanigadāttathā hi darśanam oṃ ||
chandobhidhānāt | nanu chandogaśrutisthagāyatrīpadena catuviṃśatyakṣarātmakachandasaḥ pratipādanāt gāyatrīśabdavācyo viṣṇurneticanna | kutaḥ? tathāhi darśanaṃ hi yasmāt tathā gānatrāṇakartṛtvanimittena darśanaṃ viṣṇoḥ gāyatrīśabdavācyatvapratipādikā"gāyati trāyati ca" iti śrutirasti | tasmāt vyarthaṃ gāyatrīpadena viṣṇorabhidhānamityata uktaṃ tathetyādi | tathā cetorpaṇanigadāt viṣṇoḥ tathā gāyatrayādiśabdapravṛttinimittavatvena cetasi arpaṇāya upāsanāya | nigadāt śrutau pratipādanādityarthaḥ | tathā ca na vaiyathyarmiti bhāvaḥ || 1 ||
oṃ bhūtādipādavyapadeśopapatteścaivam oṃ ||
evamityasyāvṛttiḥ | evaṃ gāyatrīśabadavācyaṃ brahmetyevameva yuktam | kutaḥ? evaṃ bhūtādipādavyapadeśopapatteḥ | "pādo'sya sarvā bhūtāni tripādasyāmṛtaṃ divi" iti | evarūpabhūtāmṛtāṃśavatvokteśca tasya ca viṣṇāvevopapatteḥ anyatra anupapatteścetyarthaḥ || 2 ||
oṃ upadeśabhedānneticennobhayasminnapyavirodhāt oṃ ||
upadeśabhedāt gāyatrījyātiṣo; dyusthatva "tripādasyāmṛtaṃ divi"  divaḥ paratva "paro divaḥ" rūpaviruddhadharmabodhakaśrutibhedānneticet taddayaṃ viṣṇavākhyamekaṃ vastu na kintu bhinnameveti cenna | kutaḥ? ubhayasmin śrutivākye'pi ekya pratipādyatve'pi avirodhāt | trisaptalokavivakṣayā virodhābhāvādityarthaḥ || 3 ||
| pādāntyaprāṇādhikaraṇam || 12 ||
oṃ prāṇastathānugamāt oṃ ||
tathā "tadvai tvaṃ" iti taittirīyoktaprāṇavat "tāvā etāḥ" iti aitareyokata prāṇo'pi viṣṇureva | kutaḥ? anugamāt | devatopāsyatvadiviṣṇuliṅgabrahmaśabdānāṃ asakṛcchravaṇādityarthaḥ || 1 ||
oṃ navakturātmopadeśāditicedadhyātmasambandhabhūmāhyasmin oṃ ||
dvitīyārthe ṣaṣṭhī | vaktuḥ bṛhatīsahasravaktāraṃ viśvāmitraṃ prati indreṇātmopadeśāt | ātmanaḥ svasya prāṇatayopadeśāt indra evāyaṃ prāṇaḥ na viṣṇuriti cet | neti śeṣaḥ | tasyāvṛttiḥ | anugamādityanuvartate | tathā ca prāguktānugamahetoḥ nendraḥ prāṇaḥ | tathāsatyupadeśavirodha ityapi na | kutaḥ? yasmāt asmin prakaraṇe bhagavataḥ adhyātmasambandhabhūmā indraviśvāmidirūpabahudehasambodhaḥ ukto'sti tasmādityarthaḥ | yadvā hi yasmāt asminnindre | adhyātmasambandhabhūmā | adhi adhikasyātmanaḥ sambandhasyāveśasya bhūmā bāhulyaṃ vidyate tasmādityarthaḥ || 2 ||
oṃ śāstraṣyayātūpadeśo vāmadevavat oṃ ||
tu śabda evārthe | vāmadevavat yathā antaryāmivivakṣayā vāmadevarṣiḥ "ahaṃ manurabhavaṃ" iti svasya manusūryādyaikyamāha tadvat | śāstradṛṣyayā śāsti sarvamiti śāstramantaryāmī taddṛṣyayā tadvivakṣayaivopadeśaḥ prāṇo'hamiti vyapadeśaḥ | natvaikyenetyarthaḥ || 3 ||
oṃ jīvamukhyaprāṇaliṅgānneticennopāsātraividhyādāśritatvādiha tadyogāt oṃ ||
jīvamukhyaprāṇaliṅgāt śatāyuṣṭvādijīvaliṅgānāṃ indriyadevaiḥ saṃvādādirūpamukhyaprāṇaliṅganāṃca śravaṇāt "tāvanti śatasaṃvatsarasya", "tā ahiṃsanteti" prāṇo anya eva na viṣṇuriticenna | kutaḥ? anugamāt | na ca liṅgavirodhaḥ | talliṅgānāṃ antaryāmiviṣayatvopapatteḥ ityādikamadhyāhāryam | kimarthamantaryāmikathanamityata uktaṃ upāsātraividhyāditi | antarbahiḥsarvagatabhedena brahmopāsanasya trividhatvāt | iha prakaraṇe | tattraividhyasya āśritatvāt uktatvāt tadyogāt teṣāṃ adhikāriṇāṃ trividhopāsanayogyatvādityarthaḥ | tathāca tattacchabdaistattadadhikāribhedenopāsanārthaṃ antaryāmikathanaṃ yuktamiti bhāvaḥ || 4 ||

|| sarvagatatvādhikaraṇam || 1 ||
oṃ sarvatra prasiddhopadaṣāt oṃ ||
tatviti vartate | sarvatra pṛthivyādiṣu | ucyamānaṃ vastu tattu nārāyaṇākhyaṃ brahmaiva na tvanyat | kutaḥ? prasiddhopadeśāt "etameva brahma" iti  sarvagate vastuni nārāyaṇavācitvena śrutyādiprasiddhabrahmaśabdaśravaṇādityarthaḥ || 1 ||
oṃ vivakṣitaguṇopapatteśca oṃ ||
caḥ samuccaye'vadhāraṇe vā | sarvagate | śrutyetiśeṣaḥ | tathāca vivakṣitānāmitaḥparaṃ sarvagate śrutyā tanniṣṭhatayā vaktuṃ yogyānāṃ vakṣyamāṇānāṃ vā aśrutatvādiguṇānāṃ viṣṇoreva yogyatvena tasminnevopapatteḥ anyatrānupapatteśca viṣṇureva sarvagata ityarthaḥ || 2 ||
oṃ anupapattestu na śarīraḥ oṃ ||
tuśabda evārthe | śārīro jīvaḥ | ādityo vā | na sarvagataḥ kutaḥ? anupapatteḥ ekasya jīvasya sarvaśarīrasthatvāyogādevetyarthaḥ || 3 ||
oṃ karmakartṛvyapadeśācca oṃ ||
sarvagate vastuni "ātmānam" iti karmatvasya jīve ca śaṃsatīti tadviruddharkartṛkatvasya vyapadeśāduktatvācca na jīvaḥ sarvagata ityarthaḥ || 4 ||
oṃ śabdaviśeṣāt oṃ ||
"emeva brahma" iti vākye brahmaśabdasyāvadhāraṇātharkaivaśabdākhyaviśeṣaṇavatvāt brahmaśabdo na jīvapara ityarthaḥ || 5 ||
oṃ smṛteśca oṃ ||
caḥ samuccaye | na kevalaṃ śrutyuktatvāt brahmaśabdo na jīvapara iti kintu "ahamātmā guḍākeśa" ityādismṛtisadbhāvācca brahmaśabdo na jīvapara ityarthaḥ || 6 ||
oṃ arbhakaukastvāttadvayapadeśācca neticenna nicāyyatvādevaṃ vyomavacca oṃ ||
arbhakaukastvāt "sarveṣu bhūteṣu" iti sarvagatavastunaḥ sarvaprāṇihṛdayākāśarūpālpasthānatvakathanāt | tadvayapadeśāt | tasya jīvasya tacchatiliṅgādinā vyapadeśāt | uktatvācca | neticet brahmaiva sarvagatamityuktaṃ neticenna | kutaḥ teṣāṃ viṣṇāvevopapatteriti śeṣaḥ | na ca vaiyarthyam | evaṃ nicāyyatvāt | evamarbhakaukastvādirūpeṇa viṣṇorevopāsyatvāt | tathāca na vaiyarthyamiti bhāvaḥ | śrutiliṅgādīnāṃ brahmaparatvena na tadvirodha iti bhāvaḥ |vyāptasya viṣṇoḥ kathamalpasthānasthitirityata uktaṃ vyomavacceti | yathā''kāśasya vyāptasyāpi gṛhādyekadeśāvasthitiḥ tadvadviṣṇorapītyarthaḥ || 7 ||
oṃ sambhogaprāptiriticenna vaiśeṣyāt oṃ ||
yato brahmaṇaḥ vyāmavat sarvajīvaśarīrasthatve sambhogaprāptiḥ samānabhogaprāptirityato bādhakānnabrahma sarvagatamiti cenna | kutaḥ vaiśeṣyāt viṣṇoḥ sāmarthyaviśeṣavatvādityarthaḥ | tathā ca na tasya jīvasamānabhogaprāptiriti bhāvaḥ || 8 ||
|| attṛtvādhikaṇam || 2 ||
oṃ attā carācaragrahaṇāt oṃ ||

oṃ prakaṇācca oṃ ||

| guhādhikaraṇam || 3 ||

|| antarādhikaṇam || 4 ||

|| antaryābhyadhikaraṇam || 5 ||

oṃ jñeyatvāvacanācca oṃ ||

oṃ vadītīti cenna prājño oṃ ||

oṃ prakaṇāt oṃ ||

oṃ trayāṇāmeva caivamupanyāsaḥ praśnaśca oṃ ||
yuktisamuccaye co yata ityathare ca | evaśabdaḥ prakāratraividhyaparāmarśakaḥ | tathā ca yataḥ evaṃ trayāṇāmeva pitṛsaumanasyasvargyāgniparamātmarūpāṇāṃ trayāṇāmeva | tadviṣayaka eva praśnaḥ naciketaṛṣiṇā yamaṃ prati kṛtaḥ | teṣāmevopanyāsaḥ prativacanaṃ cātra prakaraṇaṣa dṛśyate | ato nātra pradhāne pratipādyamityarthaḥ || 7 ||
oṃ mahadvacca oṃ ||
caḥ dārṣyāntikasamuccaye | "mahāntaṃ vibhumātmānam"  ityādivākyagatamahacchabdasya yathā viṣṇuparatvameva na mahattattvaparatvaṃ tadvadavyaktādiśabdasyāpītyarthaḥ || 8 ||
oṃ camasavadaviśeṣāt oṃ ||
camasavat yathā yajñapātre prasiddhasyāpi camasaśabdasya "idaṃ tacchiraḥ"  iti śrutibalena śirovācakatā tadvadavyaktādiśabdasya pradhānādau prasiddhatve'pi viṣṇuvācakatvameva | kutaḥ? "nāmāni" iti śruteḥ | nanu tathāpi kuto viṣṇuvācakatvam? ityata uktam | aviśeṣāditi | "nāmāni sarvāṇi" iti śruteḥ "idaṃ" iti śrutyā viśeṣābhāvādityarthaḥ || 9 ||
|| jyotirupakramādhikaṇam || 2 ||

|| māṃsādhikaraṇam || 13 ||
oṃ māṃsādibhaumaṃ yathāśabdamitarayośca oṃ ||
bhaumamityupalakṣaṇam | yaccharīre kaṭhinaṃ māṃsādi tadeva bhaumaṃ pārthivaṃ na sarvaśarīram | kiṃ tarhi yathāśabdaṃ "yatkaṭhinaṃ sā pṛthivī"  iti śrutimanusṛtya | śarīre itarayoḥ aptejasośca kāryaṃ śoṇitamajjādirūpamaṅgīkāryam | yathā māṃsādikaṃ bhaumaṃ pṛthivīkāryam | na kevalaṃ pṛthivīkāryaṃ kinnāmetarayoraptejasośca kāyaṃ yathā śrutyaṅgīkāryamityarthaḥ || 1 ||
oṃ vaiśeṣyāttu tadvādastadvādaḥ oṃ ||
pārthivamāpyaṃ taijasamiti viśeṣoktistu vaiśeṣyāt pṛthivyādibhūtānāṃ tattaccharīraviśeṣasaṃyogamapekṣya yujyate | ataḥ na tadvirodha ityarthaḥ | etadadhyāyoktasarvasyātharsyāvadhāraṇārthamadhyāyānte dviruktiḥ || 2 ||
iti śrījagannāthayatikṛtāyāṃ sūtradīpikāyāṃ dvitīyādhyāyasya caturthaḥ pādaḥ || 2 || 4 ||
|| 1. tadantarādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ tadantarapratipattau raṃhati sampariṣvaktaḥ praśnanirūpaṇābhyām oṃ||
tadantarapratipattau śarīrāntaraprāptyartham | jīvaḥ sampariṣvaktaḥ pṛthivyādibhūtasaṃyukta eva | raṃhati imaṃ dehaṃ parityajya lokāntaraṃ gacchatīti jñāyate | kutaḥ | praśnanirūpaṇābhyāṃ "" vettha yatheti""  śvetaketumṛṣiṃ prati pravāhaṇasya rājñaḥ praśnāt | tathā "iti tviti" pravāhaṇoktaparihārāccetyarthaḥ || 1 ||
|| 2 || tyātmakatvādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ tyātmakatvāttu bhūyastvāt oṃ ||
jīvaḥ sarvabhūtapariṣvakta eva gacchati | nādbhireva | āpa iti viśeṣoktistu tyātmakatvāt apāṃ kṣitisalilānakātmakatvāt | teṣvapyapāṃ bhūyastvāt | bāhulyāt | yujayate | ato na tadvirodha ityarthaḥ ||
|| 3 || prāṇagatyadhikaraṇam ||
oṃ prāṇagateśca oṃ ||
bhūtavyāptaprāṇaśabditendriyāṇāṃ jīvena saha gamanaśravaṇācca | jīvo bhūtasampariṣvakta eva gacchatītyarthaḥ |
|| 4 || agyrādyadhikaraṇam ||
oṃ agyrādigatiśruteriti cenna bhāktatvāt oṃ ||
agnyādigatiśruteḥ prāṇānāṃ yatreti agnyādīn prati gamanasyokterna jīvena saha gamanamiti cenna | kutaḥ bhāktatvāt "yatreti" śruteḥ  prāṇabhāgaviṣayatvādityarthaḥ |
|| 5 || prathamādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ prathame'śravaṇāditicenna tā eva hyupapatteḥ oṃ ||
prathame tasminnityupakrame | prathame dyunāmake | prathamāgnau homaśravaṇāt bhūtānāṃ jīvasāhityaśravaṇaśabhāvācca | na jīvo bhūtasahito gacachatīti cenna | kutaḥ hi yataḥ | tā eva prastutā āpa evopakramagataśraddāśabdenocyante | ataḥ tatkutaḥ upapatteḥ "apaḥ puruṣavacaso bhavanti"  ityupasaṃhāropapatterityarthaḥ |
|| 6 || aśrutatvādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ aśrutatvāditicennaṣyādikāriṇāṃ pratīteḥ oṃ ||
aśrutatvāt bhūtānāṃ jīvena saha gamanasya "agniṃ vāgapyeti" itivat pratyakṣaśravaṇābhāvānna teṣāṃ saha gamanamiti cenna | kutaḥ "athainamiti" (kauṇḍiṇya) śrutyā iṣyādikāriṇāṃ yāgahomakṛtām | pratīte; pratyakṣata eva bhūtaviyogasahayogayoravagamādityarthaḥ |
|| 7 || bhāktādhikaraṇam ||
bhāktaṃ vā'nātmavittvāttathāhi darśayati oṃ ||nāmyakarmiṇāṃ uktamamṛtatvaṃ bhāktaṃ amukhyam | kutaḥ anātmavittvāt teṣāmabrahmajñānitvāt | vāśabdena jijñāsujñānikṛtākāmyakarmajanyāmṛtvamapi na saṃsāranivṛvirupamukhyāmṛtatvarūpam | kintu antaḥkaraṇaśuddhayānandātiśayarūpaniratiśayaparamamukhyamiti pakṣadvayaṃ sūcayati | kuta etat hi yasmāt | "amṛto vā" iti "sa enamiti"  "karmaṇā" iti "ātmānam" iti ca śrutiḥ tathoktārthān darśayati pratipādayati | tasmādityatharḥ |
|| 8 || kṛtātyayādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ kṛtātyaye'nuśayavān dṛṣyasmṛtibhyām oṃ ||
kṛtātyaye kṛtasya karmaṇaḥ svagarādau bhogenātyaye kṣaye sati | punaranuśayavān bhuktaśeṣavānevemaṃ lokaṃ pratyāgacchatīti jñāyate | kutaḥ dṛṣyasmṛtibhyām | "tataḥśeṣeṇa" iti dṛṣyaśabditaśruteḥ | "muktaśeṣeti" smṛteścetyarthaḥ |
|| 9 || yathetādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ yathetamanevañca oṃ ||
jīvena yathā yena mārgeṇa svargādikam | itaṃ prāptam | tathā tena | anevaṃ anyena mārgeṇa ca | jīva imaṃ lokamāgacchatītyarthaḥ |
|| 10 || caraṇādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ caraṇāditi cenna tadupalakṣaṇārtheti kārṣṇājiniḥ oṃ ||
gatāgataṃ na yajñāderbhavati | kintu caraṇāt yajñāṅgabhūtācārādeva | caraṇācāra iti śruteriti cenna | kutaḥ yataḥ śrutiḥ tadupalīṇārthājahatsvārthalakṣaṇayā yajñācārobhayapratipādiketi kārṣṇājinirācāryo manyate | ata ityarthaḥ |
|| 11 || ānarthakyamiti cenna tadapekṣatvāt oṃ ||
upalakṣaṇārthatve caraṇaśabdasyānarthakyam | kutaḥ ramaṇīyakapūyapadābhyāmeva karmopalakṣaṇasambhavāditi cenna | kutaḥ tadapekṣatvāt tasya sādhvasādhuyajñādittvasya tadapekṣatvāt ācārādisāpekṣatvāt | tathā ca tadbodhārthaṃ tatpadasārthakyaṃ sambhavatītyarthaḥ |
oṃ sukaataduṣkṛte eveti tu bādariḥ oṃ ||
sukṛthaduṣkṛte sādhyasādhukarmaṇī eva śrutisthacaraṇaśabdavācye na tu tadaṅgācaraṇamiti bādarirācāryo manta ityarthaḥ | tuśabdāt svaśabdāt svasiddhānto'pi sa eva iti sūcayati |
|| 11 || aniṣyādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ aniṣyādikāriṇāmapi ca śrutam oṃ ||
caśabdo duḥkhāntarasamuccaye | na kevalamiṣyādikāriṇāmeva gatāgataṃ kintviṣyaśabditayāgādikarmaviruddhapāpakāriṇāmapi | kutaḥ yataḥ śrutaṃ "tasya iha" iti (bhāllaveya" śrutyā tathoktam | ata ityarthaḥ |
oṃ saṃyamanetvanubhūyetareṣāmārohāvarohau tadgatidarśanāt oṃ ||
tuśabdo'vadhāraṇe | saptamī dvitīyārthe | saṃyamane narake | samyamyamaśāsanamanubhūyaiva sthitānāmitareṣāmiṣyādikāribhyo'nyeṣāṃ madhye ārohāvohau keṣāñcidārohaḥ narakādutthānaṃ keṣāñcidavarohaḥ tato'pi nīcasthāne nityanarake pāto bhavati | kutaḥ tadgatidarśanāt teṣāṃ aniṣyādikāriṇāṃ tadgateḥ evaṃvidhagateḥ darśanāt "savar" iha iti (kauṇṭharavya) śrutāvuktatvādityarthaḥ |
oṃ smaranti ca oṃ ||
smṛtikartāraḥ uktamarthaṃ "gacchanti" ityādismṛtibhiḥ pratipādayanti cetyarthaḥ |
|| 12 || apisaptādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ api sapta oṃ ||
smartāraḥ rauravādisaptavidhānapi narakān "rauravaḥ" iti (bhārate" smṛtibhiḥ pratipādayantītyarthaḥ |
|| 13 || tatrāpyadhikaraṇam ||
oṃ tatrāpi ca tadvayāpārādavirodhaḥ oṃ ||
caśabdo duḥkhānubhavaṃ vinaivetyarthasūcakaḥ | tathā ca tatrāpi narake'pi | tadvayāpārāt duḥkhānubhavaṃ vinaiva tasyeśvarasya preraṇākhyavyāpārasatvādavirodhaḥ | īśvarasya narake sthitvā tatsthajīvāniyantṛtve "sarvaṃ" iti śrutivirodhaḥ | tanniyantṛtve duḥkhabhoktṛtvamityubhayavidhavirodho netyarthaḥ |
|| 14 || vidyādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ vidyākarmaṇoriti tu prakṛtatvāt oṃ ||
tuśabdo'vadhāraṇe | "athaitayoḥ pathoḥ" iti  śrutāvetayorityasya jñānakarmaṇorityevārthaḥ | na tu dūmācirādimārgayoriti | yena jīvasya phalasvātantyaṃ syāt | kutaḥ prakṛtatvāt | "tadya ittham" iti  tayorapi prakṛtatvādityarthaḥ |
|| 15 || na tṛtīyādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ na tṛtīye tathopalabdheḥ oṃ ||
tṛtīye "tiryamyātanā tamaḥ" ityuktādhogatiṣu tṛtīye tamasi | na sukhamastīti śeṣa; | kutaḥ tathopalabdheḥ tathā pratīterityarthaḥ |
oṃ smaryate'pi ca loke oṃ ||
andhe tamasi sukharāhityaṃ "nāndhe tamasi" iti (bhaviṣyataparva) smṛtikartrā smaryate ca smṛtyā ca pratipādyate | kiñcaitat loke'pi lokadṛṣyavyāptikānumānenāpi siddhamityarthaḥ |
oṃ darśanācca oṃ ||
tṛtīye sukharāhityaṃ hiraṇyagarbhapratyakṣācca siddhamityarthaḥ |
oṃ tṛtīye śabdāvarodhaḥ saṃśokajasya oṃ ||
tṛtīya ityāvartate | tṛtīye tamasi | tatrāpi tṛtīye andhe tamasi tadviṣaye | yaḥ śabdaḥ | tena saṃśokajasya samyak śokajasya | mohasyāvarodhaḥ prāptirbhavatītyarthaḥ |
oṃ smaraṇacca oṃ ||
tamastraividhyasya tṛtīyatamasaḥ śravaṇe śrotṛmohaprāptiśca "mahātamaḥ" iti (kaurma) smṛtyā tadubhayaṃ siddhamityarthaḥ |
|| 16 || tatsvābhāvyādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ tatsvābhāvāpattirupapatteḥ ||
karmaṇi tatsvābhāvyāpattiḥ tasya dhūmādidevasya svābhāvyasya svabhāvasadṛśasvabhāvasya | prāptireva | na tadaikyaṃ tatpadaprāptirvā | kutaḥ upapatteḥ anyasyānyaikyādṛṣyayāsyaivārthasya yuktatvādityarthaḥ |
|| 17 || nāticireṇādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ nāticireṇa viśeṣāt oṃ ||
svagarānnirgataḥ karmī nāticireṇa acireṇaiva | bhūmāvāgatya brāhmaṇādiyoniṃ prāpnoti | na cireṇa | kutaḥ viśeṣāt
"tasya iheti"  viśeṣavacanādityarthaḥ |
|| 18 || anyādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ anyādhiṣṭhite pūrvavadabhilāpāt oṃ ||
kamirṇo anyādhiṣṭhite anyajīvābhimanyamānavrīhyādiśarīre praveśa eva na tu tadabhimānitvam | yena duḥkhaṃ syāt | tarhi "vrīhiyavāḥ" ityabhedoktiḥ kathamityata uktam | pūrvavaditi | yathā "dhūmo bhūtvā" ityādai  dhūmādibhāvoktiḥ tathā | kuta etat abhilāpāt "so'vāmataḥ" iti (kauṣārava) śrutau tathābhidhānādityarthaḥ |
oṃ aśuddhamiti cenna śabdāt oṃ ||
hiṃsārūpatvādyajñasya karmāsiddhaṃ duḥkhasādhanamiti cenna | kutaḥ śabdāt śrutivākyavihitatvādityarthaḥ |
|| 19 || reto'dhikaraṇam ||
oṃ retaḥ sigyogo'tha oṃ || karmiṇaḥ prathamaṃ retaḥsigyogaḥ retaḥsicā pitrā yogaḥ sambandhaḥ | tatpraveśa iti yāvat | atha anantaram | mātṛpraveśo bhavati na tu prathamevetyarthaḥ |
|| 20 || yonyadhikaraṇam ||
oṃ yoneḥ śarīram oṃ ||
lyabūlope pañcamī | karmī pitṛdvārā mātṛyoniṃ praviśyaivātha tatra śarīraṃ na tadvinetyarthaḥ |
iti śrījagannāthayatikṛtāyāṃ sūtradīpikāyāṃ tṛtīyādhyāyasya prathamaḥ pādaḥ |
|| 1 || sandhyādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ sandhye sṛṣyirāha hi oṃ ||
hi yasmāt | "atha rathān" iti  śrutiḥ sandhye jāgratsuṣuptisandhau bhavatīti svapne bhagavataḥ sṛṣyimāha | ataḥ sṛṣyiḥ svapnapadārthasṛṣyiḥ | bhagavata eva jāyata ityarthaḥ |
oṃ nimarātāraṃcaike putrādayaśca oṃ ||
co yataḥ | eke śākhinaḥ | enaṃ bhagavantam | "ya eṣu supteṣu" iti  svapnapadātharnirmātāramāmananti | apare ca putrādayaḥ | tasmādviṣṇoreva jāyanta iti vadanti | tasmādviṣṇoreva svāpnasṛṣyirityarthaḥ |
oṃ māyāmātrantu kātsnayenānabhivyaktisvarūpatvāt oṃ ||
turevārthe | sandhyaṃ māyāmātraṃ nimittopādānabhūtābhyāṃ māyāśabditecchāvāsanābhyāmeva nirmitaṃ na bāhyakāraṇakam | kutaḥ kātsnayenānabhivyaktasvarūpatvāt bāhyavatsamyaganabhivyaktasvarūpatvādityarthaḥ |
oṃ sūcakaśca hi śruterācakṣate ca tadvidaḥ oṃ ||
co yataḥ | svāpnapadārthaḥ sūcakaḥ śubhāśubhajñāpakaḥ | ato'pi nāsatyaḥ | tatkutaḥ "yatā" iti  śruteḥ | pratyakṣataḥ phaladarśanācceti hi śabdaḥ | kiñca tadvidaḥ svāpnavido vyāsādayaḥ ācakṣate ca | "yadā" iti śrutau
svāpnārthānāmarthakriyākāritvaṃ vadantyato'pītyarthaḥ |
|| 2 || parābhidhyānādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ parābhidhyānāttu tirohitaṃ tato hyasya bandhaviparyayau oṃ ||
yasmāt | tataḥ paramātmanaḥ sakāśāt | asya jīvasya | bandhaviparyayau bandhamokṣau bhavataḥ | tasmātparābhidhyānāttu paramātmecchayaiva | sandhyaṃ tirohitaṃ līnaṃ bhavati | na kāraṇāntarādityarthaḥ |
|| 3 || dehayogādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ dehayogādvāso'pi oṃ ||
dehayogādvāso'pi dehābhimānenāvasthitirūpajāgradavasthāpi parābhidhyānādeva bhavati nānyata ityarthaḥ |
|| 4 || tadabhāvādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ tadabhāvo nāḍīṣu tacchaterātmani ha oṃ ||
asya jīvasya nāḍīṣu ātmani nāḍīsthite paramātmanyeva | tadabhāvaḥ jāgratsvapnayorabhāvaḥ | suṣuptirjāyate | tasya tadā tatpraveśo bhavati | kutaḥ tacchate; "āsu tadā nāḍīṣu"  śruteḥ tathā śravaṇādityarthaḥ | hetyasyārthasya yuktatāmāha |
|| 5 || prabodādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ ataḥ prabodho'smāt ||
asya jīvasya prabodhaḥ suptarutthānam | asmāt paramātmana eva bhavati nānyataḥ | kutaḥ "eṣa eva" iti (kauṇḍiṇya) śrutvādityarthaḥ |
|| 6 || karmānusmṛtyadhikaraṇam ||
oṃ sa eva ca karmānusmṛtiśabdavidhibhyaḥ oṃ ||
caśabdo'pyarthe | sa eva īśvara eva | sarveṣāmapi sarvadā sarvāvasthāprerakaḥ | na deśākālāntare'nyaḥ | kutaḥ karmānusmṛtiśabdavidhibhyaḥ "eṣa hyeva" iti  karmaṇāmīśādhīnatvaśruteḥ | pradarśaka ityanukūlasmṛteśca | "eṣa" iti śabdāccātmānamiti  vidheścetyarthaḥ |
|| 7 || sampattyadhikaraṇam ||
oṃ mugdhe'rdhasampattiḥ pariśeṣāt oṃ ||
mugdhe murchāyām | jīvasyārdhasampattiḥ paramātmanyardhapraveśaḥ | kutaḥ pariśeṣāt prasaktapratiṣedhapūrvakānumānādityarthaḥ |
|| 8 || nasthānato'pyadhikaraṇam ||
oṃ na sthānato'pi parasyobhayaliṅgaṃ sarvatra hi oṃ ||
parasya paramātmanaḥ | sthānato'pi akṣyādisthānabhedādapi | ubhayaliṅgaṃ bhinnarūpam | nāsti | kintu sarvatra sarvasthāneṣu sthitaṃ rūpamekameva | kutaḥ hi yasmāt | "sarveṣu" iti  śrutiratrāsti | ata ityarthaḥ |
oṃ na bhedāditi cenna pratyekamatadvacanāt oṃ ||
bhedāt kāryakāraṇabaddhāviti  svapnajāgradanubhavajanakatvarūpabhedakatvadharmaśravaṇāt viśvādirūpāṇāṃ nābheda iti cenna | kutaḥ pratyekaṃ parasparam | teṣāṃ rūpāṇām | atadvacanāt "eṣa ta ātmā" ityabheda śruterityarthaḥ |
oṃ api caivameke oṃ ||
yata evamabhedaṃ caśabdādanantarūpatvaṃ caike śākhinaḥ "amātraḥ" iti  śrutau paṭhanti | api abhede'pi bhedavyapadeśaḥ sthānabhedādyujyate ityarthaḥ |
|| 9 || arūpādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ arūpavadeva hi tatpradhānatvāt oṃ ||
brahma arūpavadeva prākṛtarūparahitameva | kutaḥ tatpradhānatvāt prakṛtyādyuttamatvāt | hiśabdasūcitāsthūlamiti  śruteścetyarthaḥ |
oṃ prakāśavaccāvaiyarthyam ||
co hetusūcakaḥ | prakāśavat lokavilakṣaṇacakṣurādiprakāśe nāsti prakāśa iti vyāvahārasyeva | avaiyarthyaṃ na brahmaṇo rūpitvaśrutīnāmaprāmāṇy | kutaḥ tasyārūpapitve'pi vilakṣaṇarūpitvabhyupagamādityatharḥ |
oṃ āha ca tanmātram oṃ ||
yata "aikātmyam" iti  śrutiḥ brahmaṇaḥ tanmātraṃ vijñānānandaikasvarūpaṃ rūpamāha vaktītyarthaḥ | ato na vailakṣaṇyāniruktiriti caśabdārthaḥ |
oṃ darśayati cātho api smaryate oṃ ||
co yataḥ | caḥ samuccaye | na kevalaṃ brahmaṇaḥ ānandādyātmakatvaṃ darśayati | kintu "tadvijñānena"  iti śrutiḥ jñānasya brahmātmakatvamapi darśayati pratipādayati | atho ityarthāntaravāci | atho vijñānasyāpi brahmarūpatvaṃ smaryate vyāsādibhiḥ "śuddhasphaṭikam" ityādinā (mātsye) ato na jñānānandayoḥ brahmasvarūpatvamaprāmāṇikamityarthaḥ |
|| 10 || upamādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ ata eva copamā sūryakādivat oṃ ||
ata eva īśatadrūpāṇāmabhedoktereva | ata eva cetanatvādihetoreva | upamā jīvasya matsyādisāmyam | matsyādivadīśvarābheda iti cet | neti śeṣaḥ | caśabdena bhinnatvasāmye samuccinoti | kutaḥ yataḥ sūryakādivat yathā jalapratibimbitasūryakādeḥ sūryādipratibimbatvādbhinnatvaṃ tathā jīvasya pratibimbatvāt bhinnatvamityarthaḥ | yadvā | ata eva matsyādīnāṃ brahmaṇo jñānādiguṇairaviśeṣoktereva | ata eva sūryakādivat | upamā sūryakādyupamā | jīvasya matsyāditulyāṃśatve tadaviśeṣāpattereva upamā brahmapratibimbatetyarthaḥ | ata eva brahmapratibimbatvādeva sūryakādivanna brahmābhinno jīva iti caśabdārthaḥ | athavā | ata eva prakṛtābhyāṃ tadadhīnatvatatsadṛśatvābhyāmeva | upamā sūryakādivadityapamā | jīvasyeśvarapratibimbatvāt nopādhyadhīnatvādinā na tannivṛttyā pratibimbatānivṛvirityarthaḥ |
|| 11 || ambuvadadhikaraṇam ||
oṃ ambuvadagrahaṇāttu na tathātvam oṃ ||
sādṛśyārthe vatiḥ | sādṛśyaṃ snehākhyaviśeṣeṇa vivakṣitam | tathā ca yataḥ ambuvadagrahaṇāt snehasahitamāhātmyajñānābhāvāt | bhakiṃta vineti yāvat | tathātvaṃ sukhādyātmakatvarapeśvarasādṛśyam | na tu samyagvyajyate | ato bhaktireva samyak svarūpābhivyaktijaniketi tadarthaṃ sā kartavyetyarthaḥ |
|| 12 || vṛddhihrāsādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ vṛddhihrāsabhāktvamantarbhāvādubhayasāmañjasyādevam oṃ ||
vṛddhihrāsabhāktvaṃ bhaktyādeḥ sādhakeṣvādhikyanyūnatā na tāvadaṅgīkāryam | kutaḥ antarbhāvāt mahadalpaphalavatāṃ brahmādīnāmanyeṣāṃ ca bhaktatve'ntarbhāvāt | tathāpi kuto vṛddhihrāsabhāktvam | evaṃ bhaktitāratamye satyeva | ubhayasāmañjasyāt ubhayān mahadalpaphalān brahmādijīvān prati phaladāturīśvarasya sāmañjasyāt vaiṣamyena phaladānasya bhaktitāratamyena yuktatvāt | anyathā ayuktatvāt | atastadaṅgīkāyarmityarthaḥ |
oṃ darśanācca oṃ || "
athāta" iti  śrutau "yatheti" smṛtau ca phalatāratamyadarśanāt uktatvāccoktaṃ yuktamityarthaḥ |
|| 13 || pālakatvādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ prakṛtaitāvattvaṃ hi pratiṣedhati tato bravīti ca bhūyaḥ oṃ ||
hi yataḥ | naitāvadeneti  śratiḥ prakṛtaitāvattvaṃ prakṛtasya sṛṣyayādikartṛtvasya | etāvattvaṃ sṛṣyisaṃhārakartṛkatvamātraparyavasitatvam | pratiṣedhati nirācaṣye | tataḥ sṛṣyayādikatarṛkatvāt | bhūyaḥ adhikam | pālakatvaṃ ca bravīti | ataḥ pālakatvāt sṛṣyiṃ ceti sūcitapālakatvākhyaṃ mahimāntaramapi harerastīti jñāpayatītyarthaḥ | caśabdena sṛṣyiṃ ceti smṛtiḥ (brahmāṇḍe) sūcitā |
|| 14 || avyaktatvādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ tadavyaktamāha oṃ ||
tat brahma | avyaktaṃ puruṣaprayatnena draṣyumaśakyasvabhāvam | kutaḥ hi yasmāt | avyaktaṃ ca niṣkalamiti (kauṇṭharavya)
śrutistathā'ha tasmādityarthaḥ |
oṃ api saṃrādhane pratyakṣānumānābhyām oṃ ||
api bhaktirahitārādhanenāpi na brahma vyaktīkartuṃ śakyam | kutaḥ saṃrādhane'pi sampūjane'pi | avyaktatvasya pratyakṣānumānābhyāṃ jñānipratyakṣasūkṣmatyahetukānumānābhyāṃ siddhatvādityarthaḥ |
oṃ prakāśavaccāvaiśeṣyam oṃ ||
co nañarthaḥ | prakāśavat yathāgneḥ sthūlasūkṣmābhyāṃ vyaktatvāvyaktatve | tathā brahma na kintu svato'vyaktameva | kutaḥ yataḥ avaiśeṣyaṃ brahmaṇi sthūlasūkṣmatvaviśeṣo nāsti | ata ityarthaḥ |
oṃ prakāśavacca karmaṇyabhyāsāt oṃ ||
prakāśaśca aparokṣajñānañca | karmaṇi viṣayabhūte brahmaṇi | abhyāsāt śravaṇāderāvartanāt | bhavītyarthaḥ |
oṃ ato'nantena tathāhi liṅgam oṃ ||
ataḥ avyaktatve pratyakṣatve pramāṇasadbhāvāt | tadanyathā'nupapattyā | anantena aparicchinnena brahmaṇā | tatprasādenāvyaktasyāparokṣyaṃ bhavatīti jñāyate | na kevalamanupapattimātrāt kiṃ tarhi | hi yasmāt | tathā liṅgaṃ tasyābhidhyānādityuktamīśaprasādasya bandhanivṛttihetutvarapam | prasādasya darśanahetutvajñāpakamasti tasmādityarthaḥ |
|| 14 || ahikuṇḍalādhikaram ||
oṃ ubhayavyapadeśāttvahikuṇḍalavat oṃ ||
brahma ānandaguṇakaṃ tadrūpañca bhavati | kutaḥ ubhayavyapadeśāt ānando brahma  ānandaṃ brahmaṇaḥ  ityādiśrutiṣu guṇātmakatvena guṇitvena ca brahmaṇaḥ uktatvāt | tadapi katham | ahikuṇḍalavat yathā'heḥ sarpasya | kuṇḍalatvaṃ kuṇḍalitvañca | viśeṣāttathetyarthaḥ |
oṃ prakāśāśrayavadvā tejastvāt oṃ ||
yathā prakāśāśrayasyādityasya prakāśarūpatvaṃ tadāśrayatvañca tadvat brahmaṇo'pi | anyaparityāgenādityadṛṣyānte ko heturityata uktam | tejastvāditi | brahmādityayorubhayorapi tejorūpatvāt tadabhiprāyeṇa taddṛṣyāntīkaraṇamityarthaḥ |
oṃ pūrvavadvā oṃ ||
yathā kālaḥ pūrveṇābhinno'pi pūrvakāla iti pūrvapadena viśiṣyate tathā brahma ānandena viśiṣyata ityarthaḥ | ādarārthaṃ sthūralasūkṣmabuddhivivakṣayā vā dṛṣyāntatrayoktiḥ |
oṃ pratiṣedhācca oṃ ||
brahmaguṇānāṃ brahmaṇaśca "neha nānāsti"  iti bhedasya | pratiṣedhācca brahma guṇātmakamityarthaḥ |
|| 16 || paramatādhikaraṇam ||

brahmaguṇajātaṃ paraṃ lokavilakṣaṇam | kutaḥ setūnmānasambandhabhedavyapadeśebhyaḥ "eṣa sutuḥ"  iti setutvokteḥ | "yato vācaḥ"  ityunmānaśabditapūrṇatvokteḥ | "etasyaivānandasya"  iti bimbapratibimbabhāvasambandhokteḥ | anyajjñānaṃtviti laukikānandādbhedokteścetyarthaḥ |
oṃ darśanāt oṃ ||

oṃ buddhayarthaḥ pādavat oṃ ||
yathā lokapādavilakṣaṇeṣu jīvākhyabhūteṣu "pādo'sya viśvā bhūtāni"  iti pādapadaprayogaḥ | buddhayarthaḥ bhūtānāṃ īśāṃśattvajñāpanārthaḥ | tathā'laukike'pi brahmānandādāvānandapadaprayogaḥ | buddhayarthaḥ anukūlatayā vedanīyatvādidharmajñāpanārtha ityatharḥ |

|| 18 || tathānyatvādhikaṇam ||

|| 19 || sarvagatatvādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ anena sarvagatatvamāyāmayaśabdādibhyaḥ oṃ ||
anena bhagavataiva | sarvadeśakālayoḥ sṛṣyayādirbhavati | na tu deśakālāntarayoranyena | kutaḥ sarvagatatvamāyāmayaśabdādibhyaḥ "eṣa sarvagata;" iti (bhāllaveya) sarvagatatvaśruteḥ | tasya ca sṛṣyayādyarthatvāt | viṣṇau prayuktamāyāmayaśabdavyākhyānarūpasarvatretyādi (catuvaredaśi) śruteścetyarthaḥ | anyatra pramāṇābhāvāccetyādiśabdārthaḥ |
|| 20 || phalādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ phalamata upapatteḥ oṃ ||
phalaṃ svargādi | ataḥ īśvarādeva bhavati | na karmataḥ | kutaḥ upapatteḥ īśvarasya cetanatvena phaladātṛtvasya yuktatvādityarthaḥ |
oṃ śrutatvācca oṃ ||
śrutatvācca "rātiḥ" iti  śrutyuktatvācceśvarasyaiva phaladātṛtvamityarthaḥ |
oṃ dharmaṃ jaiminirata eva oṃ ||
jaiminirācāryaḥ dharmaṃ phalapradamāha | taṃ ca dharmam | ata eva īśvarādeva janyaṃ manyate | kutaḥ ata eva "eṣa hyeva" iti  śruterevetyarthaḥ |
oṃ pūrvaṃ tu bādarāyaṇo hetuvyapadeśāt oṃ ||
bādarāyaṇācāryastu | pūrvaṃ pūrvoktam | brahma dharmaṃ cobhayaṃ phalahetuṃ manyate | tatra brahmakartṛtvena kamar karaṇatvena heturiti viśeṣastuśabdārthaḥ | kutaḥ hetuvyapadeśāt "puṇyena" iti  śrutau brahmakarmaṇoḥ kartṛtvakaraṇatvarūpahetutvasyoktatvādityarthaḥ |

|| 1 || sarvavedādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ sarvavedāntapratyayaṃ codanādyaviśeṣāt oṃ ||
pūrvasūtre pūrvapadoditaṃ brahma sarvavedāntapratyayam | bahuvrīhiḥ | yathāśakti sarvavedaviṣayakaśravaṇādyutpannanirṇayajanyajñānaviṣayam | kutaḥ codanādyaviśeṣāt "ātmā" ityādi  vidhīnāmādipadoditānāṃ "atra hyate"  ityādivākyoktayuktīnāṃ cāviśeṣāt | sarvādhikārisādhāraṇatvādityarthaḥ |
oṃ bhedānneti cedekasyāmapi oṃ ||
cediti neti bhedāditi apīti cāvartate | tarhīti śākhāyāmiti ca labhyate | tathā ca cedyadi sarvaśākhoktārthānāṃ bhedāt pṛthatkvāt | ekekaina sarvaśākhoktaṃ jñeyaṃ na | kintu svasvāśākhoktamevetyucyate | tarhi api svasvaśākhoktamapi | na jñeyaṃ syāt | kutaḥ ekasyāṃ śākhāyāmapi bhedāt arthabhedadarśanāt | atha yadi tatra dharmabhedādekasmin dharmiṇi bhinnadharmavidhānāt prasaṅgabhedācceti parihāra ucyate tasya sarvaśākhoktaparijñāne'pi sama ityarthaḥ |
oṃ svādhyāyasya tathātvena hi samācāre'dhikārācca oṃ ||
svādhyāyasya "svādhyāyo'dhyetavyaḥ" iti  vidheḥ | tathātvena sarvasādhāraṇyenādhyayanavidhāyakatvena | samācāre sarvavaidikamarkaraṇe | adhikārāt sarveṣāmadhikārasadbhāvācca | hīti sūcitāyāḥ "vedaḥ kṛtsnodhikantavyaḥ" iti smṛteśca
ekaikenāpi sarvaśākhādhyayanena brahma jñātavyamityarthaḥ |
oṃ salilavacca tanniyamaḥ oṃ ||
co'vadhāraṇe | tanniyama ityatra pañcamītatpuṣakamardhārayau | salilavat salilamiva | tanniyamaḥ yathā sarveṣāṃ salilānāṃ pratibandhābhāvamapekṣyaiva samudraikāśrayatvaniyamaḥ | tathā'trāpi tasyāḥ puruṣaśaktimapekṣyaiva tanniyamaḥ | sarvavedoktaprakāreṇa dhyānena brahma jñātavyamiti niyama ityarthaḥ |
oṃ upasaṃhāro'rthābhedādvidhiśeṣavatsamāne ca oṃ ||
vidhiśeṣavat vidhivihitasyeva | yathā vidhivihitatvāt sandhyāvandanādikamavaśyaṃ kartavyam | tathā arthābhedāt bhedo vibhāgaḥ | sarvavedārthabhūtānāmānandādiguṇānāmapahatapāpmatvādidoṣābhāvānāṃ cābhedāt avibhāgāt | tānavibhajyati yāvat | upasaṃhāraḥ sarvaguṇānāmapyekabudhyārohaṇarūpopasaṃhāraḥ | "upāsya ekaḥ" iti (bhāllaveya) śrutau vihitatvāt kartavyaḥ | so'pi samāne ca samānaviṣaya eva | ye brahmaṇi yogyāḥ pūrṇatvāvirodhino guṇāḥ | tadviṣaya eva | natvayogyarodanādau | tathā satyanarthaprāpterityarthaḥ |
oṃ anyathātvaṃ caśabdāditi cennāviśeṣāt oṃ ||
anyathātvaṃ sarvaguṇopasaṃhāraḥ kārya ityuktasyānyathābhāvaḥ | tasyākartavyatvamevāvaśyamaṅgīkāryam | kutaḥ śabdāt "ātmetyevopāsīta" ityanupasaṃhāra śruteḥ  iti cenna | aviśeṣāt ete guṇā nopasaṃhāyarāiti viśeṣapramāṇābhāvāt | sarvaiguṇairityaviśeṣeṇopasaṃhāre tu viśeṣapramāṇasadbhāvāccetyarthaḥ | ātmetyevetyevakārastvayogavyavacchedaka iti bhāvaḥ |
oṃ na vā prakaraṇabhedātparovīyastvādivat oṃ ||
parovarīyastvādivat "yathā sa eṣaḥ"  ityuktānāṃ niravadhikasarvottamatvarūpaparovarīyastvādigurānāmevopāsanārthamupasaṃho na sarvaguṇānām | tadvatsarvaguṇopasaṃhāro na kartavyo vā | kutaḥ prakaraṇebhedāt parorīyo vaiśvānarādiprakaraṇabhedāt | anyathā tanna syādityarthaḥ |
oṃ saṃjñātaścettaduktamasti tu tadapi oṃ ||
saṃjñātaḥ sarvavidyānāṃ bhagavannāmatvena tadguṇoktirūpatvāt | sarvavidyoktaguṇopasaṃhāraḥ kārya iti cet | taduktaṃ tatra codye tadiṣyameveti sāmādhānaṃ tenopasaṃhārasūtreṇoktamityarthaḥ | na kevalaṃ yuktisiddhametat | kintu tadapi tasminnarthe "nāma vā etā" iti (kauṇḍinyaśruteḥ) pramāṇamapyasti | tu asyevetyarthaḥ |

|| 16 || vedhādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ vedhādyarthabhedāt oṃ ||
arthabhedāt vedhādyupāsanaphalasya hiṃsāderyatyādiyogyaphalādvilakṣaṇatvāt vedhādi bhindhi viddhi śṛṇīhī (bṛhattantre) tyuktavedhādikaṃ na sarvopāsyamityarthaḥ |
|| 17 || hānyadhikaraṇam ||
oṃ hānau tūpāyanaśabdaśeṣatvātkuśācchandastutyupagānavattaduktam oṃ ||
turavadhāraṇe | yataḥ hānau bandhahānirūpamuktau | taduktaṃ muktānāṃ "etatsāma" iti  śrutāvupāsanamuktam | ato mokṣe'pi teṣāmupāsanamatsyeva | tadapi kuśācchandastutyupagānavadeva na vidhitaḥ | yathā niyatabrahmayajñānāṃ dvijānāṃ niyatasandhyopāsanānantaramapi kuśairācchandena svecchayaiva stutyupagāna ivobhayatra nāmnoradhyayanam | tathā svecchayaiva na vidhitaḥ | kutaḥ upāyanaśabdaśeṣatvāt | upāyanaṃ mokṣaḥ upāsanāvidhīnāṃ mokṣavākyaśeṣatvādityarthaḥ | tathā ca mokṣārthā upāsanāvidhayaḥ kathaṃ tatrāpi tā vidadhyurityarthaḥ |
oṃ sāmparāye tartavyābhāvāttathā hyanye oṃ ||
sāmparāye mokṣe | upāsanaṃ svecchayaivetyaṅgīkāyarm | na tvaniṣyanivṛttyartham | kutaḥ tartavyābhāvāt muktaiḥ parihartavyāniṣyasyaivābhāvāt | tadapi kutaḥ hi yasmāt | anye śākhinaḥ tathā "tīrṇo hi"  śrutiṃ paṭhanti | tasmādityarthaḥ |
|| 18 || chandādhikaraṇam ||
chandata ubhayāvirodhāt oṃ ||
muktāḥ chandataḥ svecchāmanusṛtyaiva karma kurvanti na vidhitaḥ | kutaḥ ubhayāvirodhāt karmakaraṇākaraṇayoḥ vidhibandhapratyavāyayoḥ abhāvādityarthaḥ |
oṃ gateratharvattvamubhayathā'nyathā hi virodhaḥ oṃ ||
hirhetau | ubhayathā hi bandhapratyavāyayorabhāvādeva | gateḥ mukteḥ | puruṣārthatvam | anyathā muktau bandhapratyavāyayorbhāve | virodhaḥ mukte; saṃsārasamatvenāpuruṣārthatvarūpavirodhaḥ syādityarthaḥ |
oṃ upapannastallakṣaṇārthopalabdherlokavat oṃ ||
yathā loke yāgāṅgaviṣṇukramaṇasya kriyānuṣṭhānaniyamaḥ na yāgasampūtyarnantaramapi asti | kintu kadācideva | tathā muktānāṃ kadācitkarma kurvanti na vetyevaṃ tāvadupapannaḥ yuktaḥ | kuta etat | tallakṣaṇārthopalabdheḥ tallakṣaṇasya paramparayā karmasādhyasyātharsya mokṣasya | upalabdheḥ prāptatvādityarthaḥ |
|| 19 || aniyamādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ aniyamaḥ sarveṣāmavirodhācchabdānumānābhyām oṃ ||
aniyamaḥ aparokṣajñānināṃ madhye keṣāñcinmokṣaḥ keṣāñcinnetyevaṃrūpavyavasthābhāvo jñāyate | kutaḥ sarveṣāmapi mokṣe | avirodhāt niṣedhakābhāvāt | kiñca śabdānumānābhyāṃ "na kaścit" iti (kauṇḍinya) śruteḥ tanmūlakānumānaccetyarthaḥ |
|| 20 || yāvadadhikaraṇam ||
oṃ yāvadadhikāramavasthitirādhikārikāṇām oṃ ||
ādhikārikāṇāṃ yogyatānusāryupāsanaśīlānāṃ brahmādīnāṃ svasvādhikārānusāreṇaiva muktau | avasthitiḥ | avasthitānāmupāsanāvattāratamyenaiva muktau teṣāṃ phalamityarthaḥ |
oṃ akṣaradhiyāṃ tvavirodhaḥ sāmānyatadbhāvābhyāmaupasadavattaduktam oṃ ||
tuśabdo'pyarthaḥ | akṣaradhiyāṃ brahmajñāninām |kivaṣamatvepyavirodhaḥ virodho nāsti | kutaḥ brahmadhītvāt | kiñca sāmānyatadbhāvābhyāṃ sarveṣāṃ doṣābhāvāt uttamebhyaḥ adhamānāṃ upakārabhāvācca | katham | aupasadavat sacchiṣyavat | kiñca taduktaṃ tat asamatve'pi virodho nāstītyetat "nānā" iti turaśrutāvuktamato'pyavirodha ityarthaḥ |
|| 21 || iyadāmananādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ iyadāmananāt oṃ ||
adhikāritāratamyaṃ iyadeva prāṇāvadhikameva | kutaḥ āmananāt "
prāṇo vāva" iti (kauṇṭharavya) śruterityarthaḥ |
oṃ antarā bhūtagrāmavaditi cettaduktam oṃ ||
bhūtagrāmavat yathā nāmādiprāṇasamūhe ekasmādeka uttamo dṛṣyaḥ | tathā antarā paramātmānaṃ śriyañca vinā prāṇādapyuttamrostviti cet | neti śeṣaḥ | yatastaduktaṃ tatra prāṇāduttamābhāve nahīti (kauṇṭharavya) pramāṇamuktam | ata ityarthaḥ |
oṃ anyathā bhedānupapattiriti cennopadeśavat oṃ ||
anyathā prāṇādapyuttamābhāve | prāṇaparamātmano; bhedānupapattiriti cenna | kutaḥ yataḥ upadeśavat yathā śrutāvuktaṃ tathā'ṅgīkriyate | ato noktadoṣa ityarthaḥ |
|| 22 || vyatihārādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ vyatihāro viśiṃṣanti hītaravat oṃ ||
vyatihāraḥ prāṇaprakaraṇādanantaraṃ viṣṇoḥ prakaraṇāt prāk praśnapraticvacanayoradhyāhāraḥ kārya; | kutaḥ hi yasmāt | iravat nāmādito vāgādikamiva prāravādinaḥ sakāśāt satyākhyaviṣṇuvādinaḥ chandogāḥ "eṣa tu vā" iti  tuśabdena viśiṃṣanti vyāvartayanti | tasmādityarthaḥ |
|| 23 || satyādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ saiva hi satyādayaḥ oṃ ||
hi yasmāt | satyādyāḥ ahamātmāntaḥ padārthāḥ | saiva īśvarākhyaparadevataiva | tasmātprāṇādhikāḥ bahavaḥ na | kintu śrītadhīśāvevetyarthaḥ |
|| 24 || kāmādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ kāmāditaratra tatra cāyatanādibhyaḥ oṃ ||
lakṣmīḥ tatra bhagavatsamīpe sthitā satī | itaratra ca bhūmyādāvapi | kāmāt īśvarecchayaivāvatārānkaroti | kutaḥ āyatanādibhyaḥ sarvagatatvapratipādakasarvāyatanetyādiśrutyādibhya ityarthaḥ |
oṃ ādarādalopaḥ oṃ ||
ādarāt bhagavati nirupādhikabhaktyatiśayāt | alopaḥ śriyo bhagavadupāsanābhāvo netyarthaḥ |
oṃ upasthitestadvacanāt oṃ ||
śriyo nityamuktatvaṃ yujyate | kutaḥ upasthiteḥ harisamīpe sadā sthiteḥ | śriyo nityasamīpasthatvaṃ ca | tadvacanāt "dvāvetau" iti (gaupavanaśruteḥ" nityasambandhitvavacanāt | jñāyata ityarthaḥ |
|| 25 || nirdhāraṇādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ tannirdhāraṇārthaniyamastaddṛṣyeḥ phalam oṃ ||
yatastannirdhāraṇārthaniyamaḥ vedārthaniścayo vedārthaniyamaścetyetadrūpam | śravaṇamananaphalam | dhyāphalabhūtāyāḥ | taddṛṣyeḥ brahmadaśarnāt | pṛthagevabhinnameva | ato na brahmadṛṣyiḥ śravaṇādinā bhavatītyarthaḥ | tathāpi na śravaṇamananavaiyarthyam | kutaḥ hi yasmāt | apratibandhaḥ brahmadṛṣyipratibandhakanivṛttiḥ | tanniścayaḥ tanniyamadvārā śravaṇamananayoḥ phalam | ato dhyānasādhyāpi dṛṣyiḥ pratibandhakanivṛttidvārā śravaṇādisādhyā bhavati | tadarthaṃ śravaṇādikamapi kāryamevetyarthaḥ | hi śabdena "ātmā vā are"
iti  śrutiḥ sūcitā |
|| 26 || pradānādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ pradānavadeva hi taduktam oṃ ||
pradānavat samyak prītipūrvakaṃ gurūpadeśayuktameva śravaṇādikaṃ jñānasādhanaṃ na kevalam | kutaḥ hi yataḥ | taduktam | tasya jñānasya guruprasādādhīnatvaṃ "ācāryavān" iti  śrutāvuktamata ityarthaḥ |
|| 27 || liṅgabhūyastvādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ liṅgabhūyastvāttaddhi balīyastadapi oṃ ||
tat guruprasādapūrvakapradānamātram | balīyaḥ atiśayena balavat | kutaḥ liṃgabhūyastvāt vṛṣabhahaṃsajalavāyasarūpebhyo vāyvagnivaruṇebhyaḥ śrutavidyenāpi satyakāmena vacanāt | liṃgabhūyatsvāt gurupradānādirūpaliṃgabāhulyadarśanāt | tathāpi tadapi śravaṇādikamapi kāryam | kutaḥ "śrotavyaḥ" iti  śruterityarthaḥ | hi śabdaḥ śrutisūcakaḥ |
|| pūrvavikalpādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ pūrvavikalpaḥ prakaraṇātsyātkriyāmānasavat oṃ ||
kriyāmānasavat mānasakriyārūpadhyāne yathā vikalpaḥ tathā pūrvavikalpaḥ pūrvaprāptena guruṇā samasya paścātprāptasya guroḥ | vikalpaḥ grāhyo vā naveti vikalpaḥ syāt | na tu svīkārya eveti vidhiḥ | kutaḥ prakaraṇāt prakṛṣyānugrahamapekṣya guroḥ svīkāyartvāditi bhāvaḥ |
oṃ atideśācca oṃ ||
pūrvaguruṇaiva svasamottamagurusvīkārāyātideśāt | "yathā" iti pauṣyāyaṇaśrutyātidiśyamānatvāt | yuktameva paścāttanasvīkaraṇamityarthaḥ |
|| 29 || vidyādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ vidyaiva tu nirdhāraṇāt oṃ ||
"nānyaḥ panthāḥ" iti  nirdhāraṇāt anyaniṣedhena jñānasyaiva mokṣasādhanatvaniścayāt | vidyaiva mokṣasādhanaṃ na karmādikamityarthaḥ | śruteḥ prābalyadyotakastuśabdaḥ |
oṃ darśanācca oṃ ||
na kevalaṃ parokṣamaptirūpavidyayaiva mokṣaḥ | kintu darśanācca brahmasākṣātkārādapi | ato na dhyānavaiyarthyamityarthaḥ |
|| 30 || śrutyadhikaraṇam ||
oṃ śrutyādibalīyastvācca na bādhaḥ oṃ ||
mokṣasya jñānasādhyatve'bādhaḥkarmaṇaiveti smṛtivirodho nāsti kutaḥ | śrutyādibalīyastvāt jñānasya mokṣasādhakatvopapādakatvaṃ "evaṃ viditvā" ityādi śrutiliṃgāderatibalavatvena smṛtigataivaśabdasyāyogavyavacchedārthatvopapatterityarthaḥ | etatsūcanāya caśabdaḥ |
|| 31 || anubandhādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ anubandhādibhyaḥ oṃ ||
na kevalaṃ śravaṇādinā | nāpi prabalaguruprasādena brahmadarśanaṃ bhavati | kintvanubandhaśabditaharigurubhaktiśamādibhyaścetyarthaḥ |
|| 32 || prajñāntarādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ prajñāntarapṛthaktvavaddṛṣyiśca taduktaṃ oṃ ||
prajñāntaraṃ prajñāviśeṣaḥ | dhyānamiti yāvat | tasya pṛthaktvavat anekaprakāratvavat | tadanusāreṇa dṛṣyiśca brahmadṛṣyirapi anekaprakārā na sarveṣāmekavidhā | kiñca taduktam | taddṛṣyeranekavidhatvaṃ "antarddṛṣyayo" iti śrutāvuktañcetyarthaḥ |
|| 33 || na sāmānyādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ na sāmānyādapyupalabdhemarṛtyuvanna hi lokāpattiḥ oṃ ||
mṛtyuvat mṛtyumātrānantaraṃ yathā na muktiḥ | kintu caramamṛtiviśeṣānantarameva | tadvadatrāpi bhagavadrūpāṇāṃ sāmye'pi upalabdheḥ | bhagavaddṛṣyisāmānyāt sādhāraṇarūpāparokṣajñānāt | na sarveṣāṃ muktiḥ | kintu svasvabimbadarśanādeva | na caivaṃ sāmānyadarśanasya
vaiphalyam | tasya maharādilokaprāptisādhanatvāt | na caitāvatā tasya mokṣahetutvaṃ | tasya maharādilokaprāptisādhanatvāt | na caitāvatā tasya mokṣahetutvaṃ prāpnoti | hi yataḥ | lokāpattiḥ lokaprāptimātraṃ na muktiḥ | tasmādityarthaḥ | hītyanena sāmānyadarśanādi (nārāyaṇatantre) tyādi pramāṇaprasiddhiśca sucitā |
|| 34 || pareṇādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ pareṇa ca śabdasya tādvidhyaṃ bhūyastvātvanubandhaḥ oṃ ||
turapi yadyapi | pareṇa ca paramātmanaiva | brahmadarśanam | tathāpi śabdasya "bhaktirevainamiti" (māṭhara) śruteḥ tādvidhyaṃ paramātmana iva bhakteḥ svātantrayeṇa brahmadarśanahetutvavācitvaṃ yujyate | kutaḥ darśanādau kāraṇatvāditi heturupaskartavyaḥ | yadvā pareṇa vācyena nimittena śabdasya bhaktiriti śruteḥ | tādvidhyaṃ yuktamityarthaḥ | tarhi kathaṃ bhaktiriti vākyena satsvapyanyasādhaneṣu anubandho bhaktiḥ viśiṣyocyata ityata uktam | bhūyastvāditi | tasyāḥ sādhaneṣu | bhūyastvāt prādhānyāt | tadabhiprāyeṇānubandho bhaktirviśiṣyocyata ityarthaḥ |
|| 35 || ekādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ eka ātmanaḥ śarīre bhāvāt oṃ ||
aṃśoṃ'śītipadayoḥ arthaḥ eka eva | aṃśāṃśinoraikyameva | kuta; ātmanaḥ aṃśinaḥ | indrādeḥ śarīre karmanirmitaśarīre | aṃśasyārjunādeḥ | bhāvāt tadabhimānitayā sattvādityarthaḥ |
oṃ vyatirekastadbhāvabhāvitvānnatūpalabdhivat oṃ ||
upalabdhivat jñānasukhādau parasparaṃ bheda iva | aṃśasyāṃśikṛtabhāvaśabditopāsanajanyaphalānubhavitvāt | anyathā tannasyādityarthaḥ |
|| 36 || aṃgāvabaddhādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ aṃgāvabaddhāstu na śākhāsu hi prativedam oṃ ||
tuśabdo viśeṣe | aṃgāvabaddhāḥ hareraṃgāśritāḥ | devā api tatparivāratvena devairupāsyā | tadupasaṃhāre tu viśeṣo'sti | kaḥ? devānāṃ prativedaṃ sarvavedagatāsu śākhāsu uktaṃ yat brahamādyaṃgadevatāvabaddhaguṇajātaṃ tatsarvaṃ rudrādiṣu nopasaṃhāryam | kintu yogyamevetyevaṃ rūpa ityarthaḥ | hītyanena samatvādveti (brahmatarka) smṛtiprasiddhiḥ sūcitā |
oṃ mantrādivadvā'virodhaḥ oṃ ||
mantrādivat yathā'gnyādyadhamadevatāvācimantrāvidyānāṃ brahmadyuttamadevatāvācacitvaṃ tathā'virodhaḥ adhamaguṇānāmuttameṣūpasaṃhāre'pi virodho nāstīti vā'bhyupagamyata ityarthaḥ |
|| 37 || nānāśabdādhikararam ||
oṃ nānā śabdādibhedāt oṃ ||
bhūmaguṇaḥ sarvairyathāyogyaṃ nānāprakāreṇopāsyaḥ | natvekaprakāreṇa | kutaḥ | śabdādibhedāt | śabdādipramāṇairadhikāribhedāt | bhūmaguṇasya nānāprakāreṇa pratīterityarthaḥ |
|| 39 || vikalpādhikaraṇam ||

|| 40 || kāmyādhikaraṇam ||

|| 41 || aṃgādhikaraṇam ||

|| 42 || navādhikaraṇam ||

aṃgadevatopāsanaṃ devānyaiḥ na kāryam | kintu devaireveti vā vyavasthitavikalpaḥ syāt | kutaḥ atatsahabhāvaśruteḥ tatsahaśruterabhāvāt | sarvakhāgataguṇopāsanena sahāṃgadevatopāsanasya śravaṇādityarthaḥ |

|| 1 || puruṣārthādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ puruṣārtho'taḥ śabdāditi bādarāyaṇaḥ oṃ ||

|| 4 || stutyadhikaraṇam ||
oṃ stutaye'numatirvā oṃ ||
jñāninaḥ sadasatpravṛttyorviśeṣo'styeva | kutaḥ yataḥ yatheṣyācāro jñānī mucyata eveti jñānistutaya eva "yena syātkena syāt" ityucyate | anumatirvā jñānināṃ yatheṣṭhācāre'bhyanujñāmātraṃ vā na tvatra yatheṣcāravidhirata ityarthaḥ |
oṃ kāmakāreṇacaike oṃ ||
eke śākhinaḥ | kāmakāreṇa ca yatheṣyācāreṇāpi jñānināṃ mokṣaṃ "kāmacārāḥ" iti (sāmaśākhā) śrutau paṭhanti | ato na jñāninaḥ sadasatpravṛttyormokṣahāniḥ | kintu phale viśeṣamātramastītyarthaḥ |
oṃ upamardañca oṃ ||
caśabdo'pyarthaḥ | yadā jñāninaḥ prārabdhakarmaṇo'pi kicidbhuktasya jñānenopamardaṃ īṣatphalahrāsam | "omityuccārya" iti (tura) śrutāveke śākhina; paṭhanti | tadā jñānottarakālīnāsatkarma na mokṣapratibandhakamiti kiṃ varṇanīyamityarthaḥ |
oṃ ūrdhvaretassu ca śabde hi oṃ ||
kāmacārāṇāmapi jñānināṃ muktyadhikāravat kāmacārāṇāṃ jijñāsūnāṃ na jñānādhikāraḥ | kutaḥ hi yasmāt | śabde ya imamiti (māṭhara) śrutāvūrdhvaretastvādiguṇavatsveva jñānopadeśo vihitaḥ | tasmādityarthaḥ |
oṃ parāmarśaṃ jaiminiracodanā cāpavadati hi oṃ ||
jaiminiḥ parāmarśaṃ "yena syāt" iti vākye prātarutthāyetyādiśrutyuktasadācārasyaiva grahaṇamabhyupetyātra sadācārasyaiva svecchayā niyamenācaraṇaṃ pratipādyam | na tu niṣiddhasya karaṇamiti manyate | kutaḥ hi yasmāt | acodanā aniṣiddhaṃ jñāninā kāryamiti vidhyabhāvaḥ apavadati ca pratyuta "brahmaṇo na hantavyaḥ" iti śrutiḥ vikarma niṣedhati ca iti tasmādityarthaḥ |
oṃ anuṣṭheyaṃ bādarāyaṇaḥ sāmyaśruteḥ oṃ ||
bādarāyaṇaḥ bhagavān bādarāyaṇastu | anuṣṭheyaṃ svecchayā śrutyādyuktānuṣṭhānayogyakiñcitkarmācaraṇābhyanujñānameva "yena syāt" iti  śruterarthaḥ | na tu savarkarmakaraṇāya yatheṣṭhācāravidhireveti manyate | kutaḥ sāmyaśruteḥ kenāpi prakāreṇa pravṛttāvapi "īdṛśa eva" iti jñāninaḥ sāmyokteḥ |
oṃ vidhirvā dhāraṇavatoṃ ||
yathā vedadhāraṇavidheḥ traivarṇikādhikārikatvameva na tu sarvādhikārikatvam | tathā vidhiḥ jñānināmeva "yena syāt" iti  svecchācāravidhiḥ na tvajñānināmityarthaḥ | vāśabdaḥ praguktapakṣadvayāt asya pakṣāntaratvadyotakaḥ |
oṃ stutimātramupādānāditi cennāpūrvatvat oṃ ||
"kena syāt" iti vākyaṃ pūvaroktastutireva na svecchācaraṇavidhiḥ | kuta; upādānāt jñānibhirapi
"sandhyāmupāsīta" ityādi sādhāraṇavidhisyīkārāditi cenna | kutaḥ apūrvatvāt aśabdavācyaparamātmādhīnatvādityarthaḥ | tathā ca jñāninaḥ
svecchācaraṇavidherapyabhāve sarvavidhyatidūratvaprasaṅgena paramātmādhīnatvaṃ na syāditi bhāvaḥ |
oṃ bhāvaśabdācca oṃ ||
bhāvaḥ icchābhāvaśabdaghaṭita "yathā vidhānam" iti (tura) śruteśca "kena syāt" ityayaṃ kāmacāravidhirityarthaḥ |
oṃ pāriplavārthā iti cenna viśeṣitatvāt oṃ ||
niyamena sarvadharmācaraṇavidhipakṣasyānuṣṭheyānāṃ madhye svecchayā katipayadharmācaraṇābhyanujñānapakṣasya svecchācaraṇavidhipakṣasya ca parasparaviruddhatvāt | "yena syāt" ityādayaḥ pāriplavātharāḥ avyavasthitārthakā iti cenna | kutaḥ viśeṣitatvāt "tredhāhi jñāninaḥ" iti (gaupavana) śrutau jñānināṃ vidhiniyatādipadaiḥ viśeṣitatvena teṣāmanekaprakāratvasyoktatvādityarthaḥ |
oṃ tathā caikavākyopabandhāt oṃ ||
uktapakṣatrayānusārivākyānāṃ adhikāribhedamāśrityāvirodhenaikavākyatāyāḥ yuktatvāt | tathā ca śrutīnāmekavākyatve siddhe "yena syāt" iti vākyasyāpi ekavākyopabandhāt adhikāribhedena vyavasthitārthakatvopapatteḥ na pāriplavārthatvamityarthaḥ | tathā ca jaiminimataṃ manuṣyajñānaviṣayam | vyāsamataṃ devatāviṣayam | "vidhirvā" itimataṃ vidhivettṛttiṣayamiti na virodha iti bhāvaḥ |
oṃ ata eva cāgnīndhanādyanapekṣa oṃ ||
jñānasya mokṣasādhanatve agnīndhanādyanapekṣā agnihodisatkarmasāpekṣitatvaṃ nāsti | kutaḥ ata eva kāmacārāṇāmapi mokṣasya pramāṇasiddhatvādityarthaḥ |
oṃ sarvāpekṣā ca yajñādiśruteraśvavat oṃ ||
aśvavat yathā gatisādhanāśvādayaḥ gatiniṣpatyarthamevāpekṣyante na tatphalaṃ grāmādiprāptyartham | tathā mokṣasādhanajñānotpattāveva | sarvāpekṣā sarvadharmāpekṣā | na tu jñānaphalamokṣe'pi | kutaḥ yajñādiśruteḥ "vividiṣanti yajñena" iti |
oṃ śamadamādyupetaḥ syāttathāpi tu tadvidhestadaṃgatayā teṣāmavaśyānuṣṭheyatvāt oṃ ||
yadyapi jñānenaiva mokṣo niyataḥ | tathāpi jñānī śamadamādyupetaḥ syāt śamadamādiyukto bhīvet | kutaḥ tadvidheḥ "ācāryāt" iti (māṭhara) śrutyā tasyā jñāninaḥ śamadamādeḥ vidhānāt | tathāpi na jñāninaḥ śamadamādivaiyarthyam | kutaḥ tadaṃgatayā jñānāṃgatayā jñānaphalopakaryamatasteṣāṃ śamadamādīnāmavaśyānuṣṭheyatvāt avaśyamanuṣṭhānārhatvāt | tuśabdaḥ śamadamādeḥ pūrṇaphalāthartvaṃ sūcayati |
oṃ sarvānnānumatiśca prāṇātyaye taddaśarnāt oṃ ||
caśabda evārthe | jñāninaḥ sarvānnānumatiḥ "yadi ha vā apyevaṃ vit" iti niṣiddhāniṣiddhasarvabhakṣaṇasyābhyanujñānaśrutiḥ | prāṇātyaye prāṇatyāgakālaviṣayaiva na tu sarvakālaviṣayā kuta taddarśanāt tasminnarthe " na vā ajīviṣyam" iti śruteḥ tadartharūpaliṃgadarśanāccetyarthaḥ |
oṃ abādhācca oṃ ||
jñānināṃ niṣiddhākaraṇe bādakābhāvācca niṣiddhaṃ na kāryamityarthaḥ |
oṃ api smaryate oṃ ||
tatkaraṇe'lpaphalatā ca "atīta" ityādinā (harivaṃśeṣu) kṛṣṇādibhiḥ smaatibhiḥ niṣiddhaṃ na kāryamiti smaryate ca | ato'pi na niṣiddhaṃ jñānibhiḥ kāryamityarthaḥ |
oṃ śabdaścāto'kāmacāre oṃ ||
co yataḥ | akāmacāre niṣiddhakarmabhāve | śabdaḥ "sa ya etadevaṃ vit" iti (kauṇḍinya) śrutirasti | ato'pi na niṣiddhaṃ jñāninā kāryamityarthaḥ |
oṃ vihitatvāccaśramakarmāpi oṃ ||
caḥ kāraṇasamuccaye | apiḥ varṇāśramadharmasamuccaye | jñāninā na kevalaṃ niṣiddhamakāryam | kintatvāśramadharmo'pi varṇāśramocitakamarāpi sampūrṇaphalārthaṃ kāryam | kutaḥ vihitatvāt "paśyannapi" iti (kauṣārava) śrutivihitatvādityarthaḥ |
oṃ sahakāritvena ca oṃ ||
jñāninā sampūrṇaphalasiddhayai satkarma kāryameva | kutaḥ "yathā rājñāḥ" iti (kamaṭha) śrutau jñokṣaphalātiśayakarmaṇo
jñānasahakāritvenoktatvāccetyarthaḥ |
|| 5 || ubhayaliṃgādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ sarvathā'pi tu ta evobhayaliṃgāt oṃ ||

oṃ api smaryate oṃ ||

oṃ viśeṣānugrahaṃ oṃ ||
caśabdo yuktisamuccaye | yataḥ "ṛṇve vī" iti śruteḥ deveṣu parameśvarasya viśeṣānugrahañca darśayati pratipādayati | ato devānāmeva samyak jñānatatphale nānyeṣāmityarthaḥ |
oṃ atastvitarañjyaye liṃgācca oṃ ||
tuśabdo'vadhāraṇe | ato devabhāgādasurabhāgasya kadāpyūnatvābhāvāt | itarat asuravṛndameva | ato devabhāgājjyāyaḥ bahulam | kutaḥ liṃgāt "tasmānna janatāmiyāt" iti janasaṅghapraveśaniṣedhoktyanyathā'nupapattirūpaliṅgāt | caśabdasūcita "tataḥ kanīyasā" iti śruteścetyarthaḥ |
oṃ tadbhūtasya tu tadbhāvo jaiminerapi niyamātadrūpābhāvebhyaḥ oṃ ||
tadbhūtasya tu devabhūtasyaiva | tadbhāvaḥ devatābhāvaḥ evamasurasyaivāsurabhāvaḥ jaiminerācāryasyāpi siddha eva | kutaḥ niyamātadrūpābhāvebhyaḥ nāsurā iti caśabdasūcitaśruteḥ "yo yadrūpaḥ" iti atadrūpaśabdoktānyasyānyarūpatvābhāvaśruteḥ | abhāvaḥ abhūtiḥ | devāsurāṇām | "te bhūtiḥ" iti iti bhāvaśabditaśruterityarthaḥ |
|| 6 || ādhikārikādhikaraṇam ||
oṃ na cādhikārikamapi patanānumānāttadayogāt oṃ ||
