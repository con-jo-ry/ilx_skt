|| śrīḥ ||
śrīmadānandatīrthabhagavatpabādapraṇītasarvamūlagranthāḥ
jayantīkalpaḥ|| śrīmaddhanumadbhimamādhvāntargatarāmakṛṣṇavedavyāsātmakaśrīlakṣmīhayagrīvāya namaḥ ||
rohiṇyāmardharātre tu yadā kālāṣṭamī bhavet |
jayantī nāma sā proktā sarvāpāpapraṇāśanī || 1 ||
yasyāṃ jāto hariḥ sākṣānniśīte bhagavānajaḥ |
tasmāttaddinamatyantaṃ puṇyaṃ pāpaharaṃ smṛtam || 2 ||
tasmāt sarvairupoṣyā sā jayantī nāma vai sadā |
dvijātibhirviśeṣeṇa tadbhaktaiśca viśeṣataḥ || 3 ||
yo bhuṅkte taddine mohātpūyaśoṇitamatti saḥ |
tasmādupavaset puṇyaṃ taddinaṃ śraddhyā'nvitaḥ || 4 ||
kṛtvā śaucaṃ yathānyāyaṃ snānaṃ kuryādatandritaḥ |
prabhātakāle medhāvī yogāyeti yathāvidhi || 5 ||
nityāhnikaṃ prakurvīta gavantamanusmaran |
yogāya yogapataye yogeśvarāya yogasambhavāya śrīgovindāya namo namaḥ || 6 ||
madhyāhnakāle ca punaḥ sāyaṃkāle tvatandritaḥ |
snāyīta pūrvamantreṇa vāsudevamanusmaran || 7 ||
tataḥ pūjāṃ prakurvīta vidhivatsusamāhitaḥ |
yajñāyeti ca mantreṇa śraddhābhaktiyutaḥ pumān || 8 ||
kṛṣṇaṃ ca balabhadraṃ ca vasudevaṃ ca devakīm |
nandagopaṃ yaśodāṃ ca subhadrāṃ tatra pūjayet |
ardhyaṃ datvā sapabhyarcyābhyudite śaśimaṇḍale || 9 ||
jātaḥ kaṃsavadhārthāya bhūbharottāraṇāya ca |
kauravāṇāṃ vināśāya daityānāṃ nidhanāya ca || 10 ||
pāṇḍavānāṃ hitārthāya dharmasaṃsthāpanāya ca |
gṛhaṇārdhyaṃ mayā dattaṃ devakyā sahito hare || 11 ||
śrīrodārṇavasambhūta atrinetrasamudbhava |
gṛhāṇārghyaṃ mayā dattaṃ rohiṇamayā sahitaḥ śaśin || 12 ||
datvā'rghyaṃ maninā'neni hyupasthāya vidhuṃ budhaḥ |
śaśine candradevāya somadevāya cendave || 13 ||
mṛgiṇe śaśibimbāya lokadīpāya dīpine |
rohiṇīsaktacittāya tārakāpataye namaḥ || 14 ||
upasaṃhṛtya tatsarvaṃ brahmacārī jitendriyaḥ |
viśvāyeti ca mantreṇa tataḥ svāpaṃ samācaret || 15 ||
tato nityāhnikaṃ kṛtvā śaktito dīyatāṃ dhanam |
sarvāyeti ca mantreṇa tataḥ pāraṇamācaret |
dharmāyeti tataḥ svastho mucyate sarvakilbiṣaiḥ || 16 ||
|| iti śrīmadānandatīrthabhagavatpādācāryaviracito jayantīkalpaḥ samāptaḥ ||
|| iti jayantīkalpaḥ ||
|| śrīkṛṣṇārpaṇamastu ||
