atha sulabhameva puṇyānubhāvādbhavāntare'pi śobhanatvamiti paroktamāśaṅkyāha -- varagātri! yadatītam, atikrāntaṃ yauvanādi tatte tava bhūyo na; kintu jarājīrṇatvameva bhaviṣyatītyarthaḥ| jātaśobhane -- varagātrītisambodhanayoḥ samānārthayorapyādarānurāgātirekānna paunaruktyaṃ doṣaḥ| taduktam -- 
anuvādādaravīpsābhṛśārthaviniyogahetvasūyāsu|
īṣatsambhramavismayagaṇanāsmaraṇeṣvapunaruktam||
nanu svecchayā'vicchinnaṃ khādane pāne dustarā paralokakaṣṭaparamparā, sulabhaṃ bhavati sukṛtasañcaye bhavāntare'pi yauvanādikamiti parāśaṅkāṃ dūṣayannāha -- na hi bhīru gataṃ nivartate| he bhīru! paroktamātreṇa narakādiprāpyaduḥkhabhayākule! gatam, iha bhavātikrāntaṃ sukhayauvanādi na nivarttate paraloke nāḍhaukate paralokasukhākāṃkṣayā tapaścaraṇādikaṣṭakriyābhirihatyasukhopekṣā vyarthetyarthaḥ|
atha janyajanakasambandhasadbhāvādamunā kāyena paraloke'pi sahetukaṃ sukhaduḥkhādikaṃ veditavyamavaśyameveti ced? āha -- samudayamātramidaṃ kalevaram, idaṃ kalevaraṃ śarīraṃ samudayamātraṃ samudayo melaḥ vakṣyamāṇacaturbhūtānāṃ saṃyogastanmātraṃ mātraśabdo'vadhāraṇe bhūtacatuṣṭayasambandha eva kāyo, na ca pūrvabhavādiśubhāśabhakarmavipākavedyamukhaduḥkhādisavyapekṣa ityarthaḥ, saṃyogāśca taruśikharāvalīlīnaśakunigaṇavat kṣaṇato vinaśvarāstasmāt paralokānapekṣayā yathecchaṃ piba khāda ceti vṛttārthaḥ || 82 ||
kiṃ ca pṛthvī jalaṃ tejo vāyurbhūtacatuṣṭayam|
caitanyabhūmireteṣāṃ mānaṃ tvakṣajameva hi || 83 ||
caitanyamāha -- kiñceti|
kiṃ cetyupadarśane, pṛthvī bhūmiḥ, jalamāpaḥ, tejo vahniḥ vāyuḥ pavana; iti bhūtacatuṣṭayaṃ teṣāṃ cārvākāṇāṃ caitanyabhūmiḥ caitanyotpattikāraṇaṃ; catvāryapi bhūtāni saṃbhūya sapiṇḍaṃ caitanyaṃ janayantītyarthaḥ| tu punaḥ, mānaṃ pramāṇaṃ hi niścitam akṣajameva pratyakṣamevaikaṃ pramāṇamityarthaḥ || 83 ||
pṛthvyādibhūtasaṃhatyāṃ tathā dehādisambhavaḥ|
madaśaktiḥ surā'ṅgebhyo yadvattadvatsthitā''tmatā || 84 ||
nanu bhūtacatuṣṭayasaṃyogajadehacaitanyotpattiḥ kathaṃ pratīyatām? ityāśaṅkyāha -- pṛthvyādibhūtasaṃhatyāmiti|
pṛthivyādīni pṛthivyaptejovāyurūpāṇi yāni bhūtāni teṣāṃ saṃhatyāṃ mele saṃyoge sati; tathetyupadarśane dehādisambhavaḥ, ādiśabdāditare bhūdharādipadārthā api catuṣṭayasaṃyogajātā eva jñeyāḥ| dṛṣṭānatmāha -- yadvavaditi| yena prakāreṇa surāṅgebhyo guḍadhātakyādibhyo madyāṅge bhyo madaśaktirunmādakatvaṃ bhavati, tadvattathā bhūtacatuṣṭayasambandhāt śarīra ātmatā sthitā sacetanatvaṃ bhavati, jātamityarthaḥ || 84 ||
tasmād dṛṣṭaparityāgādadṛṣṭe ca pravartanam|
lokasya tadvimūḍhatvaṃ cārvākāḥ pratipedire || 85 ||
iti sthite tadupadeśapūrvakamupasaṃhāramāha -- tasmāditi|
tasmāditi pūrvoktānusmāraṇe, pūrvaṃ tasmāttataḥ kāraṇād dṛṣṭaparityāgād dṛṣṭaṃ peyāpeyakhādyākhādyagamyāgamyānurūpaṃ pratyakṣānubhāvyaṃ yatsukhaṃ; tasya parityāgādadṛṣṭe tapaścaraṇādikaṣṭakriyāsādhyaparalokasukhādau pravartanaṃ pravṛttiḥ| caḥ samuccaye. yattadornaiyatyādarddhe yatsambandho jñeyaḥ, tallokasya vimūḍhatvamajñānatvaṃ cārvākāḥ laukāyatikāḥ pratipedire pratipannāḥ, mūḍhalokā hi vipratārakavacanopanyāsabhāsitajñānāḥ sāṃsārikaṃ sukhaṃ parityajya vyarthaṃ svarga mokṣapipāsayā tapojapadhyānahomādibhirihatyaṃ sukhaṃ parityajya vyarthaṃ svargaṃ mokṣapipāsayā tapojapadhyānahomādibhirihatyaṃ sukhaṃ hastagatamupekṣanta iti || 85 ||
sādhyāvṛttinivṛttibhyāṃ yā prītirjāyate jane|
nirarthā sā matā teṣāṃ sā cākāśāt parā na hi || 86 ||
sādhyasya manīṣitasya kasyacidvastuna āvṛttiḥ prāptiḥ, kasyacidvastuno'nabhīṣyasya nivṛttirabhāvaḥ, tābhyāṃ jane loke yā prītirjāyata utpadyate sā teṣāṃ cāryākāṇaṃ nirarthikā nirabhiprāyā śūnyā matā'bhīṣṭā bhavārjitapuṇyapāpasādhyaṃ sukhaduḥkhādikaṃ sarvathā na vidyata ityarthaḥ, sā ca prītirākāśād gaganāt parā na, hi yathā''kāśaṃśūnyaṃ tathaiṣā'pi prītirabhāvarūpaivetyarthaḥ || 86 ||
lokāyatamate'pyevaṃ saṃkṣepo'yaṃ niveditaḥ|
abhidheyatātparyārthaḥ paryālocyaḥ subuddhibhiḥ || 87 ||
iti śrīharibhadrasūrikṛtaḥ ṣaḍ‌darśanasamuccayaḥ
samāptāḥ
upasaṃhāramāha -- lokāyatamate'pyevamiti| evamamunā prakāreṇa lokāyatamate'pyayaṃ saṃkṣepo niveditaḥ, apiḥ samuccaye, na kevalaṃ paramate, saṃkṣepa ukto lokāyatamate'pi|
atha sarvadarśanasammatasaṅgrahe parasparakalpitānalpavikalpajalparūpe nirūpite kiṅkarttavyamūḍhaānāṃ prāṇināṃ karttavyopadeśamāha -- abhidheyeti| subuddhibhiḥ paṇḍitairabhidheyatātparyārthaḥ paryālocyaḥ| abhidheyaṃ kathanīyaṃ muktyaṅgatayā pratipādyaṃ yaddarśanasvarūpaṃ tasya tātparyārthaḥ sārārtho vicāraṇīyaḥ| subuddhibhirati| śuddhā pakṣapātarahitā buddhiryeṣāmiti, na tu kadāgrahagrahilaiḥ,
yaduktam -- 
āgrahī bata ninīṣati yuktiṃ tatra yatra matirasya niviṣṭā|
pakṣapātarahitasya hi tasya yatra tatra matireti niveśam || iti|
darśanānāṃ paryantaikasārūpye'pi pṛthak pṛthagupadeṣṭavyādvimatisambhave vimūḍhasya prāṇinaḥ sarvaspṛkatayā durlabhaṃ svargāpavargasādhakatvam, ato vimarśanīyastāttviko'rthaḥ, yathā ca vicāritaṃ cirantanaiḥ|
śrotavyaḥ saugato dharmaḥ karttavyaḥ punarārhataḥ|
vaidiko vyavahartavyo dhyātavyaḥ paramaḥ śivaḥ||
ityādi vimṛśya śreyaskaraṃ rahasyabhyupagantavyaṃ kuśalamatibhiriti paryantaślokārthaḥ || 87 ||
saptāśītiḥ ślokasūtraṃ, ṭīkāmānaṃ viniścitam|
sahasramekaṃ dviśatī dvāpañcāśadanuṣṭubhām||
