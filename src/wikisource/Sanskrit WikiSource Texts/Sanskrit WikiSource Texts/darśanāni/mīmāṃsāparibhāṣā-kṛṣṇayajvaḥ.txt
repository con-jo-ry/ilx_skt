mīmāṃsā paribhāṣā
maṅgalam
śrīmate vedapuruṣāya namaḥ 
sūryanārāyaṇaṃ vande devīṃ tripurasundarīm | 
gurūnadhigatārthāṃśca nirantaramahaṃ bhaje || 1 || 
bālānāṃ śāstrasiddhārthaleśabodhāya dhīmatā | 
mīmāṃsāparibhāṣeyaṃ kriyate kṛṣṇayajvanā || 2 ||
mīmāṃsāviṣayaḥ
iha khalu maharṣiṇā jaimininā dvodaśalakṣaṇyāṃ pūrvamīmāṃsāyāṃ dharmādharmāvevānuṣṭhānopayogitayā vicāritau | tatra vedabodhiteṣṭasādhanatāko dharmaḥ yathā yāgādiḥ | vedabodhitāniṣṭasādhanatāko dharmaḥ yathā kalejabhakṣaṇādiḥ | tayośca vedaḥ, smṛtiḥ, ācāraśca pramāṇam | tatra vedaḥ khatantraṃ pramāṇam ; itarau tu vedamūlakatayā |
vedadvaividhyam
tatra vedo dvividhaḥ -- matrarūpo brāhmaṇarūpaceti | tatra prayogakālīnārthasmaraṇahetutayā maitrāṇāmupayoga iti vakṣyate | prayogonuṣṭhānaṃ tatkālīnetyarthaḥ | vidhāyakaṃ vākyaṃ brāhmaṇam | taccheṣo'rthavādaḥ | tasya vidheyaprāśastyapratītijananadvārā vidhivākyaikavākyatayā prāmāṇyamiti vakṣyate |
brāhmaṇavākyabhedāḥ
guṇabrāhmaṇavākyaṃ cānekavidham -- karmotpattivākyaṃ, vākyaṃ, phalavākyaṃ, phalāya guṇavākyaṃ, saguṇakarmotpattivākyamityādibhedāt |
karmotpattivākyam
tatra yena vākyena ' idaṃ karma kartavyam' iti bodhyate tat karmotpativākyam; yathā 'agnihotraṃ juhoti' iti | atrāgnihotrahomaḥ kartavyatayā vidhīyata iti karmotpattivākyamidam |
guṇavākyam
vihite karmaṇi tadaṅgatayā dravyadevatādividhāyakaṃ vākyaṃ guṇavākyam; yathā 'dadhnā juhuyāt' iti | atra homamuddiśyā'ṅgatayā dadhi vidhīyata iti guṇavākyamidam | karmāṅgatayā vihitatvameva dadhyāderguṇatvam | atra homasyoddeśyatvaṃ nāma mānāntara prāptatve sati vidheyānvayitayā nirdeśyatvam | tasyaiva mānāntaraprāptasya punaḥ kathyamānarūpatvamanuvādyatvam, dadhyādiguṇānvayitayā prādhānyaṃ ca | dadhyādermānāntarāprāptatvādatraiva vidheyatvam, homasādhanatvācca homāpekṣayā guṇatvam, puruṣeṇānuṣṭhīyamānatvādupādeyatvaṃ ceti | atra mānāntaraprāptatvaṃ mānāntarajñātatvam, aprāptatvaṃ cājñātatvamiti bodhyam |
phalavākyam
utpannasya karmaṇaḥ phalākāṅkṣāyāṃ phalasaṃbandhabodhako vidhiḥ phalavidhiḥ; yathā 'agnihotraṃ juhuyāt khargakāmaḥ' iti | atra 'yaḥ kharga kāmayate sa tatsādhanatvenāgnihotranāmakaṃ homaṃ bhāvayet -- kuryāt' iti agnihotravākyotpannasya karmaṇaḥ phalasaṃbandho bodhyata iti phalavākyamidam |
phalāya guṇavākyam
prāptaṃ karmāśritya phalāya guṇavidhau phalāya guṇavākyam -- yathā 'danendriyakāmasya juhuyāt' iti | agnihotravākyotpanna homamāśrityendriyaphalāya dadhirūpo guṇo vidhīyate -- ' homāśritena daghnā indriyarūpaṃ phalaṃ bhāvayet' iti | homāśritena homakārakībhūtenetyarthaḥ | ayameva guṇaphalavidhiḥ, guṇakāmavidhiriti cocyate |
saguṇakarmotpattivākyam
dravyadevatādiguṇaviśiṣṭakarmavidhāyakaṃ vākyaṃ saguṇakarmotpattivākyam; yathā 'somena yajeta' iti | atra somalatāviśiṣṭo yāgo vidhīyate | viśiṣṭavidhāvapi viśeṣaṇasyārthādvidhiḥ |
saphalakarmotpattivākyam
kvacitkarmotpattivākyameva phalasaṃbandhabodhakamapi bhavati; yathā 'udbhidā yajeta paśukāmaḥ' iti | atrodbhinnāmako yāgo vākyāntaravihita eva paśukāmasya paśuphalāya vidhīyata ityekamevedaṃ vākyaṃ phalasādhanayāgavidhāyakam |
prayogavidhiḥ
pradhānavidhirevāṅgavidhibhirekavākyatayā mahāvākyatāmāpannaḥ san sarvāṅgaviśiṣṭapradhānaprayogavidhāyakatvāt prayogavidhirityucyate; yathā 'agnihotraṃ juhuyāt khargakāma' iti | atra 'agnihotra homena svarga bhāvayet' ityarthaḥ pratīyate | bhāvayet utpādayediti yāvat | atra 'kathamanena homena svargaṃ kuryāt' iti kathaṃbhāvākāṅkṣā jāyate; yathā 'kuṭhāreṇa dvaidhībhāvaṃ kuryāt' ityukte bhavatyākāṅkṣā 'kathamanena dvaidhībhāvaṃ kuryāt' iti | tatrodyamananipātanādisahāyeneti gamyate, tadvadatrāpi 'agniṃ praṇayati', 'agniṣu samidha ādadhāti' ityādyaṅgavidhivihitapraṇayanasamidādhānāyatanaśodhanādikāṅgakalāpajanitopakārasahitenā'gnihotrahomena svargaṃ kuryāditi prakaraṇakalpitena homavākyena svargāya sāṅgāgnihotraprayogo vidhīyata ityetādṛśaḥ prayogavidhiḥ | aṅgajātamevetthaṃbhāva iti, itikartavyatā iti cocyate | atrāgnihotrahomaḥ pradhānaṃ, praṇayanādikaṃ sarvamaṅgam |
phalāpūrvakalpanā
nanvidamanupapannam ; āśutaravināśināṃ karmaṇāṃ kālāntarabhāvikhargādiphalasādhanatvānupapatteriti cet, maivam ; vihitaniṣiddhakarmaṇāṃ tattadvākyaistattatphalasādhanatve'vagate āśutaravināśināṃ karmaṇāṃ kālāntarabhāviphalasādhanatvopapattyarthamantarā puṇyapāparūpamapūrvaṃ kalpyate | tataśca yāgāderapūrvadvārā svargasādhanatvaṃ, na sākṣāt | tadeva phalāpūrvam | tatkaraṇatvaṃ ca prācyodī cyāṅga viśiṣṭasya pradhānasya bhavati, na pradhānamātrasya | pradhānamātrādeva phalāpūrvajanane phalasyāpi tate eva siddheraṅgānāmānarthakyāpatteḥ |
utpattyapūrvakalpanā
nanu sarvāṅga viśiṣṭasya pradhānasya pūrvajanakatvamayuktam, taccāśutaravināśinaḥ pradhānasyāṅgasāhityābhāvānnaṃ saṃbhavatīti cet; na; pradhānakarmaṇaḥ svarūpeṇāṅgasāhityābhāve'pi utpattyapūrvadvārā sāhityasaṃbhavāt | pradhānasya sarvāṅgasāhitya siddhyarthaṃ pradhānakarmaparamāpūrvayormadhye pradhānamātrajanyamutpattināmakaṃ kiṃcidapūrvamastītyaṅgīkārāt | evamaṅgānāmapi parasparasahitānāmeva pradhānopakārakatvātteṣāṃ svarūpeṇa sāhityābhāvāttattadutpattyapūrvadvārā sāhityaṃ bodhyam | aṅgānāṃ pradhānopakārakatvaṃ nāma pradhānasya phalāpūrvajananasāmarthyonmukhīkaraṇameva | darśapūrṇamāsayostu viśeṣaḥ 'yadāgneyo'ṣṭākapālomāvāsyāyāṃ ca paurṇamāsyāṃ cācyuto bhavati' ityāgneyayāgo vihitaḥ, 'tābhyāmetamabhīṣomīyamekādaśakapālaṃ pūrṇamāse prāyacchat' ityagnīṣomīyayāgo vihitaḥ, 'upāṃśuyājamantarā yajati ' ityupāṃśuyāgaḥ ‘tāvabrūtāmanīṣomāvājyasyaiva nāvupāṃśu paurṇamāsyāṃ yajan‍ iti vākyātpaurṇamāsyāṃ vihitaḥ | etāni paurṇamāsyāṃ pradhānāni | eṣāmāyāmnīṣomīyopāṃśuyogānāṃ tattadvākyāvagatapaurṇamāsīkāla saṃbandha nimittīkṛtya 'ya evaṃ vidvānpaurṇamāsīṃ yajate' iti vidvadvākye paurṇamāsīpadenaikavacanāntena samudāyarūpeṇānuvādaḥ | tena vede yatra yatra paurṇamāsīśabdastatratatrāmeyādisamudāyopasthitiḥ |
yajipadāśravaṇe'pi yāgavidhānam
nanu 'yadāgneyam' iti vākye yāgavācakapadasyāśravaṇātkathaṃ yāgavidhāyakatvamiti cet, maivam; agnirdevatā asya puroḍāśasyetyarthe vihitadevatātaddhitānta 'āgneya' śabdaḥ | tasya puroḍāśapadasāmānādhikaraṇyādravyadevatāsaṃbandho'vagataḥ | sa yāgamantarā na saṃbhavati, dravyadevatāsaṃbandhasya yāgādanyatra kriyāyāmasaṃbhavāt yāgakriyāyāmeva saṃbandho vācyaḥ | devatoddeśena dravyatyāgasya yāgarūpatvāṅgīkārāt | ataḥ śrutadravyadevatāsaṃbandhānumito yāgo yajeteti kalpitapadena vidhīyate -- agnidaivatyapuroḍāśadravyakāmāvāsyādikāla kartavyayāgeneṣṭaṃ bhāvayediti, evaṃ yatra yatra dravyadevatāsaṃbandhamātraṃ śrūyate 'sauryaṃ caruṃ nirvapet' ityādau, tatra sūryadevatāca rudravyasaṃbandhānumito yāgo vidhīyata iti na kaściddoṣaḥ |
liṅpratyayādyabhāve'pi vidhāyakatvam
nanūpāṃśuyāgavākye yajeḥ śravaṇe'pi vidhipratyayaliṅāderabhāvātkathaṃ vidhāyakatvamiti cet, maivam ; 'yajati' ityasya 'yajeta' iti vipariṇāmena vidhāyakatvasaṃbhavāt | evaṃ 'śrīhīn prokṣati', 'samidho yajati' ityādāvapi vipariṇāmo bodhyaḥ | kecittu -- 'yajati' ityasya pañcamalakāratvāṅgīkārādvidhāyakatvasaṃbhava ityāhuḥ | tathā 'aindraṃ dadhyamāvāsyāyāṃ ' 'aindraṃ payo'māvāsyāyām' iti vākyavihitau, sāṃnāyyayāgau 'yadāgneyam' iti vākya vihitāgneyaścāmāvāsthāyāṃ pradhānāni | eteṣāṃ trayāṇāṃ 'ya evaṃ vidvānamāvāsyāṃ yajate' iti vākye amācāsyāmiti nāmnā dvitīyaikavacanāntena samudāyarūpeṇānuvādaḥ | tena vede yatra 'darśa' śabda ḥ 'amāvāsyā ' śabdo vā śrutastatra yāgasamudāyopasthitiḥ | trikasya trikasyāmāvāsyā paurṇamāsīśabdābhyāṃ samudāyarūpeṇa vidvadvākye'nuvādasya prayojanaṃ tu ' darśapūrṇamāsābhyāṃ svargakāmo yajeta ' ityādau dvivacanopapattiḥ; anyathā ṣaṇṇāmāmeyādiyāgānāṃ bahutvāt 'darśapūrṇamāsaiḥ' iti bahuvacanaṃ syāt | mānāntareṇa prāptārthasya punaḥ śravaṇamanuvādaḥ | tathā phalavadāmeyādisaṃnidhāvānnātāni prayājājyabhāgānuyājādīni ṣaṇṇāṃ yāgānāmaṅgabhūtāni |
trikāpūrvādyutpattiḥ
evaṃ sthite ' darśapūrṇamāsābhyāṃ svargakāmo yajeta' ityasyāyamarthaḥ darśapūrṇamāsābhyāṃ samudāyābhyāṃ parasparasahitābhyāṃ svargāpūrvaṃ kuryāditi | tatra kathaṃ kāladvayavartinoḥ samudāyayoḥ parasparasāhityamityākāṅkṣāyāṃ svarūpeṇa sāhityābhāve'pi trikātrikā dekaikamapūrvaṃ jāyate, tadvārā dvayoḥ samudāyayoḥ sāhityamucyate | ekaikasya trikasyāpi trikā pūrvajanakatvaṃ kathamityākāṅkṣāyāṃ prayājānuyājādipūrvottarāṅgakalāpaviśiṣṭasyaikaikasya trikasya samudāyāpūrvajanakatvamucyate | ekaikatrikasya sarvāṅgasāhityaṃ kharūpeṇa na saṃbhavatīti tribhiryāgaistrīvyutpattya pūrvāṇi jāyante, tadvārā sarvāṅgasāhityam | aṅgānāmapi prayājādīnāṃ svarūpeṇa pradhānasāhityābhāvāttattadutpattya pūrvadvārā sāhityaṃ vācyam | tathā ca pradhānajanyotpatyapūrvāṇāṃ prayājādijanyotpattyapūrvaiḥ sāhityaṃ yadasti tadeva pradhānānāmaṅgaviśiṣṭatvarūpaṃ sāṅgatvam | evaṃ ca paurṇamāsyāmā meyādijanyaistribhirapūrvaiḥ prayājādijanyotpattyapūrvasacivaiḥ samudāyāpūrvamekaṃ janyate, tathā darśe'pi āgneyaindradvayajanyastribhirapūrvairaṅgotpattyapūrvasacivairekaṃ trikāpūrvaṃ janyate, tābhyāṃ trikāpūrvābhyāṃ āgneyādyutpattyapūrvatritayajanyābhyāṃ phalajanakībhūtaṃ mahāpūrvaṃ phalāpūrvanāmakaṃ janyate, tataśca phalamiti | tathā ca sarvāṅgopakṛtābhyāṃ darśapūrṇamāsābhyāmapūrvadvārā svargaṃ kuryāditi phalāpūrvaniṣpattaye sāṅgapradhāna kartavyatābodhako vidhiḥ prayogavidhiriti siddham |
vidhitraividhyam
punarapi vidhistrividhaḥ -- apūrvavidhiḥ, niyamavidhiḥ, parisaṃkhyāvidhiśveti |
apūrvavidhiḥ
tatra yo vidhiratyantāprāptamarthaṃ prāpayati saḥ apūrvavidhiḥ yathā darśapūrṇamāsaprakaraṇe 'śrīhīn prokṣati' iti | etadvidhyabhāve darśapūrṇamāsīyavrīhiṣu prokṣaṇaṃ kathamapi na prāpnoti | etadvidhisattve tu tatsaṃbandhivrīhiṣu prokṣaṇaṃ prāpnotyevetyatyantāprāptaprokṣaṇaprāpakatvādayamapūrvavidhiḥ |
niyamavidhiḥ
yaśca pakṣe prāptamarthaṃ niyamayati sa niyamavidhiḥ yathā tatraiva ' trīhī navahanti' iti | etadvidhyabhāve darśapaurṇamāsikeṣu vrīhiṣūtpattivākyābagatapuroḍāśopayogitaṇḍulaniṣpattyanukūlavaituṣyakāryāyā'vahananavat kadācinnakhavidalanamapi prāpnuyāditi, tasminpakṣe'vahananasya prāpterabhāvāt kāryānyathopapatteravahananasya pākṣikī prāptiḥ syāt | sati tvasminvidhau avahananenaiva vaituṣyaṃ kāryamiti niyame sati vidalanaṃ sarvātmanā nivartata iti niyamavidhirayam | naca vaituṣyasya nakhavidalanenāpi saṃbhavādavahanananiyamo vyarthaḥ, prayojanābhāvāditi vācyam | avaghātenaiva vaitupyakaraṇe kiṃcidadṛṣṭaṃ janyata iti niyamādṛṣṭāṅgīkārāt, niyamena dṛṣṭakāryālābhe'pyadṛṣṭasyotpatteḥ | taccāpūrvaṃ yāgotpattyapūrvadvārā phalāpūrve upayujyate; tena niyamāpūrvībhāve phalāpūrvameva notpadyata iti kalpanānniyamāpūrvasya na vaiyarthyam | evaṃ somayādiniyame'pi bodhyam |
parisaṃkhyāvidhiḥ
dvayoḥ samuccitya prāptāvitaranivṛttiphalako vidhiḥ parisaṃkhyāvidhiḥ -- yathā cayanaprakaraṇe 'imāmagṛbhṇaiśanāmṛtasyetyaśvābhidhānīmādatte' ityaśvaraśanāgrahaṇāṅgatvena mantravidhiḥ | etadvidhyabhāve hi raśanāgrahaṇaprakāśako mantro raśanā''dānaprakāśanasāmarthyarūpālliṅgādaśvaraśanā’’dāna iva gardabharaśanā''dāne'pi prāpnuyāt | satyasminvidhau anena mantreṇāśvaraśanāmevādadīta natu gardabharaśanām ; sā tu tūṣṇīmeva grāhyeti gardabharaśanāyāṃ mantranivṛttirbhavatīti dvayoḥ samuccitya prāpta vitaranivṛttiphalakatvādayaṃ parisaṃkhyāvidhiḥ | evaṃ 'pañca pañcanakhā bhakṣyāḥ' ityādāvapi bodhyam |
śrutiliṅgādiṣaṭpramāṇāni
pūrvaṃ darśapūrṇamāsāṅgatvaṃ prayājādīnāmuktam | tatrāṅgatvabodhakapramāṇāni -- śrutiliṅgavākyaprakaraṇasthānasamākhyābhedena ṣaṭ | śrutyudāharaṇam tatra 'dadhnā juhuyāt' ityatrā'gnihotravākyaprāptaṃ homaṃ juhuyādityanenoddiśya tatkaraṇatvena tṛtīyāśrutyā dadhi vidhīyata iti śrutyā dano'ṅgatvam | liṅodāharaṇam liṅgaṃ nāma sāmarthyam| tacca dvividham -- arthagataṃ śabdagataṃ ceti | ādyaṃ yathā 'suveṇāvadyati' ityavadānasāmānyaśeṣatvāvagame'pisru vasya sāmarthyarūpālliṅgādājyasāṃnāyyādidravadravyasyāvadānaviśeṣāṅgatvam ; sruveṇa puroḍāśādyavadānasya kartumaśakyatvāt | śabdagataṃ tu liṅga arthaprakāśanasāmarthyam | yathā 'agnaye tvā juṣṭaṃ nirvapāmi' iti mantrasya nirvāpaprakāśanasāmarthyarūpālliṅgānnirvāpāṅgatvam | yasya mantrasya yatprakāśana sāmarthya tasya tadaṅgatvam | yathā 'berhirdevasadanaṃ dāmi' iti mantrasya barhirlavanaprakāśanasāmarthyamiti śabdasāmarthya rūpālliṅgāddarbhalavanāṅgetvam ||
vākyodāharaṇam
padāntarasamabhivyāhāro vākyam -- yathā 'īṃṣe tveti chinatti iti | atra chedanāṅgatvena 'iṣe tvā' iti mantro vākyena vidhīyate | yadvā 'agnaye tvā juṣṭam' ityatraiva agnaye tvā juṣṭamityādīnāṃ padānāṃ 'nirvanāmi' ityanenaikavākyatāpannatvānnirvāpāṅgatvam |
prakaraṇodāharaṇam
prakaraṇaṃ nāma parasparākāṅkṣā -- yathā ' darśapūrṇamāsābhyāṃ svargakāmo yajeta' iti | atra ' darśapūrṇamāsābhyāṃ svargāpūrvaṃ kuryāt ityukte bhavatyākāṅkṣā -- kathamābhyāṃ svargāpūrvaṃ kartavyamiti | tathā phalavadāgneyādisaṃnidhau 'samidho yajati', 'tanūnapātaṃ yajati', 'ājyabhāgau yajati' ityādibhiḥ prayājādayaḥ phalarahitāḥ śrutāḥ, teṣāṃ svavākyeṣu phalāśravaṇādbhavati prayojanākāṅkṣā -- kimeteṣāṃ prayojanamiti | tataśca prayājādīnāṃ prayojanākāṅkṣāyāṃ darśapūrṇamāsayoḥ kathaṃbhāvākāṅkṣāyāṃ parasparākāṅgālakṣaṇena prakaraṇena prayājādīnāṃ sarveṣāṃ darśapaurṇamāsāṅgatvaṃ niścīyate |
sthānodāharaṇam
sthānaṃ nāma saṃnidhiḥ; yathā sāṃnāyyapātra saṃnidhau ' śundhadhvam' iti mantrasya pāṭhāt saṃnidhānātsāṃnāyya pātraprokṣaṇāṅgatvam |
samākhyodāharaṇam
samākhyā yaugikī saṃjñā; yathā adhvaryukāṇḍapratipādite karmajāte ādhvaryavasamākhyāvaśādadhvaryoḥ kartṛtvenāṅgatvam, tathā 'aindrānamekādaśakapālaṃ nirvapetprajākāmaḥ' ityādiṣu kāmyeṣṭisamākhyāteṣvaindrāgnādiyāgeṣu kāmyeṣṭiyājyānuvākyākāṇḍa samākhyāvaśāt ' ubhā vāmindrānī' ityādīnāṃ yājyānuvākyatvena viniyogaḥ | viniyogo nāmāṅgatvenānvayaḥ |
śrutyādīnāṃ saṃnipāte balābalam
śrutyādīnāmekatra samāveśe pūrvapūrvasya prābalyamuttarottarasya daurbalyam | yathā ' kadācana starīrasītyainyā gārhapatyamupatiṣṭhate' ityagnihotraprakaraṇe śrūyate | tatra mantrasyendraprakāśana sāmarthyarūpālliṅgādindropasthānāṅgatve prāpte 'ainyā' iti tṛtīyāśrutyā 'gārhapatyam' iti dvitīyāśrutyā ca gāīpatyopasthānāṅgatvena vidhānāllaiṅgika indropasthāne, viniyono bādhyate | śrutirhi svato viniyojikā | liṅgaṃ tvindraprakāśanasāmarthyamālocya aindyendramupatiṣṭhate' iti śrutikalpanādvārā viniyojaka miti vācyam; taccātra na saṃbhavati | yatra śrutiviniyogo nāsti 'agnaye juṣṭaṃ nirvapāmi' ityādau, tatra mantrasya nirvāpaprakāśanasāmarthyamālocya 'anena mantreṇa nirvāpaṃ kuryāt ' iti śrutikalpanādvārā liṅgaṃ viniyojakaṃ bhavatyeva śrutikalpanāpratibandhakābhāvāt |
liṅgādiprābalyavicāraḥ
tathā 'syonaṃ te sadanaṃ kṛṇomi' 'tasminsīda' ityatra tasminniti tacchabdasya pūrvavākyārthasāpekṣatayā ekavākyatvabhānādvākyapramāṇena dvayorekamantratvaṃ bhāti, liṅgena tu bhinnamantratvaṃ bhāti | ādyasya sadanaprakāśanasāmarthyāt 'tasminsīda' ityasya sādanaprakāśakatvāt | tatra vākyāpekṣayā liṅgasya prābalyādvākyaṃ bādhitvā liṅgena 'syonaṃ te' ityasya sadanāṅgatvaṃ ' tasminsīda' ityasya sādanāṅgatvamiti nirṇayaḥ | ' syonaṃ te ' ityasya 'tasminsīda'ityanenaikavākyatvabalādyathākathaṃcitsādanasāmarthya rūpaṃ liṅgaṃ kalpayitvā ‘anena viśiṣṭamantreṇa sādanaṃ kuryāt' iti śrutiḥ kalpanīyā | sadanaprakāśana rūpapratyakṣaliṅgena kalpitayā 'syonaṃ ta ityanena sadanaṃ kuryāt' iti śrutyā 'syonaṃ te' ityasya śīghraṃ sadane viniyoge sati tenaiva mantrasya nairākāṅkṣayādvākyapramāṇālliṅgaṃ kalpayitvā śrutikalpanā pratibadhyate vilambitatvāditi liṅgena vākyasya bādhaḥ | evaṃ vākyena prakaraṇasya, prakaraṇātkramasya kramātsamākhyāyāśca bādho veditavyaḥ | tathā śrutyā vākyāderapi bādhaḥ | tadevamaṅgatābodhakapramāṇāni śrutyādīni nirūpitāni |
aṅgalakṣaṇam
taccāṅgatvaṃ śeṣatvam | pārārthyamiti yāvat | paroddeśena pravṛttakṛtivyāpyatvaṃ pārārthyam | prayājādīnāṃ darśapūrṇamāsoddeśena pravṛttapuruṣakṛtivyāpyatvāllakṣaṇasaṅgatiḥ | darśādeḥ prayājādyuddeśena pravṛttakṛti viṣayatvābhāvānnātivyāptiḥ | kevalaprayājānuddiśya kasyacidapi puruṣasyāpravṛtteḥ |
aṅgadvaividhyam
tānyaṅgāni dvividhāni -- saṃnipatyopakārakāṇi, ārādupakārakāṇi ceti | yānyaṅgāni sākṣātparamparayā vā pradhānayāgaśarīraṃ niṣpādya tadvārā tadutpattyapūrvopayogīni tāni saṃnipatyopakārakāṇi; yathā vrīhyādidravyāṇi tatsaṃyuktāvahananaprokṣaṇādīni, amyādidevatāttatsaṃyuktayājyānuvākyāvacanādīni ca | atra prokṣaṇā devahigatātiśayadvārā, avahananādestuṣavimokādirūpadṛṣṭadvārā, bīdyādīnāṃ piṣṭadvārā puroḍāśaniṣpādakatvaṃ, tadvārā yāgaśarīratadutpattya pūrva hetutvaṃ ca | yājyānuvākyāderdevatāsaṃskāradvārā devatāyāśca sākṣādyāgaśarīranirvartakatvaṃ, tadvārā tadutpatyapūrvopayogitvaṃ ca | yāgasya devatoddeśena dravyatyāgarūpatvāt 'dravyadevate hi yāgasvarūpam' iti siddhāntācca | etānyeva sāmavāyikānītyucyante | ātmasamavetāpūrvajanakānyārādupakārakāṇi -- yathā prayājājyabhāgānūyājādīni | etāni dravyagataṃ devatāgataṃ vā saṃskāraṃ na janayanti, kiṃtvātmagatamadṛṣṭaṃ janayantītyārādupakārakāṇi |
karmadvaividhyam
tatra sāmānyataḥ karma dvividham -- arthakarma, guṇakarma ceti | arthakarmalakṣaṇam tatra ātmagatāpūrvajanakaṃ karmā'rthakarma -- yathā'gnihotradarśapūrṇamāsaprayājādikam |
guṇakarmadvaividhyam
guṇakarma saṃskārajanakam | tacca dvividham -- upayuktasaṃskārakaṃ, upayokṣyamāṇa saṃskārakaṃ ca |
upayuktasaṃskārakam
tatra upayuktasaṃskārakaṃ pratipattikarma | 'upayuktasya ākīrṇakarasya dravyasya vihitadeśe prakṣepaḥ pratipatti' riti lakṣaṇāt | yathā iḍābhakṣaṇakṛṣṇaviṣāṇāprāsanacaturavattahomādikam | nanviḍābhakṣaṇasya pradhānayāgopayuktā kīrṇakarapuroḍāśadravyaprakṣepātmakatvātpratipattitvaṃ yuktam, homasya tu yāgopayuktadravyasaṃskārakatvābhāvātkathaṃ pratipattitvam, homasya yāgasamānakālīnatvena homasaṃskāryasya caturavattāderupayuktatvābhāvāditi cet | atrāhuḥ -- upayukta saṃskāramātraṃ pratipattiḥ, natu pradhānayāgopayuktatvam | tathā sati paśorviśasanānantaraṃ vapāhṛdayādyuddharaṇakālakartavyalohitaśakṛnnirasanasya pratipattitvābhāvaprasaṅgāt yāgopayuktadravyaprakṣeparūpatvābhāvāt | tathā kṛṣṇaviṣāṇāprāsanasyāpi pratipattitvaṃ na syāt yāgāṅgabhūtakaṇḍūyanopayuktatve'pi yāgopayuktatvābhāvāt | ato yatra vacanopayogamātraṃ vivakṣitam, yatra kvacidupayogastvatrāpyasti, vapāhṛdayādyuddharaṇenopayuktasya paśoḥ saṃbandhinaḥ śakṛllohitasyā''kīrṇakarasya pratipattyapekṣatvāt | evaṃ yathākathaṃcidupayogo homasthale'pyasti | tathā hi, pratipattistridhā --  pradhānottarakālā, pradhānasama kālā, pradhānapūrvakālā ceti | tatrādyā iḍābhakṣaṇādikā | dvitīyā homādikā | tathā hi -- darśapūrṇamāsaprakaraṇe śrūyate ' sakṛdupastṛṇāti' dvirhaviṣo'vadyati, ' ' sakṛdabhighārayati', 'caturavattaṃ juhoti' iti | tatra caturavattavākye homānuvādena caturavattadravyaṃ tatsādhanatvena na vidhīyate, homasyā prāptatvenānuvādāsaṃbhavāt | naca 'yadāna' vākyāddhomaprāptiḥ; tadvākyasya yāgavidhāyakatvāt | naca yāgahomayorabheda iti vācyam; devatoddeśena dravyatyāgasya yāgaśabdārthatvāt | prakṣepasya homaśabdārthatvāt | tatrāgneyacodanayā yāgasya prāptatve'pi prakṣepasya śaktyā'prāptatvenānuvādāsaṃbhavāt, kiṃtūpastaraṇadviravadānā'bhighāraṇavākyaiḥ prāptaṃ caturavattamuddiśya tatsaṃskāratvena prakṣepo juhotītyanena vidhīyate | sa ca saṃskāraḥ pratipattirūpa eva | agnaye juṣṭamabhighārayāmi' ityādi nirdeśairyā gāṅgabhūtāmyādidevatārthatayā yathākathaṃcidupayuktasya puroḍāśasya pratipattyapekṣayā tadavayavayavadāna karmakaprakṣepasya pratipattikarmatvaucityāt | sa ca saṃskāraḥ pradhānasamakālaḥ | homo hi ' vaṣaṭkṛte juhoti' iti vidhānādvaṣaṭkārottarakṣaṇe adhvaryuṇā kartavyaḥ | yāgo'pi tasminneva kṣaṇe yāgasmaraṇārthena vaṣadvāreṇa smāritaḥ san yajamānenānuṣṭheya iti tayoryogapadyasaṃbhavāt | tadidaṃ sarvapradānādhikaraṇavārtike sthitam | pradhānapūrvakālā pratipattiryathā -- ' śakṛtsaṃpravidhyati' ' lohitaṃ nirasyati' iti śakkṛtsaṃpravedhanalohitanirasane | idamapi 'paśāvanālambhāllohitaśakṛtorakarmatvam' ityedhikaraṇe sthitam |
upayokṣyamāṇasaṃskārabhedāḥ
upayokṣyamāṇasaṃskāro'pyanekavidhaḥ -- sākṣādviniyuktasaṃskāraḥ | sākṣādviniyuktasya yadupakārakaṃ tatsaṃskāraḥ, viniyokṣyamāṇa saṃskāraśceti | tatra ādyo yathā -- 'trīhīnavahanti' ityādiḥ | ' vrīhibhiryajeta' iti vākyaviniyuktatrīhīṇāmavahananasaṃskāraḥ || dvitīyo yathā -- 'vatsamālabheta ' iti | dohāṅgatvena sākṣādviniyuktasya godravyasyopakārako yo vatsaḥ tatsaṃskārakamidamālambhanam || tṛtīyo yathā -- ' tapte payasi dadhyānayati' iti | atra 'sā vaiśvadevyāmikṣā' iti vākyena tacchabdena nirdiśya vaiśvadevayāgāṅgatvena viniyokṣyamāṇaṃ yatpayastatsaṃskārakatvādviniyokṣyamāṇa saṃskārakaṃ dadhyānayanam | paśupuroḍāśayāga stūpayuktopayokṣyamāṇadevatāsaṃskārārthaḥ, tyāgāṃśe adṛṣṭārthaśca | atra tatsaṃskāyanīṣomadevatāyā vapāyāge upayuktārthatvāt hṛdayādyaṅgayāgeṣu upayokṣyamāṇatvācca | sviṣṭakṛdyāgo dravyāṃśe devatāṃśe copayuktasaṃskārārthaḥ, tyāgāṃśe adṛṣṭārthaśca | sūktavākasādhanaṃ praharaṇamapi tathaiva | uttamaprayājo yakṣyamāṇadevatāsaṃskāratvāttadaṃśe upayokṣyamāṇasaṃskāraḥ, itarāṃśe adṛṣṭārthaḥ | hṛdayādihaviryāgātpūrvaṃ kriyamāṇo vasāhomo'pi vasāṃśe pratipattiḥ, itarāṃśe adṛṣṭārtha ityāhyam |
pratipatterlakṣaṇāntaram
kecittu -- upayokṣyamāṇasaṃskārabhinnasaṃskārakarmatvaṃ pratipattikarmatvam | caturavattadravyasya homena bhasmībhūtasya homānantaramupayokṣyamāṇatvābhāvāt tadbhinnatvaṃ home'stīti lakṣaṇasaṃgatirityāhuḥ |
arthaguṇakarmaṇoḥ prādhānyam
atrāyaṃ viśeṣaḥ -- arthakarmaṇi dravyāpekṣayā karmaṇaḥ prādhānyam | karmaṇi dravyasya guṇatvam | yathā'gnihotrādau dadhyāderguṇatvam | guṇakarmaṇi dravyasya prādhānyaṃ dravye ca karmaṇo guṇatvam | 'śrīhīn prokṣati, avahanti' ityādau dvitīyayā vrīhīṇāṃ kriyāsādhyatvapratīteḥ kriyāpekṣayā dravyasya prādhānyaṃ prokṣaṇādikriyāyāstadapekṣayā guṇatvamiti |
guṇakarmaṇaścāturvidhyam
punarapi guṇakarma caturvidham utpattyāptivikṛtisaṃutpattyāptiskṛtibhedāt | tatrotpattisaṃskāro yathā -- 'agnīnādakīta' iti | mantraviśeṣaiḥ saṃbhāreṣu nidhāpitā apyāhavanīyādaya utpadyanta ityāhavanīyādyutpattihetubhūtasaṃskāratvādādhānasya utpattisaṃskāratvam || āptisaṃskāro yathā -- 'svādhyāyo'dhyetavyaḥ' iti | adhyayanena svādhyāya āpyata ityāptisaṃskāro'yam || vikṛtiryathā -- ' śrīhīnavahanti' iti trīhigatavaitupyarūpavikṛtijanakatvādavahananaṃ vikṛtisaṃskārakam || saṃskṛtiryathā -- 'trīhīn prokṣati' iti | [ atra prokṣaṇasya trīhigatātiśayarūpasaṃskṛtijanakatvātsaṃskṛtirūpaguṇakarmatvam |] atrādhānamadhyayanaṃ ca svatantraṃ guṇakarma, natu ṛtvaṅgam | prokṣaṇādikaṃ sarvaṃ ṛtva guṇakarmeti dhyeyam |
arthakarmaṇastraividhyama
arthakarma trividham -- nitya naimittika kāmyabhedāt | taṃtra 'yāvajjīvamagnihotraṃ juhoti', 'sāyaṃ juhoti', 'prātarjuhoti' iti jīvatā puruṣeṇa sāyaṃprātaḥ kālayorniyamena kartavyatayā'vagatama mihotrādikaṃ nityam | 'ammaye pathikṛte puroḍāśamaṣṭākapālaṃ nirvapet' iti darśapūrṇamāsātipattinimittatrapathikadiṣṭyādikaṃ naimittikam | nityanaimittikayorakaraṇe pratyavāya eva | kṛte phalaṃ nāstīti | kecit | anye tu -- duritanivṛttiḥ phalamasti; 'nityanaimittikaireva kurvāṇo duritakṣayam' ityādivākyaiḥ pratyavāyanivṛtteḥ phalatvena śrūyamāṇatvāt | nacaivaṃ tayorapi kāmyatvāpattiḥ phalavattvāditi vācyam; phalakāmanāpūrvakānuṣṭhānābhāvāt | tattadvidhivākye phalakāmamuddiśya tadarthaphalasādhanatvena vidhānābhāvācca kāmyatvānupapatteḥ |
kāmyakarmaṇastraividhyam
kāmyamapi karma trividham -- kevalamaihikaphalakam, āmuṣmikaphalakam, aihikāmuṣmikaphalakaṃ ceti | tatrādyaṃ yathā -- kārīyadi | tat tatsamayavartiśuṣyatsasyasaṃjīvana hetuvṛṣṭikāminā kriyate, na kālāntarabhāvivṛṣṭikāmena janmāntarīyavṛṣṭikāmena vā || kevalāmuṣmika phalakaṃ yathā -- svargādyarthaṃ darśapūrṇama | sādikam | svargasya ihalokabhogyatvābhāvāt || aihikāmuṣmika sādhāraṇaphalakaṃ yathā'vāyavyaṃ śvetamālabheta bhūtikāmaḥ' iti bhūtyādiphalaka mityanyatra vistaraḥ |
vedasyālaukikārthabodhakatvam
nanu darśapūrṇamāsādikarmaṇāṃ vrīhyādidravyāṇāṃ ca pratyakṣatvena laukikatvātkathaṃ vedasyā laukikārthabodhakatvamiti cet, na karmaṇāṃ pratyakṣatve'pi teṣāṃ svargādiphalasādhanatvamapratyakṣamiti 'tatphalasādhanatayā tat karma kartavyam' ityevaṃ phalasādhanatayā karmakartavyatābodhakasya vedasyā laukikārthabodhakatvāt | evaṃ trīhyādīnāṃ yāgādikriyāsādhanatvaṃ na pratyakṣavedyamiti tadbodhakasyāpi vedavākyasyālaukikārthabodhakatvamiti na doṣaḥ |
vidhervidhāyakatvaprakāraḥ
idānīṃ vidhivākyasya vidhāyakatvaprakāraḥ kathyate | tatra 'svargakāmo yajeta' ityatra yajidhātoruparitane tapratyaye dharmadvayamasti -- ākhyātatvaṃ, liṅtvaṃ ceti | tatrākhyātatvaṃ sarvalakāra sādhāraṇam, tadavacchedena liṃtyayaḥ puruṣapravṛttirūpāmarthabhāvanāṃ pratipādayati |
ārthī bhāvanā
sā cārthabhāvanā kiṃ, kena, kathamiti aṃśatrayaviśiṣṭā | tathā hi -- yajetetyatra prathamaṃ pratyanena 'bhāvayet' iti pratīyate | subantābhihitānāṃ sarveṣāṃ kārakāṇāṃ tiṅantārthakriyānvayaniyame sati tiṅantārthasya prādhānyāt | tatra ca prakṛtyarthāpekṣayā pratyayārthasya prādhānyātprathamaṃ pratyayārthabhāvanāyā upasthitiryuktā | tataḥ karoti -- samānārthakabhāvayateḥ sakarmakatvāt ‘kiṃ bhāvayet' iti karmākāṅkṣāyāṃ bhinnapadopātto'pi khargo bhāvyatvenānveti | bhāvyatvena karmatvenetyarthaḥ | sādhyatveneti yāvat | natu samānapadopātto'pi dhātvartho bhāvyatvenānveti | duḥkhātmakasya yāgasyepsitatamatvarūpakarmatvāyogyatvāt | khargasya tvānandā | tmatvenepsitatamatayā karmatvenānvayayogyatvāt | tataśca 'svargaṃ bhāvayet' iti bodho bhavati | tataḥ 'kena' iti karaṇākāṅkṣāyāṃ samānapadopātto yāgaḥ karaṇatvenānveti -- ' yāgena svargaṃ kuryāt' iti | tataḥ 'kathaṃ yāgena svarga kuryāt' iti kaithaṃbhāvākāṅkṣāyām abhyanvādhānā vahananādijanyadṛṣṭopakāreṇa prayājā dijanitādṛṣṭopakāreṇa ca sahitena yāgena svargaṃ kuryāditi abhyanvādhānaprayājādikamaṅgajātamitikartavyatayānveti | kathaṃbhāvākāṅkṣā pūrakatvamitikartavyatātvam | yathā 'odanakāmaḥ pacet' ityatra liṅā bhāvanā pratīyate | 'kiṃ bhāvayet' ityākāṅkṣāyāṃ odano bhāvyatvenānveti, 'kena' ityākāṅkṣāyāṃ pākeneti labhyate, 'katham ' ityākāṅkṣāyāṃ tṛṇaphūtkārasahiteneti | tataśca tṛṇaphūtkārādyupakṛtena pākena ( tejaḥ saṃyogena ) odanaṃ bhāvayediti vākyārthaḥ saṃpadyate tadvadvede'pi bodhyam |
śābdī bhāvanā
sa eva liṅpratyayo liṅgatvāvacchedena śabdebhāvanāṃ preraṇākhyāmabhidhatte | loke'pi 'gāmānaya' ityācāryavākyaśravaṇānantaraṃ ayamācāryo māṃ gavānayane prerayati' iti preraṇākhyavyāpāraṃ jñātvaiva śiṣyo gavānayane pravartata ityanvayavyatirekābhyāṃ preraṇājñānaṃ pravṛttikāraṇam | preraṇājñānasya cānvayavyatirekābhyāṃ liṅādiśravaṇajanyatvāvadhāraṇāt liṅādeḥ preraṇāyāṃ śaktirloke gṛhyata iti vede'pi tatraiva śaktikalpanaucityāt | iyāṃstu viśeṣaḥ -- loke gavānayanādipravṛttyanukūlapreraṇākhyo vyāpāraḥ prayoktṛpuruṣagatābhiprāyaviśeṣaḥ, vede tu prayoktṛ puruṣābhāvāliṅādiśabdaniṣṭha eva sa ityaṅgīkriyate | ata eva śabdaniṣṭhavyāpāratvācchābdī bhāvanetyucyate | yāgahomādiviṣayapravṛttihetutvāt pravartanā preraṇeti cocyate | saiṣā śābdī bhāvanāpi aṃśatrayaviśiṣṭā | tatra puruṣapravṛttirūpārthabhāvanā bhāvyatvenānveti | adhyayanāvagataliṅādikaṃ karaṇatvenānveti | arthavādapratipādyaprāśastyajñānamitikartavyatātvenānveti | tatra 'sāṅgavedādhyetāro 'dhītavyākaraṇanigamaniruktā divaśādyutpattimantaḥ puruṣā adhyayanagṛhītasvādhyāyagataliṅādibhiḥ prāśastyajñānasacivairyāgādyarthaṃ svakartavyatvena buddhā yāgādīnanutiṣṭheyuḥ' iti śābdabhāvanābodhaḥ | anutiṣṭheyuḥ, anuṣṭhānaṃ kuryurityarthaḥ | anuṣṭhānaṃ pravṛttiḥ | tena puruṣapravṛtteḥ śābdabhāvanābhāvyatvamakṣatam |
iyaṃ ca śabdabhāvanā jyotiṣṭomādiprāti svikavākyeṣu svarūpeṇa pratīyamānā kartavyatvena na pratīyate | arthabhāvanāyā eva teṣu kartavyatvāvagamāt kiṃtu 'svādhyāyo'dhyetavyaḥ' iti vākya eva kartavyatvena pratīyate | nacātrārthabhāvanāyā eva vidheyatvamiti vācyam | 'svādhyāyo'dhyetavyaḥ' iti vākyasthārthabhāvanāyā eva sakalavidhivākyasthaśabdabhāvanārūpatvāt | tathā hi -- adhipūrvakāt 'iṅ adhyayane' iti dhātoḥ karmaṇi tavyapratyayo vihitaḥ | karma ca svādhyāyaḥ pradhānam | tatsaṃskārakamadhyayanaṃ guṇakarma | vrīhisaṃskārakaprokṣaṇādivat | adhyayanajanitagrahaṇa saṃskāraviśiṣṭasvādhyāyasya prayojanākāṅkṣāyāṃ svādhyāyagatatattalliṅādiviśiṣṭavākyasāmarthyalabhyaṃ yadanuṣṭānaupayikaṃ yāgādirūpārthajñānaṃ tadeva dṛṣṭatvāt, tattatkarmānuṣṭhānadvārā svargādirūpā laukikaśreyaḥsādhanatvācca prayojanam | karmāvabodhaṃ vinā karmānuṣṭhānāyogāt | na tvadṛṣṭaṃ prayojanam ; dṛṣṭaphale saṃbhavatyadṛṣṭaphalakalpanā'yogāt | tathā ca svādhyāyavidhinaiva svādhyāyagatavidhivākyagataliṅādipratipāditāḥ sarvāḥ śābdabhāvanā aṃśatrayaviśiṣṭāḥ kartavyatvena vidhīyante | sāṅgavedādhyayanena vyutpannāḥ puruṣāḥ adhyayanagṛhītasvādhyāya gataliṅā dibhirarthavādāvagataprāśastyarūpāṅga sacivaiḥ phalavadyāgādikartavyatāṃ buddhā yāgādīnanutiṣṭheyuriti | anutiṣṭheyuḥ pravṛttiṃ kuryurityarthaḥ | tatra puruṣapravṛtterbhāvyatvenānvayāt, adhyayanāvagataliṅādeḥ karaṇatvenānvayāt prāśastyajñānasyetikartavyatātvenānvayāt, śabdabhāvanāyā api aṃśatrayavaiśiṣṭyamarthabhāvanāyā iveṣṭam | tatra samīhitakhargādibhāvyakatvabhānamapi vidhivaśādeva | pravartanāparo vidhiḥ yāgādeḥ puruṣārthāsādhanatve tatra puruṣaṃ pravartayituṃ na śaknotīti khabhāvyapuruṣapravṛttiviṣayasya yāgādeḥ puruṣābhilaṣitasvargādisādhanatvamāpādayati | anyathā svasya pravartanāparatvameva na syāt | pravṛttihetuvyāpārasyaiva pravartanātvāt | laḍādisthale tu pravartanātmakavirabhāvādārthabhāvanāyāḥ puruṣārthabhāvyatvaniyamo nāsti |
bhāvanāśabdārthaḥ
bhāvanātvaṃ nāma bhavituḥ prayojakavyāpāratvam | tatrārtha bhāvanāyāṃ bhaviturjāyamānasya svargādeḥ prayojakavyāpāratvāllakṣaṇasaṅgatiḥ | śābdabhāvanāyāmapi puruṣapravṛttirūpasya bhavituḥ prayojakavyāpāratvāllakṣaṇasaṅgatiḥ |
jyotiṣṭomādiśabdānāṃ nāmatvapratipādanam
nanu 'jyotiṣṭomena khargakāmo yajeta' ityatra yāgena svargaṃ bhāvayedityarthavarṇane jyotiṣṭomapadasya kathamanvaya iti cet, na; bhāvanākaraṇatvenābhimatayāganāmatvenānvayāt 'jyotiṣṭomanāma kena yāgena' iti | kathaṃ tannāmatvamiti cet, jyotirākhyāstrivṛdādistomā asminsantīti vyutpattyā | yāgasya trivṛdādistomasaṃvandhaḥ kenāvagamyata iti cet, 'trivṛt pañcadaśa saptadaśa stomāḥ' iti vākyāntareṇa; etacchabdārthasya tatsaṃbandhāvagamāt | evaṃ śāstroktaistatprakhyādibhirhetubhistatra tatra nāmatvaṃ bodhyam |
nāmadheyanimittāni
śāstre hi tatprakhyatadyapadeśayaugikavākyabhedaiścaturbhinīmatvaṃ pratipāditam |
tatprakhyanyāyena nāmatvam
tathā hi -- 'agnihotraṃ juhoti' ityatra agnihotraśabdena agnidevatārūpo guṇo na vidhīyate, 'agnijrjyotirjyotiragmiḥ svāheti sāyaṃ juhoti' iti vākyavihitena mantreṇa devatāyāḥ prāptatvāt | kiṃtu agniprakhyāpakaṃ [ agniprāpakaṃ ] yacchāstrāntaraṃ 'agnijyotijrjyotiragniḥ ' ityādikaṃ tena prāptamagnisaṃbandhaṃ nimittīkṛtya agnaye hotraṃ homo'sminiti bahuvrīhiṇā agnihotrapadasya homanāmadheyatvam | naca homasya pratyayavācyārthabhāvanāyāṃ karaṇatvāt tannāmatve ' anihotreṇa' iti tṛtīyā syāt, 'jyotiṣṭomena' itivaditi vācyam; dvitīyāyā eva lakṣaṇayā karaṇārthakatvāt, 'nāsādhitaṃ karaṇam' iti nyāyena asādhitasya karaṇatvāyogāt arthātprāptahomagatasādhyatvānuvādakatvena vā dvitīyopapatteriti tatprakhyanyāyādatra nāmadheyatvam |
tadvyapadeśanyāyena nāmatvam
tathā 'śyenenābhicaran yajeta' ityatra śyenapadaṃ yāganāmadheyaṃ, natu yāgāṅgatvena śyenapakṣirūpaguṇavidhiḥ | 'yathā ha vai śyeno nipatyādatte evamevāyaṃ dviṣantaṃ bhrātṛvyaṃ nipatyādatte yamabhicarati śyenena' ityupamānopameyavyapadeśānupapattiprasaṅgāt | ' śyenapakṣī yathā pakṣyantaraṃ nipatyādatte evamayamapi śyenanāmakaḥ karmaviśeṣo bhrātṛvyaṃ śatruṃ nipatyādatte' iti vyapadeśaḥ karmanāmatvapakṣa eva yujyate | yāgāṅgatayā śyenapakṣividhau tu khasya khenaivopamānopameyabhāvaḥ syāt sa cāyuktaḥ | tasmācchayenatulyatāvyapadeśācchyenapadaṃ karmanāmadheyam ; tataśca 'abhicaran śatruvadhakāmaḥ śyenanāmakena yāgena abhicāraṃ bhāvayet' iti vākyārthaḥ | nanvevamapyabhicārasyāpi vedoktatvādābhicārike karmaṇyāstikānāmapi pravṛttiḥ syāditi cet, na vedokto'pyabhicāro vedavihito na bhavati, phalatvāt | 'phalaṃ na vidheyaṃ kiṃtu phalamuddiśya tatsādhanatvena karmaiva vidhe yam' iti siddhāntāt | ato'bhicārasya vihitatvena pratyavāyajanakatvam |
yogena nāmatvam
evaṃ 'udbhidā yajeta paśukāmaḥ' ityatra paśurūpaphalāya vidhitsitasya yāgasya udbhitpadaṃ nāmadheyam natu yathākathaṃcitprāptayāgamuddiśya taṃdaṅgatayodbhidrūpaguṇavidhiḥ | dadhyādivadudbhinnāmakasya guṇasyāprasiddhatvāt | yāge tu 'udbhidyate phalamanena ' iti yogasaṃbhavādyāganāmatvam | nanu 'udbhidyate bhūmiranena' iti yogavaśena khanitrādāvapyudbhidaḥ prayogasaṃbhavāt khanitrādiguṇavidhiḥ kiṃ na syāditi cet, na; etādṛśasthale guṇavidhiḥ kvāpi vaktuṃ na śakyate | viruddhatrikadvayasamāveśākhyadoṣaprasaṅgāt |
viruddhatrikavicāraḥ
tathā hi -- 'udbhidā yajeta paśukāmaḥ' ityatra mānāntareṇāprāptasya yāgasyānuvādāsaṃbhavāt, paśukarmakabhāvanāyāṃ karaṇatvena yāgo vidheyaḥ -- 'yāgena paśūnbhāvayet' iti | tataśca yāgasya vidheyatvam | phalaṃ prati ca śeṣatayā pratīyamānatvādguṇatvam | śeṣatayā aṅgatayetyarthaḥ | phalasiddhyarthaṃ puṃsā'nuṣṭhīyamānatvādupādeyatvamiti vidheyatvaguṇatvopādeyatvātmakamekaṃ trikaṃ yāge'sti | tathā yāgoddeśenodbhidguṇavidhau vidhitsitaguṇāpekṣayā prādhānyam uddeśyatvam anuvādyatvaṃ ceti dvitīyaṃ trikam | tadetat trikadvayaṃ parasparaviruddha mekasminyāge prasajyeta; ato na guṇavidhiḥ | naca 'somena yajeta' ityatra viruddhatrikaṃ vinaiva somaśabdasya somavati yāge lakṣaṇāmaṅgīkṛtya 'somavatā yāgeneṣṭaṃ bhāvayet' iti somaviśiṣṭayāgavidhānādyathā somarūpaguṇavidhiḥ, tadvadudbhitpadasyāpi matvarthalakṣaṇayodbhidviśiṣṭayāgavidhānādatrodbhidguṇaviśiṣṭayāgavidhiḥ syāditi vācyam | somādau gatyantarābhāvāt matvarthalakṣaṇayā viśiṣṭavidhyaṅgīkārāt | udbhidādau nāmatvena gatisaṃbhavādviśiṣṭa vidhyaṅgīkārāyogāt | tasmādyogavaśādurdvidādīnāṃ nāmatvam |
vākya bhedabhayānāmatvam
tathā 'citrayā yajeta paśukāmaḥ' ityatra citrāpadaṃ ( vākyabhedabhayāt ) prājāpatyayāganāmadheyam | 'dadhi madhu ghṛtamāpo dhānāstaṇḍulāstatsaṃsṛṣṭaṃ prājāpatyam' ityatra ' tatsaṃsṛṣṭaṃ prājāpatyam' iti sāmānādhikaraṇyādyo dadhyādidravyaṣaṭsya prajāpatidevatāsaṃbandhaḥ śrutastenānumito yāgo vidhīyate | vihitasya yāgasya phalākāṅkṣāyāṃ 'citrayā yajeta' iti vākyaṃ phalasaṃbandhavidhāyakam | tatra phalārthatvena punarvidhiriti tasya prājāpatyayāgasya dadhyādivicitradravyakatvāccitrāpadaṃ karmanāmadheyam | natu 'agnīṣomīyaṃ paśumā labheta' iti vākyaprāptaṃ paśuyāgaṃ 'yajeta' ityanūdya citratvastrītvaguṇadvaya vidhānam | mānāntaraprāptaṃ karmoddiśya tatrānekaguṇavidhāne vākyabhedāt | taduktam -- 'prāpte karmaṇi nāneko vidhātuṃ śakyate guṇaḥ iti | mānāntarāprāpte karmaṇi tvanekadevatāṣṭākapālapuroḍāśāmāvāsyāpaurṇamāsyādyanekaguṇaviśiṣṭo dravyadevatāsaṃbandhenānumito yāgo vidhīyata iti siddhāntaḥ| taduktam -- ‘aprāpte tu vidhīyante bahavo'pyekayatnataḥ' iti atha yathā 'paśunā yajeta' ityatra mānāntaraprāptayāgamuddiśya tadaṅgataya 'paśunā' ityekapadopāttānāṃ paśudravyatadgataliṅgasaṃkhyānāṃ trayāṇāṃ vākya bhedaṃ vinaiva vidhiraṅgīkṛtaḥ, tadvatrāpyekapadopāttacitratvastrītvaviśeṣitapaśudravyakārakasya vidhānānna vākyabhedaḥ | ata eva tatra vidheyasya paśorupādeyatvāttadgatamekatvaṃ yajñāṅgatayā vivakṣitamityekenaiva paśunā yaṣṭavyam, 'grahaṃ saṃmārṣṭi ' ityatra uddeśyagatatvādekatvamavivakṣitamiti siddhāntapravādaḥ | kathaṃ, 'grahaṃ saṃmārṣṭi ' ityatra 'graham' iti dvitīyayā grahasyepsitatamatvenoddeśyatyāt, prayojanatvācca prādhānyaṃ gamyate | saṃmārgastu grahaṃ prati guṇabhūtaḥ | 'pratipradhānaṃ guṇa āvartanīyaḥ' iti nyāyāt 'yāvanto grahāsteṣāṃ sarveṣāṃ saṃmārgaḥ' iti niśvaye sati, 'kati grahāḥ saṃmārjanīyāḥ' iti bubhutsāyā abhāvāduddeśyagatamekatvaṃ śrūyamāṇamapyavivakṣitam | yadyucyeta nedamuddeśyagataṃ kiṃtu khayaṃ vidheyam -- 'grahaṃ saṃmṛjyāt', 'yaṃ saṃmṛjyāttaṃ caikam' iti | tathā sati vidheyabhedādvākya bhedaḥ syāt | tathābubhutsāyā abhāvāt vidhānāsyogāccoddeśyagatā saṃkhyā na vivakṣitā | uddeśyagatamapi viśeṣaṇaṃ kiṃcidvivakṣitaṃ yasya viśeṣaṇasya vivakṣāṃ vinā uddeśya -- pratītirna paryavasyati | yathā -- tatraiva grahatvaṃ vivakṣitam | tadvivakṣāṃ vinā uddeśyasvarūpasya jñātumaśakyatvāt | tena grahajātīya sādhanakasomayāgāpūrvārthatvāccamaseṣu na saṃmārga iti sthitam | 'paśunā yajeta' ityatra yāgaṃ prati paśurvidheyatvādguṇabhūtaḥ | pratiguṇaṃ pradhānāvṛttirnāstīti kiyadbhiḥ paśubhiḥ yāgaḥ kartavya ityākāṅkṣāyāṃ ekavacanena pratīyamānaṃ vidheyagatamekatvaṃ vivakṣitaṃ bubhutsitatvāt | kiṃca liṅgasaṃkhyāviśeṣitasya paśudravyarūpakārakasya ekapadopāttasya vidheyatvādvidheyapaśudvārā tadgataliṅgasaṃkhyāderapi kriyāṅgatvādekatvaṃ vivakṣitam | athavā tṛtīyayā vibhaktyā'bhihitayorliṅgasaṃkhyayorvibhaktayabhihitatayā karaṇakārakaśaktyā''tmasātkṛtayoḥ prātipadikārthapaśudravyeṇa saṃbandhamanādṛtya tadvadeva sākṣātkriyāṅgatvena vidhāne sati paścādaruṇaikahāyanīnyāyena parasparamanvayaḥ -- yāgāṅgatvena vihito yaḥ paśuḥ sa ekaḥ pumāṃśceti | yathā 'aruṇayā piṅgākṣyā ekahāyanyā gavā somaṃ krīṇāti' ityatra kārakāṇāṃ kriyānvayaniyamāt karaṇavibhaktibhirāruṇyapiṅgākṣītvādīnāṃ caturṇāṃ parasparamananvitānāmeva somakrayaṇāṅgatvenānvaye sati āruṇyāderguṇasyāmūrtasya svataḥ kriyāsādhanatvāyogāt kriyāsādhanaikahāyanī -- goparicchedakatvena paścāt parasparaṃ pāṣṇikānvayaḥ -- yā ekahāyanī gauḥ sā piṅgākṣī aruṇeti tadvat | tadevamuddeśyagata viśeṣaṇamavivakṣitam, upādeyagataṃ vivakṣitamiti sthitam | evaṃ ca citrāpadena citratvastrītvobhayaviśeṣitapaśukārakasya yajetetipadānūdite'gnīṣomīyapaśuyāge vidhātuṃ śakyatvāt kathaṃ nāmatvamiti cet, na; tathā sati prakṛtasya prājāpatyayāgasya phalasaṃbandhākāṅkṣasya honama, aprakṛtasyā'gnīṣomīyayāgasya kalpanā ca syāt | tadvayamayuktam ; tasmāccitrāpada karmanāmadheyam | evaṃ tatprakhyādibhiranyatamena sarvatra karmanāmatvamūhyam | itthaṃ vidhivākyasya prāmāṇyaṃ vicāritam |
arthavādavicāraḥ
athārthavādo vicāryate | arthavādānāṃ tu svārthaparatve prayojanābhāvādadhyayana vidhivaśena phalavadarthajñānārthatvasyāvaśyakatvādvidheyagataprāśastyapratipādanadvārā vidhyekavākyatayā prāmāṇyam | sa ca caturvidhaḥ -- nindāpraśaṃsāparakṛtipurākalpabhedāt | tatra nindārthavādo yathā -- 'asatraṃ vā etadyadacchandogam', 'aśrujaṃ hi rajataṃ yo barhiṣi dadāti purāsya saṃvatsarāgṛhe rudanti' ityādiḥ | praśaṃsā yathā -- 'śobhate'sya mukhaṃ ya evaṃ veda', 'vāyurvai kṣepiṣṭhā devatā vāyumeva khena bhāgadheyenopadhāvati sa evainaṃ bhūtiṃ gamayati' ityevamādiḥ | pareṇa mahatā puruṣeṇedaṃ karma kṛtamiti pratipādako'rthavādaḥ parakṛtiḥ -- yathā 'agnirvā akāmayata' ityādiḥ | parapravaktṛkārthādipratipādakaḥ purākalpaḥ, yathā -- 'tamaśapaddhiyā dhiyā tvā vadhyāsuḥ' ityādiḥ | tatra nindārthavādasya vidheyānyanindādvārā vidheyaprāśastyaparatvam | ' aśrujam' ityādirajatanindādvārā ca vidheyabhūtarajatadāna niṣedhaprāśastyaparatve virodhābhāvāt | itareṣāṃ trayāṇāṃ sākṣātprāśastyaparatvameva | 'vāyurvai' ityarthavādasya vāyuḥ kṣipragāmitvādatīva praśastā devatā, atastaddaivatyaṃ karma praśastamiti vidheyadevatā gataprāśastyapratipādanadvārā vidhyekavākyatvam | ‘agnirvā akāmayata' ityasya amidaivatyo yāgaḥ pūrvakāle agninākṛtatvātpraśastaḥ, āstikyādidānīmapyanyairyajamānairavaśyaṃ kartavya iti vidheyakarmagataprāśastyadvārā vidhyekavākyatvam | evamanyatrāpyūhyam | kvacitkāryāntaramapyasti; yathā -- 'aktāḥ śarkarā upadadhāti' iti vidhau ' aktāḥ' iti padena | dravadravyasāmānyaṃ pratīyate | tacca dravyaṃ kimiti saṃdehe 'tejo vai ghṛtam' ityarthavādāt ghṛtamiti niścīyata iti, 'tejo vai ghṛtam' ityarthavādasya saṃdigdhārthanirṇāyakatvena prāmāṇyam | ityarthavādavicāraḥ |
mantravicāraḥ
atha matravicāraḥ | mantrāṇāmapyadhyayanavidhinā kṛtsnasvādhyāyasya phalavadarthajñānārthatvameva, natvadṛṣṭārthatvam | dṛṣṭe saṃbhavatyadṛṣṭakalpanā'yogāt | prayogakāle karmajñānaṃ vinā karmānuṣṭhānāyogāt karmopayikamarthajñānaṃ mantraiḥ kriyate | na copadeṣṭṛvacanādināpyarthasmaraṇasaṃbhavādanuṣṭhānopapattiriti vācyam | mantrairevārthaṃ smṛtvānuṣṭhāne sati phalaṃ saṃbhavati nānyatheti niyamāṅgīkārāt, niyamenādṛṣṭasyāṅgīkārāt, tadabhāve tajjanyādṛṣṭalope tanmūlakaṃ phalaṃ na siddhyatīti kalpanāt | nanvevaṃ mantrāṇāṃ svaprakāśye'rthe viniyoga iti phalitam | viniyogo nāma śeṣatvenānvayaḥ | śeṣatvaṃ nāmāṅgatvam | tataśca 'imāmagṛbhṇanraśanām' iti mantrasya raśanāgrahaṇasāmarthyamastīti raśanāgrahaṇaprakāśana sāmarthyarūpāliṅgādeva raśanāgrahaṇāṅgatvopapatteḥ 'imāmagṛbhṇanraśanāmṛtasyetyaśvābhidhānīmādatte' iti vacanaṃ kimarthamiti cet, na; parisaṃkhyārthatvāt | cayanaprakaraṇe'śvaraśanāgrahaṇaṃ gardabharaśanāgrahaṇaṃ ceti dvayamasti | tatra liṅgādaśvaraśanāyāṃ mantraprāptāvucyamānāyāṃ liṅgāviśeṣāgardabharaśanāyāmapi mantraḥ prāptaḥ | ato'śvaraśanāyāmeva mantraḥ kāryo na gardabharaśanāyāmiti mantranivṛttirūpaparisaṃkhyārtho'yaṃ vidhiḥ |
parisaṃkhyāyā doṣatraividhyam
sā ca parisaṃkhyā tridoṣāsvārthatyāgaḥ, parārthasvīkāraḥ, prāptabādhaśceti bhedāt | ' aśvābhidhānīmādatte' iti vākyasya anena mantreṇāśvaraśanāssdānaṃ kuryāditi khārthaḥ, sa ca tyaktaḥ | gardabharaśanāto mantranivṛttiḥ parārthaḥ, sa ca svīkṛtaḥ | gardabharaśanāyāmapi liṅgātprāptasya mantrasya anena bādhaśca -- iti tridoṣā parisaṃkhyā gatyabhāvādaṅgīkṛtā | gatyantare sati sā na yuktā |
vikalpaḥ
evamaṣṭadoṣaduṣṭa vikalpo'pyagatiko'ṅgīkṛtaḥ | yathā -- yadāgneyo'ṣṭākapālaḥ saṃskṛtaḥ puroḍāśo yāgāṅgatvenāvagataḥ | tatra apūpaviśeṣasya puroḍāśasya prakṛtidravyākāṅkṣāyāmaniyamena yasyakasyacidravyasya prāptau 'vrīhibhiryajeta' iti vākyādrīhayo niyamyante, evaṃ 'yavairyajeta' iti vākyādyavā api niyamyante; tayorekārthatvādvikalpaḥ | evaṃ ' atirātre ṣoḍaśinaṃ gṛhṇāti' ityatirātre ṣoḍaśigraho vihitaḥ | tathā 'nā'tirātre ṣoḍaśinaṃ gṛhṇāti' iti pratiṣedhāgrahaṇābhāvo vihitaḥ | tayorgrahaṇāgrahaṇayoḥ parasparaviruddhayorekasminprayoge'nuṣṭhātumaśakyatvātkvacitprayoge grahaṇānuṣṭhānam ; prayogāntare tadabhāvānuṣṭhānamiti tatrāpi vikalpaḥ |
vikalpe doṣāṣṭakam
sa cāṣṭadoṣaduṣṭaḥ | tathā hi -- pūrvaṃ trīhiprayoge yavaśāstrasya khārthānuṣṭhāpakatvalakṣaṇaprāmāṇyaparityāgaḥ | ananuṣṭhāpakatvalakṣaṇāprāmāṇyasvī kāraḥ | tato dvitīyaprayoge yavānuṣṭhāne pūrvaṃ tyaktaṃ yatsāmāṇyaṃ tatsvīkāraḥ | svīkṛtaṃ yadaprāmāṇyaṃ tatparityāgaśceti yavaśāstre catvāro doṣāḥ | evaṃ pūrvaṃ yavaprayoge śrīhiśāstrasya svārthānuṣṭhāpakatvalakṣaṇaṃ yatprāmāṇyaṃ tatparityāgaḥ | ananuṣṭhāpakatvalakṣaṇaṃ yadaprāmāṇyaṃ tatsvīkāraḥ | punardvitīyaprayoge trīdyanuṣṭhāne vrīhiśāstrasya tyaktaprāmāṇyasvīkāraḥ | svīkṛtāprāmāṇyaparityāgaśceti trīhiśāstre catvāro doṣāḥ ityaṣṭadoṣaduṣṭo vikalpaḥ | sa ca vikalpaḥ kvacidekārthatvāt ; ekakāryakāritvādityarthaḥ | yathā vrīhiyavayorekaikasya puroḍāśa niṣpādanakṣamatvādvikalpaḥ | kvacidvacanavalāt ; yathā'bṛhat pṛṣṭhaṃ bhavati' iti bṛhat sāmasādhyaṃ pṛṣṭhanāmakaṃ stotraṃ vihitam, 'rathantaraṃ pṛṣṭhaṃ bhavati' iti rathantarasāmasādhyaṃ pṛṣṭhastotrāntaraṃ vidhīyate | stotraṃ ca prayājādivadapūrvārthatvādarthakarma | sāma tu saṃskorakarmatvādruṇakarma | stotrasādhanībhūtastotrīyākṣarābhivyaktirūpasaṃskāradvārā sāmnāṃ stotrasādhanatvāṅgīkārāt | pragītamantrasādhyamindrādiguṇiniṣṭhaguṇābhidhānaṃ stotram ; pragītamantrasādhyaṃ sāmābhivyaktaṛgakṣarasādhyamityarthaḥ | apragītamantrasādhyaguṇiniṣṭhaguṇābhidhānaṃ śastram | gānakriyāviśeṣaḥ sāma | stotrasādhanībhūtā ṛcaḥ stotrīyāḥ | tadetatrivṛtpañcadaśatvādisaṃkhyāviśeṣaḥ stoma ityeteṣāṃ bhedaḥ | tathā ca bṛhadrathantarapṛṣṭhayorbhinnāpūrvārthatvena ekārthatvābhāve'pi 'bṛhadvā pṛṣṭha kārya, rathantaraṃ vā pṛṣṭhaṃ kāryam' iti vacanabalādeva vikalpaḥ | kvacidvyavasthitavikalpaḥ | dvitīyaprayājādikarmaṇi nārāśaṃsatanūnapānmantrayorekārthatvādvikalpaḥ | sa ca ' rājanyavasiṣṭhādīnāṃ nārāśaṃso dvitīyaḥ prayājastanūnapādanyeṣām' iti vākyāvyavasthita iti vyavasthitavikalpaḥ |
kṛtsnavedasya dharmādharmākhyārthe prāmāṇyam
tadevaṃ codanāparaparyāyāṇāṃ vidhivākyānāmaśatraya viśiṣṭabhāvanāvidhāyakatvātprāmāṇyam, udbhidādīnāṃ nāmatayā, arthavādānāṃ vidheyaprāśastyaparatayā, mantrāṇāmanuṣṭheyārthasmārakatayā prāmāṇyamiti kṛtsrasya vedasyālaukike dharmādharmākhye'rthe prāmāṇyaṃ sthitam || iti vedaprāmāṇyavicāraḥ | atha smṛtyādiprāmāṇyavicāraḥ | manvādipraṇītānāṃ smṛtīnāmapi vedamūlakatvātsyādaṣṭakādau dharme prāmāṇyam | 'audumbarī sarvāṃ veṣṭayitavyā' iti smṛtirapramāṇam, 'audumbarīṃ spṛṣṭvodgāyet' iti pratyakṣaśrutiviruddhatvāt | sarvaveṣṭane sati śrutyuktasparśanasya kartumaśakyatvāt | 'vaisarjana homīyaṃ vāso'dhvaryurgṛhṇāti' ityādismṛtirapramāṇam, śrutyarthāviruddhatve'pi lobhamūlakatvāt | tathā śiṣṭācāro'pi smṛtidvārā śrutimūlakatvātpramāṇam | mātulasutāpariṇayanādiśiṣṭācārastvapramāṇam | 'mātulasya sutāmūḍhā mātṛgotrāṃ tathaiva ca | samānapravarāṃ tadvatyaktvā cāndrāyaṇaṃ caret' ityādismṛtyā niṣiddhatvena smṛtiviruddhatvāt tadevaṃ śrutismṛtyācārāṇāṃ dharmādharmayoḥ prāmāṇyamuktam |
dharmasya parasparaṃ bhedakāni pramāṇāni
sa ca dharmaḥ parasparaṃ bhinnaḥ | bhedakapramāṇāni tu śabdāntarādīni | tathā hi -- yāgadānahomānāṃ yajati dadāti -- juhotītya paryāyaśabdapratipādyatvācchabdāntarādbhedaḥ | khavatvanivṛttipūrvakaṃ parakhatvāpādanaṃ dānam | 'samidho yajati' ityādīnāṃ pañcānāṃ vākyānāṃ madhye ekasya karmavidhāyakatvam, anyeṣāṃ tasminkarmaṇi guṇavidhāyakatvam, ityatra niyāmakābhāvātsarveṣāṃ karmavidhāyakatve sthite vihitasya karmaṇaḥ punarvidhānaṃ vyarthamiti punarvidhānasāmarthyāt pūrvavākyavihitakarmāpekṣayottarottaravākyavihitasya karmaṇo bhedaḥ siddhyatītyetādṛśa sthaleṣvaviśeṣapunaḥ -- śrutirūpāt 'yajati' padābhyāsāt karmabhedaḥ | 'tisra āhutīrjuhoti' ityatra juhotipadābhyāsābhāve'pi juhotītyarthe home tritvasaṃkhyānvayātparasparaṃ bhinnāstrayo homā iti saṃkhyayātra karmabhedaḥ | 'athaiṣa jyotirathaiṣa viśvajyotirathaiṣa sarvajyotiretena sahasradakṣiṇena yajeta' iti jyotiṣṭomaprakaraṇe śrutānāmapi jyotirākhyānāṃ trayāṇāṃ yāgānāṃ jyotiṣṭomasaṃjñāpekṣayā pṛthaksaṃjñākaraṇājjyotiṣṭomāpekṣayā bhedaḥ | bhinnasaṃjñāvaśādeva trayāṇāṃ parasparaṃ bheda iti saṃjñayā karmabhedaḥ | tathā ' tapte payasi dadhyānayati sā vaiśvadevyāmikṣā vājibhyo vājinam' ityatra 'sā vaiśvadevī' itivākye viśvadevadevatā''mikṣādravyasaṃbandhānumito yāgo vidhīyate | 'vājibhyo vājinam' ityatrāpi vājidaivatyaṃ vājinadravyakaṃ karmāntaraṃ vidhīyate | natu pūrvavihite vaiśvadevayāge vājinadravyarūpo guṇo vidhīyate | pūrvayāgasyāmikṣāguṇāvaruddhatvena tatra vājinasya niveśāyogāt | naca trīhiyavavadvikalpaḥ | vājināmikṣayoḥ samaśiṣṭatvābhāvāt | viṣamaśiṣṭayorvikalpāyogāt | vaiśvadevayā gotpattivākya evāmikṣāguṇaḥ śiṣyata ityutpattiśiṣṭaḥ; śiṣyate vidhīyata ityarthaḥ | vaiśvadevavākyādutpanne karmaṇi vākyāntareṇa śiṣṭo vājinaguṇa utpannaśiṣṭaḥ, tayormadhye hyutpattiśiṣṭaḥ prabalaḥ, karmotpatti -- velāyāmeva karmāṅgatvena pramitatvāt | utpannaśiṣṭo vājinaguṇo'nantaraṃ pramito'pi vilambitatvena durbalatvātpūrvakarmaṇi niveśamalabhamāno vājirūpadevatāntarasaṃbandhāt svavākyasya karmāntaravidhāyakatvamānayatīti guṇādbhedaḥ | tatra ghanībhūtaṃ paya āmikṣā, śiṣṭaṃ jalaṃ vājinam | tathā kuṇḍapāyināmayane śrūyate -- 'upasadbhiścaritvā māsamabhihotraṃ juhoti' iti | atra pūrvaṃ karma kiṃcidapi saṃnihitaṃ na bhavati apūrvakarmasaṃnidhānarūpātprakaraṇāntarātprasiddhāgnihotradharmakaṃ tannāmakaṃ karmāntaraṃ vidhīyate | natvagnihotraśabdena prasiddhāgnihotramanūdya guṇavidhiḥ | prāpte karmaṇi ānantaryamāsarūpānekaguṇavidhau vākyabhedā | patteriti prakaraṇāntarādatra karmabhedaḥ | prasiddhāgnihotraṃ naiyamikā nihotram, nityāgnihotramiti yāvat | tadevaṃ śabdāntarābhyāsasaṃkhyāsaṃjñāguṇaprakaraṇāntaraiḥ karmabhedo darśitaḥ | iti karmabhedanirūpaṇam ||
prameyavicāraḥ
atha prameyādivicāraḥ | tatra vedādiprameyo'rthastrividhaḥ -- kratvarthaḥ, puruṣārthaḥ, ubhayārthayeti | tatra prayājādikaṃ kevalaṃ kratvarthaḥ, phalaṃ tatkaraṇaṃ ca puruṣārthaḥ -- yathā jyotiṣṭomādiḥ, svargaśca || dadhyādi tūbhayarūpaṃ -- 'dadhnā juhuyāt' iti phalāsaṃyuktavākyena tvarthatvāt 'danendriyakāmasya juhuyāt' ityanena phalāya vidhānātpuruṣārthatvāt | taduktaṃ -- 'ekasya tūbhayarūpatve saṃyogapṛthaktvam' iti | saṃyogo vākyaṃ tasya pṛthaktvaṃ bhedaḥ | sa caikasya ubhayārthatve niyāmaka ityarthaḥ |
prayojakatvavicāraḥ
katvarthe prayājādau ṛtuḥ prayojakaḥ | puruṣārthe phalaṃ prayojakam | prayojakatvaṃ nāmānuṣṭhāpakatvamityarthaḥ | vidhiryadarthaṃ yadanuṣṭhāpayati sa tatra prayojakaḥ | darśādividhiḥ svargārthaṃ darśādikamanuṣṭhāpayatīti svargādirdarśādau prayojakaḥ | prayājādividhiḥ prayājāndarśādyarthaṃ anuṣṭhāpayatīti darśādiḥ prayājānāṃ prayojakaḥ | dadhyānayanavidhiḥ dadhyānayanamāmikṣārthamanuṣṭhāpayati, na vājinārtham | āmikṣārthaṃ dadhyānayanānuṣṭhāne sati vājinasya svataḥ -- siddhatvāt | ata āmikṣaiva dadhyānayane prayojikā, na vājinam |' 'puroḍāśakapālena tuṣānupavapati' iti tuṣopavāpāṅgatvena puroḍāśakapālaṃ vidhīyate, tathāpi tuṣopavāpaḥ kapālasya prayojako na bhavati, puroḍāśārthamupāttenaiva kapālena tuṣopavāpasiddheḥ, kiṃtu puroḍāśa eva kapālasya prayojaka ityādyūhyam | iti prameyādinirūpaṇam ||
kramanirūpaṇam
atha kramanirūpaṇam | nanu vidhinā sāṅgaṃ pradhānaṃ kartavyatayā codyate | tatrāṅgānāṃ pradhānānāṃ ca karmaṇāṃ bahutvātkrameṇānuṣṭhānaṃ vācyam | tatkramaniyāmakaṃ pramāṇaṃ kimiti cet, naḥ śrutyādīnāmeva kramaniyāmakatvāt |
śrutikramaḥ
tathā hi -- 'adhvaryugṛhapatiṃ dīkṣayitvā brahmāṇaṃ dīkṣayati' ityatra ktvā śrutyā gṛhapatidīkṣānantaraṃ brahmadīkṣeti śrautakramaḥ |
arthakramaḥ
'agnihotraṃ juhoti, yavāgūṃ pacati' ityatra yavāgūpākasya homārthatvena pākātpūrvaṃ homasya kartumaśaktyatvāt pāṭhakramaṃ tyaktvā'rthakramaḥ svīkartavyaḥ | arthaḥ prayojanaṃ homādirūpaṃ tadarthaṃ kramaḥ arthakramaḥ | tena pūrvaṃ pākaḥ paścāddhomaḥ |
pāṭhakramaḥ 'samidho yajati', 'tanūnapātaṃ yajati' ityādau vidhivākyapāṭhakamādeva samidādiyāgānuṣṭhānakramaḥ |
sthānakramaḥ tathā jyotiṣṭome aupavasathyamaharārabhya krameṇānuṣṭheyānāṃ amīṣomīyasavanīyānubandhyānāṃ paśūnāṃ trayāṇāṃ sādyaskākhye somayāge 'saha paśūnālabheta' ityekadā'nuṣṭhānalakṣaṇaṃ sāhityaṃ bodhitam, tadapi savanī -- yasya sthāne vihitam | tatra prākṛtakramaṃ parityajya savanīyasya sthāne sāhitya vidhānādādau savanīyapaśorupākaraṇam, tato'gnīṣomīyasya, tato’nubandhyāyāḥ iti sthānātkramaḥ | sutyādivasātpūrvamaharaupavasathyam |
mukhyakramaḥ
tathā darśe sāṃnāyyadharmāṇāṃ śākhācchedādīnāṃ pūrvam, āgneyadharmāṇāṃ nirvāpādīnāṃ ca anantaraṃ pravṛttāvapi mukhyayorāmeyasāṃnāyyayāgayomadhye āgneyayāgasya pūrvamanuṣṭhānānmukhyayāgakrameṇādāvāgneya puroḍāśasya prayojaśeṣābhighāraṇam, tataḥ payaso'bhighāraṇam iti mukhyayāgakramādabhighāraṇakramaḥ |
pravṛttikramaḥ
tathā 'saptadaśa prājāpatyān paśūnālabhate' ityatra saptadaśapaśudravyakaprajāpatidevatākāḥ saptadaśa yāgāḥ kartavyatvena bodhitāḥ, tatropokaraṇākhya ādyaḥ padārtho yataḥ kutaścidārabhya yatra vacit samāmanīyaḥ | niyojanādikaṃ tu yena krameṇa upākaraṇaṃ pravṛttaṃ tenaiva krameṇa kartavyam | katham -- prakṛtāvanīṣomīyapaśorekatvena upakaraṇamādau kṛtvā dvitīyakṣaṇa evaṃ niyojanam, tṛtīyakṣaṇa evaṃ prokṣaṇam, vyavadhānaprayojakābhāvāt | atra tu saptadaśapaśūnāṃ sahānuṣṭheyatvavacanātprathamato yatra kvacitpaśau kṛtamupākaraṇaṃ svāśrayakartavyaniyojanāya ṣoḍaśabhiḥ kṣaṇairvyavadhānaṃ sahate, na tvadhikam | tatropākaraṇakrameṇa niyojanaṃ prathamapaśāvakṛtvā paśvantare niyojanaṃ kṛtvā'nantaraṃ prathamapaśau niyojanakaraṇe ṣoḍaśakṣaṇādhikakṣaṇavyavadhānaṃ śāstrānanumatamāpadyeta | tannirāsāyopākaraṇaṃ yena krameṇa pravṛttaṃ tenaiva niyojanādikaṃ kāryamiti pravṛttiṃkramaḥ | ityevaṃ śrutyarthapāṭhasthānamukhyapravṛttikramaireva karmānuṣṭhānam | anyathānuṣṭhāne vaiguṇyamityalam ||
iti śrīkṛṣṇayajvakṛtā mīmāṃsāparibhāṣā samāptā ||
