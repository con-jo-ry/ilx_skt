sa ca parameśvara eko'pi svopādhibhūtamāyāniṣṭhasattvarajastamoguṇabhedena brahmaviṣṇumaheśvarādiśabdavācyatāṃ bhajate|
nanvīśvarasākṣiṇo'nāditve "tadaikṣata bahu syāṃ prajāyeya" ityādinā sṛṣṭipūrvasamaye parameśvarasyāgantukamīkṣaṇamucyamānaṃ kathamupapadyate| ucyate| yathā viṣayendriyasannikarṣādikāraṇavaśena jīvopādhyantaḥkaraṇasya vṛttibhedā jāyante, tathā sṛjyamānaprāṇikarmavaśena parameśvaropādhibhūtamāyāyā vṛttiviśeṣāḥ "idamidānīṃ sraṣṭavyam, idamidānīṃ pālayitavyam, idamidānīṃ saṃhartavyam" ityākārā jāyante| tāsāṃ ca vṛttīnāṃ sāditvāttatpratibimbitacaitanyamapi sādītyucyate|
evaṃ sākṣidvaividhyena pratyakṣajñānadvaividhyam| pratyakṣatvaṃ ca jñeyagataṃ ca nirūpitam|
tatra jñaptigatapratyakṣatvasya sāmānyalakṣaṇaṃ cittvameva| parvato vahnimānityādāvapi vahnyādyākāravṛttyupahitacaitanyasya svātmāṃśe svaprakāśatayā pratyakṣatvāt| tattadviṣayāṃśe pratyakṣatvaṃ tu pūrvoktameva|
tasya ca bhrāntirūpapratyakṣe nātivyāptiḥ| bhramapramāsādhāraṇapratyakṣatvasāmānyanirvacanena tasyāpi lakṣyatvāt| yadā tu pratyakṣapramāyā eva lakṣaṇaṃ vaktavyaṃ, tadā pūrvoktalakṣaṇe abādhitatvaṃ viṣayaviśeṣaṇaṃ jñeyam| śuktirūpyādibhramasya saṃsārakālīnabādhaviṣayaprātibhāsikarajatādiviṣayakatvenoktalakṣaṇābhāvānnātivyāptiḥ|
nanu visaṃvādipravṛttyā bhrāntijñānasiddhāvapi tasya prātibhāsikatatkālotpannarajatādiviṣayakatve na prāmāṇyaṃ, deśāntarīyarajatasya klṛptasyaiva tadviṣayatvasambhavāditi cet, na| tasyāsannikṛṣṭatayā pratyakṣaviṣayatvāyogāt| na ca jñānaṃ pratyāsattiḥ| jñānasya pratyāsattitve tata eva vahnyādeḥ pratyakṣatvāpattāvanumānādyucchedāpatteḥ|
nanu rajatotpādakānāṃ rajatāvayavādīnāmabhāve śuktau kathaṃ tavāpi rajatamutpadyata iti cet, ucyate| nahi lokasiddhasāmagrī prātibhāsikarajatotpādikā, kintu vilakṣaṇaiva| tathāhi -- kācādidoṣadūṣitalocanasya purovartidravyasaṃyogāt idamākārā cākacakyākārā kācidantaḥkaraṇavṛttirudeti| tasyāṃ ca vṛttāvidamaṃśāvacchinnaṃ caitanyaṃ pratibimbate| tatra pūrvoktarītyā vṛtterbahirnirgamanenedamaṃśāvacchinnacaitanyaṃ vṛttyavacchinnacaitanyaṃ pramātṛcaitanyaṃ cābhinnaṃ bhavati| tataśca pramātṛcaitanyābhinnaviṣayacaitanyaniṣṭhā śuktitvaprakārikā'vidyā cākacakyādisādṛśyasandarśanasamudbodhitarajatasaṃskārasadhrīcīnā kācādidoṣasamavahitā rajatarūpārthākāreṇa rajatajñānābhāsākāreṇa ca pariṇamate|
pariṇāmo nāmopādānasamasattākakāryyāpattiḥ| vivarto nāmopādānaviṣamasattākakāryāpattiḥ| prātibhāsikaṃ rajataṃ cāvidyāpekṣayā pariṇāmaḥ, caitanyāpekṣayā vivarta iti cocyate| avidyāpariṇāmasvarūpaṃ ca tadrajatam avidyādhiṣṭhāne idamavacchinnacaitanye varttate| asmanmate sarvasyāpi kāryyasya svopādānāvidyādhiṣṭhānāśritatvaniyamāt|
nanu caitanyaniṣṭharajatasya katham idaṃ rajatam iti purovartinā tādātmyam| ucyate| yathā nyāyamate ātmaniṣṭhasya sukhādeḥ śarīraniṣṭhatvenopalambhaḥ, śarīrasya sukhādyadhikaraṇatāvacchedakatvāt, tathā caitanyamātrasya rajataṃ pratyanadhiṣṭhānatayā idamavacchinnacaitanyasya tadadhiṣṭhānatvena idamavacchedakatayā rajatasya purovartisaṃsargapratyaya upapadyate| tasya ca viṣayacaitanyasya tadantaḥkaraṇopahitacaitanyābhinnatayā viṣayacaitanyādhyastamapi rajataṃ sākṣiṇyadhyastaṃ "kevalasākṣivedyam" "sukhādivadananyavedyam" iti cocyate|
nanu sākṣiṇyadhyastatve "ahaṃ rajatam" iti pratyayaḥ syāt, "ahaṃ sukhī" itivaditi cet, ucyate| nahi sukhādīnāmantaḥkaraṇāvacchinnacaitanyaniṣṭhāvidyākāryyatvaprayuktam "ahaṃ sukhī" iti jñānaṃ, sukhādīnāṃ ghaṭādivacchuddhacaitanye evādhyāsāt, kintu yasya yadākārānubhavāhitasaṃskārasahakṛtāvidyākāryyatvaṃ tasya tadākārānubhavaviṣayatvam ityevānugataṃ niyāmakam| tathā ca idamākārānubhavāhitasaṃskārasahakṛtāvidyākāryyatvāt ghaṭāderidamākārānubhavaviṣayatvam, ahamākārānubhavāhitasaṃskārasahakṛtāvidyākāryyatvāt antaḥkaraṇādeḥ ahamanubhavaviṣayatvaṃ, śarīrendriyādeḥ ubhayavidhānubhavāhitasaṃskārasahakṛtāvidyākāryyatvāt ubhayavidhānubhavaviṣayatvam| tathā cobhayavidhānubhavaḥ "idaṃ śrotram" "ahaṃ badhira" iti| prakṛte ca prātibhāsikarajatasya pramātṛcaitanyābhinnedamavacchinnacaitanyaniṣṭhāvidyākāryyatve'pi "idaṃ rajatam" iti satyasthalīyedamākārānubhavāhitasaṃskārajanyatvāt idamākārānubhavaviṣayatā, na tu ahaṃ rajatam ityahaṃkārānubhavaviṣayatetyanusandheyam|
nanvevamapi mithyārajatasya sākṣātsākṣisambandhatayā bhānasambhave rajatagocarajñānābhāsarūpāvidyāvṛtterabhyupagamaḥ kimartha iti cet -- ucyate| svagocaravṛttyupahitacaitanyabhinnasattākatvābhāvasya viṣayāparokṣarūpatayā rajatasyāparokṣatvasiddhaye tadabhyupagamāt|
nanvidaṃvṛtteḥ rajatākāravṛtteśca pratyekamekaikaviṣayatve gurumatavadviśiṣṭajñānābhyupagame kuto bhramajñānasiddhiriti cet -- na| vṛttidvayapratibimbitacaitanyasyaikasya satyamithyāvastutādātmyāvagāhitvena bhramatvasvīkārāt| ata eva sākṣijñānasya sattyāsattyaviṣayatayā pramāṇyāniyamādapramāṇyoktiḥ sāmpradāyikānām|
nanu siddhānte deśāntarīyarajatamapyavidyākāryyamadhyastaṃ ceti kathaṃ śuktirūpyasya tato vailakṣaṇyamiti cet -- na| tvanmate satyatvāviśeṣe'pi keṣāñcitkṣaṇikatvaṃ, keṣāñcit sthāyitvamityatra yadeva niyāmakaṃ tadeva svabhāvaviśeṣādikaṃ mamāpi|
yadvā ghaṭādyadhyāse'vidyaiva doṣatvena hetuḥ, śuktirūpyādhyāse tu kācādayo'pi doṣāḥ| tathā cāgantukadoṣajanyatvaṃ pratibhāsikatve prayojakam| ata eva svapnopalabdharathādīnāmāgantukanidrādoṣajanyatvātprātibhāsikatvam|
nanu svapnasthale pūrvānubhūtarathādeḥ smaraṇamātreṇaiva vyavahāropapattau na rathādisṛṣṭikalpanaṃ gauravāditi cet -- na| rathādeḥ smṛtimātrābhyupagame "rathaṃ paśyāmi" "svapne rathamadrākṣam" ityādyanubhavavirodhāpatteḥ| "atha rathān rathayogānpathaḥ sṛjate" iti rathādisṛṣṭipratipādakaśrutivirodhāpatteśca| tasmācchuktirūpyavatsvapnopalabdharathādayo'pi prātibhāsikāḥ yāvatpratibhāsamavatiṣṭhante|
nanu svapnarathādyadhiṣṭhānatayopalabhyamānadeśaviśeṣasyāpi tadā sannikṛṣṭatayā anirvacanīyaprātibhāsikadeśobhyupagantavyaḥ, tathā cādhyāsaḥ kutreti cet -- caitanyasya svayaṃprakāśasya rathādyadhiṣṭhānatvāt| pratīyamāno hi rathādiḥ asti ityeva pratīyata iti sadrūpeṇa prakāśamānaṃ caitanyamevādhiṣṭhānam| deśaviśeṣo'pi cidadhyayastaḥ prātibhāsikaḥ, rathādāvindriyagrāhyatvamapi prātibhāsikaṃ, tadā sarvendriyāṇāmuparamāt| ahaṃ gaja iti pratītyāpādanaṃ tu pūrvannirasanīyam|
svapnagajādayaḥ sākṣānmāyāpariṇāmā iti kecit| antaḥkaraṇadvārā tatpariṇāma tatpariṇāmā ityanye|
nanu gajādeḥ śuddhacaitanyādhyastatve idanīṃ tatsākṣātkārābhāvena jāgaraṇe'pi svapnolabdhagajādaya anuvarteran| ucyate| kāryyavināśo hi dvividhaḥ| kaścidupādānena saha, kaścittu vidyamāne evopādāne | ādyo bādhaḥ, dvitīyastu nivṛttiḥ| ādyasya kāraṇamadhiṣṭhānatattvasākṣātkāraḥ, tena vinopādānabhūtāyā avidyāyā anivṛtteḥ| dvitīye virodhivṛtyutpattirdoṣanivṛttiśca| tadiha brahmasātkārābhāvāt svāpnaprapañco mā bādhiṣṭha, musalaprahāreṇa ghaṭāderiva virodhipratyayāntarodayena svajanakībhūtanidrādidoṣanāśena vā gajādinivṛttau ko virodhaḥ| evaṃ ca śuktirūpyasya śuktyavacchinnacaitanyaniṣṭhatūlāvidyākāryyatvapakṣe śuktiriti jñānena tadajñānena saha rajatasya bādhaḥ|
mūlāvidyākāryyatvapakṣe tu mūlāvidyāyā brahmasākṣātkāramātranivarttyatayā tatra śuktijñānānnivṛttimātraṃ mudgaraprahāreṇa ghaṭasyeva |
nanu śuktau rajatasya pratibhāsamaye pratibhāsikasattābhyupagame "nedaṃ rajatam" iti traikālikaniṣedhajñānaṃ na syāt, kintu "idānīmidaṃ na rajatam" iti, "idānīṃ ghaṭaḥ śyāmo na" itivaditi cet, na | nahi tatra rajatattvāvacchinnapratiyogitākābhāvo niṣedhadhīviṣayaḥ| kintu laukikapāramārthikatvāvacchinnaprātibhāsikarajatapratiyogitākaḥ vyadhikaraṇadharmāvacchinnapratiyogitākābhāvābhyupagamāt|
nanu prātibhāsike rajate pāramārthikatvamavagataṃ na vā| anavagate pratiyogitāvacchedakāvacchinnajñānābhāvādabhāvapratyakṣānupapattiḥ| avagate aparokṣāvabhāsasasya tātkālikaviṣayasattāniyatatvāt|
rajate pāramārthikatvamapyanirvacanīyaṃ rajatavadevotpannamiti tadavacchinnarajatasattve tadavacchinnābhāvastatra kathaṃ vartata iti cet -- na| pāramārthikatvasyādhiṣṭhānaniṣṭhasya rajate pratibhāsambhavena rajataniṣṭhapāramārthikatvotpattyanabhyupagamāt| yatrāropyamasannikṛṣṭaṃ tatraiva prātibhāsikavastūtpatteraṅgīkārāt| ata evendriyasannikṛṣṭatayā japākusumagatalauhityotpattiḥ|
nanvevaṃ yatra japākusumaṃ dravyāntaravyavadhānādasannikṛṣṭaṃ tatra lauhityapratītyā prātibhāsikaṃ lauhityaṃ svīkriyatāmiti cet, na, iṣṭatvāt| evaṃ pratyakṣabhramāntareṣvapi pratyakṣasāmānyalakṣaṇānugame yathārthapratyakṣalakṣaṇasadbhāvaśca darśanīyaḥ|
uktaṃ pratyakṣaṃ prakārāntareṇa dvividhaṃ indriyajanyaṃ tadajanyaṃ ceti | tatrendriyājanyaṃ sukhājipratyakṣaṃ manasa indriyajanyaṃ sukhādipratyakṣaṃ manasa indriyatvanirākaraṇāt|
indriyāṇi pañca ghrāṇarasanacakṣuḥśrotratvagātmakāni| sarvāṇi cendriyāṇi svasvaviṣayasaṃyuktānyeva pratyakṣajñānaṃ janayanti| tatra ghrāṇarasanatvagātmakānīndriyāṇi svasthānasthitānyeva gandharasasparśopalambhān janayanti| cakṣuḥśrotre tu svata eva viṣayadeśaṃ gatvā svasvaviṣayaṃ gṛhṇītaḥ| śrotrasyāpi cakṣurādivatparicchinnatayā bheryādideśagamanasambhavāt| ata evānubhavo "bherīśabdo mayā śruta" iti| vīcitaraṅgādinyāyena karṇaśaṣkulīpradeśe'nantaśabdotpattikalpanāyā gauravaṃ, bherīśabdo mayā śruta iti pratyakṣasya bhramatvakalpanāgauravaṃ ca syāt|
tadevaṃ vyākhyātaṃ pratyakṣam|
iti śrīmadvedāntaparibhāṣāyāḥ pratyakṣaparicchedaḥ||2||

athānumānaṃ nirūpyate| anumitikaraṇamanumānam| anumitiśca vyāptijñānatvena vyāptijñānajanyā| vyāptijñānānuvyavasāyādestattvena tajjanyatvābhāvāt nānumititvam| anumitikaraṇañca vyāptijñānam, tatsaṃskāro'vāntaravyāpāraḥ| na tu tṛtīyaliṅgaparāmarśo'numitau karaṇaṃ, tasyānumitihetutvāsiddhyā tatkaraṇatvasya dūranirastatvāt| na ca saṃskārajanyatvenānumiteḥ smṛtitvāpattiḥ, smṛtiprāgabhāvajanyatvasya saṃskāramātrajanyatvasya vā smṛtitvaprayojakatayā saṃskāradhvaṃsasādhāraṇasaṃskārajanyatvasya tadaprayojakatvāt| na ca yatra vyāptismaraṇādanumatistatra kathaṃ saṃskāro heturiti vācyam| vyāptismṛtimūle'pi tatsaṃskārasyaivānumitihetutvāt| nahi smṛteḥ saṃskāranāśakatvaniyamaḥ, smṛtidhārādarśanāt| na cānudbuddhasaṃskārādapyanumityāpattiḥ, tadudbodhasyāpi sahakāritvāt|
evaṃ cāyaṃ dhūmavāniti pakṣadharmatājñānena dhūmo vahnivyāpya ityanubhavāhitasaṃskārodbodhe ca sati vahnimānityanumitirbhavati, na tu madhye vyāptismaraṇaṃ tajjanyaṃ vahnivyāpyadhūmavānayamityādiviśeṣaṇaviśiṣṭajñānaṃ vā hetutvena kalpanīyaṃ, gauravānmānābhāvācca|
tacca vyāptijñānaṃ vahniviṣayakajñānāṃśa eva karaṇaṃ, na tu parvataviṣayajñānaṃ prati, parvato vahnimāniti jñānasya vahnyaṃśa evānumititvaṃ na parvatāṃśe, tadaṃśe pratyakṣatvasyopapāditatvāt|
vyāptiścāśeṣasādhanāśrayāśritasādhyasamānādhikaraṇyarūpā|
sā ca vyabhicārajñāne sati sahacāradarśanena gṛhyate| tacca sahacāradarśanaṃ bhūyodarśanaṃ sakṛddarśanaṃ veti viśeṣo nādaraṇīyaḥ, sahacāradarśanasyaiva prayojakatvāt|
taccānumānamanvayirūpameva, na tu kevalānvayi| sarvasyāpi dharmasyāsmanmate brahmaniṣṭhātyantābhāvapratiyogitvena, atyantābhāvāpratiyogisādhyakatvarūpakevalānvayitvasyāsiddheḥ|
nāpyanumānasya vyatirekirūpatvaṃ, sādhyābhāve sādhanābhāvanirūpitavyāptijñānasya sādhanena sādhyānumitāvanupayogāt|
kathaṃ tarhi dhūmādāvanvayavyāptimaviduṣo'pi vyatirekavyāptijñānadanumitiḥ? arthāpattipramāṇāditi vakṣyāmaḥ| ata evānumāsya nānvavyatirekirūpatvam| vyatirekavyāptijñānasyānumityahetutvāt|
taccānumānaṃ svārthaparārthabhedena dvividham, tatra svārthaṃ tūktameva| parārthaṃ tu nyāyasādhyam| nyāyo nāma avayavasamudāyaḥ|
avayavāśca traya eva pratijñāhetūdāharaṇarūpā, udāharaṇopanayanigamanarūpā vā| na tu pañcāvayavarūpāḥ, avayavatrayeṇaiva vyāptipakṣadharmatayorupadarśanasambhavenādhikāvayavadvayasya vyarthatvāt|
evam anumāne nirūpite tasmād brahmabhinnanikhilaprapañcasya mithyātvasiddhiḥ| tathā hi -- brahmabhinnaṃ sarvaṃ mithyā, brahmabhinnatvāt, yadevaṃ tadevaṃ, yathā śuktirupyam| na ca dṛṣṭāntāsiddhiḥ, tasya sādhitatvāt| na cāprayojakatvaṃ, śuktirūpyarajjusarpādīnāṃ mithyātve brahmabhinnatvasyaiva lādhavena prayojakatvāt| mithyātvaṃ ca svāśrayatvenābhimatayāvanniṣṭhātyantābhāvapratiyogitvam| abhimatapadamasambhavavāraṇāya| yāvatpadamarthāntaravāraṇāya| taduktam -- 
sarveṣāmapi bhāvānāṃ svāśrayatvena sammate|
pratiyogitvamatyantābhāvaṃ prati mṛṣātmatā|| iti|
yadvā -- ayaṃ paṭaḥ etattantuniṣṭhātyantābhāvapratiyogī, paṭatvātpaṭāntaravadityādyanumānaṃ mithyātve pramāṇam| taduktam -- 
aṃśinaḥ svāṃśagātyantābhāvasya pratiyoginaḥ|
aṃśitvāditarāṃśivaddigeṣaiva guṇādiṣu|| iti|
na ca ghaṭādermithyātve san ghaṭa iti pratyakṣabādhaḥ. adhiṣṭhānabrahmasattāyāstatra viṣayatayā ghaṭādeḥ satyatvāsiddheḥ| na ca nīrūpasya brahmaṇaḥ kathaṃ cākṣuṣādijñānaviṣayateti vācyam| nīrūpasyāpi rūpādeḥ pratyakṣatvāt| na ca nīrūpasya dravyasya cakṣurādyayogyatvamiti niyamaḥ, manmate brahmaṇo dravyatvāsiddheḥ| guṇāśrayatvaṃ samavāyikāraṇatvaṃ vā dravyatvamiti te'bhimatam, nahi nirguṇasya brahmaṇo guṇāśrayatā, nāpi samavāyikāraṇatā, samavāyāsiddheḥ| astu vā dravyatvaṃ brahmaṇaḥ, tathāpi nīrūpasya kālasyeva cākṣuṣādijñānaviṣayatve na virodhaḥ|
yadvā trividhaṃ sattvam| pāramārthikasattvaṃ brahmaṇaḥ, vyāvahārikaṃ sattvamākāśādeḥ, pratibhāsikaṃ sattvaṃ śuktirajatādeḥ| tathāca ghaṭaḥ sanniti pratyakṣasya vyāvahārikasattvaviṣayatvena prāmāṇyam| asmin pakṣe ca ghaṭāderbrahmaṇi niṣedho na svarūpeṇa, kintu pāramārthikatvenaiveti na virodhaḥ | asmin pakṣe ca mithyātvalakṣaṇe pāramārthikatvāvacchinnapratiyogitākatvamatyantābhāvaviśeṣaṇaṃ draṣṭavyam| tasmādupapannaṃ mithyātvānumānamiti|
iti śrīmadvedāntaparibhāṣāyāmanumānaparicchedaḥ||2||

athopamānaṃ nirapyate| tatra sādṛśyapramākaraṇamipamānam| tathā hi prāṅgaṇeṣu dṛṣṭagopiṇḍasya puruṣasya vanaṃ gatasya gavayendrisannikarṣe sati bhavati pratītiḥ ayaṃ piṇḍo gosadṛśa iti| tadanantaraṃ ca bhavati niścayo'nena sadṛśī madīyā gauriti| tatrāvayavyatirekābhyāṃ gavayaniṣṭhagosādṛśyajñānaṃ karaṇaṃ, goniṣṭhagavayasādṛśyajñānaṃ phalam| na cedaṃ pratyakṣeṇa sambhavati | gopiṇḍasya tadendriyāsannikarṣāt, nāpyanumānena gavayaniṣṭhasādṛśyasyātalliṅgatvāt| nāpi "madīyā gauḥ etadgavayasadṛśī, etanniṣṭhasādṛśyapratiyogitvāt, yo yadgatasādṛśyapratiyogī sa tatsadṛśaḥ, yathā maitraniṣṭhasādṛśyapratiyogī caitraḥ maitrasadṛśa" ityanumānāttatsambhava iti vācyam| evaṃvidhānumānānavatāre'pyanena sadṛśī madīyā gaurīti pratīteranubhavasiddhatvāt| upaminomītyanuvyavasāyācca| tasmādupamānaṃ mānāntaram| iti vedāntaparibhāṣāyāmupamānaparicchedaḥ||3||

athāgamo nirūpyate| yasya vākyasya tātparyaviṣayībhūtasaṃsargo mānāntareṇa na bādhyate tadvākyaṃ pramāṇam|
vākyajanye ca jñāne ākāṅkṣāyogyatā''sattayastātparyyajñānaṃ ceti catvāri kāraṇāni|

etādṛśākāṅkṣābhiprāyeṇaiva balābalādhikaraṇe "sā vaiśvadevyā''mikṣā vājibhyo vājinam" ityatra vaiśvadevayāgasyāmikṣānvitatvena na vājinākāṅkṣetyādivyavahāraḥ| nanu tatrāpi vājinasya jijñāsā'viṣayatve'pi tadyogyatvamastyeva, pradeyadravyatvasya yāganirūpitajijñāsāviṣayatāyogyatāvacchedakatvāditi cet -- na| svasamānajātīyapradārthānvayabodhavirahasahakṛtapradeyadravyatvasyaiva tadavacchedakatvena vājinadravyasya svasamānajātīyānvayabodhasahakṛtatvena tādṛśāvacchedakatvābhāvāt, āmikṣāyāṃ tu naivaṃ, vājinānvayasya tadānupasthānāt|
udāharaṇāntareṣvapi durbalaprayojaka ākāṅkṣāviraha eva draṣṭavyaḥ|
yogyatā ca tātparyyaviṣayasaṃsargābādhaḥ| vahninā siñcedityādau tādṛśasaṃsargabādhānnātivyāptiḥ| "sa prajāpatirātmano vapāmudakhidat" ityādāvapi tātparyyaviṣayībhūtapaśu(vapāyāga)prāśastyābādhād yogyatā| tattvamasyādivākyeṣvapi vācyābhedabādhe'pi lakṣyasvarūpābhede bādhābhāvādyogyatā|
āsattiścāvyavadhānena padajanyapadārthopasthitiḥ| mānāntareṇopasthitāpitapadārthasyānvayabodhābhāvāt padajanyeti| ata evāśrutapadārthasthale tattatpadādhyāhāraḥ, dvāramityādau ‘pidhehi' iti| ata eva "iṣe tvā" ityādau "chinadbhi" iti padādhyāhāraḥ| ata eva vikṛtiṣu "sūryyāya juṣṭaṃ nirvapāmi" iti padaprayogaḥ|
padārthaśca dvividhaḥ śakyo lakṣyaśceti| tatra śaktirnāma padānāmartheṣu mukhyā vṛttiḥ| yathā ghaṭapadasya pṛthubudhnodarādyākṛtiviśiṣṭe vastuviśeṣe vṛttiḥ| sā ca śaktiḥ padārthāntaram, siddhānte kāraṇeṣu kāryyānukūlaśaktimātrasya padārthāntaratvāt| sā ca tattatpadajanyapadārthajñānarūpakāryyānumeyā| tādṛśaśaktiviṣayatvaṃ śakyatvam| tacca jātereva, na vyakteḥ, vyaktīnāmānantyena gurutvāt| kathaṃ tarhi gavādipadād vyaktibhānamiti cet, jātervyaktisamānasaṃvitsaṃvedyatayeti brūmaḥ|
yadvā gavādipadānāṃ vyaktau śaktiḥ svarūpasatī, na tu jñātā, jātau tu sā jñātā hetuḥ| na ca vyaktyaṃśe śaktijñānamapi kāraṇaṃ, gauravāt| jātiśaktimattvajñāne sati vyaktiśaktijñānaṃ vinā vyaktidhīvilambābhāvācca| ata eva nyāyamate'pi anvaye śaktiḥ svarūpasatīti siddhāntaḥ| jñāyamānaśaktiviṣayatvameva vācyatvamiti jātireva vācyā|
athavā vyakterlakṣaṇayāvagamaḥ| yathā nīlo ghaṭa ityatra nīlaśabdasya nīlaguṇaviśiṣṭe lakṣaṇā, tathā jātivācakasya tadviśiṣṭe lakṣaṇā| taduktam -- ananyalabhyaḥ padārtha iti| evaṃ śakyārtho nirūpitaḥ|
atha lakṣyapadārtho nirūpyate| tatra lakṣaṇāviṣayo lakṣyaḥ| lakṣaṇā dvividhā, kevalalakṣaṇā lakṣitalakṣaṇā ceti| tatra śakyasākṣātsambandhaḥ kevalalakṣaṇā| yathā gaṅgāyāṃ ghoṣa ityatra pravāhasākṣātsambandhini tīre gaṅgāpadasya kevalalakṣaṇā| yatra śakyaparamparāsambandhenārthāntarapratītiḥ. tatra lakṣitalakṣaṇā| yathā dvirephapadasya rephadvayaśaktasya bhramarapadaghaṭitaparamparāsambandhena madhukare vṛttiḥ|
gauṇyapi lakṣitalakṣaṇaiva| yathā siṃho māṇavaka ityatra siṃhaśabdavācyasambandhikrauryyādisambandhena māṇavakasya pratīteḥ|
prakārāntareṇa lakṣaṇā trividhā -- jahallakṣaṇā'jahallakṣaṇā jahadajahallakṣaṇā|
śakyamanantarbhāvya yatrārthāntarasya pratītistatra jahallakṣaṇā| yathā viṣaṃ bhukṣveti| atra hi svārthaṃ vihāya śatrugṛhe bhojananivṛttirlakṣitā| yatra śakyārthamantarbhāvyaivārthāntarapratītistatrājahallakṣaṇā| yathā śuklo ghaṭa iti| atra hi śuklaśabdaḥ svārthaṃ śuklaguṇamantarbhāvyaiva tadti dravye lakṣaṇayā vartate| yatra hi viśiṣṭavācakaśabdaḥ svārthaikadeśaṃ vihāyaikadeśe vartate, tatra jahadajahallakṣaṇā| yathā so'yaṃ devadatta iti| atra hi padadvayavācyayorviśiṣṭayorekyānupapattyā padadvayasya viśeṣyamātraparatvam| yathā vā tattvamasi ityādau tatpadavācyasya sarvajñatvādiviśiṣṭasya tvaṃpadavācyenāntaḥkaraṇaviśiṣṭenaikyogādaikyasiddhyarthaṃ svarūpe lakṣaṇeti sāmpradāyikāḥ|
vayaṃ tu brūmaḥ| so'yaṃ devadattastattvamasītyādau viśiṣṭavācakānāṃ padānāmekadeśaparatve'pi na lakṣaṇā, śaktyupasthitaviśiṣṭayorabhedānvayānupapattau viśeṣyayoḥ śaktyupasthitayorevānvayabodhāvirodhāt| yathā ghaṭo'nitya ityatra ghaṭapadavācyaikadeśaghaṭatvasyāyogyatve'pi yogyaghaṭavyaktyā sahānityatvānvayaḥ| yatra padārthaikadeśasya viśeṣaṇatayopasthitiḥ tatraiva svātantryeṇopasthitaye lakṣaṇābhyupagamaḥ, yathā nityo ghaṭa ityatra ghaṭapadād ghaṭatvasya śaktyā svātantryeṇānupasthityā tādṛśopasthityarthaṃ ghaṭapadasya ghaṭatve lakṣaṇā| evameva tattvamasyādivākye'pi na lakṣaṇā, śaktyā svātantryeṇopasthitayostattvaṃpadārthayorabhedānvaye bādhakābhāvāt. anyathā gehe ghaṭaḥ, ghaṭe rūpaṃ, ghaṭamānayetyādau ghaṭatvagehatvāderabhimatānvayabodhayogyatayā tatrāpi ghaṭādipadānāṃ viśeṣyamātraparatvaṃ lakṣaṇayaiva syāt| tasmāttattvamasīti vākye ācāryyāṇāṃ lakṣaṇoktirabhyupagamavādena bodhyā|
jahadajahallakṣaṇodāharaṇaṃ tu kākebhyo dadhi rakṣyatāmityādikameva| tatra śakyakākaparityāgenāśakyadadhyupaghātakatvapuraskāreṇākāke kāke ca kākaśabdasya pravṛtteḥ|
lakṣaṇābījaṃ tu tātparyyānupapattireva, na tvanvayānupapattiḥ| kākebhyo dadhi rakṣatāmityatrānvayānupapattyabhāvāt| gaṅgāyāṃ ghoṣa ityādau tātparyyānupapatterapi sambhavāt|
lakṣaṇā ca na padamātravṛttiḥ kintu vākyavṛttirapi, yathā gambhīrāyāṃ nadyāṃ ghoṣa ityatra gambhīrāyāṃ nadyāmiti padadvayasamudāyasya tīre lakṣaṇā|
nanu vākyasyāśaktyā kathaṃ śakyasambandharūpā lakṣaṇā| ucyate| śaktyā yatpadasambandhena jñāpyate tatsambandho lakṣaṇā| śaktijñāpyaśca yathā padārthaḥ tathā vākyārtho'pīti na kācidanupapattiḥ| evamarthavādavākyānāṃ praśaṃsārūpāṇāṃ prāśastye lakṣaṇā| ‘so'rodīt' ityādinindārthavākyānāṃ ninditatve lakṣaṇā| arthavādagatapadānāṃ prāśastye lakṣaṇābhyupagamo ekena padena lakṣaṇayā tadupasthitisambhave padāntaravaiyarthyaṃ syāt|
evaṃ ca vidhyapekṣitaprāśastyarūpapadārthapratyāyakatayā arthavādapadasamudāyasya padasthānīyatayā vidhipadenaikavākyatvaṃ bhavatītyarthavādavākyānāṃ padaikavākyatā|
kva tarhi vākyaikavākyatā| yatra pratyekaṃ bhinnabhinnasaṃsargapratipādakayorvākyayorākāṅkṣāvaśena mahāvākyārthabodhakatvaṃ tatra vākyaikavākyatā| yathā ‘darśapūrṇamāsābhyāṃ svargakāmo yajet' ityādivākyānāṃ ‘samidho yajati' ityādivākyānāṃ ca parasparāpekṣitāṅgāṅgibhāvabodhakatayaikavākyatā| taduktaṃ bhaṭṭapādaiḥ -- 
svārthabodhe samāptānāmaṅgāṅgitvādyapekṣayā|
vākyānāmekavākyatvaṃ punaḥ saṃhatya jāyate||iti||

sā ca (sattayā) śābdabodhe hetuḥ, tathaivānvayavyatirekadarśanāt| evaṃ mahāvākyārthabodhe'vāntaravākyārthabodho hetuḥ, tathaivānvayādyavadhāraṇāt|
kramaprāptaṃ tātparyyaṃ nirūpyate| tatra tatpratītīcchayoccaritatvaṃ na tātparyyam, arthajñānaśūnyena puruṣeṇoccāritādvedādarthābhānaprasaṅgāt| ayamadhyāpako'vyutpanna iti viśeṣadarśanena tātparyyabhramasyāpyabhāvāt| na ceśvaratātparyyajñānāttatra śābdabodha iti vācyam| īśvarānaṅgīkarturapi tadvākyārthapratipattidarśanāt | ucyate| tatpratītijananayogyatvaṃ tātparyyam| gehe ghaṭa iti vākyaṃ gehe ghaṭasaṃsargapratītijananayogyaṃ na tu paṭasaṃsargapratītijananayogyamiti tadvākyaṃ ghaṭasaṃsargaparaṃ, na tu paṭasaṃsargaparamiti vyapadiśayate|
nanu saindhavamānayetyādi vākyaṃ yadā lavaṇānayanapratītīcchayā prayuktaṃ tadāpyaśvasaṃsargapratītijanane svarūpayogyatāsattvāt lavaṇaparatvadaśāyāmapyaśvādisaṃsargajñānāpattiriti cet -- na| taditarapratītīcchayānuccaritatvasyāpi tātparyyaṃ prati viśeṣaṇatvāt| yathā ca yadvākyaṃ yatpratītijananayogyatve sati yadanyapratītīcchayānuccaritaṃ tadvākyaṃ tatsaṃsargaparamityucyate| śukādivākye'vyutpannoccāritavedavākyādau ca tatpratītīcchāyā evābhāvena tadanyapratītīcchayoccaritatvābhāvena lakṣaṇasattvānnāvyāptiḥ| na cobhayapratītīcchayoccarite'vyāptiḥ, tadanyamātrapratītīcchāyānuccaritatvasya vivakṣitatvāt| uktapratītimātrajananayogyatāyāścāvacchedikā śaktiḥ| asmākaṃ tu mate sarvatra kāraṇatāyāḥ śakterevāvacchedakatvāt iti na ko'pi doṣaḥ|
evaṃ tātparyyasya tatpratītijanakatvarūpasya śābdajñānajanakatve siddhe caturthavarṇake tātparyasya śābdajñānahetutvanirākaraṇavākyaṃ tatpratītīcchayoccaritatvarūpatātparyyanirākaraṇaparam, anyathā tātparyyaniścayaphalakavedāntavicāravaiyarthyaprasaṅgāt|
kecittu śābdajñānatvāvacchedena na tātparyyajñānaṃ heturityevaṃparaṃ caturthavarṇakavākyam| tātparyyasaṃśayaviparyayottaraśābdajñānaviśeṣe ca tātparyyajñānaṃ hetureva, idaṃ vākyametatparamutānyaparamiti tātparyyasaṃśaye tadviparyaye ca taduttaravākyārthaniścayasya tātparyyaniścayaṃ vinānupapatterityāhuḥ|
tacca tātparyyaṃ vede mīmāṃsāpariśodhitanyāyādevāvadhāryyate| loke tu prakaraṇādinā|
tatra laukikavākyānāṃ mānāntarāvagatārthatayānuvādakatvam|vede tu vākyārthasyāpūrvatayā nānuvādakatvam|
tatra loke vede ca kāryyaparāṇāmiva siddhārthānāmapyapūrvatayā prāmāṇyam, putraste jātaḥ, kanyā te garbhiṇī ityādiṣu siddhārtheṣvapi padānāṃ sāmarthyāvadhāraṇāt| ata eva vedāntavākyānāṃ brahmaṇi prāmāṇyam| yathā caitattathā viṣayaparicchede vakṣyate|
tatra vedāntānāṃ nityasarvajñaparameśvarapratītatvena prāmāṇyamiti naiyāyikāḥ| vedānāṃ nityatvena nirastasamastapuṃdūṣaṇatayā prāmāṇyamiti mīmāṃsakāḥ| asmākaṃ tu mate vedo na nityaḥ, utpattimattvāt| utpattimattvaṃ ca "asya mahato bhūtasya niḥśvasitametadṛgvedo yajurvedaḥ sāmavedo'tharvavedaḥ" ityādiśruteḥ| nāpi vedānāṃ trikṣaṇāvasthāyitvam| ya eva vedo devadattenādhītaḥ sa eva mayāpītyādipratyabhijñāvirodhāt| ata eva gakārādivarṇānāmapi na kṣaṇikatvam, so'yaṃ gakāra iti pratyabhijñāvirodhāt| tathā ca varṇapadavākyasamudāyasya vedasya viyadādivatsṛṣṭikālīnotpattikatvaṃ pralayakālīnadhvaṃsapratiyogitvaṃ ca, na tu madhye varṇānāmutpattivināśau, anantagakārakalpanāyāṃ gauravāt| anuccāraṇadaśāyāṃ varṇānāmanabhivyaktistaduccāraṇarūpavyañjakābhāvānna virudhyate, andhakārasthaghaṭānupalambhavad| utpanno gakāra ityādipratyakṣaṃ tu so'yaṃ gakāra ityādipratyabhijñāvirodhāt apramāṇaṃ, varṇābhivyañjakadhvanigatotpattinirūpitaparamparāsambandhaviṣayatvena pramāṇaṃ vā| tasmānna vedānāṃ kṣaṇikatvam| nanu kṣaṇikatvābhāve'pi viyadādiprapañcavadutpattimattvena parameśvarakartṛkatayā pauruṣeyatvasiddhāvapauruṣeyatvaṃ vedānāmiti tavāpi siddhānto bhajyeteti cet -- na| nahi tāvatpuruṣeṇoccāryyamāṇatvaṃ pauruṣeyatvam, gurumate'pi adhyāpakaramparayā pauruṣeyatvāpatteḥ| nāpi puruṣādhīnotpattikatvaṃ, naiyāyikābhimatapauruṣeyatvānumāne asmadādinā siddhasādhanāpatteḥ| kintu sajātīyoccāraṇānapekṣoccāraṇaviṣayatvaṃ pauruṣeyatvam| tathā ca sargādyakāle parameśvaraḥ pūrvasargasiddhavedānupūrvīsamānānupūrvīkaṃ vedaṃ viracitavān, na tu tadvijātīyaṃ vedamiti na sajātīyoccāraṇānapekṣoccāraṇaviṣayatvaṃ pauruṣeyatvam|
bhāratādīnāṃ tu sajātīyoccāraṇamanapekṣyaivoccāraṇamiti teṣāṃ pauruṣeyatvam| evaṃ pauruṣeyāpauruṣeyabhedenāgamo dvividho nirūpitaḥ|

ityādismṛtyā ca jīvaparabhedasyāvagatatvena tattvamasyādivākyam "ādityo yūpaḥ" "yajamānaḥ prastaraḥ" ityādivākyavadupacaritārthameveti cet -- na| bhedapratyakṣasya sambhāvitakaraṇadoṣasyāsambhāvitadoṣavedajanyajñānena bādhyamānatvāt, anyathā candragatādhikaparimāṇagrāhijyotiḥśāstrasya candraprādeśagrāhipratyakṣeṇa bādhāpatteḥ| pākarakte ghaṭe "rakto'yaṃ na śyāma" itivat saviśeṣaṇe hīti nyāyena jīvaparabhedagrāhipratyakṣasya viśeṣaṇībhūtadharmabhedaviṣayatvācca| ata eva nānumānamapi pramāṇam, āgamavirodhāt, merupāṣāṇamayatvānumānavat| nāpyāgamāntaravirodhaḥ, tatparātatparavākyayostatparavākyasya balavattvena lokasiddhabhedānuvādidvāsuparṇādivākyāpekṣayā upakramopasaṃhārādyavagatādvaitatātparyyaviśiṣṭasya tattvamasyādivākyasya prabalatvāt|
na ca jīvaparaikye viruddhadharmāśrayatvānupapattiḥ| śītasyaiva jalasyaupādhikoṣṇyāśrayatvavatsvabhāvato nirguṇasyaiva tasyāntaḥkaraṇādyaupādhikakartṛtvādyāśrayatvapratibhāsopapatteḥ| yadi ca jalādāvauṣṇyamāropitaṃ, tadā prakṛte'pi tulyam| na ca siddhānte kartṛtvasya kvacidapyabhāvāt āropyapramāhitasaṃskārābhāve kathamāropa iti vācyam| lāghavenāropyaviṣayasaṃskāratvenaiva tasya hetutvāt| na ca prāthamikārope kā gatiḥ? kartṛtvādyadhyāsapravāhasyānāditvāt| tattvaṃpadavācyayorviśiṣṭayoraikyāyogyatve'pi lakṣyasvarūpayoḥ aikyamupapāditameva| ata eva tatpratipādakatattvamasyādivākyānām akhaṇḍārthatvaṃ, so'yamityādivākyavat| na ca kāryyaparāṇāmeva prāmāṇyam, "caitra putraste jātaḥ" ityādau siddhe'pi saṅgatigrahāt| evaṃ sarvapramāṇāviruddhaṃ śrutismṛtītihāsapurāṇapratipādyaṃ jīvaparaikyaṃ vedāntaśāstrasya viṣaya iti siddham| iti viṣayaparicchedaḥ|

idānīṃ prayojanaṃ nirūpyate| yadavagataṃ sat svavṛttitayeṣyate tatprayojanam| tacca dvividhaṃ -- mukhyaṃ gauṇaṃ ceti| tatra sukhaduḥkhābhāvau mukhye prayojane| tadanyatarasādhanaṃ gauṇaṃ prayojanam| sukhaṃ ca dvividhaṃ -- satiśayaṃ niratiśayaṃ ceti| tatra sātiśayaṃ sukhaṃ viṣayānuṣaṅgajanitāntaḥkaraṇavṛttitāratamyakṛtānandaleśāvirbhāvaviśeṣaḥ| "etasyaivānandasyānyāni bhūtāni mātrāmupajīvanti" iti śruteḥ|
niratiśayaṃ sukhaṃ ca brahmaiva| "ānando brahmeti vyajānāt" "vijñānamānandaṃ brahma" ityādiśruteḥ| ānandātmakabrahmāvāptiśca mokṣaḥ śokanivṛttiśca| "brahmavidbrahmaiva bhavati" "tarati śokamātmavit" ityādiśruteḥ| na tu lokāntarāvāptiḥ, tajjanyavaiṣayikānando vā mokṣaḥ| tasya kṛtakatvenānityatve muktasya punarāvṛttyāpatteḥ|
nanu tvanmatepyānandāvāpteranarthanivṛtteśca sāditve tulyadoṣaḥ, anāditve mokṣamaddiśya śravaṇādau pravṛttyanupapattiriti cet -- na| siddhasyaiva brahmarūpasya mokṣasyāsiddhatvabhrameṇa tatsādhane pravṛttyupapatteranarthanivṛttirapyadhiṣṭhānabhūtabrahmasvarūpatayā siddhaiva| loke'pi prāptaprāptiparihṛtaparihārayoḥ prayojanatvaṃ dṛṣṭameva| yathā hastagatavismṛtasuvarṇādau, tava haste suvarṇamityāptopadeśādaprāptamiva prāpnoti| yathā valayitacaraṇāyāṃ sraji sarpatvabhramavataḥ puṃso nāyaṃ sarpa ityāptavākyātparihṛtasyaiva sarpasya parihāraḥ prasiddhaḥ| evaṃ prāptasyāpyānandasya prāptiḥ, parihṛtasyaiva sarpasya parihāraḥ prasiddhaḥ| evaṃ prāptasyāpyānandasya prāptiḥ, parihṛtasyānarthasya ca nivṛttirmokṣaḥ prayojanam| sa ca jñānaikasādhyaḥ| "tameva viditvā'timṛtyumeti nānyaḥ panthā vidyate'yanāya" iti śruteḥ, ajñānanivṛtterjñānaikasādhyatvaniyamācca| tacca jñānaṃ brahmātmaikyagocaram "abhayaṃ vai janaka prāpto'si tadātmānamevāvedahaṃ brahmāsmi" iti śruteśca, "tattvamasyādivākyotthaṃ jñānaṃ mokṣasya sādhanam" iti nāradīyavacanācca| tacca jñānamaparokṣarūpaṃ, parokṣatve'parokṣabhramanivartakatvānupapatteḥ|
tacca aparokṣajñānaṃ tattvamasyādivākyāditi kecit| manananididhyāsanasaṃskṛtāntaḥkaraṇādevetyapare| tatra pūrvācāryyāṇāmayamāśayaḥ -- saṃvidāparokṣyaṃ na karaṇaviśeṣotpattinibandhanaṃ, kintu prameyaviśeṣanibandhanamityupapāditam| tathā ca brahmaṇaḥ pramātṛjīvābhinnatayā tadgocaraṃ śabdajanyaṃ jñānamapyaparokṣam| ata eva pratardanādhikaraṇe pratardanaṃ prati "prāṇo'smi prajñātmā taṃ māmāyuramṛtamupāssva" itīndraproktavākye prāṇaśabdasya brahmaparatve niścite sati "māmupāsva" ityasmacchabdānupapattimāśaṅkya taduttaratvena pravṛtte " śāstradṛṣṭyā tūpadeśo vāmadevavat" ityatra sūtre ‘śāstrīyā dṛṣṭiḥ śāstradṛṣṭiḥ' iti tattvamasyādivākyajanyam ‘ahaṃ brahma' iti jñānaṃ śāstradṛṣṭiśabdenoktamiti|
aneṣāṃ tvevamāśayaḥ -- karaṇaviśeṣanibandhanameva jñānānāṃ pratyakṣatvaṃ, na viṣayaviśeṣanibandhanam, ekasminneva sūkṣmavastuni paṭukaraṇāpaṭukaraṇayoḥ pratyakṣatvaapratyakṣatvavyavahāradarśanāt| tathā ca saṃvitsākṣāttve indriyajanyatvasyaiva prayojakatayā, na śabdajanyajñānasyāparokṣatvam| brahmasākṣātkāre'pi manananididhyāsanasaṃskṛtaṃ mana eva karaṇm| "manasaivānudraṣṭavyam" ityādiśruteḥ| mano'gamyatvaśrutiścāsaṃskṛtamanoviṣayā| na caivaṃ brahmaṇa aupaniṣadatvānupapattiḥ, asmaduktamanaso vedajanyajñānānantarameva pravṛttatayā vedopajīvitvāt| vedānupajīvimānāntaragamyatvasyaiva vedagamyatvavirodhāt| śāstradṛṣṭisūtramapi brahmaviṣayamānasapratyakṣasya śāstraprayojyatvādupapadyate| taduktam -- 
api saṃrādhane sūtrācchāstrārthadhyānajā pramā|
śāstradṛṣṭirmatā, tāṃ tu vetti vācaspatiḥ param|| iti|
tacca jñānaṃ pāpakṣayādbhavati, sa ca karmānuṣṭhānāditi paramparayā karmaṇāṃ viniyogaḥ| ata eva "tametaṃ vedānuvacanena brāhmaṇā vividiṣanti yajñena dānena tapasā'nāśakena" ityādiśrutiḥ "kaṣāye karmabhiḥ pakve tato jñānaṃ pravarttate" ityādismṛtiśca saṅgacchate| evaṃ śravaṇamanananididhyāsanānyapi jñānasādhanāni, maitreyībrāhmaṇe "ātmā vā are dṛṣṭavyaḥ" iti darśanamanūdya tatsādhanatvena "śrotavyo mantavyo nididhyāsitavyaḥ" iti śravaṇamanananididhyāsanānāṃ vidhānāt| śravaṇaṃ nāma vedāntānāmadvitīye brahmaṇi tātparyyāvadhāraṇānukūlamānasī kriyā| mananaṃ nāma śabdāvadhārite'rthe mānāntaravirodhaśaṅkāyāṃ tannirākaraṇānukūlatarkātmajñānajanako mānaso vyāpāraḥ| nididhyāsanaṃ nāma anādidurvāsanayā viṣayeṣvākṛṣyamāṇasya cittasya viṣayebhyopakṛṣya ātmaviṣayakasthairyyānukūlo mānaso vyāpāraḥ|
tatra nididhyāsanaṃ brahmasākṣātkāre sākṣātkāraṇam, "te dhyānayogānugatā apaśyan devātmaśaktiṃ svaguṇairnigūḍhām" ityādiśruteḥ| nididhyāsane ca mananaṃ hetuḥ, akṛtamananasyārthadārḍhyābhāvena tadviṣayanididhyāsanāyogāt| manane ca śravaṇaṃ hetuḥ, śravaṇābhāve tātparyyāniścayena śābdajñānābhāvena śrutārthaviṣayayuktatvāyuktatvaniścayānukūlamananāyogāt| etāni trīṇyapi jñānotpattau kāraṇānītikecidācāryyā ūcire|
apare tu śravaṇaṃ pradhānaṃ, manananididhyāsanayostu śravaṇātparācīnayorapi śravaṇaphalabrahmadarśananirvarttakatayā ārādupakārakatayā aṅgatvamityāhuḥ| tadapyaṅgatvaṃ na tārtīyaśeṣatvarūpam| tasya śrutyādyanyatamapramāṇagamasya prakṛte śrutyādyabhāve'sambhavāt| tathā hi -- vrīhibhiryajeta iti, dadhnā juhoti ityādāviva manananididhyāsanayoraṅgatve na kācittṛtīyāśrutirasti| nāpi barhirdevasadanaṃ dāmi ityādimantrāṇāṃ varhiḥkhaṇḍanaprakāśanasāmarthyavat kiñcilliṅgamasti| nāpi pradeśāntarapaṭhitapravargyasya agniṣṭome pravṛṇaktīti vākyavat śravaṇānuvādena manananididhyāsanaviniyojakaṃ kiñcidvākyamasti| nāpi darśapūrṇamāsābhyāṃ svargakāmo yajeteti vākyāvagataphalasādhanatākadarśapaurṇamāsaprakaraṇe prayājādīnāmiva phalasādhanatvenāvagatasya śravaṇasya prakaraṇe manananididhyāsanayorāmnānam|
nanu draṣṭavya iti darśanānuvādena śravaṇe vihite sati phalavattayā śravaṇaprakaraṇe tatsannidhāvāmnātayormanananididhyāsanayoḥ prayājanyāyena prakaraṇādevāṅgateti cet -- na| "te dhyānayogānugatā apaśyan" ityādiśrutyantare dhyānasya darśanasādhanatvenāvagatasya aṅgākāṅkṣāyāṃ prayājanyāyena śravaṇamananayorevāṅgatvāpatteḥ| kramasamākhye ca dūraniraste| kiñca prayājādiṣu aṅgatvavicāraḥ saprayojanaḥ, pūrvapakṣe vikṛtiṣu na prayājānuṣṭhānaṃ, siddhānte tu tatrāpi tadanuṣṭhānamiti| prakṛte tu śravaṇaṃ na kasyacitprakṛtiḥ, yena manananididhyāsanayostatrāpyanuṣṭhānamaṅgatvavicāraphalaṃ bhavet| tasmānna tārttīyaśeṣatvaṃ mananididhyāsanayoḥ| kintu yathā ghaṭādikāryye mṛtpiṇḍādīnāṃ pradhānakāraṇatā, cakrādīnāṃ sahakārikāraṇateti prādhānyāprādhānyavyapadeśaḥ, tathā śravaṇamanananididhyāsanānāmapīti mantavyam| sūcitaṃ caitadvivaraṇācāryyaiḥ "śaktitātparyyaviśiṣṭaśabdāvadhāraṇaṃ prameyāvagamaṃ pratyavyavadhānena kāraṇaṃ bhavati, pramāṇasya prameyāvagamaṃ pratyavyavadhānāt | manananididhyāsane tu cittasya pratyagātmapravaṇatāsaṃskārapariniṣpannatadekāgravṛtikāryyadvāreṇa brahmānubhavahetutāṃ pratipadyete iti phalaṃ pratyavyavahitakāraṇasya tātparyyaviśiṣṭaśabdāvadhāraṇasya vyavahite manananididhyāsane tadaṅge aṅgīkriyete" iti|
śravaṇādiṣu ca mumukṣūṇāmadhikāraḥ| kāmye karmaṇi phalakāmasyādhikāritvāt| mumukṣāyāṃ ca nityānityavivekasyehāmutrārthaphalabhogavirāgasya śamadamoparatititikṣāsamādhānaśraddhānāṃ viniyogaḥ| antarindriyaparigrahaḥ śamaḥ| bahirindriyanigraho damaḥ| vikṣepābhāva uparatiḥ| śītoṣṇādidvandvasahanaṃ titikṣā| cittaikāgryaṃ samādhānam| guruvedāntavākyaviśvāsaḥ śraddhā| atroparamaśabdena saṃnyāso'bhidhīyate| tathā ca saṃnyāsināmeva śravaṇe'dhikāra iti kecit| anye tūparaśabdasya saṃnyāsavācakatvābhāvād vikṣepābhāvamātrasya gṛhastheṣvapi sambhavājjanakāderapi brahmavicārasya śrūyamāṇatvāt, sarvāśramasādhāraṇaṃ śravaṇādividhānamityāhuḥ|
saguṇopāsanamapi cittaikāgyradvārā nirviśeṣabrahmasākṣātkārahetuḥ| taduktam --

saguṇopāsakānāṃ ca arcirādimārgeṇa brahmalokaṃ gatānāṃ tatraiva śravaṇādutpannatattvasākṣātkārāṇāṃ brahmaṇā saha mokṣaḥ|
karmavatāṃ dhūmamārgeṇa pitṛlokaṃ gatānāmupabhogena karmakṣaye sati pūrvakṛtasukṛtaduṣkṛtānusāreṇa brahmādisthāvarānteṣu punarutpattiḥ| tathā ca śrutiḥ "ramaṇīyacaraṇāḥ ramaṇīyāṃ yonimāpadyante kupūyacaraṇāḥ kupūyāṃ yonim" iti|
pratiṣiddhānuṣṭhāyināṃ tu rauravādinarakaviśeṣeṣu tattatpāpopacitaṃ tībraduḥkhamanubhūya sūkarādiṣu tiryagyoniṣu sthāvarādiṣu cotpattirityalaṃ prasaṅgādāgataprapañceneti|
nirguṇabrahmasākṣātkāravatastu na lokāntaragamanam, "na tasya prāṇā utkrāmanti" iti śruteḥ| kintu yāvatprārabdhakṣayaṃ sukhaduḥkhe hyanubhūya paścādapavṛjyate|
nanu "kṣīyante cāsya karmāṇi tasmin dṛṣṭe parāvare" ityādiśrutyā "jñānāgniḥ sarvakarmāṇi bhasmasātkurute tathā" iti smṛtyā ca jñānasya sakalakarmanāśahetutvaniścaye sati prārabdhakarmāvasthānamanupapannamiti cet -- na| "tasya tāvadeva ciraṃ yāvanna vimokṣye'tha sampatsye" ityādiśrutyā "nābhuktaṃ kṣīyate karma" ityādismṛtyā cotpāditakāryyakarmavyatiriktānāṃ sañcitakarmaṇāmeva jñānavināśyatvāvagamāt|
sañcitaṃ dvividhaṃ -- sukṛtaṃ duṣkṛtaṃ ceti| tathā ca śrutiḥ "tasya putrā dāyamupayānti suhṛdaḥ sādhukṛtyaṃ dviṣantaḥ pāpakṛtyam" iti|
nanu brahmajñānānmūlājñānanivṛttau tatkāryyaprārabdhakarmaṇo'pi nivṛtteḥ kathaṃ jñānino dehadhāraṇamupapadyata iti cet -- na| apratibaddhajñānasyaivājñānanivartakatayā prārabdhakarmarūpapratibandhakadaśāyāmajñānanivṛtteranaṅgīkārāt|
