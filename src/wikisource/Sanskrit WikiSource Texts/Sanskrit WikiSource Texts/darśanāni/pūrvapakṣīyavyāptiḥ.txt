||pūrvapakṣīyavyāptilakṣaṇam||
pūrvapakṣīyavyāptilakṣaṇam -- "sādhyābhāvavadavṛttitvam"| sādhyaṃ vahniḥ, tadabhāvavān hradaḥ, tadvṛttitvaṃ mīnaśaivālādiṣu, avṛttitvaṃ dhūme iti lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ| sādhyābhāvavannirupitāvṛttitvaṃ vyāptiriti niṣkṛṣṭo'rthaḥ| evamukte'pi "samavāyasambandhena vahnirmahānase nāsti" iti pratītyā vahnyabhāvavān mahānasaḥ tadvṛttitvameva dhūme iti avyāptiḥ,tadvāraṇāya sādhyaniṣṭhapratiyogitāyāṃ sādhyatāvacchedakasambandhāvacchinnatvaviśeṣaṇaṃ dīyate|tathā sati sādhyo vahniḥ parvate saṃyogena siṣādhayiṣito'stīti sādhyatāvacchedakasambandhaḥ saṃyogaḥ| ataḥ saṃyogasambandhāvacchinnasādhyaniṣṭhapratiyogitākābhāvo mahānase nāsti iti nāvyāptiḥ|
evamukte'pi -- mahānase catvarīyavahnirvāsti iti pratītyā (cālanīnyāyena) mahānase vahnyabhāvaṃ sādhayitvā tadvṛttitvaṃ dhūme iti punaravyāptiḥ| tadvāraṇāya sādhyatāvacchedakāvacchinnatvaviśeṣaṇaṃ pratiyogitāyāṃ niveśyate|tathā sati mahānase vahnitvāvacchinnapratiyogitākābhāvo nāstīti nāvyāptiḥ| evamukte'pi etadvṛkṣaḥ kapisaṃyogī etadvṛkṣatvāditi saddhetau saṃyogābhāvasya vṛkṣe sattvātsamavāyasambandhāvacchinnasaṃyogatvāvacchinnasaṃyoganiṣṭhapratiyogitākābhāvāvān vṛkṣaḥ tadvṛttitvameva etadvṛkṣatve iti avyāptiḥ| tadvāraṇāya pratiyogivyadhikaraṇaviśeṣaṇamabhāve niveśyate|vyadhikaraṇañcātra tadanadhikaraṇavṛttitvam| tatpadena sādhyaḥ kapisaṃyogo gṛhyate| tadadhikaraṇametadvṛkṣaḥ anadhikaraṇaṃ guṇaḥ, tadvṛttirabhāvaḥ saṃyogābhāvaḥ, tadvadavṛttitvametadvṛkṣatve iti nā'vyāptiḥ|
evamapi "saṃyogena kapisaṃyogo vṛkṣe nāsti" iti pratītyā kapisaṃyogābhāvavān etadvṛkṣatvaṃ tadvṛttitvam etadvṛkṣatve iti punaravyāptiḥ| tadvāraṇāya pratiyoginiṣṭhādheyatāyāṃ pratiyogitāvacchedakasambandhāvacchinnatvaviśeṣaṇaṃ dīyate| sa ca sambandhaḥ sādhyatāvacchedakasambandha eva bhavati| evañca samavāyasambandhāvacchinnasaṃyotvāvacchinnapratiyogitākasamavāyasambandhāvacchinnādheyatāvatpratiyogivyadhikaraṇābhāvavannirupitavṛttitvābhāvo vyāptiritilakṣaṇamaṃ sampannam|
tena ca samavāyasambandhāvacchinnādheyatāvatpratiyogyanadhikaraṇametadvṛkṣo na bhavatīti nāvyāptiḥ|
etenāpi viśiṣṭasattvāvān jāteḥ iti vyabhicāriṇi ativyaptiḥ| tathāhi -- sādhyaṃ viśiṣṭasattā| viśiṣṭaśuddhayoranatireka iti nyāyena sattā eva sādhyā| tadabhāvavat sāmānyādikaṃ tadavṛttitvañca jātau iti jātau sattāvyāpyatvamāyātamiti ativyāptiḥ| tadvāraṇāya pratiyogitāvacchedakāvacchinnatvaviśeṣaṇaṃ pratiyoginiṣṭhādheyatāyāṃ dīyate| tathā sati viśiṣṭasattātvāvacchinnādheyatāvatpratiyogyanadhikaraṇaṃ guṇaḥ tadvṛttitvameva jātau na tvavṛttitvamiti ativyāptiḥ vāritā|
etāvatā sādhyatāvacchedakāsaṃyogāvacchinnasādhyatāvacchedakāvacchinnapratiyogitākapratiyogitāvacchedakasambandhāvacchinnapratiyogitāvacchedakāvacchinnapratiyogyanadhikaraṇavṛttyabhāvavannirūpitahetuniṣṭhavṛttitvābhāvo vyāptiriti lakṣaṇam|
evamapi "vahnimān dhūmādi"tyatra vahnipratiyogikavahnivyadhikaraṇābhāvo dhūmāvayavagatavahnyabhāvaḥ| tadvān dhūmāvayavaḥ tadvṛttitvaṃ dhūme iti saddhetāvavyāptiḥ| tadvāraṇāya vṛttitve hetutāvacchedakasambandhāvacchinnatvaṃ viśeṣaṇaṃ dīyate| prakṛte dhūmaḥ saṃyogasambandhena pakṣe'stīti hetutāvacchedakasambandhaḥ saṃyogaḥ| dhūmāvayavanirūpitaṃ dhūmaniṣṭhaṃ vṛttitvaṃ samavāyasambandhāvacchinnamiti hetutāvacchedakasambandhāvacchinnavṛttitvaṃ dhūmāvayavanirupitaṃ na bhavati iti nā'vyāptiḥ|
etāvadukte'pi "dhūmavān vahne"riti vyabhicārasthale'tivyāptiḥ| tadyathā -- sādhyābhāvavannirūpito vṛttitvapratiyogitāko'bhāvo nāma ayogolake vṛttitvaghaṭobhayannāstīti pratītisiddho'bhāvaḥ| ataḥ sādhyapratiyogikapratiyogivyadhikaraṇābhāvavannirūpitavṛttitvābhāvarūpāvyāptiḥ vahnau astīti ativyāptiḥ| tadvāraṇāya vṛttitvatvāvacchinnatvaviśeṣaṇaṃ deyam| tena 'ayogolake vahniniṣṭhavṛttitvaṃ nāsti' iti pratītisiddhau vṛttitvatvāvacchinnavahniniṣṭhavṛttitvapratiyogitākābhāvo nāsti iti vyāptilakṣaṇaṃ na ghaṭate iti nātivyāptiḥ| yattu ayogolake vahnighaṭobhayabhāvamādāya vṛttitvaniṣṭhapratiyogitākābhāvaḥ sādhitaḥ sa (vṛttitvaghaṭo)bhayatvāvacchinnapratiyogitākābhāva iti na doṣaḥ|||
