|| haṃsopaniṣat ||
oṃ pūrṇamadaḥ pūrṇamidaṃ pūrṇāt pūrṇamudacyate |
pūrṇasya pūrṇamādāya pūrṇamevāvaśiṣyate ||
oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ ||
gautama uvāca | bhagavansarvadharmajña sarvaśāstraviśārada | brahmavidyāprabodho hi kenopāyena jāyate || 1 ||
sanatkumāra uvāca | vicārya sarvavedeṣu mataṃ jñātvā pinākinaḥ | pārvatyā kathitaṃ tattvaṃ śṛṇu gautama tanmama || 2 ||
anākhyeyamidaṃ guhyaṃ yogināṃ kośasannibham | haṃsasyākṛtivistāraṃ bhuktimuktiphalapradam || 3 ||
atha haṃsaparamahaṃsanirṇayaṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ | brahmacāriṇe śāntāya dāntāya gurubhaktāya | haṃsahaṃseti sadā dhyāyan || 4 ||
sarveṣu deheṣu vyāpto vartate | yathā hyagniḥ kāṣṭheṣu tileṣu tailamiva taṃ viditvā mṛtyumatyeti || 5 ||
gudamavaṣṭabhyādhārādvāyumutthāpyasvādhiṣṭhānaṃ triḥ pradakṣiṇīkṛtya maṇipūrakaṃ ca gatvā anāhatamatikramya viśuddhau prāṇānnirudhyājñāmanudhyāyanbrahmarandhraṃ dhyāyan trimātro'hamityeva sarvadā paśyatyanākāraśca bhavati || 6 ||
eṣo'sau paramahaṃso bhānukoṭipratīkāśo yenedaṃ sarvaṃ vyāptam || 7 ||
tasyāṣṭadhā vṛttirbhavati | pūrvadale puṇye matiḥ | āgneye nidrālasyādayo bhavanti | yāmye kraurye matiḥ | nairṛte pāpe manīṣā | vāruṇyāṃ krīḍā | vāyavyāṃ gamanādau buddhiḥ | saumye ratiprītiḥ | īśānye dravyādānam | madhye vairāgyam | kesare jāgradavasthā | karṇikāyāṃ svapnam | liṅge suṣuptiḥ | padmatyāge turīyam | yadā haṃse nādo vilīno bhavati tat turīyātītam || 8 ||
atho nādamādhārādbrahmarandhraparyantaṃ śuddhasphaṭikasaṅkāśaḥ | sa vai brahma paramātmetyucyate || 1 ||
atha haṃsa ṛṣiḥ | avyaktagāyatrī chandaḥ | paramahaṃso devatā | hamiti bījam | sa iti śaktiḥ | so'hamiti kīlakam || 10 ||
ṣaṭ saṅkhyayā ahorātrayorekaviṃśatisahasrāṇi ṣaṭ śatānyadhikāni bhavanti | sūryāya somāya nirañjanāya nirābhāsāyatanusūkṣmaṃ pracodayāditi || 11 ||
agnīṣomābhyāṃ vauṣaṭ hṛdayādyaṅganyāsakaranyāsau bhavataḥ || 12 ||
evaṃ kṛtvā hṛdaye aṣṭadale haṃsātmānaṃ dhyāyet || 13 ||
agnīṣomau pakṣāvoṅkāraḥ śiro ukāro bindu strinetraṃ mukhaṃ rudro rudrāṇī caraṇau | dvividhaṃ kaṇṭhataḥ kuryādityunmanāḥ ajapopasaṃhāra ityabhidhīyate || 14 ||
evaṃ haṃsavaśāttasmānmano haṃso vicāryate || 15 ||
asyeva japakoṭyāṃ nādamanubhavati evaṃ sarvaṃ haṃsavaśānnādo daśavidho jāyate | ciṇīti prathamaḥ | ciñciṇīti dvitīyaḥ | ghaṇṭānādastṛtīyaḥ | śaṅkhanādaścaturthaḥ | pañcamastantrīnādaḥ | ṣaṣṭhastālanādaḥ | saptamo veṇunādaḥ | aṣṭamo mṛdaṅganādaḥ | navamo bherīnādaḥ | daśamo meghanādaḥ || 16 ||
navamaṃ parityajya daśamamevābhyaset || 17 ||
prathame ciñciṇīgātraṃ dvitīye gātrabhañjanam | tṛtīye khedanaṃ yāti caturthe kampate śiraḥ || 18 ||
pañcame sravate tālu ṣaṣṭhe'mṛtaniṣevaṇam | saptame gūḍhavijñānaṃ parā vācā tathāṣṭame || 19 ||
adṛśyaṃ navame dehaṃ divyaṃ cakṣustathāmalam | daśame paramaṃ brahma bhavedbrahmātmasannidhau || 20 ||
tasminmano vilīyate manasi saṅkalpavikalpe dagdhe puṇyapāpe sadāśivaḥ śaktyātmā sarvatrāvasthitaḥ svayaṃjyotiḥ śuddho buddho nityo nirañjanaḥ śāntaḥ prakāśata iti vedānuvacanaṃ bhavatītyupaniṣat || 21 ||
oṃ pūrṇamadaḥ pūrṇamidaṃ pūrṇāt pūrṇamudacyate |
pūrṇasya pūrṇamādāya pūrṇamevāvaśiṣyate ||
oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ ||
|| iti haṃsopaniṣatsamāptā ||
