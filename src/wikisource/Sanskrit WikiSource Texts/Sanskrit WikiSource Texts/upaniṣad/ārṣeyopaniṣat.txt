ārṣeyopaniṣat
oṃ ṛṣayo vai brahmodyamāhvayitavā ūcuḥ parasparamivānubruvāṇāḥ | teṣāṃ viśvāmitro vijitīyamiva manyamāna uvāca | yadetadantare dyāvāpṛthivī anaśnuvadiva sarvamaśnavadyadidamākāśamivetaścetaśca stanayanti vidyotamānā ivāvasphūrjayamānā iva tadbrahmeti | tasyopavyākhyānam | yadidamagnibhirjvalayanti poplūyayantyadbhirabhiṣīvayanti vaddhībhirabhigrathnanti varatrābhirabhighnaṃtyayoghanairvidhyaṃti sūcībhiḥ nikhānayaṃti śaṃkubhirabhitṛndanti paḍavīśikābhirabhilimpanti mṛtsnayā takṣṇuvaṃti vāsībhiḥ kṛṣṇaṃti phalibhirnahaiva śaknuvata iti nāsyeśīmahi nainamatīyīmahi ||
tadu ha jamadagnirnānumene ārtamiva vā eṣa tanmene yadidamantareṇainadupayaṃti paryāsa ivaiṣa divaspṛthivyoriti | sa hovāca antarikṣaṃ vā etadyadidamitthetthopavyākhye iti | mahimānaṃ tvamuṣyeha paśyāmīti yadidamasminnanvāyattamiti | sa yadidametasminnanvāyattaṃ vedātha tathopāste'nvāyatto haivāsmin bhavati | tadetadavidvānevāsminnanvāyattamupāste vīvapadyat ārtimṛcchet | tasmādevamevopāsīta ||
tamitaraḥ papraccha | katamat tvamanārtaṃ manyasa iti | taṃ hovāca | yadidamiti dyāvāpṛthivyoranāraṃbhamiva nopayanti nābhicakṣate nāśnuvanti | tasyopavyākhyānam | yadidamitaścetaścāṇḍakośā udayanti nāpadyanta iva na visraṃsanta iva na skhalaṃtīva na paryāvartanta iva | na ha vā enatkecidupadhāvanto vindanti nābhipaśyanti | yadidameka idapa evāhustama eke jyotireke'vakāśameke paramaṃ vyomaika ātmānameka iti ||
tadu ha bharadvājo nānumene| yadiha sarve vetthettheti dyūdire nāsya tadrūpaṃ paryāptamiti | sa hovāca | ārtamivedaṃ te vijñānamapi svidenadrodasyoreva paryāyeṇopavanvīmahi yadidamitthetthopavyākhyāsyāma iti | mahimānaṃ tvevāsyopāse| yadidamatrānvāyattamiti | sa yadidamatrānvāyattaṃ vedātha tathaivopāste'traivānvāyatto bhavati | sa ya ihānvāyattamidamavidvānevaitadupāste pāpīyān bhavatyārtimārcchatyavamriyate yadevametadanvāyattamupāste sarvamāyureti vasīyān bhavati | sa ya etadenamupāste ||
tamitaraḥ papraccha | katamat tvamanārtaṃ manyasa iti | sa hovāca | yadetasmin maṇḍale'cirdīpyate baṃbhramyamāṇamiva cākaśyamānamiva jājvalyamānamiva dedīpyamānamiva lelihānaṃ tadeva me brahma | tasyopavyākhyānaṃ yadidamaddhaiva parāḥ parāvato'bhipadyate saṃpannamevaitat saṃmitameva yathopayātamātmaivābhicakṣata iti | ya etadabhipadyeva gṛhṇīyādatho visphurantīva dhāvantīvotplavaṃtīvopaśliṣyaṃtīva na haivābhipadyante | tadidamantike davīyo nedīya iva dūrato na vā asya mahimānaṃ kaścidetīti
tadu ha na mene gautamo yadidamārtamiva stimitamiva paryāyeṇa paśyantīvemaṃ moghaṃ saṃvidānā iti | ya ime puṇḍrāḥ suhmāḥ kulumbhā daradā barbarā iti | na ha vā asaṃvidānā eva drāgivābhi tatpadyanta iti | mahimānaṃ tvevāsyopāse | ya etadasminnantare hiraṇmayaḥ puruṣo hiraṇyavarṇo hiraṇyaśmaśrurānakhāgrebhyo dīpyamāna iva | sa ya evamenamupāste'tīva sarvabhūtāni tiṣṭhaṃti sarvamāyureti vasīyān bhavati | na ha vā eṣa paramatīvodeti | yastvenaṃ paramatīvodyantaṃ paśyannupāste pāpīyān bhavati vīvapadyata ārtimṛcchati | yastvenaṃ paramanūdyantaṃ vedātha tathopāste paraṃ jyotirupasaṃpadyate sarvamāyureti vasīyān bhavati | sa ya evamenamupāste ||
tamitaraḥ papraccha| katamat tvamanārtaṃ manyasa iti | taṃ hovāca | visphuraṃtīrevemā lelāyantīriva saṃjihānā iva nedīyasitamā iva davīyasitamā eva davīyasitamā iva nedīyasitamā eveti | yadapi bahudhācakṣīranna kiñca pratipadyata iti tanme brahmeti ||
tadu ha vasiṣṭho nānumene | yadimā visphūrjayata evābhipadyante vīvayanti mithu ceti vicakṣate'kāṇḍa ivemā na ha vai paramitthā kaścanāśnotyasaṃvidāna iva | apa ye saṃvidrate tadetadantarvicakṣata iti | mahimnaḥ paśyemā vijāna iti | sa ya evamimā mahimna evāsya paśyannupāste mahimna evāśnoti sarvamāyureti vasīyān bhavati | yastvimā avayatīrevopāste na parā saṃpadyamānā no eva pareti pāpīyān bhavati vīvapadyate pramīyate | atha ya imāḥ parā saṃpadyamānā evopāste paraiva saṃpadyate sarvamāyureti vasīyān bhavati |
tamitaraḥ papraccha | katamat tvamanārtaṃ manyasa iti | mahāvijñānamiva pratipadenādhyavasāyamiva yatraitaditthetthetyabhipaśyati | atha neti netyetaditthettheti | sa vā ayamātmā ananto'jaro'pāro na vā are bāhyo nāntaraḥ sarvavidbhārūpo vighasaḥ prasaraṇo'ntarjyotirviśvabhuk sarvasya vaśī sarvasyeśānaḥ sarvamamikṣipanna tamaśnoti kaścana ||
parovarīyāṃsamabhipraṇutyamantarjuṣāṇaṃ bhuvanāni viśvā |
yamaśnavanna kuśikāso agniṃ vaiśvānaramṛtajātaṃ gamadhyayī ||1||
bharebhareṣu tamupahvayāma prasāsahiṃ yudhmamindraṃ vareṇyam |
satrāsā[ha]mavase janānāṃ puruhūtamṛgmiṇaṃ viśvavedasam || 2 ||
ahighnaṃ tamarṇave śayānaṃ vāvṛhāṇaṃ tavasā pareṇa || 3 ||
tadu ha pratipedire | te vābhivādyaivopasamīyuḥ | namo'gnaye | nama indrāya | namaḥ prajāpataye | namo brahmaṇe | namo brahmaṇe ||
ityārṣeyopaniṣat samāptā
