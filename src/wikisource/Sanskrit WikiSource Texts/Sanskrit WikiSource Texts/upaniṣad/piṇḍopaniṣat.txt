Text:

piṇḍopaniṣat
oṃ devatā ṛṣayaḥ sarve brahmāṇamidamabruvan |
mṛtasya dīyate piṇḍaṃ kathaṃ gṛhṇantyacetasaḥ ||
bhinne pañcātmake dehe gate pañcasu pañcadhā |
haṃsastvastvā gato dehaṃ kasmin sthāne vyavasthitaḥ ||
tryahaṃ vasati toyeṣu tryahaṃ vasati cāgniṣu |
tryahamākāśago bhūtvā dinamekantu vāyugaḥ ||
prathamena tu piṇḍena kalānāṃ tasya sambhavaḥ |
dvitīyena tu piṇḍena māsatvakcchoṇitodbhavaḥ ||
tṛtīyena tu piṇḍena matistasyābhijāyate |
caturthena tu piṇḍena asthi majjā prajāyate ||
pañcamena tu piṇḍena hastāṅgulyaḥ śiro mukham |
ṣaṣṭhena kṛtapiṇḍena hṛtkaṇṭhaṃ tālu jāyate ||
saptamena tu piṇḍena dīrghamāyuḥ prajāyate |
aṣṭamena tu piṇḍena vācaṃ puṣyati vīryavān ||
navamena tu piṇḍena sarvendriyasamāhṛtiḥ |
daśamena tu piṇḍena bhāvanaṃ plāvanaṃ tathā |
piṇḍe piṇḍe śarīrasya piṇḍadānena sambhavaḥ ||
iti piṇḍopaniṣat samāptā
