nṛsiṃhagāyatryādiprāptau tavdyāvṛttyartham ānuṣṭubhamiti anuṣṭupchandaupādhikam ṛgviśeṣamāha, ṭha gāyatrametadaharbhavati ' iti nyāyāt | apaśyat dṛṣṭavānityabhiprāyaḥ | etaduktaṃ bhavati -- dvitīyatṛtīyacaturthapañcamopaniṣatsu mantrarājāditrayaḥ śabdāḥ prayuktāḥ tatpuruṣabahuvrīhitaddhitasāmathryāt pūrvapūrvopaniṣadarthamācakṣāṇāḥ santa eva ādau prayuktāḥ kṛtsnāmeva brāhṛvidyāṃ saṃgṛhītavantaḥ; atyantaśuddhāntaḥkaraṇatvāt śaktitrayuktāṃ brāhṛsvarūpiṇīṃ bhūtasṛṣṭipuraḥsarasarvasṛṣṭikāraṇikāmanuṣṭubṛcam apaśyadityāha | anuṣṭubho vā imāni bhūtāni jāyante anuṣṭhubhā jātāni jīvanti anuṣṭubhaṃ prayantyabhisaṃviśanti iti ṭha janmādyasya yataḥ ' ityasyā brāhṛlakṣaṇalakṣitatvaṃ darśayati | uktamevārthaṃ draḍhayitum ṛcaṃ sākṣitvenodbhāvayati -- tasya brāhṛsvarūpasya sākṣiṇī eṣā vakṣyamāṇā ṛk bhavati | anuṣṭup prathamā bhavati sarvasṛṣṭeḥ prathamā ādyā bhavati | anuṣṭup uttamā śreṣṭhā bhavati | vāgvā anuṣṭup sarvo vākprapañcaḥ anuṣṭubhi līna iti darśayati | nāmasṛṣṭipūrvakatvādrūpasṛṣṭeḥ vāgrūpatvādanuṣṭubhaḥ anuṣṭubeva mūlakāraṇam | vācaiva prayanti anuṣṭumaiva pralayaṃ gacchanti bhūtāni | vācaiva anuṣṭubhaiva udyanti utpatiṃta gacchanti | paramā vā eṣā chandasāṃ gāyatryādīnām, chandasāṃ vedānāṃ vā, paramā utkṛṣṭā | paramatvaṃ ca sāmādhāratvādanuṣṭubhaḥ | sāmna ca paramatvam ṭha devā vai narci na yajuṣyaśrayanta te sāmanyevāśrayanta ' iti śruteḥ, ṭha vedānāṃ sāmavedo'smi ' iti smṛte ca | yadanuṣṭubiti itiśabdaḥ ṛksamāpta dyotayati ||
evaṃ tāvadākhyāyikāyāmante sakalanṛsiṃhopāsanasaṃgrāhakān mantrarājanārasiṃhānuṣṭubhaśabdān vijñāya taireva śabdaiḥ sakalopāsanāṃ sṛṣṭa¬rthaṃ prajāpatyanuṣṭhitāṃ saṃgṛhra idānīmaviśeṣeṇānuṣṭhāne prāpte, tatra tāvatsāmnaḥ ṛgakṣarābhivyañjakatvāt abhivyañjakābhivyaṅgayorabhivyañjakapūrvakatvāt sāmna ca sarvavedaśreṣṭhatvāt -- ṭha devā vai narci na yajuṣyaśrayanta te sāmanyevāśrayanta ' iti śruteḥ ṭha vedānāṃ sāmavedo'smi ' iti smṛte ca, asya ca sāmarājatvāt tadupāsanāyāṃ prāptāyāṃ kramasūcanapūrvikāmāha -- 
sa hovāca prajāpatiḥ sa yo ha vai tatsāvitrasyāṣṭākṣaraṃ padaṃ śriyābhiṣiktaṃ tatsāmno'ṅgaṃ veda śriyā haivābhiṣicyate sarve vedāḥ praṇavādikāstaṃ praṇavaṃ tatsāmno'ṅgaṃ veda sa trīṃllokāñjayati caturviṃśatyakṣarā mahālakṣmīryajustatsāmno'ṅgaṃ veda sa āyuryaśaḥkīrtijñānaiśvaryavānbhavati tasmādidaṃ sāṅgaṃ sāma jānīyādyo jānīte so'mṛtatvaṃ ca gacchati sāvitrīṃ praṇavaṃ yajurlakṣmīṃ strīśūdrāya necchanti dvātiṃraśadakṣaraṃ sāma jānīyādyo jānīte so'mṛtatvaṃ ca gacchati sāvitrīṃ lakṣmīṃ yajuḥ praṇavaṃ yadi jānīyātstrīśūdraḥ sa mṛto'dho gacchati tasmātsarvadā nācaṣṭe yadyācaṣṭe sa ācāryastenaiva mṛto'dho gacchati|| 3||
