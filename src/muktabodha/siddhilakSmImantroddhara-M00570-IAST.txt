<p. 1a> <1> oṃ namaḥ paramaśambhu dharā(?) vyāptaṃ(?) yena samastaśāmbhavapadaṃ 
ājñākramaṃ sarvadā divyādivya adivya śūnyakhacaraṃ saṃvartta visvālayaḥ 
|
jñānānanda ca saṃkulāghanamṛtaṃ pītvā tu somodayaṃ krīḍantī 
ravisoma vahnibhuvane ājñājape trottalā || 3 ||

śrīśrīkaṇṭhanātha uvāca ||
śrutaṃ devi mayā sarvvaṃ tava nityābhidhānanaṃ |
adyāpi saṃśayo devi trottalā krama nirṇṇayam || 
mantrodhāra ucyate || prātaruthāya || 
ūkāraṃ vāyubījaṃ tadupari varuṇaṃ vajrapāni taddharddhakālaṃ 

<p. 1b> vaṇṇāntayuktaṃ tadupari paramaṃ vahnibīja sahaṃsaṃ |
indumbinduṃ layāntaṃ sitakamalaparaṃ kṣīradhārāśravantaṃ 
dṛṣṭākūṭa tu nityaṃ dahati kulamalaṃ merutulyo hi pāpaṃ ||

iti kuṭaṃ svāṃta arkarāsi sadṛsaṃ pāpanāśāya dhyāyet ||

jalasnānaṃ prakurvvīta pañcāṃgamathavā punaḥ ||
kṣoṇī jalatejā vāgmīśa parābījāni binduyuktāni jale 
saptadhā vinyasyaṃ śastramantracca ratnatridhāmāyāt(?) ||

<p. 2a> athavā pañcāṅgaṃ hastau pādau mukhaṃ prakṣālya astrarahita 
mantrasahitayāhitarajaḥ kumbhamūdrādi(?) dhyāṇena mantrasnānaṃ || 
bho sādhakendrāḥ śṛṇutekavākyaṃ sarvvānvayoktaṃ phalasiddhi hetoḥ 
tāvante(?) siddhiṃ pralabhanti santo yāvanna mantrāḥ pranavādi śeṣāḥ ||
pūrvvayo mūcitā śaktiḥ kujākhyā krurasaṃyutā | 
ā ityādhāra bhutāsā pratisthiti saṃhṛteḥ ||
ambikā bhujagārūḍhā guhyaśaktyāti maṇḍalaiḥ |

<p. 2b> yā(?) devī(?) pṛṣṭikarī vāribhūtā tu sā smṛtā ||
nāgasthā kusumādevī guhāyātha trimaṇḍalaiḥ |
śrīkarī tejabhūtā tu tṛtīyā śakti raudriṇī ||
dīpanī śikhi parāmbā karṇṇendu bindu visvahā |
vilomenoddhṛtā devī khecaratva pradāyikā ||
anugrahārccitaṃ haṃsaṃ prāṇināṃ hṛdi saṃsthitaṃ |
uddhṛtamākāśabhutaṃ sudhā nirvvāṇadāyakaṃ ||
(***?) kramuktaṃ caturasrapītaṃ khaṇḍedu śubhraṃ varavāricakraṃ ||
tejonikaṃ raktaguṇāradivyaṃ vātākhya ṣaṭkoṇacakra sudhūmaṃ ||

<p. 3a> svacchārkkatulyaṃ vipadādhi viśvaṃ bhutāni pañcānyuditāni śāstre |
eṣāmmahe saṃśitaśāṃtarūpaṃ(??) cāturbhujaṃ padmāśite(??) niviṣṭaṃ ||
dakṣena hastena dadhāti śūlaṃ dānaṃ krameṇaiva tathā pareṇa |
kārddhā bhayaṃ vāmakare vidhante pañcātmamantraiḥ karadehavaktrān ||
netrāṇuśūnyaṃ karakāyavaktraṃ japaṃ krameneva dharādi maṃtraḥ |
lakṣavivṛddhau tithilakṣamātraṃ aṃtedasāsaṃ havanañca dhāraiḥ ||
icchāpadaḥ śrīpraṇaveśvaroyaṃ bhaktārttiha tāṃ gunalokadātā |

<p. 3b> surairmmunīndremmanu daitya siddhaiḥ prāptaṃ padaṃ yājanahāvanānte ||
iti pañcapraṇavadhāraḥ ||

caruprāsnaphala ||
ākāsa śadāśiva | ānaṃda || 1 || vāyu isvara || khecara || 2 || tejarudra | bhocara || 3 ||
(***?) viṣṇu || jala ca || 4 || prīthivī | brahmā | sekharacaru || 5 || sarvvacaru || sarvvadeva 
|| 6 || 

ākāsaṃ bhāvate tīlaṃ rūpitakālamevacaḥ |
parvvvakāle bhuktirūpaṃ bhakṣatammalanāsanaṃ || 1 ||

<p. 4a> mātṛhā pitṛhāścaiva brahmāgoghanameva ca |
dīpabhakṣantaḥ mātrena sarvvapāpakṣayaṃ bhavet || 1 ||
garuḍātcaṭkaparjaṃtaṃ gagne(?) caratirūpitaṃ |
parvvakāle bhakṣetpakṣaṃ mokhyaṃ pakṣaṃ na pasyati || 2 ||
śiṃhnādicchāgaprajantaṃ prabhuntaḥ jaṃturupyajaṃ |
parvvakāle bhakhet jantu mokṣa jaṃtu na pasyati || 3 ||
mīnātammakarādena jale tṛṣṭhati jīvitaṃ |
parvvakāle bhakhyetmīnaḥ mokhyaṃ jala na pasyati || 4 ||
lamṛlādiplāḍuprajyaṃ abhakhyaṃ jamdhamānukhāḥ |
parvvakāle bhakhyetplāḍuṃ mokhyaṃ plāḍu na pasyati || 5 ||

<p. 4b> phalādi rasabījena bhakṣatañjasya jīvitaṃ |
parvvakāle bhakhyet bījaṃ mokhyabīja na pasyati || 6 ||
yatsarvvambhakṣabhojyañca brahmatva nāgataḥ priye |
rasadravyañca yatsarvvam viṣṇutvena vyavasthitaṃ || 
tayo samarasaṃ kṛtvā svayambhoktā maheśvaraḥ ||
varuṇenārccitā devī vāruṇīti prakīrttitā |
suraistu pīyate nityaṃ surānāmeti śabditā ||
madanenārccitānitya smadireti prakīrttitā |
paramārthe samutpannāṃ parāśaktiḥ prakīrttitā ||

<p. 5a> jihvāmūlātsamārabhya hṛdayāntantu sundari |
ṣoḍasāṅgulakaṃ jñeyaṃ sthāna svādāmṛtasya tu ||
ṣoḍasevakalāyuktaṃ ṣoḍaśāṃgulamāyatam ||
pivate devate devī madyamaṃ mṛtarūpinī |
prathamaṃ pivate vahni dvitīyaṃ śaśinā pibet |
viṣṇunārcca tṛtīyaṃ tu caturtha varuṇa pibet ||
kumāraṃ pañcamandevi ṣaṣṭhantu vaśavaḥ pibet |
saptamaṃ ṛṣiḥ sarvvagaṇeśo aṣṭamaṃ pibet ||
navamaṃ dharmmarājena maruteḥ daśamamvit ||

<p. 5b> ekādaśamaṃsthāne brahmaputro pibet priye ||
kuberaṃ dvādaśayāti vaināyakaṃ trayodaśaṃ |
caturddaśyāmpaśupatiṃ pūrṇṇamāsyāṃ prajāprati ||
ete pītvā vaśe pañca ṣoḍaśala prakāśayet |
na pītaṃ mānuṣaiḥ bhadre ve pītvāta śambhavaiḥ ||
pītvā ca kathitaṃ bhadre adhunā deva vā śṛṇu |
yā raśā sa umādevī yo madyasamaheśvaraḥ ||
yo varṇṇasvayaṃ brahmā yogadhasa janārddana |
yasvādasatu somo vego vegapavaṇasvayaṃ ||

<p. 6a> jñānavijñāmapanaṃ(?) mama devi sureśvara |
liyasthānavadiṣyāmi śṛṇuṣva vahitodhunā ||
jihvāgre līyante pūrvvaṃ (***?) śāgre tadanantaraṃ |
ghaṇṭhikānte tṛtīyantu caturthe tu bhruvo nyaset ||
pañcamaṃ tu śiṣāsthāne śikhānte ṣaṣṭha (***?) |
ataśīpuṣpasaṃkāso dhāmaniskala madhyamaṃ |
sthāne(?) līyate devi vijñātavyaṃ prayatnataḥ ||
aiṁ 5 lākṣārasa ṇī(?)bhā devī padmarāga samaprabhā |
yovaṇonaṭarūpāgī trinetra divyarūpanaṃ ||
maṃdirānanda cetanye aṣṭādaśabhujadhārṇi |

<p. 6b> khaḍga triśūlavarada ca krīḍāṃ ḍamaru mudgra ||
gadāpadmadharā pātra dakṣanena vīrājate |
pheṭaka aṃkusaghaṇṭhakapāla khaṭvāga kaṃbuyoḥ |
nīlotpala abhayaṃ bīṃduvāme hasta dhārane ||
divyaratna kṛtāṭopa hemāṃbhara avibhukhitaṃ |
setupadmasamāsīnaṃ vadhapadmāsana sthitaṃ ||
evaṃ rūpamahodevaṃ vāruṇī divyyarūpinī || 
(***?) rthite saṭhe krure dambhimāsaryamānivaiḥ |
nadadyātkolikaṃ sāramviseṣe gurunindakaiḥ ||

<p. 7a> gurubhīkṣārāṇi nityaṃ samayānāñca vatsalaḥ |
sopi dadyātmahābhāge saṃvatsara parīkṣitaṃ |
pañcacakra parameśvarīninayaḥ ||
śambhurmmānasahāntadhīkṣiti rasasparśāgni marmmālikaiḥ 
kūṭaḥ śrīnavanāya(?) asya racitaḥ svāṃtālaye dṛsyate |
arkāneka mahārcci rāśi sadṛśaiścaurddhagato ghorahā 
jantunāmbhava(?*?) hasarddanakarī śrīśāmbhavo mantrarāṭ ||
calādho yojayed vahniñcatuḥ svarorddhamajamvibhuṃ

<p. 7b> mānamadhye(?) nyaserddhaṃ saṃkūṭadvividhamīritaṃ ||
caladhaḥ śāṃbhavaḥ khyāto mantrarājaḥ sudhā pradaḥ |
ānaveśo mānamādhye(?) vijñānakoṭi siddhidaḥ ||
kādibhede calorddhasthā bhuktimukti pradāyakaḥ |
ekāśītyātmakaṃ vakṣye yathā gurumukhāgataḥ || 
kṣonīvāri mahārcci vāyuhasadhī samvarttamarmmālakaiḥ raṃddhraiḥ 
piṇḍasapiṇḍataṃ bhavagataṃ vyasta samastapara |
etattadvasakhaṇḍitaṃ śrutilayā tūṣṇī prabhā niṣkalaṃ 
svargaśūnyamagocaraḥ paradhiyā sambhāvamātraṃ śivaḥ ||

<p. 8a> iti navātmo dvāraḥ ||

pañcavarggapramā devī dvātriṃsākṣarabhūṣitā |
bhāvināṃ bhavamohayāgrahau(?) vidyā kujeśvarī ||
tāmahamakitāṃ(??) vatmi(??) yathā śrīgurutaḥ śrutāṃ |
tavargasyāntimambījaṃ kānvayā(?) (***) yutaṃ ||
bhīṣaṇī śivā tathā khaḍgantārā śūkṣmeṇa saṃyutāṃ |
śrutipraṇavaṃ tathā krodhaṃ pañcamena yuto vṛtaṃ(?) ||
nalinī kaṃkaṭā caiva āśvareṇa ca bheditāṃ |
cala śūkṣmasamākrānta śikhāsvacchandakantathā ||

<p. 8b> kavarggasvāṃtimaṃ bījāṃ śarmmaḍhāntañca pādimaṃ |
kālaṃ bhṛṇṭhasamākrāṃtaṃ svarādyantu samuddharet ||
kavarggasya caturthantu (***?) ca yutaṃ punaḥ |
dīpanpañca tatoddhṛtya mahākālī mamarārccitāṃ ||
māyādevī samopetaṃ catu (******?) naḥ |
kūrmmāchāgalī yuktantu anantaṃ krurasaṃyuta ||
punaruddhṛtyānantarahitaṃ guhyaśakti samāyutaṃ |
kaṃkaṭāñca śūkṣmeṇaiva umākānta māyāyutaṃ || 
punarukta dveśamuddhṛtya chāgalo māyayā yutaṃ |

<p. 9a> cāmuṇḍāṃ kūrmmaṇoddhṛtya jhaṇṭhīśena vibheditāṃ ||
eṣā śrīsamayā devī guruvaktrā tu labhyate |
yasyā smaraṇamātreṇa kathyate bhuvanatrayaṃ ||
kāye vādhānyakāye vā uktānukteṣu vastuṣu |
kubjīśānīṃ yajedyastu tasya pāpa na vidyate ||
yaḥ punaḥ śuddhabhāvātmā samayākhyāṃ kuleśvarī |
yajedyastu na tadvastu yanta sidhyati (***?) ||
akṣarākṣarasaṃtānaṃ yo jape lakṣa saṃkṣayā |
kubjīśa guṇatulyāsau karttāharttā svayaṃ prabhuḥ ||

<p. 9b> mantrāṇā śatakoṭīnāṃ ṣaḍāśramānuvarttināṃ |
rājñāyaṃ tatphalairyuktā dvīpamatoditamvacaḥ ||
iti samayāyato dvāraḥ ||

oṃ namastripurāyaiḥ || mantroddhāra ucyate ||
mādanantadhaḥ śaktimtadantabbindumālinī |
endramākāśabījasthaṃ adhasthādanalākṣaraṃ ||
māyāvidvīśvara yuktaṃ sarvvopariniyojitaḥ |
ayaṃ sā vāgbhavo devi vāgiśvatvapravarttakaiḥ || 1 ||
śivabījaṃ tridhāyuktvā sṛṣṭisthitilaya kramaiḥ |

<p. 10a> ādimadhyāvasāneṣu dvayamādyena rahitaṃ ||
ādyena ādivarṇṇena ādyādho madanākṣaraṃ |
