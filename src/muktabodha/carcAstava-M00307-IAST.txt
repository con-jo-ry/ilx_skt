<p. 1>

{pañcastavyāṃ carcāstavo dvitīyaḥ}

prāyaḥ panthā bahulavidhigo dhāmni śākte'dhiśāyī 
puṃsāṃ tattatphalanivahado dhyānavīryādhirūḍheḥ |
no vā puṃdhāmani jaḍapade niṣphale no parasmin 
iṣṭāptyai tat  sphuraduruphalā carcyate kāpi vallī ||

idānīṃ dvitīyaṃ carcāstavamupacikramiṣurācāryo 
nirvighnasamāptikāmatvenāmbāyāḥ pādakamalaṃ vīraśṛṅgāraraso 
(makarando) paśliṣṭatvena parāmṛśati | 

oṃ namastripurasundaryai(-)

ānandasundarapurandaramuktamālyaṃ (manthare tipāṭhāntaram)
maulau haṭhena nihitaṃ mahiṣāsurasya |
pādāmbujaṃ bhavatu me vijayāya mañju
mañjīraśiñjitamanoharamambikāyāḥ || 1 ||

ambikā nāma paripūrṇāhambhāvātmasphuraṇāvalokanacaturā 
sarvātiśayatvaśālinī parāvāk śaktiḥ | taduktaṃ catuḥśatyām(-) 

ātmanaḥ sphuraṇaṃ paśyedyadā sā paramā kalā |
ambikārūpamāpannā parā vāksamudīritā ||

<p. 2>

iti | śrīpratyabhijñāyāmapi(-) 

citiḥ pratyavamarśātmā parā vāksvarasoditā (1-5-13)

iti tasya ambikāyāḥ śakterviśvajananyāḥ pādāmbujaṃ pādaścaraṇameva 
mārdavasaurabhādiguṇayogādambujaṃ kamalaṃ me mama vijayāya vijayaṃ 
viśvotkṛṣṭapadalābhamavāptuṃ bhavatu bhūyādityāśaṃsāyāṃ lot | vijayāyeti 
tumarthācca bhāvavacanāt ityanena sampradānatvam | atha ca vijayaḥ 
sarvaniyantṛtvaṃ tatprāptaye ityarthaḥ | yā brāhmaṇo jitiryā vyuṣṭirityatra 
kauṣītakyupaniṣacchrutāvācāryaistathā vyākhyātatvāt | evamagre'pi 
yathārthasaṃgatyā jayapadodīraṇe'rtho bodhyaḥ | atra 
prakṛtapādaprahāropayogitvena prāyo dakṣiṇasyaiva 
dākṣiṇyaprasiddherdakṣiṇaṃ pādakamalaṃ jñeyam |

yadvā(-) raṇe sasaṃrambhavyāpārāsaktyā sahasā 
prahāradānāvasarāpāte'viśeṣeṇa savyetarapādayoranyataro grāhyaḥ | athavā 
mahiṣāsuramardanamekasyaiva pādasya karma iti vīrakarmāviṣkaraṇam 
ityabhiprāyeṇaikavacanopanyāsaḥ | kīdṛśaṃ mahiṣāsurasya maulau kirīṭe | 
yadvā maulinopalakṣite śirasi haṭhena sāhasena nihitaṃ kṛtaprahāraṃ yena 
so'suro raṇe jīvitāvasānamagāt | ataḥ 

śiñjānamaṇimañjīramaṇḍitaśrīpadāmbujā |

iti brahmāṇḍoktyā tatkāle pādāmbuje śiñjitārāvotpatteraucityasadbhāvāt 
mañjubhirmanojñairmañjīraśiñjitairnūpurādijhaṇatkārairmanoharaṃ 
svabhaktānāṃ hṛdayāvarjakaṃ vipakṣāṇāṃ hṛdbhaṅgakāri ca | tathā 
durjayārivighātajanyenānandena pramodena sundaro 
bhāvisveśvaryapadalābhātmakasaubhāgyasūcakena vā harṣeṇa spṛhaṇīyo yaḥ 
purandaro devendrastena vijayakālocite bhagavatīcaraṇakamalārcanaprastāve muktāni 
nikṣiptāni mālyāni divyādbhutasurabhivicitrakusumapracayobhbhitasrajo yasmin tat | 
athavā ānandaḥ śrīputraḥ sundaraḥ kāmeśvaraḥ purandara indrastripurārirvā 
tairmuktamālyamiti pūjāvasare ābadhyamānapuṣpadāmakamityarthaḥ |

<p. 3>

etaduktaṃ bhavati bhagavatyāḥ śiñjānapādaprahāro yathā tadānīmindrasya 
vijayaheturabhūt tathedānīṃ manasi smaryamāṇaṃ 
tadgataratibhāvādibhirvibhāvyamānaṃ 
mañjīraśiñjitādibhiruddīpanairanubhāvyamānaṃ tadeva pādāmbujaṃ 
svāntaścarvyamāṇatāmāpadyamānaṃ mamāpi 
lokottarasāndrānandacamatkārātiśayollāsāya bhūyādityāśāste | atra 
śṛṅgāravīrayorāśrayaikyāt spaṣṭābhāsamānatvena saṃkaraḥ | 
caraṇavidyāsaṃpradāyarahasyaṃ sugopanīyatayā gurumukhāvagamyatayā ca 
noddhāṭyate | tadavagatau satyāmekavacanatvena kathanābhiprāyo jñāyeta | nāpi 
yugalatvābhidhānaprasiddhivirodhaḥ syāt || 1 || 

atha śrīdevīviṣayakāṇāṃ praṇatīnāmapi tadvidyāṃśopāsanaucityanuguṇāni 
phalāni nirdiśati(-) 

saundaryavibhramabhuvo bhuvanādhipatyasampattikalpataravastripure (!) jayanti |
ete kavitvakumudaprakarāvabodhapūrṇendavastvayi jagajjanani (!) praṇāmāḥ || 2 ||

he jagajjanani (!) tripure (!) trīṇi purāṇi śarīrabuddhiprāṇātmakāni yasyāstripurā 
tathāhi śrīcakraṃ puratrayarūpaṃ caturasraṣoḍaśadalāṣṭadalātmakaṃ prathamaṃ 
puraṃ caturdaśāradvidaśārātmakaṃ dvitīyaṃ aṣṭāratrikoṇabinducakrātmakaṃ 
tṛtīyaṃ puramiti rahasyaśāstreṣu nirṇītam | athantirāṇi tu prākproktāni 
yathāsthānamagre'pi vakṣyante | tvayi tvadviṣaye ete bhaktimamadbhaḥ 
prahvībhūtairasmābhirviniveditāḥ praṇāmā namaskṛtayo jayanti 
sarvotkarṣaphaladānaśālinaḥ santi | kīdṛśāḥ saundaryasya saubhāgyasya 
vibhramāṇāṃ śṛṅgārajanyamanovṛttyanavasthādīnāṃ vilāsānāṃ ca bhuva 
ākarā utpattisthānāni | yaduktam(-) 

cittavṛttyanavasthānaṃ śṛṅgārādvibhramo mataḥ | 

<p. 4>

iti | atra bahuvacanasya samāsāntargatatvāllabdhena ādinā vilāsalīlādayo 
hāvabhāvādayo'pi grāhyāḥ | tatra madarāgaharṣajanito viparyāso vibhramaḥ | 
yathā(-) animittamāsanādutthāyānyatra gamanam priyārabdhakathāmākṣipya 
sakhyā sahālāpaḥ mudhaiva ruṣitakrodhau puṣpādīnāṃ yāñcyā sahasā 
tatparityāgaḥ vastrābharaṇamālyānāmakāraṇataḥ khaṇḍanaṃ manana ca | kecittu 
yoṣitāṃ yauvanajo vikāro vibhrama ityāhuḥ | tatuktam(-) 

upadiśati kāminīnāṃ yauvanamada eva lalitāni |

iti | sarvatrātra śṛṅgārarasa eva kāraṇam | punaḥ kīdṛśāḥ | 
bhuvanānāmādhipatyaṃ sāmrājyaṃ tasya saṃpatteḥ samṛddheḥ 
kalpataravastajjanakāḥ tathā kavitvānāṃ kavikarmaṇāṃ 
aṣṭādaśalakṣaṇalakṣitānāṃ rūpakādidṛśyaśravyabhedairbheditānāṃ 
kāvyānāṃ śṛṅgārādirasasandarbhāṇāṃ tadrūpāṇāṃ kumudānāṃ 
kuvalayakusumānāṃ prakarāḥ kadambāni teṣāmavabodhe jñānavikāse 
pūrṇendavo rākāsudhāṃśavo vāgvilāsakāriṇa iti yāvat | 
ebhistribhirviśeṣaṇairidaṃ sūcyate yatkūṭatrayātmakasya mahātripurasundaryā 
mahāmantrasyopāsanayā trividhaphalalābhastatrādikūṭopāsanāyāṃ 
sarasvatīprasādātkavitvaśaktijananaṃ dvitīyakūṭopāsanāyāṃ 
tadadhipatikāmarājaprasādātsaundaryādijananaṃ tṛtīyopāsanāyāṃ 
tatprasādātsāmrājyalābhaḥ iti jñeyam | śaktikūṭasadhanādiphalaṃ tu 
vāmakeśvare(-)

pravakṣyāmi mahādevi (!) śaktikūṭasya sādhanam |
śaktibījasvarūpā tu sṛṣṭvā saṃharate yadā ||

sṛṣṭisaṃhāraparyantaṃ śarīre paricintayet |
tato bhavati deveśi (!) vainateya evāparaḥ ||

nāgānāṃ darśanād devi (!) jaḍīkaraṇakārakaḥ |
dehināmamṛtāsāradhīradhārādharopamaḥ ||

sthirakṛtrimaśaṃkādiviṣopaviṣanāśanaḥ |
duṣṭavyādhigrahānokaḍākinīrūpikāgaṇaiḥ ||

<p. 5>

bhūtapretapiśācaughaistrinetra iva dṛśyate |
athavā yena vidyeyaṃ paripūrṇā vicintyate ||

janmamaṇḍalahṛtpadmamukhamaṇḍalamadhyagā |
kevalaiva maheśāni (!) padmarāgasamaprabhā ||

tasyāṣṭaguṇamaiśvaryamacirātsaṃpravartate |
manasā saṃsmaratyasyā yadi nāmāpi sādhakaḥ ||

tadaiva mātṛcakrasyavidito bhavati priye |
yadaiva japate vidyāṃ mahātripurasundarīm ||

tadaiva mātṛcakrājñā sekrāmatyasya vigrahe |
sarvāsāṃ sarvasaṃsthānāṃ yoginīnāṃ bhavetpriyaḥ ||

putravatparameśāni (!) dhyānādeva hi sādhakaḥ |
yadā tu parameśāni (!) paripūrṇāṃ prapūjayet ||

prayacchanti tadaivāsya khecarīsiddhimuttamām |
catuḥṣaṣtiryatāḥ koṭyo yoginīnāṃ mahaujasām ||

cakrametatsamāśritya saṃsthitā vīravandite (!) |
ādau sambandhini pade madhye bījāṣṭakaṃ bahiḥ ||

kalāṃ dhyātvāṅganā raṅge jāyate'naṅgavatpriyaḥ |
karaśuddhyādividyānāmekaikaṃ parameśvari ||

rudrayāmalatantre tu karma proktaṃ mayā purā |
mādanairmadano bhūtvā pāśāṃkuśadhanuḥśaraiḥ ||

kṣobhayetsvargabhūrlokapātālatalayoṣitaḥ |
tathaiva śāktairdeveśi (!) tripurīkṛtavigrahaḥ ||

sādhayetsiddhagandharvadevavidyādharānapi |
tatra śāktā mahāvajraprastāre janitāḥ śarāḥ ||

mādanāstvādiparataḥ sarvādhastānniyojitāḥ |
ādyantago mahāpāśaḥ pauruṣeyaḥ prakīrtitaḥ ||

rudraśaktiḥ kuṇḍalākhyā māyā strīpāśa ucyate |
turīyamaruṇāvarge dvitīyamapi pārvati ||

<p. 6>

puṃstrīkodaṇḍayugalaṃ kāmāgnivyāpako'ṅkuśaḥ || iti || 2 ||

adhunā svātmani stutikartakatābhimānaṃ nirasyan stotṛṣu svakalanāyāṃ 
vrīḍāmāviṣkaroti(-) 

devi (!) stutivyatikare kṛtabuddhayaste vācaspatiprabhṛtayo'pi jaḍībhavanti |
tasmānnisargajaḍimā katamo'hamatra stotraṃ tava tripuratāpanapatni (!) kartum || 3 ||

he tripuratāpanapatni (!) tripuro nāma daityastasya tāpano dāhakaḥ śivastasya patnī 
tadaviyuktavapustatsambodhanaṃ he parabrahmamahiṣi (!) devi (!) 
śivādidharaṇyantajagatsargādikrīḍātatpare (!) te tava stutivyatikare 
tvadguṇavarṇānārūpastavanavyāpāre kṛtabuddhayastīkṣṇakuśāgradhiṣaṇā 
vidvāṃso vācaspatiprabhṛtayo bṛhaspatyādayo'pi jaḍībhavanti ajaḍā api santo 
jaḍāḥ saṃpadyante mūkībhavanti | yato vāco nivartante aprāpya manasā saha 
(śāṃḍi0 2-3) iti śruteḥ | teṣvapi tvadrūpāyā vācastvadicchāyā 
ṛte prasarābhāvāditi yāvat tatra mandamatīnāṃ mādṛśānāṃ kimu vaktavyaṃ 
api śabdena vedā apyasamarthāḥ iti | yataste vidhimukhena 
pratipādayitumudyatāśca sthūlārundhatīnyāyena taṭasthalakṣaṇarītyaiva satrapaṃ 
nirdiśanti | yaduktaṃ śivastave śrīmadācāryabhāskararāyapādai(-) 

sarvajñāḥ śrutayo'pi yāḥ paraśiva tvatprāṇarūpāḥ priyāḥ śṛṅgagrāhikayā 
trapābhṛta iva tvāṃ na prajalpanti tāḥ |
anyāneva tu netineti bahuśo vācā niṣedhanti cet 
ko'nyastvāmidamitthamityanuvadettatrāpi mādṛgjanaḥ ||

iti | granthakṛtāpi pañcame stave 

śrutīnāṃ mūrdhānaḥ prakṛtikaṭhināḥ komalatare |

<p. 7>

kathaṃ te vindante padakisalaye pārvati (!) padam || (ślo0 24)

ityuktam tasmāddhetoḥ atra vācaspatyādikṛtabuddhitvagaṇanāyāṃ prastutāyāṃ 
tava stotraṃ laukikaguṇānuvādaḥ sa ca ṣaḍvidhaḥ | taduktam(-) 

namaskārastathāśīśca siddhāntoktiḥ parākramaḥ |
vibhūtiḥ prārthanā ceti ṣaḍvidhaṃ stotralakṣaṇam ||

iti | pragītamantrasādhyakaguṇiniṣṭhaguṇābhidhānaṃ vaidikaṃ stotraṃ vā 
kartumahaṃ teṣāṃ vācaspatyādīnāṃ madhye katamo na ko'pi gaṇanāyārhaḥ 
ayamasāviti nirdhāraṇīyaḥ | yato nisargajaḍimā svabhāvata eva jāḍyayukto 
mandamatiḥ vācaspatyādīnāmapyavyāpārye vastuni vyāpāracikīrṣā 
cāñcalyānmama mahatī trapetyarthaḥ | yaduktaṃ śrīlalitāstavaratne(-) 

kastu kṣitau paṭīyānvastu stotuṃ śivāṅkavāstavyam |
astu cirantanasukṛtaiḥ prastutakāmyāya tanmama purastāt ||

iti || 3 || 

nanvevamaśakyastotrakaraṇe kathaṃ tava cikīrṣodyama ityāśaṅkya 
bhaktirasavaivaśyaṃ svasya dyotayati(-) 

mātastathāpi bhavatīṃ bhavatīvratāpavicchittaye stutimahārṇavakarṇadhāraḥ 
(stavamahārṇaveti pāṭhāntaram |) |
stotuṃ bhavāni (!) sa bhavaccaraṇārabindabhaktigrahaḥ kimapi māṃ mukharīkaroti || 4 
||

he mātarviśvajanani (!) bhavāni (!) bhavasya patni (!) tathāpi nisargato jaḍimnyapi bhave 
saṃsāre tīvrāṇāṃ duḥsahānāṃ tāpānāṃ trividhādhyātmikādīnāṃ kleśānāṃ 
vicchittaye vicchedaṃ kartuṃ bhavatīṃ tvāṃ stotuṃ sa svasaṃvedyo

<p. 8>

bhavaccaraṇārabindayostvatpādāmbujayorviṣaye 
bhaktiratiśayagāḍhasnehastadrūpo graha 
āveśastvatpādāmbujamakarandarasanāveśapāravaśyaṃ kimapyatarkitameva 
māṃ mukharīkaroti vācāṭayati haṭhānmāṃ stotuṃ prayojayati | kīdṛśaḥ 
stutirūpo yo mahārṇavo 
niravadhigāmbharyādibhirduravagāhyamānatvānmahāsāgarastatra karṇadhāraḥ 
pāraprāpakatvātpotavāhakaḥ yathā potavāhenābdheḥ pāre nīyate tadvanmāṃ 
mandamatimapi tvadbhaktireva stuteḥ pāraṃ nayediti bhāvaḥ | atha ca vāg 
māndyamapi tāvadeva sañjātāyāṃ tvatkaṭākṣadṛṣṭyāṃ vāk 
pravāhaprasaraṇanirodhāt | yaduktaṃ bhaṭṭanārāyaṇena(-) 

tvayyanicchati kaḥ śambho (!) śaktaḥ kubjayituṃ tṛṇam |
tvadicchānugrahītastu vahed brāhmīṃ dhuraṃ na kaḥ || (sta0 ci0 93) || 4 ||

nanu viṣṇuviriñcyādaya eva viśvasargādikāritvāt stutyāḥ 
nāhamityāśaṅkyāha(-) 

sūte jaganti bhavatī bhavatī bibharti jāgarti tatkṣayakṛte bhavatī bhavāni |
mohaṃ bhinatti bhavatī bhavatī ruṇaddhi līlāyitaṃ jayati citramidaṃ bhavatyāḥ || 5 ||

he bhavāni (!) bhavatī jaganti śivādikṣityantaṣaṭtriṃśattattvātmakāni sūte sṛjati | 
yato vā imāni bhūtāni jāyante yena jātāni jīvanti yatprayantyabhisaṃviśanti tad 
brahmeti (taitti0 3-1) śruteḥ | tasmādvā etasmādātmana ākāśaḥ 
saṃbhūta ākāśādvāyurvāyoragniragnerāpo'dbhyaḥ pṛthivīti 
kṣityantasargaśruteḥ (taitti0 2-1-1) | yathā 
sudīptātpāvakādvisphuliṅgāḥ sahasraśaḥ prabhavante sarūpāḥ | 
tathākṣarādvividhāḥ saumyabhāvāḥ prajāyante tatra caivāpi yāntīti śruteḥ | 
yathāgneḥ kṣudrā visphuliṅgā vyuccarantīti ca (bṛha0 2-1-20)
| yājñavalkyasmṛtirapi(-)

<p. 9>

niḥsaranti yathā lohapiṇḍāttaptāt sphuliṅgakāḥ |
sakāśādātmanastadvadātmānaḥ prabhavanti hi ||

iti | sṛjyaṃ tvantastādātmyenāvasthitameva anyathā 
tadviṣayaparāmarśānupapatteḥ yaduktam(-) 

svāminaścātmasaṃsthasya bhāvajātasya bāsanam |
astyeva na vinā tasmādicchāmarśaḥ pravartate || (ī0 pra0 1-5-10)

iti | sadevasaumyedamagra āsīt (chāṃ0 u0 6-2-1)
ekamevādvitīyaṃ brahma iti śruteḥ | (paiṅgalo0 1-1) agre'pi 
vakṣyate(-) 

śivādikṣityantaṃ trivalayatanoḥ sarvamudare tavāste (sa0 ja0 sta0 29> iti 
bahvasyāṃ prajāyeyeti tadātmānaṃ svayamakuruta iti śrutericchāmātreṇaiva 
kramatāratamyato viśvotpādanāt |

svecchayā svabhittau viśvamunmīlayati (pra0 hṛ0 sū0 2) iti
citiḥ svatantrā viśvasiddhiheturiti (pra0 hṛ0 sū0 1)
svecchayaiva jagatsarvaṃ nigiratyudgiratyapi 

tathā(-) jagaccitraṃ samālikhya svecchātūlikayātmani iti bhuvanāni 
śaivāgamaprakriyayā caturviṃśatyuttaradviśatasaṃkhyakāni prakriyāntarato 
bhūrbhuvaḥsvarlakṣaṇāni vā caturdaśa vā bhuvanādhvatmakāni sūte sṛjati 
bhavatyā brāhmīśaktirūpayānāviṣṭasya brahmaṇo 
jagatsarjanakriyāyāmaśaktatvāt śaktivihīnasya 
pāṣāṇādijaḍaprāyatvātspandābhāve tatra paramukhaprekṣitvātpāratantryaṃ 
suspaṣṭamato vastuto jagatsarjanādikaṃ bhavatyeva tattacchaktisvarūpā vidhatte 
ityarthaḥ | bhavatyeva tāni sṛṣṭāni bibharti dhārayati poṣayati ca 
arthādvaiṣṇavīśaktirbhūtvā tatkāryaṃ kurute | prāgvadviṣṇorapi 
tacchaktyāveśaṃ vinā svakāryasampādanāsāmarthyāt | atra bhavatīti 
padasyāsakṛdāmreḍanenāvadhāraṇārtho'vadhāryastena bhavatyeva nānyā 
ityarthaḥ | evamagrato'pi boddhavyam | tathā teṣāṃ sṛṣṭadhṛtapuṣṭānāṃ 
jagatāṃ

kṣayakṛte yathākālaṃ yathāsvaṃ ca saṃhartuṃ bījātmanā svasminnavasthātuṃ
