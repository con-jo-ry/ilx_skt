yad antaraṃ samudrasya goṣpadasya yad antaram |
tad antaraṃ hi dānasya śīlasya ca tad antaram || [1]
yaś ca varṣaśataṃ dadyād dānaṃ prītena cetasā |
yaś caikaṃ divasaṃ rakṣec chīlaṃ tasmād viśiṣyate || [2]
dānam aurabhrikeṇāpi caṇḍālenāpi dīyate |
yena vā tena vā śīlaṃ na śakyam abhirakṣitum || [3]
samṛddho 'pi pradānena mahādānapatir na saḥ |
śīlena tu viśuddhena mahādānapatir smṛtaḥ || [4]
bhavantu mauṇḍyena śikhābhir eva vā
vahantu vā snānajalākulā jaṭāḥ |
carantu vā naikavikalpavāsaso
viḍambanaiṣā yadi śīlavarjitaḥ || [5]
paretu gaṅgām upayātu vā hradān
sarasvatīṃ gacchatu puṣkarāṇi vā |
niṣevatāṃ naimiṣam etu vā gayāṃ
vipannaśīlo yadi niṣphalaḥ śramaḥ || [6]
na mālyagandho tathā hi vikīryate
pravāti gandho na tathānulepanāt |
anuttamaḥ śīlamayo yathā satāṃ
pravāti gandho divi ceha cāvyayaḥ || [7]
ihātmatuṣṭiḥ parataḥ śubhā gatir
yaśaḥ priyatvaṃ bahumāna eva ca |
sukhaṃ ca tat tad bahu dṛṣṭadhārmikaṃ
phalaṃ hi śīlasya maharṣayo viduḥ || [8]
rujāsu śīlaṃ paramaṃ hi bheṣajaṃ
bhayeṣu rakṣā vyasaneṣu bāndhavaḥ |
tamaḥsu dīpo viṣameṣu saṃkramo
mahaty agādhe maraṇāmbhasi plavaḥ || [9]
yo bhraṣṭaśīlavinayasya vināśakāle
trāsaḥ samāviśati śīlavato na so 'sti |
tasmāt prahṛṣṭahṛdayaḥ paralokam ante
yo gantum icchati sa rakṣatu śīlam eva || [10]
āsthāya śīlam amalaṃ nikhilaṃ yathāvat
prajñā śamaś ca manasaḥ paripākam eti |
tasmāj jarāmaraṇarogabhayād vimokṣaṃ
yaḥ prāptum icchati sa rakṣatu śīlam eva || [11]
