athānyaṃ sampravakṣ[y]āmi buddhaḍākavidhikramaṃ |
pūrvoktabuddhaḍākena āgamādikrameṣu ca |
āgamo na vinā (!) yogī buddhakolo bhaviṣyati | (= buddhakaulo!)
na tyajed āgamāṃ nītiṃ na tyajec ca triratnajāṃ |
buddhe dharme ca saṃghe ca śaraṇaṃ gacchāmi nityaśaḥ |
prajñā[ 120]pāramitāvalaṃbya yogasiddhis tu sidhyati |

nīlogravikṛtaṃ raudraṃ bhayasyāpi bhayaṅkaraṃ |
draṃṣṭrākarālavadanaṃ jaṭābaddhenduśekharaṃ |
viśvavajrajaṭābhāraṃ kapālair mālabhūṣitaṃ |
sarvālaṅkārasampūrṇā ṣaṇmudrādehajaṃ baṃ hūṃ ||
vyāghracarmanivasanaṃ | ardhaparyaṅkasusthitaṃ |
trinetraṃ raudravapusaṃ caturgrīvaṃ caturmukhaṃ |

sarvaduṣṭapadākrāntaṃ kleśabhairavayor mukhaṃ |

pādatalagatāḥ sarve vighnā duṣṭabhayānakāḥ |

vajraghaṇṭāsamāpannā vārāhyāliṅgaṇaṃ mukhaṃ(!) |
samatādvayayogena sarvadharmaika[ 120]rasatayā nāma dhutaguṇaṃ śrīmahāsukhavajraherukaṃ |
kapālakhaṭvāṅgadharaṃ dharmacakrajharjharaṃ pātraṃ śūlapāśāṅkuśakaraṃ muṇḍaṃ ca ḍamaruṃ tathā |
vāmadakṣiṇapāṇibhyāṃ naracarmapaṭordhvakaṃ ||
pūrve tu mātaṅgī ḍombikā caiva raja[kī] pukkasī tathā |
khaṭṭikī śāvarī caiva dhīvarī śavarī tathā |
etāṣṭau devīnāṃ yathānukramato nyaset ||
pītā nīlā tathā raktā dhūmradhūsara[varṇajā |]
raktaharitā dhūmravarṇā sitāṣṭamī tathā |
dvāreṣu ca tathā nyastā krodhaḍākinikās tathā |
herukābhā vajracaṇḍī ca vajrarākṣasīkottamā |
vajrabhairavikā raudrāṃ sarvadurdāntabhakṣikāṃ |
bhasmaśubh[r]ā ca haritā raktanīlograbhīṣaṇāṃ |
madhye cāṣṭau dharmacakrajharjharakapālaḍamaruka[r]ttikāḥ |
skandhopari cīvaradharāṃ |
dvāreṣu caturo devyā
kapālakhaṭvāṅgābhayacarmajharjharaḍama[ 121]rukā ||
