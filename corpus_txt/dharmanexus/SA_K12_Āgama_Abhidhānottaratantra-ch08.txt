(*32r3) athāparavidhir bhavati ||
padme aṣṭadale ramye viśvapatre manorame |
sūryamaṇḍalamadhye tu padmarājaṃ vicintayet |
karṇikāviśvavajraṃ ca pañcasūkaṃ caturmukham |
tatra varaṭakamadhye tu sūryamaṇḍalalāñchitam |
tatra pṛṣṭhe ālikāliṃ tu madhye rekhāṃ tu śūnyatā |
vajrasattvaṃ parāvṛtyātmāna herukottamām |
pañcavaktraṃ dvādaśabhujaṃ trinetraṃ vikaṭotkaṭam |
m-ālīḍhapadasaṃsthānaṃ pratyālīḍhapade sthitam |
bhairavaṃ kālarātriṃ ca (*32v) pādākrāntavo[?] likhet |
vyāghracarmanivasanaṃ karuṇākrodhavihvalam |
kapālamālāmakuṭamaṇḍitaṃ ardhacandraśekharam |
viśvavajrākrāntamaulinaṃ śṛṅgārādirasānvitam |
naraśiramālādhāriṇaṃ śatārdhena tu bhūṣitam |
kaṇṭhikārucakakuṇḍalaśiromaṇivibhūṣitam |
yajñopavītaṃ bhasmeti mudrāṣaṭkaṃ vibhāvayet |
vārāhyāliṅgitabhujadvaye kapālaghaṇṭā |
aparabhujadvaye mahābhairavacarmasārdrāmbaradharaḥ
tṛtīye dakṣiṇe karālavajrolālanatripatākābhinayena |
caturthe ca pañcasūkaṃ triśūlaṃ pañcame paraśum |
ṣaṣṭhe vajraḍamarukam ||
vāme tṛtīyabhuje khaṭvāṅgaṃ caturthe vajrapāśam |
pañcame karttikā ṣaṣṭhe rudraśiraḥ ||
agrato vārāhyāliṅgita bhagavatī tadvarṇā dvibhujaikavaktrā
trinetrā makuṭakeśā nagnā khaṇḍamaṇḍitamekhalā
vāmabhujāliṅganabodhicittaparipūrṇakapālām |
dakṣi(*33r)ṇe tarjayantī diśāṃ sarvāṃ duṣṭatarjanikarttikā |
kalpāgnir iva tejogrāṃ sravantī rudhirapriyāṃ [|]
jaṅghādvayaṃ samāveṣṭa mahāsukhasukhottamā |
vikṛtā raudrarūpī ca karuṇārāgavihvalā |
vajrasattvamakuṭaṃ bhāvayet vajraherukaṃ |
gagaṇagauraṃ pītaraktaṃ harita sitam eva ca |
raudraṃ smitaśṛṅgāra bībhatsaṃ śāntam eva ca |
dhātvāyatnaskandhāṃ bhāvayet parṣadas tathā |
ḍākinī lāmayaś caiva khaṇḍarohā tu rūpiṇī |
caturbhujā ekavaktrā trinetrā rudrarūpiṇī |
ardhaparyaṅkam āsīnā digambaradharā parā |
kapālamālāmukuṭā muktakeśā virājitā |
pañcamudrā devī kāmamokṣamo[?]sādhikā |
pūrvadakṣiṇapaścimottaraṃ vinyaset |
sitā pītā tathā raktā haritā madanotsukā |
kapālakhaṭvāṅgadharā ghaṇṭāḍamarukarttikā |
vidikṣu kalaśā catvāro jñānāmṛtaprapūritāḥ |
kapāla kṣīrasaṃpūrṇa (*33v) kalaśopari sthāpitam [|]
vāmāvarttena vinyaset [||]
jñānasattvaṃ samākṛṣya pūjāstutyā prakāṣayet |
cittavākkāyavinyāsaṃ bhāvayet paramaṃ padam |
hūṃ āḥ oṃ vibhāvayet |
japej janyanayā[?] mantraṃ sarvasiddhipradāyakām |
oṃ śrī vajra he he ru ru kaṃ hūṃ hūṃ phaṭ ḍākinījālasamvara svāhā ||
oṃ hrīḥ ha ha hūṃ hūṃ phaṭ || hṛdayopahṛdayaḥ ||
oṃ vajravairocanī hūṃ hūṃ phaṭ svāhā ||
oṃ sarvabuddhaḍākinīye varṇanīye hūṃ phaṭ svāhā || devyā hṛdayopahṛdayaḥ ||
oṃ ḍākinī hūṃ hūṃ phaṭ svāhā |
<oṃ lāme hūṃ hūṃ phaṭ svāhā |>
oṃ khaṇḍarohe hūṃ hūṃ phaṭ svāhā |
oṃ rūpiṇīye hūṃ phaṭ svāhā |
spharet krodhoghanirmāṇaṃ nivyādita[?]jagatrayaṃ [|]
japed bhāvanāyuktaṃ saṃhārya spharaṇātmakaḥ |
kāyavākcittavihāro traidhātukam aśeṣataḥ |
bhāvanotthānasamaye kavacadvaye m-uccaret |
oṃ haḥ hṛdaye vajrasattvaḥ |
namaḥ hi śirasi vairocanaḥ [|]
svāhā <hūṃ> śikhāyāṃ padmanarteśvaraḥ |
vau(*34 missing, henceforth )ṣaṭ he skandhadvaye herukaḥ |
hūṃ hūṃ hoḥ cakṣuṣo vajrasūryaḥ |
phaṭ haṃ sarvāṅgeṣu paramāśvaḥ |
oṃ vaṃ nābhau vajravārāhī |
hāṃ yāṃ hṛdaye yāminī |
hrīṃ moṃ vaktre mohanī |
hreṃ hrīṃ śirasi saṃcālanī |
hūṃ hūṃ śikhāyāṃ saṃtrāsanī |
phaṭ hūṃ caṇḍikā sarvāṅgeṣv astraḥ ||      ||
iti bhavasamasamatayā sarvabhāvasvabhāvo
mantrasaṃsthānayogī yathāsukhaṃ viharet ||      ||
ity abhidhānottara pīṭhaparvakramapaṭala pañcamaḥ ||      ||
