yato vettālaḍākinyā tato āryatārikā
yato caṇḍālaḍākinyā tato buddhalocanā
siddhadūtī tu catvāraḥ dehasarvavikurvaṇam
śuddhakṣetrādikṣetrasya viśeṣaṃ dāna varddhate
śūdradvekasahasrāṇi śuciviprasya m-ekavat
vipraṃ dvekasahasraṃ vai bhikṣu-m-eka prayuñjayet
bhikṣudvekasasāhasraṃ tu raja-m-ekopamena tu
rajasatasahasrāṇi jñānīnām ekam eva tu
jñānīdvekasahasraṃ vai ācārya-m-eka kalpitam
evaṃ kṣetrasukṣetrasya kuto dānaṃ na dadyavān
tyāgamūlāni dharmasya sukṣetraṃ bīja ropayet
ānantaryantatpāpasya rajadarśana muktayet
praviṣṭā mantratattvasya śuddhāvāsopapadyate
dāpakaṃ śilpayogīnāṃ saṃbodhikramaḥ śīghrataḥ
tribhuvanajvalitamūrti anurāyaṇavinayavatī
raśmisahasratīrṇe daśasūryasahasravatī
bahuviharua veaṇa aṇurāyaṇa sattavatī
naccaï yogāmbaru parameśvaru līlagatī
hūṃkāragītikāṃkāraṃ vajrayoginī rañjayet
maṅgalair madhurair nādyair gāyate susamāhitaḥ
sahasradvādaśasaṃkṣepaṃ catuḥpīṭhaṃ tu vi 30vstarata
tasya saṃgrahavastūni piṇḍasārasamuccayam
na cchandatarkavākyasya artha arthena saṃgraham
gopitā yogatattvena mlecchabhāṣeṇa bhāṣitam
iti catuṣpīṭhamahāyoginītantrarāje sarvayoganṛtyageyapūjāvidhivistaramaṇḍalopāyikā saptaviṃśatimaḥ(?)||
kṛtir iyaṃ maṇḍalopāyikā CARYĀVRATĪPĀDĀNĀM iti ||
