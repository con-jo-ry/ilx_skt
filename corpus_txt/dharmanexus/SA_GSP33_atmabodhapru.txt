ācāryaśaṅkarakṛtam
ātmabodhaprakaraṇam
(saṭīkam)
ātmabodhaḥ
tapobhiḥ kṣīṇapāpānāṃ śāntānāṃ vītarāgiṇām
mumukṣūṇāmapekṣyo'yamātmabodho vidhīyate ||1
ātmabodhaṭīkā
oṃ namo durgāyai
tatra bhagavān śaṃkarācārya uttamādhikāriṇāṃ vedāntaprasthānatrayaṃ nirmāya tadavalokanāsamarthānāṃ mandabuddhināmanugrahāya vedāntasiddhāntasaṃgrahamātmabodhākhyaprakaraṇaṃ didarśayiṣuḥ pratijānīte--tapobhiriti
kṛcchracāndrāyaṇanityanaimittikānuṣṭhānarūpaistapobhiḥ kṣīṇāni pāpāni rāgādyantaḥkaraṇadoṣā yeṣāṃ teṣāṃ, nityanaimittikaireva kurvāṇo duritakṣayamiti vacanāt
at eva śāntānāmakṣobhitāśayāṇāṃ, vītarāgiṇāmihāmutraphalabhogecchārahitānāṃ mumukṣūṇāṃ saṃsāragranthibhedane vitataprayatātmanāṃ yathoktasādhanasampannānāmayamātmabodho vidhīyate vidhimukhenāvaśyakatayā pratipādyate ityarthaḥ
1
bodho'nyasādhanebhyo hi sākṣānmokṣaikasādhanam
pākasya vahnivajjñānaṃ vinā mokṣo na sidhyati ||2
nanu tapoyogamantrakarmayāgādyanekasādhaneṣu satsu mokṣaṃ prati bodha eva kimiti prādhānyena ucyate ityata āha--bodho'nyeti
tapoyogamantrakarmayāgādīni sādhanāṇi paramparākrameṇa jñānadvārā mokṣaṃ sādhayanti, jñānantu svajanmamātrādevājñānaṃ niḥśeṣaṃ nāśayitvā mumukṣuṃ svārājye'bhiṣecayati, ato'nyasādhanebhyo jñānasya prādhānyamuktam
tadeva dṛṣṭāntena draḍhayati--pākasyeti
yathā loke pacanakriyā kāṣṭhajalabhāṇḍādisādhaneṣu satsvapi vahniṃ vinā na sidhyati tadvat jñānaṃ vinā mokṣo na sidhyatītyartha
2
avirodhitayā karma nāvidyāṃ vinivartayet
vidyā'vidyāṃ nihantyeva tejastimirasaṃghavat ||3
nanu karmaṇāṃ vicitraśāktitvāt karmaivājñānaṃ nāśayiṣyati kimātmajñānenetyāśaṃkyāha--avirodhitayeti spaṣṭārthaḥ
3
paricchinna ivājñānāttannāśe sati kevalaḥ
svayaṃ prakāśate hyātmā meghāpāye'ṃśumāniva ||4
nanu pratiśarīramātmā paricchinna ivopalamyate kathaṃ kaivalyamityāha 'paricchinna' iti
paripūrṇasyādvitīyasyātmano'jñānavikalpitoccāvacaśarīrādhyāsakṛtaparicchinnatvameva na tu tattvadṛṣṭyā, ato vivekenāvivekahānāt ātmā kevala eva prakāśate meghāvaraṇābhāve bhānuvadityarthaḥ
4
ajñānakaluṣaṃ jīvaṃ jñānābhyāsādvinirmalam
kṛtvā jñānaṃ svayaṃ naśyejjalaṃ katakareṇuvat ||5
nanvātmajñānātadvaitasiddhirayuktā vṛttijñānena dvaitasiddherityata āha--'ajñānakaluṣamiti'
ajñānakāryabhūtairahaṃkārādibhiḥ kaluṣīkṛtaṃ katṛtvabhoktṛtvādyabhimāninaṃ jīvaṃ, jñānaṃ cirakālābhyāsādajñānahānadvārā tatkāryāntaḥkaraṇādikamaśeṣato nāśayitvā svayamapi naśyatyajñānakāryatvādvṛtteḥ tatpratibimbitaṃ jñānamātmābhāsatvādātmanyeva ekībhavati; tadānīmadvaya evātmā prakāśate, yathā katakareṇurakhilaṃ jalaṃ nirmalaṃ kṛtvā svayamapi naśyati tadvadityarthaḥ
5
saṃsāro svapnatulyo hi rāgadveṣādisaṃkulaḥ
svakāle satyavadbhāti prabodhe satyasanbhavet ||6
nanvaparokṣatayānubhūyamānasya saṃsārasya kathamatyantāsattvaṃ pratyakṣavirūddhamityāśaṃkya svapnadṛṣṭāntena tasya mithyātvaṃ sādhayati--'saṃsāra' iti
rāgadveṣādibhiḥ saṃsāraḥ saṃkulāyamānaḥ, svakāle svasya kāraṇabhūtājñāne sthite sati tāvanmithyābhūto'pi saṃsāraḥ sannevāvabhāti, prabodhe sati vākyārthaśravaṇādātmajñānotpattau asanneva bhavati
atra dṛṣṭāntaḥ svapnatulya iti
svapno'pi yāvannidrānubhūyate tāvanmithyābhūto'pi satya iva bhāti, prabodhe nidrānāśe sati svapno mithyā bhavati, tadvat saṃsāra ityarthaḥ
6
tāvat satyaṃ jagadbhāti śuktikārajataṃ yathā
yāvanna jñāyate brahma sarvādhiṣṭhānamadvayam ||7
jaganmithyātvaṃ dṛṣṭāntena draḍhayati--tāvaditi
yathā yāvannīlapṛṣṭhatrikoṇādiviśeṣajñānaṃ notpadyate tāvadrajatajñānaṃ satyamivānubhūyate, tathā sarvādhiṣṭḥānabhūtaṃ saccidānandādvayaṃ brahma yāvanna jñāyate sākṣānnānubhūyate tāvajjagadākāreṇa pariṇataḥ saṃsāraḥ satya iva bhrāntyā dṛśyate ityarthaḥ
7
upādāne'khilādhāre jaganti parameśvare
sargasthitilayān yānti budbudānīva vāriṇi ||8
saccidātmanyanusyūte nitye viṣṇau prakalpitāḥ
vyaktayo vividhāḥ sarvā hāṭake kaṭakādivat ||9
sarvaṃ dṛśyaṃ prapañcajātaṃ brahmaṇyeva māyayā kalpitamiti pratipādayati--saccidātmanīti
satyajñānānandātmani nitye sarvānusyūte vyāpake brahmaṇi sarvā vyaktayo devatiryaṅnarasthāvarajaṅgamādayo'pi vividhā api vyaktayaḥ sarvopādānabhūte parameśvare hātāke hemni kaṭakamukuṭādaya iva kalpitā eva, brahmaṇo'bhinnā ityarthaḥ
9
yathākāśo hṛṣīkeśo nānopādhigato vibhuḥ
tadbhedādbhinnavadbhāti tannāśe kevalo bhavet ||10
uktamarthamākaśadṛṣṭāntena draḍhayati--yathākāśa iti
hṛṣīkeśo manaḥprabhṛtisarvendriyapravartako niyāmako vā, vibhuḥ sarvagataḥ parameśvaraḥ svamāyākalpitanānāvidhopādhiṣu pratibimbitaḥ sannāneva bhāti, tannāśe sati brahmātmaikyajñānena taṣāṃ māyāparikalpitānāṃ nāśāt kevala eva bhāti, yathākāśo ?bopādhivaśānnāneva bhāti upādhilayādeka eva bhāti tadvadityarthaḥ
10
nanopādhivaśādeva jātināmāśramādayaḥ
ātmanyāropitāstoye rasavarṇādibhedavat ||11
nanu sarvadeheṣu tattajjātyādibhiranuviddhasya kathaṃ nityatvamucyate tatrāha--nānopādhīti
pūrvoktanānopādhivaśādevātmani jātināmāśramādaya āropitā māyāparikalpitā na tu svataḥ, yathā svabhāvamadhuraṃ jalaṃ kaṭukaṣāyāmlaraktapītādidravyopetaṃ sat tadrupaṃ bhajati na tu svarūpatastadvadityarthaḥ
11
pañcīkṛtamahābhūtasaṃbhavaṃ karmasañcitam
śarīraṃ sukhaduḥkhānāṃ bhogāyatanamucyate ||12
ātmano'vidyāparikalpitopādhitraya māha--pañcīkṛteti
pañcānāṃ bhūtānāṃ pratyekaṃ dvidhā vibhacya tatraikaikamardhaṃ caturdhā samaṃ vibhacya svāṃśaṃ parityacyetareṣu yojanaṃ pañcīkaraṇam; evaṃ pañcīkṛtebhyaḥ sthūlapṛthivyādimahābhūtebhyaḥ sambhavo yasya tat karmasaṃcitaṃ prārabdhakarmabhiḥ sañcitaṃ racitaṃ sthūlaśarīraṃ sukhaduḥkhabhogānāmāyatanaṃ bhogasthānaṃ pratyagātmana iti
12
pañcaprāṇamanobuddhidaśendriyasamanvitam
apañcīkṛtabhūtoththaṃ sūkṣmāṅgaṃ bhogasādhanam ||13
sūkṣmopādhimāha--pañcaprāṇeti
pañcaprāṇā ekasyaiva mahāprāṇasya prāṇādayo vṛttayaḥ, jñānendriyakarmendriyabhedenendriyāṇi daśa, manaḥśabdena saṃkalpavikalpātmakāntaḥkaraṇavṛtti buddhirniścayātmikā, etaiḥ saṃyuktamapañcīkṛtasūkṣmabhūtoththaṃ sūkṣmabhūtebhyo jātaṃ sūkṣmāṅgaṃ liṅgaśarīraṃ sukhaduḥkhabhogasādhanamityarthaḥ
13
anādyavidyānirvācyā kāraṇopādhirucyate
upādhitritayādanyamātmānamavadhārayet ||14
tṛtīyaṃ kāraṇopādhimāha--anādyavidyeti
sadasadbhrāmanirvācyā'nādibhūtā kāraṇopādhirūcyate, sā ca vyaṣṭisamaṣṭibhedena sthūlasūkṣmaprapañcayoḥ kāraṇabhūtatvāt kāraṇopādhirūcyate
upādhitrayakathanaprayojanamāha-upādhitritayādanyamiti
tatsākṣiṇamātmānamavadhārayejjānīyādityarthaḥ
14
pañcakoṣādiyogena tattanmaya iva sthitaḥ
śuddhātmā nīlavastrādiyogena sphaṭiko yathā ||15
nanvannamayādyeṣu koṣeṣu vyāpya tattanmayatayā sthitasya kuto nityamuktatvamityāśaṅkrāha--pañcakoṣādīti
annamaya-prāṇamaya-manomaya-vijñānamayānandamayāḥ pañcakoṣāḥ, ādiśabdenāśanādīnāṃ grahaṇam, etauryogena mithyātādātmyena tattanmaya iva sthita ātmā śuddho na tu teṣu vastuto rañcanāviśiṣṭo'pi
sa vā eṣa puruṣo'nnarasamaya ityādibhiḥ sūkṣmavastudarśanārthamevopādhisthaṃ brahma sūcayati na tu tattvadṛṣṭyā, yathā nirmalasphaṭiko nīlapītavastrādiyogena tattanmaya iva bhāti vastuto nāsti rañjanā tadvadityarthaḥ
15
vapustuṣādibhiḥ koṣairyuktaṃ yuktyavadhātataḥ
ātmānamāntaraṃ śuddhaṃ viviñcyāttaṇḍulaṃ yathā ||16
asaṅgātmasvarūpajñānavivecanaprakāramāha-vapuriti
vapuḥ sthūlaśarīraṃ tadiva tuṣastadādibhiryuktaṃ taṇḍulamiva pañcakoṣaistāvat sthitamāntaramātmānaṃ yuktyavadhātataḥ yuktirūpāvahananāt śuddhaṃ viviñcyāt vivekena jānīyādityarthaḥ
16
sadā sarvagato'pyātmā na savatrāvabhāsate
buddhāvevāvabhāsate svaccheṣu pratibimbavat ||17
nanvātmā sarvagatastarhi kiṃ sarvatra sarvairna dṛśyate ityatrāha--sadā sarvagata iti
ātmā sarvagato'pi sarvatra vyāpya sthito'pi jaḍairbahivṛttibhirakṣairna prakāśyate, rāgādibhiraviddhāyāṃ buddhāveva prakāśate na sarvatra, yathā pratibimbaḥ svaccheṣveva darpaṇādiṣu dṛśyate nānyatra tadvadityarthaḥ
17
dehendriyamanobuddhiprakṛtibhyo vilakṣaṇam
tadvṛttisākṣiṇaṃ vidyādātmānaṃ rājavat sadā ||18
deheṣvavasthitasyāpyātmana audāsīnyaṃ rājapuruṣadṛṣṭāntenāha-dehendriyeti
dehendriyamanobuddhiprakṛtayaḥ kiṃbhūtāḥ? jaḍāḥ pariṇāmiṇyo dṛśyāśceti
dehendriyamanobuddhiprakṛtibhyo jaḍadṛśyapariṇāmibhyo vilakṣaṇamātmānaṃ tadvṛttīnāṃ sākṣitayā sthitaṃ, yathā rājā svanagare sthito mantriṣu pravartamāneṣu svayaṃ sākṣitayā tān paśyan tiṣṭhati, tadvadeva jānīyādityarthaḥ
18
vyāpṛteṣvindriyeṣvātmā vyāpārīvāvivekīnām
dṛśyate'bhreṣu dhāvatsu dhāvanniva yathā śaśī ||19
nanvātmano'vikāritvamayuktaṃ yataḥ sarvadā vyāpāravān dṛśyate, tatrāha--vyāpṛteṣviti
indriyādiṣu vyāpṛteṣu satsu ātmā vyāpārīvāvivekināṃ guruśāstrādyupadeśarahitānāṃ dṛśyate na tu tattvadṛśām
yathā meghakhaṇḍeṣu vāyuvaśād dhāvatsu candro'pi dhāvatīva lokairdṛśyate na tu candramā dhāvati tathā ātmāpītyarthaḥ
19
ātmacaitanyamāśritya dehendriyamanodhiyaḥ
svakīyārtheṣu vartante sūryālokaṃ yathā janāḥ ||20
nanu dehendriyamanobuddhayaścetanā dṛśyante, kimātmanā, tatrāha--ātmacaitanyamiti
nityajñānasvarūpamātmacaitanyamāśritya dehendriyamanobuddhyādayaḥ svakīyārtheṣu svaviśayeṣu vartante, yathā prāṇinaḥ sūryaprakāmāśritya svavyāpāreṣu vyavaharanti
ataścaitanyamātmana eva, dehendriyādayo jaḍa evetyarthaḥ
20
dehendriyaguṇām karmāṇyamale saccidātmani
adhyasyantyavivekena gagane nīlatādivat ||21
nanvahaṃ jāto'smi bālyakaumārādyavasthāmanubhavāmī tyādyanavaratamanubhūyamānasyātmanaḥ kathamavikāritvamityata āha--dehendriyeti
dehendriyamanobuddhidharmāścaturṇāṃ sattvādīn karmāṇi ca karmendriyavyāpārān mithyātādātmyena sthite'male'saṅgarūpe saccidānandātmake paramātmanyeva etatsāmīpyādavivekato'dhasyanti yathā gagane nīlatādi
ataste ātmadharmā na bhavanti kintu dehādīnāmevetyarthaḥ
21
ajñānānmānasopādheḥ kartṛtvādīni cātmani
kalpyante'mbugate candre calanādi yathāmbhasaḥ ||22
nanu kartṛtvabhoktṛtvādikamātmani sadaivānubhūyamānatvād durvāramityāśaṃkya tadapi tathaiva mithyetyāha--ajñānāditi
ajñānānmānasopādhe stasyaiva kartṛtvādayo dharmā atattvajñānavaśādātmani kalpyante, yathāmbhasaścalanādikamambuni pratiphalite candre'vivekataḥ kalpyate tadvadityarthaḥ
22
rāgecchāsukhaduḥkhādi buddhau satyāṃ pravartate
susuptau nāsti tannāśe tasmādbuddhestu nātmanaḥ ||23
icchādveṣasukhaduḥkhādīn (ātmanaḥ) manyante vaiśeṣikādayaḥ, tannirākaroti--rāgeccheti
rāgecchādisukhaduḥkhaprayatnādikaṃ sarvaṃ buddhereva na tvātmanaḥ
katham? jāgratsvapnayorbuddhau satyāṃ rāgādi pravartate, susuptau tasyā buddhernāśe kāraṇe laye na pravartate, tasmāt buddhereva dharmā na tvātmana ityarthaḥ
23
prakāśo'rkasya toyasya śaityamagneryathoṣṇatā
svabhāvaḥ saccidānandanityanirmalatātmanaḥ ||24
tarhi kīdṛgātmasvabhāva ityāśaṃkyātmasvabhāvamupadiśati--prakāśa iti
yathā prakāśa evārkasya svabhāvaḥ, toyasya śaityam, agneruṣṇatā tathātmano'pi saccidānandanityanirmalatā svabhāvaḥ svarūpamityarthaḥ
24
ātmanaḥ saccidaṃśaśca buddhervṛttiriti dvayaṃ
saṃyojya cāvivekena jānāmīti pravartate ||25
yadyavikāryātmā tarhi jānāmīti pratyayaḥ kathamityapekṣāyāmāha--ātmana iti
pratyagātmanaḥ saccidaṃśaḥ, aṃśa ivāṃśa ātmābhāso buddhivṛttiścaitadda yamavivekena saṃyojya, yadyapyasaṅgasyātmanaḥ kenāpi saṃyogo nāstyeva tathāpyajñānavaśānmelanaṃ saṃbhāvya, jānāmīti pratyayo'haṃkāravṛttiḥ pravartate
kevale pratyagātmani na kaścidvikāro'stīti bhāsaḥ
25
ātmano vikriyā nāsti buddherbodho na jātviti
jīvaḥ sarvamalaṃ jñātvā jñātā draṣṭeti muhyati ||26
etadeva spaṣṭayati--ātmano vikriyā nāstīti
ātmā svabhāvādeva vikriyārahitaḥ, buddheḥ kadācidapi bodhaśaṃkaiva nāsti
tathāpi jīvaḥ sābhāsāhaṃkāraḥ--sābhāsāntaḥkaraṇaḥ sarvaṃ jñātvāhaṃ jñātā draṣṭeti muhyatibhrānto bhavatītyarthaḥ
26
rajjusarpavadātmānaṃ jīvaṃ jñāvaṃ jñātvā bhayaṃ vahet
nāhaṃ jīvaḥ parātmeti jñātaścennirbhayo bhavet ||27
ātmā svayameva svasminnavivekena anyadharmānāropya bibhetītyāha--rajjusarpavaditi
yathā mandāndhakārādau sarpāropaṃ kṛtvā bhayakampādikaṃ vahedevamātmāpi svasmin jīvatvamāropya svaṃ sadvitīyaṃ paricchinnaṃ jñātvā bhayaṃ vahed duḥkhasāgare nimagno bhavati
yadā śāstrācāryapratipāditena śāstrārthajñānamutpannaṃ bhavati tadā vivekacakṣuṣā svamātmānamakhaṇḍānandajñānaikarūpaṃ saṃpaśyan tato nāhaṃ jīvaḥ paramātmā'hamiti jñātvā bhayaduḥkharahitaḥ sukhī bhavet
27
ātmāvabhāsayatyeko buddhyadīnīndriyāṇi ca
dīpo ghaṭādivat svātmā jaḍaistairnāvabhāsyate ||28
nanvātmā atyantasāmīpyakairbuddhimano'haṃkārādibhiḥ kathaṃ nāvalokyate--na jñāyate tatrāha--ātmeti
ātmā kevala eva sannapi buddhimano'haṃkārādīnyavabhāsayati, tai jaḍairmanādibhirātmā nāvabhāsyate, yathā eka eva dīpo ghaṭādīn sarvānapyavabhāsayati, tairghaṭādibhirdīpo nāvabhāsyate, tadvadityarthaḥ
28
svabodhe nātyabodhecchā bodharūpatayātmanaḥ
na dīpasyānyadīpecchā yathā svātmaprakāśane ||29
ātmā buddhyahaṃkārādibhirnāvabhāsyate yadi tarhi ātmajñāne jñānāntareṇa bhavitavyamityāśaṃkyāha--svabodhe iti
ātmano bodharūpatvāt svabodhe nitye sati, tasyātmano bodhāya jñānāntarāpekṣā nāstyeva, anubhavarūpatvāt, yathā dīpasya svaprakāśakāle dīpāntarāpekṣā nāsti tadvadityarthaḥ
29
niṣidhye nikhilopādhīnneti netīti vākyataḥ
vidyādaikyaṃ mahāvākyairjīvātmaparamātmanoḥ ||30
yadi buddhyādibhirātmā jñēyo na bhavet tarhi ātmajñāne upāyāntaraṃ vācyam, sa upāyaḥ ka ityāha--niṣidhyeti
athāta ādeśo neti netītyatannirasanavedavākyai nikhilopādhīnniṣidhya anātmatvena jñātvā taṃ nirasya 'tattvamasi' 'ayamātmā brahme' tyādi mahāvākyairlakṣaṇayā brahmātmaikyajñānaṃ vidyāt prāpnuyāt
anena prakāreṇa jñātavyamityarthaḥ
30
āvidyakaṃ śarīrādi dṛśyaṃ budbudavat kṣaram
etadvilakṣaṇaṃ vidyādahaṃ brahmeti nirmalam ||31
ātmasvarūpajñāne atannirasanābhāve kā kṣatirityāśaṃkya atannirasanābhāve ātmā jñātumaśakyaḥ, yathā nāyaṃ sarpa ityāropitasyāpakaraṇābhāve rajjujñānaṃ notpadyate tadvadanātmanirasanābhāve ātmā na jñāyate ityāśayavānāha--āvidyakamiti
dehendriyādi sarvaṃ dṛśyajātamāvidyakaṃ dṛśyatvāt, budbudavat kṣaraṃ naśvaram, etadvilakṣaṇaṃ nirmalaṃ nirupādhikaṃ nityamekaṃ saccidānandātmakaṃ brahmaivāhamasmīti vidyāt; tataḥ kṛtakṛtyo bhavatītyarthaḥ
31
dehānyatvānna me janma jaḍākārśyalayādayaḥ
śabdādiviṣayaiḥ saṅgo nirindriyatayā na ca ||32
evamuktarītyātmānamadvayānandaṃ vākyārthajñānenāparokṣatayā jñātvā svānubhavaviṣṭambhenātmanyevātmanā mananaprakāramāha pañcabhiḥ ślokaiḥ--dehānyatvāditi
jaḍadṛśyatvādisvabhāvasthūlaśarīrānyatvāt 'me' mama janmajaḍākārśyādayo na santi, indriyādibhirasaṅgatvācchabdādiviṣayabhogaiḥ saṅgānubhavo nāstyeva
32
amanastvānna me duḥkharāgadveṣabhayādayaḥ
aprāṇo hyamanā śubhra ityādi śrutiśāsanāt ||33
amanastvāditi
athamindriyaprāṇamanasā nāvabhāsate ityāśaṃkya śrutiṃ pramāṇayati aprāṇa iti spaṣṭārthaḥ
33
nirguṇo niskriyo nityo nirvikalpo nirañjanaḥ
nirvikāro nirākāro nityasukto'smi nirmalaḥ ||34
guroḥ prasādāt svānubhavasiddhamarthamabhinayati 'nirguṇa' ityādibhistribhiḥ ślokaiḥ
ahaṃ nirguṇo buddhyabhāvāt, niṣkriya indriyādyabhāvāt, nityo'parokṣatvāt, nirvikalpo vikalpābhāvāt, nirañjano'saṅgatvāt, nirvikāro niravayavatvāt, nityamukto bandhābhāvāt, nirmalo'vidyātatkāryarahitatvāt
34
ahamākāśavatsarvaṃ bahirantargato'cyutaḥ
sadā sarvasamaḥ śuddho niḥsaṅgo nirmalo'calaḥ ||35
nanu pūrvoktahetubhirevaṃ viśiṣṭasvarūpaṃ jñānaṃ siddhamityuktaṃ tat kiṃviśiṣṭamityāśakyāha--ahamākāśavaditi
ahamākāśavat sarvasya dṛśyajaḍasya bahirantargato'cyutaḥ cyutirahitaḥ, sadā sarvasamo viṣameṣvapi samatayā vyāpya sthitaḥ, tathā śuddho nirlepaḥ, acalaḥ pūrṇasya calanābhāvāt
35
nityaśuddhavimuktaikamakhaṇḍānandamavyayam
satyaṃ jñānamanantaṃ yat paraṃ brahmāhameva tat ||36
nityaśuddheti
ekamakhaṇḍānandaṃ dvitīyābhāvādadvayaṃ satyaṃ jñānamanantaṃ yat paraṃ brahmeti śrutyā pratipādyate tadeva tat paraṃ brahmaivāhamasmīti svarūpaṃ kathitam
36
evaṃ nirantarakṛtā brahmaivāsmīti vāsanā
haratyavidyāvikṣepān rogāniva rasāyanam ||37
pūrvaślokoktamananaṃ cirakālābhyastaṃ sat kiṃ puruṣārtha sādhayatītyāha--evaṃ nirantarakṛteti
evamuktaprakāreṇa brahmaivāhamasmīti nairantaryeṇa cirakālābhyastā kāsanā avidyākṛtavikṣepānniḥśeṣato nāśayati, yathā rasāyanamauṣadhaṃ cirakālasevitaṃ sad rogānniḥśeṣato nāśayati tadvadityarthaḥ
37
viviktadeśa āsīno virāgo vijitendriyaḥ
bhāvayedekamātmānaṃ tamanantamananyadhīḥ ||38
brahmānusandhāne sādhananiyamānāha--viviktadeśa iti
janasaṃbandhavarjite deśe āsīnaḥ sukhopaviṣṭaḥ, virāgaḥ sarvatra gatābhilāṣo'ta eva nirjitānīndriyāṇi yena saḥ, anantamaparicchinnamekamadvayaṃ tamātmānam, ananyadhīrbrahmaṇyeva sthitā niścayātmikā buddhiryasya tādṛśo bhāvayeddhyāyet
38
ātmanyevākhilaṃ dṛśyaṃ pravilāpya dhiyā sudhīḥ
bhāvayedekamātmānaṃ nirmalākāśavat sadā ||39
dṛśyaprapañce jāgrati sati kathametattattvabhāvanā sambhavatī tyata āha--ātmanyeveti
akhilaṃ dṛśyajātaṃ sudhīḥ śuddhāntaḥkaraṇaḥ san śuddhayā dhiyā ātmanyeva samādhisamaye pravilāpya prakarṣeṇa nāśayitvā tadā dvitīyābhāvādekamātmānaṃ sajyotiṣkākāśavad bhāvayedavatiṣṭhet
39
rūpavarṇādikaṃ sarvaṃ vihāya paramārthavit
paripūrṇacidānandasvarūpeṇāvatiṣṭhate ||40
paramārthadarśī munirnāmarūpavarṇādikaṃ sarvaṃ vihāya paripūrṇajñānānandarūpa evāvatiṣṭhate ityata āha--rūpavarṇādikamiti spaṣṭārthaḥ
40
jñātṛjñānajñeyabhedaḥ pare nātmani vidyate
cidānandaikarūpatvāddīpyate svayameva hi ||41
nanu mātrāditripuṭijñānaṃ vartamānamupalabhyate kathamekatvabhāvanetyāśaṃkya paramātmani bhedo nāstyevetyāha--jñātriti
jñātṛjñānajñeyabhedaḥ kalpita ātmanyababhāti, jñānānandaikarupatvādātmā svayameva dīpyate prakāśate
41
evamātmāraṇau dhyānamathane satataṃ kṛte
uditāvagatijvālā sarvājñānendhanaṃ dahet ||42
evaṃ viśuddhavastudhyātuḥ pratyakṣaphalamāha--evamātmeti
ātmaiva pūrvo'raṇirantaḥkaraṇamevottarāraṇiḥ dhyānena tayormathane sarvadā kṛte tatroditā jvālārūpāvagatirjñānaṃ vidyā ajñānamevendhanaṃ mūlājñānaṃ niḥśeṣataḥ saparikaraṃ nāśayati; tadā mumukṣuḥ svārājyo'bhiṣiktaḥ kṛtakṛtyo bhavatītyarthaḥ
42
aruṇeneva bodhena pūrvaṃ saṃtamase hṛte
tata āvirbhavedātmā svayamevāṃśumāniva ||43
jīvaparayoratyantaikyajñānenāvidyāparikalpite saṃtamase niḥśeṣamapagate sati paramātmā svayamevāvirbhavedityāha--aruṇeti
aruṇaprakāśenādau andhakāre kṣaye'ṃśumān yathāvirbhavati tathātmāpītyarthaḥ
43
ātmā tu satataṃ prāpto'pyaprāptavadavidyayā
tannāśe prāptavadbhāti svakanṭhābharaṇaṃ yathā ||44
ātmā sarvadā parokṣasvabhāvatvādaprāpta eva, sa ātmā kadā prāpto bhavediti śiṣyābhiprāyamāśaṃkyāha --ātmā tu satatamiti
ātmā sarvatra pūrṇo'dvayānandarūpaḥ sarvadā prāpta evāvidyāvaśād bhrāntyā aprāpta ivābhāti, tannāśe tadāvaraṇāvidyānāśe vākyaśravaṇāt prāpta ivābhāti kanṭhagatacāmīkaranyāyenetyarthaḥ
44
sthāṇau puruṣavadbhrāntyā kṛtā brahmaṇi jīvatā
jīvasya tāttvike rūpe tasmin dṛṣṭe nivartate ||45
nanu jīvasya paramātmanaikyamayuktaṃ paramātmano jīvatvavirodhisvabhāvatvāttatrāha--sthāṇāviti
yathāndhakārasthite sthāṇau puruṣo'yamiti coraśaṃkayā bibhyati tathaivāvicārāt brahmaṇi jīvatvamāropyate, tathā śāstropadeśajanitavivekadṛṣṭyā tasya jīvasya tāttvikarūpe lakṣye tattvaṃpadārthe dṛṣṭe jñāte brahmaṇi bhrāntyā kalpitatvājjīvatvamapi nivartate, brahmaivāhamiti jānātītyarthaḥ
45
tattvasvarūpānubhavādutpannaṃ jñānamañjasā
ahaṃ mameti cājñānaṃ bādhate digbhramādivat ||46
ahaṃ mameti pratyaye jāgrati kathaṃ nāhamiti pratyaya ityāśaṃkya tadapi tathaiva mṛṣā ityāha--tattvasvarūpeti
jīvaparamātmanorekatvānubhavādutpannaṃ yajjñānaṃ tadahaṃ mametyajñānaṃ bādhate, digbhramādivaditi bhānudarśanādityarthaḥ
46
samyagvijñānavān yogī svātmanyevākhilaṃ sthitam
ekaṃ ca sarvamātmānamīkṣate jñānacakṣuṣā ||47
ahaṃ mameti pratyaye naṣṭe yogī samyagvivekavān san vijñānena cakṣuṣā sarvaṃ dṛśyaṃ prapañcaṃ svātmanyevānubhavena paśyatītyāha--samyagiti
ekamātmānaṃ sarvaṃ paśyati, tadā tadrūpo bhavatīti bhāvaḥ
47
ātmaivedaṃ jagat sarvamātmano'nyanna vidyate
mṛdo yadvadghaṭādīni svātmānaṃ sarvamikṣate ||48
nanvātmā sarva na bhavati pratyakṣavirodhāt, nānātvena dṛśyamānamidaṃ sarvaṃ kathamātmanyabhedena paśyatītyāha--ātmaivedamiti
idaṃ sarvaṃ jagadātmaivātmakāryatvādato'nyadātmano na kiñcidvidyate, yathā ghaṭaśarāvādikāryaṃ mṛdo'nyannāsti
ataḥ svātmanyeva sarvaṃ dṛśyajātamabhedenekṣate paśyati, vācārambhaṇaṃ vikāro nāmadheyaṃ mṛttiketyeva satyamiti śruteḥ
48
jīvanmuktastu tadvidvān pūrvopādhiguṇāṃstajet
sa saccidādidharmatvaṃ bhajedbhramarakīṭavat ||49
evambhūtāvasthaiva jīvanmuktirityāha jīvanmuktistviti
anādyavidyāparikalpitapūrvopādhiṃ dehamanobuddhyādīn guṇān sattvādīn parityajet, tadaiva jīvanmukta ityucyate; sa jīvanmuktaḥ saccidānandādiguṇakatvādbhramarakīṭavat sākṣādbrahma bhajet prāpnotītyarthaḥ
49
tīrtvā moharṇavaṃ hatvā rāgadveṣādirākṣasān
yogī śāntisamāyukta ātmārāmo virājate ||50
tattvajñāstu ātmārāmaḥ sukhaṃ tiṣṭhatītyāha--tīrtvā moharṇavamiti
moha eva arṇavo'paryantatvāt, taṃ jñānaplavena tīrtvā niḥśeṣaṃ nāśayitvā, rāgadveṣādaya eva rākṣasā mokṣaparipanthinaḥ, tān niḥśeṣato nāśayitvā śāntisamāyuktaḥ śamadamādisahita ātmārāma ātmanyeva ārāmaḥ krīḍā yasya sa virājate svārājyenābhiṣiktaḥ prakāśate
arthāntaraṃ dhvanyate--śrīrāmaḥ samurdra tīrtvā rāvaṇādirākṣasān itvā, sītā eva śāntistayā sameto virājate, ayodhyāyāṃ vijṛmbhate
50
bāhyānityasukhāsaktiṃ hitvātmasukhanirvṛtaḥ
ghaṭasthadīpavacchaśvadantareva prakāśate ||51
jīvanmuktasya lakṣaṇamāha--bāhyānityeti
bāhyandriyaviṣayodbhavāmanityasukhasyāsaktiṃ prīti savāsanāṃ hitvā, tatra heturātmasukhena nirvṛtaḥ
ghaṭasthadīpavadantareva prakāśate, svasākṣikabhevātmānubhavasukhamitarairna jñāyate ityarthaḥ
51
upādhistho'pi taddharmairalipto vyomavanmuniḥ
sarvavinbhūḍhavattiṣṭhedasakto vāyuvaccaret ||52
evamuktaprakāreṇa jīvanmuktaḥ sannārabdhasaṃkṣayādevaṃ vartitavyamityāha--upādhistho'pīti
dehādyupādhau sākṣitayā sthito'pi munirvyomavattaddharmairnānuliptaḥ, mama karmalepo nāstīti bhāvayet, sarvajño'pi mūḍhavat prākṛtavattiṣṭhet, sarvatra viṣayeṣu yudṛcchāprāpteṣvanāsakto vāyuvaccaret
52
upādhivilayādviṣṇau nirviśeṣaṃ viśenmuniḥ
jale jalaṃ viyadvomni tejastojasi vā yathā ||53
prārabdhakarmakṣaye dehopādhau līne videhakaivalyamāha--upādhivilayāditi
upādhibhūtadehadvayalaye prārabdhakarmakṣaye muniryogī viṣṇau sarvavyāpake parabrahmaṇi nirviśeṣaṃ yathā bhavet tathā viśet
atyantaikye dṛṣṭānto jale jalamityādi ||53
yallābhānnāparo lābho yatsukhānnāparaṃ sukham
yajjñānānnāparaṃ jñānaṃ tadbrahmetyavadhārayet ||54
brahmaṇaḥ sarvādhiṣṭhānatvāt taṭasthalakṣaṇenābhidhīyate ślokadvayena yallābhādityādi
yadbrahmasākṣātkāralābhādaparo lābho na vidyate sarvalābhānām tatraivāntarbhāvāt yathā brahmānande kṣudrānandānāmantarbhāvaḥ, yatsukhādbrahmānubhavasukhādaparaṃ sukhaṃ nāsti niratiśayasukhatvāt, yajjñānād brahmasākṣātkārajñānādaparaṃ jñānaṃ nāsti mokṣahetutvāt, brahmeti tajjānīyāt
54
yaddṛṣṭvā nāparaṃ dṛśyaṃ yadbhūtvā na punarbhavaḥ
yajjñātvā nāparaṃ jñēyaṃ tadbrahmetyavadhārayet ||55
yaddṛṣṭveti
yaddṛṣṭvāparaṃ draṣṭavyaṃ nāsti tenaiva puruṣārthasya siddhatvāt, yadbhūtvā yenaikyaṃ prāpya punarjanmādikaṃ nāsti, yajjñātvā yatsvarūpaṃ jñātvānyajjñeyaṃ nāsti, tadeva brahmetyavadhārayet
anyat sarvaṃ vṛthā pāṇḍityamityarthaḥ
55
tiryagūrdhvamadhaḥ pūrṇa saccidānandamadvayam
akhaṇḍaṃ nityamekaṃ yattadbrahmetyavadhārayet ||56
tiryagūrdhvamadhaḥ sarvatra brahmaiva pūrṇamityāha--tiryagūrdhvamiti spaṣṭo'rthaḥ
56
atadvyāvṛttirūpeṇa vedāntairlakṣyate'vyayam
akhaṇḍānandamekaṃ yattadbrahmetyavadhārayet ||57
akhaṇḍānandamekaṃ brahma vedāntairatadvyāvṛttirūpeṇātannirasanadvārā jñātavyamityāha-- atadvyāvṛttirūpeṇeti spaṣṭo'rthaḥ
57
akhaṇḍānandarūpasya tasyānandalavāśritāḥ
brahmādyāstāratamyena bhavantyānandino'khilāḥ ||58
nanu brahmādayo'pi niratiśayānandina eva śrūyante, tatrāha--akhaṇḍetyādi
brahmendrādayaḥ paricchinnānandina eva, brahma tu akhaṇḍānandarūpatvādaparicchinnamānandam , evaṃ svarūpaṃ yasya tasya ānandaleśamāśritya brahmendrādayo'pyānandino bhavanti ānandatāratamyeṇa, etasyaivānandasyānyāni bhūtāni mātrāmupajīvantītyādiśruteḥ
58
tadyuktamakhilaṃ vastu vyavahāraścidanvitaḥ
tasmāt sarvagataṃ brahma śrīre sarpirivākhile ||59
nanu yadi paramapremāspadamātmaiva bhātītyucyate tadā kathaṃ dehādiprapañco'pi priyataro'nubhūyate ityāśaṃkyāha--tadyuktamiti
akhiladehādyanātmavastvapi tat tenātmanā saṃyuktameva priyataraṃ laukiko'pi vyavahārastadyukta eva priyataraḥ
tasmāt sarvagataṃ brahma anantaṃ priyaṃ, yathā kṣīraṃ sarvaṃ vyāpya ghṛtamiti evamavastvapi brahmavyāptatvāt priyamiva bhāti
ata ātmaiva priyatama iti siddham
59
anaṇvasthūlamaha svamadīrghamajamavyayam
arūpaguṇavarṇākhyaṃ tadbrahmetyavadhārayet ||60
tarhyātmā kena prakāreṇa sarvaṃ vyāpya tiṣṭhatītyāśaṃkya sākṣādvaktumaśakyatvādanaṇu asthūlamiti śruteḥ sattārūpeṇa bhātītyāha-- anaṇvasthūlamiti spaṣṭo'rthaḥ
60
yadbhāsā bhāsyate'rkādi bhāsyairyattu na bhāsyate
yena sarvamidaṃ bhāti tadbrahmetyavadhārayet ||61
punastadbrahmeti jñānārthaṃ ślokatrayena pṛthaḍanirūpayati--yadbhāseti
yadvastu bhānvādīn bhāsayet yacca bhāsyairarkādibhirnaiva bhāsyate, na tatra sūryo bhāti na candratārakaṃ nemā vidyuto bhānti kṛto'yamagnistameva bhāntamanubhāti sarvaṃ tasya bhāsā sarvamidaṃ vibhātīti śruteḥ; yena sarvamidaṃ bhūtabhautikabhāvarūpaṃ jagatbhāti tadbrahmeti jānīyāt
61
svayamantarbahirvyāpya bhāsayannakhilaṃ jagat
brahma prakāśate vahniprataptāyasapiṇḍavat ||62
taptāyaḥpiṇḍavat svayamevāntarbahirvyāpya bhāsayannakhilaṃ brahma prakāśate ityāha svayamantariti spaṣṭo'rthaḥ
62
jagadvilakṣaṇaṃ brahma brahmaṇo'nyanna kiṃcana
brahmānyadbhāti cenmithyā yathā marumarīcikā ||63
punastadevāha--jagadvilakṣaṇamiti
sarvaṃ brahmaiva satyaṃ; tathāpi jagadrupeṇa yadā paśyati tadā na gṛhyate, jagadvailakṣaṇyena tatkāraṇatvena vicāryate cettadā jñātuṃ śakyaṃ brahmaṇo'nyanna vidyate
yadito'nyaddṛśyate yatkiñcit tanmṛṣaiva marumarīcikāvannirjaladeśasthasūryakiraṇāropitajalavadityarthaḥ.
63
dṛśyate śrūyate yadyadbrahmaṇo'nyanna tadbhavet
tattvavajñānācca tadbrahma saccidānandamavyayam ||64
punastadeva brahma sphuṭaṃ nirūpayati dṛśyate iti
tattvajñānāttatsarvaṃ brahma saccidānandamadvayaṃ brahmānyanna kiñcidastītyarthaḥ
64
sarvagaṃ saccidānandaṃ jñānacakṣurnirīkṣate
ajñānacakṣurnekṣate bhāsvantaṃ bhānumandhavat ||65
svargamiti
śrutiśca--na cakṣuṣā gṛhyate nāpi vācā nānyairdevaistapasā karmaṇā vā
jñānaprasādena viśuddhasattvastatastu taṃ paśyati niṣkalaṃ dhyāyamāna iti
yadi sarvagataṃ brahma tarhi sarvaiḥ kiṃ na dṛśyate ityāśaṃkya cakṣurādibhirna gṛhyate iti tat śrutyā pratipādayati
yo'jñānacakṣuḥ sa sarvagamapi saccidānandaṃ brahma na paśyapi yathā prakāśamānamapi bhānumandho na paśyati; jñānaprasādena jñānacakṣuṣā sarvatra brahmaiva paśyatītyarthaḥ
65
śravaṇādibhiruddīptajñānāgniparitāpitaḥ
jīvaḥ sarvamalānmuktaḥ svarṇavaddyotate svayam ||66
evaṃ tāvaduktarītyānubhavasampannasyāpi tadabhyāsarahitasya vāsanāvaśāt kiñcidajñānaṃ sambhavati tatparihārārthaṃ punaḥ śravaṇādikaṃ kuryādityāha--śravaṇādibhiriti
jīvaḥ pratyagātmā etatprakāreṇa uktārthasya śravaṇamananādibhiruccairdīptaḥ prakāśita jñānamevāgnistena paritāpitaḥ parito viśeṣataśca śodhita ityarthaḥ
sarvasaṃsāramūlājñānamalānmuktaḥ svayameva samyak prakāśate, yathāgninā paritāpitaṃ suvarṇaṃ prakāśate
66
hṛdākāśodito hyātmā bodhabhānustamo'pahṛt
sarvavyāpī sarvadhārī bhāti bhāsayate'khilam ||67
evaṃ viśodhito jīvaḥ paramātmā hṛdākāśe uditaḥ san tamo'jñānamupasaṃharan bhānuvat sarvasvarūpaprakāśe ityāha--hṛdākāśodita iti
ātmabodha eva bhānuḥ, sarvasyādhāramūtatvāt sarvavyāpī sarvadhārī ca, śeṣaṃ spaṣṭam
67
digdeśakālādyanapekṣya sarvagaṃ śītādihṛnnityasukhaṃ nirañjanam
saḥ svātmatīrthaṃ bhajate viniṣkriyaḥ sa sarvavit sarvagato'mṛto bhavet ||68
iti śrīmatparamahaṃsaparivrājakācāryaśrīgovindabhagavatpūjyapādaśiṣyasya śrīmacchaṃkarabhagavataḥ kṛtau ātmabodhaḥ sampūrṇaḥ
nanvātmajñāsyāpi jñanapratibandhakaduritanivāraṇārthaṃ prayāgāditīrthayātrodyogaḥ kartavya ityāśaṃkya svātmatīrthe snātasya na kiñcit kartavyamapītmāha --digdeśakālādīti
yo viniṣkriyaḥ paramahaṃsa ātmatīrthaṃ bhajate saḥ sarvavit sarvajñaḥ sarvatragaḥ paramātmasvarūpatvādamṛto mukto bhavet
kathaṃbhūtaṃ svātmatīrthaṃ, digdeśakālādyanapekṣyaiva sarvagataṃ śītādidvandvaduḥkhādi haratīti śītādihṛt, nityasukhaṃ mokṣānandaprāpakatvāt, itaratīrtheṣu tadviparītaṃ draṣṭavyam
tasmādātmatīrthe snātasya na kiñcidavaśiṣyat iti bhāvaḥ
68
iti ātmabodhatīkā sampūrṇā
