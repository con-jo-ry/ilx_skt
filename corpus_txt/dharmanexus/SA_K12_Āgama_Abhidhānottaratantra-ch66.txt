Abhidhānottarottara 66.
[318.4] (*194r4) atha bhagavantaḥ [5] sarvatathāgatā idaṃ jñānaguhyarahasyam udājahāra ||
niḥsvabhāvātmakā dharmā ākāśam iva nirmalam |
śūnyatājñānavajreṇa śūnyato[6]tpannabhāvanā |
sādhukāraṃ dadanti sma tantraniṣpattiyogataḥ |
sādhu sādhu mahājñānaṃ abhidhānottarottaram |
sādhu vajranayaṃ raudraṃ buddhabo[7]dhiprasādhanam |
sā(*194v)dhu advayayogaṃ ca uttarottarasādhanam |
sādhu niścayayogaṃ ca nirākākṣavibhāvanā |
sādhu sarvajagannātha mahāyānasya [319] deśakaḥ |
saṃpradāyaṃ varaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ advayajñānam uttamam |
sādhu vajraguruḥ sarvayoginyāḥ tantradeśakaḥ |
sādhu parṣadacakrebhyo siddhiṃ dada[2]ty anuttarām | iti tūṣṇīm abhavan ||      ||
idam avocata bhagavān vajrasattvas te ca sarvatathāgatā krodharājābhidhānottarottare ḍākaḍākinī[3]jālasamvaraṃ bhagavato bhāṣitam abhyanandann iti ||      ||
ity abhidhānottarottare nāmasamvaraguhyātiguhyarahasya mahātantrarājāna nikākṣatattvopaddeśabhāvanāpaṭalaḥ ṣaṭṣaṣṭhitamaḥ ||      ||
abhidhānottarottarā nāma mahātantrarājaṃ samāptaḥ ||      ||
ends:
ye dharmā hetuprabhavā hetuṃ teṣāṃ tathāgato hy avada teṣāṃ ca yo nirodha evaṃvādī mahāśramaṇaḥ ||      || hāyanaśatadvaye ca pañcāsa a pta saṃyute | likhitaṃ tantrarājānaṃ paramānandena sādhunā | sādhupuṇyena loko stu vajradhātutva prāpnuyāt || iti mahātantra samāptāḥ ||      || (*194v6)
