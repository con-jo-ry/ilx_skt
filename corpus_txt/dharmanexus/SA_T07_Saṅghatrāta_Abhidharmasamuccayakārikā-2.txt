ṣoḍhā vyavasthitāḥ sarva āśrayālambanāśritāḥ |
dhātavo 'ṣṭādaśa manorūpasaṃmohanaṣṭaye || (1 [35])
ekaṃ viṣayibhāvena mana āyataneṣv iha |
āśrayadvayajatvāt tu dhātavaḥ sapta ṣaḍ manaḥ || (2 [36])
kāmadhātvādiṣu tv ete caturṣv eva hy ato 'khilāḥ |
caturdaśa trayo 'ṣṭau ca kāmarāgādidūṣaṇāt || (3 [37])
kāmadhātur daśāvastho rūpī pañcadaśātmakaḥ |
catuḥsvabhāva ārūpyo nirmalo 'nāsravas tridhā || (4 [38])
vijñānasthitayaḥ sapta cittābhiratiyogataḥ |
parigrāhakabhāvena catasraś cittavarjjitāḥ || (5 [39])
rūpe hi veditāsvādāt saṃjñābhiḥ saṃpralobhitam |
upastabdhaṃ ca saṃskārair vijñānam avatiṣṭhate || (6 [40])
saptāsaṃjñibhavāgre ca saṃnivāsābhinandanāt |
sattvāvāsā na nirdiṣṭā apāyā duḥkhapīḍanāt || (7 [41])
te nārakāḥ satiryañcaḥ pretā devanaraiḥ saha |
gatayo 'vyākṛtāḥ kecit sāsravāḥ skandhasaṃjñitāḥ || (8 [42])
tāsāṃ prāpaka ity uktaḥ pañcadhaivāntarābhavaḥ |
gantavyagamanābhāvān nārūpyeṣūpapadyate || (9 [43])
saṃprajānan viśaty āste niryāti ca nireti na |
āste ca mūḍhas sarvāṇi garbhāvakrāntayas trayāt || (10 [44])
catasro yonayo devā nārakāś caupapādukāḥ |
pretā jarāyujāś cāpi nṛtiryañcaś caturvidhāḥ || (11 [45])
avasthābhedaniyataṃ sāsravaṃ skandhapañcakam |
karma nāśāntarotpatti bhavākhyaṃ kṣaṇikaṃ dvayam || (12[46])
sadhātau vā sabhāgasya kliṣṭāt kliṣṭasya saṃbhavaḥ |
akliṣṭasya tathākliṣṭād bhavānāṃ pratisandhiṣu || (13 [47])
pañcāṅgaś caturaṅgo vā + + + bhava iṣyate |
ekāṅgaś cāpare nāsti yathāyogaṃ bhavatraye || (14 [48])
kāmarūpabhavau pañcaskandhakau rūpadūṣaṇāt |
catuskandhaka ārūpyaḥ karma vā trividhaṃ bhavaḥ || (15 [49])
yaḥ kleśakarmavastūnāṃ mohādibhyaḥ samudbhavaḥ |
sa pratītyasamutpādaḥ pratītyotpattilakṣaṇaḥ || (16 [50])
ākṣepo yadbalād yena yathā yasya yad atra ca |
yadbalād yena nirvṛttir yathāsyāntaś ca yo yathā || (17 [51])
triparvaiṣa dvisandhiś ca dvādaśāṅgaḥ pravartakaḥ |
kāryakāraṇasaṃbandhaḥ sthitaś ca paripūriṇaḥ || (18 [52])
dharmo vipakṣo vidyāyā avidyānye 'khilā malāḥ |
saṃsṛṣṭatvān na saṃkleśād dhiyo 'prakhyātilakṣaṇā || (19 [53])
kāyavākcittasaṃskārāḥ sāsravaṃ karma dṛṣṭayaḥ |
skandhaś caturtha āhārāḥ saṃskārāḥ kṛtakaṃ ca yat || (20 [54])
yāvanto 'rūpiṇo dharmās tan nāma namanāt kila |
anyan nāmākṣaracchedaḥ saṃjñākarmasamāśrayaḥ || (21 [55])
rūpaṃ tu varṇasaṃsthānaṃ kvacid dṛṣṭyā nirūpaṇāt |
rūpadhātuḥ kvacid rūpaṃ śreṣṭharūpasamāśrayāt || (22 [56])
asmin manasikārāntāḥ pañca nāma vidādayaḥ |
rūpaṃ tu rūpaṇād uktaṃ yat kiṃcit bhūtabhautikaṃ || (23 [57])
sparśas trayasya saṃghātaḥ so 'dhivākpratighāśrayaḥ |
vidyāvidyādvayāc caikaḥ pañcaikaikaṣaḍātmakāḥ || (24 [58])
veditatrayajā tredhā tṛṣṇā pañcavidhā tataḥ |
rāgaḥ kāmādyupādānam evaṃ karmabhavīkṛtaṃ || (25 [59])
bhūjalāgnyanilavyomnāṃ sāsravasya ca cetasaḥ |
dhātutvaṃ rūpacaittādijanmasaṃdhāraṇād iti || (26 [60])
ādhyātmikāni strīpuṃstve jīvitaṃ pañca vedanāḥ |
śraddhādīny amalāny evā'jñāsyāmītyādikaṃ trayam || (27 [61])
viṣayagrahaṇārthena ṣaṇḍhastrīnarabhedataḥ |
saṃtānadhāraṇād āyur vedanāḥ kleśapakṣataḥ || (28 [62])
vimokṣapakṣataḥ pañca trayaṃ bhūmiviśeṣataḥ |
indriyāṇi matis trīṇi vā kāyāt strīpumindriye || (29 [63])
ṣaṭ tridhā kuśalāny aṣṭāv aṣṭāv avyākṛtāni tu |
caturdaśa daśa trīṇi trayodaśa ca dhātuṣu || (30 [64])
aṣṭau na caitasāny eva caitasāni trayodaśa |
ekaṃ tu cittaṃ rūpīṇi sapta karmākhilāni na || (31 [65])
vā sapta karmajāny āhur aṣṭau bhāvyāni sarvathā |
naikam aṣṭa pradeśaś ca ṣaṭ ca labhyāni naiva hi || (32 [66])
gandhādiḥ kabaḍīkāraḥ sāsravaḥ sparśa eva ca |
manaḥsaṃcetanā cittam ādhārāharaṇād iti || (33 [67])
sarve kāme trayo rūpe yāvad eke sukhodayam |
ekaḥ parastāt pratyekaṃ vikalpo narakādiṣu || (34 [68])
āyuḥkṣayād dhi sattvānāṃ cyutiḥ puñyakṣayād api |
dvayakṣayāt tathānyeṣām anyeṣāṃ nobhayakṣayāt || (35 [69])
dhātusamuccayaḥ samāptaḥ || laru ||
