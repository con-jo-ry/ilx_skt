ekāṃ bāhyaprajñāṃ (234r/471) vinā mudrābhisaṃyutāṃ bāhyamudrāsaṃpuṭitāṃ siddhiṃ mahāmudrāsiddhiṃ na dadāti | bāhyamudrāsamāpatter eva jñānasya mahāsukhasyotpatteḥ | ata eva bāhyamudrāyogo mahāmudrāsiddheḥ sākṣādupāya uktaḥ |
kiṃ tūtpannakrame 'pi strī puruṣaś cotpannadevatārūpam ātmānam adhimuñcet | tataś cobhayātmikā prajñā syād ity ākūtam ekaikām iti vadataḥ | utpattikrame tu (234v/472) jñānamudraikāpi bhavet | siddhisamaye tu sāpy ubhayātmikaiva | tasmād yojyāś caturmudrāḥ kramadvaye 'pi | tatrotpannakrame bāhyaprajñā karmamudrā |
āliṅganādikarmaṇā mudaṃ rātīti kṛtvā |
adhyātmaprajñā avadhūtī śūnyataikarasasahajānandātmā dharmadhātur dharmamudrā | mahāsukhamayaṃ niruttarabodhicittaṃ phalarūpaṃ mahāmudrā | vividhadevatānirmānaṃ (235r/473) samayamudrā | athavā karmamudrā saiva uktamahāmudrāyā eva viśuddhiniṣṭhādhiṣṭhitā dharmamudrā dharmakāyaś cittadharmatāsvabhāvatvāt tasya |
karmamudrāsampuṭitaṃ tu yathāyogaṃ lakṣaṇavyañjanavirājitaṃ ca sambhogakāyo niravadhivicitrānantamaṇḍalacakrādyākāro nirmāṇakāyaś ca bhavyavineyajanānugrahakāle 'natikramaṇāt samayamudrā | bodhicittaṃ tu mahāsukha(235v/474)kāyaḥ śūnyatārūpatvāt svābhāvikakāyaś ca vyāpitvān mahāmudrā |
mudrā ca mudraṇaṃ dharmāṇāṃ yatsvarūpam iti mukhyato bodhicittam eva tatsvarūpatvāt tu mudrāntaram api mudreva mudrā | athavā mahatīṃ mudaṃ rātīti mudrā lakṣaṇavyañjanojjvalabhujamukhacihnādyupetarūpaḥ sambhogakāyaḥ | samayamudrā cittasvabhāvaḥ pratyātmavedyo dharmakāyaḥ samayo hi (236r/475) sambodha iti kṛtvā |
dharmamudrā dharmasambhogakāyādhiṣṭhānān nānākāradharmatattvaprakāśanavāgātmikā | karmamudrā kṛtyānuṣṭhānarūpatvān nānānirmāṇaiḥ kṛtyasampādanān nirmāṇakāyaḥ | athavā karmamudrā nirmāṇakāyaḥ | dharmamudrā dharmakāyaḥ | mahāmudrā sambhogakāyaḥ | samayamudrā bodhicittaṃ mahāsukhaśūnyatākaruṇānāṃ sarvadharmāṇāṃ ca milanarūpatvāt |
(236v/476) milanaṃ tu rūpādīnāṃ tatsvabhāvenopalabhyamānatvāt | eṣā ca vyavasthā evaṃmayetivyākhyāne kṛtā | etāś ca catasro 'pi mudrāḥ pratyekaṃ caturmudrātmikā itīta eva prājñair vijñeyāḥ | anuktis tu vistarabhayāt |
utpattikrame tu bāhyaprajñaiva bhāvitābhimatadevīrūpā karmamudrā | svābhāṅganā jñānamudrety aparanāma śarīre bhāvitahūṃkārādiś ca (237r/477) dharmamudrā | samayamudrā tu bījādiniṣpannā | maṇḍalacakramilitadevatāḥ spharaṇasaṃharaṇādikāriṇyaś cihnam api | svayaṃ nāyakadevatāmūrtir mahāmudrā | tataḥ spharaddevatācakram api samayamudrā |
sarvāś caitā vyavasthās tantrāntareṣu bhagavataiva vineyāśayabhedam anuvartatayā kṛtāḥ | tattadgranthas tu vistaratrāsād atro noktaḥ |
