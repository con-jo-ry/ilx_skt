Abhidhānottaratantra ch. 32 [Tib. & Ś. 35]
= Lokesh Chandra facsimile ed.
= 1138 AD palm-leaf codex
[ 1242']
atha paraṃ vakṣye saṃkṣepāṅ (!) guhyam uttamāṃ |
jhaṭiti m-ātmabhāvaṃ ca śrīherukarūpam udvahet |
nīlogravikṛtaṃ trinetraṃ ṣaṇmukhottamam [|]
ardhacandrajaṭādhāryaṃ viśvavajrottamaṃ śivaṃ |
kapālamālāmakuṭaṃ vajramālāvibhūṣitam |
muṇḍasragdāmadehogrā ṣaṇmudrādehalaṅkṛtāṃ |
vyāghracarmaṇivasanaṃ vārāhyā deha sthapitaṃ (!?) |
ekapādaṃ sadā sthitam |
«dakṣiṇaṃ pītaṃ» dhūmraṃ ca vāma raktaṃ haritakaṃ |
ūrdhvam aśvamukhaṃ śvetaṃ śubhraṃ raudrabhīṣaṇaṃ |
vajraghaṇṭāsamāpannaṃ devyādharanipīḍitaṃ |
jinacarmambaradharāṃ ḍākinīkuladhāriṇaṃ |
kapālakhaṭvāṅgadharaṃ vajracihnādyalaṃkṛtaṃ |
pāśāṃtuśa(!)karavyagraṃ ḍamaru[ 124]muṇḍadhāriṇaṃ |
karttikā paraśuṃ caiva bhujai dvādaśabhis tathā |
sarvās tā maṇḍaleyāś ca ekapādād vibhāvayet |
gajavaktrā kharavaktrā govaktrā ha(?)yavaktrās tathaiva ca |
śvānānanā prathamā dvitīyā ulūkavaktrajā [|]
tṛtīyā śūkaramukhā caturthī vāyasīmukhā |
pīṭhasaṃsthānadharmātmā nānārūpadharo mahāṃ |
anantavijāyākṣo [']haṃ sarvajño [']haṃ tu sarvaśa[ḥ] |
yathārucita dehogra[ṃ] tathā bhāvaṃ vibhāvayet |
krīḍate [']sau mahātmāno viśvarūpo maṇir yathā ||      ||
Abhidhānottarottare viśvarūpasādhanapaṭalo dvātriṃśamaḥ ||      ||
