Abhidhānottara
[ 1024'] atha yogaṃ pravakṣyāmi buddhakāpālikaṃ paraṃ |
sarvayogottamaṃ ntattvaṃ sarvasiddhipradāyakaṃ |
saṃvṛtyā gupta guhyaṃ na kathyayed yasya kasyacit |
bhāvagrāhyaṃ paraṃ tattvaṃ yoginā hṛdisaṃsthitaṃ |
gūḍha guhyaṃ suguhyaṃ ca bhāvayeṅ(!) guruvaktrataḥ |
śmaśānodyānaramyeṣu sādhako dṛḍhaniśvayaḥ |
utpattikramayogena bhūmipīṭhādyanukramāt |
hūṃkarodbhavaṃ ramyaṃ tadvaraṭakamadhyayoḥ |
tatparāvṛtyam ātmānaṃ buddhakāpā[ 102]linaṃ paraṃ |
śāntaṃ nirāmayaṃ śuddhaṃ sarvasaṅkalpavarjitaṃ |
ālikāliṣu sambhūtaṃ nirdvand[v]aṃ paramaṃ śivaṃ |
dvibhu[ja]m ekavaktraṃ tu ṣaṭmudrācihnabhūṣitaṃ |
jambūnadasamaprakṣaṃ raśmirākulojvalaṃ (!) |
kapālā(!)mālāmakuṭaṃ trinetraṃ vihasitānanaṃ |
bhāvayed bhāvasarvāś ca dharmadhvanir anuttarāṃ |
vyāghracarmanivasanaṃ naracarmapaṭāvṛtaṃ |
kapālakhaṭvāṅgadharam abhayaṃ dakṣiṇakarāṃ |
pañcabuddhamakuṭaṃ muṇḍamālāvibhūṣitaṃ |
tadbhairavamadhye tu kapālatarjjapūritaṃ |
pañcajñānāmṛtaṃ miśraṃ sarvasattvārthahetunā |
khaṭvāṅgadevatāyogaṃ kapālaṃ vīrayoginī |
dakṣiṇenābhayaṃ śuddhaṃ ābhayā bhavaśeṣapramokṣakaṃ |
anayā harate sarvapāpaṃ duṣṭabhayānakaṃ |
ardhacandradharaṃ śāntaṃ viśvavajrāṅkitaṃ śiraṃ
lālāṭe(!) vajramālā tu nūpuraiś ca manoharaiḥ |
ucchritaḥ karṇikāmadhye viśvābja [a]ṣṭapatrake |
dṛṣṭā[ 103]dveṣapadākrāntaṃ bhairavaṃ kālarātrikāṃ |
tadrūpaṃ jñānasattvaṃ tu vaṃkārodbhava<|bhava|>bhāvanāṃ |
cittacakraṃ tu hṛdaye herukaṃ taṃ vibhāvayet |
vākcakraṃ vāpkathe(!) bhāvya kāyacakraṃ tu mūrddhaje |
nīlaraktasitākāraṃ ṣaḍbhujāṃś ca trivaktrajāṃ |
vajraghaṇṭāsamāpannā trinetrā vikṛtānanā |
kakapālakhaṭvāṅga[dharās] triśūlamuṇḍadhāriṇaḥ |
svābhavidyāṅgasatsaṅgā mahāsukhapade sthitāḥ [|]
akṣarotpannasambhūtāṃ caturmaṇḍalamadhyamān |
kapālamālāmakuṭāṃ svacihnotpannam uttamān |
pīṭhādikramayogena triyogasamvarottamaṃ |
ḍākinī tu tathā lāmā khaṇḍarohā tu rūpiṇī |
nyaset padma<|si|>śasthāne kapālaiś caturottamaiḥ |
ḍākinī pītavarṇā ca lāmā palāśavarṇajā |
khaṇḍarohā tathā nīlā rūpiṇī raktam ujvalāḥ |
kapālakhaṭvāṅgadharā dakṣiṇe abhayottamāṃ |
dvāreṣu caturo devyā kākā[ 103]syādi tu ḍākinī |
nīlā pītā raktā haritā raudrarūpiṇī |
koṇeṣu yamadā[ḍhyā]dyā karālī bhīmabhīṣaṇī |
kapālakhaṭvāṅgadharā abhayottamam eva ca |
ḍamaruṃ karttikā caiva caturmāroparisthitāḥ |
trinetrā muktakeśā ca digambaradharā parāḥ |
kapālamālāmakuṭā pañcamudrāvibhūṣitāḥ |
darpaṇaṃ vastragandhaṃ ca rasa pūjācatuṣṭayaṃ |
daśabhūmikramenaiva cittavākkāyayogataḥ |
viśuddhi bodhipakṣānāṃ dhyātmānāṃ dhyātmabāhyakaṃ |
kakārādi caturviṃśā bindunākrāntamastakān |
praṇavahūṃdvayasaṃyutān phaṭkārāntaṃ prayojitān |
mantraṃ vai sarvavīrāṇāṃ sarvakāmaphalapradaṃ |
ḍākinī praṇavo nāma hūṃhūṃphaṭkāramantrataḥ | (= -m antataḥ?)
evaṃ pracaṇḍādi ḍākinyā mantratattvaṃ sukhapradaṃ |
kāyavākcittavajreṣu hūṃ āḥ oṃ iti guhyakān |
padmapatreṣu ḍākinyāṃ praṇavaṃ [ 104] napuṃsakai sahaḥ ||
hūṃoṃkāradvaya phaṭkāra ante yojyās tu kāmatā |
kākāsyādi tu mantrāṇāṃ praṇavā praṇaveṇa sahaḥ ||
ante hūṃphaṭkāra yojayet |
yamadāḍhyādikaṃ [śa]saṣaha praṇavaṃ caiva hūṃ haṃ vauṣaṭ kārayet ||
madhye tu buddhakāpālī mantram evam anusmaret |
oṃ aṃ āṃ iṃ īṃ uṃ ūṃ eṃ aiṃ oṃ auṃ aṃ aṃḥ kṣaḥ svāhā ||
pṛthivyākandhāyatna<|ta|>kavacaṃ yojayet
tatra jñānacakrasamayaṃ pūjāsamarasīkṛtaṃ |
catuḥpūjāntamantrāṇāṃ svanāmaṃ praṇavahūṃkṛtaḥ |
eva maṇḍalayā yojya buddhakāpālikodbhavam |
vajrābharaṇagātraṃ tu sarveṣā yojayet kramāt ||
prāṇāyāmaprayogena jagaṅ guhyacatuṣṭayaṃ |
spharaṇasaṃhārayogena cakratritayān kramāt |
anena vidhiyogena japet samayacakrajaṃ |
catuḥsandhyāprayogena siddhyate samayottamaṃ || [ 104]
samayaṃ sarvamantrāṇāṃ buddhakāpālikottamaṃ |
asamāhitayogena sarvam eva prakalpayet |
samayaṃ pālayen nityaṃ pañcajñānāmṛtaṃ paraṃ |
athavā svanāmajāpaṃ ca mantratattvaṃ ca jāpataḥ |
oṃ śrībuddhakāpāli buṃ āḥ hūṃ ḍākinījālasamvara hūṃ phaṭ svāhā ||
iti yogavaraṃ ramya yoginījñānam uttamaṃ |
yadā samayabhraṣṭānāṃ eṣa yogaṃ ca dāpayet |
śāntiṃ bhavati saubhāgyaṃ yoginyābhisiñcati |
bāhye guhyodake snānaṃ catusandhyānukramāt |
samayaṃ samvaraṃ gṛhya puna prārthayate guruṃ |
ajñānād vāpi mohātmā bhraṣṭo smi samayād vṛthā |
karoṣi trāṇaṃ me nātha guruvajradharaprabhuḥ |
mahākāruṇikaś cāpi mama trāṇaṃ jagatprabho |
iti niścayaṃ kṛtvā siddhir apy āhatā dhruvaṃ ||   ||
ity Abhidhānottarottare samayotthāpaṇabuddhakāpālotpatti trayoviṃśatimaḥ ||   || [ 1046']
