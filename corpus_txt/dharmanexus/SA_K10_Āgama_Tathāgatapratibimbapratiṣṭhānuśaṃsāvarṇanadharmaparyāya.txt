[§1] mahāvimāno bhavati maheśākhyo 'pramāṇo mahābalo mahotsāhī dīrghāyur yaśasvī sukhabahulo 'yaṃ bhikṣavaḥ prathamo 'nuśaṃso dāpake pudgale tathāgatasya pratibimbakaraṇe |
[§2] punar aparaṃ bhikṣava āḍhyo bhavati mahādhano mahābhogaḥ | prabhūtavittopakaraṇaḥ prabhūtasvantaḥsvāpateyaḥ (?) prabhūtadhanadhānyasuvarṇakośakoṣṭhāgārasaṃnicayaḥ | prabhūtahastyaśvagadeuka(?)dāsīdāsakakarmakarapauruṣeyaḥ | prabhūtamitrāmātyajñātisālohitaḥ(?) | ayaṃ bhikṣavo dvitīyo 'nuśaṃso dāpaye pudgale tathāgatasya pratibimbakaraṇe |
[§3] punar aparaṃ bhikṣavo 'bhimayo (?) bhavati darśanīyaḥ | prāsādiko gauravarṇaḥ kanakavarṇacchatrākāraśirāḥ pralambabāhu vistīrṇalalāṭaḥ | saṅgatabhrūbhaṅganāśaḥ | sarvāṅgapratyaṅgopetaḥ sarvābharaṇavibhūṣito 'yaṃ bhikṣavaḥ | tṛtīyo 'nuśaṃso dāpake [7r/`3r'] pudgale tathāgatasya pratibimbakaraṇe |
[§4] punar aparaṃ bhikṣavaḥ | rājā bhavati suparivāraś cakravartī saptaratnasa[ma]nvāgataḥ putrahasraparivṛto ye cāsya bhavanti putrā vā dārā vā bhṛtyā vā | aupajīvino vā te sarve 'sya śuśrūṣante ājñācittam upasthāpayanti | ayaṃ bhikṣavaś caturtho 'nuśaṃsā (!) dāpake pudgale tathāgatasya pratibimbakaraṇe |
