Stanzas 1–4 actually appear in a single leaf (folio 1 verso), partly damaged in the bottom, that contains approximately the first eight and a half stanzas (pāda c) of the work.
This leaf has been included with another 20 leaves in a codex listed as MS 117 and microfilmed in NGMPP Reel C 13/5, which is labelled ‘Pañcarakṣā (Prakīrṇa)’, even though none of the leaves seems to contain the Pañcarakṣā. The leaf containing the beginning of the Paramārthasevā has seven lines and is written in gomola script.
