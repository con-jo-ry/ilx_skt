[1] [siddham] namaḥ śrīcakrasaṃvarāya ||
jayati jagati kāntākāntabāhūpagūḍhaḥ
satatasukhitacittaś cittanāthaḥ sa eṣaḥ |
bhuvanahitanimittaṃ nirmitaṃ yena rūpaṃ
vikṛtataram asatyaṃ satyam antaḥsvarūpam ||
atra tantrādāv evaṃ mayetyādinā nidānavākyaṃ kasmān nāstīti codye bhagavataḥ sadātanatvāt parinirvāṇam eva na saṃbhavatīty ācakṣate kecid ācāryāḥ | uktaṃ ca |
na buddhaḥ parinirvātīti |
saṃbhave ca parinirvṛteḥ saṃgītakṛtā nidānavākyaṃ vācyam | tathā ca sūtram |
mayi parinirvṛte bhikṣava evaṃ mayetyādikayā mama dharmaḥ saṃgātavya iti |
anye punar āhuḥ | tantrāntarān nidānavākyaṃ vinā tantram idam ākṛṣṭam iti | prathamaṃ tāvad abhidhānābhidheyasaṃbandhaprayojanaprayojanaprayojanāny upalabhya prekṣāvantaḥ pravartante iti | tāny eva tāvat pratipādyante | tad uktam |
siddhārthaṃ siddhasaṃbandhaṃ śrotuṃ śrotā pravartate |
śāstrādau tena vaktavyaḥ saṃbandhaḥ saprayojana iti ||
tatra tāvad athāta ity ārabhya samastaḥ saṃcārākhyo granthasaṃdarbho 'bhidhānam | tena yad vācyaṃ bhagavadbhagavatīrūpaṃ tad abhidheyam | anayor abhidhānābhidheyabhāvalakṣaṇaḥ saṃbandhaḥ | upāyopeyabhāvalakṣaṇaḥ saṃbandha ity apare | prayojanaṃ ca saṃcārārthasya sādaranirantaradīrghakālābhyāsaḥ | śrīsaṃvarapadaprāptiś ca prayojanaprayojanam iti |
athāta ity ādi | athāta ity asmin nipātasamudāye | athaśabda ānantarye | ataḥśabdaś ca krame | khasamatantrād anantaram anena vakṣyamāṇakrameṇety arthaḥ | saṃpravakṣyāmīti | samyag aviparītaṃ kathayiṣyāmi | saṃcāravidhim iti saṃcāraḥ pracāro yoginīnāṃ yogiśarīre maṇḍale ca | tasya vidhir vidhānaṃ vyavasthā | uttamam iti |
niruttarapadaprāpter nidānabhūtatvād uttamam | tārayeti tāraṇam iha plāvanam | atas tāraya plāvaya sattvebhyaḥ prakāśayeti yāvat | vajrapāṇe iti śeṣaḥ | ata eva vajrapāṇiḥ saṃgītikṛt | idam iti | imaṃ buddhisthīkṛtaṃ saṃcāravidhim | kiṃbhūtam | tricakrātmayogamārgavyavasthitam iti | tricakrātmā bhagavān | tasya yogo bhāvanā |
sa eva krameṇa sādhyasyopasthāyakatvān mārgaḥ | tena rūpeṇa vyavasthitam | punar api kiṃbhūtam ity āha | gopitam ityādi | gopitam aśrāddheṣu na vyaktīkṛtam | yad iti yam | tatheti tathāpi sati | mārgatve 'pi satīty arthaḥ | tantra iti lakṣābhidhāne | kathaṃ gopitam ity āha | anulomavilomasaṃsthitam iti |
kramavyatikramābhyām abhihitatvāt tasya gopanam ity arthaḥ | kramavyatikramābhyām uddeśādikaṃ tatra proktam iti darśayitum āha | uddeśam ityādi | uddiśyata ity uddeśaḥ sūtram | yathāproktam iti yathāvadabhihitam | niḥśeṣeṇa deśyata iti nirdeśaṃ vṛttisvarūpam | avyaktaṃ nirdeśaṃ prati nirdeśyata iti pratinirdeśaṣ ṭīkā | samastaṃ vai iti |
etat trayam | saṃketavidhivistaram iti | saṃketavidhinā bahutaram ity arthaḥ | [2] kiṃprabhāvo 'sāv ity āha | yenetyādi | yeneti saṃcāravidhinā | vijñātamātreṇeti | bhāvanābalāt krameṇa viśeṣato jñātamātreṇa sākṣātkārīkṛtena | sādhakaḥ saṃvarātmakaḥ | siddhiṃ buddhatvam | āpnuyāl labhate | idānīṃ tantrārthaṃ darśayitum āha | saptetyādi |
saptatriṃśadyogeneti | bhagavatā saha saptatriṃśaddevatāsaṃbandhena | bodhipakṣā iti smṛtyupasthānādayo bodhipākṣikā dharmāḥ | loke prabhāvya vikhyātā iti upāyarūpatvāt loke prabhāvyamānatvena prasiddhiṃ gatāḥ | upāyatvam eva katham eṣām ity ata āha | śrīherukajagatparam iti | śrīherukety upalakṣaṇam | pracaṇḍādikā api veditavyāḥ |
yasmād amī jagati pradhānabhūtā bhāvyamānāḥ śrīherukādayaḥ smṛtyupasthānādibodhipākṣikāḥ | tasmād upāyatvam iti bhāvaḥ | adhunā bhāvanākramaṃ darśayitum āha | prathamam ityādi | prathamam iti bhāvanārambhe | yogeśvareṇeti adhimokṣeṇa jhaṭiti śrīsaṃvarayogavān | pañcaskandhāhaṃkāram iti ātmanaḥ pañcaskandhake devatāhaṃkāraḥ |
rūpaskandha ityādi sugamam | dhātur iti dhātavo 'pi devatākāreṇa kathyante | pṛthivīdhātur ityādi subodham | evam ityādinopasaṃhāraḥ | iti prathamo nirdeśaḥ || 1 ||
athetyādi | ye devatāyogena yojanīyā bodhipākṣikā dharmās te sarve 'pi katame iti yojanīyam | ṛddhir utpadyate 'neneti ṛddhyutpādaḥ | chanda eva rddhyutpādaś chandarddhyutpādaḥ | evam anye 'pi | eta ityādinopasaṃhāraḥ | iti dvitīyo nirdeśaḥ || 2 ||
tatraiteṣām ityādi | amunā sarveṇa smṛtyupasthānādīnāṃ devatārūpatvaṃ darśitam | śeṣaṃ sugamam | iti tṛtīyo nirdeśaḥ || 3 ||
athāta ityādi | vīrādvayam iti | śrīsaṃvarātmakasya yogino vīraiḥ khaṇḍakapālaprabhṛtibhiḥ sahādvayam aikyam | katham advayam ity āha | śrīheruketyādi | aṅgāṅgety uddeśaḥ | sarvāṅgāvayaveti nirdeśaḥ | nakhadantayor ityādi pratinirdeśaḥ | iti caturtho nirdeśaḥ || 4 ||
athānyatamam ityādi subodham | ādhyātmikaṃ yathetyādi | yathādhyātmikaṃ tathā bāhyaṃ maṇḍalaṃ jñeyam iti saṃbandhaḥ | nyāsaṃ yathā prayoktavyam iti yathānantaram eva devatānāṃ nyāso vaktavyaḥ | tathā so 'pi jñeya ity arthaḥ | idānīṃ devatānyāsam avatārayitum āha | ādhāretyādi |
ādhārādheyarūpi yac cakraṃ tat yaṃkārādipariṇatavāyumaṇḍalādyuparigataṃ cintayed iti bhāvaḥ | jñātvetyādi | etan maṇḍalacatuṣṭayaṃ vibhāvya | yas tu vidhiṃ hīti yo 'nyo vidhiḥ | eṣa guruparvayathākramam iti sa eṣa gurūpadiṣṭo yathākramaṃ vibhāvayitavya iti śeṣaḥ | girītyādi |
anantaroktavāyvādimaṇḍalacatuṣṭayopari sūṃkārapariṇatasumerupṛṣṭhe paṃkārapa[2]riṇatavicitrāṣṭadalakamalakarṇikāyām ity arthaḥ | tatra madhye sthito vīra iti tatra karṇikopari | ālikālipariṇatacandramaṇḍalasthād ākāśadeśasthānād janitahūṃkārāt pariṇamya gaurīṃ tatpatiṃ cālīḍhenākramya vīreśvaraḥ sūryamaṇḍale sthitavān iti bhāvaḥ | ḍākinītyādi |
anayā gāthayā pūrvādidikpatreṣu vāmāvartena ḍākinyādīnāṃ nyāsaḥ kathitaḥ | dvibhujām ekavaktrāṃ veti | sarvā etā dvibhujā ekavaktrāś cety arthaḥ | nānārūpavilāsinīm iti nānārūpiṇyo vilāsinyaś cety arthaḥ | vidiśenetyādi sugamam | pukārādi samāyojyeti pullīramalayādikaṃ buddhāv āropya |
deśe deśe vyavasthitam iti teṣu pullīramalayādiṣu vyavasthitaṃ pracaṇḍādisahitaṃ khaṇḍakapālādikam | ānīte ity ānīya | cakragarbhastham iti cittacakrādicakratrayamadhyasthaṃ kṛtvā | pūjayed iti bhāvayet | sadeti pratisandhyam | pu itītyādi | pu iti uddeśaḥ | pullīramalaya iti nirdeśaḥ | evaṃ sarvatra | sarvaṃ sugamam | mukha iti diṅmukheṣu dikṣu |
vimukha iti vidikṣu | evam ityādi | evam iti yathoktaṃ maṇḍalam | bāhyādhyātmeti bahir adhyātmyaṃ ca | ālambyeti ālambayet | kiyat kālam ity āha | yāvan nirvṛtigocaram iti | yāvan nirvāṇaṃ viṣayo na bhavatīty arthaḥ | iti pañcamo nirdeśaḥ || 5 ||
athetyādi | sarvādhipatyādicihnamudrāgaṇasya tv iti sarvāsāṃ devatānām adhipatir bhagavān | ādiśabdena vajrāvārāhyādīnāṃ parigrahaḥ | teṣāṃ cihnasvarūpamudrāsamūhasya nirṇayam iti pareṇa saṃbandhaḥ | vakṣya iti śeṣaḥ | svavarṇabhujasaṃsthānam iti devatānāṃ svakīyaṃ varṇādikaṃ ca | gopyatantre tv iti vistaratantre saṃgopya iha vakṣya iti bhāvaḥ |
śrīheruketyādi sugamam | hastadvayasamāliṅgyeti āliṅganabhujadvayena vajravajraghaṇṭe dadhānam iti śeṣaḥ | savyam udyatam iti udyatadakṣiṇabhujeṣu cihnāny amūnīty arthaḥ | kapālāsṛg iti asṛkpūrṇakapālam | muṇḍeti brahmaśiraḥ samudyatam | avasavyeti pūrvavat samudyatavāmabhujeṣu cihnāni | iti samākhyātam |
caturviṃśārdhabhūṣitam iti anena dvādaśacihnadhāraṇena dvādaśabhujabhūṣitaṃ vikhyātaṃ bhagavantaṃ bhāvayed ity arthaḥ | śatam ardhaṃ tu vijñeyaṃ muṇḍamālā tv iti pañcāśanmuṇḍamālāvalambinam | yajñakam iti yajñopavītadhāriṇam | bhagavatyā pure sthāpyeti bhagavatīṃ purataḥ sthitāṃ paśyed iti śeṣaḥ |
āliṅganakapālaṃ ca duṣṭamārādyasṛgdharām iti āliṅganavāmabhujena duṣṭānāṃ rudhirāntrapūrṇakapāladhāriṇīm | dakṣiṇena sadā dhāryaṃ tarjikā duṣṭavajrikam iti dakṣiṇabhujena duṣṭatarjanīsahitavajraṃ sarvadā dhārayantīm | khaṇḍamaṇḍitamekhalām iti khaṇḍīkṛtamekhalāmaṇḍitakaṭipradeśām |
ubhayasaṃpuṭādvayam iti bhagavadbhagavatīrūpaṃ samāpattisamāpannam ity arthaḥ | ḍākinyādi tv iti ḍākinyādayaḥ | catvāra iti catasraḥ | kha[3]ṭvāṅgapātreti khaṭvāṅgasahitaṃ kapālaṃ vāmena sarvāsām | śastrikā iti dakṣiṇena kartikā | pañcamudrādigātrasyeti gātreṇa yathāyogaṃ pañcamudrādhāriṇyaḥ | ādiśabdena keyūrādīnāṃ parigrahaḥ |
muktakeśavirājitām iti muktakeśair virājamānāḥ | caturviṃśativīreṇeti caturviṃśati vīrāḥ | pañcamudrādimaṇḍitam iti kaṇṭhikādipañcamudrādharāḥ | ādiśabdena nūpurakeyūrādayo gṛhyante | dvibhujam ekavaktraṃ veti | dvibhujā ekavaktrāś ca | jaṭāmukuṭāṅkapaṭṭakam iti | prathamā bahuvacane |
samāliṅgya svacihnasya mahārāgānurāgaṇād iti mahārāgaḥ sahajānandaḥ | tannimittam anurāgaṇaṃ mahārāgānurāgaṇam | tasmād dhetor āliṅganāya pravṛttena bhujadvayena vajravajraghaṇṭādharā iti bhāvaḥ | pañcamudrādiśiromaṇīti ādiśabdena keyūrādayaḥ | śiromaṇisahitapañcamudrādidhāriṇya ity arthaḥ | mekhalamaṇḍitam iti mekhalālaṃkṛtakaṭibhāgāḥ |
yogapātrikam ālambyeti vāmena kapālaṃ dadhānāḥ | śrīherukādita iti śrīherukādayaḥ | sarveti sarve | ālīḍhāsanasaṃyutam iti prathamā bahuvacane | śrīherukasya viśeṣaṇam āha | kaṇṭhiketyādinā | yajñopavītabhasmena mudreti ṣaṭ prakīrtitam iti | ity anena krameṇa yajñopavītabhasmabhyāṃ saha kaṇṭhikādayaḥ ṣaṇ mudrāḥ prakīrtitā ity arthaḥ |
ṣaṭpāramitāviśuddhitaḥ ṣaḍbhir mudrair vibhūṣitam iti ṣaṭpāramitāviśuddhaṣaṇmudrāvibhūṣitadeho bhagavān iti bhāvaḥ | kāyavākcittabhedena tryakṣarasvabhāvata iti kāyādisvabhāvaṃ yat tryakṣaraṃ nādarūpāpannam | tasmād utpanno yaḥ svabhāvo hūṃkārākāraḥ sa tryakṣarasvabhāvaḥ | tatas tasmād utpannam ity adhyāhāryam |
dhyāyāt svabhāvātmānam iti śrīsaṃvarasvabhāvenātmānaṃ dhyāyāt | kim anyaiḥ siddhikāṅkṣiṇa iti buddhatvābhilāṣiṇo yoginaḥ svabhāvāntarair anyaiḥ kiṃ prayojanam iti māhātmyakathanam | anuśaṃsam āha | yat puṇyam ityādinā | bhavate iti bhavati | tasyeti saṃvarayoginaḥ | mukhānekam ity uddeśaḥ | sahasrānanam iti nirdeśaḥ |
mukhānāṃ sahasreṇāpīty arthaḥ | tasya puṇyasyeti tatpuṇyam | sādhaka iti sādhakasya | guṇavistara iti vistīrṇaguṇam | kākāsyādi tu catvāra iti kākāsyādayaś catasraḥ | dvārasarve 'pi rudhyata iti sarvā api etā dvāracatuṣṭayam avarudhya sthitā ity arthaḥ | yamadāḍhīti yamadāḍhyādayaś catasraḥ | koṇe catvārīti caturṣu koṇeṣu |
tadvat pādeneti ālīḍhena | dvau hi rūpaṃ manoharam iti yamadāḍhyādidevatīcatuṣṭayaṃ pratyekaṃ manoharadvidvirūpam ity arthaḥ | yathā ḍākinījanasyeti yathā ḍākinyādīnāṃ varṇā iti śeṣaḥ | kākāsyādi tu bhedata iti yathā kākāsyādīnāṃ bhedato 'saṃkīrṇena rūpeṇa sthitānām ity arthaḥ | eva[3]m ityādinopasaṃhāraḥ |
samastacakrastham iti samastānāṃ cakrasthitānām | varṇabhedavidhikramam iti prathamārthe | upalakṣaṇam idaṃ bhujādayo 'pi veditavyā iti | iti ṣaṣṭho nirdeśaḥ || 6 ||
atha kavacadvayaṃ vakṣya ityādi | kavacadvayam iti bhagavadbhagavatyoḥ kavacamantrāṇāṃ ṣaṭkadvayam | kiṃ kṛtvety āha | gopyatantreṣu nirṇayam iti tasya ṣaṭkadvayasya nirṇayaṃ niścayaṃ vistaratantre saṃgopya yena tasya niścayo bhavati tat tatra saṃgopya iha tantre vyaktaṃ kathayiṣyāmīty arthaḥ | astreti dharmasaṃketena sarvāṅge yojitam iti |
oṃ ha ityādīnāṃ hṛdayādiṣu yojanaṃ kartavyam iti bhāvaḥ | prathamaṃ vajrasattvaṃ tv iti hṛdi vajrasattvaḥ sākṣād adhimokṣato vā draṣṭavyaḥ | evaṃ vairocanādayo vajravārāhyādayo 'py anantaraṃ vaktavyā veditavyāḥ | astram eva ceti sarvāṅge | vīrādvayayoginīti vīreṇa sahādvayajñānāya yogaḥ samāpattiḥ | sa yasyā asti sā vīrādvayayoginīti bhāvaḥ |
iti saptamo nirdeśaḥ || 7 ||
tata ityādi | kavacadvayeneti anantaroktena | ātmānam iti śrīsaṃvarātmakaṃ svaśarīraṃ yathāsthānaṃ kavacayitveti śeṣaḥ | jñānacakraṃ vibhāvitam iti hṛdbījaraśmibhir ānītaṃ jñānamaṇḍalaṃ purato vibhāvayet | praveśyeti praveśayet | āśv iti arghapādyādikaṃ vinā | mudrāmantreṇeti vakṣyamāṇamudrāmantrābhyām | yogina iti yogī | ākrāntapādeti |
vāmapādena dakṣiṇaṃ pādam ākramya | ūrdhvadṛṣṭiṃ tv iti vāmavalitordhvadṛṣṭyā | mūrdhneti mukhena | pheṃkāranādata iti pheṃkāramantroccāraṇāt | daśadiglokadhātuḥ syād vīrayoginyākarṣita iti mantreṇānena daśadiglokadhātusthā vīrā yoginyaś cākṛṣṭā bhavantīty arthaḥ | saṃcodanaṃ tu mantreṇeti anenaiva mantreṇa jñānacakraṃ saṃcodayet |
āvāhayet kṣaṇād iti vakṣyamāṇamudrayā ākarṣayet jhaṭitīty arthaḥ | kṛtvetyādi | tām iti mudrām | madhyalalāṭadeśa iti lalāṭamadhye | āvartāvartabhrāmiteneti āvartyāvartya bhrāmayet | idānīṃ samāpattiṃ darśayitum āha | adhiṣṭhayed ityādi | adhiṣṭhayed iti mantroccāraṇapūrvakaṃ yathāsthānam anyonyaṃ cumbayet | yoginīvīram iti devī devaś ca |
advayam iti samāpattinimittam | nāsikāgra iti kamalanāsāyām | liṅgavaraṭake iti vajravaraṭake | adhiṣṭhayen mantragaṇasya sārdham iti bhagavadbhagavatyor hṛdayopahṛdayamantroccāraṇapūrvakaṃ nābhiprabhṛtiṣu bhagavān bhagavatīṃ cumbayed ity arthaḥ | yoginī buddhaḍākinīti vajravārāhī | jihveti jihvāyām |
avadhūtadvayeneti bhagavaddhṛdayopahṛdayamantrābhyām | vīram adhiṣṭhayed iti | vīraṃ bhagavantaṃ nābhiprabhṛtiṣu cumbayet | yoginīvīram advayam iti sarvam etat kṛtvā yoginyā saha vīraṃ samāpannaṃ bhāvayed ity arthaḥ | samāpattiprayojanam āha | ānandam ityādi | vīram iti vīra iva | yoginīti yoginīva |
suratānandaṃ prati prajñopāyatvam anayor iti bhāvaḥ | samastaṃ vai iti sarvam eva traya[4]m apīty arthaḥ | tad evāha | tad ityādinā | tatsukheti sasuratānandaḥ | upāyeti cālanalakṣaṇo vyāpāraḥ | sarvavid iti saṃbhogakāyātmakaṃ sāṃvṛtabodhicittam | sahajasāmānyena trayam api sahajānandasvarūpam ity arthaḥ | sahajānandasya māhātmyam āha |
martyetyādinā | ekamūrtim iti ekasvarūpam | kṣaṇād iti sahajānandāt | sahajānandabalān martyādikaṃ mahāsukhasvabhāvaṃ bhavatīty arthaḥ | bhāvanā hi mayā proktam iti mudrāmantrābhyāṃ jñānacakrākarṣaṇabhāvanā proktaiva | saṃhāraṃ tv iti | ānītajñānacakrasyātmani praveśaḥ | vidhismṛtam iti yathāvidhi vistaratantre kathita |
sa idānīṃ kathyata iti śeṣaḥ | saṃhāram eva darśayati | vāmetyādinā | bhramantam iti bhrāmaṇena | ḍākinī tatsukham iti tayā mudrayā yoginīnāṃ saṃtoṣo jāyate | oṃ yogaśuddhāḥ sarvadharmā yogaśuddho 'ham iti mantroccāraṇapūrvakam anayā mudrayā jñānamaṇḍalaṃ samayamaṇḍale praveśayed iti bhāvaḥ | ity aṣṭamo nirdeśaḥ || 8 ||
tata ityādi | nairmāṇikam iti jagadarthaṃ prati spharaṇasaṃharaṇātmakaṃ devatādehaṃ nirmāṇakāyasvabhāvaṃ jñātveti śeṣaḥ | jñātavyaṃ saṃbhogam ataḥ śṛṇv iti ato 'nena vakṣyamāṇakrameṇa jñātavyaṃ saṃbhogakāyaṃ śṛṇv iti saṃbandhaḥ | mahāyānam iti yānti tad iti yānam | mahac ca tad yānaṃ ceti mahāyānam | mahāmudreti śrīsaṃvaraśarīram |
yoginīnāṃ siddhidās tatheti tathā tādṛśena rūpeṇa yoginyādīnāṃ kūṭāgārasthitānāṃ dharmadeśanādvāreṇa siddhiṃ dadātīty arthaḥ | ata eva dharmasaṃbhogadvāreṇa kūṭāgārasthitadevatābhiḥ saṃbhujyamānatvāt saṃbhogakāya ity ucyate | mahāmudrād iti mahāmudrāyāḥ paraṃ nāstīti | anyac chreṣṭhaṃ na vidyate | kuta ity āha |
sarvayogeṣu cottamam iti yasmād ayaṃ sarvayogānāṃ madhye uttamo yogas tasmād ity arthaḥ |
kaḥ punar asāv ity āha | śrīherukamahārājaṃ sarvayogeśvaṃ prabhum iti sarvaṃ sugamam | sarvatra prathamārthe dvitīyā | khasamoddhṛta iti uttamatvād eva khasamasaṃjñakāt tantrād uddhṛta ity arthaḥ | sarvajñajñānatanmayam iti sarvajñānāṃ samyaksaṃbuddhānāṃ yaj jñānaṃ mahāsukhākhyaṃ tatsvabhāvo 'sāv iti bhāvaḥ |
śrīherukaṃ paraṃ vīram ity anena sugamaślokena madhye gaṇabhojanaṃ sūcitam | anenaiva yogena samānāśayayogibhiḥ saha somapānādividhinā laukikalokottarasiddhiṃ sādhayed iti khyāpanārtham | pukārādi samāyojyeti pullīramalayādikaṃ buddhāv āropya | deśe deśe vyavasthitam iti teṣu teṣu deśeṣu vyavasthitaṃ pracaṇḍādikdevatīgaṇam |
śrīherukātmeti śrīherukātmani bhāvayed ity adhyāhāryam | kathaṃ bhāvayed ity āha | vinyasya caturviṃśatim akṣaram iti | pukārādīni caturviṃśatyakṣarāṇi śiraḥprabhṛtiṣu vinyasya tattadgatā nāḍīḥ pracaṇḍādirūpeṇa bhāvayed iti bhā[4]vaḥ | upasaṃharann āha | sāṃbhogikam ityādi | ity anena krameṇa | sāṃbhogikam iti saṃbhogakāyaḥ |
khyātam iti kathitaḥ | mahāyogavyavasthitam iti mahāyogasamādhim avadhīkṛtya vyavasthāpita ity arthaḥ | pravṛttiṃ tv iti anena krameṇa śrīherukasyotpattiṃ jñāpayed iti jānīyāt | nivṛttiṃ tu yathoddiṣṭaṃ kathyata iti yathā tasya nivṛttir niṣedho dharmakāyabhāvanayā kathitā sā kathyata ity arthaḥ | śrīkāram ityādi sugamam |
ete svabhāvataḥ śūnyam iti ete bhujamukhādayaḥ svabhāvato grāhyagrāhakākāreṇa śūnyaṃ niḥsvabhāvāḥ | paramārthato mahāsukhasvabhāvatvāt | tac ca mahāsukhaṃ sarvagāmīti sarvasya jagato vyāpakam | anāhatam iti grāhyādivikalpair apratihatam | bhāvayitvetyādi sugamam |

viśeṣata iti vāmadakṣiṇābhyāṃ bhrāmaṇaviśeṣeṇa | kṛṣṇapakṣa ityādi sugamam | bhojayitavyacakrākarṣaṇavidhim āha | ākrāntetyādinā prāg eva vyākhyātam | etad eva vyaktīkartum āha | pūrvoktetyādi | pūrvoktamudreti jvālāmudrā | ādiśabdena pādākramaṇapūrvakordhvadṛṣṭiḥ | mantreti pheṃkāraḥ |
yogair iti eteṣāṃ mudrādīnāṃ yojanena ānīya maṇḍalam iti śeṣaḥ | saṃrakṣya saṃrakṣyena yathāvidhānata iti ātmānaṃ ca yathāvidhi śrīsaṃvararūpeṇa vibhāvitaṃ kavacadvayena kavacayitvety arthaḥ | mantram āha | oṃ ityādi | evam iti idaṃ mantram | samayadravyādisusthitam abhimantritam iti pareṇa saṃbandhaḥ | samayadravyaṃ gokudahanādikam |
ādiśabdena bhaktavyañjanādikam avaseyam | susthitam iti amṛtāsvādavidhinā susaṃskṛtya sthāpitam | ūrdhvavikaceti ūrdhvavikacayā | vīrayoginīti vīrāya saprajñāya ḍākinyādiyamamathanīyaparyantābhyaś ca yoginībhyo dadyād iti śeṣaḥ | khaṇḍakapālādivīrebhyaḥ punaḥ oṃkarakarādimantreṇa ekavāroccāritena dadyāt | oṃ kha kha khāhi [5] khāhītyādi |
anena mantreṇa vāradvayoccāritena dikpālebhyo deya ity arthaḥ | devatāyogayukto baliṃ dadyād iti darśayitum āha | vīretyādi | vīro bhagavān | sa eva tattvamananāj jagattrāṇanāc ca mantraḥ | ādiśabdena vajravārāhyādisarvadevatānāṃ saṃgrahaḥ | teṣāṃ cakraṃ kūṭāgāram | śmaśānādimaṇḍitam iti aṣṭaśmaśānādibhir yuktam |
ādiśabdād bāhyavajrāvalī veditavyā | dakṣiṇābhimukha ity upalakṣaṇaparam | tena nagno muktakeśaś cety avaseyam | uccasthāneṣv iti śmaśānaparvatādāv uccasthāne mṛtakopaviṣṭaḥ | pūjitam iti bhāvayet | bhāvayitvā kiṃ kartavyam ity āha | dātavyam ityādi | dātavyam iti dravadravyādikam | devatābhya upaḍhaukayet |
ātmaśaktyeti yathāśakti vibhavānurūpeṇeti bhāvaḥ | yathākramalakṣitam iti saṃskāraṃ pratibhāvanākramānatikrameṇa bhāvitam ity arthaḥ | idānīṃ gaṇabhojanam āha | puṣyetyādinā | pūjyeti | yoginīnāṃ kṛte pañcopacārapūjāṃ kṛtvā | dātavyaṃ sarvasadbhāvam iti sarvaṃ vidyamānaṃ bhaktavyañjanādikaṃ saṃpādanīyam | sarvasajjanamelakam iti gaṇamaṇḍale |
tatreti gaṇamaṇḍale | dvipātrāśeṣam iti dravadravyeṇa saha dvipātragatam aśeṣaṃ bhaktavyañjanādikam | saṃskṛtam iti amṛtāsvādavidhiniṣpāditam | anuśaṃsam āha | tasyetyādinā | varāṇām iti varāṇi | iti daśamo nirdeśaḥ || 10 ||
tata ityādi | pāramitāṣaṇmātrādīti yāḥ kaṇṭhikādyāḥ ṣaṇmātrāḥ | ādīti ādisiddhāḥ | pāramitā iti tāḥ pāramitāsvabhāvā ity arthaḥ | bodhītyādi | bodhiṣaṭtriṃśam eva cety asya paramāyurdevatātmakam ity anena padena saṃbandhaḥ kartavyaḥ | paramāyur iti samyaksamādhiḥ |
devatātmakam iti ye śrīherukādīnāṃ devatānāṃ dehās te samyaksamādhinā saha saptatriṃśadbodhipākṣikasvabhāvā iti bhāvaḥ | pīṭhādidaśabhūmyasyeti asya sādhakasya yat pīṭhādikaṃ pullīramalayādi tan muditādidaśabhūmisvarūpam iti | anena sarveṇedaṃ pratipāditaṃ sarvam idaṃ mātrādikaṃ pāramitādirūpeṇa vibhāvya viśodhayitavyam iti |
bhāvanayā katham etad anyathā kartuṃ śakyata ity āha | yena yenetyādi | bhāveneti pāramitādinā | tanmayatām iti pāramitādisvabhāvatām | viśvarūpo maṇir yatheti yathā sphaṭikopalaḥ svaccho nīlapītādipadārthasaṃnidhāne nīlapītādimān bhavati tathedaṃ mana ity arthaḥ | upasaṃharann āha | evam ityādi | evam anena krameṇa |
yathā yathā rūḍham iti yatra yatra pāramitādau pravṛttaṃ bhavati | sabhaye nirbhaye 'pi ceti sthāna iti śeṣaḥ | dhyāna iti tasya pāramitāder buddhatvāvadhidhyānena | cintitamātraṃ ceti buddhatvād arvāg bhāvanayā | sarvakarmaprasādhaka iti yat kiṃcid abhilaṣitaṃ tat sarvaṃ svapareṣāṃ sādhayati tan mana iti arthaḥ | ata evāha | prāpyata ityādi |
janmasyeti janmani | bhuktimuktiphalapradam iti buddhatvaṃ bodhisattvatvaṃ vā svapareṣām abhilaṣitaphalapradānasamarthaṃ prāpyata iti pūrveṇa saṃbandhaḥ | bhāvanā hītyādi | [5] kaścit kiṃcit | mithyājñānaṃ tu ity ajñānahetukam | jñāninām iti parijñānārthinām | yenetyādi | seti taj janmakoṭisahasraṃ vai iti janmakoṭisahasreṇāpi |
mayā jñānena coditeti tasya parijñānārthaṃ mayā vyaktīkṛtam | yaj janmakoṭisahasreṇa kṣipraśabdavācyena jñātavyaṃ sādhakena tad adhunaiva sphuṭīkṛtam ity arthaḥ | yogacaryānayasthitam iti tṛtīyāyām | idaṃ ca bhāvyamānaṃ jaḍādiviṣayo na bhavatīty āha | jaḍetyādi | kūpāyasyeti upāyarahitasya | bālajñānam iti bālajanasyeva jñānam |
jñāninām iti parijñānārthināṃ madhye | aparipācitasattvasyeti prathamārthe ṣaṣthī | mānādisaptacetaseti mānātimānetyādinā | śrīherukasamāveśam iti śrīherukasvarūpam | ācāryasya mahātmaneti guror mahāśayasya | antikād viditvety adhyāhāryam | anuśaṃsam āha | saṃcāretyādinā | saṃcāravidhīti saṃcāravidhim | vidhānavidhisevitam iti yathāvidhi sevayet |
te 'pīti sa ca | sarvajñavarṇasyeti sarvajñānāṃ māhātmyaṃ labhata iti śeṣaḥ | samastaṃ bhuvanatrayam iti atra cakāro draṣṭavyaḥ | darśanam ityādi atra sarvatra tṛtīyārthe prathamā | tasya bhagavato darśanādinety arthaḥ | viparītadevatānyāsam iti paryantabhūtamahāvīryāyāḥ praceto'bhidhānād vistaratantre |
viparītamantrabhedanam iti hā svāhetyādinā viparītamantroddhārāt | viparītabhāvanāgopyam iti astavyastakrameṇa bhāvanākramasyābhidhānāt | sarvasāraprasādhaka iti sarvam idaṃ sarvasārasya śrīsaṃvarasya prasādhakam ity arthaḥ | sāmānyaṃ sarvatantrāṇām iti ayaṃ bhagavān niruttararūpatvāt sarvānyatatrasādhāraṇaḥ |
aparipācitacetasām iti aparipācitacetaso 'pi krameṇa budhyante iti teṣām api sādhāraṇaḥ | anuyogādimantrāṇāṃ samayadarśakas tatheti bhāvyamānatvena yogātiyogādisamādhiṣv antargatatvāt teṣāṃ svarūpadarśaka ity arthaḥ | ayaṃ cāpuṇyavatāṃ viṣayo na bhavatīty āha | uktam ityādi | tāthāgatāśvāsa iti tathāgatā eva tāthāgatāḥ | teṣām āśvāsas tasmin |
āśvāsanimittam ity arthaḥ | yadi puṇyavatām evāyaṃ viṣayaḥ puṇyarahitānāṃ tarhi ka upāyo bhaviṣyatīti | tathāgatānām asukham utpannaṃ tadanantaraṃ tathāgatānām āśvāsanimittam uktaṃ bhagavatā | kim uktam ity āha | śuddhakāyetyādi sugamam | ity ekādaśo nirdeśaḥ || 11 ||
tato na rakṣaṇīyam ityādi | na homa itiparyantaṃ subodham | yadi rakṣaṇīyaprabhṛtikam anupayuktaṃ kas tarhi bhāvya ity āha | samāsata ityādi | samāsato rakṣaṇīyādi sarvasaṃgrahataḥ | cittasamājarūpīti cittaṃ mahāsukhamayam advayaṃ bodhicittam | tad eva samājo milanaṃ sarvadharmāṇām ekarasarūpatvāt | tadrūpī bhagavān iti śeṣaḥ | grāhyam ityādi |

evaṃvido yogakṛte  mārga iti evaṃvidaḥ sādhakasya kṛte devadevīyogalakṣaṇo mārga iti arthaḥ | pīṭhādi yoginīsthānakam iti pīṭhādisthāneṣu yoginyaḥ kathyanta ity arthaḥ | vīram advayasaṃsthitā iti vīrasamāpannāḥ sthitā ity arthaḥ | pullīretyādi sugamam | vīrādvayayoginīti vīreṇa sahādvayayogam āpannā ity arthaḥ |
vāmasādhana iti yoginīsādhanārtham | sopāyeneti ārādhanārtham upāyasahitena | uttāryeti uttīrya | bhāvanākramam āha | kaṇṭhiketyādinā sugamam | vartayed iti udvartayet | vāriṇeti akṣobhyeṇa | gandhakair iti vairocanena | varṇakācchādirūpam iti varṇakaiḥ śvetādirūpaṃ kuryāt | svareṇa parivartanam iti svaraḥ krodhānāṃ vacanaṃ hūṃkāraḥ |
tenātmano niṣpattiṃ bhāvayet | caryāvratapravṛttasyeti saprapañcacaryādhāriṇaḥ | yogasusaṃsthitam iti yoge samyag avasthānam | sāttvika iti vīrabībhatsyādiko nāṭyarasaḥ | subhago mahān iti nāṭye kṛte yoginīnām atiśayena priyo bhavatīty arthaḥ | avedhetyādy uddeśaḥ | avedhyam ityādi nirdeśaḥ |
ekayaṣṭi bhavet sūtram iti ekayaikāvalyā brahmasūtraṃ kartavyam | mekhalā tv aṣṭayaṣṭiketi aṣṭabhir ekāvalībhir yuktā mekhalā kāryā | rasanā ṣoḍaśā jñeyeti rasaneti mekhalāviśeṣaḥ | sā ca ṣoḍaśabhir ekāvalībhir yuktā veditavyāḥ | pañcaviṃśat tu mālinam iti muṇḍamālā pañcaviṃśatyā muṇḍaiḥ kartavyā | atha vā mālinam iti kaṇṭhikā |
sā ca pañcaviṃśatyā gulikābhir veditavyeti | iti saptadaśo nirdeśaḥ || 17 ||
yat puṇyam indusadṛśaṃ gaganāvarodhi
saṃcāratantraviṣadīkaraṇān mayāptam |
tenāstu sarvajagato jagato hitāya
sambodhilabdhir acireṇa vinaiva pīḍām ||
iti saṃcāratantranibandhaḥ samāptaḥ ||
