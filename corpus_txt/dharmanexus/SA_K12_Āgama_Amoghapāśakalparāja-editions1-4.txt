§1 atha vajrapāṇir mahāyakṣasenāpati avalokiteśvaraṃ bodhisattvaṃ mahāsattvam etad avocat | bhāṣasva bhagavann amoghapadmoṣṇīṣamaṇḍalaṃ amoghapāśahṛdayaṃ sopacāravidhisādhanaṃ kuru bhagavan sarvavidyādharāṇāṃ siddhyāśāparipūraṇaṃ tattvasādhanasidhividhim |
§2 athāryāvalokiteśvaro bodhisattvo mahāsattvo vajrapāṇiṃ mahāyakṣasenāpatim etad avocat. śṛṇu vajrapāṇe pravakṣyāmi upacārasādhanavidhim. asya vajrapāṇe mūlamantrasya amoghapāśapadmoṣṇīṣahṛdayaguhyasya vidyādharasya vā kulaputrasya vā kuladuhitur vā bhikṣur vā bhikṣuṇīr vā upāśaka-m upāsikā ekavelāsmāritamātreṇa pariśuddhamanasikārātmabhāvā mahākaruṇāmaitrīsahagatenādhyāśayena sarvasattvālambanena. na ca kaścit sattvas triṣv apāyeṣūpapadyate. narakatiryagyonipretaviṣayeṣu vā nedaṃ sthānaṃ vidyate. eṣa sarvasattva sādhanamahākuśalamūlasamanvāgato bhaviṣyanti.

§3 dvirudāhṛtamātreṇa sarvavyādhiṃ praśamayati. trirudāhṛtamātreṇa sarvaviṣalūtagaṇḍalohaliṅgaṃ vicarcikakuṣṭhapāmādīṃ nāśayati. caturodāharaṇamātrayā sarvagarakākhordam apanayati. pañcamodāharaṇamātreṇa sarvakalikalahavigrahavivādaṃ praśamayanti. ṣaḍvārodāharaṇamātreṇa sarvabhayagrahajvarāṇi vināśayanti. saptavārodāhṛtamātreṇa sarvākālamṛtyubhayebhyaḥ sarva-ītayo sarvasattvānāṃ praśamayanti.
§4 tasmād vajrapāṇe sarvasattvānām arthāya nipatite anālāpataḥ saptavārā smārayitavyam. yaḥ kaścit sarvasattva narakaparāyaṇo kālagataḥ, yaḥ kaścid bhikṣavo khaṇḍaśikṣāsaṃvaraśīlasaṃvaraḥ, ācāryopādhyāya agauravaḥ kṛtaḥ kālagataḥ, tasyārthe śucivālukāṃ gṛhya saptavārāṃ parijapya śmaśāne vā cite vā kūṭe vā tatra vāluko kiritavyam. samanantarāvakīrṇena vālukā yatra yatra te sattvā asthīni vālukāni patanti tatra tatra ye sattvā niraye preteṣūpapannā piśācena yakṣarākṣasayonir vā upapannāḥ sakṣaṇena cyavitvā sarve svargaparāyaṇā bhaviṣyanti. na ca bhūya narakaparāyaṇā bhaviṣyanti.
§5 athavā bhūrjapatre likhitvā caityamadhye sthāpayan ye ca tatra sthāne dvipadacatuṣpadā-m-upasaṃkramanti te sarve cchāyānipātamātreṇa sarvapāpāvaraṇāni viśudhyante. sarve svargaparāyaṇā bhaviṣyanti. antaśa mṛgapakṣiṇa-d-antaśa śalabhanakulavṛścikāśīviṣaśvānakūrmabhujago vā tatra cchāyānipātamātreṇa sarvakarmāvaraṇāni viśudhyante. sarve te svargalokaparāyaṇā bhaviṣyanti.
% NB eds. anne śarabha unlikely
§6 śaṅkha saptavārā parijapya uccasthāne-m-āpūrayan ye ca sattvā taṃ śaṅkhaśabdaṃ śrutvā strīpuruṣadārakadārikāmṛgapakṣiṇatiryagyonigatā te sarve svargalokaparāyaṇā bhaviṣyanti. ye ca pariśuddhaśīlā ye ca sattvā dānābhiratāḥ, ye ca upavāsam upavasanti, ye ca triśaraṇadhāriṇā, ye ca bodhisattvasaṃvarasaṃvṛtā, te ca śaṅkhaśabdaṃ śrutvā te sarve avinivartanīyā bhaviṣyanti. anuttarāyāṃ samyaksaṃbodhau pratilapsyante. cyuto janmaparivarte sukhavatīlokadhātum upapadyate. jātau jātismaraś ca bhaviṣyati.
§7 candanaṃ saptavārā parijapya saṃghārāme vā caityāṅgane vā saṃghamadhye vā mahājanamadhye vā rājakule vā dhūpayan ye ca tatra gandham āghrāyanti te sarve buddhākāyapariniṣpannā bhavanti.
§8 cīvaraṃ dhūpayatā yasya śarīre prāvarati tasya sarvapāpāvaraṇāni vinaśyanti. śarīranipatitamātreṇa sarvamalamātsaryadoṣaprahīṇo bhavati. vastradhāraṇamātreṇa sarvaloka vaśavartā bhaviṣyanti.
§9 ātmānaṃśa vastraṃ dhūpayatā rājakulaṃ praviśed rājānaṃśāntaḥpuraparivārā vaśagatā bhaviṣyanti. mahāpūjanīyaś ca bhaviṣyati. sarvalokagurukaś ca bhaviṣyati. āryasaṅghamadhye praviśet sarvabhikṣavamahāpūjanīyārho bhaviṣyati. mahāghanamadhye praviśen mahādhanavṛddhir bhaviṣyati. mahāpūjārhaś ca bhaviṣyati.
% anne mahāgaṇamadhye
§10 ādityamukha śikharamūrdhni dhūpayatā mūlamantraḥ saptavārā japtā sadyaḥ ādityam avatarati. sarvakāmatāvaram anupradāsyati. guggulumiśraṃ mahādevasyāgrato dhūpa dātavyaḥ saptavārā mūlamantreṇa omārjayan sadyaiva mahādevo avatarati. vidyādharasya agrata-m-upatiṣṭhati. sarvābhiprāyaṃ paripūrayati.
§11 bolacandanaguggulumiśreṇa nārāyaṇasya agrataḥ dhūpo dātavyaḥ. mūlamantreṇa ekaviṃśativārā śarīravṛndam omarjayitavyam. nārāyaṇaḥ svarūpeṇa agrata-m-upatiṣṭhati. sarvavārām anupradāsyati. viśvarūpam api carayati.
§12 sarṣapamiśreṇa brāhmaṇaṃ dhūpayatā caturdaśavārā mūlamantra omārjayan brahmāṇaṃ svarūpeṇa agrata-m-upatiṣṭhati. sarvavaraprado bhaviṣyati.
§13 padmacūrṇamiśreṇa mātṛgaṇaṃ mahākālasya dhūpo dātavyaḥ. mahākālaḥ samātṛgaṇaparivāraṃ svarūpeṇa agrata-m-upatiṣṭhati. sarvavarakāryasādhanavaraprado bhaviṣyati. nāgapuṣpakavacena saha nāgasare dhūpo dātavyaḥ. saptavārā mūlamantra sarṣapam parijapya nāgasare prakṣiptavyaḥ. amoghapāśahṛdayasūtra ekavārā smārayet. sarvanāgā nāgakanyā nāgadārakadārikā sarvabhavanā nirgacchanti. vidyādharasya pādayoḥ prapatanti. yad ājñāpayati tat sarva kariṣyanti. sarvakāmadavaśavartī bhaviṣyanti. tribhiḥ prayuktena sāgaranāgarājā-m-api āgacchanti. nandopanandanāgarājā āgacchanti. sarve ājñākarā vaśagatā tiṣṭhanti.
% NB ref to Amoghapāśahṛdayasūtra
§14 yakṣarākṣasān āvāhayitukāmena śmaśānamadhye citāṅgāreṇa dhūpayan saptavārā mūlamantram smārayan uccasvareṇa pradhāvamānāni yakṣarākṣasā-d-āgacchanti. vidyādharasya pādayoḥ prapatanti. "ājñāpaya bhaṭṭāraka." tathā vidyādhareṇa yathājñayā tad vaktavyam. sarvam ājñā kariṣyanti. preṣyakarmakarā bhaviṣyanti. yāvajjīvam upatiṣṭhanti. kapālasaṃpuṭe saptavārāṃ parijapya kapālā pūrṇā dhūpaṃ dātavyaḥ. sarvabhūtā-d-āgamiṣyanti. sarve vaśagatakarmakarā bhaviṣyanti.
§15 yakṣiṇīm āvāhayitukāmena gaurikena yakṣiṇīm ālikhya gomayamaṇḍalakaṃ kṛtvā śuklapadma citrayitavyam. śuklapadme baliṃ sthāpitavyam. anena dhūpena dātavyam. mūlamantram aṣṭottaraśataṃ japitavyam. saptavārā parijapya yakṣiṇī tāḍayitavyam. yakṣiṇī svarūpeṇa agrata-m-upatiṣṭhati yāvad devalokaṃ gacchati punar āgacchati. sarvaguhyakāryāṇi ājñāpitamātreṇa kariṣyati. athavā mātā bhāryā bhaginīti vaktavyam. yadi mātā putrasnehayā sarvabhogavimānānuvartī rājaputram iva paripālayati. yadi bhaginī vaiśravaṇapratispardhī bhaviṣyati. yadi bhāryā tridaśādhipati-m-iva ratibhogāṃ krīḍāpayati.
§16 śriyā devyā dhūpayatā aṣṭavārā mūlamantraṃ japatā śriyā devyā śriyo mārjyo śriyā mahādevyā agrata-m-upatiṣṭhati. mahādhanaskandhāni nivedayati. yāvajjīvaṃ gṛhe upatiṣṭhati. mahatā dhanavṛddhir bhaviṣyati.
§17 vaiśravaṇaṃ dhūpayan suvarṇasahasraṃ dadāti. maṇibhadraṃ dhūpayatā dīnārasahasraṃ dadāti.
§18 vanavivare dhūpayatā vanavivāsitadevatā svarūpeṇa agrata-m-upatiṣṭhanti. sarvadravyauṣadhayaḥ svarūpeṇa agrata-m-upatiṣṭhanti.
§19 parvateṣu dhūpayatā sarvatṛṇagulmauṣadhayaḥ dravyāṇi jvalanti.
§20 caturṣu nagaradvāreṣu dhūpayatā samantadvāreṣu dhūpayatā sarvajvarā parimucyādhayaḥ sarvakalikalahavigrahavivādaṃ praśamiṣyanti.
§21 aṭavyāṃ dhūpayatā sarvacoradhūrtataskarasiṃhavyāghravyāḍā ṛkṣatarakṣabhayā na bhaviṣyanti. sarvacaṇḍamṛgabhayā na bhaviṣyanti.
§22 sarvajvareṣu dhūpayatā sarvajvarā parimucyante.
§23 sarvapāpa-m-āvaraṇo candanadhūpavidhisādhanaṃ tṛtīyaḥ.

§2 tilakā kriyamānasya lalāṭe upari sadā virājite bhāskaratulye ca sahasrajālīraśmir avabhāsitam. sphaṭikam iva śuddha †śyāmavarṇa† sugandhaṃ gajabāhukumbhī gokulānavadyaṃ samāsahitasudāntam ekasārthamaṇi syāt. mantraśatasahasrajaptā susādhyaṃ kanakam iva vastraṃ prajvalantaṃ samantaṃ tilakam iti.
§3 susūkṣma bhrūdharāntare lalāṭaṃ virājati sadā nityaṃ, khe gate pūrṇacandraṃ jvalati ca janamadhye candranakṣatrarāje ca madhyaṃ bhavati, svajanamadhye śakrarājeśvara iva jvalati ca janamadhye lokarājaikachattram, sugatam iva sudāntaṃ, lokamadhya ekachatraṃ, śākyakulam iva vaṃśaṃ sarvasattvahitaiṣī. evaṃ maṇivara viśuddhaṃ sarvasattvahitaiṣī. yathā-r-iva sadevako lokeśvaraiko varapravaradātā sarvasattvahitārtham. evaṃ tilakamaṇiprabhāvaḥ sarvakāmapradātā. vaśagata-m-upetadevanāgayakṣa tridaśapati indro vaśavṛttasyānujāte gandharvāsuragarūḍakinnaramahoragāḥ cāpi bhūtaiḥ sarve ca vaśagatā vaiḥ. gaganatārakābhālalāṭe nityaṃ vaśagatā vaiḥ sarvasattvā bhavanti praṇataśirā vaiḥ pādamūle ca nityam agnir na dahati hrīyate nodakam vā. viṣagarakākhordadāruṇaṃ mantravividhaduṣṭā nākramante śarīram. maṇi-r-iva prabhāvāt sarve-m-ete na bhonti.
§4 jvaramahatiduḥkhavyādhivātaśirortiḥ tilakakṛtya-m-iva sadyas sarvam etat praśāntam. bandhanagatasattvā daṇḍavadhyārho vā tilaka-m-iva kṛto vaiḥ sarvam etad vimuci. yakṣaśatasahasrāṇi rākṣasaghorarūpaiḥ grahaśatasahasrai nākramante śarīraṃ. gajamṛgapatiyūthaṃ taskaraṃ bhraṃśakūṭaṃ giriprapātaṃ vidyutaṃ śītavātāśinondurabhayaṃ sarvaṃ praśamam upeti tatkṣaṇaṃ tilaka-m-iva kriyamāṇaṃ bhrūdharāntare lalāṭaṃ.
§5 śatruśatasahasrasainyamadhye yodhyān tilaka-m-iva prabhāvāt śīghram eva jināti. raṇakalivyavahāre sadyas eva praśānti. tadānena maṇibandhena mūrdhacūḍāmaṇir yaḥ tilakaṃ ca kṛtvā cakraṃ lepayet paracakraraṇamadhye praveṣṭavyam, ekenātmabhāvena caturaṅgaṃ balakāyaṃ vijeṣyati. anayā sarveṣu praviṣṭamātreṇa anayā vyasanam āpadyante, daśavidiśāni prapalāyante, sarve pralayam āpadyante. tilakaṃ kṛtvā rājakulaṃ praviśed rājānaṃ sāṇtaḥpuraparivārāṃ vaśagatām upatiṣṭhanti. yāvaj jīvena pūjanīyo bhaviṣyati. dakṣiṇabāhuṃ maṇiṃ baddhvā tilakam aṅga-m-aṅgāni kārayan sarvatra jayam āpnoti, spṛṣṭamātrā vaśaṃkara, yaṃ yaṃ yāpayase arthaṃ tat sarvaṃ labhiṣyati.

§11 athavā smṛtibuddhipravardhayitukāmena śrutim āsādya tiṣṭhati. tena vidyādhareṇa śucinā saptarātreṇa kṣīraghṛtakvathitena dine dine saptaguḍikā tatra bhāvaya sukhoṣṇe. prathamena proktena ekaviśaṃtivārān parijapya pītavyaṃ paścād bhojanaṃ bhoktavyaṃ. saptarātreṇa mahāśrutisāgarasaṃnicayo bhavati, sarvaśāstrāṇi nayavinayakāvyavyākaraṇasūtrageyagāthodānanidānetivṛttakajātakābhidharmagaṇanakalpamantramudrāsarvamahāyānāt mukhāgre 'vatiṣṭhanti.

§12 trisaptāhaprayuktayā mahābalavīryaparākramo bhavati, daśanāgasahasrabalo bhavati. māsamātraprayuktena pañca varṣaśatāni jīvati, valīpalitavarjitaḥ, ākuñcitakuṇḍalakeśo bhaviṣyati, padmagaurasamaśarīravarṇo bhaviṣyati. saṃvatsaraprayuktena divyarasāyanavaraṃ pravartate, devaiḥ saha samānagaganatale ca vicarati, śakraś ca saha pratispardhā bhaviṣyati. divyadevāpsaragaṇadivyavimānakrīḍārati, saptaratnabhavanavimānavāsī bhaviṣyati, sūryasadṛśaprabhāpramuñcanamānair vicarati, bhavanavarair anuvicarati. na sahamānaṃ tejasā jvalantaṃ suprabhāsvaraṃ viśuddhair bhaviṣyatīti na saṃśaya-m iti.

§1 athāntardhānavaihāyasaṃgamam agadottamam |
amoghaprabhāvasiddhiṃ pravakṣyāmi
vidyādharāṇāṃ paramasiddhāya tattvataḥ ||
§2 rasaṃ brahmakṣīrasphaṭikaśyāmakukṣīsugandhikaṃ kuṣṭham anavadyotpalavaraṃ padmākṣaṃ gugguluparṇapatrakuṅkumaṃ sanāgapuṣpaṃ saha gandhamāṃsīśirīṣapuṣpaṃ saha candanasitena bhāvyaṃ śubhaśūkṣmacūrṇam, amoghapadmoṣṇīṣaguhyaṃ japtā suyuktaṃ kularājamantreṇa japatā aṣṭasahasreṇa krodhamaṃtreṇābhiṣiñcayed ekaviṃśati. ekākṣaraṃ vai japatā ca lakṣam. tadā vai daśadhāraṇyā japatā pṛthagmantrā-d-ekaviṃśati sarvam āpūrṇaṃ mantrāṇi japitvā tu vai badarapramāṇam agadam amoghavipulaprabhāsaṃ nāma agadottamaṃ bhagavatāryāvalokiteśvarasya pādamūle sthāpya chāyāyāṃ pariśoṣayet. punar amoghapāśoṣṇīṣa japatā aṣṭaśataṃ śuciprasevikā sthāpya yadā kāryaṃ bhaviṣyati tadā prayojayaṃ sarvatra sarvakāryeṣu siddhiṣu.
§3 tato vidyādhareṇa śucir bhūtvā śucivastrāṇi pravaret susunātagandhānuvāsitaśarīreṇa bhavitavyaḥ. ayam agadottamaṃ kaṭukatailaṃ bhāvayet. svaśarīram unmārjayaṃ sahonmārjita antardhito bhaviṣyati. sarvaguhyabhavanāni apāvṛtadvārāṇi paśyati. sarvatra bhavanāni yathākāmataḥ praviśati. sarvakāryāṇi kariṣyati. sarvabhūtayakṣarākṣasabhūtapiśācādīni prabhurājāni bhaviṣyanti. sarve te vaśagatā-m-upatiṣṭhanti, kimkarakāraka yāvajjīvam upatiṣṭhanti, sarvakarmakāryāṇi anuvṛttā bhaviṣyanti. sarvanāgabhavanāni apāvṛtadvārāṇi tiṣṭhanti, sarvanāgaparivārāgrata-m-upatiṣṭhanti, sarvakāryāṇi kiṃkarakārakāṇi bhaviṣyanti. sarvakarmakaraṇīyāni kāryāṇi kariṣyanti. yāvajjīvam upatiṣṭhanti. rātradivasam upasthāsyanti. tato sarvanāgā yāvajjīvam ājñākarā sthāsyanti.
§4 siṃhajihvāyā gāvikṣīreṇa agadaṃ bhāvayaṃ sakalaśirasy añjya yathā manasi vartamānāni svamataracitāni nānārūpāṇi darśayati. sarvasthānagatāni yathā vartamānāni darśayati. sarvasthānagataṃ yathā pārṣadi madhye nānāvikurvitarūpa darśayati. yāvajjīvena vartamānāni darśayati. sarvakāryāṇi kariṣyati.
§5 gandharvatailam agadaṃ bhāvayaṃ jaṅghaṃ pādam abhyañjya ākāśe utpatati, ākāśataś ca ākramati, yatra icchati tatra gacchati, udakam upari ākramati. na ca kadācid bhūya bhūmau pādaṃ sthāpayati. dakṣiṇapādaṃ lepayet, yatra yatra hastaṃ spṛśati sa ca saṃkramayati. na ca kasyacit paśyati.
§6 akṣīṇy añjayet. yavastrīkṣīreṇa miśrayet. rājakulaṃ praviśed adṛśyaṃ bhaviṣyati. yam icchasi kāryāṇi kariṣyati. yam arthadravyāṇi gṛhṇāti. na ca kasyacit paśyati. yasya strīsaṃsargatā bhaviṣyati. na ca kasyacit paśyati. yāvajjīvaṃ vaśati na ca kaścij jānāti. mukham unmārjya yatra yatraiva naṭa†pāñcārakaravaka†madhye sarvakāryāṇi kariṣyati na ca śarīraṃ dṛśyati.
§7 kalaśam ālepayam udakaṃ pūrya udakadhārāṃ dṛśyate taṃ ca vardhanikaṃ na dṛśyate. udaka kuṇḍaṃ pūrya tatra madhyaṃ vicārayaṃ taṃ ca udaka plavamānaṃ dṛśyati taṃ ca kuṇḍaṃ na dṛśyati. ātmānaṃ lepayet kalaśodaka hastaṃ kalaśam udakavāhaṃ dṛśyati taṃ ca puruṣātmaṃ na dṛśyati.
§8 akṣīṇy añjayed mahājanamadhye praviśed adṛśyo bhaviṣyati. taṃ ca śabda śrūyate ātmabhāvaṃ ca na dṛśyate. sarvasthānagatā praviśe na ca ātmabhāvaṃ dṛśyati yāvan na snāti. yasya kasyacid akṣīṇy añjayet, śarīram anulepyet te sarve adṛśyo bhaviṣyanti. yatra yatraiva gacchati tatra tatraiva antardhito bhaviṣyati. snātamātreṇa pratyāyanaṃ bhaviṣyati.
§9 sakalaśarīraṃ lepayet †mahārājñaṃ† praviśed adṛśyo bhaviṣyati. yasya yasyaiva-m-ākāṃkṣamāṇasya suvarṇa rūpyarajato maṇimuktāpravāḍaśaṃkhaśilāvajravaiḍūryamarakatendranīlajātarūpavastrābharaṇālaṃkālavibhūṣaṇaṃ sarva gṛhya nirgacchanaṃ na kasyacid paśyati. yatrecchati tatraiva saṃkrāmayiṣyati. yāvan na snāpayate ātmaṃ tāvad adṛṣyaṃ sarvatra dṛṣyati.
§10 antaḥpuraṃ praviṣṭakāmena tena vidyādhareṇa śucinā snātena kartavyam. tato gandhodakena snāpitavyam. tato gomūtreṇa snāpitavyaḥ punaḥ kuṅkumodakenaṃ snāpayam. tato pañcagavyena snāpayitavyam. tato anena guḍikā śarīram anulepayaṃ gandhatailena saha miśritam. tato akṣīṇy añjyaṃ yuvatikṣīleṇa miśritam. tato guḍikātraya dakṣiṇabāhau bandhitavyam. tato śucivastraṃ prāvarena mantram usumārayaṃ rājakulaṃ praveṣtavyam adṛśyo bhavati. na ca kasyacid paśyati. śarīraṃ ca snāpitavyaṃ divasena vā dvidivasena vā saptāhena vā dvisaptāhena vā trisaptāhena vā tatrāntaḥpuramadhye krīḍati ramati pravicārayati. na ca kaścij jānāti. nirgacchati praviśati na cāvatāraṃ labhate. kaścid yāvan na snāpayate ātmabhāva antardhānaṃ bhaviṣyati.

§11 bilapraveśaṃ sakalaśarīraṃ gandhatailena lepayaṃ vivaram, praveṣṭavyaṃ sarva vivaradvārāṇi apāvṛtāni bhaviṣyanti, tatra bhavanamadhye praveṣṭavyaḥ. yathā yathā karmāṇi tathā tathā kuruṣva iti. udakamadhyaṃ praviśeta yathā gṛhaṃ na codakena lipyate, na ca udakavāsinā tatra kaścit paśyati. agnimadhye praviśen na cāgniśarīraṃ spṛśati, candanam iva śītalaṃ bhaviṣyati. nāgabhavanasare praviśe na ca bhavanavāsināṃ tasya paśyanti, anyāni vividhakāryāṇi kuruṣva iti. yathā svamanasi vartamānaṃ karma kartavyam.

§7 taṇḍulaṃ sarṣapaṃ gugguluṃ ghṛtamadhvāktaṃ juhūyād aṣṭasahasrāṇi āryāvalokiteśvaraṃ sarvavaradāyako bhaviṣyati. sarvakāryeṣu sādhanaṃ bhaviṣyati. candanapadmanāḍaṃ ca lājāsarṣapa agaru dadhimadhughṛtāktaiḥ śamīkāṣṭhasamidbhir agni prajvālya aṣṭasahasrāṇi juhuyāt. tato āryāvalokiteśvaraḥ svarūpeṇa agrata-m-upatiṣṭhati. sarvavaradāyako bhaviṣyati. sarvakāryasiddhiś ca bhaviṣyati. sarvamanorathaparipūraś ca bhaviṣyati. sarvakalpasiddhiś ca bhaviṣyati. sarvatrāmoghāpratihatasiddhiś ca bhaviṣyati. sarvapuṇyākarṣaṇasiddhiś ca bhaviṣyati. sarvamaṇḍalamudrāpaṭalakalpasādhanavidhiḥparamasiddhir bhaviṣyati. sarvatathāgatakulasamayaguhyānupraviṣṭo bhaviṣyati.
§8 khadiranimbakāṣṭhasamidbhir agni prajvālya śālitilasarṣapadadhighṛtāktalavaṇaṃ juhuyāt aṣṭasahasrāṇi. sarvavighnavināyakā yakṣarākṣasagrahā sarvaduṣṭapratyarthikapratyamitrā praśamanti. sarvakalahavigrahavivādeṣu uttārayati. sarvayuddheṣu jayo bhaviṣyati. sarvaparacakrapratyarthikapratyamitreṣu jayado bhaviṣyati.
§9 padmahomena candanalājā dadhimadhughṛtāktā aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt. sarvarāṣṭrakarvaṭagrāmanagarajanapadeṣu samantaparimaṇḍalayojanasahasraṃ mahāśāntir bhaviṣyati. sarvasattvānāṃ ca strīpuruṣadārakadārikāṇāṃ rakṣāvaraṇaguptaye saṃvidhāsyati. sarvatra sarvavyādhiḥ praśānto bhaviṣyati. sarvacaṇḍamṛgāśīviṣaduṣṭāni maitravatā bhaviṣyanti. sarvarājārāṣtrāntaḥpuraparivārā svastikṣemeṇa bhaviṣyanti.
§10 mataṅgasarṣapaghṛtāktāṃ nāgapuṣpalājām arkakāṣṭhasamidbhir agni prajvālya aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt anāvṛṣṭi patati. tato mahānāgā varṣadhārān utsṛjanti. yāvad arthakāmato varṣayanti. sarvasasyavanaspatitṛṇagulmauṣadhidravyāni puṣpaphalāni suparipūrṇāni bhavanti supariniṣpannāni bhaviṣyanti. sarvamahānāgarājānāṃ saparivārāṇāṃ mahāprīṇitāni bhaviṣyanti. kālena kālaṃ yathārtu varṣadhārām utsṛjanti.
§11 ativṛṣṭike tasyaiva bhasmanā aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt. ativṛṣṭi praśamayati. lājāgolasarṣapaṃ caṇḍabījaghṛtāktaṃ juhūyāt. sarvavātāśaninivāraṇaṃ samantayojanasahasraṃ mahāsīmābandhaḥ kṛto bhavati. vātabandhe sarṣapataṇḍulam aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt sarvavātamaṇḍalikā baddhā bhaviṣyanti. yāvad vairambhavātaṃ baddhā bhaviṣyanti. nāgabandhe tu sarṣapatilahomena aṣṭasahasraṃ dvādaśavarṣako nāgabandhaḥ kṛto bhaviṣyati.
§12 sarvavyādhipraśamanāya ca sarṣapaghṛtāktāṃ aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt sarvavyādhiṃ praśamayati. paracakranivāraṇe karavīrakāṣthair agni prajvālya nimbapatralavaṇatuṣam aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt sarvaparacakrapratyarthikapratyamitrāṇi vinaśyanti. yāvajjīvena na bhūyo bhaviṣyanti. diśābandhaḥ samantayojanasahasreṇa. tilasarṣapalājāpadmapatraghṛtāktām aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt. samantayojanasahasraṃ mahāsīmābandhaḥ kṛto bhaviṣyati.
§13 sarvavināyakabandhe sarṣapakṛṣṇatilamāṣatilaghṛtāktām aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt.  sarvavighnavināyakamārādiduṣṭayakṣarākṣasabandhaḥ kṛto bhaviṣyati. yakṣaniṣkāsane bhaṅgabījalavaṇatailāktām aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt sarvayakṣarākṣasabhūtapiśācāpasmārādīni sarvalokapratyakṣān niṣkramiṣyanti. sarva-ītyupadravopasargā sarvabhayā praśamiṣyanti. sarvasvastinā bhaviṣyanti.
§14 brāhmaṇavaśīkaraṇe lājārkasamidghṛtāktām aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt sarvabrāhmaṇā vaśyā bhavanti. kṣatriyavaśīkaraṇe guḍaghṛtalājārkapatram aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt sarvakṣatriyavaśīkaraṇaṃ bhaviṣyatīti. śūdravaśīkaraṇe madanaphalakarṇikārikām aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt. sarvaśūdrāṇāṃ vaśīkaraṇaṃ paramasiddhiḥ. vaiśyavasīkaraṇe trividhaghṛtāktām aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt sarvavaiśyavaśīkaraṇam. jātīsumanām aṣṭasahasraṃ ghṛtāktāṃ juhūyāt sarvastrīvaśīkaraṇaṃ bhavati. kumārīvaśīkaraṇe jalakusumaghṛtāktāṃ juhūyād aṣṭasahasraṃ sarvakumārīṇāṃ vaśīkaraṇaṃ bhaviṣyati.
§15 sarvabhayapraśamane vrīhighṛtam aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt sarvabhayāḥ kalikalahavigrahavivādeṣu praśamayanti. dhanadhānyapravartane saptadhānyaghṛtāktām aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt. dhanadhānyavṛddhir bhaviṣyati.
§16 aiśvaryalabhe sarṣapamadhvām aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt. mahān aiśvaryavṛddhir bhaviṣyati. viṣayalabhe trikaṭukaghṛtamadhvām aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt mahāviṣayalambho bhaviṣyati. grāmārthe karavīrakapuṣpasarṣapalājāghṛtāktāṃ juhūyād aṣṭasahasraṃ grāmalābhī bhaviṣyati. nidhi-r-utpāṭane padmānāḍām aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt ghṛtāktāṃ mahānidhi pratilabhate.
§17 yakṣam āvāhayitukāmena guggulucaṇḍabījakamadhughṛtāktām aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyād yakṣaḥ svarūpeṇa agrata upatiṣṭhati. kiṃkarakārakaḥ sarvakarmakaro bhaviṣyati.
§18 kāmarūpīm āvāhayitukāmena lājāsarṣapadadhimadhughṛtāktāṃ gandhapriyaṅgu aṣṭasahasraṃ juhuyāt. karavīrakāṣṭhasamidbhir agni prajvālya tato kāmarūpī āgacchati. yathākāmatā cārayati. tṛbhi prakārāṇi vaktavyam. mātā bhāryā bhaginī. yadi mātā putrasnehayā mahādhanadhānyaparibhogavivṛddhiṃ saṃpādayati. yāvajjīvaṃ avaikalpatāṃ saṃtiṣṭhati. yadi bhāryā-m-anekavividharatikrīḍayā paribhogaṃ paricarati.  vividhavastrābharaṇavibhūṣaṇagandhamālāvilepanapānabhojyavividharasarasāgraparibhogāṃ paricarati. vividhaśayyāsanadhanadhānyakośakośāgāravivṛddhiṃ paricarati. yāvajjīvena cchāyānubaddhā bhaviṣyati. jīvitam api parityajati. yadi bhaginī tadā vividha-aiśvaryadivyavimānaparibhogām paricarati. yāvajjīvam upasthānaparicaryāṃ karoti.
§19 paracakranirvāpaṇe tuṣalavaṇahomena yojanaśatasahasrāntare na śatru-m-utpadyate. aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt mahāyuddhasaṃgrāmajayo bhaviṣyati.
§20 ādityāvatāraṇe madhumaricataṇḍulaghṛtāktāṃ juhūyāt parvataśikhare aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt. dine dine āditya-m-avatarati. sarvavarāṇi-m-anuprayacchati. yāvajjīvaṃ †sarvakāmikā-m-†upasthānaparicaryām upatiṣṭhati.
§21 candrasanakṣatratārāgaṇam avatarayitukāmena trikaṭukasaindhavalavaṇakṣīramadhughṛtāktām uccasthāne juhūyāc candrasanakṣatratārāgaṇam avataranti. sarvakāmikamanorathabhogaiśvaryamahāvṛddhi prayacchati. yāvajjīvena upatiṣṭhanti. yāvajjīvena rakṣāvaraṇagupti saṃvidhāsyanti. dine dine darśanakāmatām avataranti.
§22 sarvākarṣaṇe devanāgayakṣagandharvāsuragaruḍakinnaramahoragamanuṣyāmanuṣyāḥ strīpuruṣadārakadārikāḥ śramaṇabrāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyaḥ vaiśyaśūdras tilasarṣapataṇḍulaviśvoṣadhaṃ dadhimadhughṛtāktām aṣṭasahasraṃ juhūyāt. mahatī ākarṣaṇasiddhir bhaviṣyati. sarvalokapriyaś ca bhaviṣyati. rājānaṃ sāntaḥpuraparivāraṃ vaśagatā bhaviṣyanti. tasyaiva nityānubaddhā bhaviṣyanti. vācānuvartā bhaviṣyanti. ātmanena dhanena vā. sarvatraikaguruko mahāpūjanīyo bhaviṣyatīti.
§23 homavidhisādhanam.
