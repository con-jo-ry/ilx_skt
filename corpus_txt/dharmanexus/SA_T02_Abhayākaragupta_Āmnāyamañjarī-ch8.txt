evaṃ bhagavataḥ sādhanavidhim upadiśya cakrādinā śāntikādyarthaṃ nānādevatābhāvanāyāṃ tārayati | śṛṇv ityādi | vajro vajragarbhaḥ | bhīmo bhīmasena itivat | sādhyām iti | śāntiṃ puṣṭiṃ vaśyaṃ rakṣā(265v/534)m abhicāraṃ ca | viśeṣato varṇādiviśeṣeṇa | śāntiṃ vartamānasyāpakārasya rakṣā tu bhaviṣyataḥ |
navakoṣṭhādicakrasyeti | aṣṭabhir arair varaṭena saha nava koṣṭhā yasya tac ca ādiś ca vakṣyamāṇacakrāpekṣayā cakraṃ ca | tasya bāhye nemim | karmavarṇādīti karmānurūpaṃ varṇāśayādikaṃ bhāvayet | asyeti mantrasya bījaṃ tādibījāni | sarveṣāṃ pūrvāgnyādikoṣṭhānām | (266r/535) praṇava oṁ | hutaṃ svāhā | tasyāntimā lipir hā | madhya nāmam iti |
varaṭe oṁhākārayor madhye sādhyasya nāma rakṣāvākyasahitam | samatājñānaṃ śūnyatājñānaṃ tad eva śūnyaṃ tena yuktam ātmānaṃ kuryāt | samatāsarvavit śūnyatājñānaṃ paramā rakṣā sthāne cakre ca bhāvitā | atas tāṃ yojayet | evaṃ sati rakṣā bhavati jñāninām | bhayam upadravabhūtaṃ | dūrato na bhavatīty arthaḥ |
tato 'bhāvena śūnyatayā sattvā(266v/536)nām ity upalakṣaṇaṃ bhājanalokasya | yogabhāvaṃ devatāniṣpattim | cakṣur iti cakṣuṣoḥ | nāsam iti nāsayoḥ | śiraḥprabhṛtīnām antaḥsuṣirasthāny akṣarāṇi | sādhayet sarvakarmāṇīti śāntikādīni | sarvadevānām iti kvacit pāṭhaḥ | sarvadevān apīty arthaḥ |
ayam arthaḥ | sarvadharmaśūnyatāṃ vicintya jhaṭitijātasitāravinde candre tāṁpariṇataṃ tāṁkārādhiṣṭhitavaraṭam utpalaṃ tatpariṇatām āryatārāṃ sattva(267r/537)paryaṅkiṇīṃ vicitraratnādinepathyām abhayasavyakarāṃ vāmenotpalaṃ bibhratīṃ vairocanamudritām ātmānaṃ vibhāvya yathoktamantraiḥ kavacayitvā hṛdabjacandrasthāṣṭāracakrasya nābhau oṁ devadattaṃ
rakṣa hā | pūrvāgnyādyareṣu dakṣiṇāvartena tāre tuttāre ture svā iti bhāvayet | varṇas tu bhagavatyāḥ padmasya cakrasyākṣarāṇāṃ raśmīnāṃ ca karmānusāraṃ jñātavyaḥ | (267v/538) tatra śāntau rakṣāyāṃ ca śuklaḥ | puṣṭau pīṭaḥ | vaśye raktaḥ | abhicāre kṛṣṇaḥ | svadehacakrākṣararaśmibhiḥ prabhūtair vyāpyamānaṃ sādhyaṃ cintayan mantraṃ japet |
oṁ tāre tuttāre ture svāheti | svāhāpūrvaṃ yathāyogaṃ vidarbhaṇam | pūrvasevākāle tu na vidarbhayet |
bhūrje tu padmenducakrākṣarāṇi likhitvā sabhūrjāny ūrdhvarandhreṇāntaḥ praveśya hṛtpadmādibhir ekīkṛtyādho bhagavatīpadmadvāreṇa niṣkāśya (268r/539) dhārayed abhimataṃ syāt | pratiṣṭhāpītthaṃ vidyāyā ity āmnāyaḥ | ayaṃ ca vidhir vakṣyamāṇayogayor api yathāyogaṃ boddhavyaḥ | vaśyakarmaṇi kurukullā viśiṣyate | sā bhagavatī tāraiva |
śūnyatābhāvanānantaraṃ jhagiti raktābjaśavasūrye raktahrīḥkārapariṇatādhiṣṭhitaraktotpalapariṇatāṃ kurukullāṃ raktām ekavaktrāṃ caladraktavartulatrinetrāṃ piṅgordhvamūrdhajām amitābha(268v/540)mudritāṃ śiraḥsthapañcakapālacakryādyābharaṇāṃ galāvalambitamuṇḍamālāṃ caturbhujāṃ dakṣiṇābhyāṃ raktotpalakalikāśaraṃ raktapuṣpakṛtāṅkuśaṃ ca vāmābhyāṃ
raktakusumadhanū raktotpalaṃ ca bibhratīm ardhaparyaṅkeṇa nṛtyantīṃ vicintya svahṛtsūryasthacakrākṣararaśmibhir ākṛṣṭaṃ sādhyaṃ vihvalībhūtaṃ vyāpayantīm utpalena gale baddhvā puruṣaṃ hṛdi striyaṃ yonā(269r/541)v aṅkuśena pīḍayantīṃ śarajālair vidhyantīm ātmānaṃ bhāvayan mantraṃ japed vaśyārtham |
cakraṃ tu vakṣyamāṇaṃ ṣaṭkoṇam aṣṭapattrapadmasūryastham | areṣu oṁ kurukulle svā madhye hrīḥ amukīṃ vaśīkuru hrīḥ hā akṣarāṇi yojayet | likhanadravyaṃ tu vakṣyate | bandhanaśamanādiṣu yathāyogaṃ vidhir unneyaḥ | ghoreti | nāgānām | jvarāpamṛtyuviṣaśamane śubhreyam | idaṃ rakṣeti | iyaṃ cāsau rakṣā ceti kṛtvā |

prajñācakram āha | aṣṭetyādi | dalam araḥ | pūrvaṃ tārācakram | pūrvoktena nyāsādividhinā | atha vā prathamaṃ śūnyatāṃ vicintya prakṣālya mala dehināṃ rūpadhātvetyādilakṣaṇasaṃyuktaṃ samatārūpam iti saṃbandhaḥ | pūrvoktena nyāyena | asya vakṣyamāṇamantrasya | bhāvabhāvena muhur muhuḥ sātiśayābhi(270r/543)prāyeṇa |
prajñātmyeti ātmā prajñāpāramiteti bhāvayet | jāpitaṃ jāpāt | bījaṃ mantraḥ | prajñāvardhanayogaḥ prajñāpāramitācakrayogaḥ |
ayam arthaḥ | śūnyatānantaraṃ sitābjendau hūṁjādhiṣṭhitābjaprajñāpāramitāpustakapariṇatāṃ prajñāpāramitāṃ śuklāṃ sattvaparyaṅkāṃ ratnādinepathyām akṣobhyamudritāṃ dvibhujāṃ prajñāpāramitā(270v/544)pustakāṅkakamaladvayaṃ vāmadakṣiṇapārśvayor antarutthitaṃ kaphoṇibhyāṃ dhṛtvā karābhyāṃ vyākhyānamudrayā sthitām ātmānaṃ bhāvayet |
cakre 'kṣaranyāsas tu tārāmantravad iti | mohādikleśanāśāya vairocanādiyogam āha | candretyādi | akṣaraṃ vuṁkāram | cakraghaṇṭe dakṣiṇayoḥ |
ayam arthaḥ | śūnyatānantaraṃ viśvābjaraktāpūrṇakapālacandre vuṁkārajā(271r/545)dhiṣṭhitacakracihnajātaṃ vairocanaṃ śuklaṃ jaṭāmukuṭinam ardhaparyaṅkatāṇḍavinaṃ hṛccandrasthabījajasaraśmisvābhadevatābhir jagadajñānatamo 'panīya buddhatve pratiṣṭhāpayantam ātmānaṃ dhyāyād iti | uttaratrāpi yathāyogam ayaṃ vidhiḥ | ata āha | etenetyādi |
asya bījaṃ āṁ cihnaṃ navāṃśaratnam | ratnādibhir mānalobhādya(271v/546)panayanam | dakṣiṇayo ratnāṅkuśe | amitābhasya bījaṃ hrīḥ cihnaṃ padmam | savyayoḥ sarojaśarau | rāgaśamanam | amoghasiddher bījaṃ khaṁ cihnaṃ khaḍgaḥ | savyayoḥ khaḍgāṅkuśe | īrṣyāśamanam | khadhātur vakṣyamāṇam | bhagaṃ gaganaṃ cātra dharmodayā |
