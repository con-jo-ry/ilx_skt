Title: *Manasvināgarājasūtra
Published: Strauch 2014
Source: Bajaur ms.
This is the earliest hard evidence for any rakṣā text. The texts from the Bajaur Collection can be dated to the first two centuries of our era. The nāga-king Manasvin approaches the Buddha and hands him over a special mantra to be used in all kinds of calamities. Only five excerpts published in this article. Concludes that it is very close to the Mahāmāyūrī (for which a Gandhari origin is posited based on a linguistic argument).
