(1) tato jñātvā sarvavipattidoṣarahite bhūkhaṇḍamadhye 'pi ca ropyās tatra yavāḥ prajāpataharite saṃcārayed gās tataḥ | tāsāṃ mūtrapurīṣamiśritapayaḥsaṃsiktaśāntyātmake pūte bhūmitale samastakaraṇaṃ nāgādikaṃ lakṣayet |
(2) tadanu |
vihāracaityāśramaharmyakūṭadevālayādīn pravidhātukāmaiḥ |
tair vāstunāgo 'tra parīkṣaṇīyo yad bhūmikhaṇḍaṃ caturasrakaṃ tat ||
pratyekato dikṣu bhuvāṃ vibhāgo māsatraye vāsarasaṃkhyayātra |
aiśānato vahnidiśo'ntarāle prāgbhūmibhāgaṃ navatiṃ vibhajya ||
āgneyanairṛtyadigantarāle nairṛtyavāyavyadiśaṃ tathaiva |
vāyavyabhūtādhipater digante kuryād vibhāgaṃ navatiṃ tathaiva ||
(3) bhādrāśvine kārtikamāsiṃ tāvad īśānakoṇādigatottamāṅgaḥ |
nairṛtyakoṇādigapucchabhāgo bhūkhaṇḍamadhyārpitaguhyadeśaḥ ||
nābher adho bhāgabhujaṅgamāṅgaḥ ūrdhvaṃ phaṇāyuktamanuṣyamūrtiḥ |
yāmyānano vāmakareṇa karṇe vāmetareṇāpi kaṭiṃ saratnam ||
saṃchādya śete navabhūmibhāgān diktālasāsthānagato hi nāgaḥ |
prākpaścimāntādigatāṃgayaṣṭir bhūkhaṇḍamaṇḍaṃ pramitaṃ yathaiva ||
rātriṃdivaikena vibhāgam ekam ādityagatyā pratipaddinādau |
prācyāṃ yathāyuktir vibhāgacāro prācīpratīcyuttaradikṣu tadvat ||
śuklādito mārgasahasyamāghe vāruṇyadiksaṃgatadṛṣṭivaktraḥ |
vaiśvānarāśāgamitottamāṅgo vāyavyakoṇādigatādharāṅgaḥ ||
phalgau madhau mādhavamāsi tasya yakṣādhipāśāgatadṛṣṭivaktraḥ |
nairṛtyakoṇādigatottamāṅga īśānakoṇādigapucchabhāgaḥ ||
jyeṣṭhe śucyau śrāvaṇamāsakāle prācīnadigyātasavaktradṛṣṭiḥ |
vāyavyakoṇādigatottamāṅgo vaiśvānarāśādigapucchabhāgaḥ ||
saṃvatsaraikena bhuvaṃ samantāt saṃvyāpayan naikasuvṛttamātram |
(4) jñātvā viśeṣeṇa tu māsi māsi saṃcārabhedaṃ bhujagādhipasya ||

(5) vighnāntakākārajanaṃ vibhāvya kuddālavajrāṅkakaraṃ sudhīram |
saṃtoṣya nirvāsakamārasainyaṃ tenādito vāmakaroddhareṇa ||
devendravitteśvaradiṅmukhena saṃsphālayan ghātacatuṣṭayasya |

tat pañcamāṃ karmakarair anekair ākhanyamānāṃ ca vidikṣu dikṣu ||
(6) prāntaikaviṃśativibhāgabhuvaṃ vihāya saṃsthānato navavibhāgapare nikhanyāt |
tat kroḍabhāgapurataḥ śubhakāribhūmir dharmārthakāmaguṇavṛddhisusiddhidātrī ||
mātāpitāduhitṛnandanasodarāṇāṃ bhāryāvadhūsvajanabandhusuhṛjjanānām |
mṛtyur bhaved yadi khanen navabhāgakoṣṭhe samasthite sa khalu bhūmiṃ bhujaṅgamasya ||
pṛṣṭhena cen nikhanataḥ svavināśatā vā svasthānataś cyutir analpadhanakṣayo vā |
pucche gajāśvamahiṣājagavapaśūnāṃ syān nāśatānucaradāsabalaprasiddheḥ ||
āghātapañcasu yadi prabhavec ca kāṣṭhaṃ valmīkakeśanakhabhasmani kṣudrakāni |
pāṣāṇato 'nilabhayaṃ tuṣadagdhakāṣṭhe nānājvaraprabhavatāsthiṣu śūlarogaḥ ||
jñātvā śubhāśubhanimittam anekarūpam ācāryaśilpiyajamānajanair vidheyam |
kṛtvā viśalyadharaṇītalam āśu sarvaṃ khātaṃ supūrya pariśuddhamṛdā samantāt ||
