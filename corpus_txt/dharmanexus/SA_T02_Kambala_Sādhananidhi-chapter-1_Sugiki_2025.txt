mlecchitair buddhamudrāyā mahābje guṇāḥ santīti viśeṣataḥ sthānaṃ rahasyam ity āha girir iti | ukte 'rthād vanagahanapradeśe vijane tasya sthitir iti | atra pradhānagrahaṇe pārṣadyānāṃ grahaṇam iti | rāgādikleśavanagahanā strīmudrā | saivopāyaviśuddhyā bodhipanthā bhavati tasyā viśuddhiprakarṣaparyantagamanāt | pradeśo dharmodayākhyaḥ |

ādisiddheti sadāsiddhiḥ pariniṣpattir udayāstamanarahitā nabhaḥsamā sadā | śrāvakādi buddhānāṃ rāgānalair dagdhaṃ śmaśānam | athavā samatvāt sarvasattvasaṃtānādhivāsanaṃ śmaśānam | tatrety evaṃviśiṣṭe | maṇḍaṃ sāraṃ taṃ lāti gṛhṇātīti maṇḍalam | ālikhen manasā bhāvayed ity arthaḥ | bāhyam eva sugamam |76|
