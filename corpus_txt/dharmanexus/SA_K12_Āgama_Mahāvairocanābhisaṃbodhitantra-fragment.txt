Title: Mahāvairocanābhisambodhi fragment
Source: \textsc{Wang}, Junqi. 2022. “A Preliminary Study of a Newly Discovered Sanskrit Manuscript of the \textit{Mahāvairocanābhisaṃbodhi}.” \textit{Journal of Indian and Buddhist Studies} 70/3: (64) 1102–(70) 1108.
Tibetan: D 494, P 126, N 447, C 129, H 462 (Uttaratantra section, ca. 711 CE apud Hodge), D 255b6–257b2 (ends on 260a7, so this might be the antepenultimate folio)
[23r]manvāharatv ācāryaḥ aham amukanāmā imāṃ velām upādāya yāvad ā bodhimaṇḍaniṣadanāt | dadātu me ācāryo mahāsamayāvatārasamvaraṃ mūrdhnā dhārayiṣye śāstur ājñām iti |
tata ācāryeṇāpi vaktavyam | samanvāhara kulaputra yāvad ā bodhimaṇḍaniṣadanāt | sarvabuddhabodhisattvebhyaḥ tvayā ātmā niryātayitavyaḥ | adhyāśayenānuttarāṃ samyaksaṃbodhim ākāṅkṣayeti |
etad guhyakādhipate mahāsamayāvatāro nāma samvaraḥ | asmin samvare vyavasthitā mantramukhabodhisattvacaryācāriṇo bodhisattvāḥ susaṃvṛtaśīlā bhavanti | anuttaratāthāgataśīlapratiṣṭhitā bhavanti | tasmā tarhi guhyakādhipate iha mantramukhabodhisattvacaryāmukhe śikṣitukāmena bodhisattvena iha mahāsamayāvatāre 'bhiyogaḥ karaṇīya iti ||
śṛṇu guhyakādhipate bodhicittaṃ yena cittena samanvāgatā bodhisattvā jātā bhavanti tathāgatakule pratiṣṭhitā bhavanty atītānāgatapratyutpannānāṃ buddhānāṃ bhagavatāṃ jñāne |
atha vajrapāṇir bhagavantam etad avocat | deśayatu bhagavān asmākam anukampām upādāya tad bodhicittam |
evam ukte bhagavān vajrapāṇiguhyakādhipatim etad avocat | tatra guhyakādhipate bodhiḥ katamā | bodhir ākāśam ākāśaṃ cārūpyānidarśanam agrāhyaṃ yallakṣaṇā ca bodhiḥ tallakṣaṇaṃ ca cittam iti hi guhyakādhipate cittaṃ cākāśaṃ ca bodhiṃ cādvayam etat | yā cātra samatānubodhā dharmāṇāṃ dharmadhātor adhigamaḥ sānuttarā samyaksaṃbodhiḥ | tasmin yac cittam utpadyate 'nutpādayogenānupalambhayogena idam ucyate guhyakādhipate bodhicittam |
api tu guhyakādhipate pañcabhir avasthānapadair bodhisattvā bodhim abhisaṃbudhyante | katamaiḥ pañcabhiḥ | iha guhyakādhipate bodhisattvāḥ sarvabhāvavigatam abhisaṃbudhyante | kecit punaḥ skandhadhātvāyatanagrāhyagrāhakavigataṃ śūnyaṃ māyopamam ity abhisaṃbudhyante | kecid dharmālambanatayā | api tu guhyakādhipate mantramukhabodhisattvacaryācāriṇā bodhisattvenaivam abhisambodhavyam | sarvabhāvavigataṃ skandhadhātvāyatanagrāhyagrāhavivarjitaṃ dharmanairātmyasamatayā svacittam ādyanutpannaṃ śūnyatāsvabhāvam iti ||
tatra guhyakādhipate sarvabhāvavigatam iti laukikamārgaparityāga eva tathāgatena saṃdarśitaḥ | na punar atrātmā sattvo jantur vā manujo vā saṃvidyate | api tu skandhadhātvāyatanādīni | skandhadhātvāyatanagrāhyagrāhavivarjitam iti | śrāvakamārgābhisamayaparijñāmukhena bodhisattvānāṃ mahāyānābhisamayo vyavasthāpitaḥ | dharmanairātmyasamatayā tu mantramukhabodhisattvacaryācāriṇo bodhisattvāḥ svacittam ādyanutpannaṃ paśyantaḥ śūnyatāsvabhāvam adravyam alakṣaṇam abhisaṃbudhyante | tasmā tarhi guhyakādhipate bodhisattvenālayānāvaraṇasarvaprapañcarahitaṃ cittam utpādayitavyam |

tad anenāpi paryāyeṇaiva veditavyam | yathā nāsti mahāyānābhisaṃprasthitānāṃ bodhisattvānāṃ mantracaryānayanirhāravimokṣamukhaṃ muktvā anuttarāyāḥ samyaksaṃbodher upakārī veditavyaḥ tat kasmād dhetoḥ yad anekān kalpān bodhisattvā ghaṭanto vyāyacchanto duṣkaraparityāgāni kurvanto na bodhim abhisaṃbudhyante | tad ihaiva janmani mantramukhabodhisattvacaryācāriṇo bodhisattvā anuttarāṃ samyaksaṃbodhim abhisaṃbudhyante | tat kasmād dhetor janma iti guhyakādhipate utpādasyaiṣā saṃjñā kṛtā tathāgatena | tan teṣāṃ bodhisattvānāṃ sā saṃjñā prathamataram eva niruddhā sarvadharmasvabhāven 'dhigamāyāḥ samādheḥ pratilambhād iti ||
atha bhagavān vairocanaḥ punar api vajrapāṇiṃ guhyakādhipatim āmantrayate sma | iha guhyakādhipate guhyasāramudrāmaṇḍalasamayam avatartukāmena bodhisattvena pañca dharmāḥ dūrataḥ parivarjayitavyāḥ | katame pañca | mātsaryaṃ sattveṣv āghātaḥ vicikitsā ālasyam agauravatā mantrādiṣu | ete dharmā guhyakādhipate ihaiva janmani lokottarāṃ pratipadam avatartukāmair bodhisattvair ete dūrataḥ parivarjayitavyāḥ | nāhaṃ guhyakādhipate tasya bodhisattvasya eṣu dharmeṣu sthitasya svalpām api bodhisattvacaryāparipūriṃ vadāmi | tat kasmād dhetor ma-
