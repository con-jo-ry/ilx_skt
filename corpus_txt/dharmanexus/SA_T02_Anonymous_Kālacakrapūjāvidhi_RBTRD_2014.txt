etacca sarvamavaśyamityupagantavyamanyathā bahudhoktaṣaṭtriṃśadicchetyādi- vākyānāṃ parasparavirodhaḥ syāt
āsāmicchānāṃ nivartanasvarūpāḥ pratīcchāḥ ṣaṭtriṃśat kāyamaṇḍalavediṣu pūrvavat svakulavaśāt
svasvadikṣu cakreśāditathāgatebhyo'nyāḥ sarvā devatā vicitraratnamukuṭinyaḥ
iha yadā bhagavān kālacakro vajrasattvasvabhāvo vyavasthāpyate tadā haritavarṇokṣobhyena mudritaḥ
evaṃ viśvamātā yadā tvakṣobhyasvabhāvā tadā nīlākṣobhyamūrtyā vajrasattvena
evaṃ viśvamātā dharmadhātvīśvarīsvabhāvā
evaṃ hi jñānavijñānayoḥ parasparamudraṇaṃ bhavati
