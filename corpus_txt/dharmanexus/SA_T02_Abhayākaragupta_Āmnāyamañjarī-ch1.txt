tattvam iha prajñopāyamayaṃ tadvatsalas tatpriyaḥ san na tu rāgitādibhiḥ | prayatno guptau yatnaḥ | bheda ity udbhedaḥ | svayam eva yuktipāṭhabalād abhyūhaḥ | mantrabhedaś ca bhedaḥ | madauddhatyakopādibhiḥ svayaṃ tayā sahāyena vā parasmai kathanaṃ (53r/109) mantrabhedaḥ | aguptau narakapātādir adṛṣṭadoṣaḥ prasiddha eva | dṛṣṭadoṣam apy āha |
agupta ityādinā | agupte 'gopane | sarpavyāghrādayaḥ | bhūcarā narapiśācādayaḥ śāsakāḥ |
tattvajijñāsoḥ kim etābhir ity āha | mudretyādi | atra brahmāṇīkṣatriyāvaiśyāśūdryo vairocanakulinyaḥ | amṛtavajreti kṣatriyaivāmṛtam iva (53v/110) kṣataduḥkhebhyas trāṇāt | karmakulajeti vāṇijyādikarmapradhānaṃ kulaṃ vaiśyakulaṃ tajjā | gopālikā śudrī | mahāvairocano 'tra vairocana eva |
tathatāyām ityādi tathatā tathātvaṃ dharmaviśeṣasyānirdeśāt sarvadharmāṇām asau | ataḥ sarvadharmāṇāṃ yathābhūtaṃ tattvaṃ tathatocyate | tat punaḥ śūnyatāmātraṃ pāramitānaye pratipādyate | iha tūpadekṣyamāṇo(54r/111)pāyaviśeṣopetā śūnyatā tathateti | tasyāṃ gatas tathāgataḥ takāralopāt | śrīmān ubhayasaṃbhāravān sann iti pūrveṇa saṃbandhaḥ |
āgataś ca tathaiveti | yathā gatas tathaivāgataḥ | prajñopāyamayasyaiva jagadarthaṃ pañcakulādibhāvena spharaṇāt | anayā tathatayā prakṛṣṭajñānābhinnatayā prajñayā yuktyā tādātmyayogena |
iha hi viśvaṃ cittaṃ pradhānaṃ cittasantatilakṣaṇāś ca sattvanaiḥsvābhāvyā(54v/112)tmatathāgatagarbhās tathāgatānāṃ ca dharmasarvasvam ādarśādipañcajñānāni tāni ca dharmatāśarīreṇa prakṛtipariśuddhāny eva kevalam āgantukāvaraṇaviśuddhau svasvabhāveva tiṣṭhamānāni pañcajñānākhyāṃ buddhabodhisattvākhyāṃ ca labhante pūrvaṃ tu bījākhyām eva |
ataś ca bodhisattvabījatvād gotrāṇi kulāni cocyante |
nanv evaṃ sarve tathāgatā ādarśādi(55r/113)pañcajñāninaḥ pratyekaṃ teṣām ādarśādīni pañcakulāni tat kathaṃ kulabhedaḥ | gotrabhedāt | ādarśādīnām ekaikasya hi cchāyāgotram anādisiddhaṃ bodhisattvānām | anekasya tu cchāyāgotraṃ ṣaṣṭham |
anekacchāyasyāpi ṣaṣṭhasyaikaikacchāyābalīyastvena tattatkulānukārāt tatra tatra saṃgraha iti pañcatvam ity āha | kulaṃ pañcavidhaṃ proktam iti | atha vaivam avatāryate |
nanu pañcakulasaṃgṛhītā(55v/114)nām anyāsām avyāpanāt prādeśikaṃ spharaṇaṃ syāt | na | tāsāṃ sattvakulena saṃgrahāt |
nanv evaṃ sattvakulasyottamatā syāt vajrasattvasya sarvādhipatitvāt | naivam | anyeṣām api tatprasūtatvāt | prakarṣaviśeṣāt tu kulāntaratvaṃ teṣām upadiśyate | yatra tu tādṛśaḥ prakarṣo nāsti tatra kuśalāntaravyapadeśanibandhanābhāvāt sattvakulenaiva vyapadeśaḥ |
sattvakulasya tu tattatkulānu(56r/115)kāriṇas tatra tatra saṃgrahāt pañcatvam ity āha | kulaṃ pañcavidham iti | tac ca prapañcāt pañca skandhāś catvāro dhātavaḥ pañcendriyāṇi pañca viṣayāś ca pratyekaṃ pañcatathāgatātmakatvena śāntikapauṣṭikavaśyakrūraviśvarūpabhedena vā | ādarśādipañcajñānaiḥ saheti śatavidham |
tad api bhidyamānam anantam aparyavasānam asaṃkhyam ity arthaḥ | trividham iti | traividhyam api tattatkulānukāriṇas tatra tatra saṃgrahāt | tathā ca samatā(56v/116)jñānasyādarśajñāne saṃgrahaḥ kṛtyānuṣṭhānajñānasya pratyavekṣaṇājñāna iti sopapattikaṃ vakṣyāmaḥ | atha vā mānerṣyayoḥ prajñāsvabhāvayo rāgakule 'ntarbhāvāt |
mānasya vā kāyakule mohāśrayatvāt | dveṣakule vā piśunerṣyayor dveṣaprabhavatvāt | sac ca sarvam apy ekaṃ vajrasattvakulaṃ jñātavyam | yad vakṣyati | kulaṃ pañcavidhaṃ proktam ekam eva hi śūnyavajriṇam iti | samāje ca trivajrābhedyasvarūpaṃ vya(57r/117)vasthāpitam |
% Q/R Guhyasamājatantra
utpādayanta bhavantaś cittaṃ kāyākāreṇetyādinā |
kāni punas tāny ādibhūtāni kulāni yataḥ kulāny aparāṇi bhaviṣyanti | kathaṃ ca tāni kulam ity ucyante ity āha | kulānām ityādi | pañca bhūtāni skandhā vā kulāni | tadviśuddhirūpatvād eva buddhānāṃ tadrūpatvena tv ādau vajradharasya spharaṇād āditvam | teṣāṃ ca tadanantaram aparimitena rūpeṇa |
yat tv eṣāṃ kulam ity abhidhīyate tattadrūpeṇa tatprasūtānāṃ kulanād ga(57v/118)ṇāta ity arthaḥ | tatra pañca bhūtāni pṛthivyaptejovāyuvijñānāni | pañca skandhā rūpavedanāsaṃjñāsaṃskāravijñānāni | prasūtikulāni ceti | tebhyaḥ prasūtāny aparāṇi kulānīty arthaḥ |
nanv evaṃ śūnyatātattvaṃ mudrāyogāj jñāyamānārthopāyopetam upasūcitaṃ na tv iha kiṃcid bhāvyam upadiṣṭam | utpannarūpāṇām eva pañcakulānāṃ yoginīnāṃ nirdeśāt (58r/119) yogināṃ caturdvāreṇaiva tathā sūcanāt | satyaṃ tattvato bhāvyabhāvakabhāvanānām anutpatter ādiśāntatvāt sarvadharmāṇāṃ tad darśayati | nāsti bhāvaka ityādi |
tat kiṃ mantradevatayor uccheda evotpannakrama ity āha | tiṣṭhetāv iti niṣprapañcaḥ śūnyataikarasatvāt svabhāvaḥ sahajamahāsukharūpaḥ tadrūpeṇa mantradevau tiṣṭhataḥ | tad eva nirdiśati | vairocanetyādi | āroliko 'mitābhaḥ |
(58v/120) sāttviko mūrtimān vajrasatvaḥ śivo 'mitābhaḥ sarvo 'moghaḥ vibuddho ratnasaṃbhavaḥ vairocanādirūpeṇa niṣprapañcasvabhāvaṃ tattvam evocyata ity arthaḥ | brahmādipadānāṃ svasamaye 'prasiddhatvād artham āha | brahmetyādi | nirvṛtir nirvāṇam | tato buddha eva vastuto brahmā na punar anyas tasya svarūpato 'nivṛtatvāt | evam uttareṣv api |
(59r/121) viśanād iti vyāpanāt | sukalyāṇād ity atyantakalyāṇatvāt | sarveṣām ātmā nijaṃ rūpam | tatra santatyā sthito yatas tataḥ sarva ity arthaḥ | tattvam eva tad adyāpi na jñāyate yadātmakam etat sarvam ity āha | satsukhatveneti | satsukhaṃ niṣprapañcaṃ mahāsukham | vibuddho bodhanād rater iti tasyaiva mahāsukhasya bodhād vibuddho buddha eva |
nanu devatāviśeṣāḥ pratipāditā devatātvam eva tu sāmānyaṃ na pratipāditam ity āha | deha iti | (59v/122) dehe saṃbhavatīti devatāvarṇāgamalopābhyāṃ evaṃbhūtaḥ sa eva bhagavān ucyata ity āha | bhaga ityādi | evaṃ pariśuddhe manasi mahāsukharasānuṣiktā aiśvaryādayo guṇā nijabījadā jāyanta iti te 'sya santi | mudrāṇāṃ nītārtham āha | jananītyādi |
jagaj janam iti | jagadvartinam āryalokam | vibhāgam iti | heyopādeyavibhāgam | rajakītyādinā uddiśya (60r/123) rañjanād ityādinā nirdiśati | rañjanād ity anurāgaṇāt |
prajñayā hy āśayānuśayaṃ vijñāya yathāyogam anuṣṭhānāt sarvasattvā anurāgitā bhavantīti prajñaiva tathocyate guṇasya balavaiśāradyādirūpasyākāśadhenor duhanād utpādanāt prajñaiva tathocyate | cañcalatvād iti bhavanirvāṇāpratiṣṭhitatvāt | asparśeti śūnyataikarasatvāt |
(60v/124) bāhyānām api mudrāṇām eteṣām eva prajñāguṇānām anukaraṇāt prajñātvam anugantavyam | ata eva bāhyamudrāṇām evaṃbhūtaprajñāsvarūpāṇām evādhimokṣeṇāpi bhagavattathāgatakulānāṃ śūnyataikarasamahāsukhatattvajijñāsoḥ seveti sevitavyā ity uktaṃ |
utpannarūpāṃ devatāṃ pratipādya mantraṃ pratipādayann āha | japam ityādi | ālikālyoḥ prajñopāyarūpayoḥ paramamantratvāt* (61 deest?) (62r/125) tad dhyeyam ityādi |
nanv evam aparimitacittaprasarānirodhe vikṣepa eva na dhyānam iti dhyeyam api tan na syād ity āha | pitarītyādi | tatsukhaṃ bhuñjyate svayam iti yathopadeśam abhyāsād ajasram | yauvanābhiṣekayor vā evam ādimadhyāvasāneṣu | yat sukhaṃ tad anuparamam anubhūyamānaṃ dhyānam ucyate |
evam aparāparāpareṣv api cintiteṣv anuparatapravāhiṇā mahāsukhasyānusmaraṇāt | (62v/126) kuto vikṣepaḥ yato na dhyānadhyeye syātām ity abhiprāyaḥ | atha vā pitarīti gurāv abhiṣekakāle yat prāptam anubhūtaṃ sahajamahāsukhaṃ tad bhujyate ābhujyate svayam anyadāpi | mudrāyogato vā tathaivopabhujyate |
yenopabhuktena sukhena maraṇam indriyāṇāṃ nirodhaḥ syāt | iheti dṛṣṭa eva janmani tat sukhaṃ dhyānam ucyate |  evaṃbhūtaṃ cotpannayogino dhyānam anābhogatas tathānusmaraṇamātreṇāna(63r/127)ntarokte 'py upadeśe 'nujñāyata iti ||
itīti samāptam | ādiśabdena kavacādiḥ | abhidhānābhidheyabodhicittotpādakavacanādibhāvanānāṃ tattvam aviparītatvaṃ prakriyate prastūyate 'neneti prakaraṇaṃ paricchedaḥ paṭalam ity anarthāntaraṃ prathamādau nirdiṣṭatvād iti ||
mahāpaṇḍitābhayākaraguptakṛtāyām āmnāyamañjaryāṃ saṃpuṭaṭīkāyāṃ prathamā mañjarī ||
