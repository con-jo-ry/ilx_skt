brahmasūtrāṅgamuktā tu bandhanīyā ca sarvadā |
prakṣepyaṃ kuṣuraṃ vidyād hastābharaṇam eva ca || 17.30 ||
ekayaṣṭi bhavet sūtraṃ mekhalā tv aṣṭayaṣṭikā |
rasanā ṣoḍaśa jñeyā pañcaviṃśat tu mekhalā || 17.31 ||
iti yoginīsaṃcāre tattvaviśuddhipīṭhasthānanāṭyacaryānirdeśaḥ saptadaśamaḥ ||
