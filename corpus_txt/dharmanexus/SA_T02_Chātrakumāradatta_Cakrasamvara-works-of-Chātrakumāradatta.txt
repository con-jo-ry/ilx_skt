CUL Or. 155
This ms. contains various Cakrasamvara texts of one Chātrakumāradatta, described as a paramopāsaka, [and another work?] following the tradition of Luyīpāda (the name is spelt thus quite consistently). He identifies his guru as `he residing in Kanyārāma'. Kanyārāma is depicted on fol. 127r of CUL Add.
1643 as the seat of a Lokanātha in Rāḍhā, now in West Bengal. The toponym is given as a synonym of Lokeśvara in Puruṣottama's Trikāṇḍaśeṣa, v. 1.1.15cd (cca. 1100-1150 \textsc{ce}). Possibly another guru mentioned is Sauṣṭhavapāda, styled mahāmaṇḍalācārya.
The person requesting at least one of the works is identified (if my emendation is correct) as Nandadāsa.
