athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi maṇḍalaṃ maṇḍalottamam |
herukābhyudayaṃ nāma maṇḍalaṃ sārasaṃgraham || 1
samayaṃ pālayed yogī sādhakaḥ susamāhitaḥ | [6]
bhedena samayānāṃ tu na dīkṣe maṇḍale siddhir avāpyate || 2
madhu raktaṃ sakarpūraṃ raktacandanayojitam |
gaṇamadhye pratiṣṭhantu sarvocchiṣṭarasāyanam || 3
sarvavajrāṅkacihnāgra kara-m-aṅguli dhāryatām |
anāmāṅguṣṭhavaktrābhyāṃ lehayed yogavit sadā || 4
somapānavad āsvādya siddhim āpnoti śāśvatīm |
pañcāmṛtaṃ bhaved etat sarvasiddhipravartakam || 5
catuḥpūjā tathā virā
dvayendriyasamāpattisaṃsamāpannaṃ ca tattvakaḥ |
pūrvasevā ca kartavyā lakṣajāpavidhānataḥ || 6
cakrimantraṃ japel lakṣaṃ lakṣaṃ vā svādhidaivatam |
anyeṣāṃ m-ayutaṃ dadyāc cakriṇāṃ vājñayālikhet || 7
divyamanuṣyatāṃ saukhyaṃ maṇḍalaṃ lekhayāmy aham |
pūrvoktasthānam uddeśaṃ tatra maṇḍalam ālikhet || 8
bhūmisaṃśodhanaṃ kṛtvā kodyāvasukodyatām |
samāṃ śalyavivarjitām |
pānagomayena maṇḍalabhūmiṣu lepayet || 9
śmaśānabhasmanā yuktaṃ pañcāmṛtasamanvitam |
upalipya tato bhūmi tatra maṇḍalam ārabhet |
śmaśānaṃ tu samācaret || 10
cityaṅgārakacūrṇena śmaśāneṣṭakasaṃyutam |
ālikhet maṇḍalaṃ divyaṃ ācārya susamāhitaḥ || 11
sarvalakṣaṇasaṃpūrṇaḥ sarvālakṣaṇavarjitaḥ |
samyagjñāneṣu tattvajñaḥ śrīherukamantrajñaḥ || 12
akrodhanaḥ śucir dakṣo yogajña yogapāragaḥ |
kapārakṛtamūrddhajaḥ
bhasmānuliptadehāṅga mātrai vibhūṣitaḥ || 13
grātastu [see above for ]
asthimālāsaṃsthitaś ca ekakhaṇḍakamūrddhajaḥ |
asthimālāvilambī ca khaṭvāṅgakarasaṃsthitaḥ || [238] 14
ātmānaṃ śrīherukaṃ kṛtvā herukaṃ tu tataḥ smaret |
cakrasya hṛdaye nyaset || 15
evaṃ sannahya m-ātmānaṃ prākāraṃ tu diśā nyaset |
evaṃ sannahya m-ātmānaṃ madhye hy astraṃ niveśayet || 16
cakrasya svayam ātmānaṃ ūrdhvaṃ tu śarajālakam |
ātmānaṃ khecarapañjaraṃ kṛtvā [ ||]
evaṃ sannahya sakavaco abhedyas tridaśair api || 17
evaṃ surakṣyam ātmānaṃ mudrāmantrair alaṃkṛtam |
ālikhet maṇḍalaṃ ghoraṃ mahāsiddhipradāyakam || 18
tato mṛtakasūtreṇa mahārudhirarañjitam | [4]
sūtrayet maṇḍalaṃ ghoraṃ herukasya puraṃ varam || 19
aṣṭahastaṃ caturhastaṃ vā caturdvāraṃ caturasraṃ samantataḥ |
vicare dviguṇato mantrī japeḍ ḍākinījālasaṃvaram || 20
tasya madhye pratiṣṭhāpya sapatraṃ karṇikojjvalam |
puṣkarāṃ keśarānvitaṃ viśvavarṇadalāṣṭakam || 21
karṇikāyāṃ nyased vīraṃ mahābhairavarūpiṇam |
ojaskaṃ tu sudīptaṃ tu aṭṭahāsaṃ mahāravam || 22
kapālamālāmukuṭaṃ trinetraṃ caturmukhaṃ [ |]
hasticarmāmbaradharaṃ [ |] vajrasaṃbhinnasabhruvaṃ [ |]
bhavet khaṭvāṅgakṛtahastaṃ ca mālārdhaśatabhūṣitam || 23
tasyāgrata saṃsthitā mudrā vajravārāhī sughorā |
mahābhairavābhimukhīṃ kṛtvā trinetrāṃ raudrarūpiṇīm || 24
kapālāntrasaṃpūrṇā sravantī rudhiraṃ mukhāt |
tarjayantī diśāṃ sarvān sadevāsuramānuṣān || 25
ḍākinyas tu caturviṃśā vārāhyākulasaṃbhavā |
cakrabhāva tu pūjayed diśāsu vidiśāsu ca || 26
vīrāṃś ca tathaiva hi cakrasaṃsthā tu pūjayet |
vīram uttamāṃ yadicchet siddhi sādhakaḥ || 27
kalaśāṃ ca tataḥ kuryāt mūlakālādivarjitān |
mauktikair hemaratnaiś ca pravālarajatais tāmraiḥ || 28
sarvabhakṣais tu saṃpūrṇāṃ kapālopari saṃsthitāṃ |
sūtreṇa veṣṭayet kaṇṭhaṃ pallavāgrasamanvitāṃ || 29
aṣṭau dvāreṣu vinyasya vastrayugmaiḥ suveṣṭitāṃ [||]
navamaṃ madhyakalaśaṃ vastrayugmena veṣṭitāṃ | 30
rajataṃ vā hiraṇyaṃ ca ratnamaulikaśobhitam |
vikiren maṇḍalaṃ sarvaṃ ratnasauvarṇaśobhanaiḥ |
pūjayet tatpuraṃ ramyaṃ prakiraṃ siddhir eva na saṃśayaḥ || 31
gandhodakena saṃśṛtya ātmānaṃ sarvatomukham |
dīpānāṃ tu śataṃ dadyād yadicchet siddhim uttamām || 32
ākāśe ḍākinīkās sarvā manasā ūrdhvato nyaset |
bhūloke yāś ca ḍākinyo maṇḍale sarvato nyaset || 33
pātāle ḍākinyo ye kecit pātāle tāś ca vinyaset |
diśāsu mātarās sarvā vidiśāsu ca yojayet ||
puṣpai gandhais tathā dhūpaiś ca vividhottamaiḥ |
pūjayed vīratān sarvā herukamaṇḍilosthitāṃ ||
dhvajair uktais tathā [2] pītaiḥ svetaiḥ kṛṣṇapiṅgalaiḥ |
śragdāmair vividhai ramyair vastrair nānāvidhais tathā ||
vitānaiḥ kāṇḍapaṭakair divyastrai suśobhanaiḥ
bhakṣyabhojyai vividhaiḥ || [3] khādyapānai samanvitaiḥ ||
ācārya toṣayet pūrvaṃ sarvabhāvena sādhakaḥ |
yathāśaktyā pūjayed guruṃ siddhikāmaṃ susamāhitaḥ ||
ghaṇṭāninādam ālambya [4] puṣpai dhūpair alaṃkṛtām |
ghaṇṭā vādayet susvarā paṭāhikāṃ cāpi sādhakaḥ ||
hāhākāraṃ ca kārayet ||
evaṃ ca vidhivat pūjyāṃ mmaṇḍalaṃ sarvakāmikāṃ | [5]
saṃsthādya paṭavastreṇa mukhaṃ teṣāṃ tu putrakāṃ ||
puṣpajālāñjali kṛtvā kamalāvartta tu vāmakam |
vāmāvarttapraṇāvaṃ tu hūṃphaṭkāraś ca samuccaret || [6]
ālīḍhapadasaṃsthānāṃ sādhayet siddhiṃ pūrvikām |
puṣpāñjali tato kṣipya cintayet kṣatrabhat patan ||
pradakṣiṇaṃ ñca tataṃ kṛtvā sādhakaḥ susamā[7]hitaḥ |
praveśayet tatpura paścā dakṣiṇāmūrtam āśritya punaḥ puṣpāñjali tato maṇḍalasyopari kṣipet ||
yasmin patati tat puṣpaṃ kula[241]ṃ tasya vinirdiśet ||
tato hi pūjayen mudrā ācāryas susamāhitaḥ
śiṣyāṇāṃ ca dvitīye ahani raktena tṛjapte tilakaṃ tasya kārayet || [2]
mukham utpādya śiṣyasya darśayet maṇḍalaṃ ntataḥ |
yo yasya devatāsthānaṃ tatraitāṃ darśayet ||
samyak praṇipatya tataḥ kṛtvā ācāryasahitaṃ puraṃ | [3]
pradakṣiṇaṃ ca punaḥ kṛtvā pratyārambhena vāmataḥ
maṇḍalaṃ guruve praṇipatya yathāvidhiḥ
tatas tu guruve dadyāt tathāgatoktadakṣiṇām ||
niryātya [4] suvarṇasatasahasraṃ ratnāni vividhāni ca |
vastrayugmaśataṃ caiva gajavāji rāṣṭram eva vā ||
karaṇābharaṇakarakaṃ kaṇṭhikākarāṅgulīkaiś ca utta[5]maiḥ |
yajñopavītasauvarṇa svabhāryā duhitāny api ||
...[149]tmāna sarvabhāvena praṇipatya nivedayet |
adyaprabhṛti dāso haṃ samarpitaṃ mayā tavaḥ (!) ||
evaṃ vidhit (!) tataḥ kṛtvā sādhakena suniścitaṃ |
tatas tasya dattastasyantri (!) ḍākinyo yogamātarāḥ |
yoginyo lāmayaś caiva khaṇḍarohā viśeṣataḥ |
etair vicare jagat sarvaṃ ḍākinyaiḥ saha sādhakaḥ |
sarva te kiṅkirās (!) tasya sādhakasya na saṃśayaḥ |
yogasiddhir bhavet tasya trailokyam aṇivāritaṃ (!) |
antarddhānaṃ bilokṛ tiṣṭhet | (!) khecaratvaṃ pādalepārthaṃ rasāyanaṃ |
jāyante tu sadā nityaṃ ṛcchayā (!?) sādhakasya tuḥ (!)
rūpāñ ca nekāni (!) kurute |
ākāśena tu gacchati ḍākinyā ca viśeṣataḥ |
nisumbhayati sarvajantūnāṃ sakṛddiṣṭe yogeṇa yogitvaṃ jāyate kṣaṇāt |
adṛṣṭamaṇḍalo mantrī yogitvaṃ yaḥ samīhati |
harate (!) muṣṭinākāśaṃ pibate mṛgatṛṣṇikāṃ |
eṣa yogavaraḥ śreṣṭha sarvayogeṣu cottamām |
yaḥ kākṣiṣyate nityaṃ (!) sa devāsuramānuṣāṃ |

tattvasaṅgare samvare vāpi guhye vā vajrabhairave |

uktānuktaṃ tu yat kiṃcit tat sarve śrīheruke sthitaṃ ||   ||
