evaṃpramukhaiḥ sarvākāśadhātumātumātṛgaṇaparipūrṇaṃ sakalakulapatisuviśuddhadharmadhātusvabhāvādvayajñānodvayaśrīheruka(*5r)prajñāpāramitātmakasamayamahāvajrakulasamayam adāt|
abhāvāṃ sarvadharmāṃ vai dharmalakṣaṇavarjitāḥ|
dharmanairātmyasaṃbhūtā idaṃ samayanayottamam|| iti ||
atha bhagavān vajrakavacādvayaṃ nāma vajrasamaya[8=4r]m udājahāra||

vajrasattvena ca mahāyogeśvareṇa|
padmanartteśvareṇa ca mahāyogeśvareṇa|
vajraherukena ca mahāyogeśvareṇa|
vajrasūryeṇa ca mahāyogeśvareṇa|
paramāśvena ca mahāyogeśvareṇa||
atha te ṣaṭkulaṣaḍaṅganyāsasamayottamarakṣādvayatathatāprajñopāyādvayabhūtaṃ mahāsamayottamaguhyarahasya smaret ||

athāyaṃ nānāmantranāmamahāsamayavikṛmbhitavajran nāma samādhi samāpadyedaṃ aśeṣānavaśeṣasarvadharmadhātunāpūrṇam iva dṛṣṭvā| vividhaguṇaparṣadā svaromakūpāgraṃvivare nānāvinayam upāgaṇam āpūryam adṛśyate sma||
bhagavān sakalapatin nāma vidharesadharaṃ sattvahitānudarśī mahākaruṇākrodharasādvayayogānandavicitraś ca mahāprātihārya darśanaparajaṃ samastatraidhātukādhipatitvaṃ darśayati sma||
vāruṇyāgnivāyavyamāhendrasyopari sucinutagirivararājasumeruvicitraratnanānātejopaśobhitam| tanmūrdhni śrīvajrasattvadharmadhātuspharaṇamahākrodhabhyudayaṃ nāma mahāprāsādaṃ maṇḍalarājaṃ nirmāya mahāsamantabhadrodbhavamahāvajra(*6 missing)parṣadaḥ sannipatitāḥ sanniṣaṇḍā 'bhuvan||
nānāvividhavicitraśobhitavajrālaṃkārabhūṣitacandrasūryopakhacitropaśobhitaiḥ [5] ghaṇṭācāmaradhvajakiṅkinījālākṣakapāṭhoparacikaiḥ|| vajramayasūtradharmacakrapravartanādiyogasamvaraiḥ |
nānādevanāgayakṣarākṣa[6]sabhūtapretapiśāconmādāspasmāragrahopagrahaduṣṭopaduṣṭair vetāḍasacacitiś ca śṛgālasivīḍuṇḍubhivīraḍākinīphetkāra[7]lalataśabdaiḥ ghaṇṭāḍamaruśabdakabandhanṛtyopaśobhitaiḥ|| vajrapāṇiparicārakakuśalāvatārasattvasattvahitakaruṇārasavibhra[10=5r]tavibhramaiḥ||
kavalāvarttavitte narttiteṣāṃ kṛtāñjalim ālīḍhāsanaiḥ kṛtakarapuṭo bhūtvā deśayatu bhagavān buddhakā[pā]liko dharmam paramagambhīra[2]nayam mahāvajrakrodharājānaṃ vilasanamahāmūrtidharagambhīrasvaḥ svaparṣanmaṇḍala abhidhānottaraṃ nāma ḍākinīsamvararājājālaiḥ||
