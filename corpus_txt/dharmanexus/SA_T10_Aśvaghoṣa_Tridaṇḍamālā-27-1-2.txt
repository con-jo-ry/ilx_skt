toyaughe patitasya cetasi bhaved duḥkhapravṛttir na vā
kleśaughe patitasya duḥkham atulaṃ kāyasya cittasya ca || 27.1.4
vegodbhrāntajalā nadī jalanidhiṃ kiṃcit kadācin nayet
saṃsārārṇavam arṇavād api paraṃ tṛṣṇānadī karṣati |
paryāptiṃ samupaiti parvatanadī meghāgame cāmbubhir
vistīrṇair api tṛptim eti viṣayair na tv eva tṛṣṇānadī || 27.1.5

nāprāptir asti viṣayasya ca kāṅkṣitasya
tan me sukhaṃ matam ato 'nyad avaimi duḥkham || 27.1.7
gatvāpi pāram udadher bahusaṃśayasya

saṃsārasāgarajalasya tu pāram etya

yathā vaidyas samyak prakṛtim avagamyāturagatāṃ
kṣayārthaṃ vyādhīnāṃ kathayati hitān auṣadhavidhīn |
vicitrān sattvānāṃ prakṛtim avagamyāśayagatāṃ
tathā buddho dharmaṃ sadṛśam avadad bheṣajam iveti || 27.1.13
§1 evaṃ mayā śrutam ekasmin samaye bhagavāñ chrāvastyāṃ viharati jetavane 'nāthapiṇḍadasyārāme | tatra bhagavān bhikṣūn āmantrayate sma |
§2 tadyathā bhikṣavaḥ kṣāranadyāḥ kukūlakūlāyāḥ kaṇṭakopāstaraṇāyā andhāyās tamaso 'ndhakāratamisrāyā mahājanakāyo 'nusrota uhyate |
§3 tatra syād ekaḥ sattvo 'bālajātīyo 'mūḍhajātīyaḥ saprajñājātīyaḥ sukhakāmo duḥkhapratikūlo jīvitukāmo na martukāmo maraṇapratikūlaḥ | tasyaivaṃ syāt | kiṃ punar aham anayā kṣāranadyā kukūlakūlayā kaṇḍakopāstaraṇāyā andhayā tamasā andhakāratamisrāyā anusrota uhye | yan nv ahaṃ hastābhyāṃ pādābhyāṃ pratisroto vyāyaccheya |
sa idaṃ pratisaṃkhyāya bhūyasyā mātrayā hastābhyāṃ pādābhyāṃ pratisroto vyāyacchate |
§4 sa tatra paśyed ālokam | tasyaivaṃ syāt | mahāndhatāyaṃ viśeṣo 'dhigato yaduta ālokaḥ | yan nv ahaṃ bhūyasyā mātrayā hastābhyāṃ pādābhyāṃ pratisroto vyāyaccheya | sa idaṃ pratisaṃkhyāya bhūyasyā mātrayā hastābhyāṃ pādābhyāṃ pratisroto vyāyacchate |
§5 sa tatra paśyet samaṃ pṛthivīpradeśam | sa tatra pratiṣṭhet | sa tatra pratiṣṭhāya caturdiśaṃ vyavalokayet | sa tatra paśyen mahāśailaṃ parvatam akhaṇḍam acchidram asuṣiraṃ saṃvṛttam ekaghanam | sa tam abhirohet |
§6 sa tatra paśyed aṣṭāṅgopetaṃ pānīyaṃ śītalaṃ ca svādu ca laghu ca mṛdu ca svacchaṃ ca niḥpūtigandhikaṃ pibataś ca kaṇṭhaṃ na kṣiṇoti pītaṃ ca kukṣiṃ na vyābādhate | sa tatra snātvā ca pītvā ca vigatajvaro vigataparidāhas tam eva mahāśailaṃ parvatam abhirohet |
§7 sa tatra paśyet sapta puṣpajātāni tadyathā utpalaṃ padmaṃ kumudaṃ puṇḍarīkaṃ saugandhikaṃ mṛdugandhikam atimuktam eva saptamam | sa tāny eva sapta puṣpajātāny upajighraṃs tam eva mahāśailaṃ parvatam abhirohet |
§8 sa tatra paśyec catuṣkaḍevaraṃ sopānam | sa tad abhirohet | sa tatra paśyet pañcasthūṇaṃ vimānam | sa tatra praviśet | sa tatra paśyet paryaṅkaṃ paṭṭikopetaṃ goṇikāstṛtaṃ tūlikāstṛtaṃ bṛhatikāstṛtaṃ citrikāstṛtaṃ paṭalikāstṛtaṃ kācalindaprāvārapratyāstaraṇaṃ sottarocchadapaṭam ubhayatopahitalohitopadhānaṃ nānāpuṣpāvakīrṇaṃ ramaṇīyam |
sa tatrābhiniṣīded vābhinipadyeta vā | tasya tatrābhiniṣaṇṇasya vābhinipannasya vā caturdiśaṃ śītalā vāyavo vāyeyuḥ |
§9 sa tatra śabdam udīrayet | ghoṣam anuśrāvayet | anenāryā anena bhadramukhā asyāḥ kṣāranadyāḥ kukūlakūlāyāḥ kaṇṭakopāstaraṇāyās tamaso 'ndhakāratamisrāyāḥ pratisroto niḥsaraṇam | apare evam āhuḥ | katamena āryāḥ katamena bhadramukhā asyāḥ kṣāranadyāḥ kukūlakūlayāḥ kaṇṭakopāstaraṇāyā andhāyās tamaso 'ndhakāratamisrāyāḥ pratisroto niḥśaraṇam |
anye evam āhuḥ | eṣo 'pi mārṣā na jānāty eṣo 'pi na paśyaty eṣo 'py anayā kṣāranadyā kukūlakūlayā kaṇṭakopāstaraṇayā andhayā tamasā andhakāratamisrāyā 'nusrota uhyate |
§10 iti hi bhikṣava upameyaṃ kṛtā yāvad evārthasya vijñaptaye | ayaṃ ca punar atrārtho draṣṭavyaḥ | kṣāra iti bhikṣavas trayāṇāṃ pāpakānām akuśalānāṃ vitarkāṇāṃ etad adhivacanam | tadyathā kāmavitarkasya vyāpādavitarkasya vihiṃsāvitarkasya | nadīti bhikṣavas tisṛṇāṃ tṛṣṇānām etad adhivacanam | tadyathā kāmatṛṣṇāyā rūpatṛṣṇāyā ārūpyatṛṣṇāyāḥ |
kukūlakūleti ṣaṇṇām adhyātmikānām āyatanānām etad adhivacanam | andhakāratamisrety avidyāndhakārasyaitad adhivacanam | mahājanakāya iti bālapṛthagjanānām etad adhivacanam |
§11 ekaḥ sattvo 'bālajātīyo 'mūḍhajātīyaḥ saprajñajātīya iti bodhisattvasyaitad adhivacanam | srota iti saṃsārasrotasa etad adhivacanam | hastābhyāṃ pādābhyāṃ pratisroto vyāyaccheyeti vīryārambhasyaitad adhivacanam |
§12 āloka iti dharmanidhyānakṣānter etad adhivacanam |
§13 samaḥ pṛthivīpradeśa iti śīlānām etad adhivacanam | caturdiśaṃ vyavalokanam iti caturṇām āryasatyānām etad adhivacanam | mahāśailaḥ parvata iti samyagdṛṣṭer adhivacanam |
§14 aṣṭāṅgopetaṃ pānīyam ity āryāṣṭāṅgasya mārgasyaitad adhivacanam | sapta puṣpajātānīti saptānāṃ bodhyaṅgānām etad adhivacanam | catuḥkaḍevaraṃ sopānam iti caturṇām ṛddhipādānām etad adhivacanam | pañcasthūṇaṃ vimānam iti pañcānām indriyāṇām etad adhivacanam |
§15 paryaṅkam iti sopadhiśeṣanirvāṇadhātor etad adhivacanam | puṣpāvakīrṇaṃ ramaṇīyam iti dhyānavimokṣasamādhisamāpattīnām etad adhivacanam | sa tatrābhiniṣaṇṇo vābhinipaṇṇo veti tathāgatasyārhataḥ samyaksaṃbuddhasyaitad adhivacanam | caturdiśaṃ śītalā vāyavo vāntīti caturṇām ādhicaitasikānāṃ dṛṣṭadharmasukhavihārāṇām etad adhivacanam |
§16 śabdam udīrayati ghoṣam anuśrāvayatīti dharmadeśanāyā etad adhivacanam |
§17 tatra ye te evam āhuḥ | katamenāryāḥ katamena bhadramukhā asyāḥ kṣāranadyāḥ kukūlakūlāyāḥ kaṇṭakopāstaraṇāyā andhāyās tamaso 'ndhakāratamisrāyāḥ pratisroto niḥsaraṇam iti śāriputramaudgalyāyanayor bhikṣvor etad adhivacanam | pañcakānām upapañcakānāṃ ṣaṣṭer bhadravargīyāṇāṃ pūgānām etad adhivacanam iti |
ye vā punar anye 'pi samyagdṛṣṭayaḥ samyakpratipannāḥ |
§18 tatra ye te evam āhuḥ | eṣo 'pi mārṣā na jānāty eṣo 'pi na paśyaty eṣo 'py anayā kṣāranadyā kukūlakūlayā kaṇṭakopāstaraṇayā andhayā tamasā andhakāratamisrayānusroto uhyata iti ṣaṇṇāṃ śāstṝṇām etad adhivacanam |
tadyathā pūraṇasya kāśyapasya maskariṇo gośālīputrasya saṃjayino vairaṭṭīputrasya ajitasya keśakambalasya kakudasya kātyāyanīputrasya nirgranthasya jātiputrasyeti | ye vā punar anye ʼpi mithyādṛṣṭayo mithyāpratipannāḥ |
§19 iti hi bhikṣavo yat tac chāstā śrāvakāṇāṃ karaṇīyam anukampakena kāruṇikenārthakāmena hitaiṣiṇā karuṇāyamānena kṛtaṃ vas tan mayā | yuṣmābhir idānīṃ karaṇīyam |
etāni bhikṣavo 'raṇyāni vṛkṣamūlāni śūnyāgārāṇi parvatakandarāṇi giriguhāpalālapuñjābhyavakāśaśmaśānavanaprasthāni prāntāni śayanāsanāni dhyāyata bhikṣavo mā pramādyata mā paścād vipratisāriṇo bhūta | idam asmākam anuśāsanam || idam avocat ||
