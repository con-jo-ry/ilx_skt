Title: Vanaratnastotra
Author: Āditya
Source: Hahn 1996
Tibetan: D1177
Input: Péter-Dániel Szántó, some time ago
# || rgya gar skad du | shr'i gu ru ba na ratna sto tra sa bta kaM | bod skad du | dpal ldan bla ma nags kyi rin chen gyi bstod pa bdun pa || dpal ldan bla ma nags kyi rin {255a4} chen gyi zhabs la phyag 'tshal lo ||
nepāle deśarāje sugatamatasadācāraniṣṭhāpratiṣṭho
vidvadgoṣṭhīgariṣṭho guṇagaṇagahano rājate bhikṣurājaḥ |
vikhyātātulyakīrtir dhvajavitatapaṭodāragambhīrabuddhiḥ
sattvatrāṇaikatānaḥ samadhigatamahāyānasiddhāntakoṣaḥ || 1
bal po'i yul gyi rgyal po bde bar gshegs pa yi ||
lugs la rtag tu rjes su brtson pa la bkod cing ||
mkhas pa rnams kyi mdzod gyur mthar thug yon tan gyi ||
tshogs kyi zab mo snang mdzad dge slong rgyal po ste ||
rnam par grags shing ma chungs pa med pa'i {255a5} grags pa yi ||
rgyal mtshan bla bre dar dpyangs zab cing rgya che'i blo ||
sku yang sems can rnams kyi skyabs ni gcig pu ste ||
theg chen yang dag rtogs pas grub pa'i mtha' yi mdzod ||
yativaravanaratnanāmadheyaḥ
sthavira ihādvayadharmatattvavettā |
vinayaniyamasaṃyamābhiyuktaḥ
paramatavādikarīndramṛgādhirājaḥ || 2
nags kyi rin chen zhes bya sdom brtson mchog gi blo ||
gnas brtan 'di na gnyis med chos kyi de nyid rig |
'dul ba'i sdom {255a6} pas yang dag sdom la mngon par brtson ||
phas rgol glang po'i dbang la ri dags rgyal po lags ||
praṇamāmi mahāyatirājavaraṃ
varadharmanayapravinītajanam |
janaduḥkhasauddharaṇaikaparaṃ
paramaṃ vanaratnamahāsthaviram || 3
phyag 'tshal sdom brtson chen po'i rgyal po rnams kyi mchog |
mchog gi tshul la rab tu rnam par nges pa skye ||
skye ba sdug bsngal dag las 'byin byed gcig pu mchog |
mchog tu nags kyi rin chen gnas {255a7} brtan chen po lags ||
viratākhilasattvavibodhakaraṃ
karapaṅkajalakṣaṇacāpadharam |
dharaṇītalabhūṣaṇakāyaruciṃ
ruciraṃ vanaratnamahāsthaviram || 4
chags bral sems can ma lus pa dag sad par mdzad ||
phyag gi chu skyes dag gis mtshon gyi gzhu 'chang ba ||
'dzin pa'i mthil gyi rgyan du gyur pa mdzes pa'i sku ||
gnas brten chen po nags kyi rin chen rab tu mdzes ||
viratāviratāvaraṇādimalaṃ
malatāmalatāpagamānubhavam |
bhavatābhavatādvayalakṣaśaraṃ
śaraṇaṃ vanaratnamahāsthaviram || 5
dga' bral dga' ba'i glang po la sog dri ma yi ||
dri ma dri ma {255b1} med cing bral ba nyams su myong ||
srid dang srid pa ma yin gnyis kyi 'ben la mnga' ||
gnas brtan chen po nags kyi rin chen skyabs su gyur ||
viramādisukhāmṛtapānarataṃ
ratanītivinītavikalpaśatam |
śataśaḥ paripācitasattvagaṇaṃ
gaṇinaṃ vanaratnamahāsthaviram || 6
dga' bral dang po'i bdud rtsi'i btud pa bdag la dgyes ||
dgyes shing nges la dul ba'i yan lag brgya phrag gis ||
sems can brgya phrag tshogs rnams yongs {255b2} su smin pa dag |
rtsi mdzad gnas brtan chen po nags kyi rin chen no ||
virasīkṛtamāragaṇādimukhaṃ
mukhapaṅkajanirjitapūrṇavidhum |
vidhurāparavādigirīndrapaviṃ
pavinaṃ vanaratnamahāsthaviram || 7
bdud sde la sogs rnams kyi kha la ro med mdzad ||
kha yi chu skyes dag ni dgra yis gang ba 'dzems ||
skye mched 'dar ba'i phas rgol ri yi dbang po 'khru ||
khrus mdzad nags kyi rin chen gnas brtan chen po'o ||
samāptam idaṃ vanaratnastotrasaptakam |
dpal ldan bla ma nags {255b3} kyi rin chen gyi bstod pa bdun pa 'di ni rdzogs so || ||
kṛtir magadhadeśīyādityānām iti ||
yul ma ga dh'a nas byung ba'i dge bsnyen dam pa nyi ma pa zhes bya bas mdzad pa'o ||
'di ni spyan mnga' chos kyi rgyal pos gser gyi me tog dang bcas pa'i bka' lung gis || rtse thang lo ts'a ba gzhon nu dpal gyis bsgyur ba'o || ||
