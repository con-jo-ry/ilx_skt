evaṃ dharmasmṛtyupasthānena mārgasatyam avataratīti |
avatāro 'tra pratyekasatye satyalakṣaṇāvabodhaḥ |

vīryaṃ caturdhā pravartata iti |

vistaravacanena śeṣagranthābhisambandhaḥ |

teṣāṃ prayogāsammoṣāvisārapravicayānām ādhipatyād vīryasmṛtisamādhiprajñākhyāś caittā indriyākhyā bhavantīti |
atra tv etad vaktavyam: ropite mokṣabhāgīye kuśalamūle ropitāvasthātaś chandādyādhipatyasya śraddhādīnāṃ ca na kaścid ātmātiśayaḥ |
tathā hi tadavasthāyāṃ tadādhipatyāt teṣām indriyatvam avasthāpyata iti |
