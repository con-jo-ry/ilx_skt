XXX   Citrādirūpalakṣaṇanirdeśapaṭala
%Claudio Cicuzza, Roma
%Il capitolo XXX del Saṃvarodayatantra
%R.Gnoli pp.213~228
p.216
athānyatamaṃ vakṣye śṛṇute guhyakādhipaḥ /
citritaṃ devarūpasya ākrāntarūpalakṣaṇam //30-1//
brahmarekhādirūpasya pañcabhāgaṃ tu m āsanam /
p.217
tribhiḥ khaṇḍitam aṅgasya vadano samamaṃśakaiḥ //30-2//
mukha<ṃ> dvādaśabhāgaṃ tu caturaṅguligrīva ca /
śiravālādi tasyaiva karṇadvau suvicakṣuṣaṇam //30-3//
vadano tribhi bhāgasya lalāṭamukhanāsikaiḥ /
bhrumantāmukha ūrdhvasya dvibhir aṅgulicakṣuṣaiḥ //30-4//
caturaṅgulicchinnasya tasyāpi suprasāritaḥ /
p.218
grīvāmadhyeti madhyasya krāntadvesurabhāgataḥ //30-5//
hṛdisthāne 'pi nābhasya krāntād eketi sarvataḥ /
stanam antarakrāntasya stanābhyam urakrāntavat //30-6//
hastakrāntadvitīyaṃ tu pāṇau dvau 'syeti krāntakaiḥ /
caturaṅgulayuktasya ūrujaṃghāsamanvitam //30-7//
p.219
caturaṅgulam <aṃśaḥ syāt> pādakrāntas tathaiva ca /
dvādaśatālakrāntasya devatārūpacitritam //30-8//
caturaṅgulaśeṣaṃ tu m āsanām aṣṭadeśakaiḥ /
āsanāsanabhir baddhaiḥ dvibhir aṅgulam aṅgulam //30-9//
candramaṇḍalagarbhaṃ tu antrav antrādikārayet /
gambhīro vajradevasya dvādaśatālalakṣaṇam //30-10//
nimnabhāgādibhāgasya upaśobhāvicitritam /
p.220
so 'pi m ākrāntatālasya bāhudvau saṃyameṇa tu //30-11//
vajrayed dvibhim aṅgulyāṃ saumyadevaṃ tu citritam /
so 'pi tālamukhaṃ kṛtvā udarāt prīṇayet sadā //30-12//
vajrayed dvibhm aṅgulyāṃ sandhisandhiṃ tu sarvasaḥ /
krodhakrodheśvarī devī ceṭiceṭiṃ tu kiṃkārī //30-13//
p.221
ghorabībhatsarūpaṃ tu daśatālābhicitritam /
manomayasvarūpaṃ tu navatālābhicitritam //30-14//
kramakrameṇa yoktavyaṃ maṇḍalacakradevatā<ḥ> /
madhye vajrarājasya etā lakṣaṇavicitritam //30-15//
trīṇi dīrghamahādoṣaṃ cibukagrīvasya jaṃghayoḥ /
uccāṭāsthānabhraṣṭaṃ tu mantrīṇāṃ niyatātmanaḥ //30-16//
trīṇi hrasvamahādoṣa<ṃ> nāsākarṇādim aṅguliḥ /
p.222
niyataṃ siddhihāniṃ tu śriyāhāniṃ tu tathaiva ca //30-17//
trīṇi sthalamahādoṣa<ṃ> jaṃghāpādādigaṇḍayoḥ /
māravighnaśriyā dūraṃ sādhakā niyatātmanaḥ //30-18//
trīṇi nimnamahādoṣaṃ kapāla-ūrupañjaram /
bhavate-arthahāniṃ tu śīghraṃ nānyatra śamsayaḥ //30-19//
trīṇi vaṃkamahādoṣaṃstanakarṇalalāṭayoḥ /
p.223
kalahaśatrupīḍaṃ tu acireṇaiva sādhakāḥ //30-20//
trīṇi hīnamahādoṣa<m> ūrukāya-uradvayam /
bhayaṃ cāpi caurāṇāṃ mṛtyūnāṃ tu na saṃśayaḥ //30-21//
kekaraṃ bhinnanāsasya karṇa-oṣṭalalāṭayoḥ /
nānāvighnaṃ ca maraṇaṃ ca mantrīṇāṃ niyatātmanāṃ //30-22//
ūrdhvadṛṣṭiśvāsasya calam āsanasabhinnakaiḥ /
bhavate arthahāniṃ tu svāsanaṃ calate sadā //30-23//
p.224
uragrīvādi aṅgasya asaṃkhyadoṣakīrtitam /
cakṣubhinnādimātrasya viparyācihnamūdravān //30-24//
śokasaṃtāpaduḥkhasya viśvadoṣādilakṣaṇam /
tribhiḥ saṃkaṭadoṣasya apāśrayachatram āsanam //30-25//
mṛtyur bhavati śriyābhraṣṭa-apriyāpriyavācakaiḥ /
edad doṣamahādoṣaṃ nirīkṣyā suvicāriṇam //30-26//
p.225
vinītasarvaśilpeṣu citritaṃ susamāhitam /
sarvalakṣaṇasaṃpūrṇaṃ surūpāvidhidarśanam //30-27//
saumyasaumyaṃ tu rūpasya raudraraudrabhayānakam /
vīravīrāṅgahaṃkāraṃ śṛṅgārahasitānanam //30-28//
evaṃ lakṣaṇalakṣaṇasya mantrīṇāṃ paṭacitritam /
ādikarmikamantrīṇāṃ pratibimbādikārayet //
siddhim āpūryate śīghraṃ paṭaṃ vistārya sādhayet //30-29//
iti citrādirūpalakṣaṇanirdeśapaṭalas triṃśatitamaḥ //
