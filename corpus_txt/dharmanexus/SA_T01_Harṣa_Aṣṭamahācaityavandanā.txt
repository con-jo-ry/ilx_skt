Title: Aṣṭamahācaityavandanā
Author: (king) Harṣa
Source: Lévi 1894 (Sanskrit restored from the Chinese transcription by Fatian), also cf. Bauddhastotrasaṃgraha no. 22.
Tibetan: D 1168
Input: Péter-Dániel Szántó (silent corrections)
[1] jātiṃ bodhiṃ pravaram atulaṃ dharmacakraṃ ca ramyaṃ
caityaṃ cādyaṃ tribhuvanamahitaṃ śrīmahāprātihāryam |
sthānaṃ cedaṃ himagirinilayaṃ devadevāvatāraṃ
vande 'haṃ praṇamitaśirasā nirvṛtā yatra buddhāḥ ||
% anne vande bhaktyā
[2] vaiśālyāṃ dharmacakre śiṣumagiritaṭe bhīṣmakāyoditīre
śrāvastyāṃ bodhimūle kuśinagaravare lumbinīkāpilālye |
% anne °kāpilākhye
kauśāmbyāṃ smerakoṣṭhe mathuravarapure nandagopasya rāṣṭre
ye cānye śāstu caityā daśabalabalinas tān namasyāmi mūrdhnā ||
% anne śāstṛcaityā vel dhātucaityā
[3] kaśmīre cīnadeśe khaṣataṭayamune marvare siṃhale vā
lāṭoḍre sindhupauṇḍre samataṭamagadhe mekhale kosale vā |
% anne khasataṭa°
% anne barbare
nepāle kāmarūpe kalaśavarapure kāñcisaurāṣṭrarāṣṭre
ye cānye dhātugarbhā daśabalabalinas tān namasyāmi mūrdhnā ||
% anne dhātucaityā
[4] kailāse hemakūṭe himagirinilaye mandare meruśṛṅge
pātāle vaijayante dhanapatinilaye siddhagandharvaloke |
brahmāṇḍe viṣṇubhūmau paśupatibhavane candrasūryādiloke
ye cānye dhātugarbhā daśabalabalinas tān namasyāmi mūrdhnā ||
% anne dhātucaityā
[5] ye cāṣṭau dhātugarbhā daśabalabalinaḥ kumbhasaṃjñāś ca caityā
aṅgārākhyās tathānye himarajatanibhāḥ stūparatnaprakāśāḥ |
pātāle ye ca bhūmyāṃ giriśikharagatāḥ sarvato dhātugarbhā
buddhānāṃ yāni bimbāḥ pratidinam asakṛt tāni namasyāmi mūrdhnā ||
% yāni ... tāni? anne ye ca/tu/hi ... tān
aṣṭamahāsthānacaityavandanā samāptā.
