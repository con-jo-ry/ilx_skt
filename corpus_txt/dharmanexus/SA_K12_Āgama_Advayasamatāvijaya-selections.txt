anyathā yadi mūḍhātmā pacyate 'vīcisaṃnidhau |
\
ebhir guṇair yadā kaścid jāyate puṇyasambhavaḥ |
tantrāni tasya ācāryo naivam ādiṃ prakāśayet |
evaṃ saṃprāpya śuddhātmā dharmatattvaṃ vidhisthitaḥ |
prakāśayet tadācāryo śīghraṃ sidhyati nānyathā |
\
tathāgataguhyavajrasattvārādhanapaṭalo 'ṣṭādaśamaḥ samāptaḥ ||
\
japen mantram abhinnāṅgaṃ dhruvaṃ māsena sidhyati |
\
sevādikaṃ tu sampādya mantrasādhanam ācaret |
samvaraṃ tatra saṃgṛhya dhruvaṃ sidhyati nānyathā |
vidhiṃ samprāpya mantrāṃ sidhyati mantrinā sevitā |
tasmān mantrī viśeṣeṇa samvaraṃ pratipādayet |
yad eva samvaraṃ proktaṃ tad eva rakṣayed vratī |
anyathāvīcinarake yāvad ākāśasambhavaḥ |
ācāryasamvaram rakṣe vidhivad yāvajjīvayā |
sidhyate tasya buddhatvaṃ viśvadharālayam |
\
sarvamantrārādhanapaṭala ekaviṃśatimaḥ samāptaḥ ||
\
viṇmūtro nāvamantavyo sarvasattvasukhapradaḥ |
sa eva vajradharo rājā sarvāgro bhuvaneśvaraḥ |
navavraṇasambhūtā nāvamanyā kadācana |
sa eva vajradharo rājā sarvāgro bhuvaneśvaraḥ |
rāgo hi nāvamantavyo sarvasaukhyahitapradaḥ |
sa eva vajradharo rājā sarvāgro bhuvaneśvaraḥ |
dveṣo hi nāvamantavyo vajrajñānaprabodhataḥ |
sa eva vajradharo rājā sarvāgro bhuvaneśvaraḥ |
\
rājakanyā yadā mantrī buddhakanyāpi vā yadi |
gṛhītvā upabhuñjīta yāvat tṛptamano bhavet |
\
stambhāveśa prathamaṃ parikīrtitam |
dvitīyaṃ bhūmim ity uktaṃ tṛtīyaṃ divyam uttamam |
caturtham āryadeśe ca kīrtitaṃ vajrapāninā |
\
nagno muktaśikho bhūtvā vikaṭotkaṭabhūṣaṇam |
śmaśānāṅgārasaṃyogaṃ nāman tasya likhet svayam |
sahasram ekaṃ japitvā tu dhruvaṃ buddho 'pi gṛhyate |
sārdravastravidhānajñaḥ pravṛtya krodhacetasaḥ |
liṅga pādau samākramya dhruvaṃ buddho 'pi naśyati |
viṣṇudaityādideveṣu pādau kṛtvā samārabhet |
saptāhnena vidhānajñaḥ traidhātukam api nāśayet |
\
mahāguhyapaṭala ekonaviṃśatimaḥ samāptaḥ ||
\
viṇmūtro nāvamantavyo sarvasattvasukhapradaḥ |
saiva vajradharo rājā sarvāgro bhuvaneśvaraḥ |
navavraṇasamudbhūtā nāvamanyā kadācana |
sarvavajradharo rājā sarvāgro bhuvaneśvaraḥ |
rāgo hi nāvamantavyo sarvasaukhyahitapradaḥ |
sa hi vajradharo rājā sarvāgro bhuvaneśvaraḥ |
dveṣo hi nāvamantavyo vajrajñānaprabodhakaḥ |
sa eva vajradharo rājā sarvāgro bhuvaneśvaraḥ |
moho hi nāvamantavyo sarvajñānaprabodhakaḥ |
sa eva śāntadharo rājā buddho vairocanālayaḥ |
duḥkhasukho hi nāvamantavyo ācāryo yāvajjīvayā |
sa eva śāntadharo rājā buddho vairocanālayaḥ |
dharmādharmo nāvamantavyo ācāryo yāvajjīvayā |
sa eva vajradharo rājā sarvāgras tattvadeśakaḥ |
sattvadhātur nāvamantavyo yāvajjīvaṃ kadācana |
sa eva vajradharo rājā sarvāgro bhuvaneśvaraḥ |
jugupsā sarvakāryeṣu na kāryaṃ yāvajjīvayā |
sa eva padmadharo rājā sarvāgro bhuvaneśvaraḥ |
atha sarvatathāgatāḥ sarvatathāgatajñānadharmatāṃ cākṣaraṃ śrutvā āścaryaprāptāḥ sarvavajradharān evam āhuḥ | āścaryam idaṃ kulaputra yatra hi nāma sarvajñajñānādhipatayo 'pi na jānīyaḥ | kuto 'nye iti |
\
homaṃ kurvīta vidhānajño yadicchet siddhim uttamām |
guhyatattvasamādhistho vividhaṃ homaṃ samārabhet |
\
śāntike maṇḍalaṃ vṛttaṃ pauṣṭike catuḥkoṇakam |
abhicāre trikoṇāgraṃ vaśye tu dhanuṣākṛtiḥ |
agnimadhyagataṃ saumyaṃ bhāvayed amṛtopamam |
tatraiva devatābimbaṃ svarūpeṇaiva bhāvayet |
ātmānam adhipatirūpeṇa idaṃ vajranayodbhavam |
\
advayasamatāvijaye mahākalparāje sarvatathāgatamantracintāmaṇipaṭalo viṃśatimaḥ samāptaḥ ||
\
advayasamatāvijaye mahākalparāje sarvamantrasamuccayo nāma samādhipaṭalaḥ ekaviṃśatimaḥ samāptaḥ ||
\
bodhisvabhāvāḥ samprāptāḥ bodhisattvā viśāradāḥ |
ākāśadhātusampūrṇā bhogā vajrabodhipratiṣṭhitāḥ |
alakṣaṇeṣu dharmeṣu prapañco na hi vidyate |
vajrasvabhāvajñānena ākāśam iva nirmalam iti ||
\
sarvatathāgatakāyavākcittapariśuddhisvabhāvavajraḥ nāma paṭalo dvāviṃśatimaḥ samāptaḥ ||
āryādvayasamatāvijayan nāma mahākalparājā samāptaḥ ||
