KĀMANDAKI
NĪTISĀRA
prathamaḥ sargaḥ
indriyajayaprakaraṇam
yasya prabhāvād bhuvanaṃ śāśvate pathi tiṣṭhati |
devaḥ sa jayati śrīmān daṇḍadhāro mahīpatiḥ ||1||
vaṃśe viśālavaṃśyānām ṛṣīṇām iva bhūyasām |
apratigrāhakāṇāṃ yo babhūva bhuvi viśrutaḥ ||2||
jātavedā ivārciṣmān vedān vedavidāṃ varaḥ |
yo ’dhītavān sucaturaś caturo’py ekavedavat ||3||
yasyābhicāravajreṇa vajrajvalanatejasaḥ |
papātāmūlataḥ śrīmān suparvā nandaparvataḥ ||4||
ekākī mantraśaktyā yaḥ śaktyā śaktidharopamaḥ|
ājahāra nṛcandrāya candraguptāya medinīm ||5||
nītiśāstrāmṛtaṃ śrīmān arthaśāstramahodadheḥ |
ya uddadhre namas tasmai viṣṇuguptāya vedhase ||6||
darśanāt tasya sudṛśo vidyānāṃ pāradṛśvanaḥ |
rājavidyāpriyatayā saṅkṣiptagrantham arthavat ||7||
upārjane pālane ca bhūmer bhūmīśvaraṃ prati |
yat kiñcid upadekṣyāmo rājavidyāvidāṃ matam ||8||
rājāsya jagato hetur vṛddher vṛddhābhisammataḥ |
nayanānandajananaḥ śaśāṅka iva toyadheḥ ||9||
yadi na syān narapatiḥ samyaṅ netā tataḥ prajā |
akarṇadhārā jaladhau viplaveteha naur iva ||10||
dhārmikaṃ pālanaparaṃ samyakparapurañjayam |
rājānam abhimanyante prajāpatim iva prajāḥ ||11||
āyattaṃ rakṣaṇaṃ rājñi vārtā rakṣaṇam āśritā |
vārtācchede hi loko ’yaṃ śvasann api na jīvati ||12||
parjanya iva bhūtānām ādhāraḥ pṛthivīpatiḥ |
vikale ’pi hi parjanye jīvyate na tu bhūpatau ||13||
prajāṃ saṃrakṣati nṛpaḥ sā vardhayati pārthivam |
vardhanād rakṣaṇaṃ śreyas tannāśe ’nyat sad apy asat||14|
nyāyapravṛtto nṛpatir ātmānam atha ca prajām |
trivargeṇopasandhatte nihanti dhruvam anyathā ||15||
dharmād vaijavano rājā cirāya bubhuje mahīm |
adharmāc caiva nahuṣaḥ pratipede rasātalam ||16||
tasmād dharmaṃ puraskṛtya yatetārthāya bhūpatiḥ |
dharmeṇa vardhate rājyaṃ tasya svādu phalaṃ śriyaḥ ||17||
svāmy amātyaś ca rāṣṭraṃ ca durgaṃ kośo balaṃ suhṛt |
etāvad ucyate rājyaṃ sattvabuddhivyapāśrayam ||18||
ālambya balavatsattvaṃ buddhyālocitanirgamaḥ |
saptāṅgasyāsya lābhāya yateta satatotthitaḥ ||19||
nyāyenārjanam arthasya rakṣaṇaṃ vardhanaṃ tathā |
satpātrapratipattiś ca rājavṛttaṃ caturvidham ||20||
nayavikramasampannaḥ sūtthānaś cintayec chriyam |
nayasya vinayo mūlaṃ vinayaḥ śāstraniścayaḥ ||21||
vinayo hīndriyajayas tadyuktaḥ śāstram ṛcchati |
tanniṣṭhasya hi śāstrārthāḥ prasīdanti tataḥ śriyaḥ ||22||
śāstraṃ prajñā dhṛtir dākṣyaṃ prāgalbhyaṃ dhārayiṣṇutā |
utsāho vāgmitā dārḍhyam āpatkleśasahiṣṇutā ||23||
prabhāvaḥ śucitā maitrī tyāgaḥ satyaṃ kṛtajñatā |
kulaṃ śīlaṃ damaś ceti guṇāḥ sampattihetavaḥ ||24||
ātmānaṃ prathamaṃ rājā vinayenopapādayet |
tato ’mātyāṃs tato bhṛtyāṃs tataḥ putrāṃs tataḥ prajāḥ ||25||
sadānuraktaprakṛtiḥ prajāpālanatatparaḥ |
vinītātmā hi nṛpatir bhūyasīṃ śriyam aśnute ||26||
prakīrṇe viṣayāraṇye dhāvantaṃ vipramāthinam |
jñānāṅkuśena kurvīta vaśyam indriyadantinam ||27||
ātmā prayatnād arthebhyo manaḥ samadhitiṣṭhati |
saṃyogād ātmamanasoḥ pravṛttir upajāyate ||28||
viṣayāmiṣalobhena manaḥ prerayatīndriyam |
tan nirundhyāt prayatnena jite tasmiñ jitendriyaḥ ||29||
vijñānaṃ hṛdayaṃ cittaṃ mano buddhiś ca tatsamam |
anenātmā karotīha pravartananivartane ||30||
dharmādharmau sukhaṃ duḥkham icchādveṣau tathaiva ca |
prayatnajñānasaṃskārā ātmaliṅgam udāhṛtam ||31||
jñānasyāyugapadbhāvo manaso liṅgam ucyate |
nānārtheṣu ca saṅkalpaḥ karma cāsya prakīrtyate ||32||
śrotraṃ tvakcakṣuṣī jihvā nāsikā ceti pañcamī |
pāyūpasthaṃ hastapādaṃ vāk cetīndriyasaṅgrahaḥ ||33||
śabdaḥ sparśaś ca rūpaṃ ca raso gandhaś ca pañcamaḥ |
utsargānandanādānagatyālāpāś ca tatkriyāḥ ||34||
ātmā manaś ca tadvidyair antaḥkaraṇam ucyate |
tābhyāṃ tu saprayatnābhyāṃ saṅkalpa upajāyate ||35||
ātmā buddhīndriyāṇy arthā bahiṣkaraṇam ucyate |
saṅkalpādhyavasāyābhyāṃ siddhir asya prakīrtitā ||36||
ubhe ete hi karaṇe prayatnānantarīyake |
tasmāt prayatnasaṃrodhād bhāvayen nirmanaskatām ||37||
evaṃ karaṇasāmarthyāt saṃyamyātmānam ātmanā |
nayāpanayavid rājā kurvīta hitam ātmanaḥ ||38||
ekasyāpi na yaḥ śakto manasaḥ sannibarhaṇe |
mahīṃ sāgaraparyantāṃ kathaṃ nu sa vijeṣyate ||39||
kriyāvasānavirasair viṣayair apahāribhiḥ |
gacchaty ākṣiptahṛdayaḥ karīva nṛpatir graham ||40||
sajjamāno hy akāryeṣu viṣayāndhīkṛtekṣaṇaḥ |
āvahaty ugrabhayadāṃ svayam evāpadaṃ nṛpaḥ ||41||
śabdaḥ sparśaś ca rūpaṃ ca raso gandhaś ca pañcamaḥ |
ekaikam eṣāṃ bhavati vināśapratipattaye ||42||
śuciḥ śaṣpāṅkurāhāro vidūrakramaṇakṣamaḥ |
lubdhakād gītalobhena mṛgo mṛgayate vadham ||43||
girīndrasadṛśākāro līlayonmūlitadrumaḥ |
kariṇīsparśasammohād ālānaṃ yāti vāraṇaḥ ||44||
snigdhadīpaśikhālokavilobhitavilocanaḥ |
mṛtyum ṛcchaty asandehāt pataṅgaḥ sahasā patan ||45||
dūre ’pi hi bhavan dṛṣṭer agādhe salile caran |
mīnas tu sāmiṣaṃ loham āsvādayati mṛtyave ||46||
gandhalubdho madhukaro dānāsavapipāsayā |
abhyety asukhasañcārān gajakarṇajhanañjhanān ||47||
ekaikaśo ’pi nighnanti viṣayā viṣasannibhāḥ |
kṣemī tu sa kathaṃ nu syād yaḥ samaṃ pañca sevate ||48||
seveta viṣayān kāle muktvā tatparatāṃ vaśī |
sukhaṃ hi phalam arthasya tannirodhe vṛthā śriyaḥ ||49||
nikāmāsaktamanasāṃ kāntāmukhavilokane |
galanti galitāśrūṇāṃ yauvanena saha śriyaḥ ||50||
dharmād artho ’rthataḥ kāmaḥ kāmāt sukhaphalodayaḥ |
ātmānaṃ hanti tau hatvā yuktyā yo na niṣevate ||51||
nāmāpi strīti saṃhlādi vikaroty eva mānasam |
kiṃ punar darśanāt tasyā vilāsollāsitabhruvaḥ ||52||
rahaḥpracārakuśalā mṛdugadgadabhāṣiṇī |
kaṃ na nārī ramayati raktaṃ raktāntalocanā ||53||
muner api mano ’vaśyaṃ sarāgaṃ kurute ’ṅganā |
prasannaṃ kāntijananaṃ sandhyeva śaśimaṇḍalam ||54||
manaḥ prahlādayantībhir mandaṃ yāntībhir apy alam |
mahānto ’pi hi bhidyante strībhir adbhir ivācalāḥ ||55||
mṛgayākṣās tathā pānaṃ garhitāni mahībhujām |
dṛṣṭās tebhyo ’pi vipadaḥ pāṇḍunaiṣadhavṛṣṇiṣu ||56||
kāmaḥ krodhas tathā lobho harṣo māno madas tathā |
ṣaḍvargam utsṛjed enaṃ tasmiṃs tyakte sukhī nṛpaḥ ||57||
dāṇḍakyo nṛpatiḥ kāmāt krodhāc ca janamejayaḥ |
lobhād ailas tu rājarṣir vātāpir harṣato ’suraḥ ||58||
paulastyo rākṣaso mānān madād dambhodbhavo nṛpaḥ |
prayātā nidhanaṃ hy ete śatruṣaḍvargam āśritāḥ ||59||
śatruṣaḍvargam utsṛjya jāmadagnyo jitendriyaḥ |
ambarīṣaś ca mahābhāgo bubhujāte ciraṃ mahīm ||60||
vidyāvṛddhasaṃyogaprakaraṇam
vardhayann iha dharmārthau sevitau sadbhir ādarāt |
nigṛhītendriyaḥ sādhu kurvīta gurusevanam ||61||
śāstrāya gurusaṃyogaḥ śāstraṃ vinayavṛddhaye |
vidyāvinīto nṛpatir na kṛchreṣv avasīdati ||62||
vṛddhopasevī nṛpatiḥ satāṃ bhavati sammataḥ |
preryamāṇo ’py asadvṛttair nākāryeṣu pravartate ||63||
ādadānaḥ pratidinaṃ kalāḥ samyaṅ mahīpatiḥ |
śuklapakṣe pravicarañ chaśāṅka iva vardhate ||64||
jitendriyasya nṛpater nītimārgānusāriṇaḥ |
bhavanti jvalitā lakṣmyaḥ kīrtayaś ca nabhaḥspṛśaḥ ||65||
iti sma rājā vinayaṃ nayānvito
niṣevamāṇo naradevasevitam |
padaṃ samākrāmati bhāsvaraṃ śriyaḥ
śiro mahāratnagirer ivonnatam ||66||
iyaṃ hi lokavyatirekavartinī
svabhāvataḥ pārthivatā samuddhatā |
balāt tad enāṃ vinayena yojayen
nayasya vṛddhau vinayaḥ puraḥsaraḥ ||67||
parāṃ vinītaḥ samupaiti sevyatāṃ
mahīpatīnāṃ vinayo vibhūṣaṇam |
pravṛttadāno mṛdusañcaratkaraḥ
karīva bhadro vinayena śobhate ||68||
gurus tu vidyādhigamāya sevyate
śrutā ca vidyā mataye mahātmanām |
śrutānuvartīni matāni vedhasā-
masaṃśayaṃ sādhu bhavanti bhūtaye||69||
sunipuṇam upasevya sadguruṃ
śuciranuvṛttiparo vibhūtaye |
bhavati hi vinayopabṛṃhito
nṛpatipadāya śamāya ca kṣamaḥ ||70||
avinayaratam ādarād ṛte
vaśam avaśaṃ hi nayanti vidviṣaḥ |
śrutavinayanidhiṃ samāśritas
tanur api naiti parābhavaṃ kvacit ||71||
dvitīyaḥ sargaḥ
vidyāvibhāgaprakaraṇam
ānvīkṣikīṃ trayīṃ vārtāṃ daṇḍanītiṃ ca pārthivaḥ |
tadvidyais tatkriyopetaiś cintayed vinayānvitaḥ ||1||
ānvīkṣikī trayī vārtā daṇḍanītiś ca śāśvatī |
vidyāś catasra evaitā yogakṣemāya dehinām ||2||
trayī vārtā daṇḍanītis tisro vidyā hi mānavāḥ |
trayyā eva vibhāgo ’yaṃ yeyam ānvīkṣikī matā ||3||
vārtā ca daṇḍanītiś ca dve vidye ity avasthite |
lokasyārthapradhānatvāc chiṣyāḥ surapurodhasaḥ ||4||
ekaiva daṇḍanītis tu vidyety auśanasī sthitiḥ |
tasyāṃ hi sarvavidyānām ārambhāḥ sampratiṣṭhitāḥ ||5||
vidyāś catasra evaitā iti no gurudarśanam |
pṛthak pṛthak prasiddhyarthaṃ yāsu loko vyavasthitaḥ ||6||
ānvīkṣiky ātmavijñānaṃ dharmādharmau trayīsthitau |
arthānarthau tu vārtāyāṃ daṇḍanītyāṃ nayetarau ||7||
ānvīkṣikītrayīvārtāḥ satīr vidyāḥ pracakṣate |
satyo ’pi hi na satyas tā daṇḍanītes tu viplave ||8||
daṇḍanītir yadā samyaṅ netāram adhitiṣṭhati |
tadā vidyāvidaḥ śeṣā vidyāḥ samyag upāsate ||9||
varṇāś caivāśramāś caiva vidyāsv āsu pratiṣṭhitāḥ |
rakṣet tā rakṣaṇāt tāsāṃ taddharmasyāṃśabhāṅ nṛpaḥ ||10||
ānvīkṣiky ātmavidyā syād īkṣaṇāt sukhaduḥkhayoḥ |
īkṣamāṇas tayā tattvaṃ harṣaśokau vyudasyati ||11||
ṛgyajuḥsāmanāmānas trayo vedās trayī smṛtā |
ubhau lokāv avāpnoti trayyāṃ tiṣṭhan yathāvidhi ||12||
aṅgāni vedāś catvāro mīmāṃsā nyāyavistaraḥ |
dharmaśāstraṃ purāṇaṃ ca trayīdaṃ sarvam ucyate||13||
pāśupālyaṃ kṛṣiḥ paṇyaṃ vārtā vārtānujīvinām |
sampanno vārtayā sādhu nāvṛtter bhayam ṛcchati ||14||
damo daṇḍa iti proktas tātsthyād daṇḍo mahīpatiḥ |
tasya nītir daṇḍanītir nayanān nītir ucyate ||15||
tayātmānaṃ ca śeṣāś ca vidyāḥ pāyān mahīpatiḥ |
vidyā lokopakāriṇyas tatpātā hi mahīpatiḥ ||16||
vidyābhir ābhir nipuṇaṃ caturvargam udāradhīḥ |
vidyāt tadāsāṃ vidyātvaṃ vida jñāne nirucyate ||17||
varṇāśramavyavasthāprakaraṇam
ijyādhyayanadānāni yathāśāstraṃ sanātanaḥ |
brāhmaṇakṣatriyaviśāṃ sāmānyo dharma ucyate ||18||
yājanādhyāpane śuddhe viśuddhāc ca pratigrahaḥ |
vṛttitrayam idaṃ proktaṃ munibhir jyeṣṭhavarṇinaḥ ||19||
śastreṇājīvanaṃ rājño bhūtānāṃ cābhirakṣaṇam |
pāśupālyaṃ kṛṣiḥ paṇyaṃ vaiśyasyājīvanaṃ smṛtam ||20||
śūdrasya dharmaḥ śuśrūṣā dvijānām anupūrvaśaḥ |
śuddhā ca vṛttis tatsevā kārucāraṇakarma ca ||21||
gurau vāso ’gniśuśrūṣā svādhyāyo vratacāraṇam |
trikālasnāyitā bhaikṣaṃ gurau prāṇāntikī sthitiḥ ||22||
tadabhāve gurusute tathā sabrahmacāriṇi |
kāmato vāśramānyatvaṃ svadharmo brahmacāriṇaḥ ||23||
samekhalo jaṭī muṇḍo daṇḍī vā gurusaṃśrayaḥ |
ā vidyāgrahaṇād gacchet kāmato vāśramāntaram ||24||
agnihotropacaraṇaṃ jīvanaṃ ca svakarmabhiḥ |
dharmadāreṣu kalyeṣu parvavarjaṃ ratikriyā ||25||
devapitratithibhyaś ca pūjā dīnānukampanam |
śrutismṛtyarthasaṃsthānaṃ dharmo ’yaṃ gṛhamedhinaḥ ||26||
jaṭitvam agnihotritvaṃ bhūśayyājinadhāraṇam |
vanevāsaḥ payomūlanīvāraphalavṛttitā ||27||
pratigrahanivṛttiś ca triḥsnānaṃ vratacāritā |
devātithīnāṃ pūjā ca dharmo ’yaṃ vanavāsinaḥ ||28||
sarvārambhaparityāgo bhaikṣāśyaṃ vṛkṣamūlitā |
niṣparigrahatādrohaḥ samatā sarvajantuṣu ||29||
priyāpriyāpariṣvaṅgaḥ sukhaduḥkhāvikāritā |
sabāhyābhyantaraṃ śaucaṃ vāgyamo vratacāritā ||30||
sarvendriyasamāhāro dhāraṇā dhyānayuktatā |
bhāvasaṃśuddhir ity eṣa parivrāḍdharma ucyate ||31||
ahiṃsā sūnṛtā vāṇī satyaṃ śaucaṃ dayā kṣamā |
varṇināṃ liṅgināṃ caiva sāmānyo dharma ucyate ||32||
svargānantyāya dharmo ’yaṃ sarveṣāṃ varṇiliṅginām |
tasyābhāve tu loko ’yaṃ saṅkarān nāśam āpnuyāt ||33||
sarvasyāsya yathānyāyaṃ bhūpatiḥ sampravartakaḥ |
tasyābhāve dharmanāśas tadabhāve jagaccyutiḥ ||34||
varṇāśramācārayuto varṇāśramavibhāgavit |
pātā varṇāśramāṇāṃ ca pārthivaḥ sarvalokabhāk ||35||
daṇḍamāhātmyaprakaraṇam
iti yasmād ubhau lokau dhārayaty ātmavān nṛpaḥ |
prajānāṃ ca tataḥ samyag daṇḍaṃ daṇḍīva dhārayet ||36||
udvejayati tīkṣṇena mṛdunā paribhūyate |
daṇḍena nṛpatis tasmād yuktadaṇḍaḥ praśasyate ||37||
trivargaṃ vardhayaty āśu rājño daṇḍo yathāvidhi |
praṇīto ’thāsamañjasyād vanasthān api kopayet ||38||
lokaśāstrānugo neyo daṇḍo ’nudvejanaḥ śriye |
udvejanād adharmaḥ syāt tasmād dhvaṃso mahīpateḥ ||39||
parasparāmiṣatayā jagato bhinnavartmanaḥ |
daṇḍābhāve paridhvaṃsī mātsyo nyāyaḥ pravartate ||40||
jagad etan nirākrandaṃ kāmalobhādibhir balāt |
nimajjyamānaṃ niraye rājñā daṇḍena dhāryate ||41||
idaṃ prakṛtyā viṣayair vaśīkṛtaṃ
parasparastrīdhanalolupaṃ jagat |
sanātane vartmani sādhusevite
pratiṣṭhate daṇḍabhayopapīḍitam ||
niyataviṣayavartī prāyaśo daṇḍayogāj
jagati paravaśe’smin durlabhaḥ sādhuvṛttaḥ |
kṛśam atha vikalaṃ vā vyādhitaṃ vādhanaṃ vā
patim api kulanārī daṇḍabhītyābhyupaiti ||43||
iti parigaṇitārthaḥ śāstramārgānusārī
niyamayati yatātmā yaḥ prajā daṇḍanītyā |
apunarapagamāya prāptamārgapracārāḥ
sarita iva samudraṃ sampadastaṃ viśanti ||44||
tṛtīyaḥ sargaḥ
ācāravyavasthāpanaprakaraṇam
daṇḍaṃ daṇḍīva bhūteṣu dhārayan dharaṇīpatiḥ |
prajāḥ samanugṛhṇīyāt prajāpatir iva svayam ||1||
vāk sūnṛtā dayā dānaṃ dīnopagatarakṣaṇam |
iti saṅgaḥ satāṃ sādhu hitaṃ satpuruṣavratam ||2||
āviṣṭa iva duḥkhena tadgatena garīyasā |
samanvitaḥ karuṇayā parayā dīnam uddharet ||3||
na tebhyo ’bhyadhikāḥ santi santaḥ satpuruṣavratāḥ |
duḥkhapaṅkārṇave magnaṃ dīnam abhyuddharanti ye ||4||
dayām āsthāya paramāṃ dharmād avicalan nṛpaḥ |
pīḍitānām anāthānāṃ kuryād aśrupramārjanam ||5||
ānṛśaṃsyaṃ paro dharmaḥ sarvaprāṇabhṛtāṃ mataḥ |
tasmād rājānṛśaṃsyena pālayet kṛpaṇaṃ janam ||6||
na hi svasukham anvicchan pīḍayet kṛpaṇaṃ nṛpaḥ |
kṛpaṇaḥ pīḍyamāno hi manyunā hanti pārthivam ||7||
ko hi nāma kule jātaḥ sukhaleśopalobhitaḥ |
alpasārāṇi bhūtāni pīḍayed avicārayan ||8||
ādhivyādhiparītāya adya śvo vā vināśine |
ko hi nāma śarīrāya dharmāpetaṃ samācaret ||9||
āhāryair nīyamānaṃ hi kṣaṇaṃ duḥkhena hṛdyatām |
chāyāmātrakam evedaṃ paśyed udakabinduvat ||10||
mahāvātāhatibhrāntameghamālātipelavaiḥ |
kathaṃ nāma mahātmāno hriyante viṣayāribhiḥ ||11||
jalāntaś candracapalaṃ jīvitaṃ khalu dehinām |
tathāvidham iti jñātvā śaśvatkalyāṇam ācaret ||12||

pradānakāle bhavati mātāpi hi parāṅmukhī ||57||

durvṛtte ’py akulīne ’pi jano dātari rajyate ||61||

vayo’vasthāṃ ca saṃvīkṣya svādriyeta mahāmanāḥ ||68||
kulīnān nāvamanyeta samyagvṛttān manasvinaḥ |
tyajanty ete hi bhartāraṃ ghnanti vā mānahetavaḥ ||69||

mahattāṃ prāpnuvantas te vardhayanti nareśvaram ||70||

lakṣmyā lakṣmīvatāṃ loke vikāsinyāpi kiṃ tayā |
bandhubhiś ca suhṛdbhiś ca visrabdhaṃ yā na bhujyate ||74||

kuryād udyogasampannān adhyakṣān sarvakarmasu ||76||
yo yad vastu vijānāti taṃ tatra viniyojayet |

khanyākaro vanādānaṃ śūnyānāṃ ca niveśanam ||79||

gatabhīr bhītijananaṃ bhogaṃ bhogīva darśayet |
yathābalaṃ ca kurvīta ripor daṇḍanipātanam ||17||
prakṛtikarmaprakaraṇam
prakṛtivyasanaṃ yatsyāttatpraśāmya samutpatet |
anayāpanayābhyāṃ taj jāyate daivato ’pi ca ||18||
yasmāt tad vyasyati śreyas tasmād vyasanam ucyate |
vyasany adho ’dho vrajati tasmāt tat parivarjayet ||19||
hutāśano jalaṃ vyādhir dubhikṣo marakas tathā |
iti pañcavidhaṃ daivaṃ vyasanaṃ mānuṣaṃ param ||20||
daivaṃ puruṣakāreṇa śāntyā ca praśamaṃ nayet |
utthāyitvena nītyā ca mānuṣaṃ kāryatattvavit ||21||
svāmyādimitraparyantaṃ prākṛtaṃ maṇḍalaṃ hi yat |
tasya karma pravakṣyāmi vyasanaṃ ca yathākramam ||22||
mantro mantraphalāvāptiḥ kāryānuṣṭhānam āyatiḥ |
āyavyayau daṇḍanītir amitrapratiṣedhanam ||23||
vyasanasya pratīkāro rājarājyābhirakṣaṇam |
ity amātyasya karmedaṃ hanti tadvyasanānvitaḥ ||24||
amātyair vyasanopetair hriyamāṇo hi bhūpatiḥ |
aśakta evotpatituṃ chinnapakṣa ivāṇḍajaḥ ||25||
hiraṇyavastradhānyādi vāhanāni tathaiva ca |
tathānye dravyanicayāḥ prajātaḥ sambhavanti hi ||26||
vārtāṃ prajā sādhayati vārtā vai lokasaṃśrayā |
prajāyāṃ vyasanasthāyāṃ na kiṃcid api sidhyati ||27||
prajānām āpadi trāṇaṃ rakṣaṇaṃ kośadaṇḍayoḥ |
paurāś caivopakurvanti saṃśrayāyeha durgiṇām ||28||
tūṣṇīṃ yuddhaṃ janatrāṇaṃ mitrāmitraparigrahaḥ |
sāmantāṭavikābādhānirodho durgasaṃśrayāt ||29||
svapakṣaiḥ parapakṣaiś ca durgasthaḥ pūjyate nṛpaḥ |
etad dhi durgavyasanāt sarvam eva vipadyate ||30||
bhṛtyānāṃ bharaṇaṃ dānaṃ bhūṣaṇaṃ vāhanakriyāḥ |
sthairyaṃ paropajāpaś ca durgasaṃskāra eva ca ||31||
setubandho vaṇikkarma prajāmitraparigrahaḥ |
dharmakāmārthasiddhiś ca kośād etat prarvatate ||32||
kośamūlo hi rājeti pravādaḥ sārvalaukikaḥ |
etat sarvaṃ jahāty āśu kośavyasanavān nṛpaḥ ||33||
kṣīṇaṃ balaṃ vardhayati svato gṛhṇāti ca prajāḥ |
kośavān pṛthivīpālaḥ parair apy upajīvyate ||34||
mitrāmitrahiraṇyānāṃ bhūmīnāṃ ca prasādhanam |
dūrakāryāśukāritvaṃ labdhasya paripālanam ||35||
paracakravighātaś ca svadaṇḍasya parigrahaḥ |
daṇḍād etat prabhavati yāti tadvyasane kṣayam ||36||
arayo ’pi hi mitratvaṃ yānti daṇḍavato dhruvam |
daṇḍaprāyo hi nṛpatir bhunakty ākramya medinīm ||37||
saṃstambhayati mitrāṇi śatrūn utsādayaty api |
bhūkośadaṇḍair vrajati prāṇaiś cāpy upakāritām ||38||
tat tat karoti ca bahir mitraṃ snehanibandhanam |
tasmin vyavasanam āpanne mitrakarma na vidyate ||39||
upakārād ṛte ’py āśu mitraṃ śreyasi tiṣṭhati |
mitravān sādhayaty arthān duḥsādhyān apy anādarāt ||40||
anvīkṣaṇaṃ ca vidyānāṃ sadvarṇāśramarakṣaṇam |
grahaṇaṃ śastraśāstrāṇāṃ yuddhamārgopaśikṣaṇam ||41||
vyāyāmaḥ śastravijñānaṃ varmaṇāṃ lakṣaṇāni ca |
gajāśvarathapṛṣṭheṣu yathāvat sampravartanam ||42||
niyuddhakauśalaṃ māyā paracittapraveśanam |
dhūrtatā śāṭhyayukteṣu satsu sadvṛttadarśanam ||43||
mantrodyogo ’numantratvaṃ tadrakṣā tātsthyam eva ca |
upekṣā sāma dānaṃ ca bhedo daṇḍaś ca sādhanam ||44||
praśāstṛsūtasenānīmantryamātyapurodhasām |
samyak pracāravijñānaṃ duṣṭānāṃ cāvaropaṇam ||45||
gatāgataparijñānaṃ dūtasampreṣaṇāni ca |
prakṛtivyasanāpohaḥ kruddhapraśamanāni ca ||46||
gurūṇām anuvṛttiś ca pūjyānām anupūjanam |
dharmāsanapratiṣṭhānaṃ rājyakaṇṭakaśodhanam ||47||
bhṛtābhṛtaparijñānaṃ kṛtākṛtaparīkṣaṇam |
tuṣṭātuṣṭavicāraś ca sarveṣām anujīvinām ||48||
madhyodāsīnacaritajñānaṃ tatsiddhipālanam |
pratigrahaś ca mitrāṇām amitrāṇāṃ ca nigrahaḥ ||49||
putradārādiguptiś ca bandhuvargaparigrahaḥ |
khanidvipavanādīnāṃ[1] svavṛttīnāṃ pravartanam ||50||
asatāṃ ca parikṣepaḥ satāṃ ca parigūhanam |
ahiṃsā sarvabhūtānām adharmāṇāṃ ca varjanam ||51||
akāryapratiṣedhaś ca kāryāṇāṃ ca pravartanam |
pradānaṃ ca pradeyānām adeyānāma saṅgrahaḥ ||52||
adaṇḍanam adaṇḍyānāṃ daṇḍyānāṃ cāpi daṇḍanam |
agrāhyāgrahaṇaṃ caiva grāhyāṇāṃ grahaṇaṃ tathā ||53||
arthayuktasya karaṇam anarthasya ca varjanam |
nyāyataś ca karādānaṃ svayaṃ ca pratimokṣaṇam ||54||
samarthanaṃ pradhānānāṃ nirasyānāṃ nirākṛtiḥ |
vaiṣamyāṇāṃ praśamanaṃ bhṛtyānāṃ cāvirodhanam ||55||
avijñātasya vijñānaṃ vijñātasya ca niścayaḥ |
ārambhaḥ karmaṇaḥ śaśvad ārabdhasyāntadarśanam ||56||
alabdhalipsā nyāyena labdhasya ca vivardhanam |
parivṛddhasya vidhivat pātre sampratipādanam ||57||
adharmapratiṣedhaś ca nyāyamārgeṇa vartanam |
upakāryopakāritvam iti vṛttaṃ mahīpateḥ ||58||
etat sarvam amātyādi rājā nayapuraḥsaraḥ |
nayaty unnatim udyukto vyasanī kṣayam eva tu ||59||
tasmin dharmārthayor vyagre tathaivāsvasthacetasi |
sarvam etad viśeṣeṇa mantrī san netum arhati ||60||
prakṛtivyasanaprakaraṇam
vāgdaṇḍayoś ca pāruṣyam arthadūṣaṇam eva ca |
pānaṃ strī mṛgayā dyūtaṃ vyasanāni mahīpateḥ ||61||
ālasyaṃ stabdhatā darpaḥ pramādo vairakāritā |
iti pūrvopadiṣṭaṃ ca sacivavyasanaṃ smṛtam ||62||
ativṛṣṭir anāvṛṣṭir mūṣikāḥ śalabhādayaḥ |
asatkaraś ca daṇḍaś ca paracakrāṇi taskarāḥ ||63||
rājānīkapriyotsargo mārakavyādhipīḍanam |
paśūnāṃ maraṇaṃ rogo rāṣṭravyasanam ucyate ||64||
viśīrṇayantraprākāraparikhātvam aśastratā |
kṣīṇaghāsendhanānnatvād durgavyasanam ucyate ||65||
vyayīkṛtaḥ parikṣipto bhakṣito ’sañcitas tathā |
muṣito dūrasaṃsthaś ca kośavyasanam ucyate ||66||
uparuddhaṃ parikṣiptaṃ vimānitam amānitam |
abhṛtaṃ vyādhitaṃ śrāntaṃ dūrāyātaṃ navāgatam ||67||
parikṣīṇaṃ pratihataṃ prahatāgrajavaṃ tathā |
āśānir vedabhūyiṣṭham anṛtaprāptam eva ca ||68||
kalatragarbhyatikṣiptam antaḥśalyaṃ tathaiva ca |
bhinnagarbhaṃ hy apasṛtam avamuktaṃ tathaiva ca ||69||
kruddhamaulārimitraṃ ca niviṣṭaṃ cāpi vidviṣā |
dūṣyayuktaṃ svavikṣiptaṃ mitravikṣiptam eva ca ||70||
vicchinnavīvadhāsāraṃ śūnyamūlaṃ tathaiva ca |
asvāmisaṅgataṃ caiva bhinnakūṭaṃ tathaiva ca ||71||

atra kiṃcid asādhyaṃ tu kiṃcit sādhyaṃ tad ucyate ||72||
uparuddhaṃ tu yudhyeta nirgatyānyata ūrjitam |
parikṣiptaṃ na nirmārgaṃ sarvataḥ pariveṣṭitam ||73||
amānitaṃ hi yudhyeta kṛtamānārthasaṅgraham |
na vimānitam atyarthaṃ pradīptakrodhapāvakam ||74||
yudhyetābhṛtam atyarthaṃ tadātvakṛtavetanam |
na vyādhitam akarmaṇyaṃ vyādhitaṃ paribhūyate ||75||
pariśrāntaṃ hi yudhyeta viśrāntaṃ suvidhānataḥ |
dūrāyātaṃ hataprāṇaṃ na śastragrahaṇe kṣamam ||76||
navāgataṃ hi yudhyeta taddeśyair miśritaṃ nayāt |
hatamukhyapravīraṃ tu parikṣīṇaṃ na yudhyate ||77||
yudhyate hi pratihataṃ pravīraiḥ saha saṅgatam |
hatāgravegaṃ śaktaṃ na pramāthitapuraḥsaram ||78||
āśānirvedalabdhārthaṃ pūrṇāśatvaṃ na yudhyate |
abhūmiṣṭhaṃ prasāre na niruddhe ’lpatayā bhuvaḥ ||79||
yudhyetānṛtasamprāptaṃ yathārhāyudhavāhanam |
kalatragarbhi connītakalatraṃ samarakṣamam ||80||
anekarājyāntaritam atikṣiptaṃ na yudhyate |
antargatāmitraśalyam antaḥśalyaṃ hi na kṣamam ||81||
anyonyasmād vinirbhinnaṃ bhinnagarbhaṃ na yudhyate |
tathaivāpasṛtaṃ śaktaṃ naikarājyāntarīkṛtam ||82||
avamuktam apakrāntamukhyaṃ tan na kṣamaṃ yudhi |
pitṛpaitāmahaṃ maulaṃ tat kruddhaṃ sāntvitaṃ kṣamam ||83||
mitraṃ śatrubhir ekasthaṃ tadākrāntatayākṣamam |
śatror upaniviṣṭaṃ tat sāmarthyān na kṣamaṃ yudhi ||84||
dūṣyayuktaṃ taduddhārād yudhyetoddhṛtakaṇṭakam |
pradhānayodhasaṃguptaṃ dūṣyayuktaṃ samutpatet ||85||
svavikṣiptaṃ svaviṣaye kṣiptam āpady udāhṛtam |
prakṛṣṭadeśakālatvān mitrakṣiptam ayaugikam ||86||
dhānyāder vīvadhaḥ prāptir āsāras tu suhṛdbalam |
vicchinnavīvadhāsāraṃ balaṃ yuddhāya neṣyate ||87||
pitṛpaitāmahaṃ maulaṃ tena śūnyaṃ hi na kṣamam |
kṛtajānapadārakṣaṃ śūnyamūlaṃ yudhi kṣamam ||88||
yudhyate śūnyamūlaṃ hi yatnān maulena pālitam |
asvāmisaṅgataṃ naiva svāminā yad vinākṛtam ||89||
na yudhyate bhinnakūṭaṃ bhinnakūṭam anāyakam |
paścātkopātisantaptaṃ duṣpārṣṇigrāham akṣamam ||90||
adeśikaṃ smṛtaṃ hy andhaṃ tanmūḍhatvāt kriyākṣamam |
balavyasanam ityādi tat samīkṣya samutpatet ||91||
daivopapīḍitaṃ grastaṃ mitraṃ śatrubalena ca |

narendrādyāḥ prakṛtayaḥ sapta yāḥ parikīrtitāḥ |

buddhyā ca śaktyā vyasanasya kuryād
akālahīnaṃ vyavaropaṇaṃ saḥ ||94||
prakṛtivyasanāni bhūtikāmaḥ
samupekṣeta na hi pramādadarpāt |

nacirāt taṃ ripavaḥ parābhavanti ||95||

pañcadaśaḥ sargaḥ
saptavyasanavargaprakaraṇam

varam andho na cakṣuṣmān madād ākṣiptasatpathaḥ ||4||
mantribhir mantrakuśalair andhaḥ pathi niveśyate |
cakṣuṣmāṃs tu madāndhaḥ san nātmānaṃ hanty aśeṣataḥ ||5||
śāstracakṣur nṛpas tasmān mahāmātramate sthitaḥ |
dharmārthapratighātīni vyasanāni parityajet ||6||
vāgdaṇḍayoś ca pāruṣyam arthadūṣaṇam eva ca |
smṛtaṃ vyasanatattvajñaiḥ krodhajaṃ vyasanatrayam ||7||
kāmajaṃ mṛgayā dyūtaṃ striyaḥ pānaṃ tathaiva ca |
vyasanaṃ vyasanārthajñaiś caturvidham udāhṛtam ||8||
vākpāruṣyaṃ paraṃ loka udvejanam anarthakam |
na kuryād vipriyāṃ vācaṃ prakuryāj janam ātmasāt ||9||
akasmād eva yaḥ kopāt paruṣaṃ bahu bhāṣate |
tasmād udvijate lokaḥ sasphuliṅgād ivānalāt ||10||
hṛdaye vāgasis tīkṣaṇo marmacchin nipatan muhuḥ |
tejasvinaṃ dīpayati sa dīpto yāti vairitām ||11||
nodvejayej jagadvājā rūkṣayā priyavāg bhavet |
prāyeṇa priyavākkarmā kṛpaṇo ’pi hi sevyate ||12||

taṃ yuktyaiva nayed daṇḍaṃ yuktadaṇḍaḥ praśasyate ||13||

bhūtāny udvejyamānāni dviṣatāṃ yānti saṃśrayam ||14||
āśritāś caiva lokena samṛddhiṃ yānti vidviṣaḥ |

lokānugrahakartāraḥ pravardhante mahībhujaḥ |

sa rukmī rukmaśailābho dyūtadoṣād gataḥ kṣayam ||52||
rājā kāśikarūśānāṃ dantavaktro ’pi mandadhīḥ |
tīvrād dyūtakṛtād doṣād dantabhaṅgam avāptavān ||53||
dyūtād anarthasaṃrambho dyūtāt snehakṣayo mahān |
pakṣāṇāṃ cāpi mahatāṃ dyūtād bhedaḥ pravartate ||54||
iti kevaladoṣaṃ hi rājā dyūtaṃ vivarjayet |
samāhvayaṃ ca medhāvī darpiṇāṃ vinivārayet ||55||
kālātipātaḥ kāryāṇāṃ dharmārthaparipīḍanam |
nityābhyantaravartitvāt sādhuprakṛtikopanam ||56|
rahasyabhedas tat paścād akāryeṣu pravartanam |
īrṣyāmarṣas tathā krodho virodhaḥ sāhasāni ca ||57||
ityādi ca strīvyasane yac ca pūrvaṃ prakīrtitam |
tasmāt strīvyasanaṃ rājā rājyakāmaḥ parityajet ||58||
strīmukhālokanatayā vyagrāṇām alpacetasām |
īhitānīha gacchanti yauvanena saha kṣayam ||59||
vamanaṃ vihvalatvaṃ ca saṃjñānāśo vivastratā |
bahvabaddhapralāpitvam akasmād vyasanaṃ muhuḥ ||60||
prāṇaglāniḥ suhṛnnāśaḥ prajñāśrutamatibhramaḥ |
sadbhir viyogo ’sadbhiś ca saṃyogo ’narthasaṅgamaḥ ||61||
skhalanaṃ vepathus tandrā nitāntaṃ strīniṣevaṇam |
ityādi pānavyasanam atyantaṃ sadvigarhitam ||62||
śrutaśīlabalopetāḥ pānadoṣeṇa bhūyasā |
kṣayam akṣīṇanāmāno jagmur andhakavṛṣṇayaḥ ||63||
yogīśvaras tu bhagavān bhārgavo bhṛgutulyadhīḥ |
śukraḥ pānamadāt tīvrād bubhuje śiṣyam aurasam ||64||
pānākṣipto hi puruṣo yatra tatra pravartate |
yāty asaṃvyavahāritvaṃ yatra tatra pravartanāt ||65||
kāmaṃ striyo niṣeveta pānaṃ vā sādhu mātrayā |
na dyūtamṛgaye vidvān atyantavyasane hi te ||66||
tadapanayavidhijñaiḥ śreyasāṃ vighnakāri
vyasanam idam udāraṃ saptadhehopadiṣṭam |
janayati hi niṣaṅgād ekadhaivāśu nāśaṃ
kim u na bhavati hantā yaugapadyodayena ||67||
paṭayati paribhogagrāhitām indrīyāṇāṃ
śrutam api vinihanti śreṣṭhatāṃ praṣṭhatāṃ ca |36
calayati hi vibhūtiṃ bhūyasīm apy anīcair
api vibudhamatīnāṃ saptako ’yaṃ durantaḥ| |68||
arigaṇā niyataṃ vyasane sthitān
paribhavanti bhavanti ca duśchidaḥ |
apagatavyasanās tu budhā ripūn
paribhavanti bhavanti ca duśchidaḥ ||69||
ṣoḍaśaḥ sargaḥ
yātrābhiyoktṛpradarśanaprakaraṇam
nānāprakārair vyasanair vimuktaḥ
śaktitrayeṇāpratimena yuktaḥ |
paraṃ durantavyasanopapannaṃ
yāyān narendro vijayābhikāṅkṣī ||1||
prāyeṇa santo vyasane ripūṇāṃ
yātavyam ity evam upādiśanti |
tatraiṣa pakṣo vyasanaṃ hy anityaṃ
kṣamas tu sann abhyudito hi yāyāt ||2||
yadā kṣamas tu prasabhaṃ nihantuṃ
parākramād ūrjitam apy amitram |
tadā hi yāyād ahitāni kartuṃ
parasya vā pīḍanakarśanāni ||3||
sampannasasyaṃ viṣayaṃ parasya
yāyāt pramṛdnan vijayāya rājā |
sasyopaghātena parasya vṛtteś
chedaḥ svasainyopacayaś ca sādhu ||4||
viśuddhapṛṣṭhaḥ purato vicinvan
bhayapradeśān parakarmavedī |
svavīvadhāsāraviśuddhamārgo
viśed dharitrīṃ dviṣato ’pramattaḥ ||5||
same pradeśe viṣame ca bhūmer
nimne sthale vā svamukhena yāyāt |
anāturaḥ sann abhayo hi vidvān
sannaddhasainyo vihitānnatoyaḥ ||6||
grīṣme prabhūtāmbuvanena yāyān
nirvāpaṇārthaṃ kariṇāṃ pathā tu |
ṛte ’mbhaso grīṣmakṛtāt pratāpād
bhavanti kuṣṭhāni mataṅgajānām ||7||
svasthakriyāṇām api kuñjarāṇām
ūṣmā śarīreṣu hi jājvalīti |
āyāsayogena ca sampravṛddhaḥ
prasahya hanti dviradān pratāpaḥ| |8||
sarvāṇi sattvāni khalūṣṇakāle
vināmbunā yānti parām avasthām |
atyartham uṣṇapratitaptakāyāḥ
prayānti sadyaḥ kariṇo ’pibantaḥ ||9||
sugandhidānasrutiśīkareṣu
dantābhighātasphuṭitopaleṣu |
gajeṣu nīlābhrasamaprabheṣu
rājyaṃ nibaddhaṃ pṛthivīpatīnām ||10||
saṃkalpitaḥ saṃyati dṛṣṭamārgaḥ
svadhiṣṭhito vīratamena puṃsā |
turaṅgamānām api kalpitānām
eko gajaḥ ṣaṣṭiśatāni hanti ||11||
jale sthale ca drumasaṃkaṭe ca
same ’same sādhu cale ’cale ca |
prākāraharmyāṭṭavidāraṇe ca
jayo dhruvaṃ nāgavatāṃ balānām ||12||
tasmād yato bhūrijalas tu panthā
yato ’nnapānopacayo ’viśaṅkaḥ |
tato hi yāyāj janayan pratāpaṃ
śanaiḥ śanair viśramayan balāni ||13||
abhyunnatānām aṇur apy udāraṃ
paścāt prakopaṃ janayed arīṇām |
taṃ cāpramattaḥ prasamīkṣya yāyān
na nāśayed dṛṣṭam adṛṣṭahetoḥ ||14||
paścāt prakopaḥ purataḥ phalaṃ ca
paścāt prakopas tu tayor garīyān |
randhraṃ hi tadviprakṛtā mahattvaṃ
nayanti tasmāt pravidhāya yāyāt ||15||
puraś ca paścāc ca yadā samarthas
tadābhiyāyān mahate phalāya |
puraḥ prasarpann aviśuddhapṛṣṭhaḥ
prāpnoti tīvraṃ khalu pārṣṇibhedam ||16||
yāsyan puro rakṣyam anekavargam
anekamukhyaṃ ca balaṃ vidadhyāt |
anekamukhyasya hi naikamatyam
anekamukhyaṃ dviṣatām abhedyam ||17||
avaśyayātavyatayodyataḥ san
paścāt prakopāhitayānaśaṅkaḥ |
senāpatiṃ vāpy atha vā kumāraṃ
balaikadeśena puro nidadhyāt ||18||
ābhyantarād bāhyakṛtāc ca kopād
ābhyantaras tv eva tayor garīyān |
ādāya gacched abahiḥpracārān
bāhyāṃś ca kṛtvā vihitārthakalyān ||19||
purohitāmātyakumārakulyāḥ
senābhigoptāra ime pradhānāḥ |
eṣāṃ hi santo ’nyatamaprakopam
antaḥprakopaṃ samupādiśanti ||20||
rāṣṭrāntapālāṭavikābhisīmnāṃ
bāhyaprakopo ’nyatamaprakopaḥ |
utpadyamāno nipuṇapracārais
taṃ mantribhiḥ samyag upādadīta ||21||
sāmādibhiḥ saṃśamayet prakopaṃ
parasparāvagrahabhedanaiś ca |
tathā ca dhīraḥ śamayed vikāraṃ
yathā bhajeran na parān prataptāḥ ||22||
manuṣyayugyāpacayaḥ kṣayo hi
hiraṇyadhānyāpacayo vyayas tu |
tasmād imāṃ naiva vidagdhabuddhiḥ
kṣayavyayāyāsakarīm upeyāt ||23||
avaśyaniṣpattimahāphalāḍhyām
adīrghasūtrāṃ pariṇāmakalyām |
kāmaṃ vyayāyāsakarīm upeyān
na tv eva jātu kṣayadoṣayuktām ||24||
vastuṣv aśakyeṣu samudyamaś ca
śakyeṣu cākālasamudyamaś ca |
śakyeṣu mohād asamudyamaś ca
tridhaiva kāryavyasanaṃ vadanti ||25||
kāmo ’kṣamādakṣiṇatānukampā
hrī sādhvasaṃ krauryam anāryatā ca |
dambho ’bhimāno ’py atidhārmikatvaṃ
dainyaṃ svayūthasya vimānanaṃ ca ||26||
droho bhayaṃ śaśvad upekṣaṇaṃ ca
śītoṣṇavarṣāsv asahiṣṇutā ca |
etāni kāle samupāhitāni
kurvanty avaśyaṃ khalu siddhivighnam ||27||
nijo ’tha maitraś ca samāśritaś ca
sambandhajaḥ kāryasamudbhavaś ca |
bhṛtyo gṛhīto vividhopacāraiḥ
pakṣaṃ budhāḥ saptavidhaṃ vadanti ||28||
sadānusṛtyā guṇakīrtanena
nindāsahatvena ca randhraguptyā |
tadarthaśaucodyamasaṅkathābhiḥ
pakṣo ’nurāgīti sa veditavyaḥ ||29||
kulīnam āryaṃ śrutavad vinītaṃ
mānonnataṃ sabhyam ahāryabuddhim |
kṛtajñatorjāmatisattvayuktaṃ
sadvṛttapakṣaṃ khalu taṃ ca vidyāt ||30||
udyogamedhādhṛtisattvasatya-
tyāgānurāgasthitigauravāṇi |
jitendriyatvaṃ prasahiṣṇutā hrīḥ
prāgalbhyam ity ātmaguṇapravekaḥ ||31||
mantrasya śaktiṃ sunayapracāraṃ
svakośadaṇḍau prabhuśaktim āhuḥ |
utsāhaśaktiṃ balavadviceṣṭāṃ
triśaktiyukto bhavatīha jetā ||32||
śaighryaṃ sudākṣyaṃ vyasaneṣv adainyam
utsāhasampatsv atidhīratā ca |
autpādikī śāstrasamudbhavā ca
sāṃsargikī dhīḥ pariṇāminī ca ||33||
utsāhasattvādhyavasāyaceṣṭā
dārḍhyaṃ ca karmasv iha pauruṣaṃ ca |
arogatā karmaphalopapattir
daivānukūlyaṃ ca nirādhitā ca ||34||
pakṣādinānena gṛhītakośaḥ
pakṣādihīnaṃ ripum abhyupeyāt |
iti prasarpan niyataṃ samudra-
prakṣālitāṃ tāṃ labhate dharitrīm ||35||
kālo gajānāṃ sajalābhrajālo
yātuṃ tadanyaś ca turaṅgamāṇām |
nātyarthavarṣoṣṇatuṣārayuktaḥ
sampannasasyas tv iti kālasaṃpat ||36||

kāko ’py ulūkān rajanīvyapāye |
iti sma kālaṃ samudīkṣya yāyāt

śvānaṃ ca nakraḥ salilābhyupetaṃ |
vyāyacchamāno dhruvam abhyupaiti

tathā jalāḍhyaṃ samahīdharaṃ ca |
nāvādbhir vṛtaṃ pattībalānuyātair
yathābalaṃ ca prasamīkṣya miśram ||39 ||

grīṣme tv anupodakakakṣadurgam |

gachen narendro vijayāya deśam ||40||

yuktaṃ ca samyag yavasendhanena |

sukhaṃ prayāṇai ripum abhyupeyāt ||41||

viśvāsitākrāntajanaṃ viśuddham |

ghaṇaṭāsvanāpūritakarṇarandhraḥ |

śrīmadvibhūṣojjvalitaḥ prahṛṣṭaḥ |

priyaṃ vaded vṛttyadhikaṃ ca dadyāt |

sannaddhasainyānugataḥ prakurvan |

pravīrayodhāntaritena yāyāt ||51||

etair viyukto bhavati kṣitīndro
janair anetraiś ca samānadharmā ||52||
vilobhayan kiṃcid api prayacchan
kurvīta mitraṃ dviṣato ’ntapālam |
rāṣṭrād abhīkṣṇaṃ dviṣataḥ prapaṇyaiḥ
paṇyaṃ hitaṃ nāḍikayādadīta ||53||
upakramaṃ vāñchitam āśu kuryād
dūtopayānāt kriyamāṇasaṃdhiḥ |
sa ced visaṃdhiḥ sa tu tatra caikaḥ
kṛto bhavaty ātmasamucchrayaś ca||54||

āyāti yas tasya gatiṃ prapaśyet || 56||

prāg eva kāryo nipuṇaṃ vicāraḥ |
doṣṇāṃ balān mantrabalaṃ garīyaḥ

phalāya karmodyamam āsthitaḥ param |
akālahīnaṃ nayavit samācaret

prabhāvitānāṃ śrutaśauryaśālināṃ
yathāvad ālokitamārgacāriṇām |

paramabhiyoktumanāḥ samutpatet |

saptadaśaḥ sargaḥ

yātvā vairipurābhyāśaṃ bhūbhāge sādhusammate |
skandhāvāraniveśajñaḥ skandhāvāraṃ niveśayet ||1||

śṛṅagāṭakārdhacandraṃ vā maṇḍalaṃ dīrgham eva vā |

parasainyapracārāṃś ca sattvāḍhyāḥ śīghragāminaḥ |

toraṇābaddhamālyeṣu yantravatsu patākiṣu |

nirgacchet praviśec caiva sarva evopalakṣitaḥ |

vṛthākolāhalāddhāsyād dyūtāt pānāc ca vāritaḥ |

nirvṛkṣakṣupapāṣāṇasthāṇuvalmīkanirdare |

yasmin deśe yathākāmaṃ sainyavyāyāmabhūmayaḥ |
parasya viparītāś ca śruto deśaḥ sa uttamaḥ ||19||

arātisainyavyāyāmasuparyāptamahītalaḥ |

ākrānta iva kenāpi rogānīkair abhidrutaḥ |

utprekṣitabhayatrāso nirghātolkābhidūṣitaḥ |

muhuratyugratādīptaḥ saṃsikto raktavṛṣṭibhiḥ ||26||

akasmān mattamātaṅgaḥ suṃśuṣyaddānaśīkaraḥ |

prahṛṣṭanaranārīkaḥ praśastasvanadundubhiḥ |

kūṭayuddhavikalpaprakaraṇam
viśiṣṭo deśakālābhyāṃ bhinnāriprakṛtir balī |
kuryāt prakāśayuddhaṃ hi kūṭayuddhaṃ viparyaye ||54||
teṣv avaskandakāleṣu paraṃ hanyāt samākulam |
abhūmiṣṭhaṃ sa bhūmiṣṭhas tadbhūmau copajāpataḥ ||55||
prakṛtipragrahākṛṣṭaṃ pāśair vanacarādibhiḥ |
hanyāt pravīrapuruṣair bhaṅgadānāpakarṣaṇaiḥ ||56||
purastād darśanaṃ dattvā tallakṣakṛtaniścayaḥ |
hanyāt paścāt pravīreṇa balenotpīḍya veginā ||57||
paścād vā saṅkulīkṛtya hanyāt sāreṇa pūrvataḥ |
ābhyāṃ pārśvāvaghātau tu vyākhyātau kūṭayodhane ||58||
purastād viṣame deśe paścād dhanyāt tu vegavān |
puraḥ paścāc ca viṣama evam eva tu pārśvayoḥ ||59||
prathamaṃ yodhayitvā ca dūṣyāmitrāṭavībalaiḥ |
śrāntaṃ sannaṃ nirākrandaṃ hanyād aśrāntavāhanaḥ ||60||
dūṣyāmitrabalair vāpi bhaṅgaṃ dattvā prayatnavān |
jitam ityeva viśvastaṃ hanyāt sattvavyapāśrayaḥ ||61||
skandhāvārapuragrāmasasyasārthavrajādiṣu |
vilobhya ca parānīkam apramatto vināśayet ||62||
phalgusainyapraticchannaṃ kṛtvā vā sāravadbalam |
mandayatnaṃ tadvilope hanyād utplutya siṃhavat ||63||
mṛgayāsamprasaktaṃ vā hanyāc chatrum apāśrayam |
atha vā gograhākṛṣṭyā tallakṣaṃ mārgabandhanāt ||64||
avaskandabhayād rātrau prajāgarakṛtaśramam |
divāprasuptaṃ vā hanyān nidrāvyākulasainikam ||65||
ahaḥsannāhataḥ śrāntam aparāhṇe vināśayet |
niśi visrambhasuptaṃ vā tatsauptikavidhānavit ||66||
sapādakośāvaraṇair nāgaiḥ kuryāt tu sauptikam |
kuryād ugrajavopetair narair vā khaḍgapāṇibhiḥ ||67||
pratisūryamahāvātaṃ hanyāt sammilitekṣaṇam |
ityevaṃ kūṭayuddhena hanyāc chatrūn laghūtthitaḥ ||68||
nīhāratimirāṅgāraśvabhrāgnivananimnagāḥ |
vadanti satram ityādi satraṃ chadma prakīrtitam ||69||
sādhvapramatto vyavasāyavartī
yena prakāreṇa paraṃ nihanyāt |
caraiḥ samāveditatatpracāraḥ
śaṅketa tenaiva tato ’pramattaḥ ||70|
suniyatam upahanyāt kūṭayuddhena śatrūn
na hi tirayati dharmaṃ chadmanā śatrughātaḥ |
acakitam avasuptaṃ pāṇḍavānām anīkaṃ
niśi suniśitaśastro droṇasūnur jaghāna ||71||
viṃśaḥ sargaḥ
gajāśvarathapattikarmaprakaraṇam
prayāṇe pūrvayāyitvaṃ vanadurgapraveśanam |
akṛtānāṃ ca mārgāṇāṃ tīrthānāṃ ca pravartanam ||1||
toyāvatārasantārāv ekāṅgavijayas tathā |
abhinnānām anīkānāṃ bhedanaṃ bhinnasaṅgrahaḥ ||2||
bibhīṣikābhighātaś ca prākāradvārabhañjanam |
kośanītir bhayatrāṇaṃ hastikarma pracakṣate ||3||
caturaṅgasya sainyasya niṣedho balarakṣaṇam |
abhinnabhedanaṃ bhinnasandhānaṃ rathakarma tat ||4||
vanadiṅmārgavicayo vīvadhāsārarakṣaṇam |
anuyānāpasaraṇe śīghrakāryopapādanam ||5||
dīnānusaraṇaṃ ghātaḥ koṭīnāṃ jaghanasya ca |
ity aśvakarma patteś ca sarvadā śastradhāraṇam ||6||
śodhanaṃ kūpatīrthānāṃ mārgāṇāṃ śibirasya ca |
yavasādi ca yat kiṃcid vijñeyaṃ viṣṭikarma tat ||7||
jātiḥ sattvaṃ vayaḥ sthānaṃ prāṇitā varṣma vegitā |
tejaḥ śilpam udagratvaṃ sthairyaṃ sādhu vidheyatā ||8||
suvyañjanācārateti pattikuñjaravājinām |
iti lakṣaṇam etena yuktān karmasu yojayet ||9||
patyaśvarathagajabhūmiprakaraṇam
sasthūlasthāṇuvalmīkavṛkṣagulmāpakaṇṭakā |
sāpasārā paddhātīnāṃ bhūr nātiviṣamā matā ||10||
svalpavṛkṣopalā kṣipralaṅghanīyadarā sthirā |
niḥśarkarā vipaṅkā ca sāpasārā ca vājibhūḥ ||11||
niḥsthāṇusikatāpaṅkā nirvalmīkopalā samā |
kedāravratatiśvabhravṛkṣagulmavivarjitā ||12||
nirutkhātā nirdaraṇā khuracaṅkramaṇakṣamā |
sthirā cakrasahā caiva rathabhūmiḥ prakīrtitā ||13||
mardanīyataruśchedyavratatiḥ paṅkavarjitā |
sugamyaśailaviṣamā hastibhūr iti kīrtitā ||14||
nāgamyā nāma bhūr eṣāṃ tena nāgāḥ smṛtā budhaiḥ |
tasmād etā bhuvo ’tyarthaṃ nāgānāṃ samprakīrtitāḥ ||15||
turagādīni bhinnāni pratigṛhṇāti yad balam |
pratigraha iti khyātaḥ sa tu kāryo bharakṣamaḥ ||16||
tena śūnyas tu yo vyūhaḥ sa bhinna iva lakṣyate |
tasmād vidvāñ jayākāṅkakṣī na yudhyetāpratigrahaḥ ||17||
jayārthī nṛpatis tiṣṭhed dūraṃ gatvā pratigrahaḥ |
bhinnaṃ ca pratigṛhṇīyān na hi yudhyeta taṃ vinā ||18||
dānakalpanāprakaraṇam
gajeṣv āropitaḥ sādhuḥ śīghrapātair adhiṣṭhitaḥ |
yatra rāja tatra kośaḥ kośādhīnā hi rājatā ||19||
pratyagre karmaṇi kṛte ślāghamānaḥ kṛtādaraḥ |
yodhebhyas tu tato dadyāt ko hi dātur na yudhyate ||20||
dadyāt prahṛṣṭo niyutaṃ bharāṇāṃ rājaghātine |
tadardhaṃ tatsutavadhe senāpativadhe tathā ||21||
pravīrāṇāṃ tu mukhyasya śataṃ śataguṇaṃ vadhe |
tadardhaṃ kuñjaravadhe pradeyaṃ syandanasya ca ||22||
sāhasro ’śvavadhaḥ śatyaḥ pattimukhyavadhaḥ smṛtaḥ |
śiro viṃśatikaṃ śeṣaṃ bhogadvaiguṇyam eva ca ||23||
yugyaṃ hema ca kupyaṃ ca yo yaj jayati tasya tat |
dadyād vastvanurūpaṃ vā hṛṣṭo yodhān praharṣayet || 24||
vyūhavikalpaprakaraṇam
pañcāratni dhanus tasmin dhanvinaṃ sthāpayed yudhi |
triguṇe ’śvaṃ gajarathau yojyau pañcasu pañcasu ||25||
śamāntaras tu puruṣas turaṅgas triśamāntaraḥ |
kuñjaraḥ syandanaś caiva smṛtau pañcaśamāntarau ||26||
tathā vā khalu yudhyeran pattyaśvarathadantinaḥ |
yathā bhaven na sambādho vyāyāmavinivartane ||27||
asaṅkareṇa yudhyeran saṅkaraḥ saṅkulāvahaḥ |
mahāsaṅkarayuddhe ca saṃśrayeran mahāgajān ||28||
aśvasya pratiyoddhāro bhaveyuḥ puruṣās trayaḥ |
iti kalpyās tu pañcāśvā vidheyāḥ kuñjarasya ca ||29||
pādagopāś ca tāvantaḥ puruṣā daśa pañca ca |
vidhānam iti nāgasya kathitaṃ syandanasya ca ||30||
anīkam iti vijñeyam iti kalpyā nava dvipāḥ |
anīkasya ca randhraṃ ca pañcacāpaṃ pracakṣate ||31||
ityanīkavidhānena sthāpayed vyūhasampadaḥ |
urasyakakṣapakṣāṃś ca tulyān etān pracakṣate ||32||
uraḥ kakṣo ca pakṣau ca madhyaḥ pṛṣṭhaḥ pratigrahaḥ|
koṭī ca vyūhaśāstrajñaiḥ saptāṅgo vyūha iṣyate ||33||
urasyakakṣapakṣais tu vyūho ’yaṃ sapratigrahaḥ |
guror eṣa ca śukrasya kakṣābhyāṃ parivarjitaḥ ||34||
abhedyāḥ kulajā maulā labdhalakṣāḥ prahāriṇaḥ |
senāṅgapatayaḥ kāryā yuddhadṛṣṭapratikriyāḥ ||35||
pravīrapuruṣair ete tiṣṭheyuḥ parivāritāḥ |
abhedenaiva yudhyeran rakṣeyuś ca parasparam ||36||
phalgu sainyasya yat kiṃcin madhye vyūhasya tad bhavet |
yuddhavastu ca yat kiṃcit prāyas tajjaghane bhavet ||37||
yuddhārthaṃ yuddhakuśalaṃ muṇḍānīkaṃ prayojayet |
yuddhaṃ hi nāyakaprāṇaṃ hanyeta tadanāyakam ||38||
vyūho ’nupṛṣṭham acalaḥ pattyaśvarathadantibhiḥ |
tathāpratihato jñeyo hastyaśvarathapattibhiḥ ||39||
urasi sthāpayen nāgān pracaṇḍān kakṣayo rathān |
hayāṃś ca pakṣayor vyūho madhyabhedī prakīrtitaḥ ||40||
madhyadeśe hayānīkaṃ rathānīkaṃ tu kakṣayoḥ |
pakṣayoś ca gajānīkaṃ vyūho ’ntabhid ayaṃ smṛtaḥ ||41||
rathasthāne hayān dadyāt padātīṃś ca hayāśraye |
rathābhāve tu sarvatra nāgān eva prakalpayet ||42||
vibhajya prakṣiped vyūhe rathapattyaśvakuñjarān |
yadi syād daṇḍabāhulyaṃ sa cāvāpaḥ prakīrtitaḥ ||43||
maṇḍalo ’saṃhato bhogo daṇḍaś ceti manīṣibhiḥ |
catvāraḥ prakṛtivyūhā bhedās tv eṣāṃ prakīrtitāḥ ||44||
tiryagvṛttis tu daṇḍaḥ syād bhogo ’nvāvṛttir eva ca |
maṇḍalaḥ sarvatovṛttiḥ pṛthagvṛttir asaṃhataḥ ||45||
pradaro dṛḍhako ’sahyaś cāpas tatkukṣir eva ca |
pratiṣṭhaḥ supratiṣṭhiś ca śyeno vijayasañjayau ||46||
viśālavijayaḥ sūcī sthūṇākarṇaś camūmukhaḥ |
jhaṣāsyo valayaś caiva daṇḍabhedāḥ sudurjayaḥ ||47||
atikrāntaḥ pratikrāntaḥ kakṣābhyāṃ kakṣapakṣataḥ |
atikrāntaś ca pakṣābhyāṃ trayo ’nye tu viparyaye ||48||
pakṣorasyair atikrāntaḥ praviṣṭo ’nyo viparyaye |
sthūṇāpakṣo dhanuḥpakṣo dvisthūṇo daṇḍa ūrdhvagaḥ ||49||
dviguṇāntyas tryabhikrāntapakṣo ’nyo ’sya viparyaye |
dvicaturdaṇḍa ity ete jñeyā lakṣaṇataḥ kramāt ||50||
gomūtrikāhisārī ca śakaṭo makaras tathā |
bhogabhedāḥ samākhyātās tathā paripatantikaḥ ||51||
daṇḍapakṣo yugorasyaḥ śakaṭas tadviparyaye |
makaro vyavakīrṇaś ca śeṣaḥ kuñjaravājibhiḥ ||52||
maṇḍalavyūhabhedau ca sarvatobhadradurjayau |
aṣṭānīko dvitīyas tu prathamaḥ sarvatomukhaḥ ||53||
ardhacandraka uddhāno vajro bhedās tv asaṃhate |
tathā karkaṭaśṛṅgī ca kākapādī ca godhikā ||54||
tricatuḥpañcasainyānāṃ jñeyā ākārabhedataḥ |
iti vyūhāḥ samākhyātā vyūhabhedaprayoktṛbhiḥ ||55||
ete saptadaśa proktā daṇḍavyūhās tu bhedataḥ |
tathā vyūhadvayaṃ caiva maṇḍalasya samāsataḥ ||56||
asaṃhatās tu ṣaḍ vyūhā bhogavyūhās tu pañcadhā |
vyūhajñais te prayojyāḥ syur yuddhakāla upasthit e||57||
prakāśayuddhaprakaraṇam
pakṣādīnām athaikena hatvā śeṣaiḥ parikṣipet |
urasā vā samāhatya koṭibhyāṃ pariveṣṭayet ||58||
parakoṭī samākramya pakṣābhyāṃ sapratigrahaḥ |
koṭibhyāṃ jaghanaṃ hanyād urasā ca prapīḍayet ||59||
yataḥ phalgu yato bhinnaṃ yato dūṣyair adhiṣṭhitam |
tato ripubalaṃ hanyād ātmanaś copabṛṃhayet ||60||
sāraṃ dviguṇasāreṇa phalgu sāreṇa pīḍayet |
saṃhataṃ ca gajānīkaiḥ pracaṇḍair apasārayet ||61||
durjayān kariṇaḥ siṃhavasāsiktair mataṅgajaiḥ |
āhanyāt kariṇīnāṃ vā samūhaiḥ sādhvadhiṣṭhitaiḥ ||62||
salohajālair dṛḍhabandhadantaiḥ
sukalpitair ūrjitapādarakṣaiḥ |
pravīrayodhair madadurnivārair
hanyād gajendrair dviṣatām anīkam ||63||
eko ’pi vāraṇapatir dviṣatām anīkaṃ
yuktaṃ nihanti madasattvaguṇopapannaḥ |
nāgeṣu hi kṣitibhṛtāṃ vijayo nibaddhas
tasmād gajādhikabalo nṛpatiḥ sadā syāt ||64||
