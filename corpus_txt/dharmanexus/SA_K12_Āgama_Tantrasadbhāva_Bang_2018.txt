kaṇṭhasthaṃ hi tathaiveha prāṇamevaṃ nirundhayet | plutakoṭisamāyogātsvapravṛttiḥ suto bhavet || 54
bhrūmadhye binduyuktena prāṇarodhaṃ tu kārayet | suṣuptaṃ jāyate tatra kṣaṇāccaivaṃ prabudhyati || 55
mūrdhni dvāraṃ samāsṛtya niṣkalaṃ dhyānamārabhet | evamabhyasatastasya pratyayastu tadā bhavet ||56
pipīlikaṇṭakāvedhānmūrdhni dvāraṃ vibhidyate |
bhitvā krameṇa sarvāṇi unmanāntaṃ hi yāvataḥ || 57||
pūrvoktalakṣaṇāṃ devi tyaktvā svacchandatāṃ vrajet |
jāyate tatsamatvaṃ hi dehenānena sādhakaḥ ||58||
saṃkrāmetparadeheṣu kṣuttṛṣābhyāṃ na bādhyate |
atītānāgataṃ caiva trailokye yatpravartate || 59 ||
pratyakṣaṃ tadbhavettasya sarvajñatvaṃ prajāyate |
evaṃ cāragatiṃ jñātvā śarīre sarvadehinām || 60 ||
gacchate cāṣṭabhiḥ patraiḥ punarvarṇāntaraṃ vrajet |
kādibhānte tu deveśi vikāraṃ ca prakurvati || 61 ||
satvastho rajasaṃsthaśca tamastho guṇavedakaḥ |
evaṃ paryaṭate devi sthānātsthānāntaraṃ vrajet || 62 ||
padmasūtranibhā nāḍyasteṣu saṃcarate 'nilaḥ |
aṅguṣṭhe caiva aṅgulyastatra nāḍyā vyavasthitāḥ || 63 ||
tarjanyā madhyamānāmā tatra nāḍyā kasaṃjñikā |
aṅguṣṭhe ca khasaṃjñā ca cakāreṇa kanīyasī || 64||
dakṣahastasya suśroṇi trayo nāḍyāstu sūcitā |
vāmāṅguṣṭhe ghakārasthā hakāre madhyamā smṛtāḥ || 65 ||
akāre tu kanīyā tu vāmahastasya suvrate |
pādanāḍyaḥ pravakṣyāmi yathāvadanupūrvaśaḥ || 66||
ṇakāreṇa kanīyā tu nakārasthā tu madhyamāḥ |
pakāre 'ṅguṣṭhakā jñeyā nāḍītraya-m-udāhṛtam || 67 ||
dakṣapādasya suśroṇi sāmprataṃ vāmagā tathā |
kathayāmi samāsena  varānane || 68||
suṣumnā guhyadeśasthā yāvadbrahmavilāntaram |
gatā sā paramā nāḍī jñātavyā deśikena tu || 69 ||
tatrārūḍho masaṃjñastu adhaścordhvaṃ ca gacchati |
tāluke ṣoḍaśāraṃ tu adhovaktravyavasthitaḥ || 70 ||
tatra prāpto nivarteta nāsādvāreṇa dhāvati |
dvādaśāntaṃ tataḥ prāptaḥ puraṃ caiva viśetpunaḥ || 71 ||
bindusthāne tathāṣṭāraṃ jñātavyaṃ satataṃ priye |
śaktisthāne ṣaḍāraṃ tu sūkṣmamārge vyavasthitaḥ || 72 ||
brahmasthāne tu saṃpūjyaṃ devatācakramuttamam |
viṣṇusthā yoginī caiva dūtī rudrapadāśritāḥ || 73 ||
kiṅkārya īśvare devi mātarastu sadāśive |
śaktisthā caiva deveśi viśvādyāḥ kramaśo nyaset || 74 ||
prathamaṃ devatācakraṃ dvitīyaṃ yoginī smṛtam |
tṛtīyaṃ dūtikaṃ devi kiṃkāryastu caturthakam || 75 ||
mātṛcakraṃ smṛtaṃ devi pañcamaṃ parikīrtitam |
viśveśvaryāstu ṣaṣṭhaṃ syāttava devi mayoditam || 76 ||
ṣaṭcakraṃ bhāvayeddevi adhyātme parameśvari|
kaulikīsiddhi samprāpya yogasiddhiṃ pravartate || 77||
evaṃ cāragatiṃ jñātvā sṛṣṭivedhaṃ tu kārayet |
yatra cakre tu vedho vai dīyate parameśvari || 78||
taccakraṃ tasya deveśi pratyakṣeṇa prapaśyati |
tadātmakaṃ tu vijñānaṃ bhavate sādhakasya tu || 79 ||
athavā sṛṣṭivedhaṃ tu kriyate parameśvari |
yakāre vāyavīsṛṣṭi rakāre māraṇātmikā || 80||
lakāre stambhasṛṣṭi syādvakāre varuṇātmikā |
hakāre prāṇasṛṣṭiḥ syātsakāreṇa samanvitā ||81||
amṛtārthe samuddiṣṭā kārayetsādhakottamaḥ |
kakārādimakārāntāḥ pañcaviṃśa-m-udāhṛtāḥ || 82||
etaistu sṛṣṭivedho 'sti jñātvā kāmavibhāgaśaḥ |
mantrasṛṣṭiḥ samuddiṣṭā tattvasṛṣṭistathaiva ca|| 83||
jīvasṛṣṭiḥ samākhyātā varṇasṛṣṭistathā parā |
bhūtasṛṣṭistathā devi yogasṛṣṭistathaiva ca || 84||
yadrūpaṃ bhāvayitvā tu vedhayetparameśvari |
tadātmakaṃ tu vijñānaṃ bhavate sādhakasya tu || 85||
sṛṣṭirūpaṃ bhavetsarvaṃ jñātvavyaṃ sādhakena tu |
dvārāṇi pañca deveśi kathayāmi samāsataḥ || 86||
hastau pādau tathā guhye pañca vedhāḥ prakīrtitāḥ |
etanmārgarahitastu yo vedhaṃ kartumicchati || 87||
hanate muṣṭinākāśaṃ pibate mṛgatṛṣṇikām |
nāsā karṇau ca cakṣurthyāṃ tasminvedho na vidyate || 88||
apadvārāstu te proktāḥ |
tena te niṣphalā vedhā vidveṣoccāṭamāraṇe ||89||
ityevaṃ sarahasyaṃ tu tantre 'smin dvidaśātmake |
sāhasre sāramādāya sukhabodhamanākulam || 90 ||
kathitaṃ saprapañcedaṃ nānāvijñānasaṃkulam |
yena janmasahasraistu bhaktyā cārādhito hyaham || 91 ||
sa prāpnoti tvidaṃ tatra anyo dṛṣṭvā tu muhyati |
etattantraṃ varaṃ devi nākhyātaṃ kasyacitmayā || 92||
tava devi samākhyātaṃ rahasyaṃ prakaṭīkṛtam |
ṛṣidevagaṇairvandyaṃ yoginībhiḥ prapūjitam || 93 ||
asmāttantrātparaṃ nāsti rudraśakti-r-adhiṣṭhitam |
ye yoginīkule jātāstadbhāvagatacetasaḥ || 94||
jñāyante te tvidaṃ śāstraṃ gūḍhārthaṃ jñānasāgaram |
likhitaṃ tiṣṭhate devi yatra tantraṃ mahāyaśe || 95 ||
te 'pi muktiṃ gamiṣyanti piṇḍapātādvarānane |
yoginyaścaiva rakṣanti putravatpālayanti hi || 96 ||
na tasya vartate mārī nāśubhaṃ vidyate kvacit |
yastu pūjayate śāstramamṛtaṃ sāravatpriye || 97||
tena yaṣṭaṃ śrutaṃ hyetaṃ kṛtaṃ samayarakṣaṇam |
svabuddhyā yo durātmā na pīṭhamārgavikalpakaḥ ||98||
na teṣāṃ punarāvṛttigatāste rauravāntikam |
tasmātparaṃparāyā taṃ mantrārthaṃ siddhikaulikam|| 99 ||
śrotavyaṃ gurusāmīpye yāvantaṃ nirmalaṃ bhavet |
gopanīyaṃ prayatnena na śrāvyaṃ tadabhāvitam || 100||
pramādācchrāvayedyastu avijñātvā tvanukramam |
gacchate narakaṃ ghoraṃ gurudrohī durātmanaḥ || 101 ||
nāhaṃ tasya paritrātā majjamāne bhavārṇave |
yoginīgaṇamadhyasthaḥ paśurevātra sādhakaḥ || 102||
samadrohī gurudrohī devadrohī varānane |
yāṃ gatiṃ gacchate devi tāṃ gatiṃ gacchate tu saḥ || 103 ||
tasmātprakaṭaṃ na kartavyaṃ yadīcchecchubhamātmanaḥ |
parīkṣāṃ yatnataḥ kṛtvā anekaistu prakārakaiḥ || 104||
abhiṣekaṃ tato datvā śrāvayeddāpayedatha |
anyathā bhavate doṣa iti śāstrasya niścayaḥ |
etacchāstravaraṃ devi na deyaṃ yasya kasyacit || 105 ||
iti bhairavasrotasi mahātantre vidyāpīṭhe saptakoṭipramāṇe śrītantrasadbhāve ātmācārādhikāro nāmāṣṭāviṃśatimaḥ paṭalaḥ
