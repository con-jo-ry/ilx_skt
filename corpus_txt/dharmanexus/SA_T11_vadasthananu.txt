svabhāvābhedaṃ ca dravyaparyāyayorvarṇayatā dravyasya kāryaṃ yattat paryāyajātasya; yacca paryāyajātasya taddravyasyeti sphuṭataramāveditaṃ bhavati
tathā ca sati dravyaparyāyayoḥ kāryabhedo na sambhavatyeva prāgeva tannibandhano bhedaḥ
ekasyāpyanekakāryakāritvadarśanānna kāryabhedādbhedaḥ sidhyati
na hi malayajamṛgāṅkādayo yugapadbahūnāṃ bahūni vijñānāni janayanto'pi svabhāvanānātvamanubhavanti! eka eva pradīpaḥ karajalālokādikamanekadhā kurvannavalokyata iti na kāryabhedādapi bhedavyavahāro jyāyān
etena hi taanyadapi pratyuktam
yadāha kumārilaḥ-- sarvaṃ hi vastu rūpeṇa bhidyate na parasparam
iti
yadi hi yadeva vastutvaṃ tadeva vedacāṇḍālālāpayoḥ brāhmaṇacāṇḍālayorapi; tathā vedacāṇḍālālāpayoḥ brāhmaṇacāṇḍālayoścaikatvaṃ syāt
atha vastutvenābhede'pi prakārāntareṇa bheda iṣyata iti cet; prakārāntaraṃ tadvato vasturūpādanarthā taraṃ bā syādarthāntaraṃ vā ? anarthāntaratve kathaṃ tadāśrayo bhedaḥ
arthāntaratve svarūpeṇāpi bhedāt bheda eva viśīryate
tasmāt sarva eva bhāvāḥ parasparavyāvṛttamūrtayo na kenacidaṃśena miśrā bhavanti
taduktam--
sarvaṃ hi vastu rūpeṇa bhidyate na parasparam
sarve bhāvāḥ svabhāvena svasvabhāvavyavasthitāḥ
svabhāvaparabhāvābhyāṃ yasmādvyāvṛttibhāginaḥ
iti | ato'nekāntavādo viduṣā na vidhātavyaḥ
digambaramataparikṣā samāptā kṛtiriyaṃ jitāripādānām
pratyabhijñānasaṃjñātaṃ pramāṇaṃ kaiścidipyate ekatvaṃviṣayam...
