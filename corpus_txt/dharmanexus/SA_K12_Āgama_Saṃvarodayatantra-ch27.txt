cu saṃcālayat (?) mantrī amṛtaṃ prāśayet tataḥ ||
svādhidaivatayogena balidānapuraḥsaram |
sarvaḍākinīsamāyogam ācāryaḥ susamāhitaḥ ||
trikoṇamaṇḍalaṃ ramyaṃ trirekhā bāhya veṣṭayet |
dharmodayāmahodyāne nānākusumavirājite ||
tatrālikālibhedena aṣṭau vargān kramāl likhet |
kāma rudras tathā randhra muni bāṇāgni rūpam eva ca ||

ūnapañcāśatkoṣṭhaṃ tu vinyāsya upadeśataḥ |
ākārādi samārabhya hakārākṣaram antataḥ ||
pradakṣiṇākārayogena jñātvā guhyakādhipa |
prathamakoṣṭhasya pañcamaṃ gṛhya tatraiva trayodaśakoṣṭhakenordhva dāpayet | navakoṣṭhatṛtīyenordhvabinduvibhūṣaṇam | saptakoṣṭhakasya pañcamaṃ grāhyaṃ vahnibījenādhaḥ śobhitam | prathamasya caturthena bhūṣitaṃ varakāminī | saptamasya dvitīyākṣaraṃ grāhyam | ekādaśakoṣṭhasya prathamaṃ grāhyaṃ navamakoṣṭhasya pañcamaṃ gṛhya | jakārādhaḥ śobhitam | saptakoṣṭhasya caturthaṃ grāhyam | prathamakoṣṭhasya ekādaśam enaṃ śirabhūṣitam | navakoṣṭhase pañcamaṃ gṛhya prathamasya pañcamenādhaḥ bhūṣitam | pañcamakoṣṭhasya pañcamaṃ gṛhya | saptakoṣṭhasya caturthaṃ grāhyaṃ trayodaśakoṣṭhasya pañcamenādho bhūṣitam | dvir uccāraṃ tu kartavyā | navamakoṣṭhasya tṛtīyenopari bindubhūṣitam | pañcamakoṣṭhasya dvitīyaṃ gṛhya | ekādaśakoṣṭhasya caturthena samanvitam | hṛdmantraṃ sadā japet sarvasiddhiguṇālayam ||
saptamakoṣṭhasya caturakṣaraṃ gṛhya | navamakoṣṭhasya pañcamenādho bheditam | prathamasya caturthena śobhitam | saptamasya saptamasya pārśve bindudvayaṃ bhavati | saptamakoṣṭhasya caturthākṣaraṃ grāhyaṃ dvir uccāraṇaṃ kartavyā | bhūyas tatraivākṣaraṃ gṛhya pañcamasvareṇādhaḥ śobhitam | navamakoṣṭhatṛtīyena bindubhūṣitāṃ dvir uccāraṇaṃ kuryāt | pañcamakoṣṭhasya dvitīyākṣaraṃ grāhyam | ekādaśakoṣṭhasya caturthaṃ gṛhya | praṇavaṃ pūrvapadāyaryat (?) | jāpayan satataṃ nityaṃ saubhāgyaṃ siddhikāraṇam ||
sarvantamakoṣṭhadvitīyākṣaraṃ gṛhya | ekādaśasya prathamākṣaram | vahneḥ putreṇādho bhūṣitam | saptamase dvitīyākṣaraṃ gṛhya | dvādaśasvaśobhitam | navamakoṣṭhasya pañcamākṣaraṃ gṛhya | trayodaśabhūṣitam | saptamasya prathamākṣaraṃ saṃgṛhya paramaṃ padam | pañcamakoṣṭhasya caturthākṣaraṃ saṃgṛhya caturthasvareṇa bhūṣitam | navamakoṣṭhasya caturthākṣaraṃ gṛhya | ekādaśasvarabhūṣitam | saptamasya caturthākṣaraṃ gṛhya pañcamasvareṇādhaḥ śobhitam | navamasya tṛtīyena śobhitaṃ śirasaṃ tathā | dvir uccāraṇaṃ tat kāryaṃ mokṣadaprabhamākṣaram (!) | pañcamasya dvitīyākṣaraṃ gṛhya | ekādaśakoṣṭhaṃ caturthena yojitam | saptamakoṣṭhasya dvitīyākṣaraṃ gṛhya | navamakoṣṭhasya saptākṣareṇādho bhūṣitam | dvitīyasvarabhūṣitam | saptamakoṣṭhasya caturthākṣaraṃ grāhayet | dvitīyasvarabhūṣitam | praveṇa tu prathamaṃ dadyāt | yoginīhṛdayanandanaṃ jāpayed bhāvayet nityaṃ sarvasiddhivarapradā ||
oṃ prathamaṃ dadyāt saptamasya tṛtīyaṃ tu saṃgṛhya pañcasvarabhūṣitam | navamakoṣṭhase dvitīyākṣaraṃ gṛhya tatraiva tṛtīyena śirasi bhūṣitam | pañcamakoṣṭhasya caturakṣaraṃ saṃgṛhya tṛtīyasvareṇa bhūṣitam | saptamakoṣṭhasya tṛtīyākṣaraṃ gṛhya pañcamasvaraśobhitam | navamakoṣṭhasya dvitīyākṣaraṃ grāhayet | tatraiva tṛtīyena śobhitam | saptamakoṣṭhasya caturakṣaraṃ gṛhya pañcasvareṇa yojitam | navamasya tṛtīyākṣareṇa bindubhūṣitam | dvir uccāraṃ tatra kamaḥ (?) || pañcamakoṣṭhasya dvitīyākṣaraṃ saṃgṛhya | ekādaśakoṣṭhasya caturthākṣareṇa yojayet parameśvaram || koṣṭhaikasyākṣaraṃ gṛhya saptamasvareṇa yojitam | saptamakoṣṭhasya samākṣaraṃ saṃgṛhya pañcamakoṣṭhasya caturthena yojitaṃ śubham | dviruccāraṇapadaṃ tataḥ || saptamakoṣṭhasya caturakṣaraṃ gṛhya pancamasvareṇādho yojitam | ardhendubinduvibhūṣitaṃ dviruccāraṇapadaṃ tataḥ | pañcamakoṣṭhasya dvitīyākṣaraṃ gṛhya ekādaśakoṣṭhasya caturthena yojayet paramasaukhyadaḥ | praṇavapūrveṇa saṃsthāpya | ekakoṣṭhasyākṣaraṃ gṛhya saptamasvarayojitam | saptamakoṣṭhasya caturthaṃ saṃgṛhya pañcakoṣṭhasya caturthena yojitam | dvitīyasvareṇa bhūṣitam | tṛtīyakoṣṭhasya dvitīyaṃ gṛhya navamakoṣṭhasya dvitīyaṃ gṛhya navamakoṣṭhasya caturthaṃ gṛhya | navamakoṣṭhasya caturthaṃ gṛhya dviruccāraṇaṃ paraṇapadaṃ (!) | saptamakoṣṭhasya caturthākṣaraṃ grāhya pañcamasvarayojitam | ardhacandrabindubhūṣitam | dvirucāraṇapadaṃ yathā | pañcamakoṣṭhasya dvitīyākṣaraṃ gṛhya ekādaśakoṣṭhasya caturthena yojitaṃ parameśvaram | pūrve praṇavaṃ saṃyojya sādhayet paramākṣaram ||
trayodaśakoṣṭhasya prathamasvaraṃ gṛhya dvitīyasvareṇa yojitam | pañcakoṣṭhasya caturakṣaraṃ gṛhya sthāpayet paramaṃ padam | navamakoṣṭhasya caturakṣaraṃ grāhayet parameśvaram | saptamakoṣṭhasya caturthākṣaraṃ gṛhya trayodaśasvareṇa śirasi bhūṣitaṃ bhavet | saptamasya saptamena pārśve bindudvayaṃ sthāpayet savicakṣaṇaḥ | saptamasya dvitīyakoṣṭhākṣaraṃ gṛhya tṛtīyasvareṇa śirasi bhūṣitam | navamakoṣṭhasyāṣṭamākṣaraṃ gṛhya tatraiva caturthenādho bandhayet | dvitīyasvareṇa yojitaṃ paramākṣram | navamakoṣṭhasya pañcamākṣaraṃ gṛhya tṛtīyasvaraśobhitam | ekādaśakoṣṭhasya prathamaṃ gṛhya sthāpitaṃ sarvasiddhidam | saptamakoṣṭasya caturakṣaraṃ gṛhya pañcamasvarayojitam | ardhendubindubhūṣitam | dvir uccāryaṃ parameśvaram | pañcamasya dvitīyākṣaraṃ gṛhya ekādaśakoṣṭhasya caturthena yojitaṃ paramaṃ padam ||
gurupradeśamāgneramantroddharaṃ jñāyet (!?) |
pustakāt paṭhitai tmakaiḥ (!?) saṃpradāyavivarjitaiḥ |
naiva siddhipadaṃ yāyāt kalpakoṭiśatair api ||
daśame parvaṇi prāpte vajrayoginīpūjite |
puṣpair nānāvidhaiś caiva khādyapānaṃ ca saṃyutam ||
mantro vai yoginīrūpaṃ yoginī mantrarūpiṇī |
tayor bhedo na kartavyo yadicche paramaṃ padam ||
varaṃ prāṇaparityāgo varaṃ prāṇaṃ samāgataḥ |
yadi siddhiṃ parām icched rakṣayet samayaṃ tadā ||
vāmodbhavaṃ jagat sarvaṃ trailokyaṃ sacarācaram |
vāmācāraḥ sadā yogī vāmapādapuraḥ kramet ||
pūjayed vāmahastena vāmatatpaṇarakṣaṇam |
pañcavarṇasamācāram ekavarṇaṃ tu kalpayet |
sarvasaṃkalpanirmuktaḥ sarvadvandvavivarjitaḥ ||
gurur buddho gurur dharmo guruḥ saṅghas tathaiva ca |
gurum ārādhayet tasmād buddhatvapadavāñchayā ||
sarvabuddhasamāyogaḍākinījālasamvaraḥ ||   ||
iti mantroddhāravidhipaṭalaḥ saptaviṃśatimaḥ ||   ||
