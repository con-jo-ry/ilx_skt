Bukkyo-Gaku (Journal of Buddhist Studies)

athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi yathārthatattvabhāvanām |
bhāvanābhavarūpatvam abhāvaṃ niṣprapañcatām || 1 ||
svasaṃvedyamayaṃ karma bhāvanāpy abhāvakam |
ekānekaviyogāsau kṣaṇaad eva prajāyate || 2 ||
svasaṃvedyam idaṃ jñānaṃ vākyārthātītagocaram |
jāyate adbhutākāraṃ sarvajñajñānatanmayam || 3 ||
^31
sarvabhāvasvabhāvasamaḥ | laghuduravagāhaḥ | hetuvarjitaḥ | nijapratibhāsaḥ | prakṛtiprabhāsvaraḥ | vicārāgocaraḥ | nirvivādaḥ | śāntaḥ | niścitaḥ | asaṃcoditaḥ | avācyaḥ | alakṣyaḥ | sarvabhāvasvabhāvasthitaḥ | śūnyākāraḥ | śabdādiguṇavarjitaḥ || 4 ||
idaṃ buddhasukhodayaṃ yeṣu jagad vyavasthitam |
nodghāṭitaṃ mayā kaścid bodhyāsthānam anupamam || 5 ||
apratisamaṃ tattvaṃ deśitaṃ jinaiḥ |
dvayākāratyāgaprakaṭapaṭusaṃvittisubhago |
ghanānandotkīrṇe pravararasasaṃpūrṇāmbaratale
sphuradnānākārair upacitasamastāntaragatāv
idaṃ trailokyaṃ layam iva gataṃ bhāti manasi |
svabhāvaṃ sahajam ity uktaṃ sarvākāraikasaṃvaram || 6 ||
maṇḍalaṃ saiva saṃvittiḥ sarvākāravilakṣaṇā |
ḍākinyo nāḍikāḥ sarvāḥ sarvāyatanamaṇḍalam || 7 ||
sarvāny ekarasāpattau sphuritā herukākṛtiḥ |
nirābhāsam anākāśam aśeṣaṃ anālayam || 8 ||
sarvabhāvasvabhāvatvāt sarvabhāve sadā sthitam |
svarūpaṃ yatra bhāvānāṃ svarūpeṇaiva budhyate || 9 ||
nīrūpaṃkṛtam ānandaḥ svayambhūr udayaty asau |
tathācittatayā cittam ekaviśvāvabodhakam || 10 ||
^32
bhāvābhāvavivekatāvirahito yatra svayaṃ rājate |
sāndrānandamayaḥ prabodhamahimā vyomāntaravyāpakaḥ |
nānākāravisāri nirmalatayādarśasphuradmaṇḍalam |
prāyaḥ sarvasukhālayaḥ sa sahajānandaś caturthākhyayā || 11 ||
% sphuradmaṇḍalam] \emend, suphrad maṇḍalam \ed
nātra prajñā na copāyaḥ samyaktattvāvabodhakaḥ |
yoginyaḥ kalpanāḥ sarvā maṇḍalaṃ bhuvanatrayam || 12 ||
dharmasaṃbhoganirmāṇamahāsukham iti kramaḥ |
mahāsukhasvabhāvena samaṃ cakracatuṣṭayam || 13 ||
niḥsaṅgo vicared yogī sarvabhāveṣu sarvadā |
nirlepaḥ paṅkamadhye 'pi saṃsāre nirvṛtāyate || 14 ||
śubhaguṇasusameto jñānavān vīryayukto |
gurujanam atha bhaktyā vīkṣate buddhatulyam |
adhigatajinadharmaḥ śāsane suprasannaḥ |
sa iha bhavati pātraṃ tasya kuryāt prasādam || 15 ||
tathā rūpādinirbhinnatraidhātukaikasaṃvidām |
sa yoginām avicchinnam ānando 'pi mahābaliḥ || 16 ||
yāvanto bodhisaṃbhārās tāvanto 'traiva nirmalāḥ |
prajñopāyātmako yogī anuttaraphalaṃ yataḥ || 17 ||
iti tattvanirdeśapaṭala ekonatriṃśatitamaḥ ||   ||
