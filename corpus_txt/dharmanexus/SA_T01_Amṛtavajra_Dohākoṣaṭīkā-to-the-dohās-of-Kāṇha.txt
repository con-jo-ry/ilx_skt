jeṃ bujjhia virala sahajakhaṇa kāhiṃ veapurāṇa |
teṃ tuḍia visaaviappa jagu re asesa parimāṇa || 30 ||
jeṃ kia ṇiccala maṇaraaṇa ṇia ghariṇī laï ettha |
aïso so vājira ṇāhu re mayiṃ vutto paramattha || 31 ||
jeṃ bujjhia ityādi | sugamam | jeṃ kia ṇiccaletyādi | yena kṛtaṃ pracaṇḍacaṇḍālīvahniṃ drāvayitvā cālayitum aśakyatvān niścalaṃ manoratnaṃ bodhicittam | kiṃ kṛtvety āha— ṇiaghariṇī laï ityādi | nijagṛhiṇīṃ tām eva divyamudrāṃ tatra vaṃkāramahāsukhasthāne | aïso so vājira ityādi | sa eva vajradharo nāthaḥ | kāyavākcittaprabhuḥ | re sambodhākṣaram |
vutto paramattha iti | ukto mayā kṛṣṇavajreṇa paramārtho 'kṛtrimo 'yam arthaḥ | atra nānyo 'stīty arthaḥ ||
etad eva dṛṣṭāntam āha—
jima loṇa vilijjaï pāṇiehi tima ghariṇī laï citta |
samarasa jāi takkhaṇe jaï puṇu te sama ṇitta || 32 ||
jima loṇa vilijjaï ityādi | yathā lavaṇaṃ vilīyate pānīyena tathā gṛhiṇīṃ jñānarūpiṇīṃ gṛhītvā cittaṃ bodhicittaṃ samarasatvaṃ yāti | tathā ca śrīhevajre—
śukrākāro bhaved bhagavān tatsukhaṃ kāminīsukham || iti |
tadubhe 'pi śukrasukhe ādhārādheyarūpe | sarvaśūnyasvabhāvasukhe | samarasam ekalolībhāvaṃ gacchata iti tatkṣaṇam |
tathā ca śrīādibuddhe—
ādhārādheyasaṃbandho yāvad akṣaratāṃ vrajet |
cittam akṣaratāprāptaṃ nādhārādheyalakṣaṇam || iti |
tathā ca saṃpuṭe—
nāsāpuṭadvirandhreṇāgataḥ sukāla ucyate |
gataś caiva duṣkālaḥ syāt tayor ekaḥ prakīrtitaḥ ||
asahāyo bhaved ekaḥ kālas tv acintyatāṃ gataḥ |
prāṇāyāmavihīnas tu praśvāsāśvāsalakṣaṇaḥ ||
gatyāgativinirmukta ekaḥ samaya ucyate |
na rāgo na virāgaś ca madhyamā nopalabhyate ||
rāgeṇa ca virāgeṇa dvābhyām astam anāhatam |
ghṛte ghṛtaṃ yathā kṣiptaṃ miśrībhūtam anāvilam |
tathā rāgavirāgābhyām ekaḥ samarasakṣaḥ || iti ||
jahi puṇu tāhi ṇitta iti | yadi punas tābhyām anyonyaṃ nityatvam avacchinnam | yadā punar etat syād iti | ayam arthaḥ tathā tathā darśitopāyaiḥ śukrarajorūpayor anyonyam anavacchinnam | nānyatve sati | śukrasvabhāvaś candrasvabhāvaḥ sambhogakāyaḥ | rajaḥsvabhāvaḥ sūryābhāsanirmāṇakāyaś cāstaṃgate | sukhavartirūpagṛhiṇyā saha |
etad bodhicittaṃ ubhayayogābhyāṃ piṇḍībhūyotpattipralayarūpasaṃsāramārgam atikramya samarasībhavati niṣyandarūpaḥ śrīheruko bhavati | tathā ca sampuṭe—
yadā kaṇṭhe mahārāgarūpeṇa candramā sthito bhavati |
sambhogas tu tad ākhyāto buddhānāṃ kāya uttamaḥ ||
nāsāgre sadā cāsau vajrāgre tu yadā sthitaḥ |
astaṃgatas tu sambhogaḥ kāyo 'pi hi tadā bhavet ||
bhagamadhye gataś cāsau sarṣapa iti viśrutaḥ |
sūryarūpasamākhyāto nirmāṇakāya ucyate ||
buddhānāṃ bodhisattvānāṃ spharaṇaṃ tena jāyate |
padmanarteśvaro rājā padmaprabhur iti smṛtaḥ || iti ||
śrīkṛṣṇavajrapādānāṃ dohākoṣasya ṭīkā samāptā ||
kṛtir iyaṃ paṇḍitaamṛtavajrasya |
