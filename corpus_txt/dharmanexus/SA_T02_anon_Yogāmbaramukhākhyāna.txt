śrīyogāmbaramukhākhyāna devatāghorapajikā samāptaṃ ||   ||
prakṛtyā pañca sāvadya prajñaptyāvadyam eva caḥ |
ya kicit kāyakaṃ pāpaṃ ya kicit mānasaṃ tathā |
ya kiñcid vācikaṃ pāpaṃ tat sarva kṣapayiṣyasiḥ ||
deśayikṣāmi te nātha nāthānām agrata sthita |
kṛtāñjalitaya sph((ī))taḥ pranipatya puna punaḥ ||
atyeyam atyeye tena pratigṛgnantu nāyakaḥ |
na bhadrakam idaṃ nātha na kartavya puna mayā ||
mantrahīnaṃ kriyāhīnaṃ bhāvanāṃ ca tathaiva ca |
bhaktahīnaṃ maśuddham vā tat sarva kṣapayiṣyasiḥ ||
pṛṣṭhate na smṛtimārgato vā
pṛṣṭhakathāyogam upāgato vā |
sarvaprakāraṃtā gato hitāya
kurmāmatasra sukhasaṃhitayaḥ ||   ||
