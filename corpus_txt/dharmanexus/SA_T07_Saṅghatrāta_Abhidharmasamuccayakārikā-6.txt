satyatyāgaśamaprajñā nirvāṇānuguṇā hi yāḥ |
adhiṣṭhānāni buddhiś ca yayaitāny adhitiṣṭhati || (6.1 [208])
nirvāṇam apy adhiṣṭhānatrayam uktam ṛtādi tat |
tan muktvā dve adhiṣṭhāne dhīḥ kṛtvā nikhilāni tu || (6.2 [209])
asamāt tena śīlena samāt tena tu kecana |
śrutvāśrutvāthavā tattvaṃ vipaśyanti parīkṣakāḥ || (6.3 [210])
śṛṇvan svādhyāyam abhyasyan deśanāṃ pravicintayan |
samādhīnāṃ nimittāni bhāvayan vaiti satpatham || (6.4 [211])
premagauravaniṣpattiḥ hryapatrāpyasamudbhavā |
yeṣu te guravaḥ sevyā buddhās tacchrāvakāś ca te || (6.5 [212])
gurubhyaḥ śrutaniṣpattiḥ śrutād arthavicāraṇam |
arthāvabodhāt prāmodyaṃ prāmodyāt prītisaṃbhavaḥ || (6.6 [213])
prīteḥ prasrabdhiniṣpattiḥ prasrabdheḥ sukhasaṃbhavaḥ |
sukhāt samādhiprabhavaḥ samādhes tattvadarśanam || (6.7 [214])
tattvadarśanajā nirvid virāgo nirvidudbhavaḥ |
virāgāc ca vimuktiḥ syān nirvāṇaṃ ca vimuktitaḥ || (6.8 [215])
śrutaṃ satyakathā jñeyā taccintā tu vicāraṇaṃ |
avabodhas tadarthasya samyaksantīraṇaṃ tu yat || (6.9 [216])
jātyādi duḥkhaṃ sūtreṣu tṛṣṇā duḥkhasya saṃbhavaḥ |
tṛṣṇākṣayaś ca nirvāṇam aṣṭāṅgo mārga ity api || (6.10 [217])
sadoṣadoṣanirdoṣadoṣaghnāni yathākramaṃ |
duḥkhaprabhavanirvāṇamārgasatyāni kecana || (6.11 [218])
duḥkhatātrayaduṣṭatvān manaāpādayas trayaḥ |
duḥkhās tajjananatvāt tu hetus tṛṣṇaita eva vā || (6.12 [219])
viśuddhaśīlaḥ saṃtuṣṭa indriyeṣu susaṃvṛtaḥ |
viviktavāsābhiratir dhīvīryasmaraṇānvitaḥ || (6.13 [220])
ānāpānasmṛtiḥ ṣoḍhā caturdhā vāśubhā tataḥ |
smṛtyupasthānam ekaikaṃ tridhākāranayena vā || (6.14 [221])
viparyāsavipakṣeṇa kāyaveditacetasām |
dharmāṇāṃ cāvaśiṣṭānāṃ smṛtyupasthānatā matā || (6.15 [222])
dravyato lakṣaṇāc caiva śuddhāśuddhavicāraṇā |
anityahetūparodhaniryāṇādyair anukramaṃ || (6.16 [223])
vā samastāviruddhā ca kṣāntir nāma ca jāyate |
nimittaṃ cāgradharmāś ca prāmodyādikramo yathā || (6.17 [224])
tato duḥkhaṃ tribhir jñānaiḥ śeṣāny evaṃ tribhis tribhiḥ |
paśyaty ādyatṛtīyābhyāṃ hanti kleśān iha dvayoḥ || (6.18 [225])
tatas trayodaśe citte phalam āryo 'dhigacchati |
jugupsamāno nirvetti tataḥ kāmān dvidhāśucīn || (6.19 [226])
dvitīyaṃ bahunirviṇṇaḥ phalam āpnoty anāsravam |
nirviṇṇaḥ sarvaśas tv āryas tṛtīyam adhigacchati || (6.20 [227])
bahirmukhapravṛttānāṃ kleśānāṃ sarvaśaḥ kṣayāt |
etad vairāgyam ity uktaṃ vītarāgas tv ataḥ paraṃ || (6.21 [228])
antarmukhapravṛttānāṃ bandhanānāṃ vimocanāt |
sa vimuktim avāpnoti caturthaṃ cāmalaṃ phalaṃ || (6.22 [229])
pūrvāparādhanirjātabhavasaṃdhinirodhataḥ |
parinirvāti niṣkleśo nirindhana ivānalaḥ || (6.23 [230])
mithyātvaniyatau kecit tathānye 'niyatau sthitāḥ |
pṛthagjanā dṛṣṭapadāḥ samyaktvaniyatau sthitāḥ || (6.24 [231])
ājñāsyāmīndriyasthau dvau traya ājñendriye sthitāḥ |
ājñātāvīndriyasthau tu dvāv evaṃ sapta te punaḥ || (6.25 [232])
śraddhānusārī mṛdvakṣo jñeyo darśanavartmani |
dharmānusārī tīkṣṇākṣas tasminn eva vyavasthitaḥ || (6.26 [233])
śraddhāvimuktadṛkprāptāv etāv eva phale sthitau |
vimokṣālābhinau jñeyau lābhinau kāyasākṣiṇau || (6.27 [234])
prajñāvimuktāv arhantau vimokṣāṇām alābhinau |
bhāgadvayavimuktau tu tāv eva yadi lābhinau || (6.28 [235])
phalasthau srotaāpannasakṛdāgāmināv iha |
triṣv anāgāmyaśaikṣau tu pūrvatyāgānyalābhataḥ || (6.29 [236])
catvāraś ca yathāsaṃkhyaṃ phalānāṃ pratipannakāḥ |
sakṛdāgāmyanāgāmipratipannau dvidhā matau || (6.30 [237])
saptakṛtvaḥparo jñeyas trisaṃyojanasaṃkṣayāt |
kāmānām upalekhāc ca trijanmeha kulaṃkulaḥ || (6.31 [238])
tanutvāc caiva kāmānāṃ sakṛdāgāmy anantarāt |
bhūyasā ca kṣayāt teṣām ekabījo nṛdevayoḥ || (6.32 [239])
pañcasaṃyojanatyāgād anāgāmī sa paṃcadhā |
ūrdhvaṃsrotāḥ sasaṃskārāsaṃskārotpannanirvṛtiḥ || (6.33 [240])
antarā ca tridhā deśakālabhedād ayaṃ smṛtaḥ |
pūrvo 'kaniṣṭhago dhyāne vyavakīrṇo 'paro 'paraḥ || (6.34 [241])
ārūpyagās trayaś cānya iha tv ekasya nirvṛtiḥ |
parikṣīṇākhilakleśabandhano 'rhann asau punaḥ || (6.35 [242])
hānabhaś cetanābhavyas tathā bhavyo 'nurakṣituṃ |
sthitākampyas tathā bhavyaḥ prativeddhum akopyavān || (6.36 [243])
ete samayanirmuktāḥ kṣayajñānasya lābhinaḥ |
ṣaṣṭho 'samayanirmuktaḥ so 'kopyāyāś ca tasya ca || (6.37 [244])
ādyo 'ntaś cāmalaḥ panthā yāvat kleśanibarhaṇam |
tāvac chaikṣaḥ parastāt tu jñeyo 'śaikṣas tridhā ca saḥ || (6.38 [245])
samāpattiprahāṇābhyām indriyaiś cābhidhīyate |
eṣāṃ paraspareṇaiva hīnatulyaviśiṣṭatā || (6.39 [246])
gotrābhyāsaprahāṇebhya indriyāṇāṃ viśiṣṭatā |
ebhyo jayaviśeṣāc ca samāpattiviśiṣṭatā || (6.40 [247])
bahuprahāṇam alpānāṃ prahāṇād dhi viśiṣyate |
sākṣāt prahāṇavaiśeṣyān nāviśiṣṭā vimuktayaḥ || (6.41 [248])
phalamārgasamāpattiprahāṇaparihāṇayaḥ |
prāptāprāptopabhogebhyas tisra eva samāsataḥ || (6.42 [249])
parivṛtte bhave prāptaparihāṇir na vidyate |
āryasya parihīṇasya maraṇaṃ na vibhāṣayā || (6.43 [250])
nirvidvirāgau rūpebhyo vimuktiś cādhigamyate |
samalāmalamārgābhyāṃ vā cārūpyatrayād api || (6.44 [251])
ārūpye nikhilatyāgo dṛggheyakṣāya eva ca |
anāsraveṇa kriyate laukiko 'sau sabandhanaḥ || (6.45 [252])
hetur mārgaprahāṇāya na prahāṇasya śāśvatam |
parijñāyai dvayāyānyo nobhayasmai tathāparaḥ || (6.46 [253])
sākṣātkriyete kāyena prajñayāpi tathā kvacit |
mokṣamārgau na mārgasya sthityaṃśādyā vibandhakāḥ || (6.47 [254])
bhāvyamānā vivardhante mārgā hi balavattayā |
hetuḥ pūrvaḥ parasyāsau phalaṃ tasya na kāraṇaṃ || (6.48 [255])
vartamānā hi bhāvyante mārgāḥ kecid anāgatāḥ |
kleśaghnā vartamānās tu vā vinaśyanta eva ca || (6.49 [256])
tatpūrvaḥ phalasaṃprāptyā parihāṇena tūttaraḥ |
tyajyate 'nāsravo mārgaḥ prahāṇaṃ tūttareṇa vā || (6.50 [257])
pṛthagjano na dṛggheyaprahāṇam adhigacchati |
nārūpyabhāvanāheyanirnāśaṃ nāmalaṃ phalaṃ || (6.51 [258])
śraddhā vīryaṃ smṛtiś caiva samādhir buddhir eva ca |
prītiprasrabdhyupekṣāś ca cchandasaṃkalpasaṃvarāḥ || (6.52 [259])
samyakpradhānarddhipādasmṛtyupasthānasaṃjñakāḥ |
indriyāṇi balāny ete buddhyaṅgāny atha satpathaḥ || (6.53 [260])
śraddhādhimuktiḥ satyeṣu tathā ratnatraye 'pi ca |
satyayāthātmyacintāyāṃ pragraho vīryam ucyate || (6.54 [261])
evam evābhidhātavyāḥ sarve smṛtyādayo guṇāḥ |
kāyaprasrabdhir apy asti śīlaskandhas tu saṃvaraḥ || (6.55 [262])
kuśaleṣu prayogeṣu ye 'nyeṣv evaṃvidhā guṇāḥ |
sarve te bodhipakṣāṇāṃ vijñeyāḥ pratirūpakāḥ || (6.56 [263])
mārgaḥ pradhāno dṛgbhūmau buddhyaṅgāni tataḥ param |
balāny aśaikṣabhūmau tu śeṣās tv anye 'nuvartakāḥ || (6.57 [264])
vicitrakleśatāṃ matvā vineyānāṃ svayaṃbhuvaiḥ |
tattatkleśaprahāṇāya mukhais tais tair udāhṛtāḥ || (6.58 [265])
cittaṃ kuśalamūlāni manaskāraś ca yoniśaḥ |
hetavo bodhipakṣyāṇām anye cāpy amalā guṇāḥ || (6.59 [266])
kuśalā nirmalā śīlaprasrabdhyṛddhau na caitasau |
sālambanau saṃprayuktau vā cānuparivarttinau || (6.60 [267])
sarve sahabhuvo 'mūrtāḥ karma vā na vipākajāḥ |
praheyāś ca dhiyā jñeyāḥ sākṣātkaryyā dvidhā matāḥ || (6.61 [268])
vartante vā yutāḥ kṛtsnās sarve dhyāne vivekaje |
saṃkalparahitās tv ete vijñeyā antarādiṣu || (6.62 [269])
prīter vvinā ca jāyaṃte tṛtīyādiṣv arūpiṣu |
śīlaprasrabdhivarjā vā yeṣu santy amalā guṇāḥ || (6.63 [270])
srotastīrthaṃ tathā mārgo brahmacaryaṃ ca nau rathaḥ |
virecanaṃ ca doṣaghnam añjanaṃ caiva deśitam || (6.64 [271])
audārikanimittaṃ vā yad vā syāc chāntasūcanam |
samyak tad udgrahītavyaṃ viduṣā sevyam eva ca || (6.65 [272])
śamathapragṛhopekṣākālān yogī vibhāvayet |
nimittāni ca teṣāṃ ca nimittāni yathākramam || (6.66 [273])
samāpattāv avasthāne vyutthāne caiva kauśalam |
satkṛtya satataṃ caiva prayogo 'bhipraśāsyate || (6.67 [274])
svayaṃbhūr dṛṣṭatattvo vā vimuktim adhigacchati |
nimittaṃ bhāvayan prājñaḥ samāpattiṃ pṛthagjanaḥ || (6.68 [275])
sukhā dhandhā tathā duḥkhā pratipan mandamedhasaḥ |
kṣiprāpy evaṃ paṭumater akṣamādyā tathāparā || (6.69 [276])
cīvaraiḥ piṇḍapātaiś ca tuṣṭiḥ śayyāsanair api |
āryavaṃśā alobhās te prahāṇārāmatā ratiḥ || (6.70 [277])
mārgasamuccayaḥ ṣaṣṭhaḥ samāptaḥ || ṛbha ||
