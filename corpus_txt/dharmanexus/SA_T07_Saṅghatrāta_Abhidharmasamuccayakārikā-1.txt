[siddham] namo buddhāya ||
sattvadharmagaṇāgratvaṃ buddhiśuddhiśamāptibhiḥ |
prāptān saṃbuddhadharmāryasaṃghān abhyarcya sarvathā || 1
abhidharme prasiddhānāṃ saddharmāṇāṃ samuccayaḥ |
kariṣyate yato 'nyeṣāṃ bhavaty āyatanādayaḥ || 2
dvidhā cakṣuḥśrutighrāṇajihvākāyamanāṃsi ṣaṭ |
ādhyātmikāny āśrayatvāc cetaso 'nyatvam ātmanaḥ || 3
rūpadhvanighreyarasaspṛśyadharmās tu gocarāḥ |
bāhyāḥ sādhāraṇatvāc ca prādhānyād rūpadharmayoḥ || 4
rūpālocanam atrākṣi śrotrādīni yathākramam |
vijñānādhyuṣitāny ebhiḥ sama mantṛ manas tridhā || 5
cakṣuṣo viṣayo rūpaṃ śabdādīni yathendriyam |
manasaḥ sarvam ekasya svakalāpam apāsya vā || 6
rūpaṃ viṃśatidhā śabdas tridhā gandhaś ca ṣaḍ rasāḥ |
saptadhehāṣṭadhā spṛśyaṃ kecid ekādaśātmakam || 7
arūpiṇo manovarjyā dharmāyatanam anyathā |
vānye ca pudgalo 'vācyaḥ sarvam evānyad anyathā || 8
samutthānaṃ yad ākṣeptṛ janako hetur eva ca |
āntantaryaṃ vinaśyad yad avibandhāya kalpate || 9
ālambanam abhipretaṃ yad ārabhya samudbhavaḥ |
ādhipatyaṃ tu janyasya sarve 'nye vighnatāṃ prati || 10
utthānaṃ prāksahotpannaṃ virūpe 'pi pravartakam |
sabhāgahetuḥ prāgjāto bhūnikāyasadṛk sa vā || 11
sarvatragaḥ saṃprayukto vipākas sahabhūs tathā |
sarvatragāḥ sānuvṛttadvividhānāṃ sadhātukāḥ || 12
yatra ye saṃprayuktās te sarve 'nyonyaṃ vipacyate |
yato 'taḥ paktir ity anyas tatphalas sahabhūr iti || 13
jāyamānasya yo yasya sthānadātārthato bhavet |
ānantaryaṃ sa tasyānye cittacittikayor manaḥ || 14
vijñaptisaṃprayuktānām ālambanam idaṃ dvidhā |
kevalaṃ saṃprayuktānām icchanti nikhilaṃ ca tat || 15
niṣyandaḥ sadṛśo hetoḥ vipākaḥ karmaṇaḥ kila |
sattvākhyo 'vyākṛtaḥ kecid balāj jātaṃ tu pauruṣam || 16
avighnabhāvādhigatam ādhipatyaphalaṃ dvidhā |
prahāṇaṃ yo visaṃyogo dhiyā prāptiḥ kvacit phalam || 17
dvayor niṣyanda ekasya vipākaḥ pauruṣaṃ dvayoḥ |
ādhipatyaṃ tu sarveṣāṃ prahāṇaṃ mokṣavartmanaḥ || 18
adhvadvaye dvau triṣv anye hetavaḥ phaladās tv amī |
dvau vartamānau bhagnāś ca śeṣā bhagnā kriyāpare || 19
sarva eva tu gṛhṇanti vartamānāḥ phalaṃ kila |
dvyekādhvakā jāyamānajātayoḥ karma kurvate || dha || 20
anyeṣām api hetūnāṃ phalānāṃ cātra saṃgrahaḥ |
etad evānuśaṃsaś ca guṇaś cādīnavo 'thavā || 21
bhūtabhautikavijñānadharmāṇāṃ hi parasparam |
catustridvyekakaraṇaṃ svajāteś caikadheha saḥ || 22
bhūtāni bhūtasādṛśyāt pṛthivyambvagnimārutāḥ |
dhṛtyādikaṭhinatvādikṛtyāṅkāni sahaiva vā || 23
rūpaśabdamanodharmāḥ pañcadhāvyākṛtāny adaḥ |
sarvāṇi kāme rūpeṣu daśāntye dve arūpiṣu || 24
anāsrave ca caittās tu dharmā anuśayādayaḥ |
vidādyāḥ saṃprayuktāś ca tathānuparivartinaḥ || 25
sahabhūni tu sarvāṇi vā na dve manasī saha |
daśa rūpīṇi vaikaṃ tu triṣu vijñaptisaṃbhavaḥ || 26
sālambanaṃ manaḥ kiṃcit trayāṇāṃ trīṇi karma vā |
dve vipāko 'pare śabdavarjyāni sa tu yatnajaḥ || 27
nava bhāvayitavyāni vā guptiguṇasaṃbhavāt |
abhijñeyāni sarvāṇi sākṣātkāryāṇi ṣaṭ tu vā || 28
daśa dvayoḥ pradeśaś ca prahātavyāni vartmanā |
parijñeyāni sarvāṇi sāsravatvāc ca duḥkhavat || 29
vā rūpaśabdau pañcāptau mano dharmāś ca ṣaḍvidhāḥ |
nābhiprāyo yato 'to 'nyad bhāvanāheyam aṣṭakam || 30
vitarkaś ca vicāraś ca kāme dhyāne vivekaje |
dhyānāntare vicāras tu parastād dvayam apy asat || 31
dvitīyād ā smṛtā prītis tṛtīyād ā sukhodayaḥ |
prāmodyaijāniruddhatvād antare dve kilāpare || 32
aduḥkhāsukhaniṣpattir ā bhavāgrāt prayogataḥ |
saṃjñāsaṃlekhavaiśeṣyāc catasro 'rūpabhūmayaḥ || 33
saṃbhavāt saṃprayogād vā savitarkādideśanā |
saṃbhavād bhūmiṣu jñeyā saṃprayukteṣv ato 'nyathā || 34
abhidharmasamuccaye āyatanasamuccayaḥ prathamaḥ samāptaḥ ||
