rahasye parame ramye sarvātmani sadā sthitaḥ |
sarvaḍākinīmayaḥ sattvo vajraḍākaḥ paraṃ sukham || 1.1 ||
asau svayaṃbhūr bhagavān eka evādhidaivataḥ |
saṃbhavān nādarūpād viniṣkrāntāḥ samayācāragocarāḥ || 1.2 ||
durlabhaṃ triṣu lokeṣu ādimadhyāntasaṃsthitam |
na rāgo na virāgaś ca madhyamā nopalabhyate || 1.3 ||
sarvastrīmāya[= -māyā]mudreyam advayajñānam uttamam |
duścāriṇyo 'pi sidhyante sarvalābhasukhotsavaiḥ |
sarvākāśacarā siddhir ḍākinīti prasidhyati || 1.4 ||
sarvato viśvamudrā tu sarvato viśvasaṃvaraiḥ |
sarvaḍākinīsamāyogavajraḍākaḥ prasidhyati || 1.5 ||
atattvāśayayogānāṃ devatālambanaṃ prati |
pratibimbasamāyogo niṣiktādiṣu jāyate || 1.6 ||
sutattvāśayayogānāṃ devatālambanaṃ prati |
ātmayogasvasamayaḥ sidhyate paramākṣaraḥ || 1.7 ||
bodhicittasamāyogo yogaiśvaryādhipaḥ svayaṃ |
bodhicittam idaṃ vajraṃ sarvabuddhasvabhāvataḥ || 1.8 ||
tasmāt sarvaprayatnena pūjayed viśvarūpiṇam |
jagadbandhusvabhāvena vyāpya viśvaṃ vyavasthitam || 1.9 ||
ātmā vai sarvabuddhatvaṃ sarvasauritvam eva ca |
svādhidaivatayogena tasmād ātmaiva sādhayet || 1.10 ||
anena sarvabuddhatvaṃ sarvasauritvam eva ca |
sarvavajradharatvaṃ ca sidhyatīhaiva janmani || 1.11 ||
ity āha bhagavān vajrī vajrasattvas tathāgataḥ |
sarvaḍākinīsamāyogavajraḍākaḥ paraṃ sukham || 1.12 ||
ākāśalakṣaṇaṃ sarvam ākāśaṃ cāpy alakṣaṇam |
māyopamaṃ ca sarvaṃ vai traidhātukam aśeṣataḥ || 1.13 ||
dṛśyate spṛśyate caiva yathā māyā hi sarvataḥ |
na copalabhyate caiva sarvasya jagataḥ sthitiḥ |
anayā mudrayā yogī bhakṣayed bhuvanatrayam || 1.14 ||
yenaiva mūḍhā badhyante budhāḥ krīḍanti tair iha |
sarvasaṃpūrṇayogena anyatha yāti dīpavat || 1.15 ||
rūpaṃ vairocano buddho vajrasūryas tu vedanā |
padmanarteśvaraḥ saṃjñā saṃskāro vajrarāṭ tathā || 1.16 ||
vijñānaṃ vajrasattvas tu |
sarvarūpasvarūpo 'sau vajraḍākaḥ prasidhyati || 1.17 ||
eka eva mahābhūtaḥ sarvānandapradāyakaḥ |
virajaṃ rajam ity uktaṃ mahāmāyāsvabhāvataḥ || 1.18 ||
pṛthivī pātanī caiva abdhātur māraṇī tathā |
ākarṣaṇy agnidhātuś ca vāyur narteśvarī tathā || 1.19 ||
ākāśadhātur uktas tu mahāmāyā svagāminī |
pañcabhūtasvabhāvena sthitā sā vajranāyikā |
sarvabhūtasamāyogaḍākinī paramojjvalā || 1.20 ||
cakṣuṣī mohavajrākhyaṃ dveṣavajrabhidhā śrutiḥ |
īrṣyāvajras tathā ghrāṇaṃ rāgavajro mukhaṃ smṛtam || 1.21 ||
sparśo mātsaryavajras tu |
sarvāyatanasamāyogavajraḍākaḥ paraṃ sukhaṃ || 1.22 ||
śūnyarūpam idaṃ sarvaṃ śūnyākāreṇa cakṣuṣā |
paśyatāṃ nirvikalpānāṃ satāṃ niḥśaṅkatā bhavet || 1.23 ||
tatra vyomarūpāṇi sarvāṇi vyomarūpeṇa cetasā |
niḥśaṅkabhāvanāyukto nirvikalpaḥ prajāyate |
ity āha bhagavān vajrī vajrasattvas tathāgataḥ || 1.24 ||
sarvatathāgatāmoghāpratihatasamayamahāsukhaṃ nāma samādhikramaṃ kṛtvedaṃ uvāca |
yāvat saṃsāravāsasthā bhavanti varasūrayaḥ |
tāvat sattvārtham atulaṃ śaktāḥ kartum anirvṛtaḥ || 1.25 ||
ata eva hi satsattvā bhavapāśe sadābhayāḥ |
aviṣaṇṇāḥ susattvārthaṃ mahāvīryeṇa kurvate || 1.26 ||
ākāśaniṣṭhavāsas tu bhavet praṇidhiśuddhaye |
sattvārthodyatacittānāṃ sauriṇāṃ bhavaśuddhaye || 1.27 ||
eṣāṃ ca devaputrāṇāṃ nityaṃ rāgapramādinām |
anyeṣāṃ rāginām arthe sarvarāgaviśuddhaye || 1.28 ||
dāsyāmi kalpavisaraṃ mahāsukhasusiddhaye |
anādinidhanaṃ cādya sarvakalpādibhāṣitam || 1.29 ||
bhūtapūrvam atīte 'pi kalpādau hi mahāsukhaḥ |
vajrasattvamahat tatra āsīd eko 'dhidaivataḥ || 1.30 ||
sarvasattvodbhavārthāya sarvalokāgradhātuṣu |
gītavādyādigandharvas tataḥ sarvabhavādhipaḥ || 1.31 ||
surato 'haṃ hi saṃvṛttaḥ sarvasattvasamudbhavaḥ |
tataḥ prasūtās sattvās tu kāmarāgavivarjitāḥ || 1.32 ||
teṣāṃ tu vinayaṃ jñātvā cākṣarodbhavavarjitaḥ |
jagāma brahmatāṃ paśyan nirātmā gaganopamaḥ || 1.33 ||
bhūyaḥ kaścin na sattvārthaṃ na buddhārtham anuttaram |
śaśāka kartum ātmārthaṃ parārthaṃ hi tataḥ param || 1.34 ||
tataḥ sarvajagadartham agamac chūnyatāṃ sadā |
asattvārthatayā sarvaṃ prayātaṃ narakāt [= -ke] punaḥ || 1.35 ||
tatas tv anyeṣu sarveṣu lokadhātuṣu saṃsthitāḥ |
sarvabuddhāḥ samājena codayām āsur māṃ laghu || 1.36 ||
oṃ mahāsukha vajrasattva jaḥ hūṃ vaṃ hoḥ suratas tvam |
saṃcoditamātre tu-m api [-> tu mayi] sarvasukhaprade |
śuddhakāmārthasiddhaṃ hi jaṭaṃ sarvasukhī [-khi] jagat || 1.37 ||
ahaṃ ca kāmarūpāgraḥ sarvabuddhair adhiṣṭhitaḥ |
mayāpi dharmakāyāgraḥ sarvabuddhatvādhipatiḥ || 1.38 ||
sarvabuddhamahārāgaḥ sarvabuddhamahāsukhaḥ |
sarvabuddhamahākāyaḥ sarvabuddhasarasvatiḥ || 1.39 ||
sarvabuddhamahācittaḥ sarvabuddhamahāmahaḥ |
sarvabuddhamahārājā sarvavajradharādhipaḥ |
sarvalokeśvarapatiḥ sarvaratnādhipeśvaraḥ || 1.40 ||
sarvaḍākinīsamāyogāt sarvabuddhasamo bhavet |
sarvabuddhamayatvāc ca vajraḍākaḥ prasidhyati || 1.41 ||
atha
mahāmaṇḍalayogena sarvamaṇḍalam ālikhet |
tasya madhye yathānyāyaṃ vajrasattvaṃ niveśayet || 1.42 ||
sasmitaṃ candravarṇaṃ tu raktajvālākulaṃ tathā |
buddhamālāviracitaṃ sarvābharaṇabhūṣitam || 1.43 ||
caturvaktraṃ caturbhujaṃ trinetraṃ jaṭāmakuṭamaṇḍitam |
caturvidyāsamāyuktaṃ buddhabimbopaśobhitam || 1.44 ||
dakṣiṇam asitaṃ saumyaṃ vāmaṃ raktaṃ tathā param |
paścimaṃ pītasaṃnibham || 1.45 ||
kapālasthaṃ tāṇḍavānvitaṃ tridaśabhayaṃkaram |
vajrakhaḍgadharaṃ caiva ghaṇṭākalpālam eva ca || 1.46 ||
ḍākinīcakrasamakulaṃ likhed vividhatāyinaiḥ [= -yibhiḥ] |
sarvaḍākinīsamāyogavajraḍākaḥ paraṃ sukham |
ity āha bhagavān vajrī vajrasattvas tathāgataḥ || 1.47 ||
mātā bhaginī caiva duhitā bāndhavī tathā |
kāmayed dvandvayogena sarvasiddhiprasādhakaḥ || 1.48 ||
gomāṃsahayamāṃsādi bhakṣayed aviśaṅkitaḥ |
vāmācāraḥ sadā loke vāmatarpaṇabhakṣaṇam || 1.49 ||
vāmodbhavaṃ jagat sarvaṃ sthāvarādyasajaṅgamam |
dūtīṃ tyaktvā tu gacchet sarvakāmavimohitaḥ || 1.50 ||
dūtī raktā sadā mokṣe bhavaty eva na saṃśayaḥ |
avrataghno vratapūrṇaḥ sādhakaḥ kṛtyatatparaḥ || 1.51 ||
samayakulācāro vai ekatra cārubhojakaḥ |
pañcavarṇasamācāra ekavarṇaṃ tu lakṣayet || 1.52 ||
madhuraktaṃ sakarpūraṃ raktacandanayojitam |
kuliśāmbusamāyuktam || 1.53 ||
pañcāmṛtaṃ bhaved etat sarvasiddhiprasādhakam |
anāmāṅguṣṭhavaktrābhyāṃ lehayed yogavit sadā || 1.54 ||
somapānavad āsvādya siddhim āpnoti śāśvatīm |
etat paramasamayaṃ kathitaṃ tava varānane || 1.55 ||
sopāyaṃ sarvakarmāṇi nirviśaṅkaś cared yadā |
nirvikalpena bhāvena vratānām uttamottamam || 1.56 ||
nirvikalpena bhāvena sarvakarmāṇi sarvadā |
ācaren nirviśaṅkena tat teṣām uttamottamam || 1.57 ||
viṣayān sevamānasya nirviśaṅkena cetasā |
keśoṇḍukānubhāvena [= -nubhavena] tat tapo duratikramam || 1.58 ||
yas [= yat] tu sarvāṇi karmāṇi prajñayā viniyojayet |
sā ca śūnyapade yojyā tapo hy etan mahātmanām || 1.59 ||
prajñāsaṃkāśarūpāṇi nirvikalpena cetasā |
niḥśaṅkācārasaṃcāras tapas teṣāṃ mahātmanām || 1.60 ||
prajñopāyaṃ vinānyatra yadi cittaṃ na saṃkramet |
niyataṃ tat samuddiṣṭaṃ mahābodhipradāyakam || 1.61 ||
yoginām abhiyuktānāṃ nirvikalpānugāminām |
teṣāṃ sarvāṇi bhūtāni prajñāpāramitāni ca || 1.62 ||
prajñā darpaṇasaṃkāśaṃ [= -śā] tadākāraṃ ca saṃskṛtam |
prajñāyattāḥ smṛtās teṣāṃ nirvikalpātmacetasām || 1.63 ||
darpaṇapratibimbaṃ ca svapnamāyārthabudbudam |
indrajālasya sādṛśyaṃ yaḥ paśyet sa prabhuḥ smṛtaḥ || 1.64 ||
taḍitagandharvanagaraṃ vipākaṃ caiva saṃskṛtam |
yathākāraṃ prapaśyed yas tasyāyattāḥ prajñā [= -jñāyāṃ] smṛtāḥ || 1.65 ||
tena na kasyacit sthitir asti yatrābhiniveśaḥ syāt || 1.66 ||
evaṃ caiva saṃbhūtair api māyopamair bhāvair viśiṣṭasaṃbhogasaṃjātasukhapariṇāmanayā kiṃcid viśiṣṭaphalaṃ prāpyate, tadā kiṃcin neṣyate | na hy ete sthiracalā bhāvāḥ, kiṃ tv avabhāsamātralakṣaṇāḥ | yady ete viśiṣṭabhāvanābhyāsabalād viśiṣṭasukhasaumanasyādikaṃ kāryam utpādyānuttaraphalāptau hetubhāvaṃ pratipadyante, tadā bhagavati devi na kaścid doṣaḥ || 1.67 ||
tasmāt sarvaprayatnena pūjayet sarvagāminīm |
duṣkarair niyamais tīvrair mūrtiḥ śuṣyati duḥkhitā |
duḥkhād vikṣipyate cittaṃ vikṣepāt siddhir anyathā || 1.68 ||
manomūrtidṛḍhatvāc ca sarvasaukhyair dṛḍhībhavet |
duḥkhaiś calatvam āyāti nirodhaṃ vādhigacchati || 1.69 ||
kāyavākcittasausthityaṃ prāpya bodhim avāpnute |
anyathākālamaraṇaṃ pacyate narake dhruvam || 1.70 ||
ity āha bhagavān vajrī vajrasattvas tathāgataḥ |
sarvaḍākinīsamāyogavajraḍākaḥ paraṃ sukham || 1.71 ||
iti vajraḍākamahātantre paramatattvāvalokaviṣayāvatārajñānapaṭalaḥ prathamaḥ samāptaḥ ||
