(296v/596) nairātmyetyādiyoginīcakram adhikṛtya madhyakrameṇa nirdeśārtham āha | athetyādi | yathā kathitaṃ bhagavatā saṃkṣepatas tathaiva vakṣya iti saṃgītikāraḥ | khadhātuvacanena śūnyatābodhirūpa eko yogo darśitaḥ | tadaṅgāni tv āsanahṛdbījādyupekṣāparyantā bhāvanāḥ saptamoktā draṣṭavyāḥ |
khadhātor madhye sūryamaṇḍalam ity anena repheṇa sūryaṃ purata ityādi sūcitam |
(297r/597) vajrapañjaraṃ bījasaṃgrahārtham | tadanu pūrvapūjitaśūnyībhūtajhaṭitadṛṣṭeṣṭadevatāpariṇatāṃ trikoṇatvena dharmodayāsādharmyāt sarvāvaraṇavarjitatvāc ca śaraccandradhavalāṃ mahatīṃ kāyatrayātmakavajradharasvabhāvatvena trikoṇāṃ dharmodayamudrāṃ vicintya tanmadhya ādhāracakraṃ cintayet | katham ity āha | cakram iti kūṭāgāram |
pūrvaṃ prathamam | yathānyāyaṃ nyāyā(297v/598)natikramataḥ pṛthivyādipariṇatam | tadanantaraṃ devatānāṃ yathodayam iti pañcākārānullaṅghanena cintayed ity anuvartate | imam uddeśaṃ nirdiśati | cakram iti maṇḍalaṃ sarvatra saṃbadhyate | kṣoṇīmaṇḍalaṃ jalamaṇḍalam ityādi |
pūrvam iti prathamam agnivāyumaṇḍalāpekṣayā kṣoṇījalamaṇḍalayor api nirdeśānukramād eva pūrvāparābhāvo veditavyaḥ | yathānyāyam iti vāyumaṇḍalopary agnis tadupari jalaṃ tadupari pṛthvīmaṇḍalaṃ bhāvyam ity eṣa nyāyaḥ | tathaiva (298r/599) bhājanalokasya maṇḍalānām avasthānāt | devatānām ity api sarvatra saṃbandhanīyam |
devatānāṃ tattadviśuddhirūpāṇāṃ locanādīnām imāni maṇḍalāni tadrūpāṇi draṣṭavyānīti yāvat | bījāni tu vāyvādīnāṃ yaṁ-raṁ-vaṁ-laṁ-kārāḥ |
bhāvakaś ceti śūnyatākāruṇābhinnabodhicittarūpo yathodayam udayānatikrameṇa nirmāteva pṛthivyādimaṇḍalānāṃ draṣṭavyaḥ | dharmodayodbhavam iti | dharmodayāyām udbhava utpādo 'syeti | cakraṃ kūṭāgāraṃ caturmahābhūtasaṃhāra(298v/600)jam iti sāmarthyāt | dvipuṭaṃ vajrāvalīparicchinnapuṭadvayopetatvāt | śuddhaṃ svacchatvāt | nirāmayaṃ nirdoṣatvāt |
kiñjalkena bhaved ekam iti | kiñjalkopalakṣitam aṣṭadalakamalaṃ garbhapuṭam | trikāyadharavajriṇam iti kāyatrayātmakavajradhare trikoṇadharmodayārūpe dharmodayāyāṃ sthitakuṭāgārodare madhyapadmād bāhyaṃ dvitīyaṃ bahiḥpuṭam ity arthaḥ | caturasratvādayaś cāsya guṇā (299r/601) vakṣyante | āsanāni candramaṇḍalāni |
tasyeti śavasya sacandrasya upari hṛdi candraḥ | mārtaṇḍam ākrāntam iti | ākrāmati smety ākrāntaḥ sūryasthaṃ candrasthabījopari paścād bhāvayed ity arthaḥ | yathoktaṃ prāk | candrāliḥ kāli mārtaṇḍa bījaṃ madhyagataṃ bhaved iti | dvayor melā mahat sukham iti | vijñānādhiṣṭhiteti śeṣaḥ | tathaiva janmādāv anubhūtatvāt |
iha ca tathaiva vijñānarūpa(299v/602)bījādhiṣṭhitaś candrasūryasamāgamo 'yaṃ bhāvyate |
candrasūryayor bījam āha | sthitetyādi | ālikālipariṇatau tau | gauryādīnām apy evam evotpattir ity āha | candretyādi | candrasūryamilanam eva kāraṇe kāryopacārāt | gauryādyā ity uktam | vajrādyā iti vaktavye | gauryādyeti yad uktaṃ tatkramotpattinirāsārtham | sarvāsām ekadaivotpādāt |
nanu pañcajñānamayapañcākārābhisaṃbodhād buddhatvaṃ na puna(300r/603)ś candrādibhāvanayety āha | ādarśetyādi | ādṛśyante bimbāni pratisvarūpeṇāsminn ity ādarśaḥ | svacchaṃ darpaṇādi | evaṃ jñānam apy atisvaccham ādarśajñānam ucyate | yasmin jagad darpaṇa iva pratibhāti tad vidyate | tādātmyenāsminn ity ādarśajñānavāṃś candraḥ |
candrabhāvanaivādarśabhāvanety arthaḥ | candrarūpam eva mano 'tisvaccham ādarśaṃ sarvasya jagata ity abhisandhiḥ | samatājñānaṃ yena (300v/604) tathā pratibimbitasya jagataḥ samatāṃ pratipadyati | tadātmakaḥ samatājñānasaptasaptikaḥ sūryaḥ saptasaptayo 'śvā asyeti kṛtvā | sūryabhāvanaiva samatājñānabhāvanety arthaḥ |
candrasūryasamāgame mahāsukhamayasya manasaḥ sarvadharmāṇāṃ pratibimbopamatvenāpi bhedānīkṣaṇāt | pratyavekṣaṇājñānaṃ yena tathā samāhita eva sarvasattvānāṃ cittacaritāni pratyavekṣate tad iha bījaiś cihnaṃ svadevasya pratyavekṣaṇam ucyate |
bījotpannatvena (301r/605) bījādhiṣṭhitatvena ca bījair lakṣitaṃ svadevatācihnaṃ pratyavekṣaṇājñānam ity arthaḥ | tad dhi jagadarthanirūpaṇā paramāvirbhūtam ity abhisandhiḥ |
bījair iti bahuvacanaṃ devīnāṃ bījanānātvāt | cihnam ity ekavacanaṃ cihnasya nīlavajrasyaikatvāt pañcadaśānām | kṛtyānuṣṭhānaṃ yena tathā pratyavekṣitasya jagato 'parimitair nirmāṇaiḥ kṛtyam anutiṣṭhati |
tad iha sarvair ekam anuṣṭhānam ucyate (301v/606) sarvair iti candrasūryabījacihannirmāṇair jagatāṃ ca sahaikyaṃ sarvathā sarvaprapañcopaśamāt samarasībhāvaḥ | ayam atraikakṣaṇābhisaṃbodha ekasminn eva kṣaṇe sarvasya jagataḥ sarvaprapañcarahitasya tathābhisaṃbodhāt | bimbaniṣpattiḥ śuddhita iti |
tadanantaraṃ devatākārapariniṣpattiḥ suviśuddhadharmadhātujñānam ity arthaḥ | tasyā eva suviśuddhadharmadhātujñānarūpatvāt | ākārāṃ pañcajñānamayāṃ (302r/607) vidhānaiḥ kathitaiḥ candrādibhiḥ | ādikarmikaḥ tadadhimokṣataḥ | kiṃcitpariniṣpannas tu kathitair eva vidhānais tadarthakiṃcitsākṣātkāraḥ |
iha hetoś caturjñānamayatve 'pi mahāsukham ādhimokṣikam asty eva | ata evāsau hetur vajradhara ucyate | pañcākārābhisaṃbodhāv api hi mahāsukham ādhimokṣikam | ata eva devīsaṃpuṭitatvena mahāsukhābhisaṃbodhipariniṣpattā eva vajradharotpattir iti | saiva vastutaḥ pañcākārābhisaṃbodhiḥ | saiva ceha (302v/608) nairātmyāherukamaṇḍale darśitā |
iha tu nairātmyāmaṇḍale mahāsukham upadeśagamyaṃ na cāpy evaṃ dhag ity āmukhībhavati | ata eva madhyo 'yaṃ kramaḥ | prajñopāyasaṃpuṭatve tu parisphuṭaṃ tad ity adhimātro 'sāv upadiśyate | ata eva ca tantrāntareṣu candrādikrameṇa pañcākārābhisaṃbodhāya vuktāyām api na mahāsukharūpatopadiśyate |
evaṃvidheṣv eva tu yogayoginītantreṣu mahāsukhamayī vajradharabhūmiḥ (303r/609) parisphuṭā | tadanu sārato 'nyatrāpi pratīyate na tūpadiśyate sahajasekam antareṇa | bhagavadājñayaiva | sahajasekas tv evaṃvidheṣv eva tantreṣu |
hetuvajradharam antareṇa na phalavajradharotpattir ity āha | ālītyādi | viṣṭara āsanam | candrasūryamadhyasthacihnāntargatabījarūpo 'tra hetuvajradharaḥ | akṣarād udbhavataḥ piṇḍasya vajrasattvadehasya | hūm iti padmacālana(303v/610)kāloccāryamāṇaṃ pītaphaṭkāraś ca vajrāgrarandhrayor bodhicittadhāraṇāya bhāvyamāno neṣyate 'yogātreti śeṣaḥ |
sattvabimbasamudbhūtam iti | anantaroktahetuvajradharajātam | maṇḍaleśam iti nairātmyām | etad uktam | catvāri jñānāni hetubhūtāni pañcaphalam iti | tat kiṃ phalavajradharo na pañcajñānamayaḥ | naivaṃ śuddhiśuddhānām eṣāṃ tadrūpeṇotpatteḥ | indriyādyudayakrameṇa spharaṇasaṃharaṇakrameṇa (304r/611) tāny eva hi suviśuddhāni dṛśyante |
pūrvavad iti | pūjāpāpadeśanādyartham ānītanairātmyāvat | bhagavatā hi vaktracihnābharaṇādikaṃ pratipādito vajragarbhaḥ pūrvaṃ paścāc ca saṅgītaṃ tu punaruktabhayāt paścād eva bhagavadājñayaiva | bhagavateva guruṇā pūrvaṃ pratipādayitavyam ity abhisandhinā |
candrakānto maṇir nirlanthanaḥ pūrṇacandropamas tatprabheva prabhā yasya (304v/612) candraprabham ity arthaḥ | upalakṣaṇaṃ caitad āsanasyāpi | raudratve 'pi devīcandrāsanacandraprabhety arthaḥ | evaṃ sarve ceti tadāparāś caturdaśa yoginyaḥ | prajñopāyasvabhāvata iti pratyavekṣaṇākṛtyānuṣṭhānajñānayor api tanmayatvāt |
asyaiva nirdeśaḥ prajñālītyādi | varṇabheda iti | āṁ iṁ īṁ uṁ aṁ ḷṁ eṁ aiṁ oṁ ūṁ ṛṁ ṝṁ ḹṁ aiṁ aṁ iti bījabhedo yathākramaṃ vajrādīnām | pañcaskandhasvabhāveneti | (305r/613) tattadarthaviśuddhisvabhāvā bhāvanīyā yoginyo na tv enam eveti darśayati | gauryādayo vajrā diksthā | pukkasyādaya īśānādikoṇasthā dakṣiṇāvartataḥ |
adhāvati rakṣatīti adhavatī | ekam ūrdhvavatī ke punas te ity āha | khecarī bhūcarīti | bhūcarī bhavasvabhāvena sthitādhogatitvāt | ata eva sādhomukhī | khecarī nirvāṇasvabhāvena sthitā (305v/614) ūrdhvagatitvāt | ata eva sā ūrdhvamukhī | pārśvavartinīti | nairātmyāyā adhaḥ paścimapārśve bhūcarī | ūrdhvaṃ pūrvapārśve khecarī |
viśvavarṇā iti bāhyapuṭasthā aṣṭau ṣoḍaśabhujamaṇḍalavat | pītādivarṇāḥ | mahāraudrā ityādi viśeṣaṇāni sarvāsām | viśeṣas tu mūla eva | cakryādayaḥ śiraḥkarṇakaṇṭhakarakaṭiṣu | etādṛśā iti nīlavarṇasāmyaṃ tyaktvā | kīdṛśī nairātmyety āha | (306r/615) yogapātrīti raktapūrṇakapālam | jvaladdīpteti | kāyena jvalantī cetasā dīptā | nīlavarṇeti |

catasro (306v/616) 'py etāḥ kartrīkapālabhṛdbhujadvayāḥ | nāmānurūpaikavaktrā | hasitākrodhaśṛṅgāreti viśeṣaṇaṃ trayoviṃśater api devīnām | tatpūjāsvarūpam iti | īśānādikoṇeṣu vaṃśā vīṇā mukundā murajā devyaḥ pūrvavad veditavyāḥ | etena śūnyatādiyogatrayam uktam |
tadanantarabhāvanīyaṃ tu caturthaṃ yogam āha | trītyādi | tritattvasvarūpāṇi oṁ-āḥ-hūṁ-akṣarāṇi | taiḥ śiraḥkaṇṭhahṛt(307r/617)su yuktam ātmānam | madhyamam iti | madhyāsane sthitvā mā svaparārthasaṃpattir asyeti kṛtvā | ayam atrābhisamayakramaḥ |
