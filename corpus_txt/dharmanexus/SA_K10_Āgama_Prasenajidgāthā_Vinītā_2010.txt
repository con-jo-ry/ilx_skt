uṣṇīṣavālavyajanātapatram
apāsya khaḍgān maṇipāduke ca |
kṛtāñjaliḥ samyag abhipraṇamya
prasenajit pṛcchati lokanātham || [1]
hitāśayānāṃ karuṇātmakānāṃ
tathāgatānāṃ parinirvṛtānām |
vidhāya pūjāṃ katham agrabuddheḥ
% vel agrabuddhe
sukhaṃ labhante 'nyabhaveṣu martyāḥ || [2]
pūrvāparānte bhagavān nṛsiṃhaḥ
sarveṣu dharmeṣv aparokṣacakṣuḥ |
ānandabhadrasya tataḥ samakṣaṃ
tathāgataḥ praśnam idaṃ jagāda || [3]
rājañ śṛṇu prārthitam artham
adya yathā jinānāṃ praṇidhāya pūjām |
idaṃ vicitraṃ vipulaṃ labhante
vipākam anyeṣu bhaveṣu martyāḥ || [4]
% iṣṭaṃ vicitraṃ, surely
saurūpyasaubhāgyaguṇopapannā
guptendriyā bhāskaradīptayas te |
bhavanti loke nayanābhirāmā
ye kārayantīha jinasya bimbaṃ || [5]
nirmalaṃ bhavati tasya śarīraṃ
sarvarogabhayaśokavimuktam |
padmagarbhasukumāram udāraṃ
yaḥ karoti pratimāṃ sugatasya || [6]
na yāti dāsyaṃ na daridrabhāvaṃ
na preṣyatāṃ nāpi ca hīnajanma |
na cāpi vaikalyam ihendriyāṇāṃ
yo lokanāthapratimāṃ karoti || [7]
mālyair udārair atha puṣpavarṣair
bimbaṃ samanvarcya muner manuṣyāḥ |
bhavanti te devamanuṣyaloke
svalaṃkṛtāś citramanojñaghoṣāḥ || [8]
nirnaṣṭaśokā dṛḍhasarvagātrāḥ
pradhānabhogā bahuratnakośāḥ |
bhavanti martyā jitaśatrusaṃghāḥ
stūpaṃ munīndrasya hi kārayitvā || [9]
dharmānudharmaṃ ca caritram agraṃ
śīlaṃ śrutaṃ kṣāntim athāpi loke |
sarvāṃ hi siddhiṃ ca samāpnuvanti
yaṣṭipradānena jinasya caitye || [10]
nārāyaṇāṅgāḥ sthirasarvagātrā
deveṣu martyeṣv api copapannāḥ |
bhavanti satkāraguṇair upetāś
chattrāvalīṃ stūpavareṣu datvā || [11]
śokāgnirogojjhitasarvadehā
bhavanti nityaṃ sukhino dhanāḍhyāḥ |
dīrghāyuṣo devamanuṣyaloke
munīndracaityaṃ sudhayopalipya || [12]
ādeyavākyāḥ pṛthukīrtiyuktā
jātismarā brahmaravā manojñāḥ |
bhavanti te citravibhūṣitāṅgā
ghaṇṭāpradānena jinasya caitye || [13]
hārārdhahāraiḥ kaṭakaiḥ saharṣaiḥ
keyūravaryaiś ca vibhūṣitāṅgāḥ |
caranti te lokam imaṃ hi śāstur
badhnanti caityeṣv avalambakāni || [14]
chattrāṇy udārāṇi bahuprakārāṇy
āropya caitye sugatasya hṛṣṭāḥ |
chattropamā devamanuṣyaloke
bhavanti devendranarendrabhūtāḥ || [15]
suvarṇarūpyojjvalapaṭṭabaddhaṃ
stūpaṃ munīndrasya hi kārayitvā |
dīptārciṣāś cārusubaddhadehā
martyā bhavany anyabhavāntareṣu || [16]
suvarṇamālāṃ bahuratnacitrāṃ
bibharti mūrdhnā sa hi puṇyakarmā |
yo 'bhiprasannaḥ sugatasya caitye
mālāvihāraṃ prakaroti martyaḥ || [17]
narendralakṣmīm amarendralakṣmīṃ
prāpyottamām apy anubhūya saukhyām |
vimuktipaṭṭaṃ labhate viśiṣṭaṃ
vibaddhapaṭṭaṃ sugatasya caitye || [18]
gandhārcitāś candanacitragātrā
bhavanti te bhāskaradīptabhāsāḥ |
kurvanti ye gandhavarair vimiśraṃ
tailānulepaṃ sugatasya caitye || [19]
gandhān udārān bahuyuktiyuktān
kālāgaruṃ candanakuṅkumaṃ ca |
dattvā hy udāraṃ sugatasya caitye
bhavanti gandhārcitasarvagātrāḥ || [20]
śṛṇvanti śabdān madhurān manojñān
vaṃśaśvarān gītaravāṃś ca ramyān |
te prāpnuvanti pratisaṃvidaś ca
stuvanti ye dhātuvaraṃ jinasya || [21]
% anne dhātudharaṃ
gambhīrayuktapratibhāsamṛddhāḥ
pañcāṅgasaṃpannamanojñavākyāḥ |
svareṇa lokān abhipūrayanti
vādyapradānena jinasya caitye || [22]
sūkṣmatvacāḥ kāñcanatulyavarṇā
nirmatsarāḥ sarvaguṇair upetāḥ |
ācchādya vastreṇa jinasya caityaṃ
hrīvāsasaḥ puṇyakṛtā bhavanti || [23]
vibuddhanīlotpalapattranetrāḥ
prasannasaumyāyatacāruvaktrāḥ |
bhavanti divyāmalacakṣuśas te
dīpaṃ nivedyānupamasya caitye || [24]
netrābhirāmāḥ susamṛddhakośā
bhavanti te nīlaviśālanetrāḥ |
nārīnarāṇām abhivīkṣaṇīyā
ye dīpamālāṃ pradadanti caitye || [25]
na rāgaraktā na ca doṣaduṣṭā
na mohamūḍhā na kudṛṣṭisaktāḥ |
bhavanti hṛṣṭāḥ sukhinaś ca martyā
nirmālyam uddhṛtya jinasya caitye || [26]
sudarśanīyā nayanābhirāmāḥ
prāsādikāḥ padmapalāśavarṇāḥ |
bhavanti kṛṣṇārajasā vimuktāḥ
munīndracaityaṃ pariśodhayitvā || [27]
suvarṇadaṇḍair maṇitālavṛntais
tān vījayanti pramadāḥ prahṛṣṭāḥ |
% anne prakṛṣṭāḥ
ye grīṣmakāle śucinā jalena
stūpāṅgaṇe saṃśamayanti pāṃśum || [28]
naṣṭavraṇāḥ snigdhaviśuddhagātrāḥ
sukhānvitā devamanuṣyaloke |
bhavanti te ye sugatasya caitye
kurvanti mṛdgomayalepanāni || [29]
siṃhāsanāni pravarāsanāni
martyā labhante gatiṣu bhramantaḥ |
ye śīlavadbhyo yatināṃ gaṇebhyaḥ
śayyāśanāni pratipādayanti || [30]
ye bhojanaṃ pravrajitānurūpaṃ
saṃgheṣūdāraṃ pratipādayanti |
te bhojanaṃ varṇarasādiyuktaṃ
saṃsārakāntāragatā labhante || [31]
ye pānakaṃ pravrajitānurūpaṃ
saṃgheṣūdāraṃ pratipādayanti |
te pānakaṃ varṇarasādiyuktaṃ
saṃsārakāntāragatā labhanti || [32]
tataḥ prahṛṣṭo vikasatprasādaḥ
pradakṣiṇam saṃgham ṛṣiṃ ca kṛtvā |
munīndrapādāv abhivandya caiva
viniryayau jetavanān narendraḥ || [33]
prasenajitparipṛcchāsūtraṃ ṣaṣṭamaṃ samāptam
