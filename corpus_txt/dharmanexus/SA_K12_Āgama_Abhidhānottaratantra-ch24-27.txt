athāparaṃ vakṣye guhyatattvaṃ ca durlabhaṃ | [ 105]
utpatti sarvamantrāṇāṃ jagatsthāvarajaṅgamaṃ |
yoginīsarvavīrāṇāṃ buddhānām ālayottamaṃ |
akṣayam avyaṃ<yaṃ> śuddhaṃ ādimadhyāntanirmalaṃ |
ākārajñānaniṣpannaṃ vajrasattvamahāsukhaṃ |
trimukhaṃ ṣaḍbhujaṃ śāntaṃ trinetraṃ karuṇārasaṃ
mahāmudrāsamāpannaṃ śuddhaṃ prakṛtinirmalaṃ |
jaṭārdhacandram āsīnaṃ viśvavajrāṅkitaṃ śiraḥ |
kapālamālāmakuṭaṃ pañcabuddhair alaṃkṛtaṃ |
sattvaparyaṅkam āsīnā ṣaṭmudrādehabhūṣitaṃ [|]
vajraghaṇṭāsamāpannāṃ asi aṅkuśapāṇināṃ [|]
ratnapāśakaraṃ divyaṃ sarvasiddhipradāyakaṃ |
sitanīlaraktavadanaṃ trimokṣālayaṃ paraṃ |
jñānasattvahṛdayaṃ tricakraṃ bhāvanottamaṃ |
hūṃkāra cittacakraṃ tu nīlaṃ tadbhujāyudhaḥ |
kākāro vākpathaṃ cakraṃ rakta tadbhujāyudhaṃ |
oṃkāre kāyacakraṃ tu sitaṃ tadbhujāyudhaṃ |
vīrayoginīsaṃyuktaṃ kāyavākcittanirmalaṃ |
ikāre tu mahāmudrā nābhimadhye vicintayet [ 105]
īkāraṇa bhagapadmaṃ tu bodhicandrasunirmalaṃ |
ukāraṃ hṛdaye dhyātvā ekāraṃ śirasi nyaset |
[...]
aṃkārai kavacaṃ kṛtvā aḥkāra āmugrajaṃ
ṛṃkāre locanā devī ṝkāre samayatārayā
ḷkāre paṇḍarā devī ḹkāre ratnasambhūtā
ratnolkā jñānadīpakā |
sitā palāśavarṇā ca raktā jāmbūnadā tathā |
trimukhā ṣaḍbhujās caiva trinetrā suratavihvalāḥ |
digambaradharāḥ sarvāḥ svābhoṣāyasamanvitā |
cakraṃ ca viśvavajraṃ ca ratnapadma tu ratnajāṃ |
ghaṇṭā saha samāpannāṃ sarvakāmapradāyikāṃ |
asibāṇākuśadharāṃ dhanurākarṣaṇāṃ parāṃ [|]
sarvālaṅkārasampūrṇāṃ kapālamakuṭotkaṭāṃ |
savva(!)paryaṅkam āsīnāṃ viśvapadmendrumadhyagāṃ |
etā dvāreṣu vinyastā madhye candrāsanaṃ prabhum |
koṇeṣu caturo pūjyā devyā digvāsā muktakeśikāḥ |
trimukhā ṣaḍbhujāś caiva trinetrā kapālamālinī |
darpaṇaṃ ca [ 106 deest]
savyapāṇo [!] kare dadyād anyeṣv adhipater yathā ||
sarvālaṃkārasaṃpūrṇā vajraparyaṅkasusthitī [!] |
kapolatāṃjalitāś caiva [!] pūjāvyagrakarā tathā ||
evaṃvidhas tu sarvajñaṃ ālitattvaṃ sunirmalam |
bhāṣitaṃ vajrasattvena guhyaṃ paramadurlabham ||
mantratattvaṃ ca vidhivat kāyavākcittasusaṃyutam |
oṃ śrīmahāsukhavajrasattva āḥ a hūṃ ḍākinījālasamvara svāhā ||
oṃ siddhirocane [!] ṛṃ a āḥ hūṃ svāhā ||
oṃ samāpattāre [!] tutāre ture ṝ āḥ hūṃ svāhā ||
oṃ padmodbhave paṇḍaravāsinī [!] ḷṃ ā hūṃ svāhā ||
oṃ ratnolkā ratnajālini ḹṃ āḥ hūṃ svāhā ||
oṃ rūpavajrī bhruṃ ||
oṃ śabdavajrī hūṃ ||
oṃ gandhavajrī khaṃ ||
oṃ rasavajrī hrīṃ ||
bhāvayed bhagamadhyasthaṃ sarvajñaṃ parameśvaram |
ālivajraṃ mahāguhyaṃ sarvasattvahṛdisthitam ||
sarvabhāveṣu ya guhyaṅgataṅguhyaṃ [!] ca prakāśayet |
jāpabhāvanayā yuktaṃḥ [!] catuḥsandhyāṃ ca yoginaḥ ||
dṛḍhavīryamahāsattvaḥ sarvasaṃtrāsavarjitaḥ |
pūjābhiṣekasamayaṃ jñānacakraṃ tu paśyati ||
samayas tv aikarasabhāvena spharatsaṃhārayogajāṃ |
traidhātukam aśeṣaṃ ca sarvavajradharopamāṃ ||
anāgatair atītaiś ca pratyutpanneṣu tryadhvasu |
evaṃ traiyadhvikā sattvā vajrasattvapadaṃ nyaset |
sahajānandaśuddhātmā anādinidhanasparam [!] iti ||   ||
abhidhānottarottare vajrasattvotpattipaṭalaḥ caturviṃśatitamaḥ ||   ||
