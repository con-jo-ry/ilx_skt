PiKraṬi: [p. 15] namo vajrasattvāya | āryanāgārjunapādakṛtapiṇḍīkramasya yathā bhāvanākramas tatheha sūcyate || prathamaṃ tāvad aṭavyādau manorame sthāne sukhāsanastho yogī | vikalpavāsanādoṣadūṣitān sarvasattvān samabhivīkṣya | taduddharaṇavāñchāsvabhāvāṃ mahākaruṇām āmukhīkṛtya |
oṃ svabhāvaśuddhāḥ sarvadharmāḥ svabhāvaśuddho 'ham ity uccārya śūnyatālambanena prākṛtāhaṃkāram apanīya | ātmānaṃ jhaṭiti vajrasattvarūpaṃ niṣpādya | vajradhṛg iti samuccārya | tenākṣobhyaṃ samutsṛjya | ātmani praveśya tryakṣareṇa kavacaṃ kṛtvā | anena krameṇa «dveṣavajrasamādhisthaḥ sthānarakṣāṃ samārabhed» (7cd) iti |
tato akṣobhyahṛdbījād daśa krodhān saṃskārya [= saṃsphārya] | dvitīyaṃ śumbham ājñāpya mantreṇa | dvitīyamantreṇa punar ājñāṃ dattvā tenaiva ca mantreṇa sumbhena kīlayed vighnān | tataḥ kīlakād bahiḥ ṭakkir hūṃjaḥ | iti mantreṇa | loha [?] | jala | agni | vāyu | prākārāṃś cintayed ity āmnāyaḥ |
tadanu bhrūṃkāram uccārya tatpariṇāmataś caityākāraṃ vajrapañjaraṃ dṛṣṭvā | tanmadhye svabhāvetyādinā gāthārthena śūnyatāṃ cintayed iti | tato mahākaruṇayā prabodhitaḥ san yaṃkārajaṃ vāyumaṇḍalaṃ dhanurākṛti | hūṃkāradvayena pārśvayor vajrāṅkitaṃ paśyet | evam agnivaruṇapṛthivīmaṇḍalāni | eṣāṃ pariṇāmena viśvavajramayabhūbhāgaṃ dṛṣṭvā |
tadupari bhrūṃkārajaṃ kūṭāgāraṃ viśvapadmasthacandrārūḍhaṃ māṇḍaleyānāṃ cakrāsanādyanvitaṃ dhyātvā | madhye hūṃ oṃ āḥ || ity akṣaratrayeṇa sūryacandraviśvapadmakiñjalkacandre punas tryakṣaraṃ dṛṣṭvā etat sarvaṃ pariṇamya jhaṭiti dvātriṃśaddevatāparivṛtaṃ vajrasattvarūpam ātmānam abhinirmāya |
tān māṇḍaleyān skandhādiṣu bhāvena skandhādiṣu līnān dṛṣṭvā paramārthamaṇḍale praveśayet | rūpaskandhetyādinā tad darśayati | ādarśasvabhāvo vairocanaḥ | bhūdhātur locanā | nayanendriyam iti kṣitigarbhaḥ | rūpaṃ ceti rūpavajrā | maitreyo 'pi yamāntakācalābhyāṃ saha vajradhare 'ntarbhavati | vedanāskandhasamatā ratnasaṃbhavaḥ | abdhātur māmakī |

tatra maitreyakṣitigarbhau vairocanakulinau bhavataḥ | ratnakulī khagarbhaḥ | amitābhakulī lokeśaḥ | amoghasiddhikulī sarvanivaraṇaviṣkambhī | śeṣās tu trayo 'kṣobhyakulina ity evaṃ jñātavyam | tataḥ oṃ śūnyatājñānavajrasvabhāvātmako 'ham ity uccārya vajrasattvam api darpaṇaśvāsakrameṇa prabhāsvare praveśayann anupalambham āviśati |
tataḥ pūrvapraṇidhānākṣepād ṛgiti sūcitaṃ sūryamaṇḍalākāreṇa pariṇataṃ tadupari candraṃ padmaṃ tryakṣaraṃ ca | etat sarvaṃ pariṇamya candram | candrāt punas tryakṣarasaṃbhūtaṃ sitavajraṃ vajrān mahāvajradharam | akṣobhyānupraveśena nyāsaṃ vicintayed iti | tatra oṃ āḥ hūṃ svā hā kārān mastakādiṣu vinyasya |
