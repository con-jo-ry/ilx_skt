XX caturyuganirdeśapaṭalaḥ
% \A = Matsunaga 404. 55\recto2
athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi catvāri yuga pramāṇataḥ |
% athātaḥ] \emend, anāthātaḥ \A
% pramāṇataḥ] \emend, praṇāmataḥ \A
yena vijñātamātreṇa śīghrāṃkañcas tu jāyate || 20.1 ||
aṣṭāviṃśatisahasrāṇi lakṣa saptadaśāni ca |
% lakṣa] \emend, rakṣa \A
kṛtaḥ mānaḥ samākhyāta eteṣāṃ sādhakalpitocyate || 20.2 ||
kṛtatretadvayoḥ sandhir aṣṭau sahasram eva ca |
dvādaśāni lakṣāṇi yapnavanti śce sahasrakam || 20.3 ||
tretākalpaḥ samākhyāto jñāyate tu vicakṣaṇaiḥ |
tretādvāparayoḥ sandhiḥ sahasra ṣaḍ vidhīyate || 20.4 ||
% tretā˚] \emend, tetā˚ \A
% vi[\A55\verso]dhīyate
catuḥṣaṣṭisahasrāṇi aṣṭau lakṣāni dvāpare |
piṇḍitā sarvaśāstreṣu dvāparakalpasaṃkhyayā || 20.5 ||
% piṇḍitā] \emend, piṃṇḍitā \A
dvāparakalisandhiś ca catvāri sahasram eva ca |
dvātriṃśatsahasrāṇi satvāri lakṣa vidhīyate || 20.6 ||
% vidhīyate] \emend, vidhāyate \A
kalikṛtadvayoḥ sandhiś catvāri sahasram aṅkataḥ |
% dvayoḥ] \emend, dvayo ' \A
eteṣāṃ yugam ākhyātā sidhyate nātra saṃśayaḥ || 20.7 ||
kayi vakṣyāmi te nātha catvāri dvīpabhāvanā |
% vakṣyāmi] \emend, vakhyāmi \A
pūrvavideho uttarakurur aparagodānīm eva ca || 20.8 ||
% apara˚] \emend, avara˚ \A
eteṣāṃ trayadvīpānāṃ phalabhūmi vyavasthitāḥ |
% dvīpānāṃ] \emend, dvīpānā \A
jambūdvīpe śubhe jātaḥ siddhibhūmi vidhīyate || 20.9 ||
% siddhi˚] \emend, sīddhi˚ \A
trayadvīpasya jātasya puṇyatīrthaphalodayam |
jambūdvīpasya sādhyante siddhimokṣate |
% siddhi[\A56\recto]mokṣate
eteṣāṃ dvīpasaṃjñā tu pūrvabuddhena bhāṣitā || 20.10 ||
iti caturyuganirdeśapaṭalo viṃśatimaḥ ||   ||
