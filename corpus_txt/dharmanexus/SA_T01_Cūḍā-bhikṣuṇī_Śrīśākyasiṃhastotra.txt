śrīśākyamunaye namaḥ ||
sadāhaṃ name śākyasiṃhaṃ munīśaṃ
svayaṃbhūkṛte 'trāgataṃ śiṣyasaṃghaiḥ |
sunepālamāhātmyasamyakprakāśe
tathā naiyapālān janān rakṣatuṃ ca || 1 ||
sudāsātmajaṃ rakṣituṃ kāśirājaṃ
nṛmāṃsāśinaṃ mātṛdoṣād bhramantam |
tathā pañcabhūpātmajānāṃ śataṃ ca
punar yoginaṃ somaputro babhūva || 2 ||
priyaḥ supriyaḥ kāśijaḥ sārthavāho
vadadvīpayātrāptacintāmaṇibhyaḥ |
suvarṇādiratnāni varṣan dhanāḍhyāṃ
cakārāśu kāśyān bhavāṃ yo name tvām || 3 ||
kapotaṃ rarakṣātmamāṃsapradānair
bhavan sarvadākhyo mahīpālavarya |
mahāsattvanāmā nṛpālātmajo 'sau
saputrāṃ ca vyāghriṃ prasūtāṃ name tvām || 4 ||
sa sarvārthasiddho 'bhaval lokahetor
gayāśīrṣam āsthāya kṛtvā sutāpam |
sudurlabhyabodhiṃ samāsādya buddho
bhave tvāṃ name pāhi sarvajña nātha || 5 ||
stutvā natvā padāmbhoje śrotuṃ nepālavarṇanā |
sābhilāṣā mudā cūḍātiṣṭhad ekāntam añjaliḥ || 6 ||
iti cūḍābhikṣuṇīkṛtaṃ śrīśākyasiṃhastotraṃ samāptam || śubham ||
