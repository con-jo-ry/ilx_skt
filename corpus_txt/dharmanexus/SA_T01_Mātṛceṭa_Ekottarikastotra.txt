Title: Ekottarikastotra
Author: Mātṛceṭa
Source: Terzová 2019, 20–21
Tibetan: D 1141
ekadharmābhirataye vanāntaikanivāsine |
lokaikacakṣurbhūtāya namo lokaikabāndhave || 1
dvipadānāṃ variṣṭhāya satyadvayavibhāvine |
advitīyasahāyāya namo 'stv advayabhāvine || 2
samādhitrayayuktāya triratnasamavāyine |
triduḥkhataughatīrṇāya trilokagurave namaḥ || 3
caturākāraśāstrāya caturvyākaraṇāya te |
caturyogavimuktāya catuḥsatyavide namaḥ || 4
pañcaskandhaviraktāya pañcendriyavibhāvine |
pañcāṅgaviprahīṇāya namaḥ pañcagaticchide || 5
ṣaḍindriyaughatīrṇāya ṣaṭsātatavihāriṇe |
ṣaṭpāramitapūrṇāya ṣaḍabhijñāya te namaḥ || 6
saptabodhyaṅgaratnāya saptāryadhanadāyine |
saptāṅgaviprahīṇāya namaḥ saptabhavacchide || 7
aṣṭāṅgamārgajātāya pudgalāṣṭakavedine |
lokadharmāṣṭakanude namo 'ṣṭākṣaṇahāyine || 8
navāvāsaviraktāya navāghātavinodine |
navaprasrabdhilabdhāya navabhūmivide namaḥ || 9
daśāsamhāryadharmāya daśanīvaraṇacchide |
daśāryāvāsarucaye namo daśabalāya te || 10
iti daśabalam āryaṃ vāgbhir abhyarcya nāthaṃ
kuśalam anavagītaṃ yan mayāvāptam adya |
tad iha narakatiryakpretaduḥkhāvṛtānāṃ
bhavatu bhavasamudrottāraṇāya prajānām || 11
samāptam ekottarikastotraṃ kṛtir ācāryamātṛceṭasya ||   ||
