XXII tantrādipramāṇa/pratiṣṭhā
% [\A58\recto4]
athānyat saṃvakṣe yogatantrasya niścayam |
yoginītantrapramāṇaṃ ca kathyate śṛṇu guhyaka || 22.1 ||
% yoginī˚] \emend, yogīnī˚ \A
% ˚pramāṇaṃ] \emend, praṇaṃ \A
yogatantrapramāṇaṃ ca ṣaṭkoṭiḥ khalu eva ca |
% ˚pramāṇaṃ] \emend, ˚pramānaṃ \A
yoginītantram ākhyātaṃ ṣoḍaśakoṭisaṃkhyayā || 22.2 ||
mahāyānasya pṛthak sūtrānta aśītikoṭim ucyate |
% aśīti˚] \emend, aśiti˚ \A
pañcāśalakṣakoṭiś ca pāramitānayam eva ca || 22.3 ||
% pārami[\A58\verso]tānayam
etāni sarvāṇy uktāni munindras trikāyarūpavān |
sugatasya deśanā vinayapiṭakodi sarvaṃ ca śrāvakaś cātmadeśanā || 22.4 ||
nānāpūjānta mahābhāṣaṃ bodhisattvo sya deśanā |
yoginīyogatantrasya rahasya buddhagocaram || 22.5 ||
% yoginī˚] \emend, yogīni˚ \A
mandapuṇyāni sattvānāṃ devatātmakadeśanā |
dharmasaṃbhoganirmāṇamahāsukhavibhāvanā || 22.6 ||
prajñopāyātmakaṃ yogī śīghraṃ buddhatvam āvahet |
% yogī] \emend, dyogī \A
athānyatama vakṣya pratiṣṭhā vidhipāragam || 22.7 ||
pūrvoktalakṣaṇopetam ācārya parikalpayet |
ghatitaṃ saṃskṛtapratimādīnāṃ pratiṣṭhārtha bhūmi śodhayet || 22.8 ||
% pratiṣṭhārtha] \emend, praṣṭhārtha \A
vitānadhvajapatākānāṃ śobhitaṃ bhūparigraham |
% bhūparigraham] \emend, bhūparegraham \A
% bhūpare[\A59\recto]graham
saṃpūrṇapratimāṃ vā pustakaṃ paṭaṃ vā purata sthāpayet || 22.9 ||
ākāśasthamaṇḍalacakraṃ pañcopahāraiḥ saṃpūjya
samayas tvaṃ samayo 'ham iti trir uccārayet || 22.10 ||
% ˚opahāraiḥ] \emend, ˚oprahāraiḥ \A
% uccārayet] \emend, uccārayat \A
yathā hi sarvasaṃbuddhās tuṣite saṃpratiṣṭhitāḥ |
māyādevyā yathā bhukto tadvat tiṣṭhantv ihākṛtau || 22.11 ||
atrasthaḥ satataṃ nāthā bhūtvā puṣpādikām imām |
gṛhāna amukasyārthāya bodhicittapravṛddhaye || 22.12 ||
anena tathāgatān adhivāsya stutiṃ kuryā vicakṣaṇe |
nimañcanaṃ trivāraṃ ca saṣayān bhāmayet || 22.13 ||
sugandhagandhatailena valkalena tu snāpayet |
% ˚tailena] \emend, ˚taireṇa \A
% snāpayet] \emend, snāpayat \A
punaḥ pratyekakalaśena pratimāṃ snāpayet sadā || 22.14 ||
% pratyeka˚] \emend, pratyaka˚ \A
% snāpayet] \emend, snāparyen \A
paścād ākāśasthaṃ bhagavantaṃ maṇḍalacakrapratimādiṣu praveśayet || 22.15 ||
% ākāśasthaṃ] \emend, ākāsthaṃ \A
% maṇḍa[\A59\verso]la˚
% praveśayet] \emend, praveśayat \A
daśakarmakṛtaṃ kāryaṃ vyavahārādikaṃ yāvat |
akṣiṇī unmīlayet vajracakṣumadhe tiṣṭhet || 22.16 ||
% unmīlayet] \emend, unmīrayet \A
puṣpadhūpaṃ tathā dīpaṃ gandhaṃ naivedyam eva ca |
sarvakarmasu tatpaścāt homakarmeṇa pūrayet || 22.17 ||
devāgnimukhā sarve pratiṣṭhā kārayed budhaḥ |
% budhaḥ] \emend, buddha \A
pratiṣṭhālakṣaṇasaṃprāpte stutimāṃ kalpa kārayet || 22.18 ||
% pratiṣṭhā˚] \emend, prateṣṭhā˚ \A
mṛdaṅgabhūryanirghoṣe yathālantu pūjayet |
% mṛdaṅga˚] \emend, mṛdaga˚ \A
bhojanadāna dākṣiṇyāṃ janatuṣṭiṃ ca pauṣṭikam || 22.19 ||
puṣṭiṃ tu janasaubhāgyaṃ māgalyā śāntikam |
śāntikaṃ dūṣaṇayāgaṃ nānāduḥkhādyupadravam || 22.20 ||
apratiṣṭhita yathā deva pratiṣṭhāṃ kartu śakyatām |
śiṣyasyādhyeṣaṇaṃ sārdhaṃ kartavya puṇyahetutaḥ || 22.21 ||
% ˚ādhyeṣa[\A60\recto]ṇaṃ \A
nirvikalpakarūpeṇa pratiṣṭhā devasthāpanam |
% ˚rūpeṇa] \emend, ˚rūpena \A
devatālokapālāś ca tiṣṭhante śāśvataṃ yadā |
tadā ca saṃtiṣṭhed deva pūjyāpūjyasamanvitam || 22.22 ||
iti devatāpratiṣṭhāvidhipaṭalo dvāviṃśatimaḥ ||   ||
