Abhidhānottara 1.
= Mānabajra Bajrācārya E 29260 = NGMPP E 1517/7
= Abhidhānottara-tantra, A Sanskrit Manuscript from Nepal, Śatapiṭaka vol. 263. 1981.
( *1v) namo sarvagurubuddhabodhisattvebhyaḥ||
( *1v) oṃ namaḥ śrīcakrasaṃvarāya |
ṣaḍgate yatra paryantaṃ sarvabuddhavikurvitaiḥ|
kriyate vajrasattvena namīte dharmadhātuke|| AU 1.1. ||
śrīmate vajraḍākāya ḍākinīcakravartine|
pañcajñānatrikāyāya trāṇāya jagato namaḥ|| AU 1.2. ||
yāvantyo vajraḍākinya cchinnasaṅkalpabandhanāḥ|
lokakṛtyapravarttinyas tāvatībhyo namaḥ sadā|| AU 1.3. ||
evam mayā śrutam ekasmin samaye bhagavān sarvatathāgatavajrakrodhaḍākaḍākinīguhyahṛdayeṣu vijahāra|| AU 1.4. ||
tatra bhagavān sarvatathāgatā bhagavantam anādinidhanaṃ śrīvajrasattvam adhyeṣayati sma || AU 1.5. ||
deśayatu bhagavān sarvatathāgatavajrakrodhaḍākaḍākinījālasamvarābhidhānottarottaraṃ hṛdayaṃ vajrapāṇe mahākrodhādhipate parṣadvividhanānāvikurvaṇāvijṛmbhaṇaṃ śṛṇu || AU 1.6. ||
sādhu ca suṣṭhu ca manasikuru bhāṣiṣye tan sādhu bhagavānn iti kṛtāñjalipuṭaḥ praṇipatyaivam āhuḥ || AU 1.7. ||
ato rahasyaṃ vakṣye samāsān na tu vistarāt |
śrīherukasya saṃyogakaṃ sarvakarmārthasādhakam || AU 1.8. ||
uttarād api cottaram abhidhānottarottaram|
ḍākinījālasamvaraṃ || AU 1.9. ||
rahasye parame ramye sarvātmani sadā sthitam|
sarvaḍākinīmayaṃ sattvo vajrasattvaṃ paraṃ sukham || AU 1.10. ||
asau hi svayambhū bhagavān vīro ḍākinīkālasamvaram |
bhagodbhagavā saṃbhavo nādarūpo viniṣkrāntā samayācāragocarāṃ|| AU 1.11. ||
durlabha triṣu lokeṣu ādimadhyāntanirmalam || AU 1.12. ||
manthamanthānayogena sa yogād yatra yat tathā |
prakṛtiprabhāsvaraṃ śuddhaṃ guhyapīṭhodbhavodbhavam || AU 1.13. ||
nirghaṣaṃ śāśvataṃ śāntaṃ khasamaṃ sṛṣṭikārakam |
svabhāvaśuddham adbhūtaṃ yoginīnāṃ sukhapradam || AU 1.14. ||
japadhyānādibhi yuktaṃ yogasyaiva vidhijñatā|
tatra nigaditaṃ tattvaṃ guhyakādhipate śṛṇu || AU 1.15. ||
madhyamottamaśvāsena gandhodakasahitena tu|
gulikāṃ kārayed dhīmān pūjayet parṣanmaṇḍalam || AU 1.16. ||
kālavelāviśeṣeṇa pūjaye tatra dūtayaḥ |
sahajā siddhidāḥ sattvā adhamottamamadhyamāḥ || AU 1.17. ||
antargatena manasā kāmasiddhaṃ tu sādhayet |
svaretobindubhi buddhā boddhisattvāṃś ca pūjayet || AU 1.18. ||
svādhidevatayogena svaparāś caiva pūjayet |
darśanāṃ sparśanāṃ vā śravaṇāsmaraṇena vā || AU 1.19. ||
mucyate sarvapāpais tu evam eva na saṃśayaḥ |
yogitvaṃ paramaṃ puṇyaṃ pavitraṃ pāpanāśanam |
sidhyante janmanīhaiva jāpadhyānaratena ca || AU 1.20. ||
abhidhānottarottare avatāraṇasamayaguhyarahasyapaṭala prathamaḥ || AU col. 1. ||
