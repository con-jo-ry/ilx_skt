dhīgambhīraguhaḥ sthirasmṛtiśilaḥ sadvākyadhātūjjvalo
maitrīprasravaṇaḥ samādhivalavān alpecchatākandaraḥ |
yo 'ṣṭābhir bhuvi lokadharmapavanair dṛptair na saṃkampitaḥ
taṃ saṃbuddhasumerum ūrjitaguṇaṃ jñānoccaśṛṅgaṃ name || 10.1.1 ||
loke sarvaparapravādisamayān ākramya yo dīpyate
vitrastāḥ pratiyānti tīrthyavṛṣabhā yasya prabhāvāhatāḥ |
nānādṛṣṭipataṅgavṛndam api yo bhasmīkaroti kṣaṇāt
taṃ sarvānuśayendhanapradahanaṃ saddharmavahniṃ name || 10.1.2 ||
śaikṣāśaikṣasahasranakramakaro yaḥ śūnyatāniḥsvano
nirmokṣaikarasaḥ subhāṣitamahāratnākaro hrīhradaḥ |
taṃ tattvārthavicāraṇāvaranadīvegaiḥ samāpyāyitaṃ
śikṣābandhanabaddhavelam amalaṃ vande gaṇāgrārṇavam || 10.1.3 ||
dravyapradānaṃ na tathā viśiṣṭaṃ
varāvarajñaḥ sugato jagāda |
yathā viśiṣṭaṃ vadatāṃ variṣṭho
dharmapradānaṃ bhavagān uvāca || 10.1.4 ||
dravyapradānaṃ hi mitāvakāśaṃ
dharmapradānaṃ tv amitāvakāśam |
dravyasya kāmāvacaraṃ phalaṃ hi
dharmasya tac caiva tataḥ paraṃ ca || 10.1.5 ||
pradīyate yat khalu nārthahetor
na satkriyārthaṃ na yaśonimittam |
tad dharmadānaṃ paramaṃ pradiṣṭaṃ
saddharmavāṇijyam ato 'nyathā yat || 10.1.6 ||
imaṃ viditvā paramaṃ viśeṣaṃ
dharmapradānaṃ satataṃ pradeyam |
dravyaṃ kṣayaṃ yāti hi dīyamānaṃ
vistīryate dharma ihocyamānaḥ || 10.1.7 ||
bhagavati guṇarāśau saṃprasādyeha cittaṃ
tadanutadabhidharme tatsute cāryasaṃghe |
śṛṇuta muditacittās tannimittopayātāḥ
sugatasugaditārthaṃ śrīnidhānaṃ samagrāḥ || 10.1.8 || iti ||
§1 evaṃ mayā śrutam ekasmin samaye bhagavān śrāvastyāṃ viharati sma | jetavane 'nāthapiṇḍadasyārāme | tatra bhagavān bhikṣūn āmantrayate sma | traya ime bhikṣavaḥ pudgalāḥ pudgalasya bahukarā bhavanti | katame trayaḥ |
§1.1 yaḥ pudgalo yaṃ pudgalam āgamya svākhyāte dharmavinaye keśaśmaśrūṇy avatārya kāṣāyāṇi vastrāṇy ācchādya samyag eva śraddhayā agārād anāgārikaṃ pravrajaty ayaṃ prathamaḥ pudgalaḥ pudgalasya bahukaraḥ | tena pudgalena tasya pudgalasya yāvajjīvenāpi na sukaraṃ pratikartum | yad uta cīvarapiṇḍapātaśayanāsanaglānapratyayabhaiṣajyapariṣkāraiḥ ||
§1.2 punar aparaṃ yaḥ pudgalo yaṃ pudgalam āgamya yat kiṃcit samudayadharmakaṃ tat sarvaṃ nirodhadharmakam iti viditvā virajo vigatamalaṃ dharmeṣu dharmacakṣur utpādayaty ayaṃ dvitīyaḥ pudgalaḥ pudgalasya bahukaraḥ | tena pudgalena tasya pudgalasya yāvajjīvenāpi na sukaraṃ pratikartum |
yad uta cīvarapiṇḍapātaśayanāsanaglānapratyayabhaiṣajyapariṣkāraiḥ ||
§1.3 punar aparaṃ yaḥ pudgalo yaṃ pudgalam āgamyāsravāṇāṃ kṣayād anāsravāṃ cetovimuktiṃ prajñāvimuktiṃ dṛṣṭa eva dharme svayam abhijñayā sākṣātkṛtvopasampadya pravedayate | kṣīṇā me jātir uṣitaṃ brahmacaryaṃ kṛtaṃ karaṇīyaṃ nāparam asmād bhavaṃ prajānāmīty ayaṃ tṛtīyaḥ pudgalaḥ pudgalasya bahukaraḥ |
tena pudgalena tasya pudgalasya yāvajjīvenāpi na sukaraṃ pratikartum | yaduta cīvarapiṇḍapātaśayanāsanaglānapratyayabhaiṣajyapariṣkāraiḥ ||
tat kasya hetoḥ | tasya pudgalasya tasmāt pudgalād anyaḥ pudgala upakarataro bahukarataraś ca nāsti ||
§2 idam avocad bhagavān uktaṃ caitad bhagavatoktam arhatā | dve bhikṣavo dāne dharmadānam āmiṣadānaṃ cālpamātrakam idaṃ bhikṣavo dānānāṃ yad utāmiṣadānam | etad agryaṃ dānānāṃ yad uta dharmadānam ||
§3 idam avocad bhagavān idam uktvā sugato hy athāparam etad uvāca śāstā |
annaṃ pānaṃ khādyaṃ bhojyaṃ vastraṃ śayyāsanāni ca |
evaṃrūpāṇi dānāni ye prayacchanti pudgalāḥ |
āryasya dharmadānasya kalāṃ nārghanti ṣoḍaśīm || 1 ||
yad dharmadānam adadat tathāgato hy
anuttaraḥ sarvabhūtānukampī |
taṃ tāyinaṃ devamanuṣyaśāsakaṃ
santo namasyanti bhavasya pāragam || 2 ||
idam avocat |
keṣu mānaṃ na kurvīta keṣu ca syāt sagauravaḥ |
keṣām apacitiḥ sādhuḥ keṣāṃ syāt sādhupūjanā || 10.3.1 ||
mātāpitros tathā saumya jyeṣṭhe bhrātari mānavan |
ācārye cāpy upādhyāye ye cānye gurusaṃmatāḥ || 10.3.2 ||
teṣu mānaṃ na kurvīta teṣu ca syāt sagauravaḥ |
teṣām apacitiḥ sādhus teṣāṃ syāt sādhupūjanā || 10.3.3 ||
rāgadveṣāv ubhau yeṣām avidyā ca virāgitā |
prahāya mānam astabdhas tān namasyet kṛtāñjaliḥ || 10.3.4 ||
vigarhitaṃ dravyavidhau yathā hi
mātsaryam āryaiḥ prasamīkṣya buddhyā |
saddharmamātsaryam anantadoṣaṃ
vigarhitaṃ tadvad ihātmakāmaiḥ || 10.3.5 ||
saddharmamātsaryam atīva kṛtvā
bhikṣuḥ purā kāśyapadharmaśāstre |
dharmapradānaṃ carame tu kāle
saptāham evaṃ pradadau tadānīm || 10.3.6 ||
dharmapradānāt tridivaṃ jagāma
mātsaryadoṣāt tu manuṣyaloke |
bubodha kṛcchreṇa pareṇa dharmaṃ
jinopadiṣṭaṃ sa hi sarpadāsaḥ || 10.3.7 ||
dānaṃ jinena dvividhaṃ pradiṣṭaṃ
dravyapradānaṃ ca tathaiva dharmam |
dharmapradānaṃ hi tayor viśiṣṭaṃ
jarāvipadvyādhivināśakatvāt || 10.3.8 ||
dravyapradānād adhikaṃ pradiṣṭaṃ
jinena yasmād varadharmadānam |
tasmād budhaiḥ kleśamahāmayaghnaṃ
dharmapradānaṃ satataṃ pradeyam || 10.3.9 ||
jvalatu ciraṃ dharmolkā yā ciranirvṛte 'pi lokagurau |
ajñānatimiramathanī samaviṣamaṃ saṃprakāśayati || 10.3.10 ||
