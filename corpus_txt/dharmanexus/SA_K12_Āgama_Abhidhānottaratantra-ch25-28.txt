Abhidhānottaratantra ch. 25 [Tib. & Ś. 28]
= Lokesh Chandra facsimile ed.
= 1138 AD palm-leaf codex
[ 174.6] [ begins on *106 deest]
[ 107] utpalam akuśaś caiva jñānamūrtiṃ vibhāvayet |
kāyavākittitiyaṃ (!) cakramadhye vibhāvayet ||
pūrvoktapīṭhayogena yathā vai vajrasattvayoḥ |
mantram evam anusmṛtyaṃ mañjuvajrasya guhyajaṃ |
triguhyajñānasamayaṃ vīrayoginisaṃyutaṃ ||
oṃ śrīmahāsukhamañjuvajra āḥ maṃ hūṃ ḍākinījālasamvara svāhā ||
prāṇāyāmaṃ suyantritvā japet mantraṃ mahāvratī |
sphareti guhyacakraṃ sa[ṃ]harec ca tridhātukaṃ |
etad rahasyaṃ paramaṃ na kathayed yasya kasyacit ||   ||
Abhidhānottarottare Mañjuvajrasādhanapaṭalaḥ pañcaviṃśatimaḥ ||   ||
