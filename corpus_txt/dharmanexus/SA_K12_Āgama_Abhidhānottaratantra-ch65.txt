Abhidhānottarottara 65.
[315.3] athāparaṃ vakṣye ātmabhāvavidhikramam |
māmako locanā devī tārā pāṇḍaravāsinī |
mārīcī jambaraś cai[4]va cūndādevī vasundharā |
svabījapariniṣpannāṃ bhujair dvādaśayuktatam |
trinetrālīḍhapadaṃ sattvaparyaṅkasushitām |
vajrapatyaṅkam āsīnāṃ [5] caturdaśānāṃ
digvāsasā śavārūḍhaṃ muktakeśāṃ bhayānakām |
kapālamālāmukuṭāṃ kroḍhadṛṣṭyā karuṇārasām |
prajñāpāramitā devī[6]ṃ tadva jñānodayāṃ dharām |
pratisarākārayogātmā tadva jñānasuniścitaḥ |
krodhāvajravidhākāraṃ ca nāmāvīrasambhavā
bhūtaḍāmaravīraṃ vā [7] bhināñjanasamaprabham |
ādhipatye niyojitvā ātmabhāvena cetasā |
asamāhitayogena bhāvayec ca vibhāvanāt |
sūkṣmakrodha[316]vajreṇa hṛdayāmbajamadhyamaḥ |
atraiva sarvaḍākinyo nivasanti kulākriyāḥ |
atraiva sarvasaṃbuddhā bodhisattvajagattrayaṃ |
svassaiva pūja[2]nā devapūjitāḥ sarvapūrvikāḥ |
yadi syāl lokanaṃ nityaṃ pūjanaṃ tanmataṃ jinaiḥ |
adhyātmayogavajreṇa yoginyā vajriṇo matāḥ ||
ataś ca [3] vajrapūjābhiḥ pūjā kāryā maṇiṣīnaḥ |
pañcopahārayogena laghu buddhatvam āpnuyāt |
yad etat kathitaṃ tattvaṃ bāhyapūjā matā parā |
niḥ[4]svabhāva yad ābhyāsāt devatārūpabhāvanā |
sā matā mahātī pūjā sarvabuddhaprabodhanī
deśanāmātratantrārtha tattvaṃ na paramārthataḥ |
paramārtha[5]samutpannena deśyo na ca deśanā |
caturviśabhedena pīṭhād yatraiva saṃsthitāḥ |
tat bhramaṇenaiva kheda kāryo na tāttvikaiḥ |
yadi tattvavihīnā [6] syu bhrāntyā teṣāṃ na kiṃcane |
adhyatayutās te syu bhrāntyās teṣāṃ na kiṃcanaḥ |
pīṭhopapīṭhachandohasevanaṃ nnirmalo bhavet |
athāśakta [7] pramādī vā pīṭhakīrtanato naraḥ |
yad etad uktaṃ asmābhiḥ sattvāśayayogataḥ |
dhyānato no paraṃ saukhyaṃ tasmād dhyānaparo bhavet |
kṣīya[317]nte dhātavas teṣāṃ bhrameṇād bāhyayoginām |
ato bāhyaṃ tiraskṛtyaṃ sthātavyaṃ yogalīlayā |
paśyanti vajrayoginyo dviśuddhajñānaloca[2]naiḥ |
śrīmato vajrayoginyā kāruṇyakulacetasaḥ |
tāś ca tuṣṭo parāṃ siddhāṃ ḍhaukayanti yathepsitam |
jñānabhaṭṭārakas tena sādhanīyaḥ [3] prayatnataḥ |
nānāprasādasaṃyuktaṃ caryāvratam udāhṛtam |
caryāvratam apāsyeha kuryāt tattvavratiratiḥ |
yena tena prakāreṇa kṛtvā[4]hārāsthiti vrati |
dhyānāhāraparo bhūtvā tiṣṭhed unmattaceṣṭitaḥ |
nagno vā yadi vānagno yatra tatra yathā tathā |
svādhidevatam ālambya [5] tattvam ekaṃ tu cittayet |
ṣaṇmāsamātrato vaśyaṃ mūkībhāvaprasaṅgataḥ |
dīpyate sau mahād yogī yoginībhir upāsthitaiḥ |
jñānajyo[6]tiparaṃ dṛṣṭvā yoginyas tu mupāsate |
yadvat padmākaraṃ ramyaṃ gāhate haṃsapakṣayaḥ |
śrīmān vajradharaṃ sākṣād bhavatīha na saṃśayaḥ |
indra[7]jālikavarṇākṣa sarvasattvārthasādhakaḥ |
iti me bhyarthanā kṛtvāḥ kriyatāṃ śuddhacetasaḥ |
asyā svakaraṇāntanaṃ yad uktaṃ ntad bhavi[318]ṣyati |
jñānavajraprayogena ḍākinīhṛdaye nyaset |
hṛdvajrāṅkuśayogena hṛdvajrāntarasocanam |
vajratṛtayā nyasya dharmadharmadhātuvi[2]kalpanam |
svacchākāśayogena bhāvayet tejarūpadhṛk |
svakarmākṣaravinyāsaṃ svacchaṃ prakṛtinirmalam |
svādhyāśayayogena svadha[3]rmākṣarabhāvanā |
yathā yathā vikalpena anyathārūpa bhāvayet |
cittasaṃmohasādhyasya anyam utpāda indriyam |
yathā yathā vimo[4]kṣaṇa anyathā rūpadarśayet ||      ||
abhidhānottarottare ātmabhāvapūjāpaṭalaḥ pañcaṣaṣṭitamaḥ ||      ||
