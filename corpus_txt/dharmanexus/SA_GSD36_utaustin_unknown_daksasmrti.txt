śrīgaṇeśāya namaḥ
sarvaśāstrārthatattvajñaḥ sarvavedavidāṃ varaḥ |
pāragaḥ sarvavidyānāṃ dakṣo nāma prajāpatiḥ || 1.1

eteṣāṃ tu hitārthāya dakṣaḥ śāstram akalpayat || 1.2

sa hi garbhasamo jñeyo vyaktimātrapradarśakaḥ || 1.3
bhakṣyābhakṣye tathā peye vācyāvācye tathānṛte |
tasmin kāle na doṣo ‘sti sa yāvan nopanīyate || 1.4

aprāptavyavahāro ‘sau yāvad ṣoḍaśavārṣikaḥ || 1.5

brahmacārī bhavet tāvad ūrdhvaṃ snāto bhaved gṛhī || 1.6

tṛtīye ca tathā bhāge poṣyavargārthasādhanam || 2.27

abhyāgato ‘tithiś cāpi poṣyavarga udāhṛtaḥ || 2.28

anye ‘py adhanayuktaś ca poṣyavarga udāhṛtaḥ || 2.29

dviguṇaṃ triguṇañ caiva caturthasya caturguṇam || 5.6
ardhaprasṛtimātrā tu prathamā mṛttikā smṛtā |
dvitīyā ca tṛtīyā ca tadardhaṃ parikīrtitā || 5.7
liṅge ‘py atra samākhyātā triparvī pūryate yayā |
etac chaucaṃ gṛhasthānāṃ dviguṇaṃ brahmacāriṇām | 5.8
triguṇaṃ tu vanasthānāṃ yatīnāṃ ca caturguṇam |
dātavyam udakaṃ tāvan mṛdabhāvo yathā bhavet || 5.9
mṛdā jalena śuddhiḥ syān na kleśo na dhanavyayaḥ |
yasya śauce ‘pi śaithilyaṃ vṛttaṃ tasya parīkṣitam || 5.10
anyad eva divā śaucam anyad rātrau vidhīyate |
anyad āpadi nirdiṣṭam anyad eva hy anāpadi || 5.11
yad divā vihitaṃ śaucaṃ tadardhaṃ niśi kīrtitam |
tadardham āture proktam āturasyārdham adhvani || 5.12
nyūnādhikaṃ na kartavyaṃ śauce śuddhim abhīpsatā |
prāyaścittena yujyeta vihitātikrame kṛte || 5.13
iti dākṣe dharmaśāstre pañcamo ‘dhyāyaḥ |
ṣaṣṭho ‘dhyāyaḥ
sūtakaṃ tu pravakṣyāmi janmamṛtyunimittakam |
yāvaj jīvaṃ tṛtīyaṃ tu yathāvad anupūrvaśaḥ || 6.1
sadyaḥ śaucaṃ tathaikāho dvitricaturahas tathā |
daśāho dvādaśāhas ca pakṣo māsas tathaiva ca || 6.2
maraṇārthaṃ tathā cānyad daśapakṣās tu sūtake |
upanyāsakrameṇaiva vakṣyāmy aham aśeṣataḥ || 6.3
granthārthaṃ yo vijānāti vedam aṅgaiḥ samanvitam |
sakalpaṃ sarahasyañ ca kriyāvāṃś ca na sūtakī || 6.4
rājartvigdīkṣitānāñ ca bāle deśāntare tathā |
vratināṃ satriṇañ caiva sadyaḥ śaucaṃ vidhīyate || 6.5
ekāhāc chudhyate vipro yo ‘gnivedasamanvitaḥ |
hīne hīnatare caiva dvitricaturahas tathā || 6.6
śudhyed vipro daśāhena dvādaśāhena bhūmipaḥ |
vaiśyaḥ pañcadaśāhena śūdro māsena śudhyati || 6.7
asnātvā cāpy ahutvā cādattvā yas tu bhuñjate |
evaṃvidhānāṃ sarveṣāṃ yāvaj jīvaṃ ?? tu sūtakam || 6.8
vyādhitasya kadaryasya ṛṇagrastasya sarvadā |
kriyāhīnasya sarvasya strījitasya viśeṣataḥ || 6.9
vyasanāsaktacittasya parādhīnasya nityaśaḥ |
śraddhātyāgavihīnasya bhasmāntaṃ sūtakaṃ bhavet || 6.10
na sūtakaṃ kadācit syād yāvaj jīvaṃ ?? tu sūtakam |
evaṃguṇaviśeṣeṇa sūtakaṃ samudāhṛtam || 6.11
sūtake mṛtake caiva tathā ca mṛtasūtake |
etat saṃhataśaucānāṃ mṛtaśaucena śudhyati || 6.12
dānam pratigraho homaḥ svādhyāyaś ca nivarttate |
daśāhāt tu paraṃ śaucaṃ vipro ‘rhati ca dharmavit || 6.13
dānañ ca vidhivad deyam aśubhāt tārakaṃ hi tat |
mṛtakānte mṛto yas tu sūtakānte prasūyate || 6.14
evaṃ saṃhataśaucānāṃ pūrvāśaucena śudhyati |
ubhayatra daśāhāni kulasyānnaṃ na bhujyate || 6.15
caturthe ‘hani kartavyam asthisañcayanaṃ dvijaiḥ |
asthisañcayanād ūrdhavam aṅgaśaucaṃ vidhīyate || 6.16
varṇānām ānulomyena strīṇām eko yadā patiḥ |
daśāhaṣaṭtryahaikāhaṃ prasave sūtakaṃ bhavet || 6.17
yajñakāle vivāhe ca daivayoge tathaiva ca |
hūyamāne tathā cāgnau nāśaucaṃ naiva sūtakam || 6.18
svasthakāle tv idaṃ sarvam aśaucaṃ parikīrtitam |
āpadgatasya sarvasya sūtake ‘pi na sūtakam || 6.19
iti dākṣe dharmaśāstre ṣaṣṭho ‘dhyāyaḥ |
saptamo ‘dhyāyaḥ
loko vaśīkṛto yena yena cātmā vaśīkṛtaḥ |
indriyārtho jito yena taṃ yogam prabravīmy aham || 7.1
prāṇāyāmas tathā dhyānaṃ pratyāhāra tu dhāraṇā |
tarkaś caiva samādhiś ca ṣaḍaṅgo yoga ucyate || 7.2
nāraṇyasevanād yogo nānekagranthacintanāt |
vratair yajñais tapobhir vā na yogaḥ kasyacid bhavet || 7.3
na ca padmāsanād yogo na nāsāgranirīkṣaṇāt |
na ca śāstrātiriktena śaucena sa bhavet kvacit || 7.4
na maunamantrakuhakair anekaiḥ sukṛtais tathā |
lokayātrābhiyuktasya na yogaḥ kasyacid bhavet || 7.5
abhiyogāt tathābhyāsāt tasminn eva tu niścayāt |
punaḥ punaś ca nirvedād yogaḥ sidhyati yoginaḥ || 7.6
ātmacintāvinodena śaucena krīḍanena ca |
sarvabhūtasamatvena yogaḥ sidhyati nānyathā || 7.7
yaś cātmani rato nityam ātmakrīḍas tathaiva ca |
ātmānandaś ca satatam ātmany eva samāhitaḥ || 7.8
tataś caiva svayaṃ tuṣṭaḥ santuṣṭo nānyamānasaḥ |
nityaṃ caiva sutṛpto yo yogas tasya prasidhyati || 7.9
supto’ pi yogayuktaḥ syāj jāgraṃś caiva viśeṣataḥ |
idṛkceṣṭaḥ smṛtaḥ śreṣṭho variṣṭho brahmavādinām || 7.10
yas tv ātmavyatirekeṇa dvitīyaṃ naiva paśyati |
brahmabhūtaḥ sa evaṃ hi dakṣapakṣa udāhṛtaḥ || 7.11
viṣayāsaktacitto hi kaścid yogaṃ na vindati |
yatnena viṣayāsaṅgaṃ tasmād yogī vivarjayet || 7.12
viṣayendriyasaṃyogaṃ kecid yogaṃ vadanti vai |
adharmo dharmabuddhyā tu gṛhītas tair apaṇḍitaiḥ || 7.13
ātmano manasaś caiva saṃyogañ ca tathāpare |
uttānamanaso hy ete kevalaṃ yogavañcitāḥ || 7.14
vṛttihīnaṃ manaḥ kṛtvā kṣetrajñe paramātmani |
ekīkṛtya vimucyeta yogayuktaḥ sa ucyate || 7.15
kaṣāya mohavikṣepalajjā śaṅkādicetasaḥ |
vyāpārās tu samākhyātās tān jitvā vaśam ānayet || 7.16
kuṭumbaiḥ pañcabhir grāmyaiḥ ṣaṣṭhas tatra mahattamaḥ |
devāsuramanuṣyaiś ca sa jetuṃ naiva śakyate || 7.17
balena pararāṣṭrāṇi gṛhṇan śūras tu nocyate |
jito yenendriyagrāmaḥ sa śūraḥ kathyate budhaiḥ || 7.18
bahirmukhāni sarvāṇi kṛtvā cābhimukhāni vai |
sarvañ caivendriyagrāmaṃ manaś cātmani yojayet || 7.19
sarvabhāvavinirmuktaḥ kṣetrajñaṃ brahmaṇi nyaset |
etad dhyānañ ca yogaś ca śeṣo ‘nyo granthavistaraḥ || 7.20
tyaktvā viṣayabhogāṃś ca mano niścalatāṃ gatam |
ātmaśaktisvarūpeṇa samādhiḥ parikīrtitaḥ || 7.21
caturṇāṃ sannikarṣeṇa yat phalaṃ tad aśāśvatam |
dvayos tu sannikarṣeṇa śāśvataṃ dhruvam akṣayam || 7.22
yan nāsti sarvalokasya tad astīti virudhyate |
kathyamānaṃ tadanyasya hṛdaye nāvatiṣṭhate || 7.23
svayaṃ vedyaṃ hi tad brahma kumārīmaithunaṃ yathā |
ayogī naiva jānāti jātyandho hi yathā ghaṭam || 7.24
nityābhyasanaśīlasya svayaṃ vedyaṃ hi tad bhavet |
tat sūkṣmatvād anirdeśyaṃ paraṃ brahma sanātanam | 7.25
budhā hy ābharaṇaṃ bhāraṃ malam ālepanaṃ yathā |
manyante strī ca mūrkhaś ca tad eva bahu manyate || 7.26
sattvotkaṭāḥ surāḥ sarve viṣayais tu vaśīkṛtāḥ |
kim punaś ca kṣudrasattvair manuṣyair atra kā kathā || 7.27
tasmāt tyaktakaṣāyena kartavyaṃ daṇḍadhāraṇam |
itaras tu na śaknoti viṣayair abhibhūyate || 7.28
na sthiraṃ kṣaṇam apy ekam udakaṃ hi yathormibhiḥ |
vātāhataṃ tathā cittaṃ tasmāt tatra na viśvaset || 7.29
tridaṇḍivyapadeśena jīvanti bahavo narāḥ |
yas tu brahma na jānāti tridaṇḍārho bhaven na saḥ || 7.30
brahmacaryaṃ sadā rakṣed aṣṭadhā maithunaṃ pṛthak |
smaraṇaṃ kīrtanaṃ keliḥ prekṣaṇaṃ guhyabhāṣaṇam || 7.31
saṅkalpo ‘dhyavasāyaś ca kriyāniṣpattir eva ca |
etan maithunam aṣṭāṅgaṃ pravadanti manīṣinaḥ || 7.32
na dhyātavyaṃ na vaktavyaṃ na śrotavyaṃ kadācana |
etaiḥ sarvaiḥ susampanno yatir bhavati netaraḥ || 7.33
pārivrājyaṃ gṛhītvā ca yas tv adharmeṇa tiṣṭhati |
śvapadenāṅkayitvā taṃ rājā śighraṃ pravāsayet || 7.34
eko bhikṣur yathoktas tu dvau caiva mithunaṃ smṛtam |
trayo grāmas tathā khyāta ūrdhvan tu nagarāyate || 7.35
nagaraṃ hi na kartavyaṃ grāmo vā mithunaṃ tathā |
etat trayaṃ prakurvāṇaḥ svadharmāc cyavate yatiḥ || 7.36
rājavārtā tathā teṣāṃ bhikṣāvārttā parasparam |
snehapaiśunyamātsaryaṃ sannikarṣān na saṃśayaḥ || 7.37
lābhapūjānimittaṃ hi vyākhyānaṃ śiṣyasaṅgrahaḥ |
ete cānye ca bahavaḥ prapañcāḥ kutapasvinām || 7.38
dhyānaṃ śaucaṃ tathā bhikṣā nityaṃ ekāntaśīlatā |
bhikṣoś catvāri karmāṇi pañcamo nopapadyate || 7.39
tapojapaiḥ kṛśībhūto vyādhito ‘vasathāvahaḥ |
vṛddho grahagṛhītaś ca yaś cānyo vikalendriyaḥ || 7.40
nīrujaś ca yuvā caiva bhikṣur nāvasathāvahaḥ |
sa dūṣayati tat sthānaṃ budhān pīḍayatīti ca || 7.41
nīrujaś ca yuvā caiva brahmacaryād vinaśyati |
brahmacaryād vinaṣṭas tu kulaṃ gotrañ ca nāśayet || 7.42
vasann āvasathe bhikṣur maithunaṃ yadi sevate |
tasyāvasathanāśaḥ syāt kulāny api nikṛntati || 7.43
āśrame tu yatir yasya muhūrtam api viśramet |
kiṃ tasyānyena dharmeṇa kṛtakṛtyo ‘bhijāyate || 7.44
sañcitaṃ yad gṛhasthena pāpam āmaraṇāntikam |
tat sarvaṃ nāśayet pāpam ekarātroṣito yatiḥ || 7.45
dhyānayogapariśrāntaṃ yas tu bhojayate yatim |
nikhilaṃ bhojitaṃ tena trailokyaṃ sacarācaram || 7.46
yasmin deśe vased yogī dhyānayogavicakṣaṇaḥ |
so ‘pi deśo bhavet pūtaḥ kiṃ punas tasya bāndhavāḥ || 7.47
dvaitaṃ caiva tathādvaitaṃ dvaitādvaitaṃ tathaiva ca |
na dvaitaṃ nāpi cādvaitam ity etat pāramārthikam || 7.48
nāhaṃ naivānyasambandho brahma bhāvena bhāvitaḥ |
īdṛśyāyām avasthāyāṃ avāpyaṃ paramaṃ padam | 7.49
dvaitapakṣāḥ samākhyāto ye ‘dvaite tu vyavasthitāḥ |
advaitināṃ pravakṣyāmi yathā śāstrasya niścayaḥ || 7.50
atrātmavyatirekeṇa dvitīyaṃ naiva paśyati |
ataḥ śāstrāṇy adhīyante śrūyante granthavistarāt || 7.51
dakṣaśāstre purā proktam āśramapratipādanam |
adhīyante tu ye viprās te yānty amaralokatām || 7.52
ya idaṃ paṭhate bhaktyā śṛṇuyād vāpi mānavaḥ |
sa putrapautrapaśumān kīrtiñ ca samavāpnuyāt || 7.53
śrāvayitvā tv idaṃ śāstraṃ śrāddhakāle dvijottamaḥ |
akṣayaṃ bhavati śrāddhaṃ pitṛbhyaś copajāyate || 7.54
iti dākṣe dharmaśāstre saptamo ‘dhyāyaḥ |
samāptā ceyaṃ dakṣasaṃhitā ||
