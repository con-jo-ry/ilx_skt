athānyaṃ sampravakṣyāmi navākṣarasya sādhanaṃ |
kṣaḥkārajñānaniṣpannaṃ sarvaduḥkhakṣayaṃkaraṃ |
vikalpakṣayabhakṣaṇaṃ |
khasamaṃ śūnyasambhūtaṃ gaganābhogabhoginaṃ |
ṣaḍvaktraṃ dvādaśabhujaṃ trinetraṃ vikṛtānanaṃ |
ālīḍhapadasaṃsthānaṃ māraduṣṭakṣayaṃkaraṃ ||
adhaḥpādatalākrāntaṃ bhairavaṃ bhairavīsthitāṃ |
vyāghracarmanivasanaṃ muṇḍamālāvibhūṣitaṃ |
kapālamukuṭamālā ardhacandropaśobhitaṃ ||
viśvavajrajaṭākrāntaṃ ṣaṭmu[ 107]drādehabhūṣitaṃ |
drāṣṭākarālavadanaṃ bhayasyāpi bhayaṅkaraṃ |
tataḥ nīlaṃ dhūmraharitaṃ ca raktapītaṃ tu pañcamaṃ |
ṣaṣṭhaṃ ca gardabhākāraṃ ūrdhvavaktraṃ tu bhīṣaṇaṃ ||
bhasmabhūtaṃ nibhaṃ varṇaṃ sarvaduḥkhāpahāriṇaṃ |
vajraghaṇṭāsamāpannā suratābhogabhoginaṃ ||
agrato vajravārāhyā tadvarṇām ūrdhvasūkarā |
advayayogasamāpannā muktakeśī digambarā |
jānudvayasamāveṣṭya karuṇārāgavihvalā |
kapālakhaṭvāṅgadharā brahmacarmapaṭordhvakā |
vajraśūlaṃ ḍamaruṃ karttikā pāśamuṇḍayoḥ |
asimuṇḍāmitaṃ raudraṃ sarvaduḥkhopaghātinaṃ |
hṛdaye jñānaḍāka kutu (!) kākāsyādiparivṛtaṃ |
aṣṭāracakramadhye tu vāmadakṣitavaṇṇātaḥ(!) |
śmaśānopaśmaśānaṃ ca vinyaset ḍākinīpadaṃ ||
yaṃ raṃ laṃ vaṃ [´]saṃ ṣaṃ saṃ haṃ nyastvā ḍākinījñānacakraṃ
vibhāvya saptarātraṃ tu catuḥsandhyā yathākramaṃ |
nadītīre śma[śā]neṣu bhāvayet sidhyate dhruvaṃ |
haraṇaṃ māraṇa[ṃ] kṛtvā ya[ 108]t karma manasepsitaṃ |
sidhyate ḍākinīcakraṃ nātra kāryā vicāraṇā |
nīlā palāśavarṇā ca raktapītā caturthikā |
samayadādyādi(!) tu catvāro viśvavarṇaśubhā[na]nā |
raktā nīlā ca dhūmrā ca sitavarṇānanā śubhottamā |
sarvā trinetrā trivaktrā ca muktakeśā digambarā |
kākānanā savyā ca savyā ulūkānānā tathaiva ca |
śvānānā (!) tṛtīyā tu caturthī sūkarānānā (!) |
vāmadakṣiṇayor vaktraṃ raudraṃ rūpaṃ tathovidhiḥ (!) |
yamadāḍhyādi vaktrās ca (!) vāmadakṣiṇayos tathā |
meṣoṣṭraturagāsiṃhī cchāgalagomahīṣayoḥ |
ṛkṣāni ca mukhānyāni sarvāḥ samarasīkṛtāḥ |
kapālakhaṭvāṅgadharā<|ṃ|> pāśāṅkuśakarāparā |
ghaṇṭāḍamarukarttikā kapālamālāmakuṭaṃ
pañcamudrāvibhūṣitāḥ |
pretāsanā nṛtyapadā kharvadīrghayonikāṃ |
draṃṣṭrākarālavadanā karuṇārasavihvalāḥ |
kāyavākcittaguhyākhyā bhāvayec ca vibhāvanāt |
hūṃ āḥ oṃ iti guhyokhyā (!) vajrābde cakramadhyagā [|]
jñānaḍākasya hṛdaye ḍākinyālipicakraga
jñānacakraṃ tato dṛṣṭvā pūjāsamarasībhavet |
ja<pe>t mantraṃ vidhivat | akṣarāṇāṃ sa[ 108]māhitaḥ [|]
prāṇāyāma[ṃ] suyantritvā spharej jñānaṃ triguhyajaṃ ||
śrīkṣaḍākavajra āḥ kṣaḥ hūṃ ḍākinījālasamvara hūṃ phaṭ ||
praṇavaṃ nāmahūṃkāraphaṭ|kārānta niyojayet |
ḍākinīmantram evoktaṃ sarvakāmaphalapradaṃ |
idaṃ rahasyaṃ paramaṃ na deyaṃ yasya kasyacit |
saptāhā-m-ekato vaśyaṃ mūkībhāvena bhāvayet |
sidhyate acirān tasya ḍākinīcakraṃ triguhyajaṃ ||   ||
Abhidhānottarottare kṣaḍākavajrasādhanapaṭalaḥ ṣaḍvi[ṃ]śatimaḥ ||   ||
