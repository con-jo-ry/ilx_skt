Abhidhānottaratantra ch. 28 [Tib. & Ś. 31]

= 1138 AD palm-leaf codex
[ 182.2] [ within lacuna: 110-112]
atha yogaṃ pravakṣyāmi viśvapadmaiśvarottamam |
sarvottamamahāyogaṃ mahāsiddhipradaṃ śubham ||
utpattikramayogena bhāvayec ca vibhāvanāt |
sūryamaṇḍalamadhye tu siṃ oṃ vaṃ vajrasaṃbhavam || (= dhaṃ)
tatparāvṛtyam ātmānaṃ praṇavaḍākam uttamam |
viśveśvaraṃ viśvavarṇa[ṃ] sarvavarṇottamottamam ||
ṣaṇmukhaṃ dvādaśabhuja[ṃ] trinetraṃ rāgaghūrṇita[m] |
ardha[candra]dharaṃ śāntaṃ kapālair mālābhūṣitam ||
viśvavajradharaṃ mū[r]dhni jaṭābaddhaṃ tu śekharam |
ṣaṇmudrādehavinyāsaṃ divyābharaṇabhūṣitam ||
adha[ḥ]pādatalābje tu durbhagāṃ tīrthikāṃ kṛtvā
pañcadarśanam ākramyālīḍhapadakramāt ||
ardhaparyaṅkanṛtyapadaiḥ ṣaḍdharaṇa[!]vibhūṣitau |
viṣṇucarmanivasanair muṇḍamālāvibhūṣitauḥ ||
vajradevīsamāpannān nagnāṃ ca mekhalottamāṃ |
muktakeśī trinayanāṃ aṣṭanipīḍitāṃ ||
vajraghaṇṭāsamāśliṣṭā bodhyaṅgasthānapīḍitāṃ |
jānudvayasamāśliṣṭāṃ karuṇārāgarasotsukāṃ ||
brahmacarmavitānāṅgakaradvayena tu dhāryatāṃ |
kapālakhaṭvāṅgadharāṃ maheśvaramuṇḍa dhāryatāṃ ||
triśūlāsiḍamarukāṃ caiva pāśāṅkuśakarograjām |
sitanīlaharitaṃ caiva pītadhūmrāruṇaṃ tathā ||
vāmadakṣiṇapārśve tu nīlavarṇaḍākinyā dve ca yojitā |
dharmottarā nāma vajraḍākinī dhaṃkāreṇa vāmato nyaset ||
siṃkāre sāgarottamā nāma vajraḍākinī
viśvavarṇā viśvamukhāṃ trinetrāṃ vikṛtānanām |
ardhaparyaṅkam āsīnāṃ sanṛtyapadayojitāṃ ||
pañcavaktrā daśabhujāṃ trinetrāṃ raudrabhīṣaṇāṃ [|]
bhairavakālarātryā ca pādābjatalasaṃsthitāḥ ||
[...] [ 113]drāvibhūṣitāṃ |
digambaradharāṃ muktakeśāṃ muṇḍamālā tu mekhalā [|]
nūpure ghughuraravā kapālair mālabhūṣitāṃ |
caṇḍra(!)maṇḍalamadhyasthāṃ ālikālyottamottamāṃ |
vajrollālanatarjanyā karadvayavibhūṣitāṃ |
kapālakhaṭvāṅgadharā ḍamarughaṇṭāvibhūṣitāṃ ||
pāśāṅkuśakarāṃ caiva muṇḍakarttikarāparāṃ ||
kāye vākcittamakuṭāṃ sarvasi[ddhi]pradāyikāṃ |
tricakrabhāvanopāyāṃ jñānaḍākinimadhyagāṃ |
praṇavo jñānaḍākaṃ tu cittacakreṣu madhyataḥ |
teṣāṃ hṛdi trayo bhāryā kāyavākcittaḍākajā |
pūrvoktakramayogena pīṭhanyāsād anukramāt ||
bhāvayed bhāvanāṃ tatra sphuranti tayānukramāt |
siddhir apy āhatā tasya ḍākinyā saha bhāvanāt |
jñānacakrekarasatā japet mantravibhāvanāt ||
oṃ śrīpraṇavaḍāka oṃ āḥ oṃ hūṃ ḍākinījālasamvara oṃ svāhā ||
oṃ hrīḥ padmottame mahāvajra[ 113]ḍākinīye dhaṃ hūṃ phaṭ svāhā |
oṃ sāṃ sāgarottame mahāvajraḍākinīye siṃ hūṃ phaṭ svāhā |
triguhyājñānajāpeṇa vīraḍākini jāpyatāṃ |
praṇava((ṃ)) svanāma hūṃ dvau ca phaṭkārānta niyojayet |
sphuret triguhyacakra[ṃ] tu saṃhare[t] tribhaveṣu ca |
trisamayaguhyayogaṃ tu bhagamadhye tu bhāvayet |
bodhicandraṃ sunirmalaṃ
tricakra[ṃ] tu triyo nāḍyā pradhānā ca triguhyatāṃ |
triḍākaṃ ca triḍākiṃ ca sṛṣṭisaṃhārakāparāṃ |
bhageṣu vajravārāhyā triguhyatritayabhāvanām |
bhāvayet tattvayogena yoginī rahaṃsyaṃkāra(!)cetasā ||
catuḥsandhyā sadā yogī bhāvayej japate tathā |
samayān pālayen nityaṃ sakṣīraṃ viśvasambhavam ||
mahābrahma madhuḥ sarpi mahāmānsa[ma]nvitam |
bhakṣayed āhārakṛtyarthaṃ tritattve svābhimantraye |
nissaṅke niṣparīhāram aparihārasaṃyutam |
anābhogalabdānsu (!?) [ 114] niravadyāṃ tu bhuñjayet |
pañca kāmaguṇāṃ sevyo anābhogānivadyatām |
somapāṇa pibed yogī nānyapāṇaṃ kadācana ||
adhyātmayogayuktena svādhiṣṭhānādhiṣṭhitaś ca |
saupīlavo pīlavo bhagavān ḍāko ḍākiny upapīlavaṃ
upāyaṃ pīla<vaṃ> jñeyaṃ | prajñā yogapīlavaṃ
nagarāntṛpīlavaṃ caiva grāmāntopapīlavaṃ |
candrasūryasamāyogāt pīlavopapīlavaṃ |
kṣaṇaiḥ pīlavayogena anavadyāś copapīlavā |
bahyasāmarthayuktāsau sidhyatīhaiva janmani [|] (= bahusāmarthyayukto 'sau)
paśum | bhakṣayet parvaṇi carukaṃ sādhayet tathā [|]
ḍākinīvīrasaṅgāni parvamelāpakaṃ kṛtaṃ |
nṛtyagītopahāreṇa pūjayet saṃmsam (!?) uttamaṃ |
vikalparahitāṃ sarvāṃ samayācāra + + cāritāṃ ||   ||
Abhidhānottarottare Praṇavaḍākasiddhinimitti(!)nirdeśapaṭalo nāma aṣṭāviṃśatimaḥ || o ||
