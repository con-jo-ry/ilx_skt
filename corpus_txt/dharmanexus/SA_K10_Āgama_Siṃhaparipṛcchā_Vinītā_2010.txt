evaṃ mayā śrutam ekasmin samaye bhagavān rājagṛhāt samuddhitaḥ |
vyavahared gṛdhrakūṭe jino hy apratipudgalaḥ || [1]
ajātaśatroḥ putro 'bhūt siṃho nāmnātha viśrutaḥ |
śreṣṭhiputraśataiḥ so 'tha pañcabhiḥ saṃpuraskṛtaḥ || [2]
rājagṛhād . niḥkramya mahatyā rājasampadā |
sauvarṇaṃ chattram ādāya buddhasyāntikam āyayau || [3]
. chattrāṇi dadus te maharṣiyataye . |
pādau śīrṣeṇa vanditvā sarva bodhau pratiṣṭhitāḥ || [4]
paryapṛcchata taṃ siṃhaḥ kṛtāñjalipuṭo munim |
yā caryā bodhisattvānāṃ tāṃ pṛṣṭām abhidhatsva me || [5]
bhavet prāsādikaḥ kena syāc ca kenopapādukaḥ |
jātismaro bhavet kena mām ācakṣva mahāmune || [6]
teṣām adhyāśayaṃ jñātvā buddho jñānena cābravīt |
yā caryā bodhisattvānāṃ tāṃ pṛṣṭāṃ kathayāmi te || [7]
bhavet prāsādikaḥ kṣāntyā brahmacaryopapādukaḥ |
jātismaro dharmadānāj jānīṣvaivaṃ kumāraka || [8]
dvātriṃśallakṣaṇānīha tathānuvyañjanāni ca |
āṅgīrasena kāyena kathaṃ sattvān sa toṣayet || [9]
lakṣaṇāny akhilair dānair maitryānuvyañjanāni ca |
sarvasattvasamatvena bhaved āṅgīraso jinaḥ || [10]
dhāraṇī labhyate kena samādhiḥ kena jñāyate |
ādeyavākyaś ca kathaṃ taṃ brūhi me mahāmune || [11]
dharmakṣāntyāṃ dhāraṇī hi samādhir dhyānacintayā |
% dharmakṣāntyā, surely
sattvapralāpasambarhād bhavaty ādeyavākyatā || [12]
smṛtimāñ jāyate kena matimāṃś ca kathaṃ bhavet |
gatimān buddhimāṃś caiva bhavet kena mahāmune || [13]
aśaṭhaḥ smṛtim āpnoti matimān arthacintakaḥ |
sagauravas tu gatimān paṇḍito dharmarakṣayā || [14]
saṃmukho lokanāthānāṃ karmaṇā kena jāyate |
praśnārthān pṛcchate tāṃ . bodhisattvo viśāradaḥ || [15]
na jātu dharmadānasya karoty āvaraṇaṃ yataḥ |
tenāsau labhate kṣipraṃ lokanāthaiḥ samāgamam || [16]
varjayaty akṣaṇaṃ kena kena gacchati sadgatim |
kenāpramatto bhavati yatra yatropapadyate || [17]
śrāddho 'kṣaṇaṃ varjayati sadgatiṃ yāti śīlavān |
śūnyatāṃ bhāvayen nityam apramatto vidhīyate || [18]
karmaśuddhir bhavet kena mārapāśair na badhyate |
dharmavyasananirmukto bodhisattvaḥ kathaṃ bhavet || [19]
adhimokṣāt karmaśuddhir vīriyān māradharṣaṇam |
yathāvādi tathākārī dharmavyasanavarjakaḥ || [20]
brahmasvaro bhavet kena kalaviṅkarutasvaraḥ |
nānādhimuktikān sattvān kathaṃ toṣayate mune || [21]
brahmasvaraḥ satyavākyād apāruṣyeṇa sasvaraḥ |
nānādhimuktīṃs toṣeti saṃbhinnām api bhidyate || [22]
kathaṃ bhavati dīrghāyur alpābādhaḥ kathaṃ bhavet |
abhedyaparivāraś ca kathaṃ bhavati mahāmune || [23]
bhavaty ahiṃsro dīrghāyur alpābādho 'vihiṃsayā |
abhedyaparivāraś ca bhinnānām apy abhedataḥ || [24]
arthāḥ kena samṛdhyanti kena bhogair na hīyate |
maheśākhyo bhavet kena yatra yatropapadyate || [25]
arthasiddhir alobhena bhogasiddhir amatsarāt |
anīrṣayā maheśatvaṃ nirmānāgragataḥ sadā || [26]
divyaṃ cakṣuḥ kathaṃ bhavati śrotraṃ divyaṃ tathaiva ca |
cittotpattiṃ ca sattvānāṃ jānīte kena nāyaka || [27]
dīpena labhate cakṣuḥ śrotraṃ vādyena śudhyati |
cittotpattijñatā jñānād evam eṣāṃ samudbhavaḥ || [28]
ṛddhiṃ ca labhate kena smaraṇaṃ pūrvajanmanām |
kṣīyante bodhisattvānāṃ cāsravāḥ kena nāyaka || [29]
ṛddhir yānapradānena smṛtyabhyāsād anusmṛtiḥ |
ubhāv antau varjayitvā saṃspṛśaty āsravakṣayam || [30]
saṃgrahaḥ sarvadharmāṇāṃ karmaṇā kena jāyate |
priyaś ca sarvasattvānāṃ kathaṃ bhavati nāyaka || [31]
sarvasattvapramokṣārthaṃ cittaṃ bodhāya nāmayet |
eṣa saṃgraho dharmāṇāṃ bhavate tena ca priyaḥ || [32]
śikṣitvā kīdṛśīṃ śikṣāṃ cittaṃ saṃbhāvya kīdṛśam |
sarvajño jāyate śāstā tādṛśo yādṛśo muniḥ || [33]
sarveṣv eva hi sattveṣu samacittaḥ sadā bhavet |
bhāvayitvā samaṃ cittaṃ bhavet sarvābhibhūr jinaḥ || [34]
kena jātiṣu sarvāsu bodhicittaṃ na riñcati |
svapnāntaragataś cāpi kiṃ punar yadi jāgrataḥ || [35]
yeṣu grāmeṣu deśeṣu nivāsanagareṣu vā |
samādāpayate bodhau tena cittaṃ na riñcati || [36]
mahābalo bhavet kena sadā sthāmnā samudgataḥ |
durdharṣo mārakoṭībhir yatra yatropapadyate || [37]
dattveha pañca sārāṇi bhītebhyo abhayaṃ tathā |
bhaven mahābalas tena sadā sthāmnā samudgataḥ |
durdharṣo mārakoṭībhir yatra yatropapadyate || [38]
kṣetrasya sampadā kena kena saṃghasya sampadā |
prabhāsampad bhavet kena yena bābhāsate diśaḥ || [39]
kṣetrasya sampat praṇidhānāt saṃgrahāt saṃghasampadā |
dattvā ratnavitānaṃ tu tena bābhāsate diśaḥ || [40]
śrutvaivaṃ bodhisattvānāṃ caryāvyākaraṇaṃ tathā |
saṃmukho lokanāthasya tuṣṭo 'bhūt sa nṛpātmajaḥ || [41]
siṃhaḥ saha sahāyais taiḥ prāñjalir vākyam abravīt |
yā caryaiṣā vinirdiṣtāsyāṃ śikṣiṣyāmahe mune || [42]
smitaṃ prāviṣkarod buddho 'nekakṣetrāvabhāsakam |
aprākṣīc cājito buddhaṃ smitaṃ kiṃ te vibho kṛtam || [43]
pṛṣṭaś caivaṃ samāno hi saṃbuddho 'jitam abravīt |
mayājita nibodha tvaṃ yasyārthe 'tra smitaṃ kṛtam || [44]
aśītikoṭyo buddhānāṃ niyutāni ca dvādaśa |
ebhiḥ samagrair bodhyarthaṃ sarve te pūjitāḥ purā || [45]
dīpaṃkaram upāharya maharṣiṃ dvipadottamam |
paripācitā mayā caite yadā siddhipradāyakāḥ || [46]
adhunānāgate 'py ete ṣaṣṭikoṭī maharṣiṇām |
maitreyādīn samabhyarcya bhaviṣyanti vināyakāḥ || [47]
mahāprabhāsanāmnā vai bhāvī kalpaḥ suśobhanaḥ |
tatraikasminn amī kalpe bhāvino dvipadottamāḥ || [48]
jñānaketudhvajaś caiṣām ekaṃ nāma bhaviṣyati |
yo vyūhaś cāmitābhasya sa evaiṣāṃ bhaviṣyati || [49]
ye caiṣāṃ bodhisattvānāṃ nāmaṃ śroṣyanti kevalam |
na teṣāṃ saṃśayo bodher nāpi tadgatito bhayam || [50]
śrutvā vyākaraṇaṃ cedaṃ yo 'dhimuktiṃ kariṣyati |
trailokye nāsti tat puṇyaṃ yat tasya sadṛśaṃ bhavet || [51]
buddhakṣetrasahasrāṇi kalpakoṭiśatāni yaḥ |
saṃpūrya ratnair buddhebhyo dadāti bodhilipsayā || [52]
yaś ca saṃśrutya saddharmaṃ śraddadhyād bodhikāraṇāt |
saṃkhyāṃ kalāṃ nopāyāti yat puṇyaṃ pūrvakīrtitam || [53]
puṣpāṇi vavṛṣur devās trisāhasraṃ ca kampitam |
koṭiyāśīti sattvānām agrabodhau pratasthire || [54]
labhitvā pañca so 'bhijñāḥ siṃhaḥ śreṣṭhisutaiḥ saha |
buddhaṃ saṃpūjya saptāhaṃ pravrājitāḥ sarva eva te || [55]
samādhīn dhāraṇīś caiva sarve te lebhire kṣaṇāt |
sattvārthaṃ saṃpupūrṣanto buddhadharmanidarśanāt || [56]
idaṃ hy avocad bhagavāṃs tuṣṭaḥ siṃho nṛpātmajaḥ |
sarve sadevalokā hi abhyanandan muner vacaḥ || [57]
