devetyādi | he deva manuṣyāsuranatacaraṇa he pratihatajanmajarārujamaraṇa he lokeśa aśaraṇyaṃ śaraṇahīnaṃ māṃ tvaṃ rakṣa pālaya + (pālayeti?) saṃbandhaḥ | he kṛpālo he dayālo tvaṃ kāruṇyaṃ karuṇābhāvaṃ kurv iti dvitīyaḥ saṃbandhaḥ |
devāś ca manuṣyāś cāsurāś ca te devamanuṣyāsurās tair nataṃ caraṇaṃ yasya sa devamanuṣyāsuranatacaraṇas tasyāmantraṇaṃ he devamanuṣyāsuranatacaraṇa | etena devamanuṣyeṣu bha[2r]gavān śāsteti sūcitam | janma ca jarā ca rujaṃ maraṇaṃ ca taj janmajarārujamaraṇaṃ |
pratihataṃ nirākṛtaṃ janmajarārujamaraṇaṃ yena sa pratihatajanmajarārujamaraṇas tasya saṃbodhanaṃ he pratihatajanmajarārujamaraṇa | anenāpi bhagavataḥ prahāṇasaṃpad udīritā | lokānām īśo lokeśas tasyābhimukhīkaraṇaṃ he lokeśa | anenāpi bhagavataḥ prāptisaṃpad udbhāvitā | rakṣety anena parārthasaṃpat kathitā |
kāruṇyaṃ kurv ity anena karuṇāsaṃpat prakaṭitā || 1 ||
saṃsāretyādi | he mahākṛpa he mahādayālo tvaṃ māṃ mā 'vadhīrayeti saṃbandhaḥ | kathaṃbhūtaṃ saṃsārodadhimadhyanimagnam | punaḥ kathaṃbhūtam | kleśamahormisamāhitabhagnam | punaḥ [2v] kathaṃbhūtam | viruvantam | he mahākṛpa tvaṃ māṃ pāhi iti dvitīyaḥ saṃbandhaḥ | ahaṃ bhavantaṃ naumi | iti tṛtīyaḥ saṃbandhaḥ | mā'vadhīraya mā helāṃ kuru |
saṃsāra eva udadhiḥ samudras tasya madhyaṃ tatra nimagnam adhogatam | kleśā eva mahormayaḥ kallolās taiḥ samāhitaṃ samyag āropitaṃ bhagnam | viruvantaṃ rudantam | evaṃviśiṣṭaṃ māṃ tvaṃ pāhi rakṣa | ahaṃ bhavantaṃ lokeśaṃ naumi namaskaromi trāṇāśayety arthaḥ || 2 ||
tṛṣṇetyādi | he bhagavan he aiśvaryādiguṇavan | tvaṃ māṃ pālaya rakṣa | na kevalaṃ pālaya avalokaya paśya | kathaṃbhūtaṃ bhṛṣṇā timiropadrutanetram | punaḥ kathaṃbhūtam | maraṇamahābhayavihvalagātram | yāvad ahaṃ viṣamām avīciṃ na yāmi [3r] iti saṃbandhaḥ | tṛṣṇā eva timiram andhakāraḥ | tenopadrutaṃ vyākulaṃ netraṃ nayanaṃ yasya tam |
maraṇam eva mahābhayaṃ tena vihvalaṃ samāyattaṃ (?) gātraṃ śarīraṃ yasya tam | evaṃvidhaṃ māṃ he bhagavan he bhagnamāra tvaṃ pālaya rakṣa | avalokaya paśya | yāvacchabdo 'vadhau | viṣamāṃ dāruṇām avīciṃ narakaṃ yāvan na yāmi na prāpnomi tāvad eva rakṣāyā avasara iti sūcitam || 3 ||

ity anantaroktaprakāraiś caturviṃśatibhiḥ ślokaiḥ bhagavataḥ smaraṇaṃ bhagavatsmaraṇaṃ tena yat puṇyaṃ yat kuśalam asti tena kuśalena idaṃ jagad ayaṃ lokaḥ | akṣuṇṇaṃ avandhyaṃ yathā bhavati evaṃ śrīpotalakaś cāsāv acalaś ceti śrīpotalakācalaḥ tasmin vāsaḥ sthitiṃ labhatāṃ prāpnuvantu | vividhaś cāsau vilāsaś ceti vividhavilāsaḥ |
