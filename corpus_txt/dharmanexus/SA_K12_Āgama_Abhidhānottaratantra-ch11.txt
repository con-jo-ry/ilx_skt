athānyat saṃpravakṣyāmi kulabhedottarottaram |
pīṭhādikramayogena vibhāgaṃ ca diśo kramam |
sarvottamamahāyogaṃ yogaiśvaryaṃ (*47v) prasidhyati |
vajrakāyasamudbhūtaṃ maṇḍalaṃ maṇḍalottamam ||
atha bhagavantaḥ sarvatathāgatāḥ punar vīrayoginyo samājam āgamya mahāsuratādhipatiṃ mahāvajradharaṃ praṇāṃdimānāḥ [?] praharṣotphullalocanāḥ |
saṃkṣepotkṣepapadāpadavidhikramaiḥ |
vāmadakṣiṇapāṇibhyāṃ salilāyāgraphuraiḥ |
īṣaddhasatkaṭākṣais tu muktābhrūtalāvibhramaiḥ |
praṇātā cakṣumudrais tu mudreyaṃ nāmaya jagat ||
adhyeṣayanto mahāsattvā gītenānena śauriṇam |
saṃsthitāḥ sarvayoginyo mahāsuratamaṇḍale ||
bhāṣasva bhagavan tattvaṃ maṇḍalaṃ paramaṃ śubham |
mahāsuratavajrādyaṃ sarvabuddhamahāsukham ||
laddhaha praïsaa mahāsuha aggu
alalala ho mahāsuha saalasatathāgadu dipu |
anena gīyamānena atyantabhavanirvṛtāḥ |
tathāgatā mahāśuddhāḥ kalpa(*48r)nti hi maṇḍalam ||
atha bhagavān mahāsukhavajrasattvaḥ sasmitotphullavadano vikasitārvindunayanaḥ savibhramabhrūbhaṅgāgradṛṣṭiḥ |
taḍittaraṅgacapalākṣepavibhramāḥ svakāyavākcittavajreṣu idaṃ tattvam udājahāra |
maṇḍalaṃ maṇḍalaṃ nāma vajrajñānahṛdi sthitam |
kalpanāmātrayogena maṇḍalaṃ vajram uttamam ||
maṇḍalapūrṇacandrābhaṃ mahāsuratavarjitam |
sitāṃśumālinaṃ citraṃ bodhicitta ivāparāṃ ||
nīlajvālāmayaṃ viśvaṃ anilānalapreritam |
ardhacandramayaṃ divyaṃ vajrasauriṇamaṇḍalam ||
aṣṭāṃśamaṇḍalaṃ sajvālaṃ sūryasūryasaṃnibham |
maṇḍalaṃ vajraratnasya suratotpattisādhanam ||
aṣṭapatravikāsaṃ tu karṇikādeśarākulam |
padmākāraṃ tu padme sa maṇḍala suratodbhavam ||
tryasraṃ tu maṇḍalaṃ ramyaṃ hṛdbhukajālasaṃni(*48v)bham |
saddhakarmakaraṃ siddhikarma vajriṇamaṇḍalam ||
evamādyās tu nṛntāgrā[?] maṇḍalānāṃ prakalpanā [|]
nirmitāni yathātathyaṃ vajrasattvena tāyineti ||
samantabhadra sarvātmā parāvṛtya khadhātukam |
khadhātubhavane ramye kūṭāgāraṃ prabhāvayet ||
kūṭāgārodare madhye pañcamaṇḍalakalpanā |
viśvapadmāsane ramye yathāsthānaṃ niveśayet ||
madhyamaṇḍalamadhye tu herukaṃ tu vibhāvayet |
pūrvoktavarṇasaṃsthānaṃ vārāhyā sukhasaṃsthitam ||
pūrvata pullīramalaye khaṇḍakapālinaḥ pracaṇḍā |
uttare jālandhare mahākaṅkālacaṇḍākṣī |
paścime oḍiyānake kaṅkālaprabhāvatī |
dakṣiṇe arbude vikaṭadaṃṣṭriṇamahānāsā |
evaṃ madhyamaṇḍalam ||
asyaiva pūrvamaṇḍalaṃ
madhye godāvaryāṃ surāvairiṇavīramatī |
pūrve rāmeśvare amitābhakharvarī (*49r) |
uttare devikoṭe vajraprabhalaṅkeśvarī |
paścime mālave vajradehadrumacchāyā [|]
dakṣiṇe kāmarūpe aṅkurikaairāvatī |
uttaramaṇḍalamadhye odre vajrajaṭilamahābhairavā |
pūrve triśakunau mahāvīravāyuvegā |
uttare kośalāyāṃ vajrahūṃkārasurābhakṣī |
paścime kaliṅge subhadraśyāmādevī |
dakṣiṇe lampāke vajrabhadrasubhadrā |
paścimamaṇḍalamadhye kāñcike mahābhairavahayakarṇā |
pūrve himālaye virūpākṣakhagānanā |
uttare pretapuryāṃ mahābalacakravegā |
paścime gṛhadevatāyāṃ ratnavajrakhaṇḍarohā |
dakṣiṇe saurāṣṭre hayagrīvaśauṇḍinī ||
dakṣiṇamaṇḍalamadhye suvarṇadvīpe ākāśagarbhacakravarminī |
pūrve nagare + herukasuvīrā |
uttare sindhau padmanarteśvaramahābalā |
paścime marau vairocanacakravartinī |
dakṣiṇe kulatāyāṃ vajrasattva(*49v)mahāvīryā ||      ||

ardhanārīśvarī trimukhāṃ ṣaḍbhujāṃ trinetrā ālīḍhapadasthāṃ digvāsāṃ muktakeśāṃ viśvapadmoparipretārūḍhāṃ pañcamudrādhiṣṭhitāṃ | kapālakhaṭvāṅgadharāṃ muṇḍakarttidharāparāṃ | ḍamarukam vajraśūlaṃ ca sadaṃṣṭrotkaṭabhīṣaṇaṃ | sanam[?]muṇḍasragdhāmabhūṣitā | kapālamakuṭāṃ | vajrasattvādyalaṃkṛtā | ardhacandraviśvavajrākrāntamaulinaṃ | (50v)
yadvat bhagavān tadvad vajravārāhyā vajrabhujamudrāsaṃsthāna mudrā kāryā nagnā muktakeśā khaṇḍamaṇḍitamekhalāḥ kiṃ tu ūrdhvavaktra vārāhānanaṃ sitaṃ drasthrā[!]karālaṃ|
yathā pīṭhakrameṇa lakṣāvidhāno<kta>krameṇa yogiṃnyā maṇḍalapaṭalena vajrasattvena bhāṣitaṃ | anyac ca vajraḍākavidhānoktaprathamamaprathamapaṭala saṅkanīyaḥ ākṣepamaṇḍalavidhi pañcamaṃ cātmakaṃ mahāyogasamvaravidhānaṃ guhyarahasya ehiś[!] cānyaiś ca vīrarājai ḍākinībhi samantataḥ |
nānāvidhāni kalaśāni hemarūpamayāni ca |
