śrīmad ekallavīrāya sarvasattvopakāriṇe |
namo vighnāntasaṃhārakāyatrayaikamūrttaye || 1 ||
kareṇoddhṛtakhaḍgāya savyanopāyabhāvataḥ |
vāme prajñāṅgabhāvāya tarjanīsaktapāśine || 2 ||
saptapātālapālārtham adho vāmākṣadarśine |
saptabrahmāṇḍapālatvān namaḥ savyordhvadarśine || 3 ||
bhūmau tadrakṣaṇatvāc ca vinyastavāmajānave |
māraprahāṇasaṃsthānasavyapādāya te namaḥ || 4 ||
namas te 'kṣobhyanāthāya tadaṅkaratnamauline |
vicitrāmbarabhūṣāya samāpadvayavajriṇe || 5 ||
vidhvastāmbhojajanmendradaityāriśūlapāṇaye |
namaḥ śrīcaṇḍaroṣāya lakṣārkādhikaraśmaye || 6 ||
śūnyatoktā'py akāreṇa cakāreṇa mahāsukham |
lakāreṇānayor aikyaṃ tasmān namo 'stu te 'cala || 7 ||
dviraṣṭakalasampūrṇa vilakṣakṣaṇasambhava |
virāgeṇāpy acālatvāt tasmād api namo 'stu te || 8 ||
virāgaś caṇḍatādhyāto māraṇāt tasya roṣaṇam |
saukhyarājodayas tasmāt namas te caṇḍaroṣaṇa || 9 ||
astaṃ yasyodaye yānti viṣayāḥ sārdham indriyaiḥ |
niṣpannānandarūpāya tat tubhyaṃ śirasā namaḥ || 10 ||
sarvākāravaropetamahāmudrākṣarātmane |
darpaṇapratisenāvadavabhāsāya te namaḥ || 11 ||
stutvā tvām apy asaṃstutyaṃ durlakṣyaṃ ca nirañjanāt |
prāptaṃ puṇyaṃ mayā yat tu bhūyāt tenācalo janaḥ || 12 ||
ity acalasya kramadvayastotraṃ samāptam |
kṛtir ācāryaśrīvanaratnapādānām iti |
