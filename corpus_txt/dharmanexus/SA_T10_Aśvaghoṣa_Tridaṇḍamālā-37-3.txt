bhāṣante śāstradṛṣṭaṃ bhaga iti yaśasaḥ paryāyavacanaṃ
saṃyuktas tena loke munir iha yaśasā tasmāt sa bhagavān |
atyantaṃ vā x x x x iha sa bhagavāṃs tasmāc ca bhagavān
pūjābhāgbhāgyavān vā vidur iha sa yatas tasmāc ca bhagavān ||
yathā gatās te munayaḥ śivāṃ gatiṃ tathā gataḥ so 'pi tatas tathāgataḥ |
parāvaraṃ vānugatā yathārṣayas tathāgatas tat sa yatas tathāgataḥ ||
jagāda vā tathyam atas tathāgato jagāma vā bhāvam atas tathāgataḥ |
tathāgato vā praśamaṃ tathā gato yathā gato naiti punaḥ punarbhavam ||
kṣamābhedyaṃ baddhvā dhṛtikavacam utsāhakaṭhinaṃ
yato 'rīn mārādīn sa munir avadhīd dhairyadhanuṣā |
yataś cārhaḥ pūjāṃ sasuranaranāgasya jagato
vibhus tasmād arhan bhavati bhavadṛṣṭikṣayakaraḥ ||
duḥkhaṃ taddhetus tannirodho 'tha mārgaḥ samyak saṃbuddhaṃ tena yasmād aśeṣam |
śakyaṃ kartuṃ tan nānyathā nānṛtaṃ vā samyaksaṃbuddhas tena buddhaḥ pṛthivyām ||
tisraḥ śubhāś cāsulabhāś ca vidyā yato viśuddhaṃ caraṇaṃ hi tasya |
ataḥ svayaṃbhūr munisattamaḥ sa babhūva vidyācaraṇopapannaḥ ||
yataḥ kṛtsnaṃ jñeyaṃ munir avagatas tena sugato
yato vā sanmārgaṃ svayam adhigatas tena sugataḥ |
prajābhyaḥ kāruṇyād ajaram amaraṃ trāṇam abhayaṃ
yato vā saddharmaṃ gadati sugatas tena sugataḥ ||
lokaś ca lokaprabhavaś ca tena lokasya caivāstagamo yathāvat |
mārgaś ca lokāstagamāya citto yasmād ato lokavid aprameyaḥ ||
samena sarvajñatayāvipāryayā sudeśikatvād asamena cakṣuṣā |
yato na kaścit sadṛśo 'sti nādhikas tato maharṣir bhagavān anuttaraḥ ||
yato mahākāruṇikena dhīmatā hitāya caukṣyeṇa ca mārdavena ca |
narā vinītā x x x xim utpathād ato mahātmā naradamyasārathiḥ ||
yasmāt sarvajñaḥ sarvadoṣaprahīṇo dharmasyotkṛṣṭasyācyutasyopadeṣṭā |
yasmān nirmokṣaṃ tena vāpsyanti śiṣyās tasmād devānāṃ manuṣyāṇāṃ ca śāstā ||
anupagatam atītam abhyupetaṃ caradacarat kṣayitākṣaraṃ ca kṛtsnam |
yata iha sa bubodha bodhimūle budhamahito bhagavān ataḥ sa buddhaḥ ||
