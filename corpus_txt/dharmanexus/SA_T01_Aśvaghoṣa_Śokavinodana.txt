kaścit priyaviyogārtaḥ pradīptaḥ śokavahninā |
dhṛtim ālambya yatnena svacittaṃ paribhāṣate || 1 ||
prāptakarmapathārūḍho vināhūtena yo janaḥ |
gato vināparādhena tatra kiṃ paritapyase || 2 ||
yadi tasyaiva maraṇaṃ bhaven nānyasya kasyacit |
uccair ākrandituṃ yuktaṃ mahān paribhavo hy ayam || 3 ||
atha sarvasya jātasya sthitaṃ maraṇam agrataḥ |
sāmānyaṃ vyasanaṃ dṛṣṭvā na śokaṃ kartum arhasi || 4 ||
priyasaṃgamalobhena janaḥ śokena dahyate |
camarī vālalobhena dāveneva vanāntare || 5 ||
yadā sarvaiḥ prayātavyaṃ yatra tatra gato hy asau |
kimarthaṃ kriyate śokas tasmin pūrvataraṃ gate || 6 ||
mṛtyor atyugradaṇḍasya pramattā khalv iyaṃ prajā |
vane jighāṃsoḥ siṃhasya mṛgīvābhimukhī sthitā || 7 ||
sarvopāyair yadā nāsti martavyasya pratikriyā |
dhṛtim ālambase kasmāj jalān mīna ivoddhṛtaḥ || 8 ||
kaścit tāvat tvayā dṛṣṭaḥ śruto vā śaṅkito 'pi vā |
kṣitau vā yadi vā svarge yo jāto na mariṣyati || 9 ||
traidhātukam idaṃ kṛtsnaṃ dahyate 'nityatāgninā |
dāvāgninā pradīptena vanaṃ kusumitaṃ yathā || 10 ||
nityasaṃjñāviparyastā bhavāgrād api jantavaḥ |
kṛṣyante mṛtyupāśena pāśeneva mahāgajāḥ || 11 ||
niṣevya dhyānajaṃ saukhyaṃ brahmā brahmālayāt punaḥ |
nipātyate 'nityatayā nadīkūlam ivāmbunā || 12 ||
purandarasahasrāṇi cakravartiśatāni ca |
nirvāpitāni kālena pradīpā iva vāyunā || 13 ||
gatvāpi dūram ākāśaṃ pañcābhijñā maharṣayaḥ |
tatra gantum aśaktās te yatra mṛtyor agocaraḥ || 14 ||
pṛthivī dahyate yatra meruś cāpi viśīryate |
śuṣyate sāgarajalaṃ śarīre tatra kā kathā || 15 ||
vajrasāraśarīrāṇāṃ buddhānāṃ yady anityatā |
kadalīgarbhatulyeṣu kā cintānyeṣu dehiṣu || 16 ||
hriyate mṛtyunā jantuḥ pariṣvakto 'pi bāndhavaiḥ |
sāgarāntarjalagato garuḍeneva pannagaḥ || 17 ||
hā jīvaloka hā kānte krandamānaṃ sudāruṇam |
maṇḍūka iva sarpeṇa gīryate mṛtyunā jagat || 18 ||
agatvā khalv ayaṃ lokaḥ kāryapāraṃ sudustaram |
praviśaty ānanaṃ mṛtyoḥ poto vā makarālayam || 19 ||
paralokaṃ yadā kaścid gacchantaṃ nānugacchati |
nirviśeṣo bhavet tatra dveṣyo vā yadi vā priyaḥ || 20 ||
kṛtvā parajane snehaṃ janaḥ svajanasaṃjñayā |
majjaty āśāmaye paṅke mahāpaṅke yathā gajaḥ || 21 ||
labdhās tyaktāś ca saṃsāre yāvanto bāndhavās tvayā |
na santi khalu tāvantyo gaṅgāyām api bālukāḥ || 22 ||
ya eva te prayatnena lālitaḥ putrasaṃjñayā |
sa eva janmāntaritas tāḍitaḥ śatrusaṃjñayā || 23 ||
yasyaiva te stanau pītau svajanasya bhavāntare |
tasyaiva rudhiraṃ pītaṃ hatvā māṃsaṃ ca bhakṣitam || 24 ||
ya eva te bhaved bhartā śataśaḥ śirasārcitaḥ |
sa eva te bhavaddāsaḥ pādena śataśo hataḥ || 25 ||
anyathā dṛśyate pūrvam anyathā parivartate |
janaḥ kāraṇabhāvena saptarṣīṇāṃ yathā gaṇaḥ || 26 ||
samāgamya viśīryante yathā khe varṣabindavaḥ |
samāgamya vinaśyanti saṃsāre prāṇinas tathā || 27 ||
saṃgamo vigamaś caiva muhūrtam iha dehinām |
nadyām udbhrāntavelāyāṃ kāṣṭhānāṃ plavatām iva || 28 ||
aho mohasya sāmarthyaṃ yena vyāmohitaṃ jagat |
viyogaduḥkhaṃ vismṛtya saṃyoge praṇayaḥ kṛtaḥ || 29 ||
raṅgabhūmir na sā kācic chuddhāvāsavivarjitā |
vicitraiḥ karmanepathyair yatra sattvair na nāṭitam || 30 ||
yat pītaṃ kvathitaṃ tāmraṃ narakeṣu punaḥ punaḥ |
tatpramāṇaṃ jalaṃ naiva samudreṣv api vidyate || 31 ||
purīṣabhakṣaṇaṃ yac ca śvavarāhagatau kṛtam |
meroḥ parvatarājasya pramāṇād adhikaṃ bhavet || 32 ||
ruditaṃ yac ca saṃsāre bandhūnāṃ viprayogataḥ |
teṣāṃ netrāśrubindūnāṃ samudro 'pi na bhājanam || 33 ||
śirāṃsi yāni cchinnāni bhojanārthe parasparam |
teṣām uccataro rāśir brahmalokād viśiṣyate || 34 ||
bhakṣitāḥ pāṃsavo ye 'tra kṛmibhūtair bubhukṣitaiḥ |
pūraṇaṃ tat suparyāptaṃ kṣīrodasya mahodadheḥ || 35 ||
kṣuttarṣaduḥkhaṃ yat prāptaṃ pretaloke sudāruṇam |
tac chrutvā kaḥ sahṛdayaḥ svamāṃsāny api na tyajet || 36 ||
nṛṣu dāridryadoṣeṇa yānubhūtā viḍambanā |
tāṃ vaktum asamartho 'smi jihvā lajjāvatīva me || 37 ||
nākapṛṣṭhe ciraṃ saukhyam anubhūya divaukasaḥ |
atṛptā viṣayair divyaiḥ śuṣkakāṣṭhair ivāgnayaḥ || 38 ||
punas tripiṣṭapād bhraṣṭā gāṃ patanti gatāyuṣaḥ |
śubhakarmaparikṣīṇāś chinnapakṣāḥ khagā iva || 39 ||
karmāviddhaṃ jagad idaṃ yasmād bhramati cakravat |
tasmāt saṅgaṃ parityajya mokṣe buddhir niveśyatām || 40 ||
