§1 evaṃ mayā śrutam ekasmin samaye bhagavān śrāvastyāṃ viharati sma jetavane 'nāthapiṇḍadasyārāme | athāyuṣmān ānando yena bhagavāṃs tenopasaṃkrāntaḥ, upasaṃkramya bhagavataḥ pādau śirasā vanditvaikānte 'sthāt | ekānte sthita āyuṣmān ānando bhagavantam etad avocat |
§2 1) kiṃ bhadanta bhagavan kuśalamūlaṃ saṃmārjanapradānasya | 2) kiṃ maṇḍalakapradānasya | 3) kiṃ triśaraṇagamanaśikṣāpadānām | 4) kiṃ tathāgatānām añjalikarmaṇaḥ | 5) kiṃ bhadanta bhagavan kuśalamūlaṃ saṃsāre na kṣīyate, na paryādīyate, akṣayaṃ ca nirvāṇam upanayati | 6) kiṃ tathāgatabimbakaraṇāt |
§3 bhagavān āha | pañcānuśaṃsā ānanda saṃmārjanapradāne | katame pañca | yadutātmanaś cittaṃ prasīdati | pareṣāṃ ca cittaṃ prasīdati | devatāś cāttamanaso bhavati | prāsādikasaṃvartanīyaṃ karma kṛtaṃ bhavaty upacitam | kāyasya bhedāt sugatau svargaloke deveṣūpapadyate |
§4 yaḥ kaścid ānanda prasannacittas tathāgatam uddiśya caturaśraṃ maṇḍalakaṃ kurute, tasyāham ānandottarakuruṃ dvīpaṃ vadāmy aiśvaryādhipatyāya | kāyasya bhedāt trāyastriṃśeṣu deveṣūpapadyate |
§5 yaḥ kaścid ānanda prasannacitto buddhaṃ bhagavantam uddiśyārdhacandrākṛtiṃ maṇḍalakaṃ kurute, tasyāham ānanda pūrvavidehaṃ dvīpaṃ vadāmy aiśvaryādhipatyāya | kāyasya bhedād yāmeṣu deveṣūpapadyate |
§6 yaḥ kaścid ānanda tathāgatam uddiśya vartulaṃ maṇḍalakaṃ kurute, tasyāham ānandāparagodānīyaṃ dvīpaṃ vadāmy aiśvaryādhipatyāya | kāyasya bhedāt tuṣiteṣu deveṣūpapadyate |
§7 yaḥ kaścid ānanda buddham uddiśya śakaṭākṛtiṃ maṇḍalakaṃ kurute, tasyāham ānanda jambūdvīpaṃ dvīpaṃ vadāmy aiśvaryādhipatyāya | kāyasya bhedān nirmāṇaratiṣu deveṣūpapadyate |
§8 yaḥ kaścid ānanda buddhaṃ śaraṇaṃ gacchati, dharmaṃ śaraṇaṃ gacchati, saṃghaṃ śaraṇaṃ gacchati, pañca śikṣāpadāni saṃgṛhya paripālayati, tasyāhaṃ kuśalamūlasyānandāprameyam asaṃkhyeyaṃ puṇyaṃ vadāmi | taṃ na śakyaṃ śrāvakapratyekabuddhaiḥ paryantam upagṛhītuṃ yāvannirvāṇaparyantaṃ vadāmi |
