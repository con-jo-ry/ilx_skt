dyūtādīnavapradarśane vācyam |
anekadoṣāyatane praśrayaśrīpramāthini |
dyūte kiṃ nāma tad dṛṣṭvā matir yat syāt sacetasaḥ || 12.1 ||
tatra doṣagaṇaprabhavatvād anekadoṣāyatanaṃ dyūtaṃ pratyavagantavyam | tad dhi |
ānandaḥ śātravāṇāṃ paribhavabhavanaṃ vairakarmāntaśālā
māyāśāṭhyapratiṣṭhā kalikaluṣavidhiḥ pāpasaṃkāradhānam |
mohasyākrīḍabhūmir guṇadhanadahanaḥ praśrayaśrīpramāthi
dyūtaṃ kīrtipravāsaḥ paradhanaharaṇaprārthanānarthabījam || 12.2 ||
kiṃ ca bhūyaḥ |
dhairyasyāpanayaḥ śamasya vilayaḥ sadvṛttaśasyāśaniḥ
saṃrambhoddhavalobhadoṣarajasām utkarṣaṇo mārutaḥ |
dyūtaṃ nāma guṇoparāgasamayaḥ pāpānalasyendhanaṃ
pāruṣyādyaśubhapralāpamukharo ghoraḥ pramādagrahaḥ || 12.3 ||
api ca | kadā nāma tatprasaktamateḥ svasti syāt | dyūtaparo hi |
jitvoddhavākulamatir na śamaṃ paraiti
tṛṣṇāṃ vivardhayati caiva hi doṣadhātrīm |
duḥkhaṃ paraṃ samupayāti jitaś ca yasmān
na dyūtam arhati tato 'pi budhaḥ prapattum || 12.4 ||
nindaty api svajana enaṃ
lāghavam abhyupaiti suhṛdāṃ ca |
na prārthyate svajanabhāve
yāti parikṣayaṃ ca dhanam asya || 12.5 ||
tatraitat syāt | anaikāntaḥ khalv atra yad uta dyūtaprasaktamateḥ dhanaparikṣayaḥ syāt | nalaṃ hi rājānaṃ dyūte parājitya tadrājyam avāpa puṣkaro rājā | śakunir yaudhiṣṭhiraṃ dhanaṃ viduram amātyaṃ ca lebhe | bhāryāṃ śulkārthaṃ pūrṇako yakṣaḥ prāptaḥ | vijayaśrīr dyūte | tasmād anekānta eṣa yad uta dyūtaprasaktamater dhanaparikṣayaḥ syāt |
ataś ca prārthanīya eva svajanabhāve syān na ca lāghavam upeyāt suhṛdāṃ svajananindālakṣatāṃ ca |
ity atra brūmahe | nālaṃ yadṛcchayā siddhyā vipadbhūyaskaram arthaṃ prapattum | yadṛcchayā hi kaścit kvacid dyūte kāṃcid arthalavamātrām anuprāpnoti |
prāyeṇa tu dyūte 'nuraktamatayaḥ tatropayuktasarvasvāḥ kaupīnapracchādane 'py aparyāptaprakṣīṇamalinavasanāḥ kṣutparikṣāmavivarṇadīnavapuṣaḥ śokavaktavyahṛdayā rajaḥsaṃpṛktā rūkṣadarśanāḥ suhṛdām apy aviśvāsyā jagadavajñābhājanabhūtās tattadakāryamatayaḥ paribhramantaḥ pṛthivīṃ dṛśyante, nalavad yudhiṣṭhiravac ca saṃrodhaduḥkhitāḥ,
pariluptakaracaraṇāṅguliparvāṇaś ca kecit | tasmād bāhulyatayā prasiddham etat | bhogāś cāsya parikṣayaṃ yāntīti | tataś cāśeṣadoṣotpattiḥ |
yo 'pi ca dyūtāt kāṃcid dravyamātrām adhigacchati, kadācit so 'pi tadāsvādadarśanāt samutthāpyamānaviṣamalobhaḥ pratāryamāṇo jayāśayā punar dyūte pravṛttas tāṃ ca dhanamātrāṃ taduttarāṃ ca nāśayatīty upapadyamānam evaitat |
aikāntikī yady api ca prasiddhir
dyūtaprasaṅgena bhaved dhanasya |
mīmāṃsyam etad viduṣā tathāpi
guṇānurakṣāpravaṇātmakaḥ syāt || 12.6 ||
dyūtād dhanaṃ nidhanam eva vinā ca tena
śreyo bhavet kim anayor iti cintyamāne |
śreyo bhaven nidhanam akṣatadharmabuddher
na dyūtadoṣamalinasya dhanasya rāśiḥ || 12.7 ||
api ca |
tyaktvā hriyaṃ viśadam eva sadharmacintāṃ
pāpaiḥ sametya nirayasya vivṛtya mārgam |
śocyatvam etya viduṣāṃ svayaśaḥ pramṛdya
dyūtād ya icchati dhanaṃ kim asau na kuryāt || 12.8 ||
tatraitat syāt | ratinimittā dyūte pravṛttir na dravyādānalobhāt |
ity atra brūmahe |
ratir api ca bhaved anena yā
bahuvidhadoṣamalīmasātmanām |
guṇagaṇasadayaḥ kathaṃ nu tāṃ
paravanitām iva cintayed api || 12.9 ||
ratiṃ ca dharmārthaguṇopasaṃhitām
uśanti santo na tu tadvirodhinīm |
ratiṃ hi pāpām anubhūya bhūyasīm
apāyadurgeṣu ratiṃ vigāhate || 12.10 ||
sthite tu mṛtyau hi jarorukārmuke
samudyatavyādhiśare kuto ratiḥ |
svakarmadoṣagrathite sthite 'grataḥ
punarbhave ca vyasanaśramāśraye || 12.11 ||
yad api syāt kaleyam iti tatparijñānārtham atra pravartamānasya ka iva doṣaḥ syāt |
ity atra brūmahe |
anekadoṣodayasaṃkalā kalā
vimohitatvāc ca guṇakriyā na ca |
yad eva tu syād guṇapakṣasiddhaye
yateta tenaiva yaśo'rthasiddhaye || 12.12 ||
priyam api ca na tan niṣevitavyaṃ
svahitapathād apavṛttilakṣaṇaṃ yat |
nihitam iva hitaṃ tu yatra tasminn
arucim apāsya bhajed yathauṣadhaṃ tat || 12.13 ||
dharmāśrayaṃ svahitasādhanam uttamaṃ tu
pretyeha cārthasukhamānayaśaḥphalatvāt |
dyūtādikād iti mano vinivartya pāpān
maṅgalyadānadamasaṃyamabhūṣaṇaḥ syāt || 12.14 ||
iti |
