kṛtyakalpataruḥ
śuddhikāṇḍam
śrīgaṇēśāya nama
[oṃ namo bhagavate vāsudevāya]
maṅgalācaraṇam
[1]
varṇād apy ucitārjanād api bhṛśaṃ śuddair yadīyair amī
daurgatyād api duṣkṛtād api gatāḥ śuddhiṃ dvijāḥ kāñcanaiḥ |
dharmendor udayācalaḥ sa daśame kāṇḍe ‘tra lakṣmīdharaḥ
śuddhiṃ śuddhimatāṃ varo viracayaty ācāravārāṃ nidhiḥ ||
atha pratijñā
āśaucaṃ maraṇe pūrvaṃ jananāśaucam eva ca |
niyamāś ca tayoḥ sarva āśaucaṃ varṇasaṃkare ||
[2]        āśaucaṃ saṃnipāte ‘tha tathāśaucaṃ videśage |
bālādyaśaucaṃ stryāśaucaṃ garbhasrāve tataḥ param ||
mṛyor viśeṣeṇāśaucam asapiṇḍe mṛte tathā |
nihārakādyāśaucaṃ cāpy āśaucarahitāṃs tataḥ ||
sapiṇḍalakṣaṇaṃ pretanirhāraṃ pretasaṃskṛtim |
pretasyodakadānaṃ cāpy udakārhanirūpaṇam ||
śokāpanodanaṃ tadvat praveśaḥ svīyaveśmani |
asthisaṃcayanaṃ dehasnānaśuddhī tathaiva ca ||
dravyaśuddhiṃ tathaivātra śuddhim atyantadūṣite |
bhūśuddhiṃ jalaśuddhiṃ cāpy aśuddheś ca nirākriyām ||
daśame śuddhikāṇḍe tu vrate lakṣmīdharaḥ kramāt |
aṣṭāviṃśatiparvāṇi tattvajñānaviśuddhadhīḥ ||

atha maraṇāśaucam
pretaśuddhiḥ
tatra manuḥ [5.58]

caturṇām api varṇānāṃ yathāvad anupūrvaśaḥ ||
pretagrahaṇaṃ jātasyāpy upalakṣaṇam |
hārītaḥ |
dvividham eva śaucaṃ bhavati bāhyam ābhyantaraṃ ca | tatra bāhyaṃ trividham — kulaśaucam annaśaucaṃ śarīraśaucaṃ ca | sūtakamṛtakayor daśarātram ubhayataḥ kulasyāśaucam |
“ubhayataḥ” iti, ubhayoḥ sūtakamṛtakayor ity arthaḥ |
devalaḥ |

sapiṇḍān mātṛbandhūṃś ca yatra kvacana gacchataḥ ||
[4]        yonijñātidvijeṣv iṣṭam āśaucaṃ sahavāsiṣu |

kāraṇād gacchan preṣyatvaṃ tadāśaucyaṃ na tān vrajet |

satataṃ vaiśvadevaś ca tatrāśaucaṃ na vidyate ||
dakṣaḥ |
sadyaḥśaucaṃ tathaikāhas tryahaś caturahas tathā |
ṣaḍdaśadvādaśāhaś ca pakṣo māsas tathaiva ca ||
maraṇāntaṃ tathā cānyad daśapakṣās tu sūtake |
upanyāsakrameṇaiva vakṣyāmy aham aśeṣataḥ ||
granthārthato vijānāti vedam aṅgaiḥ samanvitam |

dakṣaḥ |

[6] parāśaraḥ [3.18; 5.19] |
agnayo yatra hūyante vedo vā yatra paṭhyate |
satataṃ vaiśvadevas tu nāśaucaṃ mṛtasūtake ||
“nāśaucam” sadyaḥśauce tātparyam |
brahmacārī gṛhe yatra hūyate ca hutāśanaḥ |
saṃparkavinivṛttānāṃ na pretaṃ naiva sūtakam ||
ekāhād brāhmaṇaḥ śuddhyed yo ‘gnivedasamanvitaḥ |
tryahāt kevalavedas tu nirguṇo daśabhir dinaiḥ ||

pañcāhenāgnihīnas tu daśāhād brāhmaṇabruvaḥ | iti |
jābāliḥ |
ekadvitriguṇair yuktāḥ catustryekadinaiḥ kramāt |
sarve ‘pi sarvayogena sadyaḥśaucāḥ prakīrtitāḥ ||
“ekaguṇaḥ” kevalavedādhyāyī | “dviguṇaḥ” vedasmārtāgnimān | “triguṇaḥ” vedasmārthaśrautāgnimān | “sarve” brāhmaṇāḥ | “sarvayoga” “granthārthato vijānāti” iti vākyoktaguṇayogaḥ | atra caiṣāṃ vākyānām ayaṃ tātparyārthaḥ | vedāṅgādisahitaṃ samyag granthārthato vijānato ‘gnihotrādikriyāvataś ca puruṣasya yad atrāśucitvam uktaṃ tad aśauciputrādisaṃparkarahitasyāvagantavyam,
saṃparkād duṣyate vipro janane maraṇe ‘pi ca |
saṃparkavinivṛttānāṃ na pretaṃ naiva sūtatam ||
[8] iti parāśareṇābhidhānāt | evaṃvidhasyāpi puṃsaḥ putrādyaśaucasaṃparke saty aśaucinām ivāśaucaṃ bhavati | upapadyate caivaṃ vedāgnihotravatām api saṃparkiṇāṃ daśāhāt śaucasamācāraḥ | yaḥ punar evaṃvidho vedamātrādhyāyy amīmāṃsitatadartho ‘gnihotravān vedādhyayanasmārtāgnimān vā puruṣas tasya saṃparkād evaikāhatryahāśaucaṃ bhavati | tathāvidhāśaucaviṣayatvena ca “agnihotriṇām agnihotram” ityādi viṣṇu-gautamādyuktasyāgni-hotravahanādikriyāviṣayasyāśaucābhāvasyopapattiḥ | na viśeṣapravṛttasya śāstrasyāśvastanikādi-viṣayatvena saṅkoca ity ucitam, anenākāreṇa saṅkoce pramāṇābhāt | na cāśvastanikādi-vṛttividhānenāpy aśucasaṅkocākṣepaḥ, vedārthajñānādiguṇayogyśaucisaṃparkavarjaka-puruṣaviṣayatvenāpy aśvastanādivṛttividhānopapatteḥ | vedārthajñānādiguṇayuktāśauca-saṃparkarahitapuruṣaviṣayatvena vāśaucasaṅkoce,
granthārthato vijānāti vedam aṅgaiḥ samanvitam |
iti, tathā “agnayo yatra hūyante”, tathā
saṃparkeṣu nivṛttānāṃ na pretaṃ naiva sūtakam |
ityādi vacanāny eva [9] pramāṇam | yac cāgnivedasamanvita eva dakṣa-parāśarābhyām ekāhāśaucam uktam, śaṅkha-bṛhaspatibhyāṃ tryahāśaucam, tatraikāhatryahāśaucavacanayor ekaviṣayasamāveśābhāvāc chrautasmārtāgnimadviṣayatvena yathāsaṃkhyaṃ vyavasthā yujyate hi | śrautāgnimati puruṣa ekāhāśaucavacanaṃ tasya smārtāgnimadapekṣayābhyarhitatvāt |
