§1 evaṃ mayā śrutam ekasmin samaye bhagavān rājagṛhe viharati sma gṛdhrakūṭe parvate mahatā bhikṣusaṃghena sārdham ardhatrayodaśabhir bhikṣuśataiḥ parivṛtaḥ puraskṛto dharmaṃ deśayati sma |
§2 tena khalu punaḥ samayena nandiko nāmopāsakaḥ pañcopāsakaśataparivāro rājagṛhān nagarān niṣkramya yena bhagavāṃs tenopasaṃkrāntaḥ | upasaṃkramya bhagavataḥ pādau śirasā vanditvā bhagavantaṃ tripradakṣiṇīkṛtyaikānte nyaṣīdat | tāny api pañcopāsakaśatāni bhagavataḥ pādau śirasā vanditvaikānte niṣadanti sma |
ekānte niṣaṇṇaś ca nandikopāsako bhagavantam idam avocat |
§3 yānīmāni bhagavatā jānatā paśyatā tathāgatenārthatā samyaksaṃbuddhenopāsakasya pañcaśikṣāpadāni prajñaptāni, tāni tasya vyatikrāmataḥ kiṃ vipākāni bhavantīti |
§4 evam ukte bhagavān nandikopāsakam idam avocat | sādhu sādhu nandika, udārā te buddhiḥ, bhadrikā te mīmāṃsā, kalyāṇaṃ te pratibhānam, yas tvaṃ tathāgatam etam arthaṃ paripraṣṭavyaṃ manyase | tena hi nandika daśādīnavaṃ nāma dharmaparyāyaṃ deśayiṣyāmi | tac chṛṇu sādhu ca suṣṭhu ca manasikuru, bhāṣiṣye 'haṃ te |
sādhu bhagavann iti nandikopāsako bhagavataḥ pratyaśrauṣīt |
§5 bhagavān etad avocat | daśeme nandikādīnavāḥ prāṇātipāte veditavyāḥ | katame daśa | tadyathā sādṛṣṭikaṃ vairaṃ prasravati, anyāsu jātiṣu ca vairānubandho bhavati | amanaāpadarśano bhavati | pāpakān svapnān paśyati | svayaṃ saṃtrāsam āpadyate | duḥkhaṃ ca svapiti, duḥkhaṃ ca pratibudhyate | pāpakaṃ ca vicintayati | saṃmūḍhaś ca kālaṃ karoti |
alpāyuḥsaṃvartanīyaṃ ca karma karoty upacinoti | kāyasya bhedāt paraṃ maraṇād apāyadurgatinarakeṣūpapadyate | sacet punas tataś cyuta itthatvam āgacchati manuṣyāṇāṃ sabhāgatāyai, sa yatra yatropapadyate, tatra tatrālpāyur bhavati bahuglāniś ca | ime nandika daśādīnavāḥ prāṇātipāte veditavyāḥ |
§6 daśeme nandikādīnavā adattādāne veditavyāḥ | guruvairī ca bhavati | gurvāśaṅkī ca bhavati | akālacārī ca bhavati | pāpamitragṛhītaś ca bhavati | kalyāṇamitravivarjitaś ca bhavati | duḥśīlaś ca bhavati | rājopasargī ca bhavati, duṣṭopasargī ca bhavati, daṇḍopasargī ca bhavati | bhogavyasanasaṃvartanīyaṃ ca karma karoty upacinoti |
kāyasya bhedāt paraṃ maraṇād apāyadurgativinipātanarakeṣūpapadyate | sacet punas tataś cyuta itthatvam āgacchati manuṣyāṇāṃ sabhāgatāyai, sa yatra yatropapadyate, tatra tatra daridro bhavati, alpabhogaś ca | ye ca tasya bhogā bhavanty utthānavīryāvigatā jaṅghābāhubalopārjitāḥ svedamalāvakṣiptās te pañcabhir upakleśair upakliṣṭā bhavanti |
katamaiḥ pañcabhiḥ | rājato vā, caurato vā, agnito vā, udakato vā, apriyadāyādebhyo vā nihitāś cāsya vinaśyanti | ime nandika daśādīnavā adattādāne veditavyāḥ |
§7 daśeme nandikādīnavāḥ kāmamithyācāre veditavyāḥ | katame daśa | yeṣāṃ dārān abhimardayati taiś cāsya saha vairaṃ bhavati | bhāryayā saha vivādo bhavati | akuśalāś cāsya dharmā vivardhayanti, kuśalāś cāsya dharmāḥ parihīyante | ātmā cāsyāgupto bhavaty arakṣitaḥ, jīvitaṃ cāsyāguptaṃ bhavaty arakṣitam | putradāraṃ cāsyāguptaṃ bhavaty arakṣitam |
dhanaṃ cāsyāguptaṃ bhavaty arakṣitam, gṛhaṃ cāsyāguptaṃ bhavaty arakṣitam | mitrāmātyajñātisālohitāś cāsya viśvāsaṃ na gacchanti | sasāpatnyasaṃvartanīyaṃ ca karma karoty upacinoti | kāyasya bhedāt paraṃ maraṇād apāyadurgativinipātanarakeṣūpapadyate |
sacet punas tataś cyuta itthatvam āgacchati manuṣyāṇāṃ sabhāgatāyai, sa yadi strī bhavati, sasāpatnyaṃ bhartāraṃ pratilabhate, atha puruṣo bhavati, putradāram asyārakṣitaṃ bhavati | ime nandika daśādīnavāḥ kāmamithyācāre veditavyāḥ |
§8 daśeme nandikādīnavā mṛṣāvāde veditavyāḥ | katame daśa | pūtikaṃ cāsya mukhaṃ bhavati | devatāś cāsya kāyād apakrāmanty amanuṣyāś cāsyāvatāraṃ labhante | satyam api bhāṣyamāṇasyāśraddheyaṃ vacanaṃ bhavati | utpanneṣu kāryeṣu nainaṃ vidvāṃsaḥ paripraṣṭavyaṃ manyante | abhūtaś cāsyāvarṇo vaistāriko bhavati | apratyutthānārhaś ca bhavati |
bahuduḥkhadaurmanasyo bhavati | abhyākhyānabahulasaṃvartanīyaṃ ca karma karoty upacinoti | kāyasya bhedāt paraṃ maraṇād apāyadurgativinipātanarakeṣūpapadyate | sacet punas tataś cyuta itthatvam āgacchati manuṣyāṇāṃ sabhāgatāyai, sa yatra yatropapadyate, tatra tatrābhyākhyānabahulo bhavati | ime nandika daśādīnavā mṛṣāvāde veditavyāḥ |
§9 pañcatriṃśad ime nandikādīnavāḥ surāmaireyamadyapramādasthāne veditavyāḥ | katame pañcatriṃśat | sāṃdṛṣṭikaṃ kośakṣayaḥ | rogānām āyatanam | kalahavigrahavivardhanam | kaupīnasaṃdarśanam | akīrtisaṃjananam | prajñādaurbalyakaraṇam | anadhigatāś ca bhogā nādhigacchanti, adhigatāś ca bhogāḥ parikṣayaṃ paryādānaṃ gacchanti | guhyaṃ ca vivṛṇoti |
karmāntāś cāsya parihīyante | daurbalyakaraṇaṃ ca bhavati | amātṛjñaś ca bhavati | apitṛjñaś ca bhavati | aśrāmaṇyaś ca bhavati | abrāhmaṇyaś ca bhavati | na kulajyeṣṭhāpacāyako bhavati | buddhe 'gauravo bhavati | dharme 'gauravo bhavati | saṃghe 'gauravo bhavati | śikṣāsamādāne cāgauravo bhavati | indriyeṣv aguptadvāro bhavati |
strīṣu cātyarthaṃ pramādam āpadyate | mitrāmātyajñātisālohitānāṃ cāgrāhyo bhavati | bahulāmanaāpaś ca bhavati | bahujanapratikūlaś ca bhavati | adharmacārī bhavati | akarmapratigrāhakaś ca bhavati | saddharmaparivarjitaś ca bhavati | apatrāpyaparivarjitaś ca bhavati | na cainaṃ vidvāṃsaḥ pratyayeṣu sthāneṣu paripraṣṭavyaṃ manyante |
pramādaṃ ca kṛtvopekṣako bhavati | tathāgatavacane na saṃtiṣṭhati | dūrībhavati nirvāṇāt | unmattasaṃvartanīyaṃ ca karma karoty upacinoti | kāyasya bhedāt paraṃ maraṇād apāyadurgativinipātanarakeṣūpapadyate | sacet punas tataś cyuta itthatvam āgacchati manuṣyāṇāṃ sabhāgatāyai, sa yatra yatropapadyate, tatra tatra unmatto bhavati mūḍhasmṛtiḥ |
ime nandika pañcatriṃśad ādīnavāḥ surāmaireyamadyapramādasthāne veditavyāḥ |
% for the difference between surā etc., see AKBh(P) 219,6-10.
§10 idam avocad bhagavān | idam uktvā sugato hy athāparam etad uvāca śāstā |
prāṇātipātaṃ vadhabandhanaṃ ca
vivarjayed bhūtahitānukampī |
yathātmano rakṣasi jīvitaṃ priyaṃ
tathā pareṣām api rakṣa jīvitam || [1]
parasya vittaṃ bahuduḥkhasaṃcitaṃ
na cāpy adattaṃ manasāpi saṃspṛśet |
dṛṣṭvāpi loke priyaviprayogaṃ
tathaiva cānyeṣu hitāya tiṣṭhet || [2]
parastriyaṃ cābharaṇair alaṃkṛtāṃ
vivarjayet prajvalitām ivoktām |
svakeṣu dāreṣu labheta tuṣṭiṃ
na kāmahetor viṣatulyam ācaret || [3]
na cātmahetor na parasya kāraṇāt
sasaṃprajanyo hi mṛṣā na bhāṣet |
aparopaghātī aparopatāpī
samīkṣya vācāṃ madhurām udīrayet || [4]
na madyapānābhiratena jantunā
kāryaṃ sukham ātmahitaṃ parasya vā |
vyāmohavaivarṇyakarīṃ ca vāruṇīṃ
na tāṃ pibed dhālahalaṃ yathā viṣam || [5]
prāṇaṃ na hanyān na haret parasvaṃ
mṛṣā na bhāṣen na pibec ca madyam |
parasya bhāryāṃ manasāpi necchet
svargaṃ ya icchet gṛhavat praviṣṭum || [6]
% anne prāṇiṃ
gṛhīṇa tāvac chitavastradhāriṇām
agāram adhyāvasatām ime 'naghāḥ |
ato bhuyo pravrajitasya deśitā
jinena śikṣāpadadharmadeśanā || [7]
§11 atha nandikopāsaka imaṃ dharmaparyāyaṃ bhagavato 'ntikāc chrutvā hṛṣṭas tuṣṭa udagrāttamanāḥ pramuditaprītisaumanasyajātaḥ, utthāyāsanād bhagavataḥ pādau śirasā vanditvā bhagavantaṃ tripradakṣiṇīkṛtya bhagavato bhāṣitam abhinandyānumodya bhagavato 'ntikāt prakrāntaḥ |
idam avocad bhagavān āttamanāḥ, te ca bhikṣavo bhagavato bhāṣitam abhyanandann iti |
āryanandikaparipṛcchāsūtraṃ tṛtīyaṃ samāptam |
