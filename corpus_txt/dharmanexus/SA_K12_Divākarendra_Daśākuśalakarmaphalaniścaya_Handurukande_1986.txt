Title card has Ācaryakṛiyāsamucchaya (sic!). Six paper leaves, but only two are from the ĀKriSa. The rest is from a work by Divākarendra (anne Divākaracandra) called Daśākuśalakarmaphalaniścaya in 11 chs. An exact copy of the missing first folio can be found separately in Cambridge (Add. 1598 and Add.
1585), for an edition of the first, see Ratna Handurukande, ``Daśākuśalakarmaphala'', in: Kalyāṇī, Journal of Humanities and Social Sciences of the University of Kelaniya, Vols. V and VI, pp. 43--48, 1986--1987. RH did not have access to the latter; in fact, Add. 1585 (now available in colour) is the missing first folio of the NAK ms.
This is numbered 13v (our text begins here, so it was in a Sammelhandschrift with the Sumāgadhāvadāna), the NAK ms. continues up to fol. 17.
