XIV. Pīṭhādiyoginīpaṭala
[79a3] athāto rahasyaṃ vakṣye pīṭhamelāpakottamaṃ || [79a4] sarvabhāvasvabhāvena miśrīsamarasaṃ bhavet ||
advaidhīsamatā cakraṃ trivarṇaṃ sahitaṃ puraṃ || [79a5] caturasraṃ maṇḍalamadhye tu caturviṃśāracakram uttamaṃ ||
tatra madhye mahāpadmaṃ viśvapatraṃ suśobhanaṃ || dalāṣṭakarṇikāyuktaṃ keśarair upaśobhitaṃ ||
[79b1] caturdvāra-aṣṭastambhas toraṇais samalaṃkṛtaṃ || hārārdhahāraracitaṃ sarvalakṣaṇasaṃyutaṃ ||
sūryamaṇḍalamadhye tu vajrasattvaṃ mahāsukhaṃ || [79b2] ālikālidravībhūtaṃ pañcajñānaṃ vibhāvayet ||
tatparāvṛtya sadvajraṃ herukaṃ taṃ vibhāvayet || [79b3] mahāpralayakālāgraṃ sarvadurdāntapāṭanaṃ ||
bhairavaṃ kālarātriñ ca pādākrāntatalīkṛtaṃ || daṃṣṭrākarālavadanaṃ trinetraṃ bhayabhīṣaṇaṃ ||
[79b4] nīlāgravapurdehasthaṃ nīlajvālāgradehakaṃ || kapālamālāmukuṭaṃ pañcabuddhair adhiṣṭhitaṃ ||
viśvavajramukuṭaṃ ardhacandropaśobhitaṃ || [79b5] ṣaḍvaktraṃ vīrabībhatsaṃ śṛṅgārahasitaṃraudraṃ lalitānanaṃ ||
ṣaṇmudrāmudritaṃ dehaṃ nānābharaṇamaṇḍitaṃ || vārāhyānusamāpannaṃ jānudvayasuveṣṭitaṃ ||
[80a1] ālīḍhapadasaṃsthānaṃ pratyālīḍhapade sthitā(ṃ) || hāhākārasahūṃkārahīhīkāra(ṃ) bhayānakaṃ ||
[80a2] hūṃhūṃkārasahaṃkāra-aṭṭaṭṭakāraṃ samuñcet || nīlapītaṃ tathā raktaṃ haritaṃ sitam eva ca ||
ūrdhvavaktraṃ mahāraudraṃ gaganāsitabhoginaṃ || [80a3] vajraghaṇṭāsamāpannaṃ devīkucanipīḍitaṃ ||
rudracarmāmbaradharaṃ sārdharaktasravadrajaṃ || kapālakhaṭvāṅgadharaṃ śūlaṃ karttikāṃ aṅkuśa-
pāśaṃ muṇḍadharaṃ raudraṃ mudgaraḍamarukaṃ tathā || [80a4] śaktiṃ tu jvālamudrāṃ tu lalāṭe-r-upaśobhitaṃ ||
tadvarṇabhujasasthānā(ṃ) muktakeśā(ṃ) tu nagnikāṃ || [80a5] īdṛśīṃ bhāvayen mudrāṃ khaṇḍamaṇḍitamekhalāṃ ||
vārāhyā īdṛśī bhāvyā yoginībhrāntivarjitā || prajñopāyasamāpannā karuṇārāgasatsukhā ||
[80b1] sattvārthahetusaṃbhūtā karuṇārdreṇa cetasā || gaganābhogasaṃbhogā mahākaruṇārasotsukhā ||
ṣaḍgatibhavasaṃśodhyā sattvārthakṛtatatparā || [80b2] śūnyatājñānasaṃbhūtā nirvikalpā nirālayā ||
niḥsvabhāvā parā sūkṣmā bindunādavivarjitāḥ || samatāsamayaṃ dṛṣṭvā sabāhyābhyantarottamāḥ ||
[80b3] hṛdaye jñānasamayañ candramaṇḍalamadhyataḥ || tadvarṇabhujāyudhaṃ dṛṣṭvā candravajraṃ tu bījakaṃ ||
tasyaiva hṛdaye dhyātvā sevāyogaṃ prakalpayet || [80b4] padmanyāsaṃ prakurvīta ḍākinyās tu yathākramaṃ ||
pūrve ḍākinīnyāsam uttare lāmakā tathā || [80b5] paścime khaṇḍarohā ca dakṣiṇe rūpiṇī tathā ||
nīlā ca haritā raktā pītā caiva caturvidhā || trinetravikṛtā raudrā muktakeśā digambarāḥ ||
[81a1] sarvālaṅkārasaṃpūrṇāḥ pañcamudrāṃ tu mudritāḥ || kapālakhaṭvāṅgadharā vāmaskandhasamāśritāḥ ||
ḍamarukarttikā muṇḍaṃ ḍākinīsamayottamaṃ || [81a2] bodhicittodakaiḥ pūrṇāḥ kapālāḥ kalaśopari ||
cakranyāsaṃ kurvīta pūrvasiddhāṃ svādhiṣṭhānādyanukramāt ||
pūrvottare krame pullīrama[81a3]laye khaṇḍakapālinaḥ pracaṇḍā gaganagaurā ||
jālandhare mahākaṅkālacaṇḍākṣī pītā || oḍiyāne kaṅkālaprabhāvatī haritā ||
[81a4] arbude vikaṭadaṃṣṭriṇamahānāsā gaganagaurā ||
godāvaryāṃ surāvairiṇavīramatī pītā || rāmeśvare amitābhakharvarī haritā ||
devīkoṭe vajraprabhalaṅkeśvarī gagana[81a5]gaurā ||
mālave vajradehadrumacchāyā pītā || kāmarūpe aṅkurika-airāvatī haritā ||
oḍre vajrajaṭilamahābhairavā gaganagaurā || [81b1] triśakunau mahāvīravāyuvegā pītā ||
kośalāyāṃ vajrahūṃkārasurābhakṣī haritā || kaliṅge subhadraśyāmādevī gaganagaurā ||
[81b2] lampāke vajrabhadrasubhadrā pītā || kāñcike mahābhairavahayakarṇā haritā ||
himālaye virūpākṣakhagānanā gaganagaurā || [81b3] pretapuryāṃ mahābalacakravegā pītā ||
gṛhadevatāyāṃ ratnavajrakhaṇḍarohā haritā || saurāṣṭre hayagrīvasauṇḍinī gaganagaurā ||
[81b4] suvarṇadvīpe ākāśagarbhacakravarmiṇī pītā || nagare śrīherukasuvīrā haritā ||
atha vā kāyavākcittabhedena varṇaṃ kuryād yathā [82a1] rucau || dvāreṣu ca yathākrameṇa vāmataḥ ||
pūrve kākāsyā uttare ulūkāsyā || paścime śvānāsyā dakṣiṇe śūkarāsyā nīlaraktaharitapītā ||
[82a2] agneyāṃ yamadāḍhi raktā || nairṛtyāṃ yamadūtī nīlā || vāyavyāṃ yamadaṃṣṭrī dhūmradhūsarā ||
aiśānyāṃ yamamathanī sitagaurā ||
[82a3] khaṇḍakapālādayo vīrāḥ pracaṇḍānyādisahitā || ekavaktrāś caturbhujāḥ || ālīḍhapadasthāḥ ||
kapālamālinyo vyāghracarmanivasanāḥ pañcamudrā[82a4]dharā devyā pādasuveṣṭitāḥ || nagnā muṇḍamālāvibhūṣitā(ḥ) ||
trinetrā vajramālā lalāṭe vibhūṣitā vajraghaṇṭāliṅganādvaye yogasamāpannā devīkucanipīḍitāḥ || kapālakhaṭvāṅgaḍamaru-
karāḥ || atha vā trikulabhedataḥ || vajracakraṃ padmañ ca mūlacihnadharāḥ ||
