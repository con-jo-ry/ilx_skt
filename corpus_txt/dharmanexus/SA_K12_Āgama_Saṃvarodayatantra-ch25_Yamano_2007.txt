^76
athānyatamaṃ vakṣyāmi rasāyanavidhottamam |
yena vijñātamātreṇa śarīropāyalakṣaṇam || 1 ||
somasihlakakastūrībhaiṣajyapītacandanam |
pṛthakpṛthak sametaṃ vā jarāmaraṇavañcakam || 2 ||
śreṣṭhaḥ pañcakulotpanno madhyamo jñātisaṃbhavaḥ |
sāmānyastrībhavo hīna vṛddhas taruṇaṃ vivarjayet || 3 ||
kṛṣṇavarṇā subhadrāṅgī kṛṣṇapakṣarajasvalā |
bālikā snigdhakeśī yā sugandhā kṛṣṇatārakā || 4 ||
^77
tasyāḥ puṣpaṃ praśasta syāt sarvābhimatasiddhidam |
śuklapakṣopasaryāyā apraśastaṃ jarādikṛt || 5 ||
ādyāntyavarjitā śuddhā śuddhasphaṭikasaṃnibhā |
praśastā mṛganābhiḥ syāt saṃgrahe kasturi śubham || 6 ||
hatasya tu rasaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ bhaiṣajyasaṃgrahaṃ varam |
kṣīrādibhojanotpannaṃ pītacandanasaṃgrahet || 7 ||
śīrṣajaṃ nalikājātaṃ māṃsajaṃ ca tridhāghṛtam |
uttamaṃ śīrṣajaṃ sarpi madhyamaṃ nalikābhavam |
^78
adhamaṃ māṃsasaṃbhūtaṃ rasāyanavidhottamam || 8 ||
vapuṣaḥ puṣṭikṛc candras turuṣkaḥ sarvarakṣakaḥ |
vyādhighnī mṛganābhiḥ syā bhaiṣajaṃ śāntikārakam |
catuḥsamo bhaven nityaṃ pratyūṣe sevayet sadā || 9 ||
candre carati pātavyaṃ candre carati tāyini |
sūryaḥ sūryeṇa pātavyaṃ sūryo vahati śāstari || 10 ||
etad vastu samāyojyaṃ nasyan dadhyāt nityaśaḥ |
ṣaṇmāsāt kṛtayogena āyur vṛddhati sarvadā || 11 ||
^79
vāmanasyapradānena pariśuddhena karmaṇā |
jīven nirujaṃ sarvaṃ śuklapakṣe śaśī yathā || 12 ||
nirañjanapītaharitaṃ malāmalavivarjitam |
śuddhaś cāmbu bhaved yatra caturdoṣavivarjitam || 13 ||
prasannanirmalaṃ svacchaṃ kiñcit sphaṭikasannibham |
śuddhasphaṭikaṃ bhaved evaṃ piṇḍabandhanam uttamam || 14 ||
matsamāṃsaṃ tathā pānaṃ strīsaṅgaṃ vivarjitam |
dhīro vinito guptaṃ svadehaṃ paripālayet || 15 ||
^80
śrāddhaṃ śiṣyaṃ samāyuktaṃ guhāntasya praveśayet |
devatārādhanaṃ kāryaṃ bali-alisamanvitam || 16 ||
svādhidevatayogena avicchinnaṃ mantrajāpitam |
dānapuṇyaṃ kṛtaṃ kāryaṃ śiṣyaṃ saṃtoṣa kārayet || 17 ||
nirvātasthānasaṃprāptaṃ tiṣṭhen nirvighnīkṛtam |
sukham āsanasaṃprāptaṃ maunaṃ kṛtvā tu buddhimān || 18 ||
svadehaṃ śodhayet samyak saptadivasāni yāvat |
tataḥ paścāt sevayet karma daśavarṣādi tāvat || 19 ||
^81
varṣadvayaṃ ca saṃrakṣāḥ siddhikāle sudhī sadā |
dvādaśavarṣa saṃprāpte valipalitavarjitam || 20 ||
nānārogavinirmuktaṃ jīved ācandratārakam |
devāsuramanuṣyāṇāṃ strīṇāṃ bhavati vallabhaḥ || 21 ||
pañca eva hi vastunāṃ nityaṃ bodha tu bhakṣayet sadā |
etat karma saṃpūrṇam icchāsiddhiḥ pravarttate |
kāmadhenusamaṃ dehaṃ khecarīsiddhim āpnuyāt || 22 ||
buddhāṅgāreṣṭakāreṇunirguṇḍī cātra valgujaiḥ |
dhūttūrapatraniryāsakasturīcandanānvitaiḥ || 23 ||
^82
udvarttayati yaḥ svāṅgaṃ coravālasamanvitaiḥ |
sarvarogavinirmukto nirviṣaḥ syāt sakāntimān || 24 ||
triphalācandanotpannaṃ cūrṇaśītalayā saha |
kastūryā yaḥ pibed varṣaṃ saṃbhaven nirjarārujam || 25 ||
triphalācandanacūrṇaṃ kastūrī saha sevayet |
ṣaṇmāsamātra saṃsevyaṃ dīrghāyuḥ syāt sarūpavān || 26 ||
kastūrītriphalāyuktaṃ pāṣāṇapātrasaṃsthitam |
yaḥ pibet satataṃ śītaṃ saṃbhaven nirjarārujam || 27 ||
^83
candanaṃ trikaṣāyād yaṃ ṣaṇmāsaṃ bhakṣayen naraḥ |
yas tasya jāyate siddhir īpsitā nātra saṃśayaḥ || 28 ||
iti rasāyanavidhipaṭalaḥ pañcaviṃśatitamaḥ |
