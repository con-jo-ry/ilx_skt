Title: *Buddhastotra
Author: *Vasudhārā
Source: Cambridge University Library Or.143
Tibetan: 1114
Input: Péter-Dániel Szántó, 26/01/2025 Budapest
Notes: Fragment of about two an a half verses from an eight-verse praise of the Buddha, possibly by a female poet. Penned in a later hand to fill scribal horror vacui after the end of an extract of verses from the Bodhicaryāvatāra and the ye dharmā formula, inscribed in a bold, 11th-century hand. The manuscript otherwise contains elements of the Grahamātṛkādhāraṇī, the Mañjuśrīnāmāṣṭaśataka, and the beginning of an unidentified dhāraṇī.
