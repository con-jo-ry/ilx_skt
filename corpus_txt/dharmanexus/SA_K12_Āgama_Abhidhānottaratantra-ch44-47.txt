athāta saṃpravakṣyāmi ḍākinīnāṃ hṛdayaṃ paraṃ //
vīrāṇāñ ca viśeṣataḥ //
yasya smaraṇamātreṇa samyak siddhi krameṇa tu //
oṃ sarvabuddha[F230]ḍākinīye vajravarṇanīye hūṃ 2 phaṭ svāhā //
[...] [ 142]mantra sarvakarmaḥkaraḥ paraḥ |
paṭhitasiddhi((ṃ)) mahāmantraṃ sarvakāmārthasādhakaṃ |
ḍākinī yoginīnāṃ tu khaṇḍarohā lāmayas tathā ||
oṃ hrīḥ ha ha hūṃ 3 phaṭ ||
aya((ṃ)) mantro mahāmantra sarvakarmaḥkaraḥ paraḥ |
ayam paṭhitasiddhis tu mantreśaṃ mantranāyakaṃ |
khaṇḍakapālādayaḥ sarvavīrāṇā hṛdayaṃ paraṃ |
eṣa mantravaraḥ śreṣṭha sarvakāmārthasādhakā
uttarād api cottaraṃ vīrasamvaram uttamaṃ |
sarv<|ā|><e>ṣām eva vīrmāṇāṃ (!?) mantram eva prakīrttitaṃ |
pātāle yadi vā svarge mantre vāpi yathā punaḥ |
yatkiṃcit pravarttate karma triṣu lokeṣu sādhakaḥ |
tat sarvaṃ sādhayati mantraṃ ((yataḥ))
parataraṃ kiṃcit triṣu lokeṣu sādhakaḥ ||
sarvaḍākinyā vīrā lālaya vakṣye samāsānanta (!) vistarāt |
likhitaṃ parvataṃ divyaṃ nānāpuṣpaphalopamaṃ |
tasyopari bhāvayet maṇḍalaṃ ḍākinyo lāmayas tathā |
yoginī khaṇḍarohā ca vīrā ca | hā hā he heti
caturviṃ[ 142]śati vīrāṇāṃ jātijākinījālasamvare |
pūrvoktāni ca sarvāṇi vīravīrottamāni ca
caturviśativīrāṇāṃ yair vyāptam alikhaṃ (!) jagat |
vīrāṇāṃ ḍākinī caiva yoginī lāmayas tathā |
khaṇḍarohā tathāparā cakrasthāneṣu draṣṭavyā sarvakāryeṣu sādhakaiḥ |
cakrasthitān | pūjayen nityaṃ siddhikāmaḥ susamāhita<|ā|>ḥ |
pūrvoktena vidhānena yajñeḍ ḍākinījālasamvaraṃ |
mahācakra sarvasiddhyālayaṃs tathā |
subhāṣitaṃ buddhakoṭīnāṃ vīrāṇāṃ koṭim eva ca |
ekaikasya vīraḍākinīnāṃ koṭisaṃkhyāparivāraṃ bhavati |
yoginīlāmayas tathā |
khaṇḍarohā viśeṣeṇa sarvasiddhikarā śubhā ||   ||
Abhidhānottarottare ḍākinīvīrahṛdayādvayapaṭalaś catvāriṃśattamaḥ ||   ||
