Yoginījālamahātantra
Nep. prov. Takaoka DH 377
ch. 26 on the ācārya (|| w/ CP) (page seq. uncertain)
athātaḥ saṃpravakṣyāmi vajrācārya vidhīyate |
vinitaśāstraṃ tu sarvatra sarvatra sarvasabhayapradaḥ ||
mantratantraprayogeṇa kalāpaśāntrayovidaḥ |
mādhurya vākya sarveṣu sarvasattveṣu putravat ||
dānādibhi ratā nityaṃ yogadhyānāditatparaḥ |
satyavādī ahiṃsā ca kāruṇyāhikacetasā ||
samatā cittamudrā tu sattvānātha tu bhūtakai |
sattvāśayaviśeṣaṃ ca anāthena tu bāndhavā ||
indriyadehasaṃpurthe priyadarśana rūpavān |
abhiṣekārthatattvajña sphuṭavākyaguṇodadhiḥ ||
pīṭhasaṃvādā nityaṃ ācāryam abhidhīyate |
ācārya śiṣye saṃgrāhya kulino dhano dharmatatsavaḥ ||
niḥkṛpaṃ krodhanaṃ krūraṃ taptamaṃ ca na saṃśayaḥ |
atimukho kathoraś ca prāṇi caṃtu ca rirdayaḥ ||
paradravya hi rātraṃ tu vajrayed guruṇā sadā |
dhīrā vinīta matimāṃ kṣemāvān ārjanāśaṭhā ||
daśākulaparityāgī sattvānāṃ priyadarśanaḥ |
nṛspṛśo paradravyaṃ ca jvalitāgniviṣādivat ||
gurupujā sadā nityaṃ saṭamaṃ darśanotsavam |
dānādibhi ratā nityaṃ paralokābhikāṃcchinaḥ ||
teṣāṃ śiṣya praśasteṣu dikṣayet maṇḍalaṃ śubham |
kṛṭāñjalipuṭā kṛtvā [\verso] adhyeṣet kṛtamānasaḥ ||
tva me śāstā mahāvīra yoginīvarasaṃpuṭaḥ |
icchāmy ahaṃ mahānātha mahābodhinayaṃ dṛḍham ||
dehi me samayaka tvaṃ bodhicittaṃ ca dehi me |
vīravīreśvarīnā ca vārāhīherukasya ca ||
guhyapīṭhādisaṃśuddhiṃ kathayā dehasasthitam |
buddhadharma ca saṃghaṃ ca dehi me śaraṇatrayam ||
praveśayasva mahānātha mahāmokṣapuraṃ varam |
ehi vatsa mahāyāna mantracaryānayaṃ dṛḍham ||
deśayiṣyāmi te samyak bhāṃjana tvaṃ mahānaye |
saṃprāpto jñānam atulaṃ vajramantraprabhāvanai ||
tasmān matim imā vatsa kuru sarvajñaprāptaye |
gurupūjā tathā maitrī buddhabhakti janapriye ||
mūlāpatti parityajya sthūlāpatti vivarjayet |
tattvasyārādhanaṃ kārya hīnayānaṃ tu sevayet ||
atīrṇās tārayiṣyāmi amukto mocayāmy aham |
aśvāsayiṣyāmi sattvānāṃ saṃsāraduḥkhasāgarāt ||
tasya śiṣyasamāyuktaṃ śiṣyārthā ca dhivāsanā |
dayād vipaṃ tu kāṣṭhaṃ ca śucimānāvidhiyutam ||
raktasūtreṇa saṃśatā raktābāhūvikā viśeṣataḥ |
dhīkāramantrasaṃyuśan tasya pradāpayet ||
kāyavākcittasamvaraṃ dadyā śubhāśubhaṃ
svapnatantrasavaṃ nirīkṣayet ||
maṇḍalaṃ praveśayat tatra yatrāndhapaṭare sthitām |
puṣpaṃ prakāśa saṃgṛhya dakṣiṇā saha saṃyutām ||
pūrvajanmādipāpasya nivāpya maṇḍaladarśanāt |
kas tvaṃ ho iti pṛcchat subhāgāha iti uktavān ||
samayodakapathaṃ ca maṇḍale puṣpaṃ kṣapayet |
yadya puṣpaṃ pratitantra tatkulaṃ tu bhaviṣyati ||
udakā makuṭa vajraṃ ghaṇṭhā nāmābhiṣekakam |
pañcatathāgatātmikaṃ śeṣaṃ brahma[\recto]
