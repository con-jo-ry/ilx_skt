Abhidhānottaratantra ch. 43 [Tib. & Ś. 46]
= Lokesh Chandra facsimile ed.
= 1138 AD palm-leaf codex
[ 1384']
samayān atha vakṣe haṃ yogatantreṣu durlabhān |
vā + na yāḍira (?) strī vā siddhiceṣṭāti gacchati |
vijñāyate sudūreṇa pi gṛhe kṣetre vyavasthitāḥ |
nā herukasya parayoga ācāryo yoga + tarāḥ |
asmi tantre sādhakās sādhum eva ca
nāvamantavyā [ 139] nāpy adhiṣeptavyā pūjanīyāś ca bhaktitaḥ |
aṣṭau dūtyo hy ete bhaktiśraddhāvivarddhikāḥ |
siddhīnāṃ karaṇaṃ nityaṃ samayānāṃ ca pālanam
adaivatayogā hy ado vikalpeḥ kāmalolupāḥ |
advaitam apratihataṃ samayācāraceṣṭitaṃ |
nārīcaryā sumutthāna brāhmacaryaṃ tathā dhyāne akrodhaḥ |
śrorthasaṃcāre ity āṣṭau samayā parāḥ |
jñātavyāḥ sādhako nityaṃ sādhane sthitā |
sāmānyaṃ sarvatantrāṇāṃ na hantavyā nihetubhiḥ
śraddhāmātreṇa gṛhyante | na kāraṇa na cakṣuṣā |
aprakāsyam idaṃ guhyaṃ gopanīyaṃ prayatnataḥ |
kaniṣṭhā cālayed dhīmān dūtayaḥ samavasthitaḥ |
śuddhāśuddho tha misras ca sādhakasya vivā sthitiḥ |
ārādhako viśuddhas ca dīvako guṇavāṃ na caḥ |
grāme grāme vrajāntasya dūtayo rūpalakṣaṇaṃ |
dūtayo asidhārañ ca pravitrapuṇyavarddhanaṃ |
sādhakasiddhim āpnauti susaṃparkād dūtayas tathā |
acirād eva sidhyanti japadhyānapūjanaiḥ[ 139]r vinā |
pūjāś caiva pravarttante mantramudrāś ca sāścakāḥ |
etatpūjāparaṃ nāsti iti śāstra yaḥ |
dūtīmārgatānā tu sādhakaddhitatparaḥ |
caruñ ca bhojayen nityaṃ vidyāmatrasamopetaḥ
sādhaka susamāhitaḥ ||
ācaret sarvavarṇobhyo na ca bhakṣāṃ vicārayet |
abhedyatattvatas tasya dūtayo dvau ca sādhane |
dhivivau caraṇasya eka eva ya dūtayaḥ |
pratibodhitāḥ | bhiṣiktaṃ nātikrameta
trābhiḥ sārdhaṃ sukhaṃ kuryāt
ātmārthī tadarthī ca kṛtvā samayācāratā
tenaiva saha bhūmiviveṣṭitam ucyate |
rātrau ca satataṃ nagnaḥ kṛṣṇaraktaṃ sadā bhavet |
prāṇyaṅgavāsasā vāsaṃ sukhārthā cālayet sukhaṃ |
yajñopavītaṃ saṃtyajya saucaṃ śrīheruke sthitaṃ
pañcamudrāpratibandha sarvakālaṃ samavasthitaṃ |
callocallamukhākṣepa (?) sukhyavibandhavarjitaṃ |
dūtyā tu prathamaṃ śaucaṃ dvitīyaṃ yogam iṣyate |
ekatra prāsanañ caruṃ caiva tṛtīyaṃ śaucam ucyate |
etata śaucaprapālanāt [ 140] suddhiṃ kramāt |
prāpya kasya siddhi na jāyate |
tattvasaṅgrahe yad uktaṃ ca tathoktaṃ cakrasamvare |
guhyatantre samākhyātaṃ haritantre tathaiva ca |
mahābhairave caiva japavratādisiddhidaṃ
dhyānamātreṇa mantrī sādhayate kṣaṇāt |
yogamārgaratānāṃ ayaṃ sauca samārabhet |
saucānaṃyan bhavet puṇyaṃ ekeṇa vaktreṇa na śakyate
vaktuṃ sādhake guṇavistaraṃ |
mukhānekaṃ satasahasrān
śrīherukaṃ jñānaṃ viśuddhaṃ sarvalakṣaṇasādhanaṃ |
saukhyāmbuprāsanāt puṇyaṃ pavitraṃ pāpanāsanaṃ |
sparśanāt bhāvanād api yat samācarati naraḥ śreṣṭhaḥ
sarvapāpaviśuddhātmā tāthāgatī ca labhate śriyaṃ |
janme janme tathāgatakule utpadyate |
rājā bhavati cakravarttiṃ tu vātmikaḥ |
evaṃ pūjāṃ sadā kṛtvā balin dadyāc ca bhaktitaḥ |
varaḥ sampūjyā caiva viśuddhis ta nirvāṇaṃ nirgataṃ vīraṃ pūjayeś caivam ātmānaḥ pūjitaṃ tena sarvasyāj jagataḥ sthāvarajaṅgamam |
pūjayed bhrātṛdā[ 140]ravargas tu somasaṅgamasaṃsthitāḥ |
bhrātṛṣu traikasārddhe sukhaduḥkhasamudbhavaṃ |
na tatra prakāśaye jñānaṃ mantramudrā tu eva ca |
catuṃ tu bhakṣayec caiva pavitrāsamayācārakṛtā bhavati |
mantra((ṃ)) na kasyacid dadyāt upadeśa na vināsayet |
yathārtham upadeśaṃ tu dadyāt samayavarttināṃ |
dūtī muktvā na cānyāṃ tu gacchet kramamohitaḥ |
dūtī caktā sadā mokṣaṃ atikrameṇāpi bhojayet |
satkāraṃ na caivai kuryāt janmahīnena susthitān |
mātā ca bhaginī putrī bhāryā vai dūtayaḥ sthitāḥ |
yatraḥ saṃkṣīyate vasya cakrasya sidayas tathā |
ākarṣaye cottamaṃ sāddhram etad dravye pi viniścayaḥ |
vāmācāra sadā yogī vāmapādaṃ pura kramet |
vāmahastaparivarttanaṃ vāmārddha paripūjakaḥ |
vāmagūḍhagātras tu vāmatarpaṇabhakṣaṇaḥ |
avrataghno vratapūrṇa syā sādhakas samayatatparaḥ |
samayakulācāreṇa ekatra carubhojanām |
bāhyamaṇḍalasamācāra caturvarṇa[ 141 deest] [...]
ekavarṇo samācaro yogasiddhiprapūrakaḥ //
gomānsaṃ hayamāṃsaś ca śvanammantriṇāṃ trikoṇāṃ maṇḍalaṃ kuryāt //
dhruvaṃ buddhopi nasyati //
mudrāmantrasamopetaṃ vīrāṇāṃ vīrabhojanaṃ //
tathā śrīherukasya nirmāṇaṃm upacāraśāstravilakṣitaṃ //
dhāraṇaṃ satataṃ kuryyād rakṣā ca sarvvakarmmaṇanirvvighnasaṃ yathā siddho nānyaḥ kaści prapadyate //
tasya dūtī vara //
devyā udyuktā ca mahārthakā //
araktā ratisaṃbhogād yogadravyavibhāvitā //
gurūpadeśasaṃraktā tadathādhanasasamā //
bhātaraṃ pitaraṃ vāpi svāmin vaṃ paśyate kṣaṇāt //
sthūlajihvā piṅgalākṣā karkkasī piṅgaśirāruhā //
sāmānyaṃ sarvvadiddhināṃ japavratatarās[/ratā?] sadā // [F229]
smareṇena samāyuktaṃ dūbhyādipadānvitaṃ //
caturviṃśativīraḍākinyau bhuktimuktiphalapradāḥ //
muramantrahi jayentityaṃ sarvvakāmārthasādhakaṃ //
yogakrīḍāphalapradaṃ //
akasmāj jāyate mantrā trailokyasacarācare //
mantramudrāsamopetamudrāhīnan na sānnidhyaṃ [//]
mantraṃ bhavati dehināṃ //
atiyogenabodhavyaṃ yathā uṣmādiktayātmani ye dviṣanti mahāyogaṃ sarvvakāmārthasādhakaṃ //
svānayoni sataṃgatvā caṇḍāreṣvapi jāyate //
ydvatkaścighṛtārthīśalīlam apnāti śraddhayā //
na ca sampadyate sarpi kāyakleśas tu kevalaṃ //
anyathā ca vṛthābhadrapāraṇaṃ pūjanaṃ pivā //
jīvanopātyehetutvād anyayogasamāśritāḥ //
mahāyogadharāvīrā yasmin deśe vasanti vā //
caṇḍālamlacchādhurevāpi taddeśaṃ raṃparikalpayet //
tasyā haṃ nityaṃ niṣṭhāmi satvānagrahahetunā //
[abhidhānottarottare dūtasaukhyāmbuparatalas triṃcatvariṃśatamaḥ] [P43]
