<p. 98b> yadā hyekatra tritayaṃ nityato yo pi bhāvayet || 32 ||

yo vikalpaḥ sa ghorāstraṃ yā śuddhiḥ sā parāsmṛtā |

brahmādyāḥ śivaparyantaṃ pañcadravyāḥ prakīrtitāḥ || 36 ||

brahmāviṣṇuśca rudraśca īśvaraśśiva eva ca || 37 ||

<p. 99a> nāsā cakṣustathā śrotra māsyaṃ meḍhraṃ tu pañcamam |

pañcaite devatā mūle kulaliṅge pratiṣṭhitāḥ || 39 ||

māṃsaṃ raktaṃ tathāsthīnisnāyumajjā tathāmalam |
śukraṃ śleṣmaṃ śirolomā ye cānye cāṅga santatau || 40 ||

<p. 99b> prāṇopāneśvarīśaktirapāne gudarodhinī |

<p. 100a> gudapadmaṃ sadābhuktyā kālarudraḥ pratiṣṭhitaḥ |

yena vijñātamātreṇa na bhūyaḥ parivartanam || 1 ||

ekākī paramo devaḥ śaktyā saha tu krīḍati || 5 ||

<p. 102b> yo sau kāmeśvaro deva utpatti layakārakam |

eko devo na cānyosti kiṃ vā dvandvena kāraṇam || 7 ||

śāstrabhedaṃ kimarthaṃ vai kiṃ vā dvaita vidhikramam |
advaitaṃ kathitaṃ sarvaṃ dvaitaṃ nāsti mahāprabho || 8 ||

mṛtadehasya piṇḍasya na kiñcidupalabhyate || 11 ||

<p. 110a> nirnāmaviṣayātīte janmamṛtyu vivarjite |

na kiñciccintanāddevi na naśyati na muhyati || 30 ||
