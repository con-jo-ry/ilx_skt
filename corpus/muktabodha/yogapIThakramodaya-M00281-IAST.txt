<3a> 

koṃkaṇe samayā caiva adhikāra padesthitā |
evaṃ bhedopabhedāni gṛhasaṃjñāvalistathā ||

yugānurūpa bhāvena adhikāra kramāgataṃ |
śilāśekharamadhyasthā kudāṃvānāmaviśrutā(?) ||

guhāśca trīṇi vijñeyā aṣṭau te maṭhiko tu sā ||

ūrddharetādurādharṣaḥ siddhayogendrapūjitaḥ ||

ālāsamaṭhikāṃ suṣka(?)m tathā pālāsapālake |
valohake punaścāsau kalpaśrānaṃtu(?) saṃsthitaḥ ||

saṃkhikāmaṭhasaṃjñantu caṃdradvīpa ca viśrutā |
uttare kāmarūpasya svatamiyāmāṭhāśramaṃ ||

mahendre rājaparṇistu maṭhikāṃ rājyasaṃjñakāṃ |
trikuṭe candrapūrvāñca maṭhikāśramamuttamaṃ ||

gṛhaṃ caṃdrapuraṃ proktaṃ (**)(?) giryā dvitīyakaṃ |

gṛhe bhedakramāmnāyamājñāsiddhaṃ kramodayaṃ |

<4a>

abhiṣiktāstu ye tatra muktā sarvatvasaṃkhyayā |
dīpadarpaṇapuṣpeṇa toyena parisiñcitāḥ ||

kavohlādena tṛptāstu yojanānāṃ gamairapi |
darśanāt syanāccaiva saṃtrāṣāhlādanādapi ||

saṃkrameta mahājñānaṃ bhavabandhavimocakaṃ |
prabhuṇā bhāvarūpeṇa sṛṣṭaṃ jñānamanekadhā ||

pratyakṣamatranihitamājñāsiddhaṃ mahodayaṃ |
yoginīsiddhasaṃdohaṃ mantrapīṭhavinirgataṃ ||

nirṇayasarvasaṃkṣepādājñāsamayagocare |

[yogapīṭhe kramodayo nāma dvitīyaḥ paṭalaḥ] ||

atha ājñāvibhūtyarthaṃ maṃtrāṇāṃ saṃgrahaṃ paraṃ |
yogapīṭhasya madhyasthaṃ devyā praśnaṃ purodbhavān(?) ||

etat sarvaviditvā tu nirmalāñca vravīhi me |
guptametat sadā kārya kuśalaiḥ kuladehikaiḥ ||

ekānte vijane ramye gṛhamadhye guhāture |
puṣpaprakarasaṃkīrṇe gandhadhūpānumādite ||

ācāryapramukhaṃ śiṣyaḥ ekacittāvyavasthitaḥ |

<4b>

uddhareta parāpāni kālikīṃ kula (***)(?) |
(**)(?) sutapa saṃśliṣṭāṃ dīrghabinduralaṃkṛtāṃ ||

yogapīṭhemidaṃ proktaṃ hṛdayaṃ kaulikāparaṃ |
asthira (*)(?) samāyuktaṃ vāme vāhuṃ(?) pradīpitaṃ ||

arddhaddhāsutasaṃyuktaṃ yogadaṃ lakṣaṇānvitaṃ |
raktaprāṇaṃ tathā meḍraṃ(?) vāmavindantu lakṣaṇaṃ ||

mokṣadaṃ bhogadaṃ caiva vāgvidhānāyakevalaṃ |
vāmakukṣiñca raktaṃ ca dvije ca hṛdayena tu ||

khagaśī hṛdayaṃ proktaṃ vācāsiddhi pravartakaṃ |
prāṇaṃ śukra tathā raktaṃ adharorddhaṃ sa vā gataṃ ||

pañcākṣara mayīṃ siddhāṃ surāsura sudurlabhaṃ |
karṇātkarṇagatāṃ devī āmnāye siddhasaṃtatau ||

catuḥpīṭhasamāyuktā siddh vṛnda(?)samanvitāṃ |
bālakaumāravṛddhasthāṃ jñātavyāṃ varadāyikā || <1> ||

vajra siddheśvarī nāma vidyāmantrasamuddharet |
pretyadbhutāṃ(?) mahāraudrāṃ viśvasaṃhārakāriṇī ||

<5a>

yogapīṭhasamādau ca vāgvidhānamataḥ paraṃ |
prāṇaṃ visargasaṃduddhaṃ harakarṇe sa bindukaṃ ||

khagasaṃ ca punaḥ kukṣiṃ pūrvoktā kevalā tu sā |
dakṣapādādi saṃkrodhaṃ visargena samanvitaṃ ||

vajrasiddheśvarī nāma saptākṣarasamanvitā |
sarvagvaṃ siddhisaṃtāne siddhāmātāsurakṣitā || <2> ||

atha viśvā pravakṣāmi jvālāmālini viśrutāṃ |
yoginī hṛdayokṣa(?)mārakṣitavyā prayatnataḥ ||

vāgvidhānaṃ puroddhṛtya śoṇitaṃ ca caturguṇaḥ |
prāṇaṃ vai vāmakarṇe tu hṛdayena samanvitaṃ ||

kukṣiyāta gataṃ pūrvaṃ rakṣaṇīya kulānvaye |
yoginī siddhasadbhāvamatyanta paramaṃ bhavet || <3> ||

prāṇaṃ śukraṃ khagesaṃ ca ekīkṛtvā prayatnataḥ |
kulākula padaṃ hyetat hṛdaya yoginī kule ||

parāpara vibhāgaṃ ca pīṭhasthaṃ pīṭhasaṃyutaṃ |
yogadaṃ bhogadaṃ caiva khecarī padamuttamaṃ || <4> ||

oṃkārapīṭhamadhyasthāṃ ekavaktrāṃ caturbhujāṃ |

nānāratnavicitrāṃgīṃ karṇakuṇḍalabhūṣitāṃ |
kāṣadvasitamānāntu bhagnapuṣṭi mahadbhutāṃ ||

saṃkhahastāṃ mahāsaumyāṃ khaḍgapāśakapāladhṛk |

acirādupalambeta(?) parāvyāptirakhaṇḍitā |

<8a>

ājñārūpa uccārya agrai puṣpaṃ pradāpayet |
samayāmyasta dehastu ṣoḍānyāsena yantritaḥ ||

anantamaṇḍalaṃ madhye ūrddhamahantamaṇḍalaṃ |
yadā vyāpti maṇḍale dakṣe agre cākhaṇḍamaṇḍale ||

śūnyamaṇḍalakaṃ vāme paṃcaite parikīrtitāḥ |

ghaṭamaṇḍalake nāma tathā cākṣayamaṇḍalaṃ |

maṇḍalānāṃ catuṣkadaṃ dakṣādaupaṃktirūpakaṃ |

paṃcaratnati nāmena mahāvometi maṇḍalaṃ ||

tṛdhākaulika nāmena paṃcatatveti maṇḍalaṃ ||

tṛśūnyamaṇḍalaṃ caiva ātmamaṇḍalameva ca |
pūjayed vāmamārgairaktapuṣpaiḥ suśobhanaiḥ ||

vidyāmaṇḍalamuccārya tathā vijayamaṇḍalaṃ |

devadatānāṃ asthibhaṃjaya <2> āṇimārivasī kuru <2> svāhā || <2> ||

<17a>

aiṃ hrīṃ śrīṃ sāṃ sīṃ sūṃ saṃkhinīye saṃkhagrahena snāyuṃ (*) 
(*)(?) <2> sarvaśatrūṇāṃ devadatānāṃ āṇimārivaśī kuru <2> svāhā || <3> ||

aiṃ hrīṃ śrīṃ kāṃ kīṃ kūṃ kāriṇīye kāyaṃ mānsaṃ gṛhna <2> medaṃ 
śatrūṇāṃ devadattānāṃ āṇimāri vasī kuru <2> svāhā || <4> ||

aiṃ hrīṃ śīṃ lāṃ līṃ lūṃ lākinīye sarvaśatrūṇāṃ devadattānāṃ 
āṇimārivaśī kuru <2> mānsaṃ bhakṣa svāhā || <5> ||

aiṃ hrīṃ śrīṃ rāṃ rīṃ rūṃ rākiṇīye āṇimārivīsī kuru <2> sarvaśatrūṇāṃ 
devadattānāṃ raktadhātuṃ gṛhna <2> sādhaya <2> svāhā || <6> ||

aiṃ hrīṃ śrīṃ ḍāṃ ḍīṃ ḍuṃ ḍākiṇīye sarvaśatrūṇāṃ devadattānāṃ 
aṇimāri vaśī kuru <2> tvaca dhātuṃ gṛhna <2> svāhā || <7> ||

śataṃ śataṃ japitvā tu ekaikāyāḥ samarpayet |
gṛhnaṃ tvedaṃ mayādattaṃ atrājñā pārameśvarī ||

<17b>

eṣaduṣṭādurātmā ca dveṣṭāro mama nityasaḥ |
gṛhnantu devatāḥ kṣipraṃ mayādattaṃ mahāpaśuṃ ||

āṣṭayadi prasāsni pramāṇaṃ yadi cānvayaṃ tena satyena gṛhna tu 
mayādattaṃ marīcayaḥ ||

tato gṛhnantitā devyaḥ śīghrasaṃhārayantitaṃ |
saptamehanisaṃprāpte mayate nātra saṃśayaḥ ||

brahmaṇālipyamātmānaṃ soḍhānyāsamupantitaṃ |
dhyānaṃ paścāt prayuñjīta bhairavākārarūpiṇaṃ ||

bhairavohamiti smṛtvā tataḥ karmasamārabhet |
raktena maṇḍalaṃ kṛtvā kṛṣṇecchāśād bhavena tu ||

mahāsthi cūrṇasaṃgṛhya ālikheccakramuttamaṃ |
ṣaṭprakāraṃ susaṃpūrṇaṃ vaktralāṃcchanalāṃcchitaṃ ||

evaṃ kṛtvā vidhiḥ samyak tato devyaḥ samarcayet |
kṛṣṇavarṇā mahāraudrāḥ daṃṣṭrākucabhāsurā ||

yauvanonmādasaṃyuktā muktakeśādi śambarāḥ |
evaṃ dhyātvā dadedarghaṃ soṇite gandhamakṣataiḥ ||

<18a>

svāgatantu tataḥ kṛtvā āsanaṃ tatra dāpayet |
paścādviṣṭaramādāya pañcāsubha(?) samanvitaṃ ||

puṣpaṃ dhūpaṃ tathā gandhaṃ naivedyādi pavitrakaiḥ |
mahātailena dīyantu kṛṣṇavarti suśobhitaṃ ||

dhyātvā sādhyaṃ tatastvagrai uttānaṃ pretarūpiṇaṃ |
patitaṃ cakra madhye tu muktakeśadigambaraṃ ||

saptasaptajapitvā tu ekaikasya nivedayet |
gṛhna gṛhneti sāccārya hasta cakrā grahālayet ||

saṃharet tatkṣaṇārddhena ityājñā pārameśvarī |
sākinīnāṃ mate pūrva kīrtitaṃ vidhivistaraṃ ||

pāśapīṭhe tu saṃvṛtya sārabhūtaṃ suniścitaṃ |
gopanīyaṃ prayatnena anyaśāstaratasya ca ||

ājñāśravaṇaśiṣye tu dātavyaṃ kṛta niścaye |

[iti yogapīṭhe kramodayo nāma ṣaṣṭhaḥ paṭalaḥ] ||

pūjāvyāpti krameṇaiva kramaśuddhi kramāgataṃ |
ṣaṭprakāragataṃ śuddhaṃ pravravīmi yathoditaṃ ||

catuṣkaṃ paṃcakaṃ caiva ṣaṭkaṃ catuṣkapaṃcakaṃ |

<18b>

catuṣkaṃ tu punastatra ṣaṭprakāraṃ prakīrtitaṃ |
piṃḍaṃ padaṃ tathā rūpaṃ rūpātītaṃ catuṣṭayaṃ ||

piṇḍaṃ kuṇḍalinī śaktiḥ nābhimeḍhrāture sthitā |
uḍa(?)hemapratīkāśā taptāya sa samaprabhā ||

triparvā ca trimātrasthā tridhāśaktistu śojvalā |
uḍi(-)āṇaṃ tu taṃ pīṭhaṃ pīṭhesī sahasaṃyutaṃ ||

pūjanīyaṃ prayatnena mantroccārasamanvitaṃ |
padasthā paramā śaktiścārasthācārarūpiṇī ||

carate śiva paryantaṃ śaktimārgā valambiṇī |
māyājāla padasthaṃ ca dvimātrā sahasaṃsthitaṃ ||

aṣṭapatraṃ samāntasthaṃ pīṭhaṃ tajjālakeśvaṃ |
pīṭhesī saṃsthitā ghorā mohayantī carācaraṃ ||

sūryāyutapratīkāśā jyotirūpāṃ mahādyuti |
pīṭhaṃ vai pīṭha mātā ca hṛtpradeśe vyavasthitā ||

pūjanīyā prayatnena satve vighnanivṛttaye |
rūpasthaṃ pūrṇṇapīṭhantu sūryakoṭi samaprabhaṃ ||

sata rudra samākāramati dīptaṃ subhāsuraṃ |
vidyāccārasamāyuktaṃ yoginī rūpasaṃsthitaṃ ||

<19a>

vyāptibhūtaṃ jayet sarvaṃ pīṭhamvai pārameśvaraṃ |
rūpātītaṃ mahāvyāptiryāṃ paṃcārtha prasūyikā ||

kṛtvopalabdhi mātraṃ tu śāṃbhavīya padāntare |
kiñciddhetti navāvetti nimesonmesaspandanaṃ ||

icchāsvarūpamaṃ nandaṃ kāmarūpaṃ prakīrtitaṃ |
tat svarūpā bhavabhavaṃ dicchā pīṭheśī tu mahāvalā ||

bhakṣayantī kalāsarvāṃ kāmarūpāṃ kaleśvarīṃ |
etaccatuṣkamākhyātaṃ paṃcakaṃ kathayāmi te ||

nityānanda karaṃ sarvaṃ bhavāvandhavimocakaṃ |
vidyā tatva samutpannaṃ pañcakaṃ tu vibhakṣyate ||

śaktirātmā tathāhaṃso vāyuṣṇaijaḥ(?) prakīrtitaḥ |
etat tat paṃcakaṃ proktaṃ vimalādi sa lakṣaṇaṃ ||

suddhavyomāyujājātā tārakā ulka sannibhā |
vimalākhyaṃ samutpannaṃ saṃginī śaktiravyayā ||

tasyecchāmanuvartinyo ātmatatva vyavasthitā |
vubhuje viṣayāt sarvān spandane dehamadhyagā ||

sukhaduḥsamaṃyanti bhāvābhāva vikalpate |

<19b>

sarvabhūtāntarasthā tu sarvajñā tu pulindikā |
hansahansā vaden nityaṃ dehasthāvarajaṃgame ||

varate dehamadhye tu jagat sthāvarajaṃgamaṃ |
sarvagāsarvamūrtisthā sarvajñaṃ guṇasaṃyutā ||

nadate nādarūpeṇa yāgasaṃjñeti sāvarī |
prāṇopānasamānasthā udānavyānasaṃyutā ||

daśanāḍī kṛtā dhārā siddhasaṃjñeti campakā |
godhāmukhasamākārā vaḍavābhamanīndriyaṃ ||

taḍid vidyut samābhāsaṃ lambantaṃ vahni sannibhaṃ |
drāvayet pāśayā (**)(?) padapiṇḍāntare sthitā ||

samayācāra bhedena jagad yoni mahāmbikā |
pañcakantu samākhyātaṃ divyadivyairnna śodhitaṃ ||

yad cakrasya vibhāgantu vadisyāmi yathoditaṃ |
ādhāraṃ prathamaṃ cakraṃ svādhiṣṭhānaṃ dvitīyakaṃ ||

tṛtīyaṃ matipūraṃ tu nādarūpaṃ caturthakaṃ |
viśuddhaṃ pañcamaṃ proktaṃ ājñārūpaṃ tu pañcamaṃ ||

ṣaṭprakāra kramaṃ jñātvā konasiddhyati līlayā |
mulādhāraṃ catusthānaṃ drutahemasamaprabhaṃ ||

<20a>

bhramantaṃ cakravegena lakṣitavyaṃ gurormukhāt |
svādhiṣṭhānaṃ mahāratnaṃ ṣaḍāraṃ cakramuttamaṃ ||

dāḍimī kusumaprakṣaṃ dyotayantaṃ diśodaśa |
daśavāyu samopetaṃ daśanāḍisamanvitaṃ ||

kundendu tu hi nāḍā saṃpūrayantaṃ mahadbhutaṃ |
yathā maṭhīmanāḥ sūtrai ekatra grathitaṃ mahān ||

tathā te sarva tatvāni dṛśyate maṇipūrake |
dvādaśāraṃ mahātejaṃ dvādaśādityavarcasaṃ ||

paraṃ brahmaṇi śabdākhyaṃ hṛdisthantu anāhataṃ |
pūrṇacandrasamākāraṃ kalaiḥ ṣoḍaśabhirvṛtaṃ ||

evantu mamṛtaṃ divyaṃ visuddhaṃ śuddhatejasaṃ |
śivasya paripūrṇasya tasyecchāsaṃpravartane ||

adṛṣṭavigrahāt śātāt tadājñā pārameśvarī |
ṣaṭprakārasamuddiṣṭaṃ rahasyaṃ guhyamuttamaṃ ||

gopitavyaṃ mahāguhyaṃ akathya cānyaśāsane |
catuṣkantu punarvīkṣya mokṣamārgasuniścitaṃ ||

anena jñātamātreṇa bhavabandhe pramucyate |
yā sā devyā phalākārā cakṣuragre vyavasthitā ||

<20b>

samantā sarvato bhadrā dṛśyate gaganāntare |
brahmarandhra gatā vyomni gṛhacchidrāntaraśmibhiḥ ||

tadrūpeṇa dānenyavaṃ(?) pratyakṣaṃ saṃpravartate |
sūryavimbanibhākāraṃ kūṭasthāne vyavasthitaṃ ||

tīvrogragakṣivedena(?) vedhito vyāpti dāyakaṃ |
jale vudvudasaṃkāśaṃ vajradvāre vyavasthitaṃ ||

kṣīradhārā yutaṃ dṛṣṭvā acirād vyāpti dāyakaṃ |
etad devyā catuṣkantu sāmāśānāvakīrtitaṃ ||

ajñātvā vandhamuddiṣṭaṃ jñātvā mucyati mocati |
śabdasparśarasopetaṃ gandhaścaiva tu paṃcamaṃ ||

paṃcatanmātramādevyaḥ samajakhasusaṃjñakā |
pūrvaṃ catuṣkaṃ yatproktaṃ pīṭhesī pīṭhasaṃyutaṃ ||

siddhapādakrameṇaiva guruṃpaṅktyāvaśānagaṃ |
evaṃ vidhividhānena divyoghakramasantatiḥ ||

sasphuṭaṃ guru vaktrasthaṃ anyathā na kadācana |
guruvaśya praśādena prāpyate paramaṃ padaṃ ||

anyathā naiva jānāti sāstrakoṭi śatairapi |

<21a>

tasmāt sarvaprayatnena gopayeta vicakṣaṇaḥ |
anyathā vadate yastu sa paśu yoginī kule ||

ātmaśiṣyeṣu bhakteṣu daṃtamāyāvijite |
lolyacāpalya nirmukte satyavādi jitendriyai ||

dṛḍhabhakte tu dātavyaṃ ājñā pālana tatpare |
niścite nispṛhe gupte nisarge kānta vāsinaiḥ ||

na duṣṭe ca pale deyaṃ na śaṭhidūṣake tathā |
kāmine krodhine caiva kapaṭavratadhāriṇi ||

na deyaṃ kaulike sāraṃ yadiccheccirajīvitaṃ |
devyāyāḥ siddhasadbhāvaṃ kathanānmaraṇaṃ bhavet ||

bhakṣanti yoginī tasya ṣaḍetastu mahāvalāḥ |


[yogapīṭhe kramodayo nāma saptamaṃ paṭalaḥ samāptaḥ] ||

iti śrutvā parānandaṃ devyākṛṣṇamanocchakā |
patitā daṇḍavadbhūmau śastapādānna saṃsthitāḥ ||

bhagavannyāsa saṃskāraṃ vaktramahāṃsi tatvataḥ |
dīkṣākarmavibhāgantu samayāṃśca tathā vada ||

ūcusthevaṃ punaḥ śāntaṃ kathayāmi samāsataḥ |
pūrokte sthānake gupte paṃcagavyopalepite ||

<21b>

sudhūpadhūpitaṃ kṛtvā puṣpaprakarasaṃkule |
vastunā guṇṭhayitvā tu caṇḍodaya suśobhite ||

śragdāmālambayitvā tu toraṇaṃ cūtapallavaiḥ |
evaṃ kṛtvā vidhiṃ samyak tataḥ karmasamārabhet ||

śuklāmbara dharā bhūtvā ācāryasusamāhitaḥ |
pañcagavyaṃ tataḥ prāsya astramantreṇa mantravit ||

alinādyastu mantreṇa prokṣayed yāgamaṇḍapaṃ |
sarvavidyāsamuccārya diśāvandhantu kārayet ||

prokṣayet sarvadravyāṇi yāsārthai āgatāni vai |
nyāsaṃ kṛtvā prayatnena bhairavākārarūpiṇaṃ ||

ṣoḍādvādaśakaṃ sarvaṃ ślokadvādaśamekhalā |
paṃcaratna kṛtāṭopaṃ tadguhā valibhūṣitaṃ ||

dhyānaṃ paścāt prayuñjīta prāṇāyāmasamanvitaṃ |
jvalantaṃ pāvakākāraṃ bhaśmībhūtaṃ vicintayet ||

candramaṇḍalamadhyasthaṃ kalāṣoḍaśabhūṣitaṃ |
varṣantaṃ mamtuto(?) pañcakṣīradhāraiḥ parapsaraiḥ(?) ||

śuddhasphaṭikasaṃkāśaṃ kadambagolakā kṛtiṃ || <3> ||

kṛtvā pātrasya saṃskāraṃ seṣāla(?) dvaumṛtena tu |
gaṇḍasaṃdakṣiṇe naiva pañcaratyaiḥ samālabhet ||

gāyatryā mantritaṃ kṛtvā snānaṃ te naiva kārayet || <3> ||

<22a>

rudrayantra tadātmānaṃ niḥsṛtaṃ divyaṃ rūpiṇaṃ |
sṛṣṭirūpaṃ samutsṛjya mudrābandhantu kārayet ||

samadṛksamakāyastu mūrddha cakra vyavasthitaḥ |
recyavāyuṃ pratnena punarākṛṣya dhārayet ||

ākramyamūlacakrantu saṃghaṭṭe kaṃdamūrddhagaṃ |
ūrṇatantu nibhā tatra kandādbhinnā vinirgatā ||

bhinde granthi kapāṭāni argalāyāśca saṃsthitā |
bindumadhyā gatādūrddhaṃ pratyakṣamupajāyate ||

virajā nāmavikhyātā mudreyaṃ sṛṣṭirūpiṇī |
lagnaigranthi trayeṇaiva viśvavyāptirakhaṇḍitā ||

vyāptibhūtaṃ jayet sarvaṃ divyoghakramavaddhatiṃ |
nānyathā hṛtpradeśe tu lalāṭe brahmarandhrayoḥ ||

vākpāṇimakṣiṇī karṇau vibhajya parame vahi |
madhye dakṣe tathā vāme agraiścaiva viśuddhigaṃ ||

ojapūkā krameṇaiva pūjitavyā prayatnataḥ |
māptasavahu(?) krameṇaiva sambhumāvaraṇasthitaṃ ||

pūjitavyā yathāvastha kulākula krameṇa tu |
jñānaṣaṭkaṃ yathāvastha sthāne sthāne vyavasthitaḥ ||

pūjayen manasā sarvaṃ dakṣādau vyāpti pūrvakaṃ |

<22b>

ādhāraṃ gudamityāhu svādhiṣṭhānantu liṅgajaṃ ||

maṇipūrantu nābhisthaṃ hṛdisthantu anāhataṃ |
viśuddhi lambikāntasthā ājñākuṭa sukīrtitā ||

lalāṭe vaktramadhye tu bhāratī śaṃkhikāntare |
sāmāmānā kramenaiva pūjitavyā yathoditā ||

divyā tu paṃcake sthāne samajasisupūjayet |
krami upacaya siddhā śṛṅgāṭākṛtimastake ||

gurupaṃkti lalāṭe tu dakṣādau vāmakāvadhiṃ |
ḍaralakasahayantra yaccakraṃ pūrvamuddhṛtaṃ ||

pūjitavyaṃ prayatnena karṇaye ghrāṇavaktrayoḥ |
cibuke kaṇṭha deśe tu bhrāmaṇī nābhi saṃsthitā ||

etat krama mahādivyaṃ guruvaktrāntu lambane |
brahmaraṃdhrai lalāṭe tu bhrūmadhye kaṇṭha vaktrayoḥ ||

hṛdinābhau tathā liṅgaṃ yattaccakramudāhṛtaṃ |
brahmāṇyādyā kulāṣuñca pūjitavyā yathākramaṃ ||

gandhapuṣpasugandhī nimbātya(?) dehaṃ prapūjayet |
dhyānaṃ paścāt prayuñjīta niścale nāntarātmanā ||

svādhisthāna purāntasthaṃ mahāvṛndaṃ sadoditaṃ |

<23a>

raktāmvaradharāḥ sarve maukṣikābharaṇojvalā |
dviraṣṭākṛtayaḥ sarve pibanti madirāsavaṃ ||

dvibhujā ekavaktrāśca hārakuṇḍala (*)(?) śobhā |
śaṃkhapadmadharā sarve trinetrā madirālasāḥ ||

tadāvasthā tadātmānaṃ tena rūpeṇa cintayet |
dehasthaṃ jananaṃ kṛtvā vahisthaṃ vāhyataḥ sthita paraṃ ||

śobhane yogapīṭhe tu raktavastreṇa chādite |
tasyopari samādāya nirmalaṃ darpaṇaṃ śubhaṃ ||

karpūraṃ mṛganābhiśca kāśmīraṃ candanaṃ tathā |
rocanādhraṃ sapuṣpaṃ ca kuṇḍagolālimiśritaṃ ||

ekībhūtvā tu te sarva lepayed darpaṇaṃ śubhaṃ |
ālikhed yogapīṭhantu nādavindukalānvitaṃ ||

vartulaṃ tasya madhye tu ṣaṭprakāroparisthitaṃ |
sindūraṃ hiṅgulaṃ raktaṃ rajastatraiva pātayet ||

sudhūpadhūpitaṃ kṛtvā āsanaṃ tatra kalpayet |
nyastavyā mātrikā tatra ādikṣāntamanukramāt ||

ajāreṣā tu yā pūrvā brahmaṃ tatrādhidevatā |

<23b>

dvitīyā kāditāntasthāṃ hantristhitrāpi saṃsthitaḥ |
yo disā tādṛtī yā ca rudrastatrādhidaivataṃ ||

triprakārakramaṃ proktaṃ trimātrā triyathānvitaṃ |
vandhayet tato mudrāṃ virajākhyāṃ sadoditāṃ ||

pīṭhamāvāhayet tatra ekatatve vyavasthitaḥ |
mahāpīṭhagatāḥ sarve kaula bheda kramastathā ||

devyāyā paṃcakaṃ madhye makārādi prapūjayet |
nādavindu kalā mūle vahnisthā vahnimūlagaṃ ||

jñānaṣaṭkaṃ prapūjyeta aṅgasaṭkasamanvitaṃ |
caturasre catuṣkantu pūjitavyaṃ yathāvidhiḥ ||

ūrdhareṣā tu yā tūrṇā tiryagā kiñcidunnatā |
samajākhya(?) sunāmāni pūjitavyāni tatra vai ||

pīṭhapīṭheśvarī siddhā ekoccāreṇa pūjayet |
kāmākhyapīṭhamūle tu gurupaktiṃ niveśayet ||

ādi ṣaṭkaṃ kramenaiva ṣaṭprakāre prapūjayet |
evavidhe vidhānena kramapūjāmudāhṛtāṃ ||

kulākulavibhāgena kulāṣṭakamihocyate |
prayāge tu mahākṣetre ā (**)(?) makulasaṃyutāṃ ||

<24a>

vairīṃdū(?) ādimā pūjyā pūrvabhāga vyavasthitā || <1> ||

vārāṇasyāṃ mahākṣetre ilāvarvvarakānvitā |
māheśīkādinā pūjyā āgneyī diśimāśritā || <2> ||

kollagiryā mahākṣetre ūhāyogisamanvitaṃ |
kaumārī cādinā pūjyā(?) dakṣiṇāṃ diśimāśritā || <3> ||

aṭṭahāsamahākṣetre jaśāsiddhi(?) harānvitā |
ḍhādimā vaiṣṇavī pūjyā nairityāṃ koṇamāśritā || <4> ||

jayantyāñca mahākṣetre mahābha (*)(?) samanvitāṃ |
vārāhī tādinā pūjyā vāruṇyā diśimāśritā || <5> ||

veditre ca mahākṣetre aiśakiṇikilānvitā |
aindrī ca yā dinā pūjyā vāyavyāṃ koṇagāṃ sthitāṃ || <6> ||

ekāprake mahākṣetre kālarātrī śedhakā |
cāmuṇḍā yā dinā pūjyā kauverī diśamāśritā || <7> ||

devīkoṭe mahākṣetre ahlākṣā bhīṣaṇānvitā |
lakṣmī sādyena saṃpūjya īśānyāṃ diśi bhūṣaṇī ||
