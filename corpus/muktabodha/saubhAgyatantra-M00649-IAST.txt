<pṛ0 1a> oṃśrīgaṇeśāyanamaḥ akhaṃḍaikarasānaṃdaniṣyaṃdāmṛtavigrahācinmātrarupiṇīṃvidyāṃvaṃdesānaṃdasuṃdarīṃ (1)

<pṛ0 1ba> tthā yuktamavdhirasalavdhavihīnaṃsyādgaṇebhṛgudinekutaleca (11) kiṃca dhūsīkālayutaṃśākaṃkalpavda

yanāmakāḥ (14) ṣaḍtriṃśadbhirhṛtetadvadbhavettaduduyogakaṃ kālāṃkādhyartuvedāśvairhṛtetuyugasaṃtati (15)
taccheṣaṃgartryaśvapūrṇapakṣairbhaktaḥpariḥrvṛtiḥ śeṣaṃśarābhreṣuhṛtevarṣāṇyaṃkebhamiśritaṃ (16) varṣaśe

<pṛ0 2ba> ḍeyādyupāsitaḥ (4) vāraścaghaṭikācaivadinanityātathaivaca varṣamāsayugāścaivaparivṛtiritikramāt (5) a

evaṃyovetipaṃcānāmaṃgaṃpārāyaṇevidhau sayogīṃvrahmanirvāṇaṃprāpnuyāccanasaṃśayaḥ (11) itiśrīmatsaubhā

ṣoḍaśāṃgavidhānoktaṃyatjñātvāsiddhimāpnuyāt (1) akāraḥ pūrṇasaṃjñohiṣakārastyaṃhyamucyate hakāraḥ
