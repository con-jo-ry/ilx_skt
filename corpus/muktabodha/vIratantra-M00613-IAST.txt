|| vīratantra ||

śrīgaṇeśāya namaḥ || śrīgu (*) yo namaḥ || śrī śivaṃ saccidānaṃda 
lakṣaṇaṃ sarvakāraṇaṃ || māhāliṃgābhidaṃ naumī samupāsya mumukṣubhiḥ 
|| 1 ||
gurumanādi niraṃjana jaṃgamaṃ paramanugrahaṇaika parāyaṇaṃ ||
jagati toṭada siddha maheśvaraṃ prathitanamajuṣa praṇamāmyahaṃ || 2 ||
līlāmūrtiranādijaṃgamaya śrītoṭasiddhesvarasvairodyāmakṛpā kaṭākṣa 
laharī prāptopadeśannatiḥ ||
dharmān sādhu samīrān svabhajakān śrīvīraśaivocitān ||
bodhaṃ so vimalaṃ dadātu murigā śrīśāṃta vīroyatiḥ || 3 ||
śāṃtavīra gurubhūri satkṛpāpātratāmupagataḥ samaṃtata ||
yo vasatkṣītitale virakti māṃ saṃnnamāmi guru siddhadeśikaṃ || 4 ||
yogasiddha gurusiddha deśikānugrahādhigata śaivavaibhavaḥ ||

<pṛ0 1a> tatvabodhanaratasudhāpurī vīracaṃna basavāya edhate || 5 || 
tādṛśaṃ gurumapṛchadīchayā paṃḍitomuragayākhya sīlavān ||
tatvamuttamattamaṃ tathāvidhaṃ sannutaṃ ca mama kathyatamiti || 6 ||
itīrayaṃ taṃ murigākhya sīlinaṃ gurustavapraśna yugottaraṃ kramāt ||
kṛtiṃ vitanvannigamāgamānugāṃ vadātryahaṃ śṛṇviti so vadanūmudā || 7 
|| 
sakalaśṛtyāgamapurāṇaprasiddhaṣṭāvarṇapaṃcācāra viśiṣṭatayā 
mahāliṃgaika ye rūpākṣa sādhana niruttaraikauttaraśatasa (*) (*)(?) garbhita 
śaṭsthala pratipādaka siddhāṃtaśāstra pratipādyajñānakrīyā (*) 
bhedarupapadārtha

<pṛ0 1ba> samarpaṇa prakārī bhutāṃga liṃgabhakti śaktihasta 
maṃtramukhapadārthānusaṃdhānāśraya ṣaṭbhedabhinnasthānāvayavi 
bhūteṣṭaliṃga prāṇaliṃga bhāvaliṃga pūjopāsanaika sthāna mānasatayā 
viśiṣṭādvaitajñāna saṃpannaḥ śrīvīraśaivaḥ sarvottaraḥ || 
tathāhi śāṃkarasaṃhitāyāṃ || 
āṣṭāvaraṇasaṃpannaḥ paṃcācāra parāyaṇaḥ vaidikaṃ karma kūrvita 
jñānaika phalasādhanaṃ || 1 ||
mīti || yo hastapīṭe nijaliṃgamīṣṭaṃ vinyasya tallina manaḥ pracāraḥ |
bāhye krīyā saṃkula nispṛhātmā saṃpūjayatyaṃga savīraśaivaḥ || 2 || 
krimikīṭapaṃtagyebhyaḥ paśavaḥ prajñayādhikāḥ ||
paśubhyopi narāḥ śreṣṭhāḥ teṣu śreṣṭhā dvijātayaḥ || 1 || 

<pṛ0 2a> dvijātiṣvadhikā viprā vipreṣu kratubuddhayaḥ ||
kratubuddheṣu kartārastebhyaḥ ssaṃnyāsīnodhikāḥ || 2 ||
teṣu vijñānina śreṣṭhā teṣu śaṃkara pūjakāḥ || 
teṣu śreṣṭhā māhābhāgā mama liṃgāṃga saṃginaḥ || 3 || 
liṃgāṃga saṃgīṣvadhikaḥ ṣaṭsthala jñānavān bhavet ||
tasmādapyadhiko nāstī triṣu lokeṣu sarvadā || 4 || 
savaṃdya sarvadā pūjya saṃsāra vijigīṣubhiḥ ||
bahunoktena kiṃ vatsa sarāvāhaṃ na saṃśayaḥ || pārameśvare || 
vibhūtiryasya bhālesti kaṃṭe liṃgamadātmakaṃ ||
rudrākṣa dhāraṇaṃ dehe sohaṃ devi na saṃśayaḥ ||
ītyādi pramāṇa balādvīraśaivānāṃ sarvottamatvaṃ siddhaṃ nanu ||
śrutibhraṣṭhaḥ śrutiprokta prāyaścīte bhayaṃ gataḥ || 

<pṛ0 2ba> krameṇa śṛti siddhyarthaṃ manuṣyastaṃtramānayediti || 
śrutipatha galitānāṃ mānavānāṃ hi taṃtraśṛtipatha niratānāṃ || 
tatsadā naiva savyaṃ atyaṃta skhalitānāṃ hi narāṇāṃ vedamārgataḥ 
pāṃcarātrādayo mārgā kālenaivopapādakāḥ || 
kāpālaṃ pāṃcarātraṃ ca yāmalaṃ vā mamārhataṃ || 
evaṃ vidhāni cānyāni mohanārthāni tāni tu || 1 ||
vipakṣāścaiva ye kecitkapālāṃ kruta(?) bhūṣaṇāḥ || 
śaivāpāśupatāścānya pāṃcarātrāstathāpare || 2 || 
tathānye ca durātmānaḥ sarvepya mukhadaivatāḥ 
pāśabadhastathā dharmo jagatapālanāt smṛtaḥ || 
taṃ khaṃḍayati yasmātte pāṣāṃḍāstena hetunā || 
ītyādi

<pṛ0 3a> kaurma brahmakaivarta sūtasaṃhitā vāsiṣṭhalaiṃgya 
pārāśaropapurāṇavacanaiḥ || 
kevala taṃtramārgāśrāyāṇasyāniṃditatvādvīraśaivānuṣṭheya 
dharmāṇāṃ tādṛśa taṃtreṣvavavihitatvena vaidikeṣu śṛtismṛtīti 
sāhāseṣvadarśanā tādṛśa dharmānuṣṭhānaika tatparāṇāṃ 
vīraśaivānāṃ kathaṃkāramutkarṣaḥ || prasādyata īti cainnaivaṃ 
sāṃkhyayogaḥ pāṃcarātravedāḥ pāśupataṃ tathā || 
ātmapramāṇāṇyatāni na haṃtavyānī hetubhirītyanuśāsanīka 
māhābhāratavacanāt purāṇāṃtarairniṃditānāṃ pāṃcarātrādyāgamānāṃ 
grāhyatva prāpyā tadudita dharmāṇāmapyavaśyānuṣṭeyatvaṃ siddhameva || na 
ca bhāratavacanena pāṃcarātrapāśupatadharmāṇā

<pṛ0 3ba> mevācaraṇīyatva paryavasānepi vīraśaivadharmāṇāṃ 
pāṃcarātrapāśupatānaṃtarbhāvāt dharmānuṣṭānaika tatpārāṇāṃ 
mutkarṣa iti vācyaṃ pāśupatasya vaidikāvaidika bhedena dvaitavidyānnīṣedha 
vacanānāma vaidikapāśupata paratvena vidhiyānāṃ vaidika pāśupata 
vīraśaiva paratvena vyavasyāyā evāpyaya dikṣitaiḥ kalpataru vyākhyāne || 
parimale patyurasāmajasyādityāsīddhikaraṇe pāśupata matabhedamuktvā tatra 
laguḍīśa pāśupatasya grāhyatvamabhyadaī tata || 
tasmādviraśaivadharmāṇāṃ tāṃtrikatva niṣedhya tāvachedakaṃ na bhavati 
nanu || laguḍiśa pāśupatasyāpyayadīkṣataiḥ kṛte parimale vaidikatvena 
grāhyatva 

<pṛ0 4a> pratipādanapī vīraśaivadharmapratipādakāgamānāṃ laguḍiśa 
pāśupatānaṃtarabhāvena bhinnatvā tu teṣā vaidikatvenāgrāhyatvaṃ 
paryavasitamiti caran || 
siddhāṃtaśikhāmaṇau ||
vedasiddhāṃtayoraikayamekārtha pratipādanāt prāmāṇyaṃ sadṛśaṃ jñeyaṃ 
paṃḍitairetayoḥ sadā || kriyāsāre ||
āthavatsivaniśvāsā śrutibhyaste sivāgamāḥ || 
ādhikāḥ śivavaktrebhyassākṣādeva vinirgatāḥ || 2 ||
parasparāviruddhārthā śivoktā nigamāgamāḥ ālpabuddhibhiranyonyo virodhaḥ 
pratikalpate || 3 || 
nigamāgamānāṃ paramātpopadeśataḥ prāmāṇyamaviśeṣaṇa jñātavyaṃ 
tatvadarṣibhiḥ || 4 || 
ritivacanairvedāviruddha varṇanāt 

<pṛ0 4ba> bhramaṃ dharmapratipādakatvena sarvātma parameśvaraika kartṛkatvena 
cāgamaprāmāṇyasya siṣṭerabhyugataṃvyatvādāgamoktācāraṇāṃ sarveṣāṃ 
vai vaidikānuṣṭayatvaṃ siddhaṃ || ānyathā śivādi devatālayanirmāṇa 
śivaliṃgādi pratiṣṭhārayochavādi vidhi maṃtroddhāra maṃtradoṣa 
maṃtrasaṃskāra maṃtranyāsa mudrā vidhānādiṣu kevala sivāgamamuleṣu 
vaidikānāṃ pravṛtirna syāt || kiṃ ca sūtasaṃhitāyāṃ 
śivamahātmakhaṃḍe || vyāsavacanasya viśrame || bhaṇiyatvaṃ prasthāvye 
aṣṭādaśapurāṇānāṃ kartā satyavati sutaḥ kāmikādi prabhedānāṃ yathā 
devo maheśvara īti || tadvyākhyāne vidyāraṇyena vyāsavacaneṣu 
viśvāsotpādanārthamaṣṭādeśatyupakramya

<pṛ0 5a> kāmikādīnāṃ śaivāgamānāṃ sivoktatayā prāmāṇyaṃ yathā || 
tathā nārāyaṇāvatārarūpa vyāsapraṇitānāmaṣṭādaśapurāṇānāṃ 
prāmāṇyamiti pratipāditaṃ || tasmādāgamoktānāṃ 
viraśaivadharmāṇāmanuṣṭheyatvaṃ siddhaṃ || tathā ca tadāgameṣu 
ānādiśaivamādiśaivaṃ māhāśaivamanuśaivamavāṃtaraśaivaṃ 
pravaraśaivaṃ maṃtyaśaivamiti bhedena saptavidhānāṃ śaivānāṃ madhye 
ācārabhedabhinnānāṃ sāmānyaśaiva miśraśaiva śuddhaśaiva 
viraśaivarūpatayā caturvidhānāṃ || madhya viraśavasyācārabhedena 
sāmānyaviraśaiva viśeṣavīraśaiva nirābhāra viraśaivabhedena 
trividhānāṃ tadunuṣṭeya bhasmarudrākṣadhāraṇa paṃcākṣari

<pṛ0 5ba> japaliṃgadhāraṇa guruliṃga jaṃgamapūjā tatpādodaka prāsāda 
svīkārarūpa sādhāraṇa viraśaivadharmāṇāṃ kālāgni rudra bhasmajā 
bālacidaṃbara vedāṃtasāradyupaniśatsu liṃgadhāraṇādi pratipādakeṣu 
bahva(?) ca ttaitariyādi maṃtreṣu śaṃkarasaṃhitā sūtasaṃhitā śivarahasya 
liṃgapurāṇādiṣu kāmikādi vātulāṃ teṣu cāṣṭaviṃśati śaivāgameṣu 
pratipādita nāmārādhyaśābdita viraśaiveṣu tadita viraśaiveṣu 
tatvādvaidikatvaṃ siddhaṃ || ānyathā vaidikatvenābhimatānāṃ 
brāhmaṇānāṃ sthochāvapratiṣṭādi tāṃtrikadhārmānuṣṭhānuvatāṃ 
vaidikatvaṃ na syāt || tathā ca || tatsamāna nyāya

<pṛ0 6a> tayokta vaidikadharmānuṣṭhāna va tāṃtrikadharmānuṣṭhānepi 
vaidikatvaṃ nirayapāyaṃ nanu || viraśaiva dharmapratipādaka śrutyāgamānāṃ 
ācārabhedena sāmānya vīśeṣa nirābhāra samākhyāṃvatāṃ bhakta 
māheśvara prāsādi prāṇaliṃgī śaraṇaikya sthalānāṃ ācāraliṃga 
guruliṃga śivaliṃga jaṃgamaliṃga prasādaliṃga mahāliṃgābhidhānānāṃ 
liṃgasthalānāṃ pūrvoktāṃga sthalānāṃ ca uttarottara dharmācaraṇasya 
pūrṣaya pūrvadharmācaraṇānaṃtara bhāvitayā kramikatvena tādṛśa 
dharmācaraṇasya kālahastī śrīśailādi deśa sthita yajñopavitadhāraṇa 
liṃgadhāraṇādi vaidika tāṃtrika dharmasamuccayānuṣṭhāna

<pṛ0 6ba> parārādhyaviṣayatvena na tuṃ śakyatvākathamārādhyetara kevala 
vīraśaiva viṣayatvamiti cen || ātyāśramibhyaḥ paramapavitraṃ provāca 
samyagṛṣisaṃghajuṣṭaṃ īti śvetāśvataropaniṣadhi || ātyāśrami sarvadā 
sakṛdvā japediti kaivalyopaniṣadi ca śruyamāṇasyātyāśrami śabdasya 
brahmacāri grahasthaśca vānaprasthaśca bhikṣukaḥ ātivarṇāśrami ceti 
kramāchreṣṭo vicakṣaṇaḥ || 1 || 
grahastopi vanasthasya te rapyaṃbuje kṣaṇaḥ ||
vidyotkarṣabalenaiva gururbhavati nānyathā || 2 ||
vānaprasthāśramasthopi tathā saṃnyāsino hare ||
vidyotkarṣabalenaiva gururbhavati nānyathā || 3 ||

<pṛ0 7a> ativarṇāśrami prokto gurussarvādhikāriṇāṃ ||
na tasyāpi bhavechreṣṭho yathāhaṃ puruṣottama || 4 ||
ātivarṇāśrami sadguruṇāṃ gururuccate ||
yasya varṇāśramācāro galitaḥ svātmadarśanāt || 5 || 
savarṇānāśramān sarvān tasya svātmani sthitaḥ ||
yotītya svāśramān varṇānātmanyavasthitaḥ pumān || 6 || 
sotivarṇāśramā proktā sarvavedārthavādibhiḥ ||
paṃcākṣarī parāvidyā sivajotiravāptaye || 7 || 
voṃkāra praṇavaḥ sūkṣmo vidyānāṃ bījamuttamaṃ || 
tasmādaśeṣatatvāni praṇavena viśodayet || 8 || 
sitenoddhulayaddehamagnirityādi saptabhiḥ ||
triyāyuṣaṃ jamadagneriti kuryāt trīpuṇḍrakaṃ || 9 ||

<pṛ0 7ba> viciṃtayet svātmani śaṃbhumīśaṃ śivaṃ 
paraṃśāṃtamumāsāhāyaṃ || 
bhasmāṃgamāviṣṇu taditya dehamatyāśramācāramimaṃ vadaṃti || 10 || 
ātyāśramoyaṃ sarveṣu catuṣṭhāpyāśrameṣu ca yaḥ paśuḥ sa paśutvaṃ ca 
vratenānena saṃtyajet || 
viśodhya sarvatatvāni praṇavenāthayā punaḥ || 
saṃsthāpyamī cātmānaṃ nirmale parameśvare || 
plāvitvātmano dehaṃ te naiva jñānavāriṇāṃ eṣaḥ pāśupatoyogaḥ 
paśupāśavimuktaye sarvavedāṃtasāroyamatyaśrama īti śruti || 
vrataṃ pāśupataṃ yena samyagācaritaṃ mudā || tasya jñānaṃ vijāyet tena 
pāśavimocanamityādi kāmikā

<pṛ0 8a> skāṃda laiṃgya sivāgamavacanaiḥ ativarmāśramiṇāṃ 
varṇāśramadharmācaraṇasilebhyaḥ utkṛṣṭatva pratipādanāt || 
ātyāśramitvaṃ paṃcamamukhasamudbhutaṃ paṃcamāśramavatsu vīraśaiveṣu 
paryavasyaṃtī tena pūrvoktārādhya paratvaṃ śaṃkāvakāśaḥ || āta eva 
pūrvākta krimikīṭapataṃgebhya ītyādi vīraśaivotkarṣapratipādakaṃ 
śāṃkarasaṃhitāvacanamavyabhicaritatayā paryavasyaṃti || nanu ātyāśrama 
pratipādaka śrutyāgamapurāṇavacanāni teṣveva paryavasyaṃti || kiṃtu || 
varṇāśramācāravatsu brāhmaṇeṣvarādhyaṣu tadeti rikteṣvapi paryavasyaṃti 
|| tathāhi āti

<pṛ0 8ba> varṇāśramācāraṃ ītyatra brāhmaṇa kṣatriya vaisya 
śudrāścatvāro varṇāḥ || āśudrānṇāmaduṣṭhakarmaṇāmupāyanaṃ 
vedādhyayanamaṃ dhyāyanaṃ phalavaṃti ca karmāṇi yogyāni || brahmacarya 
grahaste vānaprastha tvaṃ sanyāsitva bhedena || āśramāścatvāreḥ 
tatvagurukulavāsa bhikṣācaraṇā vedādhyayanādikaṃ prathamāśramadharmaḥ 
garbhadānādi grāhyakarma saptapākayajñe saptahaviryajña saptasaṃsthā 
paṃcamāhāyajñe rūpāṣṭau catvāri saṃskārāḥ 
yajanayājanādhyayanādhyāpanadānapratigraharūpa ṣaṭkarmāṇi ca 
dvitiyāśramadharmaḥ ||

<pṛ0 9a> vanavāsanīvārakaṃdaphalāhāranīyamajaṭājinadhāraṇa 
kṛchracāṃdrāyaṇādayācaraṇa tṛtīyāśramadharmaḥ || pūrvoktāśrama 
traya dharmatyāgapūrvakaṃ brahmavicāra bhikṣācaraṇa rāgadveṣādi varjana 
vāṅmayamaikarātrivāsa yogābhyāsanādikaṃ caturthāśramadharmaḥ || 
ātivarṇāśramācārā ityatra atikrāṃtā varṇāśramācāraniti 
ātyādayākrāṃtā hyarthe dvitīyameti vyākarṇānuśāsanamaryādayā 
śabdārthe yojanīya pūrvokta dharmācaraṇavatsu saṃbhavatāmevāti 
varṇāśramācārādiśabdānāṃ yogiprasakti sāpekṣānāṃ prati

<pṛ0 9ba> yogasabaṃdinā kṛteṣu ārādhya vyatirikteṣu vīraśaiveṣu vṛtiḥ 
kathamupapadyate || niśadhā vācakānāṃ śabdānāṃ pratiyogī saṃbadha 
saṃbhāvanā śāliṣveva vartanaucityāditya cen ativarṇāśramācārāḥ 
saṃbhavaṃtyurdhva vakrataḥ || te vai sākṣānmāhādevā modaṃte hyagameṣu 
ceti paṃcāsya paṃcamamukhādyato jātāstatodhikāḥ paṃcamāssarvavarṇebhyo 
madbhaktāmapriyāssadeti liṃgaṃ śivo bhavet kṣetramaṃgaṃ saṃyoga aśraya || 
tasmāliṃgāṃgaśobhā mokṣamārgaikaniṣṭā || liṃgāṃga sāmarasyāśca 
śarire liṃgadhāraṇaṃ || niyamenaiva

<pṛ0 10a> tasmāliṃgāṃga saṃyukto yopi sotyāśrami bhavediti || 1 || 
īśānato syādgaṇavaṃśajānāṃ liṃgāṃgaśobhā mokṣamārgaikaniṣṭā || 
liṃgāṃga sāmarasyāśca śarire liṃgadhāraṇaṃ || niyame naiva kṛtvāte 
muktā saṃsārabaṃdhanāt || 2 || 
īti 
kāmikādyāgamakriyāsārodāhṛtavedāṃtasāropaniṣadādivacanairvīraśaivāna
amevātiśramācārādiśabdānāṃ rūpyābhiyatvasya samyagvaktuṃ śakyatvāt || 
na cātyāśramiśabdaḥ kṛtāśramadharmacaraṇeṣu pravartate || 
sapratiyogīkāśabdatvāditi yaduktaṃ tadupapannaṃ 

<pṛ0 10ba> āpahāta vākyādiśabdānāṃ brahmaṇa pratiyogīnāpāpmanāma 
saṃbhavāt pravṛttirna syāt || tathā cedvarṇāśramadharmāṇāṃ 
manuṣṭānapratiyogyabhāvepyatyāśramādiśabdavācyatvaṃ saṃgachaṃti na 
cātyādaya īti vyākarṇānuśāsanavirodha iti vāca || śuka 
vāmadevavajjaṃnmāṃtarīya varṇāśramadharmānuṣṭā prakalpanānna 
vaiyākarṇānuśāsanavirodhaḥ || na caivamatyāśramiśabdārtha saṃgati 
nirvahaṇe vīraśaivātiriktānāṃmitareṣāṃ viraktānāṃ jñānino 
śudrāṇāṃmapyatyā śramitvaṃ || prasaṃgṛti vācyaṃ || 
ativarṇāśramācāra
