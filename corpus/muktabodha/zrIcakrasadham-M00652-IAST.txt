<no.	1300 ka>
<title	śrīcakrasādhanam >
<extent	10 patra >
<subject	tantra >

<pṛ0 1a> oṃśrīgaṇeśāyanamaḥ [athaśrīcakrasādhanavidhiḥ] tatra 
jñānārṇave śṛṇusarvāṃrgesubhageśrīcakravidhimuttamaṃ yasyavi
jñānamātreṇakarttāharttāsadāśivaḥ anenavidhināyatraśrīca
krakramasaṃyutaṃ pūjyatetatrasakalaṃvaśīkuryānnasaṃśayaḥ naga
raṃvaśamāyātideśamaṃḍalamadrite yoṣitaḥsakalāvaśyājva
latkālāgnipīḍitaḥ vidyāvimūḍhahṛdayāḥsādhakenyastamā
nasaḥ taddarśanenadeveśijāyaṃtesarvayoṣitaḥ gorocanādibhi

<pṛ0 1ba> rdravyaiścakrarājaṃsamālikhet atīvasuṃdaraṃramyaṃtanmadhye
pratimāṃvarīṃ jvalaṃtīṃnāmasahitāṃmahāvījavidarbhitaṃ ma
hāvījaṃmūlavidyāyākāmarājakūṭaṃ vidarbhalakṣaṇaṃprāgevo
ktaṃ ciṃtayetutadādeviyojanānāṃsahasraśaḥ adṛṣṭapūrvāde
veśiśrutamātrāpidurlabhā rājakanyāthavāvānyābhayalajjā 
vivarjitā āyātisādhakaṃsamyakmaṃtramūḍhāsatīpriye  
cakramadhyagatobhūtvāsādhakaściṃtayedyadā udyatsūryasahasrā

<pṛ0 2a> bhāmātmānamaruṇaṃtathā sādhyamapyahaṇībhūtaṃcitayetparame
śvari anenakramayogenasvayaṃkaṃdarparūpavān sarvasauṃdarya
subhagaḥsarvalokavaśaṃkaraḥ sarvaraktopacāraiścamudrāsahita 
vigraha cakraṃprapūjayedyastuyasyanāmavidarbhitaṃ sabhaveddāsa
vaddevidhanāḍhyovāpibhūpatiḥ cakramadhyagataṃkuryānnāma 
yasyāstuyoṣitaḥ adṛṣṭāyāmaheśāniyonimudrādharo 
vudhaḥ haṭhādānayateśīghraṃpakṣiṇīṃrājakanyakāṃ nāgaka

<pṛ0 2ba> nyāmapsarasaṃkhecarīṃcāsurāṃganī vidyādharīṃdivyarūpā
mṛṣikanyāmṛṣistriyaṃ madanodbhavasaṃtāpasphurajjaghanamaṃ
ḍalāṃ kāmavāṇaprabhinnātakaraṇāṃlolacakṣuṣaṃ mahākāma
kalādhyānayogāttusuravaṃdite kṣobhayetsvargabhūrlokapātā
latalayoṣitaḥ athatilakādivaśyaprayogaḥ rocanābhā
gamekaṃtubhāgamekaṃtukuṃkumaṃ athabhāgadvayaṃdevicaṃdanaṃmardaye
tsamaṃ ekattalikaṃkuryāttrailokyavaśakāriṇaṃ aṣṭottaraśatā

<pṛ0 3a> vṛtyāmaṃtrayitvāvaśaṃnayet rājānaṃnagaraṃgrāmaṃyenayadyapradarśya
te maṃtriṇāparameśānitatsarvaṃtasyavaśyagaṃ tāṃvūlaṃdhūpamu
dakaṃpatraṃpuṣpaṃphalaṃdadhi dugdhaṃghṛtaṃcūrṇapatraṃvastraṃkarpūramevaca 
kastūrīghusṛṇaṃcailālavaṃgaṃjātipatragaṃ phalaṃvācastuyadya
stusakalaṃparameśvari śatamaṣṭottaraṃjaptvāyasmaiyasmaiprayaccha
ti savaśyojāyatedevinātrakāryāvicāraṇā striyastusaka
lāvaśyādāsībhūtābhavaṃtihi haṭākarṣaṇametattukathitaṃnā

<pṛ0 3ba> nyathābhavet athākarṣaṇaprayogaḥ rahasyesthānakemaṃtrī
likhedrovanayābhuvi cāruśrṛṃgāraveṣāḍhyāṃsarvābharaṇabhū
ṣitāṃ pratimāṃsuṃdarāṃgītāvilikhyasumanoharaṃ tadbhālakaṃ
ṭhahṛnnābhitattanmaṃḍalayoṣitāṃ janmanāmamahāvidyāmaṃku
śāṃtarvidarbhitāṃ sarvasaṃdhiṣudehasyamadanākṣaramālikhet 
līnaṃḍāḍimapuṣpābhaṃciṃtayeddehasaṃdhiṣu tadāśābhimukho
bhūtvāsvayaṃdevīsvarūpakaḥ mudrāṃtatkṣobhiṇīṃvadhvāmaṃtramaṣṭa

<pṛ0 4a> śataṃjapet niyojyadahanāgārecaṃdrasūryakalātmake tato 
vikalasarvāṃgīkāmabāṇaiprapīḍitāṃ ananyamānasaṃprema
bhramamāṇāṃmadālasāṃ evamākarṣayennārīyojanānāṃśatā 
api tathā mātṛkāṃvilikheccakravāhyataḥsakalāṃpriye 
bhūrjapatresvarṇapatrairaupyapatretathāṃvare sovadhaḥsarvajaṃtūnāṃvyā
ghrādīnāṃviśeṣataḥ tathaivamātṛkāyuktāṃsusaṃjñāṃnāmamaṃḍi
tāṃ karpūrakuṃkumādyaistuajarāmaratāṃlabhet anenaivavidhāne

<pṛ0 4ba> narocanāgurukuṃkumaiḥ likhitaṃcakrayogenasādhyanāmava
rānane vidarbhitaṃsvanāmnātuyasminkasmiṃnapisthitaṃ sthā
varaṃjaṃgamaṃvāpisakalaṃjanamaṃḍalaṃ vaśīkuryānmaheśāni 
pādākrāṃtaṃnasaṃśayaḥ mahātripurasuṃdaryāḥkāmakūṭenabhā
svatā ekamevamavaṣṭabhyasādhyanāmākṣarāṇihi vahirapya
likhairvarṇaiḥmātṛkāyāḥpraveṣṭayet hemamadhyagatekuryāchi
khākhāyāṃvāmakebhuje dhārayedyatrakutrāpitrailokyavaśakāra

<pṛ0 5a> kaṃ rājeṃdramapideveśivāsabhūtaṃkarotihi rājānovājinaḥ 
sarvemahāduṣṭāmahotkaṭāḥ vyāghrāḥkesariṇomattāvaśyāsta
syabhavaṃtihi pūrvakrameṇanagaranāmasaṃdarbhyaśailaje madhyeca
tuḥpathevāpicaturdikṣunidhāpayet mahākṣobhoyoṣitāṃ
tubhajanānāṃmahatāmapi tathaivasarvaduṣṭānāṃpurasthānāṃca 
jāyate etanmadhyagatāṃpṛthvīṃsaśailavanakānanaṃ jvalaṃti 
sarvarājeṃdramaṃḍitāṃsāgarāṃbarāṃ māsaṣaṭkaṃciṃtayedyaḥsasaṃ

<pṛ0 5ba> kṣobhakarobhavet kaṭākṣakṣetramātreṇanāryastasyavaśāḥ
priye rājānovrāhmaṇāvaiśyāḥśūdrāścapaśavojagat dṛ
ṣṭākarṣayatedevitrailaukyaṃsacarācaraṃ dṛṣṭāviṣaṃnāśayate 
nātrakāryāvicāraṇā bhūtapretapiśācāṃścajvarāṃścāturthi
kādina śūlagulmādirogāṃścadṛṣṭānāśayatikṣaṇāt 
etatsiṃdūrasubhagaṃrātrausaṃpūjitaṃpriye yojanānāṃśatādevi 
samyagākarṣayetstriyaṃ yadādikṣuvidikṣevaṃsamyakdevipra

<pṛ0 6a> pūjate tattadikṣusthitālokānvidiksthānapisuṃda
rī vaśamānayateśīghraṃsaputrayasavāṃdhavān bhūrjapatresa
mālikhyarocanāgurukuṃkumaiḥ tatmadhyenagaraṃdeśaṃmaṃḍalaṃ 
khaṃḍamevava nāmnāvidarbhitaṃsvasyapūjayitvāyathāvi
dhi bhūmimadhyegataṃkṛtvātrailokyaṃvaśamānayet atha
vādhārayetkaṃṭheśikhāyāṃvāhumūlake yatrakutrasthitaṃ
bhadrekṣobhayennagaraṃmahatarkakṣīreṇasaṃyuktaṃdhatūrarasaṃ 

<pṛ0 6ba> tathā rocanākuṃkumaicaivalākṣālaktakasaṃyutaṃ kastūrī
dravasaṃyuktamekīkṛtyatataḥparaṃ cakrametatsamālikhyaya
syanāmnāmaheśvari tasyavyāghrahayavyādhiripusargagajādi
kaṃ coragrahājalāriṣṭhaṃśākinīḍākinībhayaṃ bhayaṃnavidya
tedeviparamaṃtrābhivārakaṃ nityaṃsamarcayeddevikālamṛtyuṃ 
vināśayet athavāmadhyagāṃdevitrikoṇobhayamadhyagaṃ 
adhastāṃnāmasaṃyuktaṃrocanākuṃkumānvitaṃ nidhāpayetsa

<pṛ0 7a> ptāhāddāsavatkiṃkarobhavet pītadravyaiḥsamālikhyapī
tapuṣpaiḥsamarcayet pūrvāśābhimukhobhūtvāstaṃbhayetsarva
vādinaḥ sahasravadanodevimūkobhavatitatkṣaṇāt nāmnā 
yasyasavāgmīhipāṣāṇivaniścalaḥ jāyatedevadeveśi
mahānīlīrasenatu nāmasaṃyojyavidhivatdakṣiṇāśā 
mukhoyudhaḥ vahnaudagdhvāmaheśānimārayedvairiṇaṃpriye 
mahiṣāścapurīṣābhyāṃsamamākṛṣyaśailaje gomūtreṇa 

<pṛ0 7ba> casaṃlikhyanāmasaṃdarbhyapūrvavat laṃvamānaṃtadākāśeśatrūccā
ṭanakārakaṃ mahānīlīrocanābhyāṃdugdhalākṣārasādibhiḥ 
vilikhyadhārayenmaṃtrīsarvavarṇānvaśaṃtayet anenaivavidhāne
nasthāpayenīrasamadhyagaṃ tenodakenasaṃsrānaṃpītaṃtatsarvava
śyakṛt saubhāgyaṃjāyatetenapānīyenanasaṃśayaḥ etanmadhya
gatātenapṛthvīnagaraṃvāmalocane saptāhātkṣobhayetsatyaṃjvala
mānaṃviciṃtayetathavakṣyemaheśānimahāpātakanāśanaḥ 

<pṛ0 8a> śivāṃsaṃpūjayeddevisugaṃdhaiḥkusumaiḥpriye mahāpātakayu
ktātmātatkṣaṇātpātahābhavet śamīdūrvākurāśvatthapalla
vainathavārkajaiḥ māsenahaṃtikaluṣaṃsaptajanmakṛtaṃnaraḥ pūrvā
śābhimukhobhūtvāpītadravyaiḥsamarcayet (yasyasnāmākitaṃta
tramaṃtrahāniḥprajāyate agnirākṣase) pītasthānesamālikhya 
staṃbhayetsarvavādinaḥ uttarābhimukhobhūtvāvaṃdanenasamāli
khet saṃpūjyavidhivadbhaktyāsarvayoṣimanoharaḥ vallabhojāya

<pṛ0 8ba> tetāsāṃdāsīmivavaśaṃnayet yamāśābhimukhobhūtvācakraṃkṛ
ṣṇajadārcayet yasyanāmāṃkitaṃtatramaṃtrahāniḥprajāyate a
gnirākṣasavāyavyaśaṃbhukoṇeṣupūjitaṃ pūrvavatparameśānikra
meṇaparitoṣitaṃ staṃbhavidveṣagāvyādhiśatrūccāṭakaraṃparaṃ ro
canālikhitaṃcakraṃkṣīramadhyekṣipedvudhaḥ sarvavaśyakaraṃdevibha
vatyevanasaṃśayaḥ gomūtramadhyagaṃsamyakśatrūccāṭakaraṃparaṃtai
lasthaṃcakrarājaṃtaṃtadvidveṣāṃtakaraṃbhavet jvalajvalanamadhyasthaṃ 

<pṛ0 9a> śatrunāśakaraṃbhavet yadaikāṃtecaturbhāgesiṃdūrarajasālikhet 
sarvavāhyatārabhyayāvanmadhyaṃmaheśvari akārādikṣakārāṃ
tāṃmātṛkāṃtatravinyaset pūjayedrātrisamayekulācārakrame
ṇatā sādhakaḥkhecarodevijāyatenātrasaṃśayaḥgirāvekatarau 
tadvadarvayetkulamārgataḥajarāmaratāṃlavdhvāsukhībhavatimāṃ
trikaḥ smaśānepūjayecakraṃmahābhūtadinetuyaḥpūrvakrameṇavi
dhivatsādhakasthiramānasaḥ khaḍgasiddhiṃcavetālasiddhiṃcaguṭi

<pṛ0 9ba> kāṃlabhet pādukāṃjanasiddhiṃcamanasiddhiṃcadhātudaṃ samahāvī
rasiddhiṃtuyakṣiṇīceṭakodbhavāṃ sarvaṃlabhatemaṃtrīnātrakāryāvi
cāraṇā [itiśrīcakrasādhanaḥ]
