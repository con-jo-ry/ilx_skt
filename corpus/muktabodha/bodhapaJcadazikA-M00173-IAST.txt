{śrībodhapañcadaśikā 

mahāmāheśvarācāryābhinavaguptapādaviracitā} | 

śrīgaṇeśāya namaḥ || 

śrīśāradāmbāyai namaḥ || 

iha khalu śivādvayaśāsanāmbhodhipāradṛśvā śrīmān 
mahāmāheśvaro'bhinavaguptākhya 
ācāryavaro'titīkṣṇapratibhāprasādātiśayato'vagāhanīyeṣu 
śaivāgameṣu keṣāñcidanugrāhyāṇāṃ śiṣyāṇāṃ 
natisaukumārya paryālocyānāyāsameva 
trikaśāstrasaṃpradāyajijñāsūṃstānupadidikṣuḥ pañcadaśabhiḥ 
ślokaistatpratibodhanāya śāstrārthamupanibadhnāti | tatrādau 
nityoditasaṃvitprakāśasyāvabhāsane pramāṇavyāpārābhāva iti 
pratipādayannāha (-) 

anastamitabhārūpastejasāṃ tamasāmapi | ya eko'ntaryadantaśca 
tejāṃsi ca tamāṃsi ca || 1 || 

svatantraśivaprakāśo hi sarvābhāsakaḥ sadodito na svaṃ 
prakāśayituṃ prakāśāntaramapekṣate svaprakāśatvāt 
tasyā'stoditatvābhāvāt sakṛdvibhāto'yamātmā iti nahi 
draṣṭurdaṣṭrerviparilopo vidyate'vināśitvāt iti śruteḥ | (bṛ0 
ā0 4|3|23|) ya eko'dvitīyo'rkādīnāṃ tejasāṃ 
pramāṇādiprakāśānāṃ ca tathā madamūrchādyavasthārūpāṇāṃ 
tamasāṃ bāhyāprakāśānāmantaravasthitatvāt tadanuprāṇako 
bahirapi tasyaiva saṃvitprakāśasya sattvāttadantareva 
tānyarkādīnāṃ tejastamāṃsi svasattāmupayānti | 

<p. 2> 

nahi bhārūpaṃ saṃvitprakāśaṃ vinā kimapi ghaṭādi bhāvajātaṃ 
prakāśamānatāmeti te ca prakāśā astaṃ yānti udayanti ca | 
prakāśaḥ punarastodayānākalitaḥ pramāṇānāmapi jīvitarūpaṃ 
mūlapramāṇaṃ tamarkādiprakāśaḥ kaścidavabhāsayituṃ na 
śaknoti tasya prakāśyatvāt svābhāsane'pyasamarthasya kathaṃ 
paraṃ bhāsayituṃ prāgalbhyaṃ pratyuta svābhāsane'pi 
saṃvitprakāśamevāpekṣate sa iti svaprakāśatvādarkādīnāṃ sa 
eva bhāsakaḥ | yaduktam (-) 

yanna sūryoṃ navā somo nāgnirbhāsayate'pi ca || 
nacārkasomavahṇīnāṃ tatprakāśādvinā mahaḥ || 

kimapyasti nijaṃ kintu saṃviditthaṃ prakāśate | (taṃ0 3 | 115) 

iti | tathā gītāsu (-) 

na tadbhāsayate sūryo na śaśāṅko na pāvakaḥ | yadgatvā na 
nivartante taddhāma paramaṃ mama || (bha0 gī0 11|56) 

abodho'pi na bodhyamāno bodhādbhidyate'nyathā 
tatsattvānupapatteriti sthityā khapuppāderapyasato 
vyāpakatvādaparicchinnaḥ saṃvitprakāśo viśvatra 
nirnirodhāvaraṇaṃ tattadvaicitryātmanā vijṛmbhate ityetena kimapi 
pramāṇādi tatra sarvajīvitabhū nopayujyate ityuktaṃ bhavati || 1 
|| 

evaṃ saṃvitprakāśaḥ prakāśyaṃ prameyaṃ prakāśayatīti 
prakāśaprakāśanīyabhāve siddhe kathaṃ śivādvayabhāva 
ityāśaṃkyāha (-) 

sa eva sarvabhūtānāṃ svabhāvaḥ parameśvaraḥ | bhāvajātaṃ hi 
tasyaiva śaktirīśvaratāmayī || 2 || 

sa eva parameśvaraḥ svatantraciddhanatāsaparamārthaḥ 
sarvabhūtānāṃ samagrāṇāṃ śivādisakalāntānāṃ 
pramātṛvargāṇāṃ sakalānāṃ prameyavargāṇāṃ ca ubhayeṣāṃ 
prāhyagrāhakarūpāṇāṃ sphurattātmānuttaraprakāśavapuḥ 
svabhāvo'nyathā (-) 

<p. 3>

yattatra nahi viśrāntaṃ tannabhaḥ kusumāyate | (taṃ0 8|3) 

iti sthityā saṃvitprakāśādbhinnasyā'satvānna kimapi prakāśeta 
| etadeva hi pārameśvaryamananyāpekṣaṃ svātantryaṃ 
yattattadaniyataviśvavaicitryeṇa svecchayaiva svātmabhittau 
samunmīlanaṃ tatrāpi niyatākāradhāritve aiśvarya vighaṭeta | 
yaduktamācāryeṇaiva (-) 

asthāsyadekarūpeṇa vapuṣā cenmaheśvaraḥ | maheśvaratvaṃ 
saṃvittvaṃ tadatyakṣyaddhaṭādivat || (taṃ0 3|100) 

kevalaṃ vedyavedakatvarūpasya vyavahārasya māyābhūmau 
kalpitapramātrapekṣayā tātkālikī vyavahniyamāṇatā | 
yaduktamīśvarapratyabhijñāyām (-) 

kevalaṃ bhinnasaṃvedyadeśakālānurodhataḥ | 
jñānasmṛtyavasāyādivyavahāro'nubhūyate || (ī0 pra0 1|5|21) 

iti | vastutastu 
sadaivābhinnaprakāśamayo'nuttarānākhyeyasaṃvitsvabhāvaḥ 
parameśvaraḥ eva svātmocchalattayā tattadviśvabhāvaparisphuraṇena 
cakāstītyarthaḥ | etadeva hetudvārā spaṣṭaṃ nirūpayannāha 
bhāvajātamiti tasyaiva parameśvarasya hi yasmādīśvaratāmayī 
śaktiraiśvaryaṃrūpaṃ svātantryameva 
vācyavācakātmaviśvapadārthamayaṃ bhāvajātaṃ bhavati yaduktaṃ 
maṅgalāyām (-) 

śaktayo'sya jagatkṛtsnaṃ śaktimāṃstu maheśvaraḥ | 

itinītyā svātantryameva jagadātmanā prathate ityarthaḥ | yaduktam 
(-) 

bodho hi bdharūpatvādantarnānākṛtīḥ sthitāḥ | 
bahirābhāsayatyeva drāk sāmānyaviśeṣataḥ || (taṃ0 1|257) 

iti | tatheśvarapratyabhijñāyām (-) 

cidātmaiva hi devo'ntaḥ sthitamicchāvaśādbahiḥ | yogīva 
nirupādānamarthajātaṃ prakāśayet || ī0 pra0 (1|5|7) 

<p. 4>

iti | ataśca svaiśvayanirbharaḥ śiva evaikastathā tathā 
prasphurati ityekaiva saṃvitparamārthasatīti bhāvaḥ || 2 || 

nanvevaṃ parameśvarasya śaktayaṃ viśvamiti śaktitadvatoḥ 
parasparaṃ dharmadharmibhāvena bhedāt kathamekaśivātmabhāva 
ityāśaṃkyāha (-) 

śaktiśca śaktimadrūpād vyatirekaṃ na vāñchati | 
tādātmyamanayornityaṃ vahnidāhikayoriva || 3 || 

yathāgnestacchakteśca 
dāhakatvapācakatvaprakāśakatvādivilakṣaṇāyā 
anayorayutasiddhatvātsātatyena samavetayoraikātmyameva na kadācid 
dāhikādistacchaktiḥ svāśrayaṃ vahnimanāṭṭatya svarūpasattāṃ 
labhate yadyena vinā na tiṣṭheta tattasmānna vyatiricyate iti 
nyāyādabhinnasārūpyāt tādātmyamevāvajambate | yaduktam (-) 

mātṛkḷpte hi bhāvasya tatra tatra vapupyalam | ko bhedo vastuto 
vahnerdagdhṛpaktṛtvayoriva || (taṃ0 1|70) 

iti | evaṃ viśvabhāvavaicitryaprathāmayī svātantryaśaktiḥ 
śaktimallakṣaṇāt śivasvabhāvānna vyatirekamicchet tatsatve hi 
tasyāḥ svarūpavipralopāt svarūpavināśe 
kāryasaṃpādanaśakteranupapadyamānatvādviśvamidaṃ na prasphuret 
prasphurati ca viśvaṃ tasmācchivaprakāśābhinnā 
svātantryaśaktireva vedyavedakarūpeṇa visphurati ityamunaivāśayena 
śivasūtreṣu caitanyamātmā iti bhāvaprādhānyena nirdeśaḥ | 
nāpi kriyābhedāt kriyāvān bhidyate tāsvanavarataṃ 
pravartamānāsvapi karturekatvānapāyāt | evaṃ śaktīnāṃ 
bahusve'pi śaktimānekatvaṃ na vyabhicarati tadvahutvasyāpi 
ekasyāmeva svātantryaśaktāvantarbhāvānmukhyā saiva ekā 
śaktiśaktimadavyatirekiṇī sarvatrābhiṣicyate | yaduktam (-) 

tasmātsaṃvittvamevaitatsvātantryaṃ yattadapyalam | vivicyamānaṃ 
vahnīṣu paryavasyati śaktiṣu || (taṃ0 1|61) 

<p. 5>

iti | tathā (-) 

bahuśaktitvamapyasya tacchaktyaivā'viyuktatā | (taṃ0 1|68) 

iti | tathā (-) 

eka evāsya dharmo'sau sarvākṣepeṇa vartate | tena 
svātantryaśaktyaiva yukta ityāñjaso vidhiḥ || (taṃ0 1|67) 

iti | etadeva hṛdayaṃ vimarśaḥ sāra ityādiśabdaiḥ paribhāṣyate 
idameva jaḍaprakāśādvailakṣaṇyādhāyi | yaduktaṃ 
pratyabhijñāyām (-) 

svabhāvamavabhāsasya vimarśaṃ viduranyathā | 
prakāśo'rthoparakto'pi sphaṭikādijaḍopamaḥ || (ī0 pra0 1|5|10) 

iti | evaṃ māyādhvani durapahnavatvādbhāsamānasyāpi bhedasya 
ābhāsasāratvāt na svatantraśivādvayabhāvakhaṇḍanā iti || 3 || 

nanu parameśvarasya tattadviśvavaicitryaprathane kiṃmuddiśya 
pravṛttiḥ kiṃca nimittaṃ ityāśaṃkyāha (-) 

sa eva bhairavo devo jagadbharaṇalakṣaṇaḥ | svātmādarśe samagraṃ 
hi yacchaktyā pratibimbitam || 4 || 

sa eva śaktimān svatantro'nuttarabhaṭṭārakaḥ 
svasvātantryeṇodbhāsitasya viśvasya svātmabhittisaṃlagnatayā 
dhāraṇapoṣaṇasvabhāvatvād bhairavo viśvabharitasvarūpaḥ (-) 

mriyātsarvaṃ racayati sarvado vyāpako'khile | iti bhairavaśabdasya 
santatoccāraṇācchivaḥ || (vi0 bhai0 130) 

iti vijñānabhairavoktanītyā 
pūrṇāhantācamatkāranirbharabhairavasvarūpe viśramaṇādvā 
viśvenāpi dhāryamāṇaḥ poṣyamāṇaśca sadaiva viśvamayatvena 
prasphuraṇāt ityuktaṃ jagadbharaṇalakṣaṇa iti | bhairavasaṃvido 
hi vyatiricyamānasyānyasyācetyamānatvāt sarvatra pāripūrṇyaṃ 
viśvabharitasvabhāvatvāt | tasyaiva svaiśvaryaghanatāmahimnā 
yacchivādibhūmyantena tattvabhūtabhāvādyātmanā vijṛmbhaṇaṃ 
tadeva svātmagopanātmakrīḍārasikasya devatvaṃ 
vistaratasturbhairavadevaśabdayorvivaraṇaṃ śrītantrāloke 
draṣṭavyam | 

<p. 6> 

etadeva vaśvasṛṣṭyādau parameśvarasya nimittaṃ na punaḥ 
prāṇināṃ karmādi tatsāpekṣatve hi svātantryaṃ (na punaḥ 
prāṇināṃ karmādi tatsāpekṣatve hi svātantryaṃ) vighaṭeta 
svātantryamevānandaghanatvaṃ tadeva ghanatā'tiśayaprāptā 
samucchalatsvabhāvatvena tattadviśvavaicitryeṇa sphurati dyotate 
sarvotkarṣatayāpyāste | yaduktam (-) 

tasya svatantrabhāvo hi kiṃ kiṃ yanna vicintayet | taduktaṃ 
triśiraḥśāstre sa buddha iti vetti yaḥ || 

iti | yacchaktayā parākhyayā svārtmaiva svacchatvādinā 
pratibimbasahatvād darpaṇastasmin samagraṃ 
pramātṛprameyātmabhāvajātamupādānādisāmagrīnirapekṣatvena 
pratibimbitaṃ darpaṇo hi bimbasāpekṣaḥ 
svāntastattadracanārvaicitryaṃ dhatte eṣā 
parāśaktistannirapekṣameva 
viśvavaicitryamābhāsayatītyetadajāḍyasya vilasitam | yadāha 
bhaṭṭa utpaladevaḥ (-) 

nirupādānasaṃbhāramabhittāveva tanvate | jagaccitraṃ namastasmai 
kalāślāghyāya śūline || (22|9) 

iti | śrīśaktisūtreṣvapi (-) 

svecchayā svabhittau viśvamunmīlayate | (śa0 sū0 2) 

iti | śrīmānācāryo'pi (-) 

antarvibhāti sakalaṃ jagadātmanīha yadvadvicitraracanā 
makurāntarāle | bodhaḥ punarnijavimarśanasārayogā (-) dviśvaṃ 
parāmṛśati no makurastathā tu || (taṃ0 sā0 3 ā0) 

<p. 7>

iti | aṇupratyabhijñāyāmapi (-) 

nātha tvayā vinā bimbaṃ svacche svātmani darśitam | 
prasenādarpaṇeneva prabhāvādbhāvamaṇḍalam || 4 || 

iti | evaṃ vaiśvarūpyollāse bhedāmarśanāt kathaṃ 
bhairavasyādvaitatvaṃ syādityāśaṃkyāha (-) 

tasyaivaiṣā parā devī svarūpāmarśanotsukā | pūrṇatvaṃ 
sarvabhāveṣu yasyā nālpaṃ nacādhikam || 5 || 

tasyaiva bhairavātmanaḥ saṃvitprakāśasya eṣā 
vaiśvarūpyeṇollasantī svātantryākhyā 
śaktirdyotanādikrīḍāśīlatvād devī svātmani prakāśābhedena 
viśvasyāmarśanāvabhāsābhyāṃ parā pūrṇatayā sphurantī 
tatraiva darpaṇanagarādivadbhedyabhedatayā viśvasya 
parāmarśanādatyantaṃ bhedenāpi māyāpramāturucitena vimarśanāt 
parāparādisaṃjñāmavāptāpi svarūpasyāmarśane svātantryāt 
tattadbhāvajātaṃ svātmamayameveti viśvāhaṃbhāvacamatkṛtau 
utsukā svātmānameva tathātathā parāmraṣṭumautsukyena pravṛttā 
evaṃ tridhāpyāmarśane 
vyatiriktabhāvauṃnmukhyavirahātpāripūrṇyādanapetai 
svasvātantryeṇa svameva svātmanāmṛśati | yaduktaṃ 
pratyabhijñāyām (-) 

svātantryāmuktamātmānaṃ svātantryādadvayātmanaḥ | 
prabhurīśādisaṅkalpairnirmāya vyavahārayet || ī0 pra0 1|5|16) 

iti sthityā dvaitollāse'pi nādvaiṃtahāniriti | ata eva yasyāḥ 
sarvabhāveṣu sākalyena viśvavartiṣu nīlasukhādiṣu padārtheṣu 
pūrṇatvamakhaṇḍaṃ pāripūrṇyaṃ tacca nālpaṃ nacādhikaṃ 
bhāvānāmudayapralayayoḥ saṃvidoḥ nyūnādhikyābhāvāt 
rudrakṣetrajñādipramātṛṣvapi cito nyūnādhikatayā 
viśeṣābhāvātpūrṇatvaṃ na hīyate | yaduktaṃ brahmadarśane (-) 

yaiva cidgaganābhogabhūṣaṇe vyomni bhāskare | dharāvivarakoṣasthe 
saiva citkīṭakodare || 

<p. 8> 

iti | yathā pradeśākāśānāṃ mahākāśādanatiriktā vṛttirevaṃ 
nīlādiprakāśānāṃ brahmaṇo bṛhattvena bṛṃhakatayā ca 
tadanadhikavṛttitvamiti || 5 || 

ata evābhinnāni kṛtyāni karoti parameśvaro na bhedamayānītyāha (-) 

eṣa devo'nayā devyā nityaṃ krīḍārasotsukaḥ | vicitrān 
sṛṣṭisaṃhārān vidhatte yugapadvibhuḥ || 6 || 

eṣa pratyabhijñāpadamārūḍho 
dyotanādilakṣaṇaściddevo'nayoktalakṣaṇayā parā vākrūpayā 
viśvollāsanādikrīḍāśīlatvād devyā devasambandhinyā vā 
tatsamavāyadharmiṇyā saha nityamavirataṃ 
tattadbhūmikāviṛmbhaṇe'pi avyucchinnatvāt sadoditāyāṃ 
krīḍāyāṃ pañcakṛtyātmikāyāṃ yo raso'bhilāṣastatrotsuko 
nirapekṣaṃ prasahya tatra pravartamānaḥ | yaduktam (-) 

sadā sṛṣṭivinodāya sadā sthitisukhāsine | sadā 
tribhuvanāhāratṛptāya svāmine namaḥ || (u0 sto0 22|9) 

iti | tathā (-) 

pratikṣaṇamaviśrāntastrailokyaṃ kalpanāśataiḥ | kalpayannapi 
ko'pyeko nirvikalpo jayatyajaḥ || (sta0 ci0 112) 

iti | tacca krīḍanaṃ vicitram tathāhi parābhūmau 
prakāśaghanatāmayānāṃ bhāvānāmavicchinnāvabhāsādinā 
sadoditameva pañcakṛtyavidhayitvaṃ paśyantyāṃ 
codbhaviṣyatāmidantāvabhāsānāmahantārasodrekatayā tatrāpi 
svāṅgakalpatvena bhedāsūtraṇāṃ 
vijṛmbhayitastadbhūmikāmāpannasya tādṛśameva kṛtyakāritvam 
madhyāyāṃ punarasphuṭatayābhāsamānānāṃ 
bhāvānāmahantāyāmevedantāpradhānyodrecaṇāt tādṛśabhe 
vāsphuṭakalpaṃ kṛtyam saṃkocakāṣṭāyāṃ tu 
māyīyapramātṛtāyāmapi sarvathā svātmavismṛtau tādṛśameva 
bhedaikasāraṃ kṛtyam tatrāpi pramātṛ prakāśamantareṇa 
nīlādīnāmapratiṣṭhatvāt tatkṛtyaṃ prakāśamayameva parijñāya 
ca yatpunaḥ svarūpalābhapratyāpattyā'ṇubhāvātsvātmano 
vimuktirityāgameṣu parigīyate iti ata evāha vicitrāniti | 

<p. 9> 

naca brahmādivanniyataśaktayā saṃkucitatvānniyatameva 
tadanyataratkartu śaknuyādityabhipretyāha (-) sṛṣṭisaṃhārāniti 
bahuvacanaṃ kṛtyāntarāṇāmupalakṣaṇārtham | tena 
sṛṣṭisthitisaṃhārapidhānānugrahān vidhatte karoti | 
kālādikṛto'tra na kaścinniyama ityāha (-) yugapaditi akrameṇa 
akālakalite parameśvare tatkramābhāvāt vibhuriti 
deśādyavachedaśūnyatve hetuḥ ata evākrameṇaiva 
vicitrāsvetāsvavasthāsu vicitrāṇāṃ sṛṣṭisaṃhārāṇāṃ 
kartṛtvamavicchinnam | atha ca kalpitapramātaryapi sadaiva 
sargādivinodāsaktaḥ parameśvaraḥ svasvātantryamāhātmyena 
karaṇeśvarīcakraṃ samullāsya paryāndolanakrīḍayā nikhilaṃ 
prameyajātaṃ sṛṣṭikālyādyupārohaṇena prollāsayan paripāṭyā 
krameṇa vā kālasaṅkarṣaṇīdhāmni viśramya 
svātmaviśrāntirūpapūrṇāhaṃparāmarśacamatkāradhāmāsādayeti 
evameva bhūyobhūyaḥ krīḍārasautsukyena 
vicitrasṛṣṭayādikāritvaṃ pariśīlayatīti 
sahṛdayasaṃvedyametattanmukhādavagantavyam | gṛhītapaśubhūmikasya 
śivasya etadevābhijñānaṃ yadāha śrīkṣemarājaḥ (-) tathāpi 
tadvatpañcakṛtyāni karoti iti | ataḥ sarvamidaṃ svātantryasyaiva 
vilasitamiti || 6 || 

tadāha (-) 

atidurghaṭakāritvamasyānuttarameva yat | etadeva 
svatantratvamaiśvarya bodharūpatā || 7 || 

evamasya svātantryaśālinaḥ parameśvarasya yadevābhinnasya
