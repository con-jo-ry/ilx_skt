RE 47827 - Jñānapūjāvidhiḥ. This paper manuscript bundle contains
jñānapūjāvidhiḥ from the vātulāgama.
It is one of the 28 Mūlāgamas of the Śaiva tradition.
This paṭala provides a detailed, step-by-step guide
on performing the Jñānapūjā ritual,
beginning from the daily morning practices.
It includes instructions for nityakarma, prāṇāyāmam,
tarpaṇam, śivālayadarśanam, jñānapūjā, bhūtaśuddhiḥ,
antaryāgam, arcanādi and concludes with parāṅmukhārghyam

oṃ śrī gurubhyo namaḥ
śrīsundaragaṇapataye namaḥ

vātulāgama jñānapūjā - re 47827



1)‍ dvividhaṃ jñānam -  ௧) ஞானம் இருவகை
athātassaṃ pravakṣyāmi jñānapūjā vidhikramam| (i.4b)
jñānaṃ pravartate puṃsāṃ kathamatyanta durlabham||
jñānaṃ tu dvididhaṃ jñeyaṃ parāparavibhedataḥ|
tadādyaṃ paramaṃ jñānaṃ patipāśātma darśanam||
dvitīyamaparajñānaṃ kevalaṃ pāśadarśanam|
yathā vilakṣaṇaṃ rātrau naramārjāra netrayoḥ||
tathā lakṣaṇaṃ jñānamevametatparāparam|
yo yajjanmāntare karma paripākaviśeṣataḥ||

2) adhikāraḥ - ௨) அதிகாரி
jñānācārya kaṭākṣeṇa tripadārtha pramāṇavit|
jñānāt jñeyagato dhīmān jñānapūjādikarmani|| (i.5a)

3) nitya karmā - ௩)நித்திய கருமம்
prātaḥ pūrvaṃ samutthāya niṣṭhāyai cintayecchivam|
kriyākāṇḍa vidhānena nityakarma samārabhet||
snānakāle jalaṃ dhyātvā paramāmṛta sāgaram|
niṣkalaṃ nirmalaṃ sākṣānnityamuktaṃ śivaṃ smaret||
snātvā vaidikayuktaścet sandhyopāsanamācaret|
kartārañcintayetsarva kṛtyasākṣiṇamavyayam||

4) ācamanādi - ௪) ஆசமநாதி
ācamedātmatatvādi mantraissaha śivāntimaiḥ|
paraṃ jyotirmayaṃ jñātvā bhasmapañcākṣarairdharet||
uddhūlana vidhairvāpi tripuṇḍravidhinācaret|

5) prāṇāyāmam - ௫)பிராணாயாமம்
prāṇāyāmañca mūlena recakāt pāpanāśanam||
pūritaṃ śivapuṇyena tenakṛtvāsya kumbhakam| (i.5b)
karanyāsavidhāyaivamaṅganyāsamataḥ param||
sakalaṃ niṣkalaṃ nityaṃ cidānanda śivaṃ smaret|

6) tarpaṇam - ௬) தர்ப்பணம்
dakṣiṇāmūrtinaṃ dhyātvā mūlenārghyatrayaṃ dadet||
svasya santati mārgeṇa santānācārya tarpaṇam|
samayācārya mūrtīnāṃ tarpaṇaṃ ca yathāvidhi|
tattat svanāma mantreṇa kuryādarghyaṃ śivena ca||
svāhāntena krameṇaiva tarpayettajjalena ca|
svātmānaṃ prokṣyamūlena cācamya sakalīkṛtaḥ||

7) śivālaya darśanam - ௭) சிவாலய தரிசனம்
śivaṃ dhyātvā sadeśādīnālaye darśayettataḥ|

8) pūjāsthāna praveśanam - ௮) பூஜாஸ்தாணத்தை அடைதல்
prakṣālya pādāvācamya pūjāsthānaṃ praveśayet||
uttarābhimukho bhūtvā rucirāsana saṃsthitaḥ| (i.6a)
bhasmaṃ dhṛtvā karanyāsamaṅganyāsañca kārayet||
arghyaṃ saṃsthāpya sarvatra śuddhiṃ kṛtvā viśeṣataḥ|
snāpayet parameśānamarcayitvā yathāvidhi||

9) niṣṭhā - ௯) நிஷ்டை
athamādhyaṃ dinaṃ yāvat samādhisthobhavettathā|
jñānajyotirmayaṃ liṅgaṃ śraccandravadurjvalam||
bhrūmadhye saṃsthitaṃ tatra manaḥ prāṇāñca yojayet|
tallīnastanmayo yogī cittaṃ śūnyaṃ nayecchanaiḥ||
naṣṭe citte cidānandaṃ svayamudyo tate param|
samādhiḥ kathitohyevamaṇimādi guṇālayam||
evam kartumaśaktaścedyathā dhvātvā śivādikān|
pratīkādīn gurūktena mārgeṇa śivamantrakam||
tadabhāve japaṃ kṛtvā śāstra śravaṇamādarāt|
tato madhyāhnapūjāyāmārabhet jñānadeśikaḥ||

10) jñānapūjā - ௧௦) ஞானபூசை
dakṣiṇadvāramāśritya jñānapūjāṃ samācaret| (i.6b)
dakṣiṇasyārcanaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ kaivalyapadakāraṇam||
ācāryamūrtimāsthāya dakṣiṇābhimukho haraḥ|
paśūnāṃ pāśanāśāya kuryāt jñānopadeśakam||
tasmāddakṣiṇavaktrasya pūjā jñānavatī bhavet|
tatrapūjāṃ samārabhya kṛtvāvai bhasmadhāraṇam||
sakalīkaraṇaṃ kṛtvā cintayet parameśvaram|
pañcākṣaraṃ sakṛjjaptvā saṅkalpyārdhyaṃ tajjalaiḥ||
prokṣyecchiva mantreṇa dvārādi dravyajālakam|

11) dvārapūjā - ௧௧) துவாரபூசை
dvārapūjāṃ tataḥ kṛtvā santānācārya mantrakaiḥ||
āgneyāmakṣamālāñca naiṛtyāṃ bhasmabhājanam|
paścime śiva śāstraṃ ca vinyasyābhyarccayettataḥ||
udīcyāṃ śivabhaktānāṃ pramukhyān saṃprapūjayet|
praṇamya pūjā sākṣitvamabhiprārthya śivaṃ smaret||

12) bhūtaśuddhiḥ - ௧௨) பூதசுத்தி
śuddhātmā sakalīkṛtya dyāyedañjalipāṇinā| (i.7a)
tatvopatatva saṃyuktaṃ sthūlasūkṣmopalakṣitam||
dehaṃ ca dehinaṃ bhinnaṃ dehe'smiṃścādhva saṃyute|
pṛthivyādīni tatvāni caikenaika trayaṃ smaret||
śivājñāceṣṭitaṃ dhyātvā prārabdhaphala bhājanam|
taddehe svecchayā naṣṭaṃ vicāreṇa yathā tathā||
purātanaṃ gṛhaṃ tyaktvā nūtanaṃ prāptavāniva|
dehaṃ tyaktvā śivagṛhaṃ divya tejo vijṛṃbhitam||
cidānandamayaṃ nityaṃ prāptavāneva bhāvayet|

13) antaryāgam - ௧௩) அந்தரியாகம்
svadehamamṛtāplāvya kṣubdhaśakti parisṛtaḥ||
baha(hu)lāmṛta dhārāṃ śivālayamayīṃ tanum|
tadālayasthamātmānaṃ śivarūpaṃ vicintayet||
antaryāgaḥ prakartavyaḥ tasmādātmani nirmale|
nabhau homaṃ bhruvormadhye niṣkalaṃ sarvagaṃ śivam|| 
pūjāsthānaṃ śuddavidyā tatvarūpaṃ vibhāvayet| (i.7b) 
divyadṛṣṭyā pāśajālaṃ vyapohya śivatejasā||

14) dravyaśuddhiḥ - ௧௪) திரவியசுத்தி
paripūritamastreṇaiva bhāvayedarghyapātrakam|
prakṣālya bahurūpeṇa vauṣaḍanta śivena ca||
pūrayecchuddhatoyena gandhapuṣpādi vinyaset|
pañcakṣareṇa mantreṇa rudrasaṃkhyābhirarcayet||
digbandhanādikaṃ kṛtvā dhenumudrā'mṛtīkṛtam|
prokṣyeddravyajālāni vīkṣaṇādi viśodhayet||
śivamantreṇa puṣpāni daśavāramudīrayet|
viśuddhamantraśuddhasyāttālatraya purassaram||
digbandhanādinā sthānamamṛtāplāvanaṃ kṛtam|
śivena tejasāpūrṇaṃ sabāhyābhyantaraṃ smaret||
liṅgamādāya mūlena puṣpaṃ paryuṣitaṃ tyajet|
abhiṣicyāstramantreṇa pūrvārghyeṇābhiṣecayet||
tasminnāsanamantreṇa mūrtimantreṇa pūjayet| (i.8a)  
mūlena śivamāvāhya gandhapuṣpādi dāpayet||
dhūpadīpau hṛdādatvā mūlenārghyaṃ samarpayet|
pañcagavyena saṃsnāpya yathā pañcāmṛtādibhiḥ||
gandhodakaiśśuddhatoyairmulenārghyodakena ca|
saṃsnāpya khaṇḍavastreṇa parimṛjyāsane śivam||
nidhāya bhasmapuṣpārghyabindūni ca samarpayet|

15) arcanādi - ௧௫) அர்ச்சநாதி
kuṭilāntāsanamūrtimicchajñānakriyātmikām||
mūlamantraṃ śivamayaṃ dhyātvaiva lagulīśvaram|
punarāsana mūrtibhyāṃ mūlenaiva samarcayet||
dhyātvā śivaṃ cidānandaṃ satatoditamavyayam|
kriyāśaktyātmike pāde jñānaśaktyātmike mukhe||
icchāśaktyātmike mūrdhni dadyāt pādyādikatrayam|
gandhapuṣpādibhiścaiva mālābhiḥ pūjayecchivam|| (i.8b)
purato vṛṣabhaṃ dṛṣṭvā pārśvayośca munīśvarān|
agastyādīn samabhyarcya svaguraṃ(ruṃ) pūjayettataḥ||
śivāya dhūpadīpau ca datvā puṣpāñjalitrayam| 
naidedyaṃ ṣaḍrasopetaṃ sopadaṃ śantu dāpayet||
pānīyācamanaṃ datvā tāmbūlaṃ ca nidāpayet|
dhūpadīpādikaṃ datvā japāntaṃ pūjayecchivam||
stotraistutvā mahādevaṃ bhaktyā natvā punaḥ punaḥ|
pūjāphalaṃ ca tatsarvamudakena pradāpayet||
abhiṣṭaṃ prārthayettatra puṣpairabhyarcayecchivam|

11) parāṅmukhārghyam - ௧௧) பராங்முகார்க்கியம்
parāṅmukhārghyaṃ datvāsmai mūlena parameśvaram||
niṣkalaṃ nirmalaṃ dhyātvā yathāsthānaṃ niveśayet|
āsane svaguruṃ pūjya stutvā natvā visarjayet||
śivaṃ paramakalyāṇaṃ niṣkalaṃ nirmalaṃ prabham|
cidānandamayaṃ nityaṃ tejorūpaṃ vicintayet||
naivedyamasya bhuñjīyānnirmālyantannavidyate| (i.9a)
evaṃ pūjāṃ jñānavatīṃ karoti matimānnaraḥ||
ihaiva dhanavān śrīmān so'nte mokṣamāpnuyāt|
