<vīraśaivasiddhāntottarakaumudī>

<pṛ0 1> {vīraśaivasiddhāntottarakaumudī} ||
|| [śivapūjāvidhiḥ] ||

śrīmadrudraniṭilalocanajvalajjvālādagdha punarjīvitamadana prajvalita 
vaḍavānala santāpita viṣayasaṃga pracurataraṅga saṃgamohavarta dustara 
saṃsāraghorārṇavātārakaplavarūpa śivajñānaprada lobhapaṅktendriyārāti 
mohapiśāca krodha vyaghra matatāhi kanyā madanacora madāndhakāratāpatraya 
garta viṣaya kaṇṭakāsūyā viṣalatā ahaṃkāraviṣahradādi mahadupadrava 
viśiṣṭa saṃsāra kāntāracchedakuṭhāra śivajñānadāyaka dīkṣā guro 
śikṣā deśika bhavadarcanārthaṃ ajñādīyatāṃ iti vijñāpya dīrghadaṇḍa 
namaskārān kṛtvā pūjādravyāṇi tatra nidhāya guroścaraṇāṅguṣṭhau 
gṛhītvā |
gurumūrtiḥ śivaḥ sākṣātpāśamuktaḥ paraḥ śivaḥ |
vasanti pādayugme ca trailokyaṃ sa carācaram ||
aṅguṣṭhāgre hyaṣṭaṣaṣṭi tīrthāni nivasanti ca |
nakṣatragrahabṛndāni gulphe tasya vasanti vai ||
saptābdhayaśca pādārdhe tadardhe kulaparvatāḥ |
jaṃghayorvasate nityaṃ ṣaṭtriṃśattatvabṛndakam || iti ||
namaskṛtvā bhasmasaṃdhārya tatraiva snānādyupacārān kṛtvā 
puṣpairnānāvidhaiḥ citrai mālyaiścaiva sugandhibhiḥ |
śrīgurorvigraha sthāpya sandhau sandhau prapūjayet || 

<pṛ0 2> iti vacanena sarvasandhīḥ prapūjya | sarvopacārān kṛtvā | 
puṣpādikaṃ parihṛtya | pādaśabdenāṅguṣṭhamātraṃ gulphādhaḥ 
sarvapādaprakṣālanaṃ kṛtvā | śuktyādipātre saṃsthāpya | saṃpūjya | 
pādodakaṃ nāma kim (?) śaivasiddhāntaśikhāmapyukta bhaktastha(la)samāśrita 
pañcadaśasthaleṣu prasāda svīkṛtiruktā | pādodakaprāśanaṃ 
noktamityāśaṃkya prasādagrahaṇātprāgeva pādodaka svīkārastatraivoktaḥ | 
pādodaka māhātmya prāśanaphalāni tatraivocyate(-)
dīkṣāyopagatādvaitaṃ yajjñānaṃ guru śiṣyayoḥ |
ānandastre niryamete dīkṣā pādodakaṃ smṛtam ||
paramānanda evoktaḥ pādaśabdena nirmalaḥ |
jñānaṃ codakaśabdena pādodakamiti smṛtam ||
athavā pādaśabdena gururevaṃ nigadyate |
śiṣyaścodakaśabdena tayoraikyaṃ tu dīkṣayā ||
parasaṃvitprakāśātmā paramānandabhāvanām |
adhigamya mahāyogī na bhedaṃ kvāpi paśyati ||
jñānāmṛtamiti svacchaṃ guru kāruṇyasaṃbhavam |
āsvādya ramate yogī saṃsārāmayavarjitaḥ || iti ||
tataḥ | liṅga jaṅgama pādābja saṃbhūtāya śivātmane |
paramānandarūpāya pādatīrthāyate namaḥ ||
iti pādodakaṃ namaskṛtya | kriyārūpaṃ vijñāyamajjanyarpaṇā(-)

<pṛ0 3> dikaṃ samyag jñātvā śivapādāṃbu(ja) svīkāravadgurupādodakaṃ 
prāśayet | pādodakaṃ yathā bhaktyā svīkaroti maheśituḥ |
tathā śivātmanornityaṃ gurujaṃgamayorapi ||
śīkṣā pādodakasthale coktam(-) mayitācchāstrajaladhau yukti 
manthānavaibhavāt | guruṇā labhyate bodhā(dho) sudhā sumanasāṃ gaṇaiḥ || 
evaṃ bhaktānuyāyīnāmiti vacanena bahugaṇapakṣe tīrthaṃ svalpamiti 
jñātvā jalamiśritaṃ cet mahādoṣo bhavati | yathā(-) pādodakaṃ 
parabrahmanirmalaṃ cādhanāśanam | ajaḍaṃ jaḍamiśraṃ cetsapāpī 
brahmadhātukaḥ || iti | pātrāntarairbahu gaṇapakṣe ubhayostāvadekameveti 
vivekaḥ | tadanantaraṃ prasāda svīkāraḥ | śivaliṅgaprasādasya 
svīkārādya phalaṃ bhavet | tathā prasādasvīkāra gurujaṃgamayorapīti 
vacanena majitaṃ tatra patitaṃ vā prasādaṃ prāśayet |
mahatā liṅgarūpeṇa mahatā jaṃgamena ca |
trividhena samudbhūta prasādāya namo namaḥ || 
yathā(-) śuddhaṃ liṅga mukhaṃ tyaktaṃ siddhaṃ tu caravarjitam |
prasiddhaṃ tu gurorbhuktaṃ prasādastrividhaṃ bhavet || iti vīrāgame | 
kiñcāpastambaḥ(-) yaducchiṣṭaṃ prāśnāti havisacchiṣṭameveti | tatā 
liṅgārcanaṃ kuryāt | atha gurustotraṃ kṛtvā(-) prāṇāyāma trayaṃ kṛtvā 
vidhāya nyāsa dhyāna purassaraṃ ṣaḍakṣarī maṃtraṃ kuryāt | tadyathā(-) 
asya śrīṣaṭsthalabrahma ṣaḍakṣarī mahā(-)

<pṛ0 4> mantrasya parabrahmṛṣiḥ | daivī gāyatrīchandaḥ | śivo devatā | 
maheśvarīti śaktiḥ | hrīṃ bījam | hrauṃ kīlakam | mama dehe ṣaḍūrmyādi 
nivṛttaye ṣaṭsthala brahmanirvāṇa sukhasaṃpadavāptaye ṣaḍakṣarī 
mahāmantra japaṃ kariṣye | yaṃ aṃguṣṭhābhyāṃ namaḥ | vāṃ 
tarjanībhyāṃ namaḥ | śiṃ madhyamābhyāṃ namaḥ | maṃ anāmikābhyāṃ 
namaḥ | naṃ kaniṣṭhikābhyāṃ namaḥ | ūṃ karayoḥ madhye | ūṃ śikhāyām 
| yaṃ śirasi | vāṃ mukhe | śiṃ hṛdaye | maṃ nābhau | naṃ padadvaye | ūṃ 
ūṃkārāya hṛdayāya namaḥ | nakārāya śirase svāhā | makārāya śikhāyai 
vaṣaṭ | śikārāya kavacāya hum | vakārāya netratrayāya vauṣaṭ | 
yakārāyāstrāya phaṭ | bhūrbhuvaḥ suvaromiti digbandhaḥ | dhyānam(-) ṅamaḥ 
śivāgnibījāya harāya paramātmane | ūṃmanta mantrarūpāya mūla 
pañcākṣarātmane || ūṃkāravadanā devi vāyakāra bhujadvayā | śikāra 
dehamadhyā ca namakāra padadvayam || pañcākṣarī parāvidyā satārā 
liṅgarūpiṇī | śivamantrābhidhā śaivī mamāṃge saṃpratiṣṭhitā 
|| ṣaṭsthala brahmarūpiṇī ṣaḍakṣaramantradevate jananī ṣaṭsthalabhakti 
prasāda paramasukhaika prade namastubhyam ||
iti dhyātvā karasthale bhasmalepanaṃ vidhāya praṇavapīṭhaṃ kṛtvā liṃgaṃ 
niveśya | ṣaṭsthalarūpiṇī śrīṣaḍakṣarī vidyāṃ parādi caturvidha 
vṛttarūpāṃ bhaktādi ṣaṭsthala japavān bhavet | 
yathābhiśeṣārthaprakāśake(-) evaṃ vijñāpanaṃ kṛtvā natvā 
gurūparamparām |

<pṛ0 5> śivādhyātmānta saṃsiddhāṃ śivavidyārthapāragām |
guruṃ ṣaḍakṣarī vidyārūpiṇīṃ ṣaṭsthalātmakam ||
śreyo gurupadaṃ siddhaṃ natvā nyāsaṃ samācaret ||
asya śrīṣaṭsthalabrahma ṣaḍakṣaramanoḥ parabrahmṛṣiḥ | śaivī 
gāyatrīchandaḥ ucyate | devatāsya śivaḥ sākṣācchaktirmāheśvarī matā || 
bījaṃ māyābinduyuktaṃ śivāgne dvaya saṃyutam |
kīlalaṃ śivavahnibhyāṃ caturdaśakalānvitam ||
sabindukaṃ samāyuktaṃ viniyogo'tha kathyate ||
mameha satidehe tu ṣaḍūrmyādi nivṛttaye |
ṣaṭsthala brahma nirvāṇa sukhasampadavāptaye ||
ṣaḍakṣaramanorjāpyaṃ kariṣye śiva yājñikam ||
aṅguṣṭhādi kaniṣṭhāntaṃ karayoḥ pañcaśākhinoḥ |
madhyataḥ praṇavaṃ cātha vinyasedakṣarān kramāt ||
namaḥ svāhadaṣaḍbuṃdau ṣaḍbaḍaṃkaṃ hṛdādikam |
caturthīsaṃyutaṃ bhūya ūṃkārāyeti pūrvakam ||
vinyasyātmārpaṇaṃ caiva viniyujya punastataḥ |
namaḥ śivāgnibījāya harāya paramātmane ||
ūṃmantamantrarūpāya mūla pañcākṣarātmane |
ūṃkāravadanā devī vāyakāra bhujadvayī |
śikāra dehamadhyā ca namakāra padadvayī ||

<pṛ0 6> pañcākṣarī parā vidyā satārā liṅgarūpiṇī |
śivamantrābhidhā śaivī mamāṅgeṣu pratiṣṭhitā ||
ṣaṭsthala brahmarūpiṇī ṣaḍakṣarī mantra devate jananī ṣaṭsthalabhakti 
prasāda parasukhaika prade namastubhyam || ityanujñāṃ tayā labdhvā japet 
pañcākṣarīṃ śivām |
satārāṃ ṣaṭsthala brahmarūpiṇīṃ śrīṣaḍakṣarīm |
vidyā caturvidhā śaivī parā paśyantī madhyamā |
vaikharī cet arthavarṇā pada vākyaprabhedataḥ |
omartho nādiyāṃ tasya varṇastatra dvayaṃ triyam ||
padaṃ bhavettatsamaṣṭirvākyamityabhidhīyate ||
artharūpā parā vidyā paśyantī varṇarūpiṇī |
madhyamā padarūpā syāt vaikarī vākyarūpiṇī ||
artho manobhavo varṇo dṛgbhavo vāgbhavaṃ padam |
vākyaṃ vapurbhavaṃ teṣu manodṛgvāgvapuṣvathā |
lakṣaṇaṃ kramaśo jñeyaṃ manomananalakṣaṇam |
dṛgvilokanacihnāsyādvāgvayomātra lakṣaṇā ||
tālvoṣṭha rasanā yoga lakṣaṇāṃ vapurucyate |
mānasī cākṣuṣī bāhyā vācakī vāpuṣī parā ||
paśyantī madhyamā ceti vaikharītyucyate kramāt |
prāṇāyāma sugandhi gandha vaha eṣo'ntassamādhi sthitaḥ |
prāṇo ghrāṇa hṛṣīkato bahiranātmā nardha cāraṃditā |

<pṛ0 7> jāpye karmaṇi śāṃbhave para mayā bhaktyā svabhaktasthale nityaṃ 
vartata ātmacāra caritā cārākhya liṃṅgaṃ bhajat || 
sarasa ṣaḍakṣaramantrasvarūpa guruliṃgabhajana(,) niṣṭhātma nirataṃ 
nijajihvāyāṃ carati vā māheśvarasthale jāpyaṃ | animiṣacakṣurbhūtvā 
śivaliṅgaṃ sveṣṭarūpamavadhānādvilokayan japati sadā svayaṃ 
prasādasthale bhajanaśīlaḥ |
tvagindriyadvāravirājamānaṃ liṅgaṃ caraṃ sparśana jāpya (yogāt |)
bhajan sadā svānubhavaikavedyaṃ saprāṇaliṅgīsthala eṣa āste ||
mantroccāraṇaśabdāmṛtamaya jāpyāt prasāda ghanaliṅgam |
śrotrendriya mukharaśaraṇasthalavartī bhajati pariṇatānandaḥ ||
mantrārtharūpa mahadāhvaya liṅga sevāṃ
kurvanmanasyaharaharmanana prabodhāt |
anyaṃ na kiñcidapi vetti hi sāmarasyā(-)
daikyasthale japa samādhiriti sthitoyam ||
yatra maruttatramano yatra manastatra marudupayogaḥ |
atra ṣaḍakṣara eva na kutrāpītyeva ṣaṭsthalī japati ||
sītkāro dharasevanaṃ japavidhā vā liṃganaṃ gāḍhadorbaddhaḥ pāṇi 
mahāṃgaṇe gaṇanayā śaivākṣa vikṣepanam | saṃrambhaḥsurata kriyā 
samucitā śayyā hi padmāsanaṃ mallāpaḥ śanakairudīritamanuḥ pañcākṣarī 
saṃgate ||
ramate māmahorātraṃ parāvidyā parāṃgatā |
nāvakāśo mayārantuṃ tava māye varāṃgane ||

<pṛ0 8> evaṃ vidyānuṣṭhānaṃ vidhāya | tataḥ parāvidyātmaka 
prāṇaliṅgaṃ mūlādhāra hṛdaya bhrūmadhya brahmarandhrasthānānāṃ madhye 
hṛdayasya śreṣṭhatvān maṇḍalatraya maṇḍita hṛdaya puṇḍarīke 
samyagvibhāvya bhāva puṣpairyajet | yathā(-)
antargataṃ cidākāraṃ liṅgaṃ śivamayaṃ param |
pūjyate bhāva puṣpairyat prāṇaliṅgārcanaṃ hi tat ||
tallakṣaṇaṃ śaṃkarasaṃhitāyām(-)
snānaṃ samatva salilena hi pūrṇabhāva vastraṃ triśaktiguṇasaṃyuta 
yajñasūtram | vidyānugandhamiha bhūtadayākṣatāśca śabdādi puṣpa 
cayamarpaya nityamaṃgā (* *) ||
prajñāmanohaṃkṛti citta dhūpaṃ śrotrādi pañcendriya dīpamaṅga | 
sadvāsanā tailayutaṃ ca dehī jīvopahāraṃ sukha duḥkha bāhyam || 
guṇatrayākhya tāmbūlaṃ prāṇārpaṇa namaskṛtiḥ | 
śānti puṣpāñjaliṃ dehi caikabhāva visarjanamiti ||
evamabhyantara dravyasaṃkhyataṃ śivapūjanamityaṃtaryajanaṃ vidhāya | 
nijamiṣṭaliṅgaṃ mūlamantreṇa vibhūtyābhyukṣya talliṅgaṃ 
daśāvadhānairnirīkṣayet ||
ādau daśāvadhānāni proktāni śivapūjane |
dṛṣṭiścaturvidhā pūrvaṃ tatra dṛṣṭeḥ padutvatā ||
karāgrasthita cijjyoti dṛṅmanaḥ prāṇasaṃbhṛtam |
dṛkkuṃḍe citritaṃ liṅgaṃ dṛgrūpaṃ liṅgamucyate ||
svarṇakāreṇa saṃbandhāt pratimā lohanirmitā |

<pṛ0 9> evaṃ vidhaṃ dṛkpaṭutvaṃ dṛgvinyāsaṃ tathocyate |
guheśa iva saṃpaśyettaṃtrirūpe kṣaṇaḥ śivaḥ ||
nimeṣarahitaḥ paśyedanimeṣa iveśvaram |
atītāga guṇavrātaḥ paśyedbhṛṃgīśvaro yathā ||
avinābhāvataḥ paśyecchivaṃ nandīśvaro yathā ||
dṛgvinyāsamihaivoktaṃ dṛgvilīnaṃ tathocyate |
gurūktamantrālīyante śiṣya karṇadvayaṃ yathā || (?)
tathā dṛṣṭiḥ karāgrasthā cilliṃge vilayaṃ vrajet |
vedāntārthaḥ śivaṃ dhatte tathā dhatte dṛgīśvaram |
vyāptaṃ latābhiḥ kalpadrurdṛśā baddhastathā śivaḥ ||
śirolakṣye yathā syūtaṃ tathā syūtaṃ dṛgīśvare |
āliṃgitaḥ pitāputrairyathādṛṣṭvā śivastathā ||
badhnanti pāśaṃ gandhebhaṃ dṛgbhirbaddhastathā śivaḥ |
vāgurā vyādha nikṣiptā mṛgaṃ badhnāti dṛktathā ||
bhāryayā patirāśliṣṭastathāśliṣṭaḥ śivodṛśā |
śilpikḷptaṃ yathāsūtraṃ gra(ga)ndhadravyairyathā paṭaḥ |
sindhavo'bdhau yathāvanyāḥ kṣīrelīnaṃ yathājalam ||
taptāyasi yathātoyaṃ dṛgvilīnaṃ yathā śive |
vakṣyate dṛksthiratvaṃ tu caturthaṃ prathamesthitam ||
ādigyānamanāvarta manapāṃgamatoya bhṛt |
ācañcala mamāṃdya syāttadordhva calanaṃ na ca ||

<pṛ0 10> anaṅgaguṇamakrūraṃ dṛksthiratvamidaṃ bhavet |
evaṃ caturvidhā liṅge prathamā dṛṣṭirucyate ||
śivaṃ paśyaṃ(n) sukhaṃ yānti paśyan paśyannanekadhā |
dvitīyamavadhānaṃ syādaṣṭadhā dṛksupūjanam ||
dṛṅmajjanāṃburūpaṃ syāddṛṣṭirgandhayutākṣatāḥ |
dṛṣṭiḥ puṣpopaharaṇaṃ dṛṣṭiḥ taddhūparūpakam |
dṛṣṭirnīrājanākhyaṃ syāddṛṅnaivedyasvarūpakam ||
dṛṣṭistāmbūlarūpaṃ syāddṛṣṭiḥ puṣpāñjalirbhavet |
evaṃ dṛṣṭyavadhānena bhavatyeveśvarārcanam |
liṅgadarśanamātreṇa pibeddṛṣṭyaṣṭakāmṛtam ||
dṛgvyāptā tu tṛtīyaṃ svā(syā)davadhānaṃ tathocyate |
yathā daridro nikṣepaṃ manthe(tte)bhaṃkara(ri)ṇīṃ yathā ||
līlāmbujaṃ yathā gaurīpatirbhāryā kucaṃ yathā |
evaṃ muhurmuhuḥ śaṃbhuṃ spṛśaddarcaṃ cuti svayam ||
śivaṃ sparśakareṇaiva sparśanaṃ sukhamaśnute |
śivaṃ nānāvidhaiḥ stotraiḥ vaidikaistu purāṇajaiḥ ||
raudropaniṣadākhyātaiḥ stutvā stuti sukhaṃ vrajet |
caturthamavadhānaṃ syāt stutirūpaṃ śivasya vai ||
liṅge vilīyate samyak śarīraṃ viṣayendriyaiḥ |
navanītaṃ yathā vahnau dīpe karpūrameva ca || 
jale lavaṇavadvarṣa śilāvaṃnnīradhau tathā ||
arcanaṃ bhavatītyevamaṅgavikriyakaissadā |
pañcamaṃ cārcanaṃ hyetanmano vyāptirathocyate ||

<pṛ0 11> snānamānandavāṣpāṇi bindurudvartanaṃ bhavet ||
gandhoratiḥ satkriyāṅno romāñcaṃ puṣpamālikā |
tatvabhūtirvibhūtiśca dhūpo naijasuvāsanā ||
jñānāṃśaṃ vā dīpajālaṃ prāṇo naivedyameva ca |
atīndriyatvaṃ tāmbūlaṃ stotraṃ puṣpāñjalirbhavet ||
snānādi puṣpāṃjali pramukhāni bhāvanā purassaraṃ dṛṣṭibhedāvadhānaṃ 
kuryāt | snānādikopacāreṣu manastadrūpatāṃ vrajet |
majjane tu manovāri dhārārūpaṃ samaśnute ||
pūjāyāṃ saurabhaṃ cittaṃ gandhalepo manobhavet |
dhūpe manovāsanātvaṃ maṅgalaṃ dīpikā ruciḥ ||
rucirnaivedyadāne tu manaḥ ṣaḍvidhamaśnute ||
manaḥsnapanasamaye majjanodakarūpaṃ bhavati ||
pūjāsamaye puṣpopahārarūpaṃ bhavati | gandhārpaṇasamaye gandharūpaṃ 
bhavati | dhūpārpaṇasamaye dhūparūpaṃ bhavati | dīpārpaṇasamaye 
dīparūpaṃ bhavati | naivedyasamaye tadrucirūpaṃ bhavati | ayamapi manobhavaḥ 
|
evaṃ ṣaṣṭārcanaṃ proktaṃ sānurāgārcanaṃ manaḥ ||
śivamadhye gaṇān dṛṣṭvā śivabhaktissamudbhavān |
gaṇarudrānuvākaistānasaṅkhān praṇamet svayam ||
