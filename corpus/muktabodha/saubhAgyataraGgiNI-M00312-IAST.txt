(Description by Mark S.G. Dyczkowski)
(This is a liturgy (paddhati) for the worship of the goddess )
(Tripurāsundarī written by a certain Mukunda. He refers to Veda and Vedic  yajñā )
(with great regard as a source of the teachings concerning the goddess Tripurā whom )
(he identifies at the beginning of his work with the goddess Durgā of the )
(Devīmāhātmya.  Thus he quotes here and there short passages from the Vedas and )
(extensively from the Purāṇas. Notoriously, the Purāṇas draw freely from other )
(sources, especially Tantras.  He quotes from the Kūrmapurāṇa, Kālikā, )
(which are his favorites, especially the latter, as well as the Mārkaṇḍeyapurāṇa, Varāha, Viṣṇu, Bhaviṣya, Devī, Matysa, )
(Skanda, and Vāmana.  The Tantras from which he draws include Vāmakeśvara  which he quotes most, the Jñānārṇava, Tantrarāja,  Uttara )
(probably the late redaction of the Rudrayāmala),  and Durgātantra, )
(which suggests a relatively recent date. Other sources include the )
(Dakṣiṇāmūrtisaṃhitā and śrīkramasaṃhitā. The texts breaks off in the fifth  chapter (taraṅga).)

{saubhāgyataraṅgiṇī}

<1a>

oṃ namaḥ śrīmahātripurasundaryai ||

praṇamya cinmayīṃ śaktiṃ śrīmattripurasundarīm |
tomāya tanute tasyāḥ śrīsaubhāgyataraṅgiṇīm ||

iha khalu sarveṣāṃ jantūnāṃ sukhaṃ me bhūyād duḥkhaṃ memābhūditi 
sukhopādiḥ sāduḥkhajihāsuṃ bhavataḥ ātyantika duḥkhanivṛtti sukhāvāsī ca 
brahmajñānamantareṇa na bhavataḥ brahmaveda brahmaiva bhavati |

sīyante cāsya karmāṇi tasminṛṣṭe parākare |
ānandaṃ brahmaṇo vidvānna vibheti hutaścana ||

abhayaṃ vai janaka prāptosi tadātmānamevā vedahaṃ brahmāsmīti tasmāt tat 
sarvamabhavat | tatra ko śokaḥ ko moha ekatvamanupaśyataḥ | tarati śokamātma 
viditvādi śrutibhyaḥ brahmajñānaṃ śakti bhraktimantareṇa na bhavatīti śrūyate 
purāṇeṣu tatra mārkaṇḍeyapurāṇe stutā saṃpūjitā 
puṣpairdhūpagandhādibhistathā || 

dadāti vittaṃ putrāṃśu matiṃ dharme tathā śubhām |
śubhāṃ ātmani yayāmapi maniṃ dadānīti samvandhaḥ | sāyāki(?)tā ca 
vijñānaṃ tuṣṭā ṛddhiṃ prayacchati | pūjādibhistuṣṭāsanīyāditā 
cedbhaktecchānusāreṇa ṛddhiṃ vijñānaṃ cāparo samānaṃ prayacchati | yadvā 
paramaudāryāghācitā tuṣṭāsatī ubhayaṃ prayacchatītyarthaḥ | 

tāmupaihi mahārāja śaraṇaṃ parameśvarīm |
ārādhitā saiva nṛṇāṃ bhoga svargāpavargadā ||

evakāro'nyayoga vyavacchedakaḥ | kūrmapurāṇepi | 

tasmād vimuktimanvicchanpārvatīṃ parameśvarīṃ |
āśrayet sarvabhūtānāmātma bhūtāṃ śivātmikāṃ ||

māmanāśritya yat tatvaṃ svabhāva nirmalaṃ śivaṃ |
jñāyate na hi rājendra tano māṃ śaraṇaṃ vraja ||

tathā śrutirapi | ahameva sva(?)yamidaṃ vadāmi juṣṭaṃ devebhiruta 
mānuṣebhiḥ yaṃ kāmaye tanumuyaṃ hṛṇomitaṃ brahmāṇaṃ tamṛṣiṃ taṃ 
sumedhāṃ | ahaṃ rudrāya dhanu vā tanomi brahmadviṣeśavarehanta vāḍa(?) 
ahaṃ janāyaśamadaṃ hṛṇomyahaṃ dyāvā pṛthivī āviveśa || 

ata eva kālikāpurāṇe | svaviyuktā vastrā(?)cāra saṃyutā śubhāḥ sā ca mantrāt 
pragṛhṇā tu sa mantrī tṛdanujñayā | tathā vinā svadharma yatkiṃcid 
devatārādhanādikaṃ | paribhraśyeta tad yasmāt kṣaṇāsaikataharmyavat | iti | nanu 
śūdrāṇāṃ mantreyanadhikāraḥ śrūyate | yathā mahākapila pañcarātre | 

na vedaḥ praṇavaṃ tyaktvā mantro veda samanvitaḥ | 
tasmād vedaḥ paro mantro vedādaṃśyāgamaḥ smṛtaḥ ||

<1b>

vaśyā karmādikaṃ karma duṣṭāduṣṭa phalapradaṃ |
vedena mādhyate sarvaṃ śraddha yajñādibhiḥ kila ||

na vedena vinā yajñā na yajñā vedavarjitāḥ |
tasmād vedaḥ paro mantro na mantro veda u (*) taḥ ||

na mantre cādhikārosti śūdrāṇāṃ niyamaḥ paraḥ |
mantrā bhāvādamantreṇa bhāṣitaṃ sarvakarmahīti ||

satyaṃ asyāṣṭā harayara tvāt prakaraṇāditi ||

yadvā eṣāṃ mantraviśeṣa paratvaṃ ||

yathā merūyāmale prāsāda mantraṃ prakṛtyaḥ strīśūdrāṇāmayaṃ mantro 
namontaśca śubhāvahaḥ | etajajñādvā mahāsenaścaṇḍālānapi dīkṣayet | tathā 
yājñavalkya saṃhitāyāṃ | 

na vaidikaṃ japecchūdraḥ striyaścaiva kadācana | 
namontaṃ śivamantraṃ vā vaimhāvaṃ(?) veṣyate vudaiḥ ||

namontamityusyo bhayatra saṃvandhaḥ | devīyāmale | 

svāhā praṇavasaṃyuktaṃ mantraṃ śūdre dadaddvijaḥ |
śūdro nirayagāmī syād vipraḥ śūdratva māpnuyāt ||

iti ||

bhavedetat tathāpi dīkṣā mantareṇa nādhikāraḥ | yaduktaṃ nārāyaṇīye | 

praviśya vidhika dīkṣāmabhiṣekāvasāniḍhāṃ |
śrutvā tatraṃ gurorlabdhaṃ sā ye dīpsitaṃ manuṃ ||

tathā ||

dvijānāmanupetānā svakarmādhyayanādiṣu |
yathādhikāro nāstīha syāccopanayanādanu ||

tathā vā dīkṣitānāṃ cāmaka(?) devārcanādiṣu |
nādhikāro'styataḥ kuryād ātmānaṃ śiva saṃskṛtaṃ || iti ||

sā ca niṣidhyate sānātayeta(?) ||

yāvatryarṇāni mantrāṇāṃ śrūdrāya pratipādayet |
tāvatyoṃ brahmahatyāsyuḥ svayamāha prajāpatiriti ||

satyaṃ etad vacanasyocchiṣṭādi dānaniṣedhavarasachūdra paratvādukta yukteḥ | 
pañcarātraśravaṇādau tu nādhikāraḥ | taduktaṃ nāradapañcarātre | 

brāhmaṇa kṣatriyaviśā pañcarātraṃ vidhīyate ||

śūdrādīnāṃ na tacchodra padavīmadhirohatīti kālikāpurāṇe | 

mohādvā kāmataḥ śūdraḥ purāṇaṃ saṃhitāṃ smṛtiṃ |
paṭhannarakamāpnoti pitṛbhiḥ saha pāpa hṛt || iti ||

tathā mahābhārate ||

yadudaṃ śṛṇuyān nityaṃ yaścāpi parikīrtayet |
nāśubhaṃ prāpnuyāt kiñcit so'mutreha ca mānavaḥ ||

vedāntayo brāhmaṇaḥ syāḥ kṣatriyo vijayī bhavet |
vaiśyo dhanasamṛddhiḥ syācchūdraḥ śukhamavāpnuyāt ||

atrācārya caraṇāḥ śūdraḥ sukhamavāpnuyāt ||

śravaṇe naiva na tu japa yajñena tasmācchūdro yajñenopa (*)(?) pta iti 
śrute || 

śrāvayeccaturovarṇāniti mahābhāratādi śravaṇamanu jñāyate | 
sugatimiyo śravaṇāśca śūdrayoniriti harivaṃśe | 

<2a>

yaḥ śūdraḥ śṛṇuyāt samuvāpnuyāditi ca vyavahitena saṃvandhaḥ | 
traivarṇikānāṃ kīrtayediva saṃvandhaḥ | iti vadanti | 

ataḥ purāṇamuni praṇītastavādikaṃ na paṭhanīyamiti | itthaṃ ca śūdrādīnāṃ 
mantra niṣedho nāma saṃvandheneti samyaka vinigamakā bhāvāt | 
vṛṣotsargādāvajñānāṃ rudrādīnāmapi jaya prasaktaśca | na ca pādāntara yoge 
mantrāntara tvāttatatkāryasiddhiriti vācyaṃ | namaskārosya mantra iti gautamena 
vihite tarayāvad vaidikatāntrikamantreṣu namo mantravidhānāt tasyaiva tat 
tadapūrvajanakatvāditi | anyathā śrāddhā guṇa prabhinnā jagato'sya sṛṣṭi sthiti 
(*)(?) yasya niviṣṭa ceṣṭāḥ | iti | tathā aṇoraṇīyasīstyanā'sthūlā vyāpta 
carācarā | ādityendrādi tejo mantrastu tu tanmayī vibhuḥ | 

na śvetarakta pītādi varṇe nidhārya cocyate |
na guṇeṣu na bhūteṣu viśeṣeṇa vyavasthitā ||

antarāntarvahiścaiva dehināṃ deharūpiṇī |
svasaṃvedya svarūpā sā deśyādeśikadarśitaiḥ ||

yaṣākāśastamā vāpi labdhayānopalabhyate |
puṃ napuṃsakayostulyāpyaṅganāsuviśiṣyate ||

pradhānamiti yā māturyā śaktiriti kathyate |
yā yuṣmānapi māṃ nityamavaṣṭabhyā nivartate ||

sāhaṃ yūyaṃ tathaivānya adveyaṃ tattu sā smṛtā |
phalaye vyāpyate tasyāṃ carācaramidaṃ jagat ||

sātu kālātmanā samyaṅmayaiva jñāyate sadeti brahmaviṣṇurudrān prati 
śrīnārāyaṇa vacanaṃ | tathā kālikāpurāṇe | 

yada vyaktamanirdeśyaṃ niṣkalaṃ paramātmanaḥ |
rūpaṃ tadaiva tatsūkṣmaṃ sakalaṃ ca jagan mayaṃ ||

vicārāṣṭaka yogena saṃpādyaiva muhurmuhuḥ |
yat sthirī kriyate tatvaṃ tatvarūpaṃ sanātanaṃ ||

tvaṃvitiḥ paramānanda paramātmasvarūpiṇī |
śaktitvaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ sarveṣāṃ ca (*) pāvanī ||

sarvatrasyā sarvagādivyamūrti niyā devī sarvarūpā purākhyā(?) | 
kṛṣṇādīnāṃ sarvadā moyitrī sā strīrūpaiḥ sarvajantoḥ samantāt | tathā 
kūrmapurāṇe | 

tvayā sṛṣṭaṃ jagatsarvaṃ pradhānādyaṃ tvayi sthitaṃ |
tvayyeva līyate devi tvameva paramāgatiḥ ||

vadanti kecit tvāmeva prakṛtiṃ prakṛteḥ parāṃ |
apareyamātmajñāḥ śiveti śivasaṃśraye ||

tvayi pradhānapuruṣau mahān brahmā tatheśvaraḥ |
avidyā niyatirmāyā kālādyāḥ śataśo bhavet ||

tvaṃ hi sā paramāśakti manantā parameṣṭhinī |

avyakta vyaktarūpeṇa rajaḥsatva tamoguṇaiḥ |

paramabrahmaṇaḥ śaktistathedamaṇḍitaṃ jagat ||

sandhyā snānādikaṃ devi vidhāya manuvittaya iti viśedyānabhidhānāt

tathā vāmakeśvaratantre ||

vījatrayaṃ mūlavidyā saṃvandhi | śrīkramasaṃhitāyāṃ |

mantramuccāryeti śeṣaḥ |

<10b>

bhagaśabdānte bhuge bhagini cālikhet |

bhagāni me sthāna yeti varadekha samālikhet ||

rete sure te bhagā klinne klinna drave tataḥ |
kledaya drāvaya cātha sarvasatva bhageśvari ||

amoghe bhagaviśve cakṣu bhakṣo bhaya sarva ca |
satvān bhageśvariṃ vrūyād vāgbhavaṃ vlūṁ jamādikaṃ ||

bheṁ vlūṁ moṁ vlūṁ ca heṁ vlūṁ ca heṁ klīṁnne ca tataḥ paraṃ |
sarvāṇi ca bhagānyante me vaśaṃ cānayeti ca ||

strīvījaṃ ca haraprānte valemātmakamakṣaraṃ |
bhuvaneśīṃ samālikhya vidyeyaṃ bhagamālinīṃ ||

sarvasatvabhageśvarīti devī saṃvodhanaṃ | jamādikaṃ vlūṁ tena jaṁ vlūṁ iti | 
vale iti saṃyuktaṃ | 

parāvījaṃ samuccārya nityaklinne madadrave | 
agnijāyānvito mantro nityaklinne yamīritā ||

praṇavaṃ pūrvamuccārya tathāṅkuśayugaṃ likhet |
tanmadhye vilikhed devi paromātmakamakṣaraṃ ||

ca vargamatryahīnaṃ tu vilikhedagni saṃhitaṃ |
caturdaśa svaropetaṃ vindunādāṅkitaṃ pṛthak ||

vahnijāyānvito mantro bheruṇḍāyāḥ phalapradaḥ | 

tanmadhye aṅkuśadvayāntaḥ bharomiti saṃyuktaṃ | oṅkāramuccārya 
aṅkuśābhyāṃ suveṣṭhitamiti dakṣiṇāmūrti vacanāt | 

bhuvaneśīṃ samuccārya caturthyā vahni vāsinīṃ | 
hṛdantoyaṃ manurdevi nityeyaṃ vahnivāsinīṃ ||

praṇavaṃ bhuvaneśānīṃ phalamātmakamakṣaraṃ |

<11b>

sa visargaḥ śaśī paścānnityaklinte madadrave |

mahāvidyeśvarī nityeti | pharemitirsaṃyuktaṃ | śaśīsakāraḥ | 

parāvījaṃ samuccārya śivadūtīṃ cakṣe yutāṃ |
hṛdantoyaṃ manudevi dūtīyaṃ sarvakāmadā ||

oṃkāravījamuccārya parāṃ kavacamālikhet |
svecchandoḥ samālikhya strīvījaṃ ca samālikhet ||

tvaritā nāma vidyeyaṃ triṣu lokeṣu durlabhā ||

sarvasiṃhāsanamayī vālaiva kulasundarī |
vālayā puṭitāṃ kuryāt tathā vai nityabhairavīṃ ||

nityabhairavīṃ svayamāha |

vindunāda kalākrāntaṃ tritayaṃ girisaṃbhave ||

vacanāt |

dīrghakarṇendu vindvāḍhyamalapāntaṃ likhet kramāt |

ajapāntaṃ saḥ iti |
