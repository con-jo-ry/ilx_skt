{prācīnatāḍapustakoddhṛtaḥ amṛteśvaradīkṣāvidhiḥ}


<p. 1b> mahākaulakeyo netrāmṛtabhairavoktamatake vyāpyasthito duskṛtāt || 5 ||

tattu daśanarūpato vibhutayā sāvaraṇatve sthito 
viśvaṃ bhuktaphalai pradai karasiko mṛtyuñjayo nityaśaḥ |
kāruṇāmṛtavārisecanatayā sāṃsārikān bodhayanma (*) 
śrīgururūpavāraṇatayā devo'mṛtīśo'vatāt || 6 ||

jyotirūpaṃ śivaṃ śāntamakṣa (*) paramāmṛtam |
namasyāmi mahādevamamṛtīśaṃ śriyāyutam || 7 ||

vāmotsaṅge mahādevīṃ śrīyaṃ lokaika mātaram |
udvahanparamānanda camatkāreṇa ghūñcitam || 8 ||

namaskaromi satataṃ bhaktyā vākkarmma mānasaiḥ |
parāmṛtīśa sujyotimānandamamalaṃ vibhum || 9 ||

amṛtabhairavapūjane ṭippaṇaṃ guruvarairgaditaṃ tu yathakramam |
samavagamya maheśavarāgamaṃ viracayāmyahamārtiharasya tat || 10 ||

mṛtyujinnetra sannāmno bhairavasyāmṛtātmanaḥ |
likhyate vidhinā dīkṣā mayā viśveśvareṇa tu || 11 ||

jñātvādau guruvaktratastadanu vai divyāgamācchāmbhavāt 
paścāt svānubhavācca maṃtravilayaṃ vighneśvareṇā (* *) t |
devasyāmṛtabhairavasya vidhinā dīkṣā kramaṃ likhyate 
tattvārthādi viśodhanena parato mukti śiśorāptaye || 12 ||

iti namaskāraḥ || 
dīrghāyuṣyaṃ vīrutatvaṃ śarīre saddeśeṣu janmalābho dhanāḍhye |
yūte vatse mātavatvaṃ sulābhādvartmne vāñchā yasya nābhūtmṛto'sau || 1 ||

mānuṣyaṃ prāpya saṃsāre saddeśe janmasauṣṭhavam |
tatrāpyavikalāṅgatvaṃ dharmavāñchāmanīṣitā || 2 ||

gurau deve liṅgine ca deśike sādhake tathā |
yasyānatiśayī bhaktirvartate janmanaḥ phalam || 3 ||

mānuṣyaṃ prāpyasajjanma devairapi sudurlabham |
ye yajante'mṛtīśākhyaṃ bhairavante'marā narāḥ || 4 ||

sāphalyajanmanaḥ kartuṃ yasyamīhā pravartate |
tadā'mṛtīśvaraṃ devaṃ bhaktyā saṃpūjayecchivam || 5 ||

iti mānuṣyajanmaślāghanā ||

jalabudvudanikarasame saṃsāre ghoraghoratare |
nipatanti mandamatayo viṣayāsaktā muhurmohāt || 1 ||

<p. 2a> sambhāvayanti satatamahamiti bhāvnena naśvare jagati |
madhuviṣavatparipāte viṣayāsaktāstanuṃ mohāt || 2 ||

saṃsārantaralataraṃ ghorāghoraṃ bhīṣaṇaṃ manujāḥ |
saṃprāpyaye yajantena śivaṃte vaisunārakiṇaḥ || 3 ||
iti saṃsāranindā ||

dhanāḍhyaṃ māninaṃ śūraṃ rūpavantaṃ priyaṃvadam |
yuvānaṃ taruṇaṃ vṛddhaṃ yamonayatinirghṛṇaḥ || 1 ||

vedavijñānasaṃpannaṃ mūrkhaṃ kāvyakaraṃ naram ||
rājānaṃ sārvabhaumaṃ ca yamonayati nirdayaḥ || 2 ||

pāśajālaṃ viniṣkṣipya kaṇṭhe mṛtyusuharṣitaḥ |
mārayan mudgareṇa saumānavaṃ nayati kṣaṇāt || 3 ||

gṛhaṃ dhanaṃ putra kalatra bāndhavān sukhāni rājyāni ca karmajāni |
saṃtyajya sarvaṃ manujāḥ prayānti śubhāśubhaiḥ karmayutāḥ svamṛtyum || 4 
|| itya nityatā ||

māyī pāśavaṃ kārmapāśamakhilenāstīrya saṃyantraṇātrā 
sānnetramudīrayanti ca tato mṛtyormukhāttāraṇāt |
tasmān mṛtyujitetyayaṃ nigadito lakṣmīśvaī saṃyuto 
bhaktānāmamṛtatva yogapadavī dāne'mṛtīśaḥ smṛtaḥ || 1 ||

tasmācchī devī karātpragṛhya vidhipūrvakam |
dīkṣāṃ nirvāṇasambhoga dātrī munmanadāyikām || 2 ||

yāvanna nirmalo hyātmāva (*) śaive taducyate |
patrasthaḥ puruṣaḥ sarvvaṃ vetyatīta manāmayam || 3 ||

sanniyemyendriya grāmaṃ tattatvaṃ śaktilakṣaṇam |
nirmalaṃ na bhaveddevi yāvaccha (*) na śodhitam || 4 ||

dīkṣājñānāgninā śodhyamātmānañcaiva nirmalam |
ye vidanti na caivānyaṃ vindanti paramaṃ padam || 5 ||

rudrākṣaṃ ca tripuḍrañca arcanaṃ japameva ca |
havipradānaṃ puṣpaṃ ca homodhyānastathaiva ca || 6 ||

pratiṣṭhā snapanaṃ caiva dīkṣāhīnasya niṣphalam |
kimanyai maṃtra vṛndaiśca devatārādhanena vā || 7 ||

yatraiva devadeveśo dhyāto japtaśca pūjitaḥ |
tatra sannihito bhaktyā dīkṣānairvāṇikī kṛte || 8 ||

brahmahatyā sahasrāṇi gostrī bāla śatāni ca |
pātakāni vinaśyanti dīkṣā nairvāṇikīkṛte || 9 ||

<p. 2b> ityātmaśaktiśodhanatvena dīkṣāmāhātmyam ||

atha guruśiṣyalakṣaṇamāha ||

śubhadeśa samudbhūtaḥ śubhava (*) samāśrayaḥ |
śubhavācā sadācāraḥ śubhaṃ mārgaprabodhakaḥ || 1 ||

darśanāhlādajanakaḥ sarvāvayavasaṃyutaḥ |
sarvaśāstrakṛtābhyāsaḥ sarvamārgapravartakaḥ || 2 ||

kulākulavibhāgañca svasaṃbandho (*) sodayaḥ |
sadājñānandamuditaḥ śivadarśanapāragaḥ || 3 ||

dravyādilobharahitastyāgī śarvajanāśrayaḥ |
nirācārapadasthopi satkṛpāparipālakaḥ || 4 ||

kramāyāta kramapade vijñānaughavisāradaḥ |
yo guruḥ sa śivaḥ sākṣādbhavabandhavimocakaḥ || 5 ||

tama vāpyamahāpuṇyai śiṣyeṇā mandabuddhinā |
trividhenātmanāṃ sevyaḥ karmaṇā manasā girā || 6 ||

śivo'yamiti pāśapto bhaktiyuktena cetasā |
daṇḍapātairnamaskuryāt kṛtvā maṇḍalakaṃ puraḥ || 7 ||

yadyatpriyatarantasya yena santoṣametyasau |
tadārādhanaśīlatvāttaṃtu śiṣyaḥ samāśrayet || 8 ||

praviśya hṛdayasyāntarbhāvamāvekṣya yatnataḥ |
gurorabhimataṃ sarvaṃ kāryaṃ kāryamanulvaṇam || 9 ||

dhanadāra śarīrādi sarvasvaṃ vinivedayet |
sukhaduḥkhaika bhāvena sphuṭamārādhayedgurum || 10 ||

gurorārādhanasthasya śiṣyasya karuṇākulāḥ |
bhavanti pūrvasaṃsiddhāḥ siddhidāna samutsukāḥ || 11 ||

ato'ntarbhāvanā bhāvyā vyājamucchṛtya sarvathā |
gurumūrtimayaṃ śambhumananya śaraṇaṃ vrajet || 12 ||

iha hyandhāna paśyanti kliśyante mohitā janāḥ |
gurau satyapi sevante nṛpatiṃ dhanalipsayā || 13 ||

kaṣṭānyanubhavanto'pi vidhāya prāṇavikrayam |
naivāpnuvanti te kiṃcidihācāmutraṃ vā phalam || 14 ||

yasyātinirmalā buddhirviveke sati vartate |
śivamūrti guruṃ tyaktvā sakathaṃ sevate nṛpam || 15 ||

sārvabhaumasya nṛpatergurośca mahadantaram |
eko bhukhaṇḍakaḥ svāmī sarvatatvadhipo'paraḥ || 16 ||

tuṣṭo dadāti kṛcchreṇa grāma mātraṃ narādhipaḥ |

<p. 3a> āsadāśivaparyantaṃ helayā cāgnito guruḥ || 17 ||

phaladvayornaduṣpro (*) mūle sikte yathātaroḥ |
tathaivārādhita guroḥ pūrvasiddhāḥ samṛddhayaḥ || 18 ||

samīhate muktipadaṃ gurusevāvinaivayaḥ |
aropitebhyo vṛkṣebhyeḥ saphalānyadhi vāñchati || 19 ||

brahmādayaḥ suravarā rudrā śambhu vaneśvarāḥ |
kuvaṃntyanugrahe kasmādgururyāvanna sevitaḥ || 20 ||

ārādhite gurau samyagmudāntakaruṇmaśrayāḥ |
bhavanti devatāḥ sarvāḥ sādhake'nugrahotsukāḥ || 21 ||

ato'nanyaśaraṇyena siddhamārgābhilāṣiṇāḥ |
gurumūrtau sadāsevyā vyaktimabhyāgataḥ śivaḥ || 22 ||

gurūṇāpi sphuṭākāryā parīkṣā prathamaṃ śiśoḥ |
nāparīkṣita śiṣyāṇāṃ yogyatā siddhaśāsane || 23 ||

kadāciduttameyo (*) kadācideṣame vidhau |
kadāci (* *) raṃ vākyaṃ kadācitparuṣākṣeram || 24 ||

kadācidantaḥ kartavyaḥ kadācitpreṣayedvahiḥ |
kadācidbahuśomānyaḥ kadācidapi tāḍayet  || 25 ||

evaṃ kṛta'pi noyāti vaśaṃ harṣaviṣādayoḥ |
yo bhaktibhāvitamatiḥsayogyaḥ siddhaśāsane || 26 ||

ya ekadinavadbhuktimuttarottarato nayet | 
kṛtārtho'hamiti jñātvā sayogyaḥ siddhaśāsane || 27 ||

ya ākruṣṭopi vahuśastāḍito'pyathavā bhavet |
na jahāti gurorbhaktiṃ sayogyaḥ siddhaśāsane || 28 ||

anuktamatha vāpyuktaṃ gurukāryaṃ na yastyajet | 
vartatenanyabhāvena sa yogyaḥ siddhaśāsane || 29 ||

ityādibhiḥ samācāraiḥ parīkṣya śataśaḥ śiśum |
yadā samyakparīkṣāto (* *) yogyo tadā bhavet || 30 ||

aṣṭautrīṇi tathā paṃca abdāṁstatra vibhāvayet |
tāvanna kārayeddīkṣāmityājñāṃ pārameśvarī || 31 ||

parīkṣitasya śiṣyasya dhane strīṣu tanau tathā |
niṣkalmaspadāntasya saṃsāravimukhasya ca || 32 ||

sarvabhāvānuraktasya gudeve ca liṅgine |
tasya deyamidaṃ jñānamanyathā narakaṃ vrajet || 33 ||

nadeyaṃ pāpaśīlānāṃ krodhināṃ pāpināṃ tathā |
gurunindāparāṇāṃ ca devamaṃtra vidūṣiṇāma || 34 ||

<p. 3b> nāstikānāṃ śaṭhānāṃ ca kriyādharma bahiṣkṛtām |
deyametat svaśiṣyāṇāṃ suputrāṇāṃ ca nānyathā || 35 ||

svadīkṣitānāṃ bhaktānāṃ gurudevāgnipūjinām |
vināsamayadīkṣāṃ vai na dadyāt svapriyāya ca || 36 ||

sarvathā naiva dātavyamityājñāpārameśvarī |
ājñābhaṅgena deveśi dehapāto bhaviṣyati || 37 ||

dadāti yadi mohena snehena dhanalipsayā |
gacchati narakaṃ ghoramityājñāpārameśvarī || 38 ||

ājñāyāḥ parameśasya pālanāt siddhimāpnuyāt |
pālanācca bhaveddevi mṛtyujit parameśvaraḥ || 39 ||

pūrvasevāṃ guroḥ kāryaṃ dvādaśābdaṃ samāhitaḥ |
saptavā paṃcavā trīṇi ekaṃ vā kṛta maṇḍalaḥ || 40 ||

sāmakrameṇa vā (*) vā svabhaktyā kāryakāraṇāt |
maṇḍalaṃ navaratnaiśca prakuryāt sārvakāmikam || 41 ||

athavā hemajaiścū (*) rājatairvā subhaktitaḥ |
catuḥsamena vā vṛttaṃ datvā vai hemapāduke || 42 ||

saptapaṃcatrayaṃ vāpi praṇāma daṇḍapūrvakam |
nipātya dharaṇīdeśe jānurdhve tu samāhitaḥ || 43 ||

añjaliṃ purayitvā tu puṣpaiḥ śvetaiḥ sugandhakai |
tato vijñāyate bhaktyā guruṃ mokṣapradāyakam || 44 ||

(* * *) ca pare tatve nirvāṇākhyo kule śive |
dehi me sadguro dīkṣāṃ dīnasyārtasya sarvathā || 45 ||

svecchayā kurume nātha dīkṣāṃ nirvāṇadāyikām |
yayākṛte bhavāmbodhistīryate caiva bandhanāt || 46 ||

iti guruśiṣya lakṣaṇamaṇḍalakaraṇāntānyuktāni ||

ādau śrīdeśikaḥ śrīmānvidhyuktena krameṇa ca |
snātvā rājopacāreṇa sandhyāṃ nirvatyanukramāt || 1 ||

sudhautavastrairdravalaiśca candanairjātyādi puṣpairdha balena bhūtinā |
yathā krameṇārti harasya pūjane hyalaṅkaret svaṃtanuśavyayaṃ smaran || 2 ||

sauvarṇarājatamayaṃ suśubhraṃ viśālaṃ 
muktābalī kanakadāmavilaṃviśobham |
nityotsavaṃ hariviriñci supūjitaṃ yad
yāgālayaṃ bhavabhayārti harasya śambhoḥ || 3 ||

caturdvāraṃ śubhaṃ ramyaṃ yāgadhāmaṃ mahāprabham |
nānāpuṣpavicitrāḍhyaṃ praviśeddeśikottamaḥ || 4 ||

<p. 4a> nānāvicitravihagaiḥ sevitaṃ mattakokilaiḥ |
latāmaṇḍapa saṃchannaṃ sevitaṃ yakṣakinnaraiḥ || 5 ||

campakāśoka punnāgairnārikelaiḥ kapitthakaiḥ |
panasāmradāḍimaiśca bījapūrakaiḥ |
mātuluṅgairghaṃvaistālaiḥ kvacit kharjūravṛkṣakaiḥ |
nāraṅgaistintintiṇībhiśca kadalī karmaraṅkakaiḥ || 7 ||

lavalīkaramardaiśca jambūpūgadrumaistathā |
tamālairbilvajambīraistathā vadaravṛkṣakaiḥ || 8 ||

kvacitkuravakopetaṃ kañjikāra kadaṃbakaiḥ |
vakulaiḥ kiṃkirātaiśca pāṭalairmadhūkaistathā || 9 ||

vṛkṣaiśca pārijātaughaiḥ samantāt pariśobhitam |
tagariḥ karavīraiśca mālatīkundayūthibhiḥ || 10 ||

jātībhiścaṃpakādyaśca navamālikayā kvacit |
kumudāmbuja kahlāraiḥ kvacinnīlotpalādibhiḥ || 11 ||

sarvartu kusumaiḥ puṇyaiḥ phalaiśca paribhūṣitam |
devagandharvayakṣeśairvidyādhara mahoragaiḥ || 12 ||

rākṣasendrai dānavaiśca kinnarairgaruḍaistathā |
sevitaṃ suprayatnena samantānmakhamaṇḍapam || 13 ||

apsarobhiśca devībhiḥ kinnarībhiśca gītakaiḥ |
yakṣī vidyādharībhiśca sevitaṃ munidārakaiḥ || 14 ||

śivānandarase magnā gītanṛtyādihāsyakaiḥ |
bhaktyā kṛtāñjalipuṭo yāga vāhye sthitāḥ stuvan || 15 ||

hāhāhūhū khumburunāradau ca viśvāvasuccitraratho sumālī |
yabhiḥ sugītaiḥ khalu śambhuyāga sevārase mattallvodbhaśca || 16 ||

ajāśca mārjāramukhairgajānanairulūkagṛdhroṣṭre jhivākhuvektakaiḥ |
gaṇeśvarairmatsyamṛgendrasūkaraiḥ śaśyanai kāmbikarūpadharakaiḥ || 17 ||

anaṃtavaktraiścaraṇaiśca bāhunī sakhānamūrdhāme bhṛtāścekadharai (*) 
pritairbhṛśaṃścīgaṇamukhyahanyai(?) saṃrakṣitaṃ vāhyate(?) eva yatnataḥ || 18 ||

śivājñayā te śatakoṭisaṃ (*) yosthitā maheśvareścirarakṣeṇāya |
vighnonvitiḥ sārayato dvisaptakoṭisthitāstegaṇamukhyavṛndāḥ || 19 ||

tato lakṣmī paścime tu dvāre dhyeyā surūpiṇīm |
navaratnaugha (* *) varṣantī makhamaṇḍapam || 20 ||

<p. 4b> sudīptaṃ piṅgalajaṭaṃ bhrūbhaṅgaiśca virājitam |
daṃṣṭrākarālavadanaṃ dehalyāmastrarājakam || 21 ||

tarjayan vighnasaṃghātamiccheddhyāyedgurūṃstadā |
pūjayitvā prayatnena paścāllaṃghet svadehalīm || 22 ||

praviśyābhyantaraṃ dvāre vāmaśākhāṃ samāśrayan |
niḥsarantantu vighnaughaṃ yāgamandiramadhyataḥ || 23 ||

iti saṃcityavidhinā śivayāgamanuttamam | 
āsadāśivaparyantaṃ tatvavrātātmakaṃ mahat || 24 ||

bahunātra kimuktena jyotirmayamanuttamam |
kāñcanaṃ ratnakhacitaṃ dhyeyaṃ śaivantu maṇḍapam || 25 ||

parānandarasonmato maṃtravinyastavigrahaḥ |
sāmānyārghajalenaiva prokṣyayanmaṇḍapottamam || 26 ||

yāyādīśānadigdeśe paṃcagavyasya sādhane |
pūrvānanotha vā saumye upaviśya susaṃyataḥ || 27 ||
 
maṇḍalaṃ kārayet tatra hastamātraṃ suśobhanam |
tasminmaṇḍake sthāpyāḥ sarāvā paṃcaśobhanā || 28 ||

nivṛtyādi kalā śuddhā tasminnikṣepayecchrutam |
nivṛtyādika bījena āsanaṃ ca dadet tathā || 29 ||

gojalaṃ gośakvatsarpirdadhidugdhamanukramāt |
paścimottara yāmyeṣu pūrve madhyeṣvanukramāt || 30 ||

kuśodakaṃ ratnayutaṃ gāyatrāmṛtarūpayā |

<p. 5a> prakṣipecchasrajāloyamiti saṃcintya tarjayan |
vighnānmūbhyantarikṣasthān khecarānapi saṃsmaret || 14 ||

kuśaṃ kuryāt tu varddhanyai āsanāya prakalpayet |
nairṛtye vāstunāmānaḥ lakṣīṃ dvāre prapūjayet || 15 ||

ratnena pūrayantī tu samantān makhamaṇḍapam |
paścimābhumukhe kuṇḍe śivaṃ sāṅgāmṛtīśvaram || 16 ||

kṣīrārṇavasthamāsīnaṃ varddhanīṃ siṃhagāminīm |
yajeddevaṃ ca devīṃ ca mahābhairavarūpiṇam || 17 ||

calāsane purābhyarcya japtvā maṃtraśatakramāt |
vardhanyā taddaśāṃśena japtvā jāpyaṃ gurūstadā || 18 ||

digīśāṃ pūjayet paścāt svanāmnāvāhanā yudhaiḥ |
ghaṭānugāminīṃ kṛtvā vardhanīṃ bhrāmayet tataḥ || 19 ||

śrāvayañśaṃkarasyājñāpūrvādīśānta gocaram |
vimuñcan payaso dhārān mūlamantarjayaṃ tadā || 20 ||

ādau kumbhantu dhṛtvā vai paścācchastrākhyavardhanī |
svasthāne tau vidhṛtvā tu praṇamyātha vadedidam || 21 ||

bho bho śakrādi dikpālā vāhanāyudhasaṃyutāḥ |
rakṣathe maṃmakhaṃ yatnāt sāvadhānaṃ śivājñayā || 22 ||

samagrāsanake kumbhe amṛtīśaṃ maheśvaram | 
pūjayet sāṅgaṃ (*) malaṃ vardhanyāmastrabhairavam || 23 ||

yoniliṅgākhya mudrāntu vidadhyālliṅgamudrayā |
kumbhopari tu vinyasya śivakhaḍgaṃ śivājñayā || 24 ||

kalaśasthaṃ śivaṃ śāṅgaṃ vardhanīṃ śastrarūpiṇam |
tayornamaskṛtiṃ kṛtvā paścāt saṃpūjayedgaṇam || 25 ||

śvetaṃ caturbhujaṃ devamekavaktraṃ trilocanam |
vāyavyāṃśitapadmasthaṃ gurūmīśānadigdale || 26 ||

sauvarṇāsanake śuddhe ratnaiśca samalaṅkṛte |
bhadrapīṭhe vare ramye snāpayejjagadīśvaram || 27 ||

paṃcāmṛtādibhirdevaṃ tathā svacchajalena ca |
dhūpapuṣpāntaraṃ kṛtvā vidhinā maṃtrapūrvakam || 28 ||

divyauṣadhīśatenāpi candanā guruvāriṇā |
ratnodakena śītena yatheṣṭaṃ snāpayet sudhīḥ || 29 ||

<p. 5b> tato hemamayekha (*) navaratnapariṣkṛte |
navasūkṣmāṃvara vṛte puṣpādyaiḥ sthāpayecchivam || 30 ||
