Text:

prayogamañjarī

prathamaḥ paṭalaḥ
buddherātmabhuvopyabhūmimamalaṃ yattatvamāhurbudhāḥ
bhedo yasya jaganmayasya bahudhāpyaupādhiko lakṣyate |
dehe svāpacitikramānabhidadhe mantrātmabhiḥ śreyase
yo vā dehabhṛtāṃ himācalasutānāthāya tubhyaṃ namaḥ || 1 ||
janayatu hṛdi saukhyaṃ vastuhinakarakalāvibhūṣaṇo devaḥ |
yasya jagattrayamakhilaṃ dhāmnā kalayā kayāpi niyūḍham || 2 ||
yebhyaḥ praṇāmavidhibhissakṛdapyupāttaissaṃsārasāgarataraṅganimajjamānāḥ |
dhvastāndhakāramanaso manujāstarantitebhyo namostu niyataṃ gurupādukebhyaḥ || 3 ||
jñānāgnidagdhaduritaviṣayapramuktai mūrdhnā dhṛtā sakalayogibhirādareṇa |
ye sarvalokamahitā manasaḥ prasādaṃ puṣṇantu te gurupadāmbujapāṃsavo vaḥ || 4 ||
uddāmatā na vacaso na ca kauśalaṃ me buddheḥ śivasya ca na boddhumalaṃ hi tattvam |
