alaukikasannikarṣastvidānīmucyate alaukikastviti| vyāpāraḥ sannikarṣaḥ| sāmānyalakṣaṇa iti| sāmānyaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ yasyetyarthaḥ| tatra lakṣaṇapadena yādau svarūpamucyate, tadā sāmānyasvarūpā pratyāsattirityartho labhyate| taccendriyasambaddhaviśeṣyakajñānaprakārībhūtaṃ bodhyaṃ, tathāhi yatrendriyasaṃyukto dhūmādiḥ, tadviśeṣyakaṃ dhūma iti jñānaṃ yatra jātaṃ, tatra jñāne dhūmatvaṃ prakāraḥ, tatra dhūmatvena sannikarṣeṇa dhūmā ityevaṃ rūpaṃ sakaladhūmaviṣayakaṃ jñānaṃ jāyate| atra yadīndriyasambaddhaprakārībhūtamityevocyate, tadā dhūlīpaṭale dhūmatvabhramānantaraṃ sakasadhūmaviṣayakaṃ jñānaṃ na syāt, tatra dhūmatvena saha indriyasambandhābhāvāt| manmate tvindriyasambaddhaṃ dhūlīpaṭalaṃ, tadviśeṣyakaṃ dhūma iti jñānaṃ, tatra prakārībhūtaṃ dhūmatvaṃ pratyāsattiḥ| indriyasambandhaśca laukiko grāhyaḥ| idaṃ ca bahirindriyasthale, mānasasthale tu jñānaprakārībhūtaṃ sāmānyaṃ pratyāsattiḥ ||63||
parantu samānānāṃ bhāvaḥ sāmānyam, tacca kvacinnityaṃ dhūmatvādi, kvaciccā'nityaṃ ghaṭādi | yatraiko ghaṭaḥ saṃyogena bhūtale samavāyena kapāle vā jñātaḥ, tadanantaraṃ sarveṣāmeva tadghaṭavatāṃ bhūtalādīnāṃ kapālādīnāṃ vā jñānaṃ bhavati, tatredaṃ bodhyam| parantu sāmānyaṃ yena sambandhena jñāyate, tena sambandhenā'dhikaraṇānāṃ pratyāsattiḥ | kintu yatra tadghaṭanāśānantaraṃ tadghaṭavataḥ smaraṇaṃ jātaṃ tatra sāmānyalakṣaṇayā sarveṣāṃ tadghaṭavatāṃ bhānaṃ na syāt, sāmānyasya tadānīmabhāvāt| kiñcendriyasambaddhaviśeṣyakaṃ ghaṭa iti jñānaṃ yatra jātaṃ, tatra paradine indriyasambandhaṃ vinā'pi tādṛśajñānaprakārībhūtasāmānyasya sattvāt tādṛśajñānaṃ kuto na janyate? tasmāt sāmānyaviṣayakaṃ jñānaṃ pratyāsattiḥ, na tu sāmānyamityāha -- āsattiriti| āsattiḥ -- pratyāsattirityarthaḥ| tathā ca "sāmānyalakṣaṇaḥ" ityatra "lakṣaṇa"śabdasya viṣayo'rthaḥ| tasmāt sāmānyaviṣayakaṃ jñānaṃ pratyāsattirityartho labhyate| nanu cakṣuḥsaṃyogādikaṃ vinā'pi sāmānyajñānaṃ yatra vartate, tatra sakalaghaṭādīnāṃ cākṣuṣapratyakṣaṃ syādata āha -- taditi| asyā'rthaḥ -- yadā bahirindriyeṇa sāmānyalakṣaṇayā jñānaṃ jananīyaṃ, tadā yatkiñciddharmiṇi tatsāmānyasya tadindriyajanyajñānasya sāmagryapekṣitā| sā ca sāmagrī cakṣuḥsaṃyogālokasaṃyogādikam | tenā'ndhakārādau cakṣurādinā tādṛśaṃ jñānaṃ na janyate||64||
||iti sāmānyalakṣaṇapratyāsattinirūpaṇam||
viṣayī yasya tasyaiva vyāpāro jñānalakṣaṇaḥ|
nanu jñānalakṣaṇā pratyāsattiryadi jñānarūpā, sāmānyalakṣaṇā'pi jñānarūpā, tadā tayorbhedo na syādata āha viṣayīti| sāmānyalakṣaṇāpratyāsattirhi tadāśrayasya jñānaṃ janayati| jñānalakṣaṇāpratyāsattistu yadviṣayakaṃ jñānaṃ tasyaiva pratyāsattiḥ| atrā'yamarthaḥ -- pratyakṣe sannikarṣaṃ vinā bhānaṃ na sambhavati tathā ca sāmānyalakṣaṇāṃ vinā dhūmatvena sakaladhūmānāṃ, vahnitvena sakalavahnīnāṃ ca bhānaṃ kathaṃ bhavet? tadarthaṃ sāmānyalakṣaṇā svīkriyate| na ca sakalavahnidhūmabhānābhāve kā kṣatiriti vācyam| pratyakṣadhūme vahnisambandhasya gṛhītatvāt anyadhūmasya cā'nupasthitatvāt dhūmo vahnivyāpyo na veti saṃśayānupapatteḥ| manmate tu sāmānyalakṣaṇayā sakaladhūmopasthitau kālāntarīyadeśāntarīyadhūme vahnivyāpyatvasandehaḥ sambhavati| na ca sāmānyalakṣaṇāsvīkāre prameyatvena sakalaprameyajñāne jāte sārvajñāpattiriti vācyam| prameyatvena sakalaprameyajñāne jāte'pi viśiṣyasakalapadārthānāmajñātatvena sārvajñyābhāvāt|
evaṃ jñānalakṣaṇāyā asvīkāre surabhi candanamiti jñāne saurabhasya bhānaṃ kathaṃ sthāt? yadyapi sāmānyalakṣaṇayā'pi saurabhasya bhānaṃ sambhavati, tathā'pi saurabhatvasya bhānaṃ jñānalakṣaṇayā| evaṃ yatra dhūmatvena dhūlīpaṭalaṃ jñātaṃ, tatra dhūlīpaṭalasyā'nuvyavasāye bhānaṃ jñānalakṣaṇayā |
||iti jñānalakṣaṇapratyāsattinirūpaṇam||
yogajo dvividhaḥ prokto yuktayuñjānamedataḥ||65||
yuktasya sarvadā bhānaṃ, cintāsahakṛto'paraḥ|
yogaja iti| yogābhyāsajanito dharmaviśeṣaḥ śrutipurāṇādipratipādya ityarthaḥ| yuktayuñjānarūpayogidvaividhyāddharmasyā'pi dvaividhyamiti bhāvaḥ||65||
yuktasyeti| yuktasya tāvadyogajadharmasahāyena manasā ākāśaparamāṇvādinikhilapadārthagocaraṃ jñānaṃ sarvadaiva bhavitumarhati, dvitīyasya cintāviśeṣo'pi sahakārīti||
iti yogajapratyāsattinirūpaṇam||
||iti śrīviśvanāthapañcānanabhaṭṭācāryaviracitāyāṃ nyāyasiddhāntamuktāvalyāṃ pratyakṣaparicchedaḥ samāptaḥ||
athānumānakhaṇḍaḥ vyāpārastu parāmarśaḥ karaṇaṃ vyāptidhīrbhavet|| 66||
anumitiṃ vyutpādayati| vyāpārastviti| anumāyām -- anumitau vyāptijñānaṃ karaṇaṃ, paramarśo vyāpāraḥ|
tathāhi -- yena puruṣeṇa mahānasādau dhūme vahnivyāptirgṛhītā, paścāt sa eva puruṣaḥ kvacit parvatādāvavicchinnamūlāṃ dhūmarekhāṃ paśyati, tadanantaraṃ dhūmo vahnivyāpya ityevaṃ rūpaṃ vyāptismaraṇaṃ tasya bhavati, paścācca vahnivyāpyadhūmavānayamiti jñānaṃ, sa eva parāmarśa ityucyate| tadanantaraṃ parvato vahnimānityanumitirjāyate||
ityanumitikāraṇanirūpaṇam||
anumāyāṃ jñāyamānaṃ liṅgaṃ tu karaṇaṃ na hi|
anāgatādiliṅgena na syādanumitistadā||67||
vyāpyasya pakṣavṛttitvadhīḥ parāmarśa ucyate||
atra prācīnāstu vyāpyatvena jñāyamānaṃ liṅgamanumitikaraṇamiti vadanti| tat dūṣayati -- jñāyamānamiti| liṅgasyā'numityakaraṇatve yuktimāha -- anāgatādīti|
yadyanumitau liṅgaṃ karaṇaṃ syāt, tadā'nāgatena liṅgena, vinaṣṭena cānumitirna syāt, anumitikaraṇasya liṅgasya tadānīmabhāvāditi||66|| ||67|| iti jñāyamānaliṅgasyā'numitihetutvakhaṇḍanam||
vyāpyasyeti | vyāptiviśiṣṭasya pakṣeṇa saha vaiśiṣṭyāvagāhijñānamanumitau janakaṃ, tacca pakṣe vyāpya iti jñānaṃ pakṣo vyāpyavāniti jñānaṃ vā| anumitistu pakṣe vyāpya iti jñānāt pakṣe sādhyamityākārikā, pakṣo vyāpyavāniti jñānāt pakṣaḥ sādhyavānityākārikā|
dvividhādapi parāmarśāt pakṣaḥ sādhyavānityevā'numitirityanye|
nanu vahnivyāpyadhūmavān parvata iti jñānaṃ vinā'pi yatra parvato dhūmavāniti pratyakṣaṃ, tato vahnivyāpyo dhūma iti smaraṇaṃ, tatra jñānadvayādevā'numiterdarśanāt vyāptiviśiṣṭavaiśiṣṭyāvagāhijñānaṃ na sarvatra kāraṇaṃ, kintu vyāpyatāvacchedakaprakārakapakṣadharmatājñānatvena kāraṇatvasyā''vaśyakatvāt tatra viśiṣṭajñānakalpane gauravācceti cenna| vyāpyatāvacchedakājñāne'pi vahnivyāpyavāniti jñānādanumityutpatteḥ lāghavācca vyāptiprakārakapakṣadharmatājñānatvena hetutvam|
kiñca dhūmavān parvata iti jñānādanumityāpattiḥ, vyāpyatāvacchedakībhūtadhūmatvaprakārakasya pakṣadharmatājñānasya sattvāt|
na ca gṛhyamāṇavyāpyatāvacchedakaprakārakapakṣadharmatājñānasya hetutvamiti vācyam| caitrasya vyāptigrahe maitrasya pakṣadharmatājñānāt anumitiḥ syāt|
yadi tatpuruṣīyagṛhyamāṇavyāpyatāvacchedakaprakārakaṃ tatpuruṣīyapakṣadharmatājñānaṃ tatpuruṣīyānumitau heturityucyate, tadā'nantakāryakāraṇabhāvaḥ| manmate tu samavāyasambandhena vyāptiprakārakapakṣadharmatājñānaṃ samavāyasambandhenā'numiti janayatīti nā'nantakāryakāraṇabhāvaḥ|
yadi tu vyāptiprakārakaṃ jñānaṃ pakṣadharmatājñānaṃ ca svatantraṃ kāraṇamityucyate tadā kāryakāraṇabhāvadvayaṃ, vahnivyāpyo dhūma ālokavāṃśca parvata iti jñānādapyanumitiḥ syāt| itthaṃ ca yatra jñānadvayaṃ, tatrā'pi viśiṣṭajñānaṃ kalpanīyaṃ, phalamukhagauravasyā'doṣatvāt|| iti parāmarśanirūpaṇam||
vyāptiḥ sādhyavadanyasminnasambandha udāhṛtaḥ||68||
vyāpyo nāma vyāptyāśrayaḥ, tatra vyāptiḥ ketyata āha vyāptiriti| vahnimān dhūmādityādau sādhyo vahniḥ, sādhyavān mahānasādiḥ, tadanyo jalahradādiḥ, tadvṛṛttitvaṃ dhūmasyeti lakṣaṇasamanvayaḥ piṇḍādau vahneḥ sattvānnā'tivyāptiḥ|
atra yena sambandhena sādhyaṃ, tena sambandhena sādhyavān bodhyaḥ, anyathā samavāyasambandhena vahnimān vahneravayavaḥ, tadanyo mahānasādiḥ, tatra dhūmasya vidyamanatvādavyāptiprasaṅgāt |
sādhyavadanyaśca sādhyavattvāvacchinnapratiyogitākabhedavān bodhyaḥ| tena yatkiñcidvahnimato mahānasāderbhinne parvatādau dhūmasattve'pi na kṣatiḥ|
yena sambandhena hetutā tenaiva sambandhena sādhyavadanyāvṛttitvaṃ bādhyam| tena sādhyavadanyasmin dhūmāvayave dhūmasya samavāyasambandhena sattve'pi na kṣatiḥ|
sādhyavadanyāvṛttitvaṃ ca sādhyavadanyavṛttitvāvacchinnapratiyogitākābhāvaḥ, tena dhūmavān vahnerityatra sādhyavadanyajalahradādivṛttitvābhāve'pi nā'tivyāptiḥ|
atra yadyapi dravyaṃ guṇakarmānyatvaviśiṣṭasattvādityādau viśiṣṭasattāyāḥ śuddhasattāyāścaikyāt sādhyavadanyasmin guṇādāvavṛttitvaṃ nā'sti, tathā'pi hetutāvacchedakarūpeṇā'vṛttitvaṃ vācyaṃ, hetutāvacchedakaṃ vṛttitānavacchedakamiti phalitā'rthaḥ||68|| iti vyāptinirūpaṇe pūrvapakṣagranthaḥ||
athavā hetumanniṣṭhavirahāpratiyoginā|
sādhyena hetoraikādhikaraṇyaṃ vyāptirucyate||69||
nanu kevalānvayini jñeyatvādau sādhye sādhyavadanyasyā'prasiddhatvādavyāptiḥ| kiñca sattāvān jāterityādau sādhyavadanyasmin sāmānyādau hetutāvacchedakasambandhena samavāyena vṛtteraprasiddhatvādata āha -- atha veti| hetumati niṣṭhāvṛttiryasya sa tathā virahaḥ -- abhāvaḥ| tathā ca hetvadhikaraṇavṛttiryo'bhāvastadapratiyoginā sādhyena saha hetoḥ sāmānādhikaraṇyaṃ vyāptirucyate|
atra yadyapi vahnimān dhūmādityādau hetvadhikaraṇaparvatādivṛttyabhāvapratiyogitvaṃ tattadvahnyāderastītyavyāptiḥ| na ca samānādhikaraṇavahnidhūmayoreva vyāptiriti vācyam| tattadvahnyāderapyubhayābhāvasattvāt, ekasattve'pi dvayaṃ nā'stīti pratīteḥ| guṇavān dravyatvādityādāvavyāptiśca|
tathā'pi pratiyogitānavacchedakaṃ yat sādhyatāvacchedakaṃ tadavacchinnasāmānādhikaraṇyaṃ vyāptiriti vācyam|
nanu rūpatvavyāpyajātimattvān pṛthivītvādityādau sādhyatāvacchedikā rūpatvavyāpyajātayaḥ, tāsāṃ ca śuklatvādisvarūpāṇāṃ nīlaghaṭādivṛttyabhāvapratiyogitāvacchedakatvamastītyavyaptiriti cenna|
tatra paramparayā rūpatvavyāpyajātitvasyaiva sādhyatāvacchedakatvāt| na hi tādṛśadharmāvacchinnābhāvaḥ kā'pi pṛthivyāmasti, rūpatvavyāpyajātimānnāstīti buddhyāpatteḥ|
sādhyādibhedena vyāprterbhedāt tādṛśasthale sādhyatāvacchedakatāvacchedakaṃ pratiyogitāvacchedakatānavacchedakamityeva lakṣaṇaghaṭakamityapi vadanti|
hetvadhikaraṇaṃ hetutāvacchedakaviśiṣṭādhikaraṇaṃ vācyam| tena dravyaṃ guṇakarmānyatvaviśiṣṭasattvādityādau śuddhasattādhikaraṇaguṇādiniṣṭhābhāvapratiyogitve'pi dravyatvasya nā'vyāptiḥ|
hetutāvacchedakasambandhena hetvadhikaraṇaṃ bodhyam| tena samavāyena dhūmādhikaraṇatadavayavaniṣṭhābhāvapratiyogitve'pi vahnernā'vyāptiḥ|
abhāvaśca pratiyogivyadhikaraṇo bodhyaḥ| tena kapisaṃyogyetadvṛkṣatvādityādau mūlāvacchedenaitadvṛkṣavṛttikapisaṃyogābhāvapratiyogitve'pi kapisaṃyogasya nā'vyāptiḥ|
na ca pratiyogivyadhikaraṇatvaṃ yadi pratiyogyanadhikaraṇavṛttitvaṃ, tadā tathaivā'vyāptiḥ, pratiyoginaḥ kapisaṃyogasyā'nadhikaraṇe guṇādau vartamāno yo'bhāvastasyaiva vṛkṣe mūlāvacchedena sattvāt| yadi tu pratiyogyadhikaraṇāvṛttitvaṃ, tadā saṃyogī sattvādityādāvativyāptiḥ, sattādhikaraṇe guṇādau yaḥ saṃyogābhāvastasya pratiyogyadhikaraṇadravyavṛttitvāditi vācyam| hetvadhikaraṇe pratiyogyanadhikaraṇavṛttitvaviśiṣṭasya vivakṣitatvāt| svapratiyogyanadhikaraṇībhūtahetvadhikaraṇavṛttyabhāveti niṣkarṣaḥ|
pratiyogyanadhikaraṇatvaṃ ca pratiyogitāvacchedakāvacchinnānadhikaraṇatvaṃ vācyam| tena viśiṣṭasattāvān jāterityādau jātyadhikaraṇaguṇādau viśiṣṭasattābhāvapratiyogisattādhikaraṇatvasattve'pi na kṣatiḥ|

itthañca vahnimān dhūmādityādau dhūmādhikaraṇe samavāyena vahnivirahasattve'pi na kṣatiḥ|
nanu pratiyogitāvacchedakāvacchinnasya yasya kasyacitpratiyogino'nadhikaraṇatvaṃ, tatsāmānyasya vā, yatkiñcicpratiyogitāvacchedakāvacchinnānadhikaraṇatvaṃ vā vivakṣitam?
ādye kapisaṃyogī etadvṛkṣatvādityatra tathevā'vyāptiḥ, kapisaṃyogābhāvapratiyogitāvacchedakāvacchinno vṛkṣāvṛttikapisaṃyogo'pi bhavati, tadanadhikaraṇaṃ vṛkṣa iti|
dvitīye tu pratiyogivyadhikaraṇābhāvāprasiddhiḥ, sarvasyaivā'bhāvasya pūrvakṣaṇavṛttitvaviśiṣṭasvābhāvātmakapratiyogisamānādhikaraṇatvāt| na ca vahnimān dhūmādityādau ghaṭābhāvādeḥ pūrvakṣaṇavṛttitvaviśiṣṭasvābhāvātmakapratiyogyanadhikaraṇatvaṃ yadyapi parvatādeḥ, tathāpi sādhyatāvacchedakasambandhena tatpratiyogyanadhikaraṇatvamastyeveti kathaṃ pratiyogivyadhikaraṇābhāvāprasiddhiriti vācyam| ghaṭābhāve yo vahnyabhāvaḥ, tasya ghaṭābhāvātmakatayā ghaṭābhāvasya vahnirapi pratiyogī, tadadhikaraṇaṃ ca parvatādirityevaṃkrameṇa pratiyogivyadhikaraṇasyā'prasiddhatvāt| yadi ca ghaṭābhāvādau vanhyabhāvādirbhinna ityucyate, tathā'pi dhūmābhāvavān vahnyabhāvādityādāvavyāptiḥ, tatra sādhyatāvacchedakasambandhaḥ svarūpasambandhaḥ, tena ca sambandhena sarvasyaivā'bhāvasya pūrvakṣaṇavṛttitvaviśiṣṭasvābhāvātmakapratiyogyadhikaraṇatvaṃ hetvadhikaraṇasyeti|

maivam| yādṛśapratiyogitāvacchedakāvacchinnānadhikaraṇatvaṃ hetumataḥ, tādṛśapratiyogitānavacchedakatvasya vivakṣitatvāt|
nanu kālo ghaṭavān kālaparimāṇādityatra pratiyogivyadhikaraṇābhāvāprasiddhiḥ, hetvadhikaraṇasya mahākālasya jagadādhāratayā sarveṣāmevā'bhāvānāṃ sādhyatāvacchedakasambandhena kālikaviśeṣaṇatayā pratiyogyadhikaraṇatvāt|
atra kecit| mahākālabhedaviśiṣṭaghaṭābhāvastatra pratiyogivyadhikaraṇaḥ, mahākālasya ghaṭādhāratve'pi mahākālabhedaviśiṣṭaghaṭānādhāratvāt, mahākāle mahākālabhedābhāvāt|
vastutastu pratiyogitāvacchedakasambandhena pratiyogyanadhikaraṇībhūtahetvadhikaraṇavṛttyabhāvapratiyogitāsāmānye yatsambandhāvacchinnatvayaddharmāvācchinnatvobhayābhāvaḥ, tena sambandhena taddharmāvacchinnasya taddhetuvyāpakatvaṃ bodhyam| itthaṃ ca kālo ghaṭavān kālaparimāṇādityādau saṃyogasambandhena ghaṭābhāvapratiyogino'pi ghaṭasyā'nadhikaraṇe hetvadhikaraṇe mahākāle vartamānaḥ sa eva saṃyogena ghaṭābhāvaḥ, tasya pratiyogitāyāṃ kālikasambandhāvacchinnatvaghaṭatvāvacchinnatvobhayābhāvasattvānnā'vyāptiḥ|
nanu prameyavahnimān dhūmādityādau prameyavahnitvāvacchinnatvamaprasiddhaṃ, gurudharmasyā'navacchedakatvāditi cenna| kambugrīvādimānnā'stīti pratītyā kambugrīvādimattvāvacchinnapratiyogitāviṣayīkaraṇena gurudharmasyā'pyavacchedakatvasvīkārāditi saṅkṣepaḥ||66|| iti vyāptinirūpaṇe siddhāntagranthaḥ||

paramāṇau jale nityamanyatrā'nityamiṣyate||155||
naimittikaṃ vahniyogāt tapanīyaghṛtādiṣu|
gurutvaṃ nirūpayati atīndriyamiti|
anitye -- dravyaṇukādau| tat -- gurutvam anityaṃ, nitye -- paramāṇau nityaṃ, gurutvamityanuvartate||153||
tat -- gurutvam, asamavāyi -- asamavāyikāraṇam| patane -- ādyapatana ityarthaḥ|
||iti gurutvanirūpaṇam||
dravatvaṃ nirūpayati | sāṃsiddhikamiti| dravatvaṃ dvividhaṃ, sāṃsiddhikaṃ naimittikaṃ cetyarthaḥ|
paramāṇāviti | jalaparamāṇau dravatvaṃ nityamanyatra pṛthivīparamāṇvādau jaladvyaṇukādau ca dravatvamanityam||122||
kutracittejasi kutracitpṛthivyāṃ ca naimittikaṃ dravatvam| tatra ko vā naimittikārthastaddarśayati| naimittikamiti| vahnītipadaṃ tejo'rthakam| tathā ca tejaḥsaṃyogajanyaṃ naimittikaṃ dravatvam| tacca suvarṇādirūpe tejasi, ghṛtajatuprabhṛtipṛthivyāṃ ca vartate ityarthaḥ|
dravatvaṃ syandane heturnimittaṃ saṅgrahe tu tat|156||
sneho jale sa nityo'ṇāvanityo'vayavinyasau|
tailāntare tatprakarṣād dahanasyā'nukūlatā||17||
saṃskārabhedo vego'tha sthitisthāpakabhāvane|
mūrtamātre tu vegaḥ syāt karmajo vegajaḥ kvacit||158||
dravatvaṃ syandane heturiti| asamavāyikāraṇamityarthaḥ| saṃgrahe --saktvādisaṃyogaviśeṣe| tat -- dravatvam, snehasahitamiti boddhavyam| tena drutasuravarṇādinā na saṃgrahaḥ||156||
iti dravatvanirūpaṇam||
snehaṃ nirūpayati| sneho jala iti| jala evetyarthaḥ| asau -- snehaḥ| nanu pṛthivyāmapi taile sneha upalabhyate| na cā'sau jalīyaḥ| tathā sati dahanaprātikūlyaprasaṅgādata āha | tailāntara iti| tatprakarṣāt snehaprakarṣāt| taila upalabhyamānaḥ sneho'pi jalīya eva, tasya prakṛṣṭatvādagnerānukūlyam| apakṛṣṭasnehaṃ hi jalaṃ vahni nāśayatīti bhāvaḥ||157|| iti snehanirūpaṇam||
saṃskāraṃ nirūpayati| saṃskāreti| vegasthitisthāpakabhāvanābhedāt saṃskārastrividha ityarthaḥ| martamātra iti| karmajavegajabhedādvego dvividha ityarthaḥ| śarādau hi nodanajanitena karmaṇā vego janyate| tena ca pūrvakarmanāśaḥ| tata uttarakarma| evamagre'pi| vinā ca vegaṃ karmaṇaḥ karmapratibandhakatvāt pūrvakarmanāśa uttarakarmotpattiśca na syāt| yatra vegavatā kapālena janite ghaṭe vego janyate sa vegajo vegaḥ||158||
sthitisthāpakasaṃskāraḥ kṣitau keciccatuṣrvapi|
atīndriyo'sau vijñeyaḥ kvacit spande'pi kāraṇam||159||
sthitisthāpaketi| ākṛṣṭaśākhādīnāṃ parityāge punargamanasya sthitisthāpakaḥsādhyatvāt| keciditi| caturṣakṣityādiṣu sthitisthāpakaṃ kecinmanyante tadapramāṇamiti bhāvaḥ| asau -- sthitisthāpakaḥ| kvacit -- ākṛṣṭaśākhādau||156||
bhāvanākhyastu saṃskāro jīvavṛttiratīndriyaḥ|
upekṣānātmakastasya niścayaḥ kāraṇaṃ bhavet||160||
bhāvanākhya iti| tasya saṃskārasya upekṣātmakajñānāt saṃskārānupapatterupekṣānātmaka ityuktam| tatsaṃśayāt saṃskārānutpatterniścaya ityuktam| tenopekṣānyaniścayatvena hetutvam| tenopekṣādisthale na smaraṇam| itthaṃ ca saṃskāraṃ prati jñānatvenaiva hetutā'stviti cenna| vinigamanāviraheṇa saṃskāraṃ pratyapyupekṣānyaniścayatvena hetutāyāḥ siddhatvāt| kiṃ copekṣāsthale saṃskārakalpanāyā gurutvāt saṃskāraṃ prati copekṣānyaniścayatvena hetutāyāḥ siddhatvāt||160||
smaraṇe pratyabhijñāyāmapyasau heturucyate|
tatra pramāṇaṃ darśayati| smaraṇa iti| asau saṃskāro yataḥ smaraṇaṃ pratyabhijñānaṃ ca janayatyataḥ saṃskāraḥ kalpyate| vinā vyāpāraṃ pūrvānubhavasya smaraṇādijananāsāmātthryāt,
svasvavyāpārānyatarābhāve kāraṇatvāsambhavāt|| na ca pratyabhijñāṃ prati tattatsaṃskārasya hetutve pratyabhijñāyāḥ saṃskārajanyatvena smṛtitvāpattiriti vācyam| aprayojakatvāt|
pare tvanudbuddhasaṃskārāt pratyabhijñānudayādudbuddhasaṃskārasya hetutvāpekṣayā tattatsmaraṇasyaiva pratyabhijñāṃ prati hetutvaṃ kalpyata ityāhuḥ|
||iti saṃskāranirūpaṇam||
dharmādharmāvadṛṣṭaṃ syād, dharmaḥ svargādikāraṇam||161||
gaṅgāsnānādiyāgādivyāpāraḥ sa tu kīrtitaḥ|
adṛṣṭaṃ nirūpayati| dharmādharmāviti| svargādisakalasukhānāṃ svargasādhanībhūtaśarīrādīnāṃ ca sādhana dharma ityarthaḥ||161||
tatra pramāṇaṃ darśayitumāha yāgāditi| yāgādivyāpāratayā hi dharmaḥ kalpyate| anyathā yāgādīnāṃ ciravinaṣṭatayā nivryāpāratayā ca kālāntarabhāvisvargajanakatvaṃ na syāt| taduktamācāryaiḥ "ciradhvastaṃ phalāyā'laṃ na karmātiśayaṃ vinā" iti|
nanu yāgadhvaṃsa eva vyāpāraḥ syāt| na ca pratiyogidhvaṃsayorekatrā'janakatvam, sarvatra tathātve mānābhāvāt| na ca tvanmate phalānantyaṃ, manmate caramaphalasyā'pūrvanāśakatvānna tathātvamiti vācyam| kālaviśeṣasya sahakāritvādityata āha| gaṅgāsnāneti| gaṅgāsnānasya hi svargajanakatve'nantānāṃ jalasaṃyogadhvaṃsānāṃ vyāpāratvamapekṣyaikamevā'pūrvaṃ kalpyate, lāghavāditi bhāvaḥ|
karmanāśājalasparśādinā nāśyastvasau mataḥ||162||
nanu dhvaṃso'pi na vyāpāro'stu| na ca nivryāpārasya ciradhvastasya kathaṃ kāraṇatvamiti vācyam| ananyathāsiddhaniyatapūrvavartitvasya tatrāpi sattvāt| avyavahitapūrvavarttitvaṃ hi cakṣuḥsaṃyogādeḥ kāraṇatvaṃ, na tu sarvatra, kāryakālavṛttitvamiva samavāyikāraṇasya kāraṇatva ityata āha| karmanāśeti| yadi hyapūrvaṃ na syāttadā karmanāśājalasparśādinā nāśyatvaṃ dharmasya na syāt| na hi tena yāgādināśaḥ pratibandho vā katrtuṃ śakyate, tasya pūrvameva vṛttatvāditi|
etena devatāprītireva phalamityapāstam| gaṅgāsnānādau sarvatra devatāprīterasambhavāt| devatāyāścetanatve'pi tatprīteranuddeśyatvāt| prīteḥ sukhasvarūpatvena viṣṇuprītyādau tadasambhavāt| janyasukhādestatrābhāvāt| tena viṣṇuprītijanyatvena parābhimatasvargādireva viṣṇuprītiśabdena kathyate||162||
adharmo narakādīnāṃ heturninditakarmajaḥ|
prāyaścittādināśyo'sau jīvavṛttī tvimau guṇau||163||
adharmo narakādīnāmiti| narakaduḥkhādisakaladuḥkhānāṃ nārakīyaśarīrādīnāṃ ca sādhanamadharma ityarthaḥ| pramāṇamāha| prāyaścitteti| yadi, hyadharmo na syāt tadā prāyaścittādinā nāśyatvamadharmasya na syāt| na hi tena brahmahananādīnāṃ nāśaḥ pratibandho vā vidhātuṃ śakyate, tasya pūrvameva vinaṣṭatvāditi bhāvaḥ| jīveti| īśvarasya dharmādharmābhāvāditi bhāvaḥ||163||
imau tu vāsanājanyau jñānādapi vinaśyataḥ|
śabdo dhvaniśca varṇaśca mṛdaṅgādibhavo dhvaniḥ||164||
kaṇṭhasaṃyogādijanyā varṇāste kādayo matāḥ sarvaḥ śabdo nabhovṛttiḥ śrotrotpannastu gṛhyate||165||
vīcītaraṅganyāyena tadutpattistu kīrtitā|
kadambagolakanyāyādutpattiḥ kasyacinmate||166||
utpannaḥ ko vinaṣṭaḥ ka iti buddheranityatā|
so'yaṃ ka iti buddhistu sājātyamavalambate||167||
imau dharmāgharmauṃ| vāsaneti | ato jñāninā kṛte api sukṛtadupkṛtakarmaṇī na phalāyā'lamiti bhāvaḥ| jñānādapītyapinā bhogaparigrahaḥ| nanu tattvajñānasya kathaṃ dharmādharmanāśakatvaṃ? ""nābhuktaṃ kṣīyate karma kalpakoṭiśatairapi"" itivacanavirodhāt| itthaṃ ca tattvajñānināṃ jhaṭiti kāyavyūhena sakalakarmaṇāṃ bhogena kṣaya iti cenna| tatra bhogasya vedabodhitanāśakopalakṣakatvāt| kathamanyathā prāyaścittādinā karmaṇāṃ nāśaḥ? taduktam, ""jñānāgniḥ sarvakarmāṇi bhasmasātkurute'rjuna"" ityādinā| śrūyate ca ""kṣīyante cāsya karmāṇi tasmin dṛṣṭe parāvare|"" iti|
nanu tattvajñāninastarhi śarīrāvasthānaṃ sukhaduḥkhādi ca na syāt jñānena sarveṣāṃ karmaṇāṃ nāśāditi cenna| prārabdhetarakarmaṇāmeva nāśāt| tattaccharīrabhogajanakaṃ hi yatkarma tat prārabdham| tadabhiprāyameva nā'bhuktamiti vacanamiti| ityadṛṣṭanirūpaṇam||
śabdaṃ nirūpayati| śabdo dhvaniśceti||163|
nabhoevṛttiḥ ākāśasamavetaḥ| dūrasthaśabdasyā'grahaṇādāha| śrotreti||165||
nanu mṛdaṅgādyavacchedenotpanne śabde śrotre kathamutpattirata āha| vīcīti| ādyaśabdena bahirdaśadigavacchinne'nyaḥ śabdastenaiva śabdena janyate tena cāparastadvyāpakaḥ|
evaṃkrameṇa śrotrotpanno gṛhyate iti| kadambeti| ādyaśabdādṛśasu dikṣu daśa śabdā utpadyante| tataścānye daśa śabdā utpadyanta iti bhāvaḥ| asmin kalpe kalpanāgauravāduktaṃ kasyacinmata iti||166||
nanu śabdasya nityatvādutpattiḥ kathamata āha| utpanna iti| śabdānāmutpādavināśaśālitvādanityatvamityarthaḥ| nanu sa evāyaṃ kakāra ityādipratyabhijñānācchabdānāṃ nityatvam| itthaṃ cotpādanāśabuddhibhrramarūpaivetyata āha| so'yaṃ ka iti| tatra pratyabhijñānasya tatsajātīyatvaṃ viṣayo na tu tadvyaktyabhedo viṣayaḥ, uktapratītivirodhāt| itthaṃ ca dvayorapi buddhyorna bhramatvamiti||167||
nanu sajātīyatvaṃ so'yamiti pratyabhijñāyāṃ bhāsate iti kutra dṛṣṭamityata āha|
tadevauṣadhamityādau sajātīye'pi darśanāt|
tasmādanityā eveti varṇāḥ sarve mataṃ hi naḥ||168||
