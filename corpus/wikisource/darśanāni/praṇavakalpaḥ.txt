|| śrīḥ
śrīmadānandātīrthabhagavatpādapraṇītasarvamūlagranthāḥ
praṇavakalpaḥ|| śrīmaddhanumadbhimamadhvāntargatarāmakṛṣaaṇavedavyāsātmakaśrīlakṣmīhayagrīvāya namaḥ ||
samiccarvājyakānhutvā samyakpuruṣasūktataḥ |
sarveṣāmabhayaṃ datvā viraktaḥ pravrajeddharim || 1 ||
śrutvā bhāgavataṃ śuddhamācāryaṃ śaraṇaṃ vrajet |
adhīhi bhagavo brahmetyasmai brūyādguraḥ param || 2 ||
uccārayaṃstristāraṃ dakṣiṇe śravaṇe tathā |
ṛṣicchandodailatāni brūyāttasya kramātsudhīḥ || 3 ||
antaryāmīti gāyatrī paramātmetyanukramāt |
viśvaśca taijasaḥ prājñasturyaścākṣaradevatāḥ || 4 ||
kṛṣṇo rāmo nṛsiṃhaśca varāho viṣṇareva ca |
parañjyotiḥ paraṃbrahma vāsudeva iti kramāt || 5 ||
akārādestathā śānatātiśāntāntasya devatāḥ |
evamuktvā tu taddhyānaṃ brūyācchiṣyāya sadguruḥ || 6 ||
aṣṭapatre tu hṛtpadmamadhye sūryenduvahnigam |
pīṭhaṃ tanmadhyapagadmasthaṃ nārāyaṇamanāmayam || 7 ||
udyadādityasaṅkāśaṃ tejasā'nupamaṃ sadā |
sahasreṇāpi sūryāṇāṃ sañjñānānāndarūpiṇam || 8 ||
atiraktatalaṃ bhāsvannakharājivirājitam |
vṛttajaṅghaṃ vṛttajānuṃ hastihastorumūśvaram || 9 ||
mahākaṭitaṭābaddhakāñjīpītāmbarojjvalam |
nimnanābhiṃ sutrivalaṃ suvṛttodarabandhanam || 10 ||
viśālavakṣasaṃ bhrājatkaustubhāmuktakandharam |
vanamālādharaṃ hāravaijayaṃtyādibhiryutam || 11 ||
pṛthudīrghacaturbāhuṃ śaṅkhacakragādāmbujaiḥ |
yuktamunnidrapadmākṣaṃ sphuranmakarakuṇḍalam || 12 ||
pūrṇacandrāyutodrikāntimanmukhapaṅkajam |
subhruvaṃ sulalāṭāntaṃ kirīṭābaddhamūrdhajam || 13 ||
niḥśeṣaduḥkhaśamanaṃ nityānandaśucismitam |
viśvādīṃścaiva kṛṣṇādīnevaṃbhūtānsanātanān || 14 ||
abhinnāneva satataṃ tasmādviṣṇoḥ parātmanaḥ |
varābhayodyatakarānnityānandaikarūpiṇaḥ || 15 ||
evamaktvā gururdhyānaṃ śapathaṃ kārayettataḥ |
na viṣṇu vaiṣṇavāṃścaivāpyutsṛjeyamiti triśaḥ || 16 ||
na cānyadevatāsāmyaṃ tadaikyamathavā hareḥ |
cintayeyaṃ mṛto vā'pi na cāpyekatvavādibhiḥ || 17 ||
samatvavādibhirvā'haṃ saṅgaccheyaṃ kathañcana |
tannindakaiśca tadbaktanindakairvā mahāmune || 18 ||
evaṃ kṛte tu śapathe mastake hastapaṅkajam |
nidhāyottīrya saṃsārātsukhī bhavaḥ hareḥ priyaḥ || 19 ||
sarvaduḥkhādibhirmukto nityānandaikarūpiṇaḥ |
samprāpya viṣṇusāmīpyaṃ tatrāpi haribhaktimān || 20 ||
baktimāṃścānyadeveṣu tāratamyaṃ ca sarvadā |
sarvotkarṣaṃ smaranviṣṇorbūyāścaiva sadā sukhī || 21 ||
na muktau viṣṇunaikyaṃ vā muktānāṃ sāmyameva vā |
smarethā iti coktvā tu samayānanuśikṣayet || 22 ||
nityaśaśca hareḥ pūjā japadhyānasamarpaṇam |
kartavyaṃ tu tvayā vatsa japaśca trisahasrakaḥ || 23 ||
madhyamaḥ praṇavasyokto yo'varaḥ sa sahasrakaḥ |
trisahasrātparo yastu sa uttamajapaḥ smṛtaḥ || 24 ||
ātmānaṃ pratibimbatve dhyāyanbimbaṃ janārdanam |
dhyāyaṃśca satataṃ vatsa saparyā nityaśaḥ kuru || 25 ||
mānasairvātha puṣpairvā praṇavena samāhitaḥ |
anyāṃśca vaiṣṇavānmantrāñjapethā bhaktipūrvakam || 26 ||
śṛṇuṣva vaiṣṇavaṃ śāstraṃ sadā vedārthatatparaḥ |
vedānmantrānupaniṣatsahitānvasarvadā śṛṇu || 27 ||
itihāsapurāṇe ca pañcarātraṃ tathaiva ca |
tadarthānbrahmasūtraiśca samyaṅnirṇīya tattvataḥ |
viṣṇoḥ sarvottamattvaṃ tu sarvadā pratipādaya || 28 ||
|| iti śrīmadānandatīrthabhagavatpādācāryaviracitaḥ praṇavakalpaḥ samāptaḥ ||
|| iti praṇavakalpaḥ ||
|| śrīkṛṣṇārpaṇamastu ||
