Text:

saccidānandakandāya jagadaṅkurahetave |
sadoditāya pūrṇāya namo'nantāya viṣṇave || 1||

sarvavedāntasiddhāntairgrathitaṃ nirmalaṃ śivam |
sadācāraṃ pravakṣyāmi yogināṃ jñānasiddhaye || 2||

prātaḥsmarāmi devasya saviturbharga ātmanaḥ |
vareṇyaṃ taddhiyo yo naścidānande pracodayāt || 3||

anvayavyatirekābhyāṃ jāgratsvapnasuṣuptiṣu |
yadekaṃ kevalaṃ jñānaṃ tadevāsmi paraṃ bṛhat || 4||

jñānājñānavilāso'yaṃ jñānājjñāne ca śāmyati |
jñānājñāne parityajya jñānamevāvaśiṣyate || 5||

atyantamalino deho dehī cātyantanirmalaḥ |
asaṅgo'hamiti jñātvā śaucametatpracakṣate || 6||

manmano mīnavannityaṃ krīḍatyānandavāridhau |
susnātastena pūtātmā samyagvijñānavāriṇā || 7||

athāghamarṣaṇaṃ kuryātprāṇāpānanirodhataḥ |
manaḥ pūrṇe samādhāya magnakumbho yathārṇave || 8||

layavikṣepayoḥ sandhau manastatra nirāmiṣam |
sa sandhiḥ sādhito yena sa mukto nātra saṃśayaḥ || 9||

sarvatra prāṇināṃ dehe japo bhavati sarvadā |
haṃsaḥ so'hamiti jñātvā sarvabandhairvimucyate || 10||

tarpaṇaṃ svasukhenaiva svendriyāṇāṃ pratarpaṇam |
manasā mana ālokya svayamātmā prakāśate || 11||

ātmani svaprakāśāgnau cittamekāhutiṃ kṣipet |
agnihotrī sa vijñeyaścetarā nāmadhārakāḥ || 12||

deho devālayaḥ prokto dehī devo nirañjanaḥ |
arcitaḥ sarvabhāvena svānubhūtyā virājate || 13||

maunaṃ svādhyayanaṃ dhyānaṃ dhyeyaṃ brahmānucintanam |
jñāneneti tayoḥ samyaṅniṣedhāttattvadarśanam || 14||

atītānāgataṃ kiñcinna smarāmi na cintaye |
rāgadveṣaṃ vinā prāptaṃ bhuñjāmyatra śubhāśubham || 15||

dehābhyāso hi saṃnyāso naiva kāṣāyavāsasā |
nāhaṃ deho'hamātmeti niścayo nyāsalakṣaṇam || 16||

abhayaṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ dānamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ |
nijānande spṛhā nānye vairāgyasyāvadhirmatā || 17||

vedāntaśravaṇaṃ kuryānmananaṃ copapattibhiḥ |
yogenābhyasanaṃ nityaṃ tato darśanamātmanaḥ || 18||

śabdaśakteracintyatvācchabdādevāparokṣadhīḥ |
prasuptaḥ puruṣo yadvacchabdenaivāvabuddhyate || 19||

ātmānātmavivekena jñānaṃ bhavati niścalam |
guruṇā bodhitaḥ śiṣyaḥ śabdabrahmātivartate || 20||

na tvaṃ deho nendriyāṇi na prāṇo na mano na dhīḥ |
vikāritvādvināśitvāddṛśyatvācca ghaṭo yathā || 21||

viśuddhaṃ kevalaṃ jñānaṃ nirviśeṣaṃ nirañjanam |
yadekaṃ paramānandaṃ tattvamasyadvayaṃ param || 22||

śabdasyādyantayoḥ siddhaṃ manaso'pi tathaiva ca |
madhye sākṣitayā nityaṃ tadeva tvaṃ bhramaṃ jahi || 23||

sthūlavairājayoraikyaṃ sūkṣmahairaṇyagarbhayoḥ |
ajñānamāyayoraikyaṃ pratyagvijñānapūrṇayoḥ || 24||

cinmātraikarase viṣṇau brahmātmaikyasvarūpake |
bhrameṇaiva jagajjātaṃ rajjvāṃ sarpabhramo yathā || 25||

tārkikāṇāṃ tu jīveśau vācyāvetau vidurbudhāḥ |
lakṣyau ca sāṅkhyayogābhyāṃ vedāntairaikyatā tayoḥ || 26||

kāryakāraṇavācyāṃśau jīveśau yau jahacca tau |
ajahacca tayorlakṣyau cidaṃśāvekarūpiṇau || 27||

karmaśāstre kuto jñānaṃ tarke naivāsti niścayaḥ |
sāṅkhyayogau bhidāpannau śābdikāḥ śabdatatparāḥ || 28||

anye pāṣaṇḍinaḥ sarve jñānavārtāsudurlabhāḥ |
ekaṃ vedāntavijñānaṃ svānubhūtyā virājate || 29||

ahaṃ mametyayaṃ bandho mamāhaṃ neti muktatā |
bandhamokṣau guṇairbhāti guṇāḥ prakṛtisambhavāḥ || 30||

jñānamekaṃ sadā bhāti sarvāvasthāsu nirmalam |
mandabhāgyā na jānanti svarūpaṃ kevalaṃ bṛhat || 31||

saṅkalpasākṣi yajjñānaṃ sarvalokaikajīvanam |
tadevāsmīti yo veda sa mukto nātra saṃśayaḥ || 32||

pramātā ca pramāṇaṃ ca prameyaṃ pramitistathā |
yasya bhāsāvabhāsante mānaṃ jñānāya tasya kim || 33||

arthākārā bhavedvṛttiḥ phalenārthaḥ prakāśate |
arthajñānaṃ vijānāti sa evārthaḥ prakāśate || 34||

vṛttivyāpyatvamevāstu phalavyāptiḥ kathaṃ bhavet |
svaprakāśasvarūpatvātsiddhatvācca cidātmanaḥ || 35||

cittaṃ caitanyamātreṇa saṃyogāccetanā bhavet |
arthādarthāntare vṛttirgantuṃ calati cāntare || 36||

nirādhārā nirvikārā yā daśā sonmanī smṛtā |
cittaṃ ciditi jānīyāttakārarahitaṃ yadā || 37||

takāro viṣayādhyāso japārāgo yathā maṇau |
jñeyavastu parityāgājjñānaṃ tiṣṭhati kevalam || 38||

tripuṭī kṣīṇatāmeti brahmanirvāṇamṛcchati |
manomātramidaṃ sarvaṃ tanmano jñānamātrakam || 39||

ajñānaṃ bhrama ityāhurvijñānaṃ paramaṃ padam |
ajñānaṃ cānyathājñānaṃ māyāmetāṃ vadanti te || 40||

īśvaraṃ māyinaṃ vidyānmāyātītaṃ nirañjanam |
sadānande cidākāśe māyāmeghastaṭinmanaḥ || 41||

ahantā garjanaṃ tatra dhārāsārā hi vṛttayaḥ |
mahāmohāndhakāre'smindevo varṣati līlayā || 42||

tasyā vṛṣṭervirāmāya prabodhaikasamīraṇaḥ |
jñānaṃ dṛgdṛśyayorbhāvaṃ vijñānaṃ dṛśyaśūnyatā || 43||

ekamevādvayaṃ brahma neha nānāsti kiñcana |
kṣetrakṣetrajñayorjñānaṃ tajjñānaṃ jñānamucyate || 44||

vijñānaṃ cobhayoraikyaṃ kṣetrajñaparamātmanoḥ |
parokṣaṃ śāstrajaṃ jñānaṃ vijñānaṃ cātmadarśanam || 45||

ātmano brahmaṇaḥ samyagupādhidvayavarjitam |
tvamarthaviṣayaṃ jñānaṃ vijñānaṃ tatpadāśrayam || 46||

padayoraikyabodhastu jñānavijñānasaṃjñitam |
ātmānātmavivekasya jñānamāhurmanīṣiṇaḥ || 47||

ajñānaṃ cānyatā loke vijñānaṃ tanmayaṃ jagat |
anvayavyatirekābhyāṃ sarvatraikaṃ prapaśyati || 48||

yattattu vṛttijaṃ jñānaṃ vijñānaṃ jñānamātrakam |
ajñānadhvaṃsakaṃ jñānaṃ vijñānaṃ cobhayātmakam || 49||

jñānavijñānaniṣṭho'yaṃ tatsadbrahmaṇi cārpaṇam |
bhoktā sattvaguṇaḥ śuddho bhogānāṃ sādhanaṃ rajaḥ || 50||

bhogyaṃ tamoguṇaḥ prāhurātmā caiṣāṃ prakāśakaḥ |
brahmādhyayanasaṃyukto brahmacaryārataḥ sadā || 51||

sarvaṃ brahmeti yo veda brahmacārī sa ucyate |
gṛhastho guṇamadhyasthaḥ śarīraṃ gṛhamucyate || 52||

guṇāḥ kurvanti karmāṇi nāhaṃ karteti buddhimān |
kimugraiśca tapobhiśca yasya jñānamayaṃ tapaḥ || 53||

harṣāmarṣavinirmukto vānaprasthaḥ sa ucyate |
sa gṛhī yo gṛhātītaḥ śarīraṃ gṛhamucyate || 54||

sadācāramimaṃ nityaṃ yo'nusaṃdadhate budhaḥ |
saṃsārasāgarācchīghraṃ sa mukto nātra saṃśayaḥ || 55||

iti śrīmatparamahaṃsaparivrājakācāryasya śrīgovindabhagavatpūjyapādaśiṣyasya śrīmacchaṅkarabhagavataḥ kṛtau sadācārānusandhānaṃ sampūrṇam ||
