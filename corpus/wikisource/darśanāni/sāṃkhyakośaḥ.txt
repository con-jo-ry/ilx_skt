nityasaṃbandhābhāvaḥ - 
kaścinnityaḥ sambandho nāsti| yato hi sa sambandhaḥ saṃyogo vā bhavet samavāyo vā| nādyā - saṃyogasya karmajanyatayā nityeṣu vyāpakeṣu ca kriyāyā abhāvena saṃyogāsaṃbhavāt| anityānāṃ saṃyogasattve'pi tasya saṃyogasyāpyanityatvāt|
nanu saṃyogo mābhūta nityaḥ sambandhaḥ, samavāyastu nityaḥ sambandho bhavet iti cenna, samavāyaḥ svayamasambaddhaścet sambandhinoḥ parasparaṃ sambaddhatvāṃsaṃbhavāḥ| sambaddhaścet kena sambandhena samavāyaḥ sambaddhaḥ? sambandhāntarasyāsaṃbhavāt samavāyenaiva sambaddho vaktavyaḥ| tathā sati tasyāpyanyaḥ tasyāpyanyaḥ samavāya ityanavasthāprasaṅga iti samavāyasyāpyasiddheḥ| avasthādoṣaparihārāya samavāyasya sambaddhatvaṃ (vaiśiṣṭyam) yadi svarūpeṇeṣyeta tadā guṇaprabhṛtīnāṃ vaiśiṣṭyabuddhirapi svarūpeṇaiveṣyatām, kṛtaṃ samavāyeneti| na caivaṃ saṃyogo'pi sambandho na sidhyet, bhūtale ghaṭavattāpratīteḥ svarūpeṇaivopapatteriti vācyam viyogakāle'pi kuṇḍabadarayoḥ svarūpasattvena viśiṣṭabuddhiprasaṅgāt| samavāyasthale ca samavetasya kadāpi svāśrayaviyogo nāstīti tatra svarūpasambandhasvīkāre nāyaṃ doṣaḥ| tasmāt samavāyaḥ sambandha eva nāstīti sāṃkhyāmatam|

pramā - 
indriyādipraṇālikayā sākṣādvā yā ajñātārthaviṣayiṇī cittavṛttiḥ, tajjanyaḥ pauruṣeyo bodhaḥ pramā| tathā ca sāṃkhyasūtram - dvayorekatarasya vā'pyasannikṛṣṭārthaparicchittiḥ pramā| atra asannikṛṣṭaḥ pramātaryanārūḍho'nadhigata iti yāvat| evambhūtasyārthasya vastunaḥca paricchittiravadhāraṇaṃ pramā|| atra vṛttikāraḥ - sūtre dvayoriti padasya indriyārthayorvidyamānayorityarthaḥ pratyakṣābhiprāyeṇa| tathā ekatarasyeti padasya liṅgasya śabdasya vā vidyamānasyetyartho'numānābhiprāyeṇa śabdābhiprāyeṇa cetyāha| vācaspatimiśrā api `pramīyate'neneti nirvacanāt pramāṃ prati karaṇatvamavagamyate| taccāsaṃdigdhāviparītānadhigataviṣayā cittavṛttiḥ| bodhaśca pauruyeyaḥ phalaṃ pramā' ityāhuḥca |
pramāṇam - 
pauruṣeyabodharūpāṃ pramāṃ prati yat sādhakatamaṃ tat pramāṇam| draṣṭavyam dvyorekatarasya vāpyasaṃnikṛṣṭārthaparicchittiḥ pramā, tatsādhakatamaṃ yat tat travidhaṃ pramāṇam| bodhaśca pauruṣeyaḥ phalaṃ pramā, tatsādhanaṃ pramāṇamiti sāṃkhyatattvakaumudī| asmin pakṣe pauruṣeyo bodhaḥ pramā, tatkāraṇabhūtā cittavṛttiḥ pramāṇam| yadā tu arthākārā cittavakṛttireva pramāpadena vyapadiśyate tadā tatkāraṇabhūtāḥca indriyasaṃnikarṣādaya eva pramāṇatayā vyavahriyanta iti mantavyam|
prameyam - 
pramāviṣayībhūtā ye te prameyapadenocyante| sāṃkhyābhimataṃ prameyam - pradhānaṃ, buddhiḥ, ahaṅkāraḥ, pañcatanmātrāṇi, ekādaśendriyāṇi, pañcamahābhūtāni, puruṣa iti|ka etāni pañcaviṃśatitattvāni vyaktāvyaktajñā ityucyante| paramātmāpi ṣaḍviṃśaḥ prameyabhūtaḥ padārthaḥ puruṣa evāntarbhūta iti vijñānabhikṣuḥ|
pratyakṣam - 
`yat sambaddhaṃ sat tadākārollekhi vijñānaṃ tat pratyakṣam' iti sāṃkhyasūtram sambaddhaṃ bhavat sambaddhavastvākāradhāri bhavati yad vijñānaṃ buddhivṛttistat pratyakṣaṃ pramāṇamiti niṣkarṣaṃ iti sāṃkhyapravacanabhāṣyam| prativiṣayādhyavasāyo dṛṣṭam (pratyakṣam) iti sāṃkhyakārikā| atra gauḍapādabhāṣyam - prativiṣayeṣu śrotrādīnāṃ śabdādiviṣayeṣu adhyavasāyo dṛṣṭaṃ partyakṣamityartha iti| vācaspatimiśrāstu - viṣayaṃ viṣayaṃ prati vartata iti prativiṣayam indriyam| vṛttiśca sannikarṣaḥ arthasannikṛṣṭamityathaḥ| tasmin adhyavasāya tadāśrita ityarthaḥ| adhvavasāyaśca buddhivyāpāro jñānam| upāttaviṣayāṇāmindriyāṇāṃ vṛttau (sannikarṣe) satyāṃ tamo'bhibhave sati yaḥ sattvasamudrekaḥ, so'dhyavasāya iti vṛttiriti jñānamiti cākhyāyate| idaṃ tāvat pratyakṣaṃ pramāṇamiti|| idañca pratyakṣalakṣaṇaṃ bāhyapartyakṣābhiprāyeṇa| yogināntu pratyakṣamabāhyam ato na tallakṣyaṃ na vā tatrendriyasannikarṣāpekṣā| athavendriyasannirṣābhāve'pi yogajadharmajanyātiśayasāhāyyenaiva yogibuddhirarthaiḥ sannikṛṣyate, arthākāratāñca dhārayatīti sannikarṣadvārā'rthākāratāgrahaṇarūpaṃ sūtroktaṃ pratyakṣasāmānyalakṣaṇaṃ yogipratyakṣe'pi ghaṭata eva| indriyasannikarṣāpekṣā tu cākṣuṣādiviśeṣapratyakṣa eva bhavatīti| sarvathaiva sannikarṣānapekṣe nitye īśvarapratyakṣe uktalakṣaṇasyāvyāptiriti na śaṅkanīyam īśvare pramāṇābhāvāt| idamuttaramekadeśināmabhiprāyeṇeti vijñānabhikṣavaḥ| tadrītyā īśvarapratyakṣasādhāraṇaṃ lakṣaṇantu sannikarṣajanyajñānasajātīyatvameva sūtrakārairvivakṣitam|

śabdārthasambandhānityatvam -
śabdārthayoḥ sambandho'nitya iti sāṃkhyamatam| na tu mīmāṃsakavat nityaḥ śabdārthasambandha iti| tathā ca sāṃkhyasūtram `na sambandhanityatobhayānityatvāta|' ubhayoḥ śabdārthayoranityatvāt tatsambandhasyāpyanityatvamiti bhāvaḥ|
śarīrabhedāḥ - uṣmajam aṇḍajaṃ jarāyujam udbhijjaṃ saṃkalpajaṃ sāṃsiddhikañceti śarīrasya ṣaḍbhedāḥ| tatroṣmajā dandaśūkādayaḥ, aṇḍajāḥ pakṣisarpādayaḥ, jarāyujā manuṣyādayaḥ, udbhijjā vṛkṣādayaḥ, saṃkalpajāḥ sanakādayaḥ sāsiddhikā mantratapaādisiddhijāḥ raktabījaśarīrotpannadehāḥ| tathā ca sāṃkhyasūtram `uṣmajāṇḍajajarāyujodbhijjasāṃkalpikasāṃsaddhikaṃ ceti na niyamaḥ'  draṣṭavyañcātratyaṃ bhāṣyam|
śābdabodhaḥ - śabdapramāpajanyā pramitiḥ pauruṣeyo bodhaḥ śābdabodhaḥ|
śukavat - yathā śukapakṣī vyādhasya guṇaṃ rajjubhirbaddho bhavati, svīyaireva vā manojñarūpamadhuraśabdarūpairguṇairnimittairbandhanaṃ prāpnoti, tathaiva vivekijano'pi rāgijanasaṅgaṃ prāpya tadguṇai rāgadvaiṣādibhirbaddho bhavati| ato rāgijanasaṅgo na kartavyaḥ| 
śeṣavat - vyatirekānumānaṃ śeṣavat| śeṣo'pūrvo'rtho'sya viṣayatvenāstīti śeṣavat, aprasiddhasādhyakamiti yāvat| yathā, pṛtivītvena itarabhedānumānam| pṛtivītarabhedo hi prāgaprasiddhaḥ 
śyenavat - yathā hi sāmiṣaḥ śyenaḥ kenāpyapahatya āmiṣād viyojya duḥśī kriyate, svayaṃ cet tyajati tadā duḥkhād vimucyate| tathaiva dravyāṇāṃ svayaṃ tyāgena lokaḥ sukhī bhavati, paradvārā viyogena ca duḥśī bhavati| ataḥ parigraho na kartavyaḥ
ṣaṭpadavat - puṣpebhyo yathā ṣaṭpadaḥ sāramevādatte tathaiva śāstragurubhyaḥ sārameva gṛhlīyāt| 
satkāryavādaḥ - kāraṇavyāpārādūrdhvamiva kāraṇavyāpārāt pūrvamapi kāryaṃ sadeva bhavati na tu vaiśeṣikādivat asat iti sāṃkhyasiddhāntaḥ| yathā tileṣvavasthitasyaiva tailasyāvirbhāvo bhavati, tadvat|
satkhyātiḥ - `atyantasataḥ khyātiḥ satkhyātiḥ ityapi na sāṃkhyābhimatam| yadi guṇādayo'tyantasanto bhāseran tadā vināśakāle teṣāṃ bādho na dṛśyeta|
sadsatkhyātiḥ - sadasatkhyātireva sarvaiṣāṃ guṇādīnāmiti sāṃkhyasiddhāntaḥ| kutaḥ? bādhābādhabhāvāt| tatra svarūpeṇābādhaḥ sarvastūnāṃ, nityatvāt| saṃsargatastu bādhaḥ sarvavastūnāṃ caitanye'sti| yathā lauhityaṃ svarūpataḥ kvacidasti kintu saṃsargatastu tat svetapaṭe nāsti|
na ca sattvāsattvayorvirodha iti śaṅkanīyam, prakārabhedenāvirodhāt| yathā hi lauhityaṃ bimbarūpeṇa sat sphaṭakagatapratibimbarūpeṇa ca asat iti dṛṣṭam| yathā vā rajataṃ vaṇigvīthistharūpeṇa sat śuktyadhyastarūpeṇa cāsat| tathaiva sarvaṃ jagat svarūpataḥ sat, caitanyādāvadhyastasvarūpeṇa cāsaditi| evamavasthābhedenāpi sadasattvamaviruddham| yathāhi vṛkṣādiḥ prarūḍhādyavasthābhiḥ sannapi aṅkurādyavasthābhirasan bhavati, tathaiva prakṛtyādikaṃ sadasadātmakamiti| taduktam -
avyaktaṃ kāraṇaṃ yat tannityaṃ sadasadātmakam|
pradhānaṃ prakṛtiśceti yadāhustattvacintakāḥ || iti|
sambandhaḥ - vyāptiḥ| tathā ca sāṃkhyasūtramīśvaraprakaraṇe - sambandhābhāvannānumānam
sarpavat - yathā sarpaḥ svayaṃ gṛhaṃ na nirmāti, paragṛhe eva sukhī śete, tathaiva svabhogārthamārambho naivakartavyo yogapratibandhakatvāt| taduktam -
gṛhārambho hi duḥkāya na sukhāya kathañcana|
sarpaḥ parakṛtaṃ veśma praviśya sukhamedhate || iti|
sarvaiśvaryam - asaṃya sarvaiśvaryavāda ekeśvaravādapratirodhī sāṃkhyābhimataḥ| puruṣāṇāṃ citāmānantyāt svatonityamuktatvācca, akhilabhoktṛsaṃyogādeva pradhānena mahadādisarjanācceti| 
sākṣī - pramātā, mākṣāddraṣṭā| yasmai pradarśyate viṣayaḥ sa sākṣī| prakṛtirapi svaviṣayaṃ puruṣāya darśayatīti puruṣasākṣī| sāṃkhyasūtramapi yathā `sākṣāt sambandhāt sākṣitvamiti | ataḥ sākṣātsambandhāddhatoḥ puruṣo' buddhereva sākṣī, anyeṣāṃ tu viṣayāṇāṃ draṣṭṛmātramastīti|
sādṛśyam - sādṛśyaṃ na tattvāntaram, na vā vastunaḥ svābhāvikaśaktiviśeṣaḥ, kintu bhūyo'vayavasāmānyayogo hi sādṛśyam|
saṃjñāsaṃjñisambandhajñānahetuḥ sādṛśyamiti kecid vadanti| tadapi vyabhicārādupekṣaṇīyameva| sādṛśyābhāve'pi upadeśādeḥ saṃjñāsaṃjñisambavandhasya niścayāt| 
sāmānyam - asti tāvadekākārabuddhihetuḥ sāmānyam kintu anityamiti sāṃkhyamatam tacca sāmānyaṃ nānyāpoharūpam, ghaṭo'yamiti bhāvatvena pratīteḥ| anyathā ` ayaṃ ghaṭa' iti sthāne `nāyamaghaṭa' ityeva 'tīyeta| kiñca `aghaṭavyāvṛtti' rityatra aghaṭatvaṃ ghaṭasāmānyabhinnatvamiti sāmānyābhyupagama evāpatito'pohavādināṃ śirasi|
sāmānyatodṛṣṭam - pūrvavaccheṣavadumayabhinnamanumānaṃ sāmānyatodṛṣṭam| yathā rūpādijñāne kriyātvena hetunā karaṇavatvānumānam| 
adṛṣṭhasvalakṣaṇasāmānyaviṣayamanumānaṃ sāmānyatodṛṣṭamanumānamiti vācaspatimiśrāḥ| 
sāmyāvasthā - guṇānāmakāryāvasthā| iyameva svarūpapariṇāma ityucyate|
sāṃsiddhikam - svābhāvikam|
siddhiḥ - aṣṭaprakārā bhavati| tathā coktaṃ sāṃkhyakārikāyām - ūhaḥ śabdo'dhyāyanaṃ duḥkavighātāstrayaḥ suhṛtprāptiḥ| dānaṃ ca siddhayo'ṣṭau siddheḥ pūrvo'ṅkuśastrividhaḥ || iti| atra vihanyamānamya duḥkhasya tritvāt tadvighātāstraya itīmāḥ mukhyāstisraḥ siddhayaḥ| itarāstu tadupāyatayā gauṇyaḥ pañca siddhayaḥ|
tatrohastarkaḥ - āgamāvirodhanyāyena āgamārthaparīkṣaṇam| idameva mananamityācakṣate āgaminaḥ| iyaṃ siddhiḥ tāratāramityucyate| śabdaḥ arthāt śabdajanitamarthajñānaṃ dvitīyā siddhiḥ sutāramityucyate|
evaṃ vidhivad gurumukhādadhyātmavidyānāmakṣarasvarūpagrahaṇamadhyayanasaṃjñikā siddhiḥ tāramityucyate|
nyāyena svayaṃ parīkṣitamapyarthaṃ na śraddhatte yāvad guruśiṣyasahapāṭhibhiḥ saha na saṃvādyate| ataḥ suhṛdāṃ guruśiṣyasahapāṭhināṃ saṃvādakānāṃ prāptiḥ suhṛtpraptiḥ sā siddhiścaturthī ramyakamityucyate| dānaṃ ca śuddhirvivekajñānasya| śuddhiśca savāsanasaṃśayaviparyāsānāṃ rihāreṇa vivekasākṣātkārasya svacchapravāhe'vasthāpanam| atra vivekajñānarūpakāryeṇa tatkāraṇībhūto dīrghakālādaranairatnaryasevitābhyāsaparipāko'pi dānapadena saṃgṛhīto bhavati| seyaṃ pañcamī siddhiḥ sadāmuditamucyate|
pūrvoktāḥ tisro mukhyāḥ siddhayaḥ pramodamuditamodamānasaṃjñādibhiḥ paribhāṣyante| ityaṣṭau siddhayaḥ|| eṣāṃ pāribhāṣikaśabdānāṃ vyākhyānāntarāṇyapi vilokanīyāni sāṃkhyapravacanabhāṣye yuktidīpikādau ceti tata eva vistaro'nusandheyaḥ|
sūkṣmabhūtāni - sthūlabhūtātiriktāni sūkṣmabhūtānyapi bhavantīti vṛttikārāḥ| etānyeva pārthivādiparamāṇavaḥ| tatra `ādyahetutā taddvārā pāramparyeṇāṇuvat|' iti sūtramapi pramāṇam| gauḍapādācāryeṇāpi sāṃkhyakārikābhāṣye siddhānto'yamabhyupagataḥ taduktam - pañcabhyaḥ paramāṇubhyaḥ pañcamahābhūtānyutpadyanta iti| 
vijñānabhikṣustu pañcatanmātrāṇyeva sūkṣmabhūtānītyāha| taduktam - sthūlaśabdāt tanmātrāṇāṃ sūkṣmabhūtatvamabhyupagatam iti| 
sūkṣmaśarīram - aṣṭādaśamistattvaiḥ sūkṣmaśarīramārabhyate| idameva liṅgaśarīram| draṣṭavyo liṅgaśarīraśabdavicāraḥ|
sūdavat - yathā pāke niṣpanne pācakasya vyāpāro nivartate tathaiva puruṣārthasamāptau pradhānasya sṛṣṭarnivartata iti|
sṛṣṭiḥ - rāganimittā sṛṣṭiḥ vairāgyanimittā ca muktiḥ|
sṛṣṭiprayojanam - ātmā svabhāvavimuktaḥ, tasyābhamamānikabandhavimokṣārtha pradhānasya jagatkartṛtvam bandhavimokṣaśca heyasyaikaviṃśatiprakārasya duḥkhasya hānam| eviṃśatiprakāraṃ duḥkhañca - śarīraṃ, ṣaḍindriyāṇi, ṣaḍviṣayāḥ, ṣaḍbuddhayaḥ, sukhaṃ, duḥkhañceti| tatra śarīraṃ duḥkhāyatanatvād duḥkam| indriyāṇi, viṣayā, buddhayaśca duḥśasādhanabhāvāt| sukhamapi duḥkhānuṣaṅgāt duḥkham| duḥkhaṃ tu yātanāpīḍāsantāpātmakaṃ mukhyata eva duḥkhamiti| duḥkhahānañcātyantikī duḥkhotpattinivṛttiḥ| tasyopāya ātmaviṣayakaṃ tattvajñānam| tathā ca śrutiḥ - `ātmā vā are draṣṭava......tarati śokamātmavit' ityādiḥ| tasyātmano vivekajñānāya prakṛteḥ pravṛttiriti parārthā pradhānasya sṛṣṭipravṛttiḥ| svārthā ca pradhānasya jagatsṛṣṭiḥ| tathāhi, yaṃ puruṣaṃ pratyātmānaṃ vivekena darśitavatī taṃ pratyudāste prakṛtiriti|
sṛṣṭipralayau - sāmyāt prakṛteḥ sadṛśapariṇāmāt pralayaḥ| tathā vaiṣamyātprakṛtermahadādibhāvena visadṛsapariṇāmāt sṛṣṭiḥ tathā ca sāṃkhyasūtram - sāmyavaiṣamyābhyāṃ kāryadvayam
sṛṣṭau mahadādīnāṃ svārthābhāvaḥ - sṛṣṭirhi puruṣārthā bhavati ato mahadādayo na svārthāya sṛṣṭimārabhante| prakṛtestu nityatvāt tasyāḥ svārthāyārabhyo yujyate| mahādādīnāntu anityatvāt kāraṇalayena nāśāt sṛṣṭimātraṃ na tu kaścit svārthaḥ |
saṃskāraikyam - yena saṃskāreṇa mānavādiśarīrabhoga ārabdhaḥ, sa eka eva saṃskāraḥ taccharīrasādhyasya samagraprārabdhabhogasya samāpakaḥ| sa ca bhogasamāptināśyaḥ, na tu pratikriyam pratibhogavyakti saṃskāranānātvam, kalpanāgauravaprasaṅgāt| kulālacakrabhramaṇasthale'pi eka eva vegākhyaḥ saṃskāro bhramaṇasamāptiparyantasthāyī bodhyo na tu vegāt saṃskāraḥ, saṃskārācca punarvega iti saṃskāranānātvamastīti| 
sthūlaśarīram -
mātāpitṛbhyāṃ samutpannaṃ ṣāṭkauśikam| tatra mātṛto lomalohitamāṃsāni, pitṛtaśca snāyvasthimajjāna iti ṣaṭkośāḥ| gauḍapādāstu pṛṣṭodarajaṅghākaṭyuraḥ śiraḥ prabhṛtiṣaṭkośāḥ| draṣṭavyamuktakārikāyā bhāṣyam|
sphoṭaḥ - pratyekavarṇabhyo'tiriktaṃ `kalaśa' ityākārakamakhaṇḍamekapadaṃ sphoṭa iti vaiyākaraṇairyogaiścābhyupagamyate| yathā kambugrīvādyavayavebhyo'tirikto ghaṭādyavayavī partīyate sa ca śabdaviśeṣaḥ padākhyo'rthasphuṭīkaraṇāt `sphoṭa' ityucyate|
sa ca sphoṭātmakaḥ śabdo na sāṃkhyairabhyipagamyate| yato hi sa sphoṭaḥ pratīyamānaḥ san arthaṃ pratyāyayāti utāpratīyamānaḥ san? ādye sā sphoṭapratītinaṃ śrāvaṇapratyakṣarūpā, ananubhavāt, kintu abhivyaktirūpaiva svīkāryā| evañcayena varṇasamudāyenānupūrvīviśeṣaviśiṣṭena sphoṭo'bhivyajyate, tasyaivārthapartyāyakatvamastu, kimantargaḍunā sphoṭena? dvitīye tu ajñānatasphoṭasya nāstyarthapratyāyanaśaktiriti vyarthā sphoṭakalpaneti | 
svakarma - svāśramavihikarmānuṣṭhānam|
svataḥ prāmāṇyam - vedānāṃ svābhāvikī yā yathārthajñānajananaśaktiḥ, tasyā mantrāyurvedādāvupalambhāt samastavedānāṃ svata eva prāmāṇyaṃ na tu vaktṛyathārthajñānādhīnam | 
aniruddho'pi āha - nijaśaktijñānajanakasāmagrīmātrādhīnaṃ prāmāṇyaṃ na tu adhikaṃ guṇamapekṣate naiyāyikavat| idamutpattipakṣe| jñaptipakṣe'pi - autsargikī jñānagrāhakāṇāṃ prāmāṇyabodhaśaktiḥ| aprāmāṇyantu parataḥ, tatra doṣasyāpi kāraṇatvāt 
svarūpaṃ na śaktiḥ - yadi svarūpaṃ śaktistadā `śakto malla' ityuktauṃ punaruktadoṣaḥ syāt devadatto devadatta itivat| tathā śakto mantra iti viśeṣaṇamanarthakaṃ ca syāt| tathā ca nijā cāsau śaktiśceti nijaśaktiḥ na tu svarūpameva śaktiriti siddhāntaḥ|
svālakṣaṇyam - svaṃ svaṃ lakṣaṇam asādhāraṇīvṛttiḥ adhyavasāyābhimānasaṅkalparūpā yeṣāṃ tāni svalakṣaṇāni mahadahaṅkāramanāṃsi, teṣāṃ bhāvaḥ svālakṣaṇyam| tadeva svaṃ svaṃ lakṣaṇamadhyavasāyādirūpaṃ mahadahaṅkāramanasāṃ vṛttirastīti| 
haṃsakṣīravat - yathā dugdhajalayorekībhāvamāpannayormadhyo'sārajalatyāgena sārabhūtakṣīropādānaṃ haṃsasyaiva bhavati na tu kākādeḥ| tathaiva viraktasyaiva heyānāṃ prakṛtyādīnāṃ hānam, upādeyasya cātmana upādānaṃ saṃbhavati nāviraktasya| iti śivam|
sāraṇyamaṇḍale puṇye grāme viṣṇupurā'bhidhe|
janiṃstripāṭhino labdhvā rāmāderyo vihāriṇaḥ||
nyāyavyākaraṇe śrīmatsūryanārāyaṇād guroḥ|
vedāntaṃ viśrutācchrumīddhariharakṛpālutaḥ||
śrutvā vādavidagraṇīḥ sa viditaḥ kedāranāthaḥ kṛtī|
sāṃkhyābhīṣṭarahasyamatra prakaṭīkartuṃ samīhābhṛtaḥ||
śūnyāgnyabhrakalocanaiḥ parimite saṃvatsare vaikrame|
māghe māsi site hyapūrayadimaṃ kośaṃ mudā kāpilam ||
iti śrīmadācāryakedāranāthatripāṭhipraṇītaḥ sāṃkhyakośaḥ pūrṇaḥ |
