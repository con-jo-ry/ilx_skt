|| śrīḥ ||
śrīmadānandatīrthabhagavatpādapraṇītasarvamūlagranthāḥ
sadācārasmṛtiḥ|| śrīmaddhanumadbhīmamadhvāntargatarāmakṛṣṇavedavyāsātmakaśrīlakṣmihayagrīvāya namaḥ ||
yasminsarvāṇi karmāṇi saṃnyasyādhyātmacetasā |
nirāśīrnirmamo yāti paraṃ jayati so'cyutaḥ || 1 ||
smṛtvā viṣṇuṃ samutthāya kṛtaśauco yathāvidhi |
dhautadantaḥ samācamya snānaṃ kuryādvidhānataḥ || 2 ||
uddhṛteti mṛdālipya dviṣaḍaṣṭaṣaḍakṣaraiḥ |
trirnimajyāpyasūktena prokṣayitvā punastataḥ || 3 ||
mṛdālipya nimajya tristrirjapedaghamarṣaṇam
sraṣṭāraṃ sarvalokānāṃ smṛtvā nārāyaṇaṃ param || 4 ||
yataśvāso nimagno'psu praṇavenotthitastataḥ |
siñcayetpuruṣasūktena svadehasthaṃ hariṃ smaran || 5 ||
vasitvā vāsa ācamya prokṣyācamya ca mantrataḥ |
gāyatryā cāñjaliṃ datvā dhyātvā sūryagataṃ harim || 6 ||
mantrataḥ parivṛtyātha samācamya surādikān |
tarpayitvā nipīḍyātha vāso vistṛtya cāñjasā || 7 ||
arkamaṇḍalagaṃ viṣṇuṃ dhyātvaiva tripadīṃ japet |
sahasraparamāṃ devīṃ śatamadhyāṃ daśāvarām || 8 ||
āsūryadarśanāttiṣṭhettatastūpaviśettu vā |
pūrvāṃ sandhyāṃ sanakṣatrāmuttarāṃ sadivākarām |
uttarāmupaviśyaiva vāgyataḥ sarvadā japet || 9 ||
dhyeyaḥ sadā savitṛmaṇḍalamadhyavartī nārāyaṇaḥ sarasijāsanasanniviṣṭaḥ |
keyūravānmakarakuṇḍalavānkirīṭī hārī hiraṇmayavapurdhṛtaśaṅkhacakraḥ || 10 ||
gāyatryāstriguṇaṃ viṣṇuṃ dhyāyannaṣṭākṣaraṃ japet |
praṇamya devānviprāṃśca gurūṃśca haripārṣadān || 11 ||
evaṃ sarvottamaṃ viṣṇuṃ dhyāyannevārcayeddharim |
dhyānapravacanābhyāṃ ca yathāyogyamupāsanam || 12 ||
dharmeṇejyāsādhanāni sādhayitvā vidhānataḥ |
snātvā sampūjayedviṣṇuṃ vedatantroktamārgataḥ |
vaiśvadevaṃ baliṃ caiva kuryānnityaṃ tu tarpaṇam || 13 ||
iṣṭaṃ dattaṃ hutaṃ japtaṃ pūrtaṃ yaccātmanaḥ priyam |
dārānsutānpriyānprāṇān parasmai sananivedayen || 14 ||
bhuktaśeṣaṃ bhagavato bhṛtyātithipurassaraḥ |
bhiñjīta hṛdgataṃ viṣṇuṃ smaraṃstadgatamānasaḥ || 15 ||
ācamya mūlamantreṇa koṣṭhaṃ svamabhimantrayet |
vedaśāstravinodena prīṇayanpuruṣottamam || 16 ||
ahaḥ śeṣaṃ nayetsandhyāmupāsītātha pūrvavat |
yāmātparata evātha svapeddhyāyañjanārdanam |
antarāle tato buddhvā smareta bahuśo harim || 17 ||
kāyena vācā manasendriyairvā buddhyātmanā vā prakṛtisvabhāvāt |
karoti yadyatsakalaṃ parasmai nārāyaṇāyeti samarpayettat || 18 ||
`dvāvimau puruṣau loke kṣaraścākṣara eva ca |
kṣaraḥ sarvāṇi bhūtāni kūṭastho'kṣara ucyate || 19 ||
uttamaḥ puruṣastvanyaḥ paramātmetyudāhṛtaḥ |
yo lokatrayamāviśya bibhartyavyaya īśvaraḥ || 20 ||
yasmātkṣaramatīto'hamakṣarādapi cottamaḥ |
ato'smi loke vede ca prathitaḥ puruṣottamaḥ || 21 ||
yo māmevamasammūḍho jānāti puruṣottamam |
sa sarvavidbhajati māṃ sarvabhāvena bhārata || 22 ||
iti guhyatamaṃ śāstramidamuktaṃ mayānagha |
etadbuddhvā buddhimānsyātkṛtakṛtyaśca bhārata' || 23 ||
`rudraṃ samāśritā devā rudro brahmāṇamāśritaḥ |
brahmā māmāśrito nityaṃ nāhaṃ kañcidupāśritaḥ' || 24 ||
`ye me matamidaṃ nityamanutiṣṭhanti mānavāḥ |
śraddhāvanto'nasūyanto mucyante te'pi karmabhiḥ || 25 ||
ye tvetadabhyasūyanto nānutiṣṭhanti me matam |
sarvajñānavimūḍhāstānviddhi naṣṭānacetasaḥ' || 26 ||
`dvau bhūtasargau loke'smindaiva āsura eva ca |
viṣṇubhaktiparo daivo viparītastathā''suraḥ' || 27 ||
`smartavyaḥ satataṃ viṣṇurvismartavyo na jātucit |
sarve vidhiniṣedhāḥ syuretayoreva kiṅkarāḥ' || 28 ||
`dharmo bhavatyadharmo'pi kṛto bhaktaistavācyuta |
pāpaṃ bhavati dharmo'pi yo na bhaktaiḥ kṛto hareḥ' || 29 ||
`manmanā bhava madbhakto madyājī māṃ namaskuru |
nityaṃ bhavecca manniṣṭho bubhūṣuḥ puruśassadā' || 30 ||
eṣa nityaḥ sadācāro gṛhiṇo vaninastathā |
vaiśvadevaṃ baliṃ dantadhāvanaṃ cāpyṛte vaṭoḥ || 31 ||
evameva yateḥ svīyavittena tu vinā sadā |
mūlamantraiḥ sadā snānaṃ viṣṇoreva ca tarpaṇam || 32 ||
viśeṣo niṣkriyayaterajalāñjalitā tathā |
tarpaṇaṃ tu harereva yateranyatra coditam || 33 ||
samiddhomo baṭoścaiva smṛtvā viṣṇuṃ hutāśane |
sarvavarṇāśramairviṣṇureka evejyate sadā |
ramābrahmādayastasya parivārata eva tu || 34 ||
`kaviṃ purāṇamanuśāsitāramaṇoraṇīyāṃsamanusmaredyaḥ |
sarvasya dhātāramacintyarūpamādityavarṇaṃ tamasaḥ parastāt' || 35 ||
`vedāhametaṃ puruṣaṃ mahāntaṃ ādityavarṇaṃ tamasastu pāre |
sarvāṇi rūpāṇi vicintya dhīraḥ nāmāni kṛtvābhivadanyadāste || 36||
dhātā purastādyamudājahāra śakraḥ pravidvānpradiśaścatasraḥ |
tamevaṃ vidvānamṛta iha bhavati nānyaḥ panthā ayanāya vidyate' || 37 ||
ānandatīrthamuninā vyāsavākyasamuddhṛtiḥ |
sadācārasya viṣaye kṛtā saṅkṣepataḥ śubhā || 38 ||
aśeṣakalyāṇaguṇanityānubhavasattanuḥ |
aśeṣadoṣarahitaḥ prīyatāṃ puruṣottamaḥ || 39 ||
|| iti śrīmadānandatīrthabhagavatpādācāryaviracitaḥ sadācārasmṛtiḥ samāptā ||
|| iti sadācārasmṛtiḥ ||
|| śrīkṛṣṇārpaṇamastu ||
