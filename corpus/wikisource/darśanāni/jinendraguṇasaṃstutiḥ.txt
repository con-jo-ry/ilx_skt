jinendra ! guṇasaṃstutistava manāgapi prastutā
bhavatyakhilakarmaṇāṃ prahataye paraṃ kāraṇam |
iti vyavasitā matirmama tato'hamatyādarāt

na caitadiha divyacakṣuradhunekṣyate'smādṛśāṃ
yathā sukṛtakarmaṇāṃ sakalarājyalakṣmyādayaḥ ||2||

bibheṣyapi ca saṃsṛterasubhṛtāṃ vadhaṃ dvekṣyapi |

samastaviṣayaṃ nirakṣamapunaścyuti svātmajam |

tadeva puruṣārthasāramabhisammataṃ yoginām ||4||

na cendriyavivṛttayo na ca manaskṛtā vyāvṛtiḥ |
tathā'pi sakalaṃ jagadyugapadaṃjasā vetsi ca
prapaśyasi ca kevalābhyuditadivyasaccakṣuṣā ||9||
kṣayācca ratirāgamohabhayakāriṇāṃ karmaṇāṃ
kaṣāyaripunirjayaḥ sakalatattvavidyodayaḥ |
ananyasadṛśaṃ sukhaṃ tribhuvanādhipatyaṃ ca te
suniścitamidaṃ vibho ! sumunisampradāyādibhiḥ ||10||
na hīndriyadhiyā virodhi na ca liṅgabuddhayā vaco na cāpyanumatena te sunaya! saptadhā yojitam
vyapetapariśaṅkanaṃ vitathakāraṇādarśanādato'pi bhagavaṃstvameva parameṣṭhitāyāḥ padam ||11||
na lubdha iti gamyase sakalasaṅgasaṃnyāsato
na cā'pi tava mūḍhatā vigatadoṣavāgyadbhavān
anekavidharakṣaṇādasubhṛtāṃ na ca dveṣitā
nirāyudhatayā'pi ca vyapagataṃ tathā te bhayam ||12||
yadi tvamapi bhāṣase vitathamevamāpto'pi san
pareṣu jina kā kathā prakṛtilubdhamugdhādiṣu |
na cā'pyakṛtakātmikā vacanasaṃhatirdṛśyate
punarjananamapyaho! na hi virudhyate yuktibhiḥ ||13||

nanu svamatalokaliṅgapariniścitairvyāhitam ||23||

kathañcidavinaśvarī yadi bhavetpratijñākṣatiḥ ||24||
ananyapuruṣottamo manujatāmatīto'pi samanuṣya iti śasyase tvamadhunā narairbāliśaiḥ |

malairanupasaṃpluto varasarojapatrā'mbuvat |
hitāhitavivekaśūnyahṛdayo na garbhe'pyabhūḥ
kathaṃ tava manuṣyamātrasadṛśatvamāśaṅkayate ||26||
na mṛtyurapi vidyate prakṛtimānuṣasyeva te
mṛtasya parinirvṛtirna maraṇaṃ punarjanmavat |

prabhṛtyarujamekarūpamavatiṣṭhate prāṅmṛteḥ ||27||

sukhaṃ yuvatisevanādiparasannidhipratyayam |
tvayā tu paramātmanā na parato yataste sukhaṃ
vyapetapariṇāmakaṃ nirupamaṃ dhruvaṃ svātmajam ||28||

kṣaradrudhirabhīṣaṇadviradakṛttihelāpaṭaḥ |
