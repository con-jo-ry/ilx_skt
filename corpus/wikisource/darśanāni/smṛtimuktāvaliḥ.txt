smṛtiprāmāṇyasamarthanam
nanūktasmṛtyādīnāṃ kiṃtvena prāmāṇyamiti ced, vedamūlatveneti brūmaḥ| naca tāsāṃ tanmūlatve tatpraṇayanavaiyarthyam| vaidikaśabdānāṃ duradhigamārthatvena sukhapratipattaye vikṣiptasaṅgrahāya ca smṛtipraṇayanasya saphalatvāt| taduktaṃ marīcinā -- durbodhā vaidikāḥ śabdāḥ prakīrṇatvācca ye'khilāḥ| tañjñaista eva spaṣṭārthāḥ smṛtitantre pratiṣṭhitāḥ|| iti| evaṃ ca itihāsapurāṇabodhāyanādyaṣṭādaśasūtrāṇāmapi tulyatayā prāmāṇyam| atra śrutismṛtyorvirodhe śrutireva prābalyam| smṛtīnāṃ parasparavirodhe niravakāśena sāvakāśasya prabalena durbalasya bādhaḥ| prābalyaṃ ca niravakāśatvena bahutvena pramāṇāntarasaṃvāditvena sāttvikatvādineti bodhyam| vistarastu candrikāyāmantarnaye bodhyaḥ||

dharmaśāstrādhyayanam

atha śrutismṛtyorvirodhe nirṇayaḥ| tatra manuḥ -- śrutidvaidhaṃ tu yatra syāttatra dharmāvubhau smṛtau| ubhāvapi hi tau dharmau samyaguktau manīṣibhiḥ|| iti| yatra śrutyordvaidhaṃ parasparavirodhaḥ tatra śrutipratipāditau dvāvapi dharmau| yasmād manvādibhiḥ pūrvatarairapi tau dharmāvuktau| nanu yathā mānayorvirodha ekatarasyaiva prāmāṇyaṃ, tathā śrutyorapīti dvāvapi dharmāvityuktamayuktamiti ced, ucyate| `ko hi mīmāṃsako brūyād virodhe śāstrayormithaḥ| ekaṃ pramāṇamitaradapramāṇaṃ bhaveditī' tyuktarītyā sarvātmanā bādhāyogena viṣayāntarasañcāreṇa vā kālabhedena vādhikāribhedena vā deśabhedena vā saṅkocaparatvena vā gauṇārthaparatvena vā prayogāntaraparatvena vā tātparyāntaraparatvena vā mānāntarasyopapannatvena dvāvapi śrutipratipāditau dharmau pramāṇasiddhāviti manuvacanasya tātparyam| yadvā virodhe bādho'pyastu yatraikatarasya prābalyaṃ, yatra tulyabalatā tatra bādhābhāvena vikalpasyāvaśyakatvād dvāvapi dharmāvityevaṃparaṃ manuvacanam| evaṃ ca tulyanyāyatayā smṛtyoḥ purāṇayorācārayośca parasparavirodhe dvāvapi dharmāviti|| iti śrutyādīnāṃ virodhe nirṇayaḥ|

dharmadeśaḥ
pūrvoktadharmānuṣṭhānāya dharmadeśo nirūpyate| tatra manuḥ -- sarasvatīdṛṣadvatyordevanadyoryadantaram| taṃ devanirmitaṃ deśaṃ brahmāvartaṃ pracakṣate|| kurukṣetraṃ ca mātsyaṃ ca pañcālāḥ śūrasenayaḥ| eṣa brahmarṣideśo vai brahmāvartādanantaraḥ|| himavadvindhyayormadhyaṃ yatprāgvinaśanādapi| pratyageva prayāgācca madhyadeśaḥprakīrtitaḥ|| vinaśanaṃ nāma sarasvatyā antardhānadeśaḥ| ā samudrāttu vai pūrvādā samudrācca paścimāt| tayorevāntaraṃ giryorāryāvartaṃ vidurbudhāḥ|| iti| uktadeśeṣu pūrvaṃ pūrvaṃ praśasyate| tathāca sumantuḥ -- brahmāvartaḥ paro deśa ṛṣideśastvanantaraḥ| madhyadeśastato nūnamāryāvartastathāparaḥ|| iti| etaccatuṣṭayālābhe punardeśāntaramāha manuḥ -- kṛṣṇasārastu carati mṛgo yatra svabhāvataḥ| yajñadeśaḥ sa vijñeyo mlecchadeśastataḥ paraḥ|| iti| uktālābhe'pi yasmin deśe cāturvarṇyavyavasthā dṛśyate so'pi puṇyadeśa ityāha viṣṇuḥ -- cāturvarṇyavyavasthānaṃ yasmin deśe ca vidyate| dharmadeśaḥ sa vijñeyo hyāryāvartastataḥ paraḥ|| iti| saṃvarto yājñavalkyaśca vasiṣṭhaḥ śaṅkha eva ca| āryāvartaṃ vadantyete deśaṃ kṛṣṇamṛgāśrayam|| ukteṣveṣu kvacidapavādamāha manuḥ -- na śūdrarājye nivasennādhārmikajanāvṛte| na pāṣaṇḍajanākrānte nopaspṛṣṭe'ntyajairnṛbhiḥ|| iti| atrāpyapavādamāha pitāmahaḥ -- śūdradeśe'pi nivasedyatra madhye tu jāhnavī| so'pi puṇyatamo deśo'nāryairapi samāśritaḥ|| iti| atra jāhnavīśabdaḥ puṇyatīrthadevatāyatanasaṃyuktadeśamātropalakṣakaḥ||
niṣiddhadeśāḥ
atha niṣiddhadeśāḥ| atra bodhāyanaḥ -- ānartāścāṅgamagadhāḥ saurāṣṭrā dakṣiṇāpathāḥ| upāvṛtsindhusauvīrā ete saṅkīrṇayonayaḥ|| ādityapurāṇe taddeśagamane doṣamāha -- narmadāsindhukauśikyāḥ pāraṃ padmasya paścimam| gatvā narakamāpnoti tīrthakālādhikaṃ vasan|| aṅgavaṅgakaliṅgāśca ugramālavikāstathā| narmadādakṣiṇaṃ yacca sindhoruttarameva ca|| pauṇḍrāścaiva caidyamāgadhikāḥ kaṣāḥ| na vivāhaṃ tathā śrāddhaṃ yajñaṃ caiṣu samācaret|| pāpadeśāśca ye kecitpāpairadhyuṣitā janaiḥ| gatvā deśānapuṇyāṃstu kṛtsnaṃ pāpaṃ samaśnute|| saurāṣṭraṃ sindhusauvīraṃ cāvantyaṃ dakṣiṇāpatham| pañcaitān kāmato gatvā kaliṅgāṃśca pated‌dvijaḥ|| iti| teṣāṃ dvijānāmāpadi vṛttyarthaṃ deśānugrahaḥ skāndapurāṇe darśitaḥ -- aṅgavaṅgakaliṅgāndhrapārvateyān kaṣāṃstathā| sindhusauvīrasaurāṣṭrān pāradārāṃśca mālavān|| nivāsayedvijo nityamanāpadi vivarjayet| etānatyāpadi gṛhī saṃśrayedvṛttikarśitaḥ|| iti| ityādiniṣaddhadeśanirūpaṇam||
niṣiddhadeśāpavādaḥ|
atha niṣiddhadeśeṣu keṣucidapavāda ucyate| pitāmahaḥ -- śūdrarājye'pi nivasedyatra madhye tu jāhnavī| so'pi puṇyatamo deśo'nāryairapi samāśritaḥ| dharmaśāstrasāre -- candrāyaṇāni kṛcchrāṇi mahāsāntapanāni ca| prāyaścittānyudīryante yatra gaṅgā na vidyate|| gaṅgāśabdārtho'pi tatraiva nirūpitaḥ -- kāverī tuṅgabhadrā ca kṛṣṇaveṇī ca gautamī| bhāgīrathīti vikhyātāḥ pañca gagāḥ prakīrtitāḥ|| iti| ādityapurāṇe -- yatra gaṅgā saricchreṣṭhā sa deśastūttamo vanam| siddhakṣetraṃ ca tañjñeyaṃ gaṅgātīrasamāśritam|| prabhāse puṣkare kāṃśyāṃ naimiśe'marakuṇḍake| gaṅgāyāṃ sarayūtīre nivaseddhārmiko janaḥ|| ityādi| iti niṣiddhadeśāpavādaḥ||
deśadharmāḥ
atha deśadharmāḥ| pañcadhā pratipattirhi dākṣiṇātyeṣu dṛśyate| putreṇānupanītena pantyā ca sahabhojanam|| pitṛṣvasurmātulasya putryāścodvāhanaṃ tathā| tathā paryuṣitānnasya bhojanaṃ paridṛśyate|| tathā vikraya ūrṇāyā jīvanaṃ cāyudhīyakam| vyavahāraścobhayatodadbhirvāridhiyānakam|| sindhuyānamudīcyānāṃ pratipattiśca pañcadhā| evamanye viruddhāni dṛśyante deśabhedataḥ|| abhartṛkabhrātṛbhāryāgrahaṇaṃ cātidūṣitam| kule kanyāpradānaṃ ca deśeṣvanyeṣu dṛśyate|| tathā mātṛvivāho'pi pārasīkeṣu dṛśyate| viruddheṣveṣu dharmeṣu prāmāṇyaṃ deśato bhavet|| atra bṛhaspatiḥ -- deśajātikulānāṃ ca ye dharmāstatpravartitāḥ| tathaiva te pālanīyāḥ prajā prakṣubhyate'nyathā|| devalo'pi -- yeṣu deśeṣu ye vedā yeṣu deśeṣu ye dvijāḥ| yeṣu deśeṣu yattoyaṃ yā ca tatraivamṛttikā|| yeṣu sthāneṣu yacchaucaṃ dharmācāraśca yādṛśaḥ| tatra tānnāvamanyeta dharmastatraiva tādṛśaḥ|| yasmin deśe pure grāme traividye nagare'pi vā| yo yatra vihito dharmastaṃ dharmaṃ na vicāla(ra) yet|| iti| aviśeṣeṇa deśadharmaprāmāṇyaprasaktau mānāviruddhaṃ grāhyamityāha manuḥ -- sadbhirācaritaṃ yatsyāddhārmikaiśca dvijātibhiḥ| taddeśakulajātānāmaviruddhaṃ prakalpayet|| iti| yat sadbhirācaritaṃ tat śrutyādipramāṇāviruddhaṃ taddeśakulajātānāṃ dharmatvena prakalpayedityarthaḥ| `śāstraduṣṭaṃ yathā tyājyaṃ tathā lokavirodhyapi'| tatra mihiraḥ dośācārastāvadādau vicintyo deśe deśe yā sthitiḥ saivakāryā| lokadviṣṭaṃ paṇḍitā varjayanti daivajño'to lokamārgeṇa yāyāt|| ityādi| iti deśadharmanirūpaṇam||

atha yugadharmāḥ| āraṇyaparvaṃṇi -- kṛtaṃ nāma yugaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ yatra dharmaḥ sanātanaḥ| kṛtameva na kartavyaṃ tasmin kāle yugottame|| na tatra sīmāḥ sīdanti na jīryante ca vai prajāḥ| tataḥ kṛtayugaṃ nāma kālena guṇatāṃ gatam|| kṛte yuge catuṣpādaṃ cāturvarṇyaṃ ca śāśvatam| etat kṛtayugaṃ nāma traiguṇyaparivarjanam|| pādena hrasate dharmo raktatāṃ yāti cācyutaḥ| jñānapravṛttāśca narāḥ kriyādharmaparāyaṇāḥ|| tato yajñāḥ pravartante dharmāśca vividhāḥ kriyāḥ| svadharmasthāḥ kriyāvanto janāstretāyuge'bhavan|| viṣṇuḥ pītatvamāyāti caturdhā veda eva ca| satyasyaiva hi vibhraṃśo satye kaścidavasthitaḥ|| satyāt pracyavamānānāṃ vyādhayo bahavo'bhavan| kāmāścopadravāścaiva tathā daivatakāritāḥ|| kāmakāmā hyarthakāmā yajñāṃstanvanti cāpare| evaṃ dvāparamāsādya prajāḥ kṣīyantyadharmataḥ|| pādenaikena kaunteya dharmaḥ kaliyuge sthitaḥ| vedācārāḥ praśāmyante dharmā yajñāḥ kriyāstathā|| ītayo vyādhayastandrī doṣāḥ krodhādayastathā|| iti| bhidyante yugadharmāste yugabhedānusārataḥ| bhede pramāṇaṃ vijñeyaṃ manostu vacanaṃ hitam|| tapaḥ paraṃ kṛtayuge tretāyāṃ jñānamucyate| dvāpare yajñamevāhurdānameva kalau yuge|| dharmasyatāratamyāpādakānyāha parāśaraḥ -- abhigamya kṛte dānaṃ tretāsvāhūya dīyate| dvāpare yācamānāya sevayā dīyate kalau|| atra tretāsviti bahuvacanaṃ kṛtadvāparādiṣu jātāvekavacanapradarśanārthamityuktaṃ mādhavīye| nimittakṛtatāratamyamāha parāśaraḥ -- abhigamyottamaṃ dānamāhūyaiva tu madhyamam| adhamaṃ yācamānāya sevādānaṃ tu niṣphalam|| iti||
dharme yugabhedena pramāṇam
dharme yugabhedena pramāṇam -- kṛte tu mānavā dharmāstretāyāṃ gautamāḥ smṛtāḥ| dvāpare śaṅkhalikhitāḥ kalau pārāśarāḥ smṛtāḥ|| iti| adharmasthānamapyāha sa eva -- tyajeddeśaṃ kṛtayuge tretāyāṃ grāmamutsṛjet| dvāpare kulamekaṃ tu kartāraṃ tu kalau yuge|| iti| patitaḥ pumān grāmasamūhātmake deśe nivasati cet, so'dharmāpādaka iti tyājyaḥ kṛtayuge| evaṃ grāmādāvapi yojyam| kartṛtyāgo nāma tena sambhāṣaṇādivarjanamityartho mādhavīye darśitaḥ| pāpaniṣṭhitāni vibhajyāha parāśaraḥ -- kṛte sambhāṣaṇādeva tretāyāṃ sparśanena ca| dvāpare tvannamādāya kalau patati karmaṇā| vadhādikarmaṇākalau patito bhavati, na sambhāṣaṇādinetyarthaḥ| gurvādiśāpaphalakālaṃ vibhajyāha sa eva -- kṛte tatkṣaṇikaḥ śāpastretāyāṃ daśabhirdinaiḥ| dvāpare caikamāsīnaḥ kalau saṃvatsareṇa tu|| iti| dharmasiddheḥ kālaṃ vibhajyoktaṃ viṣṇupurāṇe -- yatkṛte daśabhirvarṣaistretāyāṃ hāyanena tu| dvāpare tacca māsena hyahorātreṇa tatkalau|| iti| yugabhedāttīrthabhedo viṣṇudharmottare -- puṣkaraṃ tu kṛte sevyaṃ tretāyāṃ naimiśaṃ tathā| dvāpare tu kurukṣetraṃ kalau gaṅgāṃ samāśrayet|| iti||
kaliyugadharmāḥ viśeṣeṇa kaliyugadharmā nirūpyante| tatra vyāsaḥ -- dhyāyan kṛte yajan yajñaistretāyāṃ dvāpare'rcayan| yadāpnopi tadāpnoti kalau saṅkīrtya keśavam|| iti| dharmotkarṣamatīvātra prāpnoti puruṣaḥ kalau| svalpāyāsena dharmajñāstena tuṣṭo'smyahaṃ kalau|| iti| ādityapurāṇe viśeṣo'bhihitaḥ -- yastu kārtayugo dharmo na kartavyaḥ kalau yuge| pāpaprasaktāstu yataḥ kalau nāryo narāstathā|| na vidvattā na śuddho'rtho na śuddhirmanasaḥ kalau| yato'taḥ satyamevaikaṃ narāṇāmupakārakam|| iti| kārtayugastatsaṃbandhī| āraṇyaparvaṇi kaliyugaprajāsthitiruktā -- brāhmaṇāḥ kṣatriyā vaiśyāḥ saṅkīryante parasparam| śūdratulyā bhaviṣyanti tapaḥsatyavivarjitāḥ|| svabhāvāt krūrakarmāṇaścānyonyamabhiśaṅkitāḥ| bhavitāro narāssarve samprāpte yugasaṃkṣaye|| iti| viṣṇupurāṇe'pi -- kalau jagatpatiṃ viṣṇuṃ sarvastraṣṭāramīśvaram| nārcayiṣyanti maitreya! pāṣaṇḍopahatā janāḥ|| sarve brahma vadiṣyanti samprāpte tu kalau yuge| nānutiṣṭhanti maitreya śiśnodaraparāyaṇāḥ|| iti| parāśaro'pi -- jito dharmo hyadharmeṇa satyaṃ caivānṛtena ca| jitāścoraistu rājānaḥ strībhistu puruṣāḥ kalau|| sīdanti cāgnihotrāṇi gurupūjā praṇaśyati| kumāryaśca prasūyante tasmin kaliyuge sati|| iti| yathā yathā kalervṛddhistathā tathā adharmavṛddhiḥ| viṣṇupurāṇe -- yadā yadā satāṃ hānirvedamārgānusāriṇām| tadā tadā kalervṛddhiranumeyā vicakṣaṇaiḥ|| na prītirvedavādeṣu pāṣaṇḍeṣu yadā ratiḥ| tadā tadā kalervṛddhiranumeyā vicakṣaṇaiḥ|| ityādi| kṛte tvasthigatāḥ prāṇāstrotāyāṃ māṃsamāśritāḥ| dvāpare rudhiraṃ caiva kalau tvannādiṣu sthitāḥ|| iti| yugasāmarthyāt phalamapi parāśareṇoktam -- yuge yuge ca ye dharmāsteṣu teṣu ca ye dvijāḥ| teṣāṃ nindā na kartavyā yugarūpā hi te dvijāḥ|| iti| āraṇyaparvaṇi -- bhūmirnadyo nagāḥ śailāḥ siddhā devarṣayastathā| kālaṃ samanuvartante tathā bhāvā yuge yuge|| ityādi| iti yugadharmāḥ||
kalau niṣiddhāḥ|
atha kaliyuge niṣiddhadharmāḥ -- tatrādityapurāṇe -- dīrghakālabrahmacaryaṃ dhāraṇaṃ ca kamaṇḍaloḥ| sagotrāttu sapiṇḍāttu vivāho govadhastathā|| narāśvamedhau madyaṃ ca kalau varjyā dvijātibhiḥ| ūḍhāyāḥ punarudvāhaṃ jyeṣṭhāṃśaṃ govadhaṃ tathā|| kalau pañca na kurvīta bhrātṛjāyāṃ kamaṇḍalum|| iti| kraturapi -- devareṇa sutotpattirdattā kanyā na dīyate| na yajñe govadhaḥ kāryaḥ kalau ca na kamaṇḍaluḥ| iti| tathānye'pi dharmajñasamayapramāṇakāḥ santi| vidhavāyāḥ prajotpattau devarasya niyojanam| bālikākṣatayonyāśca vareṇānyena saṃskṛtiḥ| ūḍhāyāmapi puṃsaṃbandhātpūrvaṃ punarvivāha ityarthaḥ| kanyānāmanyavarṇānāṃ vivāhaśca dvijātibhiḥ| ātatāyidvijāgyrāṇāṃ dharmayuddhena hiṃsanam|| dvijasyābdhau tu nauyātuḥ śodhitasyāpi saṅgrahaḥ| satradīkṣā ca sarveṣāṃ kamaṇḍaluvidhāraṇam|| mahāprasthānagamanaṃ gosaṃjñaptiśca gosave| sautrāmaṇyāṃ surāgrāhaścamasocchiṣṭabhakṣaṇam|| agnihotrahavanyāśca leho līḍhāparigrahaḥ| vānaprasthāśramasyāpi praveśo vidhicoditaḥ|| yateśca sarvavarṇeṣu bhikṣācaryā vidhānataḥ| śavodake daśāhaṃ ca dakṣiṇā gurucoditā|| vṛttasvādhyāyasāpekṣamaghasaṅkocanaṃ kalau| prāyaścittavidhānaṃ ca viprāṇāṃ maraṇāntikam|| saṃsargadoṣaḥ stenādyairmahāpātakaniṣkṛtiḥ| varātithipitṛbhyaśca paśūpākaraṇakriyā|| dattaurasetareṣāṃ tu putratvena parigrahaḥ| savarṇānāṃ tathā duṣṭaissaṃsargaḥ śodhitairapi|| hīnena saṅgrahe vṛtte parityāgo guroḥ striyaḥ| asthisañcayanādūrdhvamaṅgasparśanameva ca| svāmitvaṃ caiva viprāṇāṃ somavikrayaṇaṃ tathā| ṣaḍbhaktānaśanenānnāharaṇaṃ hīnakarmaṇaḥ|| śūdreṣu dāsagopālakulamitrārdhasīriṇām| bhojyānnatā gṛhasthasya tīrthasevā ca dūrataḥ|| etad gaṅgādiyātrāvyatiriktaviṣayam| `kalau gaṅgāṃ samāśrayedi'ti vacanāt| viprāṇāṃ bhojanaṃ tadvad‌grahe kṣatriyavaiśyayoḥ| śiṣyasya gurudāreṣu guruvṛttirīritā|| āpadvṛttirdvijāgyrāṇāmaśvastanikatā tathā| prajārthaṃ tu dvijāgyrāṇāṃ paradāraparigrahaḥ|| brāhmaṇānāṃ pravāsitvaṃ mukhāgnidhamanakriyā| balātkārādiduṣṭastrīsaṅgraho vidhicoditaḥ|| brāhmaṇādiṣu śūdrasya pacanādikriyāpi ca| bhṛgvagnipatanaṃ caiva vṛddhādimaraṇaṃ tathā|| gotṛptiśiṣṭe payasi śiṣṭairācamanakriyā|| pitāputravirodheṣu sākṣiṇāṃ daṇḍakalpanam| yateḥ sāyaṃgṛhasthatvaṃ sūribhistattvatatparaiḥ|| etāni lokaguptyarthaṃ kalerādau mahātmabhiḥ| nivartitāni karmāṇi vyavasthāpūrvakaṃ budhaiḥ|| samayaścāpi sādhūnāṃ pramāṇaṃ vedavadbhavet|| iti| iti kaliyugavarjyadharmāḥ||
ityupoddhātaprakaraṇam|
atha kālanirṇayaprakaraṇam|
kālasvarūpaṃ, tadviśeṣāśca athedānīṃ karmaṇaḥ kālasādhyatvāt kālasvarūpaṃ tadviśeṣāśca nirṇīyante| śrutismṛtyuktakarmaṇaśca kālādhīnatvam `amāvāsyāyāmaparāhṇe piṇḍapitṛyajñena carantī'ti śrutau piṇḍapitṛyajñasyāmāvāsyāparāhṇādikālasāpekṣatvena, tathā `darśe snātvā pitṛbhyastu dadyātkṛṣṇatilodakam| annaṃ ca vidhivaddadyāt santatistena vardhata'ti smṛtau tilodakadānāderuktakālasāpekṣatvena ca, tathā `amāvāsyāyāṃ nādhīyīta nādhyāpayennacchindyādārdrān dārūn nādyādannaṃ parasye'ti, `amāvāsyāyāṃ na gacchet prāptakālāmapi striyam| tailaṃ ca na spṛśedāmaṃ vṛkṣādīṃśca na chedayedi'ti vidhiniṣedhayoruktakālasāpekṣatvena, tathā `paurṇamāsyāṃ paurṇamāsyāṃ yajet,' `amāvāsyāyāmamāvāsyāyāṃ sāyaṃ juhoti prātarjuhotī'tyādau ca kālasāpekṣatvenāvagamyate| ata eva kālaḥ karmāṅgameva, na pradhānamityavagantavyam| sa ca kālo dvividhaḥ| sāmānyarūpo viśeṣarūpaśceti| kālaviśeṣeṣu sarveṣvapyanusyūtaḥ nakṣatragrahaparatvādyanumeyaḥ sāmānyakālaḥ, nimeṣādistadviśeṣaḥ| taduktaṃ bhāratekarṣeṇāpyadhikaiḥ ṣaṅ‌bhiḥ palaistāmrasya bhājanam| triṃśadaṅ‌gulavistāramucchritaṃ caturaṅgulam|| svarṇamātreṇa kṛtvā ca caturaṅgulakārṣikāḥ| madhyabhāgatayā viddhā nāḍikā ghaṭikā smṛtā|| tadranghreṇāmbhasā pātraṃ yāvatkālena pūryate| sa kālo nāḍikā tasyāḥ ṣaṣṭibhāgo vināḍikā|| nāḍīdvayaṃ muhūrtastu te pañcadaśa vāsaraḥ| evaṃ rātristathā pakṣamāsartvayanavatsarāḥ|| iti||
saṃvatsaranāmāni| ete saṃvatsarāḥ prabhavādibhedena ṣaṣṭisaṅkhyākāḥ| taduktam -- prabhavo vibhavaḥ śuklaḥ pramodo'tha prajāpatiḥ āṅgīrasaḥ śrīmukhaśca bhāvo'tha yuvavatsaraḥ|| dhātvīśvarau bahudhānyaḥ pramāthī vikramo viṣuḥ| citrabhānuḥ svabhānuśca tāraṇaḥ pārthivo vyayaḥ|| sarvajit sarvadhārī ca virodhī vikṛtiḥ kharaḥ| nandano vijayaścaiva jayo manmathadurmukhau(khī)|| hemalambī vilambī ca vikārī śārvarī plavaḥ| śubhakṛt krodhī viśvāvasuparābhavau|| plavaṅgaḥ kīlakaḥ saumyaḥ sādhāraṇavirodhikṛte| parīdhāvī pramādī ca ānando rākṣaso nalaḥ|| piṅgalaḥ kālayuktākṣī siddhārthī raudridurmatī| dundubhī rudhurodgārī raktākṣī krodhanaḥ kṣayaḥ|| iti||
cāndrādisaṃvatsaranirṇayaḥ| tatra cāndraprabhavādīnāmupakramaścaitrapratipadītyuktaṃ dīpikāyām -- `cāndromadhuśuklapratipadārambha' iti| meṣasthe sūrye sauraprabhavādīnāmupakramaḥ| sāvanastvaicchikaḥ| yattu viṣṇudharmottare māghaśuklasamārambhe candrārkau vāsavarkṣagau| jīvaśukrau yathā syātāṃ ṣaṣṭyabdādistathā smṛtaḥ|| iti māghaśuklapratipadupakramatvaṃ saṃvatsarāṇāmuktam| tajjyotiḥ śāstraprasiddhaśubhāśubhaphalapratipādanārthaṃ, na tu karmānuṣṭhānārthamityavagantavyam| vatsarādau pravṛttasyaivānuṣṭhānopayogitvāt| tadāhārṣṭiṣeṇaḥ -- smaret sarvatra karmādau cāndraṃ saṃvatsaraṃ sadā| nānyaṃ yasmādvatsarādau pravṛttistasya kīrtitā|| iti| tatra cāndrasyāvāntarabhedā ucyante -- saṃvatsarastu prathamo dvitīyaḥ parivatsaraḥ| iḍāvatsarastṛtīyaśca caturthaścānuvatsaraḥ|| udvatsaraḥ pañcamastu tatsaṅgho yugasaṃ (jñakaḥ)jñitaḥ|| iti| prabhavamārabhya kṣayānteṣu sarveṣu dvādaśa pañcakāni| tatraikasmin pañcake krameṇa saṃvatsarādayo bhavanti| etatsarvaṃ jyotiśśāstre prasiddham| vistarabhayānna likhyate| saurādīnāṃ trayāṇāmupayogo jyotirgargeṇa darśitaḥ -- sauro māso vivāhādau yajñādau sāvanaḥ smṛtaḥ| ābdike pitṛkārye ca cāndro māsaḥ praśasyate|| iti| atra prathamādiśabdena upanayanapūjāvrataniyamapratiṣṭhāgṛhakaraṇayātrādayo gṛhyante| dvitīyādiśabdena dānahomagarbhādhānasīmantonnayanajātakarmaṛṇakrayavyāpārāṇāṃ grahaṇam| ābdike sāṃvatsaripamṛtāhaśrāddhe| pitṛkārya
e ṣāṇmāsikaśrāddhādau| caśabdānmāsasaṃvatsarasādhyayajñavyatirikte sarvasminneva kārye cāndro māsaḥ praśasyata ityuktaṃ hemādau| nanu vivāhādīnā `maṣṭavarṣaṃ brāhmaṇamupanayīte'tyādau sāvanapuraskāreṇa pravṛttatvāt, `sauro māso vivāhādāvi'ti katham? tathā `yāsau vaiśākhyāmāvāsyāmādadhīte'ti cāndrāmāvāsyāyāmādhānavidhānād `vivāhavratayajñeṣu sauraṃ mānaṃ praśasyata' iti vacanena saure'pi vidhānādyajñādau sāvana iti katham?, tathā `kanyākumbhavṛṣasthe'rke kṛṣṇapakṣeṣu sarvadā| anena vidhinā śrāddhaṃ paitṛvyamiha nirvapedi'tyādau sauramānenāpi pitṛkāryavidhānāt `ābdike pitṛkārye ca cāndro māsaḥ praśasyata' iti kathamiti ced, na| ete saurādayaḥ keṣucit karmaviśeṣeṣūpasthitāḥ, keṣucidvikalpāḥ| te yathā bhaviṣyottare -- `caitraśuklapratipadamārabhya tilakavrataṃ kāryami'tyuktam| taccāndreṇaiva kartavyam| śuklakṛṣṇapratipadādīnāṃ saurasāvanānupajīvanāt| yattu viṣṇudharmottare -- `meṣasaṅkramaṇe bhānau sopavāso narottame'ti sujanmāvāptivratādikamuktam, tatsaureṇaiva kartavyam| yattu saṃvatsarasādhyagavāmayanādikaṃ, tatsāvanenaiva kartavyam| cāndrasya ṣaḍ‌bhirahorātrairnyūnatvāt| saurasya pañcabhurahorātrairadhikatvāt| nirūḍhapaśubandhe'pi cāndra eva| `amāvāsyāyāṃ paurṇamāsyāṃ ce'ti sūtrakāraiścāndratithau tadanuṣṭhānavidhānāt| tathāca etādṛśasthaleṣūpasthitaḥ saṃvatsaraḥ| anyatra tu upanayanavivāhādiṣu vikalpā iti mādhavīye'bhihitam| apare tu `praśasyata' iti vacanasvārasyāt kvacit kasyacit praśastatvamityāhuḥ| tadevaṃ cāndrādīnāmupayogo draṣṭavya iti|| itisaṃvatsaranirṇayaḥ||

ṛtunirṇayaḥ atha yathākramamayananirṇayaḥ| yātyanena ṛtutrayeṇa sūryo dakṣiṇādidiśamiti ṛtutrayamayanam|| tathāca śrutiḥ -- `tasmādādityaḥ ṣāṇmāso dakṣiṇenaiti ṣaḍḍattareṇaitī'ti| idaṃ tu saurameva| sūryamupajīvya pravṛttatvāt| atrottarāyaṇe kartavyaṃ dakṣiṇāyane niṣiddhaṃ ca jyotiḥśāstre pratipāditam -- gṛhapraveśastridaśapratiṣṭhā vivāhacaula (mauñjī)vratabandhapūrvam| saumyāyane karma śubhapradaṃ syādyadgarhitaṃ tatkhalu dakṣiṇāyane|| iti| dakṣiṇāyane ugradevatāpratiṣṭhādikaṃ kartavyamityuktaṃ vaikhānasasaṃhitāyām -- mātṛbhairavavārāhanārasiṃhatrivikramāḥ| mahiṣāsurasaṃhartrī sthāpyā vai dakṣiṇāyane|| iti| ityayananirṇayaḥ||

svāvayavaghaṭikādistutidvarā trimuhūrtapraśaṃsāparāṇi|| ata eva bodhāyanaḥ -- udayasthā tithiryā hi trimuhūrtā tu sā tithiḥ| vratakarmaṇi śastā tu snānadānajapādiṣu|| iti| evaṃ hrāsavṛddhī na kāraṇam|| iti| skānde'pi -- prātarārabhya matimān kuryānnaktavratādikam| nāparāhṇe na madhyāhne pitryakālau hi tau smṛtau|| iti| evamādivacanabalāt prātareva saṅkalpaḥ kāryaḥ| yadyapi `ye yasya vihitaḥ kālaḥ karmaṇastadupakrame| vidyamāno bhavedaṅgaṃ nojjhitopakrameṇa ca||' iti vacanena saṅkalpakāle'vaśyaṃ pratipadā bhāvyam. tathāpi tadānīṃ jyotiśśāstraprasiddhapratipadabhāve'pi smṛtiṣūpapāditāyāḥ pratipadaḥ sattvāt| ata eva devalaḥ -- yāṃ tithiṃ samanuprāpya astaṃ yāti divākaraḥ| sā tithiḥ sakalā jñeyā dānādhyayanakarmasu|| iti| atra pūrvaviddhāyāṃ pratipadi balyutsavaḥ kāryaḥ| `śrāvaṇī durganavamī tathā durgāṣṭamī ca yā pūrvaviddhā tu kartavyā śivarātrirbalerdinam||' ityādismṛteḥ| sa cotsavo brahmapurāṇe vāmanapurāṇe bhaviṣyottarapurāṇe cābhihitaḥ| tathā `pratipattu site pakṣe darśaviddhā śubhā smṛtā|| kṛṣṇe dvitīyayā viddhā trimuhūrtā bhavedyadi||' iti| jyotiḥkāśyapo'pi pratipacchuklapakṣe tu pūrvaviddhā śubhāvahā| kṛṣṇapakṣe śubhā jñeyā saṅgavasparśinī bhavet|| ityādi| balyutsave'pi viśeṣo'bhihito matsyapurāṇe -- samprāpte kārtike māsi sitā yā pratipadbhavet| balyutsavastu kartavyaḥ sammukhā yā tithirbhavet|| pratipatsammukhā kāryā śuklapakṣasya kārtike|| sammukhā sāyāhnavyāpinī| gargaḥ -- balyutsavaṃ ca pūrvedyustrisuhūrtā yadā bhavet| vṛddhagargaḥ -- mukhāyāḥ pūrvaviddhāyāḥ pratipadyadyasambhave| balyutsavastu kartavyo dyūtādīnyuttare'hani|| saṅgave pūrvaviddhe vā niṣiddhā parasammitā|| ityādi| evaṃ vatsarādipratipadādāvapi bodhyam|
atra śuklapakṣapratipadupavāsādeḥ kenacinnimittenānanuṣṭhāne sati gauṇāpyuttaraviddhā grāhyā, karmaṇastyāgāpekṣayā gauṇakālasyaiva jyāyastvāditi| tathā varāhapurāṇe saptadaśe'dhyāye pratipadāditithiṣu viśeṣo'bhihitaḥ -- nandā bhadrā jayā riktā pūrṇā syustithayaḥ kramāt| pratipatpūrvakāḥ pañca pañcaivobhayapakṣayoḥ|| aniruddhaśca pradyumnastathā saṅkarṣaṇastathā| vāsudevaḥ pañcamī tu mūrtirnārāyaṇābhidhā|| tithīnāṃ kramaśo devāḥ pūjānāṃ grāhakāḥ smṛtāḥ| navanītaṃ dadhi kṣīraṃ ghanībhūtaṃ ghṛtaṃ tathā|| aniruddhādimūrtīnāṃ catasṛṇāṃ kramāt priyam| nārāyaṇasya catvāri sarveṣāṃ śarkarāpi ca|| sarvadaiva ghṛtaṃ dadyāt sati kṣīrādike'pi ca| sarvasāraṃ ghṛtaṃ proktaṃ devānāṃ paramaṃ haviḥ| nivedya māṃ tato dattvā brāhmaṇebhyo'pi śaktitaḥ| bhuñjīta svayamapyetat sakuṭumbo dine dine|| yasya śaktiḥ sadā nāsti kartumevaṃ nivedanam(dine dine)| ekadāpi prakurvīta cāturmāsye dine dine|| navanītādinaivedyaṃ nandādidinapañcake| satyāmapi hi śaktau yo na kuryānmannivedanam| vṛthā janma bhavet tasya sampattiścāpi niṣphalā|| iti viṣṇupūjāviṣaye viśeṣo'bhihitaḥ||
