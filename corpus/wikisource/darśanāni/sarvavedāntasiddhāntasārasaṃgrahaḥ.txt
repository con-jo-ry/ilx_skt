Text:

||sarvavedāntasiddhāntasārasaṃgrahaḥ ||
||1 ||vandanam ||
akhaṇḍānandasambodho vandanādyasya jāyate |
govindaṃ tamahaṃ vande cidānandatanuṃ gurum ||1||
akhaṇḍaṃ saccidānandamavāṅmanasagocaram |
ātmānamakhilādhāramāśraye'bhīṣṭasiddhaye ||2||
yadālambo daraṃ hanti satāṃ pratyūhasaṃbhavam |
tadālambe dayālambaṃ lambodarapadāmbujam ||3||
||2 ||sādhanacatuṣṭaya ||
vedāntaśāstrasiddhāntasārasaṃgraha ucyate |
prekṣāvatāṃ mumukṣūṇāṃ sukhabodhopapattaye ||4||
asya śāstrānusāritvādanubandhacatuṣṭayam |
yadeva mūlaṃ śāstrasya nirdiṣṭaṃ tadihocyate ||5||
adhikārī ca viṣayaḥ sambandhaśca prayojanam |
śāstrārambhaphalaṃ prāhuranubandhacatuṣṭayam ||6||
caturbhiḥ sādhanaiḥ samyaksampanno yuktidakṣiṇaḥ |
medhāvī puruṣo vidvānadhikāryatra saṃmataḥ ||7||
viṣayaḥ śuddhacaitanyaṃ jīvabrahmaikyalakṣaṇam |
yatraiva dṛśyate sarvavedāntānāṃ samanvayaḥ ||8||
etadaikyaprameyasya pramāṇasyāpi ca śruteḥ |
sambandhaḥ kathyate sadbhirbodhyabodhakalakṣaṇaḥ ||9||
brahmātmaikavijñānaṃ santaḥ prāhuḥ prayojanam |
yena niḥśeṣasaṃsārabandhātsadyaḥ pramucyate ||10||
prayojanaṃ saṃpravṛtteḥ kāraṇaṃ phalalakṣaṇam |
prayojanamanuddiśya na mando'pi pravartate ||11||
sādhanacatuṣṭayasaṃpattiryasyāsti dhīmataḥ puṃsaḥ |
tasyaivaitatphalasiddhirnānyasya kiṃcidūnasya ||12||
catvāri sādhanānyatra vadanti paramarṣayaḥ |
muktiryeṣāṃ tu sadbhāve nābhāve siddhyati dhruvam ||13||
ādyaṃ nityānityavastuvivekaḥ sādhanaṃ matam |
ihāmutrārthaphalabhogavirāgo dvitīyakam ||14||
śamādiṣaṭkasaṃpattistṛtīyaṃ sādhanaṃ matam |
turīyaṃ tu mumukṣutvaṃ sādhanaṃ śāstrasaṃmatam ||15||
||3 ||vastuviveka ||
brahmaiva nityamanyattu hyanityamiti vedanam |
so'yaṃ nityānityavastuviveka iti kathyate ||16||
mṛdādikāraṇaṃ nityaṃ triṣu kāleṣu darśanāt |
ghaṭādyanityaṃ tatkāryaṃ yatastannāśa īkṣyate ||17||
tathaivatajjagatsarvamanityaṃ brahmakāryataḥ |
tatkāraṇaṃ paraṃ brahma bhavennityaṃ mṛdādivat ||18||
sargaṃ vaktyasya tasmādvā etasmādityapi śrutiḥ |
sakāśādbrahmaṇastasmādanityatve na saṃśayaḥ ||19||
sarvasyānityatve sāvayavatvena sarvataḥsiddhe |
vaikuṇṭhādiṣu nityatvamatirbhrama eva mūḍhabuddhinām ||20||
anityatvaṃ ca nityatvamevaṃ yacchṛtiyuktibhiḥ |
vivecanaṃ nityānityaviveka iti kathyate ||21||
||4 ||naispṛhyam ||
aihikāmuṣmikārtheṣu hyanityatvena niścayāt |
naiḥspṛhyaṃ tucchabuddhyā yattadvairāgyamitīryate ||22||
nityānityapadārthavivekātpuruṣasya jāyate sadyaḥ |
srakcandanavanitādau sarvatrānityavastuni viraktiḥ ||23||
kākasyaviṣṭhāvadasahyabuddhirbhogyeṣu sā tīvraviraktiriṣyate |
viraktitīvratvanidānamāhurbhogyeṣu doṣekṣaṇameva santaḥ ||24||
pradṛśyate vastuni yatra doṣo na tatra puṃso'sti punaḥ pravṛttiḥ |
antarmahārogavatīṃ vijānanko nāma veśyāmapi rūpiṇīṃ vrajet ||25||
atrāpi cānyatra ca vidyamānapadārthasaṃmarśanameva kāryam |
yathāprakārārthaguṇābhimarṣanaṃ sandarśayatyeva tadīyadoṣam ||26||
kukṣau svamāturmalamūtramadhye sthitiṃ tadā viṭkṛmidaṃśanaṃ ca |
tadīyakaukṣeyakavahnidāhaṃ vicārya ko vā viratiṃ na yāti ||27||
svakīyaviṇmūtravisarjanaṃ taccottānagatyā śayanaṃ tadā yat |
bālagrahādyāhatibhākca śaiśavaṃ vicārya ko vā viratiṃ na yāti ||28||
svīyaiḥ paraistāḍanamajñabhāvamatyantacāpalyamasatkriyāṃ ca |
kumārabhāve pratiṣiddhavṛttiṃ vicārya ko vā viratiṃ na yāti ||29||
madoddhatiṃ mānyatiraskṛtiṃ ca kāmāturatvaṃ samayātilaṅghanam |
tāṃ tāṃ yuktvoditaduṣṭaceṣṭāṃ vicārya ko vā viratiṃ na yāti ||30||
virūpatāṃ sarvajanādavajñāṃ sarvatra dainyaṃ nijabuddhihainyam |
vṛddhatvasaṃbhāvitadurdaśāṃ tāṃ vicārya ko vā viratiṃ na yāti ||31||
pittajvarārśaḥkṣayagulmaśūlaśleṣmādirogoditatīvraduḥkham |
durgandhamasvāsthyamanūnacintāṃ vicārya ko vā viratiṃ na yāti ||32||
yamāvalokoditabhītikampamarmavyathocchvāsagatīśca vedanām |
prāṇaprayāṇe paridṛśyamānāṃ vicārya ko vā viratiṃ na yāti ||33||
aṅgāranadyāṃ tapane ca kumbhīpāke'pi vīcyāmasipatrakānane |
dūtairyamasya kriyamāṇabādhāṃ vicārya ko vā viratiṃ na yāti ||34||
puṇyakṣaye puṇyakṛto nabhaḥsthairnipātyamānānśithilīkṛtāṅgān |
nakṣatrarūpeṇa divaścyutaṃstānvicārya ko vā viratiṃ na yāti ||35||
vāyvarkavahnīndramukhānsurendrānīśograbhītyā grathitāntaraṅgān |
vipakṣalokaiḥ paridūyamānanvicārya ko vā viratiṃ na yāti ||36||
śrutyā niruktaṃ sukhatāratamyaṃ brahmāntamārabhya mahīmaheśam |
aupādhikaṃ tattu na vāstavaṃ cedālocya ko vā viratiṃ na yāti ||37||
sālokyasāmīpyasarūpatādibhedastu satkarmaviśeṣasiddhaḥ |
na karmasiddhasya tu nityateti vicārya ko vā viratiṃ na yāti ||38||
yatrāsti loke gatitāratamyamuccāvacatvānvitamatra tatkṛtam |
yatheha tadvatkhalu duḥkhamastītyālocya ko vā viratiṃ na yāti ||39||
ko nāma loke puruṣo vivekī vinaśvare tucchasukhe gṛhādau |
kuryādratiṃ nityamavekṣamāṇo vṛthaiva mohānmriyamāṇajantūm ||40||
sukhaṃ kimastyatra vicāryamāṇe gṛhe'pi vā yoṣiti vā padārthe |
māyātamo'ndhīkṛtacakṣuṣo ye ta eva muhyanti vivekaśūnyāḥ ||41||
avicāritaramaṇīyaṃ sarvamudumbaraphalopamaṃ bhogyam |
ajñānamupabhogyaṃ na tu tajjñānāṃ yoṣiti vā padārthe ||42||
gate'pi toye suṣiraṃ kulīro hātuṃ hyaśakto mriyate vimohāt |
yathā tathā gehasukhānuṣakto vināśamāyāti naro bhrameṇa ||43||
kośakrimistantubhirātmadehamāveṣṭya cāveṣṭya ca guptimicchan |
svayaṃ vinirgantumaśakta eva saṃstatastadante mriyate ca lagnaḥ ||44||
yathā tathā putrakalatramitrasnehānubandhairgrathito gṛhasthaḥ |
kadāpi vā tānparimucya gehādgantuṃ na śakto mriyate mudhaiva ||45||
kārāgṛhasyāsya ca ko viśeṣaḥ pradṛśyate sādhu vicāryamāṇe |
mukteḥ pratīpatvamihāpi puṃsaḥ kāntāsukhābhyutthitamohapāśaiḥ ||46||
gṛhaspṛhā pādanibaddhaśṛṅkhalā kāntāsutāśā paṭukaṇṭhapāśaḥ |
śīrṣe pataddhūryaśanirhi sākṣātprāṇāntahetuḥ prabalā dhanāśā ||47||
||5 ||asaktabuddhiḥ ||
āśāpāśaśatena pāśitapado notthātumeva kṣamaḥ kāmakrodhamadādibhiḥ pratibhaṭaiḥ saṃrakṣamāṇo'niśam |
saṃmohāvaraṇena gopanavataḥ saṃsārakārāgṛhānnirgatuṃ trividheṣaṇāparavaśaḥ kaḥ śaknuyādrāgiṣu ||48||
kāmāndhakāreṇa niruddhadṛṣṭirmuhyatyasatyapyabalāsvarūpe |
na hyandhadṛṣṭerasataḥ sato vā sukhatvaduḥkhatvavicāraṇāsti ||49||
śleṣodgāri mukhaṃ sravanmalavatī nāsāśrumallocanam
svedasrāvi malābhipūrṇamabhito durgandhaduṣṭaṃ vapuḥ |
anyadvaktumaśakyameva manasā mantuṃ kvacinnārhati
strīrūpaṃ kathamīdṛśaṃ sumanasāṃ pātrībhavennetrayoḥ ||50||
dūrādavekṣyagniśikhāṃ pataṅgo ramyatvabuddhyā vinipatya naśyati |
yathā tathā naṣṭadṛgeṣa kathaṃ nirīkṣet vimuktimārgam ||51||
kāmena kāntāṃ parigṛhya tadvajjano'pyayaṃ naśyati naṣṭadṛṣṭiḥ |
māṃsāsthimajjāmalamūtrapātraṃ striyaṃ svayaṃ ramyatayaiva paśyati ||52||
kāma eva yamaḥ sākṣātkāntā vaitaraṇī nadī |
vivekinaṃ mumukṣūṇāṃ nilayastu yamālayaḥ ||53||
yamālaye vāpi gṛhe'pi no nṛṇāṃ tāpatrayakleśanivṛttirasti |
kiṃcitsamālokya tu tadvirāmāṃ sukhātmanā paśyati mūḍhalokaḥ ||54||
yamasya kāmasya ca tāratamyaṃ vicāryamāṇe mahadasti loke |
hitaṃ karotyasya yamo'priyaḥ sankāmastvanarthaṃ kurute priyaḥ san ||55||
yamo'satāmeva karotyanarthaṃ satāṃ tu saukhyaṃ kurute hitaḥ san |
kāmaḥ satāmeva gatiṃ nirundhankarotyanarthaṃ hyasatāṃ nu kā kathā ||56||
viśvasya vṛddhi svayameva kāṃkṣanpravartakaṃ kāmijanaṃ sasarjam.
tenaiva lokaḥ parimuhyamānaḥ pravardhate candramaseva cābdhiḥ ||57||
kāmo nāma mahāñjagadbhramayitā sthitvāntaraṅge svayam
strī puṃsāvitaretarāṅgakaguṇairhāsasca bhāvaiḥ sphuṭam |
anyonyaṃ parimohya naijatamasā premānubandhena tau
baddhvā bhrāmayati prapañcaracanāṃ saṃvardhayanbrahmahā ||58||
ato'ntaraṅgasthitakāmavegādbhogye prvṛttiḥ svata eva siddhā |
sarvasya jantordhruvamanyathā cedabodhitartheṣu kathaṃ pravṛttiḥ ||59||
tenaiva sarvajantūnāṃ kāmanā balavattarā |
jīryatyapi ca dehe'sminkāmanā naiva jīryate ||60||
avekṣya viṣaye doṣaṃ buddhiyukto vicakṣaṇaḥ |
kāmapāśena yo muktaḥ sa mukteḥ pathi gocaraḥ ||61||
kāmasya vijayopāyaṃ sūkṣmaṃ vakṣyamahaṃ satām |
saṃkalpasya parityāga upāyaḥ sulabho mataḥ ||62||
śrute dṛṣṭe'pi vā bhogye yasminkasmiṃśca vastuni |
samīcīnatvadhītyāgātkāmo nodeti karhicit ||63||
kāmasya bījaṃ saṃkalpaḥ saṃkalpādeva jāyate |
bīje naṣṭe'ṅkura iva tasminnaṣṭe vinaśyati ||64||
na ko'pi samyaktvadhiyā vinaiva bhogyaṃ naraḥ kāmayituṃ samarthaḥ |
yatastataḥ kāmajayecchuretāṃ samyaktvabuddhiṃ viṣaye nihanyāt ||65||
bhogye naraḥ kāmajayecchuretāṃ sukhatvabuddhiṃ viṣaye nihanyāt |
yāvatsukhatvabhramadhīḥ padārthe tāvanna jetuṃ prabhaveddhi kāmam ||66||
saṃkalpānudaye heturyathābhūtārthadarśanam |
anarthacintanaṃ cābhyāṃ nāvakāśo'sya vidyate ||67||
ratne yadi śilābuddhirjāyate vā bhayaṃ tataḥ |
samīcīnatvadhīrnaiti nopādeyatvadhīrapi ||68||
yatārthadarśanaṃ vastunyanarthasyāpi cintanam |
saṃkalpasyāpi kāmasya tadvadhopāya iṣyate ||69||
||6 ||nirlobhatvam ||
dhanaṃ bhayanibandhanaṃ satataduḥkhasaṃvardhanam
pracaṇḍatarakardanaṃ sphuṭitabandhusaṃvardhanan |
viśiṣṭaguṇabādhanaṃ kṛpaṇadhīsamārādhanam
na muktigatisādhanaṃ bhavati nāpi hṛcchodhanam ||70||
rājñobhayaṃ coramayaṃ pramādādbhayaṃ tathā jñātibhayaṃ ca vastutaḥ |
dhanaṃ bhayagrastamanarthamūlaṃ satāṃ naiva sukhāya kalpate ||71||
ārjane rakṣaṇe dāne vyaye vāpi ca vastutaḥ |
duḥkhameva sadā nṝṇāṃ na dhanaṃ sukha sādhanam ||72||
satāmapi padārthasya lābhāllobhaḥ pravardhate |
viveko lupyate lobhāttasmiṃllupte vinaśyati ||73||
dahatyalābhe niḥsatvaṃ lābhe lobho dahatyamum |
tasmātsantāpakaṃ vittaṃ kasya saukhyaṃ prayacchati ||74||
bhogena mattatā jantordānena punarudbhavaḥ |
vṛthaivobhayathā vittaṃ nāstyeva gatiranyathā ||75||
dhanena madavṛddhiḥ syānmadena smṛtināśanam |
smṛtināśādbuddhināśo buddhināśātpraṇaśyati ||76||
sukhayati dhanamevetyantarāśāpiśācyā
dṛḍhataramupagūḍho mūḍhaloko jaḍātmā |
nivasati tadupānte santataṃ prekṣamāṇo
vrajati tadapi paścātprāṇametasya hṛtvā ||77||
saṃpanno'ndhavadeva kiṃcidaparaṃ no vīkṣate cakṣuṣā sadbhirvarjitamārga eva carati protsārito bāliśaiḥ |
tasminneva muhuḥ skhalanpratipadaṃ gatvāndhakūpe patatyasyāndhatvanivartakauṣadhamidaṃ dāridryamevāñjanam ||78||
lobhaḥ krodhaśca daṃbhaśca mado matsara eva ca |
vardhate vittasaṃprāptyā kathaṃ taccittaśodhanam ||79||
alābhāddviguṇaṃ duḥkhaṃ vittasya vyayasaṃbhave |
tato'pi triguṇaṃ duḥkhaṃ durvyaye viduṣāmapi ||80||
nityāhitena vittena bhayacintānapāyinā |
cittasvāsthyaṃ kuto jantogṛhasthenāhinā yathā ||81||
kāntāre vijane vane janapade setau nirītau ca vā
corairvāpi tathetarairnaravarairyukto viyukto'pi vā |
niḥsvaḥ svasthatayā sukhena vasati hyādrīyamāṇo janaiḥ
kliśnātyeva dhanī sadākulamatirbhītaśca putrādapi ||82||
tasmādanarthasya nidānamarthaḥ pumarthasiddhirna bhavatyanena |
tato vanānte nivasanti santaḥ saṃnyasya sarvaṃ pratikūlamartham ||83||
śraddhābhaktimatiṃ satīṃ guṇavatīṃ putrāñchṛtānsaṃmatānakṣayyaṃ vasudhānubhogavibhavaiḥ śrīsundaraṃ mandiram |
sarvaṃ naśvaramityavetya kavayaḥ śrutyuktibhiryuktibhiḥ saṃnyasyantyapare tu tatsukhamiti bhrāmyanti duḥkhārṇave ||84||
sukhamiti malarāśau ye ramante'tra gehe krimaya iva kalatrakṣetraputrānuṣaktyā |
surapada iva teṣāṃ naiva mokṣaprasaṅgastvapi tu nirayagarbhāvāsaduḥkhapravāhaḥ ||85||
yeṣāmāśā nirāśā syāddārāpatyadhanādiṣu |
teṣāṃ siddhyati nānyeṣaṃ mokṣāśābhimukhī gatiḥ ||86||
satkarmakṣatapāpmanāṃ śrutimatāṃ siddhātmanāṃ dhīmatām
nityānityapadārthaśodhanamidaṃ yuktyā muhuḥ kurvatām |
tasmādutthamahāviraktyasimatāṃ mokṣaikakāṅkṣāvatām
dhanyānāṃ sulabhaṃ striyādiviṣayeṣvāśālatācchedanam ||87||
saṃsāramṛtyorbalinaḥ praveṣṭuṃ dvārāṇi tu trīṇi mahānti loke |
kāntā ca jihvā kanakaṃ ca tāni ruṇaddhi yastasya bhayaṃ na mṛtyeḥ ||88||
muktiśrīnagarasya durjayataraṃ dvāraṃ yadastyādimam
tasya dve arare dhanaṃ ca yuvatī tābhyāṃ pinaddhaṃ dṛḍham ||
kāmākhyārgaladāruṇā balavatā dvāraṃ tadetattrayam
dhīro yastu bhinatti so'rhati sukhaṃ bhoktuṃ vimuktiśriyaḥ ||89||
ārūḍhasya vivekāśvaṃ tīvravairāgyakhaḍginaḥ |
titikṣāvarmayuktasya pratiyogī na dṛśyate ||90||
vivekajāṃ tīvraviraktimeva
mukternidānaṃ nigadanti santaḥ |
tasmādvivekī viratiṃ mumukṣuḥ
sampādayettāṃ prathamaṃ prayatnāt ||91||
pumānajātanirvedo dehabandhaṃ jihāsitum |
na hi śaknoti nirvedo bandhabhedo mahānasau ||92||
vairāgyarahitā eva yamālaya ivālaye |
kliśnanti trividhaistāpairmohitā api paṇḍitāḥ ||93||
śamo damastitikṣoparatiḥ śraddhā tataḥ param |
samādhānamiti proktaṃ ṣaḍevaite śamādayaḥ ||94||
||7 ||śamaḥ||
ekavṛttyaiva manasaḥ svalakṣye niyatasthitiḥ |
śama ityucyate sadbhiḥ śamalakṣaṇavedibhiḥ ||95||
uttamo madhyamaścaiva jaghanya iti ca tridhā |
nirūpito vipaścidbhiḥ tattallakṣaṇavedibhiḥ ||96||
svavikāraṃ parityajya vastumātratayā sthitiḥ |
manasaḥ sottamā śāntirbrahmanirvāṇalakṣaṇā ||97||
pratyakpratyayasantānapravāhakaraṇaṃ dhiyaḥ |
yadeṣā madhyamā śāntiḥ śuddhasattvaikalakṣaṇā ||98||
viṣayavyāpṛtiṃ tyaktvā śravaṇaikamanasthitiḥ |
manasaścetara śāntirmiśrasattvaikalakṣaṇā ||99||
prācyodīcyāṅgasadbhāve śamaḥ siddhyati nānathā |
tīvrā viraktiḥ prācyāṅgamudīcyāṅgaṃ damādayaḥ ||100||
kāmaḥ krodhaśca lobhaśaca mado mohaśca matsaraḥ |
na jitāḥ ṣaḍibhe yena tasya śāntirna siddhyati ||101||
śabdādiviṣayebhyo yo viṣavanna nivartate |
tīvramokṣecchayā bhikṣostasya śāntirna siddhyati ||102||
yena nārādhito devo yasya no gurvanugrahaḥ |
na vaśyaṃ hṛdayaṃ yasya tasya śāntirna siddhyati ||103||
||8 ||manaḥprasādaḥ ||
manaḥprasādasiddhyarthaṃ sādhanaṃ śrūyatāṃ budhaiḥ |
manaḥprasādo yatsattve yadabhāve na siddhyati ||104||
brahmacaryamahiṃsā ca dayā bhūteṣvavakratā |
viṣayeṣvativaitṛṣṇyaṃ śaucaṃ dambhavivarjanam ||105||
satyaṃ nirmamatā sthairyamabhimānavisarjanam |
īśvaradhyānaparatā brahmavidbhiḥ sahasthitiḥ ||106||
jñānaśāstraikaparatā samatā sukhaduḥkhayoḥ |
mānānāsaktirekāntaśīlatā ca mumukṣutā ||107||
yasyaitadvidyate sarvaṃ tasya cittaṃ prasīdati |
na tvetaddharmaśūnyasya prakārāntarakoṭibhiḥ ||108||
smaraṇaṃ darśanaṃ strīṇāṃ guṇakarmānukīrtanam |
samīcīnatvadhīstāsu prītiḥ saṃbhāṣaṇaṃ mithaḥ ||109||
sahavāsaśca saṃsargo'ṣṭadhā maithunaṃ viduḥ |
etadvilakṣaṇaṃ brahmacaryaṃ cittaprasādakam ||110||
ahiṃsā vāṅmanaḥkāyaiḥ prāṇimātrāprapīḍanam |
svātmavatsarvabhūteṣu kāyena manasā girā ||111||
anukaṃpā dayā saiva proktā vedāntavedibhiḥ |
karaṇatritayaṣvekarūpatā'vakratā matā ||112||
brahmādisthāvarānteṣu vairāgyaṃ viṣayeṣvanu |
yathaiva kākaviṣṭhāyāṃ vairāgyaṃ taddhi nirmalam ||113||
bāhyamābhyantaraṃ ceti dvividhaṃ śaucamucyate |

||9 ||saṃnyāsaḥ ||

sukhaṃ duḥkhaṃ vivekena tasya cittaṃ prasīdati ||366||

tīrthāṭanaṃ svāśramadharmaniṣṭhām |
yamānuṣaktiṃ niyamānuvṛttiṃ
cittaprasādāya vadanti tajjñāḥ ||368||
kaṭvamlalavaṇātyuṣṇatīkṣṇarūkṣavidhāyinām |
pūtiparyuṣitādīnāṃ tyāgaḥ sattvāya kalpate ||369||

sakṛducitahitoktiḥ svalpanidrāvihāraḥ |
