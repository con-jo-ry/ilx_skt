samajñānasādhanaṃ tu madhyamam‌ | svarūpendriyamapyuttamānāṃ
viṣayasvarūpe prakāre ca yathārthameva | adhamamadhyamānāṃ tu
svarūpamātre yathārthameva | prakāre tvayathārthaṃ miścaṃ ceti |
indriyārthayostu sannikarṣo'parokṣajñānahetuḥ ṣoḍhā bhidyata ityeke |
tadyathā | saṃyogaḥ, saṃyuktasamavāyaḥ, saṃyuktasamavetasamavāyaḥ,
samavāyaḥ, samavetasamavāyo viśeṣaṇaviśeṣyabhāvaśceti | tatra
cakṣuḥsparśanayorghaṭādidravyairmanasaścātmanā saṃyogaḥ | teṣāṃ
svaviṣayagataguṇakarmasāmānyaiḥ saṃyuktasamavāyaḥ | tathā
ghrāṇarasanayorgandharasābhyām‌ | eteṣāṃ svaviṣayaguṇakarmagatasāmānyaiḥ
saṃyuktasamavetasamavāyaḥ |
śrotrasya śabdena samavāyaḥ | tadgatasāmānyaiḥ samavetasamavāyaḥ |
eteṣāmetatpañcavidhasambandhasambandhārthasyābhāvasamavāyābhyāṃ
pañcavidho viśeṣaṇaviśeṣyabhāva iti | tadasat‌ | guṇādīnāṃ
guṇyādibhirabhedena samavāyābhāvāt‌ | ātmanastaddharmāṇāṃ ca
sākṣiviṣayatvena manoviṣayatvābhāvāt‌ | varṇātmakasya śabdasya
dravyatvenākāśaviśeṣaguṇatvābhāvāt‌ | ataḥ sarvendriyāṇāṃ
svasvaviṣayaiḥ svasvaviṣayapratiyogikābhāvena ca sākṣādeva
raśmidvārā saṅnnikarṣaḥ |
pratyakṣadvaividhyam‌
nirvikalpakasavikalpakabhedena ca dvividhaṃ pratyakṣamityācakṣate |
tatra vastusvarūpamātrāvabhāsakaṃ nirvikalpakam‌ | viśiṣṭākāragocaraṃ
savikalpakam‌ | tat‌ aṣṭavidham‌ | tatra dravyavikalpo yathā daṇḍīti |
guṇavikalpo yathā śukla iti | kriyāvikalpo yathā gacchatīti |
jātivikalpo yathā gauriti | viśeṣavikalpo yathā viśiṣṭaḥ
paramāṇuriti | samavāyivikalpo yathā paṭasamavāyavantastantava iti |
nāmavikalpo yathā devadatta iti | abha vavikalpo yathā ghaṭābhāvavadbhūtalamiti |
etadapyasat‌ | viśeṣasamavāyayoraprāmāṇikatvāt‌ | nāmnaḥ
paścātsmaraṇenābhāvasya ca pratiyogismaraṇādhīnajñānatvena tadvikalpasya
prathamākṣisaṅnnikarṣānantaramevotpattyabhāve'pi dravyādivikalpānāṃ
prathamamevotpattau bādhakābhāvena nirvikalpakānupapatteḥ |
pratyakṣasya phalam‌
hānopādānopekṣābuddhayaḥ pratyakṣasya phalamiti kecidāhuḥ | tadapyasat‌ |
tāsāmanumānaphalatvāt‌ | tathāhi | kaṇṭakādisvarūpamātraṃ pradarśya
pratyakṣaṃ nivartate | athedānīṃ pūrvānubhūtasya kaṇṭakāderaniṣṭasādhanatvamanubhūtaṃ smṛtvā'sya ca kaṇṭakāditvena tadanumimānasya
hānabuddhirupajāyate | evaṃ kadalīphalādāviṣṭasādhanatvānumānānantaramupādānabuddhirutpadyate | tṛṇādau caudāsīnyamanumāyopekṣābuddhiḥ
pratipadyate | ato viśiṣṭaviṣayasākṣātkāra eva pratyakṣasya phalamiti |
iti śrīmajjayatīrthapūjyacaraṇaviracita
pramāṇapaddhatau pratyakṣaparicchedaḥ samāptaḥ |

anumānaparicchedaḥ
anumānalakṣaṇam‌
nirdoṣopapattiranumānam‌ | upapattirvyāpyaṃ yuktiliṅgamiti paryāyaḥ |
avinābhāvo vyāptiḥ | sāhacaryaniyama iti yāvat‌ | vyāpteḥ karma vyāpyam‌ |
tasyāḥ kartṛ vyāpakam‌ | yathā dhūmasyāgninā vyāptiravyabhicaritaḥ
sambandhaḥ | yatra dhūmaḥ tatra agniriti niyamāt‌ | tatra dhūmo vyāpyaḥ |
agnirvyāpakaḥ |
catuṣṭayī khalu dharmāṇāṃ gatiḥ | kecitsamavṛttayo yathā niṣiddhatvapāpasādhanatve | yatra niṣiddhatvaṃ tatra pāpasādhanatvaṃ, yatra
pāpasādhanatvaṃ tatra niṣiddhatvamiti niyamāt‌ | tatra dvayorapi
anyonyaṃ vyāpyavyāpakabhāvaḥ | kecit‌ nyūnādhikavṛttayaḥ | yathā
dhūmavattavāgnimatve | tatra nyūnavṛttidhūmavatvaṃ vyāpyameva |
adhikavṛtyagnimatvaṃ vyāpakameva | kecit‌ parasparaparihāreṇaiva
vartante | yathā gotvāśvatve | yato yatra gotvaṃ na tatra
sarvathā'śvatvam‌ | yatra cāśvatvaṃ na tatra sarvathā gotvam‌ |
teṣāṃ nānyonyaṃ vyāpyavyāpakabhāvaḥ | sambandhasyaiva abhāvāt‌ |
kecit‌ kvacit‌ samāviṣṭā api kvacit‌ parasparaparihāreṇaiva
vartante | yathā pācakatvaṃpuruṣatve | tayoḥ puruṣaviśeṣe
samoveśe'pi pācakatvaṃ puruṣatvaparihāreṇa strīṣu vartate |
puruṣatvamapi pācakatvaparihāreṇāpacati puruṣe vartate | te'pi
nānyonyaṃ vyāpyavyāpakabhāvavantaḥ | satyapi sambandhe
parasparavyabhicāritvāt‌ | tatra vyāpyo dharmo vyāpakapramitisādhanaṃ
bhavannanumānamityucyate | vyāpakaścānumeya iti |
nanu parvate vartamāno dhūmaḥ gṛhāntarvartinaḥ puruṣasya
kimityagnipramāṃ na janayati | ucyate | na pratyajñavadanumānamajñātakaraṇam‌ | kintu samyak‌ jñātameva | tathā'pi nārikeladvīpavāsino
deśāntaraṃ gatasya dhūmapramitāvati kasmānnāgnipramodayaḥ | ucyate |
tasya dhūmasvarūpajñāne'pyagnivyāptatayā tajjañānābhāvāt‌ |
jñātavyāptikasyāpi kutaḥ kadācidagnipramā'nutpattiḥ | vyāptismaraṇābhāvāt‌ |
vyāptismaraṇavataḥ parvate dhūmaṃ dṛṣṭavato'pi kathaṃ
parvatādanyatrāgnipramā'nudbhavaḥ | vyāptiprakāramapekṣya liṅgajñānasya
liṅgajñānajanakatvaniyamāt‌ |
vyāptibhedāḥ
tathāhi | kayościt‌ samānadeśakālayorvyāptiḥ | yathā rasasya rūpeṇa |
kayościt‌ samānadeśatve'pi bhinnakālayoḥ, yathā dhūmasyāgninā |
kayościtsamānakālatvepi bhinnadeśayoḥ | yathā kṛttikodayasya
rohiṇyudayāsattayā | kayościt‌ bhinnadeśakālayoḥ | yathā'dhodeśe
nadīpūrasyordhavadeśavṛṣṭyā | kasyacitkādācitkasya samānadeśatve'pi
sārvakālīnena | yathā patanasya gurutvena | kasyacitsamānadeśatve'pi
sārvakālikasya kādācitkena | yathā śarīratvasya vināśena |
kasyacitpradeśavartino vyāpyavartinā | yathā saṃyogasya dravyatvena |
kasyacidvyāpyavartinaḥ pradeśavartinā | yathā rūpasya saṃyogena |
kayościt‌ ekāvayavivartinorapyavayavabhedena | yathā
tulonnamanāvanamanayorityādi | tathā ca vyāptijñānatatsmaraṇasahitaṃ
liṅgasya samyagjñānaṃ, samyagjñātaṃ vā liṅgavyāptiprakārānusāreṇa
samucitadeśādau liṅgapramāṃ janayadanumānamityuktaṃ bhavati | ata eva
liṅgasvarūpasya jñātatve'pi deśaviśeṣādisaṃsṛṣṭatayā
jñāpakatvānnānumānasya vaiyarthyam‌ | tataścānumānasya dvayaṃ
sāmarthyam‌ | vyāptiḥ, samucitadeśādau siddhiśceti | na tu
pakṣadharmatāniyamaḥ |
vyāptijñānajanakaṃ pramāṇam‌
nanu vyāptijñānaṃ kena pramāṇena jāyate | yathāyathaṃ pratyakṣānumānāgamairiti
brūmaḥ | tatra tāvat‌ dhūmasyāgninā vyāptiḥ pratyakṣagamyā | tathāhi |
mahānasādau dhūmāgnyoḥ sāhacaryaṃ paśyataḥ puruṣasyaiṣa vimarśo
jāyate | kimatraivetau dhūmāgnī sahacaritāvanyatra deśāntare kālāntare
ca, etajjātīyau parasparaparihāreṇa vartete, utānyatara evānyataraparihāreṇa
kiṃvā sakaladeśakālayoḥ sahacaritāveveti | tataḥ bhūyobhūyaḥ dhūmaṃ
paśyan‌ agniṃ paśyati | agnyabhāve ca dhūmābhāvam‌ | tathā
dhūmābhāve'pi kvacidagnisadbhāvam‌ | tataḥ punareva vicāro bhavati |
yathā bahuṣu sthaleṣu dhūmena sahacarito'pyagniḥ kvacittaṃ parityajya
vartate | tathā dhūmo matparicitasthaleṣvagninā saha vartamāno'pi
kvacittaṃ parityajya vartate | kiṃ vā sarvatra tatsahacarita eveti |
tataḥ punareśā buddhirutpadyate | agnerdhūmasambandhe'styārdrendhana
saṃyoga upādhiḥ | sa ca dhūmavyāpakaḥ | svayaṃ vyāvartamānastaṃ
vyāvartitavān‌ | na puraragnim‌ | tadavyāpakatvāt‌ | tathā
dhūmasyāgnisambandhe yadi kaścidupādhi syāt‌ tadā so'pyagniṃ
vyabhicariṣyati | na cennirnimittasya sambandhasya dhūmasvabhāvatvena
na kvāpi tadabhāvo bhaviṣyatītyevaṃ niścitya mahānasādāvupādhigaveṣaṇe
pravartate |
tatra keciddharmā ubhayavyāpakāḥ | yathā prameyatvam‌ | naitatsambandhaṃ
vighaṭayitumīśate | kecidubhayāvyāpakāḥ | yathā mahānasatvam‌ |
te'pinaitasaṃbandhavighaṭanāya samarthāḥ | keciddhūmavyāpakā agnyavyāpakāḥ |
yathā ārdrendhanasaṃyogaḥ | te'pi agnerdhūmaviyojane śaktā api na
dhūmasyāgniviyojane | yasvagnervyāpako dhūmasya tvavyāpakaḥ sa svayaṃ
vyāvartamānaḥ śakṣyatyetatsabandhaṃ vighaṭayitum‌ | sa tu nāstyeva |
anupalambhāt‌ | atīndriyopi pramāṇāntaravedyaḥ syāt‌ | na ca
tatpramāṇamupalabhyate | na cāprāmāṇikaḥ śaṅkāmapyadhirohati | ato
nātropādhirastīti nirṇītavato'vinābhāvapramitirutpadyate |
nanu evaṃ sati na pratyakṣaṃ vyāptigrāhakam‌ | maivam‌ | yataḥ
sāhacaryagrāhiṇaḥ pratyakṣasya bhūyodarśanavyabhicārādarśanopādhyabhāvaniścayāḥ
sahakāriṇaḥ | na ca sahakāribhede'pi pratyabhijñāyāmiva pramāṇāntaratvam‌ |
vartamānasannikṛṣṭamātragrāhi pratyakṣaṃ kathamatītādyāspadānāṃ vyāptiṃ
gṛhṇīyāditi cenna | sahakārisāmarthyena karaṇānāṃ śaktyantarāvirbhāvasya
bahulamupalambhāt‌ |
kiñca pratītasya dhūmasyāgnisambandhe svābhāvikatayā niścite
yadyanyatrāpi dhūmaḥ syāt‌ tarhi tasyāpyevamityevaṃ vyāptigraho
bhavatītyataḥ kā'trānupapattiḥ | etena prameyatvābhidheyatvayoḥ
vyāptiṃ gṛhṇataḥ sārvajñaprasaṅga ityapi parāstam‌ | kuta
etatsarvaṃ kalpyata iti cet‌ | kāryadarśanādeva | asti tāvad‌
dhūmadarśanānantaramagnijñānam‌ | na ca tadayathārtham‌ |
saṃvāditvāt‌ | na cānyadarśanenānyajñānaṃ nirnimittaṃ
bhavitumarhati | atiprasaṅgāt‌ | na ca vyāptipramāṃ
samucitadeśatvapramāṃ cāpahāya nimittāntaramastīti
kāryamevaitatkalpayati | yathā khalu śilāśakalādāvanupalabdhāmapi
kṣitisalilādisahakārisamudbhūtāṃ bījasya śaktimaṅkuradarśanaṃ
kalpayatīti | anumānagamyāṃ tu vyāptimuttaratrodāhariṣyāmaḥ |
na ca tasyāpyanumānasya vyāptyapekṣāyāmanavasthā | antataḥ
pratyakṣāgamamūlatvāt‌ | 'brāhmaṇo na hantavyo', 'gaurna padā
spraṣṭavyo'tyāgamagamyā vyāptiḥ | na cātra kvacitkṣudropadravo,
nāpi dṛṣṭāntāpekṣeti |
anumānatraividhyam‌
trividhamanumānam‌ | kāryānumānaṃ, kāraṇānumānaṃ, akāryakāraṇānumānaṃ
ceti | tatrādyaṃ yathā | dhūmo'gneḥ | dvitīyaṃ yathā |
viśiṣṭameghonnatirvṛṣṭeḥ | iyāṃstu viśeṣaḥ | kāryaṃ
kāraṇamātramanumāpayati | kāraṇaṃ tu samagrameva kāryamiti | tṛtīyaṃ
yathā | raso rūpasyeti | punardvividham‌ | dṛṣṭaṃ, sāmānyato
dṛṣṭaṃ ceti | tatra pratyakṣayogyārthānumāpakaṃ dṛṣṭam‌ |
yathā dhūmo'gneḥ | pratyakṣāyogyārthānumāpakaṃ sāmānyato dṛṣṭam‌ |
yathā rūpādijñānaṃ cakṣurādeḥ | athavā, yathābhūtayoḥ vyāptigrahaṇaṃ,
tathābhūtayoreva liṅgaliṅgibhāve dṛṣṭam‌ | yathā dhūmāgnyoḥ |
vyāpyavyāpakayoranyādṛśatve'pi tatsāmānyākārānugamena
liṅgaliṅgibhāve sāmānyato dṛṣṭam‌ | yathā kṛṣīvalasya
karṣāṇādipravṛtterdhānyādi phalavatvadarśanena yajñādeḥ
svargādiphalānumānam‌ | prekṣāvatpravṛttitvaphalavattvasāmānyānugamāt‌ |
punaḥ anumānadvaividhyam‌
punardvividham‌ | sādhanānumānaṃ dūṣaṇānumānaṃ ceti | tatrādyaṃ yathā |
dhūmapramityā'gnipramitisādhanam‌ | dūṣaṇānumānamapi dvedhā |
duṣṭipramitisādhanaṃ, tarkaśceti | tatra ādyaṃ yathā | nedaṃ
svasādhyasādhanasamartham‌ | pramāṇabādhitatvādityādi |
kasyaciddharmasyāṅgīkāre'rthāntarasyāpādanaṃ tarkaḥ |
tasya pañcāṅgāni | āpādakasyāpādyena vyāptiḥ, pratitarkeṇāpratighātaḥ
āpādyasyāniṣṭatvam‌ | tat‌ dvividham‌ | prāmāṇikahānamaprāmāṇikakalpanaṃ
ceti | tatrādyaṃ tredhā | dṛṣṭānanumitaśrutahānabhedāt‌ |
dvitīyamapyadṛṣṭānanumitāśrutakalpanābhedāt‌ trividham‌ |
etadeva kalpyānekatve kalpanāgauravamityucyate | āpādyasya viparyaye
paryavasānaṃ, parasyānanukūlatvaṃ ceti | āpādyamaniṣṭaṃ punaḥ pañcadhā
bhidyate | ātmāśrayatvamanyonyāśrayatvaṃ cakrakāśrayatvamanavasthā,
kevalāniṣṭaṃ ceti | tadbhedādāttapadāpādanarūpastarko'pi pañcavidhaḥ |
tatra tasyaivotpattijñaptyarthaṃ tadutpattijñaptyapekṣāṅgīkāre svāpekṣayā
pūrvabhāvitvarūpamātmāśrayatvamāpādyate | yadi ghaṭo'yamasyaiva
ghaṭasyotpādakaḥ syāttarhi svatopi pūrvabhāvī syāt‌ | yadyasya kāraṇaṃ
tattato'pi pūrvabhāvi dṛṣṭam‌ | yathā paṭakāraṇaṃ tantavastadapekṣayā
pūrvabhāvinaḥ | na cātra pratikūlastarko'sti | yadi ghaṭasya ghaṭa eva
kāraṇaṃ na syāt‌ tarhīdamāpadyata iti | aniṣṭaṃ cedam‌ | svāpekṣayā
pūrvabhāvitvasyāprāmāṇikatvāt‌ | vyāhatatvenāṅgīkartumanucitatvācca |
pūrvabhāvitvaṃ hi tadabhāvavati kāle bhāvaḥ | na ca svābhāvavati kāle
svayaṃ bhavatīti na vyāhatam‌ | na cetpūrvabhāvitvamaṅgīkriyate tadā na
svasya svayaṃ kāraṇam‌ | na caivamevāpādakaṃ pratyāpādayituṃ śakyata iti
pañcāṅgāni | evaṃ jñaptāvapi tajjañānasya tajjañānaṃ prati
pūrvabhāvitvamāpādyam‌ | dvayoritaretare kāraṇatvāṅgīkāre tvanyonyāpekṣayā
pūrvabhāvitvarūpamanyonyāśrayatvamāpādyam‌ | bahūnāṃ
cakravatkāryakāraṇabhāvābhyupagame pūrvabhāvitvalakṣaṇaṃ cakrakāśrayatvam‌ |
anavasthitāsiddhakāraṇāpekṣāyāṃ mūlakṣayāparanāmakapratītakāryavilopalakṣaṇānavasthā |
kevalāniṣṭāpādanamapi tredhā | dṛṣṭānumitaśrutahānāpādanabhedāt‌ |
adṛṣṭānānumitāśrutakalpanāpādanabhedācca | etadeva kalpyānekatve
kalpanāgauravamityucyate | dṛṣṭahānaṃ yathā | yadi parvato
niragnikastarhi nirdhūmaḥ syāditi | evamanyasyāpyudāharaṇaṃ śāstre
draṣṭavyam‌ | anumānatve'pi tarkasyāpādakāsiddhiradūṣaṇam‌ |
parābhyupagamamātrasya tatra siddhipadārthatvāt‌ |
evamāpādyasya pramāṇavirodho'pasiddhāntaśca na dūṣaṇam‌ | nirdhūmo
bhavatītyasādhanāt‌ | yathā sādhanānumāne na vyāpti mātraṃ, nāpi pakṣa
dharmatāmātraṃ sādhyapramitisādhanaṃ, kintu militameva | evamaniṣṭāpādanaṃ
viparyaye paryavasānaṃ cobhayaṃ militamevānumitisādhanaṃ bhavatīti tarkasya
prāmāṇyamupapadyate | so'yaṃ kvacidviparītaśaṅkānirasanadvāreṇa
pramāṇānāmanugrāhako'pi bhavatīti |
paramatadūṣaṇam‌
kevalānvayikevalavyatirekyanvayavyatirekibhedāttarividhamanumānamityeke |
sādhyadharmaviśiṣṭo dharmī pakṣaḥ | tatra vyāptyāvartamānaniścitasādhyasamānadharmādharmī sapakṣaḥ | tatra sarvasmin‌ ekadeśe vartamānam‌ |
sādhyatatsamāna dharmarahito dharmī vipakṣaḥ | tadrahitaṃ kevalānvayai |
tat‌ dvividham‌ | sarvasapakṣatadekadeśavṛttibhedāt‌ | śabdo'bhidheyaḥ
prameyatvāditi sapakṣavyāpakam‌ | sarvasminnabhidheyatayā sampratipanne
prameyatvasya vṛtteḥ | guṇatvāditi sapakṣaikadeśavṛtti | rūpādau
vṛtterghaṭādāvavṛtteḥ |
dvividhā kila vyāptiḥ | anvayato vyatirekataśceti | sādhanasya sādhyena
vyāptiranvayaḥ | sādhyābhāvasya sādhanābhāvena vyāptiḥ vyatirekaḥ | tatra
asya kevalamanvaya evāsti | yatprameyaṃ tadabhidheyaṃ, yathā ghaṭa iti | na
tatra vyatirekaḥ | yadabhidheyaṃ na bhavati tatprameyaṃ na bhavatīti
nidarśanābhāvāt‌ | sarvasyāpyabhidheyatvenānabhidheyāsambhavāt‌ |
tenaitat‌ kevalānvayītyucyate |
pakṣavyāpakamavidyamānasapakṣaṃ sarvasmādvipakṣād‌ vyāvṛttaṃ kevalavyatireki |
yathā jīvaccharīrajātaṃ sātmakaṃ prāṇādimattavāditi | asya hi yatsātmakaṃ na
bhavati tatprāṇādimanna bhavati, yathā ghaṭa iti vyatireka evāsti | na tu
yatprāṇādimat‌ tatsātmakamityanvayaḥ | sarvasyāpi jīvaccharīrasya
pakṣatvāt‌ | anyasya sarvasya nirātmakatvāt‌ | tenaitat‌ kevalavyatirekītyucyate |
pakṣavyāpakaṃ sapakṣavṛtti sarvavipakṣavyāvṛttamanvayavyatirekīti | tadapi
