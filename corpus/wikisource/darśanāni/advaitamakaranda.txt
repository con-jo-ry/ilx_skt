Text:


kaṭākṣakiraṇācānta
namanmohābdhaye namaḥ|
anantānandakṛṣṇāya
jaganmaṅgalamūrtaye ||1||

ahamasmi sadā bhāmi
kadācinnāhamapriyaḥ|
brahmaivāhamataḥ siddhaṃ
saccidānandalakṣaṇam ||2||

mayyevodeti cidvyomni
jagadgandharvapattanam|
ato'haṃ na kathaṃ brahma
sarvajñaṃ sarvakāraṇam ||3||

na svataḥ pratyabhijñānān
niraṃśatvānna cānyataḥ|
na cāśrayavināśānme
vināśaḥ syādanāśrayāt ||4||

na śoṣaploṣavikleda
cchedāścinnabhaso mama|
satyairapyanilāgnyambhaḥ
śastraiḥ kimuta kalpitaiḥ ||5||

abhārupasya viśvasya
bhānaṃ bhāsaṃnidhervinā|
kadācinnāvakalpeta
bhā cāhaṃ tena sarvagaḥ ||6||

na hi bhānādṛte satvaṃ
narte bhānaṃ cito'citaḥ|
citsambhedo'pi nādhyāsāt
ṛte tenāhamadvayaḥ ||7||

na deho nendriyaṃ cāhaṃ
na prāṇo na mano na dhīḥ|
mamatāparīrabdhatvāt
akrīḍatvādidaṃ dhiyaḥ ||8||

sākṣī sarvānvitaḥ preyān
ahaṃ nāhaṃ kadācana|
pariṇāmapariccheda
paritāpairūpaplavāt ||9||

supte'hami na dṛśyante
duḥkhadoṣapravṛttayaḥ|
atastasyaiva saṃsāro
na me saṃsartṛsākṣiṇaḥ ||10||

suptaḥ suptiṃ na jānāti
nāsupte svapnajāgarau|
jāgratsvapnasuṣuptīnāṃ
sākṣyato'hamatadṛśaḥ ||11||

vijñānaviratiḥ suptis
tajjanma svapnajāgarau|
tatsākṣiṇaḥ kathaṃ me syur
nityajñānasya te trayaḥ ||12||

ṣaḍ vikāravatāṃ vettā
nirvikāro'hamanyathā|
tadvikārānusandhānaṃ
sarvathā nāvakalpate ||13||

tena tena hi rūpeṇa
jāyate līyate murhuḥ|
vikāri vastu tasyaiṣām
anusandhātṛtā kutaḥ ||14||

na ca svajanma nāśaṃ vā
draṣṭurhati kaścana|
tau hi prāguttarābhāva
caramaprathamakṣaṇau ||15||

na prakāśe'hamityuktir
yatprakāśanibandhanā|
svaprakāśaṃ tamātmānam
aprakāśaḥ kathaṃ spṛśet ||16||

tathāpyābhāti ko'pyeṣa
vicārābhāvajīvanaḥ|
avaśyāyaścidākāśe
vicārārkodayāvadhiḥ ||17||

ātmājñānamahānidrā
jṛmbhite'sminjaganmaye|
dīrghasvapne sphurantyete
svargamokṣādivibhramāḥ ||18||

jaḍājaḍavibhāga'yam
ajaḍe mayi kalpitaḥ|
bhittibhāge same citra
carācaravibhāgavat ||19||

cetyoparāgarupā me
sākṣitāpi na tātvikī|
upalakṣaṇameveyaṃ
nistaraṅgacidambudheḥ ||20||

amṛtābdherna me jīrṇir
mṛṣā ḍiṇḍīrajanmabhiḥ|
sphaṭikārdrerna me rāgaḥ
svāpnasaṃdhyābhravibhramaiḥ ||21||

svarūpameva me satvaṃ
na tu dharmo nabhastvavat|
madanyasya sato'bhāvān
na hi sā jātiriṣyate ||22||

svarūpameva me jñānaṃ
na guṇaḥ sa guṇo yadi|
anātmatvamasatvaṃ vā
jñeyājñeyatvayoḥ patet ||23||

ahameva sukhaṃ nānyad
anyaccennaiva tat sukham|
amadarthaṃ na hi preyo
madarthaṃ na svataḥ priyam ||24||

na hi nānāsvarūpaṃ syād
ekaṃ vastu kadācana|
tasmādakhaṇḍa evāsmi
vijahajjāgatīṃ bhidām ||25||

parokṣatāpariccheda
śābalyāpohanirmalam|
tadasīti girā lakṣyam
ahamekarasaṃ mahaḥ ||26||

upaśāntajagajjīva
śiṣyācāryeśvarabhramam|
svataḥ siddhamanādyantaṃ
paripūrṇamahaṃ mahaḥ ||27||

lakṣmīdharakaveḥ sukti
śaradambhojasambhṛtaḥ|
advaitamakarando'yaṃ
vidvadbhṛṅgairnipīyatām ||28||
