oṃ saha nāvastu | saha nau bhunaktu |
saha vīryaṃ karavāvahai |

oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ ||
purastādbrahmaṇastasya viṣṇoradbhutakarmaṇaḥ |
rahasyaṃ brahmavidyāyā dhṛtāgniṃ saṃpracakṣate ||

tatra devāstrayaḥ proktā lokā vedāstrayo'gnayaḥ |

akārasya śarīraṃ tu vyākhyātaṃ brahmavādibhiḥ ||
