bhāvopaniṣat
|| atha bhāvopaniṣata||
oṃ bhadraṃ karṇebhiḥ śṛṇuyāma devāḥ |
bhadraṃ paśyemākṣabhiryajatrāḥ |
sthirairaṅgaistuṣṭuvāsastanūbhiḥ |
vyaśema devahitaṃ yadāyuḥ |
svasti na indro vṛddhaśravāḥ | svasti naḥ pūṣā viśvavedāḥ |
svasti nastārkṣyo'riṣṭanemiḥ | svasti no bṛhaspatirdadhātu |
oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ ||
ātmānamakhaṇdamaṇdalākāramavṛtya sakalabrahmāndamandalaṃ
svaprakāśaṃ dhyāyet | śrīguruḥ sarvakāraṇabhūtā śakttiḥ || 1||
tena navarandhrarūpo dehaḥ || 2||
navacakrarūpaṃ śrīcakram || 3||
vārāhī pitṛrūpā kurukullā balidevatā mātā || 4||
puruṣā|rthāḥ sāgarāḥ || 5|| deho navaratnadvīpaḥ || 6||
tvagādi sapttadhāturomasaṃyukttaḥ || 7||
saṃkalpāḥ kalpataravastejaḥ kalpakodyānam || 8||
rasanayā bhāvyamānā madhurāmlatikttakaṭukaṣāyalavaṇarasāḥ ṣaḍṛtavaḥ || 9||
jñānamardhyam jñeyam havirjñātā hotā
jñātṛjñānajñeyānāmabhedabhavanam śrīcakrapūjanam || 10||
niyatiḥ śrṛṅgārādayo rasā aṇimādayaḥ || 11||
kāmakrodhalobhamohamadamātsa|ryapuṇyapāpamayā
brāhmyādyayaṣaṭaśakttayaḥ || 12||
ādharanavakam mudrāśakttayaḥ || 13||
pṛthivyaptejovāivākāśāśrotratvakcakṣurjihvaghrāṇa
vākpāṇipādapāyūpasthāni manovikārāḥ
kāmākarṣiṇyādi ṣodaśa śakttayaḥ || 14||
vacanādānāgamanavisargānandahānopādānopekṣākhya
bhuddhayo'naṅgakusumādyaṣṭau || 15||
alambusā kuhurviśvodarā vāraṇā hastijihvā yaśovatī payasvinī
gāndhārī pūṣā śaṅkhinī sarasvatīḍā piṅgalā suṣumnā ceti
caturdaśa nāḍyaḥ sarvasaṃkṣhobhiṇyadi caturdaśaśakttayaḥ || 16||
prāṇāpānavyānodānasamānanāgakū|rmakṛkaradevadattadhanaṃjayā
daśavāyavaḥ sarvasiddhipradādibahirdaśāradevatāḥ || 17||
etadvāyusaṃsargakopādhibhedhena recakaḥ pācakaḥ śoṣako
dāhakaḥ plāvaka iti prāṇamukhyavena paṃcadhā jaṭharāgnirbhavati|| 18||
kṣāraka uddhārakaḥ kṣobhako jṛṃbhako mohaka iti
nāgaprādhānyena paṃcabidhāste manuṣyāṇāṃ dehagā
bhakṣhyabhojyaśoṣyalehyapeyātmakapañcavidhamannaṃ pācayanti|| 19||
etā daśavahnikalāḥ sarvajñādyā antardaśāradevatāḥ || 20||
śītoṣṇāsukhaduḥkhecchāḥ sattvaṃ rajastamo
vaśinyādiśaktthayoaṣtau || 21||
śabdādi tanmāttrāḥ paṃcapuṣpabāṇāḥ || 22|| mana ikṣhudhanuḥ || 23||
rāgaḥ pāśaḥ || 24|| dveṣo'ṅkuśaḥ || 25||
avyaktta mahadahaṃkārāḥ kāmekṣvarī vajreśvarī
bhagamālinyo'ntasttrikoṇagā devatāḥ || 26||
nirupādhikasaṃvideva kāmeśvara || 27||
sadānandapūrṇa svātmeva paradevatā lalitā || 28||
lauhityametasya sarvasya vimarśa || 29||
ananyacittatvena ca siddhiḥ || 30|| bhāvanāyāḥ kriyā upacaraḥ || 31||
ahaṃ tvamasti nāsti kartavyamakartavyamupāsitavyamiti
vikalpānāmātmani vilāpanam homaḥ || 32||
bhavanāviṣayāṇāmabhedabhavanā tarpaṇam || 33||
paṃcadaśatithirūpeṇa kālasya pariṇāmāvalokanam || 34||
evamaṃ muhūrtattritayaṃ muhūrtadvitayaṃ muhūrtamāttraṃ vā
bhāvanāparo jīvanmuktto bhavati sa eva śivayogīti gadyate|| 35||
ādimatenāntaścakrabhāvanāḥ pratipāditāḥ || 36||
ya evaṃ veda so'tharvaśiro'dhīte || 37||
ityupaniṣat ||
oṃ bhadraṃ karṇebhiḥ śṛṇuyāma devāḥ | bhadraṃ
paśyemākṣabhiryajatrāḥ|
sthirairaṅgaistuṣṭuvāsastanūbhiḥ | vyaśema devahitaṃ yadāyuḥ |
svasti na indro vṛddhaśravāḥ | svasti naḥ pūṣā viśvavedāḥ|
svasti nastārkṣyo'riṣṭanemiḥ | svasti no bṛhaspatirdadhātu|
oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ ||
|| iti bhāvopaniṣat ||
