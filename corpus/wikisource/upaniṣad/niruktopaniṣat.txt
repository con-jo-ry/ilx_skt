niruktopaniṣat
sa yadyanurudhyate tadbhavati | yadi dharmo'nurudhyate tadevodbhavati | yadi jñānamanurudhyate tadamṛto bhavati | yadi kāmamanurudhyate saṃcaratāṃ imāṃ yoniṃ saṃdadhyāttadidamatra manaḥ śleṣmaretasaḥ saṃbhavati | śleṣmaṇo rasaḥ | rasācchoṇitam | śoṇitānmāṃsam | māṃsānmedaḥ | medasaḥ snāvā | snāvo'sthīni | asthibhyo majjā | majjāto retaḥ | tadidaṃ yonau retaḥ siktaṃ puruṣaḥ saṃbhavati | śukrātireke pumān bhavati | śoṇitātireke strī bhavati | dvābhyāṃ samena napuṃsako bhavati | śukrabhinnena yamo bhavati | śukraśoṇitasaṃyogānmātṛpitṛsaṃyogācca kathamidaṃ śarīraṃ paraṃ saṃyamyate | saumyo bhavatyekarātroṣitaṃ kalalaṃ bhavati | pañcarātrādbudbudaḥ | saptarātrāt peśī | dvisaptarātrādarbudaḥ | paṃcaviṃśatirātrasthito yonau ghano bhavati | māsamātrāt kaṭhino bhavati | dvimāsābhyantare śiraḥ saṃpadyate | māsatrayeṇa grīvāvyādeśaḥ | māsacatuṣkeṇa tvagvyādeśaḥ | pañcame māse nakharomavyādeśaḥ | ṣaṣṭhe mukhanāsikākṣiśrotraṃ ca saṃbhavati | saptame calanasamartho bhavati | aṣṭame buddhyādhyavasyate | navame sarvāṅgasaṃpūrṇo bhavati || mṛtaścāhaṃ punarjāto jātaścāhaṃ punarmṛtaḥ | nānāyonisahasrāṇi mayā yānyuṣitāni vai || āhārā vividhā bhuktāḥ pītā nānāvidhāḥ stanāḥ | mātaro vividhā dṛṣṭāḥ pitaraḥ suhṛdastathā || avāṅmukhaḥ pīḍyamāno jantuścaiva samanvitaḥ | sāṃkhyaṃ yogaṃ samabhyasye puruṣaṃ pañcaviṃśakam || iti || tataśca daśame māse prajāyate | jātaśca vāyunā spṛṣṭo na smarati janma maraṇam | ante ca śubhāśubhaṃ karmaitaccharīrasya prāmāṇyam | |
iti niruktopaniṣadi viṃśatitamodhyāyaḥ|
aṣṭottaraṃ sandhiśatamaṣṭākapālaṃ śiraḥ saṃpadyate | ṣoḍaśa vapāpalāni | nava snāyuśatāni | saptaśataṃ puruṣasya marmāṇi | ardhacatasro romāṇi koṭayaḥ | hṛdayaṃ hyaṣṭakapalāni | dvādaśakapalāni jihvā | vṛṣaṇo hṛṣṭasupaṇau | tata upasthagudayonyetanmūtrapurīṣaṃ kasmādāhārāpānasiktatvādanupacati | karmaṇā anyonyaṃ jāyata iti | taṃ vidyākarmaṇī samanvārabhete pūrvaprajñāṃ ca | mahatyajñānatamasi magno jarāmaraṇakṣutpipāsāśokakrodhadrohalobhamohamadabhayamatsaraharṣaviṣāderṣyāsūyātmakairdvandrairabhibhūyamānaḥ so'smādārjavaṃ javībhāvanāntaṃ nirmucyate | so'smādāntaṃ mahābhūbhikāvat śarīrānnimeṣamātraiḥ prakramya prakṛtibhirabhiparītya taijasaṃ śarīraṃ kṛtvā karmaṇānurūpaṃ phalamanubhūya tasya saṃkṣaye punarimaṃ lokaṃ pratipadyate ||
iti niruktopaniṣadi ekaviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ|
iti niruktopaniṣatsamāptā
