|| nirvāṇopaniṣat ||

oṃ vāṇme manasi pratiṣṭhitā | mano me vāci pratiṣṭhitam |
āvirāvīrma edhi | vedasya mā āṇīsthaḥ |

tanmāmavatu | tadvaktāramavatu | avatu māmavatu vaktāram|
oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ ||

parivrājakāḥ paścimaliṇgāḥ | manmathakṣetrapālāḥ | gaganasiddhāntaḥ|
amṛtakallolanadī | akṣayaṃ nirañjanam | niḥsaṃśaya ṛṣiḥ |

khecarīmudrā ca | paramānandī | nirgaṇaguṇatrayam | vivekalabhyam|
manovāgagocaram | anityaṃ jagadyajjanitaṃ svapnajagadabhragajāditulyam |
tathā dehādisaṇghātaṃ mohaguṇajālakalitaṃ tadrajjusarpavatkalpitam | viṣṇuvidhyādiśatābhidhānalakṣyam |
aṇkuśo mārgaḥ | śūnyaṃ na saṃketaḥ | parameśvarasattā |
satyasiddhayogo maṭhaḥ | amarapadaṃ na tatsvarūpam | ādibrahmasvasaṃvit |
ajapā gāyatrī | vikāradaṇḍo dhyeyaḥ||
manonirodhinī kanthā | yogena sadānandasvarūpadarśanam |
ānandabhikṣāśī | mahāśmaśāne'pyānandavane vāsaḥ | ekāntasthānam |
