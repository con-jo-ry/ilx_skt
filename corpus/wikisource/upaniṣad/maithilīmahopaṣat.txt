sārddhatisro mātrā omityatra | imāni tryakṣarāṇi japaṃstajjapati || trīṇi vai duḥkhāni | ādhyātmikamādhidaivikamādhibhautikam | imāni tryakṣarāṇi japaṃstāni praṇāśayati || viṣṇulokātpare loke sākete śubhaśaṃsini | rājantaṃ rāmacandreti japan bandhādvi mucyate japan bandhād vimucyate || iti prathamopaniṣat ||1||

sakalajagatkāraṇabījaṃ bhaktavatsalaḥ sa eva bhagavāṃjñeyaḥ sa eva bhagavāṃjñeyaḥ || iti tṛtīyopaniṣat ||3||
