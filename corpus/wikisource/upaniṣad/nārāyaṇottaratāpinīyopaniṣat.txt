Text:

nārāyaṇottaratāpinīyopaniṣat
prathamaḥ khaṇḍaḥ
sa hovāca bhagavān brahmā ānandaṃ brahmaṇo vidvān saccidānandasvarūpo bhavati |
dvā suparṇā sayujā sakhāyā samānaṃ vṛkṣaṃ pariṣasvajāte |
tayoranyaḥ pippalaṃ svādvattyanaśnannanyo abhicākaśīti ||
yo vedādau svaraḥ prokto vedānte ca pratiṣṭhitaḥ |
tasya prakṛtilīnasya yaḥ paraḥ sa maheśvaraḥ ||
aṇoraṇīyānmahato mahīyānātmā guhāyāṃ nihito'sya jantoḥ |
tamakratuṃ paśyati vītaśoko dhātuḥ prasādānmahimānamīśam ||
vāsanādvāsudevasya vāsitaṃ hi jagattrayam |
sarvabhūtanivāso'si vāsudeva namo'stu te ||
bhūśca nārāyaṇaḥ | bhuvaśca nārāyaṇaḥ | suvaśca nārāyaṇaḥ | mahaśca nārāyaṇaḥ | janaśca nārāyaṇaḥ | tapaśca nārāyaṇaḥ | satyaṃ ca nārāyaṇaḥ | nārāyaṇaḥ paraṃ brahma | nārāyaṇa evedaṃ sarvam | nārāyaṇānna kiñcidasti | satya jñānamanantaṃ brahma | yo veda nihitaṃ guhāyāṃ parame vyoman | tasmādvā etasmādātmana ākāśaḥ saṃbhūtaḥ | ākāśādvāyuḥ | vāyoragniḥ | agnerāpaḥ | adbhyaḥ pṛthivī | pṛthivyā oṣadhayaḥ | oṣadhībhyo'nnam | annātpuruṣaḥ | nārāyaṇaḥ sarvapuruṣa evedaṃ parabrahma | nārāyaṇaḥ sarvabhūtāntaryāmyātmā | ātmedaṃ sarvaṃ nārāyaṇaḥ | nārāyaṇaḥ svayaṃ jyotiḥ | tasmātprakāśātmā ||
nīvāraśūkavattanvī pītā bhāsvatyaṇūpamā |
tasyāḥ śikhāyā madhye paramātmā vyavasthitaḥ ||
pāśabaddhaḥ smṛto jīvaḥ pāśamuktaḥ sanātanaḥ |
tuṣeṇa baddho vrīhiḥ syāttuṣābhāvena taṇḍulaḥ ||
parabrahma svayaṃ cātmā sākṣānnārāyaṇaḥ smṛtaḥ |
nārāyaṇamanādiṃ ca yoganidrāparāyaṇam ||
jāgratsvapnasuṣuptīṣu sarvakālavyavasthitam |
nārāyaṇaṃ mahātmānaṃ mahādhyānaparāyaṇam |
sarvavedāntasaṃlakṣyaṃ tadbrahmetyabhidhīyate ||
hovāca bhagavān nārāyaṇa evedaṃ sarvaṃ pratiṣṭhitaṃ ya evaṃ veda | ityupaniṣat || ityātharvaṇarahasye nārāyaṇottaratāpinīye prathamaḥ khaṇḍaḥ
dvitīyaḥ khaṇḍaḥ
sa hovāca bhagavān brahmā nārāyaṇaḥ paravastu bhavatīti vijñāyate |
saccidānandarūpāya jñānāyāmitatejase |
parabrahmasvarūpāya nārāyaṇa namo'stu te ||
nityaśuddhāya buddhāya nityāyādvaitarūpiṇe |
ānandāyātmarūpāya nārāyaṇa namo'stu te ||
oṃ namo bhagavate śrīmannārāyaṇāya mahāviṣṇave amitabalaparākramāya śaṅkhacakragadādharāya lakṣmīsametāya garuḍavāhanāya daśāvatārāya sarvaduṣṭadaityadānavasaṃharaṇāya śiṣṭapratipālakāya parabrahmarūpāya mahātmane paramapuruṣāya puṇḍarīkākṣāya purāṇapuruṣāya śuddhabuddhāya saccidānandasvarūpāya mahātmane ghṛṇiḥ sūrya āditya oṃ namo nārāyaṇāya sahasrāra huṃ phaṭ svāhā ||
nārāyaṇāya śāntāya śāśvatāya murāraye |
yajñeśvarāya yajñāya śaraṇyāya namo namaḥ ||
sa hovāca bhagavān brahmā sarvaṃ viśvamidaṃ nārāyaṇaḥ ya evaṃ veda ityupaniṣat ||
ityātharvaṇarahasye nārāyaṇottaratāpinīye dvitīyaḥ khaṇḍaḥ
tṛtīyaḥ khaṇḍaḥ
sa hovāca bhagavān brahmā nārāyaṇaḥ parabrahmeti ya evaṃ veda | nārāyaṇātmā vedaṃ sarvaṃ nirvikāraṃ nirañjanavastu pratipādyate | sa nārāyaṇo virāṭpuruṣo bhavati | devānāṃ vāsavo bhavati | indriyāṇāṃ mano bhavati | sarveṣāṃ vastūnāṃ mukhyavastu bhavati | oṃ namo nārāyaṇādanyo mantraḥ nārāyaṇādanyopāstirnāsti | nānyannārāyaṇādupāsitavyam | sarvaṃ nārāyaṇa eva bhavatīti vijñāyate | yo'dhīte nityaṃ sarvān kāmānavāpnoti | sa brahmahatyāyāḥ pūto bhavati | surāpānāt pūto bhavati | svarṇasteyāt pūto bhavati | gurutalpagamanāt pūto bhavati | agamyāgamanāt pūto bhavati | abhakṣyabhakṣāt pūto bhavati | sa upapātakamahāpātakebhyaḥ pūto bhavati | sarvavedamadhīyāno bhavati| sarvakratuphalaṃ prāpnoti| sarvakarmakartā bhavati| catuḥsamudraparyantabhūdānaphalaṃ prāpnoti | dvijottamo bhavati | caturvargaphalaṃ prāpnoti | brahmacārī jñānavān bhavati | gṛhī putrapautramahaiśvaryavān bhavati | sanyāsī mokṣavān bhavati ||
paṭhanācchravaṇādvā'pi sarvān kāmānavāpnuyāt ||
nārāyaṇaprasādena vaikuṇṭhapadamaśnute ||
iti sa hovāca bhagavān ya evaṃ veda | ityupaniṣat ||
vedākṣarāṇi yāvanti paṭhitāni dvijottamaiḥ |
tāvanti harināmāni kīrtitāni na saṃśayaḥ ||
nārāyaṇa hare kṛṣṇa vāsudeva jagadguro |
mukundācyuta deveśa mahāviṣṇo namo'stu te ||
bhūtāni tattvasaṃjñāni kūṭastho'kṣarasaṃjñitaḥ |
uttamaścāpi puruṣo nārāyaṇa itīryate ||
ityātharvaṇarahasye nārāyaṇottaratāpinīye tṛtīyaḥ khaṇḍaḥ |
iti nārāyaṇottaratāpinīyopaniṣat samāptā
