śrīgaṇeśāya namaḥ
atha viśvāmitrasmṛtiḥ

<1>

atha prathamo'dhyāyaḥ
nityanaimittikakarmaṇāṃ varṇanam
sahasradalapaṅkaje sakalaśītaraśmiprabhe
varābhayakarāmbujaṃ vimalagandhapuṣpāmbaram |
prasannavadanekṣaṇaṃ sakaladevatārūpiṇaṃ
smarecchirasipāvanaṃ tadavidhānapūrvaṃ gurum ||1||
āhnikam
catuḥpañcaghaṭīmānaṃ muhūrtaṃ brahmasaṃjñitam
pañcapañcaghaṭī jñeyā uṣaḥkāla itīṣyate ||2||
ṛtubāṇaghaṭīmānamaruṇodayasaṃjñitam
uṣaḥ pañcaghaṭīmānaṃ prātaḥkāla iti smṛtaḥ ||3||
evaṃ jñātvāṃ prabhāte tu nityakarma samācaret
nityanaimittike kāmye kṛte kāle tu satphalam ||4||
brāhme muhūrta utthāya kṛtvā śaucaṃ samāhitaḥ
snānaṃ kuryāduṣaḥkāle ātmārthamaruṇodaye ||5||
prātaḥkāla japaṃ kuryānnityanaimittikaṃ viduḥ
raśmimantaṃ samālokya upasthānaṃ samācaret ||6||
sandhyāyāṃ mukhyakālātikrame doṣaḥ |
kālātītaṃ na kartavyaṃ kartavyaṃ kālasaṃyutam
tasmātsarvaprayatnena kāle karma samācaret ||7||
uktakāle tu yatkarma pramādādakṛtaṃ yadi ||8||
trisahasrajapaṃ kuryātprāyaścittaṃ vidhīyate
tathā proktaṃ prāṇāyāmadvayatrikam ||9||
athavā japamātreṇa kālātītena doṣabhāk
trisahasraṃ sahasraṃ vā triśataṃ śatameva vā ||10||
anulomavilomābhyāṃ japtvādapāpa kṣayo bhavet
uktakāle vyatīte tu upādhiśca pramāṇakam ||11||
anulomavilomābhyāṃ sahasrajapamācaret
dehasvasthavatāyena svasthacittavatā'pi ca ||12||
kālo'tikramyate nityaṃ tasya pāpo na gaṇyate
sa sarvamārgavibhraṣṭastiryaktvaṃ samavāpnuyāt ||13||
tasya darśanamātreṇa sacailaḥ snānamācaret
asambaddhapralāpena duḥsaṅgenāpi nidra yā ||14||
atikrāmanti ye kālaṃ te narā brahmaghātinaḥ
nityakarmākhilaṃ yastu uktakāle samācaret ||15||
jitvā sa sakalāṃllokān ante viṣṇupuraṃ vrajet
pratyahaṃ prātarutthāya snānaṃ sandhyāṃ samāpya ca ||16||
yathāśakti japedvidvān sa mukto nātra saṃśayaḥ
yāme cāntye ca sarvaryāṃ nāḍīnāṃ pañcakaṃ dvijaḥ ||17||
prātaḥkāla iti jñātvā nityakarma samācaret
karmakālo dinānte tu pādaṃ nyūnaṃ ghaṭītrayam ||18||
bimbaṃ dṛṣṭvā tyajedarghyaṃ japedātārakodaye
ṣaṇmateṣu samāpteṣu tattanmantrānusārataḥ ||19||
nityakarmāṇi yaḥ kuryātkarmasiddhiṃ labhennaraḥ
anuktakāle kṛtakarma niṣphalaṃ akālavṛṣṭiḥ patitā yathā bhuvi |
uptāni bījāni viniṣphalāni vākarotyakālaḥ kṛtakarmaniṣphalaḥ ||20||
niyuktakarmāṇi niyuktakāle kṛtāni sadyassukhasiddhidāni
yathoptabījāni yathā phalāni kāle hi vṛṣṭirbhuvi jīvanāni ||21||
sandhyātritayalakṣaṇam
uttamā tārakopetā madhyamā luptatārakā
adhamā sūryasahitā prātassandhyā tridhā matā ||22||
uttamā pūrvasūryā ca madhyamā madhyasūryakā
adhamā paścimādityā madhyasandhyā tridhā matā ||23||
uttamā sūryasahitā madhyamā luprabhāskarā
adhamā tārakopetā sāyaṃ sandhyā tridhā matā ||24||
śucirvāpyaśucirvāpi nityaṃ karma na santyajet
tatrāpi kālaniyamādarghyadānaṃ viśiṣyate ||25||
sandhyātraye pūrvamukho dvijanmā tridhaivaśuddhācamanaṃ prakuryāt
udaṅmukhovāpi samācarenna taddakṣiṇāpaścimayoḥkadāpi ||26||
sandhyāsnānaṃ parityajya vidyābhyāsaṃ karoti yaḥ
tasya vidyāvināśaḥsyādadharmo bhavati dhruvam ||27||
gurūpadeśavidhinā snānaṃ sandhyāṃ samācaret
vedādisarvavidyārthajñānasaṃpattisādhanam ||28||
ityeṣādvijavarṇānāṃ vidyābhyāsavidhiḥkramāt
anyathā yo'bhyasedvidyāṃ tasya vidyā na sidhyati ||29||
yassandhyāṃ kālataḥ prāptāṃ atikramati durmatiḥ
bhrūṇahatyāmavāpnoti kākayonau prajāyate ||30||
yathāśaktyācaretsandhyāṃ kāle'hnāphalamāpnuyāt
kāle tasmātprayatnena nityakarma samācaret ||31||
ācāro dvividhaḥ proktaḥ sopādhiranupādhikaḥ
sopādhirguṇamātraḥ syānmukhyaḥsyādanupādhikaḥ ||32||
upādhau samanuprāpte gauṇācāraṃ samācaret
anupādhau ca durbuddhyā gauṇācāraṃ karoti yaḥ ||33||
sa dāridra mavāpnoti mahārogaḥ prajāyate
apavādo mahān doṣo sambhavejjanmajanmani ||34||
mukhyācāraṃ parityajya gauṇācāraṃ karoti yaḥ
tasya karmaṇi dharmāśca nirjitāḥ syurna saṃśayaḥ ||35||
mukhyācāro mahān śreṣṭho mumukṣorupapādakaḥ
yathākālaṃ dvijaḥ kuryānmukhyācāraṃ vidhīyate ||36||
svaguruṃ pūjayatyevamupacāraiśca pañcabhiḥ
sadbhaktyā saṃhitāmetāṃ viśvāmitrasprakalpayet ||37||
prātarutthāya yo vipraḥ svātmamūlasthakuṇḍalīm
prabodhyo su prabhātāyā gāyatrī tatra cintayet ||38||
kuṇḍalinyāṃ samudbhūtāṃ gāyatrīṃ prāṇadhāriṇīm
prāṇavidyā mahāvidyā yastāṃ vetti sa yogavit ||39||
aṣṭadhā kuṇḍalījñeyā dvātriṃśadvarṇasaṃkhyayā
evaṃ jñātvā prabhātāyāṃ ṣaḍādhāre tathā nyaset ||40||
ṣaḍādhāreṣu ṣaṭkukṣiṃ vinyaseccaturakṣaram
ādipraṇavasaṃyuktaṃ ṣaṭkukṣiṃ vinyasetkramāt ||41||
sahasradalamadhyasthā saphalā sa caturthakā
so'haṃ haṃseti vijñeyā saṃkalpajñānapūrvakam ||42||
asya saṃkalpamātreṇa sarva pāpaiḥ pramucyate
anayā sadṛśī vidyā anayā sadṛśojapaḥ ||43||
anayā sadṛśaṃ jñānaṃ na bhūto na bhaviṣyati
samudra vasane devi parvatastanamaṇḍale ||44||
viṣṇupatnī namastubhyaṃ pādasparśaṃ kṣamasva me
atitīkṣṇamahākāya kalpāntadahanopamaḥ ||45||
bhairavāya namastubhyamanujñāṃ dātumarhasi
athotthāya bahirgatvā viṇmūtrādi tyajeddvijaḥ ||46||
grāmāddakṣiṇadigbhāge śatadhanvantarāvadhi
deśāśca ṛṣayaścaiva gaṇanāthāśca yoginaḥ ||47||
gacchantu devatāḥ sarvā atra śaucaṃ karomyaham
prathamaṃ ca śiroveṣṭaṃ nivītaṃ ca dvitīyakam ||48||
digdarśanaṃ tṛtīyaṃ syāt antardhānaṃ caturthakam
maunantu pañcakaṃ jñeyaṃ purīṣaṃ ṣaṣṭhameva ca
saptamaṃ mṛttikādhānaṃ udakaṃ cāṣṭamaṃ smṛtam ||49||
muṣṭimātratṛṇaṃ dattvā rātrau ceddakṣiṇāmukhaḥ
divācodaṅmukhaḥ kuryācchaucaṃ karma samāhitaḥ ||50||
vāmadakṣiṇakarṇasya upavītaṃ ca dhārayet
kramānmūtra purīṣe ca kuryācchaucaṃ dvijottamaḥ ||51||
yathāvidhyuktamārgeṇa kuryāduddhṛtavāriṇā
kūpakulyā taṭākādijalaiḥ śaucaṃ karoti yaḥ ||52||
kalpakoṭiśatairvāpi narakānna nivartate
ekāliṅge kare tisraḥ pañcāpāne tathaiva ca ||53||
pādadvaye catuḥ saṃkhyā etacchaucaṃ vidhīyate
etaddharmo gṛhasthasya itareṣāṃ pṛthakpṛthak ||54||
smārtānāṃ dviguṇaṃ kuryāt vanasthastriguṇaṃ tathā
caturguṇaṃ yatīnāṃ ca treyāṇāṃ bheda īratiḥ ||55||
durgandhatyāgaparyantaṃ kṛtvā śaucaṃ samāhitaḥ ||56||
dantadhāvanam
kṣīrakāṣṭhena kurvīta dantadhāvanamagrajaḥ
tṛṇaparṇaissadā kuryādamā ekādaśīṃ vinā ||57||
tayorapi ca kurvīta jambūplakṣāmlapaṇakaiḥ
āyubalaṃ yaśo vacaḥ prajāḥ paśuvasūni ca ||58||
brahma prajñāṃ ca medhāṃ ca tvaṃ no dehi vanaspate
niṣṭhīvanaṃ ca gaṇḍūṣaṃ vāyavyābhimukho naraḥ ||59||
īśānābhimukho bhūtvā vāyavyānte samutsṛjet
aṅgāravālukābhiśca bhasmāṃgulinakhairapi ||60||
iṣṭakāloṣṭapāṣāṇairna kuryāddantadhāvanam
svadiraśca karañjaśca kadambaśca vaṭastathā ||61||
veṇuśca tintiḍīplakṣā vāmranimbe tathaiva ca
apāmārgaśca bilvaśca arkaścaudumbarastathā ||62||
ete praśastāḥ kathitā dantadhāvanakarmaṇi
yathāśaktyanusāreṇa dantadhāvanamācaret ||63||
tato nadīṃ samāgamya gaṅgādhyānapurassaram
ācamanam
svasūtroktavidhānena kuryādācamanatrayam
vāmahaste jalaṃ nītvā trirvyāhṛtyābhimantritam ||64||
ākṛṣya dakṣiṇe bhāge recayedvāmamārgataḥ
svavāmabhāgamālokya vajrapāṣāṇatastyajet ||65||
punaḥ śuddhāmbunācamya tataḥ snānaṃ samācaret
nābhimātre jale sthitvā trivāraṃ snānamācaret ||66||
snānabhedāḥ
prāṇāyāmatrayaṃ kuryāt daśapraṇavasaṃyutam
ullikhenmārjanaṃ yantraṃ snānayantraṃ samullikhet ||67||
gaṅgāmaṃtreṇa cāvāhya salilopari mudra yā
vahnimaṇḍalamālikhya jalamadhye sabindukam ||68||
māyābījaṃ samullikhya daṇḍeṣu vyāhṛtitrayam
tataśśuddhāmbunācamya prāṇāyāmatrayaṃ tathā ||69||
deśakālau ca saṅkīrtya gāyatrīdhyānapūrvakam
sūktena mārjanaṃ kuryādyathāśāstroktamārgataḥ ||70||
aghamarṣaṇamantraṇa snāyātpañcāṅgapūrvakam
saṅkalpaṃ sūktapāṭhaṃ ca mārjanaṃ cāghamarṣaṇam ||71||
devāditarpaṇaṃ caiva snānaṃ pañcāṅgalakṣaṇam
śiraḥsnānaṃ galasnānaṃ kaṭisnānaṃ tathaiva ca ||72||
ājānupādaparyantaṃ mantrasnānaṃ caturvidham
takārādyaṣṭabhirvarṇaiḥ śirasi prokṣyamāna saiḥ ||73||
bhakārādyaṣṭabhirvarṇaiḥ kaṇṭhasnānaṃ samācaret
sakārādyaṣṭabhirvarṇaiḥ kaṭisnānaṃ samācaret ||74||
pakārādyaṣṭabhirvarṇaiḥ jānupāde samācaret
evaṃ vijñānamātreṇa gaṅgāsnānaśataṃ phalam ||75||
mantrasnānaṃ vinā vipro jalasnānaṃ karoti yaḥ
manonirmalatā tasya nāsti hi śruticoditam ||76||
śrotre nāsākṣiṇī baddhvā sahasāntarjale plutaḥ
magnaṃ kṛtvā paṭhenmantraṃ yāvadvāyunirodhanam ||77||
tataḥ snānatrayaṃ kuryācchirovyāhṛtipūrvakam
trikālaṃ trividhaṃ snāyādvāruṇaṃ mṛttikāyutam ||78||
pañcārdra kamiti proktaṃ kramātsthānatrayaṃ budhaiḥ
śirastanurdvādaśadhā prokṣayecchaṅkhamudra yā ||79||
vyāhṛtyādiśiro'ntyena manunā dvijasattamaḥ
ṣaṭsaṃkhyaṃ brahmarandhre tu tritrisaṃkhyaṃ bhujadvaye ||80||
mūlamantraṃ ca manasā pajayetpañcapajanaiḥ
brahmarṣipitṛtuṣṭyarthaṃ triścaturdhaiva tarpayet ||81||
vyāhṛtyaikakayā yuktaiḥ praṇavādinamo'ntakaiḥ
tattanchabdaistarpayet turyaistrilokyasaṃyutaiḥ ||82||
yastarpaṇaṃ vinā snāyātsalile matsyavadbhavet
tasmānsarvaprayatnena yathoktaṃ snānamācaret ||83||
yanmayā dūṣitaṃ toyaṃ śārīramalanāśanāt
tasya pāpaviśuddhyarthaṃ yakṣmāṇaṃ tarpayāmyaham ||84||
iti trirañjaliṃ datvā yakṣmapriyakaraṃ bahiḥ
tatastīraṃ samāgamya gāyatrīkavacaṃ paṭhet ||85||
guṇā daśamnānakṛto hi puṃso rūpaṃ ca tejaśca balaṃ ca śaucam
āyuṣyamārogyamalolupatvaṃ duḥsvapnanāśaṃ ca tapaśca medhā ||86||
snānārthaṃ prasthitaṃ vipraṃ devā pitṛgaṇaissaha
tṛṣṇārtāśca samāyānti na snāyānnarakaṃ vrajet ||87||
madhyāhne mṛttikāsnānaṃ kuryānnityamatandri taḥ
prātassāyāhnasamaye na kuryānmṛttikākriyām ||88||
vastradhāraṇam
sūtreṇa grathitaṃ sūcyā khaṇḍaṃ citraṃ tathaiva ca
vicitraputtalīvastramanyavastraṃ na dhārayet ||89||
etatsamastamityuktaṃ paṭṭavastraṃ na doṣabhāk
aurṇavastrāṇi sarvāṇi na doṣo dhārayedbudhaḥ ||90||
prātarmadhyāhnayoḥ snānaṃ vānaprasthagṛhasthayoḥ
yatestriṣavaṇaṃ snānamasakṛttu brahmacāriṇām ||91||
prokṣya vāsopasaṃyojya praṇavādiṣaḍakṣaraiḥ
śuddhadhautaṃ parigrāhyaṃ ṣaṭkacchavidhidharmakam ||92||
kacchadvayaṃ vastramadhye tacchṛṅgeṣu catuṣṭayam
evaṃ krameṇa badhnīyāllakṣaṇaṃ śruticoditam ||93||
bhojanottaranirmālyaṃ prakṣālyadvijasattamaḥ
sāyaṃ sandhyāṃ prakurvīta anyathā brahmaghātakaḥ ||94||
prātarmadhyāhnayoḥ snātvā pṛthaksandhyāṃ samācaret
eṣa dharmo gṛhasthasya yogināṃ prātareva hi ||95||
prāṇāyāmaḥ
uṣaḥkāle praśastaṃ syādyogināṃ vāyudhāraṇam
gaṅgādvāre tataḥsnātvā sthitvā brahmadinatrayam
tatphalaṃ samavāpnoti dvijo vāyuniraḥdhakaḥ ||96||
tatrāpi kumbhakaṃ kṛtvā prāṇāyāmaṃ samācaret
sūryodayaṃ samārabhya ghaṭikādvādaśopari ||97||
brahmayajñāṅgakasnānaṃ aparāhṇe tu tarpayet
saṅkalpya brahmayajñaṃ ca yathāśakti samācaret ||98||
mādhyāhnikaṃ prakurvīta japānte tarpayettathā
yantrahīnaṃ jalasnānaṃ bījahīnaṃ tu yantrakam ||99||
binduhīnaṃ tu yadbījaṃ vṛthā snānaṃ na saṃśayaḥ
mantrahīno jale snātvā sandhyāvandanamācaret ||100||
aśucestasyamanaso malinaṃ naiva gacchati
mantrayantravihīno yaḥ snānaṃ sandhyāṃ karoti cet ||101||
viphalaṃ mantratejassyātsatyaṃ satyaṃ na saṃśayaḥ
pañcasnānaṃ vinā yena sāyaṃ sandhyā kṛtā yadi ||102||
tasya pāpaṃ na gaccheta yathā sūrye'stage tamaḥ
paridhāya śubhaṃ vastraṃ tilakaṃ dhārayettataḥ ||103||
puṇḍradhāraṇam
gurūpadeśamārgeṇa anyathā dharmaghātakaḥ
mṛdvāricandanaṃ bhasma vāmahaste nidhāpayet ||104||
trikoṇayantraṃ saṃlekhya madhye māyāṃ sa bindukām
koṇāgre praṇavaṃ lekhyaṃ daṇḍeṣu vyāhṛtitrayam ||105||
abhimantrya tu gāyatraṃ mantrarājaṃ daśāvadhi
lalāṭe tilakaṃ kuryādgurupūjāpurassaram ||107||
mantrayantravihīnaṃ yattilakaṃ yadi dhārayet
tanmukhaṃ śavavadbhāti brahmatejo na vidyate ||108||
tilakaṃ yatra saṃyuktaṃ mantrasaṃyuktameva ca
lalāṭe yatra dṛśyeta tattejo brahmanāmakam ||109||
praṇavaṃ cordhvapuṇḍraṃ ca tripadā ca tripuṇḍrakam
lalāṭe yasya dṛśyante tejasvi brahmado bhavet ||110||
omāpojyotimantreṇa śikhābandhanamācaret
svasūtroktavidhānena sandhyāvandanamācaret
anyathā yastu kurute āsurīṃ tanumāpnuyāt ||111||
mayākṛte mūtrapurīṣaśaucaprakṣālyagaṇḍūṣaṇamehane ca
vastrasya saṃkṣālanake ca duṣkṛtaṃ kṣamasva gaṅge mama suprasannā ||112||
trikoṇamadhye hrīṃkāraṃ koṇāgre praṇavaṃ likhet
daṇḍeṣu vyāhṛtiścaiva ullikhedudake tathā ||113||
praṇavenabahirveṣṭya jalaṃ pītvā'tha mārjayet
tathaivavinyasetsaṃndhyāṃ anyathā śūdra vadbhavet ||114||
iti śrīviśvāmitrasaṃhitāyāṃ āhnikavidhiyogo nāma prathamo'dhyāyaḥ

atha dvitīyo'dhyāyaḥ
ācamanavidhivarṇanam
jalamadhye vāmakare dakṣiṇe karṇavatkṛtī
ādau guruṃ namaskṛtya paścādācamanaṃ caret ||1||
prāgācāmedamṛtaṃ syāt somyāyāṃ somapābhavet
paścānmukhoraktapā syāt surāpo dakṣiṇāmukhaḥ ||2||
caturviṃśatināmāni tattadaṃgāni saṃspṛśet
vinyasetkeśavādīni paurāṇācamanaṃ bhavet ||3||
takārādiyakārāntaiḥ caturviṃśati varṇakaiḥ
saṃspṛśettattadaṃgāni smārtamācamanaṃ caret ||4||
devyāpādaistrirācamya abliṃgairnavabhiḥ spṛśet
saptavyāhṛtigāyatrī śirasturyastadāgamam ||5||
tridhācācamanaṃ proktaṃ paurāṇaṃ smārtamāgamaṃ
śrautaṃ ca mānasaṃ ceti paṃcadhā procyate punaḥ ||6||
saṃdhyāprārambhakāleṣu kuryādācamanatrayaṃ
saṃhṛtāṅgulihastena brahmatīrthe pibejjalaṃ ||7||
muktāṅguṣṭhakaniṣṭhābhyāṃ śeṣeṇācamanaṃ bhavet
gokarṇākṛtihastena māṣamātraṃ jalaṃ pibet ||8||
nyūnātiriktamātreṇa tajjalaṃ surayāsamaṃ
ādaucānte ca maṃtraiśca kramādācamanaṃ caret ||9||
śrutismṛtipurāṇāni paryāyeṇavilomataḥ
aṅgulitrayasaṃyuktaṃ muktāṅguṣṭhakaniṣṭhakaṃ ||10||
gokarṇākṛtirityāhuḥ brāhmakarma prakīrtitaṃ
hastamadhyastha salilaṃ pītaśeṣaṃ na saṃtyajet ||11||
kvacittyāgaṃ kvacitpānaṃ kuryāddurbrāhmaṇaṃ viduḥ
keśavāditrayeṇāpo māṣadadhnaṃ pibetkramāt ||12||
govindamagratonyasya sauṣumne viṣṇumeva ca
madhusūdanamāditye sudhāṃśau ca trivikramaṃ ||13||
agrato vāmanaṃ caiva śrīdharaṃ hastayostathā
hṛṣīkeśaṃ padmanābhaṃ ubhayoḥ pādayornyaset ||14||
dāmodaraṃ brahmarandhre nāmasaṃkarṣaṇasya ca
nyasedvā nāsikāmadhye cāsyānte vā vinirdiśet ||15||

khanādyaṃ vāyupūrvaṃ syāddattayugmamathāparam

tantripādaṃ prayoktavyaṃ gāyatrīmadhyamantrataḥ

ādhārādi samuddhṛtya suṣumnāmārganirgame ||22||

raṇḍāpañcavidhaṃ jñātvā prayatnena parityajet ||57||
śmaśāne citisaṃyukte prajvālyābhīṣṭakāṣṭhavat
kanyā vaidhavyamāpannā vīretyācakṣate budhaiḥ ||58||
rohiṇī vidhavā bhartā sā raṇḍetyabhidhīyate
durbhagā daśavarṣā yā sā kanyā samudīritā ||59||
rajasaḥ paratassā tu yātukī vidhavā bhavet
asantatiśca yā nārī sā raṇḍetyabhidhīyate ||60||
nānābhāvaiḥ prayatnena raṇḍāpākaṃ parityajet
vīraraṇḍā kuṇḍaraṇḍā bālaputrāhyaputriṇī ||61||
tāsāṃ pāko na bhoktavyo bhuktvā cāndrā yaṇaṃ caret
asnātā vidhavā caṇḍī pakvāśī māsasūtakī ||62||
pañcapakvāntyajedvipraḥ tatpreṣyaṃ ca parityajet
pākaṃ kṛtvā prayatnena hyabhuktvā bhojane viṣam ||63||
raṇḍāpākaṃ mahāpāpaṃ vaiśvadeve parityajet
nāhutaṃ pākamaśnīyādanaivedyaṃ sa manyate ||64||
raṇḍāpākaṃ viṣaṃ krūraṃ ahutvānnaṃ tathā viṣam
dvividhaṃ yantrasaṃyuktaṃ tadannaṃ kālakūṭakam ||65||
nānā bhāvaiḥ prayatnena raṇḍāpākaṃ parityajet
pramādātprāpyate cānnaṃ prāṇāyāmāṃścaturdaśa ||66||
kuryātkumbhakamārgeṇa nyāsadhyānapurassaram
mantrarājahavirbhāgaṃ prathamaṃ vaiśvadevikam ||67||
kṛtvā śrāddhaṃ prakurvīta nityanaimittikaṃ caret
śrāddhāgnau karaṇātpūrvaṃ vaiśvadevaṃ vidhāya ca ||68||
tato'gnau karaṇaṃ kuryādanyathā śrāddhaghātakaḥ
vaiśvadevaṃ vinā yastu śrāddhakarma samācaret ||69||
vṛthā śrāddhaṃ bhavettacca rauravaṃ narakaṃ vrajet
nityanaimittike śrāddhe paktvā cānnaṃ prayatnataḥ ||70||
tato homaṃ prakurvīta brāhmaṇān bhojayettataḥ
yadagnau karaṇaṃ kuryādvaiśvadevapurassaram ||71||
brahmārpaṇaṃ havistatsyātpitṝṇāṃ dattamakṣayam
devebhyaśca pitṛbhyaśca ṛṣibhyaśca tathā haviḥ ||72||
ādau vahnimukhe dattaṃ tṛptyai bhavati nānyathā
yastvagnau na hutaṃ cānnaṃ daive pitrye prayacchati ||73||
gotrapānnaṃ bhavatyeva vṛthā śrāddhaṃ na saṃśayaḥ
nityaśrāddhe gayāśrāddhe tīrthaśrāddhe tathaiva ca ||74||
vaiśvadevaṃ hunedādau tataḥ śrāddhaṃ samācaret
svāhākāreṇa hutvādau svadhākāreṇa vai tataḥ ||75||
evaṃ homatrayaṃ kṛtvā tataḥ śrāddhaṃ samācaret
vaiśvadevaviṣayeḥ
haviṣyamannaṃ ghṛtasaṅkulaṃ ca vahnau samāṃśaṃ juhuyāttriyāmam |
dvayottaraṃ trijati yugmasaṃjñaṃ oṃṅkāramādau pratimantrayuktam ||76||
rasayuktaṃ haviṣyaṃ syādghṛtayuktaṃ tathaudanam
brāhmaṇo vaiśvadevārthaṃ kuryānnityamatandri taḥ ||77||
anyasya cedra saṃ tyaktvā vaiśvadevaṃ karoti yaḥ
devebhyaśśāpamāpnoti daridro bhavati dhruvam ||78||
supakvaṃ rasasaṃyuktaṃ rājānnaṃ ghṛtasaṃyutam
taddhaviṣyamiti jñātaṃ suprītāstridaśādaśaḥ ||79||
parvadvaye samāyoge
śrāddhānte vaiśvadevārthaṃ pākaṃ kṛtvāprayatnataḥ ||80||
hutvā datvā ca bhuktvā ca dvijaścāndrā yaṇaṃ caret
devānāṃ ca ṛṣīṇāṃ ca pitṝṇāṃ ca viśeṣataḥ ||81||
paryāyeṇa pradātavyaṃ śrāddhakāle havirdvijaiḥ
devarṣipitṛtuṣṭyarthamekapāko vidhīyate ||82||
pṛthakpāko na kartavyaḥ kṛtaścetpatito bhavet
akṛtvānnaṃ tu naivedyaṃ yaḥ kuryātkrimisaṅkulam ||83||
homaṃ kṛtvā prayatnena vaiśvadevaṃ prakalpayet
iti viśvāmitrasmṛtau vaiśvadeva prakaraṇaṃ nāma aṣṭamo'dhyāyaḥ samāpta
