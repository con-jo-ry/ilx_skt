vyālaśikṣā
atha śikṣāṃ pravakṣyāmi vyālamaunimatānugām
padasandhisamāyuktāṃ svarasandhiyutāṃ tathā 1
yā padasya ṣaḍāvṛttiḥ sā jaṭetyabhidhīyate
kramoktapadajātaṃ yaduttaraṃ taddvirabhyaset 2
tataḥ pūrvaṃ dvirabhyasya dvitīye tatsamāpayet
dvitīyamuttareṇaivamarddharcānte samāpayet 3
iti jaṭā
tribhiḥ padaiḥ kramo yastu sakṛduktvāya maddhyamam
antyameva dvirabhyasya maddhyamantu sakṛdvadet 4
tataḥ pūrvaṃ dvirabhyasya madhyamantu sakṛdvadet
yathāvasānamardharcāṃ kramavadveṣṭanaṃ bhavet 5
iti trikramajaṭā
jaṭāvadabhyasenmālāmādyupāntyapadaṃ smṛtam
kramaścānukramaḥ proktaḥ pūrvaṃ vyālamaharṣiṇā 6
iti mālā
kramamuktvā viparyasya punaśca kramamuttaram
ekavāraṃ śikhetīsā jaṭe vāntaṃ samāpayet 7
iti śikhā
jaṭāvadabhyasedrekhāmardharcātpūrvamuttaram
yuktvā dvābhyāṃ jaṭaivontaṃ pūrvaṃ vyāAZlamaharṣiṇā 8
iti rekhā
kramā dvitricaturthokto vargo vā ṛci vā kramāt
evaṃ daṇḍaścocyate sā varddharcānte samāpayet 9
iti daṇḍaḥ
kramamuktvā viparyasya punaśca kramamuttaram
tadutkramya punaḥ brūyāt ṛcāntaṃ kramavadrathaḥ 10
iti rathaḥ
jaṭottaraṃ dvirabhyasya caikaikaṃ madhyamādikam
sandhivatkramavacceti ghana ityucyate budhaiḥ 11 iti ghanaḥ
śikhevānukramanteṣāṃ trayāṇāṃ kramamabhyaset
jaṭe vāntaṃ veṣṭanīyyaṃ vyālena ghana ucyate 12
iti pāṭhāntaram
iti vyālaśikṣā samāptā
