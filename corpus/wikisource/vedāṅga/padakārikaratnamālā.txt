paṅktyaiḥ nidhannaikaviṃśaścāṣṭau vṛt ṣaṭca visrutāḥ
iti śrīśaṅkarācāryakṛtau vājasaneyasaṃhitāyāṃ padakārikāratnamālāyāṃ
caturdaśo'dhyāyaḥ
atha pañcadaśo'dhyāyaḥ
aṣṭādaśadhruvakṣitiścatvāri dvayameva ca
kulāyinī ṣolaśyāṃ tu dvayoratra caturthake
svairdakṣairaṣṭādaśabhiścatvāri vṛddvayorvisruk
pṛthivyāḥ purī viṃśatyā caturastriśca visrutāḥ
adityāstvekaviṃśatyā strayaḥ ṣaḍbhistu visrutāḥ
sajūtra tu caturdaśyāṃ pañcabhirdvādaśastathā
prāṇaṃ me trayodaśa ca dvayaṃ caikaṃ ca vedase
apaḥ pinvaddvādaśīṃ tu dvayaṃ catvāri visrutāḥ
mūrdhā vayastu daśabhiścaturaḥ pañcamo visruk
basto vivalamaṣṭau ca dvayaṃ tu caturastathā
sim̐ hadiḥ paṣṭa vāṣṭau velau vā catasrakaḥ
anaḍvān paṭhantiraṣṭau syāccatvāri ca trayaikatā
pañcāvirgāyatrīssapta catvāri traya īritāḥ
indrā gnī avyathamānāmekādaśīṃ ca vṛddvayoḥ
viśvakarmā tvāṣaṣṭhī syādekaṃ vṛccaikavisrutāḥ
rājñyasi prācekaviṃśa pañcavṛccaturo bhavan
āyurme saptadaśyāṃ tu dvayoścatvāri visrutāḥ
mācchandaḥ pramā saptabhirdvābhyāṃ vṛttraya īritāḥ
bṛhatyanuṣṭup ṣaṭkaṃ syādveṣṭanāstisra ādidhuḥ
pṛthivyāntarikṣaṃ ṣaḍbhirvisargādvau tathaiva ca
manaḥ kṛṣiśca saptabhyaḥ pañcamyāṃ tu visruggavait
pañcadaśyāgnirdevatā visargā dvādaśastathā
mūrdhāsirāṭ caikādaśyāṃ yantrīradyaṃ traikādaśīm
āśustrivṛdviṃśatistu trayodaśa ca viṃśatiḥ
garbhāḥ pañcasaptadaśyāṃ daśavṛtṣolaśastathā
agnerbhāgo'ṣṭādaśastu catvāri vṛttathāṣṭamaḥ
nṛcakṣasāṃ ca viṃśatyā pañcavṛccacaikādaśaḥ
vasūnāmaṣṭādaśabhiḥ pañcamo daśa visrutāḥ
aditerviṃśatiścaivaṃ caturo vṛtpañcadaśīm
yavānāṃ trayoviṃśatyā ṣaṭkaṃ vṛddvādaśo visruk
ekayāstu vata triṃśaddaśabhiśca trayodaśa
navabhirastvaṣṭāviṃśaścāṣṭau caturdaśaikatā
saptadaśa hyekatriṃśaddaśa caturdaśa stathā
pañcaviṃśatyāṣaṭ catvāriṃśatpañcadaśaikavṛt
visargāṣṭādaśaścaiva catvāri nāmamatra tu
iti śrīśaṅkarācāryakṛtau vājasaneyasaṃhitāyāṃ padakārikāratnamālāyāṃ
pañcadaśo'dhyāyaḥ
atha ṣoḍaśo'dhyāyaḥ
agne jātānviṃśatistu pañcacatvāripañcakam
sahasājātāṣṭāviṃśapañcaikādaśa caturuṣu
agneḥpurīṣaṃ viṃśatyā catvāri vṛttayo visruk
evaśchando varivoṣṭau trayo vṛtsaptabhirvisruk
sindhussamudra ssaptamyāmekaṃ caiva dvayorvisruk
akṣarapaṅktirnavamiḥ saptavṛtpañca visrutāḥ
bṛhadra thantaraṃ cāṣṭau pañcabhyaścaturo visruk
evo varivaikādaśyāṃ pañcaśvaṣṭau ca visrutāḥ
raśminā satyāyasatyaṃ trayodaśīṃ taryośca visruk
pratidhinādvādaśīṃ ca trayo vṛccaikamatra tu
uśijā vasu caturdaśyāṃ trayaścatvāri cādvayoḥ
trayodaśyailenauṣadhī pañcavatṣaṭkamucyate
daśabhyaḥ pratipadasi ṣaṭsuvṛccaikavisrugam
trivṛttrivṛte ṣabhyāṃ tu veṣṭanāṣṭau yathākramam
ākramaḥṣolaśīṃ caivaṃ daśavṛtpañcavisrutāḥ
rājñyasi prācyaikādaśyāṃ dvayośca caturo bhavan
trivṛtvā ttra trayodaśyā vṛt tribhiścaikavisrutam
ṛṣayastvā caturviṃśaḥ pañcavṛt ṣaṭ ca visrutāḥ
virālasyaikādaśyāṃ tu trayaścatvāra eva ca
pañcadaśastvā trayodaśyā tra dvau vṛddvayavisruk
samrālekādaśīṃ kuryāt trayovṛccaturastathā
saptadaśastvā stomastu trayodaśīṃ dvayaṃ dvayoḥ
ekādaśyāṃ svarālasi tribhiścatvārivisrutāḥ
trayo daśyekaviṃśastvā trayāṇāṃ vṛtribhistataḥ
adhipatnye syekādaśyāṃ triṣu vṛcca tribhiḥ kramāt
triṇavatrayastriṃśau tvā trayodaśyāṃ tu ṣaṭsuvṛt
ayaṃ paraścaikaviṃśatyāṣṭau vṛcca tvaikādaśaḥ
tebhyo namo viṃśa stu visargāścāṣṭamastataḥ
trayodaśyāyaṃ dakṣiṇā ṣaḍbhirvṛtpañcavisrutāḥ
ayaṃ paścāttrayodaśyāṃ ṣaṇṇā vṛtsaptavisrutāḥ
trayodaśyāyamuttarā triṣu visrutāḥ
trayodaśyāyamupari saptapañcaikanāntakam
ayamagnistu navabhirvisargāḥ pañcamo viduḥ
abodhyagniḥ saptadaśyāṃ ṣaḍbhirvṛccaturo visruk
pañcadaśyavocāmeti dvau vā dvābhyāṃ ca visrutaḥ
janasyagopāḥ ṣolaśyāṃ catvāri saptavisrutāḥ
tvāmagnernavadaśaścaikaṃ saptaika nāntakam
sakhāyassantrayodaśyā tribhiścaiva visarjanaiḥ
sam̐ samidyu saptadaśyāmekena caturakastathā
trayodaśīṃ tu tvāmagne haviṣmantaikavṛtribhiḥ
tvāṃ citraśnavodaśabhistrayāṇāṃ triṣu visrutāḥ
enāvogniṃ pañcadaśīṃ caikaṃ vṛddvayamucyate
viśvamasya drutaṃ trayodaśasu dvayostṛtīyakam
samudra vastu daśabhistrayaścatvāri visrutāḥ
agne vājasyaikādaśyāṃ caturnāṃ pañcabhistataḥ
ekādaśyāṃ saci idhānaḥ punarekaṃ tu ṣaṣṭhake
kṣaporājan trayodaśyāṃ caikaṃ pañca tathaikakam
bhadro no agnirdvādaśīṃ tu trayovṛnnavavisrutāḥ
bhadrā utapradaśasu catasraścaturo visruk
yenā samatsaikādaśyāṃ triṣu vṛcca dvayoratha
navadaśāgniṃ taṃ manye veṣṭanaikāṣṭamo visruk
so agniriyo navadaśaścatvāri daśasu kramāt
saptadaśobhe suścandra pañcavṛtpañcavisrutāḥ
trayodaśyāgne tamadyacaikaṃ vṛcca dvayoryadi
adhāhyagnecaikādaśyāṃ visargāstu catasrakaḥ
ebhirnorkaistrayodaśyāṃ caikaṃ vṛnnavabhistataḥ
caturviṃśāgniṃ hotāraṃ pañcabhyaścatupañcasu
ye eva ṛṣayonavadaśo dvayaṃ ṣaṣṭhe dvināntakam
taṃ patnībhirnavadaśo dvayorvṛtsaptavisrutāḥ
saptadaśyā vo cāṃ tribhyaśca navabhiḥ punaḥ
ayamagniḥ pañcadaśīṃ catvārastuṣadaikatā
saṃpracyavapañcadaśīṃ pañcadaśorathaikakam
viṃśatirudbudhyasvāgne trayo dvābhyāṃ dvayaṃ tataḥ
caturdaśīṃ enavahasyekaṃ vṛcca dvayostathā
tapastapasya ṣaṭpadāścaikaṃ vṛccaturo bhavan
dvāviṃśatiḥ prothadaśvaścaikaṃ vṛccaturaikakam
āyoṣṭvāsadane viṃśetyekaṃ vṛccadvayaṃ visruk
sahasrasyapramāṣṭau ca trayo vṛccaikamiṣyate
iti śrī śaṅkarācāryakṛtau vājasaneyasaṃhitāyāṃ padakārikāratnamālāyāṃ
ṣoḍaśo'dhyāyaḥ samāptaḥ
atha saptadaśo'dhyāyaḥ
namaste rudra dvādaśyāṃ dvau vṛcca tribhirityapi
yāte rudra caturdaśyāṃ trayovṛcca dvayorvisruk
yāmiṣu ca caturdaśyāṃ dvayaṃ vṛccaikameva ca
śivena vacasā tvātu caturdaśiṃ dvayaṃ dvayoḥ
adhyavocatṣolaśīyaṃ tu dvitīyaṃ pañcabhistrayaḥ
navadaśāsau yastāmro dvābhyāṃ daśa ca visrutāḥ
asau yovapañcadaśīṃ ca tvayyāṣṭau tathā dvayam
namostu nītrayodaśyāṃ tisṛṇāṃ caturo bhavan
pramuñcyathanvaṣolaśyāmekaṃ ghṛtsaptamastathā
pijyaṃ dhanustrayodaśyāṃ catvāri saptabhirdvayam
ṣolaśīparite dhanva dvayaṃ cature ekakam
yātehetipañcadaśīṃ caikaṃ caturbhirekatā
dvādaśāvatatya dhanuḥ pañcabhyastu catasrakaḥ
namasta āyurdvādaśyāmekaṃ vṛcca dvayoryadi
māno mahān saptaviṃśaṃ caikaṃ vṛddaśa visrutāḥ
toketanapañcadaśyāmekaṃ vṛccaturekakam
hiraṇyabāhavaḥ ṣolaśyāṃ ṣaṇṇāṃ vṛccaturastathā
babhlṛśāya dvādaśīṃ tu veṣṭanau dvau tathā sati
rohitāyeti dvādaśyāṃ triṣu dvayamathāpivaḥ
kṛtsnāvītāya dvādaśīṃ veṣṭanāḥ pañcakakramāt
vañcate tu trayodaśyāṃ saptabhistu catasrakaḥ
uṣṇīṣiṇe kādaśaṃ ca saptanṛtsaptamī visruk
visṛjadyo vidyadaṣṭau ṣaṇṇāṃ vṛccāṣṭāmī visruk
sabhābhyaḥ sabhāpatyaṣṭau caturṇāṃ cāṣṭamo visruk
gaṇeśyaścāṣṭamī yastu pañcabhyaścāṣṭamī tu vā
senābhyo'ṣṭau yadiśyāṃtu pañcamī hvāṣṭamo visruk
akṣau takṣabhya evasyātpañcamīṃ punaraṣṭamīm
śvabhyaḥ śvapatiraṣṭabhyaḥ pañcavṛddvitayaṃ tataḥ
kapardine'ṣṭabhiḥ kuryātsaptavṛccaikavisrutāḥ
hrasvāyāṣṭau vṛdekaṃ syādāśaveṣṭau vṛdekakam
jyeṣṭhāyāṣṭau trivṛtsyātāṃ sobhyāṣṭadvayaṃ ca vṛt
vanniyāṣṭacaturthaṃ vṛdbilmineṣṭau dvayaṃ bhavet
dhṛṣṇaveṣṭau ca vṛtpañca srutyāyāṣṭapadaṃ bhavet
kūpyāṣṭavṛddvau ca syātāṃ vātyāṣṭabhyo vṛdekakam
śaṅkaveṣṭavṛccaturo vṛkṣe navaṣaṭ catriṣu
pāryyāyāṣṭau dvayaṃ vṛcca sikatyāṣṭau dvivṛdbhavet
vrajajyāṣṭavṛtrayaścaiva śuṣkyāyāṣṭaikaveṣṭanam
parṇyāya ṣolaśīṃ caiva daśavṛnnava visrutāḥ
drā pendhaso navadaśīṃ catvāri triṣu visruṣu
imā rudrā yāṣṭādaśastriṣu vṛttitayaikatā
pañcadaśīṃ yāte rudrā visargāstu dvayaṃ tataḥ
pariṇo ṣṭādaśaiva syāddvayaṃ vṛtṣaṭkameva ca
mīlhaṣṭamo ṣṭādaśastu catvāri pañca visrutāḥ
pañca daśīṃ vikirida trayāṇāṃ vṛcca pañcabhiḥ
sahasrāṇi caikādaśyāṃ vṛddvayoratra pañcabhiḥ
ekādaśīmasaṃkhyātā vṛddvayorvisrugekatā
asminmahati ṣaṇṇāṃ tu caiko visruk tathaikanān
nīlagrīvā pañcabhistu trirvṛccatvārivisrutāḥ
śarvā adhastrirekaraṃ trirvyakṣe caturthī dvau vṛttriḥ
bhūtānāṃ ca caturtha dvau trīn pathāṃ catvāri dvau catuḥ

athāṣṭādaśo'dhyāyaḥ
navaviṃśatyaśmanūrjaṃ vṛccatvāryaṣṭamo dvayoḥ
imāmegne trayodaśyāṃ caikaṃ vṛttribhya eva ca
pañcadaśīmarbudaṃ ca dvābhyāṃ ṣaḍbhistathaikakam
ṛtavasthadvādaśī tu saptabhistu navāvisruk
samudra sya tvā daśabhirdvau visargau prakīrtitau
himasya tvā jarā daśa dvayamatra visrugbhavet
trayo viṃśatirūpajmannekaṃ dvau ca tathaikakam
apāmikṣaṃ caturdaśyāṃ dvayaṃ vṛttribhirekatā
agne pāvakaikādaśyāmekaṃ canāntamatra tu
sanaḥ pāvaka caturdaśyāmekaṃ vṛtpañcacaikakam
pāvake yaikaviṃśatyā visruk pañcatrayaṃścanān
caturviṃśatirnamaste pañcaṣaṭkaikameva tu
saptadaśyāṃ tu ye devāścaikaṃ pañcabhirekatā
ye devāścaturviṃśatyā dvayoḥ saptaikamucyate
caturdaśīṃ prāṇadāśca pañcavṛddaśacaikatā
agnistigmenaikādaśyāṃ visargāstraya īritāḥ
ya imā navadaśyāṃ tu vṛddvayoḥ pañcamaikatā
kiṃ svidāsīdaṣṭādaśastu caturnāṃ dvayamekakam
pañcadaśīṃ viśvataścakṣuḥ ṣaḍbhiḥ daśacaikatā
ekaviṃśe kim̐ svidvanaṃ pañcasaptabhirekakam
navadaśo yā te dhāmāni triṣu tribhirekanān
cakṣuṣaḥ pitāṣṭādaśastrayovṛttriṣu visrutāḥ
viśvakarmā navadaśa ṣaṣṭhe catvāri caikanā
yona pitā viṃśatistu trayo vṛtṣaṣṭhamatra tu
ta āyañnavadarśi dvābhyāñcaiva dvayaikanā
parodivā viṃśatiśca tatraikaṃ sapta visrugāḥ
tamidgarbhaṃ navadaśīmekaṃ triṣu ca caikakam
nataṃ vidātha saptadaśyāṃ dvābhyāṃ catvāri visrutāḥ
viśvakarmāṇyaṣṭādaśyāṃ dvayaṃ caiva catasrakam
āśuśśiśānaḥ ṣolaśīṃ tribhyaścaikādaśāvisruk
saṃkrandano pañcadaśīṃ pañcabhistritayaṃ visruk
sa iṣu hastaiḥ ṣolaśyāmaṣṭau vṛnnavamo bhavet
bṛhaspate jaṣolaśyāṃ catvāri caturuṣu trayaḥ
balavijñāya ṣolaśyāṃ ṣaṇṇāṃ vṛdaṣṭamaikanā
gotrabhidaṃ tu ṣolaśyāṃ pañcamīṃ dvayameva ca
ṣolaśyāmabhigotrāṇi triṣu vṛnnava visrutāḥ
indra āsāṃ pañcadaśīṃ dvayorvṛt ṣaṭ cavisrutāḥ
pañcadaśyenddra sya vṛṣṇaḥ tato dvābhyāṃ catasrakaḥ
saptadaśīmuddha ṛṣayaḥ dvayoścaikaṃ dvayoratha
aṣṭādaśāsmākamindra strayaḥ ṣaṇṇāṃ tathaikanā
navadaśāmīṣāṃ cittaṃ dvau vṛcca tribhya eva ca
avasṛṣṭā pañcadaśī dvayaṃ caikaṃ ca caikakam
pañcadaśyāṃ preta jayatā ca saptavisarjanāḥ
asau yā senaikaviṃśe dvayaṃ ca tribhirekatā
yatra bāṇāḥ pañcadaśīṃ dvayoḥ sapta ca visrutāḥ
marmāṇi te viṃśatistu visargāḥ pañcamo bhavan
udenaṃ pañcadaśīṃ tu trayovṛccaikavisrutam
indre mantu caturdaśyāṃ triṣu vṛccaikamucyate
yasya kurma ṣolaśīṃ tu pañcavisruta etu na
pañcadiśo navadaśīṃ triṣu triṣu cāṣṭasuvisrutāḥ
ṣolaśyāṃ samiddhe agnau catvāri pañcasu kramāt
daivyāyadya caturdaśyāṃ catvāryaṣṭastathaikanā
vītam̐ haviḥ pañcadaśyā mekaṃ saptabhya eva ca
saptadaśīṃ sūrya raśmiścatvāri caturṣu trayaḥ
vimāna eṣo'ṣṭādaśastriṣu ṣaḍbhistu caikatā
navadaśyukṣā samudro dvābhyāṃ caivāṣṭavisrutāḥ
devahūssaptadaśīṃ tu dvayoḥ ṣaṭsutathaikakam
vājasya mātrayodaśyāṃ pañcatribhirdvayaṃ tataḥ
udgrābhaṃ ca caturdaśyāṃ catvāryekaṃ ca trīn kramāt
kramadhvaṃ tu trayodaśyāṃ visargāḥ pañcamo bhavan
prācīmaṃ caikaviṃśatyā catvāryaṣṭaikamucyate
pṛthivyā ahaṃ saptadaśyā catvāri visrutāḥ
svaryantona caturdaśyā catasrastu catasrakaḥ
agne prehiti ṣolaśyāṃ tribhyaḥ saptabhirastra tu
saptadaśyagne sahasrāsstribhyastribhirathaikakam
suparṇosyāṣṭadaśabhirekaṃ vṛttraya ekakam
ājuhvānastu navabhistṛtīyaṃ vṛddvayaṃ visruk
tām̐ savituraṣṭādaśaścatvāri dvayamucyate
vidhemate navadaśaścatasro dvau tu caikakam
prendo agne trayodaśyāmekovā pañcavisrutāḥ
citiṃ juhomyaṣṭādaśīṃ catasraḥ pañcanaikakam
dvāviṃśatiḥ sapta tegne caikaṃ vṛtpañcabhiḥ punaḥ
śukrajyotiścāṣṭābhirvā pañcabhyaḥ ṣaṭtathaikakam
īdṛṅca saptamīṃ caivaṃ catvāri traya eva ca
ṛtajitsatryajitsaptā ṣaṭsucatvāri visrutāḥ
ṛtassatve dhruvassapta dvayorvṛtspta visrutāḥ
īdṛkṣāsassaptadaśīṃ pañcavṛddaśacaikanā
svatavān praghāsī saptapañcamyekaṃ tu caikakam
indra ndaivī strayoviṃśastribhyastrayodaśīṃ dvayam
iti śaṅkarācāryakṛtau vājasaneya saṃhitāyāṃ padakārikāratnamālāyāṃ
aṣṭādaśo'dhyāyaḥ samāptaḥ
atha ekonaviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ
imam̐ stamasaptadaśīṃ dvayorvṛccaikanāntakam
ghṛtaṃ mimikṣe navadaśa trivṛttridvayavisruk
samudrā drurminnavadaśīṃ dvayorvṛddvayamekanā
saptadaśīṃ vayannāma dvayoḥ pañcaikamevatu
catvāri śṛṅgānavadaśā tu dvau sapta caikanā
tridhāhitaṃ navadaśīmekena ṣaḍbhirekakam
etā aruṣaṃ trayo viṃśastribhyaśca navabhistathā
sindhoriva pañcadaśīṃ catvāri nā caikakam
abhiplavaṃ pañcadaśīṃ tribhiścāṣṭau visarjanāḥ
kaṃ cā iva cāṣṭādaśīṃ dvayaṃ vṛtpañca visrutāḥ
mā abhya ṛṣata cāṣṭādaśo dvayoścaiva dvayoratha
yā mante caikaviṃśatyā catubhiścaturekatā
nājaśca meṣṭādaśabhiścatvāri dvādaśi visrukam
prāṇo pāno dvādaśīṃ tu trayaṣṣaṭkaṃ ca visrutāḥ
ojassahodvādaśastu visargāścaturo'bhavan
jyaiṣṭhayaṃ ca me caturdaśyā ekaṃ vṛccaiva pañcamaḥ
satyāṃ śraddhā dvādaśīṃ tu yaṃ ca daṣitayaṃ tathā
pitṛṃ ve dvaṃdvādaśīṃ syāttribhyaścaturbhya eva ca
dvāśo yantāyantā ca dvayoḥ ṣaḍbhirvisarjanaiḥ
śarmayaḥ priyaṃ dvādaśyāṃ caikaṃ cāṣṭabhirityapi

dvādaśī carairāyāstribhyo vṛcca tribhiryathā
vīhayoyavā dvādaśya visargā dvādaśastathā
dvādaśī cāśmā nṛttikā pañcamo vāvisarjanāḥ
agnerāpastrayodaśyāṃ dvayo rekādaśastathā
vasurvasatiścāṣṭau vā visargāḥ pañcamaḥ utaḥ
agnirindra ssomaḥ saptacaturbhistu visarjanaiḥ
mitro varuṇāssaptamaścatvāri ca visarjanāḥ
pṛthivīyantarikṣaṃ saptacaturo visrugmatu
aṃśūraśmi dvādaśīṃ tu catvāri dvādaśo visruk
dvādaśyāgrayaṇo vaiśva pañcabhistu trayodaśa
sruśṛ ca ścamasā dvādaśī ṣaḍbhirnavabhireva ca
agnirgharmastrayo daśyāmekaṃ vṛddvādaśo nisruk
vrataṃ mṛtavastribhistribhyo vṛddvaya īritā
ekātisraḥ saptadaśīmekādaśa navā visruk
catasroṣṭau dvādaśīṃ tu pañcavṛt ṣaḍasavisrugaḥ
triyapi strīyapī daśasu vātathāṣṭau triṣu visrugāḥ
paṣṭhavāṭ ca dvādaśastu caikaṃ vṛttribhirekana
navadaśabhiḥ svā nāya svāhā saptaikameva ca
iyaṃ terāṭ caturdaśyā dvābhyāṃ vṛccaikameva ca
āyuryajñe na ṣolaśyāṃ visargaḥ saptamaṃ punaḥ
stomo yajussaptadaśīṃ trayovṛtsaptavisrugaḥ
iti śrīśāṅkarācāryakṛtau vājasaneyasaṃhitāyāṃ padakārikā ratnamālāyāṃ
ekonaviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ samāptaḥ
atha viṃśo'dhyāyaḥ
viśve adhaviṃśatiḥ syāddvayorvṛtṣaṭkameva ca
vājo nasya caturdaśyāṃ trayāṇāṃ navabhiḥ punaḥ
navadaśo vā jonotha trayāṇāmaṣṭamaikana
vājaḥ plurastāṣṭādaśo dvābhyāmaṣṭabhirekakam
sāṃ mā sṛjā pañcadaśamekavṛccaturastathā
payaḥ pṛthivyaikaviṃśo dvayoratra daśāvisruk
ṛtāṣādviṃśatiścaiva tathaikaṃ cāṣṭavisrutām
saṃhito viśvanavabhiḥ dvayoḥ ṣaṭsu visarjanāḥ
navamyāṃ suṣumnaḥ sūryaṃ vāyoścaiva ṣalatra tu
naveṣiroviśva vyacācaikaṃ saptasuvisrugāḥ
bhujyuḥ suparmo navabhiścaikena saptamī yathā
navaprajāprati rviśvakarmācaturthama pañcasu
sano cāndra vadaśa caikaṃ tribhirvisarjanaiḥ
pañcadaśa samudro si triṣu cāṣṭadvayaṃ tataḥ
rucaṃ naḥ pañcadaśyāṃ tu nathaikaṃ dvaya
tatvā yāmi viṃśatyā dvayaṃ pradaṣṭa visrutāḥ
sannavamastathā ṣṭai tā caṣṭaḍbhirvisarjanīyakaiḥ
saptadaśyagniṃ yunajmiṃ trayo vṛcca dvayaṃ visruk
navadaśīmaite pakṣau catvāri vṛcca pañcamaḥ
indurdakṣo'ṣṭādaśīṃ tu catvāri daśacaikakam
diṣo mūrdhā trayodaśyāṃ dvayoḥ ṣaḍbhistu visrugaiḥ
viśvasya mūrdhā dvāviṃśastathā dvau saptacaikakam
iṣṭo yajño strayodaśyāṃ triṣu saptasu visruṣu
ekādaśīmiṣṭo agnirdvayoḥ saptayathākramam
yadākūtādviṃśatistu saptavṛtsaptavisrutāḥ
etam̐ sathasthāṣṭādaśī sapta vṛttribhirekana
saptadaśyetaṃ tvā nātha ṣaḍbhirvṛtpañcamaikana
prastareṇa catudaśyā bhyāṃ vṛcca dvayoratha
yaddattaṃ yat ṣau caiva hi
yatra dhārāścaturdaśyā jyābhyāmaṣṭabhireva ca
caturdaśimaye agnayastathā dvau caturo bhavan
vārtra hatyā ya cāṣṭābhyo dvau veṣṭau tataḥ param
sahadānuṃ ṣolaśī syād dvayaṃ veṣṭanbamatra tu
mṛgonabhīmo viṃśatistribhiḥ saptabhirekanān
vaiśvānaro naikādaśyā dvitīyaṃ vṛtṣalatra tu
pṛṣṭo divaukaviṃśatyā visargāstu trayodaśa
abhyāmataṃ navadaśīṃ dvayaṃ caiva dvitīyakam
vayaṃ te dhyasaptadaśīṃ dvayaṃ dvitīyamekakam
dhāmaśca ta caturdaśyāṃ dvābhyāṃ vṛtsaptavisrugāḥ
iti śrīśaṅkarācāryakṛtau vājasaneya saṃhitāyāṃ padakārikāratnamālāyāṃ
viṃśo'dhyāyaḥ samāptaḥ
atha ekaviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ
svādvīṃtvā viṃśatiścaivaṃ catvāryekavisrutam
paririto saptadaśyāṃ catvāryaṣṭaikamucyate
dvādaśī vāyoḥ pūtaśca dvayaṃ saptaca visrutāḥ
punātivote canavabhirveṣṭanaṃ caikamevaca
dvāviṃśati brahmakṣatraṃ caikavṛtpañcabhistataḥ
nānā hi trayoviṃśatyā trayaścatvāri caikakam
āśvinaṃ tejodaśamīmekaṃ vṛccaikavisrutam
pañcadaśyāṃ tejo siṣadbhirvisarjanīyakaiḥ
caturdaśīṃ ca yā vyāghrāṃ visrugekaṃ tathaiva ca
yadāpipe caturdaśyāṃ dvayostriṣu ca caikakam
dvādaśastu devā yajñaṃ dvayaṃ caiva visarjanam
dīkṣāyai rūpaikādaśyāṃ dvitīyaṃ ca dvayorvisruk
ekādaśyā tibhyarūpaṃ catvāri traya īritāḥ
somasya rūpaṃ dvādaśyāṃ veṣṭanau dvau prakīrtitau
āsandīrūpaikādaśyāṃ catvāri tritayaṃ bhavet
vedyāvedistrayodaśyāṃ caikaṃ vṛddvayameva ca
havirdhānaṃ yaddvādaśī visrastrayaśca visrutāḥ
praiṣebhiḥ praiṣādaśabhiraṣṭau ṣaḍdvayaṃ tataḥ
daśabhyaḥ paśubhiḥ paśūn catvāri vṛtṣaludvayoḥ
dhānāḥ karambho dvādaśīṃ caikaṃ vṛtṣadbhiratra tu
daśabhirdhānānām̐ rūpaṃ dvayordvitayamācake
payaso rūpaṃ trayodaśyāṃ trayaścavisrutaḥ kramāt
āśrā vayetyekādaśyāṃ pañcavṛtpañcamastathā
daśārtha ṛcairūndhānāṃ trayaḥ pañcama eva ca
aśvibhyāṃ prātassavanaṃ daśatriṣvekameva ca
vāyavyairvāyaikādaśyāṃ tathaikaṃ vṛttriṣukramāt
yajurbhirāpyaṃ dvādaśyāṃ pañcamo navamaḥ sakṛt
ilābhirbhakṣāṃ navabhiḥ ṣaṭca dvau cadvayostataḥ
vratena dīkṣāmāpnoti padā dvādaśabhiḥ punaḥ
caturdaśaitāvadrū paṃ visarjanīyamekakam
surāvantaṃ pañcadaśīṃ pañcamīṃ ṣaṭkamiṣyate
yaste rasaḥ saptadaśyāṃ tathaikaṃ vṛccaturthakam
sapta caturdaśo yamaśvanānamecerekamekakam
yadatrariptoṣṭādaśastisraścaiva visarjanāḥ
pitṛbhyaḥ svadhānavabhiścaturthassaptamastathā
akṣan pitaraḥ saptamīṃ trayo visarjanaikanān
punantu mā caikādaśyāṃ dvitīyaṃ caturo visruk
ekādaśīṃ punantu mā dvayorvṛcca trayo thavai
punantu mā de dvādaśyāṃ dvau vṛddvau traya īritāḥ
pavitreṇapunīmā daśabhiścaika nāntakam
yatte pavitraikādaśyāṃ caikaṃ vṛttantu ṣaṭ tathā
ubhābhyāṃ devanavabhirekaṃ vṛttaddvayaṃ visruk
vaiśvadevīsaptadaśyāṃ trayo vṛtaśca ṣaṭsva ca
ye samānāśca dvādaśyāṃ vṛdadvayośca ṣalatra tu
jīvājīveṣvekādaśyāṃ caturo visrugekana
navadaśo dve sutī cavṛcca dvau tu yathākramam
idam̐ havistrayoviṃśo navavṛcca caturthakam
udīratām̐ navadaśaścaikaṃ vṛnnavamastathā
tvam̐ somapnanavadaśīṃ dvayoścatvāri visrutāḥ
tvayāhinastu viṃśatirdvābhyāmaṣṭadvayoratha
tvam̐ somapi saptadaśyāṃ catvāri trīṇi visrutāḥ
barhiṣado viṃśatistu dvayaṃ vṛt ṣaṭcavisrutāḥ
upahūtāsaptadaśyāṃ dvayostribhyastu caikanān
navadaśī āhaṃ pitṝn catvāri caturodvayam
agniṣvāttāḥ ṣolaśīṃ ca pañcabhyaḥ pañcamo viduḥ
āyantunaḥ saptadaśastrayassapta dvayaṃ ca nān
pañcadaśe yegniṣvāttāḥ pañcavṛcca caturthakam
ṣolaśyagniṣvāttā nṛtucatvāri ṣadakamekana
ācyājānu viṃśatistu dvayoḥ pañca visarjanāḥ
āsīnāsassaptadaśyāṃ caikaṃ vrataṃ caturthakam
yamagne kavyavāhana caturdaśyāṃ trayaṃ tribhiḥ
yo gniḥ kavyapañcadaśyāṃ catvāriṣaṭkamekatā
navadaśīṃ tvamagne īlitṛ triścaturaikakam
idaṃ pitṛbhyo viṃśatistrīṇi ṣaṇṇāṃ tu visrutāḥ
ye ce hapañcaviṃśatyā catvāritisṛṣu dvayoḥ
athā yathā navadaśaścaikamaṣṭau dvayaṃ tataḥ
uśantastvā caturdaśyāṃ trivisargo dvināntakam
apāṃ phenenaikādaśyāṃ visargāḥ ṣaḍyathā kramam
navadaśīṃ somo rājā caikaṃ vṛccaturo bhavan
caturviṃśatyādbhyakṣīraṃ pañcasapta ca visrutāḥ
reto mūtraṃ trayodaśyā dvau vṛtau traya īritāḥ
trayo daśī dṛṣṭārūpe caturastu dvayorathā
vedena rūpe ṣolaśyāṃ catvāri vṛccatasrakaḥ
sīsenatantraṃ pañcadaśīṃ vṛccaikatrāya ekatā
viṃśatistadaśyarūpaṃ dvayamaṣṭabhireva ca
tadaśvinā pañcadaśīṃ tathā traikaṃ trayo visruk
sarasvati saptadaśyāṃ tatraikaṃ triṣu sukramāt
payasā saptadaśyāṃ tu vṛtamekaṃ trayastathā
ṣolaśī mintrassutrāmā dvau trayaścaikameva ca
navadaśāntrāṇi sthālīrdvayostathā ṣalatra tu
kumbho maniṣṭhu ṣṭādaśaścaturo daśacaikana
mukham̐ sadasya dvāviṃśo visrukapañcaika nāntakam
saptadaśyāśvibhyāṃ cakṣurghṛtau dvau pañcabhiryathā
avirnameṣaṣṣolaśyāṃ dvayośca saptasukramāt
indra sya rūpo'ṣṭādaśo visargāstu trayastathā
navadaśātmannupasthe dvayordvābhyāṃ dvayaṃ tataḥ
aṅgānyātmannaṣṭādaśaścaikaṃ caturbhirekatā
sarasvatī yoṣṭādaśastrayoda dvayamekatā
tejaḥ paśūnāṃ ṣolaśyāṃ trayaśca pañcamastathā
ṣolaśyā kṣatrasya nābhirekaṃ catvāri visrutāḥ
saptadaśyāśvinorbhaiṣaṃ dvayorvṛccaikameva ca
krosikatamo navamyāṃ dvayorvṛddvayamekatā
śiromeśrīḥ saptadaśyāṃ saptadaśyāṃ dvayorvṛccatusaptabhiḥ
jihvāmebhadraṃ ṣolaśyāṃ dvayamaṣṭabhireva ca
dvādaśīṃ bāhū me balaṃ caikaṃ tatraikamiṣyate
pañcadaśīṃ puṣṭīrme rācaturastu catasrakam
nābhirme tu caturdaśyāṃ dvau vṛtau pañcabhistataḥ
caturdaśīṃ jaṅghābhyāṃ tu dvābhyāṃ dvitayameva ca
aṅgeṣvātmaṃdra ca ṣaṭ padā visrugekamathaikana
trayo devāstrayodaśyāṃ tisṛbhirnavabhiḥ punaḥ
prathamā dvitī dvādaśyāṃ dvayaṃ ca navamī visruk
sāmānmṛgbhiḥ saptadaśī sapta trayodaśaikatā
pañcadaśyāṃ lomāni prapañcamyāṃ pañcamo visruk
iti śrī śaṅkarācārya kṛtau vājasaneyasaṃhitāyāṃ
padakārikāratnamālāyāṃ ekaviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ samāptaḥ
atha dvāviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ
yaddevāstu trayodaśyāṃ vṛtamekaṃ ca pañcabhiḥ
caturdaśīṃ yādidivā visargāstu trayo bhavan
yadi jāgraccaturdaśyāṃ tribhirvisarjanīyakaiḥ
śūdre ryapako dvādaśyāṃ vṛccaikaṃ ca dvayostataḥ
dvādaśyāṃ drupadādiva trayaḥ pañcasu visrutāḥ
udvayaṃ tu trayodaśyāṃ catvāri caturastathād
apo adyāntra cātriṃśaddvayaṃ catvāri caikatā
samāvavartiviṃśatyā pañcacatvāri ca dvayoḥ
saptadaśābhyādadhāmi dvayaṃ caivaikamiṣyate
yatra brahmasaptadaśyāṃ dvau visargau samāhitau
saptadaśīṃ yatrendra ścaturastu visarjanāḥ
trayo daśyām̐ śunā tem̐ śuḥ pañcamastu visruk tataḥ
siñcanti parisiñcanti trayodaśīṃ visrugvayam
dhānā vantaṃ karambhāṣṭau dvābhyāṃ vṛcca visrugvayam
bṛhadindrā ya dvādaśyāṃ dvau vṛttribhyastu caikakam
adhvaryo adibhirdaśadvayaṃ caiva tathā bhavet
yo bhūtānāṃ navadaśo vṛdekaṃ ṣaṭkadvayoratha
prāṇapāme trayodaśyāṃ ṣaḍvṛnnavabhiratra tu
navāśvina kṛtasya te pañcamaṃ caikamena ca
samiddha indraḥ ṣolaśyā ṣaḍvṛccāṣṭama eva ca
narāśam̐ sapraṣolaśyāṃ caturaḥ ṣaṭ ca dvau canān
ṣolaśyā mīlito devai pañcavṛnnavamekatā
juṣāṇo barhiḥ ṣolaśī pañcamyāṃ navamaikakam
indra nduraḥ saptadaśyāṃ trayāṇāṃ dvādaśastathā
pañcadaśśaruṣā sānaktā vṛtaiḥ ṣaḍbhistu caikatā
daivyā mimānāṣolaśyāṃ dvayaṃ tu traya ekatā
tisrodevī ssaptadaśyāmekaṃ vṛccaiva saptabhiḥ
tvaṣṭādadhadaṣṭādaśaścaikaṃ tribhyastrayo bhavan
vanaspatiḥ saptadaśyāṃ dvābhyāṃ ṣaḍbhistu caikatā
stokānāṃ tu pañcadaśīṃ dvayoḥ sapta ca visrutāḥ
āyātvindro viṃśatistu trayaścaivāṣṭāmo visruka
āna indro ṣṭādaśastu pañcamaścāṣṭamaikatā
navadaśyāna indro haścatvāri pañcabhiḥ punaḥ
trātāraṃ saptadaśyāṃ tu caturo dvayamiṣyate
caturdaśīndraḥ sutrāmā ṣaṭkam̐ saptabhirekatā
tasya vayaṃ cāṣṭadaśaścatasraścaturaikatā
navadaśāmandrai rindr trayaścatvāri ca ddvayam
evedindraṃ viśatistu caturthaṃ saptamo visruk
caturdaśyāṃ samiddho'gnirdvau vā ṣaṣṭhe tu visrutāḥ
dvādaśīṃ tan napābhiṣaddvayaṃ caikaṃ tu aikatā
indrā yendraṃ sarasvatī padāścaikādaśī kramāt
ājuhvānāstrayodaśyāṃ caikaṃ vṛddvayamatra tu
trayodaśāśvinānamucereke vratamekatā
kavaṣyo na caturdaśyāṃ tribhiḥ pañcaikamucyate
uṣāsā naktamekādaśyā trayaścatvāri caikatā
pātaṃ no aśvinādivā caturdaśyekavisrutam
tisrastredhā dvādaśīṃ ca dvayaṃ vṛcca dvayaṃ catat
trayodhyaśvinābheja vṛccaikaṃ triṣu visruṣu
ṛtujyendra ścaikādaśyāṃ trayāṇāṃ vṛccaturthakam
gobhirnasotrayodaśyāmekamekaṃ pracakatre
trayodaśāśvinā havirvisargantu dvayaṃ tama
yamaśvinā trayodaśyāmekaṃ visruktathaikanā
tamindraṃ tu trayodaśyāṃ visargāstisra eva ca
yandra indraṃ dvādaśyā tridvayaṃ ca ṣaṣṭhake
dvādaśīṃ savitā varuṇaikaṃ vṛddvayaṃ merire
varuṇaḥ kṣatraṃ dvādaśyāṃ caikaṃ vṛcca dvitīyakam
ekādaśāśvināgobhirvisrutau dvau tu caikanā
tānāsatyācaikādaśyāṃ trayo vṛtastacaikakam
tābhīṣajā dvādaśīṃ catvāri traya ūcatu
yu uṃ surāmaikādaśyāṃ dvābhyāmekaṃ tu śasyate
yasminna śvā saṣṣolaśyāṃ catvāriptamekatā
ahā vyagne saptadaśyāṃ ṣaḍvratastu dvayoryadi
dvādaśyāśvinātejasā trayovisruta eva ca
gomadguṣuṇā daśabhiścatvāri caikameva ca
nayatparo navadaśaca tisraḥ ṣaḍbhireva ca
navabhistāna āvolha dvayaṃ ca dvitayaṃ ca tat
pāvakānassarasvāṣṭau dvābhyāṃ vṛttisṛbhirvisruk
codayitrī sūnṛtānāṃ saptacaikaṃ vrataṃ bhavet
maho arnastu daśabhirvisrutā ścaturastathā
daśendrā yāhi citrabhāno dvayaṃ caturastathā
dhiyeṣito viprasapta dvau vṛtau ta catasrakaḥ
tū tujāna upāṣṭau ca vṛtamekaṃ caturthakam
aśvinābhiṣaikādaśyāṃ trayaṃ caikaṃ pravartate
iti śrīśaṅkarācāryakṛtau vājasaneyasaṃhitāyāṃ padakārikāratnamālāyāṃ
dvāviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ samāptaḥ
atha trayoviṃśo'dhyāyaḥ
dvādaśīmimaṃ me varuṇa visargaikaṃ tu śasyate
tvanno agne saptadaśyāṃ caikaṃ ṣaṣṭhe tu caikatā
satvanno gnaikaviṃśatyā dvābhyāṃ vṛddaśavisrutāḥ
pañcadaśīṃ mahīmuṣu pañca vṛcca yathā viduḥ
sutrāmāṇaṃ pañcadaśīṃ vṛtaścaturtha eva ca
sunāvamāruhe saptadvayo vṛtorathantaraṃ
āno mitranavamyāṃ tu tathaikaṃ vṛdvisrugdvayoḥ
prabāhuvāsi srajaṃ ca viṃśati dvau visarjanau
samiddho'gnirdvādaśīṃ tu catvāri navabhirvisruk
dvādaśyāṃ tanūnapāccatustu ṣalityapi
trayodaśīlābhiragnirdvayorekādaśastathā
subarhiragnirdvādaśyāṃ caturo navamaikatā
durodevīḥ pañcadaśyāṃ caikamekādaśāparāḥ
uṣeyahvī caturdaśyāṃ vṛtaḥ pañca ṣalatra tu
daivyā hotārā trayodaśyekaṃ caturekatā
tisra ilā pañcadaśyāṃ tathaikamaṣṭabhiḥ punaḥ
tvaṣṭāturī trayodaśa strayo vṛtastu ṣaṭsu ca
śamitānaścaturdaśyāmekaṃ pañcaikamucyate
svāhā yajñaṃ trayodaśyāṃ dvayaṃ vṛtaṃ ca saptamī
vasantena dvādaśīṃ ca dvābhyāṃ ṣaṣṭhe yathā vidhi
grīṣmeṇa ca trayodaśyāṃ vidurekaṃ tu ṣaṣṭhake
trayodaśaśca varṣābhirekaṃ ca ṣaṣṭhameva ca
śāradena trayodaśastathā caikaṃ tu ṣadakakam
hemantena trayodaśyāṃ vṛccaikaṃ ca tathāṣṭamīm
trayodaśaḥ śaiśireṇa vṛtamekamathāṣṭamaḥ
hotāyakṣannavaviṃśastatra dvau daśamaḥ kramāt
tanūnapātsaptadaśa catvāriṣaṣṭhamaikanā
narāśam̐ samekaviṃśastato dvāvaṣṭamo visruk
ilekito viṃśatistu dvau vṛtau ṣaḍbhirekatā
barhirūrṇā vaṣolaśyāṃ dvābhyāṃ vṛccaturo visruk
durodiśaścaturviṃśastrayāṇāṃ ca daśaiva tu
supeśasā ca viṃśatyā triṣu vṛtsu dvayovatha
daivyā hotārāṣṭādaśo visrutau dvau tathāpivā
tisrodevīṃ viṃśatyaikaṃ ca saptasu kramāt
suretasaṃ caturviṃśaddvayorvṛccatuṣaṣṭhake
navaspatiṃ savitāraṃ saptadaśyekamekakam
pañcacatvāriṃśadagniṃ svāhāścatvāri cāṣṭame
caturviṃśāśvinau chāgau dvādaśastu visarjanāḥ

vāyuḥ punālekādaśyāṃ visrutāstraya eva ca
savitā tena va daśastrayo vṛccaikamatra tu
paraṃ mṛtyo dvāviṃśatiścaturaḥ pañcamaikana
śaṃṣātaḥ śannavadaśo visargāḥ pañcamaikana
kalpantāṃ te ṣolaśīṃ ca vṛdekaṃ ṣaṣṭhake tathā
aśmanvatī viṃśatiśca caikaṃ vṛtriścatasrakaḥ
caturdaśyapādyamapatrayo vṛddvayamucyate
anaḍvāhaṃ navamyāṃ tu trayāṇāṃ vṛccaṣaṣṭhake
aṣṭādaśā yuṣmānagne tri yāstisro dvayośca tat
navadaśīmaṃ jīvebhyaścaikaṃ vṛtaṃ tu pañcamaḥ
paramegāṃ caturdaśyāṃ caikaṃ vṛttraya ekatā
saptadaśīṃ kavyādaṃ ca pañcavṛccaturaikatā
vaha vapāṃ viṃśatiśca caturthaḥ sapta trīn kramāt
syonā pṛthivī caturdaśyāṃ dvayoścatasṛbhistataḥ
trayodaśyāsmātvamadhi vṛddvayostrirvisarjanāḥ
iti śrī śaṅkarācāryakṛtau vājasaneyasaṃhitāyāṃ padakārikāratnamālāyāṃ
pañcatriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ
atha ṣaṭtriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ
ṛcaṃ vācamaṣṭādaśastattrayaṃ ca suvisrutāḥ
yanme chidraṃ navadaśī dvābhyāṃ sapta ca visrutāḥ
kayā tvanno dvādaśīṃ ca vṛdekaṃ dvau tu caikakam
indro viśvasya rājāṣṭau dvayorvṛttisṛbhistataḥ
mitro varuṇo bhavāṣṭau vṛdekaṃ ṣaṭsu visrutāḥ
vātaḥ pavati navamīṃ visargāścaturastathā
ahāniśamaṣṭādaśaścaturo vṛcca pañcamaḥ
devīrabhiṣṭaye daśa visrutāścaturthakaḥ
dyauḥ śāntirantaṃ navamīṃ visargāḥ ṣaṭtathaiva ca
dhṛte dṛm̐ ha māmitrasya padāstu caikaviṃśatiḥ
jyok ce sandru syaṣṭamyāṃ tu vṛddvayaṃ tu bhavedyadi
namaste astu daśamīṃ catvāri traya ekatā
yato yataścaturdaśyāṃ trayaścāṣṭabhiratra ca
trayodaśyāṃ tu taccakṣurdvayorvṛtau dvayorvisruk
iti śrīśaṅkarācāryakṛtau vājasaneyasaṃhitāyāṃ padakārikāratnamālāyāṃ
ṣaṭtriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ samāptaḥ
atha saptatriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ
ādade nāriścaturastatravaikaṃ tu visrutam
devī dyāvā caturdaśyāṃ trayovṛto dvayorvisruk
devyo vamrīyaḥ ṣaṇṇāṃ tu vṛdekaṃ caturastathā
iyatyagre pañcamīṃ tu visrutaścaikamiṣyate
indra syaujaḥ pañcabhirvā visrutau dve yathā tathā
praitu brahmaṇaḥ ṣolaśyāṃ vṛdekaṃ caturo visruk
makhasya śira ṣaḍbhistu visrutau dvau pare tathā
aśvasya tvā caturdaśyāṃ dvābhyāṃ vṛcca catasrakaḥ
caturthāmṛjave sādhave tvā vṛccaikamevaca
yamā yamakhāyaṃ saptadaśyekaṃ vṛtṣaṣṭhameva ca
anādhṛṣṭā purastācca catuścatvāriṃśadvituḥ
veṣṭanā daśavijñeyā visargā viṃśatirbhavet
svāhā marudbhirnavamīṃ dvayoścaiva trayastathā
caturdaśagarbho devānāṃ pitaikaṃ trayo visruk
ekaviṃśatissamagnirvisargā dvayameva ca
dhartā divo viṣolaśyāṃ vṛttrayastu ṣalityatha
apaśyaṅgo pāṃṣṭādaśa strayo vṛtsapta visrutāḥ
navaviṃśadviśvāsāṃ tu vṛttrayaḥ pañcacaikana
pitā nosi trayo viṃśaddvayoḥ pañca dvināntakau
ahaḥ ketunā dvādaśyāṃ vṛttrayā caturo visruk
iti śrī śaṅkarācārya kṛtau vājasaneyasaṃhitāyāṃ
padakārikārantamālāyāṃ saptatriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ
atha aṣṭatriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ
ādadebhyoḥ pañcadaśyāṃ gāṇāntaṃ ca vihāya ca
ila e hyadi tathaiva padāścaikādaśastathā
adityairāsnānavamyāṃ śvibhyāṃ pinvasvaveṣṭanāstra eva ca
yaste stano viṃśatistu vṛtpañcaikādaśastathā
gāyatraṃ caturviṃśo dvayorvṛcca trayo visruk
samudrā yatvānavabhirvṛtamekaṃ tu vidyate
savitraru bhumaṣaṭsu caturo vṛcca sarvadā
yamāyāṅgirasvateṣṭau vṛtamekaṃ tu caikakam
viśvā āśādvādaśīṃ tu dvayaṃ trayastathā dvayoḥ
dividhāstu trayodaśyāṃ vṛccaikaṃ tu caturthakaḥ
aśvinā gharmaṃ daśabhirekaṃ catvāri visrutāḥ
apātāmaśvinā daśa vṛccaikamekavisrutam
iṣe pinva svāṣṭā daśa stato dvā vṛdathāntaram
svāhā pūṣṇo caikādaśyāṃ pañcavṛccāṣṭanisrutāḥ
rudrā ya rudra hataye saptadaśadvayaṃ dvayoḥ
abhīmaṃ mahyekādaśyāṃ caikaṃ vṛtaṃ tisṛṣvapi
catussraktiḥ pañcadaśyāṃ ṣaḍvṛccaikādaśastathā
gharmaitatte pañcamīṃ tu gaṇānto yatra nocyate
dvādaśya cikradadvṛṣā dvayo vṛtpañcamaikatā
yāvatī dyā saptadaśyāṃ trayo vṛdekavisrutam
ṣaḍviṃśatiṃ mayityadgi ndri yaṃ catvāri pañcamaḥ
tviṣassaṃpṛkapañcadaśyāṃ navavṛccaturo visruk
iti śrīśaṅkarācāryakṛtau vājasaneya saṃhitāyāṃ padakārikāratnamālāyāṃ
maṣṭatriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ samāptaḥ
atha ekonacatvāriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ
svāhāprāṇebhyaḥ ṣolaśīṃ tisṛṇāṃ pañcavisrutāḥ
vāce prāṇāya caturthaṃ padakāle tu dṛśyate
pañcadaśīṃ manasaḥ kāmamekaṃ pañca tu visrutāḥ
ṣaḍviṃśatiḥ prajāpatiḥ saṃbhriyamāṇaikādaśa
visargāstu sapta daśaścaikaṃ nāntaṃ tathā viduḥ
savitā prathaṣaḍviṃśo visrugekādaśaikanān
ugraśca bhīmo daśabhirvṛttrayaḥ pañca caikakam
viṃśatyāgniṃ hṛdayena ṣaḍvṛtāśca dvayorvisruk
viṃśatirugraṃ lohi saptavṛtpañcacaikatā
lomabhyaḥ svāhaikādaśyāṃ caturo vṛcca pañcamī
āyāsāya prāyāsāṣṭau caturo vṛta eva ca
tapase tapyaṣaḍbhistu vṛtamekaṃ tu caikatā
yamāyāntakā yāṣṭamyāṃ vṛdekaṃ tu dvayorvisruk
iti śrīśaṅkarācārya kṛtau vājasaneyasaṃhitāyāṃ padakārikāratnamālāyāṃ
ekonacatvāriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ
atha catvāriṃśo'dhyāyaḥ
īśāvāsyāṃ saptadaśyāṃ visrugo dvau yathāvidhi
saptadaśīṃ kurvanneva visrugdvau caikanāntakam
saptadaśyasuryā nāma vṛttribhiḥ pañcacaikanān
anejadekaṃ viṃśatirvisruk pañca trināntakam
tadejati caturdaśyā dvau vṛtau dvau tu visrutau
caturdaśīṃ yastu sarvā dvayaṃ vṛddvayamekanān
caturdaśo yasmin sarvā vṛttrayaḥ ṣaṭ ca caikanān
saparyagānavadaśa caturthe saptamaikanā
andhantamaḥ pañcadaśastrayovṛtpañca visrutāḥ
caturdaśyanyadevāhurekaṃ vṛttraya eva ca
pañcadaśīṃ vidyāṃ ca vidyā ca visrugekakam
pañcadaśyandhamaḥ prapañcavṛtpañca visrutāḥ
anyadeva caturdaśyāṃ trayo vṛttrīṇi visrutāḥ
sabhūtiṃ ca pañcadaśyāṃ caturo vṛccacaikakam
trayodaśyāṃ hiraṇmaye dvau vṛtau caika nāntakam
pañcaviṃśatiṃ pūṣannekariṣe yamatrayaṃ vṛtam
caturo visrutaścaiva dvau nāntau śruticoditau
vāyuranilaṃ ṣolaśyāṃ trayovṛdekavisrutam
iti śrīśaṅkarācāryakṛtau vājasaneyasaṃhitāyāṃ
padakārikā ratnamālā nāma catvāriṃśadadhyāyaḥ
visargaśca visruk caiva visrutau visrutastathā
visrugo visrutaścaiva visarjano visargakaḥ
visarjanīyo bhidhānāścaite visarganīyakāḥ
śuṅgavadvālavatsasyā bālikā kūcayugmahu
netravattvaṣṭasarpasya savima iti smṛtaḥ
veṣṭanaṃ veṣṭanaṃ caiva vṛdaṃ vela vṛducyate
nāntamāntakaṃ caiva syānāṃ ca caikaranakainān
nakārāntapadājñeyāścaite nāntasya saujñakāḥ
udāttaṃ cānudāttaṃ ca svaritaṃ pracayaṃ tathā
samāne sthāne kaṭhādau tridhā vibhajyabhāgavat
varṇāstatra niṣpadyante cordhvabhāge tu jāyate
udāttastu savijñeyaḥ so nudāttastu nīcake
anudāttaḥ savijñeyaḥ svaritastu tataḥ param
uccanīca samāhārāste varṇāḥ svarito viduḥ
udāttavatsaṃhitāyāṃ pada kāle tu nihitāḥ
nihitāḥ sarvavarṇāstu ca pracayastu dṛśyate
ye svaritaparā varṇāste varṇāḥ pracayo viduḥ
sādanyaṃ ca sudhukṣaṃ ca sīṣadhīdhi saduṣṭaraḥ
eteṣvagraho nāsti naiṣādaṃ prāvaṇebhiṣṣaṭ
aṣṭau sthānāni varṇānāmuraḥ kaṇṭhaḥ śirastathā
jihvāmūlaṃ ca dantāśca nāsikoṣṭhau ca tālu ca
hakāraṃ pañcamairyuktamanasthābhiśca saṃyutam
urasyantaṃ vijānīyātkaṇṭhyamāhurasaṃyutam
catvāraśca prayatvāḥ syurakṣarāṇāṃ tathaiva ca
spṛṣṭeṣatspṛṣṭatā syau ca saṃvṛtaṃ vivṛtaṃ tathā
ekamātro bhaveddhrasvo dvimātro dīrgha ucyate
trimātrastu pluto jñeyo vyañjanaṃ tvardhamātrakam
cāṣastu ravate mātraṃ dvimātraṃ vāyaso'bravīt
śikhī tu rauti trimātrī metanmātrasya lakṣaṇam
padakāle tu saṃprāpte gaṇāntaṃ lupyate yadi
padatrayaṃ padaikaṃ vā bahuvātthava lupuḥ
kāyāyanakṛtaṃ sūtraṃ vettikātyāyanaḥ svayam
tato karkī tato dhanvī vāsudevo na pañcamaḥ
gaṇāntā yatra vartante punaruktastu lupyate
punarukto gaṇāntaḥ syādvede vājasaneyake
hrasvapūrvo hyanusvāro dīrghānusvārabhāgbhavet
dvitvaṃ parañcanna tathā kramakāle tu vājinām
antasva savitaḥ punaryattrirephaṃ paro bhavet
visarjanīyamevasyādabhāve veṣṭanaṃ bhavet
iyaṃ vediśca vājāya svāhā dvau ca vihāya ca
abhāvo veṣṭanastatra tvanyathā veṣṭanaṃ bhavet
tathā cāntaḥ parārdhaśca varjayitvānyathācavṛt
iti śrī śaṅkarācāryakṛtau vājasaneyasaṃhitāyāṃ padakārikā ratnamālānām
ekacatvāriṃśadadhyāyaḥ samāptaḥ
