prācyāṃ diśi śuciṃ deśaṃ balyarthaṃ parigṛhya ca | yavadhānā vimajyaikaṃ bhāgamabhyajya sarpiṣā āditye'stamite kuryādanvādhānādi pūrvavat | havirdvayasya tūṣṇīṃ sto nirvāpaprokṣaṇe iha purovadavaghātādi syādvibhajya ca taṇḍulān | tatraikena puroḍāśamanyena śrapayeccarum ||
ekasmiṃstu puroḍāśaṃ kapāle śrapayediha | ājyena saha dhānānāṃ paryagnikaraṇaṃ bhavet ||
ājyasya kevalotpūtau tāsāṃ triḥ prokṣaṇaṃ bhavet | (carumāsādayetpūrvaṃ puroḍāśamataḥ param) barhiṣyājyāntare kṛtsnaṃ puroḍāśaṃ nimajjayet | prakāśapṛṣṭhamathavā puroḍāśaṃ nimajjayet

<2 sarpabaliḥ >

apa āsicya tānopya sarpadeveti mantrataḥ | nāntarā balimātmānaṃ vyaveyādiha kaścana || vyavāyasya niṣedho'yamukta āparidānataḥ | ye sarpā iti maṃtreṇa namaskuryātkṛtāñjaliḥ ||
