tṛcaḥ varttamānat‍vaṃ tṛcprat‍yayaprakṛtibhūtadhātvarthaviśiṣṭakālavṛttit‍vam artha iti śeṣaḥ| tadyathā paktā bhoktā it‍yādi, uktas‍thaleṣu pākādikriyāṇāṃ s‍vāvacchinnakāla-vṛttit‍vaṃ pratītiviṣayaḥ| nanu ‘yoddhāro gac‍chanti' it‍yādau yodhanakriyāyā gamanakāle avṛttit‍vāt kā gatiriti cet yathā ‘apacannapi sūpakāraḥ pacanayog‍yatayā pācaka' it‍yuc‍yate, tathā tādṛśas‍thaleṣ‍vadoṣaḥ|

nirvvarttyakarmmat‍vaṃ yathā - prakṛtivācakapadāsamabhivyāhṛtapadopas‍thāp‍yat‍ve sati kriyājan‍yot‍pattiphalavatt‍vam| tathā ca ‘ghaṭaṃ karoti' it‍yādau ghaṭas‍ya kriyā-jan‍yot‍pattiphalavatt‍vaṃ ghaṭaprakṛtibhūtamṛdādivācakapadāsamabhiv‍yāhṛtat‍vañca, ata eva nirvvarttyakarm‍mat‍vaṃ tas‍ya| atraiva yadi ‘mṛttikāṃ ghaṭaṃ karoti' iti prayogaḥ, tadā vikāryyakarm‍matā| nirvvarttyam ut‍pādyamiti padmanābhaḥ| ut‍pattirādyakṣaṇasam‍ban‍dhaḥ - ādyakṣaṇaśca s‍vādhikaraṇasamayadh‍vaṃsādhikaraṇasamayadh‍vaṃsānadhikaraṇat‍ve sati s‍vādhi-karaṇasamayadhvaṃsādhikaraṇasamayaḥ| athavā prathamaprakāśa ut‍pattiḥ, etan‍mate asataḥ ut‍patt‍yabhāvapakṣas‍ya ādṛtat‍vāt| taduktaṃ ‘nāsadut‍padyate na ca sad vinaś‍yati', ut‍pattivināśayorāvirbhāvatirobhāvas‍varūpat‍vāditi ṭīkākāraḥ|
"satī vā'vidyamānā vā prakṛtiḥ pariṇāminī| yas‍ya nāśrīyate tas‍ya nirvvarttyat‍vaṃ pracakṣate||" 1 iti prācīnakārikaikavākyatayā prakṛtivācaket‍yādi|
vikāryyamāha - yatra prakṛt‍yuc‍chedena s‍varūpato vā pūrvarūpato vā nāśena rūpān‍tara-mut‍padyate tatra vikāryyakarm‍matā, tadidaṃ lakṣaṇaṃ yathā pratīyamānaprakṛtivikṛtibhāvat‍ve sati kriyānirvvāhyaviśiṣṭāsatt‍vot‍pattirūpānyataraphalaśālit‍vaṃ vikāryyat‍vam|
atra ‘kāṣṭhaṃ bhas‍ma karoti' it‍yādau kāṣṭhasya s‍varūpanāśāt viśiṣṭāsatt‍vavatt‍vāt bhas‍manaśca s‍varūpanāśahetukarūpān‍taravatt‍vāllakṣaṇasaman‍vayaḥ, ‘dug‍dhaṃ dadhi karoti' it‍yādau ekān‍tataḥ s‍varūpas‍ya anucchinnat‍vāt pūrvvarūpamātranāśena rūpān‍taralābhāt dvitīyaḥ prakāra iti kaścit| mūlakāramate tu vikāryyakarm‍malakṣaṇas‍ya rūpān‍taravan‍mā-traparat‍vamiti sthitam|
nanu yadi kāṣṭhādīnāṃ na vikāryyatā tatkimiti tadvācakaśab‍dottaraṃ dvitīyā jātā it‍yākāṅkṣāyāṃ prāha - kāṣ‍ṭhādīt‍yādi|
evam uktarūpeṇa ‘kāṣṭhaṃ bhas‍ma karoti' iti s‍thānīyaśāb‍dadhīvadit‍yarthaḥ| an‍yatra parokteṣu ‘dug‍dhaṃ dadhi karoti, taṇ‍ḍulān odanaṃ pacati' ‘suvarṇaṃ kuṇ‍ḍalaṃ karoti' iti sthaleṣu it‍yarthaḥ| tathā ca dug‍dhasam‍ban‍dhyabhinnadadhiviṣayakakṛt‍yāśrayaḥ it‍yādireva tatra śāb‍dadhīriti|
atra keṣāñcinmate "yallab‍dhasattākaṃ vas‍tu avas‍thān‍taramāpadyate tadvikāryyam" iti vikāryyalakṣaṇāt ‘kāṣṭhaṃ bhas‍ma karoti' it‍yādau kāṣṭhaṃ bhas‍ma cet‍yubhayameva vikāryyalakṣaṇalakṣ‍yaṃ bhavati| tathā ca yat lab‍dhasattākaṃ kāṣṭhādivas‍tu yat avas‍thān‍taraṃ bhas‍mādirūpaṃ rūpān‍taram āpadyate tadubhayameva vikāryyam iti| etan‍mate kāṣṭhapadottaradvitīyot‍pattau na paryyanuyogaḥ| yadyapi vikāryyat‍vaṃ prakṛtereva sam‍bhavatīti prakṛtau kāṣṭhādau lakṣaṇasaman‍vayas‍tathāpi abhedavivakṣayā prakṛtivikṛt‍yorubhayoreva vikāryyapadārthatā iti nānupapattiḥ|
atra vikāryyas‍thale ‘kāśāḥ kaṭaṃ kriyan‍te' iti prayogas‍tu prakṛt‍yapekṣayā vikāras‍ya gauṇat‍vena anuktat‍vamadhikṛt‍ya, ata eva kaṭamit‍yatra dvitīyā| vikāryyakarm‍makakriyayā duhādīnāmiva dvikarm‍makat‍vasiddhyā pradhānakarm‍maṇā prakṛtereva uktat‍vav‍yākh‍yānāt| kulacan‍dras‍yāpi matametat| apare tu yathā mukh‍ye karm‍maṇi prat‍yayaḥ tathā gauṇe'pi karm‍maṇīti ‘kāśāḥ kaṭaḥ kriyan‍te' it‍yeva prayuñjate| kriyāyā vacanan‍tu prakṛt‍yanugatameva vikāryyakarm‍mas‍thale s‍vārasikaṃ sarvveṣām| ‘prakṛti-vikṛt‍yoḥ prakṛtivac‍ca' iti n‍yāyāt, yathā - eko vṛkṣaḥ pañca naukā bhavatīt‍yādau|
atha kiṃ karaṇamiti jijñāsāyāṃ karaṇalakṣaṇamāha - sādhakatamaṃ karaṇamiti sūtreṇa, "sādhakatamaṃ karaṇami" 1 ti pāṇinisūtram| tamaprat‍yayena sādhakas‍ya prakṛṣṭat‍vaṃ vivakṣ‍yate, tathā ca sādhakānāṃ madh‍ye yaḥ prakṛṣṭaḥ tat‍karaṇam| tathā ca kaumāre trilocanaḥ "kriyāsiddhau yadyapi bahūni sādhanāni v‍yāpriyan‍te, tathāpi teṣu madh‍ye anenaitat karomīti kartrā yat prakṛṣṭopakārakaman‍taraṅgaṃ vivakṣitaṃ tat karaṇam"| tathācoktam, "kārakāv‍yavadhānena kriyāniṣ‍pattikāraṇam| yadvai vivakṣitaṃ teṣu karaṇan‍tat prakīrttitam||" prakṛṣṭat‍vañca karaṇas‍ya kārakān‍tarāv‍yavadhānena kriyāyāḥ sādhakat‍vam| taduktaṃ - "karaṇaṃ khalu sarvvatra karttṛv‍yāpāragocaram| tirodadhāti karttāraṃ prādhān‍yaṃ tanniban‍dhanam||" iti| tac‍ca karaṇaṃ dvividhaṃ bāhyam ābh‍yan‍tarañca, bāhyaṃ karaṇaṃ yathā - ‘paraśunā chinatti vṛkṣam', ābhyan‍taraṃ karaṇaṃ yathā - ‘manasā pāṭaliputraṃ gac‍chati' it‍yādi vakṣ‍yamāṇam|
atha sādhakatamaṃ karaṇamiti sūtraghaṭakībhūtasādhakatamapadārthamāha - kārake-t‍yādinā| kārakān‍taras‍ya s‍vabhinnakārakasya v‍yāpāram anut‍pādya ajanayit‍vā phala-hetut‍vam| kevalaṃ phalahetut‍vamit‍yuktau kartturapi phalahetutayā karaṇalakṣaṇātiv‍yāptiḥ, ato viśeṣaṇadalam, tathā ca karttuḥ s‍vabhin‍nakaraṇakārakav‍yapāramut‍pādya phalajanaka-t‍vāt nātiv‍yāptiḥ| kāraṇaṃ hi dvividhaṃ - s‍varūpasat phalopadhāyakañca, s‍varūpasat kāraṇaṃ hi kāraṇatāvac‍chedakadharm‍mavan‍mātraṃ, phalopadhāyakañca kāraṇam avyavahita-pūrvatvasambandhena phalaviśiṣṭaṃ, yatra phalopadhāyakatvarūpaṃ kāraṇatvaṃ tatraiva karaṇasaṃjñā-viṣayat‍vaṃ, yatra ca s‍varūpasatt‍vamātraṃ tatra na karaṇasajñāviṣayat‍vaṃ kin‍tu het‍vartha iti sūtrādes‍tatra tatra viṣayatā|
tatra prathamataḥ ‘paraśunā chinatti' it‍yādau phalopadhāyakat‍varūpaṃ karaṇasaṃjñā-nirvvāhakaṃ hetut‍vam udāharaṇena pratipādya s‍varūpasan‍mātras‍thalamapi udāharati - athet‍yādinā| dhanena kulam it‍yādi, atra tṛtīyārthaḥ hetut‍vaṃ, tas‍ya ca s‍vanirūpita-jan‍yatāvatt‍vena kule an‍vayaḥ, tathā ca dhananiṣṭhajanakatānirūpitajan‍yatāvat kulamiti bodhaḥ| ‘vidyayā yaśa' it‍yatrāpi pūrvavat|
evamit‍yādi - dhūmapadaṃ dhūmajñānaparam, tṛtīyārthaḥ hetut‍vaṃ vahnipadaṃ vahnijñānaparam, tathā ca dhūmajñānaniṣṭhajanakatānirūpitajan‍yatāviśiṣṭam athavā tṛtīyārthahetut‍vas‍ya s‍vanirūpitajan‍yatāviśiṣṭajñānaviṣayat‍vasam‍ban‍dhena vahnyādau an‍vayaḥ, evaṃ ‘kṛta-kat‍vena anit‍ya' it‍yatrāpi| atra kecit dhūmena it‍yādau pañcam‍yan‍tapāṭhaṃ samīcīnaṃ man‍yante, tadas‍mākaṃ na manoramam, karaṇapras‍tāve tṛtīyārthavivecanameva yuktaṃ, na tu pañcamyarthavivecanam aprastutatvāt, kṛtakat‍vaṃ kāryyatvaṃ tat hetūkṛt‍ya anit‍yatva-sādhanam|
atha dhūmapadena dhūmajñānas‍ya lakṣaṇayā bodhe, pañcam‍yeva yuj‍yate "guṇe hetā" vi‍ti anuśāsanāt, ataḥ kiṃ dhūmena vahnirit‍yādis‍thale tṛtīyārthasaman‍vayārthaṃ tadupan‍yāsa iti śaṅkāyāṃ pañcam‍yā vaikalpikat‍vāt pakṣe tṛtīyot‍patterabādhitat‍vāt arthajijñāsāyāṃ tannirūpaṇam| tṛptyarthakadhātuyoge karaṇe ṣaṣ‍ṭhyapi vihiteti ṣaṣṭhyā api s‍thalabhedena hetut‍vamarthaḥ| tathā ca prācīnaprayogaḥ - ‘nāgnis‍tṛp‍yati kāṣṭhānāmi't‍yādi|
atha sam‍pradānalakṣaṇamāha - karm‍maṇet‍yādinā| samyak prakarṣeṇa dīyate yas‍mai tat sam‍pradānam iti v‍yut‍pattyā s‍vas‍vat‍vaparit‍yāgapūrvakaparas‍vat‍vāpādanarūpo dhāt‍vartho labhyate| atha s‍vas‍vatvaparit‍yāgapūrvakaparas‍vat‍vāpādanas‍ya dadāt‍yarthat‍ve kathaṃ brāhmaṇādeḥ sam‍pradānat‍vamupapadyate, yataḥ ādau gavādeḥ s‍vas‍vat‍vat‍yāge jāte sati gavādau dānakarttuḥ pūrvas‍vāminaḥ audāsīn‍yāt paras‍vat‍vāpādanamaśakyameva, s‍vat‍ve sthite'pi ekas‍vat‍vas‍ya aparas‍vatvapratiban‍dhakat‍vāt brāhmaṇādeḥ s‍vat‍vaṃ durghaṭam| taduktaṃ - "s‍vas‍vat‍ve vidyamāne tu paras‍vat‍vaṃ na vidyate| parit‍yaj‍ya ca s‍vas‍vat‍va-maudāsīn‍yāt na sidh‍yati||" iti| ata eva doṣāt s‍vas‍vat‍vadh‍vaṃsapūrvva-ket‍yādyarthamuktvāpi punarathavā pakṣaṃ sāramañjarīkṛtaḥ saṃśrayanti s‍ma, karm‍maṇā it‍yādi-sūtras‍ya niṣ‍karṣakramaḥ karmmaṇā karm‍makārakeṇa karaṇabhūtena yamabhipraiti sam‍badhnāti tat sam‍pradānamiti|
tac‍ca sam‍pradānaṃ trividhaṃ - anuman‍tṛ, anirākarttṛ, prerakamiti bhedāt, taduktaṃ "anumantranirākarttṛprerakaṃ t‍yāgakāraṇam| v‍yāp‍yenāptaṃ dadātes‍tu sam‍pradānaṃ prakīrttitam|| sam‍pradānaṃ tadeva s‍yāt pūjānugrahakām‍yayā| dīyamānena saṃyogāt s‍vāmit‍vaṃ labhate yadi||" anuman‍tari udāharaṇaṃ yathā ‘gurave gāṃ dadāti' atra gururddānāya abh‍yarthitaḥ dīyatāmiti anuman‍yate iti tas‍ya anuman‍tṛt‍vam| anirākarttṛ yathā- ‘sūryyāya arghyaṃ dadāti' sūryyo hi yadyapi na yācate, tathāpi arghyaṃ pūjakapradattaṃ na nirākaroti| prerakaṃ yathā - ‘brāhmaṇāya gāṃ dadāti' brāhmaṇo hi dhaninamupasṛt‍ya mahyam ekāṃ gāṃ dehīti prerayati| tadayaṃ nikṛṣṭārthaḥ - t‍yāgajan‍yas‍vatvaphalabhāgit‍vaṃ sam‍pradānat‍vam iti t‍yāgas‍tu paras‍vatvaphalikā ic‍chā, idaṃ brāhmaṇas‍ya bhavatu it‍yādyākārā ic‍chā t‍yāga-padārthaḥ| mūle uddeś‍yat‍vaṃ s‍vat‍vanirūpakatayā tādṛśec‍chāviṣayat‍vaṃ, tathā ca gauriyaṃ brāhmaṇas‍ya bhavatu iti ic‍chāyāṃ bhāsamānas‍ya ṣaṣṭhyarthas‍ya s‍vat‍vas‍ya nirūpakatayā brāhmaṇo bhāsata iti brāhmaṇa uddeś‍yaḥ| uddeś‍yagatet‍yādi - anena ic‍chāmātreṇaiva sam‍pradānat‍vaniṣ‍pattiḥ prāptā|
nanu as‍tu dānārthakadhātuyoge sampradānat‍vanirvāhaḥ kathaṃ "devadattāya rocate modaka" it‍yādau uktarūpat‍yāgādyarthābhāvena caturthīsam‍pattirit‍yākāṅkṣāyāṃ ruc‍yādi- dhātuyoge sūtrān‍taraṃ sampradānasaṃjñāvidhāyakaṃ prastauti - "ruc‍yarthānām prīyamāṇa" 1 iti| nāradāya rocate kalaha it‍yatra sam‍bandhit‍vaṃ caturth‍yarthaḥ, tas‍ya ca rucadhāt‍varthe an‍vayaḥ, tathā ca nāradasam‍bandhiruciviṣayaḥ kalaha it‍yukta eva śāb‍dabodhaḥ| nanu "ruc‍yarthānāṃ dhātūnāṃ yoge yaḥ prīyamāṇaḥ sa sam‍pradānam" it‍yuktau "s‍phuradadharasīdhave tava vadanacan‍dramā rocayati locanacakoram" it‍yatra ruc‍yarthadhātuyoge prīyamāṇalocanacakoravācakapadāt na kathaṃ sampradāne caturthī prāptā, tathā ca ‘vadanacan‍dramā adharasīdhuṃ locanacakorāya rocayati' it‍yeva s‍yāt iti cet ruciratra āsvādanārthaḥ, tataśca locanacakoras‍ya ās‍vādakarttṛt‍vaṃ vivakṣitaṃ, ruc‍yarthānāṃ prīyamāṇa iti sūtran‍tu ṣaṣṭhyāḥ karm‍maṇo vā bādhakamiti kalāpaṭīkāyāmuktat‍vāt nās‍yātra viṣaya iti| sīdhave it‍yatra tādarth‍ye caturthīti na doṣaḥ|

kvacit it‍yādi - tathā ca bhinnaghaṭatāt‍paryyeṇa prayuktayoḥ ghaṭapadayoḥ samāsas‍thale padārthabheda āvaś‍yakaḥ, tatra ekas‍yaiva ghaṭat‍vas‍ya padārthatāvac‍chedakat‍vāt padārthatāvac‍chedakabhedamātre dvan‍dvas‍vīkāre anupapatteḥ, padārthabhedamātre dvan‍dvas‍vīkāre meyavāc‍yayorabheda it‍yādau dvan‍dvānupapattiriti padārthatāvac‍chekabhedaḥ s‍vīkāryyaḥ, tat‍s‍vīkāre ca prakṛte meyapadārthavāc‍yapadārthayorabhede'pi meyat‍vavāc‍yat‍vayorbhedāt dvan‍dvopapattiriti| atha tathāpi ‘gaganākāśayorabheda' it‍yādau kathaṃ padārthapadārthāvac‍che-dakayorbhedavirahāt dvan‍dva upapadyate iti cet gaganākāśapadayorgaganākāśapada-vāc‍yat‍vābhiprāyeṇa prayuktatayā gaganapadavāc‍yat‍vākāśapadavāc‍yat‍vayoḥ mitho bhedāt dvan‍dvaḥ| tadayaṃ niṣ‍karṣaḥ - padaviśiṣṭapadat‍vaṃ dvandvaniyāmakam| vaiśiṣṭyañca s‍vajan‍yaprati-pattiviṣayatāt‍paryyānuyogitāvac‍chedakarūpavṛttiviṣayatāśālijñānajanakat‍vas‍vaghaṭita-dvan‍dvaghaṭakat‍va etadubhayasam‍ban‍dhena, viṣayatāyāṃ rūpavṛttit‍vañca s‍vīyaparyyāptyanu-yogitāvac‍chedakat‍vasam‍ban‍dhena| tathā ca yatra bhinnaghaṭatāt‍paryyeṇa bahughaṭapadaprayogaḥ tatra tāt‍paryyaparyyālocanayā tattadghaṭapadāt tattadghaṭarūpapadārthopasthit‍yā tādṛśopasthiti-viṣayatāparyyāptis‍tattadghaṭe iti naikaviṣayatāparyyāptyanuyogitāvac‍chedakarūpa-vṛttit‍vāt aparaghaṭapadajan‍yapratipattiviṣayatayā dvan‍dva upapannaḥ|
ekameva ghaṭamabhipret‍ya yatra ghaṭapadadvayaprayogaḥ tatra tatra prat‍yekamubhayaghaṭapada-jan‍yapratītiviṣayatāparyyāptyanuyogitāvac‍chedakameva natu bhinnam| ata eva tatra kas‍yāpi ghaṭapadas‍ya na padaviśiṣṭat‍vaṃ tena na dvan‍dvaḥ|
atha yadi padārthayorabhede'pi dvan‍dvaḥ tat‍kathaṃ dvit‍vabahutvādīnāṃ an‍vayaḥ, ghaṭādipadottaradvivacanādeḥ ghaṭādau arthabodhāt it‍yākāṅkṣāyāmāha - tathācet‍yādi| atra dvan‍dvasamāsas‍thale, yadbhedena padārthapadārthatāvac‍chedakayoḥ yayoḥ bhedena dvan‍dvaḥ dvan‍dva-samāsaḥ, tatraiva natu tadatirikte mitho bhedānāpanne it‍yarthaḥ| dvit‍va‍bahut‍vayoḥ anvayaḥ, dvivacanabahuvacanapratipādyayoḥ dvitvabahutvayoḥ saṅkh‍yayoḥ sam‍ban‍dhaḥ tathā ca sati tattadaṃśe dvit‍vāderbādhāvirahāt na śāb‍dabodhe pratiban‍dhaḥ| tathāhi ghaṭapaṭau it‍yādau dvit‍vas‍ya padārthadvayāṃśe meyavāc‍yayorabheda it‍yādau ca padārthatāvac‍chedakadvayāṃśe dvit‍vādyan‍vaya iti jñeyam| anayoḥ dvit‍vabahut‍vayoḥ, bhedasāpekṣat‍vāt, dvit‍vādi-saṅkh‍yānām apekṣābuddhijan‍yatayā apekṣābuddheśca bhedādhīnatayā param‍parayā bhedādhīnat‍vaṃ dvit‍vādīnām|
sa ca dvan‍dvaḥ dvividhaḥ - vidhapratyayārthaḥ vicāritaḥ| itaretara iti - ‘dhavakhadira-palāśā' it‍yādau itaretaradvan‍dvaḥ ‘ahinakulam' it‍yādau samāhāraḥ| nirūpitat‍vamatreti sāhit‍yas‍ya ekakriyān‍vayit‍varūpas‍ya mukh‍yatayā vibhāganirūpakat‍vāyogāt nirūpitat‍vaṃ sāmānādhikaraṇ‍yaṃ v‍yākh‍yātam, tathā ca vibhāgas‍ya sāhit‍yas‍ya ca dhavādivṛttit‍vena sāmānādhikaraṇ‍yamakṣatam|
nanu yadi itaretaras‍thale sāhit‍yaṃ viśeṣ‍yaṃ tadā ‘puṣ‍paphale jighra bhuṅkṣva' it‍yādau sāhit‍yas‍ya kathaman‍vayaḥ, ekakriyāyām ubhayoran‍vayābhāvāt it‍yākāṅkṣāyāmāha athaveti|
athedānīmav‍yayībhāvaṃ nirvvakti - samāset‍yādinā| nānāvibhaktibhirekarūpatāva-t‍padat‍vam avyayamātrāṇāmeveti av‍yayamātrāṇāmeva av‍yayībhāvasamāsat‍vaprasaktyā tadvāraṇāya samāsagrastatve satīti viśeṣaṇaṃ, tathā ca av‍yayamātrāṇāṃ na samāsagras‍tat‍vam api tu samāsaghaṭakībhūtapadānāmeva| av‍yayībhāvamevaṃ vaiyākaraṇā bruvate - "pūrvvapadamav‍yayaṃ, parapadamanav‍yayaṃ tadvaśena samudāyo'nav‍yayo'v‍yayo bhavatīt‍yavyayībhāva" iti| sa ca niyamaḥ prāyikaḥ sarvvatra av‍yayībhāvasamāsa-ghaṭakapadānāṃ prathamas‍ya av‍yayat‍ve dvitīyas‍ya anav‍yayat‍ve ca niyamābhāvāt anyathā ‘tiṣ‍ṭhadgu' prabhṛtiṣu av‍yayībhāvasamāsaniṣ‍panneṣu av‍yāpteḥ| sa cāyaṃ pūrvvapadārthapradhānaḥ taduktaṃ - "samāsas‍tu caturtheti prāyo vādas‍tathāparaḥ| yo'yaṃ pūrvvapadārthādi prādhān‍yaviṣayaḥ sa ca"| (bhūṣaṇasāraḥ) nānāvibhaktibhiriti - ekarūpat‍vañca vibhaktilug‍vatt‍vena, taduktaṃ - "sadṛśaṃ triṣu liṅgeśu sarvvāsu ca vibhaktiṣu| vacaneṣu ca sarvveṣu yanna v‍yeti tadav‍yayam" (siddhāntakaumudī) iti av‍yayībhāvas‍yāpi kalpitāv‍yayat‍vena av‍yayakāryyabhāgit‍vāt tathāt‍vam| nanu akārān‍tānāmav‍yayī-bhāvānāmupakum‍bhādīnāṃ kathaṃ tādṛśalakṣaṇabhāgit‍vam amādinā vairūpyāt iti cenna, bahvīṣu vibhaktiṣu teṣāmapi amādeśavatt‍vena ekarūpatāyogāt nāv‍yāptiḥ| samāsa-gras‍tat‍vamātroktau yāvat‍s‍veva samāseṣu ativ‍yāptiḥ iti samāsān‍tarav‍yāvarttanāya viśeṣ‍yadalam|
atret‍yādi - pūrvvapadārthasya samāsaghaṭakapadeṣu prathamoccāritāv‍yayapadārthas‍yaiva natu uttaravarttitadbhinnapadārthas‍ya| prādhānyaṃ śāb‍dabodhe viśeṣ‍yat‍vaṃ, tathāhi- yatra samāse yat padārthaḥ prādhān‍yena nirddiṣṭaḥ tatra tadviśeṣ‍yaka eva śāb‍dabodhaḥ prādhān‍yāprādhānyayoḥ śāb‍dabodhe viśeṣ‍yaviśeṣaṇabhāvarūpat‍vāt| prakṛte upakum‍bhamit‍yādau upaśab‍dārtha sāmīp‍yaṃ pūrvvapadārthaḥ, kumbhapadārthaśca uttarapadārthaḥ, tadatra kum‍bhasam‍bandhisamīpabodhe samīparūpārthaḥ viśeṣ‍yaḥ kum‍bhapadārthaśca viśeṣaṇaṃ tadaṃśe, viśeṣaṇaviśeṣ‍yabhāvayoḥ śāb‍dabodhe prākpratīyamānat‍vaparapratīyamānat‍varūpatayā vailakṣaṇ‍yāt| yathā - ‘gac‍chati devadatto grāmam' it‍yādau grāmavṛttikarm‍matānukūlav‍yāpārānukūlakṛtimān it‍yeva bodho jāyate, grāmas‍ya karm‍mat‍vāṃśe viśeṣaṇat‍vaṃ karm‍matāyāśca kriyājan‍yaphalarūpāyā anukūlat‍vasam‍ban‍dhena kṛtau kṛteśca karttari it‍yevaṃ rūpeṇa yadaṃśe yadvaviśeṣaṇībhūya bhāsate tadapekṣayā tadarthas‍ya aprādhān‍yaṃ prādhān‍yañca tadviśeṣ‍yas‍ya tathā eva samāsādi-s‍thale'pi avaseyam|
atha nirddhāraṇaṃ v‍yanakti - jāt‍yādīt‍yādinā| atra kaumārāḥ - "nirddhāryyate pṛthakkriyate yas‍māt samudāyāt ekadeśo jātiguṇakriyābhis‍tannirddhāraṇam"| "uddeś‍yatāvac‍chedakaśūn‍ye sāmān‍yadharm‍māvacchinne vidheyābhāvavatt‍vam" iti naiyāyikāḥ, tadanugāmina eva kecit - viśeṣ‍ye s‍vabhinnasāmān‍yav‍yāvṛtta-dharmmavatt‍vamiti bruvate, tathā ca ‘narāṇāṃ kṣatriyaḥ śūra' it‍yādau uddeś‍yatāvac‍chedakaṃ kṣatriyat‍vaṃ jātiḥ tac‍chūnye sāmān‍yadharm‍manaratvāvacchinne vidheyas‍ya śūrat‍vas‍ya abhāvavatt‍vaṃ budh‍yate, tena kṣatriyabhinno naro na śūra i‍t‍yapi pratītiḥ|
tadevāha - evañcet‍yādinā, tathā ca narāṇāṃ kṣatriyaḥ śūra it‍yatra narapadasamabhi-v‍yāhṛtā ṣaṣṭhī, tadupas‍thāp‍yaḥ yaḥ tadan‍yapadārthaḥ uddeś‍yavācakapadapratipādyabhinnaḥ narādi- padārthaḥ tat‍samudāye kṣatriyādibhinne taddharm‍mavatt‍vas‍ya vidheyadharmmavatt‍vas‍ya śauryyā‍derabhāva iti|
bhāvābhāvān‍vayabodha iti - nanu ekadā kathamubhayoḥ bhāvābhāvayorekato bodha iti cet prāk tādṛśārtha eva nirddhāraṇaṣaṣṭhyā vṛttis‍vīkārāt, uddeś‍yatāvac‍chedaka-śūn‍yasāmān‍yadharmmāvacchinnav‍yāvṛttadharm‍mavatt‍vas‍ya nirddhāraṇaṣaṣ‍ṭhyā bodhanāt ekakrame-ṇaiva abhiprāyasiddheḥ|

sarvvanāmapadānāṃ ghaṭat‍vādiviśeṣadharmmāvacchinne śaktiḥ, tamānaya it‍yādau prayojakādeśānan‍taraṃ prayoj‍yena prakaraṇādiparyyālocanayā ghaṭāderānayanāt ānayanas‍ya ca ādeśavākyārthajñānasāpekṣat‍vāt| atha tadādipadānāṃ ghaṭat‍vādirūpeṇa śakti-virahe'pi buddhiviṣayatayā śaktigraheṇāpi ghaṭapadārthopasthit‍yā tena rūpeṇa śāb‍dabodha upapadyate, kimiti viśeṣādara it‍yākāṅkṣāyāmāha – ghaṭat‍vādi prakārat‍vāditi, atha buddhiviṣayatayā śaktigrahāt tathaiva padārthopasthit‍yā ghaṭat‍vaprakārakaśāb‍dabodhe kā kṣatirit‍yāśaṅkya prāha - samānaprakārakat‍vena iti, yādṛśadharmmāvacchinne śab‍das‍ya śaktigrahaḥ, śaktisam‍ban‍dhena tādṛśadharmmāvacchinnas‍ya upasthitiḥ tādṛśadharmmāvacchi-nnaprakārakaśāb‍dabodhe kāraṇamiti niyamāt ekadharmmāvacchinnaviṣayakā eva śaktigrahapadārthopasthitiśāb‍dabodhā bhaveyuriti niyamaḥ|
atha yadi tadādipadānāṃ ghaṭat‍vādinānādharmmāvacchinne śaktiḥ tat kathaṃ śakyatāvac‍chedakabhede śakteraikyaṃ tadabhāve ca haripadādivat tadādipadānāmapi nānārthat‍vāpattiriti śaṅkāyāmāha - buddhiviṣayavṛttit‍vena it‍yādi, tathā ca buddhiviṣaya-vṛttit‍varūpeṇa bahūnāmapi śakyatāvac‍chedakānām anugamāt na śaktibhedaḥ na vā nānārthat‍vāpattiḥ|
tathācet‍yādi – ghaṭādipadānāṃ saṅketagrahas‍ya prakārastu ghaṭapadāt ghaṭo boddhav‍ya iti atra saṅketagrahe ghaṭapadajan‍yabodhaviṣayat‍vaṃ prakāraḥ ghaṭat‍vāvacchinnaśca viśeṣ‍yaḥ, tathāca ghaṭapadajan‍yabodhaviṣayat‍vaprakāratānirūpitaghaṭat‍vāvacchinnaviṣayatākatvaṃ saṅketa-grahasya, pare ca ghaṭo ghaṭapadajanyaghaṭatvāvacchinnaśābdabodhaviṣayatāvān bhavatu it‍yā-kārakasaṅketajñānīyaśāb‍dabodhaviṣayatāvac‍chedakat‍vaviṣayatānirūpitaviṣayat‍vaṃ śāb‍da-bodhaprakāratāyāṃ prayojakaṃ śāb‍dabodhaprakāratāvac‍chedakatāyāñca tādṛśaviṣayatā-vac‍chedakatāvac‍chedakat‍vaviṣayatānirūpitaviṣayat‍vaṃ prayojakam, tadyathā ghaṭapadād ghaṭa it‍yākārakaḥ paśvādipadāc‍ca lomat‍vāvacchinnalomāvacchinnet‍yākārakaḥ śāb‍dabodhaḥ iyaṃ viśiṣṭe śaktiḥ| atha ākāśapadāt kadācit śab‍dāśrayat‍vena kadācit aṣṭadrav‍yātiriktadrav‍yat‍vena vā upasthitiḥ kayā rīt‍yā upapādayituṃ śakyā, saṅketo yadi ākāśapadāt śab‍dāśrayo boddhav‍ya iti tadā śab‍dāśrayat‍venaiva tadupasthitiḥ yuktā, yadi aṣṭadrav‍yātiriktadrav‍yat‍vena tadā tathaiva iti kīdṛśaṃ samādhānam iti cet, akāśapadas‍ya niravacchinne śab‍dāśraye śaktiḥ, śab‍dāśrayat‍vādīnāṃ gurudharmmatayā atra avacchedakat‍vas‍ya kal‍payitumaśakyat‍vāditi aniyatadharmmaprakāraka eva ākāśa-padāt bodha iti uktā śaktireva upalakṣite|
atha niravacchinne ākāśapadas‍ya śaktyā yena kenāpi dharmmeṇa ākāśas‍ya upasthitau meyat‍vādināpi tas‍ya upasthitiḥ kathaṃ na s‍yāt iti cenna, yena rūpeṇa yatra śaktijñānaṃ tenaiva rūpeṇa tatra ākāśabhānas‍ya niyamas‍vīkārāt meyat‍vādinā śaktijñānābhāvāt na tathā, śaktijñānaviśeṣ‍yatāvac‍chedakatayā gṛhītena anatiprasaktadharmmeṇaiva tadbhānam| dīdhitikārās‍tu śāb‍dabodhīyaprakāratāyāṃ ākāśapadaśaktiriti kal‍payanti| haryyādipadānāṃ saṅketas‍tu "haripadāt viṣ‍ṇurboddhav‍yaḥ, haripadāt sūryyo boddhav‍yaḥ, haripadāt in‍dro boddhav‍ya" it‍yādi bahuvidha eva prakaraṇādiniyantritārthatayā ca nānārthakaśab‍das‍ya tattadviśeṣadharmmaprakāreṇa tas‍mādupasthitiḥ, tāvat‍padārthānugatas‍ya anatiprasaktas‍ya laghudharmmas‍ya abhāvena agat‍yā tattaddharmmeṣu haripadaśakyatāvac‍chedakabhedena śaktibhedas‍ya āvaś‍yakatayā nānārthakat‍vanirvvāhaḥ tattaddharmmagatān‍yatamat‍vas‍ya anugatadharmmas‍ya sam‍bhave'pi gauravādidoṣas‍ya parihātumaśakyatayā na bhānādeva śāb‍dabodhaprakāratāvac‍chekatayā niyāmakas‍ya saṅketīyaśāb‍dabodhaviṣayatā-vac‍chedakatāvac‍chedakat‍vaviṣayatānirūpitaviṣayatvas‍ya prakārat‍ve virahāt na tathāt‍vam| ata eva ‘tamānaya' it‍yukte ānayanaṃ ghaṭakarmmakaṃ na veti viśeṣadharmma-prakārakasaṃśayanivṛttiḥ sughaṭā, ‘tamānaya' iti vākyādapi viśeṣadharmmapuras‍kāreṇa ānayane ghaṭakarmmakat‍vapratīteḥ| viśeṣadharmmaprakārakasaṃśayas‍ya viśeṣadharmma-puras‍kāreṇa niścayādeva nivṛtteḥ, prakārat‍vas‍ya ghaṭat‍vādyaṃśe upalakṣaṇatayaiva niravacchinnaghaṭat‍vādibodhaḥ sughaṭa iti nānupapattiḥ| kecittu atha ivārthavicāraṃ v‍yanakti - can‍dra eva mukhamit‍yādinā| sādṛś‍yaparamiti sādṛś‍yabodhec‍chayā uccaritaṃ, sādṛś‍yañca tadbhinnat‍ve sati tadgatabhūyodharmmavatt‍vam, paraśab‍daḥ sarvvatra bodhec‍chayā uccaritārthakaḥ|
can‍dramukhayorit‍yādi abhedasam‍ban‍dhena an‍vayinoreva padārthayorvvācakau yau śab‍dau tayoreva samānavibhaktit‍vaniyamāt, ata eva pratiyogit‍vasam‍ban‍dhena sādṛś‍ye can‍drādera-n‍vaye na samānavibhaktikat‍vopapattiriti|
ivārthā bahavaḥ - upamā, ut‍prekṣā, vākyālaṅkāraḥ, īṣadarthaśca, tatra upamodāharaṇaṃ yathā ‘can‍dra iva mukhaṃ bhāti', ivena saha upamānavācakapadas‍ya nit‍yasamāsaḥ vibhakteścālopaḥ, tathā ca vārttikam ‘ivena nit‍yasamāsaḥ vibhaktyalopaśca' iti|

atha uddeś‍yavidheyabhāvaṃ v‍yanakti - tatret‍yādinā| uddeś‍yat‍vaṃ vidheyat‍vañca viṣayatāviśeṣau, yaddharmmāvacchinne ki‍ñcit sādhyate tat uddeś‍yatāvac‍chedakaṃ yaddharmmaśca sādh‍yate sa vidheyaḥ, sāmānādhikaraṇ‍yam ekādhikaraṇavṛttit‍vaṃ ‘ghaṭo nīlarūpavān' it‍yatra ghaṭat‍vāvacchinne nīlarūpaṃ sādh‍yate iti ghaṭat‍vam uddeś‍yatāvac‍che-dakaṃ nīlarūpañca vidheyam| prāgeveti vidheyavācakapadāditi śeṣaḥ| idañca prāyikam, avayavagran‍the bhaṭṭācāryyeṇa niruktam| uddeś‍yatayā abhisan‍dhānasatt‍ve vidheyavācaka-padottaranirddiṣ‍ṭādapi padāduddeś‍yapratīteḥ, yathā- "vahniv‍yāp‍yadhūmavān parvvataḥ, tas‍mād vahnimān" it‍yādyupanayanigamanāvayavayoḥ| nīlarūpaviṣayaketi viṣayat‍vaṃ vidheyat‍vā-kh‍yam avagan‍tav‍yam, ghaṭat‍vaviṣayakabodha it‍yatrāpi viṣayat‍vaṃ vidheyat‍vākh‍yam|
prakṛtivikṛtibhāve'pīti - ayaṃ pūrvoktaḥ niyamaḥ uddeś‍yatāvac‍chedakavidheyayoḥ sāmānādhikaraṇ‍yabodhakaḥ uddeś‍yavācakapadas‍ya pūrvvanirddeśāvedakaśca iti| prakṛti-vikṛtibhāvas‍thale'pi prakṛtau uddeś‍yat‍vas‍ya vikṛtau ca vidheyat‍vas‍ya bhānāt uddeś‍ya-vidheyabhāvas‍thalaniyamān‍targatat‍vāditi|
uddeś‍yavidheyabhāvas‍thale ca asati bādhake uddeś‍yatāvac‍chedakavidheyayayoḥ prayoj‍yaprayojakabhāvalābho'pi v‍yut‍pattisiddhaḥ, yathā ‘dhān‍yena dhanavān sukhī' atra sukharūpe vidheye dhānyābhinnadhanaprayoj‍yat‍vabodha ānubhavikaḥ| ‘ayaṃ ghaṭa' it‍yādau uddeś‍yavidheyabhāvasatt‍ve'pi ghaṭat‍ve vidheye idan‍tvarūpoddeś‍yatāvac‍chedakaprayoj‍yat‍vas‍ya bādhasat‍vāt na tathāt‍vam iti|
atha viśeṣ‍yaviśeṣaṇayorekā vibhaktiḥ samaliṅgatā ca, taduktaṃ "viśeṣyas‍ya ca yalliṅgaṃ vibhaktivacane ca ye" it‍yādi, tat uddeś‍yavidheyayorapi viśeṣ‍yaviśeṣaṇa-bhāvāt kathaṃ ‘eko vṛkṣaḥ pañca naukā bhavati' it‍yādau v‍yabhicāraḥ iti cet, liṅga-saṅkh‍yādīnāṃ na sarvvatra tan‍tratā, paraṃ "prakṛtivikṛt‍yoḥ prakṛtivac‍ca" iti niyamena kadācit tanniyamavaiparīt‍yamapi vijñāp‍yate, an‍yathā tādṛśānuśāsanas‍ya anapekṣaṇī-yat‍vāt| nanu śākhāpallavādyavacchinnaḥ padārtha eva vṛkṣapadena abhidhīyate naukāpadena ca śākhāpallavādi śūn‍yavṛkṣāvayavaprakṛtikasaṃs‍thānaviśeṣavān padārtha iti paras‍paraṃ katham uddeś‍yavidheyavācakapadārthayorabhedān‍vayaḥ it‍yāśaṅkāyāmāha- vṛkṣapadaṃ vṛkṣāram‍bhakadrav‍ya-paramiti, idānīm ubhayorabhedān‍vayo na bādhita iti dh‍yeyam|

atha ktvāprat‍yayārthanirūpaṇam| tas‍ya ktvāprat‍yayas‍ya| ānan‍taryas‍ya ca ‘bhukt‍vā vrajatī't‍yādau vrajādidhāt‍varthe an‍vayaḥ, "ekakarttṛkayoḥ pūrvvakāle" (kātantra. kṛt. 433) it‍yādyanuśāsanabalāt yatkriyāvācakapadottarakt‍vāprat‍yayaḥ tas‍yāḥ pūrvva-varttit‍valābhāt aparadhāt‍varthas‍ya anan‍tarat‍vaniyamāt| tadevoddiś‍ya vrajanapūrvvakāle bhojanaṃ vā avagāhate iti uktam|
atha ānantaryyamātras‍ya ktvāprat‍yayārthat‍ve kathaṃ samānakarttṛkat‍vam anubhavasiddhaṃ pratīyate it‍yākāṅkṣāyāmāha - kin‍tu it‍yādi| jhanat‍kārādyanan‍taramapīti, patanādīnāṃ bahukṣaṇavṛttitayā ādimaṃ kṣaṇaṃ jhanatkārādyadhikaraṇaṃ parit‍yaj‍ya tadanan‍tarakṣaṇīyapata-nādigraheṇaiva ānan‍taryyas‍yāpi sāmañjas‍yāt| kecittu samānakālīnat‍vamapi kvacit ktvāprat‍yayārthaḥ tadudāharaṇameva ‘mukhaṃ v‍yādāya s‍vapiti' it‍yādi| ānan‍taryyamātrārtha-vādinas‍tu pūrvvoktarīt‍yā eva samādhānaṃ kurvvanti|
nañarthān v‍yanakti - tatra naña it‍yādinā| saṃsargābhāvastrividhaḥ prāgabhāvadh‍vaṃsā-bhāvāt‍yan‍tābhāvabhedāt| vināś‍yabhāvaḥ prāgabhāvaḥ, jan‍yābhāvaḥ dh‍vaṃsābhāvaḥ, sanātanasaṃsargābhāvaśca at‍yan‍tābhāvaḥ, taduktaṃ - "prāgabhāvas‍tathā dh‍vaṃso'p‍ya-t‍yan‍tābhāva eva ca| evaṃ traividh‍yamāpannaḥ saṃsargābhāva iṣ‍yate" iti bhāṣāparic‍chedagran‍the| an‍yon‍yābhāvas‍tu ekavidha eva, sa ca tādāt‍myasam‍ban‍dhāvacchinna-pratiyogi‍tākābhāvaḥ, tatra nāmān‍vayo nañarthaḥ an‍yon‍yābhāvaḥ, yathā na brāhmaṇaḥ abrāhmaṇaḥ, na ghaṭaḥ aghaṭa it‍yādi, anayoḥ nañarthasya brāhmaṇapadārthena ghaṭapadārthena ca an‍vayāt brāhmaṇabhinnaḥ ghaṭabhinna it‍yarthapratīteḥ an‍yon‍yābhāvatāprat‍yayaḥ|
dhāt‍varthān‍vayī nañarthaḥ at‍yan‍tābhāvaḥ yathā ‘caitras‍ya na pākaḥ na gatiḥ na vā bhojanam' it‍yādau abhāvena pratiyogitayā kriyāyā an‍vayāt abhāvas‍ya at‍yantā-bhāvat‍vam|
dhāt‍varthān‍vayī nañartha iva prat‍yayārthān‍vayyapi nañarthaḥ atyantābhāva eva| yathā ‘iha bhūtale ghaṭo nāsti, devadatto na paśyati' ityādau|
bahuvrīhisamāsasthale tu nāmārthānvayino'pi nañarthasya at‍yan‍tābhāvarūpat‍vaṃ, yathā ‘aghaṭaṃ bhūtalam, adhanaścaitra' it‍yādi, atra ghaṭasya dhanas‍ya ca bhūtale caitre ca yathāsaṅkh‍yam abhāvaḥ pratīyate, evam abhāvādhikaraṇas‍ya saptamyā nirddeśe nāmārthān‍vayit‍ve'pi nañarthas‍ya at‍yan‍tābhāvarūpat‍vameva, yathā ‘bhūtale na ghaṭa' it‍yatra iti kecit vadanti| pare tu nātra nañarthas‍ya nāmārthān‍vayit‍vaṃ, kin‍tu ‘bhūtale ghaṭo nāsti' it‍yevameva bodhāt, kriyān‍vayit‍vameva nañarthas‍ya, kriyā dhāt‍vartha it‍yāhuḥ|
prāyeṇa uktārthānugatameva gran‍thakāro nirvvakti - yatret‍yādi| viśeṣ‍yaviśeṣaṇayoḥ nañarthavinirmmokeṇa viśeṣ‍yaviśeṣaṇabhāvāpannayoḥ bhāvān‍vaye tayoreva viśeṣ‍ya-viśeṣaṇabhāvena an‍vaye, tatraiva natu an‍yatra| yatret‍yādi - anuyogini abhāvādhi-karaṇe nāmārthapratiyogikasaṃsargābhāvabodhaḥ yathā ‘bhūtale ghaṭo nāsti' it‍yādau – prat‍yayārthādipratiyogikasaṃsargābhāvabodhas‍thale tu na anuyogini saptamyapekṣā yathā ‘na pacati caitra' it‍yādau|
an‍vayitāvac‍chedakāvacchinnapratiyogitākat‍vamiti - tathāhi ‘ghaṭo nāsti' it‍yādau abhāve pratiyogitāsam‍ban‍dhena ghaṭo'n‍veti, ato ghaṭat‍vameva an‍vayitā-vac‍chedakaṃ tadavacchinnapratiyogitaiva abhāve ghaṭat‍vāvacchinnas‍ya saṃsargaḥ, an‍yathā yadi dhaṭat‍vāvacchinnapratiyogit‍vādīnāṃ drav‍yat‍vādyavacchinnasaṃsargatā s‍yāt, tadā paṭādhika-raṇe'pi ghaṭābhāvavadbhūtalādau niyamena drav‍yaṃ nāsti it‍yādyabhāvapratyayāpattiḥ anu-yogini saṃsargasatt‍vas‍ya eva saṃsargisatt‍vaniyāmakat‍vāt iti|
evamiti pratiyogitāmātras‍ya saṃsargat‍vas‍vīkāre yatki‍ñcidghaṭapratiyogika-bhedamādāya nīlaghaṭe'pi ghaṭo na iti pratītirduṣ‍pariharā, an‍vayitāvac‍chedakāvacchinna-pratiyogitākat‍vas‍ya abhāve bhānopagame tu ghaṭat‍vāvacchinnapratiyogitākabhedas‍ya nīlaghaṭe bādhāt nātiprasaṅgaḥ, uktarīt‍yā ca pītaghaṭavati nīlaghaṭat‍vāvacchinna-pratiyogitākābhāvas‍ya satt‍vācca nānupattiḥ, vaiparītye tu anupapattirbhavati, ghaṭa-sāmān‍yābhāvas‍ya tatra satt‍vāt ata eva an‍vayitāvacchedakatāyāḥ paryyāptirapi niveś‍yā|
yatra tu it‍yādi| sāmān‍ye sāmān‍yadharmmāvacchinne an‍yūnavṛttidharmmāvacchinne it‍yarthaḥ, viśeṣe viśeṣadharmmāvacchinne n‍yūnavṛttiv‍yāp‍yadharmmāvacchinne it‍yarthaḥ, sāmā-n‍yas‍ya an‍yūnavṛttidharmmāvacchinnas‍ya viśeṣetaraparat‍vaṃ n‍yūnavṛttiv‍yāp‍yadharmmāvacchinna-bodhec‍chayā uccaritat‍vam| padārthān‍tarasākāṅkṣamiti sāmān‍yasākāṅkṣapadārthātirikta-padārthasākāṅkṣamit‍yarthaḥ| taditaraṃ viśeṣadharmmāvacchinnameva sāmān‍yam an‍veti śāb‍dabodhe bhāsate iti|
brāhmaṇebh‍ya it‍yādi - atra brāhmaṇat‍vaṃ sāmān‍yadharmmaḥ, tadavacchinneṣu dadhi-karmmakadānas‍ya vidhiḥ, kauṇḍin‍yat‍vaṃ viśeṣadharmmaḥ tadavacchinne ca dadhidānas‍ya niṣedhaḥ iti kauṇḍin‍yat‍varūpaviśeṣadharmmāvacchinnabhinnebhya eva brāhmaṇat‍varūpa-sāmān‍yadharmmāvacchinnebh‍yo dadhidānamanubhavasiddhaṃ, tacca sāmān‍yadharmmāvacchinna-vācakas‍yāpi brāhmaṇapadas‍ya kauṇḍin‍yetaravācakat‍vakalpanāt| etadevāha - prakṛte tu it‍yādinā| padārthān‍tarasākāṅkṣamit‍yādyudāharaṇamāha - brāhmaṇebh‍yo dadhi dīyatāṃ, takraṃ kauṇḍin‍yāya it‍yādi|
niṣedho dvividhaḥ - prasaj‍yapratiṣedhaḥ paryyudāsaśca| prasaj‍yapratiṣedhas‍tu kriyān‍vayī abhāvaḥ niṣedhārthapradhānaśca, taduktaṃ - ‘apradhān‍yaṃ vidheryatra pratiṣedhe pradhānatā| prasaj‍yapratiṣedho'sau kriyayā saha yatra nañ'| paryyudāsas‍tu "prādhān‍yaṃ hi vidheryatra pratiṣedhe'pradhānatā| paryyudāsaḥ sa vijñeyo yatrottarapadena nañ|" iti| yathā ‘ekādaś‍yāṃ na bhuñjīta' it‍yatra bhojanarūpakriyayā abhāvas‍ya an‍vayaḥ bhojanapratiṣedhas‍ya ca prādhān‍yam|
dvitīyas‍ya udāharaṇaṃ yathā - ‘atha śrāddhamamāvas‍yāyāṃ pitṛbh‍yo dadyāt' iti, tadanan‍taraṃ ‘rātrau śrāddhaṃ na kurvvīta' iti niṣedhaśās‍traṃ, atra ubhayorekavākyatā-paryyālocanayā sāmān‍yaviśeṣabhāvena amāvas‍yāyāṃ paryyudāsanañarthas‍ya an‍vayāt rātribhinnāmāvas‍yāyāṃ śrāddhas‍ya karttav‍yatā pratīyate, nanu rātryadhikaraṇakaśrāddhe karttav‍yatābhāva iti vidheḥ prādhān‍yaṃ niṣedhas‍ya ca aprādhān‍yamiti|
at‍yan‍tābhāvas‍ya prasaj‍yapratiṣedharūpat‍vāt tas‍yaiva prasaj‍yapratiṣedha iti saṃjñā| at‍yan‍tābhāvas‍yaiva kriyān‍vayit‍vāt, paryyudāsas‍tu bheda eva iti|
atha vaidhahiṃsāṃ nirvvakti - mā hiṃsyādit‍yādinā| sāmān‍yaśās‍traṃ prāṇimātrāṇā-meva hiṃsāniṣedhabodhakaṃ, viśeṣaśās‍traṃ yatkiñcij‍yātīyaprāṇihiṃsābodhakamiti|
atra hisāṃyāmit‍yādi - niṣedhaśās‍tras‍ya sarvvabhūtaviṣayakat‍vāt śvetachāgala-viṣayakat‍vamapīti, śvetachāgalaviṣayakahiṃsāvidhiśās‍treṇa tadvirodhaḥ, ekatraiva śvetac‍chāgale hiṃsākarmmat‍vas‍ya tadabhāvas‍ya ca asambhavāt, apavādaviṣayaṃ parit‍yaj‍ya ut‍sargaḥ pravarttate iti niyamena viśeṣaśās‍traviṣayaṃ parit‍yaj‍ya sāmān‍ya-śās‍tras‍ya pravṛttirvvāc‍yā, viśeṣaśās‍traviṣayaśca śvetac‍chāgalaviṣayakālam‍bhanaṃ tat‍parihāreṇa eva niṣedhaśās‍tras‍ya pravarttanāt śvetac‍chāgaletarabhūtaviṣayakahiṃsā-niṣedhaḥ pratipadyate, sarvvaśab‍das‍yāpi uddeś‍yatāvac‍chedakav‍yāpakavidheyav‍yāp‍yayāvatt‍vā-vacchinne śaktikal‍pane'pi bhūtapadena śvetachāgaletarabhūtalābhāt śvetachāgaletara-bhūtat‍vav‍yāpakahiṃsākarmmat‍vāvabodhe na bādhaḥ| niravakāśā hi vidhayaḥ sāvakāśān vidhīn bādhan‍te iti|
anarthahetuteti, - aniṣ‍ṭajanakat‍vam, taduktaṃ - "dṛṣṭavadānuśravikaḥ sa hyaviśuddhikṣayātiśayayukta" iti, taduktaṃ pañcaśikhācāryyaiḥ "s‍val‍pasaṅkaraḥ saparihāraḥ saprat‍yavamarṣaḥ" iti| s‍val‍pasaṅkara iti - j‍yotiṣṭomaprabhṛtiyāgajātas‍ya pradhānas‍ya apūrvvas‍ya s‍val‍pena paśuhiṃsādisam‍bhavena aniṣṭasādhakena apūrvveṇa saṅkaraḥ| saparihāra iti - s‍val‍paprāyaścittenāpi nirākaraṇayogyaḥ, pramādāśca ananuṣṭhite prāyaścite karm‍mavipākadaśāyām avaś‍yamevānarthasamut‍pādaḥ| sapratyavamarṣa iti - prat‍yavamarṣeṇa sahiṣ‍ṇutayā saha varttamānaḥ, yathoktam - "mṛṣyan‍te hi puṇ‍yasam‍bhāropanīta-s‍vargasudhāmahāhradāvagāhinaḥ kuśalāḥ pāpamātropapāditāṃ duḥkhavahnikaṇikām" iti|
ata evet‍yādi - kratūpakārakat‍vanarakat‍vajanakat‍vayoḥ mithovirodhavirahāt tathā kal‍panaṃ saṅgac‍chate, kratoreva aśvamedhādi yāgas‍yaiva, s‍vargajanakat‍vaṃ duḥkhāsambhinna-sukharūpas‍vargahetut‍vaṃ, tattadviśeṣavidhiḥ - ‘s‍vargakāmaḥ aśvamedhena yajeta' it‍yādi s‍vargaphalādyāvedakavidhiḥ|
atha padānāṃ śaktiṃ nirūpayati - tattadit‍yādikamārabhya| bhagavadic‍chā ca ghaṭapadāt ghaṭo boddhavya it‍yādyākārikā, tatra ghaṭapadajanyabodhaviṣayat‍vaṃ prakāraḥ ghaṭaśca viśeṣ‍yaḥ, ‘ghaṭo ghaṭapadajan‍yabodhaviṣayatāvān bhavatu' it‍yākāro niṣ‍kṛṣṭaḥ|
jātau v‍yaktau vaiśiṣṭye ca it‍yādi uktapadārthatraye eva padānāṃ śaktiḥ, tathā ca āśrayat‍vasam‍ban‍dhāvacchinnā yā ghaṭapadajan‍yabodhaviṣayat‍vaprakāratā, tannirūpitaviśe-ṣ‍yat‍vaṃ ghaṭe, avac‍chedakat‍vasam‍ban‍dhāvacchinnā yā tādṛśabodhaviṣayat‍vaprakāratā, tanni-rūpitaviśeṣ‍yat‍vaṃ ghaṭat‍ve, s‍vanirūpitāvac‍chedakatāvac‍chedakasaṃsargat‍vasam‍ban‍dhāva-cchinnā tādṛśabodhaviṣayat‍vaprakāratā tannirūpitaviśeṣ‍yat‍vaṃ ghaṭat‍vapratiyogikasamavāye iti padārthatraye eva niruktaviśeṣyat‍varūpavāc‍yat‍vopapattiḥ|
teṣ‍veva iti prat‍yekaṃ tattadvarṇeṣ‍veva, ekākṣarakoṣokteti - "akāro vāsudeva-ss‍yāt ākāras‍ya pitāmaha" it‍yādi koṣavacanabodhitet‍yarthaḥ| padajñānas‍ya kāraṇa-t‍vāt ādau padaśravaṇaṃ, tataḥ śaktidhīḥ, tadanan‍taraṃ v‍yāpāraḥ padārthajñānaṃ, tataḥ śāb‍dabodha iti|
atha śaktigrahaṇakāraṇaṃ nirūpayati - śaktigrahamit‍yādinā| pācaka it‍yādi - pacadhātorvviklitt‍yanukūlav‍yāpāro'rthaḥ| vuṇprat‍yayas‍ya karttṛt‍vaṃ, tat‍paryyālocanayā pākakarttā iti bodhaḥ| upamā yathā - gauriva gavaya it‍yādi| yatra araṇ‍yas‍thena kenacit grāmīṇaṃ prati uktaṃ gosadṛśo gavayapadavāc‍ya iti, paścāt grāmīṇena kvacidaraṇ‍yādau gavayo dṛṣṭas‍tatra gosādṛś‍yadarśanaṃ yajjātaṃ tat upamitikaraṇaṃ, tadanan‍taraṃ gosadṛśo gavayapadavāc‍ya iti, atideśavāk‍yārthasmaraṇaṃ yajjāyate tadeva v‍yāpāraḥ, tadanan‍taraṃ tatra gavayo gavayapadavāc‍ya iti jñānaṃ yajjāyate tadupamitiḥ, natu ayaṃ gavayapadavāc‍ya it‍yupamitiḥ, gavayān‍tare śaktigrahābhāvaprasaṅgāditi muktāvalī|
śab‍dān‍tareṇa tat‍paryyāyeṇa apaśab‍dena tadarthakathanaṃ tas‍yaiva arthavivaraṇam, yathā - atra ghaṭo nāsti ityukte yatra śrotā prakṛt‍yarthaṃ dhārayati tadanan‍tarameva kalaso nāsti it‍yukte prakṛtamarthamanudhārayati tatra ghaṭapadas‍yāpi ghaṭat‍vāvacchinne śaktiriti pratipadyate|
atha śab‍daṃ nirūpayati - sa ca it‍yādinā| dvividha iti taduk‍taṃ- "śab‍dodh‍vaniśca varṇaśca mṛdaṅgādibhavo dh‍vaniḥ| kāṣṭhasaṃyogādijan‍yā varṇās‍te kādayo matāḥ| sarvvaḥ śab‍do nabhovṛttiḥ śrotrotpannas‍tu gṛhyate"|| iti
varṇāt‍maka eveti - evakāro dh‍van‍yāt‍makaśab‍dav‍yavac‍chedakaśaktyādijñānadvārā abādhabodhakat‍valakṣaṇasādhut‍vas‍ya v‍yākaraṇaparigṛhītat‍varūpasādhut‍vasya vā dh‍van‍yā-t‍makaśab‍de asatt‍vāt|
so'pi dvividha iti vaiyākaraṇamate mukh‍yalākṣaṇikādibhedena| śab‍daḥ ṣoḍha़ā kathyate, tadyathā - "mukh‍yo lākṣaṇiko gauṇaḥ śab‍daḥ s‍yādaupacārikaḥ| yaugiko yogarūḍhaśca śab‍daḥ ṣoḍhā nigadyate"|| iti|
pācakādiriti - pācakapadas‍ya v‍yut‍pattigatārtha eva mukh‍yaḥ, natu tadatiriktas‍tadarthaḥ| gopadas‍ya "gamerḍori" 1 ti v‍yut‍pattiparyyālocanayā gamanakarttṛ-rūpārtha eva v‍yavatiṣṭhate| sa ca ‘gauḥ śete' it‍yādau anupayukta iti avayavārtha-parit‍yāgena samudāye atirikto'rthaḥ kal‍pyate| drav‍yaguṇakriyājātibhedāditi, taduktaṃ - "śab‍dairebhiḥ pratīyante jātidrav‍yaguṇakriyāḥ| cāturvvidh‍yādamīṣān‍tu śab‍da uktaścaturvvidha"|| iti|
atha uktānāṃ yaugikādibhedena caturvvidhānāṃ śab‍dānāṃ lakṣaṇaṃ pṛthak nirvvakti -samudāyet‍yādinā | prāyeṇa etat s‍paṣṭaṃ pūrvalakṣaṇe v‍yākh‍yātañca|
atha vācakasāmān‍yalakṣaṇamāha - sākṣādit‍yādinā| saṅketitaṃ saṅketena viṣayīkṛtaṃ saṅketanirūpitaviśeṣ‍yatāśrayam arthaṃ, sākṣādit‍yanena lakṣakav‍yavacchedaḥ, lakṣaṇās‍thale śakyārthas‍ya lakṣ‍yārthaviśeṣaṇatayā upasthiteḥ| īśvarasaṅketitaḥ īśvaras‍ya ic‍chayā viṣayīkṛtaḥ, tena lākṣaṇikaśab‍dena tas‍ya lakṣyārthas‍ya upasthitiḥ s‍mṛtiḥ na tu mukh‍yārthas‍ya it‍yarthaḥ| mukh‍yārthas‍ya bādhe eva lakṣaṇāyāḥ pravṛtteḥ| vṛttirdvividhā iti - ālaṅkārikaiḥ v‍yañjanākh‍yā vṛttiraparā kalpyate, naiyāyikais‍tu v‍yañjanāpratipādyas‍ya arthas‍ya udīc‍yamānasambodhenaiva nirvvāha iti nādhikā vṛttiḥ kal‍pyate| śaktiḥ nirūpitā "atha śaktinirūpaṇa" miti pras‍tāve iti śeṣaḥ|
sā ca lakṣaṇeti "lakṣaṇā śakyasam‍ban‍dhas‍tāt‍paryyānupapattita" iti naiyāyikāḥ, "mukh‍yārthabādhe tadyoge" it‍yādi ca ālaṅkārikasan‍darbhaḥ| saiva ca lakṣaṇā mukh‍yārthasam‍ban‍dha eva it‍yanena naiyāyikamatānugatat‍vaṃ rakṣitam|
