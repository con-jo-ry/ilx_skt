yohiprāpti śikṣā

śrīmadgaṇeśaṃ praṇipatya devānśrīsūryanārāyaṇasuprasādāt
vakṣye kriyāyā svaralakṣaṇaṃ tadyaddhomakanyāpurasūribhaṭṭaḥ 1

tasmāccatvāriparaścedaharaccādyudāttavān
prāyacchattriryavaṃ tasmāttasmai tveṣa parastvasi 2

prāviśattaṃ prāha tādṛgyatsomaḥ prājanayattathā
prāveśayantyādadata tatprāyuñjata tena ca 3

mindābhūnmāyāsītkuṃbhyādādasti syavapūrvakaḥ
bha āśayāddhi pūrvaśca paribhūrasi sādigam 4

jahīndraśśṛṇute māntaścinute'pātmaśīrṣa ca
prokṣitaṃ vīkṣitāyātra ādiśabdāvudāttagau 5

cinute tho sarvaṃ nīcaṃ yacchabdātpara ityapi
sāṃhitena svareṇāpi jñeyaśśeṣasvaro budhaiḥ 6

yadyāvacchabdakau yatra yathādyucco yato yadi
yadā yarhi hyadhaścādirādvādaśātkriyocca bhāk 7

yā prajāḥ parābahurvai yo vai de prāṇa uttaraḥ
ya evamaharyatsomaḥ prayattriḥ prāyayaddhiman 8

yanmaitrāvaruṇa yo vaisau yathākṣaṃśca yo'gniṣaḥ
hyeta vidviryathānasi tribhavedyatpunaścitim 9

āyurvā anyaśabdādissomaṃ vāruṇa eṣa te
pṛthivyai tveti some'pi na kuryātpūrvayāpi ca 10

vatsañcādhiśrayatyantaṃ nāvave prapurodaye
apayuṣmadādyāvartatāntamavarṇa āditaḥ 11

nakṣodhukaśca sā somamaddhvānogniḥ purā sabhām
agniramuṣmindevānāṃ trīṇi ca śakathā gavi 12

somo'gnirevāsmā ūrdhvassatyādanasthikena ca
ādityā amuṣmiṃścācyutpayo viśve'nvatiṣṭhattat 13

vānyaśabdau pūrvapakṣe pūrveṣvā pañcamastathā
savyo na yajñaṃ vasvī ca dvirninā nāyatānṛṇam 14

yavaṃ saudāsānabhordhvo rathacakraparāsu ca
saṃbandhaścāntasānnidhye tulyayorādiruccavān 15

mayati vaṣaṭ stṛṇute tṛtīṣorundha eva ca
yunakti prajāpāsyastu pāhyādi yajurādi cet 16

na ghnīṇyṛṣabhaṃ dvedvedhaḥ kriyā cānyaśrutīṃgyabhāk
yathā manurmanuṣyāśca yodayājīsayo yathā 17

śīrṣṇādidhruva ūṣāścānvañcāvājyaṃ purīṣa ca
aṅgāreṣvāruṇo veṇorajā vediḥ paraṃ ca yat 18

upahyātmano devānāṃ suvargo'śvācca cakṣuṣī
rudro mṛnmayamūrdhvo hi nīcaṃ dadhāti kevalam 19

cityāmanyāṃ ca nānyāṃ ca purastādanyā yadītaram
ghāryārabhyāpitanmadhye tacchabde'nīcake sthite 20

saṃkhyā śabde'pyanedante sānnidhyaṃ naikamaṣṭakam
apūrve'pi kriyā nityamityādyanyayajuḥsthitam 21

na hi sa taṃ hi te tāṃ hi tasya māyā hi tattathā
yānte gacchantu yastasyāṃ yattadyeṣāṃ hi cātra hi 22

yo yakṣye yasyaivaṃ yattaṃ yāvāneva ya ityapi
tatsvarasūcanārthaṃ hi bālānāmiti varṇitaḥ 23

iti yohiprāptimūlaṃ samāptam
