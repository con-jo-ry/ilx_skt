atha guḍaṃ bhuṅkte ityatra guḍatvasyeva madhuratvasyāpi prakāratayā tatprakārakaśābdabodhavāramāya taddharmaprakārakaśābdabodhaṃ prati taddharmaprakārakaśaktijñānasya hetutvaṃ vācyam| evaṃ ca ghaṭatvāvacchinnaprakāratāśāliśābdabodhaṃ prati tādṛśaśaktijñānasya hetutayā kathaṃ ghaṭaśabdatvādeḥ prakāratāvacchedakatayā bhānaṃ ghaṭaśabdasya ghaṭaśabdavācye śaktiriti śaktigrahābhāvāt| yattu sambandijñānavidhayā śabdasya bhānāmiti| tanna| hastipakaśabdāddhastino'pi bodhāpatteriti ced| śabdāṃśe smaramātmakabodhasyaivopagamāt|
