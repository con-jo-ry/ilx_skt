kramakārikā śikṣā
śrīgaṇeśāya namaḥ
praṇamyādyaṃ dinamaṇiṃ yogīśvaraśiromaṇim
sarvajñaṃ yājñavalkyaṃ tacchiṣyaṃ kātyāyanammunim 1
sarvaṃ yājuṣamantrāṇāṃ vede vājasaneyake
kramāvasānaviṣayāṃ kārikāmārabhāmahe 2
śloke vindvādirahitaḥ pratīkeṣu na duḥsahaḥ
pratyekaṃ prathamā ṣaṣṭhī saptamyuktaśca kalpate 3
padānāmānupūrvyeṇa krama eṣaḥ pravartate
gaṇāntānāṃ kramo nāsti iti kātyāyano'bravīt 4
na veṣṭyāśca dviveṣṭyāśca triveṣṭyāśca tathā pare
caturvveṣṭyo madhyaveṣṭyaḥ pañcaveṣṭya itīritaḥ 5
avagraho'nuvākānte arddharcānte ṛcāṃ tathā
yajuṣāṃ vākyāvasāneṣviti sādhāraṇo vidhiḥ 6
pratyakṣaṃ yājñavalkyaṃ śrīśambhumiśravinirmitā
kriyatāṃ kramitaiḥ kaṇṭhaiḥ kārikā ratnamālikā 7
hrasvapūrvo hyanusvāro dīrghānusvārabhāgbhavet
dveśaḥ paraścenna tathā kramakāle tu vājinām 8
om | iṣetvetyayamadhyāyo hyanuvākāntaveṣṭanaḥ
gāyatreṇa triveṣṭyo'tra purā krūratriveṣṭanaḥ 9
deva savitastrivṛdadvyekameṣā te tu dviveṣṭanaḥ
etante dvyekatā tvagnīṣomayoranuvākavṛt 10
agne dabdhāya'ekatvandevāgātuvide dvivṛt
sambarhyādiṣaṭkaikāmuruvviṣṇo dviveṣṭanaḥ 11
tato'siddhe tricatuṣkaikaṃ saṃvvarcceti dviveṣṭanaḥ
yajñasañcetyekatātve'gnaye kavyeti na veṣṭanaḥ 12
yerū dvivṛnnamo dvaikamādyantorjjarūpordvivṛt
samidhādyaṣṭasu dvivṛdagnirjjyotiḥ ṣaḍekatā 13
indraḥ pañcādiveṣṭyaḥ syātsamidaṃ pañcaveṣṭanaḥ
upapraṣaṭsvapi dvivṛttanūpāt saptakaikatā 14
upattvā pañcasu dvivṛdiḍa'ehyantaveṣṭanaḥ
somānādyaṣṭasu dvivṛtparite dūḍabhastrivṛt 15
bhūrbhuvaḥ svonuvākaikyaṃ praghāsīti dviveṣṭanaḥ
yadagrāmetyantaveṣṭyaḥ syānmo ṣūṇeti dvayordvivṛt 16
avabhṛthetyantaveṣṭyaḥ pūrṇādarvyaṣṭasu dvivṛt
eṣa taikā tryambakayordvayordvivṛtparo'ntavṛt 17
vājinītvājo dviveṣṭyo vājyahampañcakaikatā
edamāditriṣu dvivṛnmahīnāṃ yugalaikatā 18
āpo dviveṣṭanaḥ svāhā yajñamityantaveṣṭanaḥ
ākūttyai prayuje trivṛdviśvo devasya tu dvivṛt 19
ṛksāmayoścatuṣkaikyaṃ vrataṃ kṛṇu saptaikatā
tvamagnevratapāddvivṛdrāssve pañcāntaveṣṭanaḥ 20
eṣā te pañcakaikatvaṃ vasvyādiścaturaikatā
eṣa te yugalaikatvamabhityanta dviveṣṭanaḥ 21
prajābhyastvā'ntavṛccandramprathamārddhāntaveṣṭanaḥ
sagme'rdvaśukrantapasantanvādicaturekatā 22
pratipanthāndviveṣṭyaḥ syādadityāstvak dviveṣṭanaḥ
vaneṣu yugayordvivṛdu srāvetaṃ yugaikatā 23
namo mitrasya tu dvivṛdvaruṇasya trivṛtparaḥ
agnestanūryuṃgaikatvaṃ bhavatanno dvayordvivṛt 24
āpatayettvendraghoṣastvā'nuvākāntaveṣṭanaḥ
yuñjateti triṣu dvivṛddevaśrutayugaikatā 25
viṣṇvāditriṣu dvivṛdviṣṇorarāṭamantavṛt
ādade pañcakaikatvaṃ yavo'si caturaikatā 26
pari tvā girvaṇo dvivṛdindrasya syū rathāntavṛt
vibhūrasyānuvākaikyaṃ jyotirveṣṭacatuṣkavān 27
agne naya dvayordivṛddeva savitastrayaikatā
atyanyāntritayaikatvamayaṃ hi tvā dviveṣṭanaḥ 28
agreṇīrantavṛdyā te triṣu dvivṛtparo'ntavṛt
upāvīraśca manaste yatte tvetyanuvākavṛt 29
samudrādicatuṣkaikyamidamāpo dvayordvivṛt
agnervastvantaveṣṭyaḥ syādamūryyāstu dviveṣṭanaḥ 30
hṛde ttvā yugalaikatvā śṛṇotvagnirdviveṣṭanaḥ
devīrāpastrayaikatvaṃ yamagne pṛtsviti dvivṛt 31
ādade saptakaikatvaṃ tvamaṅgeti triveṣṭanaḥ
vācastraikyaṃ copayāma dvayaikyamācāryā ca dvivṛt 32
indravāyū'ayaṃ vāṃ ca rāyā yāvāṃ triveṣṭanaḥ
tampratnādyarddhayordvivṛttvantaśukraiṣate'ntavṛt 33
acchinasya caturṣu dvivṛdayaṃ venastvamadhyamaḥ
eṣa te'tyantaveṣṭyaḥ sthādye devāso dviveṣṭanaḥ 34
āgneyatraikyamindrāyadvaikyaṃ mūrddhā dviveṣṭanaḥ
upāṃśorantaveṣṭyaḥ syādapo devā dviveṣṭanaḥ 35
vṛṣṇa'ūrmistrayaikatvaṃ savitā tvanuvākavṛt
somasya tviṣiryyamaikyaṃ sadhamādī dviveṣṭanaḥ 36
kṣatrasyolbaṃ tryaikyaṃ aveṣṭhāścatuṣkāntaveṣṭanaḥ
ūrddhāmāroha cadyarddhavarjaṃ vidyāddviveṣṭanaḥ 37
dvivṛtsomasya tviṣirhiraṇyarūpau triveṣṭanaḥ
somasya tvā'ntaveṣṭyaḥ praparvatasya triveṣṭanaḥ 38
prajāpatayeti dviveṣṭyo rudra yatvantaveṣṭanaḥ
indrasya tvāntavṛnmāte dvivṛdagnaya'antavṛt 39
haṃso dvivṛcce yadasi syonāsi cāntaveṣṭanaḥ
niṣasāda dviveṣṭyo'bhibhūrādicaturaikatā 40
savitrā dvaiṣṭyamaśvibhyāṃ yugmaṃ yugmaṃ trivṛd dvivṛt
yuñjānaḥ saptasu dvivṛdādade yugalaikatā 41
hastādi caturṣu dvivṛtpratūrvaṃ yugalaikatā
anvāgnyekādaśā nadvivṛtpṛthivyāḥ sadhasthāstrivṛt 42
apā pṛṣṭandvayordvivṛtsam̐vvasāthām̐ triveṣṭanaḥ
tvāmagne pañcadaśasu dvivṛttrivṛdvṛṣāgni te 43
oṣadhyekādaśānādvivṛnmakhasya śirontataḥ
kṛtvāya sā dviveṣṭyaḥ syādvasavastvā'ntaveṣṭanaḥ 44
aditiṣṭvā yugaikatvammitrasya yugalandvivṛt
utthāya bṛhatīti dvāvākūtintvantaveṣṭanaḥ 45
mā svādiṣu ca sarveṣu dvivṛdannapate trivṛt
dvivṛddṛśāno'dhyāyadvisuparṇo'si navaikatā 46
cidasītyantavṛdyante sañjñānañca triveṣṭanam
dvivṛnmayi hyanuvāko dhruvāsi caturaikatā 47
kāṇḍāditi caturṣu dvivṛdvirāḍjyotistrayaikatā
aṣāḍhādyāstathaikonaviṃśati syurdviveṣṭanaḥ 48
imammā syātrivṛdekaśaphaṃ paśumiti dvivṛt
sahasramūrṇāyumajo hyantavarjjaṃ dviveṣṭanaḥ 49
tvaṃ yaviṣṭṭha dviveṣṭyaḥ syādapāntvetyantaveṣṭanaḥ
navāyaṃ puro na veṣṭyo hyantyāmyardhaṃ vinā dvivṛt 50
dhruvakṣitiścaturṣṣuṃ dvivṛdadittyāstvā triveṣṭanaḥ
sajūrmmūrddhā vacaśceti hyanuvākāntaveṣṭanaḥ 51
indrāgnī syādvivṛdviśvakarmā tvā yugalaikatā
ādya mūrdhā'śuragnebhārgaikayetyanuvākavṛt 52
agne jātāṃ dvivṛntrivatsahasā'gneḥ purīṣandvivṛt
evādiraśminā rājñī cāyampuraścānuvākavṛt 53
agnirmūrddhā'nuvāko dviveṣṭyo'trāntatriveṣṭanaḥ
yena'ṛṣayo'nuvāko dvivṛtpañcāntaveṣṭanaḥ 54
prodhadvivṛttvantavarjamāṇe hi dviparo'ntavṛt
namaste cānuvākeṣu saptasvādyo dviveṣṭanaḥ 55
anuvākāntavṛtṣaṭsu drāpeandhāṣṭasu dvivṛt
daśāsminmahatītyādyāḥ sarve caivāntaveṣṭanāḥ 56
asmannūrjjamidandvivṛdimāmeti trayaikatā
samudrasaptasudvivṛnnamaste harase vivṛt 57
ye devādiṣu ca dvivṛdājuhāno'ntaveṣṭanaḥ
tāṃ savituḥ pañcasu dvivṛt śukrajyotyanuvākavṛt 58
dvivṛdimaṃstānuvāka'etā'arṣatiṃ tatttrivṛt
vājādayo'nuvākāḥ syuranuvākāntaveṣṭanāḥ 59
dvivṛdvājasyānuvākaḥ payaḥ pṛthivyāntu vivṛt
ṛtāṣṭakaikya rucannodvayordvivṛtparītāntavṛt 60
viṣu hya'gniṃ yunajmyādiṣvanuvākeṣu ca dvivṛt
svāddontritparītīdvirvvāyostri dviḥ punāti te 61
tribrahmakṣatrannānā dvigagharddhānta vṛdāśvinam
modārddhayuktantejo'nta vṛghāvyāghrādiṣu dvivṛt 62
ṣaḍviṃśatiṣu mantreṣu syātpitṛbhyaḥ svadhāntavṛt
akṣandvādaśasu dvivṛdimarṭhaḥ haviriti trivṛt 63
udīratādiṣu dvivṛt kramasyaiva tu veṣṭanam
tvayāciveṣṭayaḥ prathamādvaikyaṃ lomāni ca dvivṛt 64
śūddrorvavarjjeyyaddevādhyāyaḥ sarvārdhaveṣṭanaḥ
sa ca śūdreryyamantraḥ syādamyayampitṛpade dvivṛt 65
imammetryādiṣu dvivṛddhotā yakṣatsu cāntavṛt
trivṛtpūrvāśvinau chāgādyantavṛttadanantaram 66
trivṛtparāśvinau chāgo vanaspatyagnicāntavṛt
devambarhyādiṣu dvivṛddevo hyatyantaveṣṭanaḥ 67
agnimadya ca sūpasthā tvāmadyeti trayaikatā
tejontavṛdimādvivṛdabhidhā yugalaikatā 68
nadvivṛdyo arvāgnaye'ntavṛddhiṅkārānuvākavṛt
hiraṇyapāṇyādinā vājījanaśca dviveṣṭanaḥ 69
kāyādayo'nuvākasyānuvākā hyantaveṣṭanaḥ
hiraṇyagarbhetyadhyāye prajāpataya'antavṛt 70
yaḥ prāṇato dvivṛccandramāstadatyantaveṣṭanaḥ
yujantītitriṣu dvivṛdvasavo yugalaikatā 71
sviccaturṣṣu vāyuṣṭvā pañcasu syāddviveṣṭanaḥ
agniḥ paśurmmaghyavarjjan dvivṛdatrāmyaikatā 72
utsakthyādānuvākeṣu pañcasu syāddviveṣṭanaḥ
aśvastvāramya catvāraḥ somādyardhyāṃtamekatā 73
rohitāddvisamāyuktaḥ śitirandho'ntaveṣṭanaḥ
śuddhavālaśca prabhṛtyavadityārddhāntamekatā 74
vṛddhayuktaphalagvāmi dvaikyaṃ śilpāstathāntavṛt
saptamūrddhānuvākeṣu mantrāṇāmantaveṣṭanam 75
śādaṃ caivānuvākānānnavakaṃ cānuvākavṛt
pañcamūrdvānuvākeṣu vivṛdimānuka dvivṛt 76
agniśceti trivṛddvivṛdantareṣu triṣu trivṛt
vaiśvānaraṃ caiva vidyādagnirukthena cāntavṛt 77
vaiśvānarasya mumatāvādayaḥ ṣaḍdviveṣṭanaḥ
dvivṛtsamāstvetya'dhyāyaścāpaścidantyaveṣṭanaḥ 78
sam̐vvatsaro'si dvivṛdatyanvāko'nuvākavṛt
hotāyakṣadekādaśapūrvaścaivā paro dvivṛt 79
devabarhiraparo dvivṛcchucamuṣṇihamātrikā
ūrdhvāto'rddhagaṇānteṣu tattanmantrāḥśatāntavṛt 80
dviveṣṭamindrāya vayodhasa'ityantayordvayoḥ
samiddho'ñjannadhyāye mantrāḥ sarve dviveṣṭanāḥ 81
antyānāmanuvākānāṃ trayāṇāmanuvākavṛt
dvivṛdasyājarādhyāyau bṛhan niditi cāntavṛt 82
dvivṛdyajjāgrato'dhyāyodyubhiraktubhirantavṛt
viśvāni ca vibhaktāraṃ dvivṛdanye tu veṣṭanāḥ 83
sahasraśīrṣā mantreṣu caturviṃśatiṣu dvivṛt
na tasya trivṛdeṣo iyenadyaurdaśasu dvivṛt 84
trivṛdyubhirahobhistu dvivṛdvāyuḥ punāti ca
savitātrivṛdanyedvivṛtsyonā pṛthivī dvivṛt 85
ṛcam̐vvācantrivṛdyanme'ntārddhavarjja dviveṣṭanaḥ
ūtibhiḥ sahitaḥ kayā tvamādicaturṣu dvivṛt 86
ahāniśantrivṛddevīrabhiṣṭaya'iti dvivṛt
dyauḥ śāntirantaveṣṭyaḥ syāddṝte dṛrṭha0ha triveṣṭanaḥ 87
jyokte'ntavṛnnamaste'stuyatastaccakṣuśca dvivṛt
ādade devi devyaścetīndraikyaṃ praitviti dvivṛt 88
makhasyāśvasya navaikyaṃ yamāya tritayaikatā
dvivṛdgarbho'nuvākastu viśvāsāntyaṃ vinā trivṛt 89
ādade catuṣkaikaṃ yaste dvivṛtparo'ntavṛt
samudrāya taddvitryaikyaṃ yamāyeti dviveṣṭanaḥ 90
viśvā'āśāḥ pañcakaikyaṃ svāhā pūṣṇe'nuvākavṛt
abhimantu dvivṛdyāte catuṣkaikyamṛci dvivṛt 91
yāvatītyanuvākaikyantviṣaḥ sam̐vvṛgathāntavvat
svāhāprāṇānuvākānāmaṣṭānāmanuvākavat 92
āyāmāya yugaikatvaṃ yamāyetyantaveṣṭanaḥ 93
īśāvāsyamidaṃ sarvvaṃ dviveṣṭyaṃ sarvasammatam 94
iti kramakārikā śikṣā samāptā
