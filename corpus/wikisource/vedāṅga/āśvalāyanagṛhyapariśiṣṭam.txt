<15 nārāyaṇabaliḥ |>

atha nārāyaṇabalirarvāktasaṃskārācchuddhe kāle śuklaikādaśyāṃ snātaḥ śucau deśe viṣṇuṃ vaivasvataṃ pretaṃ ca yathāvadabhyarcya tadagre tilamiśrānmadhughṛtaplutāndaśa piṇḍānviṣṇurūpaṃ pretaṃ dhyāyankāśyapagotra devadattāyaṃ te piṇḍa iti dakṣiṇāgreṣu darbheṣu dakṣiṇāmukhaḥ prācīnavītī parācīnena pāṇinā dattvā piṇḍāngandhādibhirabhyarcya pravāha ṇāntaṃ kṛtvā nadyāṃ kṣipet | atha rātrāvayuganmābrāhmaṇānnimantrayopoṣitaḥ śvo madhyaṃ dine viṣṇumabhyarcya pretamuddiśya brāhmaṇānekoddiṣṭovidhinā bhojayitvā tṛpteṣu brāhma ṇasamīpe piṇḍavidhinā ninayanāntaṃ tūṣṇīṃ kṛtvā viṣṇave brahmaṇe śivāya saparivārāya yamāya viṣṇo'yaṃ te piṇḍa ityevaṃ caturaḥ piṇḍānbhuktaśeṣeṇa dakṣiṇasaṃsthāndattvā'tha pañcamaṃ kāśyapagotra devadatteti pretaṃ dhyātvā tadrūpāya viṣṇave viṣṇā'yaṃ ta iti dadyāt | athā''cāntāndakṣiṇayā saṃtoṣyaikaṃ teṣu guṇavantamaviśeṣato vastrā bharaṇādigohiraṇyaiḥ pretabaddhyā toṣayet | atha taiḥ pavitrapāṇibhiḥ pretāya kāśyapagotrāyāyaṃ te tilodakāñjaliriti tilodakaṃ dāpayitvā'nena nārāyaṇabalikarmaṇā bhagavānviṣṇurimaṃ devadattaṃ śuddhamapāpaṃ karmārhaṃ karotviti vācayitvā visṛjet | eṣa nārāyaṇabalikalpaḥ | evamanyāmapi svābhimatadevatāṃ yamadvitīyāmabhyarcya vidhimimaṃ kuryātto'pyenamapāpaṃ karoti na tu puṇyakṛto'pi balimenamakṛtvā pāralaukikaṃ kuryātkṛtaṃ nopatiṣṭhate'ntarikṣe vinaśyati tasmādyathāsaṃbhavamapi kuryādat || 15 ||

<16 nāgabaliḥ |>

atha nāgabaliḥ sarpahatānāṃ dārumayaṃ mṛnmayaṃ vā pañcaphaṇaṃ sarpaṃ kṛtvā bhādrapadasyānyasye vā māsasya śuklapañcamīmārabhya yāvatsaṃvatsaraṃ patimāsaṃ tasyāmupoṣito rātrau pañcāmṛtaiḥ snāpayitvā śucāvāsane śuciḥ surabhigandhapuṣpadhūpadīpairabhyarcya praṇamet | anumāsamekaikamanantaṃ vāsukiṃ śeṣaṃ padmaṃ kambalaṃ karkoṭakamaśvataraṃ dhṛtarāṣṭraṃ śaṅkhapālaṃ kālīyaṃ takṣakaṃ kapilamiti pāyasasarpiḥkṣīrāpūpairbalimupahṛtya jāgaritvā śvobhūte trivṛtānnena brāhmaṇānbhojayitvā pūrṇe saṃvatsare pañcamyāṃ ca snātvā sauvarṇaṃ sarpaṃ gāṃ ca brāhmaṇāya dattvā'nyāṃśca yatheṣṭaṃ bhojayitvā dakṣiṇayā toṣayitvā nāgānprītiṃ vācayed | eṣa nāgabaliḥ athobhayoḥ pakṣayoḥ pañvamīṣu saṃmṛṣṭāyāṃ bhuvi piṣṭena sarpamullikhya śuklasurabhigandhādibhirabhyarcya kṣīramodakānnivedyopasthāya muñca muñca devadattamiti prārthya saha bandhubhirmadhuramaśnīyādevaṃ saṃvatsarānte nārāyaṇabaliṃ cokta vatkṛtvā tata ūrdhvaṃ karma kuryāt || 16 ||
