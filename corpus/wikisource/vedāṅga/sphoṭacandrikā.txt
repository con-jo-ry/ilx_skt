tallakṣaṇameva tu na samañjasam| tathāhi, ramaṇīyārthapratipādakaśabdaḥ kāvyam| ramaṇīyatvaṃ ca lokottarālhādajanakajñānaviṣayatvamiti tallakṣaṇam| idañca `tattvamasi' ityādāvanativyāptam| na cālhāde vaijātyam| kintataḥ? lokottarālhādajanakajñānaviṣayatvasyobhayatra tulyatvāt brahmānandatvena sarvairvarṇitatvācca| kiñca ramaṇīyapadasya ramaṇīyārthapratipādakatvamasti na vā? nādyaḥ, ramaṇīyaśabdasyāpi ramaṇīyārthapratipādakaśabdatvena kāvyatvāpatteḥ| idaṃ ca bhāṣye "apaśabdārthakaḥ śabdo'paśabda iti cenna, apaśabdasyāpi apaśabdatvāpatteḥ" ityanena dhvanitam| nāntyaḥ, ramaṇīyārthapratipādakatvābhāve lakṣaṇatvānupapatteḥ| tasmātprakāśoktameva lakṣaṇaṃ samyak|
evaṃ `vanhinā siñcati'iti vākyasphoṭa eva| nanu vākyārthabodhe'yogyatāniścayasya pratibandhakatvaṃ yogyatājñānasya kāraṇatvaṃ tārkikādisiddhāntasiddhaṃ, prakṛte ca vanhiḥ sekakaraṇaṃ neti viṣayabādharūpā'yogyatāniścayarūpabādhakasattvādviṣayābādharūpayogyatājñānāsattvātkathametasya vākyasphoṭatvamiti cet? satyam, tātparyaviṣayābādho hi yogyatā| tadabhāvo'yogyatā| prakṛte cātyuṣṇajalena siñcatīti tātparyaviṣayaḥ| yathā-
asya kṣoṇipateḥ parārddhaparayā lakṣīkṛtāḥ saṃkhyayā prajñācakṣuravekṣamāṇabadhiraprakhya| kilākīrtayaḥ|
gīyante svaramaṣṭabhaṃ kalayatā jātena vandhyodarāt mūkānāṃ prakareṇa varmaramaṇīdugdhodadhe rodhasi||
gaganaṃ gaganākāraṃ sāgarāḥ sāgaropamāḥ|
rāmarāvaṇayoryuddhaṃ rāmarāvaṇayoriva|
kailāsasya prathamaśikhare veṇusaṃmūrcchanābhiḥ śrutvā kīrtiṃ vibudharamaṇīgīyamānāṃ yadīyām|
srastāpāṅgāsarasabisinīkāṇḍasañjātaśaṅkā diṅ‌mātaṅgāḥ śravaṇapuline hastamāvartayanti||
paśya nīlotpaladvandvānniḥ saranti śitāḥ śarāḥ|
ityādyālaṅkārikavākyeṣu `gaṅgāyāṃ ghoṣaḥ' ityādau `tattvamasi' ityādau ca tātparyaviṣayābādho yogyatvamiti sarvasiddhāntaḥ, tathā'trāpi| yadvā, śābdabodhe'yogyatāniścayasya na pratibandhakatvaṃ tatsattve'pi `tvaṃ bṛhaspatiḥ' iti stāvakavākyātsantoṣopalabdheḥ `nāhaṃ raṇḍāputraḥ' iti bādhaniścaye satyapi `tvaṃ raṇḍāputraḥ' iti vākyāt krodhopalabdheśca; śābdabodhābhāve etadanupapatteḥ| kiñca `paśya nīlotpaladvandvāt' ityādisādhyavasānādilakṣaṇāsthale śarā nīlotpaladvandvopādānakā netyayogyatāniścaye sati tadvākyaśravaṇānantaraṃ vākyīyabodottaramayogyatāniścayaḥ pūrvaṃ vā? nādyaḥ, bādhaniścaye sati bodhābhāvāt| nāntyaḥ, bodhābhāve'nvayānupapatterabhāvāt| na hi svarūpasatī anvayānupapattirlakṣaṇābījam, kintu jñātā| na ca pūrvamayogyatāniścayasattvena tajjñānamapyastyeveti vācyam, vākyārthabodhajanyānvayānupapattijñānasya pūrvamabhāvāt| gaṅgā goṣādhikaraṇaṃ neti naukikajñānasattve `gaṅgāyaṃ dhoṣaḥ' iti vākyādgaṅgādhikaraṇako dhoṣa iti bodhottaramanvayānupapattirhi anubhavasiddhā| na ca tasyāḥ pūrvaṃ jñānaṃ sambhavati| naca bhramātmako bodha iti tārkikoktaṃ yuktim, ayogyatāniścayasya pratibandhakatvaṃ yogyatājñānasya kāraṇatvaṃ prakalpanīyam, bhramātmako bodhaśca kalpanīya iti mahadgauravam| sphoṭavādināṃ tritayamapi na kalpyamiti lāghavam; vākyārthabodhajanyapravṛttāveva tatsvīkārāt| kiñca `nāhaṃ parameśvaraḥ' iti bādhaniścaye sati `tattvamasi' iti vākyādbodhābhāve bhramātmakabodhe vā na pramātmakaṃ jñānaṃ sambhavati, bhramātmakabodhasya pramātmakabodhajananāyogyatvāt| evaṃ `daśamastvamasi' ityatrāpi|
nanu anekavarṇātirikto'nekadhvanivyaṅgyo'khaṇḍavākyasphoṭaḥ kaḥ padārtha iti cet? śṛṇu| yathā tārkikaiḥ pūrvoktamadrītyā gatyantarasambhave'pi citrarūpamatiriktaṃ svīkriyate, yathā śābdikaiḥ rephadvayājbhāgaviśiṣṭa eko varṇaḥ "ṛti ṛ vā lṛti lṛvā" ityatra vārttike svīkriyate, yathā vā "siddhametat sasthānatvādaicoścottarabhūyastvāt" itivārttike'nekācāmekavarṇātmakatvamuktaṃ tathā mayāpi anekadhvanivyaṅgyaḥ vākyātmaka eko varṇa iti svīkriyate| sa ca śabdabrahmarūpaḥ| tatra matatrayam-
śabdabrahmeti śabdārthaḥ śabdamityapare jaguḥ|
caitanyesarvabhūtānāṃ śabdabrahmeti me matiḥ|| iti|
śabdabrahmetinirvacanenāpi tasya varmatvaṃ siddham| anumānādapi| tathāhi, akhaṇḍavākyasphoṭaḥ ekavarmarūpaḥ, śrāvaṇatvāt dhvanivyaṅgyatvādvā, yannaivaṃ tannaivaṃ, yathā pṛthivyādi| anvayadṛṣṭāntastu matāntarasiddhavarṇādi|
vastutastu anvayabyatirekyanumānaṃ vyartham, upanītabhānenaiva gatārthatvāt| tathāhi, yathā candanakhaṇḍasya laukikapratyakṣaṃ saurabhyasyālaukikaṃ, tathā dhūmasya laukikaṃ vanheralaukikamiti| asminpakṣe upanītabhānamiti vivekaḥ| yadvā lāghavāddoṣābhāvād dhvanivyaṅgyasyānyasyābhāvācca varṇatvasvīkāraḥ| na ca dhvanivyaṅgyasya guṇībhūtavyaṅgyaprabhede kākvākṣiptasyāpi anyasya sambhava iti vācyam, tasya dhvanivikārakākuvyaṅgyatve'pi dhvanivyaṅgyatvābhāvāt|
kiñca "kāmaḥ saṅkalpo vicikitsāśraddhā'śraddhā bhīḥ hrīḥ ityetatsarvaṃ mana eva" iti śrutyā kāmādīnāṃ manodharmatve pratipādite'pi tārkikairātmadharmatvamuktam| tacca sarvairapyaṅgīkṛtam| tattu bhramātmakam| tadbījaṃ tu yathā jāpakusumasannidhāne sphaṭiko lohita iti jñānam| prakṛte tu manaḥsānnidhyam ātmadharme bījaṃ śrutibādhaśca| sphoṭe tu na tathā| tasmādekavarṇātmako'khaṇḍavākyasphoṭo vācaka iti siddham||
