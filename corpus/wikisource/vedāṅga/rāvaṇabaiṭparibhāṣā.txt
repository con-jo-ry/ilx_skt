rāvaṇabaiṭh
padādi nihatā kāraḥ padānto naḥ padāntataḥ
pragṛhaśca padādyātsyādiṃgyaṃ śliṣṭāvasānakam 1
sāṃhitaṣṣoṇaśca sāṃhitassandhāvaidauccorddhvapadādigaḥ
padāntondaḥ padāntādīdūdvisargaśca ṣoḍaśa 2
kramātsyuṣṣoḍaśa sveṣu gaṇasaṃjñāḥ pṛthakpṛthak
dvābhyāndvābhyāṃ gaṇāstvādau bhaveyuśca trayaḥ kramāt 3
tatasturīya ekena dvābhyāṃ pañcama ucyate
tatastribhirgaṇaṣṣaṣṭhassaptamaśca tribhistathā 4
bhavedaṣṭama ekena bbhyevamaṣṭagaṇā smṛtāḥ
saptānāmādau viṣamasamasaṃkhyāprasūcakam 5
aṣṭamasya gaṇasyeha syātsaṃkhyā pratipādakam
pratipañcāśadekaikamakṣaraṃ procyate kramāt 6
caturbhiḥ pañcabhiṣṣaṭbhistribhissaptabhireva vā
aṣṭabhirnavabhirvātra daśabhirvākṣaraiḥ kvacit 7
kriyate caivamekaikaṃ vākyaṃ baiṇṇāmalakṣaṇam
hānvesminrādau laḥ kaḥ pṭassaṃkhyeyā āditaḥ kramāt 8
rādisādipṛthakpañca tatra saṃkhyā bhavetpunaḥ
anṛvarṇaplutalṛkārālakṣyā aṣṭasvarāḥ kramāt 9
dajau bamau nakaḍagāḥ yarau vaikādaśo halaḥ
anye tu rasadādibhyo na lakṣyā ajjhalastviha 10
alakṣyavarṇā yatra syustānatra parivarjayet
ādāvījāvabāpūmāvenāvai kāciḍāvugau 11
tattat gaṇaṃ tu saṃhatya vigaṇairyaikarūpataḥ
tatsamāsamasaṃkhyāsā dṛṣṭavyā tatra tatra ca 12
yadyādījau viṣamo jñeya ādāvādigaṇassamaḥ
ūmau dvitīyo viṣama abau cettu samasmṛtaḥ 13
sama enau tṛtīya syāt yadyaikau viṣamo bhavet
etat gaṇatrayañcātra ugau cedviṣamaṃ bhavet 14
uḍau yadi tadevātra samaṃ syāditi niścitaḥ
ekalakṣyagaṇastveṣu samaṃ vā viṣamaṃ yadā 15
gaṇadvayaṃ tadanyattu bhavettadviparītakam
ādādīnāṃ dvikāṣṭānāṃ varṇau pūrvottarau kramāt 16
turīyasya gaṇasyātra samāsamavisūcakau
dvikāṣṭakeroyovā cet pañcamasya gaṇasya tu 17
vaiṣamyaṃ tatra saṃlakṣyaṃ narayau cetsamaṃ tathā
vaścet ṣaṣṭhagaṇasyātra vaiṣamyaṃ vona cetsamaḥ 18
saptamasya gaṇasyeha samaṃ syādrādipañcake
suvijñeyassaptamasya viṣamassādipañcake 19
visargānnaṣṭamagaṇā pañcakān pañcakāniha
nirasya tu krimaṇāntyagaṇanāyāṃ rasādibhiḥ 20
ekadvitricatuḥpañcetyevaṃ saṃkhyāṃ vinirṇayet
rādayondaparāścetsyurvisargābhāvasūcakāḥ 21
akhaṇḍamārgapadantvatra na lakṣyaṃ bhavatīha tu
atretyantopasargā syuḥ paretyetyādayastathā 22
na te padāntalakṣyeṣu gaṇanīyā syuratra hi
na dīrghagaṇaneṣvatra saṃkhyeyaḥpragrahastathā 23
lakṣyābhāvo yatra yatra tatra tatra samasmṛtaḥ
yajuṣi pratipañcāśadevamevonnayetsadā 24
iti rāvaṇabaiṭparibhāṣā samāptā
