pañcasaṃvatsaramayaṃ yugādhyakṣaṃ prajāpatim |
dinartvayanamāsāṅgaṃ praṇamya śirasā śuciḥ ||1||
jyotiṣāmayanaṃ puṇyaṃ pravakṣyāmyanupūrvaśaḥ |
viprāṇāṃ sammataṃ loke yajñakālārtha siddhaye ||2||
veda hi yajñārthamabhipravṛttāḥ kālānupūrvyā vihitāśca yajñāḥ |
tasmādidaṃ kālavidhānaśāstraṃ yo jyotiṣaṃ veda sa veda yajñān ||3||
yathā śikhā mayūrāṇāṃ nāgānāṃ maṇayo yathā |
tadvadvedāṅgaśāstrāṇāṃ jyautiṣaṃ mūrdhāni sthitam ||4||
ye bṛhaspatinā bhuktā mīnātprabhṛti rāśayaḥ |
te hṛtāḥ pañcabhirbhūtā yaḥ śeṣaḥ sa parigrahaḥ ||0||
māghaśuklaprapannasya pauṣakṛṣṇasamāpinaḥ |
yugasya pañcavarṣasya kālajñānaṃ pracakṣate ||5||
svarākramete somārkau yadā sākaṃ savāsavau |
syāttadādiyugaṃ māghastapaḥ śuklo'yanaṃ hyudak ||6||
prapadyate śraviṣṭhādau sūryācandramasāvudak |
sārpārdhe dakṣiṇārkastu māghaśrāvaṇayoḥ sadā ||7||
dharmavṛddhirapāṃ prasthaḥ kṣapāhrāsa udaggatau |
dakṣiṇe tau viparyāsaḥ ṣaṇmuhūrtyayanena tu ||8||
prathamaṃ saptamaṃ cāhurayanādyaṃ trayodaśam |
caturthaṃ daśamaṃ caiva dviryugmaṃ bahulepyṛtau ||9||
vasustvaṣṭā bhavo'jaśca mitraḥ sarpo'śvinau jalam |
dhātā kaścāyanādyāḥ syurardhapañcamabhastvṛtuḥ ||10||
ekāntare'hni māse ca pūrvān kṛtvādimuttaraḥ |
ardhayoḥ pañcavarṣāṇāmṛdu pañcadaśāṣṭamau ||11||
dyuheyaṃ parva cetpāde pādastriṃśattu saikikā |
bhāgātmanāpavṛjyāṃśān nirdiśedadhiko yadi ||12||
nirekaṃ dvādaśābhyastaṃ dviguṇaṃ gatasañjñikam |
ṣaṣṭyā ṣaṣṭyā yutaṃ dvābhyāṃ parvaṇāṃ rāśirucyate ||13||
syuḥ pādo'rdhantripādyāyā tridvayeka'hnaḥ kṛtasthitim |
sāmyendostṛṇo'nye tu parvakāḥ pañca sammitāḥ ||14||
bhāṃśāḥ syuraṣṭakāḥ kāryāḥ pakṣadvādaśakodgatāḥ |
ekādaśaguṇaśconaḥ śukle'rdhaṃ caindavā yadi ||15||
navakairudgatoṃśaḥ syādūnaḥ saptaguṇo bhavet |
āvāpastvayuje'rdhaṃ syātpaulasye|āstaṅgate'param ||16||
jāvādyaṃśaiḥ samaṃ vidyāt pūrvārdhe parva sūttare |
bhādānaṃ syāccaturdaśyāṃ kāṣṭhānāṃ devinā kalāḥ ||17||
jau drā gaḥ khe śve hī ro ṣā ścinmūṣakṇyaḥ sūmādhāṇaḥ |
re mṛ ghāḥ svāpojaḥ kṛṣyo ha jyeṣṭhā ityṛkṣā liṅgaiḥ ||18||
kāryā bhāṃśāṣṭakāsthāne kalā ekānnaviṃśatiḥ |
unasthāne trisaptati mudvavapedūnasambhave ||19||
tithimekādaśābhyastāṃ parvabhāṃśasamanvitām |
vibhajya bhasamūhena tithinakṣatramādiśet ||20||
yāḥ parvābhādānakalāstāsu saptaguṇāṃ tithim |
yuktyā tāsāṃ vijānīyāt tithibhādānikāḥ kalāḥ ||21||
atītaparvabhāgebhyaḥ śodhayed dviguṇāṃ tithim |
teṣu maṇḍalabhāgeṣu tithiniṣṭhāṅgato raviḥ ||22||
viṣuvantaṃ dvirabhyastaṃ rūponaṃ ṣaḍguṇīkṛtam |
pakṣā yadardhaṃ pakṣāṇāṃ tithiḥ sa viṣuvān smṛtaḥ ||23||
palāni pañcāśadapāṃ dhṛtāni tadāḍhakaṃ droṇamataḥ prameyam |
tribhirvihīnaṃ kuḍvaistu kāryaṃ tannāḍikāyāstu bhavet pramāṇam ||24||
ekādaśabhirabhyasya parvāṇi navabhistithim |
yugalabdhaṃ saparva syād vartamānārkabhaṃ kramāt ||25||
sūryarkṣabhāgān navabhirvibhajya śeṣān dvirabhyasya dinopabhuktiḥ |
titheryutā bhuktidineṣu kālo yogo dinaikādaśaken tadbham ||26||
tryaṃśo bhaśeṣo divasāṃśabhāgaścaturdaśasyāpyapanīya bhinnam |
bhārdhe'dhike cādhigate paroṃ'śodvāvuttame tannavakairavetya ||27||
triṃśatyahnāṃ saṣaṭṣaṣṭirabdaḥ ṣaṭ cartavo'yane |
māsā dvādaśa sauryāḥ syuretat pañcaguṇaṃ yugam ||28||
udayāvāsavasya syurdinarāśi sapañcakaḥ |
ṛṣerdviṣaṣṭihīnaḥ syād viṃśatyā caikayāstṛṇām ||29||
pañcatriṃśaṃ śataṃ pauṣṇam ekonamayanonyṛṣeḥ |
parvaṇāṃ syāccatuṣpādī kāṣṭhānāṃ caiva tāḥ kalāḥ ||30||
sāvanendustṛmāsānāṃ ṣaṣṭiḥ saikadvisaptikā |
dyustriṃśat sāvanaḥ sārdhaḥ saurastṛṇāṃ sa paryayaḥ ||31||
agniḥ prajāpatiḥ somo rudroditibṛhaspatī |
sarpāśca pitaraścaiva bhagaścaivāryamāpi ca ||32||
savitā tvaṣṭātha vāyuścendrāgnī mitra eva ca |
indro niṝtirāpo vai viśvedevāstathaiva ca ||33||
viṣṇurvasavo varuṇū'jekapāt tathaiva ca |
ahirbudhnyastathā pūṣā aśvinau yama eva ca ||34||
nakṣatradevatā etā etābhiryajñakarmaṇi |
yajamānasya śāstrajñairnāma nakṣatrajaṃ smṛtam ||35|||
ugrāṇyārdrā ca citrā ca viśākhā śravaṇośvayuk |
krūraṇi tu maghāsvātī jyeṣṭā mūlaṃ yamasya ca ||36||
dyūnaṃ dviṣaṣṭibhāgena jñe (he) yaṃ sauraṃ sapārvaṇam |

navamāni tu pañcāhnaḥ kāṣṭhā pañcākṣarā bhavet ||39||
yaduttarasyāyanato gataṃ syāc cheṣaṃ tathā dakṣiṇato'yanasya |
tadekaṣaṣṭyādviguṇaṃ vibhaktaṃ sadvādaśaṃ syād divasapramāṇam ||40||
yadardhaṃ dinabhāgānāṃ sadā parvaṇi parvaṇi |
ṝtuśeṣaṃ tu tad vidyāt saṅkhyāya saha sarvaṇām ||41||

ityetanmāsavarṣāṇāṃ muhūrtodayaparvaṇām |
