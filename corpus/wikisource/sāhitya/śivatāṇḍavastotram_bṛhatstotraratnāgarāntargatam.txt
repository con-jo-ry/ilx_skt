Text:

॥ rāvaṇakṛtaśivatāṇḍavastotram ॥
jaṭākaṭāhasambhramabhramannillimpanirjharīvilolavīcivallarīvirājamānamūrdhani ।
dhagaddhagaddhagajjvalallalāṭapaṭṭapāvake kiśoracandraśekhare ratiḥ pratikṣaṇaṃ mama ॥
dharādharendranandinīvilāsabandhubandhurasphuradigantasantatipramodamānamānase ।
kṛpākaṭākṣadhoraṇīniruddhadurdharāpadi kaciccidambare mano vinodametu vastuni ॥
jaṭābhujaṅgapiṅgalasphuratphaṇāmaṇiprabhākadambakuṅkumadravapraliptadigvadhūmukhe ।
madāndhasindhurasphurattvaguttarīyamedure mano vinodamadbhutaṃ bibhartuṃ bhūtabhartari ॥
sahasralocanaprabhṛtyaśeṣalekhaśekharaprasūnadhūlidhoraṇīvidhūsarāṅghripīṭhabhūḥ ।
bhujaṅgarājamālayā nibaddhajāṭajūṭakaḥ śriyai cirāya jāyatāṃ cakorabandhuśekharaḥ ॥ 4
lalāṭacatvarajvaladdhanañjayasphuliṅgabhānipītapañcasrāyakaṃ namannilimpanāyakam ।
sudhāmayūkhalekhayā virājamānaśekharaṃ mahākapālisampade śiro jaṭālamastu naḥ॥ 5
karālabhālapaṭṭikādhagaddhagaddhagajjvaladdhanañjayādharīkṛtapracaṇḍapañcasāyake ।
dharādharendranandinīkucāgracitrapatrakaprakalpanaikaśilpini trilocane matirmama ॥ 6
navīnameghamaṇḍalīniruddhadurdharasphuratkuhūniśīthinītamaḥprabandhabandhukandharaḥ।
nilimpanirjharīdharastanotu kṛtisindhuraḥ kalānidhānabandhuraḥ śriyaṃ jagadhdurandharaḥ ॥7
praphullanīlapaṅkajaprapañcakālimacchaṭāviḍambikaṇṭhakandharāruciprabandha kandharam ।
smaracchidaṃ puracchidaṃ bhavacchidaṃ makhacchidaṃ gajacchidāndhakacchidaṃ tamantakacchidaṃ bhaje ॥
agarvasarvamaṅgalākalākadambamañjarīrasapravāhamādhurīvijṛmbhaṇāmadhuvratam ।
smarāntakaṃ purāntakaṃ bhavāntakaṃ makhāntakaṃ gajāntakāndhakāntakaṃ tamantakāntakaṃ bhaje ॥
jayatvadhabhravibhramabhramadbhujaṅgamasphuraddhagaddhagadvinirgamatkarālaphālahavyavāṭ ।
dhimiddhimiddhimidhvananmṛdaṅgatuṅgamaṅgaladhvanikramapravartitapracaṇḍatāṇḍavaḥ śivaḥ ॥
dṛṣadvicitratalpayorbhujaṅgamauktikasrajorgariṣṭharatnaloṣṭhayoḥ suhṛdvipakṣapakṣayoḥ।
tṛṇāravindacakṣuṣoḥ prajāmahīmahendrayoḥ samaṃ pravartayanmanaḥ kadā sadāśivaṃ bhaje ॥
kadā nilimpanirjharīnikuñjakoṭare vasanvimuktadurmatiḥ sadā śiraḥsthamañjaliṃ vahan ।
rāvaṇakṛtaśivatāṇḍavastotram
vimuktalolalocanālalāmaphālalagnakaḥ śiveti mantramuccarankadā sukhī bhavāmyaham ॥ 12
imaṃ hi nityamevamuktamuttamottamaṃ stavaṃ paṭhansmaranbruvannaro viśuddhimeti santatam ।
hare gurau sa bhaktimāśu yāti nānyathā gatiṃ vimohanaṃ hi dehināṃ tu śaṅkarasya cintanam ॥ 13
pūjāvasānasamaye daśavakragītaṃ yaḥ śambhupūjanamidaṃ paṭhati pradoṣe ।
tasya sthirāṃ rathagajendraturaṅgayuktāṃ lakṣmīṃ sadaiva sumukhīṃ pradadāti śambhuḥ ॥ 14
॥ iti śrīrāvaṇaviracitaṃ śivatāṇḍavastotraṃ sampūrṇam ॥
