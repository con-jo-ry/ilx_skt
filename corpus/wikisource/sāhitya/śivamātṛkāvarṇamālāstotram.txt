॥ śivamātṛkāvarṇamālāstotram ॥

śrīsūta uvācaḥ 
athātassampravakṣyāmi śivasthānaṃ samāsataḥ । yatra sañcintya deveśaṃ kaivalyaṃ labhate naraḥ ॥ 1 
purā nārāyaṇaḥ śrīmān kirīṭī garuḍadhvajaḥ । śrīmatkailāsaparyante tatāpa paramaṃ tapaḥ ॥ 2 
bhasmoddhūlitasarvāṅga stripuṇḍrāṅkitamastakaḥ । rudrākṣamālābharaṇo jaṭāvalkalamaṇḍitaḥ ॥ 3 
tapasā taptadehasya keśavasya mahātmanaḥ । prādurāsīnmahādevaśśaṅkaraḥ karuṇākaraḥ ॥ 4 
taṃ dṛṣṭvā puṇḍarīkākṣo mahādevaṃ ghṛṇānidhim । ambikāsahitaṃ rudraṃ candramauliṃ sanātanam ॥ 5 
praṇamya daṇḍavadbhūmau bhaktyā paramayā saha । stotumārabhate viṣṇu ssarvabhūtahitāvaham ॥ 6 
akārāya namassākṣādanantānantamūrtaye ॥ ātmabhūtāya sarveṣāṃ matiśuddhāya śūline ॥ 7 
ākārāyātiśuddhāya sākṣiṇe sarvavastunaḥ । ambikāpataye tubhyamasaṅgāya namonamaḥ ॥ 8 
ikārāyeśvarākhyāya sarvasiddhikarāya ca । indrādilokapālānā miyattākāriṇe namaḥ ॥ 9 
īkārāya variṣṭhāya vānchitārthapradāya ca । vañcakānāmalabhyāya vasudāya namonamaḥ ॥ 10 
ukārāyograjantūnāmugrarūpāya śūline । uttamānāṃ tu jantūnāmupāsyāya namonamaḥ ॥ 11 
ūkārāyopavītāya hyūrjitāyottamātmane । uttama jñānagamyāya namaste paramātmane ॥ 12 
ṛkārāyādibhūtāyaḥ rāmapūrvārcitāyaca । ṛcāmarthasvarūpāya namassatyaparātmane ॥ 13 
ṝkārāya nisargāya nityatṛptāya śambhave । rasādibhūtarūpāya namaśśuddhacidātmane ॥ 14 
lṛkārāya lasaddaṇḍamaṇḍitābharaṇāya ca । liṅgaliṅgyādihīnāya liṅgarūpāya te namaḥ ॥ 15 
lṛkārāya layasthāya dhvaṃsakāyādihetave । lakṣāruṇaśarīrāya lābhasthānāya vai namaḥ ॥ 16 
ekārāya namaśśaśva didantāsākṣiṇe tathā । ahantāsākṣiṇe sākṣātpratyagadvayavastune ॥ 17 
aikārāyāmalajñānaprabhāvāpariśīlinām । ātmarūpatayā nityaṃ pratītāya namo namaḥ ॥ 18 
oṅkārāya viriñcāya viṣṇave rudramūrtaye । vācyavācakahīnāya svayaṃ bhāsāya te namaḥ ॥ 19 
aukārāya maheśāya mahāmantrārtharūpiṇe । mahādevāya mātrādi prapañcāya namonamaḥ ॥ 20 
bindurūpāya bījāya bījādhiṣṭhānarūpiṇe । bījanāśakarajñāna svarūpāya namonamaḥ ॥ 21 
visarjanīya rūpāya vismayāya mahātmane । visarjanīyaniṣṭhānāṃ viśeṣārthāya vai namaḥ ॥ 22 
kakārāya kapūrvādi devatāpūjitāyaca । kāraṇagrāmasaṃharttre kālātītāya vai namaḥ ॥ 23 
khakārāya khapūrvādi bhūtapañcakahetave । khamūrtāya khalaprajñā gocarāya namonamaḥ ॥ 24 
gakārāya gaṇeśāya gaṇabṛndārcitāyaca । gaṅgādharāya guhyāya guṇātītāya te namaḥ ॥ 25 
ghakārāya ghanākāra ghātakāya ghanātmane । ghaṭādi jagadākāra rahitāya namonamaḥ ॥ 26 
ṅkārāya ṅmantrārtha svarūpāya śivātmane । ṅā ṅī ṅū saṃjñitārthānā magamyāya namonamaḥ ॥ 27 
cakārāyaca mantrārtha svarūpāyāmitātmane । camantrārthaniṣaṇṇānāmamṛtāya namonamaḥ ॥ 28 
chakārāya chalālasya chādanādiviśeṣataḥ । chādanacchandanacchanna vibhāgāya namonamaḥ ॥ 29 
jakārāya jagacchakti svarūpāya jayārthinām । jayapradāya devāya jambhamohāya te namaḥ ॥ 30 
jhakārāya jhamantrārtha svarūpāyāmṛtātmane । jhamadhya candrabimbāya namassāmbāya śambhave ॥ 31 
ñakārāya ñamantrārtha svarūpāya ñamūrtaye । jñaptimātraikaniṣṭhānāṃ muktidāya namonamaḥ ॥ 32 
ṭakārāya ṭa ṭā ṭī ṭū pūrvoktaistu mahāravaiḥ । arcitāya suraśreṣṭhai rasuraiścaḥ namonamaḥ ॥ 33 
ṭhakārāya ṭhamantrārtha svarūpāya khamantrataḥ । ṭhaṭhādigaṇapūjyāya ṭhamadhyāya namonamaḥ ॥ 34 
ḍakārāya ḍa ḍā ḍī ḍū ḍe ḍo ḍaiśca mahāravaiḥ । ḍāmarai rabhipūjyāya namonṛttapriyāyaca ॥ 35 
ḍhakārāya ḍhamantrārtha parijñānavatāṃ nṛṇām । ḍhasaṃjñita mahānandapradāya satataṃ namaḥ ॥ 36 
ṇakārāya ṇa ṇā ṇī ṇū ṇe ṇai ṇo ṇau ravaissadā । ṇākinī gaṇapūjyāya ṇasaṃjñāya namonamaḥ ॥ 37 
takārāya taśabdārtha svarūpāya tatāyaca । tattvamityabhipūjyāya tatvabhūtāyavai namaḥ ॥ 38 
thakārāya thamantrārtha svarūpāyādha saṃjñitaiḥ ।mahāgaṇaiśca pūjyāya thamadhyāya namonamaḥ ॥ 39 
dakārāya dayārūpa mahāśaktimayāya ca । deśajāti vihīnāya divārātrāyavai namaḥ ॥ 40 
dhakārāya dharaṇyādi mahābhūtasvarūpiṇe । dharādharahṛdisthāya dhamadhyāya namonamaḥ ॥ 41 
nakārāya nagendrāya nāmajātyādihetave । namaśiśavāya namyāya nānārūpāya śūline ॥ 42 
pakārāya parānandasvarūpāya parātmane । parāparavihīnāya pāvanāya namonamaḥ ॥ 43 
phakārāya phalākhyāya phalākhyagaṇāya namaḥ । phaḍākhyagaṇapūjyāya namaḥ pūrṇasvarūpiṇe ॥ 44 
bakārāya bakārākhya mahābījaikajāpinām । bandhanākāranāśaikahetave vedhase namaḥ ॥ 45 
bhakārāya bhavābdhestu tārakāya bhavāyate । bhavaśabdaikavedyāya bhavānīpataye namaḥ ॥ 46 
makārāya mahāmāyā pāśanāśaikahetave । mamakāravihīnānāṃ mahānandāya vai namaḥ ॥ 47 
yakārāya yathārthāya yathārthajñānināṃ nṛṇām । yathārtha pratyagadvaitabrahmaṇe satataṃ namaḥ ॥ 48 
rakārāya ratiprīti priyāya ratihetave । raśabdavapuṣe rogabhavanāśāyavai namaḥ । 49 
lakārāya latāsoma svarūpāya latātmane । lābhālābhavihīnāya labdhirūpāyave namaḥ ॥ 50 
vakārāya variṣṭhāya vāsudevādihetave । vāñchāvāguruvicchittihetubhūtāya vai namaḥ ॥ 51 
śakārāya śaraṇyāya śambhave śaraṇārthinām । śaraṇyāya śaraccandradhavalāya namonamaḥ ॥ 52 
ṣakārāya ṣaḍādhāra ṣaṭcakrādisvarūpiṇe । ṣaḍakṣara niṣaṇṇāya namaṣṣaṇmukhahetave ॥ 53 
sakārāya saśabdārtha kharūpāya sadātmane । sākṣiṇe sākṣyarūpāṇāṃ namassādhūpakāriṇe ॥ 54 
hakārāyāhamarthāya sadāhaṅkārarūpiṇe । hāhāhūhū pragītāya haṃsarūpāya te namaḥ ॥ 55 
lakārāya lakārākhya mahāmantrapriyāyaca । ḍolācañcalasaṃsāra nāśanāya namo namaḥ ॥ 56 
kṣakārāya kṣamantrārthasvarūpāya kṣamāvatām । kṣemadāya mama kṣema svarūpāya namo namaḥ ॥ 57 
mātṛkāvapuṣe mātṛmānameyādisākṣiṇe mātṛkāmantralabhyāya mahaseca namo namaḥ ॥ 58 
mātṛkādhārabhūtāya mātṛkāmūlarūpiṇe । mahāmantraikavācyāya mahase brahmaṇe namaḥ ॥ 59 
dvidhābhūta mahāmantra svaravyañjanavyaṅgyataḥ । vācyāyāpadarūpāya padāya mahase namaḥ ॥ 60 
aṣṭadhācāṣṭavargaistu vibhaktāyāmalātmane । aśeṣaśabdabhūtāya tattadarthāya vai namaḥ ॥ 61 
śabdānvayavihīnāya śabdalakṣyāya sākṣiṇe । sarvopādhivihīnāya svayambhānāya vai namaḥ ॥ 62 
pratyakṣādipramāṇānāmagamyāya parokṣataḥ । pratītāya svayaṃ sarvasākṣiṇe mahase namaḥ ॥ 63 
svasvarūpādahīnāya vastuto vastutopi ca । arthāya mahate sākṣātsvayambhānāya vai namaḥ ॥ 64 
prasādalabhyāya śivāya satyacinmahāsukhāya paravarjitāya । 
atīva śuddhasya hṛdambujālaye vibhāsamānāya namo namaste ॥ 65 
śrīsūta uvācaḥ
evaṃ nārāyaṇenaiva śaṅkarobhiṣṭutaḥ punaḥ । prāha gambhīrayā vācā kimarthaṃ taptavāṃstapaḥ ॥ 66 
ityākarṇya mahāviṣṇuḥ praṇamya parameśvarama । tava sthānāni me deva brūhi sākṣātkṛpākara ॥ 67 
ityukto viṣṇunā śambhussarvajñaḥ karuṇākaraḥ । prāha sarvāmareśānassthānāni svasya viṣṇave ॥ 68 
yaḥ paṭhetprātarutthāya stotrametanmanoharam । ihāmutra phalaṃ tasya karasthaṃ nātra saṃśayaḥ ॥ 69
iti śrīskānde mahāpurāṇe viṣṇukṛtaśivamātṛkāvarṇamālāstotram ॥
