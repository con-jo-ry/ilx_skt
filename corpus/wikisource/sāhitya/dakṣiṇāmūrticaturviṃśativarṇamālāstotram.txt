॥ śrīdakṣiṇāmūrticaturviṃśativarṇamālāstotram ।।
omityetadasya budhairnāma gṛhītaṃ yadbhāstredaṃ bhāti samastaṃ viyadādi ।
yasyājñātaḥ svasvapadasthā vidhimukhyāstaṃ pratyañcaṃ dakṣiṇavaktraṃ kalayāmi ॥ 1
namrāṅgānāṃ bhaktimatāṃ yaḥ puruṣārthaṃ datte kṣipraṃ hanti ca tatsarvavipattīḥ।
pādāmbhojāstanitāpasmṛtimīśaṃ taṃ... ॥ 2
mohadhvaṃtyai vaiṇikavaiyāsikamukhyātsaṃvinmudrāpukhakavīṇākṣaguṇānyam ।
hastāmbhojairbibhratamārādhitavaṃtastaṃ... ॥ 3
bhadrārūḍhaṃ bhadradamārādhayitṝṇāṃ bhaktiśraddhāpūrvakamīśaṃ praṇamanti ।
ādityā yaṃ vāñcitasidhyai karuṇābdhitaṃ... ॥ 4
garbhāntasthāḥ prāṇina ete bhavapāśacchede dakṣaṃ niścittavantaḥ śaraṇaṃ yam ।
ārādhyāṅghrim prasphuradambhoruhayugmaṃ taṃ ...॥ 5
vaktraṃ dhanyāssaṃsṛtivārdheratimātrādbhītāssantaḥ pūrṇaśaśāṅkadyuti yasya ।
sevante'dhyāsīnamanantaṃ vaṭamūlaṃ taṃ ... ॥ 6
tejastomairaṅgadasaṅghaṭṭitabhāsvanmāṇikyotthairbhāsitaviśvo rucirairyaḥ।
tejomūrtiṃ khānilatejapramukhābdhitaṃ... ॥ 7
dadhyājyādidravyakakarmāṇyakhilāni tyaktvā kāṅkṣāṃ karmaphaleṣvatra karoti ।
yajjijñāsārūpaphalārthī kṣitidevastaṃ... ॥ 8
kṣipraṃ loke yaṃ bhajamānaḥ pṛthupuṇyaḥ pradhvastādhiḥ projjhitasaṃsṛtyakhilārtiḥ।
pratyagbhūtaṃ brahma paraṃ satramate yastaṃ... ॥ 9
ṇānetyevaṃ yanmanumadhyasthitavarṇaṃ bhaktāḥ kāle varṇagṛhīteḥ prajapantaḥ ।
modaṃte samprāptasamastaśrutitantrāstaṃ... ॥ 10
mūrticchāyānirjitamandākinikundaprāleyāmbhorāśisudhābhūtisurebhā ।
yasyābhrāmā hāsavidhau dakṣaśirodhistaṃ ... ॥11
taptavarṇacchāyajaṭājūṭakaṭāha prodyadvīcīvallivirājansurasindhum ।
nityaṃ sūkṣmaṃ nityanirastākhiladoṣaṃ taṃ... ... ॥12
yena jñātenaiva samastaṃ viditaṃ syādyasmādanyadvastu jagatyāṃ śaśaśṛṅgam ।
yaṃ prāptānāṃ nāsti paraṃ prāpyamanādiṃ taṃ ... ॥13
matto māro yasya lalāṭākṣibhavāgnisphūrjatkīlaproṣitabhasmīkṛtadehaḥ
tadbhasmāsīdyasya sujātaḥ paṭavāsastaṃ ...॥14
sambhorāśau saṃsṛtirūpe luṭhatāṃ tatpāraṃ gantuṃ yatpadabhaktirdṛḍhanaukā ... ॥15
sarvārādhyaṃ sarvagamānandapayodhiṃ taṃ ... ॥15
medhāvī syādiṃduvataṃsaṃ dhṛtavīṇaṃ karpūrābhaṃ pustakahastaṃ kamalākṣam ।
citte dhyāyanyasya vapurdrādaḍnimiṣārdhaṃ taṃ ...॥16
dhānnāṃ dhāma prauḍharucīnāṃ paramaṃ yatsūryādīnāṃ yasya sa heturjagadādeḥ ।
etāvanye yasya sa sarveśvaramīḍyaṃ taṃ ...॥17
pratyāhāraprāṇanirodhādisamarthaibhaktairdāntairasaṃyatacittairyatamānaiḥ ।
svātmatvena jñāyata eva tvarayā yastaṃ ... ॥18
jñāṃśībhūtānprāṇina etānphaladātā cittāntasthaḥ prerayati sve sakale'pi ।
kṛtye devaḥ prāktanakarmānusaratsan taṃ ...॥ 19
prajñāmātraṃ prāpitasaṃvinnijabhaktaṃ prāṇākṣādeḥ prerayitāraṃ praṇavārtham ।
prāhuḥ prājñā yaṃ viditānubhavatatvāstaṃ ...॥20
yastha jñānādeva nṛṇāṃ saṃsṛtibodho yasya jñānādeva vimokṣo bhavatīti ।
spaṣṭaṃ brūte vedaśirodeśikamādyaṃ taṃ... ॥21
chinne'vidyārūpavaṭe naiva ca viśvaṃ yatrādhyastaṃ jīvapareśatvamapīdam ।
bhānorbhānuṣvambuvadastākhilabhedaṃ taṃ... ॥22
svāpasvapnau jāgradavasthāpi na tatra prāṇaścetassarvagato yassakalātmā ।
kūṭastho yaḥ kevalasañcitsukharūpastaṃ ...॥23
hāhetyevaṃ vismayamīyurmunimukhyāḥ jñāte yasminsvātmatayā nātmavimohaḥ ।
pratyagbhūte brahmaṇi yātaḥ kathamitthaṃ taṃ... ॥24
yaiṣā ramyairmattamayūrābhidhavṛttairādau guptā yanmanuvarṇaitibhaṅgī ।
tāmevaitāṃ dakṣiṇavakraḥ kṛpayāsā dūrīkuryāddeśikasamrāṭparamātmā ॥25
॥ iti śrīmacchaṅkarācāryaviracita dakṣiṇāmūrtivarṇamālāstotraṃ sampūrṇam ॥
