Text:

śṛṅgagiri nikaṭastha śrīsūryanārāyaṇa devasthāne viracitam
mānasasarogataṃ me śoṣaya paṅkaṃ kharosra dinanātha |
no cetkharatvameṣāmasrāṇāṃ bhūyātkathaṃ brūhi ||1||
nivārya bāhyaṃ paramandhakāraṃ dineśa garvaṃ kuruṣe vṛthā tvam |
yadyasti śaktistava māmakīnamantaḥsthamāndhyaṃ vinivārayāśu ||2||
||iti śrīmacchṛṅgerī jagadguru śrīmatparamahaṃsaparivrājakācārya anantaśrī saccidānanda śivābhinavanṛsiṃhabhāratī mahāsvāmibhiḥ śṛṅgagiri nikaṭastha śrīsūryanārāyaṇa devasthāne viracitaṃ śrīdineśa stavaḥ||
