Text:

śrī gaṇeśāya namaḥ |
śrīmahādeva uvācaḥ |
namo'stu rāmāya saśaktikāya nīlotpalaśyāmalakomalāya |
kirīṭahārāṅgadabhūṣaṇāya siṃhāsanasthāya mahāprabhāya || 1||
tvamādimadhyāntavihīna ekaḥ sṛjasyavasyatsi ca lokajātam |
svamāyayā tena na lipyase tvaṃ yatsve mukhe'jasrarato'navadyaḥ || 2||
līlāṃ vidhatse guṇasaṃvṛtastvaṃ prapannabhaktānuvidhānahetoḥ |
nānāvatāraiḥ suramānuṣādyaiḥ pratīyase jñānibhireva nityam || 3||
svāṃśena lokaṃ sakalaṃ vidhāya taṃ bibharṣi ca tvaṃ tadadhaḥ phaṇīśvaraḥ |
uparyadho bhānvaniloḍupauṣadhīpravarṣarūpo'vasi naikadhā jagat || 4||
tvamiha dehabhṛtāṃ śikhirūpaḥ pacasi bhaktamaśeṣamajasram |
pavanamañcakarūpasahāyo jagadakhaṇḍamanena bibharṣi || 5||
candrasūryaśikhimadhyagataṃ yatteja īśa cidaśeṣatanūnām |
prābhavattanubhṛtāmiha dhairyaṃ śauryamātramakhilaṃ tava sattvam || 6||
tvaṃ viriñciśivaviṣṇuvibhedāt kālakarmaśaśisūryavibhāgāt |
vādināṃ pṛthagiveśa bibhāsi brahma niścitamananyadihaikam || 7||
matsyādirūpeṇa yathā tvamekaḥ śruto purāṇeṣu ca lokasiddhaḥ |
tathaiva sarvaṃ sadayadvibhāgastvameva nānyadbhavato vibhāti || 8||
yadyatsamutpannamanantasṛṣṭāvutpatsyate yacca bhavacca yacca |
na dṛśyate sthāvarajaṅgamādau tvayā vinā'taḥ parataḥ parastvam || 9||
tatvaṃ na jānanti parātmanaste janaḥ samastāstava māyayā'taḥ |
tvadbhaktasevāmalamānasānāṃ vibhāti tatvaṃ paramekamaiśam || 10||
brahmādayaste na viduḥ svarūpaṃ cidātmatatvaṃ bahirarthabhāvāḥ |
tato budhastvāmidameva rūpaṃ bhaktyā bhajanmuktimupaityaduḥkhaḥ || 11||
ahaṃ bhavannāmaguṇaiḥ kṛtārtho vasāmi kāśyāmaniśaṃ bhavānyā |
mumūrṣamāṇasya vimuktaye'haṃ diśāmi mantraṃ tava rāmanāma || 12||
imaṃ stavaṃ nityamananyabhaktyā śrṛṇvanti gāyanti likhanti ye vai |
te sarvasaukhyaṃ paramaṃ ca labdhā bhavatpadaṃ yāntu bhavatprasādāt || 13||
|| iti śrīmadadhyātmarāmāyaṇe śrīmahādevakṛtaṃ rāmastotraṃ sampūrṇam ||
