॥ śivanakṣatramālikāstotram ॥ .
śrīkaṇṭhassāmba īśānaḥ pārvatīprāṇavallabhaḥ ।
vṛṣārūḍho viṣṇusakhaśśiva eva gatirmama ॥ 1
paramātmā parabrahma pañcāsyaḥ parameśvaraḥ ।
parātparataraṃ dhāma śivaeva gatirmama ॥ 2
nirālambo nirākāro nirmalo nirupadravaḥ ।
nityaśuddho niṣkalaṅkaśśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 3
śuddhasphaṭikasaṅkāśassūryacandrāgnilocanaḥ ।
śūlaṭaṅkadharaśśarvaśśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 4
bhūrayo viṣṇubāṇaśca vedāśvo brahmasārathiḥ ।
tripurārirmerudhanvā śivaeva gatirmama ॥ 5
vyāghracarmāmbaragharo vyālayajñopavītavān ।
bhavābhīṣṭaprado nityaśśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 6
kālakālo mahākālo bhasmoddhūlitavigrahaḥ ।
kailāsanilayaśśambhu śiśavaeva gatirmama ॥ 7
rudrākṣamālābharaṇastripuṇḍrāṅkitamastakaḥ ।
pramatheśo mahādevaśśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 8
gaurīkānto vāmadevo nīlagrīvo digambaraḥ।
gaṅgādharaścandracūḍaśśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 9
mūrtitrayasvarūpāḍhyo guṇatrayavivarjitaḥ ।
kāryatrayavidhātā ca śivaeva gatirmama ॥ 10
sadyojāto bhairavaśva mārkaṇḍeyavarapradaḥ ।
viṣṇucakrapradātā ca śivaeva gatirmama ॥ 11
aghoramūrtissarveśo vedavedyo dayānidhiḥ ।
kapālayuktahastābja śśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 12
aṣṭamūrtistatpuruṣa stathaikādaśa mūrtimān ।
pañcaviṃśati līlātmā śivaeva gatirmama ॥ 13
śatarudrasvarūpāḍhyo nandibhṛṅgi varapradaḥ ।
sadāśivo'nantarūpaśśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 14
mahāliṅgodbhavaśśānto ruṇḍamālāvibhūṣaṇaḥ ।
triyambako virūpākṣa śśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 15
nirañjanaḥ paraṃjyoti ssaccidānandavigrahaḥ ।
māyāśabararūpāḍhya śśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 16
liṅgarūpo daśabhujaścograḥpaśupatirbhavaḥ ।
kāmeśvaro jagadrakṣaśśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 17
kalyāṇasundarākāraḥ kāmitārthavarapradaḥ ।
hālāsyalīlālolaśca śivaeva gatirmama ॥ 18
mṛtyuñjayo mahāraudrassomasskando maheśvaraḥ ।
bāṇāsuraparitrātā śivaeva gatirmama ॥ 19
jalandharaśiraśchettā nāgālaṅkārabhūṣitaḥ ।
gajacarmāmbaradharaśśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 20
bhavarogaharaḥ pūjyo bhayakṛdbhayanāśanaḥ ।
dakṣiṇāmūrti rūpāḍhya śśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 21
sandhyānaṭo haro radro bhasmāsuravarapradaḥ ।
pinākabhṛnmanmathāriśśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 22
mṛḍo brahmaśiraśchettā vināyakavarapradaḥ ।
umāmaheśvaro devaśśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 23
mahāprabhurmahāyogī bhūtasaṅgasamāvṛtaḥ ।
nāradādimunistutyaśśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 24
dakṣādhvaraharo vīraḥ kālakoṭiviṣāśanaḥ ।
gaṇeśaśāstṛjanaka śśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 25
kuberamitro bhīmeśo girīśo mohinīpriyaḥ ।
devāsurādisaṃsevyaśśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 26
sthāṇurhariharātmā ca rāmatārakamantradaḥ ।
śaṅkaraścārdhanārīśaśśivaeva gatirmama ॥ 27
śrīcāmuṇḍākṛpāpūrṇa śivadhyānaparāyaṇaḥ ।
śrīkṛṣṇendro bhaktiyuktaśśivanakṣatramālikām ॥ 28
racayitvārpayāmāsa śrīkaṇṭhahṛdayāmbuje ।
trisandhyaṃ yaḥ paṭhedbhaktyā śivasāyujyamāpnuyāt ॥
mallikāvṛttamratnaśailanagāvanīśakacandrapallavahāyane siddhamāsasitāṣṭamī śaśivāsare kanakojvalā । candraśekharapādayoḥ khakhapūrṇa rūpamitākṣarā kṛṣṇabhūpasamarpitā gajabhānunāma sumālikā ॥ 30
॥ iti śrīśivanakṣatramālikāstotram ॥
