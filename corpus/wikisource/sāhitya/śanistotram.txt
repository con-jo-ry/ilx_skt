|| atha śanistotram ||
śrīḥ || asya śrīśanaiścarastotrasya daśaratha ṛṣiḥ śanaiścaro devata triṣṭupachaṃdaḥ śanaiścaraprītyarthe jape viniyogaḥ ||
daśaratha uvāca ||
koṇā'ntako raudrayamo'tha babhruḥ kṛṣṇaḥ śaniḥ piṃgalamaṃda sauriḥ || nityaṃ smṛto yo harate ca pīḍāṃ tasmai namaḥ śrīravinaṃdanāya || 1 ||
surāsuraḥ kiṃpurūṣā gaṇeṃdrā gandharvavidyādharapannagāśca || pīḍyaṃti sarve viṣamasthitena tasmai nāmaḥ śrīravinaṃdanāya || 2 ||
narā nareṃdrāḥ paśavo mṛgeṃdrā vanyāśca ye kīṭapataṃgabhṛṃgā || pīḍyaṃti sarve viṣamasthitena tasmai namaḥ śrīravinaṃdanāya || 3 ||
deśāśca durgāṇi vanāni yatra senāniveśāḥ purapattanāti || pīḍyaṃti sarve viṣamasthitena tasmai namaḥ śrīravinaṃdanāya || 4 ||
tilairyavairmāṣaguḍannadānairlohena nīlāṃbaradānato vā || prīṇāti maṃtrairnijavāsare ca tasmai namaḥ śrīravinaṃdanāya || 5 ||
prayākūle yamunātaṭe ca sarasvatī puṇyajale guhāyām‌ || yo yogināṃ dhyānagato'pi sūkṣmastasmai namaḥ śrīravinaṃdanāya || 6 ||
anyapradeśātsvagṛhaṃ praviṣṭastadīyavāre sa naraḥ sūkhī syāt‌ || gṛhād‌ gato yo na punaḥ prayāti tasmai namaḥ śrīravinaṃdanāya namaḥ || 7 ||
sraṣṭā syaṃbhūrbhuvanatarasya trātā hariḥ saṃharate pinākī || ekastridhā ṛgyajuḥsāmamūrtitasmai namaḥ śrīravinaṃdanāya namaḥ || 8 ||
śanyaṣṭakaṃ yaḥ prayataḥ prabhāte nityaṃ suputraiḥ paśubāṃdhavaiśca || paṭhecca saukhyaṃ bhuvi bhogayuktaṃ prāpnoti nirvāṇapadaṃ paraṃ saḥ || 9 ||
koṇasthaḥ piṃgalo babhraḥ kṛṣṇā raudrā'ntako yamaḥ || sauriḥśaneścaro maṃdaḥ pippalādena saṃstutaḥ || 10 ||
etāni daśa nāmāni prātarutthāpa yaḥ paṭhet || śanaiścarakṛtā pīḍā na kadācid‍bhaviṣyati || 11 ||
iti śrīdaśarathaproktaṃ śanaiścarastotraṃ saṃpūrṇam ||
