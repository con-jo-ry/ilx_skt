Text:

atha dattātreya dvādaśa nāma stotram
||śrī gaṇeśāya namaḥ||
asya śrīdattātreya dvādaśanāma stotra maṃtrasya|
paramahaṃsa ṛṣiḥ|
śrī dattātreya paramātmā devatā|
anuṣṭup chaṃdaḥ|
sakala kāmanā siddhyarthe jape viniyogaḥ|
prathamastu mahāyogī|
dvitīya prabhurīśvaraḥ|
tṛtīyaśca trimūrtiśca|
caturtho jñānasāgaraḥ|
paṃcamo jñānavijñānam|
ṣaṣṭhasyāt sarvamaṃgalam|
saptamo puṃḍarīkākṣo|
aṣṭamo devavallabhaḥ|
navamo naṃdadeveśo|
daśamo naṃdadāyakaḥ|
ekādaśo mahārudraḥ|
dvādaśo karuṇākaraḥ|
etāni dvādaśa nāmāni dattātreya mahātmanaḥ|
maṃtrarājeti vikhyātaṃ dattātreya haraḥ parāḥ|
kṣayopasmāra kuṣṭhādi tāpajvara nivāraṇam|
rājadvārepathe aghore saṃgrāmeṣu jalāṃtare|
gire gṛhāṃtare araṇye vyāghra cora bhayādiṣu|
āvartane sahasreṣu labhante vāṃchitaṃ phalam|
trikālaṃ yaḥ paṭhennityaṃ mokṣasiddhimavāpnuyāt|
dattātreya sadā rakṣet yaśaḥ satyaṃ na saṃśayaḥ|
vidyārthī labhate vidyāṃ rogī rogāt pramucyate|
aputro labhate putraṃ daridri labhate dhanam|
abhāryo labhate bhāryām sukhārthī labhate sukham|
mucyate sarva pāpebhyo sarvatra vijayī bhavet||
iti śrī dattātreya dvādaśa nāma stotram saṃpūrṇam||
śrī dattātreyārpaṇamastu||
