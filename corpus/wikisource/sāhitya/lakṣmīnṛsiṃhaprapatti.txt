Text:

lakṣmīnṛsiṃha lalanām jagatosyanetrīm
mātṛsvabhāva mahitām haritulya śīlām
lokasya maṅgalakarīm ramaṇīya rūpām
padmālayām bhagavatīm śaraṇam prapadye ॥ 1॥

śrīyādanāmakamunīṃdratapoviśeṣāt
śrīyādaśailaśikhare satata prakāśau
bhaktānurāgabharitau bhavaroga vaidyau
lakṣmīnṛsiṃha caraṇau śaraṇam prapadye ॥ 2॥

devasvarūpa vikṛtāvapinaijarupau
sarvottarau sujana saru niśevyamānau
sarvasya jīvanakarau sadrṛśasvarūpau
lakṣmīnṛsiṃha caraṇau śaraṇam prapadye ॥ 3॥

lakṣmīśate prapadane sahakārabhūtau
tvattopyati priyatamau śaraṇāgatānām
rakṣāvicakṣaṇa paṭū karuṇālayau śrī
lakṣmīnṛsiṃha caraṇau śaraṇam prapadye ॥ 4॥

prahlāda pautra balidānava bhūmidāna
kālaprakāśita nijānya jaghanya bhāvau
lokapramāṇa karaṇau śubhadau surānām
lakṣmīnṛsiṃha caraṇau śaraṇam prapadye ॥ 5॥

kāyādavīya śubhamānasa rājahaṃsau
vedānta kalpataru pallava ṭalli jautau
sadbhakta mūladhanamityudita prabhāvau
lakṣmīnṛsiṃha caraṇau śaraṇam prapadye ॥ 6॥

॥ iti śrī vaṃgīpuram narasiṃhācārya viracitaṃ
śrī yādagiri lakṣmīnṛsiṃha prapattiḥ samāptaṃ ॥
