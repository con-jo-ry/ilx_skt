Text:

cidambaraṃ śrīnaṭarājastutī ||
namaste rūdra manyava utota iṣave namaḥ |
namaste astu dhanvane karābhyāṃ te namo namaḥ || 1||
yāte rudra śivā tanūḥ śāntā tasyai namo namaḥ |
namo'stu nīlagrīvāya sahasrākṣāya te namaḥ || 2||
sahasrapāṇaye tubhyaṃ mīḍhuṣṭamāya te namaḥ |
kapardine namastubhyaṃ kālarūpāya te namaḥ || 3||
namaste cāttaśastrāya namaste śūlapāṇaye |
hiraṇyapāṇaye tubhyaṃ hiraṇyapataye namaḥ || 4||
namaste vṛkṣarūpāya harikeśāya te namaḥ |
paśūnāṃ pataye tubhyaṃ pathīnāṃ pataye namaḥ || 5||
puṣṭānāṃ pataye tubhyaṃ kṣetrāṇāṃ pataye namaḥ |
ātatāvisvarūpāya vanānāṃ pataye namaḥ || 6||
rohitāya stapataye vṛkṣāṇāṃ pataye namaḥ |
namaste mantriṇe sākṣāt kakṣāṇāṃ pataye namaḥ || 7||
oṣadhīnāṃ ca pataye namaḥ sākṣātparātmane |
uccairghoṣāya devāya pattīnāṃ pataye namaḥ || 8||
satvānāṃ pataye tubhyaṃ ghanānāṃ pataye namaḥ |
sahamānāya śāntāya śaṅkarāya namo namaḥ || 9||
ādhīnāṃ pataye tubhyaṃ vyādhīnaṃ pataye namaḥ |
kakubhāya namastubhyaṃ namaste'stu niṣaṅgiṇe || 10||
stenānāṃ pataye tubhyaṃ dhanānāṃ pataye namaḥ |
taskarāṇāṃ namastubhyaṃ pataye pāpahāriṇe || 11||
vañcate parivañcate stāyūnāṃ pataye namaḥ |
namo nicerave tubhyaṃ araṇyānāṃ pataye namaḥ || 12||
uṣṇīṣiṇe namastubhyaṃ namaste paramātmane |
vistṛtāya namastubhyaṃ āsīnāya namonamaḥ || 13||
śayanāya namstubhyaṃ suṣuptāya namo namaḥ |
prabuddhāya namastubhyaṃ sthirāya paramātmane || 14||
sabhārūpāya te nityaṃ sabhāyāḥ pataye namaḥ |
namaḥ śivāya sāmbāya brahmaṇe sarvasākṣiṇe || 15||
|| iti sūtasaṃhitāyāṃ muktikāṇḍe sabhāpatistutiḥ sampūrṇam ||
