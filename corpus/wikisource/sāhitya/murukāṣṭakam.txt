॥śrī murukāṣṭakam ॥
siddhibuddhipradaṃ devaṃ subrahmaṇyāgrajaṃ prabhum ।
somāpratanayaṃ vande vighnarājamaharniśam ॥ 1
mukārastu mukundassyāt rukāro rudravācakaḥ।
kakārastu brahmavādī muruko guhavācakaḥ ॥
maruka śrīmatānnatha bhogamokṣapradapramo।
devadeva mahāsnena pāhipāhi sadā vibho । 3
murukaṃ muktidaṃ devaṃ munīnāṃ modakaṃ prabhum ।
mocakaṃ sarvaduḥkhānāṃ mohanāśaṃ sadānumaḥ ॥ 4
murukeṇa mukundena munīnāṃ hārdavāsinā ।
vallīśena maheśena pālitāssarvadā vayam ॥
murukāya namaḥ prātaḥ murukāya namo niśi ।
murukāya namaḥ sāyaṃ murukāya namo namaḥ ॥6
murukāsparātsatyāt ṣaṇmukhāt śikhivāhanāt ।
guhātparaṃ na jāne'haṃ tasvaṃ kimapi sarvadā ।। 7
murakasya maheśasya vallīsenāpateḥ prabhoḥ ।
cidambara vilāsasya caraṇau sarvadā bhaje ॥ 8
muruke ṣaṇmukhe deve śikhivāhe dviṣaḍbhuje ।
kṛttikātanaye śambhau sarvadā ramatāṃ manaḥ ॥ 9
murukatyāṣṭakamidaṃ somaśekharayajvamiḥ ।
saṃstutaṃ yaḥ paṭhennityaṃ śāśvataṃ tasya maṅgalam ॥10
॥ iti śrī murukāṣṭakaṃ samāptam ॥
