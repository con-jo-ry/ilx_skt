Text:

|| atha navagrahastotram ||
|| raviḥ ||
japākusumasaṅkāśaṃ kāśyapeyaṃ mahādyutim|
tamo'riṃ sarvapāpaghnaṃ praṇato'smi divākaram||1||
|| somaḥ ||
dadhiśaṅkhatuṣārābhaṃ kṣīrodārṇavasambhavam|
namāmi śaśinaṃ somaṃ śambhormukuṭabhūṣaṇam||2||
|| maṅgalaḥ ||
dharaṇīgarbhasambhūtaṃ vidyutkāntisamaprabham|
kumāraṃ śaktihastaṃ taṃ maṅgalaṃ praṇamāmyaham||3||
|| budhaḥ ||
priyaṅgukalikāśyāmaṃ rūpeṇāpratimaṃ budham|
saumyaṃ saumyaguṇopetaṃ taṃ budhaṃ praṇamāmyaham||4||
|| guruḥ ||
devānāṃ ca ṛṣīṇāṃ ca guruṃ kāñcanasannibham|
buddhibhūtaṃ trilokeśaṃ taṃ namāmi bṛhaspatim||5||
|| śukraḥ ||
himakuṇḍamṛṇālābhaṃ daityānāṃ paramaṃ gurum|
sarvaśāstrapravaktāraṃ bhārgavaṃ praṇamāmyaham||6||
|| śaniḥ ||
nīlāñjanasamābhāsaṃ raviputraṃ yamāgrajam|
chāyāmārtaṇḍasambhūtaṃ taṃ namāmi śanaiścaram||7||
|| rāhuḥ ||
ardhakāyaṃ mahāvīryaṃ candrādityavimardanam|
siṃhikāgarbhasaṃbhūtaṃ taṃ rāhuṃ praṇamāmyaham||8||
|| ketuḥ ||
palāśapuṣpasaṅkāśaṃ tārakāgrahamastakam|
raudraṃ raudrātmakaṃ ghoraṃ taṃ ketuṃ praṇamāmyaham||9||
|| phalaśrutiḥ ||
iti vyāsamukhodgītaṃ yaḥ paṭhetsusamāhitaḥ|
divā vā yadi vā rātrau vighnaśāntirbhaviṣyati||
naranārīnṛpāṇāṃ ca bhavedduḥsvapnanāśanam|
aiśvaryamatulaṃ teṣāmārogyaṃ puṣṭivardhanam||
grahanakṣatrajāḥ pīḍāstaskarāgnisamudbhavāḥ|
tāḥ sarvāḥ praśamaṃ yānti vyāso brūte na saṃśayaḥ||
|| iti śrīvyāsaviracitam ādityādinavagrahastotraṃ sampūrṇam ||
|| atha navagrahapīḍāharastotram ||
|| raviḥ ||
grahāṇāmādirādityo lokarakṣaṇakārakaḥ|
viṣamasthānasambhūtāṃ pīḍāṃ haratu me raviḥ||1||
|| somaḥ ||
rohiṇīśaḥ sudhāmūrtiḥ sudhāgātraḥ sudhāśanaḥ|
viṣamasthānasambhūtāṃ pīḍāṃ haratu me vidhuḥ||2||
|| maṅgalaḥ ||
bhūmiputro mahātejā jagatāṃ bhayakṛtsadā|
vṛṣṭikṛddṛṣṭihartā ca pīḍāṃ haratu me kujaḥ||3||
|| budhaḥ ||
utpātarūpo jagatāṃ candraputro mahādyutiḥ|
sūryapriyakaro vidvān pīḍāṃ haratu me budhaḥ||4||
|| guruḥ ||
devamantrī viśālākṣaḥ sadā lokahite rataḥ|
anekaśiṣyasampūrṇaḥ pīḍāṃ haratu me guruḥ||5||
|| śukraḥ ||
daityamantrī gurusteṣāṃ prāṇadaśca mahāmatiḥ|
prabhustārāgrahāṇāṃ ca pīḍāṃ haratu me bhṛguḥ||6||
|| śaniḥ ||
sūryaputro dīrghadeho viśālākṣaḥ śivapriyaḥ|
dīrghācāraḥ prasannātmā pīḍāṃ haratu me śaniḥ||7||
|| rāhuḥ ||
anekarūpavarṇeśca śataśo'tha sahasradṛk|
utpātarūpo jagatāṃ pīḍāṃ haratu me tamaḥ||8||
|| ketuḥ ||
mahāśirā mahāvaktro dīrghadaṃṣṭro mahābalaḥ|
atanuścordhvakeśaśca pīḍāṃ haratu me śikhīḥ||9||
|| iti brahmāṇḍapurāṇoktaṃ navagrahapīḍāharastotraṃ sampūrṇam ||
