॥ śrīdakṣiṇāmūrtistotram ॥
mandasmita sphurita mugdhamukhāravindaṃ kandarpakoṭiśatasundaradivyamūrtim ।
ātāmrakomala jaṭāghaṭitendulekhamālokaye vaṭataṭīnilayaṃ dayālum ॥ 1

kalaye kaścana rudraṃ karuṇārasapūrapūritasamudram ॥ 2

jayākhila hṛdākāśa lasadyumaṇi maṇḍala ॥ 19
jayāsaṅga sukhottuṅga saudhakrīḍana bhūmipa ।
jayasaṃvitsabhāsīma naṭanotsuka nartaka ॥ 20
jayānavadhi bodhābdhi kelikautuka bhūpate ।
jaya nirmalacidyonni cārudyotita nīrada ॥ 21
jayānanda sadudyāna līlālolupa kokila ।

jayāṇimādibhūtīnāṃ śaraṇyā khila puṇyabhūḥ ।

jayānantati kāntaika jayaśāntajaneḍita ॥ 39
jaya trayyantasaṃvedya jayāṅga tritayātiga ।

jayanityanirādhāra jayaniṣkalanirguṇa ॥ 41

sākṣādavaiti paratatvamihaiva dhīraḥ ।

dharmairaśeṣa nigamāttanirūpaṇairvā ॥ 47
avacanacinmudrābhyāmadvaitaṃ bodhamātramātmānam ।
brūte tatra ca mānaṃ pustaka bhujagāgnibhirmahādevaḥ ॥
kaṭighaṭita karaṭikṛttiḥ kāmapi mudāṃpradarśayan jaṭilaḥ
svālokinaḥ kapālī hanta manovilaya mātanotyekaḥ ।
śrutimukhacandracakoraṃ natajanadaurātmyadurgamakuṭhāram ।
