॥ aṣṭamūrtyaṣṭakam ॥
tuṣṭāvāṣṭatanuṃ hṛṣṭaḥ praphullanayanāñcalaḥ।
maulāvañjalimādhāya vadan jayajayetica ॥ 1
bhārgava uvācaḥ
tvaṃ bhābhirābhirabhibhūya tamassamastamastaṃ nyasyamimataṃ ca niśācarāṇām । 
dedīpyase dinamaṇe gagane hitāya lokatrayasya jagadīśvara tannamaste ।। 2
loketivelamativela mahāmahobhirnirmāsi kaumudamudaṃ ca samutsamudram । 
vidrāvitākhilatamāssutamohimāṃśo pīyūṣapūraparipūrita tannamaste ॥ 3
tvaṃ pāvane pathi sadāgatirapyupāsyaḥ kastvāṃ vinā bhuvanajīvana jīvatīha । 
stabdhaprabhañjana vivardhitasarvajanto santoṣitāhikula sarvaga tannamaste ॥ 4
viśvaikapāvaka natāvaka pāvakaika śakteṛte mṛtabatāmṛtadivyakāryam । 
prāṇityado jagadaho jagadantarātman tatpāvaka pratipadaṃ śamadaṃ namaste॥ 5
pānīyarūpa parameśa jagatpavitra citraṃ vicitra sucaritra karoṣi nūnam । 
viśvaṃ pavitramamalaṃ kila viśvanātha pānāvagāhanata etadato nato'smi ॥ 6
ākāśarūpa bahirantaritāvakāśa dānādvikasvara maheśvara viśvametat । 
tvattassadā sadaya saṃśvasiti svabhāvātsaṃkocameti bhavatosmi natastatastvām ॥ 7
viśvambharātmaka bibharti vibhotra viśvaṃ koviśvanātha bhavatonyatamastamore । 
tattvāṃ vinā naśamināhi phaṇāhibhūṣa stavyo paraḥ paratara praṇatastatastvām ॥ 8
ātmasvarūpa tava rūpaparamparābhirābhistataṃ hara carācararūpametat । 
sarvāntarātmanilaya pratirūparūpanityaṃ nato'smi paramātmatano'ṣṭamūrte ॥ 9
ityaṣṭamūrtibhirimābhirumābhinandha vanyātivandha tava viśvajanītamūrte। 
etattataṃ nu vitataṃ praṇatapraṇīta sarvārthasārthaparamārthatano nato'smi ॥ 10
aṣṭamūrtyaṣṭakeneṣṭaṃ pariṣṭutyeti bhārgavaḥ ।
bhargaṃ bhūmimilanmauliḥ praṇanāma punaḥ punaḥ ॥11
॥ iti śrīśukrācāryakṛtaṃ aṣṭamūrtyaṣṭakam ॥
