Text:

śrī gaṇeśāya namaḥ ॥
omiti jñānamātreṇa rogājīrṇena nirjitā ।
kālanidrāṃ prapanno'smi trāhi māṃ madhusūdana ॥ 1॥

na gatirvidyate cānyā tvameva śaraṇaṃ mama ।
pāpapaṅke nimagnosmi trāhi māṃ madhusūdana ॥ 2॥

mohito mohajālena putradāragṛhādiṣu ।
tṛṣṇayā pīḍyamāno'smi trāhi māṃ madhusūdana ॥ 3॥

bhaktihīnaṃ ca dīnaṃ ca duḥkhaśokāturaṃ prabho ।
anāśrayamanāthaṃ ca trāhi māṃ madhusūdana ॥ 4॥

gatāgatenaśrāntosmi dīrghasaṃsāravatmarstu ।
yena bhūyo na gacchāmi trāhi māṃ madhusūdana ॥ 5॥

vahavo hi mayā dṛṣṭāḥ kleśāścaiva pṛthak pṛthak ।
garbhavāse mahadduḥkhaṃ trāhi māṃ madhusūdana ॥ 6॥

tena deva prapannosmi trāṇārthaṃ tvatparāyaṇaḥ ।
duḥkhārṇavaparitrāṇāt trāhi māṃ madhusūdana ॥ 7॥

vācā yacca pratijñātaṃ karmaṇā topapāditam ।
tatpāpārjitamagnosmi trāhi māṃ madhusūdana ॥ 8॥

sukṛtaṃ na kṛtaṃ kiñcidduṣkṛtaṃ ca kṛtaṃ mayā ।
saṃsāraghore magnosmi trāhi māṃ madhusūdana ॥ 9॥

dehāntarasahastreṣu cānyonyabhrāmito mayā ।
tiryaktvaṃ mānuṣtvaṃ ca trāhi māṃ madhusūdana ॥ 10॥

vācayāmi yathonmattaḥ pralapāmi tavāgrataḥ ।
jarāmaraṇabhītosmi trāhi māṃ madhusūdana ॥ 11॥

yatra yatra ca yātosmi strīṣu vā puruṣeṣu ca ।
tatra tatrācalā bhaktistrāhi māṃ madhusūdana ॥ 12॥

gatvā gatvā nivartante candrasūryādayo grahāḥ ।
adyāpi na nibarttante dvādaśākṣaracintakāḥ ॥ 13॥

ūrdhvapātālamartyeṣu vyāptaṃ lokaṃ jagattrayam ।
dvādaśākṣarātparaṃ nāsti vāsudevena bhāṣitam ॥ 14॥

dvādaśākṣaraṃ mahāmantraṃ sarvakāmaphalapradam ।
garbhavāsanivāsena śukena paribhāṣitam ॥ 15॥

dvādaśākṣaraṃ nirāhāro yaḥ yaḥ paṭhedharivāsare ।
sa gacchedvaiṣṇavasthānaṃ yatra yogeśvaro hariḥ ॥ 16॥

iti śrīśukadevaviracitaṃ madhusūdanastotraṃ saṃpūrṇam ॥
