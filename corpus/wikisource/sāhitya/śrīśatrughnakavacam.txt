Text:

|| śrīmadānandarāmāyaṇāṃtargata śrī śatrughna kavacaṃ ||
agastiruvāca
athaśatrughna kavacaṃ sutīkṣṇa śruṇu sādaraṃ |
sarvakāma pradaṃ ramyaṃ rāmasadbhakti vardhanaṃ || 1||
śatrughnaṃ dhṛtakārmukaṃ dhṛta mahā tūṇīra bāṇottamaṃ
pārśve śrīraghunandanasya vinayātvā mesthitaṃ sundaraṃ |
rāmaṃ svīya kareṇa tālajalajaṃ dhṛtvāticitraṃ varaṃ
sūryābhaṃ vyajanaṃ sabhāsthita mahaṃtaṃ vijayaṃtaṃ bhaje || 2||
asya śrī śatrughna kavacamantrasya agasti ṛṣiḥ |
śrī śatrughno devatā |
anuṣṭup chandaḥ |
sudarśana iti bījaṃ |
kaikeyī nandanaḥ śaktiḥ |
śrībharatānuja iti kīlakaṃ |
bharatamantrītyastraṃ |
śrī rāmadāsa iti kavacaṃ |
lakṣmaṇāṃśaja iti mantraḥ |
śrīśatrughna prītyarthaṃ sakalamanaḥ
kāmanāsiddhyarthaṃ jape viniyogaḥ ||
atha aṅgulī nyāsaḥ |
oṃ śatrughnāya aṅguṣṭhābhyāṃ namaḥ |
oṃ sudarśanāya tarjanībhyāṃ namaḥ |
oṃ kaikeyīnandanāya madhyamābhyāṃ namaḥ |
oṃ bharatānujāya anāmikābhyāṃ namaḥ |
oṃ bharatamantriṇe kaniṣṭhikābhyāṃ namaḥ |
oṃ śrī rāmadāsāya karatala karapṛṣṭhābhyāṃ namaḥ ||
hṛdayādinyāsaḥ ||
oṃ śatrughnāya hṛdayāya namaḥ |
oṃ sudarśanāya śirase svāhā |
oṃ kaikeyīnandanāya śikāyai vaṣaṭ |
oṃ bharatānujāya kavacāya huṃ |
oṃ bharatamantriṇe natratrayāya vauṣaṭ |
oṃ śrī rāmadāsāya astrāya phaṭ |
oṃ lakṣmaṇāṃśajeti digbandhaḥ ||
atha dhyānam ||
rāmasya saṃsthitaṃ vāme pārśve vinayapūrvakaṃ |
kaikeyīnandanaṃ saumyaṃ mukuṭenādi rañjitaṃ || 1||
ratnakaṅkaṇa keyūra vanamālā virājitaṃ |
raśanā kuṇḍaladharaṃ ratnahāra sunūpuraṃ || 2||
vyajanena vījayantaṃ jānakī kāntamādarāt |
rāma nyastekṣaṇam vīraṃ kaikeyī toṣavardhanaṃ || 3||
dvibhujaṃ kañjanayanaṃ divyapītāmbarānvitaṃ |
subhujaṃ sundaraṃ meghaśyāmalaṃ sundarānanaṃ || 4||
rāmavākye dattakarṇaṃ rakṣoghnaṃ kaḍga dhāriṇaṃ |
dhanurbāṇadharaṃ śreṣṭhaṃ dhṛtatūṇīra muttamaṃ || 5||
sabhāyāṃ saṃsthitaṃ ramyaṃ kastūri tilakāṅkitaṃ |
makuṭasthāvataṃsena śobhitaṃ ca smitānanaṃ || 6||
ravivaṃśodbhavaṃ divyarūpaṃ daśarathātmajaṃ |
madhurāvāsinaṃ devaṃ lavaṇāsuramardanaṃ || 7||
evaṃ dhyātvā tu śatrughnaṃ rāmapādekṣaṇam hṛdi |
paṭhanīyaṃ varaṃ cedaṃ kavacaṃ tasya pāvanaṃ || 8||
kavaca prāraṃbhaḥ ||
pūrvetvavatu śatrughnaḥ pātuyāṃye sudarśanaḥ |
kaikeyī nandanaḥ pātu pratīcyāṃ sarvadā mama || 1||
pātūdīśyāṃ rāmabandhuḥ pātvadho bharatānujaḥ |
ravivaṃśodbhavaścordhvaṃ madhye daśarathātmajaḥ || 2||
sarvataḥ pātu māmatra kaikeyī toṣavardhanaḥ |
śyāmalāṅgaḥ ciraḥ pātu bhālaṃ śrīilakṣmaṇānujaḥ || 3||
bhṛvor madhye sadā pātu sumukhotrāvanītale |
śrutakīrtipatirnetre kapole pātu rāghavaḥ || 4||
karṇau kuṇḍalakarṇovyāt nāsāgraṃ nṛpavaṃśajaḥ |
mukhaṃ mama yuvā pātu vāṇīṃ pātu sphuṭākṣaraḥ || 5||
jihvāṃ subāhutātovyātyūpaketupitā dvijān |
cibukaṃ ramyacibukaḥ kaṇṭhaṃ pātu subhāṣaṇaḥ || 6||
skandhau pātu mahātejāḥ bhujau rāghava vākyakṛt |
karau me kaṅkaṇadharaḥ pātu khaḍgī nakhān mama || 7||
kukṣiṃ rāmapriyaḥ pātu pātu vakṣo raghūtamaḥ |
pārśve surārcitaḥ pātu pātu pṛṣṭhaṃ varānanaḥ || 8||
jaṭharaṃ pātu rakṣoghnaḥ pātu nābhiṃ sulocanaḥ |
kaṭiṃ bharatamantrī me guhyaṃ śrī rāmasevakaḥ || 9||
rāmarpitamanāḥ pātu liṅgamūrū smitānanaḥ |
kodaṇḍapāṇiḥ pātvatra jānunī mama sarvadā || 10||
rāmamitraḥ pātu jhaṅghe gulphau pātu sunūpuraḥ |
pādau nṛpatipūjyovyāt āntrau pātu sudhārmikaḥ || 11||
divase satyandhovyāt bhojane śarasatkaraḥ |
gamane kalakaṇṭhovyāt sarvadā lavaṇāntakaḥ || 12||
evaṃ śatrughna kavacaṃ mayā te samudīritaṃ |
ye paṭhanti narāsvetat te narāḥ saukhya bhāginaḥ || 13||
śatrughnasya varaṃ cedaṃ kavacaṃ maṅgalapradaṃ |
paṭhanīyaṃ narairbhaktyā putrapautra vivardhanaṃ || 14||
asya stotrasya paṭhena yaṃ yaṃ kāmaṃ narorthayet |
taṃ taṃ labhenniścayena satyametad vaco mama || 15||
putrārthī prāpnuyāt putraṃ dhanārthī dhanamāpnuyāt |
icchā kāmaṃ tu kāmārthī prāpnuyāt paṭhanādinā |
kavacasyāsya bhūmyā hi śatrughnasya viniścayāt || 16||
tasmādetat sadā bhaktyā paṭhanīyaṃ naraiḥ śubhaṃ |
ādau narairmāruteśca paṭhitvā kavacaṃ śubhaṃ || 17||
tataḥ śatrughna kavacaṃ paṭhanīyamidaṃ śubhaṃ |
paṭhanīyaṃ bhratasya kavacaṃ paramaṃ tataḥ || 18||
iti śrī śatakoṭirāmacaritāṃtargata śrīmadānandarāmāyaṇe
vālmikīye manoharakāṇḍe śrī śatrughnakavacaṃ saṃpūrṇaṃ ||
