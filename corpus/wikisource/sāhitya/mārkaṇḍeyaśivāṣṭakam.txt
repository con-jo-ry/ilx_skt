॥ mārkaṇḍeyaśivāṣṭakam ॥
sadā godāvaryāstaṭanikaṭavāsaṃ paśupatiṃ vadānyaṃ sarveśaṃ varadamakhalārthaikaghaṭanam । 
śivaṃ gaurīnāthaṃ śaśidharamanantārcitapadaṃ bhaje mārkaṇḍeya bhasitaniṭalaṃ bhavyanaṭanam ॥ 1
sadā kailāsādrisvapadamanaghaṃ sarvajagatāṃ prabhu śrīviśveśaṃ prakaṭitaparaṃdhāma saratham । 
naṭeśaṃ gaurīśaṃ lalitaravicandrānaladṛśaṃ bhaje ...॥ 2
mahādevaṃ sarvaṃ madhuripusakhaṃ maṅgalakaraṃ paraṃśambhuṃ sāmbaṃ puramathanamīśaṃ bhavaharam । 
smarāriṃ sarvajñaṃ smaraharamanādiṃ mṛgadharaṃ bhaje ... ॥ 3
pradoṣeṣu svāntaṃ prabalataratauryatrikakṛtaṃ trayatriṃśatkoṭitriśadapravaraiḥ saṃparivṛtam। 
ramāvāṇīndra śrīpati vidhikṛtollāsyakaraṇaṃ bhaje ...॥ 4
bhavānīhṛtpadmāruṇamahini nīlotpalagalaṃ sahasrāre padmāniśamavivasantaṃ suragurum । 
stutibhyo bhaktānāṃ nikhila sukhadaṃ susthirapadaṃ bhaje ... ॥ 5
śivastvaṃ viṣṇustvaṃ sakalabhuvanastvaṃ raviśaśī nabhastvaṃ kāyastvaṃ sakalajagadātmā tvamasi bho ।
yatirdeyaṃ sānte sukhatamakhilaṃ jaiminivaraiḥ bhaje ॥ 6
sadoccairāhvānaṃ haraharahareti pravacasā sadā bhaktyānando śivaśiva śiveti svamanasā । 
stutiṃ vāraṃ vāraṃ bhavabhavamabhaveti pratibhayā bhaje ... ॥ 7
yamāhūtaṃ bhītaṃ munivaranutaṃ pāśaniharti bhavantaṃ sadbhaktyā hṛdi śiśumavantaṃ karuṇayā। 
śaraṇyaṃ bhaktānāṃ paramagatimīśaṃ paraśivaṃ bhaje mārkaṇḍeyam ॥ 8
śrīkolācalasadvaṃśavāridhestu sudhākaraḥ ।
yajñeśo bhūsuraścakre mārkaṇḍeyaśivāṣṭakam ।। 9
