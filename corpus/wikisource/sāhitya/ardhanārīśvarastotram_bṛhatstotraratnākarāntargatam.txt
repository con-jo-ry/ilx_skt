Text:

॥ ardhanārīśvarastotram ॥

cāmpeyagaurārdhaśarīrakāyai
karpūragaurārdhaśarīrakāya ।
dhammillakāyaica jaṭādharāya
namaḥ śivāyai ca namaḥ śivāya ॥1
kastūrikākuṅkumacarcitāyai
citārajāpuñjavicarcitāya ।
kṛtasmarāyai vikṛtasmarāya namaḥ ...॥2
jhaṇatkvaṇatkaṅkaṇanūpurāyai
pādābjarājasphaṇinūpurāya ।
hemāṅgadāyai bhujagāṅgadāya namaḥ ...॥ 3
viśālanīlotpalalocanāyai
vikāsipaṅkerulocanāya ।
samekṣaṇāyai viṣamekṣaṇāya namaḥ ...॥ 4
mandāramālākalitālakāya
kapālamālāṅkitakandharāya ।
divyāmbarāyai ca digambarāya namaḥ ...॥ 5

ambhodharaśyāmalakuntalāyai
taṭitprabhātābhrajaṭādharāya ।
nirīśvarāyai nikhileśvarāya namaḥ ...॥ 6
prapañcasṛṣṭyunmukhalāsyakāyai
samastasaṃhārakatāṇḍavāya ।
jagajananyai jagadekapitre namaḥ ... ॥7
pradīptaratnojjvalakuṇḍalāyai
sphuranmahāpannagabhūṣaṇāya ।
śivānvitāyai ca śivānvitāya namaḥ ...॥8
etatpaṭhedaṣṭakamiṣṭadaṃ yo
bhaktyā sa mānyo bhuvi dīrghajīvī।
prāpnoti saubhāgyamanantakālaṃ
bhūyātsadā tasya samastasiddhiḥ ॥ 9

॥ iti śrīmacchaṅkarācāryaviracitaṃ ardhanārīśvarastotraṃ sampūrṇam ॥
