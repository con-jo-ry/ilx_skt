॥ śrī nīlakaṇṭhanamaśśivāyāṣṭakam ॥
namo namaḥ kāraṇakāraṇāya jaganmayāyāhitapāśakāya । 
tvatpādapadmāśritarakṣakāya śrī nīlakaṇṭhāya namaśśivāya ॥ 1
dhyāyanti ye tvāṃ yatayaḥ stuvanti hṛdaṃbuje dīpasamaprakāśam । 
nāsāpravinyastavilocanāya śrī nīlakaṇṭhāya namaśśivāya ॥ 2
sargasthitidhvaṃsanakāraṇāya yamādiyogāṣṭakasiddhidāya । 
tapasvināṃ śīghraphalapradāya śrī ... ॥ 3
vedādirūpāya trilocanāya vedāntavedyāya digambarāya । 
vibhūtiśubhrāya jaṭādharāya śrī ... ॥ 4
ugrāya saṃsārabhayāpahāya jñānapradāyāśusukhapradāya । 
kālāntakāya pralayānilāya śrī ... ॥ 5
vidhātṛrūpāya jagaddhitāya rameśarūpāya sadāśivāya । 
anantarūpāya caturbhujāya śrī ... ॥ 6 
viśvaikavandyāya kalādharāya samastalokaikasamāśrayāya । 
kuṭhārapāśāṅkuśapāline ca śrī ... ॥ 7
śarvāya sarvapriyakāraṇāya pinākine bāṇadharāya tubhyam । 
gaurīpriyāyākhilapālakāya śrī ... ॥ 8 
iti stutvā mahādevaṃ praṇamya ca punaḥ punaḥ । 
nidadhyū ṛṣayastatra surāḥ padmabhuvādayaḥ ।। 9
iti śrīskāndapurāṇe śrīgaralapurīnāthamāhātmye śrī nīlakaṇṭhanamaśśivāyāṣṭakam ॥
