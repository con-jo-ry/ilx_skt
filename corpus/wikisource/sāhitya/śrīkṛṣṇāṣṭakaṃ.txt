vasudevasutaṃ devaṃ kaṃsacāṇūra mardanaṃ |
devakīparamānaṃdaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vaṃde jagadgurūṃ || 1 ||

atasīpuṣpasaṃkāśaṃ hāranūpura śobhitaṃ |
ratnakaṃkaṇa keyūraṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vaṃde jagadgurūṃ || 2 ||

kuṭilālaka saṃyuktaṃ pūrṇacaṃdra nibhānanaṃ |
vilasatkuṃḍaladharaṃ devaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vaṃde jagadgurūṃ || 3 ||

maṃdāragaṃdha saṃyuktaṃ cāruhāsaṃ caturbhujaṃ |
bahirpiṃchāva cūḍāṃgaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vaṃde jagadgurūṃ || 4 ||

utphullapadma patrākṣaṃ nīlajīmūta sannibhaṃ |
yādavānāṃ śiroratnaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vaṃde jagadgurūṃ || 5 ||

rukmiṇīkeli saṃyuktaṃ pītāṃbaraṃ suśobhitaṃ |
avāpta tulasīgaṃdhaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vaṃde jagadgurūṃ || 6 ||

gopikānāṃ kucadvaṃdaṃ kuṃkumāṃkita vakṣasaṃ |
śrīniketaṃ maheṣvāsaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vaṃde jagadgurūṃ || 7 ||

śrīvatsāṃkaṃ mahoraskaṃ vanamālā virājitaṃ |
śaṃkhacakradharaṃ devaṃ kṛṣṇaṃ vaṃde jagadgurūṃ || 8 ||

kṛṣṇāṣṭakamidaṃ prātarutthāya yaḥ paṭhet |
koṭijanmakṛtaṃ pāpaṃ smaraṇena vinaśyati || 

phalaśruti ||

|| śrīkṛṣṇārpaṇamastu ||
