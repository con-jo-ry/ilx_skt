|| lakṣmīnārāyaṇastotram||
śrīnivāsa jagannātha śrīhare bhaktavatsala|
lakṣmīpate namastubhyaṃ trāhi māṃ bhavasāgarāt||1||
rādhāramaṇa goviṃda bhaktakāmaprapūraka
nārāyaṇa namastubhyaṃ trāhi māṃ bhavasāgarāt||2||
dāmodara mahodāra sarvāpattinivāraṇa|
hṛṣīkeśa namastubhyaṃ trāhi māṃ bhavasāgarāt||3||
garuḍadhvaja vaikuṃṭhanivāsinkeśavācyuta|
janārdana namastubhyaṃ trāhi māṃ bhavasāgarāt||4||
śaṃkhacakragadāpadmadhara śrīvatsalāṃchana|
meghaśyāma namastubhyaṃ trāhi māṃ bhavasāgarāt||5||
tvaṃ mātā tvaṃ pitā baṃdhuḥ sadgurustvaṃ dayānidhiḥ|
tvatto'nyo na paro devastrāhi māṃ bhavasāgarāt||6||
na jāne dānadharmādi yogaṃ yāgaṃ tapo japam|
tvaṃ kevalaṃ kuru dayāṃ trāhi māṃ bhavasāgarāt||7||
na matsamo yadyapi pāpakartā na tvatsamo'thāpi hi pāpahartā|
vijñāpitaṃ tvetadaśeṣasākṣin māmuddharārtaṃ patitaṃ tavāgre||8||
|| iti śrīvāsudevānandasarasvatīviracitaṃ śrīlakṣmīnārāyaṇastotram saṃpūrṇam ||
