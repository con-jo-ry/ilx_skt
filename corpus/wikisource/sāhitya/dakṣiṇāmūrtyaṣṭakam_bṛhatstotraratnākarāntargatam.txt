śrīdakṣiṇāmūrtyaṣṭakam

mandasmitaṃ sphuritamugdhamukhāravindaṃ kandarpakoṭiśatasundaradivyamūrtim ।
ātāsrakomalajaṭāghaṭirtendurekhamālokaye vaṭataṭīnilayaṃ dayālum ॥1
viśvaṃ darpaṇadṛśyamānanagarītulyaṃ nijāntargataṃ
paśyannātmani māyayā bahirivodbhūtaṃ yathā nidrayā ।
yassākṣātkurute prabodhasamaye svātmānamevādvayaṃ
tasmai śrīgurumūrtaye nama idaṃ śrīdakṣiṇāmūrtaye ॥ 2
bījasyāntarivāṅkuro jagadidaṃ prāṅnirvikalpaṃ śanairmāyākalpitadeśakālakalanā vaicitryacitrīkṛtam ।
māyāvīva vijṛmbhayatyapi mahāyogīva yassvecchayā tasmai... ॥ 3
yasyaiva sphuraṇaṃ sadātmakamasatkalyārthakaṃ bhāsate sākṣāttattvamasīti vedavacasā yo bodhayatyāśritām ।
massākṣātkaraṇādbhavenna punarāvṛttirbhavāmbhonidhau tasmai ...॥ 4
nānāchidraghaṭodarasthitamahādīpaprabhābhāsvaraṃ jñānaṃ yasya tu cakṣurādikaraṇadvārā bahiḥ spandate ।
jānāmīvi tameva māntamanumātyetatsamastaṃ jagattasmai ...॥ 5
dehaṃ prāṇamapīndriyāṇyapi calāṃ buddhiṃ ca śūnyaṃ viduḥ strībālāndhajaḍopamāstvahamiti bhrāntā bhṛśaṃ vādinaḥ।
māyāśaktivilāsakalpitamahāvyāmohasaṃhāriṇe tasmai ...॥ 6
rāhugrastadivākarendusadṛśo māyāsamācchādanātsanmātraḥ karaṇopasaṃharaṇato yo'bhūtsuṣuptaḥ pumān ।
prāgasvāpsamiti prabodhasamaye yaḥ pratyabhijñāyate tasmai ...॥ 7
bālyādiṣvapi jāmadādiṣu tathā sarvāskhavasthāsvapi vyāvṛttāsvanuvartamānamahamityantaṃ sphurantaṃ sadā।
svātmānaṃ prakaṭīkaroti bhajatā yo mudrayā bhadrayā tasmai ...॥ 8
viśvaṃ paśyati kāryakāraṇatayā svakhāmisambandhataśśiṣyācāryatayā tathaiva pitṛputrādyātmanā bhedataḥ।
svapne jāgratiṃ vā ya eṣa puruṣo māyāyaribhrāmitatasmai ...॥ 9
bhūrambhāṃsyanalo'nilo'mbaramaharnātho himāṃśuḥ pumānityābhāti carācarātmakamidaṃ yasyaiva mūrtyaṣṭakam ।
nānyatkiñcana vidyate vimṛśatāṃ yasmātparasmādvibhostasmai ॥10
sarvātmatvamiti sphuṭīkṛtamidaṃ yasmādamuṣmiṃstave tenāsya śravaṇāttadarthamananāddhyānācca saṅkīrtanāt ।
sarvātmatvamahāvibhūtisahitaṃ syādīśvaratvaṃ svataḥ sidhyettatpunaraṣṭadhā pariṇataṃ caiśvaryamavyāhatam ॥11
vaṭaviṭapisamīpe bhūmibhāge niṣaṇṇaṃ sakalamunijanānāṃ jñānadātāramārāt ।
tribhuvanagurumīśaṃ dakṣiṇāmūrtidevaṃ jananamaraṇaduḥkhacchedadakṣaṃ namāmi ॥12
citraṃ vaṭatarormūle vṛddhāśśiṣyā garuryuvā ।
gurostu maunaṃ vyākhyānaṃ śiṣyāstu chinnasaṃśayāḥ ॥
maunavyākhyāprakaṭitaparabrahmatattvaṃ yuvānaṃ
varṣiṣṭāntevasadṛṣigaṇairāvṛtaṃ brahmaniṣṭhaiḥ ।
ācāryendraṃ karakalitacinmudramānandamūrtiṃ
svātmārāmaṃ muditavadanaṃ dakṣiṇāmūrtimīḍe॥ 14
sunirmalajñānasukhaikarūpaṃ prajñānahetuṃ paramārthadāyinam ।
cidambudhau taṃ viharatamādyamānandamūrtiṃ gururājamīḍe॥ 15
yasyāntarnādimadhyaṃ na hi karacaraṇaṃ nāma gotraṃ na sūtraṃ
no jātirnaiva varṇā na bhavati puruṣo nā napuṃsaṃ na ca strī।
nākāraṃ naivakāraṃ na hi jani maraṇaṃ nāsti puṇyaṃ na pāpaṃ
tatvaṃ no tattvamekaṃ sahajasamarasaṃ sadguruṃ taṃ namāmi ॥ 16
alaṃ vikalpairahameva kevalaṃ mayi sthitaṃ viśvamidaṃ carācaram ।
idaṃ rahasyaṃ mama yena darśitaṃ sa vandanīyo gurureva kevalam ॥ 17
॥ iti śrīmacchaṅkarācāryaviracitaṃ dakṣiṇāmūrtyaṣṭakaṃ sampūrṇam ॥
